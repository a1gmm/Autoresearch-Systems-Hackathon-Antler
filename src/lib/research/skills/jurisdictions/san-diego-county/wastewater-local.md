## Two POTW Systems

San Diego County has two separate pretreatment programs depending on service area.

---

## A. San Diego County Sanitation District (Unincorporated County)

### Agency
**County of San Diego, Department of Public Works — Wastewater Management Section**
- Website: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/sdc/dpw/wasteh2o.html

### Governing Ordinances

| Ordinance | Title | Effective |
|---|---|---|
| **Ord. No. 10423 (N.S.)** | Provisions for Use of District Sewerage Facilities (Part I: Fees/Capacity; Part II: Industrial Pretreatment) | 2016 |
| **Ord. No. 10681 (N.S.)** | Amendment to Ord. 10423 | Subsequent to 2016 |

- Codified via reference to **SDCCO Title 6, Division 8, Chapter 2 (§ 68.201 et seq.)**.
- Part I of Ord. 10423 governs fees, sewer capacity, and wastewater discharge permits.
- Part II establishes industrial pretreatment requirements consistent with **40 CFR Part 403**

  https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/dam/sdc/dpw/SAN_DIEGO_COUNTY_SANITATION_DISTRICT/Ordinance-Policies/SDCSD-General_User_Requirements_Ordinances_(10423%2610681).pdf
- Ord. 10423 standalone:
  https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/dam/sdc/dpw/SAN_DIEGO_COUNTY_SANITATION_DISTRICT/Ordinance-Policies/Ord%2010423%20(2016),%20Provisions%20for%20Use%20of%20District%20Facilities%20(previously%20USO).pdf

### Industrial User Classification (Part II, Art. 3)

- **Class I (Categorical User):** Industrial user with discharges described in 40 CFR Parts
  410–471. All categorical pretreatment standards apply.
- **Class II User:** Industrial or commercial user whose process waste stream contains toxic
  or non-conventional pollutants of concern.
- **Class III User:** Industrial or commercial user whose process waste stream contains
  conventional pollutants (BOD, TSS, pH, fecal coliform, oil and grease).

### Significant Industrial User (SIU) Definition (Part II, Art. 3, § 33)

A user is a Significant Industrial User if it:
1. Is a Categorical User (Class I); or
2. Discharges an average of **25,000 gpd or more** of process wastewater to the POTW; or
3. Contributes a process waste stream representing **5% or more** of the treatment
   facility's hydraulic or organic capacity.

### Permit Requirement

Any commercial or industrial user whose wastewater discharge contains regulated
constituents must obtain a **Wastewater Discharge Permit (WDP)** from the District before
connecting (Ord. 10423, Part I, Art. 4, § 4.3; Part II). The Director (DPW Director) issues
all permits. New-construction WDPs are valid for the same period as the building permit.

### Local Limits

Ord. 10423 defines "Local Limits" as limits developed by the POTW to implement the
general and specific prohibitions of 40 CFR § 403.5. The ordinance cross-references SDCC
§ 68.201 et seq. for the specific numeric local limits applicable to industrial users.

**Note:** The ordinance text sets the framework and user classifications but delegates the
specific numeric discharge limit table (metals, pH, etc.) to the SDCCO Chapter 2 provisions
and to individual Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permits (IWDP). A separate local limits
study document is not publicly posted on the district's website as of the fetch date. Contact
DPW Wastewater Management for current numeric local limits applicable to a specific
discharge.

### Prohibited Discharges (Part II)

General prohibitions (40 CFR § 403.5) incorporated by reference. No user may introduce
pollutants that:
- Cause pass-through or interference at the POTW;
- Are fire or explosion hazard (flash point < 140°F);
- Cause corrosive structural damage (pH outside permitted range);
- Obstruct flow or cause POTW interference by solid or viscous substances;
- Contain toxic pollutants in sufficient amounts to injure workers or interfere with treatment.

### Fees

Capital Facility Capacity Fees and Annual Sewer Service Charges are set by separate fee
ordinance of the District. Fees available at County of San Diego PDS Department. Formula:

```
SSC = (n/N × D) + (f/F × MF) + (b/B × MB) + (s/S × MS)
```

Where n = EDUs assigned, f = annual flow, b = BOD load, s = SS load for a given user.

Commercial/industrial base rate: **1.2 EDU** per business unit.

## B. City of San Diego Metropolitan Wastewater — Industrial Wastewater Control Program

### Agency
**City of San Diego Public Utilities Department — Industrial Wastewater Control Program
(IWCP)**
- Website: https://www.sandiego.gov/public-utilities/permits-construction/industrial-user-permits
- Online permit portal: https://encromerr.epa.gov (ICIS/NetDMR)
- Contact: (619) 533-4170 (Industrial Pretreatment Section)
- Contact list PDF: https://www.sandiego.gov/sites/default/files/2025-09/iwcp-contact-list.pdf

### Governing Ordinance / Municipal Code

**San Diego Municipal Code (SDMC), Chapter 6, Article 4, Division 5**
- §§ 64.0400–64.0411 — General industrial wastewater provisions
- §§ 64.0500–64.0520 — Industrial Wastewater Control (permit program)
- Original Division 5 adopted 6-6-1983 by O-15984 N.S.; most recently amended 11-14-2000
  by O-18880 N.S.
- Full text: https://docs.sandiego.gov/municode/MuniCodeChapter06/Ch06Art04Division05.pdf

### Permit Types Administered

| Permit Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Significant Industrial User (SIU) permit | Major dischargers; comprehensive permit with effluent limits, monitoring, reporting |
| Enhanced Source Control | Pollution prevention requirements for non-SIU dischargers |
| Groundwater & Construction Dewatering | Excavation-related discharge to sewer |
| Batch Discharge Authorization | Temporary or episodic discharges |
| Trucked Waste Authorization | Off-site waste generators |
| Dental Amalgam | Mercury-containing discharge control (federal small business rule) |
| Self-Monitoring and Reporting | Periodic sampling requirements |

### SIU Definition (SDMC § 64.0500)

Significant Industrial Users include:
1. All categorical industrial users subject to 40 CFR Ch. I, Subch. N, Parts 405–471.
2. Any industrial user discharging an average of **25,000 gallons/day or more** of process
   wastewater.
3. Users contributing a process waste stream that is **5% or more** of the POTW's capacity.
4. Users with reasonable potential to adversely affect treatment operations.

### Application Process (New SIU Dischargers)

Submit **90 days before** beginning operations:
1. SIU Discharge Permit Application
2. Toxic and Prohibited Organic Chemical Management Plan
3. Baseline Monitoring Report (per 40 CFR § 403.12)

After submission, applicant attends a pretreatment conference. Application form:
https://www.sandiego.gov/sites/default/files/2024-05/app_non_siu.pdf

### Local Limits and Priority Pollutants

The IWCP regulates 126 priority pollutants including arsenic, benzene, cyanide, cadmium,
chromium, copper, lead, mercury, nickel, silver, and zinc, all of which are either prohibited
or strictly limited in the permit. Specific numeric local limits are set per permit based on
POTW headworks analysis. A published numeric local limits table is not posted on the public
website; contact IWCP at (619) 533-4170 for current facility-specific limits.

### Permit Fee

Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit fee collected annually from all permittees. Fee
amount set by City Council resolution with 10-day public notice (SDMC § 64.0508).

### Permit Duration

Permits issued for up to 5 years. If the city does not notify the permittee 30 days before
expiration, the permit is automatically extended one year (SDMC § 64.0505).
