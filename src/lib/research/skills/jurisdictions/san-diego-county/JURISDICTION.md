## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA / Hazardous Materials

- **Agency:** County of San Diego, Department of Environmental Health and Quality (DEHQ) — Hazardous Materials Division (HMD). HMD has been the CUPA for San Diego County since 1996; "HMD" and "CUPA" are used interchangeably by the county.
- **Jurisdiction:** Unincorporated county + many contract cities. City of San Diego has its own CUPA (San Diego Fire-Rescue Dept).
- **Regulatory authority:** California Health & Safety Code; California Code of Regulations (CCR); and **San Diego County Code of Regulatory Ordinances (SDCC)**.
- **Programs administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), CalARP, UST, APSA (above-ground petroleum storage), hazardous waste generator, medical waste.
- **Permit mechanism:** Unified Program Facility Permit (UPFP) — required for any business that handles/stores hazardous materials in HMBP-reportable quantities. All permit data reported through CERS (California Environmental Reporting System): https://cers.calepa.ca.gov
- **Fee schedule (FY 2025-2026):** https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/sdc/deh/hazmat/hmd_fees.html
  - UPFP fee detail: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/sdc/deh/hazmat/hmd_fees/upfp-fees.html
  - Fees are tiered by permit type and number of hazardous materials/wastes reported in CERS.
- **Contact:** (858) 505-6700
- Source: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/deh/hazmat/hmd_cupa.html
- Source: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/sdc/deh/hazmat/hmd_permits.html

### 2. Fire Code / AHJ

- **AHJ (unincorporated county):** San Diego County Fire (SDCFA) — San Diego County Fire Protection District
  - Site: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/sdc/sdcfa.html
- **AHJ (City of San Diego):** San Diego Fire-Rescue Department
- **Adopted code:** 2023 County Consolidated Fire Code, **8th Edition** — based on the 2022 California Fire Code (CFC) with local amendments. Codified in **Title 9, Division 6, Chapter 1** of the San Diego County Code of Regulatory Ordinances. Adopting ordinance: **Ord. No. 10836 (N.S.)**.
  - PDF: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/pds/docs/cosd-fire-code.pdf
- **Operational fire permits (unincorporated):** Required for hazardous materials storage/use, high-piled combustible storage (>12 ft), flammable/combustible liquids, and other CFC Chapter 1 permit activities. Unlisted project fees billed at standard hourly rate.
- **SDCFA approved fee schedule:** https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/dam/sdc/sdcfa/documents/prevention/approved-fees.pdf
- **Fire Mitigation Fee Program:** Separate development impact fee (SDCC Title 8, Division 10, Chapter 3) for new construction in unincorporated county — fee schedule FY 2024-25: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/dam/sdc/sdcfa/documents/fire-mitigation-fee/Fire%20Mitigation%20Fee%20Program%20Fee%20Schedule%20FY2024-25-Update.pdf
- **City of San Diego fire permit (hazmat/high-piled):** https://www.sandiego.gov/fire/services/permits/hazmat
- Source: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/pds/docs/cosd-fire-code.pdf

### 3. Industrial Wastewater / Pretreatment

**Two separate POTW systems serve San Diego County:**

#### A. San Diego County Sanitation District (unincorporated county)
- **Agency:** SD County Dept of Public Works — Wastewater Management Section
- **Governing ordinance:** **Ordinance No. 10423 (N.S.)** — "Provisions for Use of District Facilities" (effective 2016); amended by **Ord. 10681**. Together these establish industrial wastewater discharge permit (IWDP) requirements, pretreatment standards, and local limits consistent with 40 CFR Part 403.
- **Permit requirement:** Any commercial/industrial user proposing to discharge wastes containing regulated constituents must obtain a written IWDP from the District before connecting.
- **Contact/administration:** https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/sdc/dpw/wasteh2o.html
- Source: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/dam/sdc/dpw/SAN_DIEGO_COUNTY_SANITATION_DISTRICT/Ordinance-Policies/SDCSD-General_User_Requirements_Ordinances_(10423%2610681).pdf

#### B. City of San Diego Metropolitan Wastewater (service area within/adjacent to city)
- **Agency:** City of San Diego Public Utilities — Industrial Wastewater Control Program
- **Governing code:** San Diego Municipal Code, **Chapter 6, Article 4, Division 5** (§§ 64.0500–64.0520)
- **Permit types:** Significant Industrial User (SIU) permit, Categorical Industrial User permit, Batch Discharge Authorization, Groundwater Discharge Permit
- **Portal:** https://www.sandiego.gov/public-utilities/permits-construction/industrial-user-permits
- **Contact:** (619) 533-4170 (Industrial Pretreatment Section)
- Source: https://www.sandiego.gov/public-utilities/permits-construction/industrial-user-permits

### 4. Stormwater (MS4)

- **Program authority:** County of San Diego Dept of Public Works — Watershed Protection Program
- **Governing local ordinance:** **Watershed Protection, Stormwater Management and Discharge Control Ordinance (WPO)** — codified as **Chapter 8 of the San Diego County Code of Regulatory Ordinances**. Adopted by Board of Supervisors on January 27, 2016 (effective February 26, 2016); amended subsequently to track MS4 Permit amendments.
- **MS4 Permit:** NPDES Permit No. R9-2013-0001 (San Diego Regional Water Quality Control Board, Region 9) — applies to County as a co-permittee with incorporated cities.
- **Regulatory overview page:** https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/sdc/dpw/watersheds/ordinance.html
- **Regional Water Board (issuing authority):** https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/sandiego/water_issues/programs/stormwater/sd_stormwater.html
- Source: https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/san_diego/latest/sandiego_regs/0-0-0-129574

### 5. Zoning / Land Use

- **Governing document:** **San Diego County Zoning Ordinance** (standalone ordinance, distinct from municipal code) — accessible via AmLegal: https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/san_diego/latest/sdzoning/0-0-0-1
- **Industrial zone designations (unincorporated county):**
  - **M50** — Basic Industrial (Zoning Ordinance § 2520): general industrial, light manufacturing, warehousing, assembly. Broad permitted uses; limited commercial. Nuisance-generating uses acceptable if contained on site.
  - **M52** — Limited Impact Industrial (Zoning Ordinance § 2540): range of industrial and commercial uses associated with industrial operations (wholesaling, auto/truck repair, offices). All uses must be conducted within enclosed buildings; nuisance characteristics (noise, odor, traffic) minimized.
- **Discretionary / Major Use Permit (MUP):** Required for uses not permitted by right and for structures >60 ft height. Process governed by Zoning Ordinance §§ 7050–7099 (Administrative Permit Procedure) and Major Use Permit procedures.
  - County Discretionary Permit portal: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/pds/zoning/ZoningPermits.html
  - MUP application form (PDS-313): https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/pds/zoning/formfields/PDS-313.pdf
- **Administering agency:** Planning & Development Services (PDS), (858) 694-3962
- Source: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/sdc/pds/zoning.html
- Source: https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/san_diego/latest/sdzoning/0-0-0-1
