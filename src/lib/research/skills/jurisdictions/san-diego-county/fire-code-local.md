## Adopted Code Edition

**2023 County Consolidated Fire Code, 8th Edition** — based on the **2022 California Fire
Code (CFC)**, with local amendments adopted by the unincorporated-county fire protection
districts.

- Effective date: **April 13, 2023**
- Codified in: **Title 9, Division 6, Chapter 1** of the San Diego County Code of Regulatory
  Ordinances (SDCCO)
- Adopting ordinance: **Ord. No. 10836 (N.S.)**

## Local Amendments Structure

Amendments are prefixed `SEC. 96.1.XXX` where digits after the first three correspond to
the CFC section number (e.g., `SEC. 96.1.307.5` = CFC § 307.5). Key amendment
categories:

- Sec. 96.1.001 — Application of Consolidated Fire Code
- Sec. 96.1.002 — Explanation / scope
- Sec. 96.1.004 — Responsibility for enforcement
- Sec. 96.1.005 — **Geographic limits** (hazmat storage restrictions — see below)
- Sec. 96.1.104.13 — Cost recovery for emergency response
- Sec. 96.1.105.3.9 — Expense recovery
- Sec. 96.1.111 — Regional Fire Appeals Board
- Sec. 96.1.112.4 — Violations, penalties, compliance
- Sec. 96.1.202 — Definitions
- Sec. 96.1.325 — Mid-rise building requirements (sprinklers, standpipes, smoke detection)
- Sec. 96.1.326 — Storage of firewood (T14 1299.03 B2, Zone 1)
- Sec. 96.1.503 — Fire apparatus access roads (dimensions, grades, dead-ends)
- Sec. 96.1.3319 — Fuel modification zone requirements during construction
- Sec. 96.1.4902–4904 — Fire Hazard Severity Zones / WUI requirements
- Sec. 96.1.2808.10 — Greenwaste recycling/composting: permit required, financial
  assurance, notification, incoming waste diversion plan

## Geographic Limits for Hazmat Storage (Sec. 96.1.005)

These are **local amendments** to CFC Chapter 57 / 58 / 61 that restrict outdoor hazmat
storage county-wide:

### (a) CFC § 5704.2.9.6.1 — Class I & II liquids in above-ground tanks outside buildings
- **Prohibited** throughout the unincorporated county.
- **Exceptions:**
  1. Areas zoned for mixed, general, or high impact industrial uses.
  2. Crankcase draining in listed above-ground tanks ≤ 550 gallons (UL 2085) when
     approved by the fire code official.
  3. Above-ground storage in listed, specially designed containers (UL 2085-type) when
     approved by fire code official, with secondary containment; not allowed on residential
     or institutional property.

### (b) CFC § 5706.2.4.4 — Class I & II liquids in above-ground tanks (general)
- **Prohibited** throughout the unincorporated county.
- **Exceptions:**
  1. Non-residential zones, when approved by the fire code official.
  2. Crankcase draining tanks ≤ 550 gallons (UL 2085).
  3. Listed containers with secondary containment, when fire code official approves.

### (c) CFC § 5806.2 — Flammable cryogenic fluids in stationary containers
- **Prohibited** throughout the unincorporated county.
- **Exception:** Areas zoned for mixed, general, or high impact industrial uses.

### (d) CFC § 6104.2 — Bulk LPG storage (protection of congested areas)
- **Prohibited** throughout the unincorporated county.
- **Exception:** Areas zoned for mixed, general, or high impact industrial uses.
- **Additional exception:** Bulk tanks ≤ 30,000 gallons water capacity for above-ground
  storage supplying underground distribution to residential areas, where storage meets CFC
  requirements as determined by the fire code official.

## High-Piled Combustible Storage

The 2023 Consolidated Fire Code includes **CFC Chapter 32** (High-Piled Combustible
Storage) by reference; the county's access-road amendment at Sec. 96.1.503.1.3 cross-
references high-piled storage requirements for fire apparatus access. Operational permits
are required per CFC § 105 for high-piled storage exceeding 500 sq ft (Group A) or 2,500
sq ft (Group B) at heights over 12 ft. No county-specific amendment raises or lowers the
CFC Chapter 32 thresholds.

## Operational Fire Permits

CFC Chapter 1 permits are required in the unincorporated county for:
- Hazardous materials (storage, use, handling)
- Flammable/combustible liquid storage
- High-piled combustible storage
- Compressed gas systems (CFC § 105.5 categories)
- Greenwaste recycling/composting (Sec. 96.1.2808.10.1 — local permit required)
- Christmas tree lots (Sec. 96.1.105.5.52.1)

Unlisted or unusual projects billed at standard hourly rate per the SDCFA approved fee
schedule.

## Appeals

Regional Fire Appeals Board established by Sec. 96.1.111. Appeals of building permit
determinations, discretionary permit determinations, and other fire code matters handled by
separate tracks (Sec. 96.1.111.4.1–111.4.3).
