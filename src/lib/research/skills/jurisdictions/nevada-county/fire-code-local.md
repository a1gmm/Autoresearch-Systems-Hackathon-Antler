## Adopted Code

**Current edition:** 2025 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9)
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026 (applies to all building permit applications submitted on or after that date)
**Codified as:** Nevada County Code **Title 14** — Buildings and Construction (amends California Building Standards Code)
**Adopting action:** Nevada County Board of Supervisors unanimously approved an ordinance amending Title 14 to adopt the 2025 California Building Standards Code, December 2025. [Specific ordinance number not published online — VERIFY via Nevada County Clerk/CDA.]

> The 2025 code cycle also introduced the standalone **2025 California Wildland-Urban Interface Code (CWUIC, Title 24 Part 7)**, which replaces fire-hardening requirements formerly scattered across other code parts. The CWUIC applies to new construction in WUI areas.

Previous cycle: 2022 California Building Standards Code was effective January 1, 2023.

## Fire Safety Regulations — Title 4

Nevada County Code **Title 4 — Fire Safety Regulations** contains supplemental local fire-safety rules (driveway/fire-access standards, defensible space, etc.) that operate alongside the CFC. Key sections:

- **Chapter 3:** Private Driveway Construction Standards (§ 4.03.010 et seq.) — sets geometry, surface, turnout, and turnaround requirements for all new driveways; incorporated by reference into building permit review.
- The CFC is adopted "in its entirety"; CFC Chapter 5 governs Fire Apparatus Access Roads (applied through the CDA/permit process).

## AHJs by Zone

Nevada County has multiple fire AHJs depending on location:

| Area | AHJ |
|------|-----|
| State Responsibility Area (SRA) — unincorporated rural | **CAL FIRE Nevada-Yuba-Placer Unit (NEU)** |
| Western Nevada County unincorporated LRA (~143 sq mi, Alta Sierra, Banner Mountain, Hwy 49 corridor) | **Nevada County Consolidated Fire District (NCCFD)** — (530) 265-4431; https://www.nccfire.com/ |
| Penn Valley area LRA | **Penn Valley Fire District** |
| City of Grass Valley | **Grass Valley Fire Department** (also administers Nevada City FD under a 2020 administrative merger) |
| Town of Truckee (eastern county) | **Truckee Fire Protection District** — adopted 2025 CFC via **Ordinance 02-2025**, signed October 21, 2025; (530) 582-7850; https://www.truckeefire.org/fire-codes |

> The Grass Valley Fire Department and Nevada City Fire Department share administration under a single fire chief since 2020; the two cities and NCCFD also operate a Joint Operational Area (JOA) with closest-resource dispatch.

## WUI / High Fire Hazard Severity Zones

All of Nevada County's unincorporated area is substantially within Very High or High Fire Hazard Severity Zones (LRA and SRA). The county must adopt updated LRA FHSZ maps by ordinance within 120 days of state issuance.
