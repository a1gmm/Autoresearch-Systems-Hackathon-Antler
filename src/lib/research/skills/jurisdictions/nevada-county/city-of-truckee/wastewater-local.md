> Wastewater service for the Town of Truckee is split across two agencies: **Truckee Sanitary District (TSD)** operates the collection system (conveyance from premises to the plant); **Tahoe-Truckee Sanitation Agency (T-TSA)** operates the regional treatment plant. The Town of Truckee itself does not operate a POTW or administer a sewer-use ordinance — sewer permitting is TSD's function.

## Collection System: Truckee Sanitary District (TSD)

- **Agency:** Truckee Sanitary District — independent special district
- **Address:** 12304 Joerger Drive, Truckee, CA 96161
- **Phone:** (530) 587-3804 (after-hours emergencies: Option 2)
- **Hours:** Mon–Thu 7 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; Fri 7 a.m.–3:30 p.m.
- **Web:** https://www.truckeesan.org/
- **Service area:** ~39 square miles in Nevada and Placer counties; approximately 13,400 residential and 2,000 commercial connections; ~17,000 residents
- **Infrastructure:** ~400 miles gravity pipeline, 4,000 manholes, 17 miles pressure pipeline, 13 main lift stations, 31 smaller lift stations

## Industrial Pretreatment Program

**No formal industrial pretreatment program (IWP) has been identified for TSD.** TSD's customer base is predominantly residential and small commercial; search results and TSD's public-facing materials confirm TSD does not service heavy industrial customers, and small businesses/restaurants contribute only a small percentage of total flow. No mention of a 40 CFR Part 403 local pretreatment program is present in TSD's public documents.

Businesses with process wastewater above standard domestic strength should:
1. Contact TSD directly at (530) 587-3804 to confirm discharge acceptability
2. Inquire whether a specific discharge permit or industrial waste discharge agreement is required
3. Review any applicable categorical pretreatment standards (EPA 40 CFR Part 405 et seq.) that apply regardless of local program status

## Treatment Plant: Tahoe-Truckee Sanitation Agency (T-TSA)

- **Agency:** Tahoe-Truckee Sanitation Agency (T-TSA) — joint powers authority
- **Address:** 13720 Butterfield Drive, Truckee, CA 96161 / PO Box 669, Truckee, CA 96160
- **Phone:** (530) 587-2525
- **Web:** https://www.ttsa.ca.gov/
- **Service area:** Lake Tahoe western shore (California portion) and Truckee River Basin from Lake Tahoe to eastern limits of the Town of Truckee, including Donner Lake and surrounding lands; serves Nevada, Placer, and El Dorado counties
- T-TSA holds a Waste Discharge Requirement (WDR) permit from the Lahontan Regional Water Quality Control Board
- [VERIFY] T-TSA's NPDES/WDR permit number and any applicable local limits flowing down to industrial users

## Note on Town Role

The Town of Truckee does not operate a sewer utility or POTW, does not issue sewer-use permits, and does not administer a pretreatment program. The Town's Community Utilities & Services page routes residents to TSD and T-TSA: https://www.townoftruckee.gov/491/Community-Utilities-Services
