> The Town of Truckee is NOT its own CUPA. All six CalEPA unified programs for businesses within Truckee city limits are administered by the **Nevada County Community Development Agency — Environmental Health Department**, which is the countywide CUPA. Nevada County Environmental Health maintains a **Truckee/eastern-county field office** at 10075 Levon Ave., Suite 105, Truckee, CA 96161.

## Programs Administered

Nevada County Environmental Health implements all six CalEPA unified programs for Truckee businesses:

| # | Program | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) | Filed via CERS; Nevada County EH reviews and inspects |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator Program (HWG) | Generator ID, manifest compliance, inspections |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tanks (UST) | Permits, inspections, closure, leak response |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) | SPCC plans; CERS filing |
| 5 | California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) | RMP submissions; Nevada County EH is local implementing agency |
| 6 | California Fire Code Hazardous Materials Operational Permits | Coordinated with Truckee Fire Protection District (the fire AHJ) |

## HMBP Program

- **Filing system:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business
- **Who must comply:** Businesses storing, using, or handling hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities (H&S Code §25507 — generally 55 gallons liquid, 500 lbs solid, 200 cu ft gas)
- **CERS submissions reviewed by:** Nevada County Environmental Health (not a separate city CUPA)
- [VERIFY] Inspection frequency schedule and any Nevada County-specific local thresholds below state baseline

## UST Program

- Nevada County EH handles UST permitting, installation, modification, repair, closure, and unauthorized release response for Truckee businesses
- **Web:** https://www.nevadacountyca.gov/2184/Underground-Storage-Tank-Program
- [VERIFY] Local UST inspection scheduling requirements and advance notice period

## Aboveground Petroleum Storage (APSA)

- Facilities storing petroleum above APSA thresholds (≥1,320 gallons aggregate or single tank >660 gallons) must maintain SPCC plan
- Nevada County EH inspects for APSA compliance
- **Web:** https://www.nevadacountyca.gov/2157/Above-Ground-Petroleum-Storage

## Fees

- Fees are set by Nevada County Board of Supervisors fee schedule; hourly rates apply for work beyond minimum permit fee; re-inspection fees billed at Board-approved hourly rate
- [VERIFY] Current fee schedule — contact Nevada County EH at (530) 265-1222 Option 3 or the Truckee office at (530) 582-7884

## Relationship to Town and TFPD

- The Town of Truckee has no CUPA authority — CUPA is entirely the county's function
- The Truckee Fire Protection District (TFPD) is the fire AHJ and may coordinate on CFC hazardous materials operational permits, but TFPD does not administer the CUPA programs
- For facilities requiring both a CFC hazmat operational permit (from TFPD) and HMBP/CERS (from Nevada County CUPA), these are parallel requirements; coordinate with both agencies
