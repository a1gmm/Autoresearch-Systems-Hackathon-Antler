> The Town of Truckee does NOT have its own fire department or adopt its own fire code. Fire prevention and the fire AHJ function are held by the **Truckee Fire Protection District (TFPD)**, an independent special district. Town Ordinance 2025-09 (§3) explicitly defers fire code adoption to TFPD.

## Local Amendments

Resolution 22-2025 establishes findings of fact under H&S Code §§13869.7, 17958.5, and 17958.7 to support local amendments more stringent than state baseline. Local conditions cited: heavy snowfall (avg. 63 in./month Nov–Apr), winter avalanche risk, extended drought cycles, high WUI/wildfire exposure, steep terrain, seismic exposure (West Tahoe Fault), and tourist-traffic-induced response delays.

## Plan Review Process

- Plan review conducted during normal business hours, Mon–Fri; turnaround approximately 7–14 business days (excluding holidays)
- Residential projects within Town limits: submit through Town Building & Safety Division, which coordinates with TFPD
- Commercial projects: TFPD reviews plans directly; fire sprinkler/alarm plans are routed to **Engineered Fire Systems, Inc.** (third-party contractor) at 11832 Tammy Way, Grass Valley, CA 95949; (530) 274-9400
- Plan review fees due upon submittal; see **Fire Prevention Services Fee Schedule (effective 01/01/2026)** at https://www.truckeefire.org/ [fee schedule not directly available online — request from TFPD]

## Note on Town Adoption

Town of Truckee Ordinance 2025-09 (§3) states: "By adopting this Ordinance, the Town Council is not adopting the 2025 California Fire Code or ratifying changes and modifications thereto adopted by the Truckee Fire Protection District. Town staff are hereby directed to continue working with Truckee Fire Protection District staff regarding such changes and modifications, and to present an item regarding the 2025 California Fire Code to the Town Council for consideration when appropriate." The Town therefore does not maintain a separate fire code in TMC Title 15 — TFPD is the sole CFC authority.
