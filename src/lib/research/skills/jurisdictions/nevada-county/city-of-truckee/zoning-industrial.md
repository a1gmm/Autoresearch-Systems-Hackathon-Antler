> Industrial and manufacturing land uses in the Town of Truckee are regulated by **Title 18 — Development Code** of the Truckee Municipal Code (TMC). The Development Code was last substantially amended via Ordinance 2023-10; the zoning district table (Chapter 18.06) identifies four zones that accommodate industrial/manufacturing uses.

## Governing Code

- **Code:** Truckee Municipal Code, Title 18 — Development Code
- **Zoning districts chapter:** TMC §18.06 (Chapter 18.06 — Establishment of Zoning Districts)
- **Land use tables:** Chapters 18.08, 18.12, 18.16 (allowed uses and permit requirements by district)
- **Last major update:** Ordinance 2023-10 (July 13, 2023); Development Code page updated May 28, 2026
- **Development Code page:** https://www.townoftruckee.gov/269/Development-Code

## Industrial and Commercial/Manufacturing Zoning Districts

From TMC Table 2-1 (§18.06.020), the following districts accommodate industrial or manufacturing uses:

| Symbol | District Name | General Plan Land Use |
|---|---|---|
| **M** | Manufacturing/Industrial | Industrial |
| **CS** | Service Commercial | Industrial / Commercial |
| **DM** | Downtown Manufacturing/Industrial | Downtown Specific Plan — Industrial |
| **CG** | General Commercial | Commercial / Industrial / Public |

- **M (Manufacturing/Industrial):** The primary industrial zone; implements the General Plan "Industrial" designation.
- **CS (Service Commercial):** Implements General Plan "Industrial" and "Commercial"; intended for service-oriented businesses including those with an industrial component.
- **DM (Downtown Manufacturing/Industrial):** Applied to areas within the Downtown Study Area appropriate for manufacturing/industrial uses; implements the Downtown Specific Plan Industrial designation.
- **CG (General Commercial):** Can accommodate some industrial uses; implements both Commercial and Industrial General Plan designations.

> [VERIFY] Specific allowed-use tables for M, CS, DM, and CG districts (what manufacturing/industrial uses are permitted by right vs. requiring a use permit) are in Chapters 18.08–18.16. These chapters were not fully fetched; obtain the current PDF from https://www.townoftruckee.gov/269/Development-Code or contact the Community Development Department.

## Permit Requirements (General Framework)

TMC §18.06.040 establishes a tiered permit system:

| Permit Type | Trigger (non-residential) |
|---|---|
| **Zoning Clearance** (Ch. 18.72) | Change of use, new structures or additions < 7,500 sq ft GFA; or site disturbance < 26,000 sq ft |
| **Development Permit** (Ch. 18.74) | New structures or additions ≥ 7,500 sq ft GFA; or site disturbance ≥ 26,000 sq ft |
| **Minor Use Permit** (Ch. 18.76) + Zoning Clearance | Single-family additions > 7,500 sq ft; certain residential uses (see code) |
| **Use Permit / CUP** | Conditional uses as specified in allowed-use tables for each district |

- A **Conditional Use Permit (CUP)** — called "Use Permit" in the Truckee code — is required for uses listed as conditional in the land use tables for M, CS, DM, or CG zones. Process is governed by Chapter 18.76 (Minor Use Permit) or Chapter 18.78 (Use Permit), depending on permit tier.
- [VERIFY] Whether a Major Use Permit process exists separately from the Development Permit for large industrial projects.

## Notes

- **WUI overlay:** The Town is substantially within Fire Hazard Severity Zones; development in industrial zones near WUI boundaries may trigger additional fire safety requirements from TFPD (the AHJ). See fire-code-local.md.
- **CEQA:** Most new industrial development will require CEQA review; coordinate with Community Development.
- **Airport overlay:** The -AO (Airport Operations) overlay applies near the Truckee Tahoe Airport and may constrain certain industrial uses (height, use type). Confirm if a site is within the -AO overlay.
- **Snow/seismic:** Local building amendments (TMC Title 15 / Ordinance 2025-09) impose additional structural requirements for snow loads, frost depth, and seismic design that affect industrial building design.
