> Zoning reference for industrial land use in the **City of Nevada City**. Nevada City is a small historic city (~3,150 residents); industrial zoning is limited. Only one confirmed industrial district zone (LI — Light Industrial) was identified. Verify current zoning maps and allowed uses directly with the planning department before filing any land use application.

## Industrial Zone Districts

### Chapter 17.48 — LI (Light Industrial Zone)

- **Chapter:** 17.48 — LI Light Industrial Zone
- **Municode node:** `TIT17ZO_CH17.48LILIINZO`
- **Purpose:** Encourage sound industrial development of the non-nuisance type, providing and protecting an area for such development compatible with adjoining non-industrial uses
- **Confirmed Municode URL:** https://library.municode.com/ca/nevada_city/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT17ZO_CH17.48LILIINZO
- Section 17.48.020 covers permitted and conditional uses: https://library.municode.com/ca/nevada_city/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT17ZO_CH17.48LILIINZO_17.48.020PRPEUS

No "General Industrial" (GI/M-2) or heavy industrial zone was confirmed for Nevada City. Given the city's small size and historic character, the LI zone appears to be the only industrial district.

- Nevada City Planning Department reviews and acts on CUPs as discretionary applications
