> Nevada City is **NOT its own CUPA**. All six CalEPA unified programs for businesses located within Nevada City are administered by the **Nevada County Environmental Health Department** (the countywide CUPA). Route all HMBP, hazardous waste generator, UST, AST/APSA, CalARP, and tiered permitting inquiries to Nevada County.

## CUPA Identity

- **CUPA:** Nevada County Environmental Health Department (countywide CUPA — covers all incorporated cities and unincorporated areas of Nevada County)
- **Administering agency:** Nevada County Community Development Agency — Environmental Health Division
- **Address:** 950 Maidu Avenue, Suite #170, Nevada City, CA 95959 (PO Box 599002)

## Programs Administered

Nevada County Environmental Health administers all six CalEPA unified programs countywide, including within Nevada City:

| # | Program | Key trigger thresholds |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) | ≥55 gallons liquid, ≥500 lbs solid, or ≥200 cu ft gas (at standard T/P) |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator (HWG) | Any quantity of hazardous waste generated |
| 3 | Tiered Permitting (TP) | Onsite treatment/storage of hazardous waste |
| 4 | Underground Storage Tank (UST) | Ownership/operation of USTs |
| 5 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA/AST) | ≥1,320 gallons aggregate aboveground petroleum storage; single 55-gal container in underground area (TIUGA) |
| 6 | California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) | Regulated substances above CalARP thresholds |

## HMBP / CERS Reporting

- **Submission system:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/
- Electronic reporting required per CalEPA mandate (in effect since 2013)
- Annual update and resubmission required
- Any business storing hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities must obtain a Nevada County CUPA permit and file in CERS
- **CUPA Permit Application (Rev. 08/2025):** https://www.nevadacountyca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/59314/CUPA-Permit-App-w-ATP_Fillable-Rev-082025

## Permit Application Process

1. Download the CUPA Permit Application from the URL above (includes an Agreement to Pay form)
2. Complete CERS electronic reporting at https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/ (required before application can be processed)
3. Submit application with initial fee payment to Nevada County CDA at 950 Maidu Avenue
4. Nevada County Environmental Health reviews, inspects, and issues the permit
5. Fees are per the Board of Supervisors–approved fee schedule; invoices billed as services are rendered (payment due within 30 days)
