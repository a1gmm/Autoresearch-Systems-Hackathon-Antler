> Local fire code reference for the **City of Nevada City**. Fire services are contracted — Nevada City does NOT operate a stand-alone fire administration. Read the AHJ section carefully before routing any fire permit or inspection inquiry.

## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

Nevada City contracts its fire services to the **City of Grass Valley Fire Department** under a shared-administration arrangement that began with a 2018 pilot and became a full administrative merger in 2020. Nevada City Station 5 (201 Providence Mine Road, Nevada City) remains staffed under the arrangement.

Nevada City, Grass Valley, and the **Nevada County Consolidated Fire District** operate a long-standing Joint Operational Area (JOA) with boundary-drop / closest-resource response and shared station staffing.

## Adopted Fire Code Edition

Nevada City's fire code is codified in **Title 15 (Buildings and Construction)** of the Nevada City Municipal Code:

| Chapter | Title | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **15.04** | Building Standards | Adopts 2025 California Building Standards Code (Title 24, Parts 1–12) by reference |
| **15.08** | Fire Safety Standards and California Fire Code Amendments | Adopts 2025 California Fire Code (2025 Edition, incorporating and amending the 2024 International Fire Code) + local amendments |

- **Currently adopted edition:** 2025 California Fire Code (CFC 2025), effective statewide 2026-01-01
- The 2022 CFC was the previously adopted edition under the prior code cycle (referenced in Chapters 15.04 / 15.08 before the 2025 adoption ordinance)
- The 2025 adoption ordinance repealed and replaced Chapters 15.04 and 15.08; the adopting ordinance number was not confirmed online [VERIFY — check Municode codification history or City Clerk for ordinance number]
- Municipal code: https://library.municode.com/ca/nevada_city/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT15BUCO

## Defensible Space / Vegetation Management (Local Amendments)

Nevada City adopted **Ordinance No. 2023-03** which redesignated Chapter 10 of Title 8 as **Chapter 8.10 — Fire Control, Defensible Space, Burning, and Fireworks Regulations**. This chapter:

- Mirrors California Public Resources Code §4291 (defensible space) and Nevada County Code Title 2, Chapter IV, Article 7
- Establishes an ember-free zone of 0–5 feet from structures
- Prohibits open burning and fireworks within city limits
- Is enacted as a local amendment to the CFC as adopted in Chapters 15.04 and 15.08
- Fire Division Chief is the lead enforcement officer

Ordinance No. 2023-03 PDF: https://www.nevadacityca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/127/Nevada-City-Vegetation-Ordinance-PDF

## CAL FIRE and County Context

- Nevada City is within the SRA (State Responsibility Area) / LRA boundary zone — CAL FIRE coordinates with local departments
- CAL FIRE burn permits are required May 1 onward in Nevada County: https://www.nevadacountyca.gov/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/8712
- Open burning is **NOT ALLOWED** within Nevada City limits
- Nevada County fire-safety law page: https://www.nevadacountyca.gov/2750/California-Local-Fire-Safety-Law
