## CUPA Agency

**City of Grass Valley is NOT its own CUPA.**

The countywide CUPA is the **Nevada County Environmental Health Department**, which administers all Unified Program elements for businesses located within Grass Valley city limits.

- CUPA program page: https://www.nevadacountyca.gov/2144/Hazardous-Materials-CUPA
- Nevada County Environmental Health Department, 950 Maidu Avenue, Nevada City, CA 95959

## Programs Administered by Nevada County CUPA (for Grass Valley businesses)

| Unified Program Element | Administered by |
|---|---|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) / CERS reporting | Nevada County Environmental Health |
| Hazardous Waste Generator Program | Nevada County Environmental Health |
| Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program | Nevada County Environmental Health |
| Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) — SPCC compliance | Nevada County Environmental Health |

## Key Thresholds and Requirements

- **HMBP required** if business handles hazardous materials above CalARP/CERS threshold quantities. Electronic reporting via CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) required since 2013.
- **APSA permit required** if aboveground petroleum storage capacity is ≥ 1,320 gallons; must prepare a Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure (SPCC) Plan.
- Regulated businesses are inspected on a regular schedule; permit required for hazardous materials storage above threshold amounts.
