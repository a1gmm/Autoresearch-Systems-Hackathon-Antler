## Industrial Zone Districts (§ 17.24.020)

### M-1 — Light Industrial
Applied to areas appropriate for a range of **light industrial uses**. Implements the Manufacturing-Industrial designation of the General Plan.

### M-2 — General Industrial
Applied to areas appropriate for **heavy industrial activities** including manufacturing, assembly and processing, storage and distribution of raw materials, aggregate plants, and related uses that require locations removed from residential and visitor-serving uses. Implements the Manufacturing-Industrial designation of the General Plan.

## Permit Requirements (§ 17.24.030 and Table 2-10)

Uses in M-1 and M-2 are categorized as:

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| P | Permitted by right (subject to all Development Code standards) |
| MUP | Minor Use Permit required (Chapter 17.72.060) |
| UP | Use Permit required (Chapter 17.72.060) |
| S | Permit type determined by specific use regulations (Chapter 17.44) |
| — | Not allowed |

Representative uses and permit tiers from Table 2-10:
- **Concrete, gypsum and plaster product manufacturing**: permitted in M-2 (verify current table)
- **Crushing/sorting/recycling of concrete, asphalt, aggregate**: industrial uses in M-2
- **Outdoor storage and sales of building/construction materials**: industrial uses
- **Stump grinding and brush chipping**: industrial uses

A permitted land use (P) may also require a Development Review Permit (§ 17.72.030), a Building Permit, and/or other municipal permits.

## M-1 and M-2 Development Standards (Table 2-13, § 17.24.040)

| Feature | M-1 Light Industrial | M-2 General Industrial |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum lot area | 1 acre (width determined through subdivision review) | 1 acre (width determined through subdivision review) |
| Residential density | None allowed | None allowed |
| Front setback — Major Street | 20 ft from back of curb (buildings and parking) | 20 ft from back of curb |
| Front setback — Local Street | 30 ft from back of curb (buildings); 15 ft (parking) | Same |
| Interior side setback | 10 ft | 10 ft |
| Street-side setback | 15 ft from back of curb | 15 ft from back of curb |
| Rear setback | 10 ft | 10 ft |
| Maximum height | 50 ft / 4 stories | 50 ft / 4 stories |

## Use Permit / Minor Use Permit Process (§ 17.72.060)

- **Minor Use Permit (MUP)**: Approval authority is the Community Development Director (administrative review). Notice required; appeals to Planning Commission.
- **Use Permit (UP)**: Approval authority is the **Planning Commission**. Public hearing required. Conditions of approval may be imposed for compatibility, environmental mitigation, and operational standards.

Additional project-specific requirements may apply under § 17.30.070 (Performance Standards), which regulates noise, vibration, odor, dust, glare, and other nuisance factors for all development.
