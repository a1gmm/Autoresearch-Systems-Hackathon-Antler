## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

**City of Grass Valley Fire Department (GVFD)** is the fire AHJ for the city. GVFD is a municipal fire department; it is NOT served by CAL FIRE or the Nevada County Consolidated Fire District for structural fire prevention / inspection inside city limits. (Note: GVFD, Nevada City, and the Nevada County Consolidated Fire District share a Joint Operational Area (JOA) for closest-resource response, but GVFD is the sole AHJ for code enforcement within Grass Valley.)

Fire Code Official = Fire Chief of the City of Grass Valley or duly authorized representative (per Muni Code § 15.06.010, amending CFC Section 1.11.2.1.1).

## Adopted Code Edition

**2022 California Fire Code** (Title 24, Part 9; based on the 2021 International Fire Code) — adopted by **Ordinance No. 821** on **December 13, 2022**, effective January 12, 2023.

- Municipal Code location: **Chapter 15.04** (adoption) and **Chapter 15.06** (local amendments), within **Title 15 — Buildings and Construction**.
- Appendix Chapters adopted with the CFC: B, BB, C, CC, D, F, H, I, K, and N.

Note: The 2025 California Building Standards Code (Title 24) took effect statewide on January 1, 2026. The City's building-code-update cycle follows state adoption; verify whether a superseding ordinance adopting the 2025 CFC has been enacted for permit applications on or after 2026-01-01.

## Separate Fire Control Regulations (Chapter 8.16)

In addition to Title 15, the City maintains a separate fire-control chapter:

- **Municipal Code Chapter 8.16 — Fire Control Regulations** (Title 8 — Health and Safety).
- Most recently amended by **Ordinance No. 832** (adopted **December 10, 2024**), which repealed and replaced Articles II–V of Chapter 8.16, and **Ordinance No. 831** (urgency ordinance, adopted November 12, 2024).
- Earlier amendments: **O.824** (May 9, 2023), **O.823** (urgency, April 25, 2023), which added wildfire defensible-space rules.
- **O.835** designates Fire Hazard Severity Zones within the city.

Chapter 8.16 covers: defensible space / vegetation management, fireworks prohibition, fire hazard definitions, and enforcement. It is a separate layer from Title 15 (which covers construction/permitting under the CFC).

## Fire Hazard Severity Zone Status

A significant portion of the City of Grass Valley is classified as **Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone (VHFHSZ)** by CAL FIRE (cited in O.821 Findings of Fact). **Ordinance O.835** formally designates FHSZ boundaries within the city per Government Code § 51178.

## Defensible Space / Vegetation Management (Chapter 8.16)

- Zone 0: 0–5 ft from structure (ember-resistant zone)
- Zone 1: 5–30 ft from structure (lean/clean/green zone)
- Zone 2: 30–100 ft from structure (reduced fuel zone)
- Fire season: May 1 – December 31
- Vegetation removal required May 1 through October 31 (or end of fire season).
