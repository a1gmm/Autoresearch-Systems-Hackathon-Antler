## POTW / Sewer Authority

**City of Grass Valley Public Works Department** owns and operates the municipal sewer collection system and the **City of Grass Valley Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP)**.

- WWTP address: 556 Freeman Lane, Grass Valley, CA 95945
- Utilities Superintendent: Trever Van Noort, (530) 274-4371
- General inquiries: (530) 274-4350 or info@cityofgrassvalley.com

## NPDES Permit

- **NPDES General Order enrollee number: R5-2017-0085-019** (CAG585001)
- Regulated under the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 5) Municipal General Waste Discharge Requirements Order R5-2017-0085-01.
- WWTP is classified as a **Major** facility, Threat to Water Quality Category 2, Complexity A.
- **Design Average Dry Weather Flow (ADWF): 2.78 MGD**; Permitted ADWF: 2.78 MGD
- **Receiving water: Wolf Creek** (tributary to the Bear River, Bear River Watershed)
- Discharge Point 001: Lat 39°12'19"N, Long 121°04'10"W
- Service population: approximately 12,900

Note: In 2023, the Central Valley Water Board issued Administrative Civil Liability Order R5-2023-0511 for BOD and total coliform exceedances (Feb–Dec 2022); $12,000 MMP assessed. A compliance project was authorized in lieu of the penalty.

## Industrial Pretreatment Program

The City of Grass Valley WWTP **has an active Industrial Pretreatment Program** (confirmed by the 2021 NOA Table 1: Pretreatment Program = Yes). The threshold for a required pretreatment program (5 MGD design flow) does not apply here — the City has a program regardless, consistent with receiving industrial discharges.

Industrial dischargers must complete:
1. **Industrial Pretreatment Preliminary Survey** (general version or food establishment version, both available from Public Works)
2. **Wastewater Discharge Permit Application**
3. **Wastewater Treatment Plan** (submitted with permit application)

## Sewer Use Ordinance

The City maintains a **Sewer Use Ordinance** (available from Public Works). The governing municipal code title is **Title 13 — Public Utilities**. Specific references confirmed:

- **Municipal Code § 13.20.051** — FOG (Fats, Oils & Grease) removal system requirements for food service establishments and waste haulers. Requires grease interceptor registration and allows unannounced inspections.
- **Municipal Code Chapter 12.06** — Storm Water Management and Discharge Control (separate from sewer use)
- **Municipal Code Chapter 13.12** — Sewer System (per Municode chapter structure)

## FOG Program

All food service establishments and waste haulers must register with the FOG program. Contact: Catharine Dykes, catharined@cityofgrassvalley.com.

## Prohibited Discharges (per city guidance)

Grease, pharmaceuticals, herbicides, oil-based paints/solvents, pool/spa chlorine, and toxic substances are prohibited from the sewer system.
