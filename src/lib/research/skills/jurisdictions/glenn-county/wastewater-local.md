## System Overview

Glenn County has **no county-operated POTW**. Wastewater jurisdiction is split between the two incorporated cities:

| Entity | POTW Operator | Sewer Use Ordinance |
|---|---|---|
| County (unincorporated) | None — on-site/septic systems | County Environmental Health (PCDSA) regulates septic |
| City of Willows | City-operated POTW | Willows Municipal Code Ch. 13.10 (Ord. 639-93) |
| City of Orland | City-operated POTW | [VERIFY — Orland ordinance not confirmed] |

Industrial facilities on unincorporated land that need sewer service must annex to a city or obtain a connection agreement; they are not covered by a county pretreatment program.

## City of Willows POTW

- **Facility:** Completed $10.3 million upgrade ca. 2007. Treats to near drinking-water quality; final effluent discharged to the Glenn-Colusa Irrigation District canal.

### Prohibited Discharges — § 13.10.160

The following are **prohibited from discharge to the Willows public sewer** (Ord. 639-93):

| # | Category | Limit / Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rainwater / uncontaminated water | Prohibited — no stormwater, groundwater, yard drainage into sanitary sewer |
| 2 | Explosive / flammable substances | Flashpoint **< 190°F** prohibited (gasoline, benzene, naphtha, fuel oil, solvents) |
| 3 | Corrosive materials | **pH < 5.5** prohibited; or any waste capable of damaging structures/equipment/personnel |
| 4 | Obstructions / viscous solids | Ashes, sand, grease, metal, glass, tar, plastics, wood — any solid causing flow obstruction |
| 5 | Garbage | Only properly shredded garbage (<5% dry weight retained on 3/8" screen) from dwellings or restaurants |
| 6 | Petroleum products | Mineral oils, non-biodegradable petroleum products, cutting oils forming persistent emulsions |
| 7 | Suspended solids | Industrial process water with SS requiring unusual WWTP handling; SS dimension max **1/4-inch mesh** |
| 8 | Cooling water / pool drains | Prohibited without special written permission from Director of Public Works |
| 9 | Toxic substances | Any toxic/poisonous substance in quantities hazardous to humans, animals, plant life, or the treatment process |
| 10 | Chemical pesticides | Chlorinated hydrocarbons, organophosphates, and similar compounds at any concentration — prohibited except by written permit |
| 11 | Oxidizing / reducing agents | Strong oxidizing and reducing agents prohibited |
| 12 | Non-degradable wastes | Substances not amenable to treatment or causing effluent to fail discharge requirements |
| 13 | Unusual waste concentrations | (a) Unusual inert TSS; (b) Excessive discoloration; (c) Unusual BOD/COD/chlorine demand constituting a significant load; (d) Slug discharge — any discharge exceeding **5× average 24-hour concentration or flow** for more than 15 minutes |
| 14 | Noxious / malodorous substances | Any substance creating a public nuisance or extraordinary hazard in sewers |
| 15 | Extreme temperatures | Temperature **> 150°F** or **< 33°F** prohibited |
| 16 | Effluent / sludge violations | Any wastewater causing NPDES effluent violations or precluding cost-effective sludge disposal |

**Note:** No published numerical local limits table (specific mg/L values for BOD, TSS, metals) was found for the City of Willows. The ordinance applies narrative/categorical prohibitions consistent with the NPDES general pretreatment framework. The provisions are "subject to regulations by state and federal authorities and the terms and conditions of the NPDES permit issued to the city" (WMC § 13.10.180). Any industrial discharger should confirm with the City Director of Public Works whether local numerical limits have been established by resolution.

### Industrial Discharger Requirements — § 13.10.170

- **Waste Discharge Report:** Director of Public Works may require a written Waste Discharge Report within 30 days of written notification. Report must include: nature of process, volume, flow rates, substances and concentrations. Failure to file may result in disconnection.
- **Sampling Facilities:** Industrial dischargers must, at their own expense, install a sampling manhole and other metering/monitoring facilities as required.
- **Special Agreements:** Director of Public Works may enter into special agreements with industrial dischargers for unusual waste streams (§ 13.10.030 definition of "Industrial connection" includes washing machines, bottle washers, parts washers, creamery equipment, wash racks).
