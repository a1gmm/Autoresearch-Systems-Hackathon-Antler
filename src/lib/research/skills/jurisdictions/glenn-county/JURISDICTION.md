Glenn County is a small rural agricultural county (~30,000 residents) in the Northern Sacramento Valley Air Basin. The two incorporated cities are Willows (county seat) and Orland.

## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air — Glenn County APCD

- **Controlling district:** Glenn County Air Pollution Control District (GCAPCD) — an independent special district administered under the Agriculture / Weights & Measures Department. Covers the entire county including both cities.
- **Air basin:** Sacramento Valley Air Basin (non-attainment for ozone and PM2.5 under state and federal standards).
- **Permit program:** Authority to Construct (ATC) required before installing any stationary source. Permit to Operate (PTO) required before operating. ATC valid 2 years; PTO not transferable by location, equipment, or person (GCAPCD Article III, Sec. 50).
- **NSR threshold:** 25 tons per year of any non-attainment pollutant or its precursors triggers New Source Review with BACT and emission offsets (Sec. 51). Offsets required for modified sources whose potential-to-emit exceeds 25 tpy after modification.
- **CalARP / RMP:** RMP submittals go to the GCAPCD — **not** to the CUPA. This is a local structural quirk (see CUPA section).
- **Permit exemptions** (GCAPCD Sec. 57): No ATC/PTO required for: agricultural irrigation engines, comfort HVAC not tied to process equipment, food-preparation equipment in eating establishments, space heaters (natural gas/LPG), mobile construction equipment, identical replacements, structural changes that cannot change emissions, repairs/maintenance without structural changes, and other minor sources designated by the APCO.
- **Fee schedule:** Published as "Exhibit A – Schedule of Fees" in the GCAPCD Rule Book. [VERIFY current fee document at district regulations page.]

### CUPA — Glenn County Agriculture / Weights & Measures

- **CUPA agency:** Glenn County Agriculture / Weights & Measures Department is the certified CUPA for the **entire county, including the Cities of Orland and Willows**. No city-level Participating Agency (PA) structure is confirmed; HMBP/program is stated county-administered countywide.
- **Address:** 720 N. Colusa Street, Willows, CA 95933.
- **Six unified program elements administered:**
  1. Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) / Area Plan
  2. California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) — Note: administered by GCAPCD, not the CUPA proper
  3. Underground Storage Tank (UST) program
  4. Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) program
  5. Hazardous Waste Generator program
  6. Tiered Permitting (hazardous waste)
- **HMBP reporting:** Electronic via California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) at https://cers.calepa.ca.gov.
- **Dominant facility type:** Agricultural — farms with aboveground petroleum and pesticide/chemical storage are the primary regulated population.

### Wastewater — City-operated POTWs; no county sewer system

- **County (unincorporated):** No county-operated POTW. Unincorporated parcels use septic/on-site systems regulated by Glenn County Environmental Health (under PCDSA). No county sewer use ordinance.
- **City of Willows POTW:** City-operated. Completed $10.3M upgrade ~2007 (treats to near drinking-water quality; discharges to Glenn-Colusa Irrigation District canal). ### Stormwater

- **Regional permit authority:** Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 5) issues NPDES permits for Glenn County.
- **MS4 status:** Glenn County and its cities are below the Phase I 100,000-population threshold. Phase II Small MS4 general permit coverage is the likely instrument, but county-specific permit number not confirmed.
- **Construction stormwater:** Statewide Construction General Permit (CGP) applies to projects ≥1 acre of disturbance.
- **Industrial stormwater:** Statewide Industrial General Permit (IGP) applies to covered SIC codes.

### Zoning — Industrial Districts

**Glenn County (unincorporated) — Title 15 Unified Development Code:**
- **M Zone (Industrial):** Chapter 15.440. Single industrial zone designation (no M-1/M-2 split). Minimum parcel size 20,000 sq ft (1 acre if well/septic served). Maximum height 75 ft. Setback requirements at § 15.440.060. Permitted uses include warehouse/distribution (§ 15.440.020.B). CUP process for conditional uses via Planning Commission.
- **MP Zone (Industrial Park):** Chapter 15.510. Applies specifically to the Orland Airport Industrial Park (OAIP) — nine parcels, ~64.37 total acres. 14 permitted heavy commercial and manufacturing uses (§ 15.510.030); 4 conditionally permitted uses requiring CUP (§ 15.510.040). Site Plan Review required before building permit issuance. Design standards also apply (published separately as OAIP Design Standards document).

## Notes / GOTCHAs

- **GOTCHA:** CUPA is in the **Agriculture / Weights & Measures Department** — not Environmental Health. Environmental Health sits under PCDSA. Agents searching for "environmental health" to find the CUPA will land in the wrong place.
- **GOTCHA:** CalARP/RMP submittals go to the **GCAPCD**, not the CUPA. This split is unique to Glenn County.
- **GOTCHA:** There is **no county POTW**. Industrial wastewater questions for unincorporated sites go to on-site/septic system regulations or, if near city limits, to the applicable city's sewer utility.
- **GOTCHA:** The county's industrial zone is simply **"M"** (not M-1/M-2). The airport industrial park is **"MP"** — a separate, more restrictive zone. Do not conflate.
- **Agricultural dominance:** The dominant regulated EHS population is farms and agricultural processing, not heavy industry. Petroleum aboveground storage (APSA) and pesticide/chemical storage HMBPs are the most common CUPA file types.
