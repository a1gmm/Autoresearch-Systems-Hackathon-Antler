## CUPA Agency

**Name:** Glenn County Agriculture / Weights & Measures Department — Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA)
**Address:** 720 N. Colusa Street, Willows, CA 95933
**Coverage:** Entire county, including the incorporated Cities of Orland and Willows (no city-level Participating Agency structure confirmed; program administered countywide).
**No sub-agency split:** Both cities fall under the county CUPA umbrella for all six program elements.

> GOTCHA: The CUPA is housed in the **Agriculture / Weights & Measures Department**, not Environmental Health. Environmental Health is a separate division of the Planning & Community Development Services Agency (PCDSA).

## CalARP / RMP — GCAPCD, Not the CUPA

RMP submittals and CalARP program administration are handled by the **Glenn County Air Pollution Control District (GCAPCD)**, not the CUPA proper. This is a structural split specific to Glenn County.

- CalARP contact / RMP page: https://www.countyofglenn.net/government/departments/agriculture-weights-measures/cupa-certified-unified-program-agency/calarp-risk-management-plans
- GCAPCD administers CalARP under the unified program framework but as a distinct district function.

## HMBP Program

- **Reporting portal:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov
- **Threshold:** Any business that handles a hazardous material at or above the state threshold quantities (55 gallons liquid / 500 lbs solid / 200 cu ft compressed gas, or any quantity of an extremely hazardous substance) must submit an HMBP.
- **Dominant facility type:** Agricultural operations with aboveground petroleum storage (diesel, gasoline), pesticide/chemical storage, and fertilizers are the most common regulated businesses. APSA filings are a primary workload.
- **Inspection program:** Routine compliance inspections of all regulated facilities plus complaint-based investigations.

Source: https://www.countyofglenn.net/government/departments/agriculture/cupa-certified-unified-program-agency/hazardous-materials-business-plan
