## Adopted Fire Code — County (Unincorporated)

- **Adopted edition:** 2019 California Fire Code (California Code of Regulations, Title 24, Part 9), based on the International Fire Code 2018 (IFC 2018).
- **Adopting ordinance:** **Ordinance No. 1285** (adopted 2019).
- **Applicability:** Declared to be the fire code for the **unincorporated areas of Glenn County**.
- **[VERIFY]** Whether the county has adopted the 2022 California Fire Code (effective statewide 2023) to replace Ordinance 1285. Check the live page: https://www.countyofglenn.net/california-fire-code-adopted
- **County code location:** Glenn County Code is maintained at https://glenn.municipalcodeonline.com/ — Title 15 (Unified Development Code) governs land use; fire code chapter number within the County Code not confirmed from public search; **[VERIFY]** at municipalcodeonline.

## Fire Authority Jurisdictions (AHJ Map)

Glenn County has **no single county fire department**. The AHJ depends on location:

| Area | AHJ | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Unincorporated (general) | Multiple independent FPDs | See list below |
| City of Willows | Willows Fire Department | Municipal department |
| City of Orland | Orland Volunteer Fire Dept / Orland (Rural) FPD | Partnership; shared paid Fire Chief since 2019 |
| Wildland interface | CAL FIRE | Wildland fire support statewide |

**Named Fire Protection Districts in Glenn County (unincorporated AHJ):**
- Orland Fire Protection District
- Ord Fire Protection District
- Glenn-Codora Fire Protection District
- Artois-Glenn County Fire Protection District
- Kanawha-Glenn County Fire Protection District
- Hamilton City Fire Protection District

Each FPD is an independent special district; each sets its own inspection program and may have separate permit applications. **[VERIFY]** which CFC edition each FPD has formally adopted.

## Local Amendments

- **[VERIFY]** — No specific local amendments to the 2019 CFC were identified in public records for Glenn County. The county ordinance adopts the CFC by reference. Contact the relevant FPD or the county Building Division to confirm whether any local amendments are attached to Ordinance 1285 or its successors.

## Permit Types and Fees

- **Building/fire permit fees:** Published under "Building Permit Fee Resources" at https://www.countyofglenn.net/resources/fees-forms/building-permit-fee-resources
- **[VERIFY]** Specific fire permit fee schedule — not extracted from public sources. Each FPD may set its own operational fire permit fees independently. CUPA fee schedule (Master Fee Schedule 2024) covers CUPA-related inspections, not necessarily general fire permits.

## Inspection Program

- The California Fire Code (adopted via CFC Part 9) establishes requirements for fire safety inspections of regulated occupancies (high-piled storage, spray booths, tank farms, hazmat facilities, etc.).
- Individual FPDs conduct annual business inspections for fire code compliance. Serious violations require immediate correction; standard violations get 15 calendar days per FPD standard practice.
- Third inspection for same violation may trigger re-inspection fees (practice observed at Orland FPD level — **[VERIFY]** fee amount).
