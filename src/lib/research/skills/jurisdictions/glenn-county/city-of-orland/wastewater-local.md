The city POTW serves a small rural community; based on system size (estimated flow well below 5 MGD), a **mandatory federal industrial pretreatment program** (40 CFR Part 403) is not required. However, dischargers must meet:
- Categorical Pretreatment Standards (federal) applicable to their industrial category
- Local prohibitive discharge standards under Title 13

## Industrial Wastewater Permit (IWP) Process

No dedicated online industrial wastewater permit (IWP) portal was identified for City of Orland. Industrial dischargers should:
1. Contact City of Orland Public Works at (530) 865-1610 to determine if a local industrial discharge permit is required.
2. Confirm applicable categorical pretreatment standards via U.S. EPA (40 CFR Parts 405–471).
3. Submit any required monitoring reports or baseline reports per local ordinance.

## Stormwater (Context)

City of Orland Public Works also maintains the storm drain system (separate from the sanitary sewer). Industrial facilities must separately obtain coverage under the **California General Industrial Activities Stormwater Permit (IGP)** via SMARTS at https://smarts.waterboards.ca.gov/ — the POTW does not administer this permit.
