## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air

- **Controlling district: Glenn County Air Pollution Control District (GCAPCD)** — countywide, covers all of Glenn County including the City of Orland. Issues Authority to Construct (ATC) and Permit to Operate (PTO) for stationary sources. NOT part of the Sacramento Metropolitan AQMD or Bay Area AQMD.
- Participates in the Sacramento Valley Basinwide Air Pollution Control Council for basin-wide coordination.
- GCAPCD is co-located with the Agriculture / Weights & Measures Department.
- Contact: 720 N. Colusa St. (P.O. Box 351), Willows, CA 95988; (530) 934-6500.
- Source: https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/glenn-county-air-pollution-control-district

### CUPA (Unified Hazardous Materials Program)

- **Not its own CUPA** — Glenn County Agriculture / Weights & Measures Department is the certified CUPA for all of Glenn County, including incorporated cities. Administers all six state-mandated unified program elements:
  1. Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)
  2. California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) / Risk Management Plan
  3. Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program
  4. Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)
  5. Hazardous Waste Generator / Tiered Permitting
  6. Area Plan
- CUPA contact: Glenn County Ag/W&M Dept., 720 N. Colusa St., Willows, CA 95988; (530) 934-6500.
- Fee schedule: Glenn County Master Fee Schedule 2024 (Chapter 5.23 Appendix A). Direct URL: https://www.countyofglenn.net/sites/default/files/resources/Master%20Fee%20Schedule%202024.pdf [VERIFY exact CUPA line-item fees from PDF]

### Zoning

- Zoning code: **Title 17 – Zoning**, Orland Municipal Code. Two industrial zone designations:
  - **M-L (Limited Industrial Zone)** — Chapter 17.48: light industrial, limited manufacturing, warehousing. Storage containers permitted with site plan review (§17.76.170).
  - **M-H (Heavy Industrial Zone)** — Chapter 17.52: heavy industrial and manufacturing. Storage containers permitted with Administrative Use Permit (AUP) (§17.76.170).
- Zoning districts overview: R-1, R-2, R-3 (residential); C-1, C-2, DT-MU, C-H (commercial); M-L, M-H (industrial); O-S, P-D, P-F, F-W (special).
- Orland Airport Industrial Park (OAIP): 65-acre county-owned park ~1 mile SE of city, zoned MP (Industrial Park) under Glenn County Code Chapter 15.510. Nine parcels 3–18 acres. Phase I infrastructure (sewer, water, power) in place.
- CUP (Conditional Use Permit) process applies to conditionally permitted uses in M-L and M-H zones.
- Ordinance 2022-01: added Chapter 17.18 (Objective Design Standards for multifamily/SB 35 projects). Not an industrial zoning change.
