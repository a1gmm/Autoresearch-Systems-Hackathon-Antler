## CUPA Agency

**City of Orland does not have its own CUPA.** The City is served countywide by:

**Glenn County Agriculture / Weights & Measures Department**
Role: Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all of Glenn County, including all incorporated cities (Orland, Willows).
Address: 720 N. Colusa Street (P.O. Box 351), Willows, CA 95988
Phone: (530) 934-6500
Website: https://www.countyofglenn.net/government/departments/agriculture-weights-measures/cupa-certified-unified-program-agency

There is no separate local hazmat ordinance specific to the City of Orland — all CUPA enforcement authority flows through the county Ag/W&M Department under CalEPA certification.

## Programs Administered (Six State-Mandated Elements)

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — Required for businesses that handle hazardous materials at or above reportable quantities. Must be submitted via California Environmental Reporting System (CERS): https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/
   - Source: https://www.countyofglenn.net/government/departments/agriculture/cupa-certified-unified-program-agency/hazardous-materials-business-plan

2. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) / Risk Management Plan (RMP)** — Required for facilities with regulated substances above threshold quantities. Program administered countywide by Glenn County CUPA.

3. **Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program** — Oversight of UST systems to prevent releases into groundwater. Source: https://www.countyofglenn.net/government/departments/agriculture/cupa-certified-unified-program-agency/underground-storage-tanks

4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure (SPCC) compliance for petroleum storage. Source: https://countyofglenn.net/dept/agriculture/cupa-certified-unified-program-agency/above-ground-storage-tanks

5. **Hazardous Waste Generator / Tiered Permitting** — Oversight of hazardous waste generation, storage, and disposal. Tiered permits (Conditionally Exempt, Conditionally Authorized, Permit by Rule, and Full Permit). Source: https://www.countyofglenn.net/government/departments/agriculture/cupa-certified-unified-program-agency/hazardous-waste | https://www.countyofglenn.net/cupa-certified-unified-program-agency/tiered-permitting

6. **Area Plan (Emergency Response)** — Coordinated emergency response planning for hazardous materials incidents countywide.
