## Fire Authority (AHJ)

City of Orland does not operate an independent fire department. Fire prevention, inspections, and code enforcement are handled through a joint arrangement:

- **City of Orland Building Department** — issues building permits; enforces adopted codes for new construction.
  Address: 815 Fourth Street, Orland, CA 95963
  Phone: (530) 865-1606
  Hours: Mon–Thu for inspections; Fri by appointment
  Online permits: https://orlandciteng.portal.iworq.net/portalhome/orlandciteng

- **Orland Fire Protection District (OFPD) / Orland Volunteer Fire Department (OVFD)** — provides fire inspection and prevention services for both city and surrounding unincorporated district. City and district share a paid Fire Chief (hired jointly in 2019).
  Address: 810 Fifth Street, Orland, CA 95963
  Phone: (530) 865-1625
  Website: https://orlandfire.specialdistrict.org/

## Open Burn Permits

Open burn permits are issued by the Orland Fire Protection District / OVFD. Applicants must:
1. Complete the fire permit application.
2. Notify the Shift Battalion Chief at OFPD 24 hours prior to burn.
- Note: the contact number (708) 349-1037 appearing on orlandfire.org is from Orland Park, IL (wrong jurisdiction). Correct contact is the OFPD Orland CA: (530) 865-1625.

## Inspection Process

Annual business and multi-family building inspections conducted by Orland Fire Protection District:
- Inspections are typically unannounced.
- A business representative should accompany the fire inspector.
- Violations: 15 calendar days to correct; re-inspection follows.
- Persistent non-compliance: second notice → third inspection ($50.00 fee) → referral to municipality for court action.
