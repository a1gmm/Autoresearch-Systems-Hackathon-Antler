## M-L — Limited Industrial Zone (Chapter 17.48)

**Chapter:** 17.48
**Municode URL:** https://library.municode.com/ca/orland/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT17ZO_CH17.48LIINZO

**Character:** Light industrial, limited manufacturing, warehousing, and compatible commercial uses. Lower-impact industrial activities designed to be compatible with adjacent uses.

**Storage Containers:** Permitted as accessory use with **site plan review** (per §17.76.170).

**Accessory Uses/Buildings:** Section 17.48.090 addresses accessory use and building standards.

## CUP / Discretionary Permit Process

- **Administrative Use Permit (AUP):** Ministerial/administrative review for certain accessory or lower-impact uses (e.g., storage containers in M-H zone).
- **Conditional Use Permit (CUP):** Planning Commission review for conditionally permitted uses. Chapter 17.40 includes references to CUP process (§17.40.145 – Special provisions, §17.40.010 – Purpose and application).
- **Site Plan Review:** Required for M-L storage containers and potentially other uses. Administrative-level review.

## Orland Airport Industrial Park (OAIP)

Separate from city M-L/M-H zoning. The OAIP is a Glenn County-managed 65-acre industrial park located approximately 1 mile southeast of city limits (unincorporated Glenn County):
- **Zoning:** MP — Industrial Park District (Glenn County Code Chapter 15.510)
- **General Plan Designation:** Business Park
- Fourteen permitted heavy commercial and manufacturing uses; four conditionally permitted uses.
- Phase I infrastructure complete: paved streets, sewer, water, power, communications, storm drainage, fire hydrants.
- Parcels: nine parcels, 3–18 acres (64.37 acres total)
- Access: ~3.5 miles east of I-5; ~1.5 miles south of SR 32
- Current tenant example: Flowserve Corporation
- Contact: Glenn Grows Economic Development, 125 E. Walker Street, Orland, CA 95963; (530) 934-6400; SDeMoss@countyofglenn.net
- Note: OAIP is under **Glenn County** jurisdiction, not City of Orland — separate zoning, permits, and CUPA authority (county).

Source: https://glenngrows.countyofglenn.net/opportunity-in-glenn-county/industrial-parks/orland-industrial-park/

## EHS-Relevant Zoning Notes

- Mining operations in any zone require compliance with the **Surface Mining and Reclamation Act (SMARA) of 1975**.
- Fire marshal determines fire protection requirements for structures in non-compliant setback situations.
- Objective Design Standards (Chapter 17.18, added by Ordinance 2022-01) apply to qualifying multifamily residential in R-1, R-2, R-3, C-1, C-2, and DT-MU zones — does not affect M-L or M-H industrial zones.
- For hazardous materials storage or processing at industrial sites, CUPA (Glenn County Ag/W&M) oversees HMBP, tiered permitting, UST, and APSA requirements regardless of city zoning.
