## Adopted Code Edition

The City of Willows adopted the **2025 California Fire Code** (Title 24, Part 9, California Code of Regulations), including appendices and amendments, along with the **2024 International Fire Code and Standards** and the **2025 California Building Code Standards** (certain amendments). Adopted by **Ordinance 767-26 § 1, enacted January 13, 2026**.

NFPA standards also adopted: NFPA 10, 13, 13R, 13D, 25 (with California Amendments), and NFPA 72 (most recent editions).

Municipal code chapter: **WMC Chapter 15.15 FIRE CODE**.

## Enforcement Authority

Fire prevention division of the Willows Fire Department, under supervision of the Fire Chief. Fire chief, fire inspectors, and designated officials have citation authority under Penal Code §§ 19.7, 830.37, and 836.5. (WMC 15.15.020, 15.15.070)

## Local Amendments to the California Fire Code

### Appeals (§ 15.15.050 — amends CFC Ch. 1, § 108.1)
Appeals of Fire Chief decisions go to the **City Council within 30 days** of the decision. City Council decision is final; all findings rendered in writing.

### Fire Apparatus Access Roads (§ 15.15.090 — adds to CFC Ch. 5, § 501.4)
- All-weather driving surface capable of supporting imposed loads **up to 70,000 lbs** during all phases of construction.
- Fire hydrants capable of **≥1,000 gpm** charged and available before stacking combustible materials; hydrants within **500 ft** of furthest construction point.

### Premises Identification (§ 15.15.100 — adds to CFC § 505.1)
- Address numbers on all new and existing buildings, visible day and night.
- Illuminated numbers required for buildings constructed after January 1, 2008 (photoelectric/house meter controlled, no switch).
- Minimum 6-inch height, 1-inch stroke; single-family minimum 4-inch.
- Multi-unit complexes (condominiums, apartment complexes, mobile home parks, commercial buildings) must install illuminated directory at entry showing plot plan, hydrant locations, hazmat storage locations, key box location, and north indicator.

### Sprinkler Systems (§ 15.15.110 — amends CFC § B)
All commercial and industrial occupancies required to have an automatic sprinkler system must have a **Fire Department Connection (FDC) within 50 ft** of a City-standard hydrant, accessible and identified. (Updated by Ord. 767-26)

### Key Boxes (§ 15.15.130 — adds to CFC § 506.1)
Key box (Knox Box, approved type) required on **all new commercial, industrial, and R occupancies except R-3**. Placement approved by Fire Chief or designee.

### Problematic / Unreliable Fire Alarms (§ 15.15.140 — adds to CFC § 901.6.1.1)
Fire Chief may declare alarm unreliable upon > 4 false alarms in 12 months:
- 4th false alarm: **$150.00 mitigation fee + response costs**.
- 6th false alarm: **$300.00 mitigation fee + response costs**.
- 8th and each subsequent: **$500.00 + response costs**; Chief may require standby personnel or other measures until alarm system certified as reliable in writing by approved maintenance firm.

### Self-Inspection Program (§ 15.15.150 — adds to CFC Appendix Ch. 1, § 106.2)
Established for **"B" occupancies** (professional/service-type, offices, record storage) and **"M" occupancies** (merchandise display and sale) within the city:
- Self-Inspection Worksheet and Confidential Business Occupancy Information form delivered bi-annually.
- Non-return within 14 days: one warning letter extending period 7 days; continued failure triggers mandatory fire dept. inspection at business owner's expense per fee schedule.
- Failure to correct deficiencies: infraction citation.

### Hazardous Material (§ 15.15.160 — adds to CFC Ch. 50, § 5002)
Expanded definition: "hazardous materials" includes explosives, radioactive materials, petroleum/petroleum products, gases, poisons, etiologic (biologic) agents, flammable and corrosive substances. Fire department cost recovery for hazmat response includes actual labor, worker's comp benefits, fringe benefits, administrative overhead, equipment operation, materials, and contract labor costs.

### Burning (§ 15.15.170 — amends CFC § 307)
**Open burning prohibited within City of Willows city limits** — including burn barrels, litter, brush, leaves, debris, and weeds. Special event burning (warming fires or cooking) permitted under a fire department permit with: pre-inspection of pit location; 20-ft vegetation clearance; constant adult attendance; mesh wire top; only unprocessed wood or barbecue charcoal.

Fire Chief and Air Pollution Control Officer jointly authorized to halt burning for smoke/hazard. Fire Chief may impose a ban during critical fire danger periods.

### Fireworks (§ 15.15.180 — adds to CFC Ch. 56)
Safe-and-Sane fireworks (California State Fire Marshal seal) authorized subject to:
- Fire Chief permit required; **maximum 10 stand/booth permits** per year.
- Sales only to patrons **16 or older**.
- Sales and use permitted **June 28 through July 6 only**.

### Flammable Liquids — Aboveground Tanks (§ 15.15.200)
Limits under CFC § 5704.2.9.6.1: outside aboveground flammable liquid tank storage prohibited in **R-1, R-2, R-3, and R-P zones** as defined in WMC Chapters 18.30–18.45 (all within incorporated city limits).

### Flammable Liquids — Job Sites (§ 15.15.210)
Limits under CFC § 5706.2.4.4: outside aboveground tank storage prohibited within the incorporated limits of the City of Willows.

### Bulk LPG Storage (§ 15.15.220 — adds to CFC § 6104.2)
In residential incorporated limits: **up to 500 gallons** LPG may be stored in above-ground or in-ground tank if:
- Tank meets CFC § 6104 requirements; minimum distances per Table 6104.3.
- Tank base secured per NFIP (1993) Technical Bulletin 7-93.
- **Written notice** of tank placed in main electrical panel for emergency personnel.

### Weed Abatement (§ 15.15.190 — adds to CFC § 304.1.2)
- 30-ft cleared space around all buildings; 50-ft perimeter firebreak around property to street/sidewalk; cross breaks 30-ft wide every 50 ft maximum.
- 30-ft clearance beyond foliage of any tree group.
- Fire Chief may increase required clearances as conditions warrant.

## Penalties

Violations: infraction and/or misdemeanor; fine up to **$1,000** and/or imprisonment. Continuing violations: each 7-day period is a separate offense. (WMC 15.15.060)
