## ML — Light Industrial District (WMC Chapter 18.70)

Enacted: Ord. 632-91 § 18.01–18.04 (October 22, 1991); amended by Ord. 664-00 (June 27, 2000).

### Purpose (§ 18.70.010)
Intended for areas with **light manufacturing and heavy commercial uses of the non-nuisance type** and large administrative facilities as the predominant uses.

### Permitted Uses (§ 18.70.020)
The following uses and structures are permitted by right in the ML district:
1. All uses permitted in the CG General Commercial district (WMC 18.60.020).
2. Assembly and storage of goods, materials, liquids, and equipment — **EXCEPT storage of flammable or explosive matter or materials creating dust, odors, or fumes** (explicitly excluded).
3. Wholesale and storage warehouses.
4. Feed stores.
5. Manufacturing, processing, fabricating, refining, repairing, packaging, or treatment of goods, materials, or produce by electric power, oil, or gas — **except operations involving fish fats and oils, bones, and similar substances creating offensive conditions**.
6. Dyeing and dry cleaning plants; rug cleaning plants; laundries; veterinary hospitals and enclosed animal kennels; cabinet shops; construction and materials yards (except gravel, rock, and cement materials yards).
7. The following uses when **conducted within a building or enclosed within a solid wall or fence ≥ 6 ft** (type approved by Planning Commission): major auto repairs, body and fender repair shops, auto painting shops, cooperage and bottling works, sheet metal shops, welding shops, truck terminals, and retail lumberyards.
8. Automobile sales and service, including used car lots.
9. Caretaker's residence (where legally established use requires continuous supervision).

### Uses Requiring Conditional Use Permit (§ 18.70.030)
- Retail stores and business/service enterprises similar to those in the CG district (WMC 18.60.030), in the opinion of the Planning Commission.

### Development Standards (§ 18.70.040)
| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Minimum lot area | 10,000 sq ft |
| Front setback | None |
| Side setback | None (except as required by building code) |
| Rear setback | None |
| Maximum building height | 50 ft |
| Loading area | Private off-street space required for all materials/equipment |
| Parking | Per WMC 18.120.020 |

## MH — Heavy Industrial District (WMC Chapter 18.75)

Enacted: Ord. 632-91 § 19.01–19.04 (October 22, 1991); amended by Ord. 664-00 (June 27, 2000).

### Purpose (§ 18.75.010)
Intended for areas **devoted to normal operations of industries** subject only to regulations needed to control congestion and protect surrounding areas.

### Permitted Uses (§ 18.75.020)
1. All uses permitted in the ML district (WMC 18.70.020).
2. Wholesale lumberyards, lumber mills, grain elevators and storage.
3. Pottery kilns and ceramic works of heavy industrial types.
4. Concrete batch plants.
5. Blacksmith shops and casting foundries.
6. The following uses **enclosed with a solid wall or fence ≥ 6 ft** (type approved by Planning Commission): building material storage yards, contractors' storage yards, and junkyards and auto-wrecking yards.
7. Fuel yards.

### Uses Requiring Conditional Use Permit (§ 18.75.030)
- Retail stores and businesses similar to CG district uses (WMC 18.60.030), in the opinion of the Planning Commission.

### Development Standards (§ 18.75.040)
| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Minimum lot area | 10,000 sq ft |
| Front setback | None |
| Side setback | None (except as required by building code) |
| Rear setback | None |
| Maximum building height | 50 ft |
| Loading area | Private off-street space required for all materials/equipment |
| Parking | Per WMC 18.120.020 |

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

**WMC Chapter 18.135 Use Permits** governs the CUP process for both ML and MH districts. Planning Commission reviews and approves/denies CUPs. Administrative Use Permits for lower-intensity uses are handled under WMC Chapter 18.130.

## Key EHS Implication: Flammable Material Exclusion in ML

WMC 18.70.020(2) explicitly prohibits **storage of flammable or explosive matter** as a permitted use in ML. This means:
- A facility that stores flammable liquids in ML may require a CUP or rezoning to MH, or may need to demonstrate the storage qualifies as incidental to another permitted use.
- This interacts with WMC 15.15.200 (fire code prohibition on outside aboveground flammable liquid tanks in R zones), which does not restrict aboveground tanks in ML or MH — but the zoning code's own use restriction applies independently.
- Industrial facilities with significant hazmat storage should verify whether the specific materials and quantities are permissible as a zoning use in ML versus MH.

## Marijuana Cultivation (WMC Chapter 18.117)

Marijuana cultivation is addressed in a standalone chapter (18.117), separate from ML/MH district chapters. Industrial cannabis facilities would require specific CUP review — verify current status with Planning Dept.
