## CUPA Agency

**City of Willows is NOT its own CUPA.** The city is served by the countywide CUPA:

**Glenn County Agriculture / Weights & Measures Department**
- Role: Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all of Glenn County, including incorporated cities (Willows, Orland).

## Programs Administered (All Six State-Mandated Elements)

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) / Area Plan** — required when a single hazardous material is handled in quantities ≥55 gallons (liquid), ≥200 lbs (solid), or ≥500 cu ft (gas) at maximum daily amount. Electronic filing via CERS.
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator Program** — oversight of generation, storage, handling, labeling, and disposal of hazardous waste; routine inspections of all regulated facilities.
3. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — installation, operation, monitoring, and closure oversight.
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — tanks ≥1,320 gallons aggregate petroleum (or any single tank >660 gal) must file SPCC plan; CUPA inspects compliance.
5. **CalARP / Risk Management Plans (RMP)** — California Accidental Release Prevention Program; facilities with regulated substances above threshold quantities must submit an RMP and coordinate with Glenn County CUPA as the local implementing agency.
6. **Tiered Permitting** — hazardous waste treatment at the point of generation (conditionally exempt, conditionally authorized, or permit-by-rule tiers).

## No Local Hazmat Ordinance

The City of Willows has not enacted a separate local hazardous materials ordinance or chapter in the Willows Municipal Code. All HMBP, UST, CalARP, and hazardous waste requirements flow from state law (Health & Safety Code Div. 20, Ch. 6.95 et seq.) as implemented by the Glenn County CUPA.

The Willows Fire Department (Willows Municipal Code Ch. 15.15) does contain a local "Hazardous Material" definition section (WMC 15.15.160), but this is for fire department cost-recovery authority during emergency response — it is not a CUPA regulatory program.

## CalARP Notes

The California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) program (H&S Code § 25531 et seq.) is the state analog to the federal RMP program. Glenn County CUPA administers CalARP; facilities in Willows with listed regulated substances above threshold quantities (same as federal 112(r) thresholds) must:
- Submit an RMP to the California OES and to Glenn County CUPA.
- Conduct a process hazard analysis or worst-case/alternative release scenario.
- Coordinate with the CUPA on local emergency planning under the Area Plan.
