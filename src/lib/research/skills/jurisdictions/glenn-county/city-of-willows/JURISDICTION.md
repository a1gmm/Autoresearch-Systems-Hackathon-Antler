## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air

- **District**: Glenn County Air Pollution Control District (Glenn County APCD) — the sole air permitting authority for all stationary sources in the city and county. Not part of the Sacramento Metropolitan AQMD; operates independently.
- **Basin**: Sacramento Valley Air Basin (Northern Sacramento Valley).
- **Address/Phone**: 720 N. Colusa St. (P.O. Box 351), Willows, CA 95988 · (530) 934-6500.
- **Permit authority**: Willows-sited facilities obtain Authority to Construct (ATC) and Permits to Operate (PTO) from Glenn County APCD, not CalEPA directly.
- **SIP rules**: EPA-compiled rules and regulations document available at EPA (see Sources).

### CUPA (Hazardous Materials Unified Program)

- **CUPA agency**: Glenn County Agriculture / Weights & Measures Department — countywide CUPA; city of Willows is NOT its own CUPA and has no separate local hazmat ordinance.
- **Address**: 720 N. Colusa St. (P.O. Box 351), Willows, CA 95988.
- **Agricultural Commissioner/Sealer**: Marcie Skelton.
- **Programs administered** (all six state-mandated elements): HMBP / Area Plan, Hazardous Waste Generator, Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), CalARP / Risk Management Plans, Tiered Permitting.
- **HMBP filing**: Electronic submission via CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) required for all regulated facilities. Threshold: any single hazardous material ≥55 gallons liquid / ≥200 lbs solid / ≥500 cu ft gas triggers HMBP.
- **CalARP**: Glenn County CUPA administers CalARP/RMP program for accidental release prevention; facilities with listed regulated substances above thresholds must file with CUPA.
- **Fee schedule**: [VERIFY] — not publicly posted on county website as of 2026-06-03; contact Glenn County CUPA directly.

### Fire

- **AHJ**: Willows Fire Department (municipal). Fire Chief: Nathan Monck · nmonck@cityofwillows.org · (530) 934-3323 · 445 S. Butte St., Willows, CA 95988.
- **Adopted code**: 2025 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9), including appendices and amendments, plus 2024 International Fire Code — adopted by **Ordinance 767-26** (§ 1, enacted 1-13-26); also NFPA 10, 13, 13R, 13D, 25, and 72 (most recent editions).
- **Municipal code chapter**: WMC Chapter 15.15 FIRE CODE.
- **Key local amendments** (see fire-code-local.md for full text):
  - CFC § 108.1 Appeals: modified — appeals go to City Council within 30 days; City Council decision is final.
  - CFC § 501.4 Access roads: all-weather surface rated to 70,000 lbs; hydrant(s) at 1,000 gpm minimum within 500 ft during construction.
  - CFC § 506.1 Key boxes: required on all new commercial, industrial, and R occupancies (except R-3).
  - CFC § 901.6.1.1 False alarms: 4th false alarm = $150 fee + response cost; 6th = $300 + cost; 8th+ = $500 + cost.
  - CFC Chapter 50 Hazmat: expanded "hazardous materials" definition including petroleum products, explosives, radioactive materials, and corrosives; fire dept. cost-recovery authority.
  - CFC § 5704.2.9.6.1 Flammable liquids: outside aboveground tank storage prohibited in R-1, R-2, R-3, and R-P zones.
  - CFC § 6104.2 LPG: up to 500 gallons allowed in residential limits (above- or in-ground) per CFC § 6104 with written notice in main electrical panel.
  - Self-Inspection Program (WMC 15.15.150): "B" and "M" occupancies receive self-inspection worksheets bi-annually; non-response triggers fire dept. inspection with fee charge.
  - Open burning: prohibited within city limits (no burn barrels, brush, debris); special event permits available for warming/cooking fires.
  - Fireworks: Safe-and-Sane only; max 10 stand permits per year; sales June 28–July 6 only; buyers must be 16+.
- **Fee schedule**: Codified in WMC 15.15.230 (adopted Ord. 678-07, Exh. B). Select fees: sprinkler system plan check 1–20 appliances $288; fire alarm 0–15 devices $162; AST 60–500 gal $162; UST 1–2 tanks $108/tank; emergency generator $162; reinspection $47/hr; false alarm 4th occurrence $150.

### Wastewater (Sewer / Industrial Pretreatment)

- **POTW operator**: City of Willows — operates its own sanitary sewer collection system and wastewater treatment plant; plant is managed under contract by **Inframark**.
- **Public Works contact**: Natisa Pfyl, Superintendent · npfyl@cityofwillows.org · (530) 934-5657.
- **Sewer use ordinance**: WMC Chapter 13.10 SEWER (enacted Ord. 639-93, 10-26-93; prior Ord. 482-71).
- **Industrial discharge controls** (WMC 13.10.160): prohibited discharges include flammable/explosive substances (flashpoint < 190°F), corrosive waste (pH < 5.5), petroleum-derived oils/greases, toxic substances, chemical pesticides (chlorinated hydrocarbons, organo-phosphates), strong oxidizing/reducing agents, and wastewater > 150°F or < 33°F.
- **No numeric local limits table published online** — specific concentration limits not codified in the municipal code; limits derive from the city's NPDES permit and can be imposed by the Director of Public Works via waste discharge report (WMC 13.10.170).
- **Pretreatment/grease**: Grease traps required for restaurants, cafes, factories, school kitchens, etc. (WMC 13.10.050); director of public works may require screening (WMC 13.10.040) and installation of sampling manholes (WMC 13.10.170).
- **Waste discharge report**: director may require a written waste discharge report within 30 days of notice; industrial dischargers may be required to install monitoring manholes at own expense.
- **Formal IWP process**: [VERIFY] — no distinct Industrial Wastewater Permit (IWP) program described in the municipal code; unusual-strength industrial waste may be accepted under special agreement (WMC 13.10.170(9)).
- **NPDES permit**: City holds an NPDES permit for plant effluent; receiving water requirements imposed by Central Valley RWQCB (Sacramento/San Joaquin River Basin). Federal pretreatment regulations (40 CFR Part 403) apply if POTW design flow > 5 mgd — Willows WWTP is a small system; verify whether formal pretreatment program is required.

### Zoning (Industrial Districts)

- **Zoning ordinance**: WMC Title 18 ZONING (enacted Ord. 632-91, 10-22-91, with subsequent amendments).
- **Industrial districts**:
  - **ML — Light Industrial** (Ch. 18.70): light manufacturing and heavy commercial of the non-nuisance type; large administrative facilities. Assembly and storage of goods (except flammable/explosive or dust/odor/fume-generating materials), wholesale warehouses, feed stores, manufacturing by electric/oil/gas power, dyeing/dry cleaning, laundries, veterinary hospitals, auto repairs, welding shops, truck terminals, retail lumberyards. Minimum lot: 10,000 sq ft; max height: 50 ft; no minimum setbacks.
  - **MH — Heavy Industrial** (Ch. 18.75): all ML permitted uses plus lumber mills, grain elevators, pottery kilns, concrete batch plants, foundries, contractors' storage yards, junkyards/auto-wrecking (behind 6-ft fence), and fuel yards. Minimum lot: 10,000 sq ft; max height: 50 ft; no minimum setbacks.
- **CUP process**: Retail stores and certain commercial uses in ML or MH require a conditional use permit from the Planning Commission (WMC Ch. 18.135 Use Permits).
- **Flammable material restriction in ML**: storage of flammable or explosive matter is explicitly excluded from permitted ML uses (WMC 18.70.020(2)).

## Notes

- Willows is the Glenn County seat and runs its own municipal fire department (HazMat Team emergency-response capability). HazMat **regulatory** authority (CUPA) stays with the county Ag/W&M Dept.
- Glenn County APCD (not BAAQMD or SMAQMD) is the air permitting authority — a small independent district with its own rule set under the EPA-approved SIP.
- The sewer chapter (13.10) is from 1993 (Ord. 639-93) and has not been publicly updated since; a formal IWP/pretreatment program is not described, suggesting Willows' WWTP may be below the 5-mgd threshold triggering a mandatory federal pretreatment program — [VERIFY current NPDES permit].
- Willows Municipal Code is current through Ordinance 763-25 (Feb. 25, 2025) on CodePublishing; Ord. 767-26 and 768-26 (Jan. 13, 2026) are the most recent fire/building code update ordinances.
