## Overview

Industrial zoning in Glenn County exists at three levels: the **county unincorporated** (Title 15 Unified Development Code), the **City of Orland** (Title 17 Zoning), and the **City of Willows** (Title 17 Zoning — unconfirmed; [VERIFY]). The county code uses a single "M" industrial designation plus a specialized "MP" industrial park zone; Orland uses "M-L" (Limited Industrial) and "M-H" (Heavy Industrial).

### M Zone — General Industrial (Ch. 15.440)

**Purpose (§ 15.440):** Reserve appropriately located areas for industrial plants and related activities; protect industrial areas from residential intrusion; protect residential and commercial areas from nuisances — noise, odor, dust, dirt, smoke, vibration, heat, glare, fire, explosion, noxious fumes, radiation, and other hazards.

**Key Code Sections:**

| Section | Topic |
|---|---|
| § 15.440.020.B | Permitted uses — includes warehouse/distribution (confirmed in 2017 SPR approval); full list [VERIFY in Title 15 PDF] |
| § 15.440.050 | Minimum parcel size |
| § 15.440.060 | Minimum yard requirements (setbacks) |
| § 15.440.070 | Maximum building height |

**Development Standards:**

| Standard | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum parcel size | 20,000 sq ft (with public utilities) | 1 acre minimum if served by well and septic |
| Maximum building height | 75 feet | Per § 15.440.070 |
| Setbacks | [VERIFY] | Specific setback dimensions not extracted — see § 15.440.060 |

**Permitted Uses (§ 15.440.020.B):** Warehouses and non-manufacturing uses and associated structures are permitted. Full enumerated use list is in the Title 15 PDF at Chapter 15.440 — access via https://www.countyofglenn.net/sites/default/files/resources/County_Code_Directory/Title%2015.pdf.

**Conditional Uses:** [VERIFY] — Uses requiring a Conditional Use Permit (CUP) from the Planning Commission are listed in the Title 15 Chapter 15.440 conditional use table.

**CUP Process:** Applications go to the Glenn County Planning Commission through PCDSA.

**Environmental Compliance Conditions:** Recent project approvals (e.g., SPR 2017-018 Baker Trucking, SPR 2020-024 Distribution Center) include conditions referencing CUPA compliance, air quality permits, and building permits. Conditions are project-specific but consistent with the county's standard template.

### MP Zone — Industrial Park (Ch. 15.510)

**Applied to:** Orland Airport Industrial Park (OAIP) exclusively — nine parcels, ~64.37 total acres, adjacent to the Orland Airport.

**Purpose:** Accommodate heavy commercial and manufacturing uses in a planned setting with design standards and Site Plan Review requirements.

**Key Code Sections:**

| Section | Topic |
|---|---|
| § 15.510.030 | Permitted uses — 14 permitted heavy commercial/manufacturing uses |
| § 15.510.040 | Conditionally permitted uses — 4 uses requiring CUP via Planning Commission |
| § 15.510 (general) | Development standards: minimum lot size, setbacks, max height, performance standards |

**Parcel Inventory:**

| Parcel | Acres |
|---|---|
| 1 | 9.13 |
| 2 | 3.01 |
| 3 | 3.00 |
| 4 | 7.00 |
| 5 | 5.00 |
| 6 | 3.93 |
| 7 | 18.12 |
| 8 | 11.32 |
| 9 | 3.86 |
| **Total** | **~64.37** |

**Approval Requirements:**
- Uses under § 15.510.030: **Site Plan Review (SPR)** required before building permit issuance.
- Uses under § 15.510.040: **Conditional Use Permit (CUP)** from Planning Commission required.
- Additional: OAIP Design Standards apply in addition to Title 15 requirements.

**Design Standards:** Published at https://www.countyofglenn.net/resources/projects/orland-airport-industrial-park-design-standards
(Also available via Glenn Grows: https://glenngrows.countyofglenn.net/media/userfiles/subsite_271/files/list-maker/design-standards.pdf)

**Full Permitted Uses List:** [VERIFY] — The 14 permitted and 4 conditionally permitted uses are enumerated in § 15.510.030 and § 15.510.040. Access via the Title 15 PDF or OAIP permitted uses page: https://www.countyofglenn.net/government/departments/orland-airport-industrial-park/permitted-uses
