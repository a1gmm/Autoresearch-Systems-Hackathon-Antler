## AHJ Structure

Lake County has **no consolidated county fire department**. Fire code enforcement (AHJ) is handled by independent fire protection districts. The two most significant for EHS purposes:

| District | Primary Service Area | Contact |
|----------|---------------------|---------|
| **Lake County Fire Protection District (LCFPD)** | City of Clearlake + surrounding unincorporated areas | 14815 Olympic Drive, Clearlake, CA 95422; (707) 994-2170 |
| **Lakeport Fire District** | City of Lakeport area | lakeportfire.com |
| Kelseyville Fire Protection District | Kelseyville area | — |
| Northshore Fire Protection District | North Lake County | — |
| South Lake County Fire Protection District | South Lake County | — |

**CAL FIRE / LNU:** Sonoma-Lake-Napa Unit (LNU) has wildland fire authority in State Responsibility Area (SRA) lands — Battalions 1417 and 1418.

## County Building Code (Fire-Related Provisions)

- Lake County Community Development Department, Building Division adopts Title 24 via **Code of Ordinances, Chapter 5** (Building Regulations) at library.municode.com/ca/lake_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=COOR_CH5BURE
- **2022 California Building Code** was adopted as the Building Code of the County of Lake in **August 2023**. Confirm whether the county has adopted the 2025 edition (statewide eff. 2026-01-01) [VERIFY with Building Division at (707) 263-2382].

## Burning Regulations

- County Code of Ordinances, **Chapter 22** covers burning regulations (open burning, agricultural burning, etc.) — library.municode.com/ca/lake_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=COOR_CH22BURE
- Open burning also regulated by LCAQMD.

## Permit Types — Lake County FPD

The following permits are administered by LCFPD Fire Marshal:

- AB-38 Defensible Space Inspections (required for home sales in High/Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zones)
- Burn Permits
- Fire Sprinkler Plans (residential and commercial)
- Fire Alarm Plans
- Solar / Photovoltaic System Plans
- Water Storage Tank Plans
- Commercial Tenant Improvement Plans
- Business Occupancy Permits
