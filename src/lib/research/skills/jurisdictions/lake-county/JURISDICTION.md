## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air — Lake County Air Quality Management District (LCAQMD)

- **Which district controls:** Lake County Air Quality Management District (LCAQMD) is the sole air district. Its jurisdiction boundary equals the county boundary (a Federally and State recognized Lake County Air Basin). Regulates all stationary sources including geothermal power generation, mining, gasoline stations, and open burning. Delegates State ATCMs and Federal NSPSs/NEShAPs.
- **Permit to Operate / Authority to Construct:** Required for stationary sources; specific thresholds and rules available at lcaqmd.net. Fee schedule directed through lcaqmd.net (Master Fee Ordinance references AQMD fees separately — "Please visit Air Quality Management District's website for the fee schedule").
- **Contact:** 2617 S. Main St., Lakeport, CA; phone 707-263-7000; email lcaqmd@lcaqmd.net. Walk-ins Mon–Thu 9am–12pm.
- Source: https://lcaqmd.net/ and https://www.epa.gov/air-quality-implementation-plans/ca-sip-lcaqmd

### CUPA — Lake County Division of Environmental Health

- **CUPA agency:** Lake County Division of Environmental Health (Health Services Department) — single CUPA for the **entire county including the cities of Clearlake and Lakeport**. No city-run CUPAs or participating agencies.
- **Programs:** Hazardous waste generator permitting and inspection; hazardous waste on-site treatment; hazardous materials handlers (HMBP/CERS); aboveground storage tanks (APSA/AST); underground storage tanks (UST); Treated Wood Waste (TWW). **CalARP:** annual fee line item confirmed in Master Fee Ordinance 3056 (3-hr minimum at $137/hr effective 7/1/2025); CalARP R&P Review billed separately at hourly rate.
- **Fee authority:** Master Fee Ordinance 3056, approved by Board of Supervisors 2/7/2017; 5% increase effective 7/1/2022. Current schedule effective 7/1/2025.
- **HMBP fee tiers (annual, effective 7/1/2025):** Cat 1 (55–110 gal liquids) $412; Cat 2 $509; Cat 3 $790; Cat 4 $961; Cat 5 $1,169; Cat 6 $1,582; Cat 7 (150,001+ gal) $2,497. Marina special categories: Cat 1 $309 through Cat 7 $1,873. Conditionally exempt small quantity generator: $103/facility. Agricultural operation hazmat facility: $186/facility.
- **APSA/AST fees:** 1,320–10,000 gal $72; 10,001–100,000 gal $108; 100,001–1,000,000 gal $144; >1,000,001 gal $505 (all annual, per site, includes inspection + 1 follow-up).
- **UST:** Permit to install 1st underground container $389; additional containers and permit to modify/upgrade/repair — see hourly rate schedule. Tank abandonment/removal $309/container. UST annual permit to operate — state surcharge determined by Legislature, paid at time of permit.
- **Late payment penalties:** 0–30 days 10%; 30–60 days 15%; 60–90 days 20%; >90 days 25%.
- **Contact:** 255 N Forbes Street, Lakeport, CA 95453; 707-263-1164.
- Source: https://www.lakecountyca.gov/374/Hazardous-Materials-Management-CUPA and https://www.lakecountyca.gov/222/Fee-Ordinance

### Fire — Independent Fire Protection Districts (no county fire dept.)

- **AHJ structure:** No consolidated county fire department. Served by independent fire protection districts. Key districts: **Lake County Fire Protection District** (serves City of Clearlake and surrounding unincorporated area); **Lakeport Fire District** (serves City of Lakeport area); Kelseyville Fire Protection District; Northshore Fire Protection District; South Lake County Fire Protection District; others.
- **CAL FIRE:** Sonoma-Lake-Napa Unit (LNU) has State Responsibility Area (SRA) jurisdiction on wildland lands within the county (Battalions 1417/1418).
- **Adopted fire code — Lake County FPD:** Ordinance No. 2025-1001 (LCFPD 2025 Fire Code Ordinance). The underlying California Fire Code edition adopted is the 2025 California Fire Code (Title 24 Part 9, based on IFC 2024). Local amendments exist — details in Ord. 2025-1001.
- **LCFPD permit types:** Burn permits; fire sprinkler plans; fire alarm plans; solar plans; water storage tank plans; commercial tenant improvement plans; business occupancy permits.
- **LCFPD representative fees (Master Fee Schedule eff. 11/22/2025):** Fire sprinkler plans (residential) from $270; fire alarm plans from $735; solar plans (residential) from $90; water storage tanks from $550.
- **Fire Marshal:** Alan Lancaster, alancaster@lakecountyfire.com, (707) 994-2170, 14815 Olympic Drive, Clearlake, CA 95422.
- **County building code (fire provisions):** Chapter 5 of Lake County Code of Ordinances (Building Regulations) adopts Title 24. The 2022 California Building Code was adopted as the Building Code of Lake County in August 2023.
- Source: https://www.lakecountyfire.com/fire-marshal-prevention and https://www.lakecountyca.gov/1238/Codes-Ordinances

### Wastewater — Lake County Sanitation District (LACOSAN)

- **POTW:** Lake County Sanitation District (LACOSAN), administered by Lake County Special Districts Administration. Serves portions of unincorporated county and incorporated cities via multiple service areas.
- **Rules instrument:** Lake County Sanitation District Wastewater System Rules and Regulations (6-page document). Industrial pretreatment provisions are in Appendix A (Sewer Use Ordinance — LACOSAN) of the Lake County Code of Ordinances, with Article IV covering business/large/secondary connections.
- **Sewer Use Ordinance reference:** Code of Ordinances, Appendix A — Sewer Use Ordinance (LACOSAN); also Appendix B (other districts); Appendix C (Sewer Use Charge Ordinance — KCWWD#3); Appendix E (Water Conservation Standards — LACOSAN portions).
- **Connection charges:** Set by ordinance; capacity expansion fees due prior to building permit issuance. Computed at 210 gal/day per single-family dwelling equivalent (SFD-E) for unclassified connection types.
- **Prohibited discharges (per Rules & Regulations):** Cooling water, air conditioning water, water softener brine, groundwater, oil, hazardous materials, and roof drainage shall not be discharged into the system.
- **Industrial pretreatment:** Industrial users subject to Sewer Use Ordinance (Appendix A). Specific local numerical discharge limits [VERIFY — not published in the publicly available summary documents; contact LACOSAN directly].
- **NPDES oversight:** Regional Water Quality Control Board, North Coast Region (Region 1) issues the POTW's NPDES permit for the receiving water.
- Source: https://www.lakecountyca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/4792/Lake-County-Sanitation-District-PDF and https://www.lakecountyca.gov/997/Rules-Regulations

### Stormwater

- **MS4 permit:** Lake County and the cities of Clearlake and Lakeport operate under North Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 1) Phase II MS4 permit. Unincorporated county stormwater administered by Lake County Public Works.
- **Construction stormwater:** Projects 1+ acre must obtain State Construction General Permit (CGP) from State Water Resources Control Board and prepare a SWPPP.

### Zoning — Industrial Districts

- **Zoning ordinance:** Lake County Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 21 of Code of Ordinances. Three primary industrial district classifications:
  - **M1 (Commercial/Manufacturing District):** Light manufacturing, assembly, packaging on parcels ≤5 acres. Permitted uses by right include high-tech, metals, textiles, wood/paper products; fuel tank farms and electroplating require Major Use Permit (MUP). Article 21, Sec. 21-21.
  - **M2 (Heavy Industrial District):** Heavy industrial on parcels ≤5 acres. Hazardous/toxic waste disposal, chemical manufacturing (acids, ammonia, acetylene), quarrying, slaughterhouses all require MUP. Fuel tank farms require MUP. Article 22, Sec. 21-22.
  - **MP (Industrial Park District):** Heavy commercial/manufacturing clustered in business parks. Must front on minor-collector or larger road; served by public water and sewer. Light manufacturing, R&D labs, warehouses permitted by right; offensive uses and boat manufacturing require MUP. Article 23, Sec. 21-23.
  - **AI (Agricultural Industry Combining District):** Overlay combining district for agricultural-related industrial uses. Article 28.
- **CUP process:** Minor Use Permit and Major Use Permit issued by Planning Division. Initial MUP fee $351; initial Use Permit fee $366 (eff. 7/1/2025, per Master Fee Ordinance 3056).
- **Performance standards:** All industrial uses subject to Article 41 (performance standards — noise, odor, dust, vibration, bright lights).
- Source: https://www.lakecountyca.gov/460/Zoning-Ordinance and https://www.lakecountyca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1370/Article-21---Commercial-and-Manufacturing-District-M1-PDF

## Notes

- Lake County has only two incorporated cities: **Clearlake** (largest) and **Lakeport** (county seat). All other named places are unincorporated communities.
- GOTCHA: neither Clearlake nor Lakeport runs its own CUPA — both are covered by the single Lake County Environmental Health CUPA.
- GOTCHA: neither incorporated city runs a municipal fire department. Clearlake is served by Lake County Fire Protection District; Lakeport is served by Lakeport Fire District — both independent fire protection districts.
- GOTCHA: Lake County Building Division (Community Development Dept.) handles building permits for unincorporated county. Fire district sign-off is required before building permit issuance.
- Workflow note: LCAQMD fee schedule is maintained separately at lcaqmd.net and is not included in the county Master Fee Ordinance 3056 table.
