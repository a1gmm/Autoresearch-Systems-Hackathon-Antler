## Zoning Ordinance Location

Lake County Zoning Ordinance is housed within the **Code of Ordinances, Chapter 21** (Zoning). Available via:
- Municode: https://library.municode.com/ca/lake_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=COOR_CH21ZO
- Individual article PDFs: https://www.lakecountyca.gov/460/Zoning-Ordinance

Performance standards applicable to all industrial uses: **Article 41** (noise, odor, dust, vibration, bright lights).

## Industrial District Summary

Lake County has three primary industrial zoning districts plus one agricultural-industry combining district.

| District | Code | Parcel Size Limit | Key Distinguishing Feature |
|----------|------|------------------|---------------------------|
| Commercial/Manufacturing | M1 | ≤5 acres | Light industrial; no pulverization of clays, no kilns except electric/gas, no oil/fat refining |
| Heavy Industrial | M2 | ≤5 acres | Full heavy industrial; hazardous/toxic waste disposal requires MUP |
| Industrial Park | MP | No stated acreage limit | Business park setting; requires public water and sewer; fronts minor-collector or larger road |
| Agricultural Industry Combining | AI | Overlay district | Applied as combining designation over agricultural lands |

## M1 — Commercial/Manufacturing District

**Article 21, Sec. 21-21** | PDF: https://www.lakecountyca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1370/Article-21---Commercial-and-Manufacturing-District-M1-PDF

**Purpose:** Provide areas for heavy commercial and light industrial/manufacturing uses while maintaining an environment free from offensive or objectionable noise, dust, odor, or other nuisances.

**Applicability:** Parcels not exceeding **5 acres**.

### Permitted Uses By Right (Sec. 21.4)
Conducted within completely enclosed building; outdoor storage of finished products ≤100% of gross floor area (within screened area); ≤6,000 sq ft gross floor area per use or ≤12,000 sq ft total:
- All uses permitted in C3 (commercial) district (Sec. 20.3)
- Light manufacturing, assembly, packaging, or processing of: high-tech products (computers, optical, R&D); grains/vegetables/fruit; wood/paper; fabrics/textiles; leather; metals and alloys; glass, plastics, pottery, rubber products
- Laundry, janitorial, or facility maintenance services
- One accessory dwelling (security guard/night watchman quarters)
- Industrial and residential accessory uses

### Minor Use Permit Required (Sec. 21.5)
Same uses when exceeding 6,000 sq ft per use or 12,000 sq ft total, or when not in compliance with Article 41 performance standards, or when objectionable by reason of noise, odor, smoke, dust, bright lights, vibration, unusual traffic, or **involving the handling of explosives or dangerous materials**.

### Major Use Permit Required (Sec. 21.6)
- Uses exceeding 12,000 sq ft per use or 24,000 sq ft total
- Contractors' equipment storage yards or equipment rental yards
- Truck terminals and repair
- **Electroplating establishments**
- **Fuel tank farms, wholesale fuel sales or distributors, including natural gas or propane distributors or wholesalers** (Ord. No. 1749, 7/7/1988)

### Expressly Prohibited (Sec. 21.7)
Heavy industrial district uses are expressly prohibited in M1 unless specifically listed above.

### Development Standards (Sec. 21.11–21.19)
| Standard | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Min lot size (public water & sewer) | 12,500 sq ft |
| Min lot size (well + public sewer, or public water + septic) | 20,000 sq ft |
| Min lot size (well + septic) | 40,000 sq ft |
| Min average lot width (interior) | 100 ft |
| Min average lot width (corner) | 125 ft |
| Max length-to-width ratio | 3:1 |
| Max lot coverage | 50% |
| Front yard setback | 10 ft from lot line, or 35 ft from road centerline (whichever greater) |
| Rear yard setback | None (10 ft if contiguous to residential) |
| Side yard setback | None (10 ft if contiguous to residential) |
| Max height | 45 ft |

## M2 — Heavy Industrial District

**Article 22, Sec. 21-22** | PDF: https://www.lakecountyca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1371/Article-22---Heavy-Industrial-District-M2-PDF

**Purpose:** Provide areas for heavy industrial and manufacturing uses which can locate away from restrictive influences of non-industrial uses.

**Applicability:** Parcels not exceeding **5 acres**.

### Permitted Uses By Right (Sec. 22.4)
Conducted within completely enclosed building; outdoor storage ≤100% of gross floor area (screened); ≤6,000 sq ft per use or ≤12,000 sq ft total:
- All M1 uses (Sec. 21.4) and C3 uses (Sec. 20.3) (Ord. No. 2172, 8/12/1993)
- Wholesale sales, storage, and distribution centers with incidental retail
- Truck terminals and truck repair
- Contractors' equipment storage yards and equipment rental yards (Ord. No. 1749, 7/7/1988)
- Boat manufacturing and repair
- Sale of pre-sized rock (no on-site excavation, crushing, or sorting)
- Industrial and residential accessory uses

### Minor Use Permit Required (Sec. 22.5)
Same uses when exceeding 6,000 sq ft per use or 12,000 sq ft total; uses not in compliance with Article 41; uses involving handling of **explosives or dangerous materials**; lumber resaw mills; food services (cafes/diners 6am–6pm).

### Major Use Permit Required (Sec. 22.6)
- Auto wrecking yards, salvage/dismantling yards, junk yards
- Concrete or asphalt batch plants, rock crushing, sand and gravel plants
- Commercial excavation (quarries, gravel pits, topsoil yards)
- Lumber mills
- Processing, slaughtering, or packaging of beast/fish/fowl (canneries, meat packing, slaughterhouses, tanneries, rendering plants)
- **Manufacturing, mixing, or processing of chemicals including acetylene gas, acid, ammonia, asbestos, or explosives**
- **Electroplating establishments**
- **Hazardous or toxic waste disposal operations**
- Manufacturing involving pulverization of clays, kilns fired other than electric/gas, or refining/rendering of oils or fats
- **Fuel tank farms, wholesale fuel sales or distributors, including natural gas or propane distributors or wholesalers** (Ord. No. 1749, 7/7/1988)

### Development Standards (Sec. 22.11–22.17)
| Standard | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Min lot size (public water & sewer) | 12,500 sq ft |
| Min lot size (well + public sewer, or public water + septic) | 20,000 sq ft |
| Min lot size (well + septic) | 40,000 sq ft |
| Min average lot width (interior) | 100 ft |
| Min average lot width (corner) | 125 ft |
| Max length-to-width ratio | 3:1 |
| **Max lot coverage** | **75%** (Ord. No. 1749, 7/7/1988) |
| Front yard setback | 10 ft from lot line, or 35 ft from road centerline |
| Rear yard setback | None (30 ft if contiguous to residential) |
| Side yard setback | None (30 ft if contiguous to residential) |
| Max height | 45 ft |

Note: M2 rear/side setbacks from residential (30 ft) are stricter than M1 (10 ft).

## MP — Industrial Park District

**Article 23, Sec. 21-23** | PDF: https://www.lakecountyca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1372/Article-23---Industrial-Park-District-MP-PDF

**Purpose:** Provide areas for heavy commercial/manufacturing, research facilities, or administrative offices clustered within business parks with well-designed buildings and attractively landscaped areas.

**Applicability:** Designated community areas identified in the Lake County General Plan served by **public water and sewer systems**. Parcels must front on a "minor collector" or larger road (or a local road serving as freeway frontage road per Planning Director determination).

### Permitted Uses By Right (Sec. 23.4)
Conducted within completely enclosed building; not obnoxious/offensive; outdoor storage ≤15% of gross floor area (screened):
- Retail sales of large/bulky household items (appliances, carpet, furniture, fireplaces)
- Commercial trade services (cleaning/dyeing, bottling, cabinet/carpentry, blacksmith/welding/machine shops, furniture repair)
- Construction-related sales (hardware, contractor offices, electrical/plumbing/heating shops, light equipment rental)
- Commercial warehouses and mini-storage
- Light manufacturing, assembly, packaging of: high-tech products; grains/produce; wood/paper; fabrics/textiles; leather; metals and alloys
- Administrative and general business offices
- Professional construction support services (blueprinting, printing, engineering, surveying, architectural)
- **Research, development, and testing laboratories and facilities**
- Food services (6am–6pm)
- Laundry, janitorial, or facility maintenance services
- Industrial and commercial accessory uses

### Minor Use Permit Required (Sec. 23.5)
Uses that are obnoxious or offensive (noise, dust, odor, smoke, vibration, danger to life/property) or not in compliance with Article 41; health/child care/recreational facilities accessory to principal use.

### Major Use Permit Required (Sec. 23.6)
- Obnoxious/offensive uses when exceeding performance standards
- Home improvement centers; recycling centers
- Boat manufacturing and repair

### Development Standards (Sec. 23.11+)
| Standard | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Min lot size | 20,000 sq ft |
| Min average lot width (interior) | 100 ft |
| Min average lot width (corner) | 125 ft |

## Use Permit Process

- **Minor Use Permit (MUP):** Initial filing fee $351 (eff. 7/1/2025, per Master Fee Ordinance 3056).
- **Major Use Permit (MUP/CUP):** Initial filing fee $366.
- Both permit types processed by Lake County Planning Division (Community Development Department). Subject to CEQA review.
- Development Review also required for all uses in industrial districts per Article 56.

## EHS Considerations

- All industrial uses are subject to **Article 41 Performance Standards** (noise, offensive odor, smoke, dust, bright lights, vibration).
- Handling of explosives or dangerous materials in M1 and M2 districts always requires at minimum a Minor Use Permit.
- Hazardous/toxic waste disposal requires a Major Use Permit in M2 (expressly prohibited in M1 unless re-classified).
- Chemical manufacturing (acids, ammonia, acetylene, asbestos) requires MUP in M2.
- Fuel tank farms require MUP in both M1 (Ord. 1749) and M2 (Ord. 1749).
- Electroplating requires MUP in M1 and M2.
