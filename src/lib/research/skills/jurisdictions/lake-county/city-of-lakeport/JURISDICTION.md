## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air — Lake County Air Quality Management District (LCAQMD)

- **Controlling district:** LCAQMD — countywide district; Lakeport has NO separate city air program. LCAQMD regulates all stationary sources within Lake County, including within city limits.
- **Address:** 2617 South Main Street, Lakeport, CA 95453 | Phone: (707) 263-7000 | Email: lcaqmd@lcaqmd.net
- **Programs:** Authority-to-Construct (ATC) and Permit-to-Operate (PTO) for stationary sources; enforces federal NSPSs and State Air Toxic Control Measures (ATCMs); regulates geothermal generation (The Geysers), mining, gasoline stations, and any commercial operation with air emissions.
- **Open burning:** Regulated by LCAQMD. Burn season: open burning generally banned May 1 – Nov 1. Residential/agricultural burn permits: $34. Smoke Management Plan required for multi-day/overnight/large burns. Burn-day status: (707) 263-3121 or lcaqmd.net.
- **Funding:** Self-supporting through state subvention, permit fees, fines, and grants — not property taxes.
- Source: https://lcaqmd.net/ | https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/lake-county-air-quality-managament-district

### CUPA — Lake County Division of Environmental Health (countywide)

- **Not its own CUPA.** City of Lakeport is served by the **Lake County Division of Environmental Health**, the single countywide CUPA.
- **Programs administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generator oversight, Hazardous Waste On-Site Treatment, Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Storage Tanks / APSA (AST/APSA), and Treated Wood Waste (TWW).
- **CERS reporting:** All regulated facilities file via California Environmental Reporting System (CERS).
- **UST:** Installation permitting, closure, spill bucket repair applications, monitoring plan documentation.
- **AST/APSA:** Annual certification via CERS; SPCC Tier I & II forms.
- **Address:** 922 Bevins Court, Lakeport, CA 95453 | Phone: (707) 263-1164 | Email: environmentalhealth@lakecountyca.gov
- **Fee schedule:** Lake County Master Fee Ordinance (updated July 2022 per CUPA page). Current fee schedule: contact Environmental Health or see lake county master fee ordinance document. [VERIFY current schedule posted online]
- Source: https://www.lakecountyca.gov/374/Hazardous-Materials-Management-CUPA | https://dhs.lakecountyca.gov/

### Fire — Lakeport Fire District (independent district, AHJ)

- **AHJ:** Lakeport Fire Protection District — independent all-risk fire district (NOT a city department). Serves City of Lakeport and certain adjacent unincorporated areas. Founded 1894.
- **Station address:** 445 N. Main Street, Lakeport, CA 95453 | Phone: (707) 263-4396 | Website: lakeportfire.com
- **California Fire Code adoption:** Lakeport Municipal Code Chapter 8.32 addresses fire prevention systems. The District maintains its own ordinances page (lakeportfire.com/transparency/ordinances). The adopted CFC edition and local amendments are listed there. [VERIFY current edition — 2022 CFC (Title 19 CCR) or 2025 edition effective 2026-01-01]
- **Plan submittal:** Fire plans submitted to Lakeport Fire District before city building permit issuance. See lakeportfire.com/how-do-i/submit-building-plans.
- **Burn permits:** Residential burn permits coordinated through LCAQMD (see Air section). Fire District issues burn permits for open burning within its jurisdiction — obtain at lakeportfire.com/how-do-i/obtain-a-burn-permit.
- **Wildland-Urban Interface:** City of Lakeport has adopted Fire Hazard Severity Zone (FHSZ) designations per SB 63 (2021) and AB 211 (2022). WUI building codes apply to new construction in Very High FHSZ; 100-foot defensible space required.
- **CAL FIRE:** CAL FIRE Sonoma-Lake-Napa Unit (LNU) provides wildland protection on State Responsibility Area (SRA) lands.
- GOTCHA: The fire district collects fire fees / provides sign-off BEFORE the city building permit is processed.
- Source: https://www.lakeportfire.com/ | https://www.cityoflakeport.com/community_development/fire_hazard_severity_zones.php

### Wastewater — City of Lakeport Municipal Sewer District (CLMSD)

- **POTW operator:** City of Lakeport, Public Works Department — operates the City of Lakeport Municipal Sewer District (CLMSD).
- **Sewer Use Ordinance:** Codified in **Lakeport Municipal Code Chapter 13.20 (Sewer Use and Pretreatment)** and Chapter 13.22 (Use of Public Sewers). Chapter 13.20 adopted 2008, incorporating HMBP-aligned pretreatment requirements and FOG Program.
- **Industrial pretreatment:** Chapter 13.20 implements federal General Pretreatment Regulations (40 CFR Part 403) and Clean Water Act requirements. Class I industrial users are subject to national categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR Parts 405–471).
- **FOG Program:** Chapter 13.20 requires food service facilities to install grease interceptors (minimum 500–750 gallons for exterior). Cleaning frequency: no fewer than once every 60 days.
- **Local discharge limits:** Chapter 13.22 states the council periodically publishes maximum permissible concentrations for industrial wastewater. Specific numerical limits [VERIFY in full text of Chapter 13.22 — not publicly retrieved].

### Stormwater — Lake County Clean Water Program / MS4

- **MS4 operator:** City of Lakeport (co-permittee alongside Lake County and City of Clearlake under a joint Stormwater Management Plan).
- **NPDES permit:** Phase II Small MS4 General Permit (State Water Resources Control Board Order No. 2013-0001-DWQ, NPDES No. CAS000004). All MS4 outfalls in Lakeport drain to Clear Lake.
- **SWMP:** Lake County Stormwater Management Plan (SWMP) adopted 2003; Program Effectiveness Assessment and Improvement Plan (PEAIP) updated 2021.
- **Illicit discharge prohibition:** Lakeport Municipal Code Section 8.40.110 (Ord. 853 §1 (part), 2006) — prohibits any non-stormwater discharge that causes or contributes to city NPDES violations. Section 8.20.050 (Ord. 392 §7, 1963) — prohibits dumping refuse or materials in any stream, creek, watercourse, or Clear Lake without Public Works director written permission.
- **IDDE SOP:** City adopted SOP-PW01 (Illicit Discharge Detection, Response & Reporting, v.1, 3/3/2022). Responsibility: Compliance Officer, Public Works Superintendent, or Utilities Superintendent.
- **Reference:** Lake County Clean Water Program Illicit Discharge Investigation and Reporting Procedures Guide (2019); CASQA BMP WM-4 (2009).
- Source: https://www.cityoflakeport.com/community_development/lake_county_clean_water_program.php | https://www.lakecountyca.gov/1192/Clean-Water-Program

## Notes

- GOTCHA: Lakeport does NOT run its own CUPA or its own municipal fire department. CUPA = Lake County Environmental Health (countywide); Fire AHJ = Lakeport Fire Protection District (independent special district).
- GOTCHA: There is only ONE industrial zoning district in Lakeport (single "I" designation, Chapter 17.13) — no M-1/M-2 split.
- Workflow: fire district sign-off precedes city building permit. For hazmat facilities, CUPA enrollment via CERS required before operations begin.
- Air quality permits from LCAQMD are separate from city/county land use approvals and required for any regulated stationary source.
