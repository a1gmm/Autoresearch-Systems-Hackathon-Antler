## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

Fire code enforcement within the City of Lakeport is handled by the **Lakeport Fire Protection District** — an independent special district, NOT a city department.

- **District name:** Lakeport Fire Protection District (also styled "Lakeport Fire District")
- **Founded:** 1894
- **Jurisdiction served:** City of Lakeport and certain adjacent unincorporated areas of Lake County
- **Station address:** 445 N. Main Street, Lakeport, CA 95453
- **Phone:** (707) 263-4396
- **Website:** https://www.lakeportfire.com/

## Adopted California Fire Code Edition

- The District's ordinances are listed at: https://www.lakeportfire.com/transparency/ordinances (HTTP 403 blocked during research fetch — visit page directly).
- City of Lakeport Municipal Code **Chapter 8.32** (Fire Prevention Systems) contains fire prevention and sprinkler requirements adopted by the city.
- **[VERIFY]** The exact CFC edition (2022 or 2025) and adopting ordinance number must be confirmed via the fire district ordinances page or by contacting the district at (707) 263-4396.
- Statewide context: The 2022 California Fire Code (Part 9, Title 19 CCR) was the in-force edition through 2025. The 2025 California Building Standards Code (including 2025 CFC) became effective statewide 2026-01-01.

The Lakeport Fire District issues / reviews the following (details to confirm with district):

- Sprinkler system plan review
- Fire alarm system plan review
- Open burning / residential burn permits (coordinated with LCAQMD burn-day status)

## Plan Submittal Process

- Plans submitted to Lakeport Fire District BEFORE the city building permit is issued.
- Submittal page: https://www.lakeportfire.com/how-do-i/submit-building-plans (HTTP 403 blocked — visit directly)
- Fire district sign-off is a prerequisite for the city building permit application.

## Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI)

- City of Lakeport has adopted Fire Hazard Severity Zone (FHSZ) designations per SB 63 (2021) and AB 211 (2022).
- Properties in Very High FHSZ: 100-foot defensible space clearance required; WUI building codes apply to new construction.
- Interactive FHSZ map: https://fire-hazard-severity-zones-rollout-calfire-forestry.hub.arcgis.com/pages/access-map
- CAL FIRE Sonoma-Lake-Napa Unit (LNU) handles wildland protection on State Responsibility Area (SRA) lands outside the city.
