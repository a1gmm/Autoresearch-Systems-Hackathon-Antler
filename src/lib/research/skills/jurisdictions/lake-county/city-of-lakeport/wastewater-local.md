## Chapter 13.20 — Sewer Use and Pretreatment (Key Provisions)

**Purpose:** Sets forth uniform requirements for all contributors to CLMSD; enables compliance with Clean Water Act (1977) and General Pretreatment Regulations (40 CFR Part 403).

**Pretreatment program scope:**
- Applies to all facilities discharging to the CLMSD wastewater collection and treatment system.
- Prevents introduction of wastes that interfere with POTW operations.
- Prevents introduction of wastes that pass through inadequately treated into receiving waters (Clear Lake and connected waterways).

**Industrial User Classification:**
- **Class I User:** Any user subject to national categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR Chapter I, Subchapter N, Parts 405–471). Categorical standards specify quantities or concentrations of pollutants by industrial subcategory.

**FOG Program (Fats, Oils & Grease):**
- Applies to all residential and commercial facilities within CLMSD.
- Food service establishments must install properly sized grease interceptors:
  - Installation deadline for pre-existing FSEs: August 1, 2009
  - Minimum exterior interceptor capacity: 500–750 gallons
  - Cleaning frequency: no fewer than once every 60 days
- Permit requests: Community Development Dept., (707) 263-3056 ext. 204
- Compliance officer: (707) 263-3578 ext. 403 or compliance@cityoflakeport.com

**Pretreatment devices recognized:**
- Grease traps and interceptors
- Skimmers and separators
- Process flow treatment systems (carbon filtration, coagulation units)
