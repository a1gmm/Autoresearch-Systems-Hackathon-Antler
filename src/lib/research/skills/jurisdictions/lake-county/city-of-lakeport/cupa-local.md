## CUPA Agency

The City of Lakeport does NOT operate its own CUPA. All CUPA programs within city limits are administered by the **Lake County Division of Environmental Health** — the single countywide CUPA serving all of Lake County.

- **Agency name:** Lake County Division of Environmental Health — Hazardous Materials Management (CUPA)
- **Address:** 922 Bevins Court, Lakeport, CA 95453
- **Phone:** (707) 263-1164
- **Email:** environmentalhealth@lakecountyca.gov
- **CUPA page:** https://www.lakecountyca.gov/374/Hazardous-Materials-Management-CUPA
- **Parent department:** Lake County Department of Health Services — Environmental Health Division (https://dhs.lakecountyca.gov/)

## Programs Administered

Lake County Environmental Health administers all six state CUPA unified program elements within city limits:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — required for businesses storing hazardous materials above threshold quantities; filed via California Environmental Reporting System (CERS).
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator** — permitting and inspection of businesses that generate hazardous waste; oversight of on-site treatment.
3. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — installation permits, closure permits, spill bucket repair applications, monitoring plan documentation.
4. **Aboveground Storage Tanks / APSA (AST)** — annual certification via CERS; SPCC (Spill Prevention Control & Countermeasure) Tier I & II compliance forms; California Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) compliance.
5. **Hazardous Waste On-Site Treatment** — tiered permitting for conditionally exempt, conditionally authorized, and permit-by-rule treatment.
6. **Treated Wood Waste (TWW)** — oversight per DTSC requirements.

## CERS Reporting

All regulated facilities must report annually via the California Environmental Reporting System:
- **Portal:** https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/
- HMBP, UST, and APSA programs all require CERS filings. Deadline is typically March 1 each year for HMBP renewal.

## Hazardous Waste Disposal (Residents & Small Generators)

- Lake County operates a household hazardous waste (HHW) drop-off program at no charge to residents (most materials free; small fee for commercial generators).
- Businesses must call (707) 234-6400 to schedule commercial appointment.
