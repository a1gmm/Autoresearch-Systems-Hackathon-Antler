## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air — Lake County Air Quality Management District (LCAQMD)

- **District:** Lake County Air Quality Management District (LCAQMD) — the single district for the entire Lake County Air Basin (county boundary = air basin boundary). City of Clearlake is inside this jurisdiction.
- **Programs:** Stationary source permits (Authority to Construct / Permit to Operate), open burning / burn permits (delegated to LCAQMD), State ATCMs and Federal NSPSs (NESHAP) enforcement, smoke management plans.
- **Burn permits** are issued online by LCAQMD at lcaqmd.net, not by the City or LCFPD.
- **No city-level air program** — all ATC/PTO permitting goes directly to LCAQMD.
- **Contact:** 2617 S. Main St., Lakeport, CA 95453 | (707) 263-7000 | lcaqmd@lcaqmd.net | Mon–Thu 9am–12pm walk-in.
- Source: https://lcaqmd.net/

### CUPA — Lake County Division of Environmental Health

- **Not its own CUPA.** The countywide CUPA for all six programs is the **Lake County Division of Environmental Health**, 255 N Forbes Street, Lakeport, CA 95453.
- **Programs administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), hazardous waste generator and on-site treatment permits, Underground Storage Tank (UST) permits/inspections, Above-ground Storage Tanks (AST/APSA), Treated Wood Waste (TWW), and hazardous material release response plans.
- **Online reporting:** CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) for annual AST certification and HMBP submittals.
- **Fee schedule:** Lake County Master Fee Ordinance No. 3056 (adopted Feb 7, 2017; 5% increase effective July 1, 2022). Current Environmental Health Fee Schedule dated 06/2025 available at lakecountyca.gov.
- **Contact:** Division of Environmental Health, (707) 263-1164.
- Source: https://www.lakecountyca.gov/374/Hazardous-Materials-Management-CUPA
- Source: https://www.lakecountyca.gov/222/Fee-Ordinance

### Zoning — Industrial Uses

- **Zoning code:** Clearlake Municipal Code (CMC) Title 18 — Zoning Regulations. The current version is the 2020 Zoning Code Update (294-page document), adopted as comprehensive update.
- **Industrial district:** Single industrial zone designation — **IN (Industrial)** zone (Chapter 18-10, cross-referenced as 18-17 in the ToC). No M-1/M-2 split — Clearlake uses a single IN designation.
- **IN zone purpose:** Wholesale and heavy commercial uses, plus industrial activities that manufacture, assemble, or package products entirely within a building without emitting fumes, odor, dust, smoke, or gas beyond the building confines. Performance standards (CMC Ch. 18-22) allow more intense industrial activities with protections.
- **IN zone development standards:** Minimum lot size 3,000 sq ft; maximum height 35 ft (up to 50 ft with CUP); no maximum lot coverage.
- **Permitted (P) uses in IN:** Heavy Industry; Light Industry; Research/Testing Laboratory; Salvage Yard; Storage Yard; Trucking; Waste Transfer Station; Warehousing and Logistics/Distribution Centers; Self-Storage; Power Generation (Renewable Fuel); Bus Depot; Impound Yard.
- **CUP (Use Permit from Planning Commission, "C") uses in IN:** Composting Facility; Extraction; Heavy Retail/Lumberyards; Cannabis Cultivation/Distribution/Nursery/Processing/Testing Lab (within CB combining zone); Heliport/Helistop; Storage Yard (in O zone).
- **CUP process:** Chapter 18-28 — Use Permits. Application to Planning Commission; findings include consistency with General Plan and zoning. Chapter 18-33 — Design Review required for most non-residential development.
- Source: https://www.clearlake.ca.us/DocumentCenter/View/1602/Clearlake-Zoning-Code-Update-Final--PDF (2020 Zoning Code, 294 pp.)
- Source: https://clearlake.municipal.codes/CMC/ChXVIII

## Notes

- GOTCHA: Clearlake does NOT run its own CUPA or its own municipal fire department — CUPA is Lake County Environmental Health; fire AHJ is the Lake County Fire Protection District (LCFPD).
- GOTCHA: The wastewater POTW is the **Lake County Sanitation District** (LACOSAN), not a city utility. Industrial dischargers must coordinate with LACOSAN for any pretreatment requirements.
- GOTCHA: The single industrial zoning district is **IN** — there is no M-1/M-2 split in Clearlake's 2020 code.
- Clearlake DOES run its own Building Department (separate from the county Building Safety division). City Clerk's office: (707) 994-8201.
