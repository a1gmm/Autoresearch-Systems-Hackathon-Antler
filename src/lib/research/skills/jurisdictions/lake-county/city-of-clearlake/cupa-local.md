## CUPA Agency

**City of Clearlake is NOT its own CUPA.** The Certified Unified Program Agency for the entire county — including the City of Clearlake — is:

> **Lake County Division of Environmental Health**
> 255 N Forbes Street, Lakeport, CA 95453
> Phone: (707) 263-1164
> Website: https://www.lakecountyca.gov/374/Hazardous-Materials-Management-CUPA

The Division of Environmental Health holds the CUPA certification for all of unincorporated Lake County and all incorporated cities within it, including Clearlake.

## Programs Administered

| Program | Authority | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) | HSC § 25500 et seq. | Annual submittal via CERS |
| Hazardous Waste Generator / On-Site Treatment | HSC § 25100 et seq. | Permit + inspection |
| Underground Storage Tanks (UST) | HSC § 25280 et seq. | Permit, installation, closure |
| Above-Ground Storage Tanks / APSA | HSC § 25270 et seq. | CERS annual certification |
| Treated Wood Waste (TWW) | HSC § 25143.1.5 | Permit |
| Hazardous Material Release Response Plans | HSC § 25500 | Combined with HMBP |

## Forms Available

Forms available on the Lake County Environmental Health web page include:
- UST closure, installation, and spill bucket repair applications
- Certification of Financial Responsibility
- Forms A–E (facility permitting, UST permitting, compliance certificates, monitoring, response plans)
- SPCC Tier I & II forms
- Engineered plan samples

## Online Reporting System

- **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System) — used for HMBP annual submittals and AST annual certification. Operators submit directly at cers.calepa.ca.gov.

## Key Process Notes

- Businesses handling hazardous materials above threshold quantities in Clearlake must submit their HMBP through CERS and obtain permits from Lake County Environmental Health, not from the City.
- The CUPA is the single point of contact for all six unified programs (HMBP, HWG, UST, AST, TWW, RRPP).
- Inspections are conducted by Lake County Environmental Health inspectors.
