## Industrial District — IN (Industrial) Zone

**Chapter 18-10** (also cross-referenced in ToC as sections 18-17.010/18-17.020).

Clearlake uses a **single industrial district** designation — **IN**. There is no M-1/M-2 split.

### Purpose (CMC § 18-10 / 18-17.010)

> "The purpose of this district is to provide locations for wholesale and heavy commercial uses and services in Clearlake which are not suited for other commercial zones. It provides areas for industrial types of activities which manufacture, assemble or package products within a building and do not emit fumes, odor, dust, smoke or gas beyond the confines of the building. Performance standards have been established to allow for more intense industrial activities and to protect Clearlake residents and the environment."

### Property Development Standards (CMC § 18-10 / 18-17.020)

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Minimum lot size | 3,000 sq ft |
| Maximum height | 35 ft (up to 50 ft with CUP; also see § 18-20.060) |
| Maximum lot coverage | No maximum |
| Yards | See § 18-20.040 |
| Parking | See § 18-20.090 |

### Permitted (P) Uses in IN Zone — Table 6, CMC § 18-18.040

Industrial, agricultural, communications, and transportation uses allowed by right ("P") in the IN zone include:

- Heavy Industry
- Light Industry
- Research / Testing Laboratory
- Salvage Yard
- Storage Yard
- Trucking
- Waste Transfer Station
- Warehousing and Logistics / Distribution Centers
- Self-Storage
- Power Generation, Renewable Fuel
- Power Generation, Fossil Fuel (Administrative Permit "L")
- Bus Depot
- Impound Yard
- Freestanding Wireless Telecommunications — Non-Stealth (Administrative Permit "L") and Stealth (P)
- Electric Vehicle Charging Stations (P in all zones)

### Conditional Uses (C — Use Permit from Planning Commission) in IN Zone

Uses requiring a Use Permit (CUP) from the Planning Commission in the IN zone:

- Composting Facility
- Extraction
- Heavy Retail, Lumberyards and Equipment
- Cannabis Cultivation / Distribution / Nursery / Processing / Testing Laboratory (all must also be within the CB — Commercial Cannabis Business combining zone per § 18-19.150)
- Heliport
- Helistop
- Broadcasting Center / Satellite Farm / Server Farm / Switching Facility (also C in GC and CD)

### Uses NOT Allowed in IN Zone

- Residential uses (except as specifically permitted)
- Airport (C in MUX only)
- Disposal (C in Open Space only)

### Note on Light Industry in MUX Zone

Manufacturing and production uses in the Mixed-Use (MUX) zone are limited to uses under 5,000 sq ft with a retail outlet (CMC § 18-18.040.E).

## Use Permit (CUP) Process — Chapter 18-28

- **Application:** Filed with the City of Clearlake Community Development Department.
- **Decision body:** Planning Commission (Use Permit); City Council for appeals.
- **Required findings (CMC § 18-28.040):** Consistency with General Plan and zoning regulations; no substantial adverse effect on surrounding uses; appropriate conditions can mitigate impacts.
- **Conditions of approval (§ 18-28.050):** The Commission may impose conditions to address impacts.
- **Expiration:** [VERIFY — typical California CUP does not expire if exercised within a reasonable period; confirm with city.]

## Design Review — Chapter 18-33

Most non-residential development in the IN zone is subject to Design Review:
- Application to the Community Development Director.
- Design Review Committee reviews.
- Findings required for consistency with the Design Review Manual (§ 18-33.070).

## Performance Standards Applicable to IN Zone — Chapter 18-22

All IN zone uses must comply with CMC § 18-22 performance standards:

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Noise** (§ 18-22.010) | Must comply with Clearlake General Plan Noise Element and Noise Ordinance |
| **Vibration** (§ 18-22.020) | No ground vibrations perceptible at property line |
| **Illumination** (§ 18-22.030) | No glare creating hazard or nuisance on other property |
| **Air contaminants** (§ 18-22.040) | Must obtain any required permit from the Lake County AQMD; prevent dust from crossing property lines |
| **Wastewater discharge** (§ 18-22.050) | Pretreatment to POTW requirements required before sewer discharge; no direct discharge to rivers, creeks, ditches, or Clear Lake |
| **Heat** (§ 18-22.060) | Temperature increase of no more than 20°F at property line or public right-of-way |
| **Solid waste** (§ 18-22.070) | Containers to prevent nuisance, health, fire hazards; facilities for recyclables |
| **Odors** (§ 18-22.090) | Odorous matter must not be readily detectable beyond property line |
| **Flammable material** (§ 18-22.100) | Comply with local, state, and federal safety standards; adequate fire-fighting equipment required |
