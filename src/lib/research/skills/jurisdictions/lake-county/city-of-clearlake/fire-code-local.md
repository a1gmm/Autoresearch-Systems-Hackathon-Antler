## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

**Lake County Fire Protection District (LCFPD)**
14815 Olympic Drive, Clearlake, CA 95422
Phone: (707) 994-2170
Email: office@lakecountyfire.com
Hours: Monday–Friday, 8am–5pm (closed 12pm–1pm)
Fire Marshal: alancaster@lakecountyfire.com
Website: https://www.lakecountyfire.com/

LCFPD is an independent fire protection district — it is NOT a department of the City of Clearlake. All fire plan review, fire permits, and fire code enforcement within the City of Clearlake are handled by LCFPD.

## Municipal Code Chapter

- Fire code adoption for the City of Clearlake falls under **Clearlake Municipal Code Title IX (Building and Housing)**, which adopts the California Building Standards Code (Title 24) by reference.
- The fire plan review / permit authority is delegated to LCFPD as the AHJ; the City Building Department coordinates fire fees and directs applicants to LCFPD for fire clearances.

- Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI) provisions apply; Lake County is substantially in a High or Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone (FHSZ). Defensible space (AB-38) inspection requests are handled through LCFPD.
- **Burn permits** are issued by the Lake County Air Quality Management District (LCAQMD), not LCFPD. See lcaqmd.net for open burn permits.

## Fire Mitigation Fees

- Fire mitigation fees are required for **all new construction or additions**.
- Must be paid before the City of Clearlake Building Department will issue a building permit.

## Plan Review Process

- Plans submitted in person: Mon–Fri, 8am–12pm and 1pm–5pm at LCFPD headquarters (14815 Olympic Drive).
- Typically 2–3 sets of plans required (depending on plan type) plus calculations and site plans.
- Contact LCFPD office for electronic submission availability.
