## POTW / Sewer Authority

**Lake County Sanitation District (LACOSAN)**
Administered by: Lake County Special Districts Administration
County of Lake, 255 N Forbes St., Lakeport, CA 95453

LACOSAN is the principal publicly-owned treatment works (POTW) for portions of unincorporated Lake County. Multiple service areas exist within the district. The POTW's NPDES discharge permit is issued by the **North Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 1)**.

## Service Connection Requirements

- All applicants must submit application on District forms; property owner signature required.
- **Capacity expansion fees** are due and payable **prior to issuance of a building permit** for new construction or modifications to existing structures.
- Commercial connections: initial capacity fees computed from plumbing plans using 210 gal/day per single-family dwelling equivalent (SFD-E) for unclassified connection types; field inspection after occupancy may adjust fees.
- Change of use or increased intensity triggers new application and additional capacity expansion charges (credit given for previously paid amounts).
- Capacity expansion fees set by ordinance; rate schedules approved and adopted by LACOSAN Board of Directors.

## Prohibited Discharges (per Rules & Regulations)

The following shall not be discharged into the District sewer system:
- Cooling water
- Air conditioning water
- Water softener brine
- Groundwater
- Oil
- Hazardous materials
- Roof drainage

## Industrial Pretreatment

- Industrial users discharging to LACOSAN are subject to the Sewer Use Ordinance (Appendix A of the County Code).
- Article IV, Sec. 407 addresses copy of use / business/large/secondary connections.
- **Specific local numerical discharge limits** for industrial users [VERIFY — specific limit tables were not available in publicly downloadable summary documents as of fetch date. Contact LACOSAN Special Districts Administration directly].
- Federal categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR Part 403 and applicable categorical standards) apply to significant industrial users (SIUs) discharging to LACOSAN regardless of local adoption.

## Billing and Delinquency

- Billing occurs at two-month intervals.
- All unpaid charges, penalties, and interest constitute a lien on the property.
- Late payment charge added after 60 days delinquency (per applicable ordinance).
- $10 return check charge for dishonored payments.

## Other Sewer Districts in Lake County

Lake County Sanitation District is the primary county-operated POTW, but the County Code includes sewer ordinances for additional districts:
- **KCWWD#3** (Kelseyville County Water District No. 3) — Appendix C and D.
- Additional districts per Appendix B (other district sewer use ordinance).
