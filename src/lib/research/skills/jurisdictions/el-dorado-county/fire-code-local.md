## Overview

El Dorado County has no single county-wide fire AHJ. Multiple independent fire protection districts each adopt their own California Fire Code edition with local amendments, which must be ratified by the El Dorado County Board of Supervisors. The unincorporated county's building-department fire plan-check authority is separate from the fire districts' operational permit authority.

County Ordinance Code fire prevention chapter: **Title 8, Chapter 8.08 (Fire Prevention)** on Municode: https://library.municode.com/ca/el_dorado_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTAGECOOR_TIT8PUHESA_CH8.08FIPR

### Key Local Amendments (Ordinance 2025-01)

**§101.1 Title** — Renamed "Fire Code of El Dorado County Fire Protection District."

**§105.5 Operational Permits** — Extended through §105.5.60; added:
- §105.5.60: Automobile wrecking yards require an operational permit.

**§105.6 Construction Permits** — Extended through §105.6.27; added:
- §105.6.26: Automobile wrecking yards — construction permit required for installation/modification.
- §105.6.27: Traffic calming devices — construction permit required for installation/modification.

**§108.5.1 Permit, Plan Review, and Inspection Fees** — Fees per most current district fee schedule adopted by board of directors (H&S Code §17951).

**§108.5.2 Cost Recovery Fees** — District may charge for response costs when negligence or law violation causes response; fee shall not exceed actual suppression/response cost (H&S Code §13916).

**§108.7 Administrative Costs** — AHJ may bill for actual travel and on-site time when contractor fails a scheduled test/inspection.

**§112.5 Appeal Procedures** — Written appeal within 15 calendar days of notice postmark; filed with Secretary of Governing Board.
