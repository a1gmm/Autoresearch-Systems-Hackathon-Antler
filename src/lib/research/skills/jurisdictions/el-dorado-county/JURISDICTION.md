## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air

El Dorado County operates its own local air district — the **El Dorado County Air Quality Management District (EDAQMD)** — which is the permitting authority (ATC + Permit to Operate) for all stationary sources in the county. State and federal sources requiring Title V major permits are co-regulated with CARB/EPA but administered locally by EDAQMD. EDAQMD is NOT under PCAPCD, SMAQMD, or any neighboring district. The district adopts its own rules and fee schedule; EPA has approved EDAQMD rules into the California SIP.

### CUPA

El Dorado County Environmental Management Department (EMD) is the sole **Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA)** for the entire county, covering the unincorporated county and both incorporated cities (Placerville and South Lake Tahoe). No Participating Agencies (PAs); EMD is the single point of contact for all six Unified Program elements:

1. Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) — via CERS; threshold: ≥55 gal / ≥500 lb / ≥200 cu ft
2. Hazardous Waste Generator (SQG/LQG)
3. Underground Storage Tanks (UST) — ICC UST Inspector certified staff
4. Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) — OSFM APSA Inspector certified staff
5. CalARP / Risk Management Plan — no facilities currently covered as of last published data
6. Hazardous Materials Release Response / Area Plan

As of August 2021: ~975 regulated facilities. EMD also runs the county Household Hazardous Waste (HHW) program. Fee schedule: [VERIFY — contact EMD directly; not published on retrievable county pages].

### Fire (AHJ)

Multiple fire authorities hold jurisdiction across El Dorado County's unincorporated area. There is no single county-wide fire AHJ; which district applies depends on location:

- **El Dorado County Fire Protection District (EDCFPD)**: Adopted **2025 California Fire Code** (Ordinance 2025-01 ECF), incorporating 2024 IFC. Repeals Ordinance 2022-02. County Ordinance Code Chapter 8.08 covers fire prevention (Title 8, Public Health and Safety). Permits: operational permits (CFC §105.5, including automobile wrecking yards §105.5.60) and construction permits (CFC §105.6). Fee schedule referenced in §108.5.1; current schedule adopted by district board of directors. Contact: (530) 644-9630.
  - Source: https://www.eldoradocountyfire.com/fire-code-amendments
  - Ordinance PDF: https://eldoradocountyfire.specialdistrict.org/files/849e110c0/Ordinance+2025-XX+ECF.pdf

- **Diamond Springs-El Dorado Fire Protection District (DSEDFPD)**: Adopted **2022 California Fire Code** (Ordinance 2022-01), incorporating 2021 IFC; ratified by El Dorado County Board of Supervisors. Amendments to CFC sections 101.1, 105.5, 105.6, 503, 507, 903, 907, and others. Ratifying county resolution on file (23-0015).
  - Source: https://www.diamondfire.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Diamond-Springs-El-Dorado-Fire-Code-Ordinance-2022-01-signed.pdf

- **CAL FIRE Amador-El Dorado Unit (AEU)**: State Responsibility Area fire protection for unincorporated SRA lands.
- **El Dorado Hills Fire Department**: Separate district serving El Dorado Hills community; adopts own local amendments aligned with El Dorado County Regional Fire Protection Standards.
- **South Lake Tahoe Fire Rescue**: City-run fire department for incorporated South Lake Tahoe.

County Ordinance Code (Municode): https://library.municode.com/ca/el_dorado_county/codes/code_of_ordinances — Chapter 8.08 (fire prevention).

### Wastewater / Industrial Pretreatment

**El Dorado Irrigation District (EID)** is the POTW serving the western El Dorado County service area (Placerville and surrounding communities). EID administers an **Industrial Pretreatment and Pollution Prevention (IPP) Program**, issuing Wastewater Discharge Permits (WWDPs) to industrial and commercial dischargers. The IPP controls discharges from: food service establishments, commercial printing, manufacturing, automotive facilities, dental/medical offices, and other Significant Industrial Users (SIUs).

South Lake Tahoe area is served by South Tahoe Public Utility District (STPUD), a separate agency with its own pretreatment program.

### Stormwater

El Dorado County operates under two MS4 permit regimes depending on watershed:

- **Western El Dorado County** (American River / Sacramento River watershed): MS4 permit issued by **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board**. County published a Storm Water Management Plan (SWMP) covering six program elements: public education, public participation, illicit discharge detection, construction runoff control, post-construction runoff control, and pollution prevention/good housekeeping. Multiple county ordinances provide legal authority including: Grading/Erosion/Sediment Control Ordinance, Subdivision Design and Improvement Ordinance, Liquid Waste Management Ordinance, Hazardous Material Management Ordinance.
  - SWMP (2004 baseline): https://www.eldoradocounty.ca.gov/files/assets/county/v/1/documents/land-use/stormwater/swmp.pdf
  - Central Valley MS4 permits: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/centralvalley/water_issues/storm_water/municipal_permits/

- **Lake Tahoe Basin** (South Lake Tahoe area): Covered under Lahontan RWQCB MS4 permit; additionally subject to Tahoe Regional Planning Agency (TRPA) stormwater regulations.

Industrial facilities in El Dorado County still need separate coverage under the **State Industrial General Permit (IGP)** (NPDES General Permit CAS000001) from the State Water Board — this is statewide, not a local program.

### Zoning

El Dorado County Zoning is codified in **Title 130** of the El Dorado County Ordinance Code. The current zoning ordinance was comprehensively updated by **Ordinance No. 5030** (December 15, 2015) and last amended by Ordinance No. [see list through March 28, 2024 amendments].

Industrial zones are established in **Chapter 130.23 – Industrial and Research and Development Zones** (§130.23.010–130.23.040):

- **IL — Industrial Light**: Manufacturing, wholesaling, associated retail/service, where primary activity is within building or outdoor storage. CUP required for uses producing noise, odor, fumes, dust, smoke, vibration, glare, heat, electrical interference, or waste material beyond property boundaries.
- **IH — Industrial Heavy**: More intensive manufacturing, assembling, fabrication, processing, bulk handling, storage, warehousing, trucking. CUP required for uses posing safety hazard or producing particulate matter. Dwellings, care centers, certain commercial uses not allowed.
- **R&D — Research and Development**: High-technology, non-polluting manufacturing, R&D facilities, corporate/industrial offices in campus/business-park setting.

## Notes

- Single countywide CUPA: EMD covers unincorporated county and both incorporated cities; no city runs its own CUPA.
- Two incorporated cities only: South Lake Tahoe and Placerville. South Lake Tahoe has its own fire department (South Lake Tahoe Fire Rescue); Placerville contracts the El Dorado County Fire Protection District.
- Lake Tahoe Basin development is also subject to Tahoe Regional Planning Agency (TRPA) regulation layered on top of local building review.
- Air: EDAQMD is its own independent local air district — do not confuse with neighboring SMAQMD (Sacramento) or Feather River AQMD.
- CalARP: No facilities currently registered under CalARP/RMP in El Dorado County per last available EMD data.
