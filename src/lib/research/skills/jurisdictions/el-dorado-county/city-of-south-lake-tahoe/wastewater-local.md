## Sewer Use — Key Provisions

### Section 4.6 — Use of Public Sewers

**§4.6.11 — Types of Waste Prohibited.** No person shall discharge into any public sewer:

| Prohibition | Limit / Rule |
|---|---|
| Temperature | >150°F prohibited |
| Fat, oil, or grease (FOG) | >250 ppm by weight prohibited |
| Flammable/explosive liquids, solids, gases | Gasoline, benzene, naphtha, fuel oil — prohibited |
| Garbage | Only if properly shredded (all particles <½ inch) |
| Solids/viscous substances | Ashes, cinders, sand, metal, glass, plastics, tar, wood — prohibited |
| pH | <5.5 or >9.0 prohibited |
| Toxic/poisonous substances | Quantities injurious to sewer system, humans, animals, or receiving waters — prohibited |
| Septic tank sludge | Prohibited |
| Industrial wastewater | Violations of 40 CFR Part 403 federal pretreatment standards prohibited (§4.6.11(k)) |

**§4.6.12 — Preliminary Treatment Trigger.** District may require pretreatment before admission to sewers if influent has:
- 5-day BOD >300 mg/L, OR
- Other objectionable characteristics outside §4.6.11 limits, OR
- Excess quantities/rates of discharge.

Plans for pretreatment facilities must be submitted to District for written approval before construction.

**§4.6.13 — Grease Interceptors/Traps Required.** Required for businesses generating grease, oil, flammable wastes, or sand in liquid wastes. Not required for residential buildings. Type, capacity, and location must be District-approved; sizing per current District policy; must be accessible for cleaning and inspection.

### Section 4.2 — Sewer Permits

Two classes of permits:
1. **Sewer lateral construction permit**
2. **Public sewer construction permit**

Permit term: 3 years from issuance; extendable by 1 year upon payment of administrative fee.

### Section 4.6.14 — Special Discharge Permit

Industrial discharges of unusual strength or character may be authorized by **Special Agreement** with STPUD. Requirements:
- Written agreement (constitutes special permit) before discharge begins.
- Agreement may set maximum concentration limits, volume/composition reporting requirements, restricted connection points, District inspection/sampling rights, and fee structure.
- All costs (review, sampling, testing, permit issuance) paid by the owner/applicant.

## Industrial Wastewater Permit Process

1. Contact STPUD at (530) 544-6474 to discuss discharge characteristics.
2. Submit application with waste composition data, volume, and proposed treatment.
3. District evaluates and executes Special Agreement (§4.6.14).
4. Install required pretreatment/control manhole (§4.3.4) before discharge.
5. Ongoing inspection and sampling by District; all measurement at control manhole.

## No Numeric Local Limits Published Online

STPUD's Administrative Code references federal pretreatment standards (40 CFR Part 403) but does not publish a separate local limits table with facility-specific numeric effluent limits beyond the §4.6.11 categorical prohibitions above.
