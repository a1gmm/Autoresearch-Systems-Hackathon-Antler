## CUPA Agency

**El Dorado County Environmental Management Department (EMD)** is the Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all of El Dorado County, including the City of South Lake Tahoe. The City does **not** have its own CUPA — it is served under the county's umbrella CUPA.

## Programs Administered

EMD/CUPA administers the following Unified Program elements for regulated facilities in South Lake Tahoe:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — inventory, emergency response/contingency plans for facilities storing ≥ threshold quantities of hazardous materials.
2. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — permit, inspection, and leak detection requirements; inspected **annually**.
3. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — SPCC/AST program for facilities ≥ 1,320 gallons petroleum aboveground.
4. **Hazardous Waste Generator / Onsite Tiered Permitting (OTP)** — generator registration and inspection.
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — Risk Management Plans for facilities with listed regulated substances above thresholds.
6. **Uniform Fire Code (UFC) Hazardous Materials Permits** — coordinated with South Lake Tahoe Fire Rescue AHJ.

## Inspection Frequency

- UST facilities: **annually**
- All other regulated facilities: minimum **once every three years**
- ~1,000 regulated facilities countywide served by one manager, one supervisor, two full-time inspectors, one part-time inspector (as of program documentation)

## Geographic Notes

South Lake Tahoe is environmentally sensitive — Lake Tahoe's ecosystem makes hazmat spills of heightened concern. EMD notes this in its area plan. HMBP files for South Lake Tahoe facilities are maintained at both the Placerville and South Lake Tahoe offices.
