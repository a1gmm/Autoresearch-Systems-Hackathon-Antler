## Chapter 5.30 — Defensible Space Ordinance (2024)

Adopted second reading June 18, 2024; effective July 18, 2024. Applies to all residential, commercial, and industrial parcels within city limits (excludes state and federal lands).

**Zone requirements:**
- **Zone 0 (0–5 ft):** No vegetation or combustible materials that would propagate fire; mature trees allowed; wood piles, storage sheds, hot tubs must be >5 ft from structure.
- **Zone 1 (5–30 ft):** Clear ladder fuels and flammable ground cover; non-combustible ground cover (pea gravel, DG) preferred over wood mulch.
- **Zone 2 (30–100+ ft):** General defensible space maintenance.

Road/driveway clearance: ≥15 ft unobstructed vertical clearance for emergency vehicle access.

Real estate disclosure: Sellers of residential property in High or Very High FHSZ must provide a defensible space inspection report before escrow closes (per CA AB 38 and local Chapter 5.30).
