> Local EHS authority reference for the **City of South Lake Tahoe** (El Dorado County). Orientation for the research subagent: who administers the local programs and where their adopted codes live. Verify the live page before quoting; the 2025 California Building Standards Code (Title 24) replaced the 2022 edition statewide on 2026-01-01, so confirm the adopted edition. All development within city limits is also subject to dual-layer review by the **Tahoe Regional Planning Agency (TRPA)**.

## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air

- **Air district: El Dorado County Air Quality Management District (AQMD)** — the local air district serving all of El Dorado County including the City. South Lake Tahoe sits within the Great Basin Unified Air Pollution Control District (GBUAPCD) geographic area for some purposes, but the El Dorado County AQMD is the primary county-level district for stationary source permits within incorporated city limits. Confirm applicable district for the specific source type. TRPA's Clean Air Plan also applies as an overlay throughout the Tahoe basin.
- Source: https://www.eldoradocounty.ca.gov/Land-Use/Air-Quality-Management-District
- Asphalt plant emissions (NOx, PM) at the South "Y" Industrial Tract noted in TRPA Plan Area Statement 113 as a known concern.

### CUPA

- **Not its own CUPA.** Served by **El Dorado County Environmental Management Department (EMD)**, the countywide Certified Unified Program Agency. EMD maintains a **South Lake Tahoe satellite office** and keeps HMBP files there.
- CUPA programs administered: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), Hazardous Waste Generator/Onsite Tiered Permitting, Uniform Fire Code hazmat permits (coordinated with fire AHJ).
- UST facilities inspected annually; all other regulated facilities inspected at minimum once every three years.
- CUPA contact (Lake Tahoe office): (530) 573-3450; Placerville main office: (530) 621-5300; EMD.info@edcgov.us
- Fee schedule: El Dorado County EMD Approved Fees page — specific CUPA hazmat fee schedule [VERIFY current resolution #; last known reference: Resolution 187-2019, effective 2019-12-22].
- Source: https://www.eldoradocounty.ca.gov/Public-Safety-Justice/Waste-Disposal/Hazardous-Materials-Program/CUPA
- Source (fees): https://www.eldoradocounty.ca.gov/County-Government/County-Departments/Environmental-Management/EMD-Fees

### Fire

- **AHJ: South Lake Tahoe Fire Rescue** — the City's own municipal fire department; handles fire prevention, fire code inspections, and hazmat review within city limits. Address: 2101 Lake Tahoe Blvd., South Lake Tahoe, CA 96150. Phone: 530-542-6160. Fire Safety Inspector: Dan Brown, 530-542-6019.
- **City Code Chapter 5.10 — Fire Protection** — adopts the California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9) by reference. The 2022 edition of the California Fire Code was the in-effect edition through 2025; confirm whether city has adopted the 2025 CFC (statewide effective 2026-01-01). §§ 5.10.010–5.10.060 cover public nuisance, permits, fire service fees, penalties, abatement, and a board of appeals.
- **City Code Chapter 5.30 — Hazardous Vegetation and Defensible Space** — adopted by City Council second reading June 18, 2024, effective July 18, 2024. Establishes three defensible space zones: Zone 0 (0–5 ft, non-combustible), Zone 1 (5–30 ft, ladder-fuel clearance), Zone 2 (30–100+ ft, general clearance). Applies to all residential, commercial, and industrial properties within city limits (not federal/state land). Real estate sellers must provide defensible space inspection report before escrow closes.
- **Ordinance 2019-1131** — designated entire City of South Lake Tahoe a Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone (VHFHSZ).
- No open burning within city limits; exceptions for gas fireplaces and LP/natural-gas BBQs only.
- Source: https://www.cityofslt.gov/176/Fire-Prevention
- Source: https://www.cityofslt.gov/2521/Fire-Department

### Wastewater

- **POTW: South Tahoe Public Utility District (STPUD)** — the independent special district that operates the wastewater collection and treatment system serving South Lake Tahoe. Not a city department. Treatment plant capacity: 7.7 MGD. Address: 1275 Meadow Crest Drive, South Lake Tahoe, CA 96150. Phone: (530) 544-6474.
- **Governing document: STPUD Administrative Code** (codified water and sewer ordinances; effective 2025-01-01, updated through May 2026). Section 4 governs sewer use.
- **Pretreatment/discharge limits** in Administrative Code §4.6.11 (Types of Waste Prohibited): temperature <150°F; FOG <250 ppm; no flammable liquids, gasoline, benzene, fuel oil; pH 5.5–9.0; no toxic substances at injurious concentrations; BOD >300 mg/L triggers preliminary treatment requirement (§4.6.12). Industrial wastewater must comply with 40 CFR Part 403 federal pretreatment standards (§4.6.11(k)).
- **Special Discharge Permit** available for industrial waste of unusual strength (§4.6.14); agreement required before discharge.
- Grease interceptors/traps required for food service and businesses generating grease, oil, or flammable wastes (§4.6.13).
- Source: https://www.stpud.us/administrative-code (PDF: ACode Final 1-1-2025)

### Stormwater

- **MS4 Permit: Lahontan Regional Water Quality Control Board Order No. R6T-2022-0046, NPDES No. CAG616001** — joint permit covering City of South Lake Tahoe, El Dorado County, and Placer County for storm water/urban runoff discharges within the Lake Tahoe Hydrologic Unit. Renewed 2022; prior permit was R6T-2017-0010.
- **City Code Chapter 7.15 — Urban Runoff and Storm Water Quality Management** — local stormwater ordinance implementing NPDES permit obligations. Key rule: only rainwater or snowmelt may discharge to storm drains.
- Lake Tahoe TMDL compliance required: 10% reduction in fine sediment particles (<16 microns) discharge. Primary pollutants of concern: fine sediment, total nitrogen, total phosphorus.
- Illicit discharge hotline: 530-544-1910.
- Source: https://www.cityofslt.gov/342/Stormwater-Program
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/lahontan/board_decisions/adopted_orders/2022/docs/R6T-2022-0046_Lake-Tahoe-Muni.pdf

### Zoning

- **Governing framework:** City of South Lake Tahoe City Code Title 6 (Development Services), primarily Chapter 6.10 (Land Use Development Standards) and Chapter 6.55 (Plan Area Statements and Other Community Plans), administered jointly with TRPA regulations.
- **Industrial area: South "Y" Industrial Tract Community Plan / TRPA Plan Area Statement 113 (Industrial Tract)** — the primary industrial zone, located ~1 mile southwest of the South Tahoe "Y." Land use classification: Commercial/Public Service; Special Designation: Preliminary Community Plan Area and TDR Receiving Area.
- Permitted uses in PAS 113 (allowed "A" without special use): auto dealers, building materials, eating/drinking, food retail, mail order, nursery, outdoor retail, service stations, auto repair, business support, contract construction, health care, laundries, repair services, secondary storage, batch plants (S), fuel/ice dealers, industrial services, printing, small-scale manufacturing, storage yards, vehicle/freight terminals, warehousing, wholesale/distribution.
- Conditional/special uses (require special use approval): furniture stores, general merchandise, amusements, financial services, personal services, professional offices, vocational schools, recycling/scrap, batch plants.
- Residential uses: **no maximum allowable densities defined** — residential not a primary planned use.
- The proposed Tahoe Valley Area Plan Amendment, if adopted, will replace the South "Y" Industrial Tract Community Plan for that sub-area.
- TRPA zoning also applies and may further restrict coverage, SEZ restoration, and TDR requirements.
- Source: https://www.cityofslt.gov/2457/Land-Use-Plans-and-Zoning-Map
- Source (PAS 113): https://www.cityofslt.gov/DocumentCenter/View/84/Plan-Area-Statement-113

## Notes

- South Lake Tahoe operates its OWN municipal fire department (South Lake Tahoe Fire Rescue), unlike Placerville (verified).
- Development and building review in the city is layered with Tahoe Regional Planning Agency (TRPA) regulations in addition to the California Building Code and city ordinances (verified per city Building Division description).
- Wastewater is served by STPUD, an independent special district — not a city department. STPUD's Administrative Code is the operative sewer use ordinance, not a city code chapter.
- TRPA has its own independent permitting jurisdiction (land coverage, SEZ buffers, allocation of development) that applies in addition to city and county rules.
