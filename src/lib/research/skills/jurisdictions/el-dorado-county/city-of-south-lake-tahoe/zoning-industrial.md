## Zoning Framework

South Lake Tahoe uses a **Plan Area Statement (PAS) and Community Plan** system rather than conventional M-1/M-2 zone labels. Industrial and heavy commercial uses are concentrated in dedicated plan areas administered through:

- **City Code Title 6, Chapter 6.10** — Land Use Development Standards
- **City Code Chapter 6.55** — Plan Area Statements and Other Community Plans
- **TRPA Code of Ordinances** — an independent overlay applying to all lands within the Lake Tahoe basin; TRPA's coverage, SEZ, and TDR rules apply on top of city rules.

## Primary Industrial Zone: South "Y" Industrial Tract

**TRPA Plan Area Statement 113 — "Industrial Tract"**

- **Location:** Approximately 1 mile southwest of the South Tahoe "Y" (junction of US 50 and CA-89), within the City of South Lake Tahoe. Mapped on TRPA map F-19.
- **TRPA Land Use Classification:** Commercial/Public Service
- **Management Strategy:** Redirection (encourage industrial uses from Highway 50/89 corridor and residential neighborhoods to relocate here)
- **Special Designation:** Preliminary Community Plan Area; TDR Receiving Area for existing development
- **Existing character:** Combination of commercial, industrial, and public services; includes materials storage, warehousing, auto repair and storage, asphalt plant, and city bus maintenance yard. ~55% built out at time of PAS adoption.
- **Environmental constraints:** 55% SEZ (Stream Environment Zone), 35% low hazard, 10% high hazard soils; high water table; subject to localized short-term flooding; 50%+ land coverage with additional disturbed area.

### Special Policies (PAS 113)

1. Industrial uses from Highway 50/89 corridor or residential neighborhoods are encouraged to relocate to this area. New development and modifications subject to areawide land capability reclassification, SEZ restoration, and coverage transfer where feasible.
2. High water table flooding should be mitigated through drainage improvements in the adjacent county area.

## Pending Plan Amendment

The **Tahoe Valley Area Plan Amendment**, if adopted, will incorporate the South "Y" Industrial Tract area and replace the South "Y" Industrial Tract Community Plan. Check with City Planning Division for current adoption status.

## Planning / Permit Process

- **City Planning Division:** https://www.cityofslt.gov/142/Planning-Division
- All projects also require **TRPA review** for land coverage, SEZ buffers, and development allocation.
- Special uses (S) require a Special Use permit/CUP-equivalent from the City and TRPA review.
- Interactive GIS parcel lookup: available via City's Land Use Plans and Zoning Map page (search by parcel to get applicable PAS and land use zone).
