## CUPA Agency

**El Dorado County Environmental Management Department (EMD)** is the sole Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for the entire county. EMD covers:
- Unincorporated El Dorado County
- City of Placerville
- City of South Lake Tahoe

No Participating Agencies (PAs). EMD operates two offices: Placerville (main) and South Lake Tahoe.

## Programs Administered

EMD administers all six Unified Program elements:

| Program | Notes |
|---------|-------|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) | Submitted via CERS; threshold ≥55 gal / ≥500 lb / ≥200 cu ft |
| Hazardous Waste Generator (HWG) | SQG and LQG compliance, inspections, annual reporting |
| Underground Storage Tanks (UST) | ICC UST Inspector-certified staff; includes remediation oversight |
| Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) | OSFM APSA Inspector-certified staff |
| CalARP / Risk Management Plan | No facilities currently registered in El Dorado County |
| Hazardous Materials Release Response / Area Plan | Emergency planning and response coordination |

EMD also operates the county-run **Household Hazardous Waste (HHW) program**.

## Regulated Facilities

~975 facilities regulated in the CUPA program (as of August 2021 per EMD public data).

## Submission Portal

All HMBP submissions, updates, and annual reviews are made through the **California Electronic Reporting System (CERS)**: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov

Facilities must review, amend as necessary, and submit their HMBP annually via CERS.

## Inspection and Enforcement

EMD conducts routine inspections with outreach and education emphasis. Progressive enforcement used when necessary. Staff are trained emergency responders deployed to: hazmat spills, tanker rollovers, clandestine labs, unknown abandoned containers, and chemical incidents.
