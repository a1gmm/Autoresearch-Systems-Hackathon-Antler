## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

The City of Placerville contracts fire protection and emergency medical services to the **El Dorado County Fire Protection District (ECF/EDCFPD)**, an independent special district. The City does not maintain its own fire department. ECF serves approximately 74,000 residents across a 281-square-mile area divided into five response zones: Gold Hill, City of Placerville, Pleasant Valley, Pollock Pines/Strawberry, and Shingle Springs.

**Station serving Placerville:** Fire Station 25, 3034 Sacramento Street, Placerville, CA 95667.

ECF Contact: (530) 644-9630

## Justification: Local Climatic, Geologic, and Topographic Findings

ECF Resolution 2025-XX sets out three categories of local conditions that justify more-restrictive amendments than the base CFC:

**Climatic:** West-slope foothill communities (including Placerville) receive ~38 inches of annual precipitation, with ~88% falling November–April and only ~12% May–October. Summer afternoon relative humidity frequently drops to 10–25%; daily highs regularly exceed 90°F (historic extreme 114°F in Placerville). Dry north/east downslope wind events drive rapid fire spread. Multi-year droughts (e.g., 2012–2016, 2020–2022) increase wildfire severity. PSPS events during critical fire weather affect communications and traffic control.

**Geologic:** Dissected foothill landscape of steep canyons and narrow ridges; mapped Foothills fault system strands; moderate seismicity. Steep terrain creates canyon "chimney" fire spread. Public water system relies on electrically driven pumping stations vulnerable to earthquake-caused power outages.

**Topographic:** District spans ~1,400 to ~5,300 ft elevation; steep grades increase response times and hamper water tender operations; primary access routes (US-50) subject to storm closures and rockfall.

### Definitions (§ 202)
Added local definitions for: Campfire, Driveway, District, Executive Body, Fire Chief, May, Shall, Trained Crowd Manager. Campfire definition excludes LPG/propane appliances.

### Open Burning / Campfire Restrictions (§§ 307.4.4 – 307.4.2.2)
More restrictive campfire rules for grass, brush, and tree-covered areas due to high fire danger in El Dorado County. Added § 302.1 campfire definition.

## Permit Fees

ECF added fee authority (§ 106.6 and § 106.6.1) to recover actual costs of fire code plan checks, permits, and inspections. A penalty applies for avoidable failed inspections (§ 106.7). Specific current fee amounts are set by the ECF Board of Directors and are **[VERIFY]** — contact ECF at (530) 644-9630 or visit https://www.eldoradocountyfire.com for the current fee schedule.

## Municipal Code Chapter

The City of Placerville's fire code adoptions are effectuated by ECF ordinances (not a city chapter of the municipal code). Building-code and fire-code local amendments are processed through the City's Building Division. Source: https://www.cityofplacerville.org/BuildingDivision
