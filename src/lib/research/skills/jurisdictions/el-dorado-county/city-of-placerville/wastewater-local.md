## Governing Regulatory Permit

The City operates under the State Water Resources Control Board (SWRCB) **Statewide General Waste Discharge Requirements for Sanitary Sewer Systems**, Order No. WQ 2013-0058-EXEC, effective July 26, 2013. The City's Sewer System Management Plan (SSMP) was affirmed by City Council Resolution 8522 (May 23, 2017) and is governed by Resolution 8002.

Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) oversight: Central Valley Region (Region 5) for the Placerville area.

## Municipal Code — Title 7 (Health and Sanitation)

The sewer use and wastewater regulations are codified in **Title 7** of the Placerville Municipal Code. Key chapters and provisions:

### Chapter 5 — Wastewater Discharge / Sewer Use

| Section | Provision |
|---------|-----------|
| 7-5-5 | Public Sewer Overflows; Public Nuisance; Abatement Orders and Cleanup Costs |
| 7-5-22 | Harmful Discharge — Immediate Suspension or Termination of Wastewater Service: The director may immediately suspend/terminate service and sever connections to prevent environmental damage or damage to the collection system. Reinstatement requires proof the harmful condition has been remediated. |
| 7-5-23 | Failure to Comply with FOG Regulations — Suspension and/or Termination of Wastewater Service: Discharge of fats, oils, and grease (FOG) causing an SSO or blocking potential is declared a public nuisance. A written statement describing the cause and corrective measures must be submitted to the director within 15 days of the effective date of suspension. |

### Chapter 16 — Private Sewer Lateral Ordinance (Original)

Added to Title 7 by ordinance (previously drafted, public hearing January 9, 2018, continued off-calendar; brought back August 9, 2022). Sections 7.16.1–7.16.16. Established:
- Certificate of Lateral Compliance (COLC) — 10-year validity (original version)
- COLC required at time of property sale/title transfer
- Director of Development Services issues COLC
- Standards for inspection, testing, repair, replacement of private sewer laterals (PSLs) connecting to city main
- Authorized repair contractors list
- Fee for COLC per City Master Fee Schedule

Source: https://evogov.s3.amazonaws.com/media/17/media/97262.pdf

### Chapter 17 — Private Sewer Lateral and Systems Ordinance (Current)

Introduced October 24, 2023 (first reading waived); adopted November 14, 2023. Sections 7.17.1–7.17.16. Supersedes / replaces Chapter 16. Key changes from prior version:
- COLC validity extended to **20 years** (from 10 years)
- Building Official (not Director) issues COLC
- Shared responsibility for common sewer laterals explicitly codified (all co-owners jointly and severally liable)
- Definitions updated per current California Plumbing Code
- Expanded definition of "Common Sewer Lateral" to include Common Building Sewers serving multiple buildings
- CCTV inspection requirement for PSL compliance

Applicability: All PSLs connecting to the City municipal sewer system, including those in unincorporated El Dorado County areas served by the City system.

## Industrial Pretreatment Program

[VERIFY] — No formal Industrial Waste Pretreatment ordinance chapter or Significant Industrial User (SIU) permit program was found in the publicly available Placerville Municipal Code. The HCWRF's NPDES / WDR permit status and whether a federally-required Pretreatment Program has been established should be confirmed with:

- City Engineer / Public Works: (530) 642-5589
- SWRCB Central Valley Regional Board (Region 5): https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/centralcoast/

Industrial facilities discharging non-domestic wastewater should contact the City Engineering Department before connecting to the sewer system.
