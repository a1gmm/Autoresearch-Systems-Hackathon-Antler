## Zone Classifications Relevant to Industrial / Heavy Use

Placerville's zoning ordinance uses a single-letter "I" (not M-1/M-2) for its primary industrial district, plus a PDI overlay for planned industrial areas. There is no separate M-1 or M-2 designation.

### I — Industrial Zone (§ 10-5-19)

**Purpose:** Provide for manufacturing uses, assembly operations, and industrial services; concentrate industrial facilities in well-defined areas properly located relative to utilities, access, topography, and residential uses; control objectionable effects of necessary-but-detrimental uses.

**Permitted Uses (by right):**
1. Manufacturing, processing, assembly
2. Outdoor storage
3. Wholesale and retail sales
4. Parking facilities
5. Reconstruction of single-family dwellings (if other Title 10 regulations are met)

**Conditional Uses (use permit required per § 10-3-3):**
1. Processing facility for recyclable materials
2. Excavation of earth or building materials; mining or drilling for minerals and petroleum; garbage and rubbish fill and disposal; junk and wrecking yards
3. Small collection and large collection recycling facilities
4. Uses permitted in § 10-3-4 of this Title (general conditional uses)

**Development Standards:**
| Standard | Requirement |
|----------|------------|
| Minimum Parcel Area | 6,000 sq ft |
| Maximum Building Coverage | 60% |
| Minimum Parcel Width | 60 ft |
| Minimum Parcel Frontage | 60 ft |
| Minimum Yards — Front | 10 ft |
| Minimum Yards — Sides & Rear | 5 ft (or on property line with fireproof wall without openings) |
| Maximum Building Height | 50 ft |

Source: Ord. 1487, 14 Jan 1992 (base regulations for § 10-5-19)

### PDI — Planned Development Industrial (§ 10-5-22)

**Purpose:** Provide manufacturing, assembly, and industrial services (similar to I zone) while also accommodating incubator/cottage industries in an historic setting, tourist-oriented uses (campgrounds, RV parks, conference facilities, stores, restaurants, recreational uses), and uses that encourage economic growth and tourism.

**Permitted Uses (by right):**
1. Assembly
2. Incidental wholesale and retail sales
3. Manufacturing
4. Outdoor storage
5. Processing
6. Public and quasi-public facilities and uses

**Conditional Uses (use permit required per § 10-3-3):**
1. Processing facility for recyclable materials
2. Amphitheaters
3. Campgrounds
4. Conference facilities
5. Equestrian centers
6. Recreational vehicle (RV) parks
7. Small and large collection recycling facilities
8. Stores (retail)
9. Uses stated in § 10-3-4 of this Title

**Development Standards:**
| Standard | Requirement |
|----------|------------|
| Minimum Parcel Area | 6,000 sq ft |
| Maximum Building Coverage | 60% |
| Minimum Parcel Width | 60 ft |
| Minimum Parcel Frontage | 60 ft |
| Minimum Yards — Front | 20 ft |
| Minimum Yards — Sides | 20 ft |
| Minimum Yards — Rear | 20 ft |
| Maximum Building Height | 40 ft |
| Maximum Floor Area Ratio | 0.40 |

**Key Definitions (within § 10-5-22):**
- **Campground:** A plot of ground with 2+ campsites for public occupancy as temporary living quarters for recreation, education, or vacation purposes.
- **Equestrian Center:** Facility for horseback riding, staging, showing, keeping, training, and accessory uses for horses. (Ord. 1510, 23 Aug 1994)

Source: Ord. 1510, 23 Aug 1994 (§ 10-5-22)

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

Conditional uses in I and PDI zones require a **use permit** from the City of Placerville Planning Commission, governed by § 10-3-3. Any use not listed as permitted or conditional in the applicable zone is unlawful (§ 10-5-2(B)).
