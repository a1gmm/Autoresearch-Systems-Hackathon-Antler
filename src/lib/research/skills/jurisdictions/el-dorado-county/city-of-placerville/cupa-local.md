## CUPA Agency

**El Dorado County Environmental Management Department (EDC EMD)**

The City of Placerville is **not** its own CUPA. All Unified Program regulatory authority within the city limits is exercised by the countywide CUPA: El Dorado County EMD.

- **CUPA type:** County-level (serves entire El Dorado County, including City of Placerville and City of South Lake Tahoe)
- **Scale:** Approximately 975 regulated facilities countywide (as of 2021)
- **Staffing:** 1 CUPA manager, 1 supervisor, ~2 full-time Environmental Health Specialists (EHS/inspectors), 1 part-time inspector
- **Physical offices:** Placerville (west slope) and South Lake Tahoe (Tahoe basin)

Source: https://www.eldoradocounty.ca.gov/Public-Safety-Justice/Waste-Disposal/Hazardous-Materials-Program/CUPA

## Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)

Facilities that handle or store hazardous materials at or above CalEPA threshold quantities must submit and annually update an HMBP including:
- Inventory of hazardous materials (type, quantity, location)
- Emergency response plan and procedures
- Employee training program

**Reporting portal:** CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov — all HMBP submissions, updates, and related Unified Program data are submitted electronically through CERS. CERS data is automatically exchanged between the business, EDC EMD (CUPA), and CalEPA.

Source: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov

## Underground Storage Tanks (UST)

UST program regulated by EDC EMD under California Health & Safety Code Chapter 6.7. EDC EMD issues UST permits, conducts inspections, and oversees leak detection compliance, corrective action, and closure.

UST guidelines: https://www.eldoradocounty.ca.gov/Public-Safety-Justice/Waste-Disposal/Hazardous-Materials-Program/Underground-Storage-Tanks/Underground-Storage-Tanks-UST-Guidelines

## Hazardous Waste Generator Program

Small quantity generators (SQG), large quantity generators (LQG), and conditionally exempt small quantity generators (CESQG) operating in Placerville are regulated by EDC EMD. Manifests, storage times, and disposal requirements follow state Hazardous Waste Control Law (HWCL, H&S Code Chapter 6.5).

## Inspections and Enforcement

EDC EMD inspectors conduct routine and complaint-based inspections of HMBP, UST, HWG, and CalARP facilities. Enforcement authority derives from CalEPA Unified Program statutes. Emergency hazmat responses are coordinated with El Dorado County OES and the El Dorado County Fire Protection District.

Hazardous materials emergency planning is governed by the El Dorado County Hazardous Materials Emergency Plan (HMEP). Source: https://www.eldoradocounty.ca.gov/files/assets/county/v/2/documents/government/environmental-management/environmental-management/el-dorado-county-hmep19-gsafs.pdf
