## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air

- **District:** El Dorado County Air Quality Management District (EDCAQMD). The EDCAQMD is a county-level district and is the sole Air Pollution Control Authority for the City of Placerville; no city-level air permit exists.
- **Jurisdiction:** Covers both the incorporated City of Placerville and unincorporated El Dorado County west slope (Mountain Plan area). The Lake Tahoe portion of the county falls under the Tahoe Regional Planning Agency for some purposes.
- **Air Basin:** Mountain Counties Air Basin (MCAB).
- **Attainment:** El Dorado County is designated non-attainment for PM2.5 (wood smoke) in certain zones; check current CARB maps.
- **Stationary-source permits:** Any commercial or industrial project that may emit air contaminants requires an Authority to Construct (ATC) from EDCAQMD before construction, and a Permit to Operate (PTO) thereafter. Permit fees are set under EDCAQMD Rule 601 and adjusted annually by CPI. FY2022-23 representative fees: Aggregate Plants $2,432; Asphalt Batch Plants $4,428; Boilers/Heaters <5 MM BTU/hr $570; 5–24 MM BTU/hr $725; Internal Combustion Engine backup power <250 hp $431; Surface Coating <520 lb/yr $543, ≥520 lb/yr $787; Vapor Recovery Phase I $363, Phase II $1,213; Authority to Construct application fee $443. (Fees adjusted annually by CPI; see FY2025-26 budget for current amounts.)
- **Open burning:** Subject to EDCAQMD burn rules and El Dorado County FPD burn permit requirements.

### CUPA (Unified Hazardous Materials Program)

- **CUPA agency:** El Dorado County Environmental Management Department (EDC EMD) — the countywide CUPA. The City of Placerville is **not** its own CUPA; it is fully within the EDC EMD CUPA umbrella.
- **Programs administered:** All six CalEPA Unified Program elements: (1) Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP); (2) Underground Storage Tanks (UST); (3) Hazardous Waste Generator (HWG) / Tiered Permitting; (4) Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA); (5) California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP); (6) Hazardous Waste On-Site Treatment (Tiered Permitting).
- **Scale:** Approximately 975 regulated facilities countywide as of 2021.
- **Staffing:** 1 manager, 1 supervisor, ~2 full-time inspectors, 1 part-time inspector.
- **Reporting portal:** CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov — required for HMBP annual submission and all Unified Program data.
- **Fee schedule:** [VERIFY] — refer to EDC EMD Approved Fees page (403 at time of fetch). Contact EMD directly for current HMBP, UST, HWG, and CalARP fee schedules.
- Source: https://www.eldoradocounty.ca.gov/Public-Safety-Justice/Waste-Disposal/Hazardous-Materials-Program/CUPA

### Fire

- **AHJ:** El Dorado County Fire Protection District (ECF/EDCFPD) — an independent special district that provides fire protection and EMS to the City of Placerville under contract. The City does not operate its own fire department.
- **Station serving Placerville:** Fire Station 25, 3034 Sacramento Street, Placerville.
- **Adopted fire code:** ECF adopted the **2025 California Fire Code** via **Ordinance 2025-01** (ECF standard projects), with local amendments adopted by resolution of the ECF Board of Directors. The 2025 CFC took effect January 1, 2026 statewide. (Prior cycle: 2022 CFC via Ordinance 2022-02.)
- **Local amendments include:** Vertical clearance requirement raised to 15 ft (§ 503.2.1); road width 20 ft exclusive of shoulders; automatic fire sprinklers required for new buildings ≥3,600 sq ft (§ 903.2); sprinkler retrofit triggered when additions exceed 30% or 1,000 sq ft; fee authority added (§ 106.6/106.6.1) to recover actual costs of plan checks, permits, inspections; campfire restrictions (§§ 307.4.4–307.4.2.2); LPG tank exception above 5,000 ft elevation (§ 105.6.27); citation authority (§ 110.4.2).
- **Findings of fact resolution:** EDC FPD Resolution 2025-XX (adopted Sept 23, 2025 by City Council and FPD Board) documents local climatic, geologic, and topographic justifications for more restrictive amendments.
- **Note:** The 2010 CFC amendments had separate ordinances for projects within vs. outside the City of Placerville (Ordinance 2011-01 outside; separate COP ordinance within city limits).

### Wastewater

- **POTW:** Hangtown Creek Water Reclamation Facility (HCWRF), 2300 Cool Water Creek Road, Placerville, CA 95667. Phone: (530) 642-5244. Operated by the City of Placerville.
- **Service area:** 3,500+ residential and commercial accounts; service extends to some unincorporated areas of El Dorado County.
- **Regulatory basis:** City operates under Statewide General Waste Discharge Requirements for Sanitary Sewer Systems (State Water Resources Control Board Order No. WQ 2013-0058-EXEC, effective July 26, 2013).
- **Sewer use ordinance:** Title 7 (Health and Sanitation) of the Placerville Municipal Code governs the sewer system. Key provisions include:
  - § 7-5-22: Harmful Discharge — director may immediately suspend or terminate wastewater service and sever connections to prevent environmental damage; 15-day written statement required from FSE/property owner after suspension.
  - § 7-5-23: FOG (fats, oils, grease) discharge causing SSO declared a public nuisance; costs assessed jointly to property owner and FSE.
  - Chapter 16 (Ord. adding Ch. 16 to Title 7): Original Private Sewer Lateral Ordinance — COLC valid 10 years.
  - Chapter 17 (introduced Oct. 24, 2023; Ord. adding Ch. 17 to Title 7): Superseding Private Sewer Lateral and Systems Ordinance — extends COLC validity to 20 years; requires inspection, CCTV testing, repair or replacement at time of title transfer/sale; Certificate of Lateral Compliance (COLC) issued by Building Official.
- **Industrial pretreatment:** [VERIFY] — Specific IWP (Industrial Waste Pretreatment) permit program and categorical/local discharge limits not found in publicly available city code. Contact Placerville Engineering Dept. (530) 642-5589 to confirm whether a formal pretreatment ordinance and IWP permit program exist.
- **Sewer system:** Approximately 60 miles of sewer lines; system management governed by Sewer System Management Plan (Resolution 8002).

### Stormwater

- **MS4 permit:** City of Placerville is a Small MS4 permittee under the State Water Resources Control Board's Phase II Small MS4 General Permit. The City operates its own Stormwater Management Plan.
- **Grading ordinance:** Applications for grading within the city are subject to City Code §§ 8-7-12 and 8-7-15, and erosion/sediment control plans must comply with City Code Chapter 7-15.
- **Industrial general permit:** Facilities with industrial stormwater discharges must enroll under the State's Industrial General Permit (IGP) with the State Water Resources Control Board independently of any city permit.
- **Local BMP programs:** City promotes California-friendly landscaping, pyrethroid management, and household pesticide disposal via El Dorado County HHW programs.
- Source: https://www.cityofplacerville.org/stormwater

### Zoning (Industrial Districts)

- **Zoning ordinance:** Title 10 of the Placerville Municipal Code (Zoning Ordinance), last revised September 23, 2022.
- **Industrial zone designations:**
  - **I — Industrial Zone** (§ 10-5-19): Permits manufacturing, processing, assembly, outdoor storage, wholesale and retail sales, parking facilities by right. Conditional uses include recycling processing facilities, mining/excavation, junk/wrecking yards. Min. lot 6,000 sq ft; max coverage 60%; max height 50 ft; min. yards front 10 ft, sides/rear 5 ft.
  - **PDI — Planned Development Industrial** (§ 10-5-22): Permits assembly, manufacturing, outdoor storage, processing, incidental retail, public facilities by right. Conditional uses include recycling facilities, campgrounds, amphitheaters, RV parks, stores, restaurants (tourist-oriented). Min. lot 6,000 sq ft; max coverage 60%; max height 40 ft; min. yards front/sides/rear 20 ft; max FAR 0.40.
  - **HC — Heavy Commercial Zone** (§ 10-5-18): Also accommodates heavy commercial/quasi-industrial uses; not a pure industrial designation.
- **CUP process:** Conditional uses in I and PDI zones require a use permit from the Planning Commission under § 10-3-3.
- **Cannabis overlay:** CBO (Cannabis Business Overlay Zone, § 10-5-25) applies in certain areas; cannabis facilities require CBO designation.

## Notes

- Placerville is the El Dorado County seat. Fire protection is contracted to the El Dorado County FPD (verified). The EDCAQMD and EDC EMD (CUPA) serve the city on a countywide basis.
- The EDCAQMD separated from EMD in FY2010-11 and now operates as a standalone district with 9.0 FTE and a ~$8M annual budget (FY2025-26).
- El Dorado County Fire Protection District covers a 281-square-mile service area with ~74,000 residents; five staffed fire stations including Station 25 in Placerville.
