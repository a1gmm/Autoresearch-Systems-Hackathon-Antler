## POTW and Service Area

El Dorado County's unincorporated areas and the City of Placerville are served by the **El Dorado Irrigation District (EID)**, a special district acting as the POTW. EID administers the **Industrial Pretreatment and Pollution Prevention (IPP) Program** as required by 40 CFR Part 403 (federal pretreatment regulations).

The **South Lake Tahoe** area is served separately by the **South Tahoe Public Utility District (STPUD)**, which has its own independent pretreatment program. For South Lake Tahoe industrial dischargers, see the city-of-south-lake-tahoe folder.

Large portions of the unincorporated county are on septic systems (private sewage disposal systems) regulated by El Dorado County Environmental Management Department under a separate Private Sewage Disposal System Ordinance: https://www.eldoradocounty.ca.gov/County-Government/County-Departments/Environmental-Management/Environmental-Health/Septic-System-Components/Private-Sewage-Disposal-System-Ordinance

## EID Industrial Pretreatment and Pollution Prevention (IPP) Program

### Program Objective

Controls discharge of wastewater pollutants from industrial and commercial sources to:
1. Protect quality of reclaimed water and biosolids
2. Prevent interference with wastewater treatment and infrastructure
3. Protect worker health and safety

### Wastewater Discharge Permits (WWDPs)

EID issues **Wastewater Discharge Permits (WWDPs)** to Significant Industrial Users (SIUs) and other regulated dischargers. WWDPs may prescribe:
- Facility-specific discharge limits
- Required operational practices
- Pretreatment system installation and maintenance requirements

### Regulated Business Categories

Applicable businesses include but are not limited to:
- Food service establishments (grease interceptors typically required)
- Commercial printing operations
- Manufacturing facilities
- Automotive facilities (floor drain/oil-water separator requirements)
- Medical and dental offices (dental amalgam compliance program — see https://www.eid.org/regulatory/industrial-pretreatment-and-pollution-prevention-ipp/dental-amalgam)

### Permit Process

1. Contact EID IPP program to determine if your facility requires a WWDP.
2. EID evaluates discharge character and volume.
3. WWDP issued with facility-specific limits and conditions.
4. Periodic self-monitoring, reporting, and EID inspections.

## County Private Sewage Disposal (Septic)

For facilities not connected to the EID sewer system, El Dorado County EMD regulates private sewage disposal systems (septic). Industrial dischargers on septic are subject to Regional Water Board Waste Discharge Requirements (WDRs) rather than EID pretreatment rules.
