**Title:** Orange County Comprehensive Zoning Code
**Municipal Code Location:** County Codified Ordinances, **Title 7** (Land Use and Building Regulations), **Division 9** (Planning), **Article 2** (The Comprehensive Zoning Code)
**Adopted:** July 25, 2024 (by Board of Supervisors)
**Applicability:** All property within the unincorporated area of the County of Orange (Sec. 7-9-20).

## Industrial Zone District

Orange County's zoning code uses one primary industrial base district for unincorporated land:

### M1 "Light Industrial" District (Sec. 7-9-34)

**Code section:** Sec. 7-9-34 — "Employment Districts"
**Purpose (verbatim from Sec. 7-9-34.1):**
> "The Employment Districts are areas intended for use by employment generators, which are usually light and service industries or professional-administrative uses that have few nuisance or hazard problems. The uses shall be compatible with one another and with surrounding development."

> "The M1 'Light Industrial' District is established to provide for the development and maintenance of light industrial uses and industry-supporting activities. Industry-supporting activities are those activities which tend to promote the vitality of light industrial areas by providing a convenient location for services incidental to the conduct of business of the permitted uses, thus internalizing vehicle trips for such services."

There is only **one Employment District base zone (M1)** in the OC Comprehensive Zoning Code for unincorporated areas. There are no separate M2 or Heavy Industrial districts — the older General Industrial (M2) classification was consolidated in prior code updates. Heavy manufacturing/extraction is handled through the **SG "Sand and Gravel Extraction" District** (Sec. 7-9-35) for resource extraction only.

- **SDP** = Permitted, subject to Site Development Permit
- **UP** = Permitted, subject to Use Permit (discretionary)
- **—** = Not permitted

## Supplemental Regulations for M1 (Sec. 7-9-34.4)

- **Loading operations:** All loading on-site, screened by landscape or architectural feature — must not be visible from public street or adjacent residential/agricultural districts.
- **Trash/storage:** All cartons, containers, and trash enclosed by building or wall ≥ 6 ft. If unroofed, must be ≥ 40 ft from any residential or agricultural district.
- **Waste management and hazardous materials:** Per Sec. 7-9-118 (Waste Management and Hazardous Materials — see also Sec. 7-9-119 for waste transfer/materials recovery facilities).

## Discretionary Permits and Procedures

### Site Development Permit (SDP)
For uses designated "SDP" in Table 7-9-34.2. Ministerial-level review by Director (OC Development Services Planning Division). Section: Sec. 7-9-124.

### Use Permit (UP)
For uses designated "UP." Quasi-judicial; heard by Zoning Administrator (minor/routine) or County Planning Commission (significant impacts). Section: Sec. 7-9-125 and Sec. 7-9-126.

**Decision-making bodies:**
- **Director** (OC Development Services Planning Division): ministerial permits, administrative actions.
- **Zoning Administrator:** Use Permits and minor modifications.
- **County Planning Commission:** Appeals, discretionary permits with significant impacts, General Plan amendments.
- **Board of Supervisors:** General Plan and Zoning Code amendments, development agreements.
