## Local Amendments & Guidelines

OCFA publishes operational guidelines in its Community Risk Reduction (CRR) series. Key guidelines applicable to industrial/hazmat facilities:

### Hazardous Materials — Chemical Classification (Guideline G-04, effective January 1, 2026)

- Implements **CFC 2025 Chapter 50** (Hazardous Materials — General Provisions).
- Requires a **Chemical Classification** (equivalent to the Hazardous Materials Inventory Statement, HMIS) before any permit is approved or chemicals stored/handled on premises.
- OCFA performs a **Maximum Allowable Quantity (MAQ) review** considering control areas, open vs. closed use, and indoor vs. outdoor storage.
- Operational permit required for hazardous materials exceeding permit thresholds in CFC **Section 105.5** (Table 105.5.22). Sections 105.5.1–105.5.64 enumerate 64 permit categories.
- Chemical Classification must include: Chemical Classification Form (full inventory), Chemical Classification Summary Sheet (sorted by hazard class), and Chemical Classification Summary Totals (by storage area).
- Safety Data Sheets (SDS/MSDS) required with submittal.
- Source: https://storageocfaprod001.blob.core.windows.net/blobocfaprod01/2025/12/G-04-Chem-Class-2025.pdf

### High-Piled Combustible Storage (Guideline G-01)

- Implements **CFC 2025 Chapter 32** (High-Piled Combustible Storage).
- Operational permit required for high-piled storage (pile height > 12 ft, or > 6 ft for high-hazard commodities).
- Source: https://ocfa.org/Uploads/CommunityRiskReduction/G-01%20High%20Piled%20Storage%202023.pdf

## Operational Permit Categories (CFC §105.5, as adopted)

OCFA requires permits (apply via OCFA Public Services Portal or local Prevention Field Services office) for:

- Hazardous materials above MAQ thresholds (CFC Ch. 50 series — Ch. 50–67)
- High-piled combustible storage (CFC Ch. 32)
- Flammable/combustible liquids (CFC Ch. 57)
- Flammable gases, compressed gas (CFC Ch. 58)
- Hot work operations
- Aerosol products (CFC Ch. 51)
- Assembly occupancies ≥ 50 occupants
- Fuel dispensing
- Vehicle repair garages
- Toxic and highly toxic materials (CFC Ch. 60)

## Fee Schedule

OCFA charges inspection fees under its Prevention Field Services schedule. Key codes (from posted fee schedule):

- **Base inspection fee:** M150
- **Reinspection fee:** J200 (triggered after > 2 trips to verify corrections)
- Specialized permit/inspection fees apply to hazmat facilities, high-piled storage, assembly occupancies, daycares, and high-rise buildings.
- **Special event permit:** additional 50% surcharge if application arrives fewer than 10 working days before event.
- Fee schedule PDF: https://www.ocfa.org/Uploads/CommunityRiskReduction/OCFA%20Fee%20Schedule%20-%20Prevention%20Field%20Services.pdf
