## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials & Waste

- **Agency:** Orange County Health Care Agency (OC HCA), Environmental Health Division — designated CUPA by CA Secretary for Environmental Protection on 1997-01-01.
- **Scope:** Countywide CUPA for all unincorporated areas and all incorporated cities **except the City of Anaheim** (which operates its own standalone CUPA).
- **Participating Agencies** that run HMBP inspections within their city limits under OC HCA CUPA: Huntington Beach Fire, Orange City Fire, Fountain Valley Fire. LA County Fire runs La Habra's CalARP tier.
- **Programs administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste (HW), Underground Storage Tanks (UST), CalARP, Tiered Permitting (Onsite Treatment), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), Medical Waste, Universal Waste, Used Oil, Waste Tire.
- **Reporting portal:** CERS (CA Environmental Reporting System) — cers.calepa.ca.gov (state-level portal; OC HCA is the receiving agency).
- **Contact:** 1241 E. Dyer Road, Suite 120, Santa Ana, CA 92705 | (714) 433-6000 | Hours: 8 a.m.–5 p.m. (reception), 8 a.m.–4 p.m. (cashier).
- **Fees & online payment:** via the "Pay Online" link at ochealthinfo.com hazardous materials page.
- **Local ordinance chapter:** [VERIFY — specific OC Codified Ordinances chapter number not surfaced from official pages; cross-check OC Codified Ordinances Title 4 Health & Safety or the CUPA authorization document].
- Source: https://ochealthinfo.com/services-programs/environmental-health/hazardous-materials

### 2. Fire Code — AHJ & Permits

- **AHJ:** Orange County Fire Authority (OCFA) — JPA serving unincorporated county and 23+ member cities.
- **Adopted code (current):** California Fire Code **2025 Edition**, adopted and amended by **Ordinance No. 25-022**, effective **2026-01-01**. (Prior cycle: CFC 2022 Edition adopted by Ordinance No. 22-008, December 6, 2022.)
- **Appendices adopted:** Appendices B, BB, C, CC, and H were adopted with the 2022 cycle; confirm same for 2025 edition against Ord. 25-022 text.
- **Local fire code chapter:** County Codified Ordinances, Title 3 (Public Morals, Safety & Welfare), Division 3 (Fire Protection and Explosives). See https://library.municode.com/ca/orange_county → Title 3, Div. 3.
- **Fire permits:** OCFA issues operational permits for hazmat (CFC Ch. 50 series), high-pile storage (CFC Ch. 32), flammable/combustible liquids (CFC Ch. 57), flammable gases (CFC Ch. 58), and others. Apply via OCFA Community Risk Reduction — https://www.ocfa.org.
- **Fee schedule:** [VERIFY — no direct OCFA permit fee schedule URL retrieved; contact OCFA Community Risk Reduction].
- **Building/fire code portal:** OC Development Services — https://pwds.oc.gov/service-areas/oc-development-services/building-safety/building-grading-information/codes
- Source (codes page): https://pwds.oc.gov/service-areas/oc-development-services/building-safety/building-grading-information/codes

### 3. Industrial Wastewater — Pretreatment & Discharge Permits

- **POTW/Agency:** Orange County Sanitation District (OC San) — serves most of unincorporated OC and many cities.
- **Governing ordinance:** OC San **Ordinance OCSD-53** — Wastewater Discharge Regulations (current controlling ordinance; establishes prohibitions, effluent limits, permit classifications, and enforcement).
- **Permit classes:**
  - Class I — significant industrial users with numeric discharge limits, self-monitoring, TOMP required.
  - Class II — lower-volume industrial dischargers.
  - Zero Discharge Certification — for facilities with no discharge to sewer.
  - Special Purpose Discharge Permit — dewatering/groundwater remediation discharges.
  - Wastehauler Permit, FOG Permit, Discharge Authorization Request also available.
- **Application & contact:** permitcounter@ocsan.gov | (714) 593-7164 | 18480 Bandilier Circle, Fountain Valley, CA 92708.
- **Permits page:** https://ocsan.gov/ocsan-permits/
- **Industrial discharge page:** https://ocsan.gov/industrialdischarge/
- **Ordinance OCSD-53 text:** https://ocsan.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/OCSD53-Wastewater-Dischar.pdf
- Source: https://ocsan.gov/ocsan-permits/

### 4. Stormwater — MS4 / NPDES

- **Principal Permittee:** County of Orange (also covers Orange County Flood Control District).
- **Two-region split:** OC is bisected between two RWQCBs:
  - **North OC** (26 cities + unincorporated): Phase I MS4 Permit issued by **Santa Ana RWQCB**.
  - **South OC** (10 cities south of El Toro Road + unincorporated): Phase I Regional MS4 Permit issued by **San Diego RWQCB**.
- **Cooperative program:** H2OC — unifies all OC MS4 permittees under one stormwater program; County coordinates compliance.
- **DAMP:** Drainage Area Management Plan — OC's principal NPDES policy document, in effect since 1993 with subsequent updates.
- **Local ordinance:** County Codified Ordinances, Title 4 (Health, Sanitation & Environment), Division 13 (Stormwater/Urban Runoff Discharge, Control & Use), Article 3 (County Water Quality Management). See Municode nodeId TIT4HESAANRE_DIV13STMAURRUOURE_ART3COWAQUMA.
- **Program contact:** OC Watersheds / OC Environmental Resources — https://pwws.oc.gov/service-areas/oc-environmental-resources/regional-stormwater-program
- Source: https://pwws.oc.gov/service-areas/oc-environmental-resources/regional-stormwater-program

### 5. Zoning / Land Use (Unincorporated OC)

- **Governing code:** County Codified Ordinances, **Title 7** (Land Use and Building Regulations), **Division 9** (Planning), **Article 2** (The County Zoning Code), Subarticle 2 (Base Districts). Published as the "Orange County Zoning Code."
- **Current adopted version:** July 25, 2024 — https://pwds.oc.gov/sites/ocpwocds/files/2024-08/Adopted%20Orange%20County%20Zoning%20Code%207-25-24.pdf
- **Industrial district names:** The zoning code uses **Employment Districts** (Sec. 7-9-34) as the sole industrial base category. The only industrial base district in unincorporated OC is **M1 "Light Industrial"** — there is no M2 or heavy industrial district. Resource extraction uses **SG "Sand and Gravel Extraction" District** (Sec. 7-9-35). See [`zoning-industrial.md`](zoning-industrial.md) for the full use table and standards extracted from the July 2024 PDF.
- **CUP process:** Development applications, including Conditional Use Permits, are submitted through **myOCeServices** — https://myoceservices.ocgov.com/
- **Planning contact:** OC Development Services, Planning Division | 855-886-5400 | https://pwds.oc.gov/service-areas/oc-development-services/planning-development
- Source (zoning code): https://pwds.oc.gov/sites/ocpwocds/files/2024-08/Adopted%20Orange%20County%20Zoning%20Code%207-25-24.pdf
- Source (planning): https://pwds.oc.gov/service-areas/oc-development-services/planning-development

---

**GOTCHA:** Anaheim is the one city in OC with its own standalone CUPA — do not route Anaheim facilities to OC HCA. Several OCFA member cities have their own fire depts as Participating Agencies for HMBP. For CalARP, La Habra uses LA County Fire, not OC HCA. For wastewater, some small cities have their own local sewer agencies that are not OC San service area — confirm service area before applying to OC San.
