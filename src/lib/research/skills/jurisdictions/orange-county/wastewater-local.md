## POTW / Sanitation Agency

**Agency:** Orange County Sanitation District (OC San)
**Address:** 18480 Bandilier Circle, Fountain Valley, CA 92708
**Permit Counter:** permitcounter@ocsan.gov | (714) 593-7164
**Resource Protection Division (pretreatment):** (714) 962-2411
**Service area:** Most of unincorporated OC and the majority of incorporated cities. Some smaller local agencies have separate sewer systems not served by OC San — confirm service area before applying.

## Governing Ordinance

**Ordinance No. OCSD-53** — "Wastewater Discharge Regulations" (current controlling ordinance)

- Adopted by OC San Board of Directors; repeals Ordinance No. OCSD-48.
- Revision history: Original (09/30/05) → OCSD-48 (replaces OCSD-39, 2009) → **OCSD-53** (adopted 2016, replaces OCSD-48). Last reviewed/confirmed no changes: September 17, 2024; updated October 13, 2025.
- Legal authority: Clean Water Act (33 U.S.C. §1251 et seq.), General Pretreatment Regulations (40 CFR Part 403), Porter-Cologne Water Quality Control Act (Water Code §13000 et seq.).
- Full text (Appendix E2 of OC San SSMP): https://www.ocsan.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/SSMP-Vol-II_App_E2_Ordinance-No-OCSD-53_101325.pdf
- Also available: https://ocsan.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/OCSD53-Wastewater-Dischar.pdf

**Structure:** 8 Articles — General Provisions, Prohibitions/Limits, Permits/Fees, Facilities Requirements, Monitoring/Reporting, Enforcement, Sewer Service Charges, Severability.

## Permit Classifications (OCSD-53, Article 3)

| Permit Type | Section | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Class I Wastewater Discharge Permit** | §302 | Significant Industrial Users (SIUs); dischargers with federal categorical pretreatment standards; or any user with potential to adversely affect OC San operations, cause pass-through, or interfere with sewerage facilities. Requires self-monitoring, Toxic Organic Management Plan (TOMP), Slug Control Plan. |
| **Class II Wastewater Discharge Permit** | §303 | Users whose charge for use exceeds the special assessment "OCSD Sewer User Fee" on the County property tax bill (exclusive of debt service), discharging non-sanitary waste, not otherwise requiring Class I. |
| **Dry Weather Urban Runoff Discharge Permit** | §304 | For dry-weather urban runoff discharges. |
| **Special Purpose Discharge Permit** | §305 | Dewatering, groundwater remediation, or other discharges when no viable alternative exists. |
| **Wastehauler Discharge Permit** | §306 | For liquid waste pumpers (septage, grease trap waste). |
| **Discharge Certifications** | §307 | Zero Discharge Certification; other certifications for regulated wastewater that qualifies without full permit. |

## Maximum Allowable Local Discharge Limits (OCSD-53, §216, Table 1)

These are OC San's local limits applicable to **all non-domestic dischargers**. Federal Categorical Pretreatment Standards may impose more stringent limits for specific industry categories.

| Constituent | Limit (mg/L) |
|---|---|
| 1,4-Dioxane (p-dioxane) | 1.0 |
| Arsenic | 2.0 |
| Cadmium | 1.0 |
| Chromium (Total) | 3.0 |
| Copper | 5.0 |
| Cyanide (Total) | 2.0 |
| Lead | 0.03 |
| Mercury | 2.3 (note: see footnote) |
| Molybdenum | 10.0 |
| Nickel | 0.01 |
| Pesticides | 100.0 |
| Oil and Grease (Mineral/Petroleum Origin, SGT-HEM Non-Polar) | 0.01 |
| Polychlorinated Biphenyls (PCB) | 3.9 |
| Selenium | 15.0 |
| Silver | 0.5 |
| Sulfide (Dissolved) | 5.0 |
| Sulfide (Total) | 10.0 |
| Zinc | — (mass-based) |
| Ammonia | Mass-based (track and report) |
| Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD) | Mass-based (track and report) |

> Notes from Table 1:
> - Users subject to Federal Categorical Pretreatment Standards may need more stringent limits.
> - 1,4-dioxane is also known as "p-dioxane."
> - BOD and ammonia: mass discharged tracked; permittee must report intended technically-based mass use.
> - "Oil and Grease of Mineral or Petroleum Origin" = "Petroleum Oil and Grease as Silica Gel Treated n-Hexane Extractable Material" (SGT-HEM Non-Polar Material).

## Application Process

1. Submit **initial application** form to OC San Resource Protection Division (forms available at OC San document center).
2. For Class I: also submit Responsible Officer/Designated Signatory forms, Slug Control Plan, and Toxic Organic Management Plan (TOMP).
3. **Contact:** permitcounter@ocsan.gov | (714) 593-7164
4. **Staff facility assignments:** https://ocsan.gov/documentcenter/20260205_facilityassignments
5. **Permits overview page:** https://ocsan.gov/ocsan-permits/
6. **Industrial discharge page:** https://ocsan.gov/industrialdischarge/

## Additional Prohibitions (OCSD-53, Article 2)

Key prohibitions beyond numeric limits:
- **§201:** General prohibited discharges (fire/explosion hazard, corrosive, obstructive, toxic, etc.)
- **§202:** Prohibition on dilution to achieve compliance
- **§204:** Prohibition on non-domestic surface and floor drains discharging without permit
- **§206:** Slug discharge notification requirement
- **§209:** Hazardous waste discharge notification requirement
- **§211:** Prohibition on medical waste
- **§212:** Prohibition on disposal of spent solutions and sludges without permit
