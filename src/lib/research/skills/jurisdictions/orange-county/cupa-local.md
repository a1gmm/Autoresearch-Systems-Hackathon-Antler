## CUPA Agency

**Agency:** Orange County Health Care Agency (OC HCA), Environmental Health Division
**Designation:** Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA), designated by CalEPA (CA Secretary for Environmental Protection) on **January 1, 1997**.
**Type:** Own CUPA (countywide). The City of Anaheim operates its own standalone CUPA — do NOT route Anaheim facilities to OC HCA.
**Address:** 1241 E. Dyer Road, Suite 120, Santa Ana, CA 92705
**Phone:** (714) 433-6000
**Hours:** 8 a.m.–5 p.m. (reception) | 8 a.m.–4 p.m. (cashier/payment)

## Geographic Scope

Countywide CUPA for all **unincorporated** areas and all **incorporated cities except the City of Anaheim**.

**Participating Agencies** (run HMBP inspections within their city limits under OC HCA CUPA umbrella):
- Huntington Beach Fire Department
- City of Orange Fire Department
- Fountain Valley Fire Department
- Los Angeles County Fire Department (for the City of La Habra — CalARP tier only)

> NOTE: In La Habra, Fullerton, Huntington Beach, the City of Orange, and Fountain Valley, the local fire agencies handle HMBP program inspections. OC HCA still administers non-HMBP CUPA programs in those cities.

## Local Ordinance / Municipal Code

OC HCA administers CUPA programs primarily under authority of California Health and Safety Code Division 20, Chapter 6.95 (hazardous materials disclosure), and related state statutes (HSC §25185, §25508, §25280 for UST). The specific local codified ordinance chapter in the **Orange County Codified Ordinances** for the CUPA enabling authority was not publicly surfaced on official agency pages — cross-check OC Codified Ordinances Title 4 (Health, Sanitation & Environment). See: https://library.municode.com/ca/orange_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT4HESAANRE

## HMBP Reporting Thresholds (State Minimums, California HSC §25507)

| Form | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Liquids | ≥ 55 gallons |
| Solids | ≥ 500 pounds |
| Flammable gas | ≥ 200 cubic feet |
| Non-flammable compressed gas | ≥ 1,000 cubic feet |
| Extremely hazardous substances | At or above Threshold Planning Quantity (TPQ) |

## Reporting Portal

**CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System): https://cersbusiness3.calepa.ca.gov
OC HCA is the receiving agency. Businesses submit HMBP, HW, UST, and other unified program data through CERS.

## Fees

OC HCA charges annual program fees. The fee schedule (Hazardous Materials Management Program Fee Schedule) is adopted by Board resolution. FY 2024–25 total estimated EH Division fee-based program cost: ~$32.4 million; FY 2025–26: ~$35.5 million (Board resolution June 4, 2024, Budget Control 042). Fee payment available online via the OC HCA "Pay Online" portal at ochealthinfo.com. Specific per-program fee amounts were not published in a machine-readable format on agency pages as of the fetch date — contact OC HCA cashier at (714) 433-6000 or visit the fee schedule link on the HCA hazardous materials page.

## UST-Specific Note

- Administers UST program in most OC cities **except the City of Anaheim and City of Orange** (which have their own programs).
- **Critical deadline (passed):** Single-walled USTs were required to be permanently closed by December 31, 2025 per state regulation.
