## AHJ

**Orange County Fire Authority (OCFA), Division 6** is the sole fire code Authority Having
Jurisdiction (AHJ) for Santa Ana. The City has no independent fire department. OCFA Division 6
is headquartered at 1439 S Broadway, Santa Ana, CA 92707 | (714) 567-3236.

## Adopted Code Edition

**2022 California Fire Code (CFC), Title 24, Part 9**, with OCFA local amendments. The 2022 CFC
was adopted county-wide under **Orange County Ordinance No. 22-008**, adopted December 6,
2022. Santa Ana has no separate adopting ordinance — the OCFA serves as AHJ under a Joint
Powers Agreement, and the County ordinance applies within city limits.

Santa Ana Municipal Code **Chapter 14** (Fire Prevention, Premises, and Emergency Services)
provides the local framework: https://library.municode.com/ca/santa_ana/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIITHCO_CH14FIPRPREMSE

## OCFA Local Amendments to the 2022 CFC

Source document: OC Board Agenda 12/06/2022, Attachment K — "2022 CFC OCFA Amendment Summary"

Key OCFA-specific amendments carried forward into the 2022 adoption:

### Chapter 1 — Scope & Administration
- **§ 112.4** Violation penalties: Fines now based on OCFA adopted fee schedule.
- **§ 112.4.2** Infraction & Misdemeanor: Combined definitions under one section.

### Chapter 3 — General Requirements
- **§ 304.1.2** Vegetation maintenance aligned to OCFA Guideline C-05.
- **§ 305.6** Prohibits outdoor fires during hazardous weather conditions.
- **§ 307.6** Outdoor fireplaces/fire pits/fire rings at Group R Occupancies regulated.
- **§ 307.6.2** Gas fire pits: 3 ft setback from R-3 / 10 ft from other R occupancies.
- **§ 307.6.2.1** Solid fuel prohibited in fuel modification zones.
- **§ 325** Fuel modification requirements for new construction adjacent to combustible vegetation.
- **§ 327.1** Spark arrester equipment within wildfire-prone areas: 30 ft setback (stricter than state 10 ft).

### Chapter 5 — Fire Service Features
- **§ 501.1** References OCFA Guideline B-01 (replaces B-09a) for access/water supply.
- **§ 510.1** Emergency responder radio coverage requirements per local AHJ ordinance.

### Chapter 9 — Fire Protection Systems
- **§ 903.2** Sprinklers required in new buildings based on size; retrofit required on additions.
- **§ 903.2.8** Group R sprinkler retrofit on remodel/addition based on area criteria.
- **§ 903.3.5.3** Hydraulic calculations: 10 % safety margin required.

### Chapter 28 — Lumber Yards / Combustible Storage
- **§ 2808.2** Permit required for storage/composting of green waste (Miscellaneous Combustible Storage permit).
- **§ 2808.3** Reduced pile sizes for combustible materials.
- **§ 2808.4** 20 ft pile separation (not fire lane); 100 ft from combustible vegetation.
- **§ 2808.7** Oscillating sprinklers required to maintain moisture.

### Chapter 49 — Wildland-Urban Interface
- **§ 4903.3 / 4906.3** Fuel Modification Plans per OCFA Guideline C-05 (revised language).

### Chapter 50 — Hazardous Materials
- **§ 5001.5.2** "OCFA's Chemical Classification Packet" replaces HMIS form (consistent with Ch. 50 language).
- **§ 5003.1.1.1** Extremely hazardous substances (EHS) above disclosable amounts prohibited in residential areas.

### Chapter 56 — Explosives & Fireworks
- **§ 5608.2** Fireworks shows must be electrically fired.
- **§ 5608.3** 100 ft/inch fallout zone (stricter than standard 70 ft/inch).

### Chapter 80 — Referenced Standards (NFPA 13, 14, 24)
- **NFPA 13 § 16.12.3.3** FDC type, location, and color specified.
- **NFPA 13 § 9.2.1.7** Concealed space insulation per operational requirements.
- **NFPA 14 § 7.3.1.1** Hose connection height specified.
- **NFPA 24 § 6.2.8.1** Indicating valves painted red (exception for exterior risers).
- **NFPA 24 § 6.2.9(5)** Street valves: exception for roadway valve boxes removed.

## Permits Requiring OCFA Approval

OCFA issues permits (with inspection) for the following at Santa Ana sites:
- Fire sprinkler systems (new installation or modification)
- Fire alarm and detection systems
- Fire pumps
- Kitchen hood suppression systems
- Hazardous material storage (Chapter 50/HMBP coordination with OC HCA CUPA)
- High-piled combustible storage (OCFA Guideline G-01)
- Spray booths and aerosol product storage
- Fireworks displays
- Above-ground fuel storage (Class I/II liquids)

## High-Piled Storage (HPS)
A CFC permit is required for any high-piled storage (commodities > 12 ft high or group requiring
HPS permit per CFC Chapter 32). OCFA Guideline G-01 (January 2023, updated June 2025)
governs HPS; sprinkler systems and all control valves must be monitored by a UL-listed central
alarm station per 2022 CFC Chapter 9.
