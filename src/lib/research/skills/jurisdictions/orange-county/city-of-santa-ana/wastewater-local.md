## POTW / Sanitation Agency

**Orange County Sanitation District (OC San)** is the Publicly Owned Treatment Works (POTW)
serving Santa Ana and central/northwest Orange County (~2.6 million people).

**Address:** 18480 Bandilier Circle, Fountain Valley, CA 92708
**Phone:** (714) 962-2411
**Website:** https://ocsan.gov
**Industrial Discharge / Source Control:** https://ocsan.gov/industrialdischarge/
**Permit inquiries:** permitcounter@ocsan.gov

Santa Ana also has a **local sewer connection** layer: City of Santa Ana Public Works, Water &
Sewer Division governs connections to the local collection system under **Municipal Code Chapter
39** (Water and Sewer). A City sewer permit is required before any new connection.
- City sewer/water page: https://www.santa-ana.org/pw/water-and-sewer/

## Permit Types (OCSD-53, Article 3)

### Class I Wastewater Discharge Permit (§ 302)

Required for any industrial user that is:
1. Subject to Federal Categorical Pretreatment Standards (40 CFR 403.6 / Subchapter N); or
2. Discharges **≥ 25,000 gallons/day** of process wastewater to the POTW (excluding sanitary,
   cooling, and boiler blowdown); or
3. Contributes a process wastestream comprising **≥ 5 %** of the average dry-weather hydraulic
   or organic capacity of OC San's POTW; or
4. Designated by OC San as having a reasonable potential to adversely affect POTW operations
   or violate a Pretreatment Standard or Local Limit (40 CFR 403.8(f)(6)); or
5. May cause Pass Through affecting OC San's NPDES Permit compliance.

**Permit term:** Annual renewal. Fee set by Board Ordinance, payable at time of application.

### Class II Wastewater Discharge Permit (§ 303)

Required for any user that:
1. Has a charge-for-use greater than the "OCSD Sewer User Fee" special assessment on the
   County property tax bill (exclusive of debt service); AND
2. Discharges wastewater other than sanitary; AND
3. Is not otherwise required to obtain a Class I permit.

**Permit term:** Annual renewal. Fee set by Board Ordinance.

### Other Permit Types (§§ 304–307)
- **Dry Weather Urban Runoff Discharge Permit** (§ 304)
- **Special Purpose Discharge Permit** (§ 305) — for discharges with no alternative disposal
  point (e.g., groundwater cleanup, construction dewatering)
- **Wastehauler Discharge Permit** (§ 306)
- **Zero Discharge Certification** / **Discharge Certification** (§ 307) — for regulated
  wastewater that does not require a numeric permit

## Permit Application Process

1. Complete and file OC San application form (available at https://ocsan.gov/document-center/).
2. Submit: facility name/address/APN/NAICS; principal names; Wastewater volumes and
   constituents; water supplier and account numbers; production records if applicable.
3. Pay applicable permit fee (fee amount set by separate Board Ordinance — contact
   Adrian Siew at asiew@ocsan.gov or (714) 593-7164 for current fee).
4. OC San reviews application, issues permit with numeric discharge limits and monitoring
   conditions.

**Applications portal:** https://ocsan.gov/ocsan-permits/

## FOG / Local Sewer (City of Santa Ana)

Fats, Oils & Grease (FOG) Wastewater Discharge Permits are administered by OC San for
commercial cooking/food-service establishments. The City of Santa Ana Municipal Code Chapter
39 also imposes BMP requirements for FOG dischargers connecting to the local collection system.
