## CUPA Identity

Santa Ana has **no city CUPA**. Orange County Health Care Agency (HCA), Environmental Health
Division was designated CUPA for all of unincorporated and incorporated Orange County by
CalEPA on **January 1, 1997**. Santa Ana facilities file and pay fees directly to OC HCA.

**Agency:** Orange County Health Care Agency — Environmental Health Division
**Program:** Hazardous Materials Management Program (HMMP)
**Address:** 1241 E. Dyer Road, Suite 120, Santa Ana, CA 92705
**Phone:** (714) 433-6000 | **Email:** ehealth@ochca.com
**Hours:** Reception 8 a.m.–5 p.m. | Cashier 8 a.m.–4 p.m.

## Programs Administered

OC HCA administers all six CUPA programs under Health & Safety Code § 25404 et seq.:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — required for any facility storing ≥ state
   threshold quantities of hazardous materials.
2. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — permitting, inspection, and closure per HSC § 25280+.
3. **Hazardous Waste (Tiered Permitting / Onsite Treatment)** — Permit by Rule (PBR),
   Conditionally Authorized (CA), Conditionally Exempt (CE), and Full Permit tiers.
4. **CalARP (California Accidental Release Prevention)** — Program 1, 2, and 3 facilities.
5. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — SPCC Plan oversight for tanks > 1,320 gal
   aggregate or any single tank > 660 gal of petroleum.
6. **Medical Waste** — acute-care, SQG, large quantity generator programs.

Additional programs: Universal Waste, Used Oil Recycling, Waste Tire Management, Body Art.

## Ordinance / Legal Authority

OC HCA CUPA authority derives from CalEPA designation under **Health & Safety Code § 25404**.
There is no standalone Orange County CUPA ordinance number published on the HCA website;
the CalEPA designation document is the governing authority. Fee schedules are adopted annually
by OC Board of Supervisors resolution.
