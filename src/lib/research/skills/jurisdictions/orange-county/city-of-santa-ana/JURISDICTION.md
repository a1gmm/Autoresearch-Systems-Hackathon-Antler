> Local EHS authority reference for **City of Santa Ana**. GOTCHA: despite being the Orange County seat, Santa Ana does **not** run its own CUPA and no longer has its own fire department — both functions are delegated to county-level agencies (OC HCA-CUPA + OCFA). Use this file to route to the correct local permitting desk before researching statewide rules.

## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA (Hazardous Materials Unified Program)

- **CUPA authority:** Orange County Health Care Agency (HCA), Environmental Health Division. Santa Ana has **no city CUPA** — OC HCA was designated CUPA for all of Orange County by CalEPA on January 1, 1997.
- **Programs administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Hazardous Waste (tiered permitting / onsite treatment), CalARP, Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), Medical Waste, Universal Waste, Used Oil, Waste Tire.
- **HMBP/CERS submissions:** California Environmental Reporting System — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/
- **Office address:** 1241 E. Dyer Road, Suite 120, Santa Ana, CA 92705
- **Phone:** (714) 433-6000 | Hours: Reception 8 a.m.–5 p.m., Cashier 8 a.m.–4 p.m.

### 3. Industrial Wastewater / Pretreatment

- **POTW/Sanitation agency:** Orange County Sanitation District (**OC San**) serves central and northwest Orange County including Santa Ana. POTW phone: (714) 962-2411 | Address: 18480 Bandilier Circle, Fountain Valley, CA 92708 | Website: https://ocsan.gov
- **Governing ordinance:** OC San Wastewater Discharge Regulations **Ordinance OCSD-53** (most recently cited in semi-annual pretreatment reports; [VERIFY current ordinance number — check https://records.ocsan.gov for the active version]).
- **Permit types:**
  - Class I Wastewater Discharge Permit (significant industrial users / categorical dischargers)
  - Class II Wastewater Discharge Permit (non-significant industrial users)
  - Zero Discharge Certification
  - Fats, Oils & Grease (FOG) Wastewater Discharge Permit
- **Application portal / start here:** https://ocsan.gov/ocsan-permits/
- **Industrial discharge permit page:** https://ocsan.gov/industrialdischarge/
- **Local city sewer connection:** City of Santa Ana Public Works, Water & Sewer Division — Chapter 39 (Water and Sewer) of the Santa Ana Municipal Code governs local sewer connections and FOG BMPs. A permit is required before any sewer connection can be made.

### 4. Stormwater (MS4)

- **MS4 permit issuer:** Santa Ana Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 8) — issues NPDES Municipal Separate Storm Sewer System (MS4) permit to the City of Santa Ana.
- **Local ordinance:** Santa Ana Municipal Code **Chapter 18** (Water Quality Ordinance) governs stormwater quality and illicit discharge. The storm drain system is separate from the sewer; stormwater flows untreated to receiving waters.
- **City stormwater program contact:** (714) 647-3380 (stormwater issues); (714) 647-5074 (general inquiries)
- **Spills/emergencies:** OC-wide spill hotline 1-877-89-SPILL (1-877-897-7455); hazmat → 911
- **Mobile reporting:** mySantaAna app
- **Local Implementation Plan (2024 update):** https://www.santa-ana.org/documents/local-implementation-plan-2024-update
- **Santa Ana Regional RWQCB stormwater program:** https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/santaana/water_issues/programs/stormwater/

### 5. Zoning / Land Use (Industrial Districts)

- **Zoning code location:** Santa Ana Municipal Code, **Chapter 41** (Zoning) — https://library.municode.com/ca/santa_ana/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=SAANMUCO
- **Industrial district designations:**
  - **M1 — Light Industrial** (Division within Chapter 41)
  - **M2 — Heavy Industrial** (Chapter 41, Article III, Division 19 — §§41-493 et seq.)
- **2025 zoning update:** On June 17, 2025, the City Council approved the Transit Zoning Code (SD-84) permanent regulations, which reclassified certain M1/M2 parcels within the SD-84 boundary to an "Urban Neighborhood" zone and introduced an **Industrial Overlay Zone (I-OZ)** for areas zoned M1 or M2 that were occupied by industrial uses at adoption. Confirm current zoning designation before assuming M1/M2 applies.
- **CUP process:** Conditional Use Permits administered by the Planning Division. Contact: Santa Ana City Hall, 20 Civic Center Plaza, Santa Ana, CA 92701 | (714) 647-5400
- **Public records / planning documents portal:** https://publicdocs.santa-ana.org/WebLink/Welcome.aspx
