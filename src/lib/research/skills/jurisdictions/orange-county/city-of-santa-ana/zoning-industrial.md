## Industrial Zone Districts

### M1 — Light Industrial (Chapter 41, Article III, Division 18, §§ 41-471 et seq.)

Handout (updated 04/2026):
https://storage.googleapis.com/proudcity/santaanaca/2022/03/83e26939-light-industrial-m1-handout-7.28.2023.pdf

**Purpose:** Light manufacturing, assembly, warehousing, and related industrial uses.

#### Permitted by Right (§ 41-472)

- Compounding, processing, or treatment of raw/previously treated materials into a finished
  or semi-finished product (excludes uses listed in § 41-489.5 / M2-only CUP list)
- Manufacture of products from raw or previously treated materials (same exclusion)
- Assembly of products from raw or previously treated materials (same exclusion)
- Packaging or distribution of previously prepared products or materials (same exclusion)
- Wholesale establishments (primary trade is business-to-business)
- Storage of previously prepared goods/products/materials (owner/operator's property)
- Machine shop or other metal working shops
- Warehousing
- Impound yards (storage only; no office or dispatching)
- Laundry and dry cleaning establishments with on-premises plant operation
- Eating establishments (not operating 12 a.m.–5 a.m. within 150 ft of residential)
- Research laboratories that do not generate hazardous waste
- Service stations (≤ 2,000 sq ft non-automotive retail)
- Truck, boat, and heavy equipment sales, rental, and service
- Movie, photography, musical, or video production studios
- Bulk product sales (≥ 25 cubic ft) where bulk is primary activity
- Public utility structures
- Blueprinting, photoengraving, screen printing, and reproduction processes
- Wholesale nursery and plant storage
- Contractor's yard
- Automotive repair and service, including body and fender repair, painting, engine replacement
- Home improvement warehouse store
- Sales of industrial products, supplies, and equipment for final product manufacture
- Lumberyard, including mill and sash work (entirely enclosed building)
- Recycling facilities ≤ 45,000 gross sq ft (§ 41-1253 compliance required)
- Adult entertainment businesses (§ Article XVII compliance)
- **Storage and distribution of hazardous materials** (permitted by right; OCFA/CUPA permits still required)
- Passenger transportation services
- Retail uses on properties fronting an arterial street (per City Circulation Element)

#### Ancillary Uses Permitted (§ 41-472.1)
- Administrative office up to **30 %** of gross floor area
- Product sales/service up to **5 %** of gross floor area
- Daycare centers up to **30 %** of gross floor area (no combination > 30 %)
- Enclosed storage of owner/operator goods up to **30 %** of gross floor area
- Outside storage of Class I and II liquids (per fire code) in above-ground fixed tanks > 60 gal,
  screened per § 41-622

#### Conditional Use Permit Required (§ 41-472.5)
- Principal industrial uses where industrial floor area is < 70 % (remainder is office only,
  unless retail on arterial per § 41-472(cc))
- Storage of new or used buildings/houses
- Daycare centers other than as covered by § 41-472.1
- Trade schools requiring heavy equipment/machinery operation
- Kennels for temporary care of domesticated animals
- Eating establishments operating 12 a.m.–5 a.m. within 150 ft of residential use
- Eating establishments with drive-through window service
- Petroleum and gas storage
- Freight, bus, and truck terminal
- Industrial medical clinics (referral-only; no overnight stays)
- Administrative office > 30 % of gross floor area (ancillary to industrial)
- Light processing facilities and heavy processing facilities
- Car wash facility
- Banquet facilities ancillary to a restaurant (§ 41-199.1 standards apply)
- Commercial storage (not within 500 ft of arterial/freeway or 1,000 ft of freeway interchange)
- Mini-warehouse uses (same proximity restriction)
- Recreational vehicle/vehicle/boat storage yard (same proximity restriction)
- Data center uses (same proximity restriction)
- Indoor sport facility (§ 41-482 standards apply)

#### Development Standards (§§ 41-473 – 41-479)
- Max building height: **35 ft** (§ 41-474)
- All primary operations (compounding, processing, packaging, vehicle maintenance) must be
  conducted within a **completely enclosed building** (§ 41-473(e))
- Outdoor storage screened by solid fence/wall ≥ 8 ft; materials may not exceed fence height
  (§ 41-473(b))
- Solid wall ≥ 8 ft required along rear/side lots abutting residential use (§ 41-473(c))
- Loading areas not visible from arterial streets; setback ≥ 35 ft from property line if not facing
  a street (§ 41-473(g))

### M2 — Heavy Industrial (Chapter 41, Article III, Division 19, §§ 41-489 et seq.)

Handout (updated 04/2026):
https://storage.googleapis.com/proudcity/santaanaca/2022/03/01a1d59c-m2-heavy-industrial.pdf

**Scope:** M2 is subject to all M1 regulations (Division 18) plus the additional uses below
(§ 41-489).

#### Additional Uses Requiring a CUP in M2 Only (§ 41-489.5)
The following may NOT be located in M1; they require a CUP in M2:
- Acid manufacturing
- Junkyards, automobile wrecking yards and salvage yards, and impound yards (with office/
  dispatch)
- Cement, lime, gypsum, or plaster of Paris manufacture
- Fertilizer manufacture
- Gas or acetylene manufacture
- Glue manufacture
- Smelting of tin, copper, zinc, or iron ores
- Sites for transfer, disposal, or dumping of rubbish, garbage, trash, or refuse
- Facilities where payment is offered for blood or plasma donations
- Crematories
- **Hazardous waste treatment facilities** (CUP required in M2)
- Recycling facilities **> 45,000 gross sq ft** (§ 41-1253 compliance required)

## Industrial Overlay Zone (I-OZ)

On **June 17, 2025**, the City Council adopted permanent regulations for the Transit Zoning Code
(SD-84) update, which:
- Reclassified certain M1/M2 parcels within the SD-84 boundary to "Urban Neighborhood" zone.
- Created an **Industrial Overlay Zone (I-OZ)** for M1/M2 parcels occupied by industrial uses
  at adoption, preserving industrial entitlements in those areas.
- Confirm current zoning designation on any parcel before assuming M1/M2 applies.

Prior urgency ordinance: **NS-3063** (April 2024), 45-day moratorium on industrial use approvals
in the TZC area (extended through April 15, 2025).

Transit Zoning Code update page: https://www.santa-ana.org/transit-zoning-code-update
