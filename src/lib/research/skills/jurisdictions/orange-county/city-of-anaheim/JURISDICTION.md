## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **Own standalone CUPA** — Anaheim is the only city in Orange County that is its own CUPA (all other OC cities fall under OC Health Care Agency).
- **Agency:** Anaheim Fire & Rescue, Hazardous Materials Section (HMS)
- **CalEPA certified:** July 1, 2001
- **Six program elements consolidated under Anaheim HMS:**
  1. Hazardous Waste Generator (HW)
  2. Onsite Hazardous Waste Treatment (TP)
  3. Hazardous Materials Release Response Plans & Inventory (HMBP)
  4. California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)
  5. Underground Storage Tanks (UST)
  6. Uniform Fire Code Hazardous Materials Plans & Inventory
  - Aboveground Storage Tanks (AST) added separately
- **Local ordinance:** Anaheim Municipal Code (AMC) Title 6, Chapter 6.11 (California Unified Hazardous Waste and Hazardous Material Management Regulatory Program)
- **Permits expire:** July 31 annually; invoiced July 1
- **Late fees:** $100 assessed at 30, 60, and 90 days past expiration (Resolution No. 2025-058)
- **Contact:** 201 S. Anaheim Blvd., Suite 300, Anaheim, CA 92805 | (714) 765-4040
- **Portal/applications:** Download from anaheim.net/706; no online submission portal found — contact HMS directly

### 2. Fire Code — AHJ & Local Amendments

- **AHJ:** Anaheim Fire & Rescue (also the CUPA — single point of contact for both fire and hazmat)
- **Adopted code:** 2025 California Fire Code (CFC), AMC Title 16, Chapter 16.08
  - Section 16.08.010: Adoption of the CFC
  - Section 16.08.020: Local amendments to the 2025 CFC
  - Prior adoption: 2022 CFC via Ordinance No. 6553 (adopted March 7, 2023); current code reflects 2025 CFC amendments
- **Life Safety Section** issues one-time fire permits: pyrotechnic displays, tents/canopies/temporary membrane structures, trade shows, carnivals, fairs, outdoor assemblies
- **Hazmat & high-pile storage permits:** Issued through the Hazardous Materials Section (not Life Safety) given Anaheim's CUPA status; consult HMS at (714) 765-4040
- **Fee schedule:** https://www.anaheim.net/DocumentCenter/View/1268/Life-Safety-Fee-Schedule
- **Contact:** Anaheim Fire & Rescue, 201 S. Anaheim Blvd., Anaheim, CA 92805 | (714) 765-4000

### 3. Industrial Wastewater — Sewer Use & Pretreatment

- **POTW:** Orange County Sanitation District (OC San) — Anaheim sewage flows to OC San Plant No. 1, Fountain Valley
- **Local sewer ordinance:** AMC Title 10, Chapter 10.08 (Domestic and Industrial Waste)
  - § 10.08.090: Industrial Waste Permit — no person may discharge industrial waste to the public sewer without a permit from the District (established by Ord. 2585; amended by Ord. 5040)
  - § 10.08.040: Substances banned from public sewers (flammable liquids, toxic materials, pH limits, FOG >600 mg/L, etc.)
  - § 10.08.100: FOG requirements for food service establishments
- **Pretreatment program:** Governed by OC San's Resource Protection Division (federal 40 CFR Part 403 pretreatment); OC San issues Class I and Class II wastewater discharge permits directly to significant industrial users in Anaheim
- **IWP/permit portal:** OC San — https://ocsan.gov/regional-sewer-service/
- **City public works contact for sewer connections:** (714) 765-4311

### 4. Stormwater — MS4 & Local Ordinance

- **MS4 co-permittee:** Anaheim is a co-permittee under the North Orange County Phase I NPDES MS4 Permit, issued by the Santa Ana Regional Water Quality Control Board (SARWQCB)
- **Principal permittee:** County of Orange / OC Flood Control District
- **Prior permit reference:** NPDES Permit Order No. R8-2002-0010 (Santa Ana Region); current permit updated — verify current order number with SARWQCB
- **Local stormwater ordinance:** AMC — stream dumping regulations (cross-referenced at city code library); SB 205 business license compliance form required for applicable businesses
- **SB 205 form:** https://www.anaheim.net/DocumentCenter/View/29421/SB-205-Stormwater-Discharge-Compliance-Form
- **OC Watersheds program (North OC):** https://ocerws.ocpublicworks.com/service-areas/oc-environmental-resources/regional-stormwater-program/north-oc-stormwater
- **Contact:** City of Anaheim Public Works / Stormwater, (714) 765-4311; or dial 311

### 5. Zoning & Land Use — Industrial Districts

- **Zoning code:** AMC Title 18 (Zoning)
- **Industrial zone:** Single "I" Industrial Zone (Chapter 18.10) — Anaheim does not use traditional M-1/M-2 nomenclature; the code uses a single Industrial Zone designation
- **Primary uses table:** AMC § 18.10.030, Table 10-A — lists permitted-by-right vs. conditional use permit (CUP) required uses for the Industrial Zone
- **Key industrial areas:** Anaheim Canyon (heavy industrial/warehouse); areas near Stadium/Platinum Triangle carry mixed/specific plan overlays
- **CUP process:** Applications through Planning & Building Department; submittal requirements and portal at anaheim.net
- **Planning & Building Dept.:** 200 S. Anaheim Blvd., Anaheim, CA 92805 | (714) 765-5139
- **Zoning information handout (commercial zones reference):** https://www.anaheim.net/DocumentCenter/View/1672/Zoning-Information-Handout---Commercial-Zones

### CUPA Gotcha (re-stated for agent)

Anaheim HMS is the sole CUPA in Orange County that is a city-run program. For any Anaheim facility, do NOT route CUPA inquiries to OC Health Care Agency — go directly to Anaheim Fire & Rescue HMS. Anaheim + Huntington Beach also run OCCHMERA (OC-City Hazardous Materials Emergency Response Authority), a JPA for emergency response.
