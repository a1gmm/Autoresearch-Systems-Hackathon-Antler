## Agency

**Anaheim Fire & Rescue — Hazardous Materials Section (HMS)**  
201 S. Anaheim Blvd., Suite 300, Anaheim, CA 92805  
Phone: (714) 765-4040  

**Own-CUPA status:** Yes — Anaheim HMS is the only city-operated CUPA in Orange County.
All other OC cities fall under the OC Health Care Agency CUPA. CalEPA certified: **July 1, 2001**.

Do NOT route Anaheim facilities to OC Health Care Agency. Go directly to Anaheim HMS.

---

## Local Ordinance

**AMC Title 6, Chapter 6.11** — California Unified Hazardous Waste and Hazardous Material
Management Regulatory Program

Key sections:
- **§ 6.11.100** — Permit Required: No person may operate a facility handling hazardous
  materials or generating hazardous waste without a CUPA Consolidated Permit issued by HMS.
  Full payment of all annual fees (as established by Resolution of the City Council) is required
  before permit issuance.
- **§ 6.11.040** — Hazardous Materials Release Response Plans and Inventory (HMBP) and
  California Accidental Release Prevention Program (CalARP) requirements.
- **§ 6.11.060** — Hazardous Waste Generators and On-Site Treatment Facilities requirements.

Current fee resolution: **Resolution No. 2025-058** (rescinded prior resolution; sets FY26 fees).

## Six Unified Program Elements Administered

| Element | Program |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Waste Generator (HW) |
| 2 | Onsite Hazardous Waste Treatment (TP) |
| 3 | Hazardous Materials Release Response Plans & Inventory (HMBP) |
| 4 | California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) |
| 5 | Underground Storage Tanks (UST) |
| 6 | Uniform Fire Code Hazardous Materials Plans & Inventory |

Aboveground Storage Tanks (AST) are administered as a separate program element.

---

## Permit Cycle & Late Fees

- Permits expire: **July 31 annually**
- Invoiced: **July 1**
- Permits issued mid-July to facilities in compliance
- Late fee: **$100** assessed at 30, 60, and 90 days past due (first late fee hits August 1)

---

## Fee Schedule

Fees are set by City Council Resolution (Resolution No. 2025-058 for FY26). The specific fee
schedule amounts are not published on the public-facing website — contact HMS directly or
request the resolution from the City Clerk to obtain the current fee table.

Life Safety Division fees (separate from CUPA HMS fees) are published at:
https://www.anaheim.net/DocumentCenter/View/1268/Life-Safety-Fee-Schedule

**Note:** Group B "Special Hazard" operational permits for hazmat storage (below HMBP
thresholds) are issued by the Life Safety Section — see fire-code-local.md for tier amounts.
HMBP-level facilities use the CUPA consolidated permit through HMS exclusively.

---

## Permit Application Process

- No online submission portal found.
- Download application forms from: **https://www.anaheim.net/706/Hazardous-Materials-Section**
  (anaheim.net/706) or contact HMS directly.
- Submit completed application and fee payment to HMS; permit issued mid-July once compliance
  is verified.

---

## Additional Programs

- **SHARP (Small Hydrocarbon Acquisition and Recovery Program):** HMS administers a
  countywide program for small-quantity petroleum hydrocarbon recovery.
- **OCCHMERA:** Anaheim and Huntington Beach operate the OC-City Hazardous Materials
  Emergency Response Authority (JPA) for emergency response team deployment.
