## AHJ

**Anaheim Fire & Rescue** is the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) under AMC Chapter 16.08.
The Fire Marshal serves as Fire Code Official. Anaheim Fire & Rescue is also the CUPA — all
hazmat permits and HMBP are administered by the same department (HMS unit).

## Hazmat Permit Thresholds and High-Piled Storage Fees

Special Hazard Fire Code Permits (Group B) are tiered by quantity — issued by Life Safety
Section (Resolution No. 2025-[unnumbered]; supersedes Resolution No. 2023-052):

| Level | Flammable/Combustible Liquid | Hazardous Solids | Compressed Gas | High-Piled Storage | Aerosols (Level 2/3) | LP Gas | Fee (FY26) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | 10–100 gal | 20–100 lb | 201–3,000 cu ft | 501–2,500 sq ft | 500–999 lb | 126–500 gal | $45/permit |
| Level 2 | 101–1,000 gal | 101–1,000 lb | 3,001–6,000 cu ft | 2,501–12,000 sq ft | 1,000–2,400 lb | 501–1,000 gal | $64/permit |
| Level 3 | >1,001 gal | >1,000 lb | >6,001 cu ft | >12,001 sq ft | >2,401 lb | >1,001 gal | $81/permit |

Note: Fees are waived for businesses enrolled in the HMS Disclosure Program.  
Hazmat & high-piled storage construction permits: Hazardous Materials = $308; Combustible
Storage (low or high piled) = $381. (Source: Life Safety Fee Resolution, FY26 schedule.)

**Agent note:** Anaheim HMS (CUPA) handles hazmat operational permits; Life Safety Section
handles the above-listed Special Hazard permits. For CUPA-level HMBP/CalARP, contact HMS
directly at (714) 765-4040. Separate operational hazmat permit fees are set by Resolution No. 2025-058.
