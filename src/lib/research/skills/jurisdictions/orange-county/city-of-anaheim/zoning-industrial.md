## Zoning Code

**AMC Title 18 (Zoning)**  
**Chapter 18.10** — Industrial Zone  
§ 18.10.030 — Uses (Table 10-A Primary Uses: Industrial Zone)

Anaheim uses a **single "I" (Industrial) Zone** designation — there is no M-1/M-2/M-3 split.
Uses are controlled by Table 10-A designation letters:

- **P** = Permitted by Right
- **C** = Conditional Use Permit Required
- **M** = Minor Conditional Use Permit Required
- **N** = Prohibited

## Key Development Standards (Chapter 18.10)

**Street Setbacks:**
- Arterial highway: ≥15 ft, fully landscaped
- Collector streets: ≥15 ft, fully landscaped
- Local streets: ≥5 ft, fully landscaped
- Freeway/frontage road/on-off ramps: ≥30 ft, fully landscaped

**Interior Setbacks:**
- Non-residential property line: 0 ft
- Residential zone boundary: twice the height of the wall/structure; a 10-ft landscaped area
  adjacent to the zone boundary is required

**Maximum Building Height:** 100 ft  
- Within 200 ft of any residential zone boundary: height ≤ ½ the distance to zone boundary  
- Within 40 ft of any RS zone boundary: height ≤ 20 ft  
- Greater height may be allowed by conditional use permit

**Maximum Floor Area Ratio (FAR):** 0.50

**Parking & Loading:** Per Chapter 18.42  
**Signs:** Per Chapter 18.44  
**Landscaping:** Per Chapter 18.46  
**Fences/Walls:** Per § 18.46.110

## Key Industrial Areas in Anaheim

- **Anaheim Canyon** — primary heavy industrial and warehouse/distribution area; east Anaheim
- **Stadium / Platinum Triangle area** — carries specific plan overlays and mixed-use provisions
  (different from the base I Zone); verify specific plan applicability before assuming I Zone rules apply

## Additional Overlay Considerations for Hazmat-Intensive Uses

- High-hazard (Group H) occupancies require an operational permit from Anaheim HMS (CUPA)
  in addition to any land-use entitlement.
- Aboveground storage tanks require a fire permit from Anaheim Fire & Rescue per
  AMC § 16.08.020 (local amendment § 105.6.25).
- WUI (Wildland-Urban Interface) overlay applies east of SR-55 and south of SR-91.
