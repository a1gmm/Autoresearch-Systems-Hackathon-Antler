## Local Sewer-Use Ordinance (City of Anaheim)

**AMC Title 10, Chapter 10.08** — Domestic and Industrial Waste  
(Established by Ord. 2585; industrial waste permit amended by Ord. 5040)

Key sections:

- **§ 10.08.020** — Use of Public Sewers Required: connection to public sewer is mandatory
  where available; no direct discharge to any watercourse.
- **§ 10.08.040** — Substances Banned from the Public Sewers: prohibits discharge of gasoline,
  benzene, naphtha, fuel oil or other flammable/explosive liquids, solids or gases; toxic or
  poisonous substances in sufficient quantity to cause injury; cyanides in excess of **2 mg/L as CN**;
  materials with pH outside allowable range; FOG > 600 mg/L; and other substances that may harm
  sewers, the treatment process, or receiving waters.
- **§ 10.08.090** — Industrial Waste Permit: No person shall discharge industrial waste to the
  public sewer without a permit from the District (OC San). Permits are issued directly by OC San.
- **§ 10.08.100** — Discharges of Fats, Oils and Grease (FOG) from food service establishments:
  grease trap/interceptor installation and maintenance required.
- **§ 10.08.050** — Building Sewer Connections: construction or alteration of any industrial waste
  pretreatment system requires a permit from the City Inspector.

City public works / sewer connections contact: (714) 765-4311

## Pretreatment Program — OC San (Governing Ordinance)

**OC San Ordinance No. OCSD-53** — Wastewater Discharge Regulations  
(Repeals Ordinance No. OCSD-48)  
Full text: https://ocsan.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/OCSD53-Wastewater-Dischar.pdf

Program authority: Federal Clean Water Act (33 U.S.C. § 1251 et seq.) and 40 CFR Part 403
General Pretreatment Regulations.

### Permit Classes

**Class I Wastewater Discharge Permit** — Required for any user that:
- Is subject to Federal Categorical Pretreatment Standards (40 CFR ch. I, subch. N); OR
- Discharges ≥ 25,000 gpd of process wastewater to the POTW; OR
- Contributes a process wastestream that is ≥ 5% of the average dry-weather hydraulic or organic
  capacity of the POTW; OR
- Is designated by OC San as having reasonable potential to adversely affect the POTW.

**Class II Wastewater Discharge Permit** — Required for any user whose charge-for-use exceeds
the special assessment OCSD Sewer User Fee on the County property tax bill and who discharges
non-sanitary wastewater but does not meet Class I thresholds.

**Zero Discharge Certification** — For facilities not discharging regulated wastewater.

**Special Purpose Discharge Permit** — For temporary or non-standard discharges (e.g., tank
cleaning, construction dewatering).

Application forms (new + renewal) for all classes are available at https://ocsan.gov/industrialdischarge/

## Permit Application Process

1. Complete and file the applicable permit application form (Class I or II) with OC San
   Resource Protection Division prior to commencing discharge.
2. Application must include: facility name/address/NAICS, description of manufacturing
   process, volume of wastewater, wastewater constituents (characterized by an OC San–
   acceptable laboratory), water supplier and account numbers.
3. Pay applicable permit fee at time of application (fee amount set by OC San Board Ordinance).
4. Permit renewed annually; delinquent invoices must be cleared before renewal.
5. Class I permit conditions include mass emission rate limits, self-monitoring reports, Toxic
   Organic Management Plan (TOMP), and spill/slug control plan.
