## CUPA Agency

The City of Arcata does **not** operate its own CUPA. The countywide CUPA designation is held by:

**Humboldt County Division of Environmental Health, Hazardous Materials Program**
- Address: 100 H St., Suite 100, Eureka, CA 95501
- Phone: 707-445-6215 or 800-963-9241
- Fax: 707-441-5699
- Email: ENVHEALTH@co.humboldt.ca.us
- Hours: Monday–Friday, 8:30 a.m.–Noon and 1–5 p.m.

## Jurisdictional Relationship

- The State Secretary for Environmental Protection designated the Humboldt County Division of Environmental Health as the Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for the entire county, including all incorporated cities (Arcata, Eureka, Fortuna, etc.).
- The CUPA regulates approximately **800–1,000 facilities** countywide.
- Staffing: supervisor, one senior specialist, four field staff, one CERS technician.

## Programs Administered (All CUPA Elements)

| Program | Authority |
|---|---|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) — including HMMP and HMIS | Health & Safety Code Ch. 6.95 |
| California Accidental Release Program (CalARP) | H&S Code Ch. 6.95, Art. 2 |
| Underground Storage Tanks (UST) — construction, removal, modification, operation | H&S Code Ch. 6.7 |
| Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) | H&S Code Ch. 6.67 |
| Hazardous Waste Generation and Onsite Treatment | H&S Code Ch. 6.5 |

Community Right to Know / Business Plan program is also referenced by the county CUPA.

---

## Online Reporting Portal

- **CERS (California Environmental Reporting System):** required for all regulated businesses since January 1, 2013. All HMBP, HWG, UST, CalARP, and APSA records must be submitted through CERS: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/
- Permitting also accessible via **Accela Citizen Access** portal (link via county EH website).

## Key Contacts for Facility Registration / Inspections

For any Arcata facility subject to HMBP, UST, CalARP, or APSA requirements:
- Submit in CERS first.
- Contact Humboldt County Environmental Health at the Eureka office above.
- Inspections by county staff per CUPA inspection schedule (calendar not published online; county uses education + enforcement approach).
