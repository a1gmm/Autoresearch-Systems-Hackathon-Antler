> Local EHS authority reference for the **City of Arcata** (Humboldt County). Orientation for the research subagent: who administers the local programs and where their adopted codes live. Verify the live page before quoting; the 2025 California Building Standards Code (Title 24) replaced the 2022 edition statewide on 2026-01-01 — City of Arcata adopted 2025 CBC via Ordinance 1583, effective January 16, 2026.

## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air
- **District:** North Coast Unified Air Quality Management District (NCUAQMD) — Arcata is within NCUAQMD jurisdiction; the city has no independent air permitting authority.
- NCUAQMD issues Permits to Operate for stationary sources; Rule 1 (General Provisions) through Rule 600 series govern emissions.
- Source: https://www.ncuaqmd.org/

### CUPA (Certified Unified Program Agency)
- **Arcata does NOT operate its own CUPA.** Hazardous materials regulatory authority countywide is held by the **Humboldt County Division of Environmental Health, Hazardous Materials Program** (county-wide CUPA designation from CalEPA).
- Programs administered: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP / HMMP / HMIS), California Accidental Release Program (CalARP), Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), Hazardous Waste Generation and Onsite Treatment.
- Reporting portal: California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — required for all regulated businesses since January 1, 2013. Permitting also accessed via Accela Citizen Access portal.
- Contact: 100 H St., Suite 100, Eureka, CA 95501 | 707-445-6215 | ENVHEALTH@co.humboldt.ca.us
- Source: https://humboldtgov.org/701/Certified-Unified-Program-Agency-CUPA

### Fire
- **Fire AHJ: Arcata Fire Protection District** — an independent special district (not a city department) serving the City of Arcata plus unincorporated Bayside, Manila, McKinleyville, and Jacoby Creek.
- Adopted CFC edition: **2025 California Fire Code** (Title 24, Part 9) — District Ordinance No. 25-23 (October 2025 Board Packet). Prior adoption: Ordinance No. 22-21 (2022 CFC, adopted November 8, 2022).
- The city itself adopted the **2025 California Building Standards Code (Title 24 Part 9)** via City Ordinance 1583, effective January 16, 2026.
- Fire Marshal: Ed Laidlaw | 707-825-2000 | elaidlaw@arcatafire.org | 2149 Central Ave., McKinleyville, CA 95519.
- Fee schedule: District Ordinance 19-18 (adopted May 9, 2023); Fire Prevention Bureau hourly rate $183.19 effective January 1, 2023. Billing through Fire Recovery USA.
- Permit types: fireworks, special events/festivals, indoor events; ~1,000 occupancy inspections/year.
- Source: https://www.arcatafire.org/ordinances | https://www.arcatafire.org/fire-prevention

### Wastewater (Industrial Pretreatment)
- **POTW:** City of Arcata Wastewater Division — 2.3 MGD (dry weather average design) facility discharging treated effluent to Humboldt Bay. Infrastructure: ~65 miles of pipe, 12 lift stations, 55 acres of oxidation ponds, 154 acres of treatment marshes, Arcata Marsh and Wildlife Sanctuary.
- **Sewer Use Ordinance:** Arcata Municipal Code **Title VII, Chapter 2** (Public Works). Enacted by Ordinance 1461 (effective September 18, 2015), amended by Ordinance 1483 (2017) and Ordinance 1565 (January 19, 2024).
- Industrial user permitting: Categorical and Non-Categorical Significant Industrial Users (SNCIU) must apply for an **Industrial User Wastewater Discharge Permit**. Screening via "Industrial Process Identification & Wastewater Discharge Characteristics Survey" (contact Environmental Compliance Office, 736 F Street, Arcata; rhernandez@cityofarcata.org).
- Key code sections: illicit discharges — Title VII Ch. 2 Art. 6 § 7460; FOG limits — § 7460(C),(F),(Q); enforcement — Art. 9; extra-jurisdictional dischargers — § 7471.4.
- FOG program: dental, lab, grease interceptor, and significant industrial user sub-programs active.
- Pretreatment contact: Environmental Services Dept., 707-822-8184.
- Source: https://www.cityofarcata.org/331/Wastewater | https://www.cityofarcata.org/261/Ordinances

### Stormwater
- **Permit type:** NPDES Phase II Small MS4 — City enrolled under Statewide General Permit No. CAS000004 (Order No. 2003-0005-DWQ); current operative order: State Water Board Order No. 2013-0001-DWQ.
- **Storm Water Ordinance** in place (Ordinance 1463, July 31, 2015); separate Erosion and Sediment Control Ordinance also in force.
- Six Minimum Control Measures (MCMs) per Phase II: public education, public participation, illicit discharge detection/elimination, construction site runoff control, post-construction runoff control, pollution prevention/good housekeeping.
- Eight urban creeks traverse the city: Jacoby, Jolly Giant, Janes, Sunset, Grotzman, Fickle Hill, Beith, Campbell — most support coho, steelhead, and/or Chinook.
- Oversight: North Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 1).
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/northcoast/water_issues/programs/npdes_stormwater/ | https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/water_issues/programs/stormwater/swmp/arcata_swmp.pdf

### Zoning (Industrial)
- **Land Use Code:** Arcata Land Use Code (part of Arcata Municipal Code), administered by the Community Development Department. Current through Ordinance 1585 (February 4, 2026). Industrial districts governed by **Chapter 9.26** (Commercial, Industrial, and Public Facility Zoning Districts).
- Two industrial zoning district designations:
  - **IL (Industrial-Limited):** light and moderate impact manufacturing; limited commercial; residential allowed where compatible with production process. (§ 9.26.020.E)
  - **IG (Industrial-General):** light, moderate, and high impact manufacturing; limited commercial. (§ 9.26.020.F)
- IG minimum lot area: 1 acre; minimum lot width: 100 ft; front setback: 25 ft; side/rear: 10 ft (25 ft abutting residential); max FAR: 1.5.
- High impact manufacturing requires Use Permit (UP) in IG; moderate impact requires Minor Use Permit (MUP) in both IL and IG; low impact manufacturing is Permitted (P) in both.
- Source: https://www.cityofarcata.org/833/Zoning-Compliance-Tables | https://www.cityofarcata.org/1147/Zoning-and-Land-Use-Code

## Notes

- GOTCHA: Arcata does NOT run a municipal fire department — fire AHJ is the independent **Arcata Fire Protection District** (separate legal entity).
- Arcata does NOT run its own CUPA — county CUPA (Humboldt County Environmental Health) serves the entire county including Arcata.
- The Arcata Land Use Code uses **IL/IG** (not M-1/M-2) for industrial districts.
- Cal Poly Humboldt (formerly Humboldt State University) is entirely within the city and discharges unmetered to the city sewer system; Fieldbrook Community Services District also pipes flow to the city POTW (up to 71,200 gpd).
