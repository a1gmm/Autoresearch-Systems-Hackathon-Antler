## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

The **Arcata Fire Protection District** is the AHJ for fire and life-safety in the City of Arcata. It is an independent special district — not a city department — also serving unincorporated Bayside, Manila, McKinleyville, and Jacoby Creek areas of Humboldt County.

## Adopted CFC Edition

| Edition | District Ordinance | Adoption Date | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 California Fire Code | **Ordinance No. 22-21** | November 8, 2022 | Prior controlling edition |
| 2025 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9) | **Ordinance No. 25-23** | ~October 2025 | Current controlling edition; based on 2024 IFC with CA amendments |

The City of Arcata separately adopted the 2025 California Building Standards Code (including Part 9, CFC) via **City Ordinance 1583**, effective **January 16, 2026**. The District ordinance governs fire permit and inspection authority; the city building ordinance governs plan review for building permits.

## Plan Review and Permitting Process

- Fire Marshal is primarily responsible for plan review, new construction inspections, and fire and life-safety systems acceptance testing.
- Plan review is coordinated with the **City of Arcata Community Development Department, Building Division** and the **Humboldt County Department of Building** for projects in the district's unincorporated areas.
- Applicable standards: California Fire Code, California Building Code (Title 24), Fire District ordinances, and applicable NFPA standards.
- CCR Title 19 (State Fire Marshal regulations) also enforced during occupancy inspections.

## Inspections

- Approximately **1,000 occupancy inspections** conducted annually.
- Covers: business and multi-family residential, code enforcement for CFC, CCR Title 19, and Health & Safety Code compliance.
- Annual life-safety inspections required for certain occupancy types.
