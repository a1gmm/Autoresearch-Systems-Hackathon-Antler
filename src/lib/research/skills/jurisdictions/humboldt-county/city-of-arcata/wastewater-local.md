## POTW

**City of Arcata Wastewater Division**
- Design capacity: **2.3 million gallons per day** (dry weather average)
- Discharge point: **Humboldt Bay**
- Infrastructure: ~65 miles of sewer pipe (6–24 in. diameter), 12 sewage lift stations, 55 acres of oxidation ponds, 154 acres of treatment marshes, Arcata Marsh and Wildlife Sanctuary (240 acres used for monitoring/maintenance)
- Population served: ~19,114 (2022 census); also serves Cal Poly Humboldt (unmetered) and Fieldbrook Community Services District (up to 71,200 gpd under inter-agency agreement per § 7471.4)

## Industrial Pretreatment Program

### Who Must Obtain an Industrial User Wastewater Discharge Permit (IWP)

Any commercial or industrial user determined to be a **Categorical Industrial User** (EPA 40 CFR Part 403 / applicable categorical pretreatment standards) or a **Non-Categorical Significant Industrial User (SNCIU)** must apply for a permit.

**Categorical user categories triggering review** (partial list from City survey form):
Aluminum Forming, Battery Manufacturing, Centralized Waste Treatment, Coil Coating, Electroplating, Foundries, Iron & Steel Manufacturing, Leather Tanning, Metal Finishing, Metal Products & Machinery, Pharmaceuticals, Pulp/Paper/Fiberboard, Timber Products, Textile Mills, Transportation Equipment Cleaning, and ~40 other EPA categorical processes.

**Significant Non-Categorical User (SNCIU) categories:**
Auto Mechanic/Repair, Beer & Wine Manufacturing, Biodiesel Manufacturing, Dental Office, Embalming, Food Manufacturing, Grease Rendering, Laundry (coin-op or industrial), Laboratory (chemical or biological), Paint Contractor, Pet Grooming, Printing (screen/offset/film), Swimming Pool, Veterinarian.

### Screening Process

1. Complete **Industrial Process Identification & Wastewater Discharge Characteristics Survey** (4-page form, revised 3/2014).
2. Submit to: City of Arcata, Environmental Compliance Office, 736 F Street, Arcata, CA 95521, or email: rhernandez@cityofarcata.org
3. City determines categorical vs. SNCIU classification and whether an IWP is required.

### Discharge Limits

Specific numeric effluent limits for the Arcata POTW pretreatment program are **not published on the city's public website**. Applicable limits will be determined by:
- EPA categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR Parts 405–471) for categorical users.
- City-set local limits for SNCIUs (set to protect the POTW's NPDES permit limits to Humboldt Bay). [VERIFY — request local limits table from Environmental Compliance Office]

State Water Board and EPA mandate routine testing for: chlorine, coliform, BOD, pH, heavy metals, suspended solids, and other parameters.

### Active Sub-Programs

- **FOG (Fats, Oils & Grease):** grease interceptor requirements for food service; enforced under § 7460(C),(F),(Q).
- **Dental:** amalgam separator requirements.
- **Laboratories:** chemical/biological waste controls.
- **Significant Industrial Users:** individual IWPs with site-specific limits and reporting schedules.
