## Overview

Humboldt County is predominantly rural and septic-dependent. **The county itself does not operate a POTW (Publicly Owned Treatment Works) and has no industrial pretreatment program.** Sewer collection systems and POTWs are operated by individual cities and special districts. The only significant POTW with a formal Industrial Pretreatment Program is the **City of Eureka**.

## County Role — Onsite Wastewater (Unincorporated Areas)

**Administrator:** Humboldt County Division of Environmental Health
- Address: 100 H Street, Suite 100, Eureka, CA 95501
- Phone: 707-445-6215 / 800-963-9241
- Email: ENVHEALTH@co.humboldt.ca.us

**County Code:** Title VI Division 1 (§§ 611-1 et seq.) — Sewage and Onsite Wastewater Treatment

**Programs operated by the county:**

1. **Septage and Grease Trap Pumper Permits** — California Health and Safety Code and Humboldt County Code require registration, permitting, and annual inspection for entities engaging in septage/grease pumping in Humboldt County.

2. **Nonstandard Onsite Sewage Disposal System (OWTS) Operating Permits** — Required for advanced/nonstandard onsite systems; periodic inspections; performance tracking reported to the North Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board.

**OWTS Technical Manual:** Humboldt County OWTS Regulations and Technical Manual (filed with State Water Resources Control Board, North Coast Region).
Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/northcoast/water_issues/programs/owts/

## City of Eureka — Industrial Pretreatment Program

**POTW Operator:** City of Eureka, Utilities Operations Division, Source Control Section

**Contact:**
- Email: sourcecontrol@eurekaca.gov
- Phone: 707-441-4360
- Address: 531 K Street, Third Floor, Eureka, CA 95501
- Hours: Monday–Friday, 9 AM–4 PM

**Sewer Use Ordinance:** Chapter 50 of the Eureka Municipal Code (Ord. 908-C.S. and successor amendments).
Source: https://www.eurekaca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/388/Chapter-50-of-the-Eureka-Municipal-Code-PDF

**Permit type:** Wastewater Discharge Permit (WDP) / Industrial Wastewater Permit (IWP)

**Industries currently permitted (examples):** Industrial laundries, seafood processors, hospitals, industrial bakeries, auto repair facilities, food service establishments, photofinishers.

**IWP process:**
1. Preliminary inspection by Source Control staff.
2. Review of flow quantity, impacting characteristics, process type, and applicable federal categorical standards (40 CFR Parts 405–471).
3. Permit issued with facility-specific conditions: effluent limits, monitoring frequency, reporting requirements, and compliance schedule as appropriate.
4. Permits are tailored per user; timeframes and monitoring requirements set by impact level.

**Discharge Limits:**
[VERIFY] Specific numerical local limits (BOD, TSS, oil and grease, pH, heavy metals) are set in Eureka's local limits determination under the City's NPDES permit. Published limits are not displayed on the website — contact sourcecontrol@eurekaca.gov or obtain a copy of Chapter 50 from the city.

Source: https://www.eurekaca.gov/338/Industrial-Pretreatment

## Stormwater Context

Humboldt County Public Works Water Management Division administers a Phase II Small MS4 NPDES permit covering McKinleyville, unincorporated Eureka communities, and Shelter Cove. Separate from the wastewater/pretreatment program.

Source: https://humboldtgov.org/2486/Stormwater-Program

## Notes for Industrial Facilities

- Facilities in unincorporated Humboldt County with process wastewater must evaluate connection options:
  - If within a city or district sewer service area: contact that entity's sewer authority for connection and discharge permit requirements.
  - If outside any sewer service area: county OWTS program governs; nonstandard systems require an OWTS operating permit.
- Facilities connecting to Eureka's sewer system: contact Eureka Source Control at 707-441-4360 for IWP application.
- North Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board (NCRWQCB) has oversight of NPDES permits for any direct discharges to surface waters.
  Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/northcoast/
