## Industrial Zoning Districts (Current Chapter 155)

Eureka uses the following industrial district codes (enacted as part of the 2019+ zoning code rewrite — **not M-1/M-2**):

| Code | Name | General Plan Designation | Character |
|------|------|--------------------------|-----------|
| **HN** | Hinge Industrial | Light Industrial (LI) | Transition zone between commercial/residential and heavier industrial; protected-use rules apply if residential/office is introduced |
| **LI** | Light Industrial | Light Industrial (LI) | Standard light-industrial operations |
| **HI** | Heavy Industrial | General Industrial (GI) | Intensive uses: chemical manufacturing, petroleum refining/processing, ready-mix concrete batch plants, lumber/wood products, tires, mass-produced food/beverage, oil/gas processing |

## Legacy Districts (Pre-2019 Code)

Still referenced in older sections (§ 155.096):
- **ML — Limited Industrial:** Light industrial uses that can operate near commercial/residential with minimum mutual adverse impact; protected from heavy industrial nuisances.
- **MG — General Industrial:** Heavy industrial uses incompatible with most other land uses; operated with minimum restriction and minimum adverse effect on surrounding uses.

Source: https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/eureka/latest/eureka_ca/0-0-0-41775 (§ 155.096)

## Allowed-Uses Table

**§ 155.212.020 — Allowed Land Uses; Table 212-1**

Key permit notation in Table 212-1:
| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **P** | Permitted by right (after zoning clearance) |
| **C** | Conditional Use Permit (CUP) required |
| **M** | Minor Use Permit required |

Residential and office uses in industrial districts are allowed **only as specified in Table 212-1** (§ 155.212.030).

Special provision (§ 155.212.030): if residential/office uses (other than caretaker units) are established in the **HN** district, existing non-residential uses become a **protected use**.

## HI District Typical Uses

The Heavy Industrial (HI / General Industrial GI) district accommodates, at minimum:
- Chemical products manufacturing
- Concrete, gypsum, and plaster products
- Paving and roofing materials
- Plastics, synthetics, and rubber products
- Lumber and other wood products
- Tire manufacturing
- Mass-produced food and beverage (including alcohol)
- Petroleum refining and related industries
- Oil and gas processing facilities
- Ready-mix concrete batch plants

Source: https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/eureka/latest/eureka_ca/0-0-0-59057 (§ 155.212.010)
