## Fire Authority (AHJ)

**Humboldt Bay Fire (HBF)** — Joint Powers Authority (JPA)
- Formed 2011 by consolidation of the City of Eureka Fire Department and Humboldt No. 1 Fire Protection District; crews fully integrated 2013-01-01.
- Serves the City of Eureka and greater Eureka area (~50,000 residents), operating from 5 fire stations.
- AHJ for fire code enforcement, fire prevention inspections, and permit issuance within City of Eureka limits.
- Address: 533 C Street, Eureka, CA 95501
- Phone: 707-441-4000
- Email: Info@HBFire.org
- Web: https://www.hbfire.org/

Fire prevention and Bureau of Fire Prevention functions run through HBF's **Community Risk Reduction** division.

## Local Amendments (§ 92.02)

Local amendments include (per search results from § 92.02):
- Substitution of terminology: references to "Fire Chief" in the CFC replaced with "Fire Chief of the Humboldt Bay Fire Joint Powers Authority" (or designee).
- Modifications to chapters on General Precautions Against Fire, Fire Service Features, Interior Finish, and Fire Protection Systems.

## Fire Hazard Severity Zone

The Eureka City Council adopted a Fire Hazard Severity Zone (FHSZ) map ordinance (per CitizenPortal.ai coverage, exact ordinance number [VERIFY]). This maps local Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zones within city limits for purposes of defensible space and building material requirements under PRC § 4291 and Health & Safety Code § 13108.
