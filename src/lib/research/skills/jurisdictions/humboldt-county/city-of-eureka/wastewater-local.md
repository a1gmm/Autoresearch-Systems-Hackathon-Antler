## Permit Types and Covered Facilities

Wastewater Discharge Permits (WDPs) are required for industrial and commercial dischargers, including but not limited to:
- Industrial laundries
- Seafood processors
- Hospitals
- Industrial bakeries
- Auto repair facilities
- Food service establishments (grease/oil-generating)
- Photofinishers

Permits are facility-specific and issued for a defined period based on the facility's flow volume, wastewater characteristics, and applicable federal pretreatment standards.

## Significant Industrial Users (SIUs)

An SIU is defined as:
- Any industrial user subject to **categorical pretreatment standards** (40 CFR Parts 405–471); or
- Any other industrial user that discharges an average of **25,000 gpd or more** of process wastewater.

Categorical pretreatment standards promulgated by U.S. EPA under Clean Water Act §§ 307(b) and (c) govern SIU limits by industry category.

## Permit Application Process

1. Contact Source Control Division to initiate a preliminary inspection.
2. Division evaluates: flow volume, wastewater characteristics, federal categorical standards, and facility type.
3. Facility-specific WDP issued if discharge meets permit criteria.
4. Permits contain administrative requirements and discharge conditions that must be met at all times.
5. Compliance verified through inspections and monitoring.

## Enforcement

The Utilities Operations Division is the enforcement authority. Escalating enforcement actions:
1. Warning Notice
2. Administrative Order
3. Civil action
4. Criminal action (for discharges constituting a public health/safety risk)
