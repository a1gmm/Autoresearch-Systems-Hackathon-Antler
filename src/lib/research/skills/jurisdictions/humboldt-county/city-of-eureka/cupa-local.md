## Scope

The CUPA conducts compliance inspections of **over 800 facilities** in Humboldt County (including all facilities within City of Eureka limits) that:
- Handle hazardous materials
- Generate or treat hazardous waste
- Operate underground storage tanks (USTs)

## Programs Administered (Five Unified-Program Elements)

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP)** — including Hazardous Materials Management Plans (HMMP) and Hazardous Materials Inventory Statements (HMIS). Submitted and updated via **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System), required since 2013-01-01.
2. **California Accidental Release Prevention Program (CalARP)** — for facilities with regulated substances above threshold planning quantities.
3. **Underground Storage Tanks (USTs)** — permits for construction, modification, removal, and operation of USTs; administered through the State Water Resources Control Board program locally.
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — facilities storing petroleum above applicable thresholds.
5. **Hazardous Waste Generation and Onsite Treatment** — compliance inspections and permit oversight.

Source: https://humboldtgov.org/701/Certified-Unified-Program-Agency-CUPA

## Hazardous Materials Business Plan Thresholds

Disclosure is required when maximum quantities on-site for 30 or more days reach:
- **55 gallons** (liquids)
- **500 pounds** (solids)
- **200 cubic feet** (compressed gases)
- Any amount of **hazardous waste**
- Any **Category I or II pesticide**
- Any **extremely hazardous substance** above threshold planning quantity

Annual update required by **March 1** or within 30 days of a substantial change.

Source: https://humboldtgov.org/700/Business-Plan

## Inspection & Enforcement

- Inspectors examine hazmat storage areas, disposal records, emergency response plans, and employee training records.
- Approach: education first, then escalating enforcement.
- **Penalties:**
  - Standard violation: up to **$2,000 per day**
  - Knowing/willful violation: up to **$5,000 per day**
  - Emergency response: **full cost recovery** for county/city hazmat response and cleanup
  - Obstruction: potential **criminal misdemeanor**
