## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air

- **North Coast Unified Air Quality Management District (NCUAQMD)** is the air district for Humboldt County, including the City of Eureka. The City of Eureka has no independent air permitting authority — all Authority to Construct (ATC) and Permit to Operate (PTO) for stationary sources are issued by NCUAQMD.
- NCUAQMD also administers the Air Toxics Hot Spots program (AB 2588) and CARB indirect-source rules where applicable.
- Source: https://www.ncuaqmd.org/

### CUPA

- **Agency:** Humboldt County Division of Environmental Health, Hazardous Materials Program — the countywide Certified Unified Program Agency. The City of Eureka is **not its own CUPA**; all CUPA functions for facilities located within city limits are administered by Humboldt County.
- **Address:** 100 H St., Suite 100, Eureka, CA 95501
- **Phone:** 707-445-6215 or 800-963-9241
- **Email:** ENVHEALTH@co.humboldt.ca.us
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri 8:30 a.m.–noon, 1–5 p.m.
- **Programs administered (five unified-program elements):**
  1. Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP) — disclosure thresholds: 55 gal, 500 lb, or 200 cu ft for ≥30 days; any hazardous waste; Category I/II pesticides; EHS above TPQ. Annual update by March 1 via CERS.
  2. California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)
  3. Underground Storage Tanks (UST) — construction, modification, removal, operation permits
  4. Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)
  5. Hazardous Waste Generation and Onsite Treatment
- **Inspections:** >800 facilities countywide; inspectors check hazmat storage, disposal records, emergency response plans, and employee training. Penalties up to $2,000/day (standard) or $5,000/day (knowing/willful).
- **Online portal:** Accela Citizen Access; CERS required since 2013-01-01.
- **Fee schedule:** Humboldt County 2025 Fee Schedule (PDF) and Hazardous Materials Fee Matrix on file — see https://humboldtgov.org/264/Fee-Schedule [actual fee amounts: VERIFY from PDF]
- Source: https://humboldtgov.org/701/Certified-Unified-Program-Agency-CUPA

### Fire

- **AHJ:** Humboldt Bay Fire (HBF) — a Joint Powers Authority consolidating the former City of Eureka Fire Department and Humboldt No. 1 Fire Protection District, founded 2011, crews integrated 2013-01-01. Serves ~50,000 residents from 5 stations.
- **Address:** 533 C Street, Eureka, CA 95501 | Phone: 707-441-4000 | Email: Info@HBFire.org
- **Adopted fire code:** The City of Eureka, Muni Code Chapter 92, adopts the **2022 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9, based on 2021 International Fire Code)** with local amendments. Most recent amendment ordinance: **Ordinance 941-C.S., adopted 2022-12-06**.
- Chapter 92.01 — Adoption; Chapter 92.02 — Local Amendments; Chapter 92.03 — Enforcement by Bureau of Fire Prevention.
- The California Fire Code is on file in the Fire Marshal's office.
- Fire code enforcement and Bureau of Fire Prevention run through Humboldt Bay Fire's Community Risk Reduction division.
- Note: **2025 California Fire Code** (Title 24) took effect 2026-01-01 statewide — confirm whether Eureka has adopted the 2025 edition locally.
- Source: https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/eureka/latest/eureka_ca/0-0-0-35131 ; https://www.hbfire.org/

### Wastewater (Industrial Pretreatment)

- **POTW:** City of Eureka Elk River Wastewater Treatment Plant (Hilfiker Lane, Eureka). NPDES Permit No. **CA0024449**; current permit Order No. **R1-2023-0016**, adopted by North Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board (NCRWQCB, Region 1) on 2023-10-05. Discharge is to Pacific Ocean (ebb tide only to avoid Humboldt Bay).
- **Pretreatment program authority:** City of Eureka Utilities Operations / Source Control Division administers the Industrial Pretreatment Program under Eureka Municipal Code **Chapter 50** (Sewers).
- **Chapter 50 ordinance bill:** Bill No. 908-C.S. (see https://www.eurekaca.gov/340/Sewer-Use-Ordinance-PDF).
- Permit program: Wastewater Discharge Permits (WDPs) issued to significant industrial users (SIUs) — industrial laundries, seafood processors, hospitals, industrial bakeries, auto repair, food service, photofinishers, and others meeting flow/character thresholds. SIUs include categorical industrial users (40 CFR 405–471) and facilities discharging ≥25,000 gpd process wastewater.

### Stormwater

- **MS4 (Municipal):** The City of Eureka is a Phase II small MS4 operator, implementing State Water Board Order No. 2013-0001-DWQ. NCRWQCB (Region 1) has oversight. Regional coordination through the **North Coast Stormwater Coalition** (members: Humboldt County, Cities of Eureka, Arcata, Fortuna, Trinidad, Cal Poly Humboldt, and others).
- **Industrial stormwater:** Regulated facilities must enroll under the Statewide Industrial General Permit (IGP) — Humboldt County Environmental Health enforces IGP requirements locally. Per SB 205 (effective 2020-01-01), new/renewed business licenses require proof of NPDES permit enrollment if required.
- **Stormwater Management Plan (SWMP):** on file with NCRWQCB (Phase II NPDES Storm Water SWMP).
- Source: https://humboldtgov.org/2486/Stormwater-Program ; https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/northcoast/water_issues/programs/npdes_stormwater/

### Zoning (Industrial)

- **Zoning code:** Eureka Municipal Code **Chapter 155** (Zoning Regulations), Title XV. Inland Zoning Code PDF last updated effective 2025-09-19.
- **Current inland industrial zoning districts (Chapter 155, enacted as part of 2019+ zoning code rewrite):**
  - **HN — Hinge Industrial:** transition zone between commercial/residential and industrial; General Plan designation Light Industrial (LI). Protected-use provisions apply if residential/office introduced.
  - **LI — Light Industrial:** General Plan designation Light Industrial.
  - **HI — Heavy Industrial:** General Plan designation General Industrial (GI). Manufacturing of chemical products, petroleum refining/processing, ready-mix concrete, lumber/wood products, tire manufacturing, mass-produced food/beverage, and other intensive industrial uses.
  - Coastal variants: **LI-CZ**, **HN-CZ**, **HI-CZ**, **CDI-CZ** (Coastal Dependent Industrial).
- **Legacy districts (still referenced in older code sections):** ML (Limited Industrial) and MG (General Industrial) — see § 155.096.
- **Allowed-uses table:** § 155.212.020, Table 212-1. Key notation: P = permitted by right (after zoning clearance), C = conditional use permit required, M = minor use permit required.
- Residential/office uses in industrial districts allowed only as specified in Table 212-1 (§ 155.212.030).
- CUP and other approvals: § 155.412.
- **2026 inland zoning code update** underway — see https://www.eurekaca.gov/1063/2026-Inland-Zoning-Code-Update

## GOTCHA Notes

- Eureka does **not** have its own fire department — AHJ is **Humboldt Bay Fire** (JPA).
- Eureka does **not** have its own CUPA — **Humboldt County Environmental Health** administers all CUPA programs for city facilities.
- POTW is Elk River WWTP; NPDES permit requires discharge to Pacific Ocean on ebb tide only (Humboldt Bay discharge prohibited).
- Industrial zoning uses HN/LI/HI codes (not M-1/M-2) — confirm current district map before advising on industrial siting.
- North Coast AQMD (not BAAQMD) controls air permits here.
