## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air — North Coast Unified Air Quality Management District (NCUAQMD)

- **Controlling district:** North Coast Unified Air Quality Management District (NCUAQMD) — tri-county jurisdiction covering Humboldt, Del Norte, and Trinity counties. Headquartered at 707 L Street, Eureka, CA 95501. Phone: (707) 443-3093.
- **Permit types:** Authority to Construct (ATC) required before installing/modifying any equipment that emits pollutants; Permit to Operate (PTO) renewed annually after compliance inspection. Expedited processing available for additional fee.
- **Trigger:** Any mechanical device or container emitting pollutants; modification of existing permitted equipment; change in method of operation.
- Source: https://www.ncuaqmd.org/permit-requirements

### CUPA — Humboldt County Division of Environmental Health

- **Agency:** Humboldt County Division of Environmental Health, Hazardous Materials Program — sole CUPA for the **entire county including all incorporated cities** (Eureka, Arcata, Fortuna, Ferndale, Rio Dell, Blue Lake, Trinidad). No city runs its own CUPA.
- **Address:** 100 H St., Suite 100, Eureka, CA 95501. Phone: 707-445-6215 / 800-963-9241. Email: ENVHEALTH@co.humboldt.ca.us.
- **Programs administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP/HMMP/HMIS), California Accidental Release Program (CalARP), Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), Hazardous Waste Generation and Onsite Treatment.
- **Scope:** Inspects 800+ facilities. Violations: up to $2,000/day standard; up to $5,000/day knowing violations; emergency response full cost recovery.
- **Submissions:** All regulated businesses must submit via CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) — mandatory since 2013-01-01 per AB 2286. Online permitting via Accela Citizen Access portal.
- **Fee schedule:** FY 2025-26 Hazardous Materials and Hazardous Waste Fee Matrix (sliding scale by number of hazardous materials × hazardous waste types). Separate flat fees per Hazardous Materials Program Fees schedule effective 2026-01-19 — see cupa-local.md.
- Source: https://humboldtgov.org/701/Certified-Unified-Program-Agency-CUPA

### Fire — No unified county fire department

- **AHJ situation:** Humboldt County has no countywide fire department. Unincorporated areas are served by a patchwork of independent fire protection districts, community services districts, volunteer fire companies, Tribes, and JPAs. Areas outside any district receive goodwill/volunteer coverage only.
- **CAL FIRE:** State responsibility/wildland areas fall under CAL FIRE Humboldt-Del Norte Unit (HQ Fortuna) — https://www.fire.ca.gov/
- **Humboldt Bay Fire:** City of Eureka FD was dissolved into the Humboldt Bay Fire JPA (with Humboldt No. 1 FPD), fully integrated 2013-01-01.
- **County building/fire code adoption:** Humboldt County Code § 331-11 adopts by reference the California Building Standards Code. The code text references the **2019 California Building Code** (and all related 2019 Title 24 parts) as the express adoption; the county confirmed the **2025 edition** is effective for all applications submitted on or after **2026-01-01**. The California Fire Code (Title 24 Part 9) is adopted statewide — confirm the edition currently enforced by the AHJ fire district for each specific site.
- **Local fire safety regulations:** Title IX Division 5 of the Humboldt County Code (§§ 951-1 through 951-7) covers prevention and control of fire in unincorporated areas — burn permits, prohibition of uncontrolled fires, industrial operations, and enforcement. Title III Division 11 (§§ 3111–3116) is the SRA Fire Safe Regulations for State Responsibility Areas, adopted via Ord. 1952 (1991-12-17) and amended by Ord. 2540 (2015-11-17) — applies to new parcels, new construction, and use permits in SRA.
- Source: https://humboldtgov.org/698/Fire-Protection-Services | https://humboldt.county.codes/Code/331-11

### Wastewater — County is predominantly septic; City of Eureka is the only significant POTW

- **County role:** Humboldt County Division of Environmental Health administers Septage and Grease Trap Pumper Permits, and Nonstandard Onsite Wastewater Treatment System (OWTS) operating permits for unincorporated areas. County Code Title VI Division 1 (§§ 611-1 et seq.) governs sewage and onsite wastewater treatment for unincorporated Humboldt.
- **City of Eureka POTW:** The City of Eureka operates the only significant Publicly Owned Treatment Works (POTW) in the county and has a formal Industrial Pretreatment Program administered by the Utilities Operations / Source Control Division. Phone: 707-441-4360; email: sourcecontrol@eurekaca.gov. Industrial users discharging to Eureka's sewer must obtain a Wastewater Discharge Permit. The Eureka sewer use ordinance is codified as Chapter 50 of the Eureka Municipal Code.
- **No county-level industrial pretreatment program.** Discharge limits for industrial users are set by Eureka's IWP, not the county. Specific numerical limits (BOD, TSS, oil/grease, pH) are in Eureka's local limits — [VERIFY current limits on Eureka's portal].
- Source: https://humboldtgov.org/737/Wastewater-Programs | https://www.eurekaca.gov/338/Industrial-Pretreatment

### Stormwater — Phase II Small MS4

- **Permit type:** Phase II Small MS4 (municipal separate storm sewer system) permit under the State Water Resources Control Board General Permit adopted 2013; currently under reissuance.
- **Administered by:** Humboldt County Public Works, Water Management Division (Deputy Director Hank Seemann, hseemann@co.humboldt.ca.us, 707-445-7421).
- **Regulated communities:** McKinleyville, unincorporated Eureka area (Myrtletown, Cutten, Ridgewood Heights, Pine Hill, Humboldt Hill), and Shelter Cove. Expansion to southern Humboldt communities anticipated with permit reissuance.
- **Program elements:** Public education, public participation, illicit discharge detection and elimination (improperly connected septic systems, car-wash water, spills, illegal dumping), construction site runoff control, post-construction standards, facilities inspection, and ordinance enforcement.
- Source: https://humboldtgov.org/2486/Stormwater-Program

### Zoning — Industrial Districts

- **Building authority:** Humboldt County Planning & Building Department covers unincorporated areas only (excludes Eureka, Arcata, Fortuna, Ferndale, Rio Dell, Blue Lake, Trinidad).
- **Two zoning regimes:** (1) Coastal Zone — Title III Chapter 3 (§ 313); (2) Non-Coastal/Inland — Title III Chapter 4 (§ 314).
- **Non-coastal industrial districts (§ 314-3):** MB (Business Park), ML (Limited Industrial), MH (Heavy Industrial). No M-1/M-2 designations in current code.
- **Coastal zone industrial districts (§ 313-3):** MB (Business Park), ML (Light Industrial), MG (Industrial General), MC (Industrial/Coastal-Dependent).
- **Use permit (CUP) process:** Uses not principally permitted require a Use Permit (coastal) or Use Permit/Special Permit (non-coastal); any unlisted use that is similar and compatible may also be approved under similarity provisions.
- Source: https://humboldt.county.codes/Code/314-3 | https://humboldt.county.codes/Code/313-3

## Notes

- GOTCHA: **The county CUPA covers every incorporated city** — none of Humboldt's seven cities runs its own CUPA or is a separate Participating Agency.
- GOTCHA: **No unified county fire department.** Fire AHJ varies block-to-block by district; the City of Eureka FD was dissolved into the **Humboldt Bay Fire** JPA (with Humboldt No. 1 FPD), fully integrated 2013-01-01.
- GOTCHA: **County has no POTW / industrial pretreatment program.** Industrial dischargers connecting to sewer systems deal with the city (Eureka) or district that owns the collection system, not the county.
- The largest cities each operate their OWN building department; only the unincorporated county is served by the County Building Inspection Division.
- Code text at § 331-11 expressly names the **2019** editions; the county's building division page confirms **2025 codes** are enforced for applications submitted on or after 2026-01-01 (superseding the 2022 codes).
