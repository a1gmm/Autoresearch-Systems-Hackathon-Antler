## POTW

**City of Fortuna Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP)**

- Operator: City of Fortuna Public Works Department, Wastewater Treatment Plant Division
- Originally constructed: 1974; major upgrade completed June 2007
- Treatment processes: screening and grit removal, primary treatment, secondary treatment, mixed liquor recycle for biological nutrient removal (BNR), sludge thickening, anaerobic digestion, electrical cogeneration, solids dewatering, composting, side stream equalization, effluent disinfection
- Design capacity: 1.5 MGD average annual flow; wet-weather operational capacity 3.8 MGD
- Effluent disposition: percolation ponds during dry season (May 15–Sep 30); discharge to Strongs Creek during wet season (Oct 1–May 14)
- Biosolids: composted and provided to residents and agricultural producers as soil conditioner

Also accepts wastewater from Palmer Creek Community Services District (metered separately for billing).

Source: City of Fortuna MSR (Humboldt LAFCo, Jan. 2021), §5.2.2 (https://humboldtlafco.org/wp-content/uploads/Fortuna-MSR-Adopted_1-20-21.pdf)

## NPDES / WDR Permit

**Order No. R1-2017-0005** issued by the North Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board (NCRWQCB), effective November 1, 2017.

WWTP experienced violations in 2019 related to disinfection byproduct limits, nitrogen limits, and chlorine residual exceedances. The City has been pursuing upgrades for BNR (Modified Ludzack-Ettinger process) funded through the Clean Water State Revolving Fund (CWSRF).

## Prohibited Discharge Standards (FMC Chapter 13.32)

The following are prohibited from discharge into Fortuna's sewer system (extracted from publicly available summaries of FMC Ch. 13.32):

- Wastes creating a fire or explosive hazard — closed-cup flashpoint **less than 140°F**
- Any wastewater with **pH less than 5.5 or greater than 12.5**, or otherwise causing corrosive structural damage
- Solid or viscous substances causing obstruction; no solids **greater than 1 inch (25.4 mm)** in any dimension
- Pollutants with excessive quantities of: iron, boron, chromium, phenol, plastic resins, copper, nickel, zinc, lead, mercury, cadmium, selenium, arsenic, or other materials toxic to humans, animals, the local environment, or biological treatment processes
- Fats, oils, or greases of animal or vegetable origin that cause interference
- Sludges, screenings, or other residues from pretreatment of industrial wastes
- Dilution of a discharge (using process water to avoid treatment) is prohibited unless expressly authorized by an applicable pretreatment standard

## Industrial Wastewater Permit (IWP) — FMC Chapter 13.36

**Permit requirement**: No industrial wastewaters may be discharged to a trunk sewer or any sewer connecting to a trunk sewer until the City has approved an industrial wastewater discharge permit.

**Permit conditions may include**:
- Pretreatment of industrial wastewaters before discharge
- Restriction of peak flow discharges
- Discharge limited to specified sewers
- Relocation of discharge point
- Prohibition of specific wastewater components
- Restriction of discharge hours
- Payment of additional charges for increased treatment costs
- Other conditions as required

**Pretreatment systems**: All pretreatment systems or devices must be approved by the City Engineer. Engineering-design-required systems must have plans prepared and signed by a licensed California engineer of suitable discipline. Common required components:
- Gravity separation interceptor (settleable and floatable solids)
- Equalizing tank (flow/load equalization)
- Neutralization chamber (pH adjustment)
- Control manhole (inspection, flow measurement, sampling)

**IWP application**: Contact City of Fortuna Public Works Department.

## Stormwater (MS4)

City of Fortuna holds a **Phase II NPDES General Municipal Permit** (small MS4) under the North Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board. Enrolled under the 2003 General Permit on January 18, 2006; currently operates under **Order No. 2013-0001** (2013 Phase II General MS4 Permit). Storm Water Management Program (SWMP) adopted by City Council March 2006. City discharges stormwater to the Eel River and is part of the North Coast Stormwater Coalition.

Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/northcoast/board_decisions/adopted_orders/pdf/2013/130819_fortuna_inspection_report.pdf
