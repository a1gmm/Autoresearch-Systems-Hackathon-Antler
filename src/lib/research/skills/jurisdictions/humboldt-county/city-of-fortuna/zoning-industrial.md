## M-1 — Light Industrial

**Purpose**: To provide for the establishment of areas where light manufacturing, wholesaling, storage, and transfer functions can serve the community's need for industrial activities not offensive to nearby commercial and residential uses. The M-1 zone is consistent with the industrial land use designation of the Fortuna General Plan.

**Principal Permitted Uses (FMC 17.03.031)**: Manufacturing, processing, assembling, contracting, repair, office, research, transportation, transfer, storage, wholesale, and administrative uses — except those listed in FMC 17.03.031(C) and (D) (which require a CUP or are prohibited).

**Permitted Accessory Uses**: Incidental employee services (cafeteria in enclosed building entered from within the site); mobile food vending; other accessory uses customarily appurtenant to a permitted use.

**Uses Requiring a Conditional Use Permit (CUP) — examples from Title 17**:
- Commercial communications structures exceeding M-1 height limits (FMC 17.06.021)
- Sand, gravel, and mineral extraction (FMC 17.06.181)
- SRO (Single Room Occupancy) facilities (FMC 17.06.184.7)

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process (FMC Chapter 17.07)

- CUPs are required for uses with special site or design requirements, particular operating characteristics, or potential adverse effects on surrounding properties.
- Hearing body: **Planning Commission** (meets 2nd and 4th Tuesdays of each month at 6:00 p.m., City Hall, 621 11th Street, Fortuna).
- The Planning Commission may also hear use permit applications where the proposed use is not specifically enumerated but is similar to and compatible with uses permitted in the zone.
- Applications submitted to: Community Development Department, Planning Division, 621 11th Street, Fortuna, CA 95540; Phone: (707) 725-7600.

## Industrial Land Use Context

The Fortuna General Plan 2030 includes industrial land use designations that map to M-1 and M-2 zones. The former PALCO mill site (Pacific Lumber Company) and Alton areas are planned for future development and may require annexation and zoning actions before industrial activity can commence. As of the 2021 MSR, wastewater services had not been extended to those areas.
