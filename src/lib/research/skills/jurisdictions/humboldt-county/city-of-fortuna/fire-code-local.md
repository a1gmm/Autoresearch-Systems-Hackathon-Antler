## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

**Fortuna Fire Protection District** (operating as the Fortuna Volunteer Fire Department) — a separate local government special district, not a city department.

- Address: 320 South Fortuna Blvd, Fortuna, CA 95540 (Station 1; main station)
- Phone: (707) 725-5021
- Email: info@fortunafire.com
- Website: https://fortunafire.com/

District serves: City of Fortuna plus outlying communities of Alton, Hydesville, Metropolitan, Fernbridge, and Palmer Creek — approximately 29 square miles, population ~15,000.

Staffing: Five fire companies out of three stations (Station 1: South Fortuna Blvd; Station 2: Hydesville; Station 3: Campton Heights). Largely volunteer with career Fire Chief and Division Chief.

## Adopted California Fire Code Edition

The City of Fortuna has adopted the California Building Standards Code including the California Fire Code under **Fortuna Municipal Code Chapter 15.10 (Building Code)**. The MSR (2021) confirms "the City's adoption of the California building and fire codes incorporated local modifications to account for the climatic, topographic, and geographic conditions that exist in the City." The Fortuna Municipal Code is current through **Ordinance 2026-784** (passed April 6, 2026).

California state law (Health & Safety Code §17958) requires local agencies to adopt updated editions within 180 days of state adoption. The 2022 CFC (Title 24, Part 9) became effective statewide January 1, 2023; the 2025 CFC replaced it statewide January 1, 2026. **Contact the Fortuna Community Development Department at (707) 725-7600 or the Fortuna Fire Protection District at (707) 725-5021 to confirm the currently enforced CFC edition.**

## Permit Types (Typical for AHJ)

The Fortuna Fire Protection District website lists the following inspection categories, from which operational permits are typically triggered:

- Child & Adult Day Care occupancies
- Adult/Senior Residential Care occupancies
- Educational Facilities
- Assembly Occupancies
- Business Occupancies
- Residential properties

Burn permits (open burning): routed through NCUAQMD (ncuaqmd.org) — the FFPD website links directly to the North Coast Unified Air Quality Management District for burn permit applications.

## GOTCHA

The fire AHJ for Fortuna is the **Fortuna Fire Protection District**, a separate special district — not a city fire department. Building permit plan check for fire code is coordinated between the City Community Development Department (Building Division) and the FFPD Fire Prevention Officer. Any plan submittals or fire permit inquiries must go to the FFPD, not City Hall directly.
