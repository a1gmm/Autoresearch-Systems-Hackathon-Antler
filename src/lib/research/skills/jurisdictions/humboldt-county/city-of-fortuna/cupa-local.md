## CUPA Agency

**Humboldt County Division of Environmental Health — Hazardous Materials Program**

City of Fortuna is NOT its own CUPA. The State Secretary for Environmental Protection designated the Humboldt County Division of Environmental Health as the countywide CUPA; that designation covers all facilities within Fortuna city limits.

## Programs Administered

The Humboldt County CUPA administers the following Unified Program elements:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — facilities must submit via CERS; update annually by March 1 or within 30 days of a substantial change. Thresholds: 55 gallons, 500 pounds, or 200 cubic feet of hazardous material stored 30+ days/year; any quantity of hazardous waste; Category I or II pesticides; explosives; extremely hazardous substances exceeding EPCRA threshold planning quantities.
2. **CalARP (California Accidental Release Program)** — for regulated substances above threshold.
3. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — construction, removal, modification, and operation permits; leak detection and reporting oversight.
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — aboveground petroleum storage regulation.
5. **Hazardous Waste Generation and On-Site Treatment** — generator compliance inspections.

## Regulated Facility Count

Over 800 facilities countywide. Staffed by a supervisor, one senior specialist, four field staff, and a CERS technician.

## Reporting System

All Unified Program submissions are made through the **California Environmental Reporting System (CERS)**: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/

Required by Assembly Bill 2286 (effective January 1, 2013).

## Compliance Inspections

Inspections check: hazmat storage areas, disposal records, emergency response plans, and employee training records.

Penalties:
- Violations: up to $2,000 per day
- Knowing violations: up to $5,000 per day
- Emergency response and cleanup costs if violations cause incidents

Source: https://humboldtgov.org/702/Inspection-Compliance

## City-Specific Notes

- No city-level CUPA ordinance or city-level hazmat program exists. Fortuna relies entirely on the county CUPA.
- Industrial facilities within Fortuna city limits subject to CUPA should also check their industrial wastewater permit requirements with the City Public Works Department (FMC Chapter 13.36).
