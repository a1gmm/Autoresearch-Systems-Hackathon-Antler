## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air — North Coast Unified Air Quality Management District (NCUAQMD)

- **District**: North Coast Unified Air Quality Management District (NCUAQMD) — covers Humboldt, Del Norte, and Trinity counties; Fortuna is within this district.
- Stationary-source air permits, Title V permits, and burn permits are issued by NCUAQMD, not the City.
- Burn permits (open burning, agricultural burning): https://www.ncuaqmd.org/standard-burn-permits
- Address: 707 L Street, Eureka, CA 95501; Phone: (866) 287-6329
- Source: https://www.ncuaqmd.org/permit-requirements

### CUPA — Humboldt County Division of Environmental Health

- **Not its own CUPA** — served by the **Humboldt County Division of Environmental Health, Hazardous Materials Program** (countywide CUPA designated by the State Secretary for Environmental Protection).
- Programs administered: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), CalARP, Underground Storage Tanks, Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), Hazardous Waste Generation and On-Site Treatment.
- Over 800 regulated facilities countywide. Submissions made through CERS (California Environmental Reporting System).
- Fee matrix (FY 2025-26): matrix-based on number of hazardous materials × number of hazardous wastes. Minimum fee (1 hazmat, 0 HW): $429.89/yr; increases with material count. Source: Humboldt County Hazardous Materials Fee Matrix PDF (humboldtgov.org/DocumentCenter/View/3338).
- Address: 100 H Street, Suite 100, Eureka, CA 95501; Phone: (707) 445-6215 or (800) 963-9241
- Source: https://humboldtgov.org/701/Certified-Unified-Program-Agency-CUPA

### Fire — Fortuna Fire Protection District (AHJ)

- **Fortuna Fire Protection District** (Fortuna Volunteer Fire Department) — a separate local government agency from the City; serves the City of Fortuna plus Alton, Hydesville, Metropolitan, Fernbridge, and Palmer Creek. 29 square miles, ~15,000 population, five fire companies in three stations.
- The City adopted California Building and Fire Codes with local modifications per Fortuna Municipal Code Title 15. The adopting chapter appears to be **Chapter 15.10** (Building Code, which encompasses fire code adoption). The Fortuna Municipal Code is current through Ordinance 2026-784 (April 6, 2026).
- The MSR (2021) notes "the City's adoption of the California building and fire codes incorporated local modifications to account for the climatic, topographic, and geographic conditions that exist in the City."
- Fire District contact: 320 South Fortuna Blvd, Fortuna, CA 95540; Phone: (707) 725-5021; Email: info@fortunafire.com
- Source: https://fortunafire.com/ | Source: https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/Fortuna/html/Fortuna15/Fortuna1510.html

### Wastewater — City of Fortuna Public Works (POTW)

- **POTW**: City of Fortuna Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP), originally constructed 1974, upgraded 2007; operated by City Public Works Department.
- NPDES/WDR permit: Order No. R1-2017-0005, issued by North Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board (NCRWQCB), effective November 1, 2017.
- Dry-weather discharge to percolation ponds (May 15–Sep 30); wet-weather discharge to Strongs Creek (Oct 1–May 14). Plant design capacity: 1.5 MGD average annual; wet weather operational capacity 3.8 MGD.
- Sewer use and industrial wastewater regulated under **Fortuna Municipal Code Title 13**:
  - Chapter 13.20 — General Provisions
  - Chapter 13.24 — Definitions
  - Chapter 13.32 — Sewer Use (prohibited discharge standards; pH 5.5–12.5 allowed range)
  - Chapter 13.36 — Industrial Wastewater (IWP permit requirement; pretreatment)
  - Chapter 13.44 — Sewer Rates and Charges
  - Chapter 13.60 — Administration and Enforcement
- Industrial discharge: permit required before any industrial wastewater enters the system. No local numerical limits table found publicly; general prohibitions include metals (iron, chromium, copper, nickel, zinc, lead, mercury, cadmium, selenium, arsenic), explosive wastes, pH <5.5 or >12.5, oils/grease.
- Source: https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/Fortuna/ | Source: https://humboldtlafco.org/wp-content/uploads/Fortuna-MSR-Adopted_1-20-21.pdf

### Stormwater — MS4 / NPDES Phase II

- City of Fortuna is a designated small MS4 permittee under North Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board.
- Enrolled in the 2003 General Permit on January 18, 2006; currently operates under **Order No. 2013-0001** (2013 Phase II General MS4 Permit).
- Storm Water Management Program (SWMP) adopted by City Council March 2006; references Humboldt Low Impact Development Stormwater Manual v3.0.
- City discharges stormwater to the Eel River.
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/northcoast/water_issues/programs/npdes_stormwater/

### Zoning — City of Fortuna Community Development Department

- Zoning regulations: **Fortuna Municipal Code Title 17 — Zoning Regulations**
- Industrial zones: **M-1** (Light Industrial) and **M-2** (Heavy Industrial), established by Ord. 2011-692 § 2, amended by Ord. 2018-732 § 1.
- M-1 purpose: light manufacturing, wholesaling, storage, and transfer functions not offensive to nearby commercial/residential uses.
- Principal permitted uses in M-1: manufacturing, processing, assembling, contracting, repair, office, research, transportation, transfer, storage, wholesale, and administrative uses (with exceptions in FMC 17.03.031(C) and (D)).
- CUP required for specified uses; Planning Commission hears use permit applications.
- Source: https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/Fortuna/html/Fortuna17/Fortuna17.html

## Notes

- GOTCHA: Fortuna's fire AHJ is the **Fortuna Fire Protection District** (Fortuna Volunteer Fire Department), a largely volunteer agency, not a full-time city fire department.
- Fortuna does NOT run its own CUPA (county CUPA serves it).
- GOTCHA: Fortuna's WWTP operates under NCRWQCB Order No. R1-2017-0005. Industrial users must obtain a city IWP permit (FMC Ch. 13.36) before discharging.
- Air: NCUAQMD (not city) controls stationary-source air permits. Burn permits require NCUAQMD approval — the fortunafire.com burn permit link routes to ncuaqmd.org.
