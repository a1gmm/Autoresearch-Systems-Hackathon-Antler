## Fire Authority of Record (AHJ)

Humboldt County has **no unified county fire department**. The fire Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) varies by location:

- **Unincorporated areas:** Served by a patchwork of independent fire protection districts, community services districts, volunteer fire companies, Tribal fire programs, and JPAs. Areas outside any organized district receive goodwill/volunteer-only coverage.
- **State Responsibility Areas (SRA):** CAL FIRE Humboldt-Del Norte Unit (HQ Fortuna) has jurisdiction and reviews building/development permits for fire safety.
- **City of Eureka:** Humboldt Bay Fire JPA (merger of Eureka FD + Humboldt County Fire Protection District No. 1), fully integrated 2013-01-01.
- **Other incorporated cities:** Each city administers its own building/fire code through its city building department or contracted AHJ.

Contact CAL FIRE Humboldt-Del Norte Unit for SRA determinations. Contact the specific fire district for any given parcel in unincorporated areas.

Source: https://humboldtgov.org/698/Fire-Protection-Services

## Adopted California Fire Code Edition

**Adopting section:** Humboldt County Code § 331-11 (Incorporation of Uniform Codes by Reference), Title III Division 3 Building Regulations.

The published code text at § 331-11 (as of Ord. 2707, 2023-01-31) expressly names the **2019 California Building Standards Code** and its component parts by reference. The California Fire Code is Title 24 Part 9 — the county adopts it as part of the same statewide Title 24 bundle.

**Current enforcement edition:** Per the Humboldt County Building Division, the **2025 California Building Standards Code** (including Part 9, the California Fire Code) is mandatory for all permit and plan-review applications submitted on or after **2026-01-01**. Applications submitted through 2025-12-31 were reviewed under the 2022 codes.

[VERIFY] The county code text at § 331-11 has not yet been updated online to reflect the 2025 edition adoption. Confirm enforced edition with the Planning & Building Department at (707) 445-7245.

## SRA Fire Safe Regulations — Title III Division 11

**Adopting ordinance:** Ord. 1952, § 1, adopted 1991-12-17; amended by Ord. 2540, Section 1, adopted 2015-11-17.

**Scope:** Applies to new parcels, new construction requiring a building permit, land use/development requiring a use permit, manufactured home siting, and new road construction — all within State Responsibility Areas (SRA) in Humboldt County.

**Chapters:**

| Chapter | Title | Key Standards |
|---|---|---|
| Ch. 1 — §§ 3111-1 to 3111-13 | Administration | Scope, CAL FIRE referral, defensible space maintenance |
| Ch. 2 — §§ 3112-1 to 3112-13 | Emergency Access | Road width, surface, grade, radius, turnarounds, turnouts, dead-end rules, gate entrances |
| Ch. 3 — §§ 3113-1 to 3113-11 | Signing and Building Numbers | Sign size, visibility, height, address requirements |
| Ch. 4 — §§ 3114-1 to 3114-5 | Emergency Water Standards | Water source requirements, hydrant/fire valve specs, signing |
| Ch. 5 — §§ 3115-1 to 3115-4 | Fuel Modification Standards | Defensible space setbacks, disposal of flammable vegetation, greenbelts |
| Ch. 6 — § 3116-1 | Enforcement | Violation provisions |

**Process:** County notifies CAL FIRE of applicable permit applications; CAL FIRE reviews and makes fire protection recommendations within 30 days; conditions of approval incorporate SRA standards.

Source: https://humboldt.county.codes/Code/3_Div11 | Ord. 2540 PDF: https://humboldtgov.org/DocumentCenter/View/278/Fire-Safe-Regulations-Ord-No-2540-PDF

## Local Amendments to California Fire Code

[VERIFY] No specific local amendments to the California Fire Code (beyond the statewide Title 24 Part 9 provisions) were identified in the Humboldt County Code or county website. The county's § 331-11 local amendments are limited to administrative provisions (Board of Appeals structure, permit exemptions, grading). Contact the County Building Official at (707) 445-7245 or planningbuilding@co.humboldt.ca.us for any adopted local fire code amendments.

## Fire Permit Fees

[VERIFY] Humboldt County does not publish a separate fire permit fee schedule — building permits are issued by the County Building Inspection Division at fees set in the county's general fee schedule. Open burning permits under § 951-3 are administered by CAL FIRE Humboldt-Del Norte Unit (for SRA areas) or by the local fire protection district (for non-SRA unincorporated areas).
