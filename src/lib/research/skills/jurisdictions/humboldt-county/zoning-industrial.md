## Overview

Humboldt County Planning & Building Department administers zoning for **unincorporated areas only** (excludes Eureka, Arcata, Fortuna, Ferndale, Rio Dell, Blue Lake, Trinidad — each city has its own zoning code).

The county operates two parallel zoning frameworks:
- **Coastal Zone** — Title III Chapter 3 (§ 313); applies to lands within the California Coastal Zone in Humboldt County.
- **Non-Coastal / Inland** — Title III Chapter 4 (§ 314); applies to all other unincorporated lands.

Industrial performance standards apply in both zones — see § 313-103.1 (coastal) and cross-references within § 314-3 (non-coastal).

Contact: Humboldt County Planning & Building Department, (707) 445-7245, planningbuilding@co.humboldt.ca.us

## Non-Coastal Industrial Districts — § 314-3

Source: https://humboldt.county.codes/Code/314-3

Three industrial district types. No M-1/M-2 designations exist in the current code (former section numbering used INL# prefixes under a prior code structure; current designations are MB, ML, MH).

### MB — Business Park Zone (§ 314-3.1)
*(Former Section INL#314-44; Ord. 1800, § 1, 1987-06-23)*

**Intent:** Well-designed mixed industrial/commercial areas — nuisance-free light industrial, R&D, administrative and professional office, in a park-like environment.

**Principal Permitted Uses:** Administrative offices; Office and Professional Service; Warehousing/Storage/Distribution; Research/Light Industrial; Supportive housing above ground floor as primary use; Commercial Residential.

**Uses Permitted With Special Permit:** Residential uses subordinate to permitted use (outside Housing Opportunity Zones); Transitional Housing; Emergency Shelters; Supportive housing on ground floor.

**Uses Permitted With Use Permit:** Retail Sales; Retail Service; Transient Habitation; Mini-Storage.

**Development Standards:**

| Standard | MB |
|---|---|
| Minimum Lot Area | 10,000 sq ft |
| Minimum Lot Width | 60 ft |
| Front Setback | 30 ft |
| Rear Setback | 10 ft |
| Side Setback | 30 ft (street side) / 10 ft (otherwise) |
| Maximum Ground Coverage | 50% |
| Maximum Building Height | 50 ft |

### ML — Limited Industrial Zone (§ 314-3.2)
*(Former Section INL#314-43; Ord. 1086, § 11, 1976-07-13; Amended by Ord. 2214, 2000-06-06; Ord. 2472, § 1, 2012-02-14)*

**Intent:** Light manufacturing and heavy commercial uses of the non-nuisance type; large administrative facilities as predominant uses.

**Principal Permitted Uses:** Small animal hospitals (fully enclosed); Carpentry/cabinet shops, clothing manufacture, contractors' yards, dry cleaning/laundry plants, handicraft manufacture, lumber yards, metalworking shops, wholesale outlets, painters'/decorators' yards, plumbing shops, printing/lithographing, major auto repair; Administrative/business/professional offices; Manufacturing of electrical/electronic equipment, household effects, R&D labs; Emergency shelters.

**Uses Permitted With Special Permit:** Single-room occupancy facilities (conversions of existing buildings).

**Uses Permitted With Use Permit:** Dwellings, ADUs, manufactured homes, hotels, motels, manufactured home parks (Ord. 2335, 2004-12-14); Animal hospitals and kennels; Animal feed and sales yards; Manufacture of furniture, finished paper/paper products; Mini-storage; Emergency dependent unit villages and alternative lodge parks; Similar compatible uses.

**Development Standards:**

| Standard | ML |
|---|---|
| Minimum Lot Area | 1 acre |
| Minimum Lot Width | None specified |
| Front Setback | 50 ft |
| Rear Setback | 50 ft |
| Side Setback | 10% of avg. lot width, min. 25 ft |
| Maximum Ground Coverage | 25% |
| Maximum Building Height | 75 ft |

**Special Regulations:** All manufacturing/fabricating areas must be enclosed in buildings. Equipment and material storage adjacent to residential zones (RS, R-1, R-2, R-3, R-4) must be screened by walls, fences, or plantings to at least 6 ft in height.

### MH — Heavy Industrial Zone (§ 314-3.3)
*(Former Section INL#314-46; Ord. 1086, § 12, 1976-07-13; Amended by Ord. 2214, 2000-06-06; Ord. 2472, § 1, 2012-02-14)*

**Intent:** Areas devoted to normal industrial operations; subject only to regulations needed to control congestion and protect surrounding areas.

**Principal Permitted Uses:** All uses listed under ML as principal permitted, plus: Industrial manufacturing uses generally (except those requiring a use permit below).

**Uses Permitted With Special Permit:** Single-room occupancy facilities (conversions of existing buildings).

**Uses Permitted With Use Permit:** Dwellings, ADUs, manufactured homes, hotels, motels, emergency shelters, manufactured home parks, special occupancy parks (Ord. 2335, 2004-12-14); Emergency dependent unit villages and alternative lodge parks; All uses except one-family dwellings, general agriculture, rooming/boarding of ≤2 persons, and manufactured homes; Similar compatible uses.

**Development Standards:**

| Standard | MH |
|---|---|
| Minimum Lot Area | 1 acre |
| Minimum Lot Width | None specified |
| Front Setback | 50 ft |
| Rear Setback | 50 ft |
| Side Setback | 10% of avg. lot width, min. 25 ft |
| Maximum Ground Coverage | None specified |
| Maximum Building Height | 75 ft |

## Coastal Zone Industrial Districts — § 313-3

Source: https://humboldt.county.codes/Code/313-3

Four industrial district types in the coastal zone. "Conditionally Permitted" in the coastal zone context = requires a Coastal Development Permit (CDP) or Use Permit through the planning department.

### MB — Business Park (§ 313-3.1)
*(Former Section CZ#A313-25)*

**Principal Permitted:** Minor Utilities; Administrative; Warehousing/Storage/Distribution; Office and Professional Service; Research/Light Industrial.

**Conditionally Permitted:** Retail Sales; Retail Service; Transient Habitation; Mini-Storage; similar compatible uses.

**Development Standards:**

| Standard | MB (Coastal) |
|---|---|
| Minimum Lot Size | 10,000 sq ft |
| Minimum Lot Width | 60 ft |
| Front Setback | 30 ft |
| Rear Setback | 10 ft |
| Side Setback | 30 ft (street side) / 10 ft (otherwise) |
| Maximum Ground Coverage | 50% |
| Maximum Structure Height | 50 ft |

| Rear Setback | 25 ft |

### MG — Industrial General (§ 313-3.3)
*(Former Section CZ#A313-27)*

**Principal Permitted:** Minor Utilities; Warehousing/Storage/Distribution; Heavy Commercial; Research/Light Industrial; Aquaculture; Timber Product Processing.

**Conditionally Permitted:** Caretaker's Residence; Solid Waste Disposal; Oil and Gas Pipelines; Minor Generation/Distribution Facilities; Extensive Impact Civic Uses; Major Electrical Distribution Lines; Heavy Industrial; Hazardous Industrial; Coastal-Dependent Industrial; Coastal-Related Industrial; General Agriculture; Surface Mining (1 and 2); Oil and Gas Drilling and Processing; Metallic Mineral Extraction; Coastal Access Facilities; similar compatible uses.

**Development Standards:**

| Standard | MG (Coastal) |
|---|---|
| Minimum Lot Size | 1 acre |
| Minimum Lot Width | None specified |
| Front Setback | 50 ft |
| Rear Setback | 50 ft |
| Side Setback | 25 ft |
| Maximum Ground Coverage | None specified |

---

### MC — Industrial/Coastal-Dependent (§ 313-3.4)

**Intent:** Protects long-term use for coastal-dependent industrial uses; interim non-coastal-dependent development allowed under conditional permit with performance standards.

**Principal Permitted:** Minor Utilities; Coastal-Dependent Industrial (subject to Coastal-Dependent Industrial Development Regulations); Aquaculture.

**Conditionally Permitted:** Caretaker's Residence; Solid Waste Disposal; Oil and Gas Pipelines; Major Electrical Distribution Lines; Coastal-Dependent Recreation; Coastal-Related Industrial; Heavy Industrial (limited to alteration/improvement/relocation of existing facilities); Surface Mining (1 and 2); Oil and Gas Drilling and Processing; Coastal Access Facilities; certain ML/MG uses as interim conditional permitted uses (subject to performance standards).

## Use Permit (CUP) Process

- Non-coastal: Uses requiring a "Use Permit" go through the Planning Department. Applications filed with Humboldt County Planning & Building, 3015 H Street, Eureka, CA 95501. Fees set in county fee schedule.
- Coastal: Uses requiring a "Conditionally Permitted Use" go through Coastal Development Permit (CDP) review under the Local Coastal Program, administered by Humboldt County Planning & Building.
- Any use not specifically listed may be approved if "similar to and compatible with" enumerated uses — applicant requests a director-level determination.
- Alquist-Priolo Fault Hazard zones (§§ 313-30 / 314-22.1) and SRA Fire Safe Regulations (Title III Division 11) may modify setback requirements.
