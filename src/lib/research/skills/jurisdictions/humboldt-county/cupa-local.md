## HMBP Submission Requirements

- Facilities must disclose all hazardous materials and waste stored/used above threshold quantities at any time:
  - 55 gallons, 500 pounds, or 200 cubic feet for 30+ days/year
  - Any amount of hazardous waste
  - Category I or II pesticides
  - Explosives
  - Extremely hazardous substances above threshold planning quantities
- Annual update deadline: **March 1** each year
- Updates required within **30 days** of any substantial change
- New businesses must comply before certificate of occupancy
- All submissions via CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) — mandatory since 2013-01-01 per AB 2286

Source: https://humboldtgov.org/700/Business-Plan

## Inspection and Enforcement

- Inspection scope: hazmat storage areas, disposal records, emergency response plans, employee training records.
- Violations: standard up to **$2,000/day**; knowing violations up to **$5,000/day**; interference = misdemeanor; emergency response = full cost recovery.
- Inspection triggers: routine compliance, complaint, release event.

Source: https://humboldtgov.org/702/Inspection-Compliance
