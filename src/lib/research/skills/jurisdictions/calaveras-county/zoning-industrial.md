## Industrial Zone Districts — Chapter 17.07

Three industrial base zones established in **Chapter 17.07**:

### M1 — Light Industrial Zone
Provides locations for diverse light industrial and workplace uses near commercial and residential areas. Implements the Industrial General Plan Land Use Category.

Typical permitted uses: light industrial processing, packaging, distribution, warehousing, storage, R&D, public/quasi-public uses.

### M2 — General Industrial Zone
Provides for manufacturing, processing, storage, and similar industrial uses, including those that may create some objectionable conditions. Implements Industrial General Plan Land Use Category.

Includes: manufacturing, processing, assembly, research, wholesale, storage, trucking terminals, public/quasi-public uses.

### M4 — Business Park Zone
Comprehensive employment-generating development; business parks centered around basic employment-generating businesses and accessory/support services. Implements Industrial General Plan Land Use Category.

More office, retail, and service uses permitted than M1/M2.

## Use Classifications — Chapter 17.42

Use definitions are in Chapter 17.42. Key industrial definitions:
- **Light Industrial**: manufacturing of non-edible products and finished parts from processed materials; includes computer/electronic product mfg, furniture mfg; retail sales incidental to industrial use allowed.
- **General Industrial**: manufacturing of non-edible products from extracted or raw materials; includes chemical or biochemical reaction or alteration; biomass manufacturing.
- **Intensive Industrial**: [VERIFY definition in §17.42.040 — PDF text truncated]
- **Custom Manufacturing**: small-scale manufacturing by hand tools or small mechanical equipment.
- **Food and Beverage Manufacturing**: roasters, micro-breweries, micro-distilleries, wine manufacturing, cheese makers, wholesale bakeries, and similar.

## Overlay Zones Relevant to Industrial Sites

- **Mineral Extraction (ME) Overlay** (Chapter 17.13): required for mining/quarrying operations; separate permit/reclamation plan required; financial assurances required.
- **Environmental Protection (EP) Overlay** (Chapter 17.10): imposes additional findings for development within EP-designated areas.
- **Airport Overflight (AO) Overlay** (Chapter 17.09): height limits and noise-level reduction requirements near airports.
- **Planned Development (PD) Overlay** (Chapter 17.14): may allow flexible industrial/mixed use by project approval.
