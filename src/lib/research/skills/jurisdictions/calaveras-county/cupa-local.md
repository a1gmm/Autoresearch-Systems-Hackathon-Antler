## CUPA Agency

**Calaveras County Environmental Health Department** (a division of the Environmental Management Agency, EMA) is the single Certified Unified Program Agency for all of Calaveras County. There are no Participating Agencies and no city-level CUPA. Angels Camp, the only incorporated city, falls under the county CUPA.

- Certified by CalEPA: **effective June 1, 2002**
- Designating action: Calaveras County Board of Supervisors authorized EH Department to seek and hold CUPA status

## Program Elements Administered

The CUPA consolidates all six state-mandated Unified Program elements:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — Hazardous Materials Release Response Plans and Inventory (Community Right-to-Know / Cal HSC §25500 et seq.)
2. **CalARP** — California Accidental Release Prevention Program (Risk Management Plan for regulated substances including federally-listed EHSs and state-listed AHMs)
3. **Underground Storage Tank (UST)** — Permit, inspection, closure certification
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — SPCC Plan review and inspection
5. **Hazardous Waste Generator + Tiered On-Site Treatment** — Generator registration and tiered permitting (PBR, Conditionally Authorized, Conditionally Exempt Limited, CESQT, CESW)
6. **HMMP/HMIS** — California Fire Code Hazardous Materials Management Plan and Inventory Statement

## Reporting Thresholds

- **HMBP trigger**: ≥55 gallons liquid, ≥500 lbs solid, or ≥200 cubic feet compressed gas of a hazardous material handled at any one time during the reporting year.
- **CalARP trigger**: any regulated substance (RCRA EHS or state AHM) at any quantity.
- **UST trigger**: any underground tank storing hazardous materials including motor fuels.
- **APSA trigger**: single AST ≥1,320 gallons of liquid petroleum, or cumulative ASTs ≥1,320 gallons (≥55-gal individual tanks). Farm/nursery/logging/construction sites exempt if no single tank >20,000 gal and cumulative ≤100,000 gal.
- **Hazardous Waste Generator**: any quantity of waste that is toxic, corrosive, reactive, or ignitable triggers generator registration.

## Forms and Submission

- **Consolidated Permit Application Package**: 2024 edition, published by Calaveras County EH Dept. Includes Activity Declaration Form (use first to determine required forms), Facility Information Form, Chemical Description Form, HMBP forms, CalARP Regulated Substance Registration, UST Facility and Tank forms, APSA/SPCC guidance, and all on-site treatment tiers.
- Also accepts: **Unified Program Consolidated Forms (UPCF)** from unidocs.org.
- All forms must bear original signature. Certified mail recommended.
- Submit one copy to address above. Do not enclose payment unless specifically requested.
- Application PDF (2024): https://ema.calaverasgov.us/Portals/EMA/Documents/EH/CUPA/CUPA%20Consolidated%20Permit%20Application%20Package%202024.pdf
