## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air — Calaveras County Air Pollution Control District (CCAPCD)

- Calaveras County operates its **own independent Air Pollution Control District (CCAPCD)**, NOT San Joaquin Valley, Sacramento Metro, or Bay Area districts. The CCAPCD covers all of Calaveras County.
- Governed by the Calaveras County Board of Supervisors sitting as the Air Pollution Control Board.
- Office co-located with EMA: 891 Mountain Ranch Road, Building E, San Andreas, CA 95249; (209) 754-6601.
- Lisa Medina, Air Pollution Control Officer; Michelle Turner, Deputy APCO.
- **Stationary source permits:** Two-step — Authority to Construct (A/C) then Permit to Operate (P/O). Required for gas stations, rock quarries, foundries, auto paint spray booths, boilers, wood processing equipment, diesel generators >50 brake horsepower. Application filed with CCAPCD.
- Rules compiled and EPA-approved; Regulation II (Permits: Rules 201–209), Regulation III (Fees: Rule 301), Regulation IV (Prohibitions: Rules 407–413 covering visible emissions, particulate matter, sulfur, organic liquid loading, oil-water separators, abrasive blasting). Visible emissions limit: Ringlemann No. 2 or equivalent opacity, not to exceed 3 minutes in any one hour.
- Rule 201(b): Permit to Operate must be posted visibly on equipment or within 25 feet.
- **Not** subject to SJVAPCD or SMAQMD rules; CCAPCD is in the Mountain Counties Air Basin.
- Source: https://ema.calaverasgov.us/Air-Pollution-Control; EPA compiled rules: https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2022-08/calaveras-county-apcd-epa-compiled-rules-and-regulations.pdf

### CUPA — Calaveras County Environmental Health Department

- **Single CUPA for the entire county**, certified by CalEPA effective June 1, 2002 (Board of Supervisors designated Environmental Health Department as CUPA).
- Administers all six Unified Program elements: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) / Community Right-to-Know; CalARP (Accidental Release Prevention); Underground Storage Tank (UST); Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA); Hazardous Waste Generator + Tiered On-Site Treatment (PBR / CA / CE tiers); California Fire Code hazmat plan/inventory (HMMP/HMIS).
- **No Participating Agencies**; no city (including Angels Camp) runs its own CUPA.
- Submission address: 891 Mountain Ranch Rd., San Andreas, CA 95249; (209) 754-6399.
- HMBP threshold: ≥55 gallons or ≥500 lbs hazardous material, or ≥200 cubic feet compressed gas at any one time triggers reporting.
- APSA threshold: single AST ≥1,320 gallons or cumulative ASTs ≥1,320 gallons of liquid petroleum products.
- Forms: County's own CUPA Consolidated Permit Application Package (2024 edition) or Unified Program Consolidated Forms (UPCF) at unidocs.org.
- Source: https://ema.calaverasgov.us/Environmental-Health/CUPA-Hazardous-Materials; Application package: https://ema.calaverasgov.us/Portals/EMA/Documents/EH/CUPA/CUPA%20Consolidated%20Permit%20Application%20Package%202024.pdf

### Fire — Multiple AHJs (no single county fire department)

- Unincorporated county structure protection: CAL FIRE **Tuolumne-Calaveras Unit (TCU)**, headquartered in San Andreas, covers State Responsibility Areas (SRA). https://www.fire.ca.gov/
- Local fire code enforcement (Local Responsibility Areas) split among **ten independent fire protection districts**: Altaville-Melones FPD, CAL FIRE TCU (SRA portions), Calaveras Consolidated Fire, Central Calaveras Fire & Rescue, City of Angels Fire Dept., Ebbetts Pass Fire District, Mokelumne Hill FPD, Murphys Fire District, San Andreas FPD, West Point FPD.
- **Each district is its own AHJ** for CFC enforcement within its boundaries.
- Ebbetts Pass Fire District (Arnold area) enforces California Fire Code + NFPA Life Safety Codes + district ordinances; provides plan review for fire alarm, sprinkler, suppression systems, and commercial inspections. Contact: (209) 795-1646, 1037 Blagen Rd, Arnold CA 95223.
- County-level fire code provisions: Calaveras County Code of Ordinances **Title 8 (Health and Safety), Chapter 8.08** (Hazardous Fire Areas) and **Chapter 8.10** (Fire—Life Safety Areas); **Title 15 (Buildings and Construction), Chapter 15.08** (California Fire Code/Minimum Fire Code) — specific adopted CFC edition [VERIFY on Municode].
- Source: https://fireprevention.calaverasgov.us/; https://fireprevention.calaverasgov.us/Resources/Fire-Districts; https://www.epfd.org/inspections; Municode: https://library.municode.com/ca/calaveras_county/codes/code_of_ordinances

### Wastewater — Calaveras County Water District (CCWD) + on-site EMA

- **Calaveras County Water District (CCWD)** provides sanitary sewer collection and treatment to ~5,000 customers through **twelve wastewater treatment and disposal facilities** and fourteen sanitary sewer collection systems across the county (not a single POTW — multiple small plants).
- Legal authority: **CCWD Ordinance No. 84-1** (Rules and Regulations governing furnishing of water and/or wastewater services); District's "WWS-RR" rules.
- Industrial Pretreatment and Pollution Prevention Program (IPP) administered by CCWD Engineering staff; currently focused on FOG control for Food Service Establishments (53 FSEs in service area). Formal industrial wastewater discharge permit for non-FSE industrial dischargers [VERIFY — no published IWP schedule found for heavy industry].
- One confirmed NPDES permit: **Forest Meadows Wastewater Reclamation Plant** (Murphys/Forest Meadows area), NPDES No. CA0085278, Order R5-2024-0067 (adopted 13 Dec 2024, effective 1 Feb 2025, expires 31 Jan 2030). Minor discharge to Collierville Powerhouse Effluent Channel. Issued by Central Valley RWQCB (Region 5).
- **Most of unincorporated Calaveras County is on septic/OWTS**, not public sewer. On-site wastewater (septic) permits handled by EMA On-Site Wastewater program.
- Stormwater / MS4: Calaveras County is a small rural county — not listed as a Phase I or Phase II MS4 permittee under Central Valley RWQCB MS4 general permits [VERIFY with Central Valley RWQCB].
- Source: https://www.ccwd.org/; SSMP: https://www.ccwd.org/files/322508719/2021+Sanitary+Sewer+Management+Plan.pdf; NPDES permit: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/centralvalley/board_decisions/adopted_orders/calaveras/r5-2024-0067_npdes.pdf; On-site wastewater: https://ema.calaverasgov.us/On-Site-Wastewater

### Zoning — Industrial Districts

- Zoning authority: **Calaveras County Planning Department** (unincorporated areas). County Zoning Code is **Title 17** of the Calaveras County Municipal Code, most recently adopted July 16, 2024, effective August 15, 2024.
- **Chapter 17.07** (Industrial Zones) establishes three industrial base zones:
  - **M1 — Light Industrial**: light industrial, processing, packaging, distribution, warehousing, R&D, public/quasi-public uses.
  - **M2 — General Industrial**: manufacturing, processing, storage, assembly, wholesale, trucking terminals; uses that may create some objectionable conditions.
  - **M4 — Business Park**: employment-generating business parks; more office/service mix allowed.
- Conditional Use Permit (CUP) required for: General Industrial uses in M1/M4; Intensive Industrial in M2/M4; Lumbermill/Sawmill in M1; Salvage and Wrecking in M1/M2; chemical/mineral/explosives storage in all industrial zones; Service Stations in all industrial zones; Airports/Heliports.
- Maximum building height: 45 ft in all three zones.
- Max FAR: 0.50 (individual well/septic) or 0.75 (public water + sewer).
- Caretaker unit allowed in all three industrial zones.
- Angels Camp (only incorporated city) has its own zoning — see city file.
- Source: https://planning.calaverasgov.us/Portals/Planning/Documents/Zoning%20Code%20Updates/Zoning%20Code%20Update%202023/Calaveras%20County%20Zoning%20Code_Adopted%20071624.pdf%20Accessible.pdf; Municode Title 17: https://library.municode.com/ca/calaveras_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CD_ORD_TIT17ZO

## Notes

- Calaveras County has only ONE incorporated city: **Angels Camp** (incorporated 1912). All other communities (San Andreas the county seat, Arnold, Murphys, Valley Springs, etc.) are unincorporated and under county jurisdiction.
- EMA (Environmental Management Agency) is the umbrella: Environmental Health (CUPA), On-Site Wastewater, Air Pollution Control, Agriculture/Weights & Measures, Animal Control.
- Fire code AHJ is fragmented across 10 independent fire districts — always verify which district's boundaries cover the specific parcel before citing fire code rules.
