## POTW Operator

**Calaveras County Water District (CCWD)** is the primary wastewater utility in Calaveras County.

- Address: 120 Toma Court, San Andreas, CA 95249
- Phone: (209) 754-3543 | Fax: (209) 754-9620
- Engineering (IPP contact): same office
- Website: https://www.ccwd.org/

CCWD operates **twelve wastewater treatment and disposal facilities** and fourteen sanitary sewer collection systems serving approximately 5,000 municipal customers throughout the county.

Source: https://www.ccwd.org/files/322508719/2021+Sanitary+Sewer+Management+Plan.pdf (SSMP, June 2021)

## Most of the County Is on Septic

Calaveras County is predominantly rural. The majority of unincorporated residents and businesses are served by **on-site wastewater treatment systems (OWTS/septic)**, not public sewer. OWTS permitting is handled by EMA's On-Site Wastewater program: https://ema.calaverasgov.us/On-Site-Wastewater

## Industrial Pretreatment and Pollution Prevention Program (IPP)

- Administered by **CCWD Engineering staff**.
- Current IPP focus: **Fats, Oils, and Grease (FOG) control** for Food Service Establishments (FSEs). 53 FSEs in service area as of 2021.
- FSE requirements under IPP:
  - Install Grease Removal Device (GRD) — grease interceptor or grease trap — for all new and existing FSEs.
  - GRD maintenance minimum: every 3 months for interceptors; weekly for grease traps.
  - Maintain cleaning records and grease pick-up logs on site for District review.
  - Waste (discharge) permit issued to FSEs; staff advise on GRD sizing, maintenance, BMPs, and record-keeping.
  - FSEs inspected at minimum 4 times per year.
- **Non-FSE industrial pretreatment**: No published formal industrial wastewater permit program or local discharge limits table identified for heavy industrial dischargers as of 2026. CCWD's system serves primarily low-density residential, making large industrial discharger permitting uncommon. [VERIFY with CCWD Engineering for any sector-specific industrial discharge permits.]

Source: CCWD SSMP §8.1.1.1 (https://www.ccwd.org/files/322508719/2021+Sanitary+Sewer+Management+Plan.pdf)
