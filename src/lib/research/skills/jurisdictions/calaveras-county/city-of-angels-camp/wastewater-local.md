## POTW

The City of Angels Camp owns and operates its own wastewater collection and treatment system (publicly owned treatment works).

- **Collection system:** ~22.5 miles gravity sewer, ~4.5 miles force main, pipe 4–18 inches diameter, five sewage pump lift stations.
- **Treatment plant:** 3000 Centennial Rd., Angels Camp, CA 95222 | (209) 736-2412 | Fax: (209) 736-0709
- **Service area:** City limits plus ~64 single-family homes in neighboring Vallecito (outside city limits).
- **Planning documents on file:** Wastewater Master Plan (two-volume, per 2020 General Plan); Sanitary Sewer Management Plan; Statewide General Waste Discharge Requirements compliance document.

## Sewer Use Ordinance

**ACMC Chapter 13.12** (Sewer System) — originally adopted as Ord. 289, 1987; supplemented by Ord. 369, 1999 (grease trap inspection fees).

### Article I — General Provisions

- § 13.12.010: Unlawful to deposit human/animal excrement, garbage, or other objectionable waste on public or private property.
- § 13.12.015: Unlawful to discharge sewage, industrial wastes, or polluted waters to any natural outlet within the city.
- § 13.12.025: Mandatory connection to public sewer within 60 days of sewer main becoming available; required prior to certificate of occupancy (ACMC § 15.04.110).

### Article II — Key Definitions

- **Industrial wastes (§ 13.12.080):** "Any and all solid or liquid waste substance, not sewage, from any producing, manufacturing or processing operation of whatever nature."
- **B.O.D. (§ 13.12.055):** Biochemical oxygen demand — quantity of oxygen utilized in 5-day biochemical oxidation at 20°C, expressed in parts per million by weight.
- **Suspended solids (§ 13.12.165):** Defined separately as a regulated parameter.

### Article IV — Discharge Prohibitions (§§ 13.12.225–13.12.235)

#### § 13.12.225 — No stormwater or industrial waste to sanitary sewer

"No person shall discharge or cause to be discharged any stormwater, surface water, groundwater, roof runoff, subsurface drainage, cooling water, garbage or polluted industrial wastes to any sanitary sewer."

#### § 13.12.230 — Prohibited Discharges (verbatim limits)

No person shall discharge the following to any public sewer:

| Item | Limit / Condition |
|---|---|
| Temperature | > 150°F |
| Fat, oil, or grease | > 100 parts per million by weight |
| Flammable/explosive liquids | Any gasoline, benzene, naphtha, fuel oil, or other flammable or explosive liquid, solid, or gas |
| Garbage | Not properly shredded (domestic disposal ≤ 1.5 hp) |
| Solids/viscous substances | Ashes, cinders, sand, mud, straw, shavings, metal, glass, rags, feathers, tar, plastics, wood, manure — anything capable of causing obstruction |
| pH | < 5.5 or > 9.0; or any corrosive property capable of damaging structures, equipment, or personnel |
| Toxic/poisonous substances | In quantity sufficient to injure or interfere with sewage treatment process or constitute a hazard in receiving waters |
| Suspended solids | Character and quantity requiring unusual attention/expense at the treatment plant |
| Noxious/malodorous gas or substance | Capable of creating a public nuisance |

*(Ord. 289 (part), 1987)*

#### § 13.12.235 — Grease, Oil, and Sand Interceptors

- Required "when, in the opinion of the city, grease, oil and sand interceptors are necessary" — **mandatory for garages and service stations**; not required for single-family dwellings.
- Specifications: impervious materials, watertight, gastight, easily removable covers; located for ready access/cleaning.
- **Inspection frequency: quarterly** — fees collected per city council resolution.
- *(Ord. 369, 1999; Ord. 289 (part), 1987)*

## Chapter 13.30 — Operation and Maintenance of Public Sewers (Ord. 289, 1987)

- Public sewers as mapped on April 1981 sanitary sewer system map.
- Sewer service laterals from private connection to public main are private-property-owner responsibility.
- Private parties working in public ROW/easements need an encroachment permit (ACMC § 12.12.060).
