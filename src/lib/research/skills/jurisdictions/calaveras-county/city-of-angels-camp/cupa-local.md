## CUPA Agency

**Calaveras County Environmental Health Department** is the sole CUPA for all of Calaveras County, including the City of Angels Camp.

- **Certification date:** June 1, 2002 (certified by CalEPA under SB 1082 / Health and Safety Code authority).
- **City of Angels Camp is NOT its own CUPA and has NO Participating Agency (PA) arrangement** — all six unified program elements inside city limits are administered directly by the county agency.
- **Governing body:** Calaveras County Board of Supervisors (designated the Environmental Health Department to seek CUPA certification).

## Six Program Elements

| Program Element | Trigger Threshold / Notes |
|---|---|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) | ≥ 55 gal liquid, ≥ 500 lb solid, or ≥ 200 cu ft compressed gas handled at any one time during the reporting year |
| California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) | Handles any CalARP-listed Regulated Substance (federally listed EHS or state-listed AHM) |
| Underground Storage Tank (UST) | Any UST storing hazardous materials including gasoline; UST Facility Form + UST Tank Form per tank; new USTs require Installation-Certification of Compliance |
| Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) | Single AST ≥ 1,320 gal or multiple ASTs ≥ 55 gal with cumulative capacity ≥ 1,320 gal — SPCC plan required |
| Hazardous Waste Generator / Onsite Treatment (Tiered Permitting) | Any generator; onsite treatment tiers: CA, CEL, CESQT, CESW, PBR |
| HMMP / HMIS (Fire Code compliance) | Hazardous Materials Management Plan and Inventory Statement per Uniform Fire Code |

## Reporting Portal

- **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System) — required electronic submission for HMBP, CalARP, UST, APSA.
- Calaveras County also accepts its own consolidated permit application forms or the Unified Program Consolidated Forms (UPCF) from unidocs.org.
- Paper submissions sent via certified mail to address above; original signatures required.

Source: https://ema.calaverasgov.us/Portals/EMA/Documents/EH/CUPA/CUPA%20Consolidated%20Permit%20Application%20Package%202024.pdf

## Local Hazmat Ordinance / Chapter

- No separate City of Angels Camp hazmat chapter found in the municipal code; the city relies entirely on the county CUPA's authority under state Health and Safety Code.
- ACMC Chapter 8.52, Article III (Fire Prevention) references the Uniform Fire Code for HMMP/HMIS compliance at the local level (fire code authority sits with the city fire chief), but CUPA program administration is the county's.
