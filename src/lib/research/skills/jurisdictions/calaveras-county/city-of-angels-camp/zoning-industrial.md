## Industrial District Names

Angels Camp has a **single industrial zoning district** — there is no M-1/M-2 or I-1/I-2 split:

| District | Chapter | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **IND — Industrial District** | 17.33 | Heavy/general manufacturing, outdoor storage, auto repair, large-scale industrial |
| **BAE — Business Attraction and Expansion District** | 17.37 | Light industrial compatible with neighboring uses (lighter analog to IND) |

EHS-intensive industrial uses (hazardous materials storage, heavy manufacturing) belong in IND.

## Chapter 17.33 — Industrial District (IND)

### 17.33.010 Purpose and Intent (Ord. 509 § 4 (Att. C), 2021; Ord. 270 (part), 1984)

"To provide for manufacturing, processing, assembly, storage, distribution and other businesses including those that may require outdoor storage and including auto repair facilities and similar heavy industrial uses. Generally, uses under this designation may be incompatible with other land uses due to noise, appearance, traffic, odors or similar characteristics."

- Land zoned IND concentrated in industrial parks isolated by topography, major thoroughfares, or natural barriers.
- IND areas visible from SR-4, SR-49, or other major roads require **screening**.
- "Extensive development of new heavy industrial uses is expected to be limited" given concentrated nature of the city.

### 17.33.020 Permitted Uses (by-right in IND)

*(Ord. 509 § 4 (Att. C), 2021; Ord. 270 (part), 1984)*

Selected permitted uses relevant to EHS contexts:
- General manufacturing, processing and refining (indoors)
- Equipment repair facilities (indoors)
- Wholesale businesses (indoors)
- Equipment and material storage and/or rental (indoors)
- Mini-storage facilities (indoors)
- Package distribution and delivery facilities
- Service stations with or without indoor vehicle repair
- Car washes
- Processing agricultural products
- Nurseries and greenhouses
- Retail sales related to on-site permitted industrial use
- Recycling facilities < 500 sq ft including unattended collection containers
- Nonresidential rooftop solar (accessory)

Other permitted: one single-family dwelling per parcel; community garden; general farming/ranching (≥ 10 acres); indoor recreational/theater; public safety facilities; health care; day care; transitional/supportive housing; homeless shelter; funeral homes.

### 17.33.030 Conditional Uses — Require CUP (Chapter 17.78)

*(Ord. 509 § 4 (Att. C), 2021; Ord. 410 § 4(part), 2004; Ord. 370 § 3, 1999; Ord. 270 (part), 1984)*

**EHS-critical conditional uses:**
- **Hazardous materials distribution, storage, or processing** (§ 17.33.030(C)) — CUP required
- Outdoor sales and storage
- Commercial laundry or dry-cleaning plants
- Recycling facilities ≥ 500 sq ft
- Solid waste transfer station
- Vehicle/automotive repair services including outdoor vehicle storage
- Solar energy facilities/substations/farms
- Public utility facilities
- Telecommunications facilities
- Airports and heliports
- Outdoor recreational buildings/campgrounds/RV parks
- Firearms, weapons or ammunition sales; auction yards; kennels; flea markets
- Places of public assembly; schools; mortuaries/crematoria

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process — Chapter 17.78

- **Administering body:** Planning Commission (with appeal to City Council).
- **Filing:** Community & Economic Development Department, 200 Monte Verde St.
- **CUP Fees (2023-24 fee schedule, effective 09/01/2023):**

| CUP Type | Fee |
|---|---|
| Existing building, CEQA Exempt (valuation < $250,000) | $2,247 |
| New building, CEQA Exempt | $9,566 |
| New building, Subject to CEQA | $15,329 |
| Appeal to Planning Commission (of staff decision) | $692 |
| Appeal to City Council | $259 |
| Initial Study / Mitigated Negative Declaration deposit | $5,763 |

- CEQA-subject projects also incur CDFW filing fee (~$2,764 for MND; ~$3,839 for EIR) plus $50 county clerk processing fee.
