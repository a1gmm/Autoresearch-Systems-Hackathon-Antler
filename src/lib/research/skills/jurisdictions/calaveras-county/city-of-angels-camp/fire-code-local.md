## Chapter 8.52 — Fire Safety Regulations (Ord. 546 (Att. A), 2025)

This chapter is the city's primary local fire ordinance, enacted by Ord. 546 (2025). It has three articles:

### Article I — General Requirements (§§ 8.52.010–8.52.070)

- **Fire hydrants — residential:** Lines ≥ 6 inches diameter; hydrant spacing per California Fire Code; gate valves ≥ 6 inches between hydrant and main; minimum 2-hour uninterrupted flow per CFC minimum flow requirements.
- **Fire hydrants — commercial/manufacturing:** Lines ≥ 6 inches; main water mains in mercantile/manufacturing districts ≥ 8 inches; hydrants must have two 2.5-inch hose outlets plus one 4.5-inch steamer outlet (dry-barrel, breakaway design); 2-hour minimum flow with 20 psi residual.
- **Water mains:** Residential mains ≥ 6 inches; mercantile/manufacturing mains ≥ 8 inches; looped gridiron system required (no dead-ends unless city-engineer approved).
- **Fire sprinklers (§ 8.52.060):** Per NFPA 13; detector check valve required on each service.
- **Posting addresses (§ 8.52.070):** Per California Residential Code Title 24 Part 2.5 § R319.1 — characters ≥ 4 inches, stroke width ≥ 0.5 inch, Arabic numerals, contrasting with background.

### Article II — WUI and VHFHSZ New Construction Requirements (§§ 8.52.080–8.52.140)

- **Access gates (§ 8.52.080):** Minimum 14 feet clearance (residential), 26 feet (commercial); vertical clearance 15 feet; 30-foot setback from public ROW; Knox Box or key switch required for electronic gates; auxiliary backup power required.
- **Fire sprinklers (§ 8.52.090):**
  - Required in ALL newly constructed buildings not meeting CBC § 701A.3 exceptions.
  - Required in buildings where ≥ 10% floor area added within any 36-month period and building exceeds 3,000 sq ft.
  - Required where ≥ 50% floor area added or any "substantial remodel" within 36 months.
  - Required in all one- and two-family dwellings (per California Residential Code) regardless of square footage.
  - Required in mobile/manufactured homes with ≥ 2 dwelling units (per Title 25 CCR).
  - Change of occupancy to more hazardous use triggers sprinkler requirement citywide.
- **Defensible space zones:**
  - Zone 0 (0–5 ft): Ember-resistant zone — most stringent fuel reduction.
  - Zone 1 (5–30 ft, or to property line): Fuel break zone — remove all flammable vegetation.
  - Zone 2 (30–100 ft, or to property line): Reduced-fuel zone — remove brush/combustible growth; grass <18 inches may remain for erosion control.
- **Vegetation management applies to all parcels city-wide regardless of designated FHSZ.**

### Article III — Fire Prevention (§§ 8.52.150–8.52.190)

- **CFC adoption (§ 8.52.150):** "That edition of the Uniform Fire Code, sponsored by the International Conference of Building Officials and Western Fire Chiefs Association, and adopted by the State Fire Marshal, will also be adopted by the city on January 1st following the date adopted by the state." City council may amend by resolution. Copies on file with city clerk and fire chief.
- **VHFHSZ designation (§ 8.52.160):** Fire chief authorized to designate/decline VHFHSZ zones within 120 days of CAL FIRE recommendation. Building official enforces CBC Title 24 § 3203 in designated zones.
- **Appeals (§ 8.52.170):** Written appeal to city council within 30 days of fire chief decision; filed with city clerk.
- **New materials/processes (§ 8.52.180):** City council may convene committee to identify additional permit-required activities beyond those in the adopted CFC.
