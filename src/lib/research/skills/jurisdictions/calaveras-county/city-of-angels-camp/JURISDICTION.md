## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air — Calaveras County APCD (not city-controlled)

- **District:** Calaveras County Air Pollution Control District (APCD) — an independent special district governed by the Calaveras County Air Pollution Control Board.
- **Air Basin:** Mountain Counties Air Basin.
- **Programs:** Authority to Construct (ATC) permits; Permits to Operate; Carl Moyer Grant Program (mobile source upgrades); Woodsmoke Reduction Program; prescribed burn Smoke Management Plans.
- **Regulated sources include:** gasoline stations, rock quarries, foundries, auto paint booths, boilers, wood processing equipment, diesel generators >50 brake horsepower.
- **Contact:** 891 Mountain Ranch Road, Building E, San Andreas, CA 95249 | (209) 754-6601 | Burn Line: (209) 754-6600
- Source: https://ema.calaverasgov.us/Air-Pollution-Control
- Source: https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/calaveras-county-air-pollution-control-district

### CUPA (Unified Program) — County-administered

- **Not its own CUPA** — served by the **Calaveras County Environmental Health Department**, the countywide CUPA certified by CalEPA effective **June 1, 2002**.
- Six program elements consolidated: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP); CalARP; Underground Storage Tank (UST); Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA); Hazardous Waste Generator / Onsite Treatment (Tiered Permitting); HMMP/HMIS.
- City of Angels Camp is **NOT a Participating Agency** — all CUPA programs inside city limits go directly to the county agency.
- **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System) is the electronic reporting portal for all HMBP/UST/APSA submittals.
- **Contact:** Calaveras County Environmental Health Department, 891 Mountain Ranch Rd., San Andreas, CA 95249 | (209) 754-6399
- Source: https://ema.calaverasgov.us/Environmental-Health/CUPA-Hazardous-Materials
- Source: https://ema.calaverasgov.us/Portals/EMA/Documents/EH/CUPA/CUPA%20Consolidated%20Permit%20Application%20Package%202024.pdf

### Fire — City of Angels Camp Fire Department (own AHJ)

- **AHJ:** City of Angels Camp Fire Department — combination department (full-time/part-time/volunteer), two stations, ISO rating 4.
- **Adopted fire code:** 2025 California Fire Code, Title 24, Part 9 — adopted by reference via **ACMC § 15.04.020(J)** (Ord. 553, 2026); chapter 15.24 cross-references Article III of Chapter 8.52.
- **Local fire safety chapter:** ACMC Chapter 8.52 (Fire Safety Regulations), enacted by **Ord. 546 (Att. A), 2025** — contains three articles: General Requirements (fire hydrants, addresses), WUI/VHFHSZ Construction Requirements (sprinklers, access gates, defensible space), and Fire Prevention (CFC adoption, VHFHSZ designation authority, appeals, permit authority).
- **Permit fees:** Set by city council resolution after public hearing and fee study (ACMC § 15.04.050); the 2023-24 published fee schedule covers building/plan-check fees. Fire-specific permit fees not separately published online — [VERIFY current resolution].

### Wastewater — City-owned POTW (no formal IWP on file)

- **POTW:** City of Angels Camp owns and operates its own wastewater collection and treatment system — ~22.5 miles gravity sewer, ~4.5 miles force main, five lift stations, treatment plant at 3000 Centennial Rd. (209-736-2412).
- **Sewer use ordinance:** ACMC **Chapter 13.12** (Sewer System, Ord. 289, 1987) — Article IV governs use of public sewers: § 13.12.225 prohibits storm/industrial discharge to sanitary sewer; § 13.12.230 lists prohibited substances (see wastewater-local.md for detail); § 13.12.235 requires grease/oil/sand interceptors for garages and service stations, with quarterly inspections and fees by city council resolution.
- **No formal industrial pretreatment program (IWP)** found online — city is small (population ~3,900) and likely not a significant industrial discharger; [VERIFY with POTW whether EPA/RWQCB pretreatment program applies].
- **Stormwater (MS4):** City of Angels Camp does not appear on the Central Valley RWQCB Phase I or Phase II MS4 permitted list; stormwater regulation may flow through the State's Phase II Small MS4 General Permit or may be handled at county level — [VERIFY with Central Valley RWQCB].

### Zoning — Industrial District (IND), Title 17

- **Zoning code:** ACMC **Title 17** (Zoning), administered by City of Angels Camp Community & Economic Development.
- **Single industrial district:** **IND — Industrial District** (Chapter 17.33) — there is no separate M-1/M-2 split; light industrial that is compatible with neighbors goes to the **BAE (Business Attraction and Expansion) District** (Chapter 17.37).
- Hazardous materials distribution, storage, or processing requires a **Conditional Use Permit** in the IND zone (§ 17.33.030(C)).
- **CUP process:** Chapter 17.78; standard CUP fee (existing building, CEQA exempt) = $2,247; new building CEQA exempt = $9,566 (2023-24 fee schedule).
