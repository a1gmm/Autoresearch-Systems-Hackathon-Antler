## CUPA agency

**Shasta County Department of Resource Management — Environmental Health Division (EHD)**
is the single Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all of Shasta County, including the incorporated cities of Redding, Anderson, and Shasta Lake. No city in Shasta County operates its own CUPA.

## Unified programs administered

| Program | Statutory basis |
|---------|----------------|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) / HMRRP | H&SC §§ 25500–25545 |
| Hazardous Waste Generator & On-Site Treatment (HWGT) | H&SC §§ 25100–25250 |
| Underground Storage Tanks (UST) | H&SC §§ 25280–25299.8 |
| Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) / Spill Prevention | H&SC §§ 25270–25270.13 |
| California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) | H&SC §§ 25531–25543.3 |

## Permitting / CERS filing

Regulated businesses must file Hazardous Materials Business Plans and other required documents electronically through **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System) at https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/. The EHD reviews CERS submissions and issues Unified Program facility permits.

## GOTCHA — countywide single CUPA

There is no separate city-level CUPA for Redding, Anderson, or Shasta Lake. Industrial facilities inside any city limits in Shasta County must obtain their HMBP, UST, and CalARP approvals from Shasta County EHD, not from any city agency.
