# Fire Code — City of Anderson, CA

## Fire AHJ

The **Anderson Fire Protection District** (AFPD) is the fire authority having jurisdiction (AHJ) for the City of Anderson. It is an independent special district — NOT a city fire department and NOT Shasta County Fire — that predates the city's 1956 incorporation.

- Address: 1925 Howard Street, Anderson, CA 96007
- Phone: (530) 378-6699
- Website: https://andersonfire.org/ (city info page: https://www.ci.anderson.ca.us/community_links/anderson_fire_protection_district.php)
- Fire Chief: Steve Lowe (as of date sourced)

## Adopted Code

The City of Anderson municipal code Title 15 (Buildings and Construction) contains the building- and fire-code adoption framework. Chapter 15.02 is the Building Code chapter. Fire code adoption by the AFPD as an independent district is handled through the district's own ordinances, which are not published online.

**[VERIFY — specific CFC edition and adopting ordinance number for the Anderson Fire Protection District not found online. California statewide default is the 2022 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9); the 2025 CFC took effect 2026-01-01 — confirm which edition AFPD is currently enforcing.]**

California cities and fire districts are required to adopt the California Building Standards Code (Title 24) by reference; local amendments must be filed with the California Building Standards Commission.

## Notes

- The AFPD is a special district with its own governing board; it does not operate under the City Council's authority.
- For plan-check and fire-permit coordination, contact AFPD directly at (530) 378-6699.
- Building permits are issued by the City of Anderson Development Services & Building Department (not AFPD), 1887 Howard Street, 2nd Floor; (530) 378-6636.
