## Industrial Zoning Districts

Anderson has two industrial zoning districts:

| District | Name | Municipal Code Chapter |
|----------|------|----------------------|
| M-1 | Light Industrial | Chapter 17.24 |
| M-2 | Heavy Industrial | Chapter 17.26 |

### M-1 Light Industrial (Chapter 17.24)

Typical M-1 uses include light manufacturing, warehousing, wholesale trade, service industries, and compatible commercial uses. Yard and building height standards are set in § 17.24.050 and § 17.24.060.

CUP authority is referenced in the zoning code under Chapter 17.50 (use permits). The Planning Commission reviews rezoning requests and use permits.

## Full Zoning District Structure (Anderson, Title 17)

For context, the full district lineup is:
- R-E Rural Estate
- R-1 Low Density Residential
- R-2 Medium Density Residential
- R-3/4 High Density Residential
- AG Agriculture
- Mixed Use (form-based standards)
- C-1 General Commercial (Ch. 17.18)
- C-2 Highway Commercial (Ch. 17.20)
- C-3 Heavy Commercial (Ch. 17.22)
- **M-1 Light Industrial (Ch. 17.24)**
- **M-2 Heavy Industrial (Ch. 17.26)**
- P-SP Public/Semipublic (Ch. 17.28)
- NR Natural Resource (Ch. 17.30)
- P-D Planned Development (Chs. 17.32, 17.35)
