## Industrial Pretreatment Program

**No Industrial Pretreatment Program (IWP).** Order R5-2025-0011 explicitly states "Pretreatment Requirements — NOT APPLICABLE." The 2.0 MGD design capacity is below the 5 MGD federal threshold that triggers a mandatory pretreatment program, and the facility has not been separately required to establish one.

There is **no local IWP permit process** for industrial dischargers in Anderson at this time. Industrial dischargers must still comply with the general prohibitions in Municipal Code § 13.76.080 (no pass-through or interference to the POTW).

## Sewer-Use Ordinance

City of Anderson Municipal Code **Title 13 — Water, Sewer and Stormwater**, Chapters 13.76 through 13.88 govern sewer use and discharge.

### Key Local Discharge Limits (§ 13.76.090.F)

| Parameter | Local Limit |
|-----------|------------|
| Oil or grease (animal/vegetable origin) | 40 mg/L |
| Oil or grease (mineral/petroleum origin) | 70 mg/L |

### General Prohibition (§ 13.76.080.A)

"No user shall introduce or cause to be introduced into the Publicly Owned Treatment Works (POTW) any pollutant or wastewater which causes pass through or interference. These general prohibitions apply to all users of the POTW whether or not they are subject to categorical pretreatment standards or any other federal, state, or local pretreatment standards or requirements."

### Grease Interceptors (§ 13.76.090)

Grease, oil, and sand interceptors required when the City determines they are necessary for proper handling of liquid wastes containing excessive grease, flammable wastes, sand, or harmful ingredients. Not required for residential units.

### Lateral Maintenance (§ 13.76.140)

Property owner is responsible for ownership and maintenance of the service lateral and cleanouts, including the portion in the public right-of-way. City does not maintain sewer laterals.

### Enforcement (§ 13.88.060–13.88.070)

- Violations are an infraction; written notice required with reasonable correction period
- Public Works Director enforces Chapters 13.76–13.84
- City may seek legal/equitable relief in Shasta County court for unlawful discharge (§ 13.88.070.E)
- Entry and inspection authorized at all reasonable times (§ 13.88.070.B)
