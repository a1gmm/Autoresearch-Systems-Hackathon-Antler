# CUPA — City of Anderson, CA

## Controlling CUPA Agency

The City of Anderson is **NOT its own CUPA**. The countywide CUPA is:

**Shasta County Environmental Health Division (EHD)**
Department of Resource Management
1855 Placer Street, Suite 201, Redding, CA 96001
Phone: (530) 225-5787
Website: https://www.shastacounty.gov/environmental-health/page/hazardous-materials-program

CalEPA has designated the Shasta County EHD as the Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all of Shasta County, **including the incorporated cities of Redding, Anderson, and Shasta Lake**.

## Unified Program Elements Administered

The Shasta County CUPA administers the following programs within the City of Anderson:

- **Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP)** — required for facilities handling hazardous materials above reportable quantities; submitted/certified annually via CERS
- **Hazardous Waste Generator (HWG)** — registration and compliance inspections
- **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — permits for owners/operators of USTs containing hazardous materials; state law and county ordinance code require a permit from the local agency
- **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)**
- **Hazardous Waste On-Site Treatment (HWOST / Tiered Permitting)**
- **Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure (SPCC) — local oversight component**

All reporting (HMBP, UST, HWG) must be submitted via the **California Environmental Reporting System (CERS)**: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/

## Local Hazmat Ordinance

The county ordinance code establishes UST permitting requirements. The specific Shasta County ordinance chapter number was **not confirmed online** — [VERIFY — contact Shasta County EHD for the exact county code chapter governing hazmat/UST permitting].

The City of Anderson has no independent local hazmat ordinance; all Unified Program authority flows through the Shasta County CUPA.

## Notes

- No city-level CUPA or hazmat permit separate from the county exists for Anderson.
- Facilities in Anderson must register with the Shasta County EHD (not with the City) for all Unified Program elements.
- For enforcement questions within Anderson, the lead agency is the Shasta County EHD CUPA program.
