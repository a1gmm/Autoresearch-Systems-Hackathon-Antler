## Zoning Code

Industrial districts are regulated in **Title 17 (Zoning)** of the City of Shasta Lake Municipal Code.
- Full Title 17: https://library.municode.com/ca/shasta_lake/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT17ZO

## Industrial District Designations

The City's current zoning framework (and Interim Zoning Ordinance in effect as of 2023) includes the following industrial/manufacturing land use designations:

| Designation | Description |
|---|---|
| **I-L** | Light Industrial — General Plan Land Use / IZO Overlay District |
| **I** | Industrial (General) — General Plan Land Use / IZO Overlay District |
| **SGIP-PD** | Shasta Gateway Industrial Park — Planned Development; a distinct industrial park zone permitting significant industrial uses |

- Source: https://www.cityofshastalake.gov/DocumentCenter/View/3768/Codified-Interim-Zoning-Ordinance-IZO (2023 Interim Zoning Ordinance)

## Comprehensive Zoning Update (In Progress)

The City is conducting a long-overdue comprehensive update to Title 17 (required after adoption of the 2040 General Plan). Draft Division III (Base District Regulations) is under public review as of 2025–2026. Final adopted district names and standards may differ from the IZO once the update is complete.
- Source: https://www.cityofshastalake.gov/1265/Comprehensive-Zoning-Update

## 2040 General Plan Land Use

The 2040 General Plan is codified in Chapter 17.04 of the Municipal Code and establishes the General Plan land use designations (including Light Industrial and Industrial) that correspond to the zoning districts.
- Source: https://library.municode.com/ca/shasta_lake/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT17ZO_CH17.042040GEPL

## CUP (Conditional Use Permit) Process

[VERIFY] The Title 17 zoning code contains CUP process provisions. For industrial uses that require a CUP (e.g., heavy manufacturing, hazardous material storage, certain outdoor operations), applications are reviewed by the Shasta Lake Planning Commission.
- Planning Commission meeting schedule and application requirements: https://www.cityofshastalake.gov/478/Planning-Division
- Recent Planning Commission agendas (e.g., September 4, 2025) show active review of industrial and high fire hazard zoning topics: https://www.cityofshastalake.gov/ArchiveCenter/ViewFile/Item/1101
