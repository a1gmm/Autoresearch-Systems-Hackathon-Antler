## Industrial Pretreatment Program

[VERIFY] The City operates its own POTW and maintains Title 13.28 (Industrial Waste Pretreatment Standards) in its municipal code. Confirm with the Wastewater Division whether the City has an EPA-approved Pretreatment Program (required if the POTW receives significant industrial users) or operates under Shasta County's oversight.

Known provisions from cannabis-related utility guidance (city document on file):
- Cannabis cultivation, manufacturing, testing, and nursery facilities must install a sampling station accessible to City inspectors for industrial waste stream sampling (Muni Code Title 13, Ch. 13.28, Art. VII — Compliance Monitoring)
- Liquid solvents (e.g., hexane, alcohols) are prohibited from discharge to the sanitary sewer; if liquid solvents are used, the City requires a "Closed Loop System"
