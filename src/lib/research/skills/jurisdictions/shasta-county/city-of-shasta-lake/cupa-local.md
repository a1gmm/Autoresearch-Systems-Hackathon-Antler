## Controlling Authority

The City of Shasta Lake is **not its own CUPA**. Unified Program services are provided by:

**Shasta County Department of Resource Management — Environmental Health Division**
- Role: Countywide CUPA; serves all unincorporated areas and all incorporated cities in Shasta County, including the City of Shasta Lake
- Website: https://www.shastacounty.gov/environmental-health/page/hazardous-materials-program

## Programs Administered

As the county CUPA, Shasta County Environmental Health administers all six Unified Program elements on behalf of city businesses:

1. Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) — CalARP/CERS reporting
2. Hazardous Waste Generator Program (including tiered permitting)
3. Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program
4. Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) / Spill Prevention
5. Hazardous Materials Releases — Site Cleanup Program coordination
6. Risk Management Plan (RMP) Program (CalARP)

## Reporting Portal

All HMBP and related filings are submitted via the California Environmental Reporting System (CERS): https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/
