## California Fire Code Adoption

California adopts statewide the California Fire Code (CFC) as Title 19 / Part 9 of the California Code of Regulations. The **2022 CFC** (based on IFC 2021) was the operative edition statewide through 2025; the **2025 CFC** (based on IFC 2024) took effect January 1, 2026. ## Local FPD Ordinances

- **Ordinance No. 19-01** (Shasta Lake Fire Protection District, 2019): Establishes fire hazard and combustible vegetation abatement standards. Requires defensible space clearing in compliance with the State Board of Forestry and Fire Protection General Guidelines. For parcels < 10 acres, clearing extends no further than 30 ft from the nearest property line; for parcels ≥ 10 acres, clearing extends no further than 100 ft. The Fire Chief has authority to declare and require abatement of fire hazards.
  - Source: https://www.cityofshastalake.gov/DocumentCenter/View/2641/Ordinance-19-01-signed
