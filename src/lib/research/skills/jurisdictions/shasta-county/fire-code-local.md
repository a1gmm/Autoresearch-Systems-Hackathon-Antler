## Adopted code

Shasta County Code **Title 16 (Buildings and Construction)** adopts the California Building Standards Code (Title 24), which incorporates the California Fire Code (CFC, Part 9 of Title 24). The county posted notice that the **2025 California Building Standards Code** (based on the 2024 IBC/IFC cycle) took effect statewide **January 1, 2026**. ## Fire hazard / defensible space local chapters

In addition to the CFC adoption in Title 16, Shasta County has standalone fire-safety chapters:

- **Chapter 8.08 — Fire Hazard Regulations** (Title 8, Health and Safety): local fire-hazard abatement and weed/vegetation control.
- **Chapter 8.10 — Defensible Space for Fire Protection** (added by Ordinance No. SCC 2019-\_\_\_): requires defensible space of 100 feet from any improvement on all unincorporated parcels; applies throughout all unincorporated territory including SRA, LRA, and areas within fire protection districts. Enforcement responsibility shared between the County Fire Warden and local fire protection districts.
- **Ordinance No. SCC 2025-XX** (Board of Supervisors, 2025): updated defensible space / hazardous fuel reduction requirements, requiring full-parcel fuel reduction on parcels ≤2 acres and a 30-foot perimeter reduction or site plan on parcels 2–10 acres.

## AHJ for the unincorporated county

**Shasta County Fire Department / CAL FIRE Shasta-Trinity Unit** — combination department serving the unincorporated county (both State Responsibility Area and Local Responsibility Area). Fire Warden is the designated enforcement officer for Title 16 fire provisions and Chapter 8.10.
