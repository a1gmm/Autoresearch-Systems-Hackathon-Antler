## Controlling CUPA Authority

The City of Redding does **not** operate its own CUPA. Hazardous materials unified program compliance for Redding is administered by:

**Shasta County Department of Resource Management — Environmental Health Division (EHD)**
Designated by CalEPA as the **Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all of Shasta County**, including the incorporated cities of Redding, Anderson, and Shasta Lake.

- **Agency URL:** https://www.shastacounty.gov/environmental-health/page/hazardous-materials-program
- **Legacy URL:** https://www.co.shasta.ca.us/index/drm_index/eh_index/ehd_programs/cupa.aspx
- **CalEPA CUPA directory entry:** https://cersapps.calepa.ca.gov/Public/Directory/OpenDocument/66124?title=Shasta

## Unified Program Elements Administered

The Shasta County EHD CUPA administers the following elements countywide (including within Redding city limits):

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — Cal OES/CERS reporting; business plan, inventory, site map, emergency response plan
2. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — permitting, inspection, and enforcement under HSC Chapter 6.7
3. **Hazardous Waste Generator and On-Site Treatment** — generator tiering, accumulation time limits, tiered permitting (CA HSC Chapter 6.5)
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — spill prevention for above-ground tanks >1,320 gallons aggregate
5. **Accidental Release Prevention (ARP/CalARP)** — Risk Management Plans for extremely hazardous substances (Cal OES/EPA RMP)

## Notes for Facilities in Redding

- Facilities in Redding submit HMBPs through the California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) at https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/
- Inspections and permit fees are set by Shasta County EHD, not the City of Redding
- The City of Redding Building Division references the Shasta County CUPA when reviewing hazardous materials/gas checklists for building permits (see city building handouts)
