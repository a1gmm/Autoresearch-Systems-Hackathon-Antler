## POTW Identity

The City of Redding operates two Publicly Owned Treatment Works (POTWs), both discharging to the **Sacramento River**:

| Plant | NPDES Permit | Board Order | Avg Dry-Weather Flow | Permit Term |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Clear Creek WWTP** | CA0079731 | R5-2022-0040 | 6.0 MGD | Aug 1, 2022 – Jul 31, 2027 |
| **Stillwater WWTP** | CA0082589 | R5-2023-0017 | 2.5 MGD | Jun 1, 2023 – May 31, 2028 |

Permitting authority: **California Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (Region 5)**

Plant addresses: Clear Creek WWTP and Stillwater WWTP are both managed by the City of Redding Wastewater Utility. The Industrial Waste Division office is at 20055 Viking Way, Bldg #3, Redding, CA 96003.

## Industrial User Permit (IWP) Process

1. Complete and submit an Industrial Waste Survey form (three variants: general industrial, grease-producing facilities, interceptor systems) — available from the Industrial Waste Division
2. Division reviews discharge type/volume; classifies user (SIU / non-significant / categorical)
3. Significant and categorical users receive an Industrial User Permit with facility-specific discharge limits, monitoring schedule, and self-reporting requirements
4. Inspections and sampling conducted by city staff; annual report submitted to Central Valley RWQCB
