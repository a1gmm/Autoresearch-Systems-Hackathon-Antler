## Adopted Code Edition

The City of Redding adopted the **2022 California Fire Code (CFC), Title 24, Part 9** of the California Code of Regulations, together with the 2021 International Fire Code (IFC) published by the International Code Council, by reference in their entirety.

- **Adopting ordinance:** Ordinance No. 2657, adopted December 6, 2022
- **Redding Municipal Code chapter:** Chapter 9.20 — Fire Code (Title 9, Health and Safety)
- **AHJ:** City of Redding Fire Department, Community Risk Reduction Division; Fire Marshal reachable at (530) 225-4141; HQ at 777 Cypress Avenue, Redding, CA 96001

**[VERIFY]** The 2025 California Fire Code (based on IFC 2024) became effective statewide January 1, 2026. As of 2026-06-04 no Redding local adopting ordinance for the 2025 CFC has been confirmed in published search results. Confirm with the Fire Marshal whether a superseding ordinance is in effect.

## Appendices Adopted

The following CFC appendices were adopted with Ordinance 2657:
- Appendix Chapters 4, B, C, CC, D, E, F, H, I, J, K, L, M, N, O, and referenced standards

## Key Local Amendments / Code References

City handouts cross-reference both "2022 California Fire Code (CFC)" and "Redding Municipal Code (RMC)" for subdivision and access requirements. Notable locally-cited sections:

- **RMC 9.20.230** — Fire access road standards for subdivisions (30+ parcels require two ingress/egress routes; roads/driveways ≤12% grade; turning radius 30 ft inside/45 ft outside)
- **RMC 9.20.150** — Existing buildings (referenced in municode TOC)
- Fire hydrant spacing: 500-foot spacing, minimum 1,000 GPM fire flow plus maximum daily demand (CFC C102.1)
- Cul-de-sacs: max 750 ft length, 96-ft diameter (CFC D103.4)
- Driveways >150 ft require a turnaround per Fire Department specifications

## Community Risk Reduction Division

The Fire Department's Community Risk Reduction Division administers fire prevention through Education, Engineering, Enforcement, Economic incentives, and Emergency Response. Division handles plan review, inspections, and enforcement of CFC locally.
