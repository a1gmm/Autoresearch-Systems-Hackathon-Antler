## Industrial Districts — Chapter 18.34

**Chapter 18.34: Industrial Districts — "GI" General Industry and "HI" Heavy Industry**

PDF: https://files.cityofredding.gov/Document%20Center/Departments/Development%20Services/Planning/Zoning%20Ordinance/Chapter%2018.34%20Industrial%20Districts.pdf

### District Purposes

**GI (General Industry):** Areas for businesses with limited potential for significant adverse visual, noise, or other impacts to adjoining public and residential properties. Uses with substantial outdoor processing and/or storage are NOT allowed. Supporting retail/restaurant uses allowed subject to size limits and siting criteria.

**HI (Heavy Industry):** Areas for the broadest range of industrial uses, including those characterized by significant outdoor processing or storage. Supporting retail/restaurant uses allowed subject to size limits.

### Key Development Standards (Schedule 18.34.040-A)

| Standard | GI | HI |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum Lot Area | 20,000 sq ft | 1 acre |
| Minimum Lot Width | 100 ft | 100 ft |
| Maximum Building Height | 50 ft | 50 ft |
| Minimum Front Yard | 25 ft | 25 ft |
| Side/Rear Yard | No side yard required unless buffer applies | 25 ft |

### Permit Designation System (Schedule 18.34.020-A)

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| P | Permitted by right (subject to Section 18.34.030 new development requirements) |
| L | Permitted subject to specific limitations listed in chapter |
| Sd | Site Development Permit — Director approval |
| S | Site Development Permit — Board of Administrative Review (BAR) approval |
| U | Use Permit — Planning Commission approval |

**L14:** No outdoor storage, dismantling, or similar activities.
**L15:** Office sales allowed as accessory use not exceeding 10% of floor area.

### Permit Requirements for New Development (Section 18.34.030)

A Site Development Permit (BAR) is required when ANY of the following apply:
1. Proposed building or addition exceeds 10,000 sq ft (Director SDP) or 20,000 sq ft (BAR SDP)
2. Industrial/manufacturing use that may be objectionable by reason of odor, dust, noise, bright lights, vibration, project scale, traffic volumes, or involves storage/use of explosives or dangerous materials
3. Property has direct access to or frontage on an arterial street or frontage road
4. Property is not located on an improved public street developed for industrial traffic
5. Property abuts a residential district

## Use Permit (Conditional Use Permit) Process — Chapter 18.14

A **Use Permit** (equivalent to CUP) is required where the use schedule shows "U." The Planning Commission is the approving authority.

**Application:** Filed with Development Services Department; accompanied by required fee, site plans, elevations, topography, utility connections, stormwater detention, and adjacent street information. (Section 18.14.040)

**Public Hearing:** Planning Commission holds a public hearing; notice given per Chapter 18.11 (Common Procedures). (Section 18.14.050)

**Decision:** Planning Commission must act within 30 days of hearing conclusion; decision notice mailed to applicant within 10 calendar days of ratification. (Section 18.14.060)

**Required Findings (Section 18.14.070):** Planning Commission must find:
- Proposed use will not be detrimental to health, safety, or general welfare of persons in the neighborhood
- Proposed use will not be injurious to the general welfare of the city
- Proposed use is consistent with General Plan policies and goals and the zoning code
- Project design is consistent with City Council design guidelines (Section 18.40.050)

**Conditions:** Planning Commission may impose conditions including special yards, buffers, fences, walls, screening, building design, operational controls, and performance standards. (Section 18.14.080)

**Appeals:** Decision is effective after the appeal period; appeals go per the appeals chapter of the zoning code.
