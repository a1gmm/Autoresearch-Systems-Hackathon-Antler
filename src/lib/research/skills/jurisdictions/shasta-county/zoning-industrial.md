## Zoning authority

**Shasta County Department of Resource Management — Planning Division** administers zoning for the unincorporated county under **Title 17 (Zoning)** of the Shasta County Code.

## General zoning chapter structure (Title 17)

Title 17 uses chapter-per-district format. Nearby commercial districts include:
- Ch. 17.44 Community Commercial (C-2)
- Ch. 17.46 Office Commercial (C-O)
- Ch. 17.48 Highway Commercial (C-H)
- Ch. 17.50 Commercial Recreation (C-R)

Industrial districts (17.56 M-L, 17.58 M) sit after the commercial series. Administration and enforcement provisions are in Chapter 17.94.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) process

Certain industrial uses within M-L and M districts require a **Conditional Use Permit (CUP)** from the Planning Commission. Chapter 17.94 governs administration and enforcement; the CUP process is handled by the Planning Division. [VERIFY specific use lists requiring CUP in Ch. 17.56 and 17.58 — confirm permitted-by-right vs. CUP-required categories.]

Special uses, including certain industrial systems and operations, may be addressed under **Chapter 17.88 — Special Uses** (§ 17.88.335 references law enforcement/industrial systems).

## Planned Development overlay

Title 17 also provides for Planned Development zoning districts, which can apply to industrial campuses or mixed-use industrial projects in the unincorporated county.

## Zone map / verification

The Shasta County Zoning Plan (referenced as the "Shasta County Zoning Plan" in Title 17 and in the defensible space ordinance) is the official zoning map. Land use designations are set by the Shasta County General Plan; industrial general plan designations correspond to M-L and M zoning districts.
