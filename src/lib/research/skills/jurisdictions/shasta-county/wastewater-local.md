## Structure note — no county POTW

Shasta County (unincorporated) has **no county-operated municipal sewer system** serving industrial users. Wastewater service in the unincorporated county is predominantly on-site (septic / OWTS), governed by Shasta County Environmental Health under the county's **Local Agency Management Program (LAMP)** authorized by the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board. Industrial dischargers in unincorporated areas that tie into a city sewer system are regulated by that city's pretreatment program.

The dominant regional POTW serving the county's industrial base is operated by the **City of Redding**.

### Sewer Use Ordinance (SUO)

**Redding Municipal Code Title 14, Chapter 14.16 — Sewers** is the operative sewer use ordinance.
https://library.municode.com/ca/redding/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT14UT_CH14.16SE

The pretreatment program was established in 1983 under 40 CFR Part 403 (Clean Water Act General Pretreatment Regulations). The City is required to enforce pretreatment requirements under 40 CFR 403 and California Government Code § 54739.

### Industrial Waste permits (IWP process)

- Significant Industrial Users (SIUs) are issued individual industrial waste permits by the City's **Industrial Waste Division**.
- Categorical Industrial Users (CIUs): 4 facilities regulated under 40 CFR Part 433 (metal finishers) as of 2021.
- Total SIUs in 2021: 10 permitted.
- New facilities must submit a Baseline Monitoring Report (BMR) prior to permit issuance.
- Annual renewal required; inspection reports transmitted electronically via Cartegraph One software.
- Contact: Industrial Waste Division, (530) 224-6068.

### Local limits

Local limits (pollutant-specific discharge caps beyond federal categorical standards) are established by the City and updated periodically. The city conducted a local limits study using domestic sewer monitoring data collected 2020–2021; an updated local limits evaluation was planned for completion in 2025. ### Multi-jurisdictional agreement

The City of Redding holds a **multi-jurisdictional agreement with Shasta County** covering industrial users located outside city limits (e.g., Ameritech Industries, Inc.) that discharge to the city's collection system. Such users are regulated under Redding's pretreatment program.

### Representative SIUs (2021 annual report)

Ameritech Industries, SAF West, SECO Manufacturing, Wonder Metals, Fall River Brewery, Foothill Water Treatment Plant backwash, Big Valley Sanitation, Benton Landfill leachate, Solid Waste Transfer Station, USFS Air Attack Base.
