## Overview: Dual Wastewater Regimes

Butte County has no county-owned Publicly Owned Treatment Works (POTW). Wastewater management splits into two regimes:

1. **On-site wastewater (septic/OWTS)**: county environmental health regulates on-site systems for properties not served by a municipal sewer.
2. **Sewer-served areas (primarily Chico Urban Area)**: City of Chico Water Pollution Control Plant (WPCP) is the only large POTW in the county and operates an Industrial Pretreatment Program. Smaller incorporated cities (Oroville, Paradise, Gridley, Biggs) have their own sewer utilities — none has a published Industrial Pretreatment Program found in this search.

## On-Site Wastewater (County Environmental Health)

- **Regulatory authority**: Butte County Environmental Health Division — local permitting authority under California Water Code and State Water Resources Control Board Policy for On-Site Wastewater Treatment Systems.
- **County ordinance**: Chapter 19 — On-Site Wastewater Systems, Code of Ordinances. Specific section: §19-7 (On-site wastewater system requirements). Municode: https://library.municode.com/ca/butte_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CH19TEWASY
- **Local Agency Management Program (LAMP)**: in effect; governs OWTS siting, design, installation, operation and maintenance for unincorporated county.
- **Permit portal / forms**: https://www.buttecounty.net/838/Wastewater-Program
- **Contact**: Butte County Environmental Health, 25 County Center Drive, Oroville, CA 95965; (530) 552-3880

### Pretreatment Requirements — Key Known Facts

- **Grease interceptors**: food service establishments must install a minimum **750-gallon** grease interceptor.
- **Dental amalgam separators**: required for dental offices (grandfather date: June 14, 2027).
- **Wastewater Survey**: required for any new industrial or commercial business connecting to Chico sewer.
- **Wastewater Discharge Permit**: required for significant industrial users; maximum validity one (1) year; can take up to two weeks to prepare.
- **Temporary Discharge Permit**: for one-time or short-term groundwater/wastewater discharges to plant or designated maintenance hole. Maximum one-year duration. Wastewater analysis by state-certified lab required; results must comply with CMC 15R.40 local limits. Apply to: neil.traugh@chicoca.gov; (530) 894-4304. WPCP hours: M–F 7:00 a.m.–3:30 p.m.
- **Flow standard**: discharges compatible with sewer line capacity; surcharging prohibited.

## Notes for Research Subagent

- Industrial facilities in the unincorporated county connecting to a sewer district (if any outside Chico's service area) must check the applicable district's pretreatment rules independently.
- The Chico CIPP triggers federal pretreatment requirements because WPCP design capacity exceeds 5 MGD and receives industrial discharges that could interfere with the plant.
- Facilities outside Chico's sewer service area and not served by another sewer district must use on-site systems — contact Butte County EH for OWTS requirements.
- For questions about whether a specific parcel is in Chico's sewer service area, contact Chico Public Works or check the City's GIS parcel viewer.
