## Fire AHJ Structure

Butte County has **two distinct fire AHJs** depending on jurisdiction:

| Area | AHJ | Operated By |
|---|---|---|
| Unincorporated county + City of Oroville + Town of Paradise + Cities of Gridley & Biggs | Butte County Fire Marshal Bureau | CAL FIRE / Butte County cooperative (Schedule A agreement) |
| City of Chico | Chico Fire-Rescue | City of Chico (independent municipal department) |

**GOTCHA**: As of July 2022, the City of Oroville Fire Department is staffed and operated by CAL FIRE under a cooperative agreement; 17 Oroville firefighters transitioned to state employees. It is still branded "Oroville Fire Department" but is NOT an independent municipal department.

**GOTCHA**: Chico Fire-Rescue is the only fully independent municipal fire department in Butte County.

Source: https://www.buttecounty.net/472/Fire | https://krcrtv.com/news/local/oroville-fire-to-cal-fire-cooperative-agreement-transitions-17-firefighters-to-the-state

## Adopted Fire Code — Butte County (Unincorporated + CAL FIRE-served cities)

**Currently adopted**: **2025 California Fire Code** — confirmed by Butte County Department of Development Services Form DPC-05 "Building Design Criteria" (revised February 18, 2026). Also in effect simultaneously:
- 2025 California Building Code (CBC)
- 2025 California Residential Code (CRC)
- 2025 California Wildland-Urban Interface Code
- 2025 California Green Building Standards Code

**Note**: A prior version of Form DPC-05 (dated April 14, 2023) referenced the 2022 CBC — the 2025 editions replaced those statewide effective January 1, 2026. Always verify the current adopted edition on the live DDS page.

## County Code Chapters — Fire

**Chapter 38A — Fire Prevention and Protection** (Code of Ordinances)
- Covers: defensible space / firebreak requirements for real property where a fire hazard exists; fuel reduction requirements; administrative abatement procedures for non-compliant properties; noncompliance notices (§38A-19) and cost recovery.
- Referenced sections include §38A-8 (Firebreak requirements) and Article V (Abatement, Enforcement, Cost Accounting and Recovery).
- Enforces: Public Resources Code §4290 and §4291 (SRA requirements) plus California Fire Code provisions for fire protection planning.

## Permit Types and Fees

- **Burn permits**: residential dooryard burning requires a burn permit (effective May 1, 2026). Apply at: burnpermit.fire.ca.gov. Seasonal — requirement may be lifted in winter fire season.
- **Commercial/industrial fire permits**: regulated through the building permit process via the Development Services Department. Specific fire permit fees [VERIFY — check Master Fee Schedule at https://www.buttecounty.net/1433/Master-Fee-Schedule].

## Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI)

- Construction in WUI areas must comply with the **2025 California Wildland-Urban Interface Code** (Materials and Construction Methods for Exterior Wildfire Exposure).
- Approved building materials list: https://osfm.fire.ca.gov/what-we-do/fire-engineering-and-investigations/building-materials
- Defensible space (100 feet) enforced under Chapter 38A and PRC §4291.
