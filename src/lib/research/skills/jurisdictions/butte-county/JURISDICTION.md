## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air — Butte County Air Quality Management District (BCAQMD)

- **Controlling district**: **Butte County Air Quality Management District (BCAQMD)** — independent county AQMD; countywide jurisdiction including all incorporated cities (Chico, Oroville, Paradise, Gridley, Biggs). There is no separate City of Chico AQMD.
- **Authority to Construct + Permit to Operate**: required for stationary sources generating air contaminants "without regard to the amount of emissions" for listed source categories (engines ≥50 hp, boilers/heaters ≥1 MMBtu/hr, chemical processes, surface coating, asphalt batch plants, food/ag processing, metals, petroleum/solvent operations, and more). See Stationary Source Permit Categories page.
- **Portable Equipment Registration Program (PERP)**: for portable diesel engines >50 hp.
- **Agricultural Engine Registration Program**: for stationary and portable agricultural engines.
- **District Rules**: https://bcaqmd.org/rules-procedures/
- **Applications/fees**: https://www.bcaqmd.org/forms-applications — contact (530) 332-9400 for fee estimates
- Address: 629 Entler Avenue, Suite 15, Chico, CA 95928
- Source: https://www.bcaqmd.org/stationary-source-permit-categories | https://www.bcaqmd.org/business-permits-registration

### CUPA — Butte County Environmental Health (sole CUPA, countywide)

- **Single CUPA for entire county** including all incorporated cities (Chico, Oroville, Paradise, Gridley, Biggs). No city in Butte County operates its own CUPA.
- **Programs**: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) / HMRRP, Hazardous Waste Generator, Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), CalARP. Five inspectors + one manager; >1,200 facilities in inventory.
- **Reporting portal**: California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — cers.calepa.ca.gov
- **Contact**: 25 County Center Drive, Oroville, CA 95965 | (530) 552-3880 (M–F business hours) | ccers@buttecounty.net
- **Master Fee Schedule**: https://www.buttecounty.net/1433/Master-Fee-Schedule (effective Oct 1, 2025) — specific CUPA line items [VERIFY] by contacting EH Division
- CalEPA CERS regulator entry: https://cersapps.calepa.ca.gov/Public/Directory/RegulatorDetails/1008
- Source: https://www.buttecounty.net/816/Hazmat-CUPA | https://www.buttecounty.net/764/CUPA

### Fire — CAL FIRE / Butte County Fire Department + Chico Fire-Rescue

- **Unincorporated county, City of Oroville, Town of Paradise, Cities of Gridley and Biggs**: served by CAL FIRE / Butte County Fire Department under a Schedule A cooperative agreement. AHJ is Butte County Fire Marshal Bureau (two Fire Captains: Chris Boyd and Troy Hand). Address: 176 Nelson Avenue, Oroville, CA 95965. Phone: (530) 538-7111.
- **City of Chico**: independent Chico Fire-Rescue — only independent municipal fire department in the county. https://chicoca.gov/City-Services/Public-Safety/Fire-Department/index.html
- **Adopted fire codes (unincorporated county)**: **2025 California Fire Code** (confirmed by Butte County Development Services Form DPC-05, revised 2018-02-26; also adopts 2025 CBC, CRC, CEC, CMC, CPC, Green Building Code, Wildland-Urban Interface Code).
- **Fire Prevention ordinance**: Chapter 38A — Fire Prevention and Protection, Code of Ordinances. Covers defensible space / firebreak requirements, administrative abatement. See https://library.municode.com/ca/butte_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CH38AFIPRPR. Specific CFC local amendments in Chapter 38A [VERIFY full text — PDF at https://www.buttecounty.net/DocumentCenter/View/1739/CHAPTER-38A---Fire-Prevention-and-Protection-Ordinance-PDF could not be extracted from binary].
- **Wildland burn permits**: required for residential dooryard burning as of May 1, 2026 — apply at burnpermit.fire.ca.gov
- **Fire Marshal scope**: enforces California Fire Code and Public Resources Code §4290, §4291 (SRA) on use permits, minor use permits, commercial building permits, subdivisions, grading permits, and rezones countywide.
- **GOTCHA**: City of Oroville Fire is now staffed by CAL FIRE (as of July 2022, 17 firefighters transitioned to state employees under cooperative agreement) — still branded "Oroville Fire Department" but not an independent department.

### Wastewater — On-site (county) and POTW (City of Chico for Chico Urban Area)

- **Unincorporated county (on-site systems)**: Butte County Environmental Health administers on-site wastewater (OWTS/septic) under Chapter 19 — On-Site Wastewater Systems, Code of Ordinances. Local Agency Management Program (LAMP) in place. Permit portal: https://www.buttecounty.net/838/Wastewater-Program. No county-owned POTW for the unincorporated area.
- **City of Chico POTW (serves Chico Urban Area)**: City of Chico Water Pollution Control Plant (WPCP), 4827 Chico River Road, Chico CA 95928. Capacity 8.4 MGD (expandable to 15 MGD). NPDES Permit No. **CA0079081** (Order R5-2022-0033, issued by Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board). Discharges to Sacramento River.
- **Chico Industrial Pretreatment Program (CIPP)**: Facilities discharging industrial process wastewater to Chico sewer must comply with Chico Municipal Code (CMC) **Chapter 15.40** (Sewer Discharge Requirements) and **Chapter 15R.40** (local limits — specific pollutant concentrations). Contact: (530) 894-4304.
  - Significant Non-categorical IU: ≥25,000 GPD industrial process wastewater
  - Categorical IU: processes in 40 CFR Parts 405–471
  - Non-significant categorical (NSCIU): ≤100 GPD categorical process wastewater
  - Food service establishments: grease interceptor ≥750-gallon capacity required
  - Dental offices: amalgam separator required (grandfather date June 14, 2027)
  - Temporary/one-time discharges: Temporary Wastewater Discharge Permit (1-year max); apply via neil.traugh@chicoca.gov; (530) 894-4304
- Source: https://chicoca.gov/Departments/Public-Works/Operations--Maintenance/Water-Pollution-Control-Plant/ | https://www.buttecounty.net/838/Wastewater-Program

### Stormwater — Phase II MS4 (Butte County Public Works) + Industrial General Permit (State)

- **County MS4**: Butte County Public Works administers a Phase II Small MS4 stormwater program under State Water Resources Control Board Order **2013-0001-DWQ** (Phase II General Permit). Coverage applies to urbanized areas adjacent to Chico. Program focus: prevent and reduce pollutant discharges from the County's urbanized areas. Contact: Public Works.
- **Industrial Stormwater**: industrial facilities must obtain coverage under the State's Industrial General Permit (IGP) via SMARTS portal at smarts.waterboards.ca.gov — not a county-level permit. County MS4 permit and IGP are independent requirements.
- Source: https://www.buttecounty.net/1024/Stormwater-Program

## Notes / GOTCHAs

- **GOTCHA (fire AHJ split)**: For any parcel in Chico city limits, the fire AHJ is Chico Fire-Rescue, not CAL FIRE/Butte County Fire. For unincorporated areas and the cities of Oroville, Paradise, Gridley, and Biggs, the AHJ is Butte County Fire Marshal (CAL FIRE cooperative).
- **GOTCHA (no city CUPA)**: No city in Butte County has its own CUPA — Butte County Environmental Health is the sole countywide CUPA, even for Chico.
- **GOTCHA (wastewater split)**: The county has no POTW for the unincorporated area — on-site systems (septic/OWTS) dominate. Industrial wastewater to Chico's sewer system is regulated by Chico's CIPP (CMC 15.40 and 15R.40). Facilities outside Chico's sewer service area must use on-site systems or a regional district.
- **Code adoption**: Butte County Development Services confirmed 2025 California Building Standards editions in effect as of February 2026 (Form DPC-05 rev. 2018-02-26). Confirm current adopted edition on the live DDS website before citing.
