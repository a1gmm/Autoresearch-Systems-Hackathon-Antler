## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air

- **Controlling district: Northern Sacramento Valley Unified Air Pollution Control District (NSVUAPCD)**, headquartered in Chico, serves Butte, Glenn, Shasta, Tehama, and Trinity counties — https://www.nsaqmd.org/
- Chico has no independent local air rules; all Title V and minor permits, BACT determinations, and rule applicability flow through NSVUAPCD. Key contact: (530) 332-9400.
- Source: https://www.nsaqmd.org/

### CUPA (Unified Hazmat Program)

- **Not its own CUPA** — Chico is served by **Butte County Public Health, Environmental Health Division** (countywide CUPA, certified under H&S Code § 25404).
- CUPA administers six programs: Hazardous Materials Release Response Plan (HMRRP / Business Plan), Hazardous Waste Generator, Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), CalARP, and On-Site Hazardous Waste Treatment.
- Over 1,200 facilities in the county inventory; five inspectors plus manager.
- **Contact:** 25 County Center Drive, Oroville, CA 95965 | (530) 552-3880 (M–F) | bcenvhealth@buttecounty.net
- **Fee schedule:** Butte County Master Fee Schedule, Section 43-6 (CUPA Fees); effective October 2025. Key fee samples (see `cupa-local.md` for full table):
  - HMRRP / Hazmat Business Plan (1–5 materials): **$325/yr** base; volume tiers add $443–$682
  - Hazardous Waste Generator: CESQG $145, SQG $224, LQG $312
  - UST operational permit: first tank $1,189 + additional tank $342
  - CalARP Program 1 annual: $249; Program 2: $374; Program 3: $500
  - APSA: varies by volume tier; state surcharge $26
- Source: https://www.buttecounty.net/764/CUPA | https://www.buttecounty.net/816/Hazmat-CUPA | https://www.buttecounty.net/DocumentCenter/View/3968/CUPA-Fees-UST-HMRRP-Hazwaste-and-CalARP-PDF

### Fire

- **AHJ: City of Chico Fire Department (Chico Fire-Rescue)** — independent municipal fire department; issues all CFC permits and conducts fire prevention inspections within city limits.
- **Adopted code:** 2022 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9), adopted by **Ordinance No. 2577** (adopted 11-15-2022), codified in **Chico Municipal Code Title 16R, Chapter 16R.42** ("Fire Regulation Standards").
- Prior adoption: Ordinance No. 2491 (adopted 12-20-2016) adopted the 2016 CFC.
- The 2025 CFC (effective statewide 2026-01-01) adoption status in Chico: **[VERIFY]** — check with Building Division.
- Plan check and inspections for new construction are submitted to Chico Building Division (411 Main Street, 2nd Floor) and routed to the Fire Department; effective 7/1/2024 fee schedule applies.
- Re-inspection fee: **$124.50 per inspection**.
- Fire Prevention & Life Safety Division: (530) 896-7200

### Wastewater / Industrial Pretreatment

- **POTW: City of Chico Water Pollution Control Plant (WPCP)**, 4827 Chico River Road, Chico, CA 95928 | (530) 894-4300.
- Capacity: 8.4 MGD (expandable to 15 MGD); secondary treatment (activated sludge); discharge to Sacramento River (Discharge Point D-001) and underlying groundwater (D-002).
- **NPDES Permit:** Order R5-2022-0033, Permit No. CA0079081 (adopted 10 June 2022, effective 1 August 2022, expires 31 July 2027). Issued by Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board.
- **Industrial Pretreatment:** Chico Industrial Pretreatment Program (CIPP) — mandatory because WPCP capacity exceeds 5 MGD. Authorized under **Chico Municipal Code Title 15, Chapter 15.40** ("Sewer Discharge Requirements"), adopted March 2000.
- SIU threshold: ≥25,000 GPD of industrial process wastewater.
- Local discharge limits must be evaluated per 40 CFR § 403.5; specific local limits table: **[VERIFY with WPCP]** — contact (530) 894-4300.
- Food service establishments: grease interceptor required (minimum 750-gallon capacity).
- Dental offices: amalgam separator required by June 14, 2027 deadline for replacements.
- Source: https://chicoca.gov/Departments/Public-Works/Operations--Maintenance/Water-Pollution-Control-Plant/ | https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/centralvalley/board_decisions/adopted_orders/butte/r5-2022-0033_npdes.pdf

### Stormwater

- **MS4 permit:** Chico operates a small municipal separate storm sewer system (MS4) under the State Water Resources Control Board Phase II General Permit; co-permittees include Butte County, Chico Unified School District, and CSU Chico.
- Illicit discharge prohibition codified in **Chico Municipal Code Chapter 15.50**: non-storm water discharges to the city's storm drain system are prohibited except as specifically permitted (CMC § 15.50.040); discharges violating the NPDES permit are prohibited (§ 15.50.060).
- Storm Water Master Plan comprehensively updated in early 2025.
- Report illicit discharges: (530) 879-6951.
- Source: https://chicoca.gov/Departments/Public-Works/SewerStorm-Drain-Engineering/Storm-Water-Management/ | https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/chico/latest/chico_ca/0-0-0-11271

### Zoning (Industrial)

- **Governing code: Chico Municipal Code Title 19** ("Land Use and Development Regulations"), **Chapter 19.46** ("Manufacturing/Industrial Zones").
- Three industrial/manufacturing zone designations:
  - **IOMU** — Industrial Mixed Use
  - **ML** — Light Manufacturing/Industrial (lighter operations; hazardous material storage with use permit in some configurations)
  - **MG** — General Manufacturing/Industrial (heavier operations)
- Development standards in **Chapter 19.60**; CUP process through Planning Division.
- Contact: (530) 879-6800 | zoning@chicoca.gov
- Source: https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/chico/latest/chico_ca/0-0-0-15990 | https://chicoca.gov/Departments/Community-Development/Planning-Division/Commercial-Office-Industrial--Airport-Development/

## Notes

- Chico has its own fire department and building division but is under the **county-run CUPA** (Butte County Environmental Health) for all hazmat/hazwaste/UST/APSA/CalARP programs.
- Building authority: Community Development Department, Building Division — eTRAKiT portal for permits — https://chicoca.gov/Departments/Community-Development/Building-Division/
- Chico Municipal Code Title 16, Chapter 16.02 adopts the California Building Standards Code (Title 24); Title 16R is the codified building standards title — https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/chico/latest/chico_ca/0-0-0-11444
