## Industrial Zone Designations

Chico uses three manufacturing/industrial zone designations (not traditional M-1/M-2 nomenclature):

| Zone | Name | Character |
|------|------|-----------|
| **IOMU** | Industrial Mixed Use | Light industrial combined with compatible commercial/office uses; transition/infill areas |
| **ML** | Light Manufacturing / Industrial | Lighter manufacturing operations; non-volatile processes; smaller premises |
| **MG** | General Manufacturing / Industrial | Heavier manufacturing; wider range of industrial uses permitted |

Under § 19.46.020, Table 4-8 governs permitted vs. use-permit-required uses. Confirmed provisions:

| Use | ML | MG | IOMU |
|-----|----|----|------|
| Manufacturing — non-volatile techniques, premises <5,000 sq ft | Permitted | Permitted | [VERIFY] |

## EHS-Relevant Zoning Notes

- **Hazardous material storage** in industrial zones: activities involving volatile manufacturing techniques require a Use Permit in the ML zone; confirm whether HMRRP-threshold quantities trigger additional CUP conditions. Contact Planning Division.
- The zoning code references other applicable code sections that may impose additional requirements per use type.
- The 2024 Code Amendment (Ordinance No. [unnumbered in extracted text], Code Amendment 24-01, "Residential Readiness") amended Title 19 definitions and streamlined permitting processes but did not restructure the industrial zone designations.

## CUP (Conditional Use Permit) Process

- Decisions on Use Permits are made by the **Zoning Administrator** or **Planning Commission** depending on project scale; appeals go to Planning Commission then City Council.
- Section 19.06.030 permits the City Council to approve, modify, or deny code amendments.
- Contact: **Community Development Department, Planning Division**
