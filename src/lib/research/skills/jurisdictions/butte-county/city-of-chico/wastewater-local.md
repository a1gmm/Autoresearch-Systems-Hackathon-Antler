## Industrial Pretreatment Program (CIPP)

**Program name:** Chico Industrial Pretreatment Program (CIPP)

**Program approval:** 1999 (EPA Region 9 approved)

**Industrial user categories (per CMC § 15.40 and 40 CFR Part 403):**

| Category | Definition |
|----------|-----------|
| Significant Industrial User (SIU) | ≥25,000 GPD industrial process wastewater, OR categorical process subject to national pretreatment standards |
| Categorical Industrial User (CIU) | Processes regulated under 40 CFR Chapter I categorical standards |
| Non-Significant Categorical Industrial User (NSCIU) | ≤100 GPD categorical process wastewater |
| Non-significant, non-categorical IU | <25,000 GPD; may qualify for Pollution Prevention Program |

**Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit (IWP) requirements per CMC § 15.40.200:**
- Effluent limits based on applicable pretreatment standards or requirements
- Limits on average and maximum rate/time of discharge, or flow regulation/equalization requirements
- Installation and maintenance of pretreatment technology, pollution control or containment devices, and inspection/sampling facilities
- Self-monitoring, sampling, reporting, notification, and recordkeeping (pollutant ID, sampling location, frequency, sample type)
