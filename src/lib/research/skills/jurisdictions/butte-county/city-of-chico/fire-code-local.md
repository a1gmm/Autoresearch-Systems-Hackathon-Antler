## AHJ

**City of Chico Fire Department (Chico Fire-Rescue)** is the Authority Having Jurisdiction for fire code enforcement within city limits. The Fire Department issues all CFC permits, conducts plan reviews, and performs fire prevention inspections.

- Fire Prevention & Life Safety Division: **(530) 896-7200**
- Online permits: **eTRAKiT** portal (routed through Building Division)
- Source: https://chicoca.gov/City-Services/Public-Safety/Fire-Department/Fire-Prevention--Life-Safety/index.html

## Permit & Plan Check Process

Plans are accepted by the **Chico Building Division, 411 Main Street, 2nd Floor, Chico, CA 95928**, then routed to the Fire Department for review. Electronic plans (flash drive or CD) are preferred; two physical sets required when submitting paper (one for Fire, one for job site).

Required at permit application:
- Site location / address
- CA State Contractor License Number
- Workers' Compensation Carrier and Policy Number
- Agent Authorization (if applicant is not the license holder)
- Current City of Chico Business License

## Local Amendments (Chapter 16R.42)

Chapter 16R.42 includes Chico-specific local amendments to the 2022 CFC. Confirmed local provisions include:

- **§ 16R.42.010** — Adoption of fire regulation standards (the CFC adoption section)
- **§ 16R.42.070** — Prohibition on fires and flames; includes local exemptions
- **§ 16R.42.120** — Fire and life safety inspections (CFC permit occupancies + other occupancy classes such as schools and apartments)
- **Appeals**: Decisions on fire regulation standards are appealed pursuant to **CMC Chapter 16.42** of Title 16
- **Enforcement/prosecution**: violations enforced under **CMC Chapter 16.52** of Title 16
