## POTW: Sewerage Commission – Oroville Region (SC-OR / SCORCA)

Industrial and significant users discharging to the Oroville sewer system must obtain an **Industrial User Permit from SC-OR**, not from the city.

**SC-OR (SCORCA) — Joint Powers Agency**
- Formed: 1973
- Members: City of Oroville + Lake Oroville Area Public Utility District (LOAPUD) + Thermalito Water and Sewer District (TWSD)
- Governance: 6-member Board of Commissioners (2 per member agency)
- WWTF Address: 2880 S. 5th Ave, Oroville, CA 95965
- Phone: (530) 534-0353
- Website: https://www.scorca.gov
- Service area map: https://www.scorca.gov/sc-or-service-area-map

Treatment plant is currently undergoing an upgrade project (construction active as of 2026). Existing plant capacity accommodates over 2,300 new residential equivalents; 500 kW solar system reduces electricity cost ~40%.

## Collection System (City-Owned)

The **City of Oroville** owns and maintains the sanitary sewer collection system within city limits:
- ~66 miles of gravity mains
- ~1,200 manholes
- ~2.1 miles of force main
- 7 lift stations
- 2 flow meters

Two other districts serve adjacent portions of the greater Oroville area (TWSD and LOAPUD). Check the utility districts map at https://www.orovilleca.gov/314/Sanitary-Sewers to confirm service area.

**City Public Works contact:**
- 1735 Montgomery Street, Oroville, CA 95965
- Phone: (530) 538-2401
- Email: publicworks@cityoforoville.org
- Hours: M–Th 8 AM–5 PM; F 8 AM–12 PM

## Industrial Pretreatment Program

SC-OR administers an **Industrial Pretreatment Program** mandated by State and Federal statute (40 CFR Part 403; California Water Code).

Industrial user permit information: https://www.scorca.gov/industrial-user-permits

[VERIFY] the following from SC-OR directly or via their website:
- Sewer Use Ordinance / Pretreatment Ordinance number and adoption date
- Categorical pretreatment standards applicable (SIC codes served)
- Local limits table (prohibited substances and concentration/mass limits)
- NPDES permit number for the WWTF (issued by Central Valley RWQCB, Region 5)
- Significant Industrial User (SIU) definition and threshold
- Industrial User Permit application fee and annual fee
- Reporting requirements (baseline reports, periodic compliance reports, slug discharge notification)

Contact SC-OR at (530) 534-0353 to request the current pretreatment ordinance and local limits table.

Oroville Municipal Code contains sewer and stormwater discharge regulations. The city's eCode360 page lists:

- Illicit discharge to storm drains is prohibited for any person subject to an industrial/construction NPDES permit; prohibited materials include pollutants causing or contributing to violations of applicable water quality standards.
