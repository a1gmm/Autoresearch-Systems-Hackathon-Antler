Full code: https://ecode360.com/OR4980 (Title 17 at https://ecode360.com/44177912 and sub-chapters)

## Industrial District Names

The City of Oroville has three industrial-type zoning designations:

| District | Name | Key Character |
|---|---|---|
| **M-1** | Limited Industrial | Light/limited industrial uses; lower-impact manufacturing, warehouse, light assembly |
| **M-2** | Intensive Industrial | Heavy/intensive industrial uses; higher-impact manufacturing, major industrial operations |
| **ABP** | Airport Business Park | Airport-related industrial and commercial uses (adjacent to Oroville Municipal Airport) |

## Governing Chapter

**Chapter 17.36** — Industrial Districts
Contains development standards and use regulations for M-1, M-2, and ABP.

Section **17.36.020** — Development Standards for Industrial Districts (setbacks, height, lot coverage, landscaping, parking). ## Permitted vs Conditional Uses

Allowed land uses are specified in the use table within Chapter 17.36 (or a cross-referenced table in Chapter 17.32 / general use tables). Uses are classified as:
- **P** (Permitted by right) — subject to development standards only
- **CUP** (Conditional Use Permit required) — discretionary approval by Planning Commission
- **Not listed** — not permitted

[VERIFY] the specific use table entries for M-1 and M-2 by accessing the full code. Typical Light Industrial (M-1) permitted uses include warehousing, light manufacturing, auto repair, contractor yards, wholesale distribution. Typical Intensive Industrial (M-2) permitted uses include heavy manufacturing, recycling/salvage, concrete/asphalt plants, truck terminals.
