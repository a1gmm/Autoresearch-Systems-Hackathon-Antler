## CUPA Agency

**Butte County Public Health Department, Environmental Health Division**
City of Oroville is NOT its own CUPA. The countywide CUPA covers all of Butte County, including Oroville city limits.

- **Address:** 202 Mira Loma Drive, Oroville, CA 95965 (returned to this location February 23, 2026; previously at 25 County Center Drive)
- **Phone:** (530) 552-3880 (M–F, business hours); for emergencies call local fire department
- **Fax:** (530) 538-2164

## Programs Administered

| Program | Notes |
|---|---|
| Underground Storage Tanks (UST) | Installation, operational, closure, transfer permits |
| Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) | Conditionally exempt through non-qualified categories |
| Hazardous Waste | Generator (CESQG/SQG/LQG) and treatment tiers |

## Local Ordinance / Code References

[VERIFY] Butte County Code Chapter 43 (Environmental Health) contains the CUPA fee authority (Section 43-6). No city-specific CUPA ordinance — Oroville defers to county as the CUPA.

## Inspection Process

The CUPA inspects businesses that handle/store hazardous materials, generate/treat hazardous waste, own/operate USTs, store petroleum in ASTs above state thresholds, or store federally regulated hazardous materials above state thresholds. Compliance achieved through education, outreach, inspections, and enforcement (H&S Code Title 6.5).
