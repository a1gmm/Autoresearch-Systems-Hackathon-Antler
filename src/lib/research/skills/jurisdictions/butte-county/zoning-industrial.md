## Industrial Zone Structure (Chapter 24 Article II Division 5)

The three industrial zones are governed by three sections:

- **§24-25 — Purpose of the industrial zones**: sets out the policy intent for LI, GI, and HI.
- **§24-26 — Land use regulations for industrial zones**: the use regulation table — which uses are permitted by right (P), allowed by conditional use permit (CUP), or prohibited (–) in each zone.
- **§24-27 — Development standards for industrial zones**: setbacks, height limits, lot coverage, landscaping, and other dimensional standards for LI, GI, and HI parcels.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

CUPs in industrial zones are administered by the Butte County Development Services Department under Chapter 24, Article V (Land Use and Development Approval Procedures). General process:

1. Pre-application meeting with DDS Permit Center Planner recommended.
2. Application submitted to DDS; includes project description, site plan, environmental review documents.
3. Public hearing before Zoning Administrator or Planning Commission depending on scope.
4. Conditions of approval attached to CUP.
5. Building permits may follow after CUP grant.

## Notes for Research Subagent

- Butte County does NOT use M-1/M-2/M-3 zone designations. Always use LI/GI/HI when referencing industrial zones.
- Each incorporated city in Butte County (Chico, Oroville, Paradise, Gridley, Biggs) has its own separate zoning ordinance and zoning map — this file covers only the unincorporated county.
- EncodeOPlus is the official interactive version; Municode is a secondary mirror. Both were blocked from direct API fetch — access the industrial zone tables at EncodeOPlus secid=39 or call DDS.
- For air-quality sensitive or hazardous material uses in industrial zones, also check BCAQMD permit requirements and CUPA program thresholds — separate from zoning approval.
