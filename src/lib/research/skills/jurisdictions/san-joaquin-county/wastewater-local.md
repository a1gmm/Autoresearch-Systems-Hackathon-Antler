## Jurisdiction Structure

San Joaquin County is largely unincorporated rural/suburban land without a single county POTW. Industrial users connect to municipal POTWs operated by individual cities. The primary POTWs serving industrial areas are:

- **City of Stockton** — largest POTW in the county, covers the Stockton urban core
- **City of Manteca** — serves Manteca industrial areas
- **City of Tracy** — serves Tracy industrial corridor (South San Joaquin County)
- **City of Lodi** — serves Lodi area
- **City of Lathrop** — smaller POTW for Lathrop industrial areas

Unincorporated industrial parcels without public sewer typically use on-site treatment (septic/package treatment plant), regulated by the county Environmental Health Department's Liquid Waste Program under state law and local standards.

## Unincorporated County — Liquid Waste / On-Site Wastewater

**Regulator:** San Joaquin County Environmental Health Department — Liquid Waste Program
- Governs: design, construction, repair, and inspection of on-site wastewater treatment systems, wastewater holding tanks, package treatment plants, and septage pumper trucks.
- Standards: San Joaquin County Onsite Wastewater Treatment Systems Standards (published by EHD).
- Septic system permits via: https://permits.sjgov.org/Departments/Environmental-Health/Liquid-Waste-Program/Septic-System
- Program Coordinator: Rodney Estrada, (209) 468-0331

## City of Stockton — Industrial Pretreatment Program

**POTW:** City of Stockton Wastewater System (NPDES permitted)
**Governing Code:** Stockton Municipal Code, **Title 13 — Public Services, Chapter 13.08** (Sewer Use and Pretreatment)

Key provisions:
- General discharge prohibition at § 13.08.100 — prohibits introduction of pollutants that pass through or interfere with the POTW.
- Categorical pretreatment standards (federal) apply to applicable industrial categories.
- **Local limits** apply in addition to categorical limits (specific numerical values not published on the public-facing web page — [VERIFY] obtain current local limits table from the Environmental Compliance Division).
- **Significant Industrial Users (SIUs)** must obtain a **Wastewater Discharge Permit** (Parts A & F, or Parts A & B for special applications).

**SIU Permit Requirements:**
1. Submit completed permit application to the Environmental Compliance Division.
2. Install a monitoring maintenance hole accessible to city inspectors.
3. Install wastewater monitoring equipment: flow meter, pH recorder, automatic sampler.
4. Install pretreatment equipment as determined by specific discharge characteristics.
5. Perform self-monitoring and submit periodic compliance reports.
6. Allow unannounced site inspections.

**Contact:** City of Stockton Municipal Utilities — Environmental Compliance Division
Web: https://www.stocktonca.gov/services/water,_sewer___stormwater/sewer/industrial_pretreatment.php

## Notes

- **GOTCHA:** For industrial sites in unincorporated San Joaquin County, the applicable sewer authority depends on which municipal service area the parcel is within. Confirm with the specific city utility or San Joaquin County Public Works / Special Districts if the parcel is within a County Service Area (CSA).
- Council Resolution 02-0671 (Stockton) addresses procedures for transfer of excess capacity.
- Regional Water Board (Central Valley, Region 5) retains oversight of NPDES permits and pretreatment compliance for all POTWs in the county.
- [VERIFY] Manteca, Tracy, Lodi, and Lathrop each have their own sewer use ordinances — obtain from their respective public works / utilities departments if the facility is located in those cities.
