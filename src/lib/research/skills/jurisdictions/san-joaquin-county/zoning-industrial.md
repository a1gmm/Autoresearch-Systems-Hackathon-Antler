## Governing Code

San Joaquin County **Development Title (Title 9)** — **Division 5: Industrial Zones** (Ordinance 3675 and subsequent amendments through at least Ord. 4508, 2018).

Municode mirror: https://library.municode.com/ca/san_joaquin_county/codes/development_title

## Industrial Zone Designations (Chapter 9-500)

| Zone | Name | Intent |
|------|------|--------|
| **I-W** | Warehouse | Warehouses and service establishments; primary feature is independence from public sewer (septic); no industrial waste production. Implements Limited Industrial General Plan category. |
| **I-P** | Industrial Park | Corporate offices, warehousing, light manufacturing, wholesaling, professional offices. Development enforced via CC&Rs to allow proximity to residential zones with minimal environmental conflict. Implements Limited Industrial General Plan category. |
| **I-L** | Limited Industrial | Light-impact manufacturing, warehousing, wholesaling, construction contracting, distribution. External effects restricted to immediate area; uses conducted entirely within enclosed buildings; outdoor storage screened. Business/professional offices also permitted. Implements Limited Industrial General Plan category. |
| **I-G** | General Industrial | Wide range of manufacturing, distribution, and storage; moderate to high nuisance characteristics (noise, heat, glare, odor, vibration); may require extensive outside storage. Implements General Industrial General Plan category. |
| **I-T** | Truck Terminal | Truck terminals sited outside urban communities near freeway interchanges. Implements Trucking Terminal General Plan category. |

## Use Regulations — Chapter 9-505

- **Permitted, not permitted, and conditionally permitted** use types are set out in **Table 9-505.2** (Uses in Industrial Zones).
- Use types are defined in Chapter 9-115.
- Temporary uses: Table 9-505.4.

### Key Special Use Regulations (§ 9-505.5)

A **Site Approval (§ 9-818)** is required for any industrial use that is:
- 6,000 sq ft or greater in ground floor area; **or**
- Occupies 10 or more acres of site area; **or**
- Not served by a public wastewater treatment plant, public water system, and a public drainage system.

### Truck Terminal Zone (§ 9-505.6)

- Nighttime operations require 25-ft light standards hooded and directed downward.
- Additional regulations at §§ 9-505.6 and 9-505.7.

### Truck Fueling Stations — LNG (§ 9-505.5(e))

- Truck Sales and Services — LNG Fueling Stations permitted only within ½ mile of a highway or freeway interchange, subject to an approved **Site Approval**.

### Expansion of Existing Uses (§ 9-505.3)

Minor expansions (structures: <25% floor area increase; site: <10% site area increase) may proceed via **Improvement Plan** rather than full discretionary review, subject to Director findings of no substantial adverse effect.

### Transition to Commercial Uses

Commercial zone uses (except C-N and C-R) may be allowed in I-L and I-G zones in designated "transition areas" abutting commercial zones, per an adopted Special Purpose Plan (§ 9-505.5(c)).

## Conditional Use Permit / Discretionary Review Process

- **Improvement Plan** (Chapter 9-884): Minor expansions, temporary uses, fireworks displays.
- **Site Approval** (§ 9-818): Required for larger industrial uses (see thresholds above).
- **Use Permit**: Required for conditionally permitted uses per Table 9-505.2; change from an existing use to a new use requiring a Use Permit may be waived if new use is less impactful (§ 9-505.5(b)).

## Fireworks Displays in Industrial Zones

Improvement Plan required, submitted minimum 2 weeks before event, with approved Operational Fire Permit. Adjacent property owners and applicable Municipal Advisory Council notified minimum 1 week prior (§ 9-505.5(d)).
