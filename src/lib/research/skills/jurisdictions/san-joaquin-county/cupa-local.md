## CUPA Agency

**San Joaquin County Environmental Health Department (EHD)** is the sole, countywide Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA). State-approved since January 1997. Covers ALL jurisdictions in San Joaquin County — incorporated cities and unincorporated areas alike. No city in the county operates its own CUPA or has a delegated Participating Agency (PA) role.

Office: **1868 E. Hazelton Ave., Stockton, CA 95205**

## Programs Administered

The county EHD administers all six Unified Program elements:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — including storage, handling, and emergency response planning
2. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — risk management plans for regulated substances
3. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — spill prevention and countermeasure plans for aboveground petroleum storage
4. **Hazardous Waste Generator Program** — oversight of generators of hazardous waste
5. **Hazardous Waste Onsite Treatment — Tiered Permitting** — permit-by-rule through full permit tiers
6. **Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program** — installation, operation, monitoring, closure

## Submissions and Reporting

- **HMBP, CalARP, APSA, and Hazardous Waste Generator** reports are submitted via the California Environmental Reporting System (**CERS**): https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/
- **State UPCF consolidated forms** available at: http://www.unidocs.org/hazmat/upcf/index.html
- **UST testing appointments:** email ust@sjgov.org or fax (209) 468-3433; minimum 48-hour advance notice required.
- **Service request inspections:** contact your area EHD inspector by phone or email; minimum 48-hour notice required.
