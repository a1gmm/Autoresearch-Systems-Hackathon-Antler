## Local Amendments (Confirmed from Ordinance Text)

The following local amendments are confirmed from the 2016/2022 ordinance text (same provisions carried forward to 2025 cycle per search index; verify against current Ch. 15.12):

### Additional Operational Permits Required (SMC §15.12.020.B)

In addition to CFC §105.6 permit requirements, the following activities require a Stockton Fire Prevention Bureau operational permit:

| Permit trigger | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Medical Gas Systems | Any commercial building with a medical gas system |
| Christmas Tree Lot | Any seasonal outdoor Christmas tree lot |
| Multi-Family Dwelling | 3 units or more |
| Hood & Duct | Any hood & duct fire extinguishing system |
| Miscellaneous Combustible Storage | > 2,500 cubic feet gross volume of empty combustible packing cases, boxes, barrels, rubber, cork, or similar material |
| Rubber Tire Storage | > 1,000 cubic feet indoors or outdoors |
| Pallet Storage | > 30,000 cubic feet of pallets, palletized packing boxes, or bin boxes |
| Monitored Fire Sprinkled Buildings | Buildings with an automatic fire sprinkler system and no other operational fire permit |
| Battery Systems | Stationary battery storage capacity > 100 gallons |

### Bundled Permits (SMC §15.12.020.C)

Multiple operational permits may be combined for these facility types:
- Auto Wrecking Yard / Junk Yard
- Auto Body Shop
- Repair Garage / Service Garage
- Food Service (B Occupancy)
- Place of Assembly (A Occupancy)
- Self-Service Motor Fuel Dispensing Facility with Convenience Store

### Automatic Fire Extinguishing Systems (SMC §15.12.040.A) — Key Local Amendment

> **Stockton local sprinkler requirement:** An automatic sprinkler system shall be installed in **all new construction, regardless of occupancy classification, where the total floor area is 6,000 square feet or more.**
> Exception: Mini-storage facilities of any size shall use NFPA 13 systems. A-2/H occupancies are exempt from this provision and shall comply with the California Building and Fire Codes.

This is MORE stringent than the state CFC baseline (which has higher thresholds by occupancy type).

### Sprinkler System Standards (SMC §15.12.040)

- **Basements:** Fire protection system required in basements regardless of square footage if used for any purpose other than building services (utilities, HVAC, etc.).
- **NFPA 13R systems:** Where CFC allows NFPA 13R, Stockton requires **NFPA 13** instead.
- **NFPA 13D (one- and two-family dwellings, R-3, townhouses):** Permitted; several local conditions apply:
  - Residential additions > 50% of existing structure area require NFPA 13D throughout.
  - Detached mother-in-law quarters / guest houses require sprinklers if constructed with a kitchen.
  - Attic spaces in R-3 occupancies require pilot heads per NFPA 13D.
  - Passive purge connection required in lieu of backflow prevention device.

### Address Identification (SMC §15.12.030)

- Residential: address characters minimum 4 inches high.
- Commercial / Industrial: minimum **12 inches high on the building** and 6 inches high on the suite — more specific than state CFC default.

### False Alarms (SMC §15.12.020.E)

- Third and subsequent false alarm responses in a calendar year: Fire Department bills the responsible party for the cost of response.

### Violation Penalties (SMC §15.12.020.D)

- Misdemeanor; fine up to $500 or imprisonment up to 6 months, or both; each day a continuing violation is a separate offense (excluding weekends and holidays).
