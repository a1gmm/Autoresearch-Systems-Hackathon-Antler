## POTW Background

The Stockton RWCF occupies ~690 acres adjacent to the San Joaquin River; originally constructed 1922. It holds an NPDES permit (most recent cycle confirmed 2014; renewal status [VERIFY] with Regional Water Quality Control Board — Central Valley Region). Because RWCF design flow exceeds 5 mgd and receives industrial pollutants, federal law (40 CFR 403) requires Stockton to operate an approved pretreatment program.

## Significant Industrial User (SIU) Definition

Per SMC Ch. 13.08 (consistent with 40 CFR 403 federal baseline):
- Discharges ≥ 25,000 gallons per day of process wastewater (excluding sanitary, non-contact cooling, boiler blowdown); OR
- Contributes process wastewater comprising ≥ 5% of the dry-weather average hydraulic or organic capacity of the RWCF; OR
- Designated as SIU by the Director/Control Authority based on potential for pass-through or interference.

## Application and Compliance Process

1. Contact Environmental Control Division (209) 937-8740 to determine permit tier and applicable categorical pretreatment standards.
2. Download and complete Wastewater Discharge Permit application (Parts A & F, or A & B for special cases) — forms available at https://www.stocktonca.gov/services/water,_sewer___stormwater/sewer/industrial_pretreatment.php
3. **Infrastructure installation required:**
   - Install a monitoring maintenance hole for Environmental Compliance Officer access.
   - Install wastewater monitoring equipment: flow meter, pH recorder, automated sampler.
   - Install pretreatment equipment as required by federal categorical or local limits (solids removal, oil/grease separation, metals treatment, pH adjustment).
4. Submit application to Permit Center (209) 937-8436 or Environmental Control Division.
5. Permit issued following staff review and site inspection.
6. **Self-monitoring:** Conduct regular wastewater testing (flow, pH, pollutants) and submit self-monitoring reports to Environmental Control Division on permit-specified schedule.
7. Allow unannounced site inspections by Environmental Compliance Officers.

## Discharge Standards and Local Limits

SMC Title 13 Ch. 13.08 sets prohibited discharge standards and local limits. Facilities must also comply with applicable EPA categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR Parts 405–471).

**Specific numeric local limits** (mg/L values for metals, BOD, TSS, oil and grease) are **not published on the city's public web pages**. [VERIFY — request current local limits table from Environmental Control Division at (209) 937-8740.]

Known discharge prohibitions from SMC Title 13 framework (consistent with standard pretreatment programs):
- pH typically required within 5.0–12.0 s.u. range [VERIFY local range]
- Flammable/explosive liquids prohibited
- Solids or viscous substances that obstruct flow
- Pollutants causing pass-through or interference at RWCF
- Heat in excess of amounts that inhibit biological treatment
- Radioactive wastes (unless in compliance with NRC regulations)
- Stormwater / non-process water prohibited from sewer discharge
