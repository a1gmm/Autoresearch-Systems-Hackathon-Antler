> Research reference for the CUPA serving City of Stockton facilities. Stockton does NOT operate its own CUPA. The City of Stockton falls under the San Joaquin County Environmental Health Department (EHD), which is the countywide CUPA certified by CalEPA. All six unified programs are administered through the county EHD, not a city agency.

## HMBP Program Details

- **Submission system:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business
- **CERS email for SJC EHD:** cers@sjgov.org
- **Annual HMBP review window:** Accepted beginning November 1; must be submitted no later than **January 15** of the year due (local EHD requirement)
- **Who must comply:** Businesses storing, using, or handling hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities (H&SC §25507: generally 55 gallons liquid, 500 lbs solid, 200 cu ft gas)
- **HMBP guidelines (PDF):** https://www.sjgov.org/docs/default-source/environmental-health-documents/hazardous-materials-business-plan-program/hmbp-guidelines.pdf
- **HMBP survey (PDF):** https://www.sjgov.org/docs/default-source/environmental-health-documents/hazardous-materials-business-plan-program/hmbp-survey.pdf
- **Online permit system:** https://permits.sjgov.org/Departments/Environmental-Health/CUPA-Programs

## Cal-ARP

San Joaquin County EHD is the local implementing agency (LIA) for Cal-ARP. Facilities with regulated substances above threshold quantities must submit an RMP to USEPA; EHD reviews the local Cal-ARP portion. [VERIFY] county EHD contact for Cal-ARP inspections.

## Note on City of Stockton Fire Department

The Stockton Fire Department (Fire Prevention Division) operates a Joint Hazardous Materials Response Team (mutual aid with San Joaquin County). This is an **emergency response** function only — it does not make the city Fire Department the CUPA. All CUPA regulatory programs (HMBP, HWG, UST, APSA, Cal-ARP) are administered by SJC EHD. See JURISDICTION.md for further routing notes.
