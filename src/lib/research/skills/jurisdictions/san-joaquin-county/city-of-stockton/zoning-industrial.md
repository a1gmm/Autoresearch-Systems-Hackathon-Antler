## Industrial Zoning Districts

Stockton's current Development Code uses two industrial district designations:

### IL — Industrial, Limited

- **Purpose:** Applied to areas appropriate for light manufacturing uses that may generate more nuisance impacts than acceptable in commercial zoning districts, but whose operations are **conducted entirely indoors**.
- Includes ancillary retail stores and office uses.
- Uses that produce outdoor nuisance, noise, or hazardous impacts are generally **not** allowed in IL.

### IG — Industrial, General

- **Purpose:** Allows a wide range of industrial land uses, including uses that **may be conducted outdoors** or are associated with nuisance or hazardous impacts.
- Includes ancillary office uses.
- Intended for heavier industrial, warehouse, logistics, and outdoor-operations uses.

> **Legacy designations:** M-1 (Light Industrial) and M-2 (Heavy Industrial) designations appear in older Stockton zoning documents and some third-party references. M-1 maps roughly to IL; M-2 maps roughly to IG. Use only the current IL/IG framework for active compliance work.

Uses in the Development Code are classified by the permit required:

| Code | Permit Type | Decision Authority |
|---|---|---|

| L | Land Development Permit | Director (administrative) |
| A | Administrative Use Permit | Community Development Director |
| C | Commission Use Permit | Planning Commission (public hearing) |
| E | Special / Exceptional circumstances | As specified |
| — | Not allowed | |

For specific uses in IL and IG, consult Table 2-2 in SMC §16.20.020. [VERIFY current Table 2-2 from eCode360 or city's qcode site — 403 on direct fetch.]

> **Key rule:** All uses shown with "C" in Table 2-2 require a **Commission Use Permit** (public hearing before Planning Commission). If an existing use permit is in place and there will be no new construction or expansion, no new use permit is required.

**Logistics/warehouse note:** The City adopted SMC Title 16 amendments establishing new logistics warehouse development standards effective January 11, 2024 (adopted December 12, 2023). Warehouse/distribution uses in IG should be verified against current SMC §16.24 standards.

## Commission Use Permit (CUP) Process — Chapter 16.168

**Trigger:** Any use labeled "C" in Table 2-2 for the applicable industrial zone requires Planning Commission approval.

### Process Steps

1. **Preliminary review** — Contact Planning Division; staff review before formal application reduces revisions.
2. **File application** — Submit completed application with property owner signature, development plans (site/floor/elevation/landscaping as required), environmental review application if needed, and filing fee to: Planning Division, 501 W. Weber Ave., Building 2, 1st Floor, Stockton, CA 95203.
3. **Departmental review** — City departments and outside agencies review and provide recommendations.
4. **Notice of Public Hearing** — Public notice published per SMC Ch. 16.88 (Review Procedures).
5. **Planning Commission Hearing** — Commission meets typically the **2nd Thursday of each month at 6:00 p.m.**, City Council Chambers, 425 N. El Dorado Street, 2nd Floor.
6. Commission approves, conditionally approves, or denies the CUP.
7. **Appeal:** Commission decision may be appealed to City Council within 10 days; City Council conducts a public hearing; Council decision is final.

**Typical timeline:** ~60 days from complete application to decision.

### Administrative Use Permit (AUP)

For uses labeled "A" in Table 2-2, the Community Development Director approves after 14-day neighborhood notice (no Planning Commission hearing required). Director's decision is appealable to Planning Commission within 10-day appeal period.

### Findings Required for CUP/AUP Approval (SMC §16.168.050)

Applicant must establish evidence that:
- Proposed use is consistent with the Development Code and General Plan for the site.
- The use maintains health and safety of the community.
- The use is compatible with existing land uses in the area.
- The site can physically accommodate the proposed use.

Factors considered: building size and setbacks, parking, density, access to streets and utilities, hours of operation, landscaping, traffic generation/sight distance, nuisance potential.

## Site Plan Review

- **Required:** For new construction or change to a more intensive use (Chapter 16.152), except as provided by exemptions in SMC §16.152.040.
- Site plan review is coordinated through the Planning Division.
