## Adopted Edition

San Joaquin County has adopted the **2025 California Fire Code** (CFC) via **Ordinance 4687**. The 2025 CFC is based on the 2024 International Fire Code (IFC) with California amendments and replaced the 2022 CFC statewide on 2026-01-01.

## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

- **Unincorporated county:** San Joaquin County Community Development Department (CDD) — **Fire Prevention Bureau** serves as AHJ for fire code review, permitting, and inspection on behalf of the independent fire protection districts throughout unincorporated San Joaquin County. The county has no consolidated county fire department; suppression is provided by ~17 independent fire protection districts (Lathrop-Manteca, Mokelumne, Woodbridge, Clements, Escalon, Farmington, Linden-Peters, etc.).
- **City of Stockton:** Stockton Fire Department (handles its own fire code approvals including Fire Apparatus Access Roads within city limits).
- **Other incorporated cities** (Tracy, Manteca, Lodi, Lathrop) have their own municipal fire AHJs or are served by fire protection districts.

## Permits and Contact

- Fire prevention permits (operational fire permits, hazardous materials, fireworks displays, etc.) are issued by the CDD Fire Prevention Bureau.
- An "Operational Fire Permit" is required for public fireworks displays and must be submitted with any associated Improvement Plan (Title 9 § 9-505.5(d)).
- Contact: San Joaquin County CDD Fire Prevention Division, 1810 E. Hazelton Ave., Stockton, CA 95205.

## Notes

- The CUPA (hazmat/HMBP/CalARP/UST/APSA) is administered separately by the county Environmental Health Department — NOT the Fire Prevention Bureau.
- For industrial facilities subject to CalARP, the county EHD is the administering agency even within incorporated cities.
