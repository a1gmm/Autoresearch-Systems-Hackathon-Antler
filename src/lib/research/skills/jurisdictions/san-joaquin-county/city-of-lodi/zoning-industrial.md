## Industrial Zone Designations [VERIFY]

Lodi uses an **"M"** designation for industrial zones. The Development Code includes at least one industrial category (M — Manufacturing/Industrial). The specific sub-district breakdown (e.g., M-1 light vs. M-2 heavy) and the full table of permitted-by-right vs. Use Permit uses must be confirmed by reading LMC §17.24.030 directly.

Known 2025 amendment: **Religious facilities** were added as a Use Permit (UP) use in Industrial (M) zones per LMC §17.24.030.

## Zoning District Land Uses and Permit Requirements

Per §17.24.030, land uses in industrial zones require one of:
- **Permitted (P)** — allowed by right
- **Use Permit (UP) / Conditional Use Permit (CUP)** — requires Planning Commission approval
- **Not Allowed** — prohibited

[VERIFY] The complete permitted use table for industrial districts is in LMC §17.24.030. Full text accessible at Municode (library.municode.com) or through Planning staff.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

From City Planning FAQs:
- **Approving authority:** Planning Commission (initial decision); appeals go to City Council
- **Typical timeline:** ~10 weeks including public hearing
- **Implementation window:** 2 years to satisfy conditions of approval after CUP granted
- **Contact:** Planning Division, (209) 333-6711, planningdivision@lodi.gov

## General Development Standards

Standards for industrial zones are in **LMC §17.14** (general property development standards) and **§17.24.040** (industrial-specific standards). Additional requirements in §17.36 (specific land uses, accessory structures).
