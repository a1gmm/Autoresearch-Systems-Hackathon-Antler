# CUPA — City of Lodi, CA

## Administering Agency

The City of Lodi is **not its own CUPA**. All Unified Program services are provided by the **San Joaquin County Environmental Health Department (SJC EHD)**, which was certified as CUPA in **January 1997** and serves the entire county including all incorporated cities.

**San Joaquin County Environmental Health Department — CUPA**
- Website: https://www.sjgov.org/department/envhealth/programs/certified-unified-program-agency-(cupa)
- UST testing appointments: ust@sjgov.org or fax (209) 468-3433
- Inspections: Contact area inspector (minimum 48-hour notice required)

## Programs Administered

SJC EHD administers all six California Unified Program elements:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — CalEPA CERS submission
2. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — risk management plans
3. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — AST compliance
4. **Hazardous Waste Generator (HWG)** — generation, accumulation, manifesting
5. **Hazardous Waste Onsite Treatment (Tiered Permitting)** — permit-by-rule through full permit
6. **Underground Storage Tank (UST)** — installation, monitoring, closure

## Submission Portal

- **HMBP / CalARP / APSA:** Submit through California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) at https://cers.calepa.ca.gov
- **Other CUPA program forms:** Standardized UPCF Consolidated Forms available at https://www.unidocs.org

## Notes

Facilities within Lodi city limits route all hazardous materials and waste compliance submittals through SJC EHD, not through the city. The fire department handles emergency response (hazmat mitigation) but is not the CUPA enforcement authority.
