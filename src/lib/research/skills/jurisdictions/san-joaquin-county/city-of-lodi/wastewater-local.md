# Wastewater / POTW — City of Lodi, CA

**White Slough Water Pollution Control Facility (WWPCF)**
- Operator: City of Lodi (Public Works / Utilities Division)
- Facility Address: 12751 North Thornton Road, Lodi, CA 95242
- Mailing / Billing: 221 West Pine Street, Lodi, CA 95240 / 1331 South Ham Lane, Lodi, CA 95242
- Utilities Manager: Lance Roberts, (209) 333-6740
- Wastewater Plant Superintendent: Ken Capitanich, (209) 333-6832

- Type: Major POTW (Category 1 Threat to Water Quality, Category A Complexity)

## Capacity and Service Area

- Design ADWF: **8.5 MGD** (average dry weather flow)
- Service population: ~67,000 (City of Lodi + San Joaquin County Flag City Service Area)
- Collection system: 23,000 service laterals, 178 miles of mains, 2,880 manholes, 7 lift stations
- Treatment level: Full nitrification/denitrification, tertiary filtration (cloth media), UV disinfection
- Receiving water: **Dredger Cut** (Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta)

## Industrial Pretreatment Program

The City of Lodi operates a **formal Industrial Pretreatment Program** approved by the Central Valley Water Board per 40 C.F.R. Part 403. The program is confirmed "Yes" in the facility's NOA table.

Categories regulated under the IWP:
- Significant Industrial Users (SIUs) / Categorical Industrial Users (CIUs)
- Non-significant Industrial Users (NSIUs)
- Food service establishments (fats, oils, and grease — FOG control)

The City operates **two separate wastewater collection systems**:
1. Municipal system (domestic + commercial + some industrial — treated at WWPCF)
2. Industrial system (primarily food-processing wastewater from Pacific Coast Producers cannery — directed to storage ponds or agricultural irrigation fields under WDR R5-2007-0113-01, not treated at WWPCF)
