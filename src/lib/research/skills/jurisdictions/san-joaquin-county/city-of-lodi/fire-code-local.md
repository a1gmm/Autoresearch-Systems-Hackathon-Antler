# Fire Code — City of Lodi, CA

## Adopted Edition

**2025 California Fire Code (CFC)**, Part 9 of Title 24, California Code of Regulations.
Effective: **January 1, 2026** (statewide adoption date; supersedes 2022 CFC).

The City of Lodi Building Code Ordinance enforces the full suite of 2025 California Building Standards codes, including Part 9 (2025 CFC). Local amendments are codified in **Lodi Municipal Code (LMC) Title 15 — Building Codes, Chapter 15.20 — Fire Code**.

References in the Building Codes & Design Criteria sheet: `City of Lodi Code of Ordinances §15.02, §15.04, §15.06, §15.08, §15.12, §15.16, §15.18, §15.20`.

## Adopting Ordinance [VERIFY]

The specific adopting ordinance number for the 2025 CFC is not published on the public-facing city pages. The municipal code chapter is LMC Ch. 15.20. Contact the Community Development Building Division (comdev@lodi.gov / (209) 333-6714) to obtain the ordinance number and any locally enacted amendments.

## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

**Lodi Fire Department** is the AHJ for fire code enforcement within city limits.

- Address: 210 W. Elm Street, Lodi, CA 95240
- Phone: (209) 333-6739
- Email: Fire@Lodi.gov
- Fire Chief: Ken Johnson (as of 2021)
- Staffing: ~51 personnel; full-service all-hazard agency
- Services: structural/wildland fire suppression, EMS (ALS paramedic), technical rescue, hazmat response, water rescue

**Note:** Unincorporated areas east of Lodi (Lockeford, Victor) fall under the Mokelumne Fire District; Woodbridge under the Woodbridge Fire District — separate AHJs from the City of Lodi FD.

## Local Amendments [VERIFY]

Specific local amendments to the 2025 CFC have not been published in machine-readable form on the city website. The Municode chapter (LMC Ch. 15.20) is the authoritative source; access via Municode or contact the fire department for the current amendment matrix.

## Fire Protection Equipment Permits

A separate Fire Protection Equipment Permit is required for fire suppression systems, fire alarm systems, and similar installations. Application: https://www.lodi.gov/DocumentCenter/View/99/Application---Fire-Protection-Equipment-Permit-PDF
