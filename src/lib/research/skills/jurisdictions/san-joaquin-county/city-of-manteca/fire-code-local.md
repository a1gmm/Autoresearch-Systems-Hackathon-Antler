# Fire Code — City of Manteca, CA

## Adopted Edition

The City of Manteca has adopted the **2022 California Fire Code (CFC), Title 24, Part 9**, including Appendix Chapters B, BB, C, CC, and D, as published by the California Building Standards Commission. The 2022 CFC incorporates by reference the 2021 International Fire Code (IFC) published by the International Code Council.

- **Municipal Code Chapter:** Title 15, Chapter 15.24 — FIRE CODE
- **Adoption section:** MMC § 15.24.010 — Adoption; Copies on file
- **Local amendments:** MMC § 15.24.040 contains Manteca-specific amendments to the adopted CFC [VERIFY exact amendment text on live eCode360 page]

## AHJ

- **Authority Having Jurisdiction:** City of Manteca Fire Department — Fire Prevention Division
- The Fire Prevention Division handles fire code enforcement, plan review, and permit functions for all property within City of Manteca limits.
- Note: The **Lathrop-Manteca Fire Protection District** (a separate AHJ) covers unincorporated areas and the City of Lathrop — it does NOT cover City of Manteca parcels.

## Scope

Chapter 15.24 regulates conditions hazardous to life and property from fire, hazardous materials, or explosion, including storage, handling, and use of hazardous substances, materials, and devices.
