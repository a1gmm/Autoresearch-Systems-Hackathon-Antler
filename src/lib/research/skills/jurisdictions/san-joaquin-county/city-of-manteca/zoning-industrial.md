# Zoning — Industrial Districts, City of Manteca, CA

## Zoning Code Structure

**Municipal Code:** Title 17 — ZONING
- Article II: Zoning Districts, Allowed Uses, and Development Standards
  - Chapter 17.20 — Establishment of Zoning Districts and Land Use Classification System
  - Chapter 17.22 — Allowed Land Uses and Requirements (contains Table 17.22.020-1)
  - Chapter 17.24 — Allowed Use Definitions
  - Chapter 17.26 — Development Standards by Zoning District
  - Chapter 17.28 — Special Purpose Zoning Districts
  - Chapter 17.30 — Overlay and Combining Zoning District Standards

## Industrial and Manufacturing Zoning Districts

### M1 — Light Industrial Zoning District
Provides for industrial parks, warehouses, distribution centers, light manufacturing, public and quasi-public uses, and similar compatible uses.

Key use entitlements (Table 17.22.020-1):
- **Allowed by right (A):** Warehousing, distribution, light manufacturing, utility infrastructure, public safety facility, park-and-ride, broadcasting/recording
- **Conditional Use Permit (C):** Kennels, fuel storage/distribution [VERIFY full CUP list on live code]
- **Not permitted (N):** Residential uses (except caretaker housing — C), most agricultural uses

Development standards (Chapter 17.26):
- Max height: 75 feet
- Minimum setback from residential: 40 feet

### M2 — Heavy Industrial Zoning District
Provides for manufacturing, processing, assembling, research, wholesale, storage, trucking terminals, railroad/freight stations, and similar activities requiring separation from residential uses. Also allows many M1 (light industrial) uses.

Key use entitlements (Table 17.22.020-1):
- **Allowed by right (A):** Fuel storage and distribution, heavy manufacturing/processing
- **Conditional Use Permit (C):** Airport, ambulance service
- **Not permitted (N):** Residential (except caretaker housing — C)

Development standards (Chapter 17.26):
- Max height: 75 feet
- Minimum setback from residential: 40 feet

### CM — Commercial Manufacturing Zoning District
Intended for establishments engaged in servicing equipment, materials, and products; does not require manufacturing or processing of articles for distribution and retail sales. Lighter industrial/service impact profile than M1/M2.

### BIP — Business Industrial Park Zoning District
A separate base district for office/R&D/light industrial park uses; occupies a different niche from M1/M2.

## Entitlement Process

- **Allowed by right (A):** No planning entitlement required (subject to building permits and development standards).
- **Minor Use Permit (M):** Administrative review by planning staff.
- **Conditional Use Permit (C):** Discretionary approval pursuant to MMC § 17.10.130; public hearing required; findings of consistency with general plan and zoning standards.
- Uses not listed in Table 17.22.020-1 are not permitted in any district.

## Zoning Map

The official Zoning Map (MMC § 17.20.040) designates parcel-level district assignments and is available through the City of Manteca Development Services Department.
