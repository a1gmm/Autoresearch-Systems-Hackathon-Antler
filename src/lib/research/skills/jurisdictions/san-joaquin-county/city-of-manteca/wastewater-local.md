## Manteca Pretreatment Program (MPP)

The MPP controls and regulates discharge of industrial and hazardous waste to the Manteca water pollution control system and WQCF. Requirements include:

- **Local limits:** Chapter 13.20 establishes concentration-based local limits on pollutants in industrial waste. Where National Categorical Pretreatment Standards (CPS) apply, the more stringent of the local limit or categorical standard governs.
- **General prohibitions:** No discharge that will cause pass-through or interference with the POTW, including no pH < 6.0 (unless POTW is specifically designed to accept).
- **Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permits (IWP):** Required for significant industrial users (SIUs) and categorical industrial users (CIUs); permits issued for up to 5 years.
- **Record-keeping:** All persons subject to reporting requirements must retain monitoring records for a minimum of 3 years (or longer if litigation is pending).
- **Enforcement:** Chapter 13.20 includes an enforcement response plan for compliance with the MPP.
