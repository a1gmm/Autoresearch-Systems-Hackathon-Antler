# CUPA — City of Manteca, CA

## Administering Agency

The City of Manteca is **not its own CUPA**. All Unified Program elements are administered countywide by:

**San Joaquin County Environmental Health Department (SJC EHD)**
Certified as the CUPA for San Joaquin County by the State since January 1997.
- Phone: (209) 468-3420
- Web: https://www.sjgov.org/department/envhealth/programs/certified-unified-program-agency-(cupa)
- HMBP online portal: https://permits.sjgov.org/Departments/Environmental-Health/CUPA-Programs/Hazardous-Materials-Business-Plan-Program

## Programs Administered (Unified Program Elements)

1. Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) — submitted via CERS
2. California Accidental Release Prevention (Cal-ARP) Program
3. Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)
4. Hazardous Waste Generator Program
5. Hazardous Waste Onsite Treatment (Tiered Permitting)
6. Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program

## Reporting

All HMBP and regulated program submissions for City of Manteca facilities go through the **California Environmental Reporting System (CERS)** and are reviewed by SJC EHD — not by the City of Manteca.

UST testing appointments: ust@sjgov.org or fax (209) 468-3433 (minimum 48-hour advance scheduling required).
