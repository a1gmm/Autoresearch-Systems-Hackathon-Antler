## Industrial Pretreatment Program

The City operates a **Wastewater Pretreatment Program** under the Environmental Compliance section of the Operations and Utilities Department. The program enforces:

- Federal pretreatment standards under **40 C.F.R. Part 403** (City is Control Authority)
- Categorical pretreatment standards (40 C.F.R. Parts 405–471) for applicable industrial categories
- Local limits developed under 40 C.F.R. § 403.5(c) (local limits evaluation required per NPDES Order)

### Industrial User Permit (IWP) Process

Under the NPDES Order (Order R5-2022-XXXX, Special Provisions for POTWs):

1. The City must maintain legal authority per 40 C.F.R. § 403.8(f)(1).
2. **Significant Industrial Users (SIUs)** must be identified and tracked; annual SIU list submitted to Central Valley Water Board and U.S. EPA Region 9.
3. Annual pretreatment report due covering prior calendar year (January 1 – December 31).
4. Local Limits Evaluation — written technical evaluation of need to revise local limits submitted per Technical Reports schedule in Order Attachment E.
