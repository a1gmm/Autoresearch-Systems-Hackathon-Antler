## CUPA Agency

The City of Tracy does **not** operate its own CUPA. Tracy is served by the **San Joaquin County Environmental Health Department (SJC-EHD)**, which is the countywide Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all jurisdictions within San Joaquin County, including incorporated cities.

**Submitting agency:** San Joaquin County Environmental Health Department — CUPA Division  
**Address:** 1868 E. Hazelton Ave., Stockton, CA 95205  
**Phone:** (209) 468-3420  
**Website:** https://www.sjgov.org/department/envhealth/programs/certified-unified-program-agency-(cupa)

## Programs Administered

SJC-EHD as CUPA administers all six CUPA unified programs for Tracy businesses:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — submitted via CERS (California Environmental Reporting System)
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator Program** — generator tiering, manifest requirements
3. **Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program** — permits, inspections, leak detection
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — SPCC compliance oversight for tanks ≥ 1,320 gal
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — RMP review and inspection
6. **Hazardous Waste On-Site Treatment (Tiered Permitting)**

## Submission Portal

All HMBP, CalARP, and related annual reporting uses **CERS**: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/
