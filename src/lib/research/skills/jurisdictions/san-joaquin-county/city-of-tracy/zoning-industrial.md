## Industrial Zone Districts

Two industrial zone designations exist under Chapter 10.08:

### Article 20 — M-1 Light Industrial Zone

Permitted by right (no CUP required):

- Minor and local public services
- Crop and tree farming
- Contract construction services
- Warehousing and storage
- Small recycling collection facilities
- Light manufacturing
- Accessory uses (excluding recreation facilities and residences)
- Temporary buildings

Conditional Uses (CUP required):

- Mining and quarrying
- Animal farming (no slaughtering except home consumption)
- Agricultural processing
- Public assembly uses
- Consumer and business services
- Wholesale trade
- Intermediate and very heavy manufacturing

Development Standards (M-1):

| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| Front yard setback | 25 ft minimum |
| Side yard setback | None (15 ft when adjacent to residential zone) |
| Rear yard | No requirement |
| Building height | No restriction |
| Lot coverage | No restriction |
| Screening | 6-ft fence required where abutting residential zone |
| Loading | On-site loading/unloading required |
| Development review | Required for all permitted uses |

## CUP Process

The Conditional Use Permit (CUP) process is administered by the **Community and Economic Development Department, Planning Division**.

- CUP page: https://www.cityoftracy.org/our-city/departments/planning/development-permit-process/conditional-use-permit
- Application procedures, public hearings, notices, and fees are in **Chapter 10.08, Article 31** of the Zoning Regulations.
- Filing fees are set by City Council resolution.
