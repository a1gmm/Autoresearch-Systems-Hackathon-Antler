## Adopted Code Edition

The City of Tracy adopts the California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9) in **Tracy Municipal Code Title 9 (Building and Regulations), Chapter 9.06 (Fire Prevention and Protection)**. The chapter is the local adopting ordinance for the CFC; the current edition in force as of 2026-01-01 is the **2025 California Fire Code** (based on IFC 2024), which became effective statewide on 2026-01-01 per California Building Standards Commission schedule. [VERIFY: confirm Tracy has not published a city-specific amendment ordinance for the 2025 CFC edition — the Municode page (Ch. 9.06) is behind an auth wall as of 2026-06-04.]

## AHJ

**South San Joaquin County Fire Authority (SSJCFA)** — a joint powers authority (JPA) formed in September 1999 by the City of Tracy and the Tracy Rural Fire Protection District. The SSJCFA operates the fire prevention / Community Risk Reduction division and serves as AHJ for fire code plan review, inspection, and permits within city limits.

- SSJCFA website: https://www.sjcfire.org/
- City fire department page: https://www.cityoftracy.org/our-city/departments/fire-department

## Companion Jurisdictional Context

The **Tracy Rural Fire Protection District** (separate legal entity, also a SSJCFA JPA member) adopted the **2022 California Fire Code** via its own **Ordinance No. 2022-01** (introduced October 11, 2022 board meeting). San Joaquin County separately adopted the 2022 CFC via **County Ordinance No. 4615** (amending Chapter 1, Division 1, Title 4 of the SJ County Ordinance Code), adopted November 29, 2022, effective January 1, 2023. These confirm the regional baseline; city-incorporated areas use Ch. 9.06 TMC rather than the county ordinance.

### Key SJ County Ordinance 4615 Amendments (applicable to unincorporated areas; city mirrors many provisions)

- Section 4-1000: Adopts 2022 CFC including Appendices B, BB, C, CC; based on 2021 IFC.
- Section 4-1003: Amends CFC § 105 — two permit types: **Operational** and **Construction**; construction permits expire if work not commenced within 180 days.
- Section 4-1005: Defines "Code" as CFC Title 24 Part 9 with California amendments; "Chief/Fire Code Official" = County Fire Warden or designated representative.
- Section 4-1006: Local amendments to fire apparatus access road requirements.
- Miscellaneous combustible storage threshold: > 2,500 cubic feet gross volume triggers operational permit.
