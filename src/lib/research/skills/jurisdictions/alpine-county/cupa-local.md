## Program Structure

Alpine County HHS is CalEPA-certified as the sole CUPA for the entire county. There are **no participating agencies** — Alpine County has zero incorporated cities, so the county is the exclusive regulatory authority for all six Unified Program elements throughout all unincorporated communities (Markleeville, Bear Valley, Kirkwood, Woodfords, etc.).

## Six Unified Program Elements Administered

1. **Hazardous Materials Release Response Plans and Inventories (Business Plans / HMBP)** — facilities storing hazardous materials above threshold quantities must submit a Hazardous Materials Business Plan via CERS.
2. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — applies to facilities with regulated substances above threshold quantities posing risk to the public.
3. **Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program** — permitting, inspection, and enforcement for underground storage tank systems.
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act / SPCC Plans (APSA)** — facilities with aboveground petroleum storage exceeding 1,320 gallons aggregate must comply and maintain SPCC plans.
5. **Hazardous Waste Generator and On-Site Treatment Programs** — oversight of hazardous waste generation, storage, treatment, and manifesting at facilities.
6. **Cal Fire Code Hazardous Materials Management Plans (HMRRP)** — fire code hazmat regulatory requirements administered under the CUPA umbrella.

## CERS Reporting Requirement

Mandatory since January 1, 2013. All CUPA-regulated businesses must submit information electronically through the **California Environmental Reporting System (CERS)** at cers.calepa.ca.gov. Facilities create an account and request lead user access to their site. Reports cover:
- Hazardous Materials Business Plans
- Hazardous waste documentation
- Underground storage tanks
- Aboveground petroleum storage (>1,320 gallons)

## Local Hazmat Ordinance / Municipal Code Chapter

[VERIFY] — No locally adopted hazardous materials ordinance chapter separate from the state Unified Program statutes was found published online. Alpine County relies on state law (Health and Safety Code §§25500 et seq. — Unified Hazardous Waste and Hazardous Materials Management Regulatory Program) as the operative framework. Contact HHS to confirm whether a local ordinance supplements state requirements.

## Fee Schedule

[VERIFY] — Alpine County HHS has not published a CUPA fee schedule online. Fees are set by the Board of Supervisors. Contact the HHS Director or CUPA coordinator directly for current fee schedules.

## Inspection and Enforcement

HHS inspects all CUPA-regulated facilities. Enforcement follows the CalEPA Enforcement Response Policy framework. The 24-hour emergency hotline (888-755-8099) handles hazmat release notifications.
