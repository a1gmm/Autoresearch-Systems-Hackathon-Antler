## Local Amendments to the 2022 CFC

No standalone CFC local amendments are published in Chapter 15.04. However, the adopting ordinance (Chapter 15.04.030) amends the 2022 California Building Code (CBC) and California Residential Code (CRC) with significant local provisions driven by Alpine County's mountain climate. Directly relevant to fire/life safety:

### Snow-Shed / Exit Protection (CBC 1003, 1027, 1028; CRC R311)
For elevations above 6,200 feet, roof and eave designs of all newly constructed buildings (and roof alterations/additions) must ensure snow/ice-shedding impact areas do not occur in or on:
- Required exits for R-3 occupancies
- All entries/exits, stairways, walkways, ramps, vehicle parking areas, and LPG storage tanks for all other occupancies

Snow-shed impact areas may be deferred if an engineered snow restraint system is effectively attached to the roof structure. Drainage from roofs, awnings, canopies, or marquees may not flow over a public walking surface.

### Climatic/Geographic Design Criteria (Table R301.2(1))
| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Seismic Design Category | D |
| Winter Design Temperature | −15°F |
| Ice Barrier Underlayment | Required |
| Concrete Frost Line Depth (below 6,200 ft) | 18 inches |

### Snow Loads (CBC 1608 — Ground Snow Loads, psf)
| Region | Ground Snow Load (pg) | Roof Snow Load (pf) |
|---|---|---|
| River Ranch (5,000–5,200 ft) | 65 psf | 50 psf |
| Mesa Vista & Fredricksburg (5,200–5,800 ft) | 98 psf | 75 psf |
| Woodfords/Hawkins Ranch (5,500–5,950 ft) | 130 psf | 100 psf |

*Areas not specified: determined by the Building Official. Snow load reductions not permitted where eave heights ≤ hg.*

## Additional Local Fire Safety Regulation

### Chapter 15.12 — Fire Safe Regulations for State Responsibility Areas
- Adopts PRC §4290 fire safe regulations (14 CCR §§1270.00–1276.04)
- **Adopting ordinance:** Ord. 530 (1991); amended by Ord. 676 (2007)
- **Emergency water supply for SRAs:** New construction not connected to a qualifying water system must provide a static water system of ≥2,500 gallons (buried or above-ground, freeze-protected). In lieu of the static system, a monetary fee may be paid to a Board-established trust fund for mobile water equipment; fee set by Board of Supervisors resolution, collected before building permit issuance, refundable if qualifying system is installed before occupancy.
