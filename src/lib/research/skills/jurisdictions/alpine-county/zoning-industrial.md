## Industrial Zone: IND (Chapter 18.48)

Alpine County has a **single industrial zone designation** — "IND" — with no M-1/M-2/M-3 or light/heavy split. All industrial land in the county falls under this one chapter.

### Purpose (§18.48.010)
"The purpose of the IND industrial zone is to provide and protect areas for the development of industries including those which might create objectionable conditions if allowed in any other zone."
*(Ord. 480 (part), 1988; Ord. 453 § 14.01, 1985)*

### Permitted Uses by Right (§18.48.020)
- Agriculture
- All commercial uses permitted in C (Commercial) zoning districts
- All other commercial, manufacturing, warehousing, and commercial uses **not** requiring a use permit under §18.48.030
- Note: Uses conditionally permitted in C zones do **not** require a use permit in the IND zone unless specifically listed in §18.48.030.

*(Ord. 480 (part), 1988; Ord. 453 § 14.02, 1985)*

### Conditional Uses — Use Permit Required (§18.48.030)
The following require a Conditional Use Permit (CUP) in the IND zone:

**A.** One residence for occupancy by owner(s), caretaker(s), or security personnel employed on the premises.

**B.** All uses listed in §18.44.030(A) and (G) [refer to Neighborhood Commercial combining zone provisions].

**C.** Heavy industrial / hazardous operations (all require CUP):
1. Commercial excavation of stone or earth materials; distillation of bones
2. Drilling for and/or removal of oil, gas, or geothermal resources
3. Dumping, disposal, incineration, or reduction of garbage, sewerage, offal, dead animals, or refuse
4. Fat rendering
5. Hog raising
6. Junkyards, wrecking yards, or auto dismantlers
7. Manufacturing of acid, cement, explosives, fireworks, fertilizer, gas, glue, gypsum, inflammable fluids or gases
8. Refining of petroleum and its products
9. Smelting and/or processing of metallic or nonmetallic ores
10. Stockyards and slaughterhouses
11. Tanneries and other uses that create objectionable noise, offensive odor, smoke, dust, bright lights, or vibration, or involve handling of explosive or dangerous materials
12. Recycling, processing, handling, or storage of hazardous waste materials other than those produced by the business on the premises

**D.** Private, public, or commercial airports, landing strips or pads, and accessory structures and uses.

**E.** Additional uses determined by the Planning Commission to be similar to the above and/or requiring regulation to protect public health, safety, or general welfare.

*(Ord. 480 (part), 1988; Ord. 453 § 14.03, 1985)*

### Development Standards (§18.48.040 and §18.48.050)

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Minimum lot area | 1 acre |
| Front yard setback | 20 feet |
| Side yard setback | None (unless abutting another zone: ≥50 feet) |
| Rear yard setback | None (unless abutting another zone: ≥50 feet) |
| Maximum building height | 45 feet |

*(Ord. 480 (part), 1988; Ord. 453 § 14.04–14.05, 1985)*

### Non-Use / Reverter Provision (§18.48.060)
If IND-zoned land is not put to or maintained in industrial use for **36 months or more**, the Planning Commission (or the public by petition) may initiate rezoning proceedings. Extensions of up to 24 months may be granted by the Commission.

*(Ord. 480 (part), 1988; Ord. 453 § 14.06, 1985)*

## EHS Interaction Notes

- Hazardous waste recycling/handling/storage by third parties requires a CUP (§18.48.030.C.12) **and** CUPA oversight from Alpine County HHS.
- Oil/gas/geothermal drilling requires a CUP (§18.48.030.C.2) **and** applicable state permits (DOGGR/CalGEM).
- Facilities with air emissions require GBUAPCD Authority to Construct and Permit to Operate regardless of zoning status.
- Heavy federal land ownership in Alpine County means many potential industrial sites are on USFS or BLM land — federal land-use authorization (Special Use Permit) would supersede or supplement county zoning.
