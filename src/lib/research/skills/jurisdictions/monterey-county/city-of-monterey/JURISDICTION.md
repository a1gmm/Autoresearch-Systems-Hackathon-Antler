## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **CUPA agency:** County of Monterey Health Department, Environmental Health Bureau — Hazardous Materials Management Services (HMMS). The City of Monterey is **not** its own CUPA; all six unified program elements (HMBP, UST, HWMP, CalARP, OncoTrak/tiered permits, and aboveground petroleum storage) are administered by the county.
- **CERS reporting:** Businesses submit Hazardous Materials Business Plans and related documents through the California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://www.countyofmonterey.gov/government/departments-a-h/health/environmental-health/hazardous-material-management/cers-california-electronic-reporting-system
- **Fee schedule:** Environmental Health fees approved by the Board of Supervisors; current schedule effective July 1, 2025 — https://www.countyofmonterey.gov/government/departments-a-h/health/environmental-health/general/fees
- **CalARP:** County HMMS also administers the California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) program — https://www.countyofmonterey.gov/government/departments-a-h/health/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-management/california-accidental-release-prevention-program
- **Contact:** 1270 Natividad Rd, Salinas, CA 93906 | (831) 755-4500 (main) / (831) 755-4511 Ext. 1 (Hazmat Specialist)
- Source: https://www.countyofmonterey.gov/government/departments-a-h/health/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-management/cupa-programs

### 2. Fire AHJ — City of Monterey Fire Department

- **AHJ:** City of Monterey Fire Department (municipal). GOTCHA — Monterey FD is the regional fire-service provider and AHJ not only for the City of Monterey but also for Pacific Grove, Carmel-by-the-Sea, and Sand City under mutual-aid contracts, and federal/special-district installations (Presidio of Monterey, Naval Postgraduate School, Monterey Regional Airport District) by contract.
- **Adopted fire code:** 2025 California Fire Code (CFC), adopted via **Ordinance 3712** (codified April 2026); local amendments in Monterey City Code **Chapter 13** (current through Ordinance 3713, Nov 4, 2025). Chapter 13-0.2 enumerates local amendments to the 2025 CFC including marine-fueling tank requirements, automatic fire sprinkler thresholds, and fire alarm monitoring specs. All appendices to the 2025 CFC are adopted in their entirety.

### 3. POTW / Industrial Wastewater Pretreatment

- **POTW:** Monterey One Water (formerly Monterey Regional Water Pollution Control Agency), a Joint Powers Authority serving northern Monterey County; **City of Monterey is a member entity**.
- **Governing ordinance:** Monterey One Water **Wastewater Discharge Ordinance No. 2023-01** — "An Ordinance Modifying Regulations for the Interception, Treatment and Disposal of Sewage and Wastewater." Implements federal pretreatment standards (40 CFR Part 403) and Central Coast RWQCB requirements. Defines Categorical Industrial Users (CIUs) subject to Categorical Pretreatment Standards (40 CFR Parts 405–471).
- **City sewer code:** Monterey City Code **Chapter 30** (Sewers and Sewage Disposal) — connection requirements, sewer service charges, Fat/Oil/Grease (FOG) pretreatment equipment requirements for commercial/industrial users.
- **IWP permit process:** Industrial users subject to categorical pretreatment standards must submit a Baseline Monitoring Report per 40 CFR 403.12(b) to Monterey One Water. Contact Monterey One Water directly for permit applications.
- **Contact:** 5 Harris Court, Building D, Monterey, CA 93940 | (831) 372-3367 (admin) / (831) 422-1001 (treatment facility, 14811 Del Monte Blvd, Marina)
- Source (Ord 2023-01): https://www.montereyonewater.org/DocumentCenter/View/1411/Ordinance-No-2023-01-Modifying-Regulation-for-the-Interception-Treatment-and-Disposal-of-Sewage-and-Wastewater
- Source (service area): https://www.montereyonewater.org/266/Service-Area

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater

- **Local ordinance:** Monterey City Code **Chapter 31.5** (Storm Water Management), Article 2 — "Urban Storm Water Quality Management and Discharge Control Ordinance." Prohibits illegal discharges (§31.5-12), illicit connections (§31.5-13), and waste disposal into storm infrastructure (§31.5-14). Requires NPDES permit compliance for industrial/construction sites (§31.5-15). Grants city inspection authority (§31.5-23).
- **NPDES permit:** City operates under **NPDES General Permit No. CAS000004** (Phase II Small MS4 general permit) overseen by the Central Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board; the City is a co-permittee in the Monterey Regional Storm Water Management Program (MRSWMP).
- **Program page:** https://monterey.gov/your_city_hall/departments/public_works/engineering/environmental_regulations/storm_water_program.php
- Source (stormwater ordinance text): https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2015-12/documents/nps-ordinanceuments-city-of-monterey.pdf
- Source (Chapter 31.5): https://monterey.municipal.codes/Code/31.5

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts and CUP Process

- **Zoning code:** Monterey City Code **Chapter 38** (Zoning Ordinance). Industrial uses concentrated in the **I-R (Industrial, Administration, and Research) District** (§38-40). Land use table uses "P" for permitted-by-right and "U" for uses requiring a Use Permit (equivalent to CUP) before the Planning Commission or Community Development Director. Article 22 of Chapter 38 governs Use Permits and Variances; the Planning Commission may impose conditions to protect public health/safety and ensure compatibility.
- **Building authority:** Community Development Department, Building & Safety Division — https://monterey.gov/your_city_hall/departments/community_development/building_and_safety_services/
- Source (I-R district): https://monterey.municipal.codes/Code/38-40
- Source (Chapter 38 overview): https://monterey.municipal.codes/Code/38
