## City Sewer Code (Non-Pretreatment)

**City of Monterey Municipal Code Chapter 30 — Sewers and Sewage Disposal**

- Governs sewer connections, service charges, and Fat/Oil/Grease (FOG) pretreatment requirements for commercial/food service users within city limits.
- Source: https://monterey.municipal.codes/Code/30

### Chapter 30, Article 3 — Grease Regulations (§30-10, §30-11)

Any food service establishment or business discharging fats, oil, and grease must have an operable grease trap, grease interceptor, or other comparable device as determined by Monterey One Water, the City Engineer, and/or the Chief of Inspection Services/Building Official.

- **Grease interceptor** (outside): 300–5,000 gallons; all kitchen waste streams connected.
- **Grease trap** (inside): 80 to < 300 gallons; not all kitchen waste streams connected; minimum allowable size is 80-gallon liquid capacity (unless otherwise approved by the Chief of Inspection Services/Building Official).
- **Sizing:** Based on meals served per day, food type, and Monterey One Water's grease interceptor sizing matrix.
- **Fee Waiver Program (City only):** Businesses unable to install the preferred-size interceptor may install the minimum-allowable size AND pay a waiver fee of **$180 per quarter** toward City sewer main maintenance (adopted 2007 per the City's Waste Discharge Requirement). FY26 grease interceptor waiver fee: **$60.00/month** (per City Master Fee Schedule, updated January 28, 2026).

## Specific Discharge Prohibitions (§2.01.2)

No User may introduce the following into the Community Sanitary Sewer or RTP:
1. Pollutants with closed-cup flashpoint < 140°F (60°C) — fire/explosion hazard.
2. Corrosive substances; **pH below 6.0 or above 10.5** standard units.
3. Solid/viscous pollutants causing obstruction.
4. Compatible pollutants (BOD, TSS, etc.) discharged at flow rate causing Pass Through or Interference.
5. Filtered water discharge (except as hauled waste per §2.09).
6. Incompatible pollutants.
7. Holding tank waste or hauled waste except at designated discharge points (§§2.08, 2.09).
8. Heat causing POTW temperature to exceed **104°F (40°C)** at RTP or **140°F (60°C)** in the Community Sanitary Sewer.
9. Petroleum oil, non-biodegradable cutting oil, or mineral oil products causing Interference or Pass Through.
10. Pollutants producing toxic gases, vapors, or fumes causing acute worker health/safety problems.
11. Toxic pollutants in sufficient quantity to injure treatment processes or violate categorical pretreatment standards.
12. Stormwater, groundwater, or unpolluted water (except under a Wastewater Discharge Permit).

## User Classification and Permit Triggers

| Category | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Significant Industrial User (SIU)** | (a) Categorical Industrial User (CIU) subject to 40 CFR Parts 405–471 Categorical Pretreatment Standards; OR (b) discharges ≥25,000 gpd of process wastewater; OR contributes ≥5% of POTW design flow or treatment capacity; OR has Reasonable Potential to adversely affect the POTW |
| **Non-Significant Categorical Industrial User (NSCIU)** | CIU discharging ≤100 gpd of categorical wastewater, no Reasonable Potential, submits annual certification (§4.04.7) |
| **Middle-Tier CIU (MTCIU)** | CIU where categorical wastewater discharge ≤0.01% of RTP design dry weather hydraulic capacity (or 5,000 gpd) and ≤0.01% of organic treatment capacity and max allowable headworks loading |

## Permit Application Process

**Baseline Monitoring Report (BMR):** Required for all CIUs currently or scheduled to discharge to the Community Sanitary Sewer or RTP. Must be submitted within 180 days after the effective date of the applicable categorical pretreatment standard. New sources: submit BMR at least 90 days prior to commencement of discharge. BMR content requirements: 40 CFR §403.12(b).

**Periodic Compliance Reports:** All CIUs and SIUs submit semiannual compliance reports due **July 31** and **January 31** (§4.04.4).

**Notification of Changes:** At least 30 days advance notice required for substantial changes in volume or character of pollutants (§4.03).

**Hazardous Waste Discharge Notification (§4.01):** Written notification required to Monterey One Water, the Water Management Division Director, and state hazardous waste authorities for any discharge that would otherwise be a hazardous waste under 40 CFR Part 261. Threshold: >100 kg/month triggers full notification with constituent identification, mass/concentration estimates, and 12-month projections. Exception: ≤15 kg/month of non-acute hazardous waste does not require notification.

Contact for industrial user permits and pretreatment: **SourceControl@my1water.org**
