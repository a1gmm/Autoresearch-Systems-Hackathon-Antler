## Industrial Zone District

The City of Monterey has a **single industrial district**:

### I-R — Industrial, Administration, and Research District
**Code section:** §38-40
**Purpose:** To provide an environment exclusively for and conducive to the development of modern, large-scale administrative facilities, research institutions, and specialized manufacturing organizations, including non-nuisance production, distribution, and storage of goods. **Raw materials processing and bulk handling are excluded.**

This is the only industrial district designation in the City of Monterey zoning code. There is no M-1, M-2, or heavy industrial district within the city limits.

## Permitted and Conditional Uses

In the I-R district land use table:
- **"P"** = permitted by right
- **"U"** = requires a Use Permit (equivalent to CUP), reviewed by the Zoning Administrator (ZA) or Planning Commission (PC) depending on project complexity

Use classifications not listed in the I-R table are **prohibited**.

**Permitted (P) uses** include:
- Administrative and research office facilities
- Specialized (non-nuisance) manufacturing, production, and assembly
- Distribution and storage of finished goods (not bulk raw materials)
- Accessory uses customarily incidental to permitted industrial uses

**Use Permit (U) required** for uses with unusual site development features or operating characteristics requiring special consideration, including:
- Uses generating significant traffic, noise, or other off-site impacts
- Intensification of existing non-conforming industrial uses
- Any use not clearly within the permitted category per §38-40

Note: The land use table in §38-40 uses "P" and "U" designations. The municipal.codes website requires JavaScript rendering; consult the Planning Division at (831) 646-3885 for a specific use classification determination before applying.

## Use Permit (CUP) Process

Authority: **Monterey Municipal Code §38-156** (Use Permit findings); Article 22 of Chapter 38 (Use Permits and Variances).

**Reviewing body:**
- **Zoning Administrator (ZA):** smaller residential projects, minor changes in use, other minor projects.
- **Planning Commission (PC):** more complex projects, major changes in use, new construction with significant impacts.

**Required findings for approval (§38-156):**
1. The proposed use is in accord with the objectives of the Zoning Ordinance and the purposes of the zone.
2. The proposed use and operating conditions will be consistent with the General Plan; will not be detrimental to public health, safety, or welfare; and will not be detrimental to properties or improvements in the vicinity.
3. The proposed use will comply with any specific condition required for that use.

**Process flow:**
1. Submit Project Application Form + plans to Community Development Department.
2. Staff determines completeness; Development Review Committee staff review.
3. CEQA environmental review (exemption, Initial Study/MND, or EIR depending on project).
4. Architectural Review (if required).
5. ZA or PC public hearing (first-come, first-served scheduling after completeness).
6. Decision: approved, approved with conditions, denied, or continued for redesign.
7. If not appealed: decision is final 10 days after the hearing.

**Appeals:**
- ZA decision → appeal to Planning Commission.
- PC decision → appeal to City Council.

**CEQA note:** All projects require environmental review. Industrial projects with hazardous materials use, significant traffic, or sensitive site conditions may require technical studies (traffic, hazmat risk assessment, stormwater) and potentially an EIR.

## Submittal Requirements (Use Permit Application)

Required application materials (per Use Permit handout, updated January 1, 2023):
- Project Application Form (available at monterey.org)
- Detailed written description of use: land use type, products/services, hours of operation, number of employees, security provisions, parking.
- Electronic PDF plans at minimum 1/8"=1' scale for site plans, 1/4"=1' for all others.
- Cover sheet with project address, APN, applicant/owner/architect contact info, project description, vicinity map, sheet index, and parking plan.
- Site plan showing adjacent properties, parking lot, easements, landscaping, and trash enclosure.
- Floor plan identifying room uses, equipment layout, windows/doors, patio/storage areas, and adjacent land uses.
- Recycling and Waste Enclosure Requirements compliance documentation.

## Development Standards (I-R District)

Development standards for the I-R district (lot dimensions, setbacks, height, lot coverage) are specified in §38-40 of the municipal code. Key general rules:

- **Smaller lots** may be permitted with an approved development plan for a site with a minimum area of **10 acres**.
- All manufacturing operations must be conducted within enclosed structures (consistent with "non-nuisance" standard).
- Projects may require a General Development Plan for sites with multiple uses or subdivisions.

For exact numerical standards (setbacks, FAR, height limits), consult §38-40 directly via the Planning Division at (831) 646-3885 or at https://monterey.municipal.codes/Code/38-40.

## Environmental / Hazmat Conditions

The Planning Commission routinely imposes environmental conditions in Use Permits for industrial uses involving hazardous materials, including:
- Compliance with CUPA/HMMS Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) requirements.
- Stormwater pollution prevention plan (SWPPP) compliance per City Chapter 31.5.
- Spill containment / secondary containment requirements consistent with 2025 CFC Chapter 50 (Hazardous Materials) and MOW Ordinance 2023-01.
- CalARP applicability determination for facilities exceeding regulated substance thresholds.
