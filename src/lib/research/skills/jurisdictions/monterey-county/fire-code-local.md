## Adopting Ordinance & Edition

- **Municipal-code location:** Monterey County Code **Title 18** (Buildings and Construction), **Chapter 18.09** (Fire Code).
- **CFC edition on file:** The Municode TOC confirms section **18.09.010 – Adoption** exists, referencing the **2016 California Fire Code** (Title 24, Part 9) as the edition adopted in Chapter 18.09. The 2016 CFC is the current edition codified in Ch. 18.09 per Municode.
  - NOTE: California adopted the 2022 CFC statewide January 1, 2023, and the 2025 CFC statewide January 1, 2026. Confirm whether Monterey County has re-adopted via a subsequent ordinance; the Ch. 18.09 heading in Municode still references the 2016 edition as of June 2026. Call MCRFD Fire Prevention at (831) 455-1828 to verify.
- **Section 18.09.030 — Local Amendments:** This section exists (confirmed by Municode section index at `TIT18BUCO_CH18.09FICO`). The elaws.us mirror was unavailable at fetch time; direct ordinance text could not be extracted. Specific local amendment section numbers and thresholds are NOT confirmed by this fetch — do not cite specific numbers without direct verification.

## Fire Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

- **AHJ (unincorporated areas):** Monterey County Regional Fire Protection District (MCRFD)
  - Address: 19900 Portola Drive, Salinas, CA 93908
  - Phone: (831) 455-1828
  - Stations: 7 (Toro, Chualar, Laureles, Village, Mid Valley, Santa Lucia Preserve, East Garrison)
- **State Responsibility Areas (SRA):** CAL FIRE San Benito–Monterey Unit (BEU) has jurisdiction; confirm parcel fire-hazard severity zone at https://www.mcrfd.org/2025-fire-hazard-severity-zones
- **Incorporated cities:** Each has its own fire department (City of Monterey, Salinas, Seaside, etc.); see city-level jurisdiction files.
