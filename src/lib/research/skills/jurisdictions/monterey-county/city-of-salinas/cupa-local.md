> Research reference for the CUPA serving the City of Salinas. Salinas is NOT its own CUPA — the County of Monterey Environmental Health Bureau acts as the CUPA for the entire county including all city limits. All HMBP, CalARP, UST, APSA, HWG, and Tiered Permitting submissions go through the County.

## CUPA Identity

- **CUPA agency:** Monterey County Health Department, Environmental Health Bureau — Hazardous Materials Management Services (HMMS)
- **CUPA type:** County CUPA (serves all incorporated and unincorporated areas of Monterey County, including City of Salinas)
- **City of Salinas own-CUPA:** No — City of Salinas has no independent CUPA authority
- **Address:** 1270 Natividad Road, Salinas, CA 93906, Room 42
- **Phone:** (831) 755-4505
- **Reporting portal:** CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov — mandatory electronic submission for all regulated facilities

## Governing Ordinance

- **County ordinance chapter:** Monterey County Code **Chapter 10.65 — Hazardous Materials Registration** (Title 10, Health and Safety)
  - Municode reference: https://library.municode.com/ca/monterey_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT10HESA_CH10.65HAMARE_10.65.100MAFI
  - § 10.65.040 — Underground Storage Tank Permit
  - § 10.65.100 — Maximum Fines
- **State authority:** H&S Code § 25404 et seq. (CUPA certification framework); CalEPA certifies Monterey County HMMS as the CUPA.
- **City of Salinas local ordinance:** None — City of Salinas has no separate hazardous materials municipal code chapter. County HMMS authority flows from state CUPA certification and Monterey County Code Chapter 10.65.

## HMBP Reporting Thresholds

HMBP filing is required when a facility stores, handles, or uses hazardous materials at or above:
- **Quantity trigger:** 55 gallons (liquid), 500 pounds (solid), or 200 cubic feet (gas) — any single hazardous material.
- Facilities meeting thresholds must submit in CERS annually.

## CalARP Thresholds

CalARP Program applies when a regulated substance is present at or above its **Threshold Quantity (TQ)** specified in 19 CCR § 2770.5. Common examples:
- Anhydrous ammonia: 10,000 lb
- Chlorine: 2,500 lb
- Sulfur dioxide: 1,000 lb
- Facilities in Program 2 or 3 require a Risk Management Plan (RMP) submitted to EPA and a CUPA-reviewed Accident Prevention Program.

## Fee Schedule

The Monterey County HMMS CUPA fee schedule is set by the Board of Supervisors and adjusted annually. The fee schedule is administered under Chapter 10.65 and the County's annual fee resolution. **The specific tiered fee amounts are not publicly available in an online-accessible document as of June 2026** — contact HMMS directly for the current year's fee schedule.

## Inspection Frequency

- Annual inspections for Significant (SIC) facilities and facilities with hazardous materials, USTs, or CalARP programs.
- Semi-annual or more frequent inspections for facilities with significant compliance history.
- Priority facilities identified under the MS4 NPDES permit (Order No. R3-2019-0073) are subject to additional inspections by the City of Salinas Public Works Environmental Compliance program.

## Permit / Registration Process

1. Determine whether facility meets HMBP, UST, CalARP, APSA, HWG, or TP thresholds.
2. Register in CERS at https://cers.calepa.ca.gov — mandatory electronic reporting for all regulated programs.
3. Submit annual HMBP update by March 1 each year (state deadline).
4. Pay HMMS invoice for annual permit/inspection fee.
5. Schedule or receive HMMS inspection (typically unannounced for hazardous waste generators; scheduled for HMBP inspections).
