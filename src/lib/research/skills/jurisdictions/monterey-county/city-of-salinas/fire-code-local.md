> Research reference for the City of Salinas Fire Department AHJ. All fire-code requirements for new and existing industrial occupancies flow from this local adoption. Verify the live ordinance before quoting; current effective date is January 1, 2026.

## Adopted Code Edition

- **Adopted edition:** 2025 California Fire Code (CFC), Part 9 of the California Building Standards Code.
- **Adopting ordinance:** Ordinance No. (N.C.S.) — adopted by Salinas City Council at public hearing October 28, 2025; effective January 1, 2026.
- **Municipal Code chapter:** Salinas Municipal Code **Chapter 13, Article II** (Section 13-8 — Adoption; Section 13-9 — Amendments).
- **Repeals:** Ordinance No. 2584 and Ordinance No. 2621.
- **CFC Appendix Chapters adopted:** B, C, D, E, F, G, and I (as published by the International Code Council).
- **Code copy on file:** Permit Center, 65 West Alisal Street, Salinas, CA 93901.

## Local Amendments (Chapter 13, Section 13-9)

All amendments are to the 2025 CFC as adopted by Salinas. Key industrial-relevant amendments:

### Section 101.1 — Title
Amended: regulations known as "Fire Code of the City of Salinas."

### Section 103 — Fire Prevention Bureau
Amended: establishes the Fire Prevention Bureau within the Salinas Fire Department under the fire code official. The Bureau enforces this code and submits annual statistics to the City Manager and City Council. Section 103.2 deleted.

### Section 104.12 — Cost Recovery (added)
**104.12.1** Establishes authority to obtain reimbursement for emergency response expenses.  
**104.12.2(1)** Negligent individuals who cause a fire or hazardous substance emergency are liable for reimbursement (H&S Code § 13000 et seq.).  
**104.12.2(2)** DUI-related emergency responses are reimbursable under Government Code §§ 53150–53158.

### Section 105.1.1.1 — Permits Required (added)
All fire permits and inspections by the Salinas Fire Department are charged fees per **Salinas City Code Chapter 11B-4, Schedule of Fees and Service Charges**.

### Section 105.3.9 — Expense Recovery (added)
Establishes cost-recovery authority for permits, inspections, and enforcement actions.

### Section 105.5.60 — Christmas Tree Lots (added)
Operational permit required from Salinas Fire Department to operate a Christmas tree lot.

### Section 113.4 — Violation Penalties (amended)
Violations punishable by fine up to **$1,000 or imprisonment up to 30 days**, or both. Each day a continuing violation constitutes a separate offense.

### Section 113.4.1 — Abatement (amended)
Fire code official authorized to abate violations by force account or contract; costs charged to the owner or assessed to the property per Salinas Municipal Code.

### Section 113.4.2 — Enforcement (added)
Fire code official and designees authorized under California Penal Code § 836.5 to arrest without warrant for code violations in their presence.

### Section 113.5 — Recordation of Violations (added)
Fire chief may record a notice of fire code violation in the County Recorder's office where a building has a code violation.

### Section 202 — Definitions (amended)
Added definitions for: "All weather driving surface" (asphalt, concrete, or other surface capable of supporting 75,000 lb fire apparatus), "Combustible Vegetation," "Fire Hazard," "Hookah," "Legal representative of the jurisdiction" (Salinas city attorney), "Municipality" (City of Salinas), "Public Nuisance," "Structure," "Travel time," and "Response Time."

### Section 307.2.1 — Open Burning Authorization (amended)
Open burning requires prior approval from the **Monterey Bay Air Resource District** (MBARD). Pile burning during declared open burn season is at Fire Chief's discretion per CalFire/MBARD guidelines.

### Section 307.6 — General Burning Prohibitions (added)
Trash, yard waste, rubbish, and paper prohibited as fuel for bonfires or outdoor fireplaces. Nuisance smoke or odor prohibited; fire code official may order extinguishment.

### Section 503 — Fire Apparatus Access Roads (multiple subsections amended)
- **503.1:** Roads required per Sections 503.1.1–503.1.3 and Appendix D.
- **503.2.1 (Dimensions):** Unobstructed width not less than 20 ft; 26 ft where hydrant or FDC is present. Security gate lanes minimum 15 ft each.
- **503.2.3 (Surface):** Designed to support minimum 75,000 lb apparatus; all-weather surface required. Alternate surfaces (Grass Crete, Turf Block) allowed by AHJ approval with civil/soils engineer certification.
- **503.2.4 (Turning Radius):** Minimum 40 ft outside, 20 ft inside turning radius.
- **503.2.6 (Bridges):** AASHTO HS-20 (25-ton) standard.
- **503.2.7 (Grade):** Maximum 10%; grades over 10% require mitigation.
- **503.2.8 (Approach/Departure):** Maximum 8 degrees.
- **503.6 (Security Gates):** Located minimum 30 ft from road edge; equipped with emergency key switch; gates serving 4+ residences require approved traffic control-activating strobe sensor.

### Section 505.3 — Response Map Updates (added)
New developments must provide PDF/CAD map updates for new structures, hydrants, and roadways.

### Section 506.1 — Key Box (amended)
Buildings with monitored fire alarm or sprinkler systems must have an approved key box. Where hazardous materials are stored, a keyed cabinet containing hazardous materials data sheets (SDS) is required on-site in an approved location.
