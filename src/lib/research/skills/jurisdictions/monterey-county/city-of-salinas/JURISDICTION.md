## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **CUPA agency:** Monterey County Health Department, Environmental Health Bureau — Hazardous Materials Management Services (HMMS). City of Salinas is **not its own CUPA**; the County serves the entire county including Salinas.
- **Address:** 1270 Natividad Road, Salinas, CA 93906 (Room 42 of the Health Department building).
- **Phone:** (831) 755-4505.
- **Programs administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), CalARP, Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), Hazardous Waste Generator (HWG), and Tiered Permitting.
- **Reporting portal:** CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov; mandatory electronic submission for all regulated facilities.
- **Local ordinance:** No separate City of Salinas CUPA ordinance; County HMMS authority flows from state CUPA certification and Monterey County code.
- Source: https://www.countyofmonterey.gov/government/departments-a-h/health/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-management/cupa-programs
- Source: https://www.countyofmonterey.gov/government/departments-a-h/health/environmental-health/hazardous-material-management/cers-california-electronic-reporting-system

### 2. Fire AHJ — Fire Prevention

- **AHJ:** City of Salinas Fire Department, Fire Prevention Bureau (municipal; not County Fire).
- **Adopted code:** 2025 California Fire Code (CFC), including Appendix Chapters B, C, D, E, F, G & I — adopted by Salinas Municipal Code Chapter 13, Article II, Section 13-8. Supersedes Ordinance No. 2584/2621. Effective January 1, 2026.
- **Local amendments (Chapter 13, Section 13-9):**
  - Sec. 103: Fire Prevention Bureau established within the Fire Department under the fire code official.
  - Sec. 104.12 added: Cost-recovery authority for emergency response reimbursement (H&S Code § 13000 et seq.; Gov. Code §§ 53150–53158).
  - Sec. 105.1.1.1 added: All fire permits and inspections charged per Salinas City Code Chapter 11B-4 Schedule of Fees and Service Charges.
  - Sec. 105.3.9 added: Expense-recovery authority for permits, inspections, and enforcement actions.
  - Sec. 113.4 amended: Violations punishable by up to $1,000 fine or 30 days imprisonment; each day a separate offense.
- **Permit types (FY 2023-24 fee schedule):** CFC new-construction permit (fire protection systems/processes) $220.75; renewable operational permits $203.50; one-time special event permits $105.00; plan review $124.75/hr; on-site inspection — new construction $174.25/hr, existing operational $174.25/hr, business/assembly by square footage ($203.50–$506.00/yr).
- **Permit center / copies on file:** 65 West Alisal Street, Salinas, CA 93901.

### 3. POTW / Industrial Wastewater — Pretreatment

- **POTW:** City of Salinas Public Works Department — Environmental & Maintenance Services, Wastewater Division operates a separate **Industrial Wastewater Treatment Facility (IWTF)**, distinct from the sanitary sewer system.
- **Regulatory permit:** Waste Discharge Requirement (WDR) No. R3-2004-0066 (WDID 3 27011003), issued by the Central Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB-3). WDR replaced NPDES in 2002. Effluent disposed to percolation/evaporation ponds and rapid-infiltration drying beds along the Salinas River.
- **Active industrial users:** 22 permitted connections (agriculture-based industries) as of 2025 Annual Report.
- **Pretreatment program:** Federally required under 40 CFR 403; EPA-approved since May 1983. Semi-annual SIU sampling (April and October).
- **Local sewer-use ordinance:** Salinas Municipal Code **Chapter 36** — Industrial Wastewater and Water Discharge Ordinance (Municode node: `PTIITHCO_CH36INWAWACODI`). Key provision: Chapter 36-20.4 prohibits illicit discharges and unauthorized stormwater, chemical dumping, and grease into the collection system.
- **Contact:** Ray Lerma, Wastewater Manager, 426 Work Street, Salinas, CA 93901, (831) 758-7150.
- **IWP permit portal:** [VERIFY - no online self-service portal found; contact Wastewater Division directly].
- Source (annual report 2025): https://www.salinas.gov/files/sharedassets/city/v/1/public-works/city-of-salinas-2025-i.w.-annual-report-final-pdf.pdf
- Source (IW facility page): https://www.salinas.gov/Your-Government/Departments/Public-Works/PW-Maintenance-Services/Environmental-Maintenance-Services/Industrial-Waste-Facility

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater

- **Program type:** Phase I MS4 (individual permit).
- **NPDES permit:** Order No. R3-2019-0073, issued by the Central Coast RWQCB; renewed September 20, 2019, effective October 1, 2019.
- **Local stormwater ordinance:** Salinas Municipal Code **Chapter 29** — Stormwater Management and Discharge Control (Municode node: `PTIITHCO_CH29STMADICO`). The ordinance was revised in 2007 to comply with NPDES requirements. Post-construction stormwater management standards (August 2021) apply to new development and significant redevelopment.
- **Administered by:** City of Salinas Public Works Department, Environmental Compliance program.
- **Priority industrial facility inspections** are required under the MS4 permit.
- Source (RWQCB MS4 page): https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/centralcoast/water_issues/programs/stormwater/salinas.html
- Source (Municode Ch. 29): https://library.municode.com/ca/salinas/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIITHCO_CH29STMADICO

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts & CUP Process

- **Zoning code:** Salinas Municipal Code **Chapter 37** — Zoning (Municode node: `PTIITHCO_CH37ZO`).
- **Industrial zoning districts:**
  - **IG** — Industrial-General (heaviest industrial use; electrically-charged fences require building permit rather than CUP)
  - **IBP** — Industrial-Business Park (lighter industrial / R&D / office-industrial)
  - **IGC** — Industrial-General Commercial
- **CUP process:** Conditional Use Permits issued under Chapter 37, Article VI, Division 8. Planning Commission is the decision-maker; located at 200 Lincoln Ave / staff at Community Development Department.
- **Planning Dept contact:** (831) 758-7206; Community Development, 65 W Alisal St, Salinas, CA 93901.
- **Online permit portal (eTRAKiT):** https://pc.ci.salinas.ca.us/eTRAKiT/ — building and planning permit submissions.
- **Zoning map:** https://salinas-gis.ci.salinas.ca.us/portal/apps/mapviewer/index.html?webmap=27f26cc06a654c3f81b04b30957b14ad
