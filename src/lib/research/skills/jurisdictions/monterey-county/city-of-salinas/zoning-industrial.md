> Research reference for City of Salinas industrial zoning. The Zoning Code was originally adopted as Ordinance No. 2463 on November 7, 2006. Chapter 37 has been amended several times since, including Ordinance No. 2674 (N.C.S.) in late 2023 (ZCA 2023-001) amending Section 37-50.090 for electrically-charged fences in the IG district.

## Industrial Zone Districts

Three industrial base zoning districts are established in Table 37-20.10 (Article II, Section 37-20.010):

### IG — Industrial-General

- **Purpose:** Heaviest general industrial use permitted under the Salinas Zoning Code. Intended for manufacturing, heavy processing, warehousing, outdoor storage, and related industrial operations.
- **Outdoor storage:** Permitted subject to screening/design standards (Section 37-30.330).
- **Hazardous materials storage:** Permitted; subject to fire code (Chapter 13) and CUPA requirements.
- **Security fences:** Electrically-charged wires or fences allowed **by building permit** (not CUP) in IG — this is a IG-specific allowance distinguishing it from IBP/IGC (Ordinance No. 2674, amending Section 37-50.090(l)(2)).
- **Barbed/razor wire:** Allowed in IG when not visible from an arterial, collector street, or U.S. Highway 101.

### IBP — Industrial-Business Park

- **Purpose:** Lighter industrial, R&D, office-industrial, and technology uses in a campus or park setting. Designed to be compatible with adjacent non-industrial uses.
- **Security fences:** Electrically-charged wires or fences allowed by **conditional use permit** (CUP) per Section 37-50.090(l)(1) — CUP required (as opposed to building permit in IG).
- **Barbed/razor wire:** Allowed in IBP if not visible from any public right-of-way, subject to Site Plan Review approval.
- **Chain link:** Prohibited in front or corner side yards (applicable to all industrial districts).

### IGC — Industrial-General Commercial

- **Purpose:** Industrial uses combined with commercial retail/service uses. Bridges general industrial and commercial zones.
- **Security fences:** Electrically-charged wires or fences allowed by **conditional use permit** (CUP) per Section 37-50.090(l)(1).
- **Barbed/razor wire:** Allowed in IGC if not visible from any public right-of-way, subject to Site Plan Review approval.

### Electrically-Charged Fence Standards (all three districts — Section 37-50.090(l))

Applicable to IG (building permit), IBP (CUP), and IGC (CUP):
- Maximum height: **10 feet**
- Perimeter fence or wall minimum 6 feet in height required on exterior side
- Minimum 12-inch clearance between electric fence and perimeter fence
- Energizer: commercial storage battery, max 12 volts DC, primarily solar-charged
- Warning signs on both sides at intervals not greater than 60 feet
- Prohibited adjacent to existing or planned residential development
- Restricted to sites with limited public access
- Allowed for security purposes only (equipment, vehicles, outdoor storage areas)

## Ag-Industrial Center Specific Plan Overlay

A separate specific plan — **Salinas Ag-Industrial Center Specific Plan** (adopted 2009) — governs approximately 257 acres in the southern part of the city (bounded by Abbott St., Harris Road, and Dayton Street area). Within the Specific Plan area:

- Two land use classifications replace Chapter 37 industrial districts: **Agricultural-Industrial (IA)** and **Abbott Street Frontage Zone (ASFZ)**
- Table 3-1 of the Specific Plan completely replaces Section 37-30.310, Table 37-30.130, and associated notes for the Specific Plan area
- Uses allowed in ASFZ broadly correspond to non-agricultural IG-type uses (broader spectrum)
- Industrial uses in the Specific Plan area are classified as Major Agricultural Processing (produce processing, food products, wineries) or Minor Agricultural Processing (cooling, container manufacturing, machinery manufacturing, limited industrial complexes)
- Airport (AP) Overlay District applies to portions near Salinas Municipal Airport (Chapter 37, Article IV, Division 7)

Specific Plan document: https://www.salinas.gov/files/sharedassets/city/v/1/community-development/current-planning/salinas-ag-industrial-specific-plan.pdf

## CUP Process

- **Decision-maker:** Planning Commission (standard CUPs); City Planner for minor/administrative determinations
- **Governing section:** Chapter 37, Article VI, Division 8 — Conditional Use Permits (Section 37-60.xxx series)
- **Application submittal:** Community Development Department, 65 W Alisal St, Salinas, CA 93901
- **Online permit portal (eTRAKiT):** https://pc.ci.salinas.ca.us/eTRAKiT/ — building and planning permit submissions online
- **Planning Dept contact:** (831) 758-7206
- **Typical CUP triggers in industrial zones:** electrically-charged fences (IBP and IGC), adult entertainment facilities, certain commercial uses in IGC, uses requiring compatibility review
- **Site Plan Review (SPR):** Required for many uses in industrial districts that do not require a full CUP — lower-level discretionary review by staff

## Development Standards Reference

- **Industrial design standards:** Section 37-30.330
- **Fences/walls/hedges in industrial districts:** Section 37-50.090(d)(3) — front/corner side yards: max 3 ft (sight-obscuring) or 8 ft (non-sight-obscuring, setback 10 ft+); interior/rear yards: max 8 ft; electric fences: max 10 ft
- **Parking/driveway reductions:** Chapter 37, Article V, Division 2 (referenced in Section 503.2.1 of Fire Code)
- **Planned Unit Development Permits:** Article VI, Division 13 (Section 37-60.990)
