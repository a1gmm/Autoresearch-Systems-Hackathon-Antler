> Research reference for the City of Salinas industrial wastewater system. Salinas operates a unique THIRD collection system — separate from sanitary sewer and storm sewer — exclusively for agricultural industrial wastewater. It is not a conventional POTW/pretreatment system; industrial users connect to the Industrial Wastewater Treatment Facility (IWTF), not to the sanitary sewer. Verify with the Wastewater Division before assuming standard 40 CFR 403 categorical standards apply in the conventional sense.

## POTW / IWTF Identity

- **Operator:** City of Salinas Public Works Department — Environmental & Maintenance Services, Wastewater Division
- **Facility name:** Salinas Industrial Wastewater Treatment Facility (IWTF)
- **Type:** Agricultural industrial wastewater collection and treatment facility — facultative aeration lagoon + percolation/evaporation ponds + rapid-infiltration drying beds
- **Location:** Along the Salinas River (south of city limits); receiving station at Hitchcock Road area
- **WDR (regulatory permit):** Waste Discharge Requirement No. **R3-2004-0066** (WDID 3 27011003), issued by the Central Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB-3)
- **Permit type:** WDR (not NPDES) — effluent disposed to land (percolation ponds and rapid-infiltration beds), not discharged to a surface water body
- **Effluent disposal limit:** 3.0 MGD (on-site disposal), established July 23, 2024 by revised Notice of Applicability from CCRWQCB
- **Under Monterey One Water (M1W) agreement:** Additional effluent pumped to Regional Treatment Plant (RTP) as needed for capacity; 239.2 million gallons diverted in 2025

## Pretreatment Program

- **EPA-approved:** May 1983 (per 40 CFR Part 403 requirements)
- **Nature of program:** Non-conventional — all 22 permitted Industrial Users (IUs) are Non-Categorical (agriculture-based, no federal categorical pretreatment standards apply)
- **Significant Industrial Users (SIUs) requiring CCRWQCB sampling:** Semi-annual (April and October); includes major produce processors, cold-storage, and packing facilities
- **Flow (2025):** Annual average influent 2.48 MGD; total received approximately 906 million gallons in 2025

## Governing Ordinance

- **Sewer-use/IW ordinance:** Salinas Municipal Code **Chapter 36 — Industrial Wastewater and Water Discharge Ordinance**
  - Municode node: `PTIITHCO_CH36INWAWACODI`
  - URL: https://library.municode.com/ca/salinas/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIITHCO_CH36INWAWACODI
- **Key prohibition (§ 36-20.4):** Illicit discharges and unauthorized stormwater, chemical dumping, and grease into the collection system are prohibited.
- **General rule:** Municipal sanitary sewage flows are **prohibited** in the Industrial Wastewater System (separate system).

## Salt Management

Active educational program: Wastewater Division staff work with industrial users to reduce sodium loading. Options encouraged: high-efficiency water softeners (flow/hardness sensor type), potassium chloride substitution, portable exchange water softener service, elimination of unused salt slurry discharges.
