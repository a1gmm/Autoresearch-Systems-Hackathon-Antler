## Industrial Zone Districts

Seaside has **one industrial zoning district**: **M-1 Industrial** (SMC §§ 17.xx.100–17.xx.105, derived from original Zoning Ordinance No. 83-10, §§ 3.100–3.105). There is no M-2 or heavy industrial zone. A Military zone (Ch. 17.18 Special Purpose Zones) applies only to former Fort Ord federal lands and is not an industrial manufacturing zone.

The updated codified Title 17 (through Ord. 2031) also includes **CH — Heavy Commercial** (Ch. 17.14), which is applied to areas suitable for wholesale, heavy commercial, and light industrial uses; CH functions as the transitional zone between general commercial and M-1 industrial.

## M-1 Industrial Zone

**Purpose (§ 3.101):** To provide for limited industrial development without adversely affecting Seaside's resort character.

### Outright Permitted Uses (§ 3.102 — no permit required)

1. Assembling, fabricating, storage, or repair of electrical appliances, HVAC equipment, musical instruments, signs, or toys
2. Manufacturing, compounding, processing, packaging, or storage of cosmetics, drugs, or foods and beverages
3. Manufacturing, assembling, treating, storage, or repair of articles from previously prepared materials: bone, cellophane, clay, cork, fabric, fur, glass, hair, leather, paper, plastics, precious/semi-precious stone or metal, shell, wire, or wood
4. Commercial or industrial laundry
5. Distributing, wholesaling, and warehousing
6. Experimental firm or testing laboratory
7. Ice or cold storage plant
8. Stone monument works
9. Machine shop primarily for heavy repairs
10. Maintenance, repair, or minor modification to existing roads, sidewalks, bike paths, and public utilities (new right-of-way ≤ 25% of existing ROW for the project area)

### Conditional Uses (§ 3.103 — require CUP per Article 6 / Ch. 17.62)

1. Any non-residential use permitted outright or conditionally in a C-3 (General Commercial) Zone
2. Fish, meat, or other food product processing
3. Freighting terminal
4. Bulk petroleum storage plants
5. Blacksmithing, electroplating, machine shop, or welding shop
6. Foundry casting lightweight, non-ferrous metals
7. Major modification of existing roads and public facilities; new roads not part of a partition or subdivision

### Prohibited Uses (§ 3.104 — expressly prohibited)

1. **All residential uses**
2. **Any use constituting a potential hazard or nuisance in terms of fire, explosive, pollution, or excessive noise**

> EHS ALERT: The § 3.104(2) prohibition is broadly worded. Any industrial operation involving chemical handling, bulk flammables, explosives, or operations with significant noise/air impacts may be characterized as prohibited outright, requiring a variance or alternative siting rather than a CUP. This is particularly relevant for CalARP-regulated processes, solvent handling, and bulk hazardous material storage.

### Development Standards (§ 3.105)

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Lot size | No minimum |
| Density | No requirements |
| Front/side/rear yards | None required (except buffer below) |
| Buffer setback | 50 ft minimum where M-1 abuts or faces residential or commercial zone |
| Buffer screening | Landscaping, fencing, or other conditions to preserve adjacent zone character |
| Height limit | 45 ft maximum |
| Off-street parking | Per § 4.100 (parking standards) |
| Open storage screening | Sight-obscuring fence ≥ 6 ft where open storage abuts or faces residential or commercial zone |

## CUP / Use Permit Process (SMC Ch. 17.62)

**Chapter 17.62 — Permit Review and Decisions** governs all planning permit approvals, including Conditional Use Permits (CUPs) for M-1 conditional uses.

**Decision authority:**
- Minor/administrative CUPs: Zoning Administrator / Resource Management Services Director
- Full CUPs: **Planning Commission** (public hearing required)
- Appeals: City Council (15-day appeal window after decision date)

**Required findings for CUP approval:**
1. The proposed use is conditionally permitted within the zone and would not impair the integrity and character of that zoning district
2. The proposal complies with all applicable provisions of SMC Title 17
3. The proposal is consistent with the General Plan

**Application portal:** City of Seaside Planning Division — 440 Harcourt Avenue, Seaside, CA 93955; (831) 899-6700; https://www.ci.seaside.ca.us/267/Permits-Information

**Permit implementation:** Ch. 17.64 — Permit Implementation, Time Limits, and Extensions (governs permit expiration, extensions, and commencement of use requirements)

**Decision finality:** A CUP decision does not become final until 15 days after the date of approval, during which any party may appeal to the City Council.

## M-Military Zone Note (Ch. 17.18)

SMC Ch. 17.18 (Special Purpose Zones) contains a Military (M) zone applicable only to former Fort Ord federal lands in the northern part of the city (now largely the Campus Town / CSUMB area, governed by the Campus Town Specific Plan adopted under Ord. 2031 Ch. 17.20). The M-military zone is not an industrial manufacturing zone and should not be confused with M-1 Industrial.
