## Adopted Edition

The City of Seaside adopted the **2022 California Fire Code (CFC), Title 24, Part 9** by reference in **SMC Chapter 15.04 — Building and International Codes Adopted**. Adoption authority: **Ordinance No. 2017 § 2, 2022**. One copy of the CFC is filed with the Building Official for public examination.

Effective date: **January 1, 2023**. All projects submitted on or after that date must comply with the 2022 California Title 24 codes and Seaside's local amendments.

> **2025/2026 note:** The 2025 California Building Standards Code (Title 24) replaced the 2022 edition statewide on 2026-01-01. Confirm the currently-adopted edition with the Fire Department before citing an edition for compliance purposes.

## Local Amendments (Ord. 2017, 2022)

Ordinance 2017 contains Seaside's local Title 24 amendments. The following local amendments are confirmed from published sources:

**Roof Coverings (R902.1.1):** Any new roof on a new or existing structure, and any re-roofing of 50% or more of the total roof area within a one-year period, within all Fire Hazard Severity Zones, shall be of a fire-retardant (Class A) roof covering. This is more restrictive than the base CFC.

**Residential Sprinklers (R313.1 / R313.2):** Seaside deleted the exceptions from CFC Sections R313.1 and R313.2, making residential fire-sprinkler requirements more restrictive than the state baseline.

**Separation / Type X Gypsum (R302.1 / Table R302.6):** Section R302.1 exceptions were deleted; Table R302.6 amended to require not less than 5/8-inch Type X gypsum board in all applicable columns.

**Specific local amendments for hazmat permit threshold amounts and high-piled storage** are not enumerated on public-facing city pages — contact the Fire Prevention Division directly to obtain the full local-amendment exhibit attached to Ord. 2017.

## Fire Hazard Severity Zone (FHSZ) Update (2025)

Per AB 211, updated FHSZ maps were delivered to Seaside FD on **March 10, 2025**. The City had 120 days to adopt by ordinance (deadline: July 8, 2025). Adoption schedule: first reading May 1, second reading May 15, effective June 15, 2025. The FD cannot downgrade hazard levels — only upgrade where justified.

## Permit Types

Permits issued by Seaside Fire Prevention include:
- Building plan checks (fire & life safety review)
- Business occupancy inspections
- Tent permits (required for tents ≥ 200 sq ft)
- Knox Box rapid-entry program (loaner program available)
- Fireworks safety permits

**Fee schedule:** Not publicly itemized online. Contact Fire Prevention at (831) 899-6790 for current fees.

## Hazmat Program Note

Seaside FD operates a Hazmat program with a Cal OES Type II Hazmat apparatus that responds to incidents within Seaside and statewide on mutual aid. HMBP/hazardous-materials business registration is administered by the County CUPA (Monterey County HMMS), not by Seaside FD — see cupa-local.md.
