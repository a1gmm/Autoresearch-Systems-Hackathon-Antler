## Agency

**County of Monterey Health Department — Environmental Health Bureau, Hazardous Materials Management Services (HMMS)**

Seaside is **not** its own CUPA. The City of Seaside lies within the Monterey County CUPA jurisdiction. All HMBP, UST, hazardous waste generator, CalARP, and APSA compliance for businesses in Seaside is administered by the County HMMS.

**CUPA designation authority:** Monterey County HMMS is designated as the sole CUPA for Monterey County, including all incorporated cities.

## Programs Administered

Monterey County HMMS administers all six Unified Program elements:

| Program | Notes |
|---|---|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) | Required for facilities storing/handling hazardous materials above threshold quantities |
| Underground Storage Tank (UST) | Permit, inspection, leak detection oversight |
| Hazardous Waste Generator | Small and large quantity generator oversight |
| California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) | For facilities with regulated substances above RMP thresholds |
| Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) | For facilities with aboveground petroleum storage |
| Tiered Permitting (Hazardous Waste Treatment) | Permit-by-rule, conditional authorization, standardized permit |

## Reporting Portal

**CERS — California Environmental Reporting System**
Businesses submit HMBP, UST, and CalARP reports electronically through CERS at https://cers.calepa.ca.gov.

Monterey County CERS landing page: https://www.countyofmonterey.gov/government/departments-a-h/health/environmental-health/hazardous-material-management/cers-california-electronic-reporting-system

## HMBP Threshold Quantities

HMBP is required when a facility handles or stores:
- **Hazardous materials** in quantities at or above: 55 gallons (liquid), 500 pounds (solid), or 200 cubic feet (gas) at any one time
- **Acutely hazardous materials (AHMs)** in quantities at or above thresholds defined in HSC § 25532 (CalARP)
- Any quantity of a listed extremely hazardous substance (EHS) above the threshold planning quantity (TPQ) under EPCRA

## Local Hazardous Materials Ordinance

Seaside has **no independent local hazardous materials ordinance** — the City does not have its own CUPA chapter in the Seaside Municipal Code. The applicable ordinance framework is the state Unified Program statutes (HSC Division 20, Chapter 6.95 et seq.) as implemented by Monterey County HMMS.

The Seaside Municipal Code (Title 8 — Health & Safety) does not enumerate a separate hazmat chapter for HMBP or UST programs; those are exclusively administered at the county level via CERS.
