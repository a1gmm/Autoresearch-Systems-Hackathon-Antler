## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA / Hazardous Materials (HMBP, UST, CalARP, Hazardous Waste)

- **CUPA agency:** County of Monterey Health Department — Environmental Health Bureau, Hazardous Materials Management Services (HMMS). Seaside is **not** its own CUPA; the county serves all unincorporated and incorporated areas in Monterey County, including the City of Seaside.
- **HMBP reporting portal:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://www.countyofmonterey.gov/government/departments-a-h/health/environmental-health/hazardous-material-management/cers-california-electronic-reporting-system
- **CUPA programs administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Underground Storage Tank (UST), Hazardous Waste Generator, California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), Tiered Permitting.
- **Fees:** Board of Supervisors-approved schedule effective July 1, 2025 (prior schedule July 1, 2023). State surcharges as of Oct 13, 2023: CalARP component $370/regulated business/year; Unified Program Oversight component $94/regulated business/year.
- **Main CUPA office:** 1270 Natividad Road, Salinas, CA 93906 — (831) 755-4505.

### 3. POTW / Sewer Use & Industrial Pretreatment

- **Collection agency:** Seaside County Sanitation District (SCSD) — special district serving Del Rey Oaks, Sand City, and Seaside (~70 miles of pipeline, 2,400 acres, ~30,000 residents). Address: 440 Harcourt Avenue, Seaside, CA 93955 — (831) 899-6835; SCSD@ci.seaside.ca.us.
- **Treatment plant (POTW):** Monterey Regional Water Pollution Control Agency (MRWPCA) regional treatment plant — wastewater is pumped from SCSD to MRWPCA for treatment.
- **Governing discharge ordinance:** SCSD Ordinance 21 (adopted May 9, 2017) incorporates **MRWPCA Ordinance No. 2008-01** ("Waste Discharge Ordinance of the Monterey Regional Water Pollution Control Agency") as the applicable waste discharge framework. SCSD Code current through Ord. 21.
- **Grease/FOG ordinance:** SCSD Ordinance 15 (Oct 12, 2004) requires grease traps, grease interceptors, or comparable oil/grease removal devices for food service establishments.
- **Industrial pretreatment:** Governed by MRWPCA Ord. 2008-01 (incorporated by SCSD Ord. 21). Specific industrial waste permit (IWP) process and thresholds [VERIFY - contact SCSD or MRWPCA directly for IWP application].

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts & CUP Process

- **Zoning code:** Title 17 (Zoning Ordinance Code) of the Seaside Municipal Code; implements the City General Plan. Also codified as Zoning Ordinance No. 83-10 (originally adopted 1983, revised through February 2019).
- **Industrial zone:** **M-1 Industrial** (Zoning Ordinance §§ 3.100–3.105) — the only industrial zone designated in Seaside's zoning ordinance. Purpose: limited industrial development without adversely affecting Seaside's resort character.
  - *Outright permitted uses (§ 3.102):* assembly/fabrication/storage/repair of appliances, HVAC equipment, instruments; manufacturing/processing/packaging of cosmetics, drugs, food/beverages; commercial laundry; wholesale/warehousing; testing laboratory; ice/cold storage; machine shop.
  - *Conditional uses (§ 3.103):* C-3 uses, fish/meat processing, freight terminal, bulk petroleum storage, blacksmithing/electroplating/welding shop, foundry (light non-ferrous metals). Conditional uses require approval per Article 6 (Use Permit process).
  - *Prohibited (§ 3.104):* all residential uses; any use constituting a potential hazard or nuisance in terms of fire, explosives, pollution, or excessive noise.
  - *Development standards (§ 3.105):* no minimum lot size; 45 ft max height; 50 ft buffer setback where M-1 abuts residential or commercial zone.
- **CUP / Use Permit process:** Article 6 of Zoning Ordinance No. 83-10; also codified in SMC Title 17 Ch. 17.62 (Permit Review and Decisions). Administered by Resource Management Services Director / Zoning Administrator / Planning Commission.
- **No M-2 or heavier industrial zone** exists in Seaside's code (M military zone in Ch. 17.18 applies only to former Fort Ord federal lands).

## GOTCHA Notes

- **Fire contract:** Seaside FD provides fire protection to neighboring Del Rey Oaks under contract; Del Rey Oaks fire AHJ is Seaside FD, not a separate department or the county.
- **No own CUPA:** Seaside businesses submit HMBP/UST/hazardous waste reports through CERS to Monterey County HMMS, not to any city department.
- **M-1 use prohibition:** Any industrial use constituting a "potential hazard or nuisance in terms of fire, explosive, pollution or excessive noise" is expressly prohibited outright in M-1 and would require a variance or alternative siting — relevant for chemical handling, bulk flammables, or noisy operations.
- **Sewer pretreatment flows through MRWPCA:** Industrial users discharging to SCSD are regulated under MRWPCA Ord. 2008-01; contact MRWPCA or SCSD to confirm whether an Industrial User permit (IUP/IWP) is required before discharge.
