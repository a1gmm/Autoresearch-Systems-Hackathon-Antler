## Collection Agency (POTW Contributor)

**Seaside County Sanitation District (SCSD)**
A special district — not a city department — serving Del Rey Oaks, Sand City, and the City of Seaside.
~70 miles of pipeline, ~2,400 service acres, ~30,000 residents.

Address: 440 Harcourt Avenue, Seaside, CA 93955
Phone: (831) 899-6835

## Treatment Plant (POTW)

SCSD does **not** operate its own treatment plant. Collected wastewater is pumped to the **Monterey Regional Water Pollution Control Agency (MRWPCA)** regional plant for treatment and disposal.

MRWPCA: 5 Harris Court, Building D, Monterey, CA 93940
Web: https://montereyonewater.org/

## Governing Discharge Ordinance

### SCSD Ordinance 21 (adopted May 9, 2017)

SCSD's operative waste discharge ordinance. Ordinance 21 incorporates by reference **MRWPCA Ordinance No. 2008-01** ("Wastewater Discharge Ordinance of the Monterey Regional Water Pollution Control Agency") as the controlling framework for all discharge regulations, pretreatment standards, and industrial user permits applicable to SCSD customers.

SCSD Code is current through Ordinance 21. Prior user-fee ordinances: Ord. 19 (Oct 2015) and Ord. 20 (2016).

Ordinance list: https://scsdonline.org/district-ordinances/

### MRWPCA Ordinance 2008-01

Full title: "Wastewater Discharge Ordinance of the Monterey Regional Water Pollution Control Agency"
PDF: https://montereyonewater.org/DocumentCenter/View/210/Ordinance-2008-01-PDF

Incorporated into SCSD's regulatory framework by Ord. 21. Establishes:
- General and specific prohibitions on discharges (Article 2)
- Local discharge limits (§ 2.10)
- Pretreatment standards and permit requirements (Article 4, § 4.05)
- Enforcement and penalty provisions (Articles 5–6)

## Local Discharge Limits (MRWPCA Ord. 2008-01, § 2.10)

**§ 2.10.1 — Metal concentration limits (no person shall discharge wastewater containing in excess of):**

| Pollutant | Limit |
|---|---|
| Arsenic | 0.42 mg/L |
| Cadmium | 3.4 mg/L |
| Copper | 4.3 mg/L |
| Cyanide | 0.73 mg/L |
| Lead | 3.0 mg/L |
| Mercury | 0.018 mg/L |
| Nickel | 3.5 mg/L |
| Silver | 2.3 mg/L |
| Total Chromium | 2.7 mg/L |
| Zinc | 2.6 mg/L |

**§ 2.10.2 — Additional prohibitions:**
- Temperature: shall not exceed 150°F (65.5°C)
- pH: shall not be lower than 6.0 or higher than 10.5
- Phenolic compounds: shall not exceed 8.1 mg/L
- Oil and grease, ammonia, BOD, TSS, and toxic organics: prohibited in quantities that cause or significantly contribute to interference, pass-through, NPDES permit violations, or sludge disposal restriction violations; significant dischargers must implement BPT

**§ 2.10.3:** All applicable National Categorical Pretreatment Standards (40 CFR Chapter I, Subchapter N) apply where more stringent than local limits.

**§ 2.01.2 — Specific prohibitions (complete ban):**
- Wastewater with closed-cup flashpoint < 140°F (per 40 CFR 261.21)
- Corrosive wastewater (pH < 6.0)
- Solid/viscous pollutants causing obstruction
- Heat causing effluent > 40°C (104°F) at treatment plant
- Petroleum oil, non-biodegradable cutting oil in interfering amounts
- Toxic gases/vapors/fumes causing acute worker safety hazard
- Trucked or hauled pollutants (without separate permit)

## Industrial Pretreatment / Industrial Waste Permit (IWP)

**Mandatory Permits (MRWPCA Ord. 2008-01, § 4.05.1):** Each **Significant Industrial User (SIU)** must obtain a Wastewater Discharge Permit (IWP) before connecting to or discharging into the SCSD collection system.

**SIU defined (§ 1.03):**
- Any industrial user subject to Categorical Pretreatment Standards (40 CFR 403.6); OR
- Any user discharging an average ≥ 25,000 gpd of process wastewater; OR
- Any user whose process wastestream constitutes ≥ 5% of average dry-weather hydraulic or organic capacity of the POTW; OR
- Any user designated by the Agency as having reasonable potential for adverse impact

**Optional Permits (§ 4.05.2):** The MRWPCA Manager may issue a permit to any user requesting metered-rate billing, any user with below-normal discharge strength, or any user making temporary discharges (e.g., groundwater cleanup, tank rinsings).

**Permit Application (§ 4.05.3):** Applicant submits completed application with fees to MRWPCA Manager, including: facility name/address/SIC; wastewater volume; constituent characterization (lab analysis); flow rates; site/floor/plumbing plans; description of processes and materials; product types and production rates; and status of compliance with applicable pretreatment standards.

**Permit contact:** MRWPCA — (831) 372-3367 or https://montereyonewater.org/
