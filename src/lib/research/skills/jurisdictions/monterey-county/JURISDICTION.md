## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA (Unified Program)

- **Agency:** County of Monterey Health Department — Environmental Health Bureau, Hazardous Materials Management Services (HMMS). Single county-wide CUPA with no city-run CUPAs (Salinas, Monterey, Seaside all report here).
- **Six unified program elements administered:** HMBP, CalARP, hazardous waste generator + tiered permitting (on-site treatment), UST, APSA/aboveground petroleum storage.
- **Online reporting portal:** CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) — http://cers.calepa.ca.gov. County re-submittal deadline: December 31 annually.
- **HMBP thresholds:** ≥55 gal liquid / ≥500 lb solid / ≥200 cu ft compressed gas at any time during the reporting year triggers HMBP requirement.
- **Phone:** (831) 755-4511 ext. 1 (Hazardous Materials Specialist); (831) 755-4508 (main HMMS line).
- **Fee schedule:** Board of Supervisors-approved; fees effective July 1, 2023 (confirm current year); see Environmental Health Fees page for schedule PDF.

### 3. Industrial Wastewater / POTW / Pretreatment

- **POTW:** Monterey One Water (formerly MRWPCA — Monterey Regional Water Pollution Control Agency). Regional Treatment Plant (RTP) at 14811 Del Monte Boulevard, Marina, CA 93933. Phone: (831) 422-1001. Admin: 5 Harris Court, Building D, Monterey, CA 93940. Phone: (831) 372-3367.
- **Governing sewer-use ordinance:** Monterey One Water **Ordinance No. 2023-01** — "Modifying Regulation for the Interception, Treatment, and Disposal of Sewage and Wastewater." Enforces 40 CFR Part 403 General Pretreatment Regulations; users classified as Significant Industrial User (SIU), Middle-Tier Categorical IU (MTCIU), or reduced-oversight.
- **Prior ordinance:** Ordinance No. 2008-01 (establishing industrial waste regulations framework; superseded by 2023-01 modifications).
- **Pretreatment / IWP:** Categorical Pretreatment Standards (40 CFR Part 403) apply. Industrial users must apply for Industrial Wastewater Pretreatment (IWP) permit through Monterey One Water. Online portal for bids/permits: https://vendors.planetbids.com/portal/79520/portal-home
- **Monterey County Code:** Title 15 (Public Services), Ch. 15.20 (Sewage Disposal) and Ch. 15.24 (County Sewer and Water System) — unincorporated-area sewer connection and disposal requirements.

### 4. Stormwater / MS4

- **MS4 permit:** Monterey County (unincorporated) operates under NPDES Phase II General Permit **No. CAS000004** (SWRCB), overseen by Central Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 3). Post-Construction Requirements adopted July 2013 via Central Coast Water Board Order **R3-2013-0032** — applies to projects in urbanized areas creating or replacing ≥2,500 sq ft impervious area.
- **Local ordinances:**
  - Monterey County Code **Ch. 16.12** (Erosion Control) — erosion control plan required prior to building/grading/land clearing permits; controls runoff, sediment movement, winter operations.
  - Monterey County Code **Ch. 16.14** (Urban Stormwater Quality Management and Discharge) — prohibits non-stormwater discharges into County storm drain system; implements low-impact development (LID) techniques; applies to unincorporated urbanized areas.
- **Low-impact development:** Post-Construction Requirements (PCRs) require LID for projects ≥2,500 sq ft impervious in urbanized areas (Order R3-2013-0032).

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts / CUP Process

- **Governing codes:** Monterey County operates two parallel zoning codes:
  - **Title 20** (Coastal Zoning Ordinance, CZ) — applies to the coastal zone; uses Coastal Development Permits (CDP) in place of standard CUPs; administered by HCD Planning Services.
  - **Title 21** (Inland Zoning Ordinance) — applies to non-coastal unincorporated areas; Ch. 21.26 (Limited Industrial), Ch. 21.20 (Heavy Commercial), Ch. 21.64–21.66 (special districts/permits). Title 21 supersedes Title 20 upon adoption of Sectional District Maps for inland areas.
- **Light/Limited Industrial District (LI, Title 20 / Ch. 21.26 Title 21):**
  - Permitted: craft manufacturing, auto repair, tradesperson shops, contractor yards, mini-warehouse, accessory retail (≤25% floor area), wireless facilities.
  - Conditionally permitted (CDP/CUP): agricultural processing plants, food processing, boat storage/repair, hotels/restaurants, marine research, executive offices, subdivisions.
  - Development standards: max height 35 ft, max site coverage 50%, min site area 1 acre, landscaping ≥10% of site; General Development Plan required for lots >1 acre or multi-use.
- **Heavy Industrial District (HI, Title 20 / Ch. 20.28):**
  - Permitted: industrial use changes in existing structures, offices <5,000 sq ft, light manufacturing, tradesman shops, clothing manufacture, wireless facilities.
  - Conditionally permitted (CDP/CUP): bottling works, contractor yards, junk/wrecking yards, hazardous material manufacturing, food processing, research labs, wind energy, schools, residential (if <50% of industrial sq ft), auto repair.
  - Development standards: same as LI (35 ft height, 50% coverage, 1 acre min, 10% landscaping, General Development Plan for >1 acre).
- **CUP/CDP process:** Application submitted to HCD Planning Services; Planning Commission approval required. First step: complete Application Request Form + Scope of Work Statement. Coastal zone uses require CDP through Ch. 20.70; inland uses require CUP per Title 21.
- **Planning Services contact:** HCD Planning Services — https://www.countyofmonterey.gov/government/departments-a-h/housing-community-development/planning-services

## Gotchas

- **Single CUPA county-wide:** Monterey has ONE CUPA (Environmental Health Bureau HMMS) covering all incorporated cities and unincorporated areas — no city-run CUPAs anywhere in the county.
- **Fire district fragmentation:** Unincorporated fire protection split across multiple districts; MCRFD covers the largest portion (rural/unincorporated). Verify the responding district by specific address — CAL FIRE BEU handles SRA wildland; incorporated cities have their own fire departments (City of Monterey, Salinas, etc.).
- **Dual zoning code (Title 20 vs. Title 21):** Coastal zone uses Title 20 + CDP; inland uses Title 21 + CUP. Title 21 supersedes Title 20 for inland areas upon Sectional District Map adoption. Confirm which title applies based on parcel location.
- **Building reorganization:** The historic "RMA" (Resource Management Agency) name is obsolete; building/permitting for unincorporated land now sits under Housing & Community Development (HCD), split completed ~Nov 2023.
