## Governing Zoning Codes

Monterey County has **two parallel zoning codes** depending on parcel location:

| Code | Scope | Industrial chapters |
|---|---|---|
| **Title 20** (Coastal Zoning Ordinance, CZ) | Coastal zone parcels | Ch. 20.26 (LI), Ch. 20.28 (HI) |
| **Title 21** (Inland Zoning Ordinance) | Non-coastal unincorporated areas | Ch. 21.26 (Limited Industrial/LI) |

Coastal zone uses require a **Coastal Development Permit (CDP)** instead of a standard CUP. Inland uses require a **CUP** per Title 21. Title 21 supersedes Title 20 upon adoption of Sectional District Maps for inland areas.

**Planning Services contact:** Housing & Community Development (HCD) Planning Services

## Light Industrial (LI) District — Title 20 Ch. 20.26 / Title 21 Ch. 21.26

### Permitted Uses (Coastal Administrative Permit / Administrative Permit required)

Per §20.26.050 (coastal) / parallel §21.26 (inland):
- Change of light industrial uses within an existing structure
- Water system facilities (≤14 connections)
- Christmas tree cultivation and removal
- Clothing manufacture
- Interior decorating, picture framing, craft shops
- "Other light industries of a similar character" with no excessive odor/noise (§20.26.050.H)
- Setback reductions ≤10% of required setback
- Parking lots for adjacent commercial use
- Mini-warehouses; caretaker unit
- Accessory retail sales ≤25% of floor area
- Marine equipment storage
- Tradesman shops; contractor yards
- Service stations; photography studios
- Wireless communications facilities

### Conditional Uses (CDP or CUP required)

Per §20.26.060:
- Executive/professional offices (§20.26.060.A)
- Boat storage/repair facilities (§20.26.060.B)
- Nurseries; storage warehouses (§20.26.060.C–D)
- Animal hospitals; commercial kennels (§20.26.060.G–H)
- Wind energy systems (§20.26.060.L)
- Agricultural processing plants (§20.26.060.P)
- Hotels, motels, restaurants (§20.26.060.Q)
- Residential uses ≤equal industrial square footage (§20.26.060.V)
- Food processing; fish canning (§20.26.060.DD)
- Subdivisions; lot line adjustments (§20.26.060.LL–MM)

### Development Standards (§20.26.070)

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Maximum height | 35 ft (unless superseded by zoning map notation) |
| Max building-site coverage | 50% (excluding parking and landscaping) |
| Minimum lot area | 1 acre |
| Minimum landscaping | 10% of site area |
| Setbacks | Established via General Development Plan (GDP) approval (required for lots >1 acre or multi-use) |

## Heavy Industrial (HI) District — Title 20 Ch. 20.28

### Permitted Uses (Coastal Administrative Permit required)

Per §20.28.050:
- Change of heavy industrial uses within existing structures (§20.28.050.A)
- Water system facilities (≤14 connections) (§20.28.050.B)
- Electric power manufacture (§20.28.050.E)
- Magnesia and refractory brick production (§20.28.050.F)
- Industrial accessory uses (§20.28.050.G)
- One caretaker unit for security (§20.28.050.H)
- Offices <5,000 sq ft (§20.28.050.O)
- Tradesman shops (plumbers, electricians, etc.) (§20.28.050.P)
- Light commercial shops (§20.28.050.R)
- Clothing manufacture (§20.28.050.U)
- Day care for on-site employees (§20.28.050.V)
- Wireless communications facilities (§20.28.050.X)

### Conditional Uses (CDP required)

Per §20.28.060:
- Bottling works; carpenter shops; contractor yards (§20.28.060.A)
- Light manufacturing (clothing, printing, lithography) (§20.28.060.B)
- Industrial manufacturing uses (§20.28.060.C)
- Research and development laboratories (§20.28.060.D)
- Chemical/electronic manufacturing (§20.28.060.E)
- Dry cleaning plants; laundries (§20.28.060.F)
- Wholesale stores; warehouses (§20.28.060.G)
- Offices >5,000 sq ft (§20.28.060.H)
- Food processing; fish canning (§20.28.060.I)
- Junk/wrecking yards (§20.28.060.J)
- "Manufacture of acid, cement, electric power, explosives...petroleum refining" and similar hazardous industrial uses (§20.28.060.K)
- Wind energy systems (§20.28.060.N)
- Agricultural processing plants (§20.28.060.Q)
- Residential (gross sq ft ≤ industrial sq ft) (§20.28.060.R)
- Propane distributorships (§20.28.060.BB)
- Petroleum distributors; contractor yards (§20.28.060.CC)
- Auto repair facilities; body/fender repair (§20.28.060.GG–HH)
- Subdivisions (§20.28.060.LL)

### Development Standards (§20.28.070)

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Maximum height | 35 ft (unless superseded by zoning map notation; additional height via Use Permit) |
| Max building-site coverage | 50% (excluding parking and landscaping) |
| Minimum lot area | 1 acre |
| Minimum landscaping | 10% of site area (Director approval; must be in place before use commences) |
| Setbacks | Established via General Development Plan where required; otherwise via project review |

## CUP / CDP Process

- **Coastal zone (Title 20):** Conditional uses require a **Coastal Development Permit (CDP)** through Ch. 20.70; Zoning Administrator approval.
- **Inland (Title 21):** Conditional uses require a **Conditional Use Permit (CUP)** per Title 21; Planning Commission approval typically required.
- **First step:** Complete Application Request Form + Scope of Work Statement with HCD Planning Services.
- **General Development Plan (GDP):** Required for lots >1 acre or multi-use projects in both LI and HI districts; establishes setbacks, phasing, and site design.
