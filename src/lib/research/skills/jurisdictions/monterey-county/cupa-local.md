## CUPA Agency

- **Agency:** County of Monterey Health Department — Environmental Health Bureau (EHB), **Hazardous Materials Management Services (HMMS)**
- **Designation:** Single county-wide CUPA — NO city-run CUPAs anywhere in Monterey County. All incorporated cities (Salinas, Monterey, Seaside, Pacific Grove, etc.) and unincorporated areas are served by this one agency.
- **Phone:** (831) 755-4511 ext. 1 (Hazardous Materials Specialist); (831) 755-4508 (main HMMS line)
- **CERS deadline:** Annual submittals due December 31 (2024 submittal deadline was January 1, 2025)

## Local Ordinance / Municipal Code

- **Primary local ordinance:** Monterey County Code **Title 10** (Health and Safety), **Chapter 10.65** — "HAZARDOUS MATERIALS REGISTRATION"
  - Administers underground storage tank (UST) permits under §10.65.040
  - Fees for permits are prescribed in **§10.08.050DD** of the Monterey County Code
  - Provisional permits may be issued subject to conditions; temporary storage permits (≤30 days, no more than every 6 months) available
  - Civil penalties for violations: **$500–$5,000 per day** (per §10.65 enforcement provisions) for operating unpermitted tanks, failure to monitor, failure to report unauthorized releases, improper tank closure
- **CERS portal:** All unified program reporting (HMBP, CalARP, UST, APSA, hazardous waste generator) submitted via http://cers.calepa.ca.gov

## Six Unified Program Elements Administered

1. **HMBP** — Hazardous Materials Business Plan
2. **CalARP** — California Accidental Release Prevention Program (Risk Management Plan for regulated substances above thresholds per 19 CCR Part 2750)
3. **Hazardous Waste Generator** (including on-site hazardous waste treatment — tiered permitting)
4. **UST** — Underground Storage Tank program (inspection + permitting per Ch. 10.65)
5. **APSA** — Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (SPCC alignment)
6. **Hazardous Waste On-Site Treatment** — Tiered permitting for generators treating hazardous waste on-site

## HMBP Reporting Thresholds

A facility must complete and submit an HMBP if it handles a hazardous material or mixture at any quantity during the reporting year equal to or greater than:
- **55 gallons** (liquids)
- **500 pounds** (solids)
- **200 cubic feet** (compressed gases)
