## Governing Sewer-Use Ordinance

- **Ordinance No. 2023-01** — "An Ordinance Modifying Regulations for the Interception, Treatment and Disposal of Sewage and Wastewater" (Wastewater Discharge Ordinance / WDO)
  - Short title: "Wastewater Discharge Ordinance of Monterey One Water" (§1.01)
  - Repeals prior ordinances: Nos. 92-02, 93-03, 2000-01, 2008-01, 2015-01, and 2019-01 (§8.02)
  - Incorporates and enforces **40 CFR Part 403** General Pretreatment Regulations
  - Implements Central Coast RWQCB requirements and all applicable effluent limitations
- **Unincorporated-county sewer code:** Monterey County Code **Title 15** (Public Services), **Ch. 15.20** (Sewage Disposal) and **Ch. 15.24** (County Sewer and Water System) govern sewer connection and disposal in unincorporated areas

## User Classifications (§1.03 Definitions)

- **Significant Industrial User (SIU)** (§1.03.56):
  - A User subject to Categorical Pretreatment Standards under 40 CFR 403.6 and 40 CFR Ch. I, Subchapter N; OR
  - Discharges ≥25,000 gallons per day (gpd) of process wastewater; OR
  - Contributes a process wastestream ≥5% of average dry-weather hydraulic or organic capacity of the POTW; OR
  - Designated by Monterey One Water as having reasonable potential for adversely affecting the POTW.
- **Non-Significant Categorical Industrial User (NSCIU):** CIU discharging ≤100 gpd total categorical wastewater, in consistent compliance, with annual certification.
- **Middle-Tier Categorical Industrial User (MTCIU)** (§1.03.56.d): CIU whose categorical wastewater does not exceed:
  - 0.01% of the RTP design dry-weather hydraulic capacity, or 5,000 gpd (whichever is smaller);
  - 0.01% of RTP design dry-weather organic treatment capacity; AND
  - 0.01% of maximum allowable headworks loading (MAHL) for any pollutant with approved Local Limits.

## Local Discharge Limits (§2.11.2)

The following **Daily Maximum Limits** apply at the point of discharge to the POTW / Community Sanitary Sewer (§2.11.2). All metal concentrations are total recoverable unless noted.

| Pollutant | Max Daily Limit (mg/L) |
|---|---|
| Ammonia as N | 44 |
| Arsenic | 0.42 |
| Cadmium | 0.27 |
| Chromium | 1.7 |
| Copper | 1.9 |
| Cyanide, total | 0.53 |
| Lead | 2.5 |
| Mercury, total | 0.018 |
| Molybdenum | 90 |
| Nickel | 1.5 |
| Selenium | 0.80 |
| Silver | 1.1 |
| Phenolic compounds | 8.1 |
| **Total Dissolved Solids (TDS)** | **1,800** |
| Zinc | 2.6 |

The TDS limit of 1,800 mg/L was introduced in Ordinance 2023-01 to protect treatment process integrity for ocean discharge and downstream beneficial reuse (agricultural irrigation and indirect/direct potable reuse via Advanced Water Purification Facility — AWPF).

The General Manager may impose **mass limitations (lb/day)** in lieu of or in addition to concentration-based limits (§2.11.1).

## Permit Requirements

- **Mandatory IWP:** Each SIU must obtain a Wastewater Discharge Permit before connecting to or discharging into the POTW (§4.05.1).
- **Optional permits:** Monterey One Water may also require permits for users discharging at concentrations/volumes that could exceed Local Limits or NPDES permit conditions (§4.05.2).
- **Permit application:** Users seeking new connection or change in flow/concentration that would make them an SIU must file a permit application with applicable fees (§4.05.3). Required information includes: facility name/address/SIC code, wastewater volume, constituents and characteristics, flow rates, discharge schedule.
- **Permit portal:** https://vendors.planetbids.com/portal/79520/portal-home (PlanetBids for bids/permits)
- **Periodic compliance reports:** All CIUs and SIUs must submit periodic compliance reports (§4.04.4).
- **Slug discharge:** SIUs must notify Monterey One Water immediately of changes affecting slug discharge potential; slug control plan required if determined necessary by the agency (§2.13).

## Significant Non-Compliance Definition (§1.03.57)

A User is in Significant Non-Compliance if:
- ≥66% of measurements for same pollutant in a 6-month period exceed any numeric pretreatment standard (by any magnitude); OR
- ≥33% of measurements meet the Technical Review Criteria threshold (1.4× for BOD/TSS/FOG; 1.2× for other pollutants).
