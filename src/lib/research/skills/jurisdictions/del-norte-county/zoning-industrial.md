## Industrial District — Title 20 (Non-Coastal): M District

**Chapter: 20.30 — M District, Manufacturing and Industrial**

There is a **single** industrial district designation in Title 20 (no M-1 / M-2 split was found). The M District applies to areas suited for industrial operations; regulations are designed to control congestion and protect surrounding areas.

Source: https://www.zoneomics.com/code/del-norte-county-unincorporated-CA/chapter_25

### Permitted Uses (by-right)

The M District permits by-right:
- All commercial uses allowed in the **C-3 district** (excluding residential buildings)
- All other commercial and manufacturing uses not enumerated in the conditional-use list below
- Accessory uses, including watchman's residences

### Conditional Uses (Use Permit Required — §20.30.030)

The following uses require a **Use Permit** (equivalent to a Conditional Use Permit) in the M District:

1. Airports
2. Animal hospitals, enclosed kennels, veterinary clinics
3. Commercial cannabis cultivation
4. Commercial stone or earth material excavation
5. Bone distillation
6. Oil/gas drilling and removal
7. Garbage, sewage, refuse — dumping, disposal, or incineration
8. Fat rendering
9. Hog ranches
10. Junkyards, wrecking yards, contractor yards, lumber yards, storage yards
11. Fish or meat processing
12. Manufacturing of acid, chemicals, cement, explosives, fireworks, fertilizer, gas, glue, gypsum, inflammable fluids or gases
13. Professional offices
14. Pulp and paper mills
15. Petroleum refining
16. Sawmills and planing mills
17. Ore smelting
18. Stockyards and slaughterhouses
19. Tanneries
20. Uses producing noise, offensive odor, smoke, dust, bright lights, or vibration, or involving explosives or dangerous materials

> Note: Most heavy industrial operations — including chemical manufacturing, petroleum refining, waste processing, and food processing — are conditional uses requiring a Use Permit.

### Development Standards (M District)

| Standard | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Maximum building height | 75 feet |
| Minimum lot area (with public water & sewer) | None |
| Minimum lot area (without public water/sewer) | 12,000 sq ft |
| Minimum lot width | 25 feet |
| Maximum lot coverage (with public utilities) | Up to 100% |
| Front yard setback | None (except 10 ft minimum when adjacent to a residential district) |
| Side yard setback | None (5 ft minimum when abutting a residential district) |
| Rear yard setback | None |
| Off-street parking | Per §20.46.20 |

## Industrial District — Title 21 (Coastal Zoning): M and MP Districts

**Chapter reference: §21.06.010**

Two industrial designations exist in the Coastal Zone:
- **M District** — Manufacturing and Industrial District
- **MP District** — Manufacturing and Industrial Performance District

Definition of "Industry" in Title 21 (§21.04.370): activities involving "manufacture, fabrication, reduction [and] destruction of any article, substance or commodity," including:
- Bottling works
- Building materials or contractors' yards
- Cleaning and dyeing establishments
- Creameries
- Junkyards and wrecking yards
- Laundries and lumber yards
- Milk bottling or distribution stations
- Stockyards and storage elevators
- Truck storage and repair services
- Warehouses and wholesale storage

Detailed permitted vs. conditional uses for the coastal M/MP districts are in §§21.31–21.32 of Title 21 (text not fully retrieved online — [VERIFY by accessing Title 21 at the municipal code portal or contacting Planning Division]).

## Cannabis Combining District Overlay (§20.36)

The **Cannabis Business Combining District (CBCD)** overlay can be applied to M-zoned parcels. Within an M zone overlaid with CBCD, the following cannabis uses are allowed with a use permit:
- Cannabis retail
- Cannabis manufacturing (use permit issued only "in commercial zones where manufacturing is consistent with other conditionally permitted uses" per §20.36.20)

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

Applications are processed by the Del Norte County Planning Division. The County does not appear to have a separate streamlined ministerial permit track for industrial uses — most non-standard M-district uses require a discretionary Use Permit.
