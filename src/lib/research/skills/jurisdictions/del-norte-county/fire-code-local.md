## Fire Authorities Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

Del Norte County has two distinct fire AHJs depending on location:

| Area | AHJ |
|------|-----|
| City of Crescent City (incorporated) | Crescent City Fire & Rescue (all-volunteer; operational merger of City Fire Dept and Crescent Fire Protection District, 2015) |
| Unincorporated county — Local Responsibility Area (LRA) | Crescent Fire Protection District (CFPD) and local volunteer departments |
| State Responsibility Area (SRA) — unincorporated | CAL FIRE Humboldt–Del Norte Unit |

## State Minimum Fire Safe Regulations (SRA / VHFHSZ)

Applicable to all development permits (building, grading, use), rezones, and subdivisions located in:
- State Responsibility Area (SRA), OR
- Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone (VHFHSZ) within the Local Responsibility Area (LRA)

**Effective:** April 1, 2023 (per Del Norte County Community Development Department).

**Required submissions with development application:**
1. Intake Questionnaire (mandatory with all development applications)
2. Exception Request Form (if project cannot meet SMFSR requirements)

Key design requirements include emergency vehicle access turnarounds and turnouts. Exceptions are determined after field review.

**Fire Hazard Severity Zones Map:** February 2025 version available via Del Norte County Planning Division and the state ArcGIS portal.

## Sprinkler / Fire Alarm Approvals

For the City of Crescent City: The Volunteer Fire Department must approve the following prior to permit issuance:
- Sprinkler systems
- Fire alarm systems
- Commercial kitchen fire suppression systems
