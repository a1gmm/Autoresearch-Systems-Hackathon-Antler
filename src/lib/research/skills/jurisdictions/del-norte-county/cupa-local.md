## CUPA Agency

**Del Norte County Environmental Health Division** is the sole Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all of Del Norte County, including the incorporated City of Crescent City. There is no separate Participating Agency (PA) for Crescent City or any other city in the county.

- Address: 981 H Street, Suite 110, Crescent City, CA 95531
- Phone: (707) 465-0426
- Fax: (707) 465-0340
- Email: environmental-health@co.del-norte.ca.us
- Staff (as of 2026): Houawa Moua, REHS (Senior Environmental Health Specialist); Branden Hendrix (Environmental Health Specialist)

## CUPA Programs Administered

All six Unified Program elements are administered by the County CUPA:

| Program | Statute |
|---------|---------|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) | HSC §25500 et seq. |
| Hazardous Waste Generator / Onsite Treatment | HSC §25100 et seq. |
| Underground Storage Tanks (UST) | HSC §25280 et seq. |
| Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) | HSC §25270 et seq. (≥1,320 gal aggregate) |
| California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) | HSC §25531 et seq. |
| Tiered Permitting (Onsite Hazardous Waste Treatment) | HSC §25200.3 et seq. |

## Reporting Portal

All regulated facilities must submit Unified Program data electronically through **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System):
- URL: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/
- Paper submission to the County ended 2013-01-01 per state mandate (AB 2286).
- Facilities already in the system log in, locate their facility, and request access. New facilities register and complete setup online.
- CERS account assistance available by appointment with EH Division staff.

## Fee Schedule

The County CUPA fee schedule is **not published online** as of the fetch date. Fees are set by the Board of Supervisors. Contact the Environmental Health Division at (707) 465-0426 for current annual inspection/permit fees by program element and facility tier.

## Notes

- The CUPA Director concurrently serves as Agricultural Commissioner, Sealer of Weights & Measures, and Air Pollution Control Officer — a small-county consolidation across roughly 34 programs. Staffing is limited; allow lead time for inspections and permit actions.
- Air district program (NCUAQMD) is separate from the CUPA despite the shared director role.
