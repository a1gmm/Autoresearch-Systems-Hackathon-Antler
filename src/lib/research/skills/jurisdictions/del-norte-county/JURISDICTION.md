## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air (District)

- **North Coast Unified Air Quality Management District (NCUAQMD)** has jurisdiction over Del Norte County for stationary-source air permits, Title V, BACT/BARCT, and Rule enforcement.
- The Del Norte County CUPA Director also serves as local Air Pollution Control Officer, but substantive air permitting flows through NCUAQMD.
- Source: https://www.ncuaqmd.org/

### CUPA (Unified Program)

- **Del Norte County Environmental Health Division** is the **sole countywide CUPA** (HMBP, hazardous waste generator, UST, APSA, CalARP). No city in the county operates a separate CUPA or Participating Agency; Crescent City is covered under the County CUPA.
- All submissions go through CERS (cers.calepa.ca.gov). Paper submission ended 2013-01-01 per mandate.
- Programs administered: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generator, Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA ≥1,320 gal aggregate), CalARP.

### Wastewater (IWD / Pretreatment)

- **POTW:** City of Crescent City Wastewater Treatment Facility, 210 Battery Street, Crescent City, CA 95531. Secondary treatment, discharges to Pacific Ocean (Discharge Point 001) and recycled water system (Discharge Point 002). NPDES Permit No. CA0022756, Order No. R1-2023-0039 (adopted 2023-12-12, effective 2024-02-01, expires 2029-01-31). Classified "Major" discharge.
- **Pretreatment ordinance:** Crescent City Code Title 13, Division II, Chapter 13.33 (Wastewater Pretreatment) — governs industrial users discharging to the City POTW. Significant Industrial User threshold: ≥25,000 gpd process wastewater, or ≥5% of POTW dry-weather hydraulic/organic capacity.
- **Discharge prohibitions (§13.33.020):** no discharge causing pass-through or interference; FOG (fats, oils, grease of animal/vegetable origin) >50 mg/L prohibited; dilution to achieve compliance prohibited; pH <5.0 prohibited except by special design.
- **Local limits:** Numeric limits (Al, NH3, Sb, As, Ba, Be, Br/Cl/I, B, Cd, Cr, Cu, etc.) are set by city council resolution and maintained as a "local limits table" at the City Public Works Department — not embedded in the ordinance text. Obtain current table from Public Works.
- **County CSA-1 (unincorporated Crescent City area):** Del Norte County Service Area No. 1 provides collection (≈33 mi gravity mains, 4 mi pressure mains, 15 lift stations) to unincorporated areas; sewage conveys to the City POTW. County Code §15.20.010 and Resolution 78-032 govern sewer construction and connections in CSA-1.
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/northcoast/board_decisions/tentative_orders/pdf/2023/23_0039_CrescentCity.pdf | https://www.co.del-norte.ca.us/departments/EngineeringSurveying/CSA1

### Zoning (Industrial Districts)

- Zoning for unincorporated Del Norte County is governed by **Title 20 (Non-Coastal Zoning Ordinance)** and **Title 21 (Coastal Zoning Ordinance)**. Both codes are maintained at https://delnortecounty.municipalcodeonline.com/.
- Industrial zone in Title 20: **M District — Manufacturing and Industrial** (Chapter 20.30). Single industrial designation (no M-1/M-2 split confirmed). Most heavy industrial uses require a Use Permit (CUP equivalent).
- Industrial zone in Title 21: **M District** and **MP District (Manufacturing and Industrial Performance)** (§21.06.010) — same pattern, applicable in coastal zone.

## Notes

- GOTCHA: **Crescent City is the only incorporated city in Del Norte County** (2020 pop. ~6,000; county ~27,700). All other communities are unincorporated and subject to County code.
- GOTCHA: **Fire consolidation** — Crescent City Fire & Rescue is the product of a 2015 operational merger between the City and the Crescent Fire Protection District; it is all-volunteer. No separate paid municipal fire department exists.
- GOTCHA: **Small rural CUPA** — The Del Norte County CUPA Director concurrently serves as Agricultural Commissioner, Sealer of Weights & Measures, and Air Pollution Control Officer (~34 programs in total). Staff is very small; response times for fee schedules and permit status may be slow.
- GOTCHA: **IWD program is City-operated** — Industrial users discharging to the Crescent City POTW submit to the City of Crescent City Public Works, not the County. The County CUPA and the City wastewater program are separate agencies.
