## Significant Industrial User (SIU) Definition (§13.40.040)

An industrial user qualifies as an SIU if it:
- Discharges an average of **≥25,000 gallons per day** of process wastewater to the POTW (excluding sanitary, non-contact cooling, and boiler blowdown), OR
- Contributes a process wastestream making up **≥5%** of the average dry-weather hydraulic or organic capacity of the POTW

SIUs are required to furnish and install a calibrated flume, weir, flow meter, or approved equivalent at the control manhole for flow measurement.

## Discharge Prohibitions (§13.33.020)

General prohibitions — no user may discharge pollutants causing pass-through or interference. Specific limits include:

| Pollutant / Parameter | Prohibition |
|-----------------------|-------------|
| Fats, oils, grease (animal/vegetable) | >50 mg/L prohibited |
| pH | <5.0 prohibited (unless treatment works specially designed) |
| Heat | Raising influent temperature above 40°C (104°F) prohibited |
| Petroleum oil / non-biodegradable cutting oil | Amounts causing interference or pass-through prohibited |
| Explosive/flammable materials | Prohibited (fire/explosion hazard in treatment works) |
| Solid/viscous wastes | Amounts causing obstruction to flow prohibited |
| Dilution to achieve compliance | Prohibited — no dilution of waste streams to meet pretreatment standards |

The full list of regulated substances in the local limits table includes: aluminum, ammonia (as N), antimony, arsenic, barium, beryllium, bromine, chlorine, iodine, boron, cadmium, chlorinated hydrocarbons, chromium, cobalt, copper, and others (§13.33.020 reference).

## County CSA-1 (Unincorporated Areas)

**Del Norte County Service Area No. 1 (CSA-1)** provides sanitary sewer collection to the unincorporated Crescent City area:
- Infrastructure: approximately 33 miles gravity mains + 4 miles pressure mains + 15 lift stations
- Flow path: CSA-1 collection → City of Crescent City collection system → Crescent City WWTF
- Administration: Del Norte County Engineering and Surveying Division
- Operations/maintenance: City of Crescent City Public Works Department
- Applicable codes: Del Norte County Code **§15.20.010** (Sewer System; Construction and Connections) and **Resolution 78-032** (sewer area design standards)
- Owner responsibility: Lateral sewers from property line to sewer main are maintained by the property owner.
