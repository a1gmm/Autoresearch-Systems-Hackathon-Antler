## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

**Crescent City Fire & Rescue** — all-risk volunteer fire department operating as a joint entity between the City of Crescent City and the Crescent Fire Protection District under a 2015 operational merger agreement. The City owns one station; the Crescent Fire Protection District owns three stations.

## Adopted California Fire Code Edition

**2007 California Fire Code (CFC)** — adopted in Municipal Code Chapter 15.24.

> VERIFY: The eCode360 hosted version of the Crescent City municipal code reflects adoption of the 2007 CFC. However, California state law (Health & Safety Code §17958) requires local agencies to adopt updated editions within 180 days of state adoption. The 2019 CFC became effective statewide in January 2020, and the 2022 CFC (part of the 2022 California Building Standards Code, Title 24) became effective statewide on January 1, 2023. The 2025 CBC replaced the 2022 edition statewide on January 1, 2026. **Contact the Crescent City Building Department at (707) 464-9506 to confirm the currently enforceable CFC edition before relying on Chapter 15.24 language.**

## Local Amendments / Modifications (as codified in Chapter 15.24)

The following local rules were codified in Chapter 15.24 at the time the 2007 CFC was adopted:

1. **Outdoor aboveground flammable liquid storage** — Storage of flammable liquids in outside aboveground tanks is permitted only after a **Use Permit** is first secured. Procedures for obtaining a Use Permit are in **Chapter 17.54** of the Municipal Code (Use Permits).

2. **Bulk liquefied petroleum (LP) gas storage** — Bulk storage of liquefied petroleum gas is restricted inside the city limits pursuant to **§3804.2 of the 2007 CFC**.

3. **Explosives and blasting agents** — Storage of explosives and blasting agents is **prohibited** inside the city limits pursuant to **Chapter 33 of the 2007 CFC**.

4. **Modification authority** — The fire chief may modify provisions of the adopted CFC upon written application when practical difficulties make strict compliance impossible, provided the spirit of the code is observed and public safety is secured.
