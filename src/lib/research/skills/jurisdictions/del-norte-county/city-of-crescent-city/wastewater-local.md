## Local Discharge Prohibitions (§13.40.050)

The following local prohibitions were confirmed from available code sources:

- **pH:** Wastewater with pH < 6.0 or > 9.0, or otherwise causing corrosive structural damage to the POTW or equipment, is **prohibited**.
- **Fats, oils, and greases (FOG) of animal or vegetable origin:** Concentrations greater than **50 mg/L** are **prohibited**.
- **Dilution:** No industrial user may dilute a discharge to achieve compliance with categorical pretreatment standards, state limitations, or local limits.

Additional local limits (e.g., BOD5, TSS surcharge thresholds, metals local limits) are maintained by the Public Works Director and may be updated based on the local limits evaluation required by the NPDES permit. ## Pretreatment Program

The City operates an **EPA-approved pretreatment program** under 40 CFR Part 403. The program is an enforceable condition of the NPDES permit.

- **Control authority:** Public Works Director (or designee).
- **Monitoring requirement:** Significant Industrial Users (SIUs) and other required users must install a calibrated flume, weir, flow meter, or similar device at a control manhole to measure flow rate and total volume.
- **Annual reporting:** City must submit an Annual Pretreatment Report to the North Coast Regional Water Board by March 1 each year.
