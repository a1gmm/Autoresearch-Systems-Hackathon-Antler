## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air

- **North Coast Unified Air Quality Management District (NCUAQMD)** is the air district with jurisdiction over Del Norte County (and thus Crescent City). The City has no independent air permitting authority; all permit-to-operate and ATCM compliance matters go through NCUAQMD.
- Source: https://www.ncuaqmd.org/

### CUPA (Unified Hazardous Materials Program)

- **Not its own CUPA.** Served by the **Del Norte County Environmental Health Division** (countywide CUPA). Programs administered: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Underground Storage Tank (UST), Contingency Planning. All businesses submit via CERS (California Environmental Reporting System).

### Fire

- **AHJ:** Crescent City Fire & Rescue (volunteer), a joint operation between the City of Crescent City and the Crescent Fire Protection District under a 2015 merger agreement. The City owns one station; the District owns three.
- **Adopted code:** 2007 California Fire Code (CFC), codified at **Municipal Code Chapter 15.24**. [VERIFY — eCode360 shows 2007 edition; the city building page cites the current state Title 24 but gives no specific CFC edition update. Contact building dept. to confirm whether 2019 or 2022 CFC has since been adopted.]
- **Local amendments in Chapter 15.24:**
  - Outdoor aboveground storage of flammable liquids requires a Use Permit (ref. Chapter 17.54 — Use Permits).
  - Bulk storage of liquefied petroleum gas restricted inside city limits per §3804.2, 2007 CFC.
  - Storage of explosives and blasting agents prohibited inside city limits per Chapter 33, 2007 CFC.
  - Fire chief may grant written modifications for practical difficulty per the code's modification provision.
- Contact: (707) 464-2421 | ccfire@crescentcity.org | 255 W. Washington Blvd., Crescent City, CA 95531
- Source: https://www.crescentcity.org/departments/Fire | https://cfpd.crescentcity.org/

### Wastewater (Industrial Pretreatment)

- **POTW:** City of Crescent City Wastewater Treatment Facility, 210 Battery Street, Crescent City, CA 95531.
- **NPDES permit:** Order No. R1-2023-0039, NPDES No. CA0022756 (adopted Dec 12, 2023; effective Feb 1, 2024; expires Jan 31, 2029). Issued by California Regional Water Quality Control Board, North Coast Region. Classified as Major discharge.
- **Discharge point:** Pacific Ocean (Discharge Point 001); recycled water system (Discharge Point 002). Dry weather design flow: 1.86 mgd; peak wet weather: 6.12 mgd.
- **Pretreatment program:** City has an EPA-approved pretreatment program implemented under 40 CFR Part 403. Named industrial user: Rumiano Cheese Company (categorical industrial user; BOD5 removal credited at influent).
- **Local code:** Municipal Code Title 13, Division II (Sewers) — **Chapter 13.33** (Wastewater Pretreatment) and **Chapter 13.40** (Prohibited Discharge Standards). Sewer use ordinance review was required by the 2017 NPDES permit (update report due July 2019; ordinance revision due July 2021). [VERIFY whether ordinance was updated.]
- **Local discharge prohibitions (Chapter 13.40.050):** pH < 6.0 or > 9.0 prohibited; fats, oils, greases of animal/vegetable origin > 50 mg/L prohibited.
- **NPDES effluent limits (Table 2, Order R1-2023-0039) at Discharge Point 001 (for context):** BOD5 30/45 mg/L (monthly/weekly avg); TSS 30/45 mg/L; pH 6.0–9.0 s.u.; Oil & Grease 25/40 mg/L avg, 75 mg/L max daily; Total Ammonia Nitrogen 72 mg/L max daily; Turbidity 75/100/225 NTU; Total Copper 300 µg/L weekly avg, 840 µg/L max daily.
- Control authority: Public Works Director (or designee).
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/northcoast/board_decisions/tentative_orders/pdf/2023/23_0039_CrescentCity.pdf
- Source: https://www.crescentcity.org/departments/WaterandSewer

- **Zoning code:** Municipal Code **Title 17 (Zoning)**. Three industrial/manufacturing district designations:

## GOTCHA

- **Fire is shared, not independently city-staffed** — Crescent City Fire & Rescue operates jointly with the Crescent Fire Protection District under the 2015 merger agreement; AHJ spans both city limits and adjacent district territory.
- **Fire code edition likely stale in online code** — eCode360 shows 2007 CFC adopted in Chapter 15.24; state mandate keeps pushing forward. Confirm the current adopted edition directly with the fire department or building department before citing.
