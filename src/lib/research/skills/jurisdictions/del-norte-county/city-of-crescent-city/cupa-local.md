## Programs Administered

The Del Norte County CUPA administers the following Unified Program elements:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — businesses storing hazardous materials above thresholds must file inventory, emergency response, and training information.
2. **Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program** — oversight of UST installation, operation, closure, and leak detection/reporting.
3. **Contingency Planning** — hazardous waste generator contingency plan review.

## Reporting System

All Unified Program submissions are made through the **California Environmental Reporting System (CERS)**: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/

In-person assistance with CERS account setup is available by appointment at the Environmental Health office.
