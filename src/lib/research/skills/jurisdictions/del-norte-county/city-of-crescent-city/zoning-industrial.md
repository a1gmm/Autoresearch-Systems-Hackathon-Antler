## Use Permits / Conditional Use Permits (CUP)

- **Chapter 17.54** governs Use Permits (equivalent to Conditional Use Permits in many jurisdictions).
- Certain industrial activities within any zone — including outdoor aboveground flammable liquid storage — require a Use Permit issued through the Community Development Department.
- Reference: https://ecode360.com/44243228 (Use Permits chapter on eCode360)

## Special Zoning Uses

- **Chapter 17.48** and **Chapter 17.83** address Special Zoning Uses, which may include industrial activities with additional conditions.
- Source: https://ecode360.com/44243040

## Sign Regulations for Industrial Areas

For C-M zones, signage follows the same rules as general commercial districts (per §17.39.200 sign matrix), allowing wall signs, awning/canopy signs, monument signs (up to 10 feet), hanging and projecting signs, and banners.

## General Plan / Zoning Map

The City zoning map and General Plan land use designations are administered by the Community Development Department. A General Plan and Zoning Code amendment was processed in 2000 (GPA-20001-1, ZCA-2000-1, ZCA-2000-2, LCPA-2000-1), which included Local Coastal Program amendments.
