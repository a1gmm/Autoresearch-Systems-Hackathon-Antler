## Structure: No County POTW

Kings County has **no county-operated sewer system or industrial pretreatment program**. Industrial wastewater regulation is city-by-city. Facilities in unincorporated areas must either:
- Connect to a city POTW (if available and if city agrees to accept industrial flow), subject to that city's pretreatment ordinance; or
- Obtain Waste Discharge Requirements (WDRs) from the **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 5)** for land disposal or other non-POTW discharge.

## City of Hanford POTW (Primary Industrial POTW in Kings County)

**Operator:** City of Hanford Public Works / Utilities Division
**Contact:** (559) 585-2550 (business); wastewater plant direct: (559) 585-2576
**Website:** https://www.cityofhanfordca.com/1570/Wastewater-Treatment

**Facility capacity:** 8.0 million gallons per day (MGD); average flows approximately 4.5 MGD (as of 2022).
**Treatment type:** Ox-Ditch process (upgrade from Primary Trickling Filter; ~$60M upgrade contract awarded ~2022). Effluent used for crop irrigation (recycled water discharge).
**Collection system:** 212 miles of collector lines (4–42 inches), 22 pump stations; operated by 5 full-time staff.
**Staffing:** 6 licensed operators; 7-days/week operation.

**Municipal Code:** City of Hanford Municipal Code, **Title 13, Chapter 13.08 — Sewer Service System**

| § 13.08.075 | Fats, Oils, and Grease (FOG) control |
| § 13.08.090C | Significant Industrial User (SIU) permit requirements |

**pH limit:** No discharge with pH < 6.0 or > 11.0 (causes corrosive structural damage to POTW and equipment).
**Categorical standards:** National categorical pretreatment standards from 40 CFR Parts 405–471 apply by reference per § 13.08.062A.
**SIU permit:** Required for any Significant Industrial User (SIU) before discharging to the POTW. No SIU shall discharge without first obtaining an industrial wastewater discharge permit from the city.

**Local discharge limits table (§ 13.08.060):** The Hanford Municipal Code states that metallic substance limits are total metal unless otherwise indicated. **Self-monitoring:** The city contracts with private laboratories for all required analyses for the POTW's self-monitoring reports and the Industrial Pretreatment Program.

### Industrial Wastewater Permit (IWP) Process — Hanford

1. Determine if facility is a Significant Industrial User (SIU) or Categorical Industrial User (CIU) under 40 CFR.
2. Submit industrial wastewater discharge permit application to City of Hanford Public Works.
3. City Director of Public Works reviews; may set facility-specific local limits.
4. Permit conditions may require installation and operation of pretreatment equipment (grease traps, pH neutralization, etc.).
5. Ongoing self-monitoring, reporting, and inspection by city staff.

Contact: Hanford Utilities Division, (559) 585-2550.

## Unincorporated Areas — Stormwater and Non-POTW Discharge

Facilities in unincorporated Kings County that cannot connect to a city POTW:
- Onsite Wastewater Treatment Systems (OWTS / septic): regulated by Kings County EHS under its Liquid Waste / Onsite Wastewater program. Contact: (559) 584-1411.
- Industrial process water discharged to land (spray or percolation): requires WDRs from **Central Valley RWQCB (Region 5)**: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/centralvalley/
- Stormwater: covered under the Statewide Phase II Small MS4 General Permit (Order No. 2013-0001-DWQ) or Statewide Industrial General Permit (IGP), as applicable.
