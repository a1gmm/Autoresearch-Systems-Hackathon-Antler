## POTW (Publicly Owned Treatment Works)

**City of Lemoore Wastewater Treatment Facility**
- Lagoon-based system: four ponds (ponds 1–2 aerated, operated in parallel; ponds 3–4 partially aerated, operated in series for solids settling)
- Pond footprint: ~8.8 acres; storage capacity: 70 million gallons
- Current design flow: ~1.7 mgd domestic (serves ~27,000 residents); additional ~3.0 mgd Leprino industrial flows treated separately at Leprino's adjacent WWTF before combination
- Effluent disinfected with chlorine gas before combination with Leprino's treated effluent
- Facility located in the southwest quadrant of the city, adjacent to Leprino Foods' own WWTF

## Discharge Permit (WDRs, not NPDES)

The City's WWTF does **not** discharge to a surface waterway. Treated combined effluent (City + Leprino Foods) is land-applied for crop irrigation.

**Governing order**: Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB Region 5), **Waste Discharge Requirements Order No. R5-2019-0008**

**Current project**: City and Leprino Foods filed a ROWD (May 6, 2022) to expand flows from 5.0 mgd to 7.0 mgd and expand land application from 1,900 to ~2,420 acres (Stone Ranch + Nederend parcels = "River Ranch"). Associated CEQA IS/ND prepared May 2023.

Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/rwqcb5/board_decisions/tentative_orders/2402/13_lemoore/riverranch_ismnd.pdf

Key provisions (from search-result excerpts; full text at amlegal.com — direct fetch blocked):

- Establishes uniform discharge requirements for users of the city's wastewater collection, transport, and treatment system.
- Mandates compliance with Clean Water Act § 307(b) and (c) federal categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR Part 403 and applicable categorical standards).
- Prohibits discharge of oil and grease from industrial facilities that violates federal pretreatment standards.
- **Excessive discharge definition**: BOD or TSS concentrations greater by a factor of 5× the 24-hour permitted average concentration (in a composite sample taken at 15-minute intervals over 4 hours), or mass emission exceeding that specified in the connection permit.
- Discharge limitations may be supplemented by more stringent local limits.
- Local discharge limits are established in **Tables C1 and C2** of the ordinance; specific numerical values in those tables were not publicly retrievable online — [VERIFY by requesting from City Public Works].
- Establishes issuance of **Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permits (IWDP)** to significant industrial users.
