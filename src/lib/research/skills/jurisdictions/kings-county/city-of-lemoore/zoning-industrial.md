## Development Standards (Industrial/Commercial)

Development standards for office, commercial, industrial, and special-purpose zoning districts are established in **Table 9-5A-4B** (§ 9-5A-4), covering:
- Minimum lot area
- Allowed density / intensity
- Building setbacks
- Maximum building height
- Lot coverage

Additional building height above the base standard may be allowed through site plan and architectural review when additional height is necessary for mechanical equipment as part of an industrial operation.

Source: https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/lemooreca/latest/lemoore_ca/0-0-0-8760
