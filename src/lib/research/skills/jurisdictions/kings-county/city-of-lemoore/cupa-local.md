## CUPA Agency

The City of Lemoore is **not its own CUPA**. All Unified Program services are delivered by:

**Kings County Department of Public Health — Environmental Health Services (KCDPH-EHS)**
460 Kings County Dr., Suite 101
Hanford, CA 93230
Phone: (559) 584-1401
After-hours emergency: (559) 852-2720

KCDPH-EHS is the sole Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for Kings County, covering all incorporated and unincorporated areas including the City of Lemoore.

## Programs Administered

| Program | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) | Required for businesses storing/using hazardous materials above threshold quantities; submitted via CERS |
| Hazardous Waste Generator Program | Oversight of on-site hazardous waste generation, storage, and manifest/transporter compliance |
| Above Ground Storage Tank (AST) / SPCC | Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure compliance for petroleum ASTs |
| Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program | Permitting, removal applications, compliance certification |
| CalARP (Accidental Release Prevention) | Risk Management Plans for facilities with regulated substances above threshold quantities |
| Tiered Permitting (TP) | Tiered permitting for on-site hazardous waste treatment |

## Online Reporting

All HMBP, hazardous waste, and UST information must be submitted through **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System): https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/

## Local Hazmat Ordinance

The City of Lemoore does not have a separate local hazardous materials ordinance — CUPA compliance is governed by California Health and Safety Code Chapter 6.95 (HMBP) and the state Unified Program statutes (HSC § 25404 et seq.) as administered by KCDPH-EHS. The city's fire department (LVFD) participates in hazmat incident response but does not issue separate local hazmat permits.

## Notes

- **NAS Lemoore carve-out**: The adjacent Naval Air Station is a federal installation and is outside KCDPH-EHS CUPA jurisdiction. DoD facilities manage hazardous materials under their own federal program.
- For tiered permitting forms and approved contractor/transporter lists, see the Kings County CUPA page.
