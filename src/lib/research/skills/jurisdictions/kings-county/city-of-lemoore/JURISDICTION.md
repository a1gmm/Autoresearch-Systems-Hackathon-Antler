## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air Quality

- **District: San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution Control District (SJVAPCD / "Valley Air District")**. Kings County is one of the eight counties within the SJVAPCD's jurisdiction. The district is the ATC/APC authority for stationary sources; the city has no local air-quality ordinance.
- Permit applications, rules, and enforcement through SJVAPCD, headquartered in Fresno.
- Source: https://www.valleyair.org/about and https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/san-joaquin-valley-air-pollution-control-district
- GOTCHA: NAS Lemoore is a DoD installation — federal sources on base are outside SJVAPCD civilian jurisdiction.

### CUPA (Unified Hazardous Materials Program)

- **Not its own CUPA** — served by **Kings County Department of Public Health, Environmental Health Services (KCDPH-EHS)** as the county CUPA (Certified Unified Program Agency).
- KCDPH-EHS address: 460 Kings County Dr., Suite 101, Hanford, CA 93230. Phone: (559) 584-1401; after-hours: (559) 852-2720.
- CUPA programs administered: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Program (generator, transporter, disposal), Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program, Above Ground Storage Tank / SPCC Program, CalARP (accidental release prevention).
- Online reporting through CERS (California Environmental Reporting System).
- Source: https://www.kcdph.com/cupahazwaste

### Fire (AHJ for Fire Code)

- **Lemoore Volunteer Fire Department (LVFD)** — city's fire authority; volunteer organization with stations at 210 Fox Street and 41 Cinnamon Drive (second station). ~35 volunteer firefighters.
- LVFD conducts annual fire inspections of commercial/industrial occupancies, issues permits, and responds to hazardous materials incidents.
- **Adopted code**: 2016 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9), adopting ordinance **Ordinance 2016-14** (12-6-2016), codified at **Lemoore Municipal Code Title 8, Article 1E (§ 8-1E-1)**. ### Wastewater (POTW / Pretreatment)

- **POTW**: City of Lemoore Wastewater Treatment Facility (lagoon-based system — four ponds, first two aerated in parallel, next two partially aerated in series). Capacity: ~8.8 acres, 70 million gallons storage; treats ~1.7 mgd domestic flow (population ~27,000).
- **Discharge permit**: WDRs (Waste Discharge Requirements) Order **R5-2019-0008** issued by the **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB Region 5)**. Effluent (combined City + Leprino Foods) discharged to Stone Ranch / River Ranch land-application area (~1,900–2,420 acres, Leprino-owned) for irrigation of alfalfa, triticale, cotton. No direct surface discharge to waterways.
- **Key effluent limits under R5-2019-0008** (combined City + Leprino effluent): BOD₅ ≤ 40 mg/L monthly avg / 80 mg/L daily max; TSS ≤ 40 mg/L monthly avg / 80 mg/L daily max; FDS ≤ 1,400 mg/L annual avg; Total coliform ≤ 23 MPN/100 mL (7-day median). Disinfected secondary-23 standard (Cal. Code of Regs., tit. 22, § 60301.225).
- **Major Industrial User**: **Leprino Foods Company** operates two cheese production facilities in Lemoore generating ~2.75 mgd process water, treated at Leprino's own WWTF (HRAS reactors, DAF units, SBRs) before combining with City effluent.
- **Sewer Use Ordinance**: Lemoore Municipal Code **Title 7, Chapter 7, Article D** (§§ 7-7D-1 through 7-7D-5+). Establishes discharge prohibitions, industrial wastewater discharge permit (IWDP) requirements, and compliance with federal pretreatment standards (40 CFR Part 403). Prohibits oil/grease from industrial facilities exceeding federal pretreatment standards; BOD/TSS over 5× the 24-hour permitted average in a composite sample constitutes excessive discharge.

## Notes

- GOTCHA: **Federal-land carve-out — Naval Air Station (NAS) Lemoore**, adjacent to the city, operates its own DoD Federal Fire Department and is outside local civilian fire/building/CUPA/SJVAPCD civilian jurisdiction. https://cnrsw.cnic.navy.mil/Installations/NAS-Lemoore/Operations-and-Management/Program-Links/Fed-Fire/
