## Fire Authority (AHJ)

**Lemoore Volunteer Fire Department (LVFD)**
- Volunteer organization; ~35 volunteer firefighters
- Station 1: 210 Fox Street, Lemoore, CA
- Station 2: 41 Cinnamon Drive, Lemoore, CA (newer station, opened to address railroad-crossing access issues)
- Conducts commercial/industrial fire inspections, issues permits, and responds to hazmat incidents
- Fire department page: https://lemoore.com/departments/fire-lvfd
