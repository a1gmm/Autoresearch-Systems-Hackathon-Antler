## CUPA Agency

**Kings County Department of Public Health, Environmental Health Services (KCDPH-EHS)**

The City of Hanford is NOT a separate CUPA. Hanford falls under the Kings County CUPA, which administers the Unified Program for all jurisdictions within Kings County including the City of Hanford, unincorporated Kings County, and other incorporated cities.

## Programs Administered

Kings County CUPA administers all six California Unified Program elements:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — Businesses handling hazardous materials above threshold quantities must submit plans via the California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) portal: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator / Onsite Treatment** — Manages hazardous waste generators, transporters, and coordination with DTSC.
3. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — Permits, closures, and financial responsibility oversight.
4. **Aboveground Storage Tanks (AST)** — SPCC plan oversight and petroleum storage programs.
5. **Tiered Permitting (Hazardous Waste Facilities)** — Permit by Rule, Conditional Authorization, Conditional Exemption.
6. **CalARP (California Accidental Release Prevention)** — [VERIFY whether Kings County CUPA administers CalARP directly or defers; small county CUPAs sometimes co-administer with fire or state].

## Fee Schedule

Not published on the KCDPH or county website as of the fetch date (2026-06-03). Kings County maintains a Master Fee Schedule at https://www.countyofkingsca.gov/services/master-fee-schedule — check that document for CUPA/EHS program fees, or contact KCDPH-EHS directly at (559) 584-1401.

State surcharge: CalEPA increased the Annual State Surcharge for CUPA Oversight from $67 to $79 per regulated business, effective August 22, 2025.

## CERS Reporting

All HMBP submissions and updates must be made electronically through the CERS Business Portal. KCDPH-EHS is the local agency reviewer. CERS login: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/

## Notes

- The KCDPH-EHS office is physically located in Hanford (Kings County seat), so it is the de facto local point of contact for City of Hanford businesses.
- Two county websites serve the same EHS division: kcdph.com (Kings County Department of Public Health) and countyofkingsca.gov — both are official.
