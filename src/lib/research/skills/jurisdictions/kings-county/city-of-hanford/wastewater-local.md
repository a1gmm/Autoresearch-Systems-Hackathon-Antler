## POTW

**City of Hanford Wastewater Treatment Facility**

- Treatment capacity: 8.0 million gallons per day (MGD)
- Current average flow: ~4.5 MGD
- Last major expansion: 2004
- Effluent disposal: secondary treated, disinfected effluent discharged to Lakeside Ditch Company for crop irrigation of over 10,000 acres (reclamation permit); prevents approximately 5,000 acre-feet of groundwater pumping annually.
- Monitoring: City contracts with private laboratories for required analyses.
- Contact: 559-585-2576 (business hours)
- Tour scheduling: contact WWTP directly.

### Local Discharge Limits

Numeric local limits (BOD, TSS, pH, metals, etc.) are established in **§13.08.060**. The specific concentration values are not publicly reproduced online outside the municipal code text (eCode360 blocks automated access). [VERIFY — obtain the full §13.08.060 table by accessing eCode360 manually or requesting the ordinance from the Utility Division.]

Key provisions confirmed from public search results:
- Limits apply at the point where wastewater is discharged to the POTW.
- The Director of Public Works may impose **more restrictive standards** than categorical federal pretreatment standards on a case-by-case basis (§13.08.060).
- Site-specific limits may be established through an individual Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit (IWDP).
- Higher concentrations may be authorized site-specifically if they will not cause pass-through or interference (§13.08.060).

### FOG (Fats, Oils, and Grease) Program

- All persons/entities discharging to the sewer must implement Best Management Practices (BMPs) to minimize FOG discharge.
- Food service establishments must install, operate, and maintain adequate grease removal devices (grease interceptors/traps).
- Waste hauling records and manifests must be maintained and available for inspection.

## Industrial Wastewater Permit (IWP) Process

- Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permits (IWDPs) are issued by the City of Hanford (Utility Division / Public Works).
- Permit conditions may include: pretreatment device requirements, BMP implementation, monitoring and reporting, waste hauling records.
- **No online portal identified** for IWDP applications — contact Utility Division directly.
- Contact: Utilities Division, 900 S 10th Avenue, Hanford, CA 93230; Phone: 559-585-2550 (business hours); Emergency: 559-585-2540.

## Stormwater / Collection System

- Sanitary sewer collection system: approximately 212 miles of main lines and 22 pump stations.
- Utility Division maintains the collection system; Wastewater Treatment Facility handles treatment.
- Storm drainage is a separate program (Storm Drainage Division).
