## Authority Having Jurisdiction

Hanford Fire Department (career, municipal). Founded 1891; one of California's oldest departments.
- Headquarters: 350 W. Grangeville Blvd, Hanford, CA 93230
- Fire Prevention / Inspections: 559-585-2594
- Email: fireprevention@hanfordca.gov

## Adopted Fire Code Edition

**2022 California Fire Code (CFC)** — confirmed as the current adopted edition on the City of Hanford Design Requirements & Codes page (https://www.ci.hanford.ca.us/178/Design-Requirements-Codes). The page states "Codes listed are the 2022 Edition unless otherwise noted."

Additional enforced standards:
- California Code of Regulations (CCR) Title 19
- NFPA standards (applicable editions)
- Hanford Municipal Code (various chapters)

## Municipal Code Chapter

**Title 15 (Buildings and Construction), Chapter 15.02** — California Building Standards Code adoption and local amendments.
- Local amendments to the California Building Standards Code (which incorporates the CFC as Part 9) are codified at **§15.02.030 (Amendments)**.
- Full text: https://ecode360.com/43892281 [VERIFY specific amendment text — eCode360 access required]
- Fire Protection Development Impact Fee: Chapter 15.45 of the Municipal Code (finances capital improvements for the Fire Department). Source: https://ecode360.com/43892506

## Permit Types

Operational permits issued by Hanford Fire Department include:
- Annual fire and life safety inspection permit (businesses, assemblies, multi-family)
- Fire protection systems permit
- Fire extinguisher service permit
- Fireworks sales and use permit
- Weed abatement
- New construction review (coordinated with Building Division)
- Hazardous materials operational permits (H-1 through H-5 tiers)
- Hood suppression systems
- Tank installation/inspection
- Spray booth
- Special events with apparatus

## Notes

- Assemblies with >49 people must be inspected **quarterly**.
- Commercial hood systems must be inspected **semi-annually**.
