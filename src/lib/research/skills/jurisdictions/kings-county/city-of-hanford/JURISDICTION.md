## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air

- **Controlling district: San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution Control District (SJVAPCD / Valley Air District)** — Kings County is one of the eight counties within the district's jurisdiction (San Joaquin, Stanislaus, Merced, Madera, Fresno, Kings, Tulare, and the San Joaquin Valley portion of Kern).
- Stationary source permits (Authority to Construct / Permit to Operate) are issued by SJVAPCD; no city-level air permitting exists.
- SJVAPCD Fresno office handles Kings County facilities: https://www.valleyair.org/about
- SJVAPCD Rule 9510 (Indirect Source Review) applies to qualifying Hanford development projects.
- Open burning regulated by San Joaquin Air Pollution Control District rules (referenced in Hanford Fire Dept FAQ).
- Source: https://www.valleyair.org/about

### CUPA (Unified Hazardous Materials Program)

- **Kings County Department of Public Health, Environmental Health Services** is the CUPA for Kings County — the City of Hanford is NOT a separate CUPA; it falls under county jurisdiction.
- CUPA office address: 460 Kings County Dr., Suite 101, Hanford, CA 93230 (phone: 559-584-1401; after-hours: 559-852-2720).
- Programs administered: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP via CERS portal), Hazardous Waste, Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Above Ground Storage Tank (AST/SPCC), and Tiered Permitting.
- Businesses submit HMBP electronically through the CERS Business Portal.
- Fee schedule: [VERIFY] — not published on the KCDPH web page; contact EHS directly or check Kings County Master Fee Schedule.
- Source: https://www.kcdph.com/cupahazwaste

### Fire

- **Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ): Hanford Fire Department** — city's own career municipal fire department (founded 1891), headquartered at 350 W. Grangeville Blvd.
- **Adopted code: 2022 California Fire Code (CFC)** plus CCR Title 19 and NFPA standards, per the city's Design Requirements & Codes page (confirmed 2026).
- Municipal Code authority: Title 15 (Buildings and Construction), Chapter 15.02 (California Building Standards Code) — local amendments noted in §15.02.030 [VERIFY exact amendment text via eCode360].
- Annual fire and life safety inspections conducted for all businesses.
- Permit types: operational permits, fire protection systems, fire extinguisher service, fireworks sales, weed abatement, new construction review.
- FY 2024-25 fee schedule published (PDF on city website — see fire-code-local.md for full table).
- Fire prevention contact: fireprevention@hanfordca.gov / 559-585-2594.
- Source: https://www.ci.hanford.ca.us/1543/Inspections | https://www.ci.hanford.ca.us/178/Design-Requirements-Codes

### Wastewater / Pretreatment

- **POTW: City of Hanford Wastewater Treatment Facility** — capacity 8.0 MGD, current average flow ~4.5 MGD (last expanded 2004). Treated effluent discharged to Lakeside Ditch Company for crop irrigation (~10,000 acres).
- **Local sewer use ordinance: Hanford Municipal Code, Title 13, Chapter 13.08 (Sewer Service System)**.
- Pretreatment provisions: §13.08.050 (Prohibited Discharge Standards), §13.08.060 (Specific Limitations of Wastes into Wastewater Sewers), §13.08.062 (Pretreatment of Wastewater), §13.08.062A (National Categorical Pretreatment Standards).
- Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permits (IWDPs) available on a site-specific basis.
- Director of Public Works may impose more restrictive local limits than categorical standards on a case-by-case basis (§13.08.060).
- FOG (fats, oils, and grease) BMPs required for all food-service dischargers.
- Numeric local limits for BOD/TSS/pH: [VERIFY — specific table not publicly published; contact Utility Division or obtain Chapter 13.08 full text].
- WWTP contact: 559-585-2576; Utilities Division: 559-585-2550; Emergency: 559-585-2540.

### Stormwater

- **Permit type: NPDES Phase II Small MS4** — City of Hanford designated as a small Municipal Separate Storm Sewer System (MS4) under the State's Phase II General Permit effective March 10, 2003.
- **Administering board: State Water Resources Control Board (SWRCB)** — under Phase II General Permit (NPDES No. CAS000004).
- Storm Water Management Plan (SWMP) on file with SWRCB; published December 2005 (older document — confirm current SWMP/annual report status with city).
- Six Minimum Control Measures (MCMs): Public Education, Public Involvement, Illicit Discharge Detection and Elimination, Construction Site Runoff Control, Post-Construction Management, and Municipal Good Housekeeping.
- City utility division maintains ~212 miles of sewer main lines and 22 pump stations.
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/water_issues/programs/stormwater/swmp/hanford_swmp.pdf | https://www.ci.hanford.ca.us/1564/Utility-Division

### Zoning (Industrial Districts)

- **Zoning code: Hanford Municipal Code, Title 17 (Zoning Ordinance)** — effective June 1, 2017; administered by the Planning Division (Community Development, 315-321 N. Douty St., 559-585-2500).
- Industrial zone districts: **I-L (Light Industrial)** [Chapter 17.34] and **I-H (Heavy Industrial)** [Chapter 17.36].
- Land use table: §17.08.030 (Commercial, Office, and Industrial Use Table) governs permitted (P), conditional (C), administrative (A), and temporary (T) uses in I-L and I-H zones.
- Key industrial uses in I-L/I-H: general manufacturing (P in I-H, limited in I-L), intensive manufacturing (C in I-H), hazardous waste facility (C in I-H), agricultural processing (C/P depending on zone), research and development (P in I-L), brewery/winery/distillery (C in I-H).
- Conditional Use Permit (CUP) process governed by Chapter 17.80.
- Site plan review required for most industrial development (Chapter 17.72).
- Source: https://www.ci.hanford.ca.us/DocumentCenter/View/381/Final-Zoning-Ordinance-June-1-2017-PDF | https://ecode360.com/HA4933
