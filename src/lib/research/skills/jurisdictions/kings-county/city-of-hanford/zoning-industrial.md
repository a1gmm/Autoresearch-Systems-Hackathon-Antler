## Industrial Zone Districts

Hanford uses two industrial zone designations:

| Zone | Name | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| **I-L** | Light Industrial | Chapter 17.34 |
| **I-H** | Heavy Industrial | Chapter 17.36 |

(Note: Hanford does NOT use M-1/M-2 nomenclature — the codes are I-L and I-H.)

## Light Industrial Zone (I-L) — Chapter 17.34

### Development Standards (§17.34)

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Min. site area (south of Houston Ave.) | 20,000 sq ft (CUP allows smaller) |
| Min. site area (north of Houston Ave.) | 6,000 sq ft |
| Min. lot frontage | 60 ft (unless reciprocal access agreement recorded) |
| Front building setback | 10 ft minimum |
| Rear building setback | 0 ft (15 ft if abutting street or R/O/PF/AP/CO zone) |
| Side building setback | 0 ft (15 ft if abutting street or R/O/PF/AP/CO zone) |
| Max. structure height | 75 ft (100 ft if set back ≥200 ft from any lot line) |
| Usable open space | No minimum requirement |
| Driveways near corners | Min. 100 ft from curb radius (unless City Engineer approves shorter) |
| Gated entrances setback | 100 ft from public ROW (unless City Engineer approves) |
| Parking | Per Chapter 17.54 — all manufacturing/processing uses: 1 space per 1,000 sq ft |

## Heavy Industrial Zone (I-H) — Chapter 17.36

### Development Standards (§17.36)

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Min. site area | 20,000 sq ft |
| Min. lot frontage | 60 ft (unless reciprocal access agreement recorded) |
| Front building setback | 10 ft minimum |
| Rear building setback | 0 ft (15 ft if abutting street or R/O/PF/AP/CO zone) |
| Side building setback | 0 ft (15 ft if abutting street or R/O/PF/AP/CO zone) |
| Max. structure height | 75 ft (100 ft if set back ≥200 ft from any lot line) |
| Usable open space | No minimum requirement |
| Driveways near corners | Min. 100 ft from curb radius |
| Gated entrances setback | 100 ft from public ROW |
| Parking | Per Chapter 17.54 — same as I-L |

Both I-L and I-H share identical development standards (setbacks, height, lot size) except that I-L has a reduced minimum site area north of Houston Avenue.

## Other Key Use Categories in I-L / I-H (§17.08.030)

- **Outdoor storage ancillary to permitted use**: P in both I-L and I-H
- **Temporary materials/equipment storage yard**: T in I-L, P in I-H
- **Caretaker residence or quarters**: A (in both)
- **Day care for employees on same site**: P in both
- **Food service for employees on same site**: P in both
- **Electric vehicle recharging facility**: A in both
- **Laboratory (general, medical, or dental)**: P in I-L and I-H
- **Office (professional or commercial)**: P in both
- **Research and development facility**: P in I-L, P in I-H

## CUP Process — Chapter 17.80

A Conditional Use Permit (CUP) is required for uses designated "C" in the land use table.
- Decision-maker: Planning Commission
- Application filed with Planning Division
- Public hearing required
- Findings must be made consistent with the General Plan
- Appeals go to City Council

## Administrative Use Permit — Chapter 17.74

For uses designated "A" — processed by Community Development Director without public hearing.

## Site Plan Review — Chapter 17.72

Site Plan Review Permit required for most industrial development prior to building permit issuance. Administered by Site Plan Review Committee.

## Screening, Fences, and Walls (§17.34.130 / §17.36.130)

- Open storage of materials/equipment must be screened by a solid wall or fence, minimum 7 ft height, not visible above the fence.
- Block wall of minimum 7 ft required along any lot line abutting a residential, office, public facilities, airport protection, or conservation zone.
- No fence or wall within landscaped setback area adjacent to a street.
