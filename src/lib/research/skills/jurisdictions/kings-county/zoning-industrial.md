## Industrial Zoning Article

**Article 8 — (I) Industrial Zoning Districts**

The Development Code is organized by article per land use category. Article 8 covers the Industrial zoning district, designated **(I)** on the zoning map. ## Permitted vs. Conditional Uses — (I) District

Specific permitted-by-right uses and conditional uses in the (I) Industrial district: **[VERIFY — not retrievable from automated sources]**. Typical California county industrial zoning districts at this scale allow:

By right (typically): Manufacturing, warehousing, distribution, storage yards, truck terminals, utility installations, contractor yards.
Conditionally (CUP typically required): Hazardous materials handling, waste transfer stations, recycling facilities, outdoor storage of certain materials, agricultural processing with significant off-site impacts.

These are general patterns; the actual Kings County Article 8 text controls. Contact CDA at (559) 852-2670 for a definitive use determination.
