## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air — San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution Control District (SJVAPCD)

Kings County is entirely within the San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution Control District (SJVAPCD / "Valley Air District"), one of eight counties in the district (San Joaquin, Stanislaus, Merced, Madera, Fresno, Kings, Tulare, and the SJV portion of Kern). The district is the sole AHJ for stationary-source air permits, Title V operating permits, and CEQA air-quality review in the county — Kings County itself has no local air program.

- District jurisdiction: stationary sources (factories, plants), agricultural burning (substantially phased out by 2025 per Rule 4103), fugitive dust (Rule 8051).
- Title V and NSR permits issued by SJVAPCD, not the county.
- Source: https://www.valleyair.org/about

### CUPA — Kings County Department of Public Health (KCDPH), Environmental Health Services

Kings County Department of Public Health, Environmental Health Services (EHS) — the county's Certified Unified Program Agency. All four incorporated cities (Hanford, Lemoore, Corcoran, Avenal) are served by the county CUPA; no city runs its own CUPA.

Programs administered under the Unified Program:
- Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) — submitted via CERS
- Hazardous Waste Generator program (VSQG / SQG / LQG / non-RCRA LQG)
- Underground Storage Tanks (UST) — permit, plan check, inspection, closure
- Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)
- California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP / RMP) — Levels 1–4
- Permit by Rule (PBR) / Conditionally Exempt operations

EHS contact: 460 Kings County Drive, Suite 101, Hanford, CA 93230 | Phone: (559) 584-1411 | After-hours emergency: (559) 852-2720 | Email: ehs@co.kings.ca.us

### Wastewater — City-operated POTWs (no county-wide sewer system)

Kings County's unincorporated area has no county-operated sewer system. Industrial wastewater pretreatment is city-specific:

### Stormwater — Phase II MS4 / State General Permit

Kings County's unincorporated area is not on a Phase I MS4 permit. Small municipalities in urbanized areas (including Kings County cities below 100k population) are covered under the statewide Phase II Small MS4 General Permit (State Water Board Order No. 2013-0001-DWQ, as amended). Construction sites ≥1 acre require coverage under the Statewide Construction General Permit. Industrial facilities requiring stormwater coverage use the Statewide Industrial General Permit.

The Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 5) is the relevant regional board. Phase I-level MS4 permit (Order R5-2016-0040) covers larger municipalities in the Central Valley but not Kings County unincorporated areas. ## GOTCHAs

- **Federal-land carve-out — Naval Air Station Lemoore.** The base operates its own DoD Federal Fire Department and is outside all local civilian fire/building/CUPA jurisdiction. Source: https://cnrsw.cnic.navy.mil/Installations/NAS-Lemoore/Operations-and-Management/Program-Links/Fed-Fire/
- **Fire AHJ is split.** KCFD covers unincorporated + Avenal + Corcoran; Hanford and Lemoore run their own departments. Always confirm the specific city before assuming county is the AHJ.
- **No county-level sewer.** Wastewater regulations are city-by-city. Unincorporated industrial sites use OWTS or WDRs, not a county pretreatment program.
- **County website (countyofkingsca.gov) blocks automated crawlers.** All document IDs and fee schedules must be verified by direct staff contact or in-person visit. Phone for EHS: (559) 584-1411; Fire Marshal: (559) 852-2885.
