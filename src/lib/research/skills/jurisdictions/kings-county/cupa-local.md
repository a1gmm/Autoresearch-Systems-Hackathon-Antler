## CUPA Agency

**Kings County Department of Public Health (KCDPH), Environmental Health Services (EHS)**

- Address: 460 Kings County Drive, Suite 101, Hanford, CA 93230
- Phone: (559) 584-1411
- After-hours emergency: (559) 852-2720
- Email: ehs@co.kings.ca.us
- Hours: Monday–Thursday 8:00 am–5:00 pm; Friday 8:00 am–12:00 pm
- Web: https://www.kcdph.com/cupahazwaste

Kings County EHS is the sole CUPA for the entire county. All four incorporated cities (Hanford, Lemoore, Corcoran, Avenal) are under the county CUPA — no city operates its own sub-CUPA.

## Programs Administered

All six Unified Program elements:

| Program | State Authority | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) | H&SC §25500–25520 | Filed via CERS |
| Hazardous Waste Generator (VSQG/SQG/LQG/non-RCRA LQG) | H&SC §25100–25250 | DTSC generator categories apply |
| Underground Storage Tanks (UST) | H&SC §25280–25299.8; 23 CCR Ch.16 | Plan check, permit, inspection, closure |
| Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) | H&SC §25270–25270.13 | Tanks ≥1,320 gal aggregate or ≥660 gal single |
| CalARP / Risk Management Plan (RMP) | H&SC §25531–25543.3; 19 CCR Ch.4.5 | Levels 1–4 |
| Permit by Rule (PBR) / Conditionally Exempt | 22 CCR §67450.2 | Including PBR-HHW |

Reporting platform: **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System) — all UST information and HMBP data submitted electronically.

**UST fees confirmed from KCDPH UST Plan Check Application (rev. 7/26/2023):**
- New UST facility or tank installation: **$1,036 flat rate**
- UST System Upgrade (piping/UDC/sump): **$777 flat rate**
- Additional services (monitoring equipment, spill prevention): **$129/hr**
