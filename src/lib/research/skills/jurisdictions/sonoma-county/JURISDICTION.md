## EHS Local Rules by Family

### CUPA (Unified Program)

- **Agency:** Permit Sonoma — Fire Prevention and Hazardous Materials Division, Hazardous Materials Unit. Serves ~1,800 regulated businesses in unincorporated Sonoma County and several incorporated cities; see GOTCHA note below.
- **Local ordinance:** County Code **Chapter 29** (Hazardous Materials), enabled via **Ordinance No. 5015**. The California Fire Code's hazardous materials chapters are enforced by this division under the same umbrella.
- **Fee schedule:** Adopted via **Ordinance No. 6512** (Board of Supervisors, April 15, 2025), effective July 1, 2025 – June 30, 2026. Separate "Hazardous Materials Unit Fees" schedule (PDF) covers HMBP/Inventory, HW Generator Tiered Permitting, UST, APSA, and CalARP annual permit fees.
- **CUPA elements administered:** HMBP / Hazardous Materials Inventory Statement, Hazardous Waste Generator & Tiered Permitting, Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), CalARP.
- **Contact:** HazMat@sonomacounty.gov | (707) 565-2024 | 2550 Ventura Avenue, Santa Rosa, CA 95403 | Mon/Tue/Thu 8 AM–4 PM; Wed 10:30 AM–4 PM.
- **Portal (public access viewer):** https://permitsonoma.org/divisions/firepreventionandhazmat/servicesandfees/hazardousmaterialsunitandcupaprogram/hazmatcupapublicrecords
- Source: https://permitsonoma.org/divisions/firepreventionandhazmat/servicesandfees/hazardousmaterialsunitandcupaprogram
- Source: https://www.permitsonoma.com/permitservices/permitguidelinesanddocuments/feeschedules/feeschedules2025-2026

- **GOTCHA — multi-CUPA county (split):** Four separate CUPAs operate in Sonoma County. Permit Sonoma's CUPA does NOT cover the cities of Santa Rosa, Petaluma, Healdsburg, or Sebastopol: (1) **City of Santa Rosa Fire Department** (Santa Rosa); (2) **City of Petaluma Fire Department — Fire Prevention Bureau** (Petaluma); (3) **Healdsburg/Sebastopol CUPA** — City of Healdsburg Fire Department runs Healdsburg AND Sebastopol via a 1994 Joint Powers Agreement. Permit Sonoma covers unincorporated areas plus Cloverdale, Cotati, Rohnert Park, Sonoma, and Town of Windsor — confirm each facility's city against the live UNIDOCS member page.

### Fire Authority / AHJ — Fire Code

- **AHJ for unincorporated county (Fire Prevention / CFC permitting):** Permit Sonoma — Fire Prevention and Hazardous Materials Division. Contact: FirePrevention@sonomacounty.gov | (707) 565-2361 | 2550 Ventura Avenue, Santa Rosa, CA 95403 | Mon–Thu 1–4 PM (in-person by appointment).
- **Fire suppression AHJ (operations):** Much of unincorporated Sonoma County is served by the **Sonoma County Fire District (SCFD)**, an independent special district formed April 2019 (Windsor, Rincon Valley, Bennett Valley, Mountain agencies consolidated; Russian River FPD joined July 2020; Forestville FPD joined July 2021). Other unincorporated areas fall under independent fire protection districts and County Service Area (CSA) 40.
- **Adopted fire code:** Sonoma County Code **Chapter 13 — Sonoma County Fire Safety Ordinance** adopts by reference, with local amendments, selected provisions of the California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9). The **2025 California Fire Code** edition (Title 24, 2025) was adopted with local amendments by the Board of Supervisors on **December 9, 2025** (codified via **Ordinance No. 6535**, effective **January 1, 2026**). Previous adoption was Ordinance No. 6396 (2022 CFC).
- **Local fire-safe ordinances:** Chapter 13 also contains the Sonoma County Fire Safety Ordinance for WUI/VHFHSZ construction requirements. Chapter 13A — "Duty to Maintain Defensible Space and Abate Hazardous Vegetation and Combustible Material" — applies to unimproved and improved parcels in unincorporated areas.
- **Fire prevention permit fees:** Covered in the "Fire Prevention Fees" 2025-2026 schedule (PDF) adopted via Ordinance 6512.

### Industrial Wastewater / Sewer Use (POTW + Pretreatment)

- **POTW operator:** Sonoma County Water Agency (SCWA / Sonoma Water). Operates five sanitation zones: Occidental County Sanitation District, Russian River County Sanitation District, Sonoma Valley County Sanitation District, South Park County Sanitation District, and Sonoma Water proper.
- **Sewer use / pretreatment ordinance:** **Sonoma County Water Agency Sanitation Code Ordinance** — originally adopted as SCWA Ordinance 15 (December 13, 1994); most recently amended as **SCWA Ordinance 97** (July 9, 2024). Document #116611. Cited authority: 40 CFR Part 403 (General Pretreatment Regulations) and CWA of 1977.
- **Code structure:** Article VI — Source Control Program (Sections 6.01–6.43) governs industrial discharges. Key sections: §6.07 (Categorical Pretreatment Standards); §6.16 (User Classifications — Categorical, Significant Non-Categorical, Non-Significant); §6.19 (Wastewater Discharge Permit Application); §6.21 (Permit to Discharge Required); §6.27 (Wastewater Discharge Permit Fees); §6.40 (Pretreatment Facilities).
- **IWP process:** Industrial/significant users must file a Wastewater Discharge Permit application with SCWA (form prescribed by the Agency). New users apply at least 30 days before connecting. Existing users had 180-day grace period post-ordinance effective date. Applications require SIC codes, process flow diagrams, discharge characterization, and existing environmental permits list.
- **Categorical industries** (examples subject to EPA effluent limits per 40 CFR 403.6/Subchapter N): metal finishers, electronic component manufacturers, canned/preserved seafood processors, timber products processors, soap/detergent manufacturers.
- **Unincorporated-county sewer:** County Code **Chapter 24 — Sewers and Sewage Disposal** (Article IV — Wastewater Discharge Regulations, § 24-52 et seq.) provides parallel authority for unincorporated-county sewer facilities not in SCWA zones.

### MS4 / Stormwater

- **Stormwater ordinance:** County Code **Chapter 11** — applies to all construction projects in unincorporated Sonoma County, including those exempt from the State General Permit.
- **MS4 permits (dual watershed):** Sonoma County holds two Municipal Separate Storm Sewer System (MS4) permits because the county straddles two RWQCB regions:
  - **Phase I (North Coast RWQCB):** Order R1-2015-0030; co-permittees: County of Sonoma, City of Santa Rosa, Sonoma County Water Agency. Covers City of Santa Rosa and unincorporated areas near Healdsburg, Windsor, Santa Rosa, Rohnert Park, Cotati, and Sebastopol.
  - **Phase II (San Francisco Bay RWQCB):** co-permittees: County of Sonoma, Sonoma County Water Agency. Covers unincorporated areas near Petaluma and Sonoma.
- **Industrial general permit:** State Industrial Stormwater General Permit (Order No. 97-03-DWQ); industrial facilities in covered categories must prepare a SWPPP and file annual reports by July 1.
- **Stormwater permit fees:** Covered in the "Storm Water Fees" 2025-2026 schedule (PDF) adopted via Ordinance 6512.
- **Contact / program admin:** Permit Sonoma Natural Resources Division — (707) 565-1900, option 5 | Planner@sonomacounty.gov | 2550 Ventura Avenue, Santa Rosa, CA 95403.

### Zoning — Industrial Districts / CUP Process

- **Zoning code:** County Code **Chapter 26 — Sonoma County Zoning Regulations** (codified through Ordinance No. 6535, November 4, 2025). Administered by Permit Sonoma — Planning Division.
- **Industrial district designations (Article 12, non-Coastal Zone):**
  - **M1** — Limited Urban Industrial District (Article 12)
  - **M2** — Heavy Industrial District (Article 8 cross-reference)
  - **M3** — Limited Rural Industrial District (Article 12)
  - **MP** — Industrial Park District (Article 12)
- **CUP process:** Certain industrial uses in M1, M3, and MP zones require a Conditional Use Permit (Use Permit) reviewed by the Sonoma County **Planning Commission**, which meets monthly. Planning staff pre-screens applications; contact Permit Sonoma Planning at (707) 565-1900, option 5 or Planner@sonomacounty.gov for a pre-application conference.
- **Zoning look-up:** https://permitsonoma.org/zoningcodes (parcel-level zoning search + code text)

## Notes

- GOTCHA (CUPA split): FOUR CUPAs operate in Sonoma County — Permit Sonoma (county + most cities), Santa Rosa FD, Petaluma FD, and the Healdsburg/Sebastopol JPA CUPA. A facility's HMBP/CUPA submittal must go to the correct CUPA. — https://permitsonoma.org/divisions/firepreventionandhazmat/servicesandfees/hazardousmaterialsunitandcupaprogram
- GOTCHA (fire consolidation): The independent **Sonoma County Fire District** (SCFD, formed 2019) is an operations-only district; it is NOT Permit Sonoma and does NOT issue CFC permits or CUPA approvals. Fire prevention permitting for the unincorporated county flows through Permit Sonoma's Fire Prevention and Hazardous Materials Division.
- GOTCHA (dual RWQCB): Sonoma County's stormwater compliance obligations split between North Coast RWQCB (Order R1-2015-0030) and SF Bay RWQCB depending on watershed. Verify the facility's watershed before citing a permit order number.
- Fee schedules (FY 2025-2026, effective July 1, 2025 per Ordinance 6512): Building Construction, Fire Prevention, Hazardous Materials Unit, Project Review (Planning), Storm Water, and Sewer & Water fees — all available as PDFs at https://www.permitsonoma.com/permitservices/permitguidelinesanddocuments/feeschedules/feeschedules2025-2026
