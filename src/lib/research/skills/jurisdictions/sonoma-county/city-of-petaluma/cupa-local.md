> Research reference for the City of Petaluma Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA).
> CRITICAL ROUTING NOTE: Petaluma runs its own CalEPA-certified CUPA through the Fire Prevention Bureau. Facilities inside city limits do NOT report to Permit Sonoma (the Sonoma County CUPA). This is a frequent error for multi-site operators.

## CUPA Identity

- **CUPA:** City of Petaluma Fire Department — Fire Prevention Bureau (full CUPA, independently certified by CalEPA)

## Programs Administered

Petaluma Fire Prevention Bureau implements all six CalEPA unified programs within city limits:

| # | Program | Acronym | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan | HMBP | Filed via CERS; Petaluma Fire reviews and inspects |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator Program | HWG | All tiers (conditionally exempt, small, large quantity generator) |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tanks | UST | Installation, operation, modification, closure, leak response |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act | APSA | Facilities with ≥1,320 gallons aggregate aboveground petroleum storage |
| 5 | California Accidental Release Prevention Program | CalARP | Local implementing agency for RMP submissions |
| 6 | CA Fire Code Hazardous Materials Permits | CFC HazMat | CFC Chapter 50+ operational permits — annual renewal |

## Enabling Ordinance

- **PMC Ch. 17.21** — Hazardous Materials Unified Program
- **PMC § 17.21.040** — Requires a CUPA permit before storing, dispensing, using, or handling hazardous materials above UFC § 105.8 thresholds
- **PMC § 17.21.020** — Definitions
- **PMC § 17.21.010** — Findings and purpose

## HMBP Details

- **Submission system:** CERS at https://cers.calepa.ca.gov — Petaluma Fire Prevention Bureau is the receiving CUPA
- **Who must comply:** Any business storing, using, or handling hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities (Health & Safety Code § 25507 — generally 55 gallons liquid, 500 lbs solid, 200 cubic feet gas)
- **Initial filing:** Within 30 days of bringing threshold materials onsite
- **Annual resubmission:** Required every year
- **Update triggers:** Material quantities increase ≥100%; new materials; address/ownership/name changes; significant operational changes (30-day filing trigger)

## Relationship to Permit Sonoma (County CUPA)

- **Permit Sonoma** (Sonoma County's CUPA) administers CUPA programs for unincorporated Sonoma County and cities that are not full CUPAs.
- **Petaluma does NOT use the county CUPA.** Petaluma Fire Prevention Bureau is a separate, full CUPA with independent authority.
- Permit Sonoma CUPA contact (for reference — unincorporated county and non-Petaluma cities only): https://permitsonoma.org/divisions/firepreventionandhazmat/servicesandfees/hazardousmaterialsunitandcupaprogram
