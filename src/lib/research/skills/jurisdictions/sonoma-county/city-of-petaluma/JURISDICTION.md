## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA / Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **AHJ:** City of Petaluma Fire Department — Fire Prevention Bureau is its OWN CalEPA-certified CUPA. Facilities inside city limits do NOT report to Permit Sonoma's county CUPA.
- **Location / contact:** 22 Bassett Street, Petaluma CA 94952; (707) 778-4389; [email protected]
- **Unified Program elements administered:** HMBP, Hazardous Waste Generator (all tiers), UST, APSA (Aboveground Petroleum Storage Acts), CalARP, and hazardous-materials portions of the California Fire Code.
- **Enabling ordinance:** Petaluma Municipal Code Ch. 17.21 — § 17.21.040 requires a CUPA permit before storing, dispensing, using, or handling hazardous materials above UFC § 105.8 thresholds.
- **Annual CUPA permit fees (FY 2024-25 Master Fee Schedule):**
  - Hazardous waste — small quantity generator Level 1: $163; Level 2: $327; Level 3: $490 (approx.)
  - Hazardous waste — large quantity generator Level 4: $655; Level 5: $818; Level 6: $982
  - APSA / Aboveground storage (flammable/combustible liquid, 1,320–4,999 gal, Tier I): $327/yr (HMP-1)
  - Aboveground storage (quantity ranges 1–7): $491–$1,474/yr (HMP-4 through HMP-10)
  - CalARP annual permit: $508 + state surcharge (HMP-13)
  - UST — permit to operate, first tank: $982/yr (HMP-24); each additional tank: per Cal/EPA schedule
  - Annual State Surcharge (HMP-12): billed per Cal/EPA, added to all permits
- **HMBP submission:** via California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) at cers.calepa.ca.gov — Petaluma Fire Prevention Bureau is the receiving CUPA.

### 2. Fire Code (AHJ & Adopted Edition)

- **AHJ:** Petaluma Fire Department — Fire Prevention Bureau (same bureau as CUPA above).
- **Adopted code:** 2022 California Fire Code (CFC), California Code of Regulations Title 24, Part 9, incorporating the 2021 Edition of the International Fire Code — codified at Petaluma Municipal Code Ch. 17.20, § 17.20.010.
- **Local amendments:** PMC § 17.20.050 contains Petaluma-specific amendments to the 2022 CFC; PMC § 17.20.070 adopts CFC Chapter 56 (Explosives and Fireworks) with local modifications.
- **Appendices adopted:** A, B, BB, C, CC, D, E, F, G, H, I, N, and O.
- **Permit types:**
  - *Construction permits* — required for fire sprinkler/suppression installation, high-piled storage systems, and construction involving hazardous materials or processes.
  - *Operational permits* — required for businesses with hazardous materials/operations, day care/residential care facilities, tent installations, tire storage, and similar uses.
- **Plan review:** online submittal only via city portal; fees assessed post-review by staff.
- **Fee schedule:** FY 2024-25 Master Fee Schedule — fire prevention section (FLS/FP series) and CUPA hazmat construction fees (HM series).
- **Contact:** (707) 778-4389; [email protected]

### 3. Industrial Wastewater / POTW Pretreatment

- **POTW operator:** City of Petaluma — Environmental Services Division; treatment at Ellis Creek Water Recycling Facility, 3890 Cypress Drive, Petaluma CA 94954.
- **Local sewer-use ordinance:** Petaluma Municipal Code Title 15, Water and Sewage:
  - Ch. 15.44 — Sewer Use and Source Control — General Provisions (definitions, national pretreatment standards framework)
  - Ch. 15.48 — Sewer Use and Source Control Regulations (local limits, BMPs as pretreatment limits per § 15.48.170)
  - Ch. 15.52 — Sewer Use Discharge Permits (IWP issuance, reporting requirements; categorical industrial users must submit baseline/periodic reports per § 15.52.070)
- **Industrial Wastewater Permit (IWP):** defines discharge amount, flow rate, and pollutant strength limits. Required for significant industrial users discharging to city sewer.
- **Special Discharge Permit:** short-term version (<1 year) for pretreated wastewater discharges; issued by Environmental Services Pretreatment group.
- **Application process:** Contact Environmental Compliance Inspector at (707) 776-3777 or [email protected] to determine permit need; mail completed application + processing fee to Ellis Creek Water Recycling Facility, Attn: Environmental Compliance, 3890 Cypress Drive, Petaluma CA 94954.
- **Local limits:** set in coordination with Regional Water Quality Control Board and EPA; BMPs expressly allowed as pretreatment limits where numerical limits are impractical (PMC § 15.48.170).

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater

- **Program:** Petaluma is a Permittee under California State Water Resources Control Board Phase II Municipal Stormwater NPDES permit (reissued 2013).
- **Local ordinance:** Petaluma Municipal Code Title 15, Part IV — Stormwater Management; key sections:
  - § 15.80.040 — Definitions (NPDES permit, MS4)
  - § 15.80.150 — New development and redevelopment: industrial/commercial facilities must install, implement, and maintain BMPs consistent with California Stormwater Quality Association BMP Handbooks.
- **Industrial facilities:** must implement site-specific BMPs; new development triggers post-construction stormwater controls under § 15.80.150.
- **Grading / erosion control:** Ch. 17.31 (§ 17.31.220 fee schedule) governs grading permits, which incorporate erosion and sediment controls.

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts & CUP Process

- **Zoning instrument:** Petaluma Implementing Zoning Ordinance (IZO) — zone districts and allowable uses in Ch. 4; use tables in Ch. 4 Tables and Ch. 6.
- **Industrial zone districts (PMC IZO § 4.020):**
  - **I (Industrial):** full-range manufacturing, industrial processing, general service, warehousing, storage, and distribution; maximum FAR 0.6.
  - **BP (Business Park):** business/professional offices, technology clusters, R&D, light industrial, visitor services; maximum FAR 1.5 (3.0 if parking is structured).
- **Uses permitted outright (P) in I/BP:** light manufacturing, R&D, printing/publishing, wholesale distribution, media production, laboratories, business support services, catering.
- **Uses requiring Conditional Use Permit (CUP) in I/BP:**
  - Petroleum product storage/distribution
  - Recycling facilities
  - Warehousing/indoor storage (BP zone)
  - Kennels/animal boarding (I zone)
- **CUP process:** Major CUPs processed at Planning Commission discretion; public notice required per IZO § 24.100.
- **Planning authority:** City of Petaluma — Community Development Department, 11 English Street, Petaluma CA 94952; (707) 778-4301.

## Key Gotchas

- **Own CUPA:** Petaluma runs its own CUPA through the Fire Prevention Bureau — facilities inside city limits do NOT go to Permit Sonoma's county CUPA. This is a common error for multi-site operators.
- **Single bureau for fire + CUPA:** The Fire Prevention Bureau is the AHJ for both the fire code (Ch. 17.20) and the CUPA/hazmat program (Ch. 17.21) — one stop for operational fire permits and HMBP compliance.
- **Sewer pretreatment separate from fire:** IWP and Special Discharge Permits are administered by Environmental Services / Ellis Creek facility, not the Fire Department.
