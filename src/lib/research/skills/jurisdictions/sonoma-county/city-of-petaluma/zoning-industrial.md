- **Instrument:** Petaluma Implementing Zoning Ordinance (IZO)

## Industrial Zone Districts (IZO § 4.020)

Petaluma has two industrially-oriented zone districts:

### I — Industrial

- **Purpose:** Full-range manufacturing, industrial processing, general service, warehousing, storage, and distribution consistent with the General Plan industrial land use classification.
- **Maximum FAR:** 0.6

### BP — Business Park

- **Purpose:** Business/professional offices, technology clusters, R&D, light industrial, visitor services.
- **Maximum FAR:** 1.5 (up to 3.0 if parking is structured)

## Permitted Uses (P) — I and BP Districts

Uses permitted outright in one or both industrial districts include:
- Light manufacturing
- Research and development (R&D)
- Printing and publishing
- Wholesale distribution / warehousing (I zone — check Tables for BP)
- Media production
- Laboratories
- Business support services
- Catering facilities

> [VERIFY] Confirm the exact "P" vs. "CUP" classification for each use type against the IZO Ch. 4 Use Tables at https://petaluma.municipal.codes/ZoningOrds/4.Tables before relying on any specific use classification.

## Uses Requiring Conditional Use Permit (CUP) — I / BP Districts

| Use | District |
|---|---|
| Petroleum product storage and distribution | I |
| Recycling facilities | I |
| Warehousing / indoor storage | BP |
| Kennels / animal boarding | I |

Other uses may also require a CUP; consult the IZO Ch. 4 Use Tables.

## CUP Process

- **Major CUPs:** Processed at Planning Commission discretion
- **Public notice:** Required per IZO § 24.100
- **Submission:** Through Community Development Department
- **Contact:** (707) 778-4301

## Interaction with EHS Permits

Industrial uses in Petaluma may require parallel approvals from:
- **Fire Prevention Bureau** — CFC operational permit + CUPA HMBP permit for hazardous materials (see cupa-local.md, fire-code-local.md)
- **Environmental Services** — IWP / Special Discharge Permit for industrial wastewater (see wastewater-local.md)
- **BAAQMD** — Air district permit for stationary sources (City of Petaluma is in Bay Area Air Quality Management District jurisdiction)
