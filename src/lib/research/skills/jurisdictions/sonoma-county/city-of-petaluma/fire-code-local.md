## Adopted Fire Code

- **Code edition:** California Fire Code (CFC) **2022 Edition** (California Code of Regulations Title 24, Part 9), incorporating the **2021 Edition of the International Fire Code** — adopted by reference via Petaluma Municipal Code Ch. 17.20, § 17.20.010
- **Appendices adopted:** Chapter 4, A, B, BB, C, CC, D, E, F, G, H, I, N, and O
- **Municipal code chapter:** PMC Ch. 17.20 (Title 17 — Building and Construction)
- **PMC current through:** Ordinance 2908 NCS, passed August 4, 2025

> [VERIFY — 2025 CFC adoption status] The 2025 California Fire Code became the statewide baseline on 2026-01-01. As of search results indexed through mid-2025, Petaluma's codified text still references the 2022 CFC. Confirm whether the city has adopted the 2025 edition via a subsequent ordinance.

## Local Amendments

- **PMC § 17.20.050** — Petaluma-specific amendments to the 2022 CFC (defensible space, fire protection systems, and other local modifications)
- **PMC § 17.20.070** — Adoption of CFC Chapter 56 (Explosives and Fireworks) with local modifications
- **Fire Prevention Policy Manual** — adopted by reference as supplemental interpretation guidance for the fire code

## Permit Types

### Construction Permits
Required for fire sprinkler/suppression system installation, high-piled storage systems, and construction involving hazardous materials or processes.

### Operational Permits
Required annually for businesses with:
- Hazardous materials/operations above CFC threshold quantities (per CFC Chapter 50 et seq.)
- Day care and residential care facilities
- Tent installations
- Tire storage
- Any other use listed in CFC § 105.6

## Combined Fire + CUPA Situation

Many industrial facilities require both:
- **CFC Operational Permit** (from Fire Prevention Bureau, § 17.20.010) for fire-code-triggered operations
- **CUPA HMBP permit** (PMC Ch. 17.21) for hazardous materials above UFC § 105.8 thresholds

Both are issued by the same bureau. Coordinate with Fire Prevention Bureau at (707) 778-4389 to handle both simultaneously. See cupa-local.md for CUPA details.
