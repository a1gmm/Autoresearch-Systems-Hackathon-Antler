> Research reference for the City of Petaluma Industrial Wastewater Pretreatment Program.
> The city operates its own POTW and pretreatment program — do NOT route to Sonoma County or the Regional Water Board for IWP issuance.
> Pretreatment authority is with Environmental Services / Ellis Creek facility, separate from the Fire Department CUPA.

## POTW / NPDES Overview

- **Receiving waters:** Petaluma River (Discharge Points 001 and 002, with 002 as the planned relocation of 001)
- **NPDES Permit:** CA0037810 (Tentative Order R2-2021-00XX, issued by CA Regional Water Board San Francisco Bay Region — effective July 1, 2021, with five-year term expiring June 30, 2026)
- **Classification:** Major NPDES discharger (U.S. EPA and RWQCB classification)
- **Permit renewal:** Discharger required to file reapplication no later than October 1, 2025; reissued permit status [VERIFY]
- **Dry weather flow cap:** Average dry weather influent flow may not exceed 6.7 MGD
- **Service area:** City of Petaluma and Penngrove

## Sewer-Use Ordinance — Municipal Code References

Petaluma Municipal Code Title 15 (Water and Sewage) governs sewer use and pretreatment:

| Chapter | Subject |
|---|---|
| **Ch. 15.44** | Sewer Use and Source Control — General Provisions (definitions, national pretreatment standards framework) |
| **Ch. 15.48** | Sewer Use and Source Control Regulations (local limits, BMPs as pretreatment limits per § 15.48.170) |
| **Ch. 15.52** | Sewer Use Discharge Permits (IWP issuance, reporting requirements; categorical industrial users must submit baseline/periodic reports per § 15.52.070) |

- **BMPs expressly allowed as pretreatment limits** where numerical limits are impractical — PMC § 15.48.170
- **Categorical industrial user (CIU) reporting:** Baseline Monitoring Reports and Periodic Compliance Reports required per § 15.52.070

## Permit Types

| Permit | Who Needs It | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **Industrial Wastewater Permit (IWP)** | Significant industrial users discharging process wastewater to city sewer (defines discharge amount, flow rate, pollutant-strength limits) | Annual |
| **Special Discharge Permit** | Short-term or one-time discharges of pretreated wastewater or large volumes of non-contaminated water | Up to 1 year |

Sector examples known to require pretreatment compliance: restaurants/food processing, dental facilities, auto mechanic shops, and categorical industries (metal finishing, food/beverage, etc.).

## Local Limits

Local pollutant limits are set in coordination with the San Francisco Bay Regional Water Quality Control Board and the U.S. EPA, based on Ellis Creek's treatment capacity and receiving-water quality objectives.

> [VERIFY — numeric local limits] Specific numeric mg/L limits for metals, BOD, TSS, oil & grease, and pH are not published on the public-facing pretreatment web page. Contact the Environmental Compliance Inspector at (707) 776-3777 or [email protected] for the current local limits table before quoting values in permits or permits applications.

Parameters known to be regulated under PMC Ch. 15.48 framework:
- BOD / TSS / oil & grease (surcharge basis)
- Metals (federal categorical + local limits)
- pH
- Temperature
- Toxic organics / VOCs
- Radioactive wastes, noxious gases (prohibited)

## Application Process

1. Contact Environmental Compliance Inspector at (707) 776-3777 or [email protected] to determine whether an IWP or Special Discharge Permit is required.
2. Obtain and complete the appropriate application form from Environmental Services.
3. Mail completed application + processing fee to: Ellis Creek Water Recycling Facility, Attn: Environmental Compliance, 3890 Cypress Drive, Petaluma, CA 94954.
4. Environmental Services reviews application; may conduct a site inspection before permit issuance.
5. Permit sets discharge conditions; ongoing self-monitoring and reporting required.

## Discharge Prohibitions (NPDES Permit CA0037810 basis)

Per the NPDES permit and PMC Ch. 15.44–15.48:
- Bypass of untreated wastewater to waters of the U.S. is prohibited
- Discharge during dry season (May 1 – October 20) to the Petaluma River is restricted (recycled-water demand must be met first)
- Industrial dischargers must not cause pass-through or interference at Ellis Creek
