**Sonoma County Water Agency (SCWA / "Sonoma Water")**
- Address: 404 Aviation Boulevard, Santa Rosa, CA 95403
- Administration: (707) 547-1900
- Operations desk (24-hour): (707) 523-1070
- Industrial Waste contact: IndustrialWaste@scwa.ca.gov

SCWA operates five sanitation zones serving unincorporated Sonoma County:
1. Occidental County Sanitation District
2. Russian River County Sanitation District
3. Sonoma Valley County Sanitation District
4. South Park County Sanitation District
5. Sonoma Water (agency-direct operations)

**Note on coverage:** Incorporated cities generally have their own sewer utilities or separate agencies. The SCWA sanitation zones primarily serve unincorporated areas. Confirm a facility's receiving utility before citing this file.

**Parallel county authority:** For unincorporated county sewer facilities not within SCWA sanitation zones, **County Code Chapter 24 — Sewers and Sewage Disposal** (Article IV — Wastewater Discharge Regulations, § 24-52 et seq.) provides parallel authority.

## Local Discharge Prohibitions (§ 6.05)

Specific numeric limits in § 6.05:
- **Flash point:** No wastewater with closed-cup flash point < 140°F (60°C) per 40 CFR Part 261.21
- **LEL:** No single combustible-gas reading > 10% LEL; no two successive readings > 5% LEL at point of discharge
- **pH:** No discharge with pH < 5.5 or > 10.0
- **Temperature:** No discharge causing treatment plant influent to exceed 40°C (104°F); no discharge to collection system exceeding 65°C (150°F)
- **Slug loads:** No slug flow/concentration exceeding 5× the 24-hour average for any period > 15 minutes

**§ 6.10 — Specific Pollutant Limitations:** Additional numeric limits on specific constituents may be set by separate Board resolution. The ordinance text does not enumerate fixed local numeric limits for metals or organics — those flow from 40 CFR Subchapter N categorical standards or individual permit conditions. ## User Classification (§ 6.16)

Two-tier structure:
1. **Significant Industrial Users (SIUs):**
   - **Class A — Categorical Users:** Subject to 40 CFR 403.6 / Subchapter N (40 CFR parts 405–471) categorical pretreatment standards; must pretreat to EPA toxic pollutant limits before discharge.
   - **Class B — Significant Non-Categorical Users:** High volume or high-strength industrial users not subject to categorical standards but requiring a Wastewater Discharge Permit.
2. **Non-Significant Industrial Users:** Lower-volume/lower-risk industrial users; may require permit contract rather than full permit depending on Agency determination.

Examples of categorical industries in SCWA service area: metal finishers, electronic component manufacturers, canned/preserved seafood processors, timber products processors, soap/detergent manufacturers.

## Industrial Wastewater Permit (IWP) Process (§§ 6.19–6.21)

1. **Who must apply:** Categorical users, significant non-categorical users, and any industrial user so designated by the Agency.
2. **New users:** Apply at least **30 days** before connecting to or contributing to Agency facilities.
3. **Existing users:** Must have applied within **180 days** of the Ordinance 97 effective date (July 9, 2024) if not previously permitted — deadline passed; existing unpermitted users are now overdue.
4. **Application form:** Agency-prescribed form; submit to SCWA.
5. **Required application contents** (§ 6.19) include: operator/owner name and facility address; SIC codes; list of all existing environmental permits and spill plans; process discharge time/duration/batch schedule; daily/monthly/seasonal flow rates per regulated stream; site/floor/mechanical/plumbing plans showing all sewer connections; process description and materials list; flow diagram for each major process; nature and concentration of regulated pollutants per stream; pretreatment compliance status and schedule if out of compliance.
6. **Items always required:** Items Q and R of the application form (contact SCWA for current form).
7. **Permit fee:** New industrial permit fee assessed at time of application (§ 6.19); annual fees set by Board resolution under § 6.27.

## Industrial Pretreatment Program Status

SCWA operates a full EPA-approved industrial pretreatment program under 40 CFR Part 403. The program includes monitoring, annual significant noncompliance (SNC) publication (§ 6.41), periodic compliance reports from SIUs (§ 6.32), and right of entry for inspection (§ 6.39).
