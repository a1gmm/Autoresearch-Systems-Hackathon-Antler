## Industrial Permit Types Issued

The EC Section issues seven permit types:

| Permit Type | Description / Trigger |
|---|---|
| Categorical Industrial User (CIU) | Industries defined in 40 CFR Ch. I, Subchapter N (40 CFR 405–471) — metal finishers, electronics manufacturers, porcelain enamelers, etc. Annual inspection + minimum 2 sampling events/yr. |
| Non-Significant Categorical Industrial User (NSCIU) | Categorical industries discharging <100 gpd process flow; annual certification + inspection; reduced regulatory requirements. |
| Industrial Waste / Significant Industrial User (IW/SIU) | Discharge ≥25,000 gpd process wastewater, or ≥5% of LTP average dry-weather hydraulic or organic capacity, or designated by Director as posing adverse potential. Annual minimum inspection and sampling. |
| Ground Water Clean-Up (GW) | Discharges from LUST or other groundwater remediation operations. Annual inspection; monthly self-monitoring for petroleum hydrocarbons, lead, solvents; quarterly reports. |
| Non-Residential User | Food service, dental, auto repair/body, car wash, machine shops, printing, beer/wine making, photo finishers. 992 active non-residential permits as of 2022. |
| Wastehauler (WH) | Trucked waste delivered to LTP. Accepts animal/beverage/food processing waste, gray water, leachate, septic, portable toilets, grease interceptors. 65 permits (Santa Rosa portion) as of 2022. |
| One-Time Discharge | Single discrete discharge events; PDF application required. |

**2022 permit counts (Santa Rosa portion):**
- Significant Industrial Users: 13
- Non-residential permits: 729
- Groundwater: 5
- Wastehaulers: 65
- **Total: 812 active permits**

## Key Discharge Prohibitions (§ 15-08)

- No user may **dilute a discharge as a substitute for pretreatment**; must install pretreatment equipment or containment to meet local limits.
- No discharge that causes pass-through, interference, or falls within specific categorical prohibitions (40 CFR 403.5).
- pH discharge range: **5.0–12.0 SU** (local limit confirmed by search; [VERIFY exact local limits table with EC Section — ecode360 source blocked])
- Grease and oil: Local limit exists for FOG; fats, oils, and grease control is part of the Sanitary Sewer Management Plan.
- Prohibited discharge standards codified at § 15-08.070.

## Permit Application Process

- **Food Service permits:** Online application via Microsoft Forms (City website).
- **All other permit types:** PDF application submitted to EC Section; staff reviews proposed pretreatment equipment adequacy before issuance.
- Periodic inspections for compliance monitoring and pollution prevention education.
- **Key prohibition:** Dilution as a substitute for pretreatment is expressly prohibited.

## Industrial Pretreatment Program — Regulatory Notes

- **Categorical industries** in the service area include: electrical/electronic components manufacturers, metal finishers, and a porcelain enameling shop (per 2022 Annual Report).
- **Non-residential sector programs** (formalized pollution prevention): machine shops, photo finishers, printers, food service, beer/wine, dental offices, car washes, auto repair, auto body.
- The EC Section is also contracted by the **Town of Windsor** to implement Windsor's pretreatment program.
- **PFAS monitoring** was added to the pretreatment program (noted in 2022 Annual Report).

## POTW NPDES / Water Reuse Context

- Treated water is beneficially reused (recycled water program); tertiary treatment (Title 22) is provided.
- Laguna Treatment Plant generates approximately 20% of its electricity from digester gas (≈7.8 million kWh/yr) plus solar.
- Basin Plan and NPDES permit compliance monitored by North Coast RWQCB.
