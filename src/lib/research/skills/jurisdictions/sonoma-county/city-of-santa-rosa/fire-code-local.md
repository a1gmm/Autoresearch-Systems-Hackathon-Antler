## Fire Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

**Agency:** Santa Rosa Fire Department, Prevention Bureau
**Address:** 2373 Circadian Way, Santa Rosa, CA 95407
**Phone:** 707-543-3500
**Hours:** Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–4 p.m. (limited Friday staffing)
**Note:** This is NOT a county fire district or CAL FIRE jurisdiction. Santa Rosa operates its own full-service municipal fire department and is the AHJ within city limits.

## Adopted Fire Code

**Edition:** 2025 California Fire Code (CCR Title 24, Part 9), with local amendments.
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026.
**Adoption process:** Ordinances introduced October 21, 2025; adopted by City Council November 18, 2025. [Specific ordinance number was not assigned in the publicly available draft — VERIFY with City Clerk.]
**Municipal code chapter:** Santa Rosa City Code **Ch. 18-44** (entitled "2025 California Fire Code").
**Codification note:** For citation purposes, sections of the CFC are renumbered by prepending "18-44" (e.g., CFC § 101.1 becomes SRCC § 18-44.101.1).

## Appendices Adopted

The following CFC appendices are locally adopted: 4, B, BB, C, CC, D, E, F, G, H, I, N, P, and Q.

## Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI) Code

**Adopted simultaneously:** 2025 California Wildland-Urban Interface Code.
**Municipal code chapter:** Santa Rosa City Code **Ch. 18-46** (new chapter added by the November 2025 ordinance).
**WUI fire area:** A specific Wildland-Urban Interface Fire Area is designated within city limits (§ 18-44.4702.1), reflecting Santa Rosa's significant wildfire risk. Vegetation management permits and fire protection plans are required for development in this zone.

## Local Amendments (Key Items)

Amendments were developed through the Sonoma County Fire Prevention Officers Fire Code Committee for county-wide uniformity. Key local additions:

**§ 18-44.105.6.52 — Local Operational Permits** (in addition to standard CFC § 105.6 permits):
1. Apartment, hotel, or motel operations
2. Cannabis operations: cultivation, distribution, manufacturing, testing/lab
3. Care facilities: day care with >8 persons; residential/commercial institutional care (H&S Code § 13235 compliant facilities are exempt)
4. Emergency responder radio systems
5. High-rise buildings (as defined in Title 24, Part 2)
6. Live entertainment at alcohol-serving businesses
7. Model rockets — rental, sale, or operation

**§ 18-44.105.7.26 — Local Construction Permits:**
- Vegetation management plan implementation
- Fire protection plan implementation

**§ 18-44.106.6 — Cost Recovery:** SRFD may recover suppression, investigation, and emergency medical costs under H&S Code §§ 13009 and 13009.1. Persons who negligently or intentionally cause an emergency response (traffic accident, toxic/flammable spill, etc.) are liable for all costs under Govt. Code § 53150 et seq.

**§ 18-44.109.1 — Appeals:** Appeal fire code official decisions to the Fire Chief within 15 days; appeal Fire Chief decisions to the Board of Building Regulation Appeals within 15 days. Decision of that Board is final administrative remedy.

**§ 18-44.110.4 — Violations:** Violations are misdemeanors punishable by up to $1,000 fine and/or 6 months imprisonment. Each day of continuing violation is a separate offense.

## Operational Permit Renewal

All operational permits expire **July 30** annually. The Fire Department sends invoices by mid-July. Online renewal is available. Inspections proceed regardless of permit status.
