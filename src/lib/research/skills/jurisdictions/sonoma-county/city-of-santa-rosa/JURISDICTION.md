> Local EHS authority reference for the **City of Santa Rosa** (Sonoma County's largest city and county seat). Orientation for the research subagent: who administers the local programs within city limits and where their adopted codes live. GOTCHA: Santa Rosa runs its OWN CUPA through the Fire Department — facilities inside city limits do NOT report to Permit Sonoma's county CUPA.

## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **Agency:** Santa Rosa Fire Department (SRFD) is CalEPA-certified as its own CUPA for facilities within city limits. It does NOT share CUPA authority with Permit Sonoma (the county CUPA). Source: https://www.srcity.org/591/Hazardous-Materials---CUPA
- **Programs administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Materials Inventory, Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), Spill Prevention Control & Countermeasure (SPCC), Underground Storage Tanks (UST), and CalARP (Risk Management). Source: https://www.srcity.org/591/Hazardous-Materials---CUPA
- **Reporting portal:** CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) — businesses must submit HMMP/Business Plans online via CERS; no-cost portal. Source: https://www.srcity.org/591/Hazardous-Materials---CUPA
- **Local ordinance:** Santa Rosa City Code Ch. 17-34 (CERTIFIED UNIFIED PROGRAM AGENCY). Source: https://ecode360.com/42966301
- **CUPA contact:** 707-543-3532; address: 2373 Circadian Way, Santa Rosa, CA 95407; hours Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–4 p.m. (limited Friday staff). Source: https://www.srcity.org/591/Hazardous-Materials---CUPA
- **Fees:** Published annually in Santa Rosa Fire Fee Schedule (FY 2025-2026 PDF). Source: https://www.srcity.org/DocumentCenter/View/43145/Santa-Rosa-Fire-Fee-Schedule-2025-2026-PDF
- **UNIDOCS:** Santa Rosa Fire is a UNIDOCS member agency. Source: http://www.unidocs.org/members/Santa_Rosa_CUPA.html

### 2. Fire AHJ — Fire Code & Operational Permits

- **AHJ:** Santa Rosa Fire Department, Prevention Bureau — 2373 Circadian Way, Santa Rosa, CA 95407; 707-543-3500; Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–4 p.m. Source: https://www.srcity.org/558/Fire-Code-Enforcement
- **Adopted fire code:** 2025 California Fire Code (CCR Title 24, Part 9), with local amendments — effective January 1, 2026. Adopted by ordinance introduced October 21, 2025 and adopted by City Council November 18, 2025; codified as **Santa Rosa City Code Ch. 18-44**. Appendices adopted: 4, B, BB, C, CC, D, E, F, G, H, I, N, P, and Q. Source: https://santa-rosa.legistar.com/View.ashx?M=F&ID=14941433&GUID=E9EE4321-0A83-4AAB-83E9-AF51782946C6
- **WUI code:** 2025 California Wildland-Urban Interface Code adopted simultaneously; codified as **Santa Rosa City Code Ch. 18-46** (new chapter). Source: https://santa-rosa.legistar.com/View.ashx?M=F&ID=14941433&GUID=E9EE4321-0A83-4AAB-83E9-AF51782946C6
- **Local amendments process:** County-wide consensus document prepared through Sonoma County Fire Prevention Officers Fire Code Committee to promote uniformity across city and county jurisdictions. Source: https://santa-rosa.legistar.com/View.ashx?M=F&ID=14941433&GUID=E9EE4321-0A83-4AAB-83E9-AF51782946C6
- **Local operational permits (§ 18-44.105.6.52):** Cannabis operations (cultivation/distribution/manufacturing/testing), apartment/hotel/motel, day care (>8 persons), high-rise, live entertainment/alcohol, emergency responder radio systems, and model rockets — all require SRFD operational permits in addition to standard CFC permits. Source: https://santa-rosa.legistar.com/View.ashx?M=F&ID=7810815&GUID=F187623C-0308-4B86-87A8-625BE45F9087
- **Operational permit renewal:** Permits expire July 30 annually; Fire Dept sends invoices mid-July. Online renewal portal available. Source: https://www.srcity.org/536/Fire-Permit-Renewal
- **Fire construction permits:** Plan review and inspection through SRFD; submit minimum 10 working days before start. Source: https://www.srcity.org/546/Fire-Construction-Plan-Review-Inspection
- **Fees:** Santa Rosa Fire Fee Schedule FY 2025-2026. Source: https://www.srcity.org/DocumentCenter/View/43145/Santa-Rosa-Fire-Fee-Schedule-2025-2026-PDF

### 3. Industrial Wastewater / POTW Pretreatment

- **POTW operator:** City of Santa Rosa Water Department; facility address 4300 Llano Road, Santa Rosa, CA 95407. Source: https://www.srcity.org/1239/Wastewater-Discharge-Permits
- **Local sewer-use ordinance:** Santa Rosa City Code **Title 15 (Sewers), Ch. 15-08 (PRETREATMENT)**; general sewer use requirements in Ch. 15-04. Framework conforms to 40 CFR Part 403; pretreatment program approved by federal regulators August 25, 1992. Source: https://ecode360.com/42965065
- **Permit types issued by Environmental Compliance Section:** Industrial User Permits, Food Service Permits, Auto Facility Permits, Dental Office Permits, Photo Processing Permits, Groundwater Treatment Permits, One-Time Discharge Permits, Trucked Waste Program Permits, General Permits. Source: https://www.srcity.org/1239/Wastewater-Discharge-Permits
- **IWP application process:** Food Service permits available via online Microsoft Forms; all other types require PDF submission reviewed by staff. Periodic inspections for compliance. Source: https://www.srcity.org/1239/Wastewater-Discharge-Permits
- **Contact:** Environmental Compliance Section — phone 707-543-3369; fax 707-543-3398; address 69 Stony Circle, Santa Rosa, CA 95401. Source: https://www.srcity.org/1239/Wastewater-Discharge-Permits
- **Key prohibition:** Industrial users may not dilute discharges as a substitute for pretreatment; must install pretreatment equipment or containment to meet local limits. Source: https://ecode360.com/42965065

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater

- **Permit type:** Phase I Municipal Separate Storm Sewer System (MS4) NPDES permit, issued by North Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board.
- **Local enabling ordinance:** Ordinance No. 3272 (enacted July 30, 1996); codified at **Santa Rosa City Code Ch. 17-12 (STORM WATER)**. Source: https://ecode360.com/42965567
- **NPDES permit:** Order R1-2015-0030 (joint permit with County of Sonoma and Sonoma County Water Agency); co-permittees include Cloverdale, Cotati, Rohnert Park, Healdsburg, Sebastopol, Ukiah, Windsor. Source: https://www.srcity.org/1151/Storm-Water
- **Industrial/commercial obligations:** City may require a Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP) from businesses whose outdoor activities (manufacturing, storage, loading, fueling, maintenance) may cause prohibited discharges; SWPPP must include employee training. Source: https://ecode360.com/42965567
- **Spill reporting:** Non-hazardous spills — 707-543-3800; hazardous/unknown — 911. Source: https://www.srcity.org/1151/Storm-Water
- **Contact:** City Water Dept stormwater line 707-543-4200; Creek Stewardship: [email protected]. Source: https://www.srcity.org/1151/Storm-Water

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts & CUP

- **Zoning code location:** Santa Rosa City Code **Title 20 (Zoning)**; industrial districts in **Ch. 20-24 (INDUSTRIAL ZONING DISTRICTS)**. Source: https://ecode360.com/42980746
- **Industrial district types:**
  - **BP (Business Park):** Planned centers for business without nuisances (noise, odor, noxious emissions); visually attractive. Source: https://ecode360.com/42980746
  - **IG (General Industrial):** Industrial/manufacturing, warehousing, wholesaling, distribution; may generate truck traffic, operate 24 hrs; potential for noise, smoke, odor, dust, noxious gases, glare, heat, vibration, or industrial wastes. Source: https://ecode360.com/42980746
- **CUP process:** Conditional Use Permit governed by **§ 20-52.050**; uses listed as "C" in the zoning tables require CUP approval before operation. Source: https://www.srcity.org/1266/Zoning-Requirements
- **Zoning contact:** City of Santa Rosa Planning Division, 100 Santa Rosa Avenue, Santa Rosa, CA 95404. Source: https://www.srcity.org/1266/Zoning-Requirements
- **Zoning lookup:** https://www.srcity.org/1263/Find-Your-Zoning-District
