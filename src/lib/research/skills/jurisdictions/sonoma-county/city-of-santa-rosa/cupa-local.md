## CRITICAL ROUTING NOTE

**Santa Rosa operates its OWN CalEPA-certified CUPA through the Fire Department.** Facilities inside city limits do NOT use Permit Sonoma (the Sonoma County CUPA). This is the primary gotcha for this jurisdiction — confirm site address is within city limits before routing.

## Programs Administered

All six CUPA programs are administered by SRFD for city-limits facilities:
1. **HMBP / HMMP** — Hazardous Materials Business Plan and Hazardous Materials Management Plan
2. **Hazardous Materials Inventory** — annual inventory reporting
3. **Emergency Contingency Planning** — Business Plan Tier II / emergency planning
4. **APSA** — Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (tank registration and SPCC compliance)
5. **SPCC** — Spill Prevention Control and Countermeasure plans
6. **UST** — Underground Storage Tank program (registration, inspections, permits)
7. **CalARP** — California Accidental Release Prevention (Risk Management Plans for covered processes)

## Reporting Portal

**CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System) is the mandatory submission platform for HMMP/Business Plans. Businesses must submit current Business Plans online via CERS. CERS reporting is available at no cost to the business.

## Local Ordinance

**Santa Rosa City Code Ch. 17-34 — CERTIFIED UNIFIED PROGRAM AGENCY (CUPA)**

Purpose (§ 17-34): Implements within SRFD all hazardous materials and hazardous waste programs under Health and Safety Code § 25404 et seq. (Unified Hazardous Waste and Hazardous Materials Management Regulatory Program). The chapter is explicitly no less stringent than State law.

Key requirements under Ch. 17-34:
- Any person intending to **store, handle, or use hazardous materials**, or **generate hazardous wastes**, or **operate underground storage tanks** for hazardous substances must first apply for, pay fees, and obtain a permit from the CUPA before commencing operations.
- The CUPA administers and enforces hazardous materials and hazardous waste laws and regulations under Health and Safety Code program authority.
- SRFD enforces hazardous materials management plan requirements of the (adopted) Fire Code plus H&S Code programs for HMBP, Risk Management, and UST.

## Permit Fee Schedule

Fees are published annually in the Santa Rosa Fire Department Fee Schedule. The FY 2025-2026 schedule lists CUPA-related fees under the operational permits section, cross-referencing the CUPA AST ranges (Ranges 1–7 for aboveground storage tanks). The general administrative rate is $183/hr for inspections/consultations.

Fee schedule: https://www.srcity.org/DocumentCenter/View/43145/Santa-Rosa-Fire-Fee-Schedule-2025-2026-PDF

## Key Distinctions from County CUPA

| Item | City of Santa Rosa | Permit Sonoma (County CUPA) |
|---|---|---|
| Geographic scope | City limits only | All unincorporated Sonoma County + cities that contract in |
| CUPA contact | SRFD Haz Mat Unit 707-543-3532 | Permit Sonoma 707-565-1152 |
| Local ordinance | SRCC Ch. 17-34 | Sonoma County Code |
