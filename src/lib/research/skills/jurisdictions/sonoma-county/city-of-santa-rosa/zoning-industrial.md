## Industrial District Types

Santa Rosa has three industrial zoning districts:

### BP — Business Park
**Purpose (§ 20-24.020):** Applied to areas appropriate for planned, visually attractive centers for business that do not generate nuisances (noise, clutter, noxious emissions, etc.). Intended for office, R&D, and light assembly uses in park-like settings.
- Retail and general-services uses require a CUP or Minor CUP finding that the approval **will not significantly reduce the City's inventory of land available for manufacturing and processing uses**.
- Has its own separate development standards section (§ 20-24.050).

### IL — Light Industrial
**Purpose (§ 20-24.020):** Applied to areas appropriate for light industrial uses, commercial service uses, and activities that may be incompatible with residential, retail, and/or office uses. Lower-impact manufacturing and service industrial.

### IG — General Industrial
**Purpose (§ 20-24.020):** Applied to areas appropriate for industrial and manufacturing activities, warehousing, wholesaling, and distribution uses. Uses may generate truck traffic and operate 24 hours. Potential for noise, smoke, odor, dust, noxious gases, glare, heat, vibration, or industrial wastes.
- Consistent with General Industry land use classification of the Santa Rosa General Plan.

## Permit Approval Types (Table 2-10)

The zoning code uses three approval tiers for industrial district uses:

| Code | Approval Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| P (or Zoning Clearance) | Permitted by right | Administrative approval; no public hearing |
| MUP | Minor Use Permit / Minor CUP | Zoning Administrator approval; limited discretion |
| C (CUP) | Conditional Use Permit | Planning Commission approval; full discretion |

## CUP Process

**Governing section:** § 20-52.050
- Uses listed as "C" in Table 2-10 require CUP approval before operation.
- Approved uses may also require design review, building permits, SRFD operational permits, or CUPA permits in addition to the CUP.
- CUP approval is discretionary; the Planning Commission is the review authority for CUPs.

## EHS-Relevant Zoning Notes

- **Hazardous materials storage/use** in industrial districts will also require CUPA permit from SRFD (Ch. 17-34) — coordinate with Fire Department regardless of zoning approval.
- **Cannabis manufacturing** operations: Require both a CUP (zoning) and an SRFD operational permit (§ 18-44.105.6.52).
- **24-hour operations:** Permitted in IG; may require CUP in IL or BP if noise/traffic impacts are significant.
- **Outdoor storage and operations** (loading, fueling, maintenance) may trigger stormwater SWPPP requirements under Ch. 17-12 — coordinate with City Water Department stormwater program.
