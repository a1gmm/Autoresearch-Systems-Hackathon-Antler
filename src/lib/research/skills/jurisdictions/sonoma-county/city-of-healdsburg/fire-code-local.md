## Adopted Fire Code

- **Currently adopted edition:** 2025 California Fire Code (CFC), California Code of Regulations Title 24, Part 9
- **Adopting ordinance:** Ordinance No. 1250 (effective 2026-01-01), which adopted the full 2025 California Building Standards Code (Title 24)
- **Note on Ord. 1250:** This ordinance updates statutory/code section references only to align with the 2025 CFC. It does not create more restrictive standards than those adopted in Ord. 1222 (2022 CFC cycle). The local amendments from Ord. 1222 remain fully in effect.
- **Separate fire-code ordinance:** Ordinance No. 1252 specifically adopted the 2025 Building Fire Code.
- **Prior ordinance chain:** Ord. 1194 (2019 CFC, eff. 2020-01-01) → Ord. 1222 (2022 CFC, eff. 2023-01-01) → Ord. 1250/1252 (2025 CFC, eff. 2026-01-01)

- **Appendices adopted:** Chapters 4, B, BB, C, CC, D, E, F, G, H, N, and O (confirmed under Ord. 1194; carried forward)

## Local Amendments (from Ord. 1194; confirmed carried forward through 2025 cycle)

All amendments were justified under California Health and Safety Code §§17958, 17958.5, 17958.7, 17941.5 based on the following local conditions:

- **Climatic:** Annual dry season (May–October) with temperatures >100°F, gusty winds, highly flammable WUI vegetation; heavy-fog winters delaying response.
- **Geological:** High seismic risk from multiple known faults; earthquake-induced fire risk; unstable slopes with landslide potential.
- **Topographical:** Narrow, steep hillside roads (grades up to 25%, widths <12 ft); Highway 101, Russian River, and railroad right-of-way create barriers delaying apparatus response; wide variation in water supply pressure (250–4,000 gpm).

### Key Local Amendments (HMC §15.08.020)

**Access Roads (CFC §503):**
- Roads serving 4+ parcels: ≥20-ft unobstructed width curb-to-curb (consistent with Healdsburg General Plan).
- Driveways: 3 structures = 16 ft; 2 structures = 12 ft; 1 structure = 10 ft.
- **WUI vegetation clearance (§503.2.1):** In areas designated Wildland Urban Interface per the Healdsburg General Plan, access roads shall have ignitable vegetation clearance of ≥10 ft on each side, unless waived by the Fire Chief.
- Maximum grade: 15% (up to 20% for ≤300-ft segments with Fire Chief approval).
- Driveways/roads >15% grade: concrete surface required.
- Dead-end roads >150 ft: width and turnaround per Healdsburg public-street standards or as approved by Fire Code Official.
- Minimum inside turning radius: 40 ft.
- Private bridges on fire access roads: must be evaluated by a CA-licensed structural/civil engineer per AASHTO Manual for Bridge Evaluation; load limits posted.

**Sprinkler Requirements (CFC §903 — Significantly Expanded from State Baseline):**
- **Group A (Assembly):** Sprinklers throughout (no size threshold — all Assembly occupancies).
- **Group E (Educational):** Sprinklers throughout.
- **Group F (Factory/Industrial):** Sprinklers throughout (no area exception).
- **Group H (High Hazard):** Sprinklers throughout.
- **Group M (Mercantile):** Sprinklers if gross floor area >500 sq ft (state baseline is higher).
- **Group S (Storage/Warehouse, including self-storage):** Sprinklers throughout ALL Group S occupancies — no size exception. (State baseline CFC has area and height thresholds; Healdsburg deletes them.)
- **Group B (Business):** Sprinklers if gross floor area >500 sq ft.
- **Group R-3:** Sprinklers in all one- and two-family dwellings regardless of square footage; also required in mobile homes/manufactured homes.
- **High-piled combustible storage (§903.2.11.7):** Sprinklers required throughout any building containing high-piled combustible storage.
- **Undetermined use buildings (§903.2.11.8):** Additional sprinkler trigger for design/undetermined use.
- Exception deletions: Multiple CFC exceptions to sprinkler requirements are deleted (e.g., §§903.2.1.1–.5, 903.2.4.1, 903.2.6.1, 903.2.9.1–.2, 903.2.10.1).

**Key Boxes (CFC §506.1):** Required when access to a structure is unduly difficult due to secured openings or where hazardous materials are present.

**Fire Hydrant Spacing (Appendix C §C103.1):** Hydrants required along fire apparatus access roads per City of Healdsburg standards or as approved by Fire Code Official. Sections C103.2 and C103.3 are deleted (city standards govern).

**Fire-Flow Tables (Appendix B, Tables B105.1(1) and B105.2):** Amended to reflect Healdsburg-specific flow and duration requirements; minimum fire flow not less than 1,500 gpm even where reduction is allowed for sprinklered buildings.

**Alarm System Service Tags (§14.7):** Local tag/label requirements for fire alarm service contractors; must include company name, address, license number, service date, and technician signature.

## Geographic Limits for Hazardous Materials Storage (CFC Section 4, Ord. 1194)

These prohibitions apply citywide EXCEPT in the industrial and agricultural zoning districts designated in the Healdsburg Zoning Ordinance (see zoning-industrial.md):

| CFC Section | Substance | Citywide Restriction |
|---|---|---|
| §5608.2 | Fireworks displays | Prohibited in any area per land-use/zoning standards |
| §5704.2.9.6.1 | Class I & II liquids in aboveground tanks outside buildings | Prohibited everywhere EXCEPT industrial and agricultural districts |
| §5706.2.4.4 | Class I & II liquids in aboveground tanks | Prohibited everywhere EXCEPT industrial and agricultural districts |
| §5806.2 | Flammable cryogenic fluids in stationary containers outdoors | Prohibited everywhere EXCEPT industrial and agricultural districts |
| §6104.2 | LPG storage (protection of congested areas) | Storage restricted citywide EXCEPT industrial districts |

## Fire Permits

- Operational permits issued by Healdsburg Fire Department per CFC Chapter 1.
- Applications via healdsburg.gov/306 or contact Fire Department.
- **Fee schedule:** Available at healdsburg.gov/306/Documents-Forms-Permits; city-adopted fee schedule (exact amounts not published online — contact Fire Department).
- Annual renewal required for most operational permits.

## WUI Designation

- Healdsburg has formally designated Wildland Urban Interface areas per the Healdsburg General Plan.
- Facilities/buildings in WUI areas are subject to additional access road, vegetation clearance, and potential sprinkler/construction requirements.
- WUI map: http://www.ci.healdsburg.ca.us/278/Wildland-Urban-Interface
