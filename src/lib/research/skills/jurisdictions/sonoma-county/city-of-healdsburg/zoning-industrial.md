> Industrial zoning and conditional use permit reference for the City of Healdsburg. Healdsburg has a single Industrial (I) base zone — no I-Light / I-Heavy split. The zoning code is Title 20 of the Healdsburg Municipal Code (Healdsburg Land Use Code). Note: the online codified version on CodePublishing is current through Ordinance No. 1145 (March 2, 2015); later amendments are on file with the City Clerk.

## Industrial District

- **District name:** Industrial District (I)
- **Code location:** HMC Title 20, Chapter 20.08 — Base Zoning Districts, Article VII (Industrial District)
- **Article VII eCode360:** https://ecode360.com/48342807
- **Structure:** Single Industrial (I) base zone — no I-L/I-H sub-classification found in publicly accessible code
- **Industrial zoning summary PDF (city-published):** https://healdsburg.gov/DocumentCenter/View/842/Industrial-PDF

### Stated Purpose

The Industrial (I) District is intended to:
- Implement the Industrial land use designation of the Healdsburg General Plan
- Accommodate manufacturing, production, warehousing, wholesaling, distribution, maintenance and repair uses
- Accommodate non-industrial uses requiring large amounts of floor and/or site area
- Accommodate on-site employee housing
- Control operating characteristics of uses that may generate objectionable visual impacts, noise, odors, insect nuisance, dust, dirt, smoke, vibration, heat, cold or glare, and/or create a risk of fire, explosion, noxious fumes, radiation, or other hazards

### EHS-Relevant Performance Controls

The Industrial District zoning code explicitly regulates (as stated purpose) uses that generate or create risk of:
- Fire and explosion hazards
- Noxious fumes
- Radiation hazards
- Noise, odors, dust, smoke, vibration, heat, glare

Specific numeric thresholds for these performance standards are not published in the publicly accessible online code; contact the Planning Division for applicable standards and the Standards for Specific Land Uses chapter (HMC Chapter 20.20).

### Geographic Note: Hazmat Storage Permitted Only in Industrial/Agricultural Districts

Per the fire code local amendments (HMC §15.08.020 / Ord. 1194), the following are restricted citywide EXCEPT in the Industrial (I) and agricultural zoning districts:
- Class I & II flammable liquids in aboveground tanks outside buildings (CFC §§5704.2.9.6.1, 5706.2.4.4)
- Flammable cryogenic fluids in outdoor stationary containers (CFC §5806.2)
- LPG storage above protection-threshold quantities (CFC §6104.2)

This makes industrial zoning a prerequisite for certain hazardous materials storage operations.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

- **CUP chapter:** HMC Chapter 20.28 — Administration, Article V (CUP procedures)
- **CUP types:** Minor and Major CUPs (distinction based on project scope and impact)
- **Decision body:** Planning Commission (for Major CUPs); Zoning Administrator may handle Minor CUPs — [VERIFY current delegation with Planning Division]
- **Required findings:** The decision body must find that the proposed use:
  1. Is in accord with the Healdsburg General Plan
  2. Is consistent with applicable development standards
  3. Will not be detrimental to public health and safety
  4. Will not be materially injurious to nearby uses and properties
- **Change of occupancy rule:** Change to a conditional use in an existing commercial or industrial building may be approved when the use will not alter the existing structure or increase parking demand beyond what is already provided on-site

### Major CUP Submittal Requirements

Required materials per Community Development Department checklist (Rev. 11/6/2020):
- Completed Planning Permit Application (signed by applicant and property owner)
- Application fee (per Planning Fee Schedule, updated annually each July; verify after June 30)
- Preliminary Title Report (≤3 months old, with legal description) — 2 copies
- Project narrative/description
- Full-size plan set (24" × 36") — 1 copy; reduced (11" × 17") — 5 copies; CD/flash drive — 1 copy
- Site Plan (existing and proposed)
- Grading, Drainage & Utilities Plan
- Stormwater Management Control Plan
- Low Impact Development (LID) Determination Sheet (if applicable)
- Building Elevations; Floor Plans; Exterior Lighting Plan; Landscape Plan; Sign Program; Site Photos
- Note on Site Plan: indicate whether project is in Wildland Urban Interface (WUI) — see http://www.ci.healdsburg.ca.us/278/Wildland-Urban-Interface
- **May also require:** Water System Fire Flow Calculations; Traffic Impact Analysis; other environmental studies

### Landscape Plan Triggers

Landscape plans are required (HMC §20.16.105) for:
- New industrial construction
- Additions/remodels to industrial buildings with new/replaced landscaped area ≥2,500 sq ft (contractor-installed) or ≥5,000 sq ft (owner-installed)

### LID / Stormwater Control

- Post-construction stormwater management required per 2017 Stormwater Technical Design Manual
- LID applies to projects creating/modifying ≥10,000 sq ft of impervious surface
- Source: https://ci.healdsburg.ca.us/618/Storm-Water-Management
