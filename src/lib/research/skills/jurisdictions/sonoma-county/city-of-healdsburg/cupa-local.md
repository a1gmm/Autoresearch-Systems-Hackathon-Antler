> CUPA routing reference for the City of Healdsburg. CRITICAL ROUTING NOTE: Healdsburg operates its own city-level CUPA — facilities in Healdsburg do NOT report to Permit Sonoma (Sonoma County's CUPA). Additionally, the Healdsburg CUPA covers both Healdsburg and the City of Sebastopol under a Joint Powers Agreement; a Sebastopol facility also routes here, not to Permit Sonoma.

## CUPA Identity

- **CUPA name:** Healdsburg / Sebastopol Unified Program Agency (UPA)
- **Administering agency:** City of Healdsburg Fire Department, under the Fire Marshal's direction
- **Jurisdiction scope:** City of Healdsburg AND City of Sebastopol (per 1994 Joint Powers Agreement)
- **CUPA type:** City-operated CUPA (NOT Permit Sonoma / Sonoma County CUPA)
- **Address:** 601 Healdsburg Avenue, Healdsburg, CA 95448
- **Phone:** (707) 431-3360
- **Fax:** (707) 431-3146
- **Email:** jboaz@ci.healdsburg.ca.us
- **Hours:** Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM

## HMBP / CERS Reporting

- **Submission portal:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov
- **Who must file:** Businesses storing, using, or handling hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities (generally 55 gallons liquid, 500 lbs solid, 200 cu ft compressed gas — per H&S Code §25507)
- **Filing schedule:** Initial filing within 30 days of reaching threshold; annual renewal; 30-day update required on material changes (quantity increases ≥100%, new materials, address/ownership changes, significant operational changes)
- **Required CERS content:** Hazardous materials inventory, emergency response/contingency plan, site map, employee training records
- **HMMP/HMIS:** All Hazardous Material Management Plans and Hazardous Material Inventory Statements must be submitted electronically as approved by the fire code official (per HMC §15.08 amendments)
- **Unauthorized release:** Any suspected or actual hazardous material release must be reported immediately to the Fire Chief upon discovery, per HMC §15.08 and Chapter 8.12

## Local Hazmat Ordinance (HMC Chapter 8.12)

- **Chapter:** HMC Chapter 8.12 — Hazardous Materials Release
- **Reporting duty:** Handler or their representative shall immediately report any release or threatened release of a hazardous material to the administering agency (Fire Department) and the State Office, per H&S Code §25503
- **Area Emergency Response Plan:** City has adopted (by resolution) the Sonoma County Area Plan for emergency response to hazardous material releases; updated via resolution as the county plan is revised
- **Penalties:** Violation of HMC §8.12.030: up to $25,000 per day fine, and/or up to one year county jail. If violation causes or contributes to an emergency requiring city/county response, the violator is also assessed full cost of emergency response plus cleanup/disposal costs

## UST Program

- **Administrator:** Healdsburg / Sebastopol CUPA (Healdsburg Fire Department)
- **Permit triggers:** Installation, removal, repair, modification, or testing of any UST system
- **Forms:** Available at healdsburg.gov/322 (Forms A–E cover tank specs, monitoring, response planning; financial responsibility documents updated annually)
- **CERS upload:** All UST permit submittals uploaded to CERS
- **Unauthorized Release Form:** Required on discovery of any release
- Source: https://healdsburg.gov/322/Underground-Storage-Tank-Program

## Relationship to Sonoma County (Permit Sonoma)

- Permit Sonoma is the CUPA for unincorporated Sonoma County and certain participating cities.
- The City of Healdsburg opted out and operates its own CUPA under the 1994 JPA with Sebastopol.
- Do NOT route Healdsburg or Sebastopol facilities to Permit Sonoma for CUPA programs.
