> Wastewater discharge and pretreatment reference for the City of Healdsburg. Healdsburg operates its own POTW discharging to the Russian River system. The City does NOT currently operate a formal EPA-approved industrial pretreatment program (IWP) — the NPDES permit requires source control but imposes an IWP only if categorical industrial users are identified. Non-domestic dischargers must obtain a Wastewater Discharge Permit from the city.

## Treatment Facility

- **Facility name:** City of Healdsburg Wastewater, Recycling and Disposal Facility
- **Address:** 340 Foreman Lane, Healdsburg, CA 95448
- **System size:** 36 miles of sewer mains; 9 sewer lift stations
- **Design dry-weather flow:** 1.4 mgd (daily average limit per NPDES permit)
- **Peak wet-weather flow:** 4.0 mgd (daily maximum limit)
- **Treatment level:** Tertiary treatment; generates approximately 2,000 wet tons of biosolids annually
- **Biosolids disposal:** Redirected to agricultural processing facilities (landfill diversion — conveyor system installed late 2023)
- **Discharge point:** Basalt Pond, part of the Russian River (Discharge Point 001); disinfected tertiary recycled water also distributed for reuse (Discharge Point 002)
- **Seasonal restriction:** Discharge to the Russian River and its tributaries is prohibited May 15 – September 30 each year

## Sewer Use Ordinance

- **Chapter:** HMC Chapter 13.20 — Sewer System
- **Permit requirement:** Any person discharging anything other than domestic sewage must obtain a Wastewater Discharge Permit from the City Engineer before discharging
- **Existing non-domestic dischargers:** At time of ordinance adoption, existing non-domestic dischargers had 2 months to apply and 9 months before the prohibition on unpermitted non-domestic discharge took effect
- **Permit conditions may include:** pretreatment requirements, peak-flow restrictions, discharge point relocation, constituent prohibition, time-of-day limits, and surcharges
- **Pretreatment systems:** Design and construction of pretreatment facilities must comply with state laws, city ordinances, city standard drawings, and regulations adopted by city council resolution; engineering plans for required pretreatment systems must be signed by a California-licensed engineer
- **IWP application form:** Wastewater Discharge Permit Application — available at healdsburg.gov/306/Documents-Forms-Permits
- Source: https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/Healdsburg/html/Healdsburg13/Healdsburg1320.html

## Industrial Pretreatment Program Status

**Healdsburg does NOT currently maintain a formal federal Industrial Pretreatment Program (IWP) under 40 CFR Part 403.** The NPDES permit (Order R1-2022-0017, §6.3.5.2) establishes a conditional framework:

- The City is required to perform source control functions and conduct annual public outreach to industrial, commercial, and residential users about preventing toxic discharges.
- The City must implement legal authorities to monitor, enforce source control standards, restrict toxic material discharges, and inspect connected facilities.
- **If** the City identifies industrial wastes subject to the NPDES Pretreatment Program or if the Regional Water Board determines pretreatment is warranted, the City must: (1) notify the Regional Water Board within 30 days; (2) submit a revised ROWD and pretreatment program within one year; and (3) enforce federal categorical pretreatment standards on all categorical industrial users (CIUs).
- Specific prohibitions applicable to ALL non-domestic users regardless of IWP status (NPDES §6.3.5.2.1.6):
  - No discharges creating fire or explosion hazard in the WWTF
  - No corrosive discharges (pH < 5.0 prohibited)
  - No solid/viscous pollutants causing obstruction or interference
  - No pollutants at flow rate/concentration causing interference with the WWTF
  - No heat raising WWTF temperature above 40°C (104°F)
  - No petroleum oil, non-biodegradable cutting oil, or mineral oil products in amounts causing interference or pass-through
  - No toxic gases/vapors/fumes causing acute worker health/safety problems

**Practical implication for industrial users:** No formal local limits or categorical pretreatment standards exist beyond the state/federal prohibitions above. However, a Wastewater Discharge Permit under HMC Chapter 13.20 IS required for any non-domestic discharge. Contact the City Engineer to determine permit conditions.
