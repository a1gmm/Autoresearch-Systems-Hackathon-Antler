## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **Agency:** Healdsburg / Sebastopol Unified Program Agency (UPA), operated by the **City of Healdsburg Fire Department** under the **Fire Marshal's direction**.
- **Jurisdiction scope:** Covers BOTH the City of Healdsburg AND the City of Sebastopol under a **1994 Joint Powers Agreement**. Facilities in Sebastopol report to the Healdsburg FD CUPA, not to Permit Sonoma.
- **Programs administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP/CFC), Hazardous Waste Generator Program, Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program, Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), and Hazardous Waste Treatment.
- **Reporting portal:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — cers.calepa.ca.gov.
- **Fees:** Set by city-adopted fee schedule; funding comes from fees charged to regulated businesses. Specific fee schedule: contact Fire Department or download from healdsburg.gov/306.
- **Contact:** Healdsburg Fire Department, 601 Healdsburg Avenue, Healdsburg, CA 95448 — (707) 431-3360 — jboaz@ci.healdsburg.ca.us — Mon–Fri 8 AM–5 PM.
- Source: https://healdsburg.gov/308/Certified-Unified-Program-Agencies-CUPA
- Source: https://healdsburg.gov/282/Hazardous-Materials

### 2. Fire AHJ — Fire Code & Permits

- **AHJ:** Healdsburg Fire Department; Fire Marshal is the Fire Code Official. Fire Prevention Division created under Healdsburg Municipal Code (HMC) **Chapter 15.08**.
- **Currently adopted code:** 2025 California Fire Code (CFC) — adopted by **Ordinance No. 1250** (effective 2026-01-01), which adopted the full 2025 California Building Standards Code Title 24 including CFC Part 9 with local amendments. The local amendments previously adopted under **Ordinance No. 1222** (2022 CFC, effective 2023-01-01) remain in effect; Ord. 1250 updates code section references only and does not add more restrictive standards.
- **Prior ordinance chain:** Ord. 1194 (2019 CFC) → Ord. 1222 (2022 CFC) → Ord. 1250 (2025 CFC/Title 24).
- **Local amendments basis:** Climatic (WUI, extreme fire weather, dense dry-season vegetation), geological (seismic/fault proximity), and topographical (narrow winding hillside roads, Russian River/Hwy 101 barriers delaying response).
- **WUI amendment:** Access roads in areas designated Wildland Urban Interface must have ignitable vegetation clearance of ≥10 ft on each side, unless waived by Fire Chief.
- **Fire permits:** Applications to Healdsburg Fire Department or CUPA; fee schedule available from healdsburg.gov/306. Inspections conducted by Fire Prevention Bureau (life safety and HMBP/CUPA) and Engine Companies (pre-plans).
- **Inspectors:** Robert Morris (707) 431-3357; Haile Duffy (707) 431-3360; Lance Macdonald, Fire Marshal (707) 431-3125.
- Source: https://healdsburg.gov/273/Fire-Prevention

### 3. UST Program

- **Administrator:** Healdsburg / Sebastopol CUPA (Healdsburg Fire Department).
- **Permit triggers:** Installation, removal, repair, or modification of any UST system requires a permit from the CUPA. UST Repair Permit Application and Closure Permit / Temporary Closure Permit Application available at healdsburg.gov/322.
- **Forms:** Forms A–E cover tank specs, monitoring, and response planning; financial responsibility documents updated annually. Unauthorized Release Form required on discovery.
- **CERS upload:** All permit submittals uploaded to CERS.
- Source: https://healdsburg.gov/322/Underground-Storage-Tank-Program

### 4. Industrial Wastewater / POTW Pretreatment

- **POTW operator:** City of Healdsburg Electric, Water & Wastewater Department (401 Grove St., (707) 431-3346).
- **Sewer use ordinance:** HMC **Chapter 13.20 — Sewer System**. Any discharge other than domestic sewage requires an application and a **Wastewater Discharge Permit**. Permit conditions may include: pretreatment requirements, peak-flow restriction, discharge point relocation, constituent prohibition, time-of-day limits, and surcharges.
- **Pretreatment:** Pretreatment systems/devices must be approved by the City Engineer; systems requiring engineering design need plans signed by a California-licensed engineer.
- **IWP application:** Wastewater Discharge Permit Application available at healdsburg.gov/306/Documents-Forms-Permits.
- **WWTF NPDES permit:** Order No. R1-2022-0017 (North Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board) — NPDES permit for Healdsburg WWTF discharging to Russian River.
- **FOG Program:** Grease interceptor requirements enforced under SSMP (2020 update, certified by City Council Resolution 2020-23).

### 5. Stormwater / MS4

- **Program:** City is a **Phase I MS4 Co-Permittee** under North Coast RWQCB **Order No. R1-2015-0030** / NPDES No. CA0025054, alongside Santa Rosa, Cloverdale, Cotati, Rohnert Park, Sebastopol, Windsor, Ukiah, and Sonoma County.
- **Local ordinance:** HMC **Chapter 13.28 — Urban Storm Water Quality Management and Discharge Controls**, enacted by **Ordinance No. 1054** (adopted January 8, 2007).
- **Construction thresholds:** <1 acre disturbed → Erosion and Sediment Control Plan by QSD; ≥1 acre → SWPPP required. LID applies to projects creating/modifying ≥10,000 sq ft of impervious surface.
- **Industrial/commercial:** Industrial and commercial businesses subject to MS4 permit provisions; best management practices (BMPs) required; 12+ BMP fact sheets available from city.
- **Contact:** 401 Grove Street, (707) 431-3317.
- Source: https://ci.healdsburg.ca.us/618/Storm-Water-Management
- Source: https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/Healdsburg/html/Healdsburg13/Healdsburg1328.html

### 6. Zoning — Industrial Districts & CUP

- **Code:** HMC **Title 20 — Land Use Code** (Healdsburg Land Use Code), implements the Healdsburg General Plan. Current codified version current through Ordinance No. 1145 (March 2, 2015); later amendments on file with City Clerk (administration@ci.healdsburg.ca.us).
- **Industrial district:** **Article VII — Industrial District (I)** (HMC Chapter 20.08, Section 20.08.200). A single Industrial (I) base zone; no I-L/I-H split found in publicly accessible code. Designed for manufacturing, production, warehousing, wholesaling, distribution, maintenance and repair, and uses needing large floor/site areas.
- **Performance controls:** Zoning code regulates uses generating visual impacts, noise, odors, insect nuisance, dust, smoke, vibration, heat, cold, glare, and/or risk of fire, explosion, noxious fumes, radiation, or other hazards.
- **CUP process:** Conditional Use Permit process administered through Planning Division; specific CUP chapter in Title 20 Chapter 20.28 (Administration). Contact Planning at healdsburg.gov/375.
- Source: https://www.ci.healdsburg.ca.us/375/Zoning-Development-Regulations
- Source: https://ecode360.com/48342807 (Article VII Industrial District)
- Source: https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/Healdsburg/html/Healdsburg20/Healdsburg20.html

## Key GOTCHA

- **Cross-jurisdiction CUPA:** The Healdsburg/Sebastopol CUPA covers TWO cities under a JPA. A facility in **Sebastopol** reports to the Healdsburg FD CUPA — NOT to Permit Sonoma and NOT to a separate Sebastopol agency. Verify facility address carefully before routing.
