## Industrial District Designations (Non-Coastal Zone, Article 12)

| Zone Code | Zone Name | Character |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| **M1** | Limited Urban Industrial District | Light industrial uses adjacent to urban areas; emphasis on compatibility with surrounding uses |
| **M2** | Heavy Industrial District | Higher-intensity industrial uses; also cross-referenced in Article 8 |
| **M3** | Limited Rural Industrial District | Rural-scale industrial uses on smaller parcels in unincorporated areas |
| **MP** | Industrial Park District | Planned industrial park format; design standards apply |

All four industrial zone types are defined in **Article 12** of Chapter 26. M2 has an additional cross-reference in Article 8.

**Rural context note:** Sonoma County is primarily agricultural and rural. M3 (Limited Rural Industrial) is the most common industrial designation in unincorporated areas. Large industrial facilities in M1, M2, or MP zones tend to cluster in Rohnert Park, Windsor, and near Santa Rosa's urban fringe.

---

## Coastal Zone

The Sonoma County Local Coastal Program (LCP) governs zoning in the coastal strip. Industrial uses in the Coastal Zone are subject to separate zoning provisions and California Coastal Commission oversight. This file covers non-Coastal Zone only.

---

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP / Use Permit) Process

Many industrial uses in M1, M3, and MP zones require a **Use Permit (Conditional Use Permit)** rather than being permitted by right:

- **Review body:** Sonoma County **Planning Commission** (meets monthly)
- **Pre-application:** Planning staff pre-screening / pre-application conference strongly recommended before submitting a formal Use Permit application
- **CUP process authority:** Chapter 26, **Article 92 — Administrative and Public Hearing Procedures** governs the use permit hearing and decision process
- **Environmental review:** Projects above ministerial thresholds require CEQA review; Planning Division coordinates
- **Permit Sonoma online portal:** Applications submitted via https://permitsonoma.org

**Note:** Some minor industrial uses may qualify for ministerial (administrative) approval without a public hearing. Verify with Permit Sonoma Planning whether the specific SIC/NAICS activity is a permitted use, conditional use, or prohibited use in the applicable zone before routing.

## Other Relevant Overlays / Combining Zones

- **Agriculture & Open Space districts** blanket most of unincorporated Sonoma County; industrial uses in these areas are generally prohibited or severely restricted.
- **Williamson Act (Agricultural Preserve)** contracts further restrict non-agricultural uses on enrolled parcels.
- **Riparian Corridor and Wetland overlays** apply near creeks and wetlands county-wide.
- **VHFHSZ / WUI overlay:** Fire hazard severity zone mapping affects development standards for industrial buildings in high-risk areas.
