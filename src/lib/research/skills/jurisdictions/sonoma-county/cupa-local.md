## CRITICAL GOTCHA — Four Separate CUPAs in Sonoma County

Sonoma County is NOT a single-CUPA county. Four separate Certified Unified Program Agencies (CUPAs) operate within the county boundaries. A facility's HMBP and other CUPA submittals must go to the CUPA with jurisdiction over that facility's city/address:

| CUPA | Jurisdiction |
|------|-------------|
| **Permit Sonoma — Fire Prevention & Hazardous Materials Division** | Unincorporated Sonoma County + Cities of Cloverdale, Cotati, Rohnert Park, Sonoma, and Town of Windsor |
| **City of Santa Rosa Fire Department** | City of Santa Rosa only |
| **City of Petaluma Fire Department — Fire Prevention Bureau** | City of Petaluma only |
| **City of Healdsburg Fire Department (JPA)** | City of Healdsburg AND City of Sebastopol (via 1994 Joint Powers Agreement) |

Always confirm the facility's municipal address against the UNIDOCS member page before routing a CUPA submission.

## Permit Sonoma CUPA (Unincorporated County + Most Cities)

### Agency
**Permit Sonoma — Fire Prevention and Hazardous Materials Division, Hazardous Materials Unit**
County of Sonoma

### Local Ordinance / County Code Authority
- **County Code Chapter 29 — Hazardous Materials Management**
- Enabled via **Ordinance No. 5015**
- California Fire Code hazardous materials chapters are enforced by this division under the same CUPA umbrella

### Fee Schedule
- **Ordinance No. 6512** (Board of Supervisors, April 15, 2025), effective July 1, 2025 – June 30, 2026
- Separate "Hazardous Materials Unit Fees" schedule covers: HMBP/Inventory, HW Generator Tiered Permitting, UST, APSA, and CalARP annual permit fees
- Fee schedule PDF: https://www.permitsonoma.com/permitservices/permitguidelinesanddocuments/feeschedules/feeschedules2025-2026

### CUPA Elements Administered
1. **HMBP / Hazardous Materials Inventory Statement (HMIS)** — Business Plan reporting
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator & Tiered Permitting** — all generator tiers (CESQG through LQG)
3. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — installation, operation, closure
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — SPCC-equivalent state program
5. **CalARP** — California Accidental Release Prevention Program

### Regulated Businesses
Approximately 1,800 businesses in the CUPA's service area.

## Local Hazmat Ordinance
County Code Chapter 29 supplements state CUPA law (Health & Safety Code §§ 25404 et seq.). The California Fire Code hazardous materials chapters (Parts 3 and 4 of Title 24, Part 9) are co-enforced by this same division, creating a single point of contact for both CUPA compliance and CFC hazmat permitting in unincorporated county.
