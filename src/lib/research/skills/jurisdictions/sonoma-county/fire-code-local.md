## Adopted Fire Code

- **Edition adopted:** 2025 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9, 2025 Edition)
- **Adoption date:** December 9, 2025 (Board of Supervisors action)
- **Effective date:** January 1, 2026
- **Adopting ordinance:** Ordinance No. 6535 (also codifies general code updates through November 4, 2025)
- **Previous adoption:** Ordinance No. 6396 (2022 CFC, superseded)
- **Municipal code chapter:** Sonoma County Code **Chapter 13 — Sonoma County Fire Safety Ordinance**

## Local Amendments / Additional Chapters

- Chapter 13 adopts by reference, **with local amendments**, selected provisions, chapters, and appendices of Title 24, Part 9 (California Fire Code).
- Local amendments include wildfire-resistant construction standards for WUI/VHFHSZ zones and updated energy-efficiency provisions incorporated during the December 2025 code cycle.
- **Chapter 13A — Duty to Maintain Defensible Space and Abate Hazardous Vegetation and Combustible Material:** Applies to both improved and unimproved parcels in unincorporated Sonoma County; independent of CFC adoption cycle.
- State Fire Safe Regulations (Title 14, CAL FIRE) also apply in State Responsibility Areas (SRAs) that overlap unincorporated Sonoma County.

## Fire AHJ

### Permit/Prevention AHJ (CFC permitting, plan check, inspections)
**Permit Sonoma — Fire Prevention and Hazardous Materials Division**
- Phone: (707) 565-2361
- Email: FirePrevention@sonomacounty.gov
- Address: 2550 Ventura Avenue, Santa Rosa, CA 95403
- In-person hours: Monday–Thursday, 1:00 PM–4:00 PM (by appointment)
- Issues fire prevention permits, conducts CFC-required inspections, and enforces Chapter 13 for unincorporated Sonoma County.

### Fire Suppression / Operations AHJs
Much of unincorporated Sonoma County is served by independent special districts rather than a county department:

| District | Areas Served | Notes |
|----------|-------------|-------|
| **Sonoma County Fire District (SCFD)** | Windsor, Rincon Valley, Bennett Valley, Mountain area, Russian River (joined July 2020), Forestville (joined July 2021) | Formed April 2019 from consolidated agencies |
| **Sonoma Valley Fire District** | Sonoma Valley area | Independent district |
| Various other FPDs and CSA 40 | Remaining unincorporated pockets | Verify by parcel |

**GOTCHA:** SCFD is an operations-only district. It does NOT issue CFC permits or conduct code-enforcement inspections — that function belongs to Permit Sonoma's Fire Prevention and Hazardous Materials Division.

### Incorporated Cities — Separate AHJs
Each incorporated city in Sonoma County has its own fire AHJ; this file covers unincorporated county only. City-specific files: Santa Rosa, Petaluma, Healdsburg.
