## Industrial Zone Districts

Plumas County Code Chapter 2 establishes two industrial zoning designations:

### I-1 — Heavy Industrial Zone (Article 25)

- **Purpose:** Provide for heavy industry where access is available to transportation routes and public service facilities, and where surrounding land use and environmental setting will permit most industrial uses without major impacts.
- **Permitted uses (§ 9-2.2502):** Industrial operations, public utility facilities, heavy equipment sales/services/storage — subject to Site Development Review. Full enumerated list in Article 25.
- **Special/Conditional uses (§ 9-2.2503):** Certain uses require a **Special Use Permit** (functionally equivalent to a CUP — reviewed by Planning Commission or Zoning Administrator depending on project scale).
- **Height limit:** Timber product manufacturing structures: max 125 ft; other structures: max 75 ft; dwelling units: max 35 ft.
- **Setbacks:** No required side or rear yards, except 10 ft where adjacent to residentially zoned parcels.

### I-2 — Light Industrial Zone (Article 26)

- **Purpose:** Accommodate lighter industrial and compatible uses in areas where full heavy industrial impacts would be inappropriate.
- **Permitted uses (§ 9-2.2602):** Subject to Site Development Review; specific use list in Article 26.
- **Special/Conditional uses (§ 9-2.2603):** Special Use Permit required for specified uses.
- **Setbacks:** No required side or rear yards, except 10 ft where adjacent to residentially zoned parcels.

## Combining Zones Affecting Industrial Use

The **Limited Combining Zone (Ltd)** may overlay industrial districts to "permit and mitigate uses that have the potential to have significant adverse social, economic, or environmental effects." This combining zone can add conditions beyond the base zone's requirements.

---

## Conditional/Special Use Permit Process

- Industrial uses requiring a Special Use Permit are reviewed by the **Planning Director** (minor projects) or **Planning Commission** (major proposals) with public hearing.
- The **Zoning Administrator** handles certain administrative matters.
- Application materials, fees, and processing timelines: contact Planning Department at (530) 283-6214.

---

## Key Gotchas

- **Only one incorporated city:** Portola is the only incorporated municipality. All other communities (Quincy, Chester, Greenville, Graeagle, etc.) are unincorporated and fall under county zoning authority.
- **Large federal land footprint:** Plumas National Forest, Lassen National Forest, and BLM lands are not subject to county zoning; USFS/BLM land-use authorizations apply instead.
