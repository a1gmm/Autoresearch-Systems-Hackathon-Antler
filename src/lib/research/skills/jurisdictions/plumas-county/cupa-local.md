# CUPA — Plumas County, CA

## Administering Agency

**Plumas County Public Health Agency — Environmental Health Division** is the single Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for the entire county, including the only incorporated city (Portola). There is no participating-agency (PA) split.

- Address: 520 Main Street, Room 309, Quincy, CA 95971
- Phone: (530) 283-6355
- Email: quincyenv@countyofplumas.com

## Administered Program Elements

The CUPA consolidates five regulatory program elements under a single permit:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — inventory, emergency response/contingency plans
2. **Underground Storage Tanks (USTs)** — installation, operation, closure, corrective action
3. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA/ASTs)** — SPCC-tier tanks, spill prevention
4. **Hazardous Waste Generators (HWG)** — generator status, manifests, storage requirements
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — risk management plans for acutely hazardous materials

## Electronic Reporting

All reporting is done through the **California Environmental Reporting System (CERS)** at cers.calepa.ca.gov. Businesses must register in CERS before submitting HMBPs, UST records, or other unified program submittals.

## Fees

Annual permit fees apply. A fee schedule is available from the Environmental Health Division. Specific amounts not published in the main webpage; contact the office for current schedule.

## Statutory Note

California Health & Safety Code § 25507(f) is cited for temporary emergency generator exemptions from HMBP requirements.

## Additional Activities

The Environmental Health Division also administers emergency response planning, pollution prevention programs, and site mitigation (brownfields/cleanup) activities under the unified program umbrella.
