| Point | Description | Receiving Water |

**Seasonal restriction:** Discharge to the Middle Fork of the Feather River is **prohibited between May 1 and October 31**. Discharge must not exceed 2% of river flow (measured at DWR "MFP" gauge). Dry-weather flow is typically observed August 1 through October 31; this period uses land discharge exclusively.

## Pretreatment Program

**No formal pretreatment program** — the permit (Order R5-2024-0023, Fact Sheet) designates Pretreatment Program: **No**. The facility is classified as a Minor Discharge (Complexity B). Discharge of hazardous waste classified under CCR Title 22 § 66261.1 et seq. to the municipal system is prohibited. The city is still required to ensure compliance with any EPA categorical pretreatment standards for industrial users that discharge to the collection system.

## Receiving Water

The **Middle Fork of the Feather River** is designated a Scenic River and supports prime trout fishing. The Central Valley Basin Plan (§ 4.2.2.4.1) prohibits direct discharge of municipal and industrial wastes into the "Middle Fork, Feather River (from Delleker to Lake Oroville)." High inflow and infiltration occurs due to springs, snowmelt, and high groundwater persisting into summer.
