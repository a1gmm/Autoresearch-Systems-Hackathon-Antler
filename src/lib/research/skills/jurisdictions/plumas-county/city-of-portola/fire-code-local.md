# Fire Code — City of Portola, CA

## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

The City of Portola does **not** maintain a municipal fire department and is **not** the AHJ for fire code enforcement. As of **July 1, 2024**, fire protection service is provided by the **Beckwourth Peak Fire Protection District (BPFPD)**, a consolidated special district formed by merging the former City of Portola FPD, Sierra Valley FPD, Gold Mountain CSD, and Beckwourth FPD. Eastern Plumas Rural FPD annexed into BPFPD in December 2024.

- **AHJ:** Beckwourth Peak Fire Protection District
- **District address:** 180 Main Street, Beckwourth, CA 96129
- **Phone:** (530) 832-1008
- **District website:** https://bpfpd.ca.gov/

Wildland fire in the surrounding State Responsibility Area (SRA) is under **CAL FIRE Lassen-Modoc Unit** jurisdiction.
