# Wastewater — Plumas County, CA

## Structure Overview

Plumas County has no single countywide POTW. Wastewater collection and treatment is handled by independent Community Services Districts (CSDs) and Public Utility Districts (PUDs) serving individual communities. The county's Environmental Health Division administers onsite wastewater treatment systems (OWTS/septic) for unincorporated areas not served by centralized sewers. NPDES permits for POTWs are issued by the **California Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (Region 5)**.

## County Code — Onsite Wastewater (OWTS/Septic)

- **Code location:** Plumas County Code of Ordinances, Title 6 (Sanitation and Health), Chapter 6 (Sewage Disposal)
- Title 6, Chapter 6 governs onsite sewage disposal (septic systems) for properties outside of centralized sewer districts
- Section 6-6.07 addresses permit notifications for sewage disposal systems
- Section 6-6.11 addresses subsequent system evaluations
- Administration: Plumas County Environmental Health Division

The county adopted a **Local Agency Management Plan (LAMP)** for OWTS, approved by the State Water Board, describing the county's onsite wastewater treatment and disposal program for unincorporated areas.

## Key Gotcha

Most of Plumas County is on septic/OWTS — centralized sewer service is limited to discrete community service districts. An industrial facility in an unincorporated area will likely need an OWTS permit from county Environmental Health rather than a connection to a POTW. Only facilities in the AVCSD, IVCSD, Chester PUD, or similar district service areas can connect to a public sewer system.
