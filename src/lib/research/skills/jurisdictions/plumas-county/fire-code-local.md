# Fire Code — Plumas County, CA

## Adopted Code

California enforces the California Fire Code (CFC), Title 24 Part 9. The 2025 CFC (based on IFC 2021 with California amendments) took effect statewide on 2026-01-01. Plumas County's specific local adoption ordinance number for the 2025 CFC is **UNVERIFIED** — the county Building Department page references "California Building Codes" generically and Municode returns HTTP 403 on automated fetch. **[VERIFY]** with Plumas County Building Department at (530) 283-7011.

A local ordinance adopting Fire Hazard Severity Zone (FHSZ) maps in the Local Responsibility Area (LRA) was adopted as **Ordinance No. 25-1159** (with Resolution No. 25-9023) in June 2025, in compliance with state requirements under the State Fire Marshal's model ordinance. This establishes Moderate, High, and Very High FHSZs in the unincorporated area under Title 9, Chapter 9.5 of the Plumas County Code of Ordinances.

## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

There is no single countywide fire department or AHJ in Plumas County. Fire code enforcement is fragmented across numerous independent fire protection districts (FPDs) and community services districts (CSDs):

### Eastern Plumas County
**Beckwourth Peak Fire Protection District (BPFPD)**
- Formed July 1, 2024 by voter-approved consolidation of Beckwourth FPD, Sierra Valley FPD, Gold Mountain CSD, and City of Portola fire services (Measures E & F, November 2023 ballot)
- Merged with Eastern Plumas Rural FPD in December 2024; now operates 9 stations
- Service area: Delleker, Portola, Gold Mountain, White Hawk, Loyalton, Graeagle, and surrounding eastern Plumas communities
- Contact: 180 Main Street, Beckwourth, CA 96129; (530) 832-1008
- No specific fire code ordinance number published on district website; policy manual developed through Lexipol **[VERIFY]** adopted CFC edition with district
- Source: https://bpfpd.ca.gov/history

### Western/Central Plumas County (partial list)
Multiple independent FPDs serve unincorporated communities including Quincy FPD, Meadow Valley FPD, Crescent Mills FPD, Hamilton Branch FPD, La Porte FPD, Peninsula FPD, Prattville-Almanor FPD, Indian Valley CSD, and Plumas Eureka CSD. Each district is a separate AHJ; adopted fire code editions must be verified with each individual district.

Source: https://www.plumascounty.us/2126/Plumas-County-Fire-Districts

### Wildland / State Responsibility Area (SRA)
CAL FIRE Lassen-Modoc Unit (covers Plumas, Lassen, Modoc, and portions of Shasta counties) provides wildland fire protection for SRA lands. The large federal land footprint (Plumas National Forest, Lassen National Forest, BLM) falls under USFS/BLM jurisdiction, not county FPDs.
