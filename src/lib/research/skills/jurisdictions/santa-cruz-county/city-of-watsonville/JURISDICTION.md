## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **Agency:** Santa Cruz County Environmental Health Services (EHS) — the single county-wide CUPA, designated by CalEPA in 1996; covers all four incorporated cities including Watsonville. Watsonville does NOT operate its own CUPA.
- **Programs administered:** HMBP, Hazardous Waste Generator, Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP), Medical Waste.
- **Local ordinance:** Santa Cruz County Code **Chapter 7.100** (Hazardous Materials Storage, Use and Disposal).
- **Reporting portal:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — all permit maintenance and facility updates.
- **Fee schedule:** Unified Fee Schedule at unifiedfeeschedule.co.santa-cruz.ca.us.
- **Contact:** (831) 454-2022 | EnvironmentalHealth@santacruzcountyca.gov | 701 Ocean Street, 3rd Floor Room 312, Santa Cruz CA 95060. Walk-in Mon–Thu 8 am–12 pm; appts Mon–Thu 1–5 pm, Fri 8 am–5 pm.
- Source: https://scceh.com/NewHome/Programs/HazardousMaterialsWaste.aspx

### 2. Fire AHJ — Fire Code

- **AHJ:** City of Watsonville Fire Department — municipal department; fire code enforcement and fire/life-safety inspections within city limits.
- **Address:** 372 Airport Boulevard, Watsonville, CA 95019. Main: (831) 768-3200 | Non-emergency: (831) 471-1170 | Fire Prevention: (831) 768-3231.
- **Adopted code:** Watsonville Municipal Code **Chapter 8-9** (Fire Code) adopts the **2022 California Fire Code** (based on IFC 2021) with local amendments, including Appendices B, BB, C, CC, I, and N. Fees set by City Council resolution.
- **Key local amendment:** CFC Section 502.1 (bridge definitions) amended; residential sprinklers (CRC Section R313) not adopted — Chapter 9 of the City Fire Code applies instead.
- **Permit/inspection fees (FY 2024-25):** Life Safety Permit (residential ≤2 units) $201; Life Safety Inspection (residential ≤2 units) $236; Commercial Life Safety Permit $201/hr; Commercial Life Safety Inspection $236; Fire Code Violation Investigation $236; Certification-related Fire Inspection $236.
- **Plan review:** Construction documents for fire apparatus access, fire lanes, and fire hydrant hydraulic calculations must be submitted to the Fire Department prior to construction.

### 3. POTW / Sewer Use — Industrial Wastewater Pretreatment

- **POTW:** City of Watsonville Wastewater Treatment Facility — constructed 1920s, expanded; capacity ~12 MGD dry weather / 36 MGD wet weather; serves ~60,000 residents, 21 sq-mi service area; >120 miles sanitary sewer + 13 lift stations. Advanced secondary (ocean discharge) and advanced tertiary (agricultural reuse) treatment. Located at 500 Clearwater Lane, Watsonville, CA 95076.
- **Governing code:** Watsonville Municipal Code **WMC 6-3** (City Utilities) — pretreatment and source-control provisions, including WMC **6-3.516** (Industrial User Wastewater Discharge Permit).
- **Permit required if:** subject to National Categorical Pretreatment Standards; OR discharges ≥25,000 gpd process wastewater; OR contributes ≥5% of WWTP dry-weather capacity; OR designated by the City as having reasonable potential for adverse impact.
- **Fee:** No fee for permit issuance (as of 2024-25).
- **Contact:** Source Control — source.control@cityofwatsonville.org | (831) 768-3173 | Water Resources Center, 500 Clearwater Lane, Watsonville, CA 95076.
- Source: https://www.watsonville.gov/2332/Industrial-Wastewater-Discharge-Permit
- Source: https://www.watsonville.gov/812/Wastewater-Division

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater

- **MS4 permit:** City operates under Phase II Municipal General Stormwater Permit **2013-0001-DWQ** (State Water Resources Control Board); covers municipal storm drain systems serving populations under 100,000.
- **Post-construction ordinance:** Watsonville Post Construction Ordinance effective **February 27, 2014** — requires development and redevelopment projects to manage runoff at source; mandates Low Impact Development (LID) practices. [VERIFY - specific municipal code chapter not found online]
- **Industrial General Permit:** Industrial stormwater discharges regulated under NPDES Order **97-03-DWQ**; facilities must prepare a Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP).
- **Construction:** Sites disturbing ≥1 acre require NPDES coverage.
- **Contact:** Public Works & Utilities, 250 Main Street, Watsonville, CA 95076 | (831) 768-3100.
- Source: https://www.watsonville.gov/747/State-Stormwater-Permits

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts and CUP Process

- **Zoning code:** Watsonville Municipal Code **Title 14** (Zoning). Current through Ordinance 1482-25 (CM), passed September 9, 2025. Administered by the Community Development Department (CDD), Planning Division.
- **Industrial districts (Chapter 14-16, District Regulations):**
  - **IP — Industrial Park District** (WMC 14-16.500–14-16.506): principal permitted uses, accessory uses, conditional uses, and prohibited uses.
  - **IG — General Industrial District** (WMC 14-16.600–14-16.604): principal permitted uses, accessory uses, conditional uses, and prohibited uses.
- **Use definitions:** Chapter 14-18.
- **Parking for manufacturing:** Articles 5–6 of Chapter 14-17.
- **CUP process:** Certain industrial activities in both IP and IG districts require a Conditional Use Permit (Special Use Permit) before operation. [VERIFY - specific CUP fee and timeline not found online]

- **Counter hours:** Mon/Wed/Fri 8 am–12 pm and 1–4:30 pm; Tue/Thu 8 am–12 pm.

**GOTCHA:** Watsonville runs its own municipal Fire Department and Building & Safety Division, but does NOT run its own CUPA — hazardous-materials/Unified Program permitting routes to Santa Cruz County EHS (Chapter 7.100).
