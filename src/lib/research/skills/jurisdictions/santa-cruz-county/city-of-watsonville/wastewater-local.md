## POTW Overview

**Watsonville Wastewater Treatment Facility (WWTF)** — also referred to as the Watsonville Area Water Recycling Facility.

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Location | 500 Clearwater Lane, Watsonville, CA 95076 |
| Capacity (dry weather) | ~12 MGD |
| Capacity (wet weather) | ~36 MGD |
| Service area | ~21 sq mi; ~60,000 residents; includes City of Watsonville, Pajaro, Freedom, and Salsipuedes sanitary districts |
| Sewer infrastructure | >120 miles of sanitary sewer + 13 lift stations |
| Treatment level | Advanced secondary (ocean discharge); advanced tertiary (agricultural reuse / food crop irrigation) |
| Ocean discharge | Outfall >1 mile offshore into Monterey Bay |
| Biosolids | Anaerobic digestion with methane recovery for energy generation |

## Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit (IWP)

### Permit Triggers

A permit is required when a facility discharges to the sanitary sewer AND meets **any one** of the following criteria:

1. Subject to National Categorical Pretreatment Standards (40 CFR Part 403 / applicable categorical standards).
2. Discharges an average of **≥25,000 gallons per day (gpd)** of process wastewater.
3. Contributes process waste representing **≥5%** of the WWTF's dry weather hydraulic, BOD, or suspended solids (SS) capacity.
4. Designated by the City as having reasonable potential to adversely affect the WWTF or interfere with biosolids disposal.
