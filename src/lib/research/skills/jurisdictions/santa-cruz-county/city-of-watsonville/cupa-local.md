## CUPA Structure

**Watsonville does NOT operate its own CUPA.** All Unified Program permitting within Watsonville is administered by the county-level CUPA:

**Santa Cruz County Environmental Health Services (EHS)** — designated by CalEPA as the single county-wide CUPA in 1996. Geographic jurisdiction: the entire County of Santa Cruz, including all four incorporated cities (Watsonville, Santa Cruz, Capitola, Scotts Valley).

## Programs Administered

1. Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)
2. Hazardous Waste Generator (tiered permit system)
3. Underground Storage Tanks (UST)
4. Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)
5. California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)
6. Medical Waste Management
7. Incident Response coordination

## Governing Ordinance

**Santa Cruz County Code Chapter 7.100** — Hazardous Materials Storage, Use and Disposal. This county ordinance controls within Watsonville city limits for CUPA purposes.

## Reporting Portal

All facility permit maintenance, business plan submissions, and annual updates: **California Environmental Reporting System (CERS)** — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov
