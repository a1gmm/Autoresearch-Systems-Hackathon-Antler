## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA (Hazardous Materials Unified Program)

- **Agency:** County of Santa Cruz Health Services Agency, Environmental Health Services (EHS) — designated CUPA county-wide since 1996. No city-run CUPA; no participating agencies (PAs). All four cities (Santa Cruz, Watsonville, Capitola, Scotts Valley) are covered by the county CUPA.
- **Local ordinance:** Santa Cruz County Code (SCCC) **Chapter 7.100** — governs hazardous materials storage, use, and disposal including quantities below State thresholds. Local lower thresholds apply: contact EHS to determine applicability below 55 gal liquid / 200 cf compressed gas / 500 lb solid.
- **HMBP portal:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) at https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/ — mandatory electronic submittal since 2013 (HSC §25404(e)(2-5), AB 2286).
- **Six Unified Program elements administered:** HMBP/Hazardous Materials Inventories; CalARP; Underground Storage Tanks (UST); Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA); Hazardous Waste Generators; On-site Hazardous Waste Treatment.
- **Permit fees:** Fee-for-service; based on chemical quantities, hazard classes, and number of regulatory programs at the facility. Larger/more complex facilities pay higher fees. Invoice sent after HMBP is accepted. Fee schedule: http://unifiedfeeschedule.co.santa-cruz.ca.us/index.html (SSL certificate issues noted — verify at the EHS office).
- **Contact:** (831) 454-2022 | EnvironmentalHealth@santacruzcountyca.gov | 701 Ocean St., Room 312, Santa Cruz, CA 95060. Walk-ins Mon–Thu 8am–12pm; appointments Mon–Thu 1–5pm, Fri 8am–12pm and 1–5pm.
- Source: https://scceh.com/NewHome/Programs/HazardousMaterialsWaste.aspx
- Source (permit info sheet): https://scceh.com/Portals/6/Env_Health/hazardous_materials/Hazardous%20Materials%20Permits%20Information%20Sheet.pdf

### 2. Fire AHJ and Adopted Fire Code

**Unincorporated county (CAL FIRE / Santa Cruz County Fire Dept):**
- AHJ: Santa Cruz County Fire Department (SCCFD), operated under contract by CAL FIRE San Mateo–Santa Cruz Unit (CZU). Fire Marshal office: 6059 Highway 9, Felton, CA 95018 — (831) 335-5353 / Fax (831) 335-4053.
- **Adopted code:** SCCC **Chapter 7.92** — adopts the **2022 California Fire Code** (IFC 2021 Edition with California amendments). Enacted by **Ordinance 5415** (2022); subsequently amended by **Ordinance 5503** (December 9, 2025).
- **Notable local amendment:** New automatic fire sprinkler systems required in all new occupancies regardless of construction type or floor area, unless preempted by California HSC.
- Fee schedule and Fire Prevention Standards available at: https://countyfire.santacruzcountyca.gov/

### 3. Industrial Wastewater / POTW Pretreatment

- **POTW:** City of Santa Cruz Wastewater Treatment Facility, 110 California Street, Santa Cruz. The Santa Cruz County Sanitation District (SCCSD) collects and transmits all sewage from unincorporated county service areas to this City facility for treatment.
- **SCCSD pretreatment program:** Operated by the Environmental Compliance Unit (ECU) under NPDES Permit **CA0048194** / WDR Order **WQ 2013-0058-EXEC** (re-issued, effective June 5, 2023). District mirrors City of Santa Cruz pretreatment ordinances per inter-jurisdictional agreement.
- **District code:** SCCSD District Code available at https://sccsd.district.codes/ — Section **7.04.510** governs the Industrial Discharge Permit (IDP) system; Section **7.04.280** restricts nondomestic sewage; Section **7.04.310** lists prohibited wastes.
- **Significant Industrial Users (SIUs):** Six SIUs as of 2023 reporting year; all inspected and sampled annually. Compliance sampling minimum once/year. SIU permits valid up to 3 years; semi-annual self-monitoring reports (SMRs) required.
- **IWP process:** (1) Submit Wastewater Discharge Permit Application to ECU; (2) Site assessment by ECU staff; (3) Baseline monitoring / wastewater analysis may be required; (4) Permit issued. Program under federal 40 CFR Part 403.
- **County Code (sewer-use):** SCCC **Chapter 7.39** (Public Sewers — unincorporated county public sewer areas).
- **Contact ECU:** (831) 477-3907 | dpwenv@santacruzcountyca.gov | Monica Tomlinson, Environmental Programs Coordinator: (831) 477-3988 | 701 Ocean St., Rm. 410, Santa Cruz, CA 95060.
- Source: https://cdi.santacruzcountyca.gov/PublicWorks/SewerWater/EnvironmentalCompliance.aspx

### 4. Stormwater / MS4

- **Ordinance:** SCCC **Chapter 7.79** (Runoff and Pollution Control) — requires BMPs and stormwater pollution control plans for construction activities; any person subject to an industrial NPDES stormwater permit must comply with all provisions of that permit. Proof of compliance may be required before county permits are issued.
- **County MS4:** Operates under California SWRCB General Permit for Small MS4s (WQ Order No. 2003-0005-DWQ, Phase II NPDES).
- **Stormwater permit thresholds at UPC:** Projects involving new/replaced impervious surface are routed to county Stormwater Section; small (<500 sf), medium (500–5,000 sf), and large (>5,000 sf) tiers with different submittal requirements (Appendices B/C/D of County Design Criteria).
- **Contact:** Planning Division (831) 454-2580; Public Works (831) 454-2160 | 701 Ocean St., 4th Floor, Santa Cruz, CA 95060.

### 5. Zoning / Industrial Districts and CUP Process

- **Zoning code:** SCCC **Chapter 13.10** (Zoning Regulations), Article IV — Industrial Districts (Sections 13.10.341–346). Three industrial zones:
  - **M-1 Light Industrial** — light manufacturing, assembly, wet labs, dry labs, handmade product fabrication/maker spaces; no major pollution, visual impact, or nuisance potential. Warehouses require AUP (administrative use permit with public notice, no hearing) in M-1.
  - **M-2 Heavy Industrial** — lumber mills, major manufacturing/processing plants; transit stations for freight use only.
  - **M-3 Mineral Extraction** — mining, rock/sand/gravel extraction; subject to mining permits.
- **CUP process (Conditional/Use Permits):** Discretionary approval required for many industrial uses. Heard before Zoning Administrator at a noticed public hearing (10-day notice; signage on site; mailing to owners within 500 ft and occupants within 100 ft). Appealable to Planning Commission under SCCC Chapter 18.10. Required findings per **SCCC §18.10.230(a)**: project must not be detrimental to health/safety/welfare, must comply with county ordinances and General Plan, and must satisfy CEQA.
- **UPC (building/planning permits):** Community Development & Infrastructure (CDI), Unified Permit Center — https://cdi.santacruzcountyca.gov/UPC.aspx | Contact Planning: Planning.ZoningInfo@santacruzcountyca.gov
- Source (industrial districts code draft, Nov 2022 Board hearing): https://cdi.santacruzcountyca.gov/Portals/2/County/Planning/SustainabilityUpdate/Code_Amendments/13_10_341-346_IndustrialDistricts_public_draft.pdf
- Source (CUP process): https://cdi.santacruzcountyca.gov/UPC/DiscretionaryPermitsZoning/DiscretionaryPermits/ConditionalPermits-ZoningAdministrator.aspx

## Notes / GOTCHAs

- **Single county-wide CUPA:** Santa Cruz County EHS is the ONLY CUPA in the county. All four cities — including Santa Cruz and Watsonville (with their own fire/building departments) — are served by the county CUPA for HMBP/hazwaste/UST/APSA/CalARP. No city-run CUPA; no PA split.
- **County fire is CAL FIRE-contracted:** "Santa Cruz County Fire Department" is staffed and operated by CAL FIRE (CZU Unit) under contract for unincorporated areas not within a local FPD.
- **Fire consolidation (2021–2022):** Former Central FPD + Aptos/La Selva FPD consolidated into "Central Fire District of Santa Cruz County." Older references to either predecessor district are obsolete.
- **SCCSD is a pass-through:** The County Sanitation District does not own a POTW — it transmits all collected sewage to the City of Santa Cruz's facility. IWP applicants in unincorporated areas deal with SCCSD/ECU, not the City, but City standards mirror-apply.
- **CZU wildfire context:** 2020 CZU Lightning Complex burned large portions of north county (Bonny Doon, Boulder Creek, Davenport). Rebuild/permitting in burn-scar areas may involve additional CDI and CAL FIRE requirements; verify per-parcel.
- **Building code edition:** SCCC Title 12, Ch. 12.10 adopts Title 24 with local amendments. The 2025 CBS Code took effect statewide 2026-01-01; confirm current adopted edition at https://cdi.santacruzcountyca.gov/UPC/BuildingPermitsSafety/BuildingPermitsIndex/BuildingCodesResources.aspx [VERIFY — not confirmed online whether county has formally adopted 2025 edition as of this writing].
