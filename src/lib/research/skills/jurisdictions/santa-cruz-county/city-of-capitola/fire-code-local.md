## Authority Having Jurisdiction

**Central Fire District of Santa Cruz County** — an independent special district formed by the 2021–2022 consolidation of the former Central Fire Protection District and Aptos/La Selva Beach Fire Protection District. Capitola has no municipal fire department; the District is the sole AHJ for fire code enforcement and fire permits inside city limits. Serves Capitola, Aptos, Live Oak, La Selva Beach, Rio Del Mar, and Soquel.

- Community Risk Reduction Division: **(831) 685-6698**
- Website: https://www.centralfiresc.org

## Adopted Code Edition

**Ordinance 2025-03** (Central Fire District of Santa Cruz County) adopts the **2025 California Fire Code (CFC)**, which incorporates the International Fire Code (IFC) 2024 Edition, together with Appendices D, I, N and O of the IFC 2024 Edition, and Appendix P as published by the California Building Standards Commission, as modified by the ordinance's local amendments.

- Prior adoption repealed: **Ordinance 2023-01** (which had adopted the 2022 CFC).
- Signed/ratified: **November 13, 2025** by the Capitola City Council (vote: Jensen, Orbach, Westman, Morgan, Clarke — AYES; none opposed).
- Code numbering prefix: District local amendments use the prefix **35.** (e.g., § 35.503.2.1).

A separate **"2025 Fire Code Amendments — City of Capitola"** document (distinct from the county-wide amendments) is published on the District site but is a scanned/image PDF. Full text must be obtained directly from the District.

- Capitola amendments PDF: https://www.centralfiresc.org/DocumentCenter/View/4990/2025-Fire-Code-Amendments---City-of-Capitola-PDF
- County-wide amendments PDF: https://www.centralfiresc.org/DocumentCenter/View/4991/2025-Fire-Code-Amendments---Santa-Cruz-County-PDF

## Municipal Code Chapter

The District has no assigned chapter in the Capitola Municipal Code. The Fire Code of the Central Fire District is adopted by the District Board by ordinance and applies throughout District territory, including all Capitola city limits. The prior pre-consolidation adoption for Capitola was **Ordinance 987** (Central Fire Protection District / City of Capitola); superseded.

## Local Amendments (Ord. 2025-03, Part 2)

The following sections of the 2025 CFC / IFC 2024 are amended by Ord. 2025-03:

### Chapter 1 — Administration
- **§ 35.101.1** (Title): "Fire Code of the Central Fire District."
- **§ 35.102.1** (Construction/design applicability): Extends code to existing structures where the fire official finds a distinct hazard; alterations/repairs affecting ≥ 50 % of external walls or internal structural framework within 5 years trigger full compliance.
- **§ 35.103.5** (added): Fire code officials and deputies have law enforcement powers while on duty.
- **§ 35.105.1** (General permits): Permits required per §§ 105.1.1–105.6.25 as required by the AHJ. Fees paid prior to issuance; permit must be kept on premises.
- **§§ 35.112.1–35.112.4** (Board of Appeals): For District violations, the Board of Directors of the Fire District (or sub-committee) serves as the appeals body. Appeal must be filed in writing within **14 calendar days** of order. First $100 / second $200 / additional $500 per violation within one year (infraction, not imprisonment).
- **§ 35.113.4.1** (Abatement): Violations deemed public nuisances; abatement costs may be charged to the owner of record or assessed to property.

### Chapter 3 — General Precautions
- **§ 35.311.5** (Placards): Amended.
- **§ 35.307.2** (Open burning): Open burn season is **December 1 – April 30** (calendar year); within State Responsibility Area (SRA), season does not start until CAL FIRE lifts hazardous-fire-conditions proclamation. Open burning in the Local Responsibility Area (LRA) requires local fire chief approval. Pile burning allowed under CAL FIRE permits; Monterey Bay Area Unified Air Pollution Control District (MBARD) permits may also be required.

### Chapter 5 — Fire Service Features
- **§ 35.503.2.1** (Dimensions): Fire apparatus access roads ≥ **20 feet unobstructed width** and ≥ **13 feet 6 inches vertical clearance**. Vegetation within 10 feet each side of roadway must be cleared of flammable growth.
- **§ 35.503.2.1.1** (added): Vegetation clearance — 10 ft horizontal / 15 ft vertical on each side of roads and driveways.
- **§ 35.503.2.3** (Surface): All-weather surface = minimum 6" compacted Class II base rock for grades ≤ 5%; oil-and-screen for grades ≤ 15%; asphaltic concrete for grades > 15%. Maximum grade: **16 % in SRA**, **20 % in LRA**.
- **§ 35.503.2.4** (Turning radius): SRA — minimum inside horizontal radius 50 ft; LRA — minimum centerline radius 35 ft.
- **§ 35.503.2.5.1** (added): New dead-end roads prohibited without secondary access beyond specified distances (Urban/Suburban: 500 ft) in minor land divisions or subdivisions; in no case to exceed ½ mile.
- **§§ 35.503.2.6.1–35.503.2.6.5** (Bridges): All private bridges ≥ **20 ft clear width**; must be engineered by licensed civil/structural engineer and certified to fire code official. Recertification every 10 years; cost at owner's expense.
- **§ 35.503.2.7** (Grade): Same as 503.2.3 above (16 % SRA / 20 % LRA).
- **§ 35.503.6** (Gates): Security gates across fire access roads must be approved by fire code official; must have an approved emergency-operation means.

### Chapter 9 — Fire Protection Systems (Sprinklers)
- **§§ 35.903.2–35.903.2.10.2** (Automatic sprinkler systems, deleted and replaced): Sprinklers required in **all new occupancies** regardless of type of construction or floor area, with limited exceptions:
  - Sheds ≤ 1,000 sq ft: no sprinklers required.
  - Sheds 1,001–3,000 sq ft: at fire chief discretion if no increased fire hazard.
  - Agricultural buildings (Appendix C definition) with ≥ 60 ft unobstructed side yard, ≤ 25 ft height, in Agricultural zone: exempt.
  - Detached Group B/M occupancies ≤ 500 sq ft with exterior wall and opening protection: exempt.
  - Group R: sprinklers required throughout all buildings with a Group R fire area (NFPA 13 or 13D).
- **§ 35.903.3.1.3** (NFPA 13D systems): Amended.
- **§ 35.903.3.7** (FDC): Fire department connections — consistency amendment.

### Chapter 53 — Compressed Gases
- **§ 35.5303.5.3**: Compressed gas containers/cylinders on carts, in racks, nested, or in process of filling are exempt from general securing requirements; containers in examination, filling, transport, or servicing are also exempt.

### Chapter 56 — Explosives and Fireworks
- **§ 35.5601.1.3**: Possession, manufacture, storage, sale, handling, and use of fireworks are **prohibited** within Santa Cruz County. Exception: permitted display fireworks, proximate-audience pyrotechnics, and special effects productions per Title 19, Division 1, Chapter 6 and H&SC Division 11.

### Chapter 90 (added) — Wildfire Risk Areas
New chapter governing restricted entry to wildfire risk areas, fire road use, posting of closed areas, and liability for fire suppression costs caused by code violations.

## Geographic Limits (Part 3)

- Storage of Class I flammable liquids or Class II combustible liquids in aboveground outside tanks is **restricted** throughout the Central Fire District's incorporated area, with exceptions for A/A-1 zones, M-1/M-2/M-3 zones, and NR zones.
- Storage of flammable cryogenic fluids in stationary containers is **prohibited** throughout Santa Cruz County; exception: M-2 Zone with a Conditional Use Permit.
