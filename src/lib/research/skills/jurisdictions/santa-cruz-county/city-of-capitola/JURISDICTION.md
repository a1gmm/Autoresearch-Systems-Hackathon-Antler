## EHS Local Rules by Family

### CUPA (Hazardous Materials Unified Program)

- **Agency:** Santa Cruz County Environmental Health Services (SCCEH) — the single county-wide CUPA certified by CalEPA in 1996, covering all four incorporated cities including Capitola.
- **Programs administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generators, Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP), Onsite Hazardous Waste Treatment.
- **Local ordinance:** Santa Cruz County Code **Chapter 7.100** — Hazardous Materials, Hazardous Waste, Underground Storage Tanks. Note: County Code 7.100 imposes HMBP thresholds *below* state minimums, so some small-quantity handlers that are exempt statewide still require a county permit.
- **Fee schedule:** Unified Fee Schedule — http://unifiedfeeschedule.co.santa-cruz.ca.us/index.html
- **Permit / invoice portal:** https://sccounty01.co.santa-cruz.ca.us/env/invoice
- **Contact:** (831) 454-2022 | EnvironmentalHealth@santacruzcountyca.gov | 701 Ocean St., Room 312, Santa Cruz CA 95060
- **Walk-in hours:** Mon–Thu 8 am–12 pm; appointments Mon–Thu 1–5 pm and Fri 8 am–5 pm.
- Source: https://scceh.com/Home/Programs/HazardousMaterialsPrograms-CUPA.aspx

### Fire AHJ — Central Fire District of Santa Cruz County

- **Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ):** Central Fire District of Santa Cruz County — an independent special district (formed by the 2021–2022 consolidation of the former Central and Aptos/La Selva Fire Protection Districts). It serves Capitola, Aptos, Live Oak, La Selva Beach, Rio Del Mar, and Soquel; there is no municipal fire department inside Capitola city limits.
- **Adopted fire code:** The District adopted the **2022 California Fire Code** by local ordinance; **Ordinance 2025-03** (drafted 2025) repeals the 2022 adoption and adopts the **2025 California Fire Code** with local amendments — confirm effective date with the District before quoting.
- **Capitola-specific amendments:** Separate "2025 Fire Code Amendments — City of Capitola" document published on centralfiresc.org (distinct from the county-wide amendment set). [VERIFY — full text requires download from District document center.]
- **Historical ordinance:** Ordinance 987 (Central Fire Protection District / City of Capitola) — pre-consolidation; superseded.
- **Permit fee schedule:** Adopted by Board resolution; effective 07-01-2021 fee schedule posted as PDF on the District site. Contact Community Risk Reduction Division at **(831) 685-6698** for current schedule.
- **Fire Prevention Standards:** Set of 20 numbered FPO standards (cannabis, sprinklers, private water systems, road/addressing, hydrant markings, etc.) published by Santa Cruz County Fire Prevention Officers Association.

### Wastewater / POTW / Industrial Pretreatment

- **Collection agency:** Santa Cruz County Sanitation District (SCCSD) — collects wastewater from Live Oak, City of Capitola, and portions of Aptos and Soquel (~6 million gallons/day from the District's Lode Street Facility).
- **POTW / treatment plant:** Neary Lagoon Wastewater Treatment Facility, owned and operated by the **City of Santa Cruz** under a service agreement with SCCSD. Located at Neary Lagoon, Santa Cruz (~12 million gallons/day capacity, discharges to Monterey Bay under NPDES permit).
- **Industrial Discharge Permit (IDP):** All non-residential users proposing to discharge must obtain an Industrial Discharge Permit from SCCSD before connecting. See **SCCSD Code § 7.04.510** (Industrial Discharge Permit System) and **§ 7.04.310** (Prohibited Wastes). SCCSD Code is current through Ordinance 156 (January 16, 2025).
- **Sewer use ordinance:** SCCSD Code **Chapter 7.04** — Sewer Construction and Use — governs pretreatment standards, prohibited discharges, and noncompliant-user compliance schedules; mirrors federal 40 CFR Part 403 pretreatment requirements. Annual Pretreatment Program Reports filed with CIWQS.
- **District code portal:** https://sccsd.district.codes/Code/7.04
- **SCCSD contact:** (831) 454-2160 | dpwsanitation@santacruzcountyca.gov | 701 Ocean St., Room 401, Santa Cruz CA 95060 | Sewer Spill Hotline 24/7: (831) 477-3907
- Note: The **City of Capitola Municipal Code Chapter 13.04** (Sewers) requires all buildings intended for human habitation to connect to available public sewerage; it cross-references SCCSD authority for pretreatment.
- Source: https://cdi.santacruzcountyca.gov/PublicWorks/SewerWater/SantaCruzCountySanitationDistrict_.aspx

### Zoning — Industrial Districts

- **Zoning code:** Title 17 of the Capitola Municipal Code, published via CodePublishing at https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/Capitola/
- **Industrial district:** **Chapter 17.24** — Commercial and Industrial Zoning Districts. The "I" (Industrial) district is the single industrial designation; purpose is to provide for heavy commercial and light industrial uses that are desired but could be incompatible with other districts. It accommodates businesses contributing to a diverse local economy.
- **Permitted/conditional uses:** Table 17.24-1 lists land uses permitted by right vs. by Conditional Use Permit (CUP). Industrial uses incompatible with adjacent districts typically require a CUP through the Community Development Department.
- **CUP process:** Applications filed with Community Development; public hearing before Planning Commission required for CUPs.
- **Zoning map / districts:** Chapter 17.12 defines zoning districts and the official Zoning Map.
- Source: https://www.cityofcapitola.org/communitydevelopment/page/zoning-ordinance
- Source (code chapter): https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/Capitola/html/Capitola17/Capitola1724.html

## Notes / GOTCHAS

- **Fire AHJ is not the city:** Capitola has no municipal fire department. Central Fire District of Santa Cruz County (a special district) is the AHJ for fire code enforcement and permits inside city limits.
- **CUPA is the county, not the city:** All six CUPA programs (HMBP, UST, APSA, CalARP, hazwaste generator, onsite treatment) are administered by Santa Cruz County EHS — Capitola has no local CUPA authority.
- **Sewer collection ≠ treatment:** SCCSD collects wastewater; the City of Santa Cruz operates the actual POTW (Neary Lagoon). Industrial pretreatment permits are issued by SCCSD, not the City of Capitola and not the treatment plant operator.
- **Lower HMBP thresholds:** County Code Chapter 7.100 requires HMBPs at quantities below the state threshold — confirm with SCCEH before advising a client they are exempt.
- **Capitola.org → Capitola.gov redirect:** As of 2026 the official city website redirects from cityofcapitola.org to cityofcapitola.gov; deep-link pages may 404 on the .org domain.
