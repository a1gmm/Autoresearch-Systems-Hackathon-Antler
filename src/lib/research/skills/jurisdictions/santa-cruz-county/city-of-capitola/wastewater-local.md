## Collection and Treatment Structure

| Role | Agency |
|---|---|
| Sewer collection | **Santa Cruz County Sanitation District (SCCSD)** — collects wastewater from Live Oak, City of Capitola, portions of Aptos and Soquel (~6 MGD from Lode Street Facility) |
| Treatment plant / POTW | **City of Santa Cruz Wastewater Treatment Facility** (Neary Lagoon, 110 California Street, Santa Cruz) — operated by the City of Santa Cruz under inter-jurisdictional agreement; ~12 MGD capacity |
| NPDES permit | CA0048194 / WDR Order No. R3-2017-0030 (Central Coast RWQCB, Region 3) |
| Pretreatment authority | SCCSD issues Industrial Discharge Permits; the District and City of Santa Cruz have an approved inter-jurisdictional agreement differentiating pretreatment responsibilities. SCCSD is required to mirror any City of Santa Cruz pretreatment requirements, ordinances, or codes. |

## Capitola Municipal Code Cross-Reference

**Capitola Municipal Code Chapter 13.04 — Sewers** requires all buildings intended for human habitation or use to connect to available public sewerage (§ 13.04.050). Variance allowed only by appeal to the building official (§ 13.04.030). The chapter cross-defers to SCCSD for pretreatment authority; it does not set independent discharge standards.

## Prohibited Discharges (§ 7.04.310)

Key categorical prohibitions (verbatim from the district code):

- **Temperature**: No discharge > **104°F**; wastewater influent at treatment plant must not exceed 104°F.
- **Oil/Grease (industrial sources)**: Wax, grease, or oil (petroleum, non-biodegradable cutting oil, vegetable, or animal origin) of more than **300 mg/L** whether emulsified or not, or substances that may solidify/become viscous at 32–150°F at point of discharge.
- **pH**: No discharge with pH < **5.0** or > **10.0**, or having corrosive properties capable of causing damage to structures, equipment, or personnel.
- **Flammables/Explosives**: No two successive readings on explosion hazard meter > **5 % LEL** and no single reading > **10 % LEL**. Streams with closed-cup flashpoint < **140°F** (60°C) prohibited. Prohibited materials include gasoline, kerosene, naphtha, benzene, toluene, xylene, ethers, alcohols, ketones, aldehydes, peroxides, chlorates, perchlorates, bromates, carbides, hydrides, sulfides.
- **Suspended Solids**: No discharge containing > **1,000 mg/L** suspended solids (§ 7.04.310.J).
- **Chlorine demand**: No discharge exerting > **20 ppm** fifteen-minute chlorine demand.
- **Slug loading**: No concentration or flow rate that exceeds, for more than 15 minutes, more than 5 times the 24-hour average concentration or flow.
- **Radioactive wastes**: Regulated under § 7.04.305.
- **Dilution prohibition**: Diluting wastewater to meet discharge standards is itself a violation.

## Industrial Discharge Permit System (§ 7.04.510)

**Permit requirement**: All non-residential users proposing to discharge into SCCSD sewer lines must obtain an **Industrial Discharge Permit (IDP)** prior to connection. Existing non-residential users must have obtained permits within 90 days of ordinance effective date.

**Application requirements** (§ 7.04.510.B):
- Name, address, APN, and SIC code.
- Volume, constituents, and characteristics of wastewater (including parameters in § 7.04.310).
- Time/duration and peak flow rates (daily, monthly, seasonal).
- Site plans, floor plans, mechanical and plumbing plans showing all sewers by size, location, and elevation.
- Description of all activities, processes, materials, and products on premises.
- Number and type of employees and hours.
- Signed by authorized representative.

**Permit conditions** (§ 7.04.510.D): Include unit charges/fees, concentration/constituent limits, rate/timing limits, monitoring/sampling facility requirements, reporting obligations, compliance schedules, and technology requirements.

**Permit duration** (§ 7.04.510.E): Not to exceed **3 years**. If SCCSD does not notify discharger 30 days before expiration, permit auto-extends 6 months. Permit conditions may be modified during the permit term with 30-day notice.

**Permit transfer**: Permits are **not transferable** — new owners or operations must reapply.

**Delinquency penalties** (§ 7.04.510.B.4):
- ≤ 30 days late: 50 % of permit fee.
- 31 days–1 year late: 100 % of permit fee.
- > 1 year late: 1,000 % of permit fee.

**Categorical user coordination** (§ 7.04.510.C): Within 72 hours of application (excluding weekends/holidays), SCCSD notifies the City of Santa Cruz of potential categorical users; updated list forwarded every 90 days.

## Significant Industrial Users (SIUs)

As of the 2022 Annual Pretreatment Report, SCCSD had **six SIUs** in its service area, all subject to:
- Annual inspection minimum (more frequent if deficiencies found).
- Semi-annual self-monitoring reports (SMR).
- Minimum annual sampling by District staff.

Industries subject to routine light-industry inspection in 2022: 354 food establishments and 134 light industrial users (vehicle service, dry cleaners, machine shops, etc.).
