## Industrial (I) District — Purpose Statement (§ 17.24.010.C)

> "The purpose of the I zoning district is to provide an area for heavy commercial and light industrial uses in Capitola. The I zoning district allows for nonresidential uses which are desired in the community but could be incompatible with land uses in other zoning districts. The I zoning district shall continue to accommodate businesses that contribute to a diverse economy, provide local jobs, and serve the needs of residents and other businesses in Capitola." (Ord. 1043 § 2 (Att. 2), 2020)

There is only **one industrial zoning designation** in Capitola — the "I" district. There are no M-1/M-2/M-3 subclasses within the city.

### Residential Uses in I District
All residential uses — Single-Family, Multifamily, Residential Mixed Use, Large Residential Care — are **not allowed (—)** in the I district.

### Retail in I District
General Retail is **not allowed** in I. Retail Cannabis Establishments require a C (and cannabis retail license under Ch. 9.61).

## Development Standards (§ 17.24.030)

Development standards for the I district are in **Table 17.24-3** of § 17.24.030 (Revised 6/25 per Ord. 1075). The full table is in the municipal code text at https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/Capitola/html/Capitola17/Capitola1724.html — verify current setbacks, height limits, lot coverage, and parking requirements there directly, as these were revised in 2025.

Additional permit overlays may apply:
- **Design Permit** (Chapter 17.120) — may be required for commercial/industrial development projects.
- **Historic Alteration Permit** (Chapter 17.84) — if modifying a historic resource.
- **Coastal Development Permit** (Chapter 17.44) — for development in the coastal overlay zone (portions of Capitola are in the coastal zone).

## Key EHS-Relevant Notes

- **Aboveground flammable liquid storage**: The Central Fire District (Ord. 2025-03, Part 3) allows storage of Class I flammable or Class II combustible liquids in aboveground outside tanks in M-1/M-2/M-3 zones — but Capitola's I district does not use M-zone designations; verify with the fire code official whether the I-district is treated as equivalent to M-1 for this purpose.
- **Hazmat compatibility**: Industrial uses with HMBP thresholds may still be located in the I district but require SCCEH CUPA permitting — zoning approval does not substitute for CUPA program compliance.
- **No heavy industrial**: The I district is designated for "heavy commercial and light industrial" — uses classified as heavy industrial (e.g., chemical manufacturing, bulk liquid terminals, metal smelting) are not listed as permitted or conditional and would require a city council finding under § 17.24.020.A.
