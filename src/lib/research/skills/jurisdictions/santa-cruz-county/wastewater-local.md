## POTW and Collection Agency

**Santa Cruz County Sanitation District (SCCSD)** owns and operates the collection system serving unincorporated county areas (approx. 186 miles gravity sewers, 14 miles force main, 35 pump stations). SCCSD does **not** own a POTW — it collects and transmits all sewage via the D.A. Porath Pump Station (2750 Lode Street) through a force main to:

**City of Santa Cruz Wastewater Treatment Facility**
110 California Street, Santa Cruz, CA 95060

The City facility treats combined City + District flows and holds the NPDES permit.

## Regulatory Framework

- **NPDES Permit:** CA0048194
- **WDR Order:** WQ 2013-0058-EXEC (re-issued; effective **June 5, 2023**)
- **Federal authority:** 40 CFR Part 403 (National Pretreatment Program)
- **Inter-jurisdictional agreement:** State RWQCB-approved agreement differentiates City and District pretreatment responsibilities. SCCSD is required to **mirror** all City of Santa Cruz pretreatment requirements, ordinances, and codes.

## Local Ordinances / District Code

### SCCSD District Code (Chapter 7.04 — Sewer Construction and Use)

Available at: https://sccsd.district.codes/

Key sections:

- **§7.04.280 — Nondomestic Sewage Restricted:** Restricts discharge of nondomestic sewage; triggers industrial discharge permit requirement for certain users.
- **§7.04.310 — Prohibited Wastes Designated:** Lists prohibited wastes and sets local discharge limits. Key provisions known from annual reports and search results:
  - Temperature: discharges >104°F (40°C) prohibited.
  - Oil and grease (wax, grease, or oil): concentration >300 mg/L prohibited.
  - pH: violation confirmed at pH <5.0 s.u. (Bargetto Winery enforcement action, 2016 and 2023 reports). Allowable range consistent with federal pretreatment standards (5.0–12.0 s.u. typical; confirm current local limits at §7.04.310).
  - Note: "Unless State or Federal limits are lower, in which case those limits shall control. Dilution of wastewater discharge for the purpose of satisfying pretreatment standards is prohibited."
  - Sampling: Grab samples required for pH, cyanide, oil and grease, sulfide, and VOCs; 24-hour composite for all other parameters (flow-proportional where feasible).
- **§7.04.510 — Industrial Discharge Permit (IDP) System:** Governs permit issuance, terms, and conditions. Permits issued for specified period not to exceed **3 years**. Permit must include daily average and daily maximum discharge rates, or other appropriate conditions, for pollutants subject to limitations.

## Local Discharge Limits (Table 1.2 — SCCSD Wastewater Discharge Limitations)

The District's local discharge limits are documented annually in the Pretreatment Program Annual Report (Table 1.2) and codified in SCCSD District Code §7.04.310. The published 2023 Annual Report references "Table 1.2 — Santa Cruz County Sanitation District Wastewater Discharge Limitations" but the numerical table is in PDF image format in the report. Known limits from enforcement records and narrative:

| Parameter | Known Local Limit | Notes |
|-----------|-------------------|-------|
| Temperature | 104°F (40°C) max | §7.04.310; grab sample |
| Oil & Grease (wax/grease/oil) | 300 mg/L max | §7.04.310 |
| pH | ≥5.0 s.u. (lower bound confirmed by enforcement) | Grab sample; upper bound: confirm at §7.04.310 |
| All other parameters | Per §7.04.310 table | Full limits table at sccsd.district.codes/Code/7.04.310 |

For the complete numerical table (metals, BOD, TSS, cyanide, sulfide, etc.), access: https://sccsd.district.codes/Code/7.04.310

Federal Categorical Pretreatment Standards (40 CFR Parts 405–471) apply to Categorical Industrial Users (CIUs) and control where more stringent than local limits.

## Industrial Discharge Permit (IDP) Process

**Administered by:** Environmental Compliance Unit (ECU), SCCSD

**ECU Contact:**
- Monica Tomlinson, Environmental Programs Coordinator: (831) 477-3988
- ECU general line: (831) 454-2907 (Public Works)
- Email: dpwenv@santacruzcountyca.gov
- Address: 701 Ocean Street, Room 410, Santa Cruz, CA 95060
- Website: https://cdi.santacruzcountyca.gov/PublicWorks/SewerWater/EnvironmentalCompliance.aspx

**IDP Process Steps:**
1. Potential discharger identified — Wastewater Discharge Permit Application sent by ECU.
2. Completed application returned to ECU; Environmental Programs Coordinator reviews.
3. ECU determines whether baseline monitoring and/or formal permitting required.
4. Site assessment by ECU staff.
5. Baseline monitoring / wastewater analysis may be required.
6. IDP issued for up to 3-year term.
7. Significant Industrial Users (SIUs): must submit semi-annual self-monitoring reports (SMRs); inspected and sampled at least annually by ECU.
8. Light industrial users: inspected annually; pretreatment sampling triggered by operational review.

**Current SIU count (2023 reporting year):** 6 Significant Industrial Users in the District; all inspected and sampled annually; no SIUs in significant non-compliance as of 2023 report.

**BMP outreach sectors:** Vehicle service, dental offices, photo processors, restaurants, dry cleaners, printers, machine shops, surfboard manufacturers, paint contractors, mobile carwash, medical facilities.

## Enforcement

Enforcement Response Plan governs escalating actions. Letters of Violation (LOVs) → formal enforcement → potential Board of Directors penalty assessment for repeat non-compliance. Each violation day is a separate offense.
