## Overview

Santa Cruz County has two principal fire AHJs in the unincorporated area: the **Santa Cruz County Fire Department (SCCFD)**, operated under CAL FIRE CZU contract, and the **Central Fire District of Santa Cruz County (CFD)**, serving Capitola, Soquel, Aptos, Live Oak, La Selva Beach, and Rio Del Mar.

---

## 1. Santa Cruz County Fire Department (Unincorporated County / CAL FIRE CZU)

### Adopted Code and Ordinance

- **Municipal Code chapter:** SCCC **Chapter 7.92** (Fire Code)
- **Edition adopted:** 2022 California Fire Code (based on IFC 2021 Edition with California amendments)
- **Adopting ordinance:** **Ordinance No. 5415** (2022)
- **Subsequent amendment:** **Ordinance No. 5503** (December 9, 2025) — minor amendments; code current through Ord. 5503 as of 2025-06-24
- **Prior adoption lineage:** Ord. 5076 (2010, IFC 2009 / CFC 2010); repealed and replaced by 2013 adoption (IFC 2012 / CFC 2013, no separate Ord. number confirmed in documents reviewed); then Ord. 5415 (IFC 2021 / CFC 2022)

### Local Amendments of EHS Significance (SCCC Chapter 7.92)

**§7.92.010 — International Fire Code Adopted.** Adopts IFC 2021 Edition plus CFC 2022, with specified appendices (B and C of IFC not included in CFC), as modified by local amendments.

**§7.92.903.1 — Automatic Sprinkler Systems (IFC §§903.1–903.2.10.1 deleted and replaced):**
- New automatic fire sprinkler systems required in **all new occupancies**, regardless of construction type or floor area, unless preempted by California Health & Safety Code.
- Exceptions: detached private garages/carports/sheds ≤1,000 sf; sheds 1,001–3,000 sf at Fire Chief discretion; agricultural buildings in Agricultural zone districts meeting specific side-yard/height criteria.

**§7.92.903.3.1.3 — NFPA 13D Systems.** One- and two-family dwellings and certain congregate living facilities may use NFPA 13D-compliant systems.

**§7.92.903.3.7 — Fire Department Connections.** Amended for countywide consistency on FDC distance requirements.

**§7.92.503.2.1 — Fire Access Road Dimensions.** Amended.

**§7.92.503.2.6 / .503.2.6.1–.5 — Bridges and Elevated Surfaces; Width; Certification; Recertification; Existing Private Bridges; Fees.** Added — bridge specs for fire access.

**§7.92.503.7 — Gates.** Added — distance and width requirements for gates obstructing fire access roads.

**§7.92.5303.5.3 — Securing Compressed Gas Containers.** Amended — consistent with state CFC but locally amended.

**§7.92.Chapter 56 (Explosives and Fireworks; §7.92.5608).** IFC Chapter 56 deleted and replaced with local explosives/fireworks provisions; §7.92.5608 adds display fireworks rules.

**§7.92.Chapter 90 (Wildfire Risk Areas).** New chapter added starting at §7.92.9001 — suppression and control measures for wildfire risk areas (relevant to CZU burn scar areas).

**§7.92.308.1.6.3 — Sky Lanterns.** Added — prohibits release of untethered sky lanterns.

**§7.92.109.6 — Overcrowding.** Added — prohibits admittance beyond approved building capacity.

**§7.92.108.1 — Board of Appeals.** Board of Supervisors (or appointed sub-committee) serves as appellate body.

## 2. Central Fire District of Santa Cruz County

### Adopted Code and Ordinance

- **Draft ordinance (pending adoption as of records reviewed):** **Ordinance No. 2025-03** — adopts the **2025 California Fire Code** (IFC 2024 Edition with California amendments), repealing **Ordinance 2023-01** (which had adopted the 2022 CFC / IFC 2019).
- **IFC appendices included:** D, I, N, O (from IFC 2024 not in 2025 CFC); Appendix P (California BSC).
- **Prior code at time of this file:** CFD was operating under 2022 CFC / IFC 2019.

### Local Amendments of EHS Significance (Ord. 2025-03)

**§35.903.2.1 — New Structures (Sprinklers).** Automatic fire sprinkler system required in **all new occupancies** regardless of type of construction and floor area, unless preempted by California Health and Safety Code. Same exception structure as SCCFD (detached garages/sheds ≤1,000 sf; 1,001–3,000 sf at Fire Chief discretion; agricultural buildings meeting side-yard/height tests).

**§35.903.2.1.4 — Group R.** Automatic sprinkler system required throughout all new Group R (residential) buildings.

**§35.304.1.3 — Vegetation / Defensible Space.** 30-foot firebreak required around structures; 100-foot zone where required by state law.

**§35.103.5 — Law Enforcement Powers.** Fire code official granted law enforcement officer powers.

**§35.102.1 — Existing Structures.** Alterations/repairs removing or modifying ≥50% of external walls or internal structural framework within 5 years require full code conformance.

**§35.112 — Board of Appeals.** For independent fire districts, Board of Directors (or sub-committee) hears appeals; 14-day notice of appeal window; stay of order on appeal (except immediate life/property threats).

**§35.113.4 — Violation Penalties.** Infractions: $100 (1st), $200 (2nd within 1 year), $500 (each additional within 1 year). Each day a violation continues is a separate offense.

**§35.509.1.2 — Alternate Power Sources.** All permanent generators/solar/wind require building code approval plus warning signage on electrical panels.

**FPO Standards (22 adopted).** Full list of Santa Cruz County Fire Prevention Officers Standards enforced by CFD:
FPB-51 (Burn Regs), FPO-000–FPO-020 (covering: sprinkler requirements for 1/2-family dwellings, private water systems, water storage, bridge specs, residential CNG fueling, large family day cares, festivals/carnivals/fairs, access grades/clearances, turnouts, roadbed construction, turnarounds, addressing, hydrant markings, emergency responder comm systems, approved building materials, construction site safety).

## High-Piled Storage

No distinct local amendment to CFC Chapter 32 (High-Piled Combustible Storage) beyond the baseline CFC 2022/2025 was found in the documents reviewed. State CFC Chapter 32 thresholds apply (500 sf pile at or above 12 ft height, or 2,500 sf at any height, for Group S-1 occupancy triggers). Confirm with Fire Marshal for specific commodity classes.

## Permit Requirements

Fire prevention permits are required per CFC §105.1 as locally amended (§7.92.105.1 / §35.105.1). Fees set by each AHJ separately. SCCFD fee schedule: https://countyfire.santacruzcountyca.gov/. CFD fee schedule (2021 edition): https://www.centralfiresc.org/2191/Fee-Schedule (verify current year).
