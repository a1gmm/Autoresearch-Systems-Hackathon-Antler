## Adopted Code Edition

- **Code in effect (as of 2026-01-01):** 2025 California Fire Code (CFC), Title 24 Part 9 — adopted by **Ordinance No. 2025-25**.
- **Prior adoption:** 2022 California Fire Code adopted by **Ordinance No. 2022-14**.
- **Municipal code chapter:** Santa Cruz Municipal Code (SCMC) **Chapter 19.05 — Fire Code**.
- **AHJ:** City of Santa Cruz Fire Department, Fire Prevention & Life Safety Division. (Central Fire District of Santa Cruz County is a separate district serving unincorporated county areas only — not the AHJ within city limits.)

## Adopting Ordinance Structure

Ordinance 2025-25 adopts the 2025 CFC (including Appendix Chapters B, BB, C, CC, D, O, I, and P) by reference, then amends specific sections. Ordinance 2022-14 previously performed the same function for the 2022 CFC. Both ordinances are codified in SCMC Chapter 19.05.

## Local Amendments (Chapter 19.05)

The following local amendments are confirmed from codified text and ordinance searches:

### Administration (Chapter 1 amendments)
- **§19.05 / CFC §104.1–104.2** (added by Ord. 2025-25): Establishes fire code official's authority and compliance-determination procedures.
- **§19.05 / CFC §105.1.1** (amended): Permits required prior to operation for any operation, business, or system regulated by code; permit fees paid before issuance.
- **§19.05 / CFC §109.1** (amended): Inspection authority of the fire code official.
- **§19.05 / CFC §110.6** (added by Ord. 2025-25): Overcrowding — no person shall be admitted beyond the approved occupancy capacity of any building or portion thereof.
- **§19.05 / CFC §113.4** (added): Violation penalties.

### Sprinkler Systems
- **§19.05.140 / CFC §903.3.1.3** (NFPA 13D): Automatic sprinkler requirements address detached structures; local interpretation confirmed through Ord. 2025-25 text.
- Local amendment (Ord. 2022-14): Automatic sprinkler system required throughout all stories, including basements, of all buildings where the floor area exceeds **1,500 sq ft** AND where there is not at least one qualifying type of exterior wall opening.

### Special Occupancies / Hazardous Materials
- **CFC §308.1.6.3** (amended at SCMC §19.05.140): Sky lanterns prohibited within city limits.
- **CFC §5303.5.3** (Ord. 2025-25): Local requirement for securing compressed-gas containers in hazardous-materials operations.
- **CFC §1103.5.5**: Pyroxylin plastics local provision retained.
- **CFC §4901.3** (Ord. 2025-25): Wildland-Urban Interface fire areas — city's topography/climate cited as justification for stricter local standards.
- Hazardous material incident response (Chapter 27 / IFC reference): Actual cost recovery charged per fee code FIRE-PVT46.
