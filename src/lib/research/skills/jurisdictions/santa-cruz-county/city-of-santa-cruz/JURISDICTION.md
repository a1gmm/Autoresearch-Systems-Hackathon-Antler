## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials / Unified Program

- **CUPA agency:** Santa Cruz County Environmental Health Division — designated county-wide CUPA by CalEPA in 1996. The City of Santa Cruz is NOT its own CUPA; all six Unified Program elements for facilities inside city limits route to the county.
- **Programs covered:** Hazardous Materials Management Plans (HMBP), Hazardous Material Release Response Plans & Inventories, CalARP, Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Tanks (APSA), Hazardous Waste Generators, Onsite Hazardous Waste Treatment.
- **Local ordinance:** Santa Cruz County Code Chapter 7.100 (hazardous materials storage, use, and disposal).
- **Reporting portal:** CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) — statewide web-based system; facilities submit HMBP and inventory data electronically.
- **Fee schedule:** http://unifiedfeeschedule.co.santa-cruz.ca.us/index.html
- **Contact:** (831) 454-2022 | EnvironmentalHealth@santacruzcountyca.gov | 701 Ocean St., Room 312, Santa Cruz CA 95060
- Source: https://scceh.com/NewHome/Programs/HazardousMaterialsWaste.aspx

### 2. Fire AHJ — Fire Code & Permits

- **AHJ:** City of Santa Cruz Fire Department — municipal department; sole fire AHJ within city limits (distinct from Central Fire District of Santa Cruz County, which serves unincorporated areas).
- **Adopted code:** California Fire Code (CFC) 2022 Edition, enacted as Santa Cruz Municipal Code (SCMC) Chapter 19.05; Ordinance No. 2022-14 adopted the 2022 CFC with local amendments. Ordinance No. 2025-25 applied additional local amendments. (The 2025 CFC took effect statewide 2026-01-01 — confirm current adopted edition with Fire Prevention Bureau before citing.)
- **Local amendments include:** Automatic sprinkler requirements, overcrowding enforcement (CFC §107.6), inspection authority, and sky lanterns prohibition (SCMC §19.05.140 amending CFC §308.1.6.3).
- **Permit trigger (CFC §105.1.1):** Any operation, business, or system regulated by the code requires a fire permit prior to operation; fees paid before issuance.
- **Fire Prevention Bureau contact:** City of Santa Cruz Fire Department, Fire Prevention & Life Safety Division — https://www.santacruzca.gov/Government/City-Departments/Fire-Department/Fire-Prevention-Life-Safety
- Source: https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/SantaCruz/html/SantaCruz19/SantaCruz1905.html

### 3. POTW / Industrial Wastewater — Sewer Use & Pretreatment

- **POTW operator:** City of Santa Cruz Public Works Department — Environmental Compliance Unit.
- **NPDES permit:** CA0048194 (issued by Central Coast RWQCB, Order No. R3-2017-0030).
- **Local ordinance:** SCMC Chapter 16.08 — Sewer System Ordinance (Industrial/Commercial Pretreatment).
- **Significant Industrial User (SIU):** Categorical pretreatment standard users, OR facilities with ≥25,000 gpd process discharge, OR ≥5 % of WWTF hydraulic/organic capacity, OR reasonable potential for adverse impact — all require an Industrial Waste Discharge Permit (IWP).
- **Key prohibitions (Ch. 16.08):** Federal categorical limits (40 CFR Parts 405–471), accidental-discharge secondary containment plans required for new facilities, compliance with RCRA 40 CFR 261 for sludge/chemical disposal.
- **Monitoring:** SIUs sampled minimum once per year by city inspectors; periodic discharge reports filed at director-set intervals; records retained ≥ 3 years.
- **Contact / application:** City Environmental Compliance Inspector — (831) 420-6050; program page at https://www.cityofsantacruz.com/government/city-departments/public-works/environmental-compliance/industrial-waste-discharge-program
- Source: https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/SantaCruz/html/SantaCruz16/SantaCruz1608.html | https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/rwqcb3/board_decisions/adopted_orders/2017/2017_0030_order.pdf

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater

- **Ordinance:** SCMC Chapter 16.19 — Storm Water and Urban Runoff Pollution Control.
- **Program type:** Phase II NPDES General Permit MS4 — City operates its own Small MS4 program and Storm Water Management Program (SWMP).
- **Key prohibitions (Ch. 16.19):** Connections to storm drain system from inside buildings prohibited; any prohibited discharge to storm drain must be reported immediately by phone to the director (include location, material type, estimated volume).
- **Industrial/commercial requirement:** NPDES-permitted industrial sites must retain on-site: copy of permit or NOI, waste discharge ID number, and SWPPP/monitoring plan.
- **Stormwater program info:** https://www.santacruzca.gov/Government/City-Departments/Public-Works/Stormwater
- Source: https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/SantaCruz/html/SantaCruz16/SantaCruz1619.html

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts & CUP Process

- **Zoning code:** City of Santa Cruz Municipal Code Title 24 — Zoning (administered by Planning & Community Development, 809 Center St., Room 206).
- **Industrial districts:** SCMC Title 24 uses I-G (General Industrial) and I-L (Industrial Laboratory & Research) designations; I-G is governed by Chapter 24.16 Part 17. Older M-1/M-2 designations are superseded.
  - I-G overlays: I-G/Per (Performance Overlay) and I-G/Per-2 (Performance District) apply additional operational standards.
- **Land use permits (Chapter 24.08):** Uses by right are permitted outright; uses requiring discretionary review go through a Use Permit (equivalent to CUP) processed by the Zoning Administrator or Planning Commission per SCMC §24.08. If the Zoning Administrator finds a proposed use is more in character with conditional uses for the zone, a Use Permit is required.
- **Planning & Community Development contact:** 809 Center St., Room 206, Santa Cruz CA 95060; https://www.santacruzca.gov/Government/City-Departments/Planning-and-Community-Development
- Source: https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/SantaCruz/html/SantaCruz24/SantaCruz24.html | https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/SantaCruz/html/SantaCruz24/SantaCruz2410.html

## Standing GOTCHA Notes

- CUPA routes to **Santa Cruz County EHS**, not the city — do not cite city fire or planning departments for hazmat permitting.
- City Fire Dept. is the fire AHJ; Central Fire District of Santa Cruz County is a separate district serving unincorporated areas only.
- Pretreatment/IWP is administered by the **City Public Works Environmental Compliance Unit** (NPDES CA0048194), not the county sanitation district (which has its own separate pretreatment program under county code §7.04).
- Zoning industrial districts are designated **I-G** and **I-L** (not M-1/M-2).
