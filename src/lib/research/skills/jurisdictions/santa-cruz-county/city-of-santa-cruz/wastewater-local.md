## Significant Industrial User (SIU) Definition

An industrial user is a Significant Industrial User (SIU) if it meets **any** of the following:

1. Subject to federal categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR Chapter I, Subchapter N, Parts 405–471 — "categorical industry").
2. Discharges an average of **≥ 25,000 gallons per day** of process wastewater (excluding sanitary, non-contact cooling, and boiler blowdown).
3. Contributes a process wastestream that is **≥ 5 %** of the average dry-weather hydraulic or organic (BOD, TSS) capacity of the City POTW.
4. Determined by the Director to have reasonable potential for adverse impact on the POTW or for violating pretreatment standards.

SIUs must obtain an **Industrial Waste Discharge Permit (IWP/wastewater discharge permit)** per §16.08.160 before discharging.

## Local Discharge Limits (§16.08.110(c))

The following are the **maximum concentrations of pollutants** allowable in wastewater discharges to the City POTW. If state or federal limits are lower, those limits control. Dilution to meet limits is prohibited.

Sampling method: Grab samples required for pH, cyanide, oil and grease, sulfide, and VOCs. Composite (24-hour, flow-proportional) samples required for all other parameters.

|-----------|----------------------------|
| Arsenic | 0.21 mg/L |
| Cadmium | 1.57 mg/L |
| Chromium, total | 35.65 mg/L |
| Chromium, hexavalent | 5.26 mg/L |
| Copper | 2.17 mg/L |
| Cyanide | 0.08 mg/L |
| Lead | 6.04 mg/L |
| Mercury | 0.32 mg/L |
| Nickel | 1.15 mg/L |
| Selenium | 96.28 mg/L |
| Silver | 0.71 mg/L |
| Sulfide (dissolved) | 0.20 mg/L monthly average; 1.0 mg/L maximum |
| Suspended solids | 3,000 mg/L |

Additional specific prohibitions (§16.08.110(b)) include:
- pH < 5.0 prohibited at any time; operational range 5.0–10.0.
- Temperature: must not cause POTW to exceed 40 °C (104 °F).
- Oil and grease: petroleum/cutting/mineral oil in amounts causing interference or pass-through prohibited; animal/vegetable oil and grease: see §16.08.110 (food service interceptor requirements in §16.08.190).
- Flammables/explosives: no discharge where explosion meter reads > 5 % LEL (two successive) or > 10 % LEL (single reading); closed-cup flash point < 60 °C / 140 °F prohibited (per 40 CFR 261.21).
- Waste radiator coolant and engine/vehicle parts cleaning wastes prohibited.
- Hazardous waste as defined by 22 CCR 66261.3 prohibited.

## Wastewater Discharge Permit Process (§16.08.160)

**Who must apply:** All SIUs. The Director may also require any industrial user to apply.

**Timeline:**
- Existing un-permitted SIUs: submit application within **10 business days** of notification.
- Existing SIUs with permits: notify Director ≥ 60 days before proposed increase in quantity or strength.
- New/potential SIUs: submit application **60 days prior** to proposed discharge.

**Application requirements (§16.08.160(d)(2)):** All information deemed necessary by Director, including flow rates, chemical inventory, secondary containment plans (required per 22 CCR, Title 22, Division 4, Chapter 30 and City of Santa Cruz Hazardous Materials Ordinance), and self-monitoring program.

**Permit issuance:** Director must issue or deny within **90 days** of complete application; interim permit available if more time is needed.

**Grounds for denial include:** incomplete application, false information, fee not paid, secondary containment plan absent, or discharge would violate Chapter 16.08.

**Permit duration:** Issued for a fixed term; renewal application due **60 days before expiration**. Permits are non-transferable (§16.08.160).

**Annual inspection:** Facilities with a discharge permit are subject to annual inspection by City inspectors (§16.08.160).

**Monitoring / reporting:** Periodic discharge monitoring reports filed at intervals set by Director; records retained ≥ 3 years (§16.08.170).

## Significant Noncompliance (SNC) Criteria

SNC is triggered if (§16.08.010(gg)):
- Chronic violations: ≥ 66 % of measurements in a 6-month period exceed the daily maximum or average limit (by any magnitude).
- TRC violations: ≥ 33 % of measurements equal or exceed daily maximum limit × TRC (TRC = 1.4 for BOD, TSS, fats/oil/grease; 1.2 for all other pollutants except pH).
- Any single violation causing interference, pass-through, or endangerment.
