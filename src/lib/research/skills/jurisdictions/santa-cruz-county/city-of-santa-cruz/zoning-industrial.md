## Zoning Code Structure

- **Municipal code title:** SCMC **Title 24 — Zoning** (administered by the Planning & Community Development Department).
- **Administration chapter:** Chapter 24.04 — Administration.
- **Land use permits chapter:** Chapter 24.08 — Land Use Permits and Findings.
- **Land use districts chapter:** Chapter 24.10 — Land Use Districts (sets the district map and basic district regulations including industrial zones).
- **Development standards chapter:** Chapter 24.16 — contains standards applied to specific districts; **Part 17** governs I-G and **Part 18** governs IL-R districts.
- **Definitions:** Chapter 24.22.
- **Contact:** Planning & Community Development, 809 Center St., Room 206, Santa Cruz CA 95060; https://www.santacruzca.gov/Government/City-Departments/Planning-and-Community-Development

## Industrial Zone Districts

The City uses **I** (Industrial) designations. Legacy M-1/M-2 designations do not appear in the current SCMC.

### I-G — General Industrial District
- **Code location:** Chapter 24.10, **Part 16/Part 17** (land use district regulations); development standards in Chapter 24.16 Part 17.
- **SCMC section for principal permitted uses:** §24.10.1505.
- **SCMC section for use permit requirements:** §24.10.1510.
- **Overlays:** I-G/Per (Performance Overlay) and I-G/Per-2 (Performance District) apply additional operational standards to performance-sensitive industrial uses.
- **Character:** General industrial operations; all industrial use classifications 125–145 must comply with all performance standards listed in Part 2 of the Environmental Resource Management provisions.

### IL-R — Industrial Laboratory and Research District
- **Code location:** Chapter 24.10, **Part 18**; development standards in Chapter 24.16 Part 18.
- **Character:** Clean, light, research and development type industries that have minimal environmental effect. Distinct from I-G in that it targets technology/lab/R&D uses rather than heavy manufacturing.
- The JURISDICTION.md uses the abbreviated designation I-L; the full designation confirmed in LCP documents is IL-R (Industrial Laboratory and Research).

## Permitted vs. Conditional Uses (I-G)

Under §24.10.1505, uses in the I-G district fall into three categories:

| Category | Description | Permit Type |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| Principal Permitted Uses | Uses by right — manufacturing, warehousing, distribution, industrial service and repair, contractor yards, wholesale trade, and similar uses consistent with General Industrial character | No discretionary permit required |
| Administrative Use Permit | Uses requiring administrative discretionary review by the Zoning Administrator (ZA); ZA may approve without public hearing unless ZA elevates it | Administrative Use Permit (ZA action; appeal to Planning Commission/City Council) |
| Special Use Permit | Uses requiring Planning Commission action with public hearing | Special Use Permit (CPC action; appeal to City Council) |

Per §24.10.1510: If the ZA finds a proposed use is more in character with conditional uses for the zone, a use permit is required even for uses not explicitly listed as conditional.

**Design permit** (§24.08.410): Required for all new structures intended for industrial use and for commercial/industrial uses of land not involving a building. Also required for exterior remodeling/site alterations of ≥ $50,000 or ≥ 25 % additional floor area to any existing industrial building.

**Industrial design standards** (§24.08.430): In all Industrial (I) zone projects:
- Building design must include measures for reusing heat generated by machinery, computers, and artificial lighting (§24.08.430 item 13).
- All buildings shall be designed/oriented to make use of natural lighting (§24.08.430 item 14).

## Use Permit Types and Process (Chapter 24.08)

| Permit Type | Decision Body | Public Hearing | Appeal |
|-------------|--------------|---------------|--------|
| Administrative Use Permit | Zoning Administrator (ZA) | No (except if ZA elevates) | Planning Commission → City Council |
| Special Use Permit | Planning Commission (CPC) | Yes | City Council |
| Design Permit | ZA (or CPC if accompanying SUP) | ZA: No; CPC: Yes | Planning Commission → City Council |
| Use Permit Modification | Treated as new use permit application | Depends on type | Same as original |

**Use permit modification (§24.08.025):** An application to modify a use permit is treated as an application for a new use permit. Minor modifications consistent with General Plan and current zoning regulations may be processed as Minor Modifications per §24.04.160(4)(a)–(b) without a public hearing.

## Coastal Zone Note

The City of Santa Cruz Local Coastal Program (LCP) applies to the coastal zone portion of the city. Industrial uses in the coastal zone require a **Coastal Permit** in addition to any land use permit. The Zoning Administrator acts on coastal permits for ADUs; all others require Planning Commission or City Council action (with Coastal Commission as final appeal body, CCC).
