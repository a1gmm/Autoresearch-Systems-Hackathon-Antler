## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA (Unified Hazardous Materials Program)

- **Agency:** County of Santa Cruz Health Services Agency — Environmental Health Services (EHS) is the single county-wide CUPA. Scotts Valley is **not** its own CUPA; all six Unified Program elements (HMBP, hazardous waste generator, UST, APSA, CalARP, Tiered Permitting) for facilities inside Scotts Valley route to County EHS.
- **Local ordinance:** Santa Cruz County Code **Chapter 7.100** — governs storage, use, and disposal of hazardous materials and hazardous waste throughout the county (including all four incorporated cities).
- **CUPA designated since 1996** covering the entire county geographic boundary.
- **Permit portal:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — all regulated facilities submit and maintain permit/HMBP data electronically via CERS.
- **Fee schedule:** Unified Program fees posted at http://unifiedfeeschedule.co.santa-cruz.ca.us/index.html
- **Contact:** (831) 454-2022 | EnvironmentalHealth@santacruzcountyca.gov | County Government Center, 701 Ocean Street, 3rd Floor, Room 312, Santa Cruz, CA 95060 | Walk-in: Mon–Thu 8 a.m.–noon & 1–5 p.m.; Fri 8 a.m.–noon & 1–5 p.m.
- Source: https://scceh.com/NewHome/Programs/HazardousMaterialsWaste.aspx

### 2. Fire AHJ — Fire Code & Operational Permits

- **AHJ:** Scotts Valley Fire Protection District (SVFPD) — independent special district, NOT a city department and NOT CAL FIRE. Provides fire/EMS to the City of Scotts Valley and surrounding area.
- **Adopted fire code:** **2025 California Fire Code** (based on IFC 2024 Edition) — adopted by SVFPD **Ordinance No. 2025-2**, which repeals and replaces Ordinance 2022-1. Appendices B, BB, C, CC, D, I, N, O (IFC) and P (CBC) are adopted. Local amendments include sections 25.101.1, 25.102.1, 25.102.9, 25.103.5, 25.105.1, and others per Part 2 of the ordinance.
- **Effective date:** Ordinance 2025-2 was posted in 2025; effective January 1, 2026 (statewide CFC cycle).
- **Operational permits:** Issued under CFC Section 105; permit fees paid prior to issuance. Fees set by SVFPD **Ordinance 2021-1** (Fee Schedule adopted November 10, 2021, effective January 10, 2022). Inspection fee: $110/hour (1-hour minimum); $55/half-hour thereafter. Covers inspection services, plan review/required permits, false alarm fees, and administrative fees.
- **Plan submittal:** Applications and plan review process at https://www.scottsvalleyfire.com/plan-submittal/
- **Contact:** 7 Erba Lane, Scotts Valley, CA 95066 | (831) 438-0211 | info@scottsvalleyfire.com | Mon–Thu 7 a.m.–4 p.m. (closed noon–1 p.m.)
- Source: https://www.scottsvalleyfire.com/fire-prevention/2016-fire-code/

### 3. POTW / Sewer Use — Industrial Pretreatment

- **POTW operator:** City of Scotts Valley — Public Works Department, Wastewater Division. Facility address: 700 Lundy Lane, Scotts Valley, CA 95066.
- **Sewer use ordinance:** Municipal Code **Title 13 (Public Utilities) — Chapter 13.04 (Sewer Use Regulations and Fees)**. Article II of Ch. 13.04 covers pretreatment. Chapter 13.08 covers sewage disposal system regulation.
- **Pretreatment program:** City has a categorical industrial pretreatment program; Source Control monitors grease interceptors (FOG program) and enforces stormwater regulations at light industrial facilities (auto repair, restaurants, etc.). Categorical industries and groundwater pump-and-treat sites within the city are permitted through the pretreatment program.
- **Industrial wastewater permit (IWP) contact:** Environmental Compliance Officer — Julianne Rhodes (compliance@environmentalin.com); Wastewater Division Manager — Jeremy Long (jlong@scottsvalley.gov) | svpw-wastewater@scottsvalley.gov | (831) 438-0732 | Emergency: (831) 440-5670

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts & CUP Process

- **Zoning code:** Municipal Code **Title 17 — Zoning**. Official Zoning Map at Chapter 17.08.
- **Industrial district:** **Chapter 17.26 — I-L (Light Industrial Zoning District)**. Scotts Valley's industrial lands (~144 acres, ~5% of city) are primarily light industrial; areas include Technology Circle/Janis Way/El Pueblo corridor (Carbonero Creek), Watkins-Johnson area (north of Mt. Hermon Rd). Primary uses are high-tech/computer-oriented manufacturing, R&D, and supporting commercial.
- **Conditional use permit (CUP) process:** Conditional uses in I-L and other districts require a use permit per **Chapter 17.50** (§ 17.50.020). Development permit procedures governed by **Chapter 17.39**. General procedures and standards at **Chapter 17.44**.
- **Planning contact:** City of Scotts Valley Planning Department: (831) 440-5600 | https://www.scottsvalley.gov/265/Zoning-Design-Regulations

## GOTCHA Notes

- Fire protection is provided by the **independent Scotts Valley Fire Protection District**, not a city fire department and not CAL FIRE. The city runs its own Building Department and Wastewater Division.
- CUPA/hazmat permitting routes entirely to **Santa Cruz County EHS** (Ch. 7.100), using CERS for electronic submissions.
- The SVFPD adopted the **2025 CFC (Ordinance 2025-2)** — confirm with district whether effective date is January 1, 2026 or another date; the prior 2022 adoption (Ordinance 2022-1) is now repealed.
- Scotts Valley's only industrial zone is **I-L (Light Industrial)** under Ch. 17.26 — no heavy industrial (I-H) district exists in the city.
