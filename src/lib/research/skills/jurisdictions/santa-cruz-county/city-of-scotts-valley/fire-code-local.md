## AHJ

Scotts Valley Fire Protection District (SVFPD) — independent special district; NOT a city
fire department and NOT CAL FIRE. Provides fire and EMS to the City of Scotts Valley and
surrounding unincorporated areas.

Contact: 7 Erba Lane, Scotts Valley, CA 95066 | (831) 438-0211 | info@scottsvalleyfire.com
Prevention/Plan Review: Fire Marshal Erin Collins — ecollins@scottsvalleyfire.com
Office hours: Mon–Thu 7 a.m.–4 p.m. (closed noon–1 p.m.)

## Adopted Fire Code

**Ordinance No. 2025-2** — "An ordinance of the Scotts Valley Fire Protection District adopting
the 2025 edition of the California Fire Code … repealing the Previous Adoption of the 2025
California Fire Code [sic Ordinance 2022-1]."

- **Base code:** 2025 California Fire Code (CFC), which incorporates the International Fire Code
  (IFC) 2024 Edition published by ICC with California Building Standards Commission errata.
- **Appendices adopted:** B, BB, C, CC, D, I, N, and O (IFC); Appendix P (CBC/CBSC).
- **Repeals:** Ordinance 2022-1 (prior 2022 CFC adoption).
- Board hearing: November 12, 2025 (agenda item 7.2 / 8.2); effective January 1, 2026
  (statewide CFC cycle). NOTE: The board packet references the hearing date; confirm
  effective date directly with SVFPD.

## Sprinkler Thresholds (Key Local Amendments)

- **Group R:** Automatic sprinkler throughout all Group R buildings.
- **Group F-1:** Required when fire area > 6,000 sq ft; woodworking operations > 2,500 sq ft.
- **Group S-1:** Motor vehicles > 5,000 sq ft; upholstered furniture/mattresses > 2,500 sq ft.
- **Existing buildings (non-1&2 family):** Sprinkler required when addition causes total > 6,000 sq ft
  (entire structure), or addition ≥ 10% of existing sq ft / renovation > 50% gross floor area
  (entire structure).
- **Existing 1&2 family:** Sprinkler when addition increases total sq ft by ≥ 50%, or proposed total
  exceeds available fire flow. Exception: additions ≤ 500 sq ft where total does not exceed fire
  flow.

## Hazardous Materials — Flammable Liquid & Gas Storage Limits

From the SVFPD district-specific appendix sections in Ordinance 2025-2:

**Aboveground tank storage of Class I flammable liquids or Class II combustible liquids
outside of buildings** (per CFC §§ 5704.2.9.6.1 and 5706.2.4.4):
- **Restricted** throughout the entire Scotts Valley Fire Protection District political boundary.
- **Exceptions (allowed zones):** A or A-1 (agricultural); M-1, M-2, or M-3; NR.
  > Note: Scotts Valley's own zoning has only an I-L (Light Industrial) district — these zone
  > designations (M-1/M-2/M-3) appear to reference Santa Cruz County unincorporated zones
  > within the district's broader service territory. Verify with SVFPD for city parcel applicability.

**Flammable cryogenic fluids in stationary containers** (per CFC § 5806.2):
- Prohibited within the County of Santa Cruz boundary.
- Exceptions: M-2 Zone with a Conditional Use Permit from Planning Department; M-3 Zone (no CUP required).

**Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG)** (per CFC § 6104.2):
- Maximum 2,000 gallons water capacity within the County of Santa Cruz political boundary.

## Plan Submittal Process

All plans submitted electronically to Fire Marshal Erin Collins at ecollins@scottsvalleyfire.com.
Plan review time: up to two weeks for most projects.

Types of plans reviewed by SVFPD directly:
- Fire alarm systems (new, additions, remodels)
- Fire sprinkler systems — NFPA 13, 13R, 13D
- Hood and duct / fixed fire suppression systems
- Fire code use and special events applications
- Energy Storage Systems (application guide available)

Other permits (building, mechanical, electrical) route through City of Scotts Valley Building Dept:
City Hall, 1 Civic Center Drive, Scotts Valley | (831) 440-5600.
