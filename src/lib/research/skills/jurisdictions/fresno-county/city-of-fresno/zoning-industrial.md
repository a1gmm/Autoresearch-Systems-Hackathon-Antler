## Industrial District Names

City of Fresno uses **two industrial base districts** (no M-1/M-2 legacy codes — those are county designations):

| Code | Name | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| **IL** | Light Industrial | Diverse light industrial — limited manufacturing, R&D, fabrication, utility equipment/service yards, wholesaling, warehousing, distribution; ancillary small-scale retail and office permitted |
| **IH** | Heavy Industrial | Broadest range of industrial — manufacturing, assembly, wholesaling, distribution, storage; ancillary commercial services and office permitted |

Note: IL areas may serve as buffers between IH districts and other land uses. Both IL and IH are generally located near railroads and freeways or areas with good transportation access.

### Specific Limitations (from Table 15-1002)

1. Permitted if existing; no new residential units allowed.
2. One caretaker dwelling allowed where on-site residence is necessary for conduct of on-site business.
3. Not to include industrial training (welding, automotive repair) involving tools appropriate to an industrial use area.
4. Limited to establishments with gross floor area ≤5,000 sf.
5. Not allowed on the ground floor.
6. Permitted only as accessory use supporting business/office parks, corporate offices, and industrial uses.
7. Limited to membership club retailers on arterial or higher classification street.
8. **Outdoor storage shall be incidental to a primary use and screened from public view.**

## CUP / Development Permit Process

- **Development Permit**: Required for certain uses in IL even where "P" — e.g., office uses in IL per FMC §15-1303 require Development Permit approval by the Director.
- **Conditional Use Permit (CUP)**: Approved by Director for "C" uses. Appeals filed with Planning and Development Department within 15 days of Director decision.
- **Contact**: Planning and Development Department, 2600 Fresno Street, 3rd Floor, Fresno, CA 93721; (559) 621-8277; PublicCommentsPlanning@fresno.gov.
- **Zone Clearance (ZC)**: Required to verify compliance with FMC and General Plan before building permits issued; use the City's interactive GIS zoning map to determine parcel zoning.
- General Plan land use designation for IL areas: "Employment – Light Industrial"; for IH areas: "Employment – Heavy Industrial" [VERIFY current General Plan designations].

## Additional Standards Relevant to Industrial EHS

- **FMC §15-2321**: Concrete Batch Plants, Storage Yards, and Similar Uses — additional standards for construction/material yards.
- **FMC Article 25 (Performance Standards)**: Noise, vibration, dust, odor, and glare standards applicable to all IL/IH uses.
- **Airport Land Use Compatibility**: Fresno County Airport Land Use Compatibility Plan (ALUCP) governs height and safety zones overlapping parts of the city near Fresno Yosemite International Airport (FAT) — verify for any project near the airport.
- **California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA)**: Projects in IL/IH may require environmental review; City of Fresno Planning and Development is lead agency.
