## Significant Industrial User (SIU) Threshold

A discharger is an SIU if it meets **any** of the following:
- Subject to a **Categorical Pretreatment Standard** (e.g., electroplaters, metal finishers — 40 CFR Parts 405–471), OR
- Discharges an average of **≥25,000 gpd** of process wastewater (excluding sanitary, non-contact cooling, and boiler blowdown wastewater), OR
- Contributes a process waste stream making up ≥5% of RWRF design flow or capacity, OR
- City determines the discharge has reasonable potential to adversely affect the POTW.

## Regulated Sectors (Historical)

The following industry types have historically been regulated as SIUs or categorical dischargers in Fresno:
- Packing houses and food processors
- Metal finishers and electroplaters
- Poultry processors and slaughterhouses
- Industrial laundries
- Bakeries (high-BOD dischargers)
- Rendering facilities

- **Permit required** for all SIUs prior to discharge.
- Process: preliminary inquiry → detailed application → facility inspection → conditional permit approval.
- Contact Wastewater Management Division at (559) 621-5100 to initiate.

- Permits include site-specific local limits, monitoring requirements, reporting schedules, and self-monitoring requirements.

## General Discharge Prohibitions (FMC §6-316 / 40 CFR §403.5)

National general prohibitions incorporated locally. The following are prohibited from discharge to the City sewer:
- Discharges creating fire or explosion hazards (flash point ≤140°F at point of discharge).
- Discharges with **pH below 5.0** (unless the POTW is specifically designed to handle such waste).
- Discharges at **temperature >140°F** at the point of discharge to the sewer.
- Solid or viscous pollutants that obstruct flow or interfere with POTW operations.
- Any pollutant that causes pass-through or interference at the POTW.

Specific local numeric limits (e.g., for metals, FOG, TSS) are established in individual IWPs and may be published in a Local Limits document — [VERIFY; request from Wastewater Management Division].

## Stormwater (MS4 Co-Permittee)

- City of Fresno is a co-permittee on Phase I MS4 permit **Order No. R5-2013-0080** issued by the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board.
- Co-permittees: Fresno Metropolitan Flood Control District (lead), City of Fresno, City of Clovis, County of Fresno, CSU Fresno.
- Local stormwater ordinance: **FMC Chapter 6, Article 7 — Urban Storm Water Quality Management and Discharge Control**.
  - Prohibits illicit discharges (any non-stormwater discharge to the storm drain system).
  - Requires BMPs for industrial and commercial facilities.
  - Requires compliance with any applicable industrial NPDES permit (State General Industrial Permit Order 2014-0057-DWQ, as amended).
