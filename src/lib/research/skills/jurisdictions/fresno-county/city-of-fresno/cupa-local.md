## CUPA Agency

**City of Fresno has NO city-run CUPA.**

The designated CUPA for the City of Fresno is the **Fresno County Department of Public Health (FCDPH), Environmental Health Division**.

## Jurisdiction Coverage

The county CUPA covers all facilities within Fresno County, including incorporated cities (City of Fresno, City of Clovis, etc.) and unincorporated areas. Fresno County EH does **not** sub-delegate CUPA authority to any city; the county is the single CUPA for the region.

GOTCHA: Fresno Fire Department's HMRT (Hazardous Materials Response Team) handles emergency response only. FFD does issue **Special Hazard User Permits** for hazmat storage under the CFC (a separate fire code program, not the CUPA program). Do not conflate the two.

## Programs Administered

The Fresno County CUPA administers all six Unified Program elements per Health & Safety Code §25404:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP)** — required for any business handling hazardous materials above threshold quantities; submitted via CERS.
2. **California Accidental Release Prevention Program (CalARP)** — for regulated substances above Program 1/2/3 thresholds; risk management plans reviewed by CUPA.
3. **Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program** — installation, operation, monitoring, closure.
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — for facilities with ≥1,320 gallons aggregate aboveground petroleum storage; SPCC coordination.
5. **Hazardous Waste Generator Program** — CESQG, SQG, LQG generator compliance.
6. **Tiered Permit Program** — onsite hazardous waste treatment (CE, CA, PBR tiers).

## HMBP / CERS Reporting

- All regulated facilities must submit HMBP, UST, and CalARP documents electronically through **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System) at https://cers.calepa.ca.gov.
- Annual HMBP update due by **March 1** each year (or within 30 days of a qualifying change).
- CERS is the statewide portal; Fresno County EH reviews and processes submissions as the CUPA.
