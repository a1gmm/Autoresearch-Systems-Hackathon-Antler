## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air

- **San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution Control District (SJVAPCD)** is the sole air quality regulator for City of Fresno. The city has no independent air program.
- SJVAPCD issues Permits to Operate (PTO) for stationary sources; Title V federal operating permits for major sources; and enforces Rule 2010 (Permits Required) and related district rules.
- San Joaquin Valley is a non-attainment area for PM2.5 and ozone — BACT/TBACT requirements apply to new/modified equipment.
- Source: https://www.valleyair.org/permits/permits_home.htm

### CUPA (Hazardous Materials Unified Program)

- **No city CUPA** — the City of Fresno does NOT operate its own CUPA.
- The designated CUPA is the **Fresno County Department of Public Health, Environmental Health Division**.
- GOTCHA: Fresno City Fire Department's Hazardous Materials Response Team (HMRT) is emergency-response only — it is not the CUPA and does not issue HMBP or hazmat permits.
- Geographic coverage: Fresno County EH acts as CUPA for both unincorporated county and all cities within Fresno County, including the City of Fresno.
- Programs administered: Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP), CalARP, UST, APSA, Hazardous Waste Generator, Tiered Permits for onsite treatment.
- All HMBP and regulated-program reporting via CERS (California Environmental Reporting System): https://cers.calepa.ca.gov
- Fee authority: Fresno County Ordinance — current schedule effective per Ord. No. 26-002 (adopted 2025-26, effective 4/1/2026); see cupa-local.md for fee table.
- Source: https://www.fresnocountyca.gov/Departments/Public-Health/Environmental-Health/HazMat-Compliance-The-Designated-CUPA

### Fire (AHJ)

- **Fresno Fire Department (FFD)** is the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) for fire code within City of Fresno limits.
- Adopted code: **2025 California Fire Code** (based on 2024 IFC with California amendments), effective 2026-01-01, repealing the 2022 CFC.
- Previous: 2022 California Fire Code (based on 2021 IFC), adopted via City Council action, effective until 2025-12-31.
- Municipal code: **FMC Chapter 10, Article 5 — Fire Prevention**. Specific local amendments (e.g., ERRC radio coverage per Ord. ID 24-723, adopted 6/13/2024) are layered into Article 5.
- Special Hazard User Permit required for hazmat storage above CFC Table 105.6.20 thresholds — issued and enforced by FFD Fire Prevention Division.
- Fire Prevention Division phone: (559) 621-4181; HQ: 911 "H" Street, Fresno, CA 93721.
- Fee schedule: Master Fee Schedule Amendment #580 (effective 7/1/2024) — see fire-code-local.md.
- Source: https://www.fresno.gov/fire/ | https://library.municode.com/ca/fresno/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=MUCOFR_CH10REREPUNUREPRCOUS_ART5FIPR

### Wastewater (Industrial Pretreatment)

- **POTW**: City of Fresno operates two facilities: (1) **Fresno-Clovis Regional Wastewater Reclamation Facility (RWRF)** (NPDES Order R5-2018-0080, ~68 MGD); (2) **North Fresno Wastewater Reclamation Facility (NFWRF)** (NPDES Order R5-2014-0162, 0.71 MGD avg).
- Industrial pretreatment program administered by **City of Fresno Wastewater Management Division** — has operated since 1983.
- Local sewer-use authority: **FMC Chapter 6, Article 3 — Sewage and Water Disposal** (includes Section 6-316 Prohibitions). National categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR Part 403 and applicable category standards) apply directly.
- SIU threshold: categorical discharger OR ≥25,000 gpd process wastewater.
- Regulated sectors historically: packing/food processors, metal finishers, electroplaters, poultry/slaughter, industrial laundries, bakeries, rendering.
- Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit (IWP) issued by Wastewater Management Division; contact (559) 621-5100.
- Stormwater: **FMC Chapter 6, Article 7 — Urban Storm Water Quality Management and Discharge Control**. MS4 co-permittee under Order R5-2013-0080 (Fresno Metropolitan Flood Control District et al.).
- Source: https://www.fresno.gov/publicutilities/sewer-wastewater/programs-permits/ | https://library.municode.com/ca/fresno/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=MUCOFR_CH6MUSEUT_ART3SEWADI

### Stormwater

- Co-permittee on Phase I MS4 permit **Order No. R5-2013-0080** (Central Valley RWQCB) jointly with Fresno Metropolitan Flood Control District, City of Clovis, County of Fresno, and CSU Fresno.
- Industrial stormwater: State General Industrial Stormwater Permit (Order 2014-0057-DWQ, as amended) administered by State Water Board — separate from MS4.
- Local ordinance: **FMC Ch. 6, Art. 7** prohibits illicit discharges to the municipal storm drain system; requires BMPs; requires compliance with any applicable industrial NPDES permit.
- Fresno Metropolitan Flood Control District Ordinance No. 96-1 (Ch. 6) also governs certain stormwater controls within district boundaries overlapping the city.
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/centralvalley/water_issues/storm_water/municipal_permits/

### Zoning (Industrial)

- Zoning regulated under **FMC Chapter 15 — Citywide Development Code** (adopted by Ord. No. 2015-39, effective 1/9/2016, repealing former Chapter 12 land use articles).
- Industrial districts: **IL (Light Industrial)** and **IH (Heavy Industrial)** — governed by Chapter 15, Article 13 (Employment Districts), Section 15-1002 (Use Regulations).
- IL purpose: diverse light industrial — limited manufacturing, R&D, fabrication, utility yards, wholesaling, warehousing, distribution; ancillary small retail/office permitted.
- IH purpose: broadest industrial range — manufacturing, assembly, wholesaling, distribution, storage; ancillary commercial/office permitted.
- Use designations: P = permitted by right; C = Conditional Use Permit (CUP) by Director; dash = prohibited.
- Development permits for certain uses within IL/IH processed by **Planning and Development Department**, 2600 Fresno Street, 3rd Floor; (559) 621-8277.
- Source: https://library.municode.com/ca/fresno/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=MUCOFR_CH15CIDECOINRE_PTIIBAOVDI | https://www.fresno.gov/planning/zoning/
