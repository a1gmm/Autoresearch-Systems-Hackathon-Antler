## Industrial Zone Designations (Chapter 814.2)

Four industrial zones are established (Chapter 806.2, Table 2-1):

| Symbol | Name | General Plan Designation |
|---|---|---|
| **C-M** | Commercial and Light Manufacturing | Mountain Commercial / Mountain Urban |
| **M-1** | Light Industrial | Limited Industrial / General Industrial |
| **M-2** | General Industrial | General Industrial |
| **M-3** | Heavy Industrial | General Industrial |

### Zone Purposes (§ 814.2.010)

**C-M (Commercial and Light Manufacturing)**
Provides for close integration of retail, wholesale, light manufacturing, warehousing, and distribution operations that do not create adverse impacts (dust, gas, lighting, odor, smoke, sound, soot, or vibration) on adjacent uses.

**M-1 (Light Industrial)**
For restricted, non-intensive manufacturing and storage uses — fabrication, manufacturing, and assembly of processed materials — that do not adversely impact surrounding uses.

**M-2 (General Industrial)**
Wider range of fabrication, manufacturing, processing, and storage including warehousing, distribution, mini-warehouse storage, and a variety of light manufacturing, even adjacent to non-industrial uses.

**M-3 (Heavy Industrial)**
For heavy industrial uses including mechanical or chemical processing, assembling of component parts, blending of materials. Includes animal/poultry slaughtering, automobile assembly/recycling, machine shops, petroleum refining, and heavy manufacturing. May require buffering from residential/commercial uses.

| Process | Chapter | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Conditional Use Permit (CUP) | 842.5 | Required for C-designated uses |
| Director's Review and Approval | 846.5 | Required for D-designated uses |
| Site Plan Review | 854.5 | Required for all construction on P uses |
| Temporary Use Permit | 858.5 | Required for TUP-designated uses |
| Variance / Minor Deviation | 860.5 | For deviations from development standards |
| Unclassified CUP / Director's Review | 842.5 / 846.5 | For uses not listed in Table 2-8 |

Application filing and fees: Chapter 838.5.

## Performance Standards

Industrial operations are subject to performance standards (nuisance control) under Chapter 820.3, addressing noise, vibration, dust, smoke, odor, glare, heat, and radiation. See also Ordinance Code Chapter 6.33 (adult businesses) and Chapter 6.60 (medical marijuana).
