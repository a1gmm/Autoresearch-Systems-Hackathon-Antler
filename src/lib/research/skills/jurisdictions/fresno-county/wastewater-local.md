## Jurisdictional Split — Read This First

Fresno County (unincorporated) is largely rural/agricultural. Most industrial facilities in the urbanized Fresno metro area connect to the **City of Fresno** municipal sewer. The two regulatory contexts are:

| Context | Authority | Governing Instrument |
|---|---|---|
| Unincorporated county — on-site systems | Fresno County Public Works & Planning / EHD | County Ordinance Code Ch. 8.24, Ch. 15.20 (Plumbing Code); County LAMP |
| Industrial discharge to Fresno city sewer | City of Fresno Wastewater Management Division | Fresno Municipal Code Ch. 6, Art. 3 (Sewage and Water Disposal) |
| County-owned water/sewer treatment facilities | Fresno County (limited) | Ordinance Code Title 14, Chapter 14.13 |

## A. Unincorporated County — On-Site Wastewater Treatment Systems (OWTS)

### Regulatory Authority

**Fresno County Public Works & Planning Department / Environmental Health Division (EHD)**

- Oversees design, installation, and operation of OWTS (septic systems) in unincorporated areas.
- Authority: California Health & Safety Code § 101000 et seq.; Fresno County Code Chapters 8.24 and 15.20.

### Local Ordinance

- **Chapter 8.24** — OWTS permitting and sanitation requirements.
- **Chapter 15.20** — Plumbing Code (adopted 2016 California Plumbing Code with local amendments); governs OWTS design and construction.

### LAMP (Local Area Management Program)

- Adopted 2019; approved by Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB).
- Provides the regulatory framework for all OWTS in unincorporated Fresno County under the State OWTS Policy (SWRCB, 2012/2013).
- Approved LAMP = Conditional Waiver of Waste Discharge Requirements for OWTS within jurisdiction.
- Annual report submitted to RWQCB by February 1 each year.
- Source: https://www.fresnocountyca.gov/files/sharedassets/county/v/1/vision-files/files/39300-fresno-county-lamp-2019.pdf

### Who Must Obtain a Permit (Significant Industrial Users)

A **Significant Industrial User (SIU)** is:
1. A discharger subject to a Categorical Pretreatment Standard (e.g., electroplaters, metal finishers); OR
2. A discharger averaging 25,000+ gallons per day of process wastewater (excluding sanitary, noncontact cooling, and boiler blowdown wastewater).

### Permit Classes

| Class | Users | Validity |
|---|---|---|
| Class I | SIUs (categorical or high-volume) | 1 year |
| Other classes | Smaller industrial/commercial dischargers | Varies by type |

### Categorical Industries Regulated

Commonly regulated in Fresno area:
- Packing houses, food processors, bottling companies
- Industrial laundries and dry cleaners
- Rendering facilities
- Metal finishers and electroplaters
- Slaughterhouses and poultry processors
- Tortilla manufacturers

### General Discharge Prohibitions (from City program)

Prohibited discharges (partial list; consult the municipal code for complete list):
- Materials creating fire or explosion hazard in the collection system or treatment plant
- Corrosive discharges with pH < 5.0 (unless POTW is specifically designed to handle)
- Solid or viscous pollutants that obstruct flow
- Discharges sufficient to interfere with POTW biological treatment processes
- Wastewater with temperature > 140°F upon reaching the treatment plant

Categorical limits (federal): Apply per 40 CFR Part 400 et seq. for each industrial category.

### Application Process

1. Contact Wastewater Management Division for preliminary discussion.
2. Submit permit application and supporting data.
3. Facility inspection conducted.
4. Permit issued with monitoring and reporting requirements.

### Pretreatment Program History

Implemented since 1983; has reduced regulated pollutants discharged to RWRF by more than 50%.
