Verify the live page before quoting; the 2025 California Building Standards Code (Title 24) replaced the 2022 edition statewide on 2026-01-01.

## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air

- **San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution Control District (SJVAPCD / "Valley Air District")** is the air authority for ALL of Fresno County — both incorporated cities and unincorporated areas.
- Facility permits, New Source Review (NSR), Title V major permits, and toxic air contaminant (TAC) rules are administered by SJVAPCD.
- Public permit search: https://apps.valleyair.org/PublicPermits/Search/Permit
- Fresno County has no independent air district; SJVAPCD is an eight-county multi-county district (San Joaquin, Stanislaus, Merced, Madera, Fresno, Kings, Tulare, Kern valley portion).
- Source: https://www.valleyair.org/

### CUPA (Unified Hazardous Materials Program)

- **Fresno County Department of Public Health, Environmental Health Division** — designated CUPA countywide including all incorporated cities (certified 1996).
- GOTCHA: The City of Fresno does NOT operate its own CUPA. The City Fire HMRT is emergency-response only; regulatory permitting/inspection is the County's.
- Programs administered: Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP), Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Storage Tanks (AST / APSA), Hazardous Waste Generator, Onsite Hazardous Waste Treatment (Tiered Permit), California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP).
- ~4,000 regulated facilities countywide.
- Reporting portal: California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) at https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/
- Contact: HazMatCUPA@fresnocountyca.gov | (559) 600-3271
- Source: https://www.fresnocountyca.gov/Departments/Public-Health/Environmental-Health/HazMat-Compliance-The-Designated-CUPA
- See also: cupa-local.md

### Fire

- **Fresno County Fire Protection District (FCFPD)** — AHJ for unincorporated Fresno County (CAL FIRE cooperative agreement). City of Fresno Fire Department is AHJ within city limits only.
- FCFPD adopted **2025 California Fire Code (CFC)** via **Ordinance No. 2025-04** (passed December 17, 2025; effective **February 1, 2026**). Replaces Ordinance 2023-01.
- Also supplemented by Urgency Ordinance 2025-03 (BESS/PV requirements, Appendix R).
- Local violation penalty: misdemeanor; fine up to $500; up to 90 days imprisonment (§ 113.4 local amendment).
- SRA fire-safe construction: Fresno County Ordinance Code Title 15, Chapter 15.60.
- Fee schedule effective July 1, 2024.
- Source: https://www.fresnocountyfire.org/protection-and-planning
- See also: fire-code-local.md

### Wastewater / Pretreatment (Unincorporated Areas)

- Unincorporated Fresno County relies on on-site wastewater treatment systems (OWTS / septic) regulated under **Fresno County Ordinance Code Chapters 8.24 and 15.20** (Plumbing Code — adopted 2016 California Plumbing Code) and the County LAMP (Local Area Management Program, 2019, approved by Central Valley RWQCB).
- For industrial facilities connecting to the City of Fresno sewer system, the POTW is the **Fresno-Clovis Regional Wastewater Reclamation Facility (RWRF)**. Industrial pretreatment/IWP permits are issued by the **City of Fresno Wastewater Management Division** under Fresno Municipal Code Chapter 6, Article 3 (Sewage and Water Disposal). Contact: (559) 621-5100.
- Fresno County also has Ordinance Code **Title 14, Chapter 14.13** (Restrictions on Wastewater Discharge to County Water Treatment Facilities).
- Source (OWTS/LAMP): https://www.fresnocountyca.gov/files/sharedassets/county/v/1/vision-files/files/39300-fresno-county-lamp-2019.pdf
- Source (City pretreatment): https://www.fresno.gov/publicutilities/sewer-wastewater/programs-permits/
- See also: wastewater-local.md

### Stormwater

- Fresno County is a co-permittee on the **Fresno-Clovis Metro Area MS4 NPDES Permit** issued by the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB Order 2013, permit 5-01-048). Co-permittees: Fresno-Clovis Metropolitan Flood Control District, City of Fresno, City of Clovis, County of Fresno, California State University Fresno.
- Industrial stormwater: regulated by California NPDES General Permit for Stormwater Discharges Associated with Industrial Activities (State Water Board); no separate county industrial stormwater permit — facilities apply directly to the State.
- Policy manual on post-development and industrial stormwater BMPs: Fresno Metropolitan Flood Control District.
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/centralvalley/water_issues/storm_water/municipal_permits/

### Zoning (Industrial)

- Zoning authority for unincorporated Fresno County: **Fresno County Public Works & Planning Department**.
- Governing document: **Fresno County Zoning Ordinance**, Division 6 of the Fresno County Ordinance Code (updated February 2024).
- Industrial zone designations: **C-M** (Commercial and Light Manufacturing), **M-1** (Light Industrial), **M-2** (General Industrial), **M-3** (Heavy Industrial).
- Industrial zones chapter: **Chapter 814.2**; development standards in Table 2-9 (Chapter 814.2.030).
- CUP process: Chapter 842.5; Site Plan Review: Chapter 854.5.
- Source: https://www.fresnocountyca.gov/files/sharedassets/county/v/3/public-works-and-planning/zoning-ordinance/zoning-ordinance-final.pdf
- See also: zoning-industrial.md
