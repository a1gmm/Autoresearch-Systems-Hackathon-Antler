## CUPA Agency

**Fresno County Department of Public Health, Environmental Health Division (EHD)**

- Designated CUPA countywide — covers unincorporated areas AND all incorporated cities including the City of Fresno.
- Certified by CalEPA since 1996.
- ~4,000 regulated facilities in inventory.
- County is large (6,000 sq mi); ~25% of population below poverty level; ~44% English as second language.

Contact:
- Email: HazMatCUPA@fresnocountyca.gov
- Phone: (559) 600-3271

Source: https://www.fresnocountyca.gov/Departments/Public-Health/Environmental-Health/HazMat-Compliance-The-Designated-CUPA

## Programs Administered

Fresno County EHD administers all six Unified Program elements as the consolidated CUPA:

| Program | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP)** | Inventories, emergency response plans, employee training plans; CERS filing required |
| **Hazardous Waste Generator (HWG)** | Large and Small Quantity Generators; onsite accumulation, manifests, disposal records |
| **Onsite Hazardous Waste Treatment — Tiered Permit Program** | Conditional authorization, permit by rule, conditional permit, full permit tiers |
| **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** | Installation, operation, closure, leak detection, corrective action |
| **Aboveground Storage Tanks (AST / APSA)** | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act facilities; SPCC coordination |
| **CalARP (California Accidental Release Prevention)** | Extremely hazardous substances above threshold quantities; RMP coordination with USEPA |

Source: https://www.fresnocountyca.gov/Departments/Public-Health/Environmental-Health/Hazardous-Materials-Business-Plans

## CUPA Scope — City of Fresno GOTCHA

The City of Fresno Fire Department operates a Hazardous Materials Response Team (HMRT) — this is an **emergency response** unit, NOT a regulatory CUPA. All HMBP, UST, hazardous waste, and CalARP permitting within City of Fresno limits is administered by Fresno County EHD.

## CERS (California Environmental Reporting System)

All regulated businesses must submit annual HMBP updates, UST information, and hazardous waste data via CERS:

- Portal: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/
- HMBP threshold: any business that handles a hazardous material in a quantity at or above the applicable threshold (55 gallons / 500 lbs / 200 cubic feet for most substances).
- CERS filing is required annually; updates required within 30 days of significant changes.

Source: https://www.co.fresno.ca.us/departments/public-health/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-certified-unified-program-agency-cupa/california-environmental-reporting-system-cers

## CalARP Program

- Covers stationary sources with Regulated Substances above threshold quantities (per 19 CCR § 2770.5).
- Requires Risk Management Plan (RMP) submitted to USEPA and Process Hazard Analysis (PHA) or Hazard Review.
- Local review by Fresno County EHD; coordination with USEPA RMP*eSubmit system.
- State surcharge (FY 2023-24): $370 per facility per year (state-set; collected with county permit fee).

Source: https://www.fresnocountyca.gov/Departments/Public-Health/Environmental-Health/Hazardous-Materials-Business-Plans/California-Accidental-Release-Prevention-Program-CALARP

## Enforcement

- EHD conducts routine compliance inspections for each UP program element.
- In-house training program: staff trained across all programs monthly.
- Violations may result in notices of violation, administrative penalties, or referral to DA.
