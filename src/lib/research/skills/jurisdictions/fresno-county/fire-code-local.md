## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

**Fresno County Fire Protection District (FCFPD)**

- AHJ for unincorporated Fresno County; operates under a cooperative agreement with CAL FIRE.
- City of Fresno Fire Department is AHJ within the City of Fresno corporate limits (separate entity).
- FCFPD also serves Auberry and Shaver Lake CSAs, and Bald Mountain Fire Protection District under contract.
- Address: 210 S. Academy Ave (mailing) / 1700 Jensen Ave, Suite 103, Sanger, CA 93657 (plan submittal)
- Phone: (559) 493-4300

## Adopted Fire Code Edition

**2025 California Fire Code (CFC)** — California Code of Regulations, Title 24, Part 9

Adopted via **Ordinance No. 2025-04** of the Fresno County Fire Protection District:
- Passed (first reading): December 17, 2025, by the Board of Directors
- **Effective date: February 1, 2026**
- Replaces: Ordinance No. 2023-01 (2022 CFC adoption)
- Also supplemented by: Urgency Ordinance No. 2025-03 (BESS and PV systems — Appendix R)

Source: https://www.fresnocountyfire.org/files/3a60f1fa7/Ordinance+2025-04+Adopting+2025+California+Fire+Codes.pdf

## Municipal Code Chapter

- **Fresno County Ordinance Code, Title 15 (Buildings and Construction), Chapter 15.10 — Fire Code**
  - Contains the base adoption of the California Fire Code for the county/district.
  - Source: https://library.municode.com/ca/fresno_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT15BUCO_CH15.10FICO

- **Title 15, Chapter 15.60 — State Responsibility Area (SRA) Fire Safe Regulations**
  - Established by Ordinance No. 19-025.
  - Applies to new construction in State Responsibility Areas within the district.
  - Covers: emergency access standards, perimeter wildfire protection measures, building numbering/signage, private water supply reserves, vegetation modification requirements.
  - PDF: https://www.fresnocountyfire.org/files/1e76ce9b5/SRA+Fire+Safe+Regulations+of+the+County+Title+15.60.pdf

## Local Amendments (Ordinance 2025-04, Section 3)

Key local amendments to the 2025 CFC:

| CFC Section | Amendment |
|---|---|
| § 101.1 Title | Replace `[NAME OF JURISDICTION]` with "Fresno County Fire Protection District" |
| § 104.9.2 (new) | Compliance with current FCFPD Policies and Procedures Manual = code-compliant design |
| § 113.4 Violation Penalties | Offense type: **Misdemeanor**; Fine: up to **$500.00**; Jail: up to **90 days** |
| § 507.2.3 (new) | Reservoir standards for fire flow supply (lining, treatment, measurement, maintenance per NFPA 25) |
| § 5704.2.9.6.1 | Local amendment to aboveground flammable/combustible liquid storage locations (details in ordinance) |
| Appendix R (new) | BESS and photovoltaic (PV) system requirements (incorporated from Urgency Ordinance 2025-03) |

Local amendments are justified by: climatic conditions (drought, reduced water supply) and topographic conditions (steep roads, snow/ice blockage, flood/mudslide-prone areas) within the district.

Source: https://www.fresnocountyfire.org/files/3a60f1fa7/Ordinance+2025-04+Adopting+2025+California+Fire+Codes.pdf

## Permit Types

FCFPD issues fire permits for the following submittal types (each requires a separate permit application and separate payment):

1. Plans for Fire Apparatus Access and Site Water Supply
2. Fire District Building Final
3. Underground Water Delivery System
4. Fire Pump & Pump House
5. Commercial Fire Sprinkler
6. Residential Fire Sprinkler
7. Clean Agent System
8. Hood Suppression System
9. Fire Alarm System

Also administered:
- AB-38 Real Estate Inspections (SRA compliance)
- School facility inspections (checklist updated April 1, 2024)
- Construction plan reviews

Source: https://www.fresnocountyfire.org/fire-permit-application

## Plan Submittal Process

1. Submit two plan sets + completed Fire Permit Application + payment to FCFPD Protection and Planning office at 1700 Jensen Ave, Suite 103, Sanger, CA 93657.
2. Plans reviewed by technical specialists for CFC compliance and Fresno County Ordinance 15.60 (SRA) compliance.
3. Decision rendered in writing.

## Appeals

Written appeal must be received within 10 business days of the decision. Appeals go to Protection and Planning/Code Enforcement Division Chief, then Fire Chief, then State Fire Marshal (CFC § 103.1.4.2).
