### 2. Fire AHJ — Adopted Fire Code & Permits

- **Structural fire AHJ (City of Hollister and unincorporated county):** Hollister Fire Department / Fire Prevention Bureau. The City of Hollister has historically provided fire services for the unincorporated county under contract (since 2013); as of June 2024 the City submitted notice of intent to terminate that contract — verify current AHJ before relying on this.
- **CAL FIRE unit:** San Benito–Monterey Unit (BEU) — State Responsibility Area wildland.
- **Adopted fire code:** **2022 California Fire Code** (based on IFC 2021), with local amendments adopted by the City of Hollister (Exhibit A, finalized 10/27/2022). All appendices to the 2022 CFC are adopted in their entirety.
- **Key local amendments:** Access road grades ≤15% (paved if >8%); private bridge HS-20 loading minimum; Knox Box required where access is restricted; agricultural explosive device operational permits required (§ 105.5.0); 50% rule for existing-building upgrades.
- **Fire Prevention Bureau contact:** (831) 636-4325, Option 1 | 450 Hill Street #101, Hollister, CA 95023
- **Burn permits:** Backyard (expire April 30 annually) and Agricultural (1-year validity) — apply through Fire Prevention Bureau.
- **County fire design standards:** Title 23 (Engineering), Chapter 23.27 (Fire Design Standards) of the San Benito County Code of Ordinances.
- **Fire sprinkler ordinance:** Board of Supervisors approved a local fire sprinkler ordinance (exact number [VERIFY - not found online]).

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater

- **Permit framework:** City of Hollister holds coverage under the **California Phase II Small MS4 General Permit** (State Water Board Order No. 2013-0001-DWQ). Post-construction requirements governed by Central Coast RWQCB Order R3-2013-0032.
- **Controlling RWQCB:** Central Coast Region 3 (most of the county); small portions of the county's eastern watershed may fall under Region 5 — confirm watershed for specific sites.
- **Stormwater contact:** City Engineering Dept., 420 Hill St. Building C, Hollister, CA 95023 | (831) 636-4340
- **Unincorporated county:** Largely rural/agricultural; industrial sites in unincorporated areas with regulated stormwater discharges (e.g., construction ≥1 acre) must obtain individual NPDES permit from Central Coast RWQCB (Region 3) — no separate county MS4 general permit found.
- Source: https://hollister.ca.gov/government/departments/community_services/stormwater_management/index.php

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts & CUP Process

- **Zoning authority:** San Benito County Resource Management Agency (RMA), Planning Division — unincorporated areas. City of Hollister Development Services — within city limits.
- **Zoning code:** Title 25 (Zoning) of the San Benito County Code of Ordinances. Chapter 25.03 (Zoning Map and Zoning Districts).
- **Industrial districts (§ 25.03.007):**
  - **BP — Business Park District:** High-quality industrial uses in a park-like setting compatible with residential/institutional uses.
  - **M-1 — Light Industrial District:** Light industrial near transportation systems; includes warehouses, contractor yards, nurseries, lumber yards, auto repair, light manufacturing/assembly, R&D.
  - **M-2 — General/Heavy Industrial District:** Heavier industrial uses; more intensive than M-1.
- **Use table:** § 25.03.007(E) designates uses as Permitted, Administratively Permitted, Conditionally Permitted (CUP), or Prohibited in BP/M-1/M-2. Development standards in Chapter 25.08.
- **CUP process:** § 25.02.003 governs Conditional Use Permits; apply through RMA Planning Division.
- **Non-residential design standards:** § 25.07.003 applies to all new non-residential structures in C-1, C-2, BP, M-1, M-2, RRP, and PQP zones.

## Notes / Gotchas

- CUPA and fire AHJ are separate agencies: county Environmental Health = CUPA; Hollister Fire Department = fire AHJ (verify unincorporated coverage given 2024 contract termination notice).
- San Benito has only two incorporated cities (Hollister, San Juan Bautista). Nearly all industrial land is in or near Hollister city limits.
- RWQCB split: county spans mostly Region 3 (Central Coast); confirm watershed for any specific parcel before citing the controlling board.
- The 2025 California Building Standards Code took effect statewide 2026-01-01; confirm whether the county/city has formally re-adopted for local amendments.
