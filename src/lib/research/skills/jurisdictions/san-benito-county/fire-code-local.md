## Adopted Code Edition

- **Base code:** 2022 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9), itself based on IFC 2021.
- **Local amendments adopted:** Exhibit A — "2022 Hollister Fire Code Amendments, FINAL VERSION – 10/27/2022."
- **Adopting ordinance / municipal code chapter:** Hollister Municipal Code § 15.04.050 ("Construction codes adopted by reference"), adopted under **Ordinance 1224**. All appendices to the 2022 CFC are adopted in their entirety (§ 101.2.1 of amendments).
- **Fire AHJ:** Hollister Fire Department / Fire Prevention Bureau. Hollister has historically served unincorporated county areas under contract; as of June 2024 the City served notice of intent to terminate that contract — verify current AHJ for unincorporated parcels.
- **County fire design standards:** San Benito County Code of Ordinances Title 23 (Engineering), Chapter 23.27 (Fire Design Standards) — applies to unincorporated county.

## Key Local Amendments (Verbatim Exhibit A Provisions)

### Scope / Construction Provisions (§ 102.1)
Alterations and/or repairs that remove, alter, or replace ≥50 % of external walls or internal structural/non-structural framework within a five-year period require the **entire building** to conform to current code provisions.

### Agricultural Explosive Devices (§ 105.5.0)
An operational permit is required for storage or use of any agricultural explosive device, including "bird bombs."

### Access Roads (§§ 503.2.7, 503.2.7.1)
- Maximum fire apparatus access road grade: **15 %** (unless specifically approved by Fire Code Official).
- Roads >8 % grade: must be paved with minimum 0.17 ft asphaltic concrete on 0.34 ft aggregate base.
- Roads >15 % (where approved): perpendicularly grooved concrete required.

### Private Bridges (§§ 503.2.6.1–503.2.6.2)
- Minimum design load: **HS-20** (AASHTO).
- Unobstructed vertical clearance: ≥15 ft.
- Minimum width: 20 ft clear (may be reduced to 12 ft for Group U or R-3).
- Maximum approach grade change: ≤8 % for ≥10 ft.
- Re-certification by licensed engineer every 10 years.

### Knox Box (§ 506.1)
Where access is restricted, the Fire Code Official may require a key box. **This jurisdiction utilizes the KNOX Box Security Systems.**

### Fire Hydrant Marking (§ 507.5.2)
When required by Fire Code Official, hydrants shall be painted per the most current edition of NFPA 291.

### Spark Arresters (§§ 605.3.1.1–605.3.1.2)
Required on all chimneys, incinerators, and smokestacks using solid fuel. Opening size: minimum 3/8 in., maximum 1/2 in.

### Plant Processing / Cannabis Extraction (§ 901.11)
All buildings housing plant post-harvest or indoor cultivation operations require:
- Fire sprinklers designed to Ordinary Group II (NFPA 13, § 903); Extra Hazard if Group H; waivable for approved buildings <500 sq ft.
- Complete fire alarm system with sprinkler monitoring and occupant notification (NFPA 72, § 907).
- Dry chemical systems where volatile solvents are used in extraction booths.

### Rural Water Supply (Appendix Q)
- Structures <3,000 sq ft: minimum 4,900-gallon fire protection water supply.
- Structures ≥3,000 sq ft: minimum 9,800 gallons.
- Fire hydrant/valve: located 4–12 ft from roadway; not closer than 50 ft or farther than 1,000 ft by road from the building served.

### Defensible Space (Appendix Q, § Q109.1)
Must meet Public Resources Code § 4291 or the Fire Code, whichever is more restrictive.

### Roof Assembly (Appendix R)
- New buildings: minimum Class B roof assembly.
- New buildings in Very High Hazard Severity Zones: minimum Class A.
- Existing buildings reroofing ≥50 % of area within one year: same minimums apply.

## Operational Permit Fees (Resolution 2024-106, effective 8/17/2024)

All California Fire Code annual operational permits: **$50 per permit + inspection fee.**

Operational permit categories in Hollister (as listed in the 2024 fee schedule, adopted pursuant to the 2022 CFC):
Hazardous Materials, High-Piled Storage, HPM (Hazardous Production Materials), Flammable and Combustible Liquids, Cryogenic Fluids, Explosives, Cutting and Welding, Hot Work Operations, Combustible Dust-Producing Operations, Industrial Ovens, Dry Cleaning Plants, Fumigation and Thermal Insecticide Fogging, LP Gas, Organic Coatings, Refrigeration Equipment, Spraying or Dipping, Repair Garages and Motor Fuel-Dispensing Facilities, Lumber Yards and Wood Working Plants, Waste Handling, and others.

General inspection fee: **$179.00**; Plan review: **$299.00**; Re-inspection: **$179.00**.
