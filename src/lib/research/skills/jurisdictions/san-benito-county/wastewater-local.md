## POTW Operator

Nearly all industrially zoned land in San Benito County is within or immediately adjacent to the City of Hollister, which operates the relevant POTW infrastructure.

- **City of Hollister — Water Reclamation Facility (WRF):** Treats domestic, commercial, and industrial wastewater. Produces Title 22 recycled water for irrigation and groundwater recharge.
- **City of Hollister — Industrial Wastewater Treatment Plant (IWTP):** Operated under contract by Sunnyslope County Water District; located at 3570 Airline Hwy, Hollister. Primarily treats seasonal industrial wastewater from San Benito Foods' tomato cannery (July–September).

## Municipal Code / Sewer Use Ordinance

- **Hollister Municipal Code, Title 13 (Public Utilities), Chapter 13.08 (Water Service System):** This chapter governs sewer service and includes Article II ("Water and Sewer General") and Section 13.08.030 ("Fees — Industrial Waste Amendments"). This is the operative sewer use ordinance chapter for the City of Hollister.
- **Specific local sewer use ordinance number / discharge limit table:** Not published on the city's public website as of 2026-06-03. The city references the chapter through Municode but the specific discharge limit tables and pretreatment standards within Ch. 13.08 are not reproduced on the city's web portal. Contact the Environmental Programs Division for the current ordinance text and numeric limits.
- **Unincorporated county:** Facilities in unincorporated San Benito County that are not served by the City of Hollister sewer system must obtain individual Waste Discharge Requirements (WDR) or NPDES permit from the Central Coast RWQCB (Region 3). San Benito County Water District (SBCWD) manages water supply for parts of the unincorporated county; for sewer in unincorporated areas contact SBCWD at (831) 637-8218 or DocumentRequest@sbcwd.ca.gov.

## Industrial Waste Permit Process

- The City of Hollister Environmental Programs Division administers the industrial pretreatment program under the federal pretreatment regulations (40 CFR Part 403) as required by the WRF's NPDES permit.
- Significant Industrial Users (SIUs) and Categorical Industrial Users (CIUs) must apply for an Industrial Waste Permit (IWP) through the Environmental Programs Division.
- Application materials, discharge characterization data, and wastewater flow information must be submitted to the Division. Contact EnvironmentalPrograms@Hollister.ca.gov to initiate the IWP process.
- **Discharge limit table:** Numeric local limits are embedded in the IWP itself and in the sewer use ordinance (Ch. 13.08). Hollister does not publish a standalone local limits table on its public website — obtain directly from Environmental Programs.

## Industrial Wastewater Treatment Plant (IWTP) — Sunnyslope Contract

- **Operator:** Sunnyslope County Water District (under contract with City of Hollister and San Benito Foods / Neil Jones Foods Company).
- **Address:** 3570 Airline Hwy, Hollister, CA 95023 | (831) 637-4670
- **Seasonal operation:** July through September (tomato canning season).
- **Process:** Transmission pipeline from canning facility → headworks (pH/DO monitoring, flow metering) → aeration basins → polishing pond → percolation basins (percolation to groundwater).
- **Discharge:** Treated effluent meeting regulatory standards is directed to percolation basins; no surface discharge.
- **Industrial discharge to IWTP:** Reserved for seasonal cannery flow; new industrial dischargers to city infrastructure route through WRF pretreatment program — contact Environmental Programs Division.

## RWQCB Oversight

- **Controlling RWQCB:** Central Coast Region 3 (most of San Benito County). Small portions of the eastern watershed may fall under Region 5 — confirm watershed for specific sites.
- The WRF operates under a Central Coast RWQCB NPDES permit; the pretreatment program is a condition of that permit.
