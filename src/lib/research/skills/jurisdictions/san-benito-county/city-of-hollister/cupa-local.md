## Own-CUPA vs. County CUPA

The county Environmental Health Division is the sole CUPA for San Benito County. There is no city-level CUPA or sub-CUPA for Hollister. Businesses within Hollister city limits file all unified program elements with the county agency.

## Programs Administered

San Benito County HHSA Environmental Health administers the following California Unified Program elements as CUPA:

| Program | Abbreviation | Federal / State Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan | HMBP | HSC § 25500 et seq. |
| Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act | APSA | HSC § 25270 et seq. |
| Hazardous Waste Generator (onsite treatment, tiered permitting) | HWG | HSC § 25200 et seq. |
| Underground Storage Tank | UST | HSC § 25280 et seq. |
| California Accidental Release Prevention | CalARP | HSC § 25531 et seq. |
| Stormwater Tier II (SWPPP) | SWPPP-II | (when required by permit) |

All electronic reporting for HMBP, APSA, UST, CalARP, and hazardous waste generator elements is submitted through **CERS (California Environmental Reporting System)** at cers.calepa.ca.gov.

## State Surcharges (Statewide — FY 2025/2026, apply to all CUPAs including San Benito County)

Per CalEPA, effective August 22, 2025:
- **Oversight surcharge:** $79.00 per regulated business/facility per year
- **UST program surcharge:** $20.00 per tank per year
- **CalARP surcharge:** $370.00 per regulated business per year
- **APSA surcharge:** $26.00 per tank facility per year

**Note:** San Benito County Environmental Health sets additional local permit/inspection fees. No local fee schedule was publicly posted online as of 2026-06-03. Contact the Environmental Health Division at (831) 636-4035 for current local fee schedule.

## Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) Thresholds

Businesses must file an HMBP through CERS if they handle hazardous materials above these state thresholds:
- **Any hazardous material:** ≥ 55 gallons (liquid), ≥ 500 pounds (solid), ≥ 200 cubic feet (gas) at any one time
- **Acutely hazardous material:** ≥ applicable Cal/OSHA PSM threshold
- **Extremely hazardous substance (EHS):** ≥ 500 lbs or TPQ, whichever is less

(Thresholds per Health & Safety Code § 25507(a); San Benito County has not adopted lower local thresholds as of 2026-06-03 — verify with CUPA.)

## Local Ordinance / Municipal Code

No local San Benito County hazardous materials ordinance that modifies or supplements state CUPA program requirements was identified in a publicly accessible online source as of 2026-06-03. San Benito County's primary code for environmental health fees is Title 11 of the San Benito County Code of Ordinances (§ 11.12.019 — Fees), though the CUPA-specific fee schedule addendum is not published online. Contact the CUPA directly.

## Notes

- The CUPA also provides forms for: Underground Storage Tank removal/closure, temporary closure, upgrade/repair; Aboveground Storage Tank removal/closure.
- The CUPA is the Lead Agency for CalARP Risk Management Plan reviews for facilities in the county.
- Businesses that are BOTH a hazardous waste generator AND a HMBP facility file unified program elements in a single CERS submission.
