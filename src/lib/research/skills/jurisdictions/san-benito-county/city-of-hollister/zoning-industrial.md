## Industrial Zone Districts

Hollister has **two primary industrial/manufacturing zoning districts** plus an Airport Support district:

### M1 — Light Industrial
- **Purpose:** Low-intensity research and development, warehousing, and manufacturing activities, including production, processing, assembly, packaging, or treatment of products from previously processed materials or finished products from previously prepared materials.
- **Outdoor activities:** Limited to accessory storage and loading areas.
- **Heavy industrial uses:** May be allowed in M1 with a **Conditional Use Permit (CUP)**.
- **Location:** Industrial uses concentrated in the northern portion of the city, immediately south of Hollister Municipal Airport.

### IBP — Industrial Business Park
- **Purpose:** Manufacturing, light industrial, and office uses generally characterized by well-designed, master-planned developments with high standards of architecture, landscaping, and options for outdoor fitness for employees.
- **Outdoor storage:** Limited needs for outdoor storage and other outdoor activities.
- **Character:** Master-planned; higher design and landscaping standards than M1.

### AS — Airport Support
- **Purpose:** Intended for areas adjacent to and with direct access to Hollister Municipal Airport where the most appropriate private uses are commercial, industrial, and recreational uses that provide support to the airport and are compatible with airport operations.
- **Character:** Well-designed, master-planned developments with high standards of architecture and landscaping.
- **Overlay:** Subject to Airport Overlay Zone District (ASZ) regulations protecting persons and property in the airport vicinity.

### NG — North Gateway (Commercial with Industrial Flexibility)
- Long-term mobile food vendor operations and certain industrial/manufacturing uses may also locate in NG district (confirmed by Planning FAQ re: mobile food operations).

## General Plan Industrial Land Use Designations (informational context)

Per Hollister 2040 General Plan Update (2020 baseline):
- **Industrial (General Plan):** ~1,774 acres (14.2 % of study area); provides for business/research parks, corporate establishments, offices, and industrial complexes; examples include computer software, research labs, printing, warehousing, equipment manufacturing, trucking. Also allows limited commercial uses serving industrial/employment centers.
- **Airport Support (General Plan):** ~202 acres; industrial/commercial/recreational uses with direct airport access.
- **Airport (General Plan):** ~374 acres; publicly owned airport lands.

## Permitted vs. Conditional Uses in Industrial Zones

Per § 17.10.020 (Title 17 Chapter 17.10):
- **M1 permitted by right:** Research and development, warehousing, light manufacturing, processing/assembly/packaging/treatment of products from previously prepared materials, accessory outdoor storage and loading.
- **M1 conditional use permit required:** Heavy industrial uses (confirmed by JURISDICTION.md and Hollister 2040 baseline).
- **IBP permitted by right:** Manufacturing, light industrial, office uses consistent with master-planned development standards.
- Full use table (Table 17.10-1) at § 17.10.020 is on Municode but blocked by JavaScript rendering — verify interactively or contact Planning Division for printed copy.

## Overlay Districts Affecting Industrial Areas

- **Airport Overlay Zone District (ASZ):** Applies to properties in the vicinity of Hollister Municipal Airport. Regulates height, land uses, and safety hazards to aircraft. M1/IBP/AS zones near the airport are subject to ASZ restrictions.
- **Earthquake Hazard Overlay Zone (EZ):** Applies where earthquake fault zones have been delineated by the State Geologist. Industrial facilities in EZ areas have additional development requirements.

## CUP Process

- **Authority:** Hollister Planning Commission
- **When required:** Conditional Use Permits for heavy industrial uses in M1, and for certain uses in other districts. Also required for some home occupation types and temporary uses.
- **Application submittals:** In-person at Planning Division (appointment strongly recommended)
  - Address: 339 Fifth Street, Hollister, CA 95023 (operational address for walk-in; mailing address is 375 Fifth Street)
  - Hours: Monday – Friday, 8:30 AM – 12:00 PM and 1:00 PM – 4:30 PM
  - Phone: (831) 636-4360
  - Email: planning@hollister.ca.gov
- **Hearing body:** Planning Commission meets at **6:00 PM on the fourth Thursday of each month**; exceptions — no meetings in July; third Thursday in November and December.
- **Agendas/minutes:** https://pub-hollister.escribemeetings.com/
- **Fee schedule:** "Building Division 2025 Fee Schedule" (PDF) is available from the Development Services page. Separate planning application fee schedule not publicly posted online as of 2026-06-03 — contact Planning Division for CUP application fees.
- **Zoning confirmation:** To confirm the zoning designation of a specific parcel, use San Benito County WebGIS or contact Planning Division.
