## Adopted Code Edition

- **Adopted edition:** 2022 California Fire Code (CFC), Title 24 Part 9
- **Adopting ordinance:** Ordinance 1224 (also adopts all 2022 California Building Standards Code parts)
- **Codified at:** Hollister Municipal Code Title 15, § 15.04.050 — Construction Codes Adopted by Reference
- **Local amendments document:** "2022 Hollister Fire Code Amendments — FINAL VERSION 10/27/2022" (Exhibit A), presented jointly by the Monterey County Fire Prevention Officers Association and the San Benito County Fire Prevention Officers
- **AHJ:** Hollister Fire Department, Fire Prevention Bureau, 375 Fifth Street, Hollister, CA 95023; (831) 636-4325 Option 1

## Local Title of Code

§ 101.1 — "These regulations shall be known as the Fire Code for the City of Hollister."

## Appendices

§ 101.2.1 — Provisions in **all appendices** to the 2022 California Fire Code are adopted in their entirety and shall apply.

## Construction & Design — Alterations/Repairs Trigger (§ 102.1)

Existing structures undergoing alterations or repairs that result in the removal, alteration, modification, replacement, or repair of **≥ 50 % of the external walls** OR **≥ 50 % of the internal structural/non-structural framework** (independently or in combination, within a five-year period) must be brought into full compliance with current provisions.

## Police Powers (§ 104.1.1)

Fire code official and deputies have powers of police officers. Fire chief may request police officer assistance for enforcement.

## Operational Permit — Agricultural Explosive Devices (§ 105.5.0)

An **operational permit is required** for storage or use of any agricultural explosive device, including "bird bombs."

## Violation Penalties (§ 112.4)

Violations are guilty of an **infraction**, punishable by a fine in conformance with the City of Hollister penalty schedule. Continuing work after a stop work order is also an infraction (§ 113.4).

## Fire Apparatus Access Roads (§ 503)

- **Maximum grade:** 15 % unless specifically approved by Fire Code Official (§ 503.2.7)
- **Paving — grades > 8 %:** minimum 0.17 ft asphaltic concrete on 0.34 ft aggregate base (§ 503.2.7.1)
- **Paving — grades > 15 %:** perpendicularly grooved concrete required (§ 503.2.7.1)
- Road names must be issued or approved by the appropriate governmental agency (§ 503.7)

## Private Bridges (§ 503.2.6)

- Designed for AASHTO **HS-20 loading** minimum (§ 503.2.6.1 item 1)
- Unobstructed vertical clearance: ≥ **15 ft** clear (item 2)
- Width: ≥ **20 ft** clear (minimum 12 ft for Group U or R-3) (item 3)
- Maximum approach grade change: ≤ **8 %** for ≥ 10 ft approach distance (item 4)
- Certification by licensed PE required at construction; **recertification every 10 years** (§ 503.2.6.2)

## Key Box (§ 506.1)

KNOX Box Security Systems are the approved key-box system. Required where secured openings restrict emergency access.

## Fire Hydrants (§ 507.5.2)

When required by Fire Code Official, hydrants shall be painted per the most current edition of NFPA 291.

## Spark Arresters (§ 605.3.1)

Required on all chimneys, incinerators, smokestacks using solid fuel. Openings: minimum 3/8 inch, maximum 1/2 inch per CBC § 2113.9.2(3) and CRC § 1003.9.2.

## Plant Processing / Cannabis Extraction Facilities (§ 901.11)

Applies to Chapter 39 occupancies (plant post-harvest operations and indoor cultivation):
- **Fire sprinklers:** All buildings/portions housing plant processing or indoor cultivation — designed to Ordinary Group II per NFPA 13. Group H occupancies may require Extra Hazard design. Buildings < 500 sq ft may be exempted by Fire Code Official.
- **Fire alarm:** Full system with sprinkler monitoring and complete occupant notification per NFPA 72.
- **Special hazard systems:** UL-listed dry chemical protection for extraction booths with volatile solvents.

## Fire Protection System Records (§ 901.6.5)

All documentation from scheduled inspections or tests of fire protection systems must be forwarded to the Fire Code Official within **15 calendar days** of the inspection or test.

## Permit Types & Fees

- Burn permits: backyard and agricultural (seasonal December 1 – April 30, weather permitting)
- Fire plan review permit; fire inspection permit
- Fee schedule available as downloadable PDF from Fire Department website; contact Station 1 at (831) 636-4325
- Online permit applications listed as "coming soon" as of 2025

## Notes

- No hazmat quantity thresholds or high-piled storage thresholds were published in the local amendments document. Hazmat permit thresholds default to base 2022 CFC Chapter 27/50 tables. Confirm with Fire Prevention Bureau for any locally adopted quantity modifications.
- Hollister Fire also provides fire/EMS services by contract to City of San Juan Bautista and unincorporated San Benito County — it is the structural fire AHJ but NOT the CUPA (hazmat programs administered by San Benito County Environmental Health).
