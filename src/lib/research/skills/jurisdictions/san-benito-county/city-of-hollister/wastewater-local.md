## POTW / Sewer Agency

**City of Hollister Utilities Division** — owns and operates two distinct treatment systems:

### 1. Hollister Regional Domestic Wastewater Treatment Plant (RDWWTP)
- Treats domestic, commercial, and industrial wastewater from city and surrounding areas
- Operated under contract by **Veolia**; phone (831) 637-7100 / fax (831) 637-7177
- Produces Title 22 reclaimed water for park irrigation, airport landscaping, and groundwater recharge
- Service area: ~108 miles of wastewater collection, interceptor, and force main lines; 6 lift stations

### 2. Hollister Industrial Wastewater Treatment Plant (IWTP)
- Seasonal operation (July – September/October) treating tomato cannery process wastewater
- Operated in coordination with **Sunnyslope County Water District**, 3570 Airline Hwy, Hollister; (831) 637-4670
- Served by a combined storm/sewer conveyance line (18"–30" diameter) repurposed each season
- During wet season (winter/spring) the combined line diverts to storm water outfall on San Benito River
- One industrial customer (San Benito Foods tomato processing plant at Hawkins and East Street)

## Municipal Code — Sewer Legal Authority

**Hollister Municipal Code Title 13.04 — Sewer Service System (Articles I–IX)**
is the City's primary sewer-use ordinance governing the collection system.

Per the City's 2022 Sewer System Management Plan (SSMP Rev. 2, September 2022, WDID 3SSO11408):

## Industrial Pretreatment Program

**Status:** The City operates one industrial customer (the seasonal tomato cannery) via a dedicated Industrial Wastewater Treatment Plant. **No formal EPA-approved pretreatment program (40 CFR Part 403)** has been identified for the RDWWTP; the City's pretreatment authority is exercised primarily through Title 13.04 discharge prohibitions and the combined industrial sewer line management.

Per the 2022 SSMP, the city is also "in the process of developing a Fats, Oils and Grease (FOG) Control Program":
- § 13.04.100: Director may prohibit specific materials from discharge
- § 13.04.120: Grease traps required (food service establishments)
- FOG Program includes: public education, grease hauler contracts, inspection schedule, legal authority under Title 13.04 discharge prohibition

**Industrial Discharge Permit:** No separate Industrial Wastewater Permit (IWP) process or online portal was identified as publicly available as of 2026-06-03. Contact Wastewater Treatment Division at (831) 636-4340 for industrial discharge requirements.

## Local Discharge Limits

No locally published numeric discharge limit table (beyond state categorical pretreatment standards in 40 CFR Subchapter N) was identified online as of 2026-06-03. The 2022 SSMP references compliance with:
- State Water Resources Control Board WDR Order No. 2006-0003-DWQ (statewide SSO/collection system requirements)
- Amended MRP Orders WQ 2008-0002-EXEC and WQ 2013-0058-EXEC
- NPDES permit conditions for the RDWWTP (Central Coast RWQCB, Region 3)

For categorical industrial user (CIU) discharge limits, applicable federal standards in 40 CFR Chapter I, Subchapter N apply. Verify any local limits directly with the Engineering/Wastewater Division.
