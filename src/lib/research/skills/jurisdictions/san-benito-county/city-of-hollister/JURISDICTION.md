## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **CUPA agency:** San Benito County Health Department, Environmental Health Division — Hollister is NOT its own CUPA; the county agency serves the entire county including the city.
- **Address:** 1111 San Felipe Road, Suite 102, Hollister, CA 95023
- **Phone:** (831) 636-4035 | **Email:** sandrade@cosb.us
- Programs administered: HazMat Business Plans (HMBP), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), Hazardous Waste Generator, UST, CalARP, SWPPP Tier II.
- CUPA directory ID 1052 in CERS (CalEPA unified reporting portal): businesses file HMBP and other unified program elements at cers.calepa.ca.gov.
- Source: https://cersapps.calepa.ca.gov/Public/Directory/RegulatorDetails/1052

### 2. Fire AHJ — Hollister Fire Department

- **AHJ:** Hollister Fire Department, Fire Prevention Bureau
- **Address (Station 1/Prevention):** 110 Fifth Street, Hollister, CA 95023 (permits/inspections); Prevention Bureau admin at 375 Fifth Street, Hollister, CA 95023
- **Phone:** (831) 636-4325 Option 1 | General: (831) 636-4300
- **Adopted code:** 2022 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9) — adopted by reference via Hollister Municipal Code, Ordinance 1224, codified at Title 15. Local amendments finalized 2022-10-27 (jointly presented by Monterey County and San Benito County Fire Prevention Officers Associations).
- **Key local amendments (Exhibit A, Final 10/27/2022):**
  - All CFC appendices adopted in their entirety (§ 101.2.1).
  - Alterations/repairs triggering ≥ 50 % wall or structural removal within 5 years require full code compliance (§ 102.1).
  - KNOX Box key-box system required where secured openings restrict access (§ 506.1).
  - Fire apparatus access road grade ≤ 15 %; roads > 8 % must be paved AC on aggregate base; > 15 % must use grooved concrete (§ 503.2.7).
  - Private bridge engineering to AASHTO HS-20, 15 ft vertical clearance, 20 ft width, re-certified every 10 years (§ 503.2.6).
  - Agricultural explosive devices (bird bombs) require operational permit (§ 105.5.0).
  - Violations are infractions per City of Hollister penalty schedule (§ 112.4).
- **Permit types:** Burn permits (agricultural and backyard), fire plan review, fire inspection — online applications listed as "coming soon" as of 2025.
- **NOTE:** Hollister Fire also provides fire/EMS by contract to City of San Juan Bautista and unincorporated San Benito County — it is the structural fire AHJ but NOT the CUPA (hazmat is county Environmental Health).
- Source (local amendments PDF): https://files.hollister.ca.gov/Fire%20Department/Documents/2022%20City%20of%20Hollister%20Fire%20Code%20Amendments%20-%20Exhibit%20A.pdf
- Source (permits page): https://hollister.ca.gov/government/fire/permits_and_fees.php
- Source (fire prevention bureau): https://hollister.ca.gov/government/fire/fire_prevention_bureau/index.php
