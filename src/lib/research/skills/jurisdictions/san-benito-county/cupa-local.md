## Agency

- **CUPA name:** San Benito County Health and Human Services Agency (HHSA), Environmental Health Division
- **CUPA type:** Sole countywide CUPA — San Benito County is its own CUPA. Neither Hollister nor San Juan Bautista operates a separate city CUPA; both are served by the county agency.

## Programs Administered

The San Benito County CUPA administers all six Unified Program elements:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP)** — reported via CERS (Centralized Electronic Reporting System at cers.calepa.ca.gov)
2. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — Permit applications available for removal/closure, temporary closure, upgrade/repair
3. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — Aboveground storage tank removal/closure permits
4. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — Process safety management for acutely hazardous materials
5. **Hazardous Waste Generator** — Oversight of hazardous waste generators (small and large quantity)
6. **Hazardous Waste On-Site Treatment** — Tiered permits for on-site treatment

## Local Ordinance / Fee Authority

- **Fee ordinance:** San Benito County Code of Ordinances, § 5.01.091 (Environmental Health Division fees within the General Fee Ordinance, Chapter 5.01, Article III, Subarticle V). Fees are set by the Board of Supervisors.
- **Annual CPI adjustment:** Fees may be adjusted annually each July 1 by the Board of Supervisors up to the percentage change in the CPI for the San Francisco–Oakland–San Jose area, not to exceed 3 % per year.
- **Specific local CUPA hazmat ordinance chapter:** No separate local CUPA hazardous-materials ordinance chapter was found published online. The county relies on state law (Health & Safety Code §§ 25500 et seq. for HMBP; §§ 25281 et seq. for UST; § 25531 for CalARP) as incorporated into the CUPA framework. Contact HHSA Environmental Health for the current fee schedule.
- **Fee schedule dollar amounts:** Not published online as of 2026-06-03; contact HHSA at (831) 636-4035 or request via public records.

## CERS Reporting

All HMBP submissions, hazardous waste generator reports, and CalARP reports are filed electronically through CERS: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov

## Notes

- San Benito County is a small CUPA jurisdiction with two incorporated cities (Hollister and San Juan Bautista). The majority of regulated industrial facilities are in or adjacent to Hollister city limits.
- There is no published local CUPA ordinance that modifies state program thresholds — the state Health & Safety Code thresholds and reporting quantities govern.
