## Zoning Authority

- **Unincorporated county:** San Benito County Resource Management Agency (RMA), Planning Division.
  - Contact: (831) 637-5313
  - Website: https://www.sanbenitocountyca.gov/departments/resource-management-agency/building-planning/ordinances
- **City of Hollister (within city limits):** City of Hollister Development Services Department.

## Zoning Code Structure

- **Code:** San Benito County Code of Ordinances, **Title 25 (Zoning)** (American Legal Publishing / amlegal.com).
- **Current edition:** Comprehensively updated in 2022 (adopted by Board of Supervisors following Planning Commission recommendation April 27, 2022; prior comprehensive update was 1984). The 2022 update reorganized the code, added use tables, and simplified the list of industrial uses into general categories ("Light Industrial," "Heavy Industrial").
- **Zoning map:** https://opendata2-cosb.opendata.arcgis.com/datasets/COSB::county-of-san-benito-zoning-1/explore
- **Chapter 25.03:** Zoning Map and Zoning Districts (§ 25.03.003 — establishment and designation; § 25.03.007 — industrial districts).
- **Chapter 25.08:** Development standards for specific uses.
- **Chapter 25.07.003:** Non-residential design standards — applies to all new non-residential structures in C-1, C-2, BP, M-1, M-2, RRP, and PQP zones.

## Industrial Zone Districts (§ 25.03.007)

Three industrial base zones exist in San Benito County (unincorporated areas):

### BP — Business Park District
High-quality industrial uses in a park-like setting designed to be compatible with surrounding residential and institutional uses. Emphasizes clean, light industry with controlled design standards.

### M-1 — Light Industrial District
Light industrial uses near transportation systems. Permitted uses include (but are not limited to):
- Warehouses and distribution
- Contractor yards
- Nurseries and lumber yards
- Auto repair
- Light manufacturing and assembly
- Research and development

Heavier industrial uses may be conditionally permitted in M-1 with a Conditional Use Permit (CUP). Development standards in Chapter 25.08.

### M-2 — General / Heavy Industrial District
Heavier and more intensive industrial uses than M-1. The 2022 code update simplified the use list to more general categories. Uses incompatible with M-1 (due to noise, odor, traffic, or hazard potential) are the target of M-2 designation.

## Use Classification in Industrial Zones (§ 25.03.007(E))

Uses are designated as one of four categories in the BP/M-1/M-2 use table:
- **P — Permitted:** By-right, subject to building permit and development standards.
- **AP — Administratively Permitted:** Staff-level approval without public hearing.
- **CUP — Conditionally Permitted:** Requires Conditional Use Permit (public hearing before Planning Commission or RMA).
- **Prohibited:** Not allowed in that zone.

Note: The complete use table is in § 25.03.007(E) of the amlegal code. Industrial uses added via the 2022 update are organized as "Light Industrial" and "Heavy Industrial" general categories rather than exhaustive lists.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process (§ 25.02.003)

- **Applying authority:** RMA Planning Division (unincorporated county).
- **Decision maker:** Planning Commission (public hearing required).
- **Required findings:**
  1. The proposed use is conditionally permitted within the applicable zone and complies with all other applicable provisions of the county code, general plan, and any applicable specific plan.
  2. The use will not be adverse to public health, safety, or general welfare.
  3. The use complies with applicable design or development standards.
  4. The site is physically suitable for the proposed use.
- **Application:** Submit through RMA Planning Division. Forms available at: https://www.sanbenitocountyca.gov/departments/resource-management-agency/building-planning/forms-and-applications
- **Fee:** Set by the Current Planning Division Fee Schedule (last noted update: August 21, 2018; re-check current schedule with RMA). Fees are established under San Benito County General Fee Ordinance § 5.01.091.
- **Environmental review:** CEQA review required; categorical exemption or Negative Declaration depending on project scope.
- **Online submittal:** https://www.sanbenitocountyca.gov/departments/resource-management-agency/building-and-code-enforcement-division/building-services-forms-and-applications/online-submittal

## Administrative Use Permit (§ 25.02.002)

Lower-intensity conditionally permitted uses may qualify for an Administrative Use Permit (staff approval, no public hearing). Confirm with RMA whether a specific industrial use qualifies.

## Notes / Gotchas

- The 2022 zoning code update (implemented post-2022 Planning Commission recommendation) is the current controlling document. Confirm the codified version in amlegal reflects the adopted text.
- San Felipe Road corridor has seen zoning dispute activity (cannabis → business park → builder's remedy discussions as of 2025); verify current zoning status for any parcel on that corridor.
- City of Hollister has its own zoning code (Title 17 of the Hollister Municipal Code, available on Municode at library.municode.com/ca/hollister); industrial land within city limits is governed by Hollister's code, not Title 25.
