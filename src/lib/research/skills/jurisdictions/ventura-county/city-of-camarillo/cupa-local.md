> Camarillo is **not its own CUPA**. All six CalEPA unified programs for businesses within Camarillo city limits are administered by the **Ventura County Resource Management Agency (RMA), Environmental Health Division CUPA**. Only the City of Oxnard and City of Ventura (as a Participating Agency) have separate CUPA arrangements within Ventura County.

## Programs Administered for Camarillo Facilities

The Ventura County CUPA implements all six CalEPA unified programs for facilities in Camarillo:

| # | Program | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) | Filed via CERS; Ventura County RMA reviews and inspects |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator Program | Generator ID, manifest compliance, inspection |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tanks (UST) | Installation, operation, modification, closure, leak response |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) | SPCC plan for above-threshold petroleum storage |
| 5 | California Accidental Release Prevention Program (CalARP) | RMP review; county RMA is local implementing agency |
| 6 | California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) | State filing system; county CUPA is reviewing agency |

## HMBP Filing (CERS)

- **Submission system:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business
- **Who must comply:** Any business storing, using, or handling hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities (generally 55 gallons liquid, 500 lbs solid, 200 cubic feet gas — per Health & Safety Code § 25507)
- **Initial filing:** Within 30 days of bringing threshold materials onsite
- **Annual review and resubmission:** Required every year
- **Update triggers:** Material quantities increase ≥ 100%; new previously-undisclosed materials; address/ownership/name changes; significant operational changes
- **Reviewing agency for Camarillo:** Ventura County RMA Environmental Health CUPA — not Oxnard Fire, not City of Camarillo

## UST Program

- Ventura County RMA administers UST permitting for Camarillo facilities
- Permit required for UST installation, modification, repair, and closure
- Unauthorized releases: verbal report within 24 hours, written report within applicable state timeline
- [VERIFY] Contact Ventura County RMA at (805) 654-2494 for current UST application forms and inspection scheduling requirements

## CalARP / RMP

- Ventura County RMA Environmental Health is the Local Implementing Agency (LIA) for CalARP within Camarillo
- Facilities with regulated substances above threshold quantities must submit RMP to USEPA
- County RMA conducts Program 2/3 inspections and coordinates with OES for worst-case scenario planning

## CUPA vs. Fire Code Hazmat Permits

Two parallel programs apply to many Camarillo industrial facilities:
1. **CUPA/HMBP** (via CERS, administered by Ventura County RMA) — covers hazardous materials inventory and emergency response planning
2. **CFC Hazmat Operational Permit** (via VCFD Fire Prevention Bureau, Form 610) — covers fire code operational permits for hazmat storage and use

These are separate requirements administered by separate agencies. See fire-code-local.md for the VCFD fire permit process.
