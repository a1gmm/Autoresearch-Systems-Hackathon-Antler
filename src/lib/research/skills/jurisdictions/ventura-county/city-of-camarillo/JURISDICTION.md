## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA (Hazardous Materials Unified Program)

- **Not its own CUPA.** Camarillo is served by the **Ventura County Resource Management Agency (RMA) Environmental Health Division CUPA**. Only Oxnard and City of Ventura operate separate programs within the county.
- Programs covered: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste/Tiered Permits, Underground Storage Tanks, CalARP, Aboveground Petroleum Storage (APSA), California Environmental Reporting System (CERS).
- **CUPA Manager:** Rick Bandelin — (805) 654-2435 — Rick.Bandelin@venturacounty.gov
- **Main office:** 800 S. Victoria Ave., Ventura, CA 93009 — (805) 654-2494

### 2. Fire Code (AHJ: Ventura County Fire Department)

- **AHJ:** Ventura County Fire Department (VCFD) — not a city fire department. Camarillo stations: Station 50 (189 S. Las Posas Rd.), Station 52 (5353 Santa Rosa Rd.), Station 54 (2160 Pickwick Dr.), Station 55 (403 Valley Vista Dr.).
- **Adopted code:** 2022 California Fire Code (CFC) with local amendments — adopted by VCFD via **Ordinance No. 32**, which also adopts Appendix O, portions of the 2021 International Fire Code, and portions of Title 19 CCR. Incorporates prior amendments in Ordinances 29–31.
- **Local amendments** include more restrictive standards under CFC Sections 507 (water supply) and 903 (sprinklers), authorized by Health & Safety Code § 13869.7 (local climatic/topographic conditions).
- **Fire Prevention counter:** (805) 389-9738 — fireprevention@venturacounty.gov — Mon–Fri 7:30 AM–5:00 PM
- **Hazmat permits:** All buildings/equipment used for storage, transfer, or application of hazardous materials require fire code permit (Form 610 — Fire Permit Application).
- **High-piled combustible storage permit:** Separate supplemental form required (FP13.5.6-HPS-Permit-Info.pdf) submitted with Form 610.
- **Permit applications:** https://vcfd.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/610-Fire-Permit-Application.pdf
- **High-piled form:** https://vcfd.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/FP13.5.6-HPS-Permit-Info.pdf
- **Fee schedule (FY 2023-2024):** https://vcfd.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/FY2023-2024-Fee-Schedule.pdf — [VERIFY — confirm current FY fee schedule at vcfd.org/ordinances-and-fees/]

### 3. Industrial Wastewater / Pretreatment

- **POTW:** Camarillo Sanitary District (CSD) — treats ~3.5 million gallons/day to tertiary level. CSD is a separate district administered by the City of Camarillo Public Works Department.
- **Governing ordinance: CSD Ordinance No. 96** — adopted June 14, 2023; effective July 14, 2023. Restated Industrial Waste Supplement to the CSD Operations Code. Establishes: discharge quality/quantity standards, pretreatment requirements, industrial wastewater discharge permit (IWP) issuance, and fees/charges.
- **Supplemental documents:** CSD Operations Code (governing rules) and CSD Sewer Standards (2015) — available at the CSD page.
- **Fats, Oils & Grease (FOG) Program:** FOG Source Control Program applies to food service establishments.
- **Source Control Inspector:** Kayla Corcoran — kcorcoran@cityofcamarillo.org — (805) 383-5663
- **CSD general contact:** 601 Carmen Dr., Camarillo, CA 93010 — (805) 388-5300

### 4. Stormwater (MS4)

- **Local ordinance:** Camarillo Municipal Code **Chapter 9.32 — Stormwater Quality** (Title 9, Health & Safety). Establishes local discharge prohibitions and BMPs for stormwater from development and industrial/commercial sites.
- **MS4 permit:** Camarillo participates in the Ventura County MS4 NPDES permit program administered by the Regional Water Quality Control Board (Los Angeles Regional Board covers southern Ventura County).
- **Contact for stormwater compliance:** City of Camarillo Public Works — 601 Carmen Dr. — (805) 388-5300

### 5. Zoning / Land Use (Industrial Districts)

- **Zoning code:** Camarillo Municipal Code **Title 19 — Zoning** (administered by Community Development Department).
- **Industrial zoning districts:**
  - **L-M — Limited Manufacturing Zone** (Ch. 19.28): lightest industrial; some uses require CUP (§ 19.28.040); radiation/hazardous equipment regulations at § 19.28.300.
  - **M-1 — Light Manufacturing Zone** (Ch. 19.30): general light industrial uses permitted by right or with CUP.
  - **M-2 — General Manufacturing Zone** (Ch. 19.32): heavier manufacturing; broadest permitted uses.
- **CUP process:** Some industrial/hazmat-adjacent uses require a Conditional Use Permit filed with the Community Development Department. Planning commission review applies.
- **Online permitting:** City uses OpenGov for digital plan check and permits (Building & Safety is digital-only as of 2025-07-01).
- **Planning/zoning contact:** Community Development Department — planning@cityofcamarillo.org — (805) 388-5360 — 601 Carmen Dr., Camarillo, CA 93010
