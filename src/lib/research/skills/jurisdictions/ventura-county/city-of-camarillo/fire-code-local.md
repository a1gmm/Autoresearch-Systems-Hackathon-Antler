> Camarillo has no independent fire department or fire AHJ. All fire code authority — including plan review, operational permits, and hazmat permits — is held by the **Ventura County Fire Protection District (VCFD)**, which serves Camarillo through four local stations.

## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

- **AHJ:** Ventura County Fire Protection District (VCFD) — not a city fire department
- **Headquarters:** 2400 Conejo Spectrum Street, Thousand Oaks, CA 91320

## Adopted Fire Code

- **Ordinance:** Ventura County Fire Protection District **Ordinance No. 32**
- **Adopted code:** 2022 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9) together with:
  - Appendix O
  - Specified portions of the 2021 International Fire Code (not adopted by the State Fire Marshal, except portions deleted per Table 1.4.3)
  - Specified portions of Title 19 CCR (State Fire Marshal regulations)
  - Ventura County Fire Protection District Ordinance No. 29 (Ventura County Fire Apparatus Access Code)
- **Repeals:** Ordinance No. 31 (2019 CFC cycle) in its entirety
- **Effective date:** January 1, 2023 (effective 30 days from final passage or January 1, 2023, whichever is later — per Article 4 of Ordinance 32)
- **Authority:** California Health & Safety Code §§ 13869, 13869.7, 17958.7, 18941.5

> [VERIFY] Whether the 2025 California Fire Code (effective January 1, 2026) has been locally adopted by VCFD via a new ordinance superseding Ordinance 32. Check https://fire.venturacounty.gov/Ordinances-and-fees/ for any Ordinance No. 33.

## Local Amendments (Ordinance 32, Article 3)

VCFD has established **more restrictive standards** than the state baseline, authorized by Health & Safety Code § 13869.7 due to local climatic, topographic, and geological conditions. Key local amendments in Ordinance 32:

- **CFC Section 507 (Fire Water Supply):** More restrictive standards for water supply to fire suppression systems.
- **CFC Section 903 (Automatic Sprinkler Systems):** More restrictive standards for sprinkler coverage requirements.
- Prior amendments from Ordinances 29–31 are incorporated by reference where not superseded.

> [VERIFY] For the full text of Section 507 and 903 local amendments, obtain the complete Ordinance 32 body (Article 3 amendments) from VCFD. The draft adopting ordinance PDF (Exhibit 2) contains the amendment framework; the full amendment text is in the adopted body.

## Permit Requirements and Application Process

**Forms required:**
- **Form 610** — Fire Permit Application (required for all fire code operational permits, including hazmat storage/use, sprinkler systems, fire alarms, and other permit-required activities)
  - PDF: https://vcfd.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/610-Fire-Permit-Application.pdf
- **Form 625** — Fire Flow verification
- **Form 611** — Inspection Request
- **High-Piled Combustible Storage supplement** — FP13.5.6-HPS-Permit-Info.pdf required alongside Form 610 for high-piled storage permits
  - PDF: https://vcfd.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/FP13.5.6-HPS-Permit-Info.pdf

**Plan submission:** Plans submitted to fireprevention@venturacounty.gov

**Plan check categories:**
1. New structures or additions (fire flow verification required)
2. State Fire Marshal–regulated occupancies (Groups A, E, I, R, H, L) — full construction drawings required
3. Fire protection systems (sprinklers, alarms, cooking suppression hoods, private water systems)
4. Landscape and fuel modification zones (wildfire interface)

**Fee payment:** Online via Ventura County Citizen Access portal — https://vcca.venturacounty.gov/landing.html — or by mail.

**Fee schedule:** https://vcfd.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/FY2023-2024-Fee-Schedule.pdf — [VERIFY — confirm current FY fee schedule at https://fire.venturacounty.gov/Ordinances-and-fees/]

## Hazardous Materials Operational Permits

Buildings and equipment used for storage, transfer, or application of hazardous materials above CFC permit threshold quantities require a fire code operational permit from VCFD Fire Prevention Bureau, submitted via Form 610.

Hazmat permit coordination also involves the Ventura County CUPA (see cupa-local.md) for the HMBP/CERS component — these are parallel requirements.
