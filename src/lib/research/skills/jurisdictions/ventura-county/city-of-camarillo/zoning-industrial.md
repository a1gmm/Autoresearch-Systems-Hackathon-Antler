> Industrial land use in Camarillo is regulated under **Title 19 (Zoning)** of the Camarillo Municipal Code, administered by the Community Development Department. Three industrial zoning districts exist with graduated intensity. Some industrial/hazmat-adjacent uses require a Conditional Use Permit (CUP).

## Governing Authority

- **Zoning code:** Camarillo Municipal Code **Title 19 — Zoning**
- **Adopted:** 1976; periodically amended by City Council ordinance

## Industrial Zoning Districts

Camarillo zoning designates three industrial districts within the broader five land-use categories (Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Agricultural, Open Space):

### L-M — Limited Manufacturing Zone (Chapter 19.28)

- **Character:** Lightest industrial classification; buffer between commercial and heavier manufacturing
- **Permitted uses:** Light industrial, warehousing, research and development; uses listed as permitted by right vs. CUP-required are specified in § 19.28.040
- **CUP threshold:** Some uses in L-M require a Conditional Use Permit — see § 19.28.040 (Uses Permitted)
- **Special regulations:** Radiation and hazardous equipment regulations at § 19.28.300
- **Municode:** https://library.municode.com/ca/camarillo/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT19ZO_IIZO_CH19.28LIMAZO_19.28.040USPE

### M-1 — Light Manufacturing Zone (Chapter 19.30)

- **Character:** General light industrial uses; broader scope than L-M
- **Permitted uses:** General light manufacturing, assembly, processing; some uses permitted by right, others require CUP
- **Municode:** https://library.municode.com/ca/camarillo/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT19ZO_IIZO_CH19.30LIMAZO

### M-2 — General Manufacturing Zone (Chapter 19.32)

- **Character:** Heaviest industrial classification; broadest range of permitted manufacturing uses
- **Permitted uses:** Heavier manufacturing, fabrication, industrial processing; most permissive industrial district in Camarillo
- **Municode:** https://library.municode.com/ca/camarillo/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT19ZO_IIZO_CH19.32GEMAZO

> [VERIFY] Specific uses permitted by right vs. by CUP in M-1 and M-2 require direct review of §§ 19.30.040 and 19.32.040 respectively — Municode returned 403 on direct fetch. Contact Planning Division for the current use matrix or request a zoning verification letter.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

- Some industrial and hazmat-adjacent uses require a CUP filed with the Community Development Department
- Planning Commission review applies for CUP applications
- [VERIFY] CUP application form and fee schedule — Community Development fees were updated effective July 1, 2025, adding two surcharges:
  - General Plan Maintenance Surcharge: 17.89% on base fees (Exhibits A and F)
  - Technology Surcharge: 11.34% on fees in Exhibits A, B, and F
- Full Master User Fee Schedule: available from the Finance Department accounting services page at cityofcamarillo.org

## Digital Permitting

- City uses **OpenGov** for digital plan check and building permits
- Building & Safety transitioned to digital-only as of 2025-07-01
- Planning applications (including CUPs) are submitted through the Community Development Department

## EHS-Relevant Zoning Notes

- L-M zone has explicit provisions regulating radiation and hazardous equipment (§ 19.28.300) — relevant for labs, medical device manufacturers, and semiconductor facilities
- Industrial uses involving hazardous materials storage require coordination with VCFD (fire code permit, Form 610) and Ventura County CUPA (HMBP via CERS) in addition to satisfying zoning requirements
- Hazmat-adjacent CUP conditions may include fire protection, secondary containment, or buffer requirements — [VERIFY] with Planning Division for current standard conditions
