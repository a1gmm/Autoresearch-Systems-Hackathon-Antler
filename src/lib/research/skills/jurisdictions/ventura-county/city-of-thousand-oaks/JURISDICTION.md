> Local EHS authority reference for **City of Thousand Oaks**. The city itself is not a CUPA and does not run its own fire AHJ — both are administered at the county level by Ventura County. The city owns its wastewater treatment plant and is the direct IWP/pretreatment authority. Verify adopted code editions before quoting; confirm the 2025 CFC (Ord. 34) effective date (January 1, 2026).

## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials / Hazardous Waste

- **Not its own CUPA.** Served by the **Ventura County Resource Management Agency (RMA), Environmental Health Division — CUPA**.
- Programs administered: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste / Tiered Permitting, Underground Storage Tanks (UST), California Accidental Release Program (CalARP), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Tanks (APSA), and CERS reporting.
- **Reporting portal:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/
- **Agency address:** 800 S. Victoria Ave, Ventura, CA 93009
- **Phone:** (805) 654-2494 | CUPA Manager Rick Bandelin: (805) 654-2815
- **Fees:** See Ventura County Environmental Health fee schedule (link from RMA site; updated annually).
- Source: https://rma.venturacounty.gov/divisions/environmental-health/cupa-certified-unified-program-agency/

### 2. Fire Code — AHJ: Ventura County Fire Protection District (VCFPD)

- **AHJ:** Ventura County Fire Department (VCFD) — Stations 30, 31, 33, 34, 35, 36 serve Thousand Oaks / Newbury Park / Oak Park.
- **Adopted fire code:** Ventura County Fire Code (VCFC) — **Ordinance No. 32** (based on 2019 California Fire Code, effective January 1, 2023). Succeeded by **Ordinance No. 34** (2025 VCFC + Ventura County Wildland-Urban Interface Code, effective **January 1, 2026**).
- **Thousand Oaks city alignment:** City Council voted December 2, 2025 to align local fire ordinance with Ord. 34 / 2025 CFC, including updated Wildland-Urban Interface (Zone Zero / 5-ft perimeter) defensible space rules and hazardous material permit provisions.
- **City municipal code fire chapter:** Thousand Oaks Municipal Code, Title 5, Chapter 6 (Fire Control and Prevention) — https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/thousandoaks/latest/thousandoaks_ca/0-0-0-4791
- **Permit types (VCFD):** Fire Code Permits (general), High-Pile Combustible Storage, Hazardous Materials, Special Events, Recreational Fire. Apply via Ventura County One-Stop Permitting portal.
- **Fire Prevention Counter (Thousand Oaks):** 2400 Conejo Spectrum St, Thousand Oaks, CA 91320 | Phone: (805) 389-9738 | Inspections: (805) 389-9744 | Hours: 7:30 a.m.–5:00 p.m.
- **Fee schedule:** VCFD FY 2025-2026 Fee Schedule (effective July 1, 2025) — https://fire.venturacounty.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Fee-Schedule-2024-2025-Final.pdf
- **Permit portal:** https://onestoppermits.venturacounty.gov/departments/fire-department
- Source (ordinances/fees): https://fire.venturacounty.gov/Ordinances-and-fees/

### 3. Industrial Wastewater — IWP / Pretreatment

- **POTW:** City of Thousand Oaks — **Hill Canyon Treatment Plant (HCTP)** — city-owned and operated; serves all of Thousand Oaks.
- **Local authority:** City of Thousand Oaks Public Works, Water and Wastewater Division is the pretreatment authority (not the county).
- **Sewer Use Ordinance:** Thousand Oaks Municipal Code, **Title 7, Chapter 1: Wastewater** — https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/thousandoaks/latest/thousandoaks_ca/0-0-0-26233
  - Ordinance consolidated all sewer-use requirements and updated language to meet federal pretreatment requirements.
  - Wastewater discharge permits are tiered based on discharge risk level.
  - Local limits were updated October 2023 (Final Draft Local Limits Report); new limits proposed for ammonia-N, molybdenum, sulfate; increases for selenium/silver/TDS; decreases for arsenic, BOD, Cd, Cl, Cr, Cu, CN, Pb, Hg, Ni, TSS; fluoride, phenols, sulfide, total identifiable chlorinated hydrocarbons removed.
- **IWP/permit process:** Contact Water and Wastewater Division for Industrial Wastewater Pretreatment (IWP) permit application. [VERIFY — online portal/form not found; contact city directly.]
- **Contact:** City of Thousand Oaks Public Works — (805) 449-2100 | 2100 Thousand Oaks Blvd, Thousand Oaks, CA 91362 | Hours: Mon–Thu 7:30 a.m.–5:00 p.m. (alternating Fridays closed)
- Source: https://toaks.gov/index.php?section=environmental&projectsection=5&project=70

### 5. Zoning / Land Use — Industrial Districts

- **Zoning code:** Thousand Oaks Municipal Code, **Title 9: Planning and Zoning** — https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/thousandoaks/latest/thousandoaks_ca/0-0-0-11157

- **Planning contact:** Community Development Department — (805) 449-2100 | toaks.gov
