## Fire AHJ

**Ventura County Fire Protection District (VCFD)** is the AHJ for all unincorporated and member-city areas including Port Hueneme. Port Hueneme is served by VCFD **Battalion 5, Station 53**. The City is NOT a self-administered fire department — VCFD handles all fire prevention, inspections, permits, and enforcement within city limits.

Naval Base Ventura County (NBVC) is a federal enclave served by the **Navy Region SW Federal Fire Department**; VCFD authority does not extend on-base.

## Adopted Fire Code

- **Effective code:** 2025 California Fire Code (CFC), Title 24 Part 9, together with local amendments
- **Adopting ordinance:** VCFPD **Ordinance No. 34** (repeals prior Ordinances 29 and 32)
- **Effective date within Port Hueneme:** January 1, 2026
- **City ratification:** Port Hueneme City Council adopted a Resolution ratifying VCFPD Ord. 34 on **November 17, 2025** (ratifying Article 3 §903, Article 3 Appendix B, Article 4 §604.11 — the sections that impose more restrictive local standards)
  - Source: https://www.ci.port-hueneme.ca.us/DocumentCenter/View/9501/2025-11-17---03-CC---Resolution-Ratifying-VCFPD-Ord-34-Bldg-Standards

## What Ordinance 34 Adopts by Reference

In addition to the 2025 CFC (Title 24 Part 9), Ord. 34 incorporates:
- Appendix Chapters D and P of the 2025 CFC
- Specified portions of the **2024 International Fire Code (IFC)** not already in the CFC (see Table 1.4.3 for deleted IFC sections)
- Specified portions of the **2025 California Wildland-Urban Interface Code** (Title 24 Part 7), including Appendix Chapter A (Chapter 5 excepted)
- Specified portions of the **2024 International Wildland-Urban Interface Code** (see Table 1.4.4 for deleted sections)
- Specified portions of **CCR Title 19, Division 1** (Regulations of the State Fire Marshal)

## Key Local Amendments (Ventura County Fire Code, Article 3)

**Sprinkler requirements (§903) — more restrictive than state baseline:**
- §903.1.2: Concurrent permits — additions/modifications where combined area of all open permits exceeds thresholds trigger full sprinkler retrofit of entire structure
- §903.1.3: Area separation walls — if approved fire-area separation walls (pre-Nov 1, 2002 buildings) are not maintained, affected fire areas must be retrofitted with sprinklers
- §903.1.4: Change of use from Group U Occupancy to any classification that would not have been exempt from sprinklers at original permit date triggers full sprinkler installation
- §903.2 (amended): **Automatic fire sprinkler system required in all new buildings** (with limited exceptions for Group U, small agricultural produce stands ≤400 sq ft, certain picnic/lunch shelters, and S-2 carport structures of noncombustible construction)
- §903.2 item 2: Additions >25% of legally existing floor area (non-R-3 occupancies) require sprinklering entire building; additions to R-3 >1,000 sq ft AND >50% of existing GSF require sprinklers

**Fire apparatus access roads (Appendix D — deleted and replaced):**
- Full local apparatus road standards; minimum 20-ton vehicle capacity after 10-year storm; deviations require Fire Code Official approval

**Fire-flow (Appendix B — amended):**
- §B103.3: Areas without adequate water supply — Fire Code Official may use NFPA 1142 or Ventura County WUI Code
- §B103.4 (added): New buildings/uses requiring water demand beyond what existing purveyor can supply **shall not be permitted**

**Fencing in WUI areas (§604.11 — added):**
- Article 4, §604.11 requires non-combustible fencing/gates in Wildland-Urban Interface areas (this provision was specifically ratified by Port Hueneme City Council)

**Fee delinquency (§108.7 added):**
- 10% penalty per month after 15 days past due (max 50% of original); 1% monthly interest; Fire District may use all legal means to collect

## Permit Types Administered

Fire Prevention Bureau issues permits for:
- New structures/additions (Form 610 — Fire Permit Application)
- State Fire Marshal regulated occupancies (Groups A, E, I, R, H, L)
- Fire protection systems (sprinklers, alarms, cooking hoods, private water systems)
- Landscape and fuel modification plans (defensible space compliance)
- Operational permits (open burning, high-piled combustible storage, hazardous materials storage/use, etc.)

## High-Piled Combustible Storage

Required for storage >12,000 sq ft (Class I–IV commodities) or >2,500 sq ft (high-hazard commodities/plastics). Administered by VCFD Fire Prevention Bureau.
- Standards reference: https://fire.venturacounty.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/14.7.5-High-Piled-Combustible-Storage.pdf
