## CUPA Authority

**Port Hueneme is NOT its own CUPA and does NOT have a Participating Agency (PA) agreement.** Businesses in Port Hueneme are regulated directly by the **Ventura County Resource Management Agency (RMA), Environmental Health Division** acting as the county CUPA.

The only separate in-county programs are:
- **City of Oxnard** — independent CUPA administering all six programs within Oxnard city limits
- **City of Ventura** — Participating Agency (PA) implementing four programs (HMBP, APSA, CalARP, UST)

All other Ventura County cities, including Port Hueneme, fall under the county CUPA directly.

Note: Naval Base Ventura County (NBVC) is a federal enclave; on-base CUPA/hazmat oversight is federal, not county.

## Programs Administered (Six-Program CUPA)

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — Cal. H&S Code §6.95; threshold quantities trigger HMBP requirement; submitted via CERS
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator / Tiered Permit** — tiered permitting (Conditionally Exempt, Permit by Rule, Conditionally Authorized, Full Permit)
3. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — Cal. UST Law; CCR Title 23; regulated installation, operation, and closure
4. **California Environmental Reporting System (CERS)** — electronic submission portal for all hazmat/waste documents
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — CCR Title 19; regulated substances above threshold quantities require Risk Management Plans
6. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — Assembly Bill 1130; spill prevention plans for petroleum above threshold volumes
