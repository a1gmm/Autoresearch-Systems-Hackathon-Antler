## Collection System Operator

The **City of Port Hueneme Wastewater Division** owns and operates the local collection system:
- ~32 miles of underground collection piping
- 5 pump stations
- >5,000 service connections
- Total flow: ~2 million gallons per day

**Service area:** City of Port Hueneme, Naval Base Ventura County (NBVC Construction Battalion Center), and Oxnard Harbor District.

## Treatment — Oxnard POTW

Port Hueneme does NOT operate its own treatment plant. All collected wastewater is conveyed to and treated by the **City of Oxnard Wastewater Treatment Plant (POTW)** located at 6001 South Perkins Road, Oxnard, CA. Oxnard's POTW discharges to the Pacific Ocean through a 5-mile outfall under its own NPDES permit.

- Oxnard POTW NPDES permit: **CA0054097** (issued by the Los Angeles Regional Water Quality Control Board)
- The Oxnard Wastewater Division is the entity that holds and implements the federally-approved **Industrial Pretreatment Program** for the regional system, including flows contributed by Port Hueneme.

## Industrial Pretreatment Program

Because Port Hueneme's flows discharge to Oxnard's POTW, Oxnard's pretreatment program and local limits govern significant industrial users (SIUs) in Port Hueneme. Industrial dischargers in Port Hueneme that qualify as SIUs must:

1. Obtain an **Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit** from the City of Oxnard Wastewater Division
2. Comply with Oxnard's local discharge limits (established in the Oxnard Sewer Use Ordinance, Oxnard Municipal Code Article XIX, §19-25 et seq.)
3. Submit pretreatment compliance reports to Oxnard
