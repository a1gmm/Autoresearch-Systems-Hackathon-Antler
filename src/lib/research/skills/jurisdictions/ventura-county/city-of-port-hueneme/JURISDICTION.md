## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA (Unified Hazardous Materials Program)

- **CUPA authority:** Ventura County RMA Environmental Health Division — Port Hueneme is NOT its own CUPA and is NOT a participating city with its own separate implementing agency (unlike Oxnard, which has its own CUPA, and Ventura, which is a PA). Port Hueneme falls under the **county CUPA** directly.
- **Two separate city-level programs exist in VC:** Oxnard CUPA (cupa@oxnard.org, 805-385-7722) and City of Ventura PA (Brett Reed, 805-320-8337). Port Hueneme is neither — use county contacts below.
- **County CUPA contact:** (805) 654-2815 (manager); (805) 654-2494 (general); (805) 662-6511 (hazmat regulations); (805) 654-2127 (hazmat waste / pollution prevention)
- **Reporting system:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — calepa.ca.gov/cupa/
- **Fee schedule (2025–2026, effective July 2025):** Hazardous Materials Program Fees — Business Plans, Hazardous Waste Producers, Underground Tanks
  - Source: https://rmadocs.venturacounty.gov/environmental-health/fees/publications/environmental-health-hazardous-materials-programs-schedule-of-fees.pdf
- **Program page:** https://rma.venturacounty.gov/divisions/environmental-health/cupa-certified-unified-program-agency/
- **Contact page:** https://rma.venturacounty.gov/divisions/environmental-health/cupa-contact-information/
- **Authorizing state law:** Health & Safety Code §6.95 (HMBP); CCR Title 19 (UST/CalARP)
- **Local ordinance chapter:** [VERIFY — county ordinance code chapter not published on web pages; call (805) 654-2815]

### 2. Fire Code / AHJ

- **AHJ:** Ventura County Fire Protection District (VCFD) — Port Hueneme is served by VCFD Battalion 5, Station 53
- **Adopted fire code:** 2025 California Fire Code (CFC), effective **January 1, 2026**, adopted via **VCFPD Ordinance No. 34**, which also incorporates: Appendix Chapters D and P, portions of the 2024 International Fire Code (IFC), portions of the 2025 California Wildland-Urban Interface Code, and portions of CCR Title 19
- **City ratification:** Port Hueneme City Council adopted a Resolution ratifying VCFPD Ord. 34 (Article 3 §903, Article 3 Appendix B, Article 4 §604.11) on November 17, 2025
  - Source: https://www.ci.port-hueneme.ca.us/DocumentCenter/View/9501/2025-11-17---03-CC---Resolution-Ratifying-VCFPD-Ord-34-Bldg-Standards
- **Prior code:** VCFPD Ordinance No. 31 (2019 CFC, effective January 1, 2020) — superseded
  - Source: https://fire.venturacounty.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/Ord-31-Appendix-V-Excerpt-Fire-Safety-Provisions-for-Hazardous-Fire-Areas.pdf
- **High-piled storage permits:** Required for >12,000 sq ft (Class I–IV commodities) or >2,500 sq ft (high-hazard / plastics). VCFD Fire Prevention Bureau administers.
  - Standard: https://fire.venturacounty.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/14.7.5-High-Piled-Combustible-Storage.pdf
- **Fire permit fees:** [VERIFY — fee schedule not publicly posted; contact VCFD Fire Prevention Bureau]
- **VCFD main site:** https://fire.venturacounty.gov/
- **Fire hazard severity zones:** https://fire.venturacounty.gov/cal-fire-fire-hazard-severity-zone-map-update/

### 3. Industrial Wastewater / Pretreatment

- **POTW operator:** City of Port Hueneme Wastewater Division (own POTW — not Oxnard Sanitation)
- **System:** ~32 miles of collection piping, 5 pump stations, >5,000 service connections; serves Naval Base Ventura County and Oxnard Harbor District as well; processes ~2 million gallons/day
- **Sewer use code:** Article VII, Chapter 3 of the Port Hueneme Municipal Code covers Industrial/Commercial Wastewater Management (Municode node: ARTVIIPUUT_CH3INWAMA). Part K covers Prohibited Acts and Enforcement (node: ARTVIIPUUT_CH3INWAMA_PTKPRACEN).
  - Source: https://library.municode.com/ca/port_hueneme/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=ARTVIIPUUT_CH3INWAMA_PTKPRACEN_7250CIACCO
- **Commercial wastewater rates (adopted July 1, 2014):** Volumetric, $2.40–$5.40 per hundred cubic feet (based on wastewater strength); $36.00/month minimum; assumes 75% of water use discharged to sewer
- **IWP/pretreatment contact:** Wastewater Division — (805) 986-6561; 250 N Ventura Road, Port Hueneme, CA 93041
- **Wastewater page:** https://www.ci.port-hueneme.ca.us/880/Wastewater
- **Specific IWP application portal:** [VERIFY — not found online; call (805) 986-6561]
- **Note:** City operates own pretreatment program. Confirm current fee schedule and IWP threshold directly with Wastewater Division, as rate sheet dates from 2014.

### 4. Stormwater (MS4)

- **Permit:** Ventura Countywide MS4 NPDES Permit — **Order R4-2021-0105**, effective September 11, 2021 (superseded prior Order R4-2010-0108). Port Hueneme is a **co-permittee** along with the Ventura County Watershed Protection District, County of Ventura, and 10 other incorporated cities.
- **Program coordinator:** Ventura County Stormwater Quality Management Program (VCStormwater)
  - Source: https://vcstormwater.org/regulations/our-ms4-permit/

### 5. Zoning / Land Use

- **Zoning code:** Port Hueneme Municipal Code, **Article X — Zoning Regulations (ARTXZORE)**; Chapter 4 covers Zoning Classifications
  - Municode: https://library.municode.com/ca/port_hueneme/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=ARTXZORE_CH4ZOCL
- **Industrial zoning districts:**
  - **M-1: Light Industrial Zone** — coastal-related industrial uses not requiring direct water adjacency
  - **M-PR: Port-Related Industry Zone** — ship off-loading, warehousing, port-related offices, fuel storage; max FAR 0.75; access controlled to minimize impacts on City streets

### 6. Federal Enclave Note (NBVC)

Naval Base Ventura County (NBVC — Port Hueneme, Point Mugu, San Nicolas Island) is **federal land**. Facilities on-base use the **Navy Region SW Federal Fire Department**, not VCFD. CUPA, wastewater, stormwater, and zoning authorities above do NOT apply on-base.
Source: https://www.cnic.navy.mil/Regions/cnrsw/installations/navbase_ventura_county/
