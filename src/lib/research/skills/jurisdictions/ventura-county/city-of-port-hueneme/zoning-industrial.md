## Industrial Zoning Districts

Port Hueneme has two industrial zoning districts:

### M-1: Light Industrial Zone (Part H, Chapter 4)

**Purpose:** Provides and regulates a zone for coastal-related industrial uses that do not require direct water adjacency, along with compatible commercial and industrial activities.

**Permitted uses (by right) include:**
- Harbor-related manufacturing, processing, storage, and warehousing of port goods
- Administrative offices tied to industrial operations
- Marine science and research facilities
- Up to 28 additional miscellaneous uses including: appliance/electronics/plastics manufacturing, beverage production, food processing, motion picture/recording studios, home improvement centers, marine supply stores, and similar uses

**Conditional uses (Development Permit required):**
- Fish processing
- Auto transport preparation/staging
- Vehicle, lumber, or equipment storage exceeding 50% of site area
- Energy facilities and public works facilities
- Port Master Plan-approved uses not otherwise listed
- Research or manufacturing uses not listed as permitted above

**Development standards:**
| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Minimum lot area | 15,000 sq ft |
| Maximum building coverage | 75% of net lot area |
| Maximum height | 35 feet / 2 stories |
| Street setback | Minimum 20 ft from right-of-way |
| Side setback | Minimum 10 ft (may be 0 ft for interior lots if abutting structure is also 0 ft) |
| Residential adjacency setback | Distance = 2× height of the structure |

**Emergency shelters in M-1:**
- Minimum 50 gross sq ft per resident; maximum 60 occupants per facility; 6-month stay limit per 12-month period; on-site management and security required at all times; parking: 1 space per 10 adult beds plus employee spaces

Source: https://www.zoneomics.com/code/port-hueneme-CA/chapter_9

### M-PR: Port-Related Industry Zone (Part I, Chapter 4)

**Purpose:** Regulates property contiguous to the Port of Hueneme under a **certified Port Master Plan approved by the California Coastal Commission**. This is a single zone classification for all port-adjacent parcels; development standards default to M-1 zone standards.

**Permitted uses (by right):**
- Coastal-related or coastal-dependent development as defined in the California Coastal Act of 1976
- Seawalls, waterfront access, recreation and visitor facilities consistent with the Hueneme Beach Master Plan (limited to ocean revetment and 50-ft strip abutting north boundary)

**Categorical exclusions (no development review required):**
- Petroleum and bulk fuel receiving, storage, and distribution
- General cargo handling and movement
- Fishing berth access
- Internal port circulation and central gate operations
- Off-street parking for port operations
- Equipment storage for cargo operations
- Oil module/platform fabrication (Mon–Sat, 8 am–6 pm)

**Administrative Permit required** for uses not exceeding thresholds of:
- 10,000 sq ft building area, or
- 20,000 sq ft land area, or
- 10% increase in cargo throughput

**Development Permit required** for all other uses, if applicant demonstrates no injurious effects to public health, safety, or coastal resources.

**Development standards:** Apply M-1 Zone development standards (see above).

Source: https://www.zoneomics.com/code/port-hueneme-CA/chapter_10

## CUP / Permit Process

**Three-tier system under Article X, Chapter 3:**

1. **Administrative Permit** — Staff-level approval for smaller-scale uses or expansions (typically within stated thresholds); processed by Community Development Department
2. **Development Permit (equivalent to CUP)** — Planning Commission approval required for uses listed as conditional; requires findings that the use will not be injurious to public health, safety, or welfare
3. **Categorical Exclusion** — No permit or review required for specific port operations listed in M-PR zone

**All permit applications** must be submitted online via OpenGov portal (in-person applications no longer accepted):
- https://porthuenemeca.viewpointcloud.com/categories/1082

## Coastal Commission / Port Master Plan Note

Port-adjacent development in M-PR may also require **California Coastal Commission** review or notification, depending on the size and location of the project. The Port of Hueneme Port Master Plan is a separately certified document. Contact the Port of Hueneme and the Coastal Commission's South Coast District Office for projects within or adjacent to port jurisdiction.
