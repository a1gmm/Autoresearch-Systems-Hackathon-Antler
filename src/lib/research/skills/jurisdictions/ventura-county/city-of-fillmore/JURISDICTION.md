## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials

- **CUPA authority**: Ventura County Resource Management Agency (RMA) — Environmental Health Division. Fillmore is NOT a separate CUPA or Participating Agency (only Oxnard and City of Ventura have their own PA arrangements); Fillmore businesses file with the county CUPA.
- **Programs administered**: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste/Tiered Permitting, Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage (APSA), CalARP.

### 4. Stormwater — MS4

- **MS4 permit**: Fillmore is a co-permittee under the countywide Order No. R4-2021-0105 (NPDES Permit No. CAS004004) — "Waste Discharge Requirements and NPDES Permit for MS4 Discharges within the Coastal Watersheds of Los Angeles and Ventura Counties." Administered by RWQCB Los Angeles Region.
- **Local stormwater ordinance**: FMC Chapter 8.06, Title 8 (Stormwater Quality Management Ordinance) — principal city ordinance controlling urban runoff and prohibiting illicit discharges into the MS4.
- **Industrial/construction coverage**: Industrial facilities with exposed materials need either coverage under State General Industrial Stormwater Permit (IGP) or demonstration of no exposure; construction sites ≥1 acre need SWPPP under State General Construction Permit.
- **Local contact**: City of Fillmore Public Works — (805) 524-1500 ext. 233

### 5. Zoning / Land Use — Industrial Districts

- **Zoning code**: FMC Title 6, Chapter 6.04 (Zoning Ordinance). Industrial/manufacturing uses: Section 6.04.08.
- **Industrial zone**: Single industrial zone designation — **MPD** (Manufacturing/Planned Development). There is no separate M-1/M-2 split; all manufacturing land uses are mapped to MPD.
  - Light manufacturing (enclosed, low-impact): **Permitted (P)** — assembly, distribution, dry cleaning, recycling, repair, R&D, storage, testing, wholesaling.
  - Heavy manufacturing (high-impact or outdoor): **Conditional Use Permit (C)** required — chemicals, concrete/petroleum processing, high-volume freight, recycling compaction, large-scale repair, potentially toxic coating/plating, scrap yards, truck repair/stops.
  - Hazardous Waste Facilities: **CUP (C)** required.
- **South Fillmore Industrial Park (SFIP) Overlay Zone**: ~30 acres south of SR-126, bounded by River St (N), Santa Clara River (S), approx. 500 ft west of C St / E St (W). Allows light manufacturing uses by Development Permit (D); relaxes 500-ft residential separation requirement applicable in MPD base zone.
- **Business Park District**: BP-1 zone (campus retail/commercial + light industrial) and BP-2 zone (light + medium industrial + support retail) — added by Ord. 05-782 (August 1, 2005) along Highway 126 corridor.
- **CUP process**: Section 6.04.70 of FMC. Development Permit (for less-intensive uses): Section 6.04.66.
