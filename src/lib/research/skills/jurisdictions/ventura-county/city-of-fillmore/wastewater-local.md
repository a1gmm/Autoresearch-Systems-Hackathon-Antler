> Research reference for industrial wastewater and sewer use in the City of Fillmore.
> Fillmore owns and operates its own POTW (Fillmore Water Recycling Plant). The city has a sewer use ordinance under FMC Title 12.
> No public-facing industrial pretreatment program page was found online — the city appears to be a small POTW without a formally published pretreatment program. Industrial dischargers should contact City Public Works directly.

## POTW Identity

- **POTW name:** City of Fillmore Water Recycling Plant (also referred to as Fillmore Wastewater Recycling Plant)
  - Replaced a 50-year-old facility; completed August 2009; certified for operations September 2009
- **Design capacity:** 2.4 MGD (million gallons per day) — sized for projected flows through 2028
- **Treatment technology:** Membrane bioreactor (MBR) primary treatment + ultraviolet (UV) disinfection
- **Effluent reuse:** Title 22-compliant recycled water applied to public lands (parks, greenbelts, schools) via conventional irrigation, subsurface drip irrigation, and percolation
- **Operation:** City-owned; operated under contract with a private operator (overseen by City Engineer)
- **Operator of record (former DBO contractor):** American Water (design-build-operate contract)
- **AECOM project page:** https://aecom.com/projects/fillmore-water-recycling-plant/

## Industrial Pretreatment Program

- **No separate formal pretreatment program page found publicly** as of fetch date
- Given Fillmore's small population (~16,000) and predominantly agricultural/light-industrial base, the POTW may not be required to maintain an approved pretreatment program under 40 CFR Part 403 (the federal trigger is POTWs with design flows ≥5 MGD that receive industrial wastes, or POTWs that receive categorical industrial dischargers)
- The MBR plant design capacity is 2.4 MGD — below the 5 MGD threshold that automatically triggers a federal pretreatment program requirement
- **Categorical standards still apply directly:** Industrial users who are categorical industrial users (CIUs) under 40 CFR Parts 405–471 must comply with applicable categorical pretreatment standards even without a local program
