> Research reference for the City of Fillmore Fire Department acting as AHJ.
> Fillmore operates its own independent municipal fire department — do NOT route to Ventura County Fire Department (VCFD). VCFD operates Station 27 in Fillmore under mutual aid only.
> The adopted CFC edition and local amendment ordinance number are NOT published on city or Municode web pages; contact the Fire Marshal directly to confirm.

## Fire Hazard Severity Zones (FHSZ)

- City of Fillmore falls under Local Responsibility Area (LRA) jurisdiction (not a State Responsibility Area)
- New FHSZ map (as designated by CAL FIRE / OSFM) was adopted by City Council on **May 13, 2025**; effective **July 1, 2025**
- Fillmore chose not to propose additions or increases beyond the OSFM-recommended zones
- Interactive FHSZ map viewer: https://www.fillmoreca.gov/1339/Fire-Hazard-Severity-Zones-and-CAL-Fire-

## Permit Types

- Annual business fire safety inspections conducted by the Fire Department
- Occupancy certifications for new construction
- Hazardous materials: CUPA/HMBP compliance routes through Ventura County RMA (see cupa-local.md); local fire code compliance for hazmat operations is enforced by Fillmore Fire Department as AHJ
- High-piled storage / hazmat occupancy: Subject to CFC requirements enforced by local Fire Marshal; no local fee schedule found online

## Response Standards (per City)

- First unit arrival capable of initial fire attack or victim rescue: within 5 minutes, 90% of the time
- Sufficient additional units for interior firefighting operations: within 10 minutes, 90% of the time
- Level 2 paramedic / ALS assist: within 5 minutes, 90% of the time

## Key Gap

The adopting ordinance number and any local CFC amendments are **not published on the city website or Municode** as of the fetch date. This is common for small combination departments. Contact the Fire Marshal directly before citing a specific ordinance number.
