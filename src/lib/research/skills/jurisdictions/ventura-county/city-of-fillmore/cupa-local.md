> Research reference for CUPA and hazardous materials programs in the City of Fillmore.
> Fillmore is NOT a separate CUPA and has NO Participating Agency (PA) arrangement. All six CalEPA unified programs are administered by the Ventura County RMA Environmental Health Division for businesses within Fillmore city limits.
> Contrast with City of Oxnard (full independent CUPA) and City of Ventura (PA for selected programs) — Fillmore has neither arrangement.

## HMBP / CERS Filing

- **Submission system:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business
- **HMBP program page:** https://rma.venturacounty.gov/divisions/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-business-plan-program/
- **Who must comply:** Any business storing, using, or handling hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities (Health & Safety Code §25507 — generally 55 gallons liquid, 500 lbs solid, 200 cubic feet gas)
- **Reviewing agency for Fillmore businesses:** Ventura County RMA EH (not any city department)

## Local Hazmat Ordinance

- Ventura County CUPA implements state and federal law plus County ordinance code and local policies
- No City of Fillmore-specific local hazmat ordinance found online [VERIFY — contact Ventura County RMA for any Fillmore-specific local amendments]

## Relationship to Fillmore Fire Department

- The Fillmore Fire Department is the AHJ for local fire code compliance (see fire-code-local.md), but it is NOT the CUPA and does NOT administer HMBP, UST, APSA, or CalARP programs
- For a facility requiring both CUPA registration and fire code compliance: file HMBP via CERS with Ventura County RMA, AND contact Fillmore Fire Dept for fire code operational permits
