> Research reference for industrial and manufacturing zoning in the City of Fillmore.
> Fillmore uses MPD (Manufacturing/Planned Development) as its primary industrial designation, with an M-1/M-2 legacy distinction apparent in older General Plan documents. A Business Park (BP-1, BP-2) overlay was added by ordinance in 2005 along the SR-126 corridor.
> Note: Fillmore is a small (≈16,000-person) rural/agricultural city. Industrial land totals roughly 141 acres (~8% of city area), concentrated in North Fillmore and the South Fillmore Industrial Park.

## Industrial Zone Designations

### MPD — Manufacturing/Planned Development Zone

The primary industrial designation. Fillmore does not use a traditional M-1/M-2 split as the base zone names; all manufacturing/industrial land is mapped MPD. (Legacy General Plan documents reference M-1 Light Industrial and M-2 Heavy Industrial sub-categories, which correspond to the permitted vs. conditional distinction within MPD.)

| Use Category | Approval |
|---|---|
| Light manufacturing — enclosed, low-impact (assembly, distribution, dry cleaning, recycling, repair, R&D, storage, testing, wholesaling) | **Permitted (P)** |
| Heavy manufacturing — high-impact or outdoor (chemicals, concrete/petroleum processing, high-volume freight, recycling compaction, large-scale repair, potentially toxic coating/plating, scrap yards, truck repair/stops) | **Conditional Use Permit (C)** |
| Hazardous Waste Treatment/Storage/Disposal Facilities | **Conditional Use Permit (C)** |

- **500-ft residential separation:** Required in MPD base zone for certain uses; may be relaxed within the SFIP overlay (see below)

### South Fillmore Industrial Park (SFIP) Overlay Zone

- **Location:** ~30 acres south of SR-126, bounded by River St (N), Santa Clara River (S), approximately 500 ft west of C St/E St (W)
- **Key feature:** Light manufacturing uses allowed by Development Permit (D) instead of full CUP; relaxes the 500-ft residential separation requirement applicable in MPD base zone

### Business Park District (BP-1 and BP-2)

Added by **Ordinance No. 05-782** (adopted August 1, 2005) along the SR-126/Highway 126 corridor.

| Zone | Allowed Uses |
|---|---|
| **BP-1** | Campus retail/commercial + light industrial |
| **BP-2** | Light + medium industrial + support retail |

- Municode reference for BP district: https://library.municode.com/ca/fillmore/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT6LADEUS_CH6.04ZORE_ARTIIZODI_6.04.12BUPADINESEZOORAMAU12005PEOR05-782

## CUP Process

- **Section reference:** FMC Section 6.04.70 (Conditional Use Permit process)
- **Development Permit** (for less-intensive uses requiring discretionary review but below CUP threshold): FMC Section 6.04.66
- Decision-maker for CUP: Planning Commission (standard); minor/administrative CUP approved by Director
- Application form: https://www.fillmoreca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/295/Planning-Application-PDF
- Applications/checklists: https://www.fillmoreca.gov/227/Applications-Plans-Checklists

## Industrial Land Geography

- **North Fillmore Industrial Area / North Fillmore Master Plan:** ~49 acres of vacant industrial land (as of General Plan baseline); estimated capacity ~640,000 sq ft of industrial floor area. Largest single concentration of industrial land.
- **South Fillmore Industrial Park (SFIP):** ~30 acres; operated as an overlay zone with relaxed standards
- Total industrially-designated land: ~141 acres (~8% of city area)
