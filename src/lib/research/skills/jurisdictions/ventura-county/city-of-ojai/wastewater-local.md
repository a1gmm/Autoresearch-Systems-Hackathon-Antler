## Industrial Pretreatment Program

OVSD operates an **EPA/LARWQCB-approved Industrial Pretreatment Program** under 40 CFR Part 403, mandated through the federal NPDES program.

**Permit required for:**
- Food service establishments (grease interceptor required)
- Auto service facilities and dealerships
- Retail gasoline stations
- Car washes
- Medical offices (certain types)
- Manufacturing facilities
- Laundries
- Grocery stores

**Permit type:** Commercial/Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit (WDP)
**Application fee:** $75.00
**Process:** Environmental Compliance Inspectors conduct site visits; pretreatment requirements (grease interceptor, full pretreatment system, etc.) set on a facility-by-facility basis depending on industry type and discharge characteristics.

Pretreatment page: https://www.ojaisan.org/commercial_industrial/pretreatment/index.php

## IWP / Permitting Process

1. Contact OVSD at (805) 646-5548 or visit 1072 Tico Road to request WDP application.
2. Submit application with $75.00 fee.
3. Inspector conducts site visit to determine pretreatment requirements.
4. WDP issued with facility-specific conditions.

Permits / Rates & Fees page: https://www.ojaisan.org/permits/rates_and_fees/index.php

## Note on Program Scale

Ojai is a small, predominantly residential/commercial city with minimal heavy industry. The pretreatment program is active and EPA-approved, but industrial discharge volumes are limited. No large categorical industrial users (CIUs) have been identified in publicly available documents.
