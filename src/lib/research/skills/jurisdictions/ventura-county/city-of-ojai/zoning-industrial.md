## Industrial / Manufacturing Districts

### M-1 — Light Manufacturing Industrial

- **Purpose:** Areas appropriate for complementary commercial and light manufacturing land uses.
- **General Plan designation consistency:** "Commercial Manufacturing."
- **Maximum floor area ratio (FAR):** 0.45
- **Established/amended:** Ordinance No. 856 (M-1 Zoning).
- Ordinance 856 PDF: https://www.ojai.ca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1143/Ordinance-856---M1-Zoning-PDF

### MPD — Manufacturing Planned Development

- **Purpose:** Campus-style planned industrial/business parks; accommodates manufacturing, R&D, office headquarters, and compatible uses.
- **Review:** Project-by-project compatibility review required (no standard development envelope — each project evaluated on its own merits).
- No heavy industrial M-2 district identified in the Ojai Municipal Code. [VERIFY — confirm absence of M-2 or heavier industrial tier with Planning Division.]

## CUP Process

**Decision authority:** Planning Commission (majority vote).

**Process steps:**
1. Pre-application conference strongly encouraged before formal submittal.
2. Submit CUP application to Planning & Zoning Division (available at https://ojai.ca.gov/246/Planning-Zoning-Division).
3. Processing runs concurrently with environmental review (CEQA) and design review.
4. If both a CUP and a variance are required, Planning Commission is the decision authority for both.
5. Appeal to City Council.

**Development and land use approval requirements:** Title 10, Chapter 2 (general); specific thresholds for ministerial vs. discretionary approval vary by district and use type. [VERIFY threshold table with Planning Division — eCode360 reference https://ecode360.com/44872062 returned 403.]

## Notes for EHS / Industrial Siting

- Ojai is a small, rural-adjacent city with minimal heavy industrial land. M-1 and MPD zones are limited in extent.
- The Ojai Valley is VHFHSZ (Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone); WUI overlay requirements apply to all development regardless of zone.
- Industrial projects in Ojai will typically trigger CEQA review and VCFD Fire Prevention Bureau consultation.
- Any facility storing hazardous materials above HMBP thresholds must register with the Ventura County CUPA (see cupa-local.md).
