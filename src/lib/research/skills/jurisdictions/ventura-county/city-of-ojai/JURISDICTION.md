### 2. Fire Code — AHJ and Permits

- **AHJ:** Ventura County Fire Department (VCFD) — City of Ojai contracts with VCFD for all fire services. Fire Prevention Bureau handles plan review and permitting.
- **Adopted fire code:** VCFD Ordinance No. 34 — "Ventura County Fire Code (VCFC) and Ventura County Wildland-Urban Interface Code (VCWUIC)", **effective January 1, 2026**. (Based on the 2022 California Fire Code / Title 24, Part 9; local VCFD amendments apply.) Note: VCFD Ord. 34 covers unincorporated county; Ojai adopts its own parallel building-code ordinances — confirm CFC adoption ordinance with Ojai Building & Safety.
- **Ojai Building-Code Update:** Ordinance No. 969, adopted December 9, 2025 (most recent building code update; CFC adoption ordinance number [VERIFY — search Ojai ordinance list]).
- **Wildland-Urban Interface:** Ojai Valley is designated Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone (VHFHSZ); WUI requirements apply.
- **Permit types (VCFD Fire Prevention Bureau):**
  - Fire protection systems (sprinklers, alarms, cooking hoods, private water systems)
  - High-piled combustible storage — dedicated form required with permit application
  - Hazmat / operational fire code permits
  - New structures/additions; State Fire Marshal regulated occupancies (Groups A, E, I, R, H, L)
  - Landscape and fuel modification zones

### 3. Industrial Wastewater — POTW and Pretreatment

- **POTW/Sanitation agency:** **Ojai Valley Sanitary District (OVSD)** — the receiving agency for all wastewater in the Ojai Valley; operates the Ojai Valley Wastewater Treatment Plant (OVWWTP).
- **Permit type:** Commercial/Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit (WDP); application fee **$75.00**.
- **Pretreatment program:** OVSD maintains a pretreatment program per federal categorical pretreatment standards; a dedicated pretreatment page exists (see below). FOG (Fats, Oils & Grease) program is separate.

### 4. Stormwater — MS4 / NPDES

- **Permit type:** City of Ojai is a **Phase I MS4 co-permittee** under the Ventura Countywide NPDES MS4 Permit (Regional Water Board — Los Angeles Region, Region 4; H-7 permit designation).
- **Co-permittees:** Ventura County Watershed Protection District, County of Ventura, and incorporated cities including Ojai.
- **Local stormwater ordinance:** City of Ojai non-stormwater discharge ordinance — **Municipal Code Title 5, Chapter 12** (illicit connection/discharge prohibition).
- **Annual MS4 reporting:** City files annual reports to the Regional Water Board; FY 2023–24 report available publicly.
- **Stormwater program contact:** City of Ojai Public Works / Community Development, (805) 646-5581
- **Stormwater program portal:** https://vcstormwater.org/ (Ventura County Watershed Protection District manages countywide program)
- **Annual report (FY 23-24):** https://vcstormwater.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Ojai_23-24_MS4AnnualReport.pdf
