## CUPA Authority

The City of Ojai is **not its own CUPA**. All CUPA-regulated programs in Ojai are administered by the:

**Ventura County Resource Management Agency (RMA) — Environmental Health Division**
Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA)
800 S. Victoria Ave., Ventura, CA 93009
Phone: (805) 654-2494
CUPA Manager: Rick Bandelin — (805) 654-2815

CUPA jurisdiction extends to all incorporated and unincorporated areas of Ventura County, **except the City of Oxnard** (its own CUPA) and Ventura/Santa Paula (participating agencies for HMBP/CalARP). City of Ojai is fully served by the County CUPA with no local participating-agency carveout.

## Authorizing Ordinance

**Ventura County Ordinance Code, Division 4 (Public Health), Chapter 5 (Hazardous Substances), Article 1 (Unified Program Generally)**, Sections 4500–4504. Key provisions:

- §4500: Authorized under HSC Ch. 6.11 (§25404 et seq.); purpose is to implement the unified hazardous waste/materials program and establish fees.
- §4501(e): "CUPA jurisdiction means all of the unincorporated and incorporated areas of Ventura County, except for the incorporated area of the City of Oxnard."
- §4503: Participating agencies (City of Ventura Fire Dept., City of Santa Paula Fire Dept.) implement HMBP/CalARP/SPCC elements within their own city limits only; City of Ojai has no participating agency — County CUPA handles everything.

## Programs Administered (County CUPA covers all six for Ojai)

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — HSC §6.95 / CCR Title 19; submit via CERS
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator / Tiered Permitting** — HSC Ch. 6.5
3. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — HSC Ch. 6.7 / Ch. 6.75
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA / SPCC)** — HSC Ch. 6.67
5. **California Accidental Release Program (CalARP)** — HSC Ch. 6.95 Part 2
6. **Hazardous Materials Management Plan / Inventory Statement (HMMIS)** — HSC §25404(c)(6)
