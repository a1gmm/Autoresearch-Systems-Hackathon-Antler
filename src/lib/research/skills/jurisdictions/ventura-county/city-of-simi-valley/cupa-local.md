> Research reference for CUPA (Certified Unified Program Agency) compliance in Simi Valley. Simi Valley has **no municipal CUPA** — it is fully covered by the Ventura County RMA Environmental Health Division CUPA. Route all HMBP, hazardous waste, UST, CalARP, and APSA filings to the County CUPA, not to the City.

## CUPA Authority

- **CUPA Agency:** Ventura County Resource Management Agency (RMA) — Environmental Health Division
- **Certified CUPA for:** All of Ventura County, including the City of Simi Valley
- **CUPA Manager:** Rick Bandelin — (805) 654-2815

**Note:** The City of Oxnard has its own separate CUPA (Oxnard Fire CUPA). Simi Valley does NOT — the County CUPA controls.

## Programs Unified Under the County CUPA

The Ventura County CUPA consolidates and implements the following six state programs for facilities in Simi Valley:

| Program | Filing System |
|---|---|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) | CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) |
| Hazardous Waste Generator / Tiered Permitting (OGTP) | CERS |
| Underground Storage Tanks (UST) | CERS |
| Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) | CERS |
| California Accidental Release Program (CalARP / RMP) | CERS + County review |
| Unified Hazardous Waste and Hazardous Materials Management Regulatory Program | CERS |

## Local Day-to-Day Contact (City of Simi Valley)

The City of Simi Valley Public Works Environmental Compliance Division provides local hazmat compliance coordination and referrals — it is a **participating agency** and city contact point, not the CUPA itself.

- **Division:** City of Simi Valley Public Works — Environmental Compliance Division
- **Phone:** (805) 583-6421
- **Services:** HMBP coordination, UST/AST referrals, hazmat emergency response coordination, stormwater illicit-discharge inspections
- **Web:** https://www.simivalley.org/departments/public-works/environmental-compliance/hazardous-materials

## Local Hazmat Ordinance

The County CUPA "implements State and Federal laws and regulations, County ordinance code, and local policies." No separate City of Simi Valley hazmat ordinance was found online. ## Filing and Compliance

- **Primary filing portal:** CERS (cers.calepa.ca.gov) — used for HMBP, Hazardous Waste, UST, APSA, and CalARP submissions
- Facilities with hazardous materials inventories above HMBP reporting thresholds must file an HMBP in CERS and keep it current
- Simi Valley businesses that are Significant Industrial Users of the sewer system and also handle hazardous materials may have parallel obligations to the City's Environmental Compliance Division (IWP) and the County CUPA (HMBP)
