> Research reference for fire code compliance in Simi Valley. The City has no municipal fire department — the Ventura County Fire Protection District (VCFD) is the sole AHJ. All fire code permits, plan checks, and inspections go through VCFD, not the City.

## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

- **AHJ:** Ventura County Fire Protection District (VCFD)
- **Stations serving Simi Valley:** Stations 41, 43, and 46
- **Fire Prevention Bureau address:** 2400 Conejo Spectrum Street, Thousand Oaks, CA 91320
- **Phone (general):** (805) 389-9710
- **Phone (permit counter):** (805) 389-9738
- **Phone (inspection scheduling):** (805) 389-9744
- **Hours:** 7:30 a.m. – 5:00 p.m., Monday – Friday
- **Email:** vcfd@venturacounty.gov
- **Web:** https://fire.venturacounty.gov/ordinances-and-fees/

There is no separate City of Simi Valley fire AHJ or fire code. Ventura County's VCFD Ordinance controls.

## Adopted Fire Code

- **Ordinance:** VCFD Ordinance No. 34
- **Effective date:** January 1, 2026
- **Base codes adopted:**
  - 2025 California Fire Code (CFC), Title 24 Part 9 — including Appendix Chapters D and P
  - Specified portions of the 2024 International Fire Code (IFC) not adopted by the State Fire Marshal, except portions deleted per Table 1.4.3 of the ordinance
  - 2025 California Wildland-Urban Interface Code (CWUIC), Title 24 Part 7 — including Appendix Chapter A (except Chapter 5)
  - Specified portions of the 2024 International Wildland-Urban Interface Code (IWUIC), except portions deleted per Table 1.4.4
  - Specified portions of California Code of Regulations Title 19, Division 1 (State Fire Marshal Regulations)
- **Prior ordinances repealed:** VCFD Ordinances No. 29 and No. 32 (2022-CFC-based codes) repealed in their entirety on January 1, 2026
- **Governing title:** "Ventura County Fire Code" (VCFC) and "Ventura County Wildland-Urban Interface Code" (VCWUIC)

## Key Local Amendments (Article 3 of Ordinance 34)

The following amendments to the 2025 CFC are expressly made in Ordinance 34:

- **Sprinklers — all new buildings (Section 903.2 amended):** An approved automatic fire sprinkler system shall be installed in ALL new buildings, with limited exceptions (Group U occupancies approved by the Fire Code Official; small agricultural produce stands; area additions not exceeding area thresholds in Sections 903.2.1–903.2.21 for non-sprinklered buildings; manufactured homes up to two dwelling units).
- **Concurrent permits (Section 903.1.2 added):** Additions or modifications with open building permits require sprinklers throughout if the combined permitted area across all open permits exceeds the exemption thresholds.
- **Change of use (Section 903.1.4 added):** Any change of occupancy from Group U that would not have been exempt from sprinklers under the prior ordinance must retrofit with fire sprinklers.
- **Area separation walls (Section 903.1.3 added):** Walls used to create fire areas below 5,000 sq ft (5,000 GSF for R-3) under pre-November 1, 2002 permits must be maintained without openings; failure requires retrofit sprinklers.
- **Attic protection (Section 903.3.1.2.3 amended):** Attics shall be protected by an automatic fire sprinkler system.
- **Hazardous materials — sale/delivery without permit (Section 105.1.7 added):** No person shall sell, deliver, or cause delivery of any hazardous commodity to a person not holding a valid permit where one is required.
- **Open burning (Sections 307.1, 307.1.1, 307.1.2 amended):** Prohibition authority retained by Fire Code Official; Open Burning in WUI areas regulated under Article 4 (Sections 105.2, A104.7.1, A104.7.2).
- **Citations (Section 104.6.1 added):** Fire Code Official may issue administrative citations under Article 3, Section 113 and Appendix U.
- **More restrictive standards filed with HCD** per Health & Safety Code § 13869.7 (sprinkler requirements, Appendix B, and Article 4 Section 604.11).

## IWP / Hazmat Operational Permits

Under CFC Chapter 50 and related chapters, VCFD operational permits are required for hazardous materials storage, use, and handling above CFC threshold quantities. Contact VCFD Fire Prevention Bureau for the current permit list and tier determination.

## Note on Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI)

Simi Valley includes significant WUI and Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone (VHFHSZ) areas. Ordinance 34's Article 4 (Ventura County Wildland-Urban Interface Code) applies to WUI areas. Defensible space inspections for residential sales in High/VHFHSZ are required as of January 1, 2026 (AB 38 implementation). See Ordinance 33 (effective July 1, 2025) for updated LRA Fire Hazard Severity Zone maps.
