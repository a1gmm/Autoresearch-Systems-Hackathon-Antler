## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA (Hazardous Materials / Unified Program)

- **CUPA authority**: Ventura County RMA Environmental Health Division is the certified CUPA for all of Ventura County, including Simi Valley — Simi Valley has **no separate municipal CUPA**.
  Source: https://rma.venturacounty.gov/divisions/environmental-health/cupa-certified-unified-program-agency/
- **Local day-to-day contact**: City of Simi Valley Public Works — Environmental Compliance Division administers local hazardous-materials compliance and referrals (HMBP, UST, hazmat emergency response, stormwater illicit-discharge inspections).
  - Phone: (805) 583-6421
  - Source: https://www.simivalley.org/departments/public-works/environmental-compliance/hazardous-materials
- **Regulated-facilities list**: Ventura County RMA publishes the county-wide CUPA facility/program list (PDF) covering Simi Valley facilities.
  Source: https://rmadocs.venturacounty.gov/environmental-health/programs/certified-unified-program-agency/publications/environmental-health-certified-unified-program-agency-facilities-and-programs-list.pdf
- **Programs unified**: Hazardous Waste Generator, UST, AST (SPCC), Business Plan (HMBP), Risk Management Plan (RMP), Tiered Permit (OGTP).

### 2. Fire Code (AHJ: Ventura County Fire Protection District)

- **AHJ**: Ventura County Fire Protection District (VCFD) — Simi Valley has no municipal fire department; VCFD stations 41, 43, and 46 serve the city.
- **Currently adopted code**: VCFD Ordinance 34 (effective January 1, 2026) adopts the 2022 California Fire Code (CFC) with local amendments as the Ventura County Fire Code (VCFC). Prior code was Ordinance 32 (eff. Jan 1, 2023, 2022 CFC base).
  Source: https://fire.venturacounty.gov/ordinances-and-fees/
  Full text of Ord. 34: https://s48417.pcdn.co/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Ordinance-34.pdf
- **Permit fee schedule**: FY 2025–2026 fee schedule (effective July 1, 2025).
  Download: https://s48417.pcdn.co/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Fee-Schedule-2025-2026.pdf
- **Permit portal**: Ventura County Citizen Access (online) and County of Ventura One-Stop Permitting.
  Source: https://onestoppermits.venturacounty.gov/departments/fire-department
- **Key permit types**: Fire code permits (general), high-piled combustible storage (form required; Guideline 14.7.5), special events, alternate method/code interpretation.
- **Fire Prevention Bureau contact**:
  - Address: 2400 Conejo Spectrum Street, Thousand Oaks, CA 91320
  - Phone: (805) 389-9710 (general) / (805) 389-9738 (permit counter) / (805) 389-9744 (inspection scheduling)
  - Hours: 7:30 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.
  - Email: vcfd@venturacounty.gov
  Source: https://fire.venturacounty.gov/ordinances-and-fees/

### 3. Industrial Wastewater / Pretreatment

- **POTW**: City of Simi Valley Water Quality Control Plant (NPDES Permit No. CA0055221, issued by LA RWQCB).
- **Sewer Use Ordinance**: Simi Valley Municipal Code (SVMC) **Title 6, Chapter 13** — Sewer Use Ordinance **No. 1170**.
  Source: https://library.municode.com/ca/simi_valley/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT6SAHE_CH13SEUS
  Adopted version on file with RWQCB: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/losangeles/board_decisions/tentative_orders/individual/npdes/City_of_Simi_Valley/SVWQCP%20CA0055221%20City%20of%20Simi%20Valley%20SUO%201170%20(Adopted).pdf
- **Industrial Wastewater Permit (IWP)**: Required for food service, auto service, car washes, medical offices, manufacturing, laundries, grocery stores, and others discharging non-sanitary wastewater.

### 4. Stormwater (MS4)

- **MS4 permit**: City is a co-permittee under the **Ventura Countywide NPDES MS4 Permit (CAS004004)**, issued by the Los Angeles Regional Water Quality Control Board. Permit covers Simi Valley, County of Ventura, and nine other Ventura County cities.
- **Local stormwater ordinance**: SVMC **Title 6, Chapter 12** — Stormwater and Water Quality Management.
  Source: https://library.municode.com/ca/simi_valley/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT6SAHE_CH12STWAQUMA_ART8EN
- **Local program scope**: Public outreach, industrial/commercial stormwater inspections, illicit-discharge response, public-agency activities; coordinates with Ventura Countywide Stormwater Quality Management Program.
- **Contact**:
  - Phone (hotline): (805) 583-6420
  - Email: EnvironmentalCompliance@simivalley.org
  Source: https://www.simivalley.org/departments/public-works/environmental-compliance/stormwater-quality-management
