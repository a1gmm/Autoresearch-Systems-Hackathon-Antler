## CUP Process

A Conditional Use Permit (CUP) is required for any use listed as "CUP" in the use matrix of Chapter 9-26. The CUP process involves:

1. Pre-application meeting with Planning Division recommended (not mandatory)
2. Submit CUP application to Planning Division (Environmental Services Building, City Hall)
3. Planning Division reviews for completeness and routes for environmental review (CEQA)
4. Planning Commission holds a public hearing; neighboring property owners notified
5. Planning Commission makes required findings, including that the proposed use is compatible with surrounding uses, and either approves, conditions, or denies the CUP
6. Approval conditions typically address hours of operation, traffic, noise, hazmat handling, and aesthetic compatibility
7. Appeals to City Council within applicable time period
