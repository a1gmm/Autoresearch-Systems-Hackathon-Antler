> Research reference for industrial wastewater and pretreatment compliance in Simi Valley. Unlike smaller rural cities, Simi Valley operates its own POTW and a fully approved federal pretreatment program. IWP authority is with the City's Environmental Compliance Division — do NOT route to Ventura County.

## Industrial Wastewater Permit (IWP)

The City's Pretreatment Program requires an IWP for commercial and industrial facilities that discharge non-sanitary wastewater to the sewer system. Businesses required to obtain an IWP include (confirmed from RWQCB NPDES permit fact sheet):

- Food service establishments
- Auto service facilities and auto dealerships
- Retail gasoline stations
- Car washes
- Some medical offices
- Manufacturing facilities
- Laundries
- Grocery stores

The Pretreatment Program currently covers **4 Significant Industrial Users (SIUs)** that collectively contribute approximately 2.2 MG of industrial wastewater annually to the WQCP (per NPDES permit Attachment F fact sheet, 2024 renewal).

## Significant Industrial Users (SIUs) and Categorical Standards

- The program applies federal categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR Part 403) to applicable categorical industrial users
- SIUs must submit baseline monitoring reports, periodic compliance reports, and immediate slug-discharge notifications
- Any change to the Pretreatment Program must be reported to the LA RWQCB in writing and approved by the Executive Officer per 40 CFR § 403.18

## Pretreatment Program Requirements

Per NPDES Order R4-2024-XXXX, Attachment I (Pretreatment Reporting Requirements), the City must:
- Maintain and update the list of SIUs with names, addresses, applicable categorical standards, and local limits applicability
- Characterize compliance status of each SIU annually
- Submit an annual pretreatment program report to the LA RWQCB
- Conduct a written technical evaluation of local limits within 180 days of NPDES permit issuance or reissuance (per 40 CFR § 122.44(j)(2)(ii) and 40 CFR § 403.5(c)(1))

## Application Process

- Contact Environmental Compliance Division at (805) 583-6420 or EnvironmentalCompliance@simivalley.org to determine if an IWP is required and to obtain the current application form.
- [VERIFY — online permit portal not confirmed; the city's general permit page is https://www.simivalley.org/our-city/quick-links/apply-for-a-permit]

## Connection to CUPA

Simi Valley's IWP (pretreatment) program is run by the City's Environmental Compliance Division, which also coordinates local hazmat compliance. The County CUPA (for HMBP/hazardous waste) is a parallel requirement — not a substitute. See cupa-local.md for County CUPA details.
