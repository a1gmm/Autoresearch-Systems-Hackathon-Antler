## Controlling CUPA Agency

**City of Moorpark is NOT its own CUPA.** Moorpark is served by the:

> **Ventura County Resource Management Agency (RMA), Environmental Health Division — Hazardous Materials Program (CUPA)**

The two cities in Ventura County with independent CUPAs are the **City of Oxnard** and the **City of Ventura** (as a Participating Agency). All other incorporated cities, including Moorpark, fall under the County CUPA.

## Programs Administered

The Ventura County CUPA administers all six unified program elements for Moorpark facilities:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — required for facilities that handle regulated quantities of hazardous materials; submitted via CERS (https://cers.calepa.ca.gov)
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator Program** — tiered permitting under Health & Safety Code §25200 et seq.
3. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — permits, inspections, closure requirements
4. **California Accidental Release Program (CalARP)** — Risk Management Plans for facilities meeting threshold quantities
5. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — Spill Prevention Control and Countermeasure (SPCC) plan oversight for tanks ≥1,320 gal aggregate
6. **Hazardous Waste Cleanup Program (HWCP)** — oversight of corrective action

## Local Hazmat Ordinance

The County CUPA operates under **Ventura County Code** provisions. Moorpark has no independent municipal hazardous materials chapter. [VERIFY — no Moorpark Municipal Code chapter found online for local hazmat rules; the city relies entirely on state law and county ordinance as administered by the RMA CUPA.]

## Fee Schedule

Fees are set by Ventura County RMA for all CUPA-element permits. A current fee schedule PDF is available at:
https://rma.venturacounty.gov/divisions/environmental-health/environmental-health-division-fees/

Fee categories include: Hazardous Materials Program (HMBP, HWG, UST), CalARP, and APSA programs. Online payment for annual permit fees is available through the RMA portal. Contact (805) 654-2494 for current fee amounts.

## CERS Reporting

All HMBP submissions are made electronically through the California Environmental Reporting System (CERS): https://cers.calepa.ca.gov. Facilities must report annually or upon a significant change in hazardous materials handled.
