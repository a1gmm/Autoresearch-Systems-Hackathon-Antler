## Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit (IWP)

### Who Needs a Permit
Any person proposing to connect to or discharge industrial wastewater into the District's sewage system must first obtain an **Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit** (Rule 8-E-2). "Industrial user" includes producing, manufacturing, processing, institutional, commercial, and agricultural dischargers.

### Permit Application (Rule 8-E-3)
Submit completed application to the District at 6767 Spring Road (no online portal found). Required information includes:
1. Applicant name, address, SIC number
2. Discharge location, timing, and duration
3. Estimated average and peak flow rates (daily, monthly, seasonal)
4. Major constituents/characteristics analyzed by a certified lab
5. Site plans, floor plans, and plumbing plans showing all sewer connections
6. Description of toxic/hazardous materials stored or used on premises
7. Products by type and production process
8. Applicable categorical pretreatment standards
9. Number of employees and hours of operation

### Permit Duration
Permits issued for **up to 5 years** (Rule 8-E-6). Non-transferable (Rule 8-E-7).

## General Prohibitions (Rule 8-C-2)

Prohibited discharges include: flammable/explosive materials (readings >5% LEL continuously or >10% LEL at any single reading); toxic pollutants causing POTW interference; solids/viscous materials that obstruct flow (grease, bones, metals, plastics, tar, etc.); stormwater, groundwater, or roof drainage; undiluted cooling tower blowdown (>1/3 of makeup water without approval); radioactive wastes exceeding applicable limits; materials causing biosolids to fail beneficial reuse criteria.

## Monitoring and Reporting (Part 8, Section F)

Industrial users subject to IWPs must maintain self-monitoring records and submit technical reports per permit conditions. The District conducts an independent inspection and sampling program to verify compliance.

## Enforcement (Part 8, Section H)

Violations result in a **Notice of Violation (NOV)** specifying the deficiency and a compliance deadline. Permit suspension or revocation available for uncorrected violations. Non-compliance penalties (fees) may be levied.

## Fees

Sewer fees for industrial users (including IWP fees and excess capacity/treatment charges) are set in **Part 8, Section G** of the Rules and Regulations. Contact District at (805) 378-3000 for current IWP application and annual permit fees — not posted publicly online as a standalone schedule.

## Notes

- Moorpark has no city-level sewer use ordinance; the VCWWD No. 1 Rules and Regulations (Part 8) and the RWQCB-approved Sewer Use Ordinance serve as the local pretreatment control documents.
- The reclaimed water distribution system (Part 6 of R&R) covers recycled water connections and conditions of service — relevant for industrial users seeking recycled water for process use.
