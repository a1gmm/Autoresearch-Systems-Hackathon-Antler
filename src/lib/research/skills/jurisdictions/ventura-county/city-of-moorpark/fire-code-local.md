## AHJ

**Ventura County Fire Department (VCFD), Fire Prevention Bureau** — serves City of Moorpark under contract. Moorpark has no independent fire department or fire marshal; all operational and construction fire code permits are issued by VCFD.

- Phone: (805) 389-9710
- Email: vcfd@venturacounty.gov
- Headquarters: 165 Durley Ave, Camarillo, CA 93010

## Adopted Code Edition

| Code | Edition | Local Instrument | Effective |
|------|---------|-----------------|-----------|
| California Fire Code (CFC) | 2025 CFC (Title 19 CCR) | VCFD Ordinance No. 34 | January 1, 2026 |
| California Wildland-Urban Interface Code | 2025 CWUIC | VCFD Ordinance No. 34 | January 1, 2026 |

Ordinance No. 34 designates the locally amended version as the **Ventura County Fire Code (VCFC)** and the **Ventura County Wildland-Urban Interface Code (VCWUIC)**.

## Permit Process

All construction and operational fire code permits are submitted to VCFD Fire Prevention Bureau via:
- **Online portal:** https://onestoppermits.venturacounty.gov/departments/fire-department
- **Primary permit form:** Form 610 — Fire Permit Application
- **High-pile combustible storage:** Form FP 13.5.6 (filed with Form 610)
- **All permit forms:** https://fire.venturacounty.gov/fire-prevention-applications-forms/

## Hazmat Permits

Hazardous materials storage and use permits (operational permits under CFC Chapter 50+) are coordinated between VCFD Fire Prevention Bureau and the Ventura County CUPA. Industrial facilities with regulated quantities must obtain both an operational fire permit from VCFD and an HMBP/permit from the CUPA (see cupa-local.md).

## Notes

- Moorpark is entirely within VCFD jurisdiction (no CAL FIRE contract area within city limits).
- The city's Building & Safety Division (805-517-6272) processes building permits; VCFD handles all fire prevention/inspection authority.
- Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI) requirements apply to portions of Moorpark adjacent to wildland areas — verify with VCFD whether a specific parcel is in the WUI overlay.
