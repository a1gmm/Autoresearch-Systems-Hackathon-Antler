## Industrial Zone Districts

Three industrial zone classifications exist in Moorpark (Chapters 17.12 and 17.16; uses in Table 17.20.060):

### M-1 — Industrial Park Zone
**Purpose (MMC § 17.16):** Provides suitable areas for the exclusive development of light industrial, service, technical research, and related business office uses in an industrial park setting. Stringent design, noise, and landscaping standards apply to ensure compatibility with surrounding uses.

**Typical allowed uses:** Research and development, light manufacturing, warehousing, office uses accessory to industrial, business services.

### M-2 — Limited Industrial Zone
**Purpose (MMC § 17.16):** Provides suitable areas for a broad range of industrial and quasi-industrial activities of a light manufacturing, processing, or fabrication nature, while providing appropriate safeguards for adjoining industrial and nonindustrial properties.

**Typical allowed uses:** Light manufacturing, assembly, processing, fabrication, moderate noise and small-scale assembly-line processes, light metal work.

### I-F — Industrial Flex Zone
**Purpose (MMC §§ 17.12.010(N), 17.16.060(C), 17.24.035):** Provides a broader mix of light industrial and specialty or destination commercial uses, and services for employees/businesses in the vicinity. Added to the zoning code by ordinance amending §§ 17.12.010(N), 17.16.060(C), 17.20.060 (Column I-F), and 17.24.035.

**Typical allowed uses:** Makerspaces, small business incubators, start-up spaces, business offices, service-based commercial (restaurants, brew pubs, coffee houses), light industrial.

## Zoning Clearance Requirement

**All changes of use or ownership on commercial/industrial property** require a **Zoning Clearance** (MMC § 17.44) before building permits are issued. Submit the Zoning Clearance Application to the Planning Division.

## Planned Development Permit (PDP)

A **Planned Development Permit** is required in all commercial and industrial zones for:
- New construction of **50,000 sq ft or more** of industrial building floor area (threshold is 10,000 sq ft for commercial zones)

PDP applications are processed through the Planning Commission with a public hearing.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP)

Uses marked "C" in Table 17.20.060 require a **Conditional Use Permit**. CUPs are heard by the Planning Commission. The Commission must adopt written findings by resolution based on substantial evidence in the record (MMC § 17.44.040). A CUP is not a matter of right; it may be approved, conditionally approved, or denied.

**Universal Discretionary Permit Application** (covers CUP, GPAs, Planned Developments, Variances, Specific Plans, Subdivisions):

## Pre-Application Process

For complex projects, a **pre-application meeting** ($1,400 fee) is available to gather agency comments before formal submittal. Contact Philip Neumann at (805) 517-6230 to schedule.

## Key Restrictions

- **Outdoor storage / shipping containers:** Shipping containers are **generally prohibited** on commercial or industrial properties as permanent storage. A **Temporary Use Permit (TUP)** may allow them for up to 90 days (extendable 90 days) (per city FAQ).
- **Performance standards:** M-1 and M-2 zones have noise and operational standards; M-2 allows moderate noise levels and light metal work (MMC § 17.36 and § 17.40.110).
