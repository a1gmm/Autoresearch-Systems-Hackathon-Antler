### 2. Fire Code (AHJ — Fire Permits & Hazmat)

- **AHJ:** Ventura County Fire Department (VCFD), Fire Prevention Bureau — serves City of Moorpark under contract
- **Adopted code:** VCFD enacted **Ordinance No. 34** ("Ventura County Fire Code" / VCFC and Ventura County Wildland-Urban Interface Code / VCWUIC), effective **January 1, 2026**. City of Moorpark Building & Safety confirms the **2025 California Fire Code** is the base edition enforced locally.
- **Local amendments:** Local Ord. 34 introduces a "Ventura County Fire Code" with local modifications; specific amendment text on VCFD site.
- **Fire permit application:** All construction and operational fire code permits use **Form 610 (Fire Permit Application)** — submit to VCFD Fire Prevention Bureau.
- **Hazmat permits:** Administered through the CUPA (see above); VCFD Fire Prevention Bureau coordinates on hazardous materials storage/use permits.
- **High-pile combustible storage:** **Form FP 13.5.6 (High-Piled Combustible Storage Permit Application)** required; filed with Fire Permit Application (Form 610).
- **Fee schedule (FY 2024-2025):** https://fire.venturacounty.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Fee-Schedule-2024-2025-Final.pdf — FY 2025-2026 schedule expected July 1, 2025; check https://fire.venturacounty.gov/ordinances-and-fees/ for current PDF
- **VCFD contact:** vcfd@venturacounty.gov | (805) 389-9710
- **Online permitting:** Ventura County One-Stop Permitting — https://onestoppermits.venturacounty.gov/departments/fire-department
- **Applications/forms page:** https://fire.venturacounty.gov/fire-prevention-applications-forms/

### 5. Zoning / Land Use (Industrial)

- **Governing code:** Moorpark Municipal Code **Title 17 — Zoning** (eCode360: https://ecode360.com/44075660)
- **Industrial zone names (Chapter 17.16 / Table 17.20.060):**
  - **M-1 (Industrial Park Zone):** Light industrial, service, technical research, and related business office uses in an industrial park setting; stringent design, noise, and landscaping standards.
  - **M-2 (Limited Industrial Zone):** Broader range of light manufacturing, processing, and fabrication; appropriate safeguards for adjacent properties.
  - **I-F (Industrial Flex Zone):** Mix of light industrial + specialty/destination commercial (makerspaces, incubators, offices, restaurants, brew pubs); broadest use mix.
- **Permitted uses:** Set forth in **Table 17.20.060** (uses by zone); conditional uses marked "C" require a Conditional Use Permit.
- **Zoning clearance:** Any change of ownership or use of commercial/industrial property requires a **Commercial/Industrial Zoning Clearance** from the Planning Division before building permits issue.
- **CUP process:** Universal Discretionary Permit Application covers CUP, GPAs, Planned Developments, Variances — https://moorparkca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/5569/Universal-Application
- **Planning Division contact:** planning@moorparkca.gov | Blake Erickson, Assistant Planner (805) 517-6258
