## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials / Unified Program

- **AHJ:** Ventura County Resource Management Agency (RMA), Environmental Health Division — countywide CUPA for unincorporated areas and most cities.
- **Six unified programs administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste/Tiered Permitting, Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), CalARP (California Accidental Release Program), Voluntary Cleanup Program.
- **Local ordinance:** Ventura County Ordinance Code, Division 4 (Public Health), Chapter 5 — Hazardous Substances.
- **HMBP submission:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov (state portal; required as of 2013-01-01).
- **Fee schedule (FY 2025-2026):** https://rma.venturacounty.gov/divisions/environmental-health/environmental-health-division-fees/ — fee PDFs linked from that page; online credit-card payment available for annual permit fees.
- **Contact:** RMA Environmental Health, 800 S Victoria Ave, Ventura CA 93009; (805) 654-2494; CUPA Manager Rick Bandelin (805) 654-2815.
- **GOTCHA — two city carve-outs:**
  - City of **Oxnard** — own full CUPA (Oxnard Fire Dept administers all six programs).
  - City of **Ventura (San Buenaventura)** — Participating Agency under County RMA CUPA; Ventura City Fire implements HMBP, APSA, CalARP, and UST within city limits.

Source: https://rma.venturacounty.gov/divisions/environmental-health/cupa-certified-unified-program-agency/

### 2. Fire Code — AHJ and Local Amendments

- **AHJ:** Ventura County Fire Protection District (VCFPD) for unincorporated areas and most contract cities.
  - Exceptions: City of Oxnard (Oxnard Fire Dept), City of Ventura (Ventura City Fire Dept).
- **Adopted fire code:** VCFPD **Ordinance No. 34** (effective **January 1, 2026**) — adopts the **2025 California Fire Code** (CFC) with Appendix Chapters D and P, portions of the 2024 International Fire Code (IFC), the 2025 California Wildland-Urban Interface Code (CWUIC), portions of the 2024 IWUIC, and Title 19 CCR, all with local additions/deletions. Repeals Ordinances 29 and 32.
- **Wildland-Urban Interface:** VCFPD Ordinance No. 33 (effective July 1, 2025) adopts 2025 Local Responsibility Area Fire Hazard Severity Zones.
- **Permit portal:** https://onestoppermits.venturacounty.gov/departments/fire-department (One-Stop Permitting) and https://vcca.venturacounty.gov/citizenaccess (Citizen Access for plan submittals).
- **Fee schedule (FY 2025-2026, effective July 1, 2025):** PDF linked from https://fire.venturacounty.gov/ordinances-and-fees/ — fees charged at hourly staff review rate plus fixed permit/plan-review fees.
- **High-pile combustible storage permits:** Required form "High-Piled Combustible Storage" must accompany fire code permit application.
- **Fire Prevention contact:** (805) 389-9738; inspections (805) 389-9744; counter at 2400 Conejo Spectrum St, Thousand Oaks CA 91320; vcfd@venturacounty.gov.

Source: https://fire.venturacounty.gov/ordinances-and-fees/

### 3. Industrial Wastewater — Pretreatment / IWP

- **Structure:** No single county-level wastewater pretreatment authority for unincorporated areas — served by individual city utilities or special water/sewer districts. Industrial users must identify their serving POTW.
- **City of Ventura Water (Ventura Water):** Has its own industrial pretreatment program and issues Industrial User (IU) permits for discharges to Ventura's sewer system. Contact: https://www.cityofventura.ca.gov/1913/Industrial-Pretreatment.
- **County Ordinance Code reference (Airport area only):** Division 6, Chapter 5, Article 15 governs industrial discharge permits for Ventura County Airport service area (§ 6515-3 et seq.).
- **For unincorporated parcels:** Identify the serving sanitation/water district (e.g., Camrosa Water District, Calleguas MWD service area, United Water Conservation District); contact that district directly for IU permit/pretreatment requirements.
- **One-Stop permitting portal (county environmental health):** https://onestoppermits.venturacounty.gov/departments/environmental-health

### 4. Stormwater — MS4 / Industrial NPDES

- **MS4 permit:** Ventura Countywide NPDES MS4 Permit **Order No. R4-2010-0108**, issued by the Los Angeles Regional Water Quality Control Board (LARWQCB). Covers unincorporated County and all 10 incorporated cities.
- **Local program administrator:** Ventura County Public Works Agency, Water Resources Division — County Stormwater Program.
- **Industrial stormwater (state permit):** Industrial facilities discharging to MS4 or waters of the state must obtain coverage under California's General Industrial Stormwater NPDES Permit (State Board SMARTS portal); the county program conducts compliance inspections.
- **Contact:** PWA.CountyStormwater@venturacounty.gov; (805) 650-4064 (illicit discharge reporting); 800 S Victoria Ave, Ventura CA 93009.
- **State board LARWQCB page:** https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/losangeles/water_issues/programs/stormwater/municipal/ventura.html

### 5. Zoning / Land Use — Industrial Districts

- **Zoning authority:** Ventura County RMA, Planning Division — 800 S Victoria Ave, Ventura CA 93009; (805) 654-2494.
- **Controlling ordinance:** Ventura County Non-Coastal Zoning Ordinance (VCNCZO), Division 8, Chapter 1 of the Ventura County Ordinance Code (Ord. No. 4303 base, periodically amended).
- **Industrial zone designations** (VCNCZO Article 4/5):
  - **M1** — Industrial Park Zone
  - **M2** — Limited Industrial Zone
  - **M3** — General Industrial Zone
  - **IND** — Light Industrial Zone
- **Permitted-use lookup:** Section 8105-5 (commercial/industrial uses); development standards in Section 8106-1.2.
- **CUP process:** Certain industrial uses require a Conditional Use Permit; apply through RMA Planning Division or the One-Stop Permitting portal (https://onestoppermits.venturacounty.gov).
- **Zoning lookup tool (APN-based):** https://rma.venturacounty.gov/divisions/planning/whats-my-zoning/
- **Full VCNCZO PDF:** https://docs.vcrma.org/images/pdf/planning/ordinances/VCNCZO_Current.pdf
- **Municode (online code):** https://library.municode.com/ca/ventura_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=DIV8PLDE_CH1ZO

### 6. Air (VCAPCD) — Reference Only

- **AHJ:** Ventura County Air Pollution Control District (VCAPCD) — controls all of Ventura County including all cities.
- Key rules: Rule 10 (permit to operate), Rule 23 (exemptions), Rule 74.19 (graphic arts/solvents).
- Permits: https://www.vcapcd.org/permitting; (805) 645-1400.
