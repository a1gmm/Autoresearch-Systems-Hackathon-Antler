> Research reference for the Ventura CUPA structure.
> City of Ventura is NOT its own CUPA — the certified CUPA of record is Ventura County RMA.
> The City of Ventura Fire Department acts as a Participating Agency (PA) for four of the six programs.
> Contrast with City of Oxnard, which IS a full CUPA administering all six programs independently.

## City of Ventura — Participating Agency (PA)

The City of Ventura Fire Department is a Participating Agency. As a PA, the Fire Department implements the following four CUPA programs within city limits on behalf of the County CUPA:

| # | Program | Acronym | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan | HMBP | Submitted via CERS / Unidocs statewide portal |
| 2 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act / SPCC | APSA | Facilities ≥ 1,320 gal aggregate or any single tank > 660 gal petroleum |
| 3 | California Accidental Release Prevention Program | CalARP | Local implementing agency for RMP submissions above threshold quantities |
| 4 | Underground Storage Tank compliance | UST | Installation, modification, operation, closure, leak response |

## HMBP Program Details

- **Submission system:** CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) via Unidocs statewide portal — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business
- **Who must comply:** Any business storing, using, or handling hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities (generally: ≥ 55 gallons liquid, ≥ 500 lbs solid, ≥ 200 cubic feet compressed gas — per Health & Safety Code § 25507)
- **Initial filing:** Within 30 days of commencing operations with threshold materials onsite
- **Annual review and resubmission:** Required each year
- **Update triggers:** Material quantity increase ≥ 100%; new previously-undisclosed materials; address/ownership/name changes; significant operational changes
- **City PA review and inspection:** Ventura Fire Prevention Division inspects facilities for HMBP compliance within city limits; inspection frequency [VERIFY — contact Brett Reed at (805) 320-8337]

## UST Program Details (City PA)

- Ventura Fire Department handles UST permitting and inspections within city limits
- UST permits, modifications, and closures go through Ventura Fire Prevention Division
- Unauthorized releases: immediate verbal notification to Fire Dept; written report per state requirements

## CalARP (City PA)

- Ventura Fire Department is the local implementing agency (LIA) for CalARP within city limits
- Facilities with regulated substances above CalARP threshold quantities must file an RMP with USEPA and coordinate local review with Ventura Fire Prevention Division
- [VERIFY] Whether city has any locally adopted CalARP thresholds below state baseline

## APSA / Aboveground Storage Tanks (City PA)

- Facilities storing petroleum above APSA thresholds (aggregate ≥ 1,320 gallons aboveground, or any single tank > 660 gallons) must maintain a Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure (SPCC) plan
- Ventura Fire CUPA PA inspects for APSA compliance within city limits

## Relationship to County CUPA

- For the two programs NOT administered by the PA (or for any uncertainty about program scope), contact Ventura County RMA Environmental Health: (805) 654-2815
- Oxnard, by contrast, is a full CUPA — City of Oxnard Fire Dept administers all six programs; Oxnard CUPA does NOT interact with Ventura County RMA for this purpose
- Unincorporated Ventura County areas: Ventura County RMA administers all six programs directly
