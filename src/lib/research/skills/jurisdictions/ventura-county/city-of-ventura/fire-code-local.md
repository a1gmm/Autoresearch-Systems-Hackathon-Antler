## Local Amendments (2025 CFC Cycle)

- **General approach:** The city adopts local amendments that are at minimum as stringent as state code, justified by local climatic, geological, or topographical conditions. The majority of amendments in the 2025 cycle are continuations of prior-cycle (2022) amendments — not new requirements.
- **Notable amendment topics confirmed in prior cycle and carried forward:**
  - Open burning / recreational fires: operational permit required (SBMC § 14.10.020)
  - Hazardous vegetation / fuel management in high fire severity zones
  - Landscape plan submittals
  - Emergency response cost reimbursement from responsible parties

## Hazardous Materials Operational Permits

Under the 2025 CFC (and prior 2022 CFC), the following trigger a Ventura City Fire Department operating permit:

- Hazardous materials stored > 55 gallons (liquid) / 500 lbs (solid) / 200 cubic feet (gas) for more than 30 days — per Health & Safety Code § 25503.5 and CFC § 105.1
- Flammable and combustible liquid storage (CFC § 105.6.16)
- Cryogenic fluids (CFC § 105.6.10)
- Spray booths (CFC § 105.6.41)
- Refrigerant systems > 220 lb Group A1 (CFC § 105.6.38)
- Underground storage tanks (UST) — jointly administered with CUPA PA program

**Note:** Ventura Fire Department is a CUPA Participating Agency (PA) for four programs (HMBP, APSA, CalARP, UST). CFC hazmat operational permits and the HMBP are related but distinct filings — see cupa-local.md.

## Relationship to County and State

- The City of Ventura (San Buenaventura) is its own fire AHJ — not served by the Ventura County Fire Protection District or CAL FIRE for structural fire/code enforcement within city limits.
- Ventura County Fire Protection District Ordinance No. 28 governs unincorporated county areas and other contract cities — does NOT apply within City of Ventura.
