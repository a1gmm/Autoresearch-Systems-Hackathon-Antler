## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA (Unified Program)

- **Not its own CUPA.** The certified CUPA of record is **Ventura County Resource Management Agency (RMA)**, Environmental Health Division. RMA manager: Rick Bandelin, (805) 654-2815.
- **City of Ventura Fire Department is a Participating Agency (PA)** — implements four of the six CUPA programs within city limits: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act / SPCC (APSA), CalARP, and Underground Storage Tank (UST) compliance.
- City PA contact: Brett Reed, (805) 320-8337. Fire Prevention offices at 1425 Dowell Drive, Ventura, CA 93003; hours Mon–Fri 8 am–5 pm (closed alternate Fridays).
- HMBP submissions go through **Unidocs** (the statewide portal); new facilities must submit within 30 days of commencing operations.
- GOTCHA: contrast with City of Oxnard, which IS its own full CUPA.
- Source: https://www.cityofventura.ca.gov/182/CUPA
- Source: https://rma.venturacounty.gov/divisions/environmental-health/cupa-certified-unified-program-agency/

### 2. Fire Code — AHJ: Ventura City Fire Department

- **Adopted code:** 2022 California Fire Code (CFC), effective 2023-01-01, with local amendments. The city also adopted the 2022 editions of CBC, CEC, CMC, CPC, and CRC on the same cycle.
- Earlier adoption on record: CFC 2019 (superseded). Confirm 2022 CFC is current before quoting.
- **Permit AHJ:** Ventura City Fire Department, Fire Prevention Division, (805) 658-4717.
- **Hazmat operating permits** (CFC §§ 105.1.1 & 105.1.2; Health & Safety Code § 25503.5): required for storage of hazardous materials exceeding 55 gal / 500 lb / 200 cf for >30 days. Permit types: flammable & combustible liquids (CFC § 105.6.16), cryogenic fluids (CFC § 105.6.10), spray booths (CFC § 105.6.41), refrigerant systems >220 lb Group A1 (CFC § 105.6.38).
- **Hazmat permit submissions:** 501 Poli Street, Room 117, Ventura, CA 93001.

### 3. Industrial Wastewater / Pretreatment

- **POTW / administering agency:** Ventura Water Environmental Compliance (city utility), 336 Sanjon Road, Ventura, CA 93002; (805) 667-6500; after-hours emergencies (805) 650-8010.
- **NPDES permit:** Order R4-2025-00272, NPDES No. CA0064726 (Los Angeles RWQCB).
- **Sewer Use Ordinance:** San Buenaventura Municipal Code, **Division 22, Part 2 — Sewer Service** (governs prohibited discharges, local limits, and pretreatment requirements).
- **Local discharge limits:** Municipal Code §§ 22.230 & 22.240; Local Limits Evaluation Report available at https://www.cityofventura.ca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/22903/Local-discharge-limits-and-prohibited-discharge-standards

  - *Non-Domestic Wastewater Discharge Permit* — for significant industrial users (SIUs) subject to 40 CFR 405–471 categorical standards, or discharging ≥25,000 gpd process wastewater.
  - *General Wastewater Discharge Permit* — for smaller commercial/industrial facilities designated by the city.
- **Application:** Non-Domestic Wastewater Discharge Permit Application (PDF): https://www.cityofventura.ca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/23064/VW-07-IU-BMR-Application-Fillable

### 4. Stormwater (MS4)

### 5. Zoning / Land Use

- **Zoning code:** San Buenaventura Municipal Code, **Title/Division 24 — Zoning Regulations**. Zones and development standards in Chapter 24S.200.
- **Industrial district names:** M-1 (Limited Industrial) and M-2 (General Industrial). M-2 allows greater height (up to 6 stories / 75 ft max) vs. M-1; both carry 20 ft front setback, 10 ft interior side, 20 ft street-side, 20 ft rear, 50% lot coverage, 15% landscaping.
- **Note:** Some industrial tracts have Special Development Criteria (tract-specific requirements above the base ordinance; check by tract number).
- **CUP process:** City of Ventura Planning Division administers conditional use permits. Applications submitted via Ventura OPS portal: https://land.cityofventura.ca.gov/EnerGov_Prod/SelfService#/home
- **Planning contact:** (805) 654-7893; Planning Division page: https://www.cityofventura.ca.gov/432/Planning
- **Permit Center:** https://www.cityofventura.ca.gov/2544/Permit-Center
