> Research reference for Ventura Water's industrial pretreatment / source control program.
> The City of Ventura operates its own POTW and maintains an active Industrial Pretreatment Program — this is NOT a county-administered program.
> The city has both a Non-Domestic Wastewater Discharge Permit (for SIUs) and a General Wastewater Discharge Permit (for smaller designees).

## Industrial Wastewater Permit Types

### Non-Domestic Wastewater Discharge Permit (SIU Permit)
- **Who must obtain:** Significant Industrial Users (SIUs) — facilities subject to 40 CFR 405–471 categorical pretreatment standards (e.g., metal finishing, electroplating, pharmaceutical manufacturing, etc.), OR facilities discharging ≥ 25,000 gallons per day of process wastewater
- **Application form (fillable PDF):** https://www.cityofventura.ca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/23064/VW-07-IU-BMR-Application-Fillable
- This permit imposes: self-monitoring and reporting requirements, sampling frequencies, compliance schedules if limits are being exceeded, slug discharge control plans

### General Wastewater Discharge Permit
- **Who must obtain:** Smaller commercial/industrial facilities that the city designates as requiring a permit, even if not subject to federal categorical standards or the 25,000 gpd threshold
- **Application:** Contact Ventura Water Environmental Compliance at (805) 667-6500 for the General permit application form

## Pretreatment Program Compliance

- **Inspections:** Ventura Water Environmental Compliance conducts site inspections, discharge sampling, and self-monitoring report reviews
- **Discharge sampling:** Periodic sampling by city inspectors to verify compliance with local limits and categorical pretreatment standards
- **Self-monitoring:** SIU permittees are required to self-sample and submit periodic monitoring reports (frequency set in individual permit)
- **Slug discharge control:** SIU permittees are typically required to have a slug discharge notification plan; contact Ventura Water for specifics

## Industrial Pretreatment Program Status

The City of Ventura maintains an **active Industrial Pretreatment Program (IPP)** approved under 40 CFR 403. This is a medium-sized coastal California city with real industrial activity — the program is not nominal. Any facility discharging process wastewater to the city sewer system should contact Ventura Water Environmental Compliance before commencing operations to determine permit requirements.
