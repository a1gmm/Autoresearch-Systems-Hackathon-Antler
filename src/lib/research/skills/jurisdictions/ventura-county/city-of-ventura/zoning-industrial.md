> Research reference for industrial zoning in the City of San Buenaventura (Ventura).
> Zoning code is SBMC Title 24 — Zoning Regulations. Industrial zones are M-1 (Limited Industrial) and M-2 (General Industrial).
> CUP applications go through the City Planning Division via the Ventura OPS online portal.

## Zoning Code Reference

- **Code title:** San Buenaventura Municipal Code (SBMC) **Title 24 — Zoning Regulations**
- **Industrial zone chapters:** SBMC Chapter 24.260 (M-1 Limited Industrial Zone) and Chapter 24.270 (M-2 General Industrial Zone) [VERIFY Chapter 24.270 number — confirmed M-1 is 24.260; M-2 chapter number derived from code structure; confirm against live code]
- **General provisions:** SBMC Chapter 24.200 et seq. (Zones and Development Standards)
- **CUP procedure:** SBMC § 24.380.020 (Use Permit Required)
- **Saticoy & Wells area:** Has its own Development Code (Saticoy & Wells Development Code, Chapter 24S.200) with transect sub-zones; industrial/mixed-use in this area follows the 24S.200 framework rather than M-1/M-2 — confirm zone designation by parcel before applying M-1/M-2 standards
- **Full zoning code (Municode):** https://library.municode.com/ca/san_buenaventura/codes/code_of_ordinances (navigate to Division 24)
- **Zoning code (sanbuenaventura.municipal.codes):** https://sanbuenaventura.municipal.codes/SBMC/24
- **Coastal Zoning Code** (separate code for coastal overlay area — also contains M-1 zone at CZC Chapter 24.260): https://sanbuenaventura.municipal.codes/CZC/24.260

## Permitted and Conditional Uses in M-1 / M-2

- **Permitted uses:** Light manufacturing, warehousing, distribution, wholesale trade, auto repair (M-1); heavier manufacturing, bulk storage, certain hazmat operations (M-2 with CUP)
- **Conditional uses (require CUP):** Any use not listed as outright permitted; hazardous material storage above certain quantities; facilities with significant traffic, noise, or environmental impact — specific use tables are at SBMC § 24.260.020 (M-1) and the equivalent M-2 section
- **Use permit required:** SBMC § 24.380.020 — any use designated "C" in the land use table requires a conditional use permit
- [VERIFY] Specific uses requiring CUP in M-1 vs. M-2 against the current land use tables in SBMC Chapter 24.260

## CUP Process

1. **Pre-application:** Contact Planning Division at (805) 654-7893 or visit the Permit Center at 501 Poli St., Room 117, Ventura, CA 93001 (Mon–Thu 8:00 am–1:00 pm walk-in; 1:00–3:00 pm by appointment)
2. **Application submission:** Via Ventura OPS online portal: https://land.cityofventura.ca.gov/EnerGov_Prod/SelfService#/home
3. **Review:** Planning Division processes and circulates for departmental comments (Fire, Public Works, etc.)
4. **Hearing:** Conditional use permits typically go to Zoning Administrator or Planning Commission depending on project type and size [VERIFY hearing body threshold]
5. **Approval/conditions:** Conditions of approval may include fire code compliance requirements, environmental mitigation measures, and operational restrictions
6. **CEQA:** Projects above categorical exemption thresholds require CEQA review; City acts as lead agency
