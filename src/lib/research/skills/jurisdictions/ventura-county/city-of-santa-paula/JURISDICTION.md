## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials & Waste

- **CUPA agency**: Ventura County Resource Management Agency (RMA) — Environmental Health Division — Hazardous Materials Program
- **Jurisdiction scope**: All incorporated and unincorporated areas of Ventura County **except** the City of Oxnard. Santa Paula falls under county CUPA jurisdiction.
- **Participating-agency note (GOTCHA)**: The Ventura County Ordinance Code (Div. 4, Ch. 5) previously listed the *Santa Paula Fire Department* as a Participating Agency responsible for implementing HMBP/RMP and SPCC elements within the city. The Santa Paula FD was dissolved July 8, 2018 and annexed into VCFD. Confirm with county RMA whether VCFD Station 26/29 or the county directly now holds that PA role for HMBP inspections in Santa Paula.
- **Programs administered**: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste/Tiered Permitting, Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage (APSA), CalARP
- **Governing ordinance**: Ventura County Ordinance Code, Division 4 (Public Health), Chapter 5 (Hazardous Substances)
- **Contact**: 800 S. Victoria Ave, Ventura, CA 93009 | (805) 654-2494 | Manager: Rick Bandelin (805) 654-2815
- **Fee schedule**: https://rmadocs.venturacounty.gov/environmental-health/fees/publications/environmental-health-hazardous-materials-programs-schedule-of-fees.pdf
- **CUPA program page**: https://rma.venturacounty.gov/divisions/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-program-cupa/
- Source: https://rma.venturacounty.gov/divisions/environmental-health/cupa-certified-unified-program-agency/

### 2. Fire Code — AHJ & Permits

- **AHJ**: Ventura County Fire Department (VCFD) — Santa Paula served by Stations 26 and 29 (since July 8, 2018)
- **Adopted fire code**: VCFD Ordinance No. 34 — adopts the **2025 California Fire Code** (with Appendices D and P), portions of the 2024 International Fire Code, and the 2025 California Wildland-Urban Interface Code. Effective **January 1, 2026**. (Repeals Ord. 29 and Ord. 32.)
- **Previous code**: Ord. 31 adopted 2019 CFC; Ord. 32 adopted 2022 CFC — now superseded by Ord. 34.
- **Fire prevention permits**: Required for hazardous materials storage, high-pile combustible storage, and other CFC-regulated operations. Submit via Ventura County One-Stop Permitting portal or at the Fire Prevention Counter.
- **Fire Prevention Counter**: 2400 Conejo Spectrum St, Thousand Oaks, CA 91320 | Hours: 7:30 am – 5:00 pm
- **General VCFD**: (805) 389-9710 | vcfd@venturacounty.gov
- **Fee schedule (2025-2026, eff. July 1, 2025)**: https://s48417.pcdn.co/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Fee-Schedule-2025-2026.pdf
- **Ordinances & fees page**: https://fire.venturacounty.gov/ordinances-and-fees/
- Source: https://fire.venturacounty.gov/ordinances-and-fees/ | https://spcity.org/804/Fire

### 3. Industrial Wastewater — POTW & Pretreatment

- **POTW operator**: Ventura Regional Sanitation District (VRSD) operates the Santa Paula Water Recycling Facility (capacity 3.4 MGD) on behalf of the City
- **Facility address**: 921 Calpipe Road, Santa Paula, CA 93060 | (805) 933-1254
- **VRSD contact**: (805) 389-9406
- **Local sewer-use code**: Santa Paula Municipal Code **Chapter 51** (Wastewater / Sewer)
- **Industrial discharge permit**: § 51.110 INDUSTRIAL WASTEWATER DISCHARGE PERMIT — unlawful to discharge industrial wastewater to city sewer without a city permit. Permit conditions may include pretreatment requirements, peak-flow restrictions, point-of-discharge constraints, discharge-hour limits, and surcharges for elevated loading. Last amended: Ordinance No. 1250 (May 2013).
- **Permit/billing contact**: Sewer customers 805-933-4211 (press 1)
- **Wastewater page**: https://spcity.org/248/Wastewater
- Source: https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/santapaula/latest/santapaula_ca/0-0-0-51021

- **Local ordinance**: Santa Paula Municipal Code **Chapter 54** (Stormwater Quality Management)
- **Stormwater program page**: https://spcity.org/244/Storm-Water
- **Countywide program**: https://vcstormwater.org/
- **City contact**: 970 Ventura St, Santa Paula, CA 93060 | (805) 525-4478 | M–F 9 am–4 pm (closed alternate Fridays)

### 5. Zoning / Land Use

- **Zoning code**: Santa Paula Municipal Code **Title 16** (Development Code)
- **Industrial zones chapter**: **Chapter 16.21** (Industrial Zones) — M-1 (Manufacturing) designation confirmed
- **Other zone chapters**: Ch. 16.13 Residential, Ch. 16.15 Commercial, Ch. 16.25 Specific Plan Zone, Ch. 16.42 Performance Standards
- **CUP process**: Conditional use permits for industrial uses are governed within Title 16; consult Planning Division for project-specific determination
- **Performance standards**: Chapter 16.42 governs noise, odor, vibration, and other nuisance controls for industrial operations — relevant to EHS siting reviews
- **Cannabis/hemp restriction**: Chapter 16.75 prohibits industrial hemp cultivation
- **Planning Division contact**: 200 S. 10th St, Santa Paula, CA 93060 | plan@spcity.org | (805) 933-4214 | M–F 8:30 am–Noon & 1–4:30 pm (closed alternate Fridays)
