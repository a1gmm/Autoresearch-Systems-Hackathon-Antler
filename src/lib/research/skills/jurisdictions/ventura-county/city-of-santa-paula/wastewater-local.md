# Industrial Wastewater — City of Santa Paula

## POTW / Treatment Facility

**Santa Paula Water Recycling Facility**
- **Operator**: Ventura Regional Sanitation District (VRSD), operating on behalf of the City
- **Address**: 921 Calpipe Road, Santa Paula, CA 93060
- **Design capacity**: 3.4 MGD (expandable to 4.2 MGD)
- **Facility phone**: (805) 933-1254

VRSD is a regional special district serving eight cities (including Santa Paula, Camarillo, Fillmore, Ojai, Oxnard, Port Hueneme, Thousand Oaks, and Ventura) plus unincorporated Ventura County. Santa Paula has been a VRSD member/client agency for wastewater operations.

## Sewer-Use Ordinance

**Santa Paula Municipal Code Chapter 51 (Wastewater Services)**

The chapter governs sewer connections, discharge standards, and industrial wastewater permits. The most recent codified amendment identified is **Ordinance No. 1250 (May 2013)**.

Key provision:

**§ 51.110 — Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit**
> It is unlawful for any person to discharge or cause to be discharged any industrial wastewater directly or indirectly to any city sewer without first obtaining a city permit for industrial wastewater discharge.

Permit conditions that may be imposed under § 51.110 include:
- Pretreatment of industrial wastewaters before discharge
- Restriction of peak flow discharges
- Discharge of certain wastewaters only to specified city sewers
- Relocation of point of discharge
- Prohibition of discharge of certain wastewater components
- Restriction of discharge to certain hours of the day
- Payment of additional surcharges to defray increased costs created by the discharge
- Other conditions as required to effectuate the purpose of the chapter

## Industrial Pretreatment Program

**No standalone city Industrial Pretreatment Program (IPP) was identified online** for Santa Paula. This is consistent with the city's size and largely rural/agricultural character. The VRSD-operated facility may handle pretreatment oversight, but VRSD's public website does not describe an active IPP for the Santa Paula service area. [VERIFY — contact VRSD at (805) 389-9406 to confirm whether a formal 40 CFR Part 403 pretreatment program exists or whether Santa Paula operates under general categorical standards only]

The § 51.110 permit mechanism in the municipal code is the city's primary local tool for controlling industrial discharges, even absent a full federally-approved pretreatment program.

## Stormwater (Related — MS4)

Industrial dischargers must also consult Santa Paula Municipal Code **Chapter 54** (Stormwater Quality Management) and the Ventura Countywide MS4 Permit (NPDES No. CAS004002). Industrial stormwater discharges are separately regulated under SWPPP/IGP requirements administered by the Regional Water Quality Control Board — not the city's wastewater program.
