# Fire Code — City of Santa Paula

## Fire AHJ

Santa Paula is served by the **Ventura County Fire Department (VCFD)** — **Stations 26 and 29** — since July 8, 2018, when the former City of Santa Paula Fire Department was dissolved and annexed into VCFD. All fire-code enforcement, plan review, and fire prevention permits are administered by VCFD, not the city.

## Adopted Fire Code

**VCFD Ordinance No. 34** — effective **January 1, 2026** — is the controlling ordinance. It repeals Ordinances 29 and 32.

Ordinance 34 adopts by reference:
- **2025 California Fire Code** (CCR Title 24, Part 9), together with **Appendix Chapters D and P**
- Specified portions of the **2024 International Fire Code** (IFC 2024), with deletions listed in Table 1.4.3 (deleted IFC sections include 104.8.1, 105.5.34, mobile-food-prep definitions, §§ 308.1.5/308.1.5.1, §§ 503.1.1–503.6, 805.1, 4106, and Appendices A/E/F/G/I/J/K/L/M)
- Specified portions of the **2025 California Wildland-Urban Interface Code** (CCR Title 24, Part 7), together with Appendix Chapter A (Chapter 5 excepted)
- Specified portions of the **2024 International Wildland-Urban Interface Code**, with deletions in Table 1.4.4
- Applicable portions of **CCR Title 19, Division 1** (State Fire Marshal regulations)

The combined document is formally titled the **Ventura County Fire Code (VCFC)** and **Ventura County Wildland-Urban Interface Code (VCWUIC)**.

## Key Local Amendments (More-Restrictive Standards)

VCFD has filed more-restrictive findings under Health & Safety Code § 13869.7. The principal deviations from the 2025 CFC are in **Article 3, Section 903** (automatic fire sprinklers) and **Article 3, Appendix B** (fire-flow):

| Amendment | Substance |
|---|---|
| § 903.2 (amended) | Automatic fire sprinkler system required in **all new buildings** (with narrow exceptions for Group U occupancies approved by Fire Code Official and small agricultural produce stands meeting VCBC criteria) |
| § 903.1.2 (added) | Concurrent-permit rule: additions/modifications where combined open-permit area exceeds § 903.2 thresholds trigger whole-building sprinkler retrofit |
| § 903.1.3 (added) | Area-separation walls used to create sub-5,000 sf fire areas in pre-Nov-2002 buildings must be maintained; loss of wall integrity triggers retrofit |
| § 903.1.4 (added) | Change of use from Group U to any other occupancy classification that would trigger sprinklers triggers full sprinkler installation |
| Article 4, § 604.11 | WUI-specific local standard (filed with HCD) |
| Appendix B | More-restrictive fire-flow requirements |

## Municipal Code — City-Level Fire Chapter

Santa Paula Municipal Code **Chapter 150** (Building Regulations) contains the city's local adoption of the California Fire Code by reference (section 150.035). The chapter title in the amlegal codification is "CALIFORNIA FIRE CODE." Prior to VCFD annexation in 2018, city plan-check documents referenced "California Fire Code, as amended by the City of Santa Paula Municipal Code" with the city fire department as AHJ. Post-annexation, VCFD Ord. 34 controls for fire prevention; city Building & Safety handles building-permit plan check in coordination with VCFD.

## Fire Hazard Severity Zone

VCFD Ordinance 33 (eff. July 1, 2025) adopted updated 2025 LRA Fire Hazard Severity Zone maps. Portions of Santa Paula's wildland-urban interface are in High or Very High FHSZ. Properties in High/Very High FHSZ are subject to AB 38 defensible-space inspection requirements at point of sale (VCFD Ord. 34, § 617).
