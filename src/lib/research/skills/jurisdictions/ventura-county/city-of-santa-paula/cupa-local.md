# CUPA — City of Santa Paula

## Controlling CUPA

Santa Paula falls under the **Ventura County Resource Management Agency (RMA) — Environmental Health Division** as CUPA. The county CUPA jurisdiction covers all incorporated and unincorporated areas of Ventura County **except** the City of Oxnard (which has its own CUPA) and the City of Ventura (which is a Participating Agency with separate fire-department enforcement of certain programs).

**CUPA defined in ordinance**: Ventura County Ordinance Code, Division 4, Chapter 5, Article 1, § 4501(e) — "CUPA jurisdiction means all of the unincorporated and incorporated areas of Ventura County, except for the incorporated area of the City of Oxnard."

## Participating Agency — Critical History

**Ventura County Ordinance Code § 4501(h)** (as printed in the current ordinance) defines "Participating agency" to include **the City of Santa Paula Fire Department**. Under § 4503, the Santa Paula Fire Department was authorized to implement the following unified program elements in Santa Paula's incorporated area:
- Hazardous Materials Business Plan / Risk Management Plan (HMBP/RMP)
- Spill Prevention Control and Countermeasure Plan (SPCC)
- Hazardous Materials Management Plan and Hazardous Materials Inventory Statement

**However**: The Santa Paula Fire Department was **dissolved July 8, 2018** and annexed into VCFD. The VCFD Station 26 / Station 29 now serves Santa Paula. The county CUPA ordinance still references the "City of Santa Paula Fire Department" as a participating agency, but that department no longer exists as a separate entity.

Note from § 4503: "If at any time certification of any participating agency with respect to any of these elements is terminated, the CUPA shall assume responsibility for such elements unless otherwise directed by the State of California." ## Programs Administered (County CUPA)

| Program | Notes |
|---|---|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) | CalEPA CERS submission; annual inspection |
| Hazardous Waste / Tiered Permitting | License required for HW producers (§ 4512) |
| Underground Storage Tanks (UST) | County CUPA administers in Santa Paula (UST was not delegated to the city PA) |
| Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) | County CUPA |
| CalARP (California Accidental Release Prevention) | County CUPA |
| Voluntary Cleanup Program | County CUPA |

## Governing Ordinance

**Ventura County Ordinance Code, Division 4 (Public Health), Chapter 5 (Hazardous Substances)**
- Article 1 — Unified Program Generally (§§ 4500–4509): administrative structure, fees, participating agencies
- Article 1.1 — Hazardous Waste Producers (§§ 4510–): licensing, fees
- Additional articles cover UST, APSA, CalARP, and SPCC

Full text: https://rmadocs.venturacounty.gov/images/pdf/eh/cupa/VCORDINANCECODE.pdf

## Fees

- **Basic inspection fee**: Formula-based (2.75 × EHS III contract hourly rate, per § 4507); set annually by Board of Supervisors resolution
- **Late payment surcharge**: 10% if paid within 60 days of due date; 30% thereafter (§ 4508)
- **Fee schedule (current)**: https://rmadocs.venturacounty.gov/environmental-health/fees/publications/environmental-health-hazardous-materials-programs-schedule-of-fees.pdf
