## Industrial Zone — Chapter 16.21

**Chapter 16.21: Industrial Zones** establishes the **M-1 (Manufacturing / Light Industrial)** district as Santa Paula's primary industrial zoning designation. The chapter is confirmed in the amlegal codification at: https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/santapaula/latest/santapaula_ca/0-0-0-60957 (returns 403 on direct fetch; confirmed via search engine).

Chapter 16.21 includes the following sections (structure confirmed from search results):
- Purpose and intent
- Land use and permit requirements (including permitted vs. conditionally-permitted uses)
- Development standards (setbacks, lot coverage, building height, parking, loading)
- Accessory uses
- Outdoor use and storage
- Landscaping and buffering requirements
- Design principles

**M-1 zone typical permitted uses**: manufacturing, assembly, light industrial operations, warehousing and distribution, industrial support services. Commercial uses are conditionally permissible in M-1 where they do not undermine the industrial intent of the zone.

**Note on M-2 or heavier zones**: A separate heavy-industrial or M-2 classification was not confirmed from online sources. The specific use table for Chapter 16.21 (permitted by right vs. CUP vs. prohibited) requires direct review of the municipal code or Planning Division. ## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

**Chapter 16.218: Conditional Use Permits** governs the CUP process. Key provision confirmed:

**§ 16.218.090 — Time Limit for Implementing a Conditional Use Permit**: CUPs carry an implementation time limit after approval.

CUPs are required in the M-1 zone for uses that:
- Generate significant noise or vibration
- Involve hazardous materials storage above threshold levels
- Generate heavy vehicle traffic with off-site impacts
- Are commercial in character within the industrial zone

The CUP is processed through the **Planning Commission**. Application submittal to Planning Division at 200 S. 10th St.

Source confirming CUP chapter: http://santapaula-ca.elaws.us/code/muco_txvi_ch16.218_sec16.218.090

## Performance Standards — Chapter 16.42

**Chapter 16.42 — Performance Standards** governs nuisance controls for industrial operations throughout the city, including:
- Noise
- Odor
- Vibration
- Other off-site impacts

These standards are applicable to industrial operations in M-1 zones and are directly relevant to EHS siting reviews. Specific numerical thresholds are in the chapter text; the full chapter requires direct access to the municipal code. [VERIFY — pull Chapter 16.42 for numeric limits]

## West Business Park Specific Plan (SP-6)

The **Santa Paula West Business Park Specific Plan (SP-6)**, adopted December 2018, applies to the West Area 2 annexation area. This specific plan zone overlays and modifies base M-1 standards for parcels within its boundaries. Industrial users in the SP-6 area should consult this plan in addition to Chapter 16.21.

Reference: https://www.goventura.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/ITEM-8G-ATTACHMENT_13-CDP-04-SP-Biz-Pk-W-SP-West-Business-Park-Specific-Plan-SP-6-2018....pdf

## Cannabis / Hemp

**Chapter 16.75** prohibits industrial hemp cultivation in Santa Paula. Commercial cannabis operations are subject to separate restrictions; confirm current status with Planning Division.

## Airport Operational Considerations

Recent ordinances (confirmed in search results) include zone changes related to airport operational considerations affecting M-1 designations near the Santa Paula Airport. Industrial projects within the airport influence zone should consult the Planning Division for overlay requirements.
