## GOTCHA — No Single County-Wide POTW or Pretreatment Authority

Ventura County has **no single county-wide sewer utility or industrial pretreatment program**. The applicable POTW and pretreatment requirements depend entirely on which sanitation district or city utility serves the parcel. Identify the correct POTW before drafting any Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit (IWDP) application.

---

## Key POTWs Serving Industrial Users

### 1. Ventura Water — Ventura Water Reclamation Facility (VWRF)

- **Operator:** City of Ventura (San Buenaventura), Ventura Water Environmental Compliance Division
- **Service area:** City of Ventura limits
- **NPDES permit:** Order R4-2025-00272, Permit No. CA0064726 (Los Angeles RWQCB)
- **Governing ordinance:** San Buenaventura Municipal Code, **Division 22, Part 2 — Sewer Service** (§§ 22.230–22.240 et seq.)
- **Pretreatment program:** Active; implements 40 CFR Part 403 national pretreatment standards

#### Industrial User Permit Types
| Permit Type | Trigger |
|-------------|---------|
| Non-Domestic Wastewater Discharge Permit | Facilities subject to categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR 405–471), or discharging ≥ 25,000 gallons/day of process wastewater, or any user designated by City as adversely impacting the system |
| General Wastewater Discharge Permit | Facilities impacting the sewer system but not meeting federal pretreatment criteria |

#### Application Process
- Submit application at least 90 days before new/changed discharge begins.
- Permits are non-transferable; change of ownership or operation requires new permit.

#### Local Discharge Limits
- Detailed numerical limits are in the **Ventura Local Limits Evaluation Report** and **Industrial Wastewater Prohibited Discharge Standards** (San Buenaventura Municipal Code §§ 22.230–22.240).

## County Ordinance Reference (Airport Area Only)

Ventura County Ordinance Code **Division 6, Chapter 5, Article 15 — Industrial Discharge Permit System** (§ 6515-3 et seq.) applies specifically to the Ventura County Airport service area and establishes the industrial discharge permit conditions for that limited jurisdiction. Permits specify performance specifications that industrial users must meet to maintain authorization to discharge to the county's sewerage system.
