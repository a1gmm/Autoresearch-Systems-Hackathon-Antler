## Industrial Zone Designations (VCNCZO Article 4, Section 8104-5)

| Zone | Full Name | Section | Purpose |
|------|-----------|---------|---------|
| **M1** | Industrial Park Zone | § 8104-5.1 | Exclusive development of light industrial, service, technical research and related business office uses in an industrial park context; stringent standards for building design, noise, landscaping, and performance |
| **M2** | Limited Industrial Zone | § 8104-5.2 | Broad range of industrial and quasi-industrial activities of a light manufacturing, processing, or fabrication nature; safeguards for adjoining sites and nearby nonindustrial properties |
| **M3** | General Industrial Zone | § 8104-5.3 | Broad range of general manufacturing, processing, and fabrication activities; heaviest manufacturing zone; for uses specifically excluded from M1 (high-temperature processes, outdoor storage yards, chemical storage in excess of accessory quantity, explosives, hazardous emissions, assembly-line operations) |
| **IND** | Light Industrial Zone | § 8104-5.4 | Light industrial, manufacturing, and commercial uses compatible with adjacent residential and commercial uses; applies only within the **Old Town Saticoy** community under the Old Town Saticoy Development Code, Article 19 (§ 8119-1.3.4) |

## Permitted Uses (Section 8105-5)

Permitted uses in commercial and industrial zones are set out in the use matrix at **Section 8105-5**, which uses the following entitlement designations:

| Symbol | Entitlement |
|--------|-------------|
| **PD** | Planned Development Permit (Planning Director approval — ministerial or administrative) |
| **CUP** | Conditional Use Permit (discretionary review; Planning Commission or Board of Supervisors) |
| **E** | Exempt (no planning entitlement required) |
| **—** | Not permitted |

Permitted-use matrix is at Municode (§ 8105-5) or in the VCNCZO PDF (Article 5, pages 5-24 et seq.).

## Key M1 Zone Restrictions (§ 8109-3.2)

The following are **not permitted** in M1:
- High temperature processes
- Outdoor storage yards (unless decision-making authority finds no nuisance/visual impact)
- Chemical storage in excess of what is accessory to the principal use (except CESQG hazardous waste collection)
- Storage or use of explosives in any form
- Uses producing obnoxious or dangerous gases, odors, fumes, or smoke
- Assembly-line construction operations

M1 also requires: all operations enclosed within buildings; minimum 10% landscaping of permit area; finished metal exteriors (no bare/unfinished metal); outdoor storage confined to rear of principal building.

## M2 Zone (§ 8109-3.3)

- M1 restrictions also apply to M2, except M2 allows moderate noise levels, small-scale assembly-line processes, and light metal work.
- Minimum 5% landscaping; street-side facade of principal building must be faced with masonry, stone, concrete, or similar material; outside storage confined behind front wall of principal building.

## M3 Zone (§ 8109-3.4)

- Heaviest manufacturing zone — designed for uses excluded from M1.
- Minimum 5% landscaping; outside storage yards must be fenced for security and public safety; metal buildings must have finished exterior or be screened from street view.

## IND Zone (§ 8109-3.5 / § 8104-5.4)

- Applies only to Old Town Saticoy; governed by Old Town Saticoy Development Code, Article 19 (§ 8119-1.3.4).
- Light industrial, manufacturing, and commercial compatible with adjacent residential/commercial.

## Industrial Performance Standards — All Zones (§ 8109-3.1)

All industrial zones must comply with:
- **Underground utility lines** for all new development.
- **Objectionable factors** (measured at lot/ownership line): smoke, odors, vapors, gases, acids, fumes, dust, fly ash, air pollution; noise, vibration, pulsations; glare or heat; radioactivity or electrical disturbance — all must be maintained at levels appropriate for the zone and not objectionable at the point of measurement.
- **Hazardous materials:** All activities involving combustible, explosive, caustic, or hazardous materials must comply with applicable state, local, and federal safety standards and conform to the **Ventura County Fire Code** (Ordinance 34).
- **Liquid and solid wastes:** All liquid/solid wastes discharged from premises must be treated in compliance with applicable law before discharge; no dumping of solid wastes on premises.

## CUP Process

- **Conditional Use Permit (CUP):** Required for certain industrial uses designated "CUP" in the § 8105-5 use matrix.
- **Application:** Submit Discretionary Permit Application to RMA Planning Division: https://rmadocs.venturacounty.gov/planning/forms/planning-discretionary-entitlement-zone-change-and-subdivision-application-packet.pdf
- **Decision authority:** Planning Director (PD permits); Planning Commission or Board of Supervisors (CUP/discretionary).
- **Entitlement types and procedures:** Article 11 (§ 8111-1 et seq.) — ministerial entitlements (§ 8111-1.1) and discretionary entitlements (§ 8111-1.2).
