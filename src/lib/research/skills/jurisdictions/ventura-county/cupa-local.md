## CUPA Agency

- **Agency:** Ventura County Resource Management Agency (RMA), **Environmental Health Division** — the county-wide CUPA.
- **Structure:** County-administered CUPA covering all unincorporated areas of Ventura County and most incorporated cities (see carve-outs below).
- **CUPA type:** County CUPA; not administered by a fire department.
- **Address:** 800 S Victoria Ave, Ventura, CA 93009
- **Main phone:** (805) 654-2494
- **CUPA Manager:** Rick Bandelin, (805) 654-2815

## Programs Administered (All Six)

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — inventory of hazardous materials; submitted via CERS
2. **Hazardous Waste / Tiered Permitting** — generator tiers: Conditionally Exempt (CE), Conditional Authorization (CA), Permit-by-Rule (PBR), Full Permit
3. **Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program** — installation, closure, monitoring, and leak response
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — facilities with aggregate aboveground petroleum storage ≥ 1,320 gallons
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — RMP-equivalent program; Program 1, 2, or 3 depending on process
6. **Voluntary Cleanup Program**

## Governing Local Ordinance

- **Ordinance Code:** Ventura County Ordinance Code, **Division 4 (Public Health), Chapter 5 — Hazardous Substances**
  - Establishes the hazardous materials management regulatory program and fees for the county's CUPA functions.
  - Includes provisions for Emergency Response Contingency Plan and Hazardous Materials Inventory Statement.
- **PDF:** https://rma.venturacounty.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/ventura-county-ordinance-code-division-4-public-health-chapter-5-hazardous-substances.pdf

## CERS Reporting

- All HMBP, CalARP, and UST submissions via California Environmental Reporting System (CERS): https://cers.calepa.ca.gov
- Required electronically as of January 1, 2013.

## GOTCHA — Two City Carve-Outs

- **City of Oxnard:** Oxnard Fire Department administers **all six** CUPA programs for facilities within Oxnard city limits — Oxnard is a full own-CUPA, not under county RMA.
- **City of Ventura (San Buenaventura):** Participating Agency (PA) under county RMA CUPA — Ventura City Fire implements HMBP, APSA, CalARP, and UST within Ventura city limits; county RMA retains hazardous waste generator program for Ventura.
