## CUPA agency

**Tulare County Health & Human Services Agency — Environmental Health Division**
Single Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all of Tulare County.

> IMPORTANT: There is NO city-level CUPA in Tulare County. ALL incorporated cities — including Visalia, Tulare, Porterville, Dinuba, Lindsay, Exeter, Farmersville, and Woodlake — are served by the county CUPA. There are no Participating Agencies. Confirmed by CalEPA 2023 Final Summary of Findings for Tulare: https://calepa.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Tulare-2023-FSOF.pdf

## Programs administered

The Tulare County Environmental Health Division administers all six Unified Program elements:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — facilities that handle/store hazardous materials above threshold quantities must submit via CERS
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator** — oversight of hazardous waste generation, storage, and disposal
3. **Tiered Permitting (Onsite Treatment)** — authorization for onsite treatment of hazardous waste
4. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — permits, inspections, and oversight
5. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — facilities with aggregate petroleum storage ≥1,320 gallons
6. **California Accidental Release Program (CalARP)** — Risk Management Plans for facilities with regulated substances above threshold quantities

## Electronic reporting

All HMBP, hazardous waste, and related submissions must be made through the **California Environmental Reporting System (CERS)** at https://cers.calepa.ca.gov. Tulare County also maintains a local reporting portal at tularecountyeh.org.
