# CUPA — City of Tulare

## Controlling Agency

The City of Tulare is **not its own CUPA**. All Unified Program elements within city limits are administered by:

**Tulare County Health & Human Services Agency — Environmental Health Division**
Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all of Tulare County (incorporated and unincorporated).

- CUPA portal: https://tularecountyeh.org/eh/our-services/hazardous-materials-cupa
- CUPA e-reporting portal: https://www.tcehealth.org/
- Phone: (559) 624-7400 (business hours 7:30 a.m.–5:30 p.m.)
- After hours / spill emergencies: (559) 624-7400 (answering service) or CalEPA (916) 845-8911; also call 911.

## Programs Covered (Six UP Elements)

1. Hazardous Waste Generation (including onsite treatment under Tiered Permitting)
2. Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) / SPCC Plans — tanks ≥ 1,320 gallons petroleum
3. Underground Storage Tanks (UST)
4. Hazardous Materials Release Response Plans and Inventories (HMBP)
5. California Accidental Release Prevention Program (Cal ARP / RMP)
6. Uniform Fire Code Hazardous Materials Management Plans and Inventories

## HMBP Thresholds

A Hazardous Materials Business Plan is required if a business handles or stores:
- ≥ 55 gallons of a hazardous material or hazardous waste, OR
- ≥ 500 pounds, OR
- ≥ 200 cubic feet of compressed gas.

## Hazardous Waste Storage Time Limits

| Monthly generation rate | Max on-site storage |
|-------------------------|---------------------|
| < 100 kg/month | Until 100 kg accumulated, then 180 days |
| 100–1,000 kg/month | 180 days from first drop added |
| > 1,000 kg/month | 90 days |
