# Fire Code — City of Tulare

## Fire AHJ

The City of Tulare operates its own career municipal **Tulare Fire Department** (three stations, approximately 52 sworn personnel). The Fire Department is the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) for fire code enforcement within city limits; it is NOT served by a county fire district or CAL FIRE.

- Fire Department home page: https://www.tulare.ca.gov/government/departments/fire
- Prevention Bureau: https://www.tulare.ca.gov/government/departments/fire/prevention-and-programs/prevention-bureau

| **Prior adoption** | Ord. 19-11, adopted 12-3-2019 (2019 CFC) |

## Municipal Code Structure — Title 3

Title 3 of the Tulare Municipal Code (American Legal Publishing) covers Fire Regulations. Chapter 3.08 is the operative fire prevention code chapter with local amendments.

- Title 3 index: https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/tulare/latest/tulare_ca/0-0-0-898
