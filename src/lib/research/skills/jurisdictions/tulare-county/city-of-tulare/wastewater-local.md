# Wastewater — City of Tulare

## POTW Overview

The City of Tulare owns and operates its own **Wastewater Treatment Facility (WWTF)** located approximately seven miles southwest of the city center (Tulare County). The WWTF is a significant, two-plant facility:

- **Domestic Plant**: Activated sludge, designed for 6.0 mgd (expandable to 8.0 mgd). Serves residential and commercial connections.
- **Industrial Plant**: Separate plant for high-strength industrial wastewater; designed capacity ~4.39 mgd (expandable to 9–12 mgd). Serves industrial dischargers via gravity mains (north from Levin Avenue, and southeast along 'K' Street).
- Commingled effluent is discharged to ~245 acres of storage/percolation ponds and recycled on ~2,620 acres of farmland.

## Industrial Pretreatment Program (IWP)

The City **does have an active Industrial Pretreatment Program**, required under Title 40 CFR Part 403 and California Code of Regulations, title 23, § 2233. Key facts:

- **Program approved**: CVWB Resolution R5-2004-0019 (adopted 30 January 2004); revised March 2012.
- **Significant Industrial Users (SIUs)**: Five SIUs identified in February 2012 Industrial Waste Survey (one subsequently closed). SIUs discharge to the Domestic Plant.
- **Separate Industrial Plant**: High-strength industrial users (food processing, etc.) also discharge directly to the Industrial Plant via dedicated gravity mains.
- **Enforcement Response Plan**: Adopted as part of the March 2012 ordinance revision.
