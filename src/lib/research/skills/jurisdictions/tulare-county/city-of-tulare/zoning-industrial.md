## Use Authorization

Industrial uses are governed by Chapter 10.64. The chapter distinguishes:
- **Permitted uses** (by right in LI or HI zones)
- **Conditional uses** (require a Conditional Use Permit — CUP)

## CUP Process

Conditional Use Permits are reviewed by City of Tulare Planning staff and typically require Planning Commission approval. Applications are submitted to:

**City of Tulare — Community Development Department, Planning Division**
411 E. Kern Avenue, Tulare, CA 93274
Phone: (559) 684-4218 (Front Counter)
Principal Planner: Steven Sopp — ssopp@tulare.ca.gov / (559) 684-4216

Planning forms (including CUP application): https://www.tulare.ca.gov/government/departments/community-development/planning/planning-forms
Planning home: https://www.tulare.ca.gov/business/planning
Zoning Map: https://www.tulare.ca.gov/business/zoning-map

## Notes

- The City's zoning is independent of Tulare County zoning (which applies only to unincorporated areas).
- For parcel-specific zoning verification, use the City's interactive zoning map or contact Planning at the number above.
- Environmental review (CEQA) is required for discretionary approvals including CUPs; Planning staff determines the appropriate CEQA document.
