## Governing document

**Tulare County Zoning Ordinance (Ordinance No. 352)** — adopted and amended by the Board of Supervisors; maintained as individual PDF sections by the Resource Management Agency (RMA).

Index page: https://tularecounty.ca.gov/rma/rma-documents/planning-documents/tulare-county-zoning-ordinance

The zoning ordinance is organized as **Chapters** (Chapter 3 contains zone-district sections). The two industrial zones for unincorporated Tulare County are:

## Industrial zone designations

### M-1 — Light Manufacturing Zone (Section 13)
- **Section**: Chapter 3, Section 13
- **URL**: https://tularecounty.ca.gov/rma/rma-documents/planning-documents/tulare-county-zoning-ordinance/chapter-3-section-13-m1/
- Typical permitted uses: light manufacturing, warehousing, welding/fabrication shops, agricultural processing, business support uses.
- Specific use tables and development standards are in the PDF (access blocked to automated fetchers; retrieve directly from the county site).

### M-2 — Heavy Manufacturing Zone (Section 14)
- **Section**: Chapter 3, Section 14
- **URL**: https://tularecounty.ca.gov/rma/rma-documents/planning-documents/tulare-county-zoning-ordinance/chapter-3-section-14-m2/
- Typical permitted uses: full range of heavy industrial and manufacturing uses that may generate noise or odor impacts — including manufacturing, processing, fabrication, ethanol plants, asphalt batch plants, mills, and wood processing yards.
- Heavy industrial uses with significant impacts typically require a Special Use Permit (SUP) rather than being permitted by right.

## Conditional / Special Use Permit (SUP) process

Section 16 of the Zoning Ordinance governs variances and Special Use Permits:
- URL: https://tularecounty.ca.gov/rma/rma-documents/planning-documents/tulare-county-zoning-ordinance/chapter-3-section-16-variances-sup/

Section 17 governs Zoning Amendments:
- URL: https://tularecounty.ca.gov/rma/rma-documents/planning-documents/tulare-county-zoning-ordinance/chapter-3-section-17-amendments/

For most industrial uses in M-2 that may generate significant impacts, a Special Use Permit (SUP) — effectively Tulare County's CUP analog — is required. Applications are submitted through the County's Citizen Self-Service Portal.

General zoning entitlements / CUP information: https://tularecounty.ca.gov/rma/planning-building/zoning-entitlements

## Notes

- The county General Plan 2010 (as amended) identifies industrial land use designations as Light Industrial and Heavy Industrial on the General Plan Land Use Diagram; zoning district M-1/M-2 must be consistent with the GP designation.
- Draft updates to the Tulare County Zoning Ordinance (TCZO) are tracked at: https://tularecounty.ca.gov/rma/rma-documents/planning-documents/tulare-county-zoning-ordinance-updates
- Incorporated cities (Visalia, Tulare, Porterville, etc.) each have their own zoning codes — the county ordinance applies only to unincorporated areas.
