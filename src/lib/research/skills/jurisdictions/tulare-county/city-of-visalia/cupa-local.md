> Research reference for the CUPA (Certified Unified Program Agency) serving the City of Visalia.
> Visalia is NOT its own CUPA. The City of Visalia is served by the **Tulare County Environmental Health Division**, which is the countywide CUPA for all of Tulare County, including the City of Visalia.
> All six CalEPA unified programs are administered by Tulare County EH — NOT by the Visalia Fire Department or any city agency.

## CUPA Identity

- **CUPA:** Tulare County Health & Human Services Agency — Environmental Health Division
- **Role:** Countywide CUPA (certified by CalEPA); administers all six unified programs for incorporated and unincorporated Tulare County, including City of Visalia
- **Address:** 5957 S. Mooney Boulevard, Visalia, CA 93277-9394
- **Phone:** (559) 624-7400
- **Hours:** 7:30 a.m. – 5:30 p.m. (business days)
- **Web:** https://tularecountyeh.org/eh/our-services/hazardous-materials-cupa

## Programs Administered

Tulare County EH administers all six CalEPA unified programs within City of Visalia limits:

| # | Program | Acronym | Trigger / Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan | HMBP | Stored/handled hazmat ≥ 55 gallons liquid, 500 lbs solid, or 200 cu ft compressed gas; submitted via CERS |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator Program | HWG | Generator ID, manifest compliance, onsite inspection |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tanks | UST | Installation, operation, modification, closure, leak response |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act / Aboveground Storage Tanks | APSA/AST | Total petroleum storage capacity ≥ 1,320 gallons; SPCC plan required |
| 5 | California Accidental Release Prevention Program | CalARP | Facilities with regulated substances above threshold quantities; RMP submitted to US EPA; Tulare County EH is the local implementing agency |
| 6 | Hazardous Waste On-Site Treatment | TiHW | Permit-by-rule and conditional authorization for on-site treatment |

## HMBP Program Details

- **Submission system:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business
- **Who must comply:** Businesses storing, using, or handling hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities: ≥ 55 gallons liquid, ≥ 500 pounds solid, or ≥ 200 cubic feet of compressed gas (including hazardous wastes counting toward inventory)
- **Filing:** Annual review and resubmission required via CERS
- **Inspections:** Tulare County EH conducts routine facility inspections [VERIFY current inspection frequency schedule with Tulare County EH]

## APSA Details

- Applies to owners/operators with total petroleum storage capacity ≥ 1,320 gallons aboveground (aggregate), or any single tank > 660 gallons
- Requires Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure (SPCC) plan
- CERS filing required for aboveground petroleum storage data
- Tulare County EH inspects for APSA compliance

## CalARP Details

- Tulare County EH is the local implementing agency (LIA) for CalARP within Visalia
- Facilities with regulated substances above threshold quantities must submit a Risk Management Plan (RMP) to US EPA

## Relationship to City of Visalia

- The Visalia Fire Department does **not** administer CUPA programs. Fire code operational permits (CFC Chapter 50+) are separate from CUPA and are handled by the Visalia Fire Prevention Bureau (see fire-code-local.md).
- Do NOT route HMBP, UST, CalARP, APSA, or hazardous waste generator inquiries to the City — route to Tulare County EH at (559) 624-7400.
