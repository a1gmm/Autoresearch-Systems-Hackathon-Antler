> Research reference for the City of Visalia fire code adoption and the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ).
> Visalia operates its own career municipal fire department — it is NOT served by a county fire district or CAL FIRE unit.
> The 2022 CFC was the most recently confirmed adopted edition; verify whether the 2025 California Fire Code (effective 2026-01-01 statewide) has been locally adopted yet.

## Adopted Fire Code

- **Edition adopted:** California Fire Code (CFC) **2022 Edition**
- **Municipal Code chapter:** Chapter 8.20 CALIFORNIA FIRE CODE (Visalia Municipal Code Title 8)
- **Adopting ordinance:** Ord. 2022-15, § 2 (part), 2022
- **Adoption section:** VMC §8.20.010 — "Adoption of the 2022 California Fire Code"
- **Copies on file:** Fire Department offices and Building Division offices, City of Visalia

> [VERIFY] Confirm whether Ord. adopting the 2025 CFC (effective 2026-01-01 statewide) has been passed; if not, the 2022 CFC remains operative locally.

## Local Amendments Confirmed (Chapter 8.20)

The following local amendments to the adopted CFC are confirmed from the municipal code:

| Topic | Local Rule |
|---|---|
| Fire hydrant proximity — new sprinklered buildings | A newly constructed building equipped with an NFPA 13 or NFPA 13R fire sprinkler system must have a fire hydrant within **50 feet** of the fire department connection (FDC). |
| Fire hydrant proximity — retrofitted existing buildings | Where an existing building is retrofitted with an NFPA 13 or NFPA 13R system, a fire hydrant must be within **75 feet** of the FDC. |
| Tamper protection | Exterior risers and drain/test valves on fire sprinkler systems require tamper protection (confirmed in code text; specific section [VERIFY]). |
| Alternative materials/methods | VMC §8.20.060 — Fire Chief may approve alternate materials and methods per CFC process. |
| Fee schedule | Fire Chief may establish a schedule of fees (approved by City Council) for plan checking, inspections, permits, and fire service cost recovery for responsible parties. |

> [VERIFY] Full local amendment text at VMC Chapter 8.20 sections beyond §8.20.010 and §8.20.060; AMLegal returned HTTP 403 on direct fetch — consult the city's physical code or American Legal Publishing portal.

## Outdoor Burning Regulations

- VMC Chapter 8.44 governs outdoor burning in Visalia.
- §8.44.040 specifies types of authorized outdoor burning.
- §8.44.050 covers outdoor cooking fires.
- Burn authorization subject to San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution Control District (SJVAPCD) no-burn day restrictions.

## Relationship to State and County Fire Programs

- **CAL FIRE:** Visalia is NOT in a State Responsibility Area (SRA) for fire protection within city limits. CAL FIRE jurisdiction does not apply for structural fire response inside the city.
- **County Fire:** Tulare County Fire Department serves unincorporated Tulare County; Visalia Fire Department is independent of it.
- **CUPA hazmat operational permits:** Visalia Fire Department is NOT the CUPA — see cupa-local.md for CUPA authority (Tulare County Environmental Health).
