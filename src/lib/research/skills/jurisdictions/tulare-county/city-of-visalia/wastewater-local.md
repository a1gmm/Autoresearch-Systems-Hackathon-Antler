## Industrial Pretreatment Program

Visalia has a federally-required local pretreatment program under 40 CFR Part 403. The program is consistent with NPDES requirements imposed on the WRF by the Regional Water Quality Control Board (Central Valley Region, Region 5).

### Who Must Obtain a Permit

The Pretreatment Division classifies and permits the following industrial user categories:

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| **Significant Industrial User (SIU)** | Non-domestic users discharging > 25,000 gallons/day of process wastewater, or users with pass-through or interference potential, or categorical industrial users |
| **Categorical Industrial User (CIU)** | Facilities subject to EPA national categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR Parts 405–471; e.g., metal finishing, food processing, electroplating) |
| **Commercial/Non-SIU industrial users** | Food service establishments, automotive repair, carwashes, dental facilities, and other commercial dischargers classified and permitted per local rules |

### Monitored Facility Types

The Pretreatment Division actively monitors:
- Industrial and commercial process wastewater dischargers
- Food service establishments (grease trap compliance)
- Automotive repair facilities
- Carwashes
- Dental facilities (amalgam separators)
- Illicit/illegal discharge sources into the city conveyance system and WRF

### Application Process

1. Contact the Pretreatment Division at (559) 713-4463 or Pretreatment@visalia.city to determine permit tier.
2. Submit permit application with facility SIC code and process description.
3. Pretreatment Division reviews; may conduct site inspection prior to permit issuance.
4. Permits are typically annual; renewal and compliance monitoring/reporting required throughout.

> [VERIFY] Confirm application form availability and current permit fee schedule directly with the Pretreatment Division. The city's .asp web pages returned HTTP 500 during this research session.

## Discharge Standards

Standards are established in VMC Chapter 13.08, Article 8 (Regulation of Wastewater Discharges), with federal categorical standards in 40 CFR overlaid on top.

> [VERIFY — specific numeric local limits (mg/L values for metals, BOD, TSS, O&G, pH, TDS) not publicly available online during this research session. Obtain Visalia's current local limits table directly from Pretreatment@visalia.city.]

General prohibited discharge categories (standard under state and federal law; Visalia local ordinance mirrors):
- Pollutants causing pass-through or interference at the WRF
- Flammable/explosive materials (flash point < 140°F)
- Corrosive discharges (pH outside permitted range)
- Solid or viscous substances causing obstruction
- Pollutants with objectionable color, odor, or temperature > 104°F at point of discharge
- Radioactive wastes
- Petroleum oil or grease in amounts causing interference

## WRF NPDES Context

- The Visalia WRF discharges treated effluent to Mill Creek (with upgrades implemented to manage this discharge).
- The facility is regulated under an NPDES permit issued by the Central Valley RWQCB (Region 5).
