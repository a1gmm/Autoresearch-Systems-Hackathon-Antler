## Industrial Zone Districts

Visalia's zoning ordinance includes the following industrial zone designations in Chapter 17.22:

| Zone | Name | General Character |
|---|---|---|
| **BRP** | Business Research Park | Low-intensity research and development; office/R&D campus environment |
| **I-L** | Light Industrial | Low-intensity manufacturing, warehousing, R&D, limited production/processing/assembly; emergency shelters allowed by right in I-L per state law (Gov. Code §65583) |
| **I** | Industrial | General industrial; broader range of manufacturing, processing, and heavy uses |

### Permitted vs. Conditional Uses

Uses in industrial zones are determined by **Table 17.25.030** (VMC §17.25.030):

- A use listed as **"P"** (Permitted) may be established by right with site plan review and building/grading permits.
- A use listed as **"C"** (Conditional) requires a **Conditional Use Permit (CUP)** before establishment.
- A use listed as **"PD"** (Planned Development permit) requires a more intensive discretionary review.

Notable use determinations confirmed from code:
- **Emergency shelters:** Permitted by right (no CUP) in I-L zone per state mandate
- **Recycling facilities (small/light processing):** Generally permitted or conditionally permitted in industrial zones
- **Heavy processing recycling facilities:** Require CUP in I-L zone; Planned Development permit in I zone
- **Fast food with drive-through:** Conditionally permitted in I-L with location criteria (250-ft residential separation; 10-vehicle stacking requirement)

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

- **Deciding body:** Planning Commission (for most CUPs); appeals to City Council within 10 days of Planning Commission decision (per VMC §17.02.145 and Subdivision Ordinance §16.04.040)
- **Application submittal:** Community Development Department, Planning Division
- **Planning Division address:** 315 E. Acequia Avenue, Visalia, CA 93291 (City Hall East)
- **Planning phone:** (559) 713-4444
- **CEQA review:** CUPs are subject to California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) environmental review; Planning Division administers

## Development Standards (Industrial Zones)

Key standards applicable to I-L and I zones per Chapter 17.22 and cross-referenced chapters:

| Standard | Note |
|---|---|
| Height limits | Established by zone district; wireless facilities may exceed standard heights by up to 25 ft |
| Setbacks | Specified in underlying zone district regulations (Chapter 17.22) |
| Parking and loading | Off-street parking and loading per Chapter 17.34 |
| Drive-thru stacking | Minimum 10-vehicle stacking; 250-ft residential separation (fast food/commercial in I-L) |

> [VERIFY] Numeric setbacks, lot coverage, and FAR limits for I-L and I zones by consulting VMC Chapter 17.22 directly or Planning Division at (559) 713-4444.
