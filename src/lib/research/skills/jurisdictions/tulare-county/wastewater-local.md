## Sewer authority — unincorporated areas

In Tulare County's unincorporated areas, water and sewer services are provided through **County-owned systems within designated County Service Areas (CSAs)** — primarily **County Service Area No. 1** and **County Service Area No. 2** — operated and maintained by the Resource Management Agency (RMA), Public Works Division.

Web: https://tularecounty.ca.gov/rma/public-works/water-sewer-services

## Governing code

**Tulare County Ordinance Code, Part 8, Chapter 8-3 (Sewer Service)**
https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/TulareCounty/html/TulareCounty08/TulareCounty0803.html

Key provisions:
- No person may connect to, alter, or disturb any public sewage system within a CSA except under **written permit** issued by the County of Tulare or the County's designated operator.
- Permittees are bound by the **Sewer Improvement Standards Ordinance**, an uncodified county ordinance adopted and amended by the Board of Supervisors, which contains regulations and limitations on discharge in accordance with state and federal environmental control laws.
- Specific discharge limits and pretreatment standards are in the Sewer Improvement Standards Ordinance (not fully published online as a standalone document). [VERIFY — obtain Sewer Improvement Standards Ordinance from Public Works for current discharge limits]

## Industrial pretreatment program (IWP)

No formal industrial pretreatment program (40 CFR Part 403) was identified for the county CSA systems. The unincorporated county systems are small rural systems; industrial users should confirm with Public Works whether a pretreatment permit is required and what categorical standards apply. Discharge must comply with state and federal categorical pretreatment standards by default.

## Major POTW systems in Tulare County (incorporated cities — separate authorities)

Large industrial dischargers in incorporated cities are subject to those cities' own POTW/pretreatment programs:

| City | Facility | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| Visalia | Water Conservation Plant (WCP) | ~12 MGD, tertiary, MBR; ~15% industrial influent |
| Tulare | Wastewater Pollution Control Facility (WPCF) | Municipal POTW |
| Porterville | Wastewater Treatment Facility (WWTF) | Has pretreatment agreement with Foster Farms for industrial flow |

Each city operates its own sewer-use ordinance and pretreatment program — those are separate from the county CSA system. For city-specific details see the city-level jurisdiction files.
