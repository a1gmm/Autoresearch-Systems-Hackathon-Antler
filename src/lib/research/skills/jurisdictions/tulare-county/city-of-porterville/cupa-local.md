## Controlling Authority

The City of Porterville is **not its own CUPA**. All six elements of the California Unified Hazardous Waste and Hazardous Materials Management Regulatory Program are administered by:

**Tulare County Health & Human Services Agency — Environmental Health Division**
Designated by CalEPA as the Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all of Tulare County, including the City of Porterville.

- **CUPA program page**: https://tularecountyeh.org/eh/our-services/hazardous-materials-cupa
- **Phone**: (559) 624-7400 (M–F, 7:30 a.m.–5:30 p.m.)

## Six Unified Program Elements Administered

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — required for facilities that handle or store ≥ 55 gallons of liquid, ≥ 500 lbs of solid, or ≥ 200 cubic feet of compressed gas hazardous materials
   - Source: https://tularecountyeh.org/eh/our-services/hazardous-materials-cupa/hazardous-materials-business-plan-hmbp/
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator Program** — oversight of onsite generation, accumulation, and disposal
3. **Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program** — permitting, inspection, and corrective action
   - Source: https://tularecountyeh.org/eh/our-services/hazardous-materials-cupa/underground-storage-tanks/
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) / Spill Prevention**
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) Program**
6. **Tiered Permitting for onsite treatment of hazardous waste**
   - Source: https://tularecountyeh.org/eh/our-services/hazardous-materials-cupa/tiered-permitting

## Electronic Reporting

California law (AB 2286) requires electronic submission of HMBPs through the **California Environmental Reporting System (CERS)**. Contact Tulare County EH for submission guidance.
- CERS information: https://tularecountyeh.org/eh/about-us/faqs/cupa-electronic-reporting
