## POTW Identity

The City of Porterville owns and operates its own municipal Publicly Owned Treatment Works (POTW):

- **Facility Name**: City of Porterville Wastewater Treatment Facility
- **Address**: 1333 W. Grand Avenue, Porterville, CA 93257
- **Phone**: (559) 782-7501
- **Design Capacity**: 8 million gallons per day (8 MGD)
- **Process**: Mechanical and biological treatment; treated effluent is used for agricultural irrigation at farms approximately 4.5 miles outside the city
- **City page**: https://www.ci.porterville.ca.us/departments/public_works/field_services_division/wastewater_treatment_facility.php

> Note: At 8 MGD design capacity, Porterville exceeds the federal 5 MGD threshold that triggers a mandatory POTW pretreatment program under 40 CFR Part 403.

### Industrial Pretreatment Program (§ 25-107)

The City administers a local Industrial Pretreatment Program:

- Industrial users must pretreat wastewater to levels acceptable to the City at their own expense before discharge
- Detailed plans for pretreatment facilities and operating procedures must be submitted to the City for review and approval **before construction**
- Users must achieve compliance with all applicable federal categorical pretreatment standards within EPA-specified timelines
- Any subsequent changes to pretreatment facilities or methods must be reported to and approved by the City prior to implementation
- Both **permitted** and **non-permitted industrial users** must provide notice of changes in wastewater flow and characteristics
- Monitoring records must be maintained for a minimum of **3 years** and be available for inspection and copying by the City, State, and EPA

The Laboratory Division of the Public Works Department oversees the Industrial Pretreatment Program and monitors discharges into the wastewater system.
