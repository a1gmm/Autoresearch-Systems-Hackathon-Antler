## Zoning Ordinance

The City of Porterville's land use regulations are contained in the **Porterville Development Ordinance**, adopted originally by Ordinance 1764 (effective June 4, 2010) and subsequently amended through at least Ordinances 1870/1871/1873 (effective July 2, 2020). The current consolidated text (updated March 22, 2023) is available from the Community Development Department.

## Structure

Zoning regulations are organized in numbered Series:
- **Series 100**: Introductory Provisions (Chapters 100–103)
- **Series 200**: Base Districts (Chapters 200–207) — where industrial/employment district rules live
- **Series 300**: Additional Use and Development Regulations
- **Series 500**: Overlay Districts
- **Series 600**: Administration and Permits (Chapter 604 — Conditional Use Permits)

## Employment/Industrial District Designations (Chapter 204)

Industrial and employment uses are grouped under "Employment Districts" (E districts). Four base Employment district zones are established (§ 101.01):

| Map Symbol | Full Name | Key Allowed Uses |
|---|---|---|
| **PO** | Professional Office | Offices, medical clinics, R&D, limited retail/restaurant. Max FAR 0.50 |
| **IP** | Industrial Park | Light industrial, warehouse, mini-storage, distribution, wholesale, R&D, bulk retail. Max FAR 0.40 |
| **IG** | General Industrial | Warehousing, manufacturing, food processing, distribution, support services. No retail. Max FAR 0.60 |
| **IA** | Airport Industrial | Airport and aviation-related industrial; secondary small-scale retail/service. Max FAR 0.60 |

Overlay districts that may combine with Employment base districts:
- **AE** (Airport Environs)
- **HZ** (Hillside Development Zone)
- **S** (Single Story)

## Key Permitted / Conditional Uses in IG and IP (Table 204.02)

Uses in the Employment Districts are designated:
- **P** = Permitted by right
- **C** = Requires Conditional Use Permit (City Council)
- **M** = Requires Minor Conditional Use Permit (City Council)

## Conditional Use Permit Process (Chapter 604)

CUPs in Employment Districts are heard by the **City Council** (not the Planning Commission).

1. **Application**: Filed with Community Development Department per Chapter 601 (Common Procedures)
2. **Completeness Review**: Zoning Administrator determines completeness
3. **Public Hearing**: Scheduled within 30 days of complete application; City Council is the decision-making body
4. **Notice**: Provided per Chapter 601 requirements
5. **Required Findings** (all must be met):
   - Consistent with General Plan goals and objectives
   - Location, size, design, and operating characteristics consistent with the district purpose and General Plan
6. **Conditions**: City Council may impose conditions reasonably related to the application
7. **Decision**: City Council decision is final

Minor Conditional Use Permits (M) follow the same criteria but involve less staff review time; they are still decided by City Council.
