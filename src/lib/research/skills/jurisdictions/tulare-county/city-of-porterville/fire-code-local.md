## Adopted Code & Municipal Code Chapter

The City of Porterville Fire Prevention program is codified in **Municipal Code Chapter 12 (Fire Prevention)**, hosted at:
https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/portervilleca/latest/porterville_ca/0-0-0-3671

### Current Adopted Edition

The **2025 California Fire Code** (CFC, based on the 2024 IFC with California amendments) was introduced for adoption at the Porterville City Council meeting of **November 7, 2025**, passed unanimously, and returned for final adoption at the next regular meeting. The ordinance finalizing this adoption is **Ordinance 1925**, passed **December 2, 2025**, with an effective date of **January 1, 2026**.

The prior edition was the **2022 California Fire Code**, adopted by **Ordinance 1898**, passed **November 15, 2022** (Section 12-1.1 of the Municipal Code).

### Local Amendments (2025 Adoption)

Three local amendments were adopted with the 2025 CFC:

1. **Fire Hydrant Spacing — Commercial/Mixed-Use/Multifamily**: Porterville maintains a stricter **300-foot maximum spacing** requirement, more restrictive than the state's permissible 500-foot spacing. (Based on preconnect hose-link lengths for fire engines.)
2. **Address Signage on Main Street**: Commercial buildings along Main Street are required to display rear-facing address numbers on the alley side to aid emergency response.
3. **Gate Width Standards**: Updated to accommodate the dimensions of modern fire engines.

### Fire AHJ

- **Agency**: City of Porterville Fire Department (municipal career department; three stations; approximately 53 sworn personnel)
- **Prevention Division**: Responsible for enforcement of all fire-related codes in the Municipal Code
- **Code Enforcement contact**: (559) 782-7526, weekdays 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.
- **City fire dept page**: https://www.ci.porterville.ca.us/departments/fire/index.php
- **Fire Prevention Division page**: https://www.ci.porterville.ca.us/departments/fire/fire_prevention_code_enforcement_division.php

> Note: Porterville operates its own municipal Fire Department. It is **not** served by Tulare County or CAL FIRE for structural fire protection within city limits.
