## Adopted code edition

Tulare County adopted the **2025 California Building Standards Code** (Title 24, based on 2024 International Code Editions) by amending **Chapter 15 of Part VII** of the Tulare County Ordinance Code. This 2025 amendment replaced prior references to the 2022 Building Standards Code. The fire-specific provisions (Title 24, Part 9 — California Fire Code) are incorporated through Chapter 15.

- Prior effective edition: 2022 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9, based on 2021 IFC).
- Current effective edition: **2025 California Fire Code** (Title 24, Part 9) via 2025 Part VII Ch. 15 amendment.

The ordinance amendment is on file at the Clerk of the Board's Office, the Resource Management Agency (RMA), and the Tulare County Fire Chief's Office and is available for public review during normal business hours. ## Fire AHJ — split authority (IMPORTANT)

Tulare County has a **split fire AHJ**:

| Area | AHJ |
|------|-----|
| Unincorporated county | Tulare County Fire Department (operated cooperatively with CAL FIRE Tulare Unit / TUU) |
| Exeter, Farmersville, Lindsay, Woodlake | Tulare County Fire (contract) |
| City of Visalia | Visalia Fire Department (municipal) |
| City of Tulare | Tulare Fire Department (municipal) |
| City of Porterville | Porterville Fire Department (municipal) |

The **Fire Chief of the County of Tulare** (or authorized representatives, including Fire Warden, Fire Code Official, Fire Marshal) enforces the California Fire Code within the **unincorporated areas** of the County.

- Tulare County Fire Department: https://tularecounty.ca.gov/fire
- Clerk of the Board (code requests): (559) 636-5002

## Notes

- CAL FIRE Tulare Unit (TUU) cooperatively operates the county fire department; for State Responsibility Area (SRA) fire prevention matters, CAL FIRE TUU is the enforcement agency.
- Always confirm the specific city/unincorporated location — the county AHJ does NOT cover Visalia, Tulare, or Porterville.
