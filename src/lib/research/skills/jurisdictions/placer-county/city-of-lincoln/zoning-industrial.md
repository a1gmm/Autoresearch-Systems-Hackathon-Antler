## Industrial District Names and Codes

Title 18 establishes two industrial zoning districts:

| District | Code | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Light Industrial | **L-I** | Automobile body/paint shops, auto repair, heavy equipment sales and service, and similar light-industrial uses |
| Industrial (Heavy) | **I** | All uses allowed in L-I plus wholesale operations, mining, and uses determined by the Planning Commission to be similar in nature |

Both industrial districts require a minimum district depth of 100 feet.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

- Applications are submitted to the **Community Development Department — Planning Division**.
- The **Planning Commission** holds a public hearing and issues or recommends the CUP.
- City Council may review Planning Commission decisions for approval or disapproval.
- Development permits are required for each phase of a phased project.
- Division VI of Title 18 specifically addresses "Conditional Use Permits, Variances, Mobile Home Certificates, Home Occupations and Temporary Buildings in Industrial Areas" (Chapter 18.56 governs CUP procedures). [VERIFY chapter number via Municode]

## Nonconforming Uses

Nonconforming uses are declared incompatible with permitted uses and may not be enlarged, expanded, or extended. A nonconforming use may be converted to a conforming use through a CUP process following a Planning Commission hearing.
