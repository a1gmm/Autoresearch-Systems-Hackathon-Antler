# CUPA / Hazardous Materials — City of Lincoln, CA

## Controlling Authority

**City of Lincoln is NOT its own CUPA.**

All hazardous materials/unified-program regulated facilities within Lincoln city limits are served by:

> **Placer County Environmental Health — CUPA Program**
> 11434 B Avenue, Auburn, CA 95603
> Phone: 530-886-1870 (Auburn office)
> https://www.placer.ca.gov/3164/Hazardous-Materials

Placer County Environmental Health is the CalEPA-certified CUPA for the entire county **except** the City of Roseville (which operates its own CUPA through the Roseville Fire Department). Lincoln businesses must work directly with Placer County Environmental Health.

## Regulated Programs (via Placer County CUPA)

The Placer County CUPA administers all five unified-program elements:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — reporting and inventory for businesses that handle reportable quantities of hazardous materials; submit via the California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) at cers.calepa.ca.gov.
2. **Hazardous Waste Generators (HWG)** — inspections and compliance for hazardous waste generators.
3. **Underground Storage Tank (UST)** — permits, inspections, and leak oversight.
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — facilities storing ≥ 1,320 gallons of petroleum.
5. **California Accidental Release Program (CalARP)** — risk management plans for facilities with regulated substances above threshold quantities.
