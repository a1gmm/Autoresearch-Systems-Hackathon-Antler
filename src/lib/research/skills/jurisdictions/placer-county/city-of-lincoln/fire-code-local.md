# Fire Code — City of Lincoln, CA

## Fire Authority (AHJ)

**City of Lincoln Fire Department** is the municipal fire authority having jurisdiction (AHJ) for all areas within city limits. The department is an ISO Class 2-rated agency with three full-time stations and 32 personnel. A **Fire Marshal** position within the department oversees fire prevention and development services, including fire inspections, development plan reviews, and permit issuance.

- Fire Non-Emergency / Prevention Bureau: **916-645-4040**
- City Hall address: 600 6th Street, Lincoln, CA 95648

## Wildland-Urban Interface

Lincoln participates in **Firewise USA** and the **Ready, Set, Go!** wildfire preparedness program. Some portions of the city or its sphere of influence may be in a State Responsibility Area (SRA) or locally designated WUI zone; verify defensible-space and fire-hardening requirements with the Fire Marshal for specific parcels.

## Notes

- Lincoln operates its own municipal fire department (not served by CAL FIRE or a county fire district).
- Plan checks and fire flow requests for commercial/industrial construction go through the Community Development Department (Fire Prevention participates in plan review).
