# Wastewater / Sewer — City of Lincoln, CA

## Controlling Authority

Wastewater collection and treatment in Lincoln involves two entities:

### 1. City of Lincoln — Sewer Collection System (Public Works)
The City's Public Works Department owns and operates the sewer collection infrastructure:
- Over 200 miles of main sewer lines
- 58 miles of laterals, 17 miles of force mains
- 3,558 manholes, 10 sewer lift stations
- Contact: 916-434-2450 | 600 6th Street, Lincoln, CA 95648

### 2. LiSWA — Regional POTW
**Lincoln-SMD1 Wastewater Authority (LiSWA)** is a joint powers authority (JPA) between the **City of Lincoln** and **Placer County**, established under Government Code § 6500 et seq. LiSWA owns and operates the **LiSWA Wastewater Treatment and Reclamation Facility (WWTRF)** at 1245 Fiddyment Road, Lincoln, CA 95648.

- Website: https://liswa-ca.gov/
- General Manager: George Barber
- Governance: 4-member board (2 Lincoln City Council members + 2 Placer County Supervisors)
- The WWTRF serves two member agencies: the City of Lincoln and Placer County (unincorporated areas served by Sewer Maintenance District 1).

## NPDES Permit

LiSWA discharges under:
- **Order R5-2024-0024, NPDES No. CA0084476** (adopted 19 April 2024; effective 1 June 2024; expires 31 May 2029)
- Issued by: California Regional Water Quality Control Board, **Central Valley Region (Region 5)**
- Receiving water: Auburn Ravine Creek (Discharge Point 001) and groundwater recharge (DPs 002, 003)
- Classified as a **Major Discharge**

Effluent limitations at DP 001 (EFF-001A) include (from Order R5-2024-0024):
- BOD5: 20 mg/L (monthly avg), 30 mg/L (weekly avg) — tertiary treated
- TSS: 10 mg/L (monthly avg), 15 mg/L (weekly avg) at filter clearwell; 30 mg/L at EFF-001A
- Ammonia (as N): 1.2 mg/L (monthly avg), 2.8 mg/L (weekly avg)
- Nitrate + Nitrite (as N): 10 mg/L (monthly avg), 17 mg/L (weekly avg)
- Total Coliform: 2.2 MPN/100 mL (7-day median)

## Industrial Pretreatment Program (IPP)

LiSWA is required by its NPDES permit (Order R5-2024-0024, Special Provisions §V.C.5.a) to implement a **40 C.F.R. Part 403 pretreatment program** as Control Authority. Key provisions:

- LiSWA must enforce federal categorical pretreatment standards (CWA §§ 307(b), 307(c), 307(d), 402(b)).
- Significant Industrial Users (SIUs) must be identified and required to install pretreatment before discharge.
- As of the NPDES record, the IPP includes three identified SIUs.
- Industrial dischargers must comply with applicable federal categorical standards or face enforcement, penalties, and EPA action.

**Sewer Use Ordinance (SUO):** LiSWA has authority to adopt and enforce a sewer use ordinance (40 C.F.R. § 403.8(f)(1)). The full text of the current LiSWA SUO was not located online during this research.
