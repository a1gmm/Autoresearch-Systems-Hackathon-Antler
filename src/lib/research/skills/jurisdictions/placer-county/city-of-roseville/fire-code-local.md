| Item | Detail |

Prior adoption (2022 CFC): Ordinance 6594.

## Local Amendments in Chapter 16.16

All amendments justified under Health and Safety Code § 17958 based on climatic, geological, and topographical conditions (arid summers ~18 in/year rainfall, 70–112°F, seismic zone D, creek/canyon access barriers).

| Section | What It Amends | Local Rule |
|---------|---------------|-----------|
| 16.16.040 | CFC Ch. 1, § 108 (Fees) | Fees set by City Council resolution; fire chief may waive/modify for good cause in writing |
| 16.16.070 | Open burning, recreational fires | Local rules on open burning and portable outdoor fireplaces |
| 16.16.080 | General storage | Local storage provisions |
| 16.16.090 | CFC Ch. 5, § 503.2.3 (Access roads) | Fire apparatus roads must be AC pavement or equivalent (all-weather surface) |
| 16.16.100 | Premises identification | Multi-tenant buildings: address numbers on rear-access tenant spaces required prior to occupancy |
| 16.16.110 | Fire protection systems | Dedicated fire control room required for all auto-suppression systems; min. 5 ft × 7 ft; contains only fire system equipment; location approved by fire code official |
| 16.16.130 | Fire safety during construction | Local construction site fire safety rules |
| 16.16.140 | Fireworks | Local fireworks regulations |
| 16.16.160 | CFC App. B §§ B105.1 & B105.2 (Fire flow) | One/two-family and R-3/R-4: minimum 1,500 gpm for prescribed duration; other buildings: per Table B105.2 and B105.1(2) |
