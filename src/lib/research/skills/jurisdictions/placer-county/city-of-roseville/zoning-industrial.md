## Industrial and Manufacturing Zones (Chapter 19.14)

Chapter 19.14 establishes the industrial zoning districts. Four industrial/manufacturing district designations exist:

| Zone | Full Name | Character |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| **MP** | Industrial/Business Park | Mix of light industrial, office, and commercial; no outdoor manufacturing; limited outdoor storage permitted |
| **M-1** | Light Industrial | Limited outdoor storage; limited emission of gases, particulates, steam, heat, odor, vibration, glare, dust, and noise; compatible near commercial and residential uses |
| **M-2** | General Industrial | Broad range of industrial uses: manufacturing, assembly, wholesale distribution, warehousing |
| **MMU** | Industrial Mixed Use | Flexible mix of industrial use types; must always be combined with a DS (Development Standards) or SA (Special Area) overlay zone |

Permitted uses and structures in each district must also comply with the City's adopted **Community Design Guidelines**, applicable specific plans, and other applicable requirements.

## Use Type Classifications

Industrial use types are defined in **Chapter 19.08** (Use Type Classifications), which governs how specific operations are categorized and assigned to districts.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

CUP requirements are in **Chapter 19.74** (Permit and Variance Requirements):

- A CUP must be obtained **prior to** initiation, modification, or expansion of any conditionally permitted use.
- **Approving Authority:** Planning Commission (seven members, appointed by City Council for overlapping four-year terms).
- **Type A applications:** Public hearing may be waived; notice mailed at least 10 days before action to applicant, property owner, owners within 300 feet, and those who requested notice.
- **Approval findings required:** Proposed use must be consistent with the City of Roseville General Plan and any applicable specific plan.
- **Appeal:** Decisions appealed to City Council by written appeal filed with the city clerk within 10 days of decision, with required fee.

Application procedures are detailed in Chapter 19.76 (Application Processing Procedures).
