## Industrial Wastewater Contribution Permit (IWCP)

A wastewater contribution permit (city permit, equivalent to an Industrial User Permit / IWP) is required if a facility:

- Discharges **≥ 25,000 gallons/day** to the sanitary sewer; OR
- Is a **Categorical Industrial User** (CIU) under 40 CFR Subchapter N (includes aluminum forming, electroplating, metal finishing, petroleum refining, etc.) discharging ≥ 1 gallon; OR
- Contributes high-strength waste equal to ≥ 5% of treatment capacity; OR
- Wishes to discharge groundwater, trucked wastes, or concentrated wastes.

**Application lead time:** Minimum **60 days** before planned discharge begins. Failure to obtain a required permit can result in service suspension and civil penalties.

**Permit fee:** No permit fee is charged (as of information available online); operational costs may include surcharges, staff time, and sampling expenses.

## Commercial Pretreatment Program

Facilities without a formal permit but subject to inspection and education include: automotive/equipment maintenance shops, machine shops, dental offices, car washes, and any facility operating pretreatment equipment before discharge to the sanitary sewer.
