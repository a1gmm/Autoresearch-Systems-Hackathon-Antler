## Governing Code

Industrial land use in unincorporated Placer County is regulated by **Placer County Code, Title 17 (Zoning)**, administered by the **Community Development Resource Agency (CDRA), Planning Services Division**.

Full zoning ordinance: https://www.placer.ca.gov/3701/Zoning-Ordinance (hosted on ecode360)

## Industrial Zone Districts (Title 17, Division III — Industrial Districts)

| Zone Designation | Article | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Airport (AP)** | § 17.36.010 | Airport-related uses |
| **Business Park (BP)** | § 17.38.010 | Office/R&D/light industrial campus; lower-impact uses |
| **Industrial (IN)** | § 17.40.010 | General industrial; broad range of manufacturing, assembly, wholesale distribution, storage, and related uses |
| **Industrial Park (INP)** | § 17.42.010 | Industrial park with design standards; mix of light manufacturing, R&D, warehousing; stricter site standards than IN |

Allowed land uses for each zone are established by **§ 17.06.030** (allowable uses and permit requirements) and the land use/permit tables at **§ 17.06.050**.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

**Trigger**: CUPs are required for uses identified in the §17.06.050 land use tables that are not outright permitted in the applicable zone. Specific INP trigger: any manufacturing or processing use on a site ≥ 10 acres requires a CUP. Subdivision in the INP zone also requires a CUP in addition to a subdivision map.

**Approval body**: **Planning Commission** (public hearing required).

**Process**:
1. Submit Universal Application + Environmental Questionnaire (CEQA) + project description + plan set + fees online or at the CDRA counter.
2. Planning Director schedules Planning Commission public hearing per **PCC § 17.60.140**.
3. Commission considers staff report, CEQA documents, public testimony.
4. Commission approves, conditionally approves, or denies within the time limits of **PCC § 17.58.160(A)**.
5. Approval requires findings under **PCC § 17.58.140(A)** (consistency with zoning code and General Plan).
6. Conditions may be imposed per **PCC § 17.58.140(B)**.
7. **Appeal**: Planning Commission decisions may be appealed to the **Board of Supervisors** per **PCC § 17.60.110**.

Minor use permits (lower-intensity uses) may be approved by the Zoning Administrator and may be referred to the Planning Commission at the Planning Director's discretion.

## Discretionary Use Requirements

Detailed CUP procedures are in **PCC Article 17.58** (Discretionary Use Requirements), with zoning administration rules in **Article 17.60**.

## General Plan Note

Industrial projects must be consistent with the **Placer County General Plan** land use designation for the parcel as well as any applicable community plan or specific plan. A General Plan Amendment may be required for parcels not designated for industrial use.
