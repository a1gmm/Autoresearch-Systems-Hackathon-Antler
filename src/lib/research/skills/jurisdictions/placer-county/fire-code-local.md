## Adopted Edition

Placer County adopted the **2025 California Building Standards Code, Title 24** — including **Part 9 (2025 California Fire Code)** — via **Ordinance No. 6345-B**, introduced November 4, 2025 and effective **January 1, 2026**. This ordinance repeals and re-enacts **Placer County Code, Chapter 15, Article 15.04** in its entirety.

The county code home for fire provisions is **PCC § 15.04.700**, which adopts the California Fire Code (as amended) by reference and directs enforcement to the chief of each fire district or CSA. Where CFC provisions conflict with other statutes or county ordinances, the more restrictive governs.

## Local Amendments of Note (within Article 15.04)

- Higher fire-resistive roofing material requirements (climatic/topographic basis).
- Fire sprinkler requirements for new commercial buildings ≥ 3,600 sq ft (or where additions bring total to that threshold).
- Fire and smoke alarm requirements for new/expanded commercial buildings ≥ 1,500 sq ft.
- Agricultural building exemption.
- Swimming pool barrier adjustments (§ 15.04.320) — perimeter fencing waiver for parcels ≥ 3 acres when pool edge is > 100 ft from property line.
- Snow load and Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI) provisions (Part 7) applicable to high-elevation zones.

The 2025 code adoption was adopted under Authority B of AB 130 (signed June 30, 2025), which freezes residential code modifications from Oct 1, 2025 – June 1, 2031 unless substantially equivalent to pre-AB 130 standards.

## Fire AHJ — Unincorporated Placer County

Placer County does **not** have a single countywide fire department for all unincorporated areas. Fire authority is split among:

| Agency | Type |
|---|---|
| **Placer County Fire Department (PCFD)** | County department; operates under a CAL FIRE Cooperative Agreement (est. 1952) with the **CAL FIRE Nevada-Yuba-Placer Unit (NEU)**; serves unincorporated LRA and SRA areas under agreement |
| **South Placer Fire Protection District** | Independent district; last formally adopted the 2022 CFC via Ordinance No. 1-2022 (ratified by County BOS) |
| **Penryn Fire Protection District** | Independent district; adopted 2016 CFC via Ordinance No. 2017-1 (ratified 2017); 2022/2025 update status UNVERIFIED |
| **Placer Hills Fire Protection District** | Independent district; adopted 2019 CFC via Ordinance No. 2020-01; 2022/2025 update UNVERIFIED |
| **North Tahoe Fire Protection District** | Independent district; adopted 2022 CFC via Ordinance No. 02-2022 (ratified Nov 2023) |
| **Northstar CSD Fire** | Serves Northstar community |
| **Truckee Fire Protection District** | Serves Truckee and Donner Summit area |
| Other districts | Foresthill, Iowa Hill, Loomis, Newcastle, Alpine Meadows, Olympic Valley, Alta, Colfax — each operate independently |

**Municipalities**: Roseville, Rocklin, Lincoln, Auburn, Loomis, and Colfax each operate their own fire departments with separate fire code adoptions (see city-level files).
