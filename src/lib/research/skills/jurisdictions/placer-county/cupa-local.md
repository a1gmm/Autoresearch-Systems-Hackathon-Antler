## Administering CUPA Agency

**Placer County Environmental Health** is the designated **Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA)** for all areas of Placer County **except** within the incorporated limits of the **City of Roseville** (which has its own CUPA administered by the Roseville Fire Department).

No other city in the county (Rocklin, Lincoln, Auburn, Loomis, Colfax) operates its own CUPA — all are covered by the county CUPA.

## CUPA Program Elements Administered

Placer County Environmental Health consolidates administration, permitting, inspection, and enforcement for all five state Unified Program elements:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP)** — Cal OES/CERS submittal
2. **Hazardous Waste Generators (HWG)** — including manifests and accumulation
3. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — permits, leak detection, closure
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — SPCC-adjacent, tanks ≥ 1,320 gal aggregate
5. **California Accidental Release Program (CalARP)** — for facilities with RQ-threshold regulated substances

## Facility Compliance Portal

HMBP submittals are made through the **California Environmental Reporting System (CERS)**: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov. Inspection and compliance records are publicly available via CalEPA's **Regulated Site Portal**, **GeoTracker** (UST), and **EnviroStor**.
