## POTW / Collection System Operator

Placer County does **not** own or operate a centralized treatment plant. The **Placer County Department of Public Works and Facilities** operates and maintains **eight separate sanitary sewer collection systems** in the unincorporated county, organized as Sewer Maintenance Districts (SMDs) or County Service Areas (CSAs):

- North Auburn SMD
- Granite Bay SMD / CSA
- Horseshoe Bar
- Several CSA 28 zones (including Zone 55 / Livoti, which contracts with **Sacramento County Sanitation District 1** for treatment)

Each district/zone is an independent revenue unit governed by the Placer County Board of Supervisors. Treatment is typically provided by downstream regional agencies (e.g., Sacramento County) or by the cities (Roseville's treatment plant serves adjacent areas). Large incorporated city systems (Roseville, Rocklin, Lincoln) run their own sewer utilities with separate ordinances and, in Roseville's case, a formal industrial pretreatment program.

## Governing Ordinances

| County Code Article | Subject |
|---|---|

| **Article 13.14** (PCC § 13.14) | Industrial Pretreatment Ordinance — regulates industrial discharges to protect district wastewater systems; also addresses discharges with adverse effects on collection systems. |

## Industrial Pretreatment Program

Article 13.14 exists on the books as the county's industrial pretreatment ordinance. However, because the county's collection systems are largely residential/rural with no major industrial dischargers, **a formal EPA-delegated Industrial Pretreatment Program (IPP) with facility-specific Industrial Waste Permits (IWPs) is not expected to be active for these county-run districts**. The systems that do serve commercial/light-industrial uses rely on Article 13.14 prohibitions (no prohibited discharges) rather than individual pretreatment permits.

## Fats, Oils, and Grease (FOG)

Each district's SSMP includes an FOG source control and public education program. FSE (food service establishment) requirements are enforced under Article 13.12/13.14.

## Note on Incorporated Cities

Facilities within city limits should use the city's own sewer utility and ordinances:
- **City of Roseville**: Roseville has a full pretreatment program with IWPs for significant industrial users (SIUs). Contact Roseville Public Utilities.
- **City of Rocklin, City of Lincoln**: Contact respective city public works departments.
