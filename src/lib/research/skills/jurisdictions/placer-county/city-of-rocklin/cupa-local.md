## Controlling CUPA

**Placer County Environmental Health** is the designated Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for the City of Rocklin.

Rocklin is NOT its own CUPA. The countywide CUPA covers all Placer County jurisdictions except the City of Roseville (which has its own CUPA through the Roseville Fire Department).

## Programs Administered

Placer County Environmental Health administers all five CUPA-consolidated programs for businesses in Rocklin:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — Cal/OES CERS online submittal required for facilities storing hazardous materials above thresholds
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator (HWG)** — oversight of generator compliance
3. **Underground Storage Tank (UST)** — permit, inspection, and closure oversight
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — for facilities storing petroleum ≥ 1,320 gallons aboveground
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention Program (CalARP)** — for facilities with regulated substances above threshold quantities

## CalARP / RMP

CalARP facilities in Rocklin are regulated under Placer County Environmental Health's CalARP program. Federal RMP (40 CFR Part 68) filings go to US EPA; the local implementing agency for CalARP is Placer County Environmental Health.
