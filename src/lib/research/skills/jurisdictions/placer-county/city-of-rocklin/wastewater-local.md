## POTW / Collection System Operator

**South Placer Municipal Utility District (SPMUD)** provides sewer collection service to the City of Rocklin. Rocklin does not own or operate a municipal POTW.

- Address: 5807 Springview Drive, Rocklin, CA 95677
- Phone: (916) 786-8555
- Website: https://spmud.ca.gov
- Hours: Monday–Thursday 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; Friday 7:00 a.m.–3:30 p.m.

## Treatment Plant

Wastewater treatment is managed through the **South Placer Wastewater Authority (SPWA)**, a Joint Powers Agreement (JPA) between SPMUD, the City of Roseville, and Placer County. The two regional treatment plants are **operated by the City of Roseville**. SPMUD's collection system conveys flows from Rocklin, Loomis, Newcastle, Penryn, and parts of Granite Bay to Roseville's regional plants.

## Industrial / Nonresidential Discharge

**Key provision (Sewer Code § 2.02.050.C):** "The admission into the public sewers of any wastewater or wastes generated from any nonresidential type use shall conform to SPMUD Ordinance 88-3, the District's ordinance adopting Chapter 14.26 of the Municipal Code of the City of Roseville relating to industrial wastewater."

This means industrial discharge standards for Rocklin facilities discharging to SPMUD track the City of Roseville's Chapter 14.26 industrial wastewater ordinance, incorporated by reference via SPMUD Ordinance 88-3.

### Prohibited Discharges (Sewer Code § 2.02.050)

Section 2.02.050 prohibits (among other things):
- Stormwater, surface water, groundwater, roof runoff
- Flammable/explosive liquids or solids
- Garbage (except properly shredded)
- Grease, oils, or toxic substances in amounts causing interference at the treatment plant
- Suspended solids requiring unusual attention or expense at the treatment plant
- Noxious or malodorous substances creating a public nuisance

### Fats, Oils & Grease (FOG)

Food service establishments (FSEs) must comply with **Chapter 3** of the Sewer Code (FOG program), adopted via Ordinance 09-01.

### High-Strength / High-Quantity Industrial Users

Sewer Code § 2.03.030 addresses capacity charges for commercial/industrial users with BOD or COD > 300 mg/L and/or suspended solids > 50 mg/L and/or flow ≥ 25,000 gpd ("High Strength-High Quantity Commercial/Industrial Users"), indicating SPMUD tracks these users for capacity purposes.

## Industrial Pretreatment Program (IWP / IUP)

SPMUD does not appear to operate its own formally-designated EPA-approved industrial pretreatment program (40 CFR Part 403) independent of the Roseville regional system. Industrial users discharging to SPMUD are subject to:
1. SPMUD Sewer Code Chapter 2 (prohibited discharges, pretreatment standards)
2. SPMUD Ordinance 88-3 (adopting Roseville's Chapter 14.26 industrial wastewater standards)
3. Any pretreatment program requirements of the receiving Roseville treatment plants under their NPDES permits

## Penalties

Civil penalties for violations, including pretreatment standard violations: up to $10,000 per day per violation (Sewer Code § 2.06.040).
