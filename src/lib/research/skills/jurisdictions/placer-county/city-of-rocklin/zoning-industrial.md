## Zoning Code Location

Industrial and business-park zoning is in **Title 17 (Zoning)** of the Rocklin Municipal Code.

Full Title 17 table of contents: https://library.municode.com/ca/rocklin/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT17ZO

The Planning Division is currently undertaking a comprehensive zoning code update called **ZONE Rocklin** — verify whether any industrial zone chapters have been updated through that process.

## Industrial Zoning Districts

### B-P Zone — Business Park (Chapter 17.42)
- Provides locations for professional offices, research and development activities, and **light industrial uses** that contribute revenues and jobs to the local economy
- Restricted to uses with limited/no impact on air quality, electrical/electronic interference, hazardous materials, light and glare, liquid/solid wastes, noise, odors, ground vibration, or water quality
- Municode: https://library.municode.com/ca/rocklin/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT17ZO_CH17.42ZO

### M-1 Zone — Manufacturing (Chapter 17.54)
- Light/medium industrial manufacturing zone
- Contains sections 17.54.010–17.54.025 (permitted uses, conditional uses, prohibited uses), height/lot/setback regulations, and off-street parking requirements (§ 17.54.080)
- Municode: https://library.municode.com/ca/rocklin/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT17ZO_CH17.54ZO

### M-2 Zone — Heavy Industrial (Chapter 17.56)
- Heavy industrial zone
- Contains sections 17.56.010–17.56.020 (permitted and conditional uses), height regulations, lot specifications, setback standards, zone size minimums (§ 17.56.090)
- Municode: https://library.municode.com/ca/rocklin/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT17ZO_CH17.56ZO

> NOTE: Rocklin is not a rural jurisdiction — it is a suburban city (~72,000 population) with an established industrial base. BP, M-1, and M-2 zones are all active districts.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

**Chapter 17.70** governs Conditional Use Permits and variances:
https://library.municode.com/ca/rocklin/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT17ZO_CH17.70COUSPEVA

CUP approval standard (§ 17.70.140): The Planning Commission must find that the use "will not, under the circumstances of the particular case, be detrimental to the health, safety, or general welfare of persons" in the area.

Decision-making body: **Planning Commission** (for most CUPs in industrial zones).
