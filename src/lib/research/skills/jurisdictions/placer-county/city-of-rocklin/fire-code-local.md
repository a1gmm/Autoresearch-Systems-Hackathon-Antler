## Adopted Fire Code Edition

**2022 California Fire Code (CFC)**, effective January 1, 2023 (statewide triennial adoption cycle).

The City of Rocklin's current-code-adoption page confirms all new permit submittals received on or after January 1, 2023 must meet the 2022 California Building Standards Codes, including the 2022 CFC.

The Fire Prevention Bureau enforces: California Fire Code, NFPA standards, and the Rocklin Municipal Code.

> NOTE: As of the 2026 triennial cycle, verify whether Rocklin has adopted the 2025 California Fire Code (effective January 1, 2026). Contact the Fire Prevention Bureau or check rocklin.ca.us/current-code-adoption.

## Municipal Code Chapter

Fire code adoption is codified in **Title 15 (Building Codes), Chapter 15.04** of the Rocklin Municipal Code (Municode reference: `TIT15BUCO_CH15.04UNCOCO_15.04.50FICODO`).

Section **15.04.50** contains the fire code adoption. The full municipal code is at:
https://library.municode.com/ca/rocklin/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=ROCAMUCO

## Adopted Appendices

Per the Fire Prevention Bureau's public guidance, Rocklin adopts the CFC **in its entirety**, including:
- Appendix BB — Fire-Flow Requirements for Buildings
- Appendix C — Fire Hydrant Locations and Distribution
- Appendix D — Fire Apparatus Access Roads

## Local Amendments / Supplements

- **Vegetation Maintenance Standard** (updated 2024) — local supplement for defensible-space maintenance; available at rocklin.ca.us/permits-and-guidelines
- **Fire Hazard Severity Zones (FHSZ)** — Rocklin has Moderate, High, and Very High FHSZ designations effective no later than June 10, 2025. High/Very High zones trigger CBC Chapter 7A (Wildland-Urban Interface) construction requirements. See rocklin.ca.us/fhsz.
- No other local CFC amendments found online. [VERIFY with Fire Prevention Bureau whether any additional local amendments were filed with the California Building Standards Commission.]

## Operational Fire Permits

Fire operational permits are required through the Fire Prevention Bureau. Permit types listed online include: Emergency Responder Radio Coverage Systems, On-Demand Mobile Fueling, Vegetation Maintenance, Residential Energy Storage Systems, and others.
