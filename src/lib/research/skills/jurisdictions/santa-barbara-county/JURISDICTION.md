## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA (Unified Hazardous Materials Program)

- **Agency:** County of Santa Barbara, Public Health Department, **Environmental Health Services (EHS) — Hazardous Materials Unit** — the certified **Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA)** for all of Santa Barbara County (unincorporated areas and all eight incorporated cities).
- **Programs administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP via CERS), CalARP, Hazardous Waste Generator / Tiered Permitting, Underground Storage Tank (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA/AST).
- **Local ordinance:** County Code **Chapter 18C (Environmental Health Services)**, with CUPA Fee Schedule codified at **Chapter 18C, Section 52** (amended by Ordinance 5079, adopted 4/9/2019; prior schedule was at Chapter 15, Article V, Section 15-104).
- **Site Mitigation fees** amended by Ordinance 5078 (also adopted 4/9/2019). Delinquent invoices accrue a 10% penalty after 30 days.
- **Reporting portal:** CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) — cers.calepa.ca.gov.
- **Landing page:** https://www.countyofsb.org/2171/Hazardous-Materials
- Source: https://santabarbara.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=3903681&GUID=E5192147-18C4-4D42-A779-E9EB08574B87

### 2. Fire AHJ and Fire Code

- **AHJ (unincorporated + cities without own fire dept):** **Santa Barbara County Fire Department** (sbcfire.com) — covers all unincorporated county land plus the cities of Buellton, Goleta, and Solvang. Excludes the Carpinteria/Summerland Fire Protection District, the Montecito Fire Protection District, Vandenberg SFB, and Bureau of Indian Affairs lands.
- **Cities with their own fire departments:** Santa Barbara, Santa Maria, Lompoc (see city files).
- **Adopted fire code:** **2025 California Fire Code (CFC)** plus selected portions of the **2024 International Fire Code (IFC)** — Chapters 1–80 and Appendices 4, A, B, BB, C, CC, D, E, F, G, H, I, J, K, L, M, N, O, P, Q — adopted via draft ordinance amending **County Code Chapter 15 (Fire Prevention), Article I** (replaces prior Article I adopting the 2022 CFC). Projected effective date: **January 1, 2026** (Board of Supervisors first reading November 18, 2025).
- **Local amendments:** County Code Chapter 15, Section 15-3 (amendments to the CFC/IFC); Section 15-2 defines "jurisdiction," "fire chief," "building department," "police department," and "fire protection certificate (FPC)."
- **Permit process — Fire Protection Certificate (FPC):** Two tiers:
  - *FPC-Minor*: New R-3 ≤ 3,600 sf or ADUs inside the Urban Limit Line; tenant improvements to occupancies with existing fire systems; change of occupancy equal or less hazardous.
  - *FPC-Major*: All new occupancies other than R-3/U; new R-3 > 3,600 sf or any size outside Urban Limit Line; additions ≥ 500 sf to non-R-3/U occupancies.
- **Fee schedule:** Chapter 15, Article V (Fees) — updated by the same 2025 CFC adoption ordinance with categorical fee changes; existing local fee amounts carried forward.
- **Ordinance reference:** Draft Ordinance (Ord. No. TBD); copy on file with Board of Supervisors Clerk.
- Source: https://sbcfire.com/article/2025-fire-code-adoption/
- Source (draft ordinance PDF): https://sbcfire.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Draft-Ordinance-Clean-Copy.pdf

### 3. Industrial Wastewater / Pretreatment (POTW)

- **No single county-wide sewer utility.** Santa Barbara County operates multiple independent sanitation districts; the applicable POTW depends on facility location:
  - **City of Santa Barbara / El Estero Water Resource Center** — POTW for the City of Santa Barbara service area. Pretreatment authority under **Santa Barbara Municipal Code Chapter 16**. Significant Industrial User threshold: ≥ 10,000 gpd of process wastewater or reasonable potential to affect operations. Permit application required 90 days before discharge; permits valid up to 5 years. Contact: Pretreatment@SantaBarbaraCA.gov / (805) 568-1093. Source: https://santabarbaraca.gov/government/departments/public-works/water-resources/wastewater-system/wastewater-pretreatment
  - **Goleta Sanitary District** (1 William Moffett Place, Goleta, CA 93117; (805) 967-4519) — POTW for the Goleta area. Pretreatment program governed by **GSD Ordinance #92**; enforces 40 CFR Part 403 general and specific prohibitions. One of the first pretreatment programs in CA requiring removal of contaminants on-site. Source: https://goletasanitary.org/environmental-protection/pretreatment-and-pollution-prevention/industrial-waste-control
  - **Laguna County Sanitation District** — county-operated district; pretreatment/sewer use ordinance details [VERIFY - not found online].
  - Other county service areas: see https://www.countyofsb.org/2547/Wastewater-Districts-County-Service-Area
- **Key rule:** Industrial users must identify the correct receiving POTW and apply to that district's pretreatment program — not the county CUPA or fire department.

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater

- **Permit authority:** California State Water Resources Control Board (SWRCB); regulatory oversight by the **Central Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board (CCRWQCB)**.
- **County MS4 permit:** Santa Barbara County operates as a **Phase II Small MS4** under SWRCB Water Quality Order No. 2003-005-DWQ, **NPDES General Permit No. CAS0000004** (Waste Discharge Requirements for Storm Water Discharges from Small Municipal Separate Storm Sewer Systems). Permit area covers unincorporated urbanized areas on the South Coast, Santa Ynez Valley, and Orcutt area of the Santa Maria Valley.
- **County program:** **Project Clean Water** (Storm Water Management Program, SWMP 2006–11 and subsequent renewals) — countyofsb.org/project_cleanwater.
- **Local stormwater ordinance:** Chapter 14 (Grading Code) incorporates erosion/sediment control requirements; a separate **Storm Water Ordinance** is referenced in SWMP Section 3.2.2 for illicit discharge detection and elimination [chapter number not confirmed online — VERIFY via current county code].
- **Industrial facilities:** Must comply with California's General Industrial Activity Stormwater Permit (IGP) from SWRCB and county MS4 requirements; illicit discharge to storm drains prohibited.
- **Cities independent:** Carpinteria, Santa Barbara, Goleta, Buellton, Solvang, Lompoc, and Santa Maria each implement separate SWMPs within their own boundaries.
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/water_issues/programs/stormwater/swmp/sb_county_swmp.pdf

### 5. Zoning Code — Industrial Districts

- **Code:** **Santa Barbara County Code, Chapter 35 — Land Use and Development Code (LUDC)**; administered by the **Planning & Development Department**. Accessible at: https://www.countyofsb.org/1043/Land-Use-Code and https://library.municode.com/ca/santa_barbara_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CH35ZO
- **Industrial zone designations (Article 35.2):**
  - **M-RP** — Industrial Research Park (lighter industrial / tech / R&D uses)
  - **M-1** — Light Manufacturing / Industrial
  - **M-2** — Heavy Manufacturing / Industrial
- **Chapter 35.25** identifies allowable land uses and required permit types within M-RP, M-1, and M-2 zones. Performance standards (e.g., noise: max 75 dBL10 at property line for M-1/M-2) apply to all existing and proposed development.
- **Design Review** required prior to approval of a planning permit for structures in M-RP, M-1, and M-2 zones.
- **Conditional Use Permit (CUP):** Referenced at Chapter 35, Section 35-172 for uses requiring discretionary review within industrial zones; processed through Planning & Development.
- **Coastal zone:** County also administers a **Coastal Zoning Ordinance (Article II of Chapter 35)** in the Coastal Zone; California Coastal Commission retains permit authority for certain coastal development.
- Source: https://library.municode.com/ca/santa_barbara_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CH35ZO
- Source (Coastal ZO): https://documents.coastal.ca.gov/reports/2010/8/Th17b-s-8-2010-a4.pdf

## Notes

- **CUPA structure:** Single county-wide CUPA run by Public Health / Environmental Health Services Hazardous Materials Unit — not by the Fire Department, and not split among cities. Fee ordinance: Ord. 5079 (2019), Chapter 18C §52.
- **GOTCHA (fire AHJ boundary):** The 2025 CFC adoption ordinance explicitly carves out the Carpinteria/Summerland FPD and Montecito FPD — those districts adopt fire codes separately (Montecito FPD: Ordinance No. 2025-02 per Legistar). Confirm AHJ for any project in those areas.
- **GOTCHA (sewer — no county-wide POTW):** Unlike CUPA, there is no single county sanitation utility. The correct pretreatment program depends on which district serves the parcel. Use the county wastewater districts map (countyofsb.org/2547) to identify the correct POTW before drafting an IWP application.
- **GOTCHA (MS4 permit number):** The 2005 SWMP references General Permit CAS0000004 under Order 2003-005-DWQ. This is the Phase II General Permit; confirm whether the county is now under a renewed or modified permit from the CCRWQCB.
