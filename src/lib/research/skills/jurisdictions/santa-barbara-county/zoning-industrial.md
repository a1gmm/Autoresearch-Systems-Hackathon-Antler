## Governing Code

- **Code:** Santa Barbara County Code, **Chapter 35 — Zoning**
  - **Section 35-1:** County **Land Use and Development Code (LUDC)** — applies to unincorporated inland areas
  - **Section 35-2:** Montecito Land Use and Development Code (MLUDC) — Montecito community only
  - **Article II of Chapter 35:** Coastal Zoning Ordinance (CZO) — Coastal Zone (California Coastal Commission retains CDP authority)
- **Administered by:** Planning & Development Department
- **Municode:** https://library.municode.com/ca/santa_barbara_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CH35ZO
- **County portal:** https://www.countyofsb.org/1043/Land-Use-Code

## Industrial Zone Districts (LUDC Article 35.2, Chapter 35.25)

The LUDC contains five industrial zone designations. All are mapped via the County Zoning Map.

| Zone | Full Name | Purpose |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **M-RP** | Industrial Research Park | Light industry, technical research, and business headquarters offices as primary uses; park-like environment with design/landscaping standards; ensures compatibility with adjacent non-industrial areas |
| **M-1** | Light Industry | Exclusively light industrial uses; protects nearby residential/commercial uses from hazards, noise, and other disturbances |
| **M-2** | General Industry (also "General Industry") | All types of industrial uses, subject to project review to minimize adverse impacts and ensure compatibility with surrounding properties |
| **M-CR** | Coastal-Related Industry | Inland area only; coastal-related industrial uses; avoids/minimizes environmental damage |
| **M-CD** | Coastal-Dependent Industry | Coastal Zone only; energy and industrial uses requiring a location on or adjacent to the sea |

## LUDC Chapter 35.25 — Industrial Zones

Chapter 35.25 of the LUDC contains the following sections:
- **35.25.010** — Purpose
- **35.25.020** — Purposes of Industrial Zones (by zone)
- **35.25.030** — Industrial Zones Allowable Land Uses (permitted vs. conditional use tables for M-RP, M-1, M-2)
- **35.25.040** — Industrial Zones Development Standards
- **35.25.050** — Industrial Zones Additional Standards

For the complete permitted-use table (which uses a P/CUP/— format), access the live LUDC at the Municode link or the county's Land Use Code portal. The table was amended most recently by **Ordinance 5230** (adopted February 4, 2025, Board of Supervisors vote: 5-0), which revised permitted uses in the **M-2 (General Industry) zone district** alongside SB 9 ADU provisions.

## Ordinance 5230 (February 4, 2025)

Ordinance 5230 amended LUDC Article 35.2 (Zones and Allowable Land Uses), Article 35.3 (Site Planning and Other Project Standards), and Article 35.4 (Standards for Specific Land Uses) to:
- **Revise permitted uses in the General Industry (M-2) zone district** (principal industrial amendment)
- Add SB 9 two-unit residential provisions (GC §65852.21 and §66411.7)
- Clarify ADU/JADU provisions per state law

File reference: 24ORD-00016

## Performance Standards

Per the LUDC, all development and new land uses in M-RP, M-1, and M-2 zones must comply with development standards including:
- **Noise:** Maximum **75 dBL10 at property line** for M-1 and M-2 zones
- Design Review required for all structures in M-RP, M-1, and M-2 prior to approval of a planning permit

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

- **CUP authority:** Chapter 35, **Section 35-172** (Conditional Use Permit) — for uses requiring discretionary review within industrial zones
- **Permit type (fire dept):** Development Review permits processed through the Santa Barbara County Fire Department also require a CUP-Major ($519) or CUP-Minor ($103) fire review fee (Chapter 15, Article V, Sec. 15-101(b))
- **Administering body:** Planning & Development Department, Building and Safety Division
- **Application portal:** https://aca-prod.accela.com/SBCOEH/ (county Accela system for EHS; verify planning CUP portal separately via countyofsb.org/plandev)

## Coastal Zone

Within the Coastal Zone, the **Coastal Zoning Ordinance (Article II of Chapter 35)** applies. Certain development requires a **Coastal Development Permit (CDP)** from the California Coastal Commission or county (depending on whether the parcel is in the retained jurisdiction area). The M-CD zone is the relevant industrial zone in the Coastal Zone.

Amended by Ordinance 5234–5236 (also adopted February 4, 2025) for SB 9 and telecom updates.
