## Adopted Fire Code Edition

- **Primary code:** 2025 California Fire Code (CFC), adopted by Santa Barbara County Board of Supervisors (first reading November 18, 2025; projected effective date January 1, 2026).
- **Supplemental code:** 2024 International Fire Code (IFC) — Chapters 1–80 and Appendix Chapters 4, A, B, BB, C, CC, D, E, F, G, H, I, J, K, L, M, N, O, P, Q adopted in their entirety except as amended in Sec. 15-3.
- **Replaces:** Article I of Chapter 15 that previously adopted the 2022 California Fire Code.

## Adopting Ordinance

- **Ordinance No.:** Not yet numbered at time of draft (draft shows "Ord. No. XXXX, §§ 1, 2, XX-XX-XXXX"); Board first reading November 18, 2025.
- **Municipal Code chapter:** Santa Barbara County Code **Chapter 15 — Fire Prevention**, Article I "Adoption of the 2025 California Fire Code and portions of the 2024 International Fire Code."
- **Draft ordinance on file:** Clerk of the Board of Supervisors; public copy at sbcfire.com.

## Territorial Scope

Chapter 15 applies to all areas within the **Santa Barbara County Fire Protection District** and all unincorporated areas of the county **except**:
- Carpinteria/Summerland Fire Protection District
- Montecito Fire Protection District (adopted its own code via Ordinance No. 2025-02)
- Vandenberg Air Force Base
- Bureau of Indian Affairs Lands

## Key Local Amendments (Sec. 15-3)

### Section 102.1 — Construction and design provisions
Agricultural buildings ≤ 3,000 sf used to house farm implements, hay, grain, poultry, or livestock are exempt, provided no hazardous materials, no public access, no plumbing/mechanical/electrical that would reclassify occupancy.

### Section 105 — Permits
Operational permits remain in effect until reissued, renewed, or revoked; not transferable — any change in occupancy, operation, tenancy, or ownership requires a new permit.

### Section 105.5.18 — Flammable and combustible liquids (local addition)
Permit required to **maintain non-operational Petroleum Facilities** (petroleum facilities defined to include tanks, compressors, pumps, vessels, and other oil-field equipment at a single location).

### Section 105.5.37 — Open flames and torches (local addition)
Permit required to use a torch or open-flame device in the **State Responsibility Area, Very High Fire Hazard Local Responsibility Area, or a wildfire risk area.**

### Section 105.5.40 — Outdoor assembly events (amended)
Permit required for outdoor assembly events with planned attendance **exceeding 300 people** or when other fire-code-regulated activities are involved.

### Section 903.7 — Automatic Sprinkler Systems (local expansion)
Beyond the state baseline, automatic sprinkler systems are required for:
1. All new one- and two-family dwellings (R-3) — per 2025 CRC §R313.
2. New non-residential buildings with enclosed building area ≥ **3,600 sf**.
3. All new buildings and structures **outside the Urban Limit Boundary** (regardless of square footage).
4. Existing non-residential structures ≥ 3,600 sf where alterations affect ≥ 70% of exterior walls or floor-plan configuration, or any addition causes total to reach ≥ 3,600 sf.
5. Existing structures outside the Urban Limit Boundary where additions are ≥ 500 sf (non-residential) or ≥ 1,000 sf (residential).

Exceptions: Agricultural buildings (including greenhouses), certain Group U buildings (case-by-case written approval), and Group S-2 buildings (case-by-case written approval by fire chief).

## Fire Protection Certificate (FPC) Process — Construction Permits

**FPC-Minor** ($536 for FPC-Major; Minor fee not specified in draft — contact AHJ):
- New R-3 ≤ 3,600 sf inside Urban Limit Line
- New detached ADUs inside Urban Limit Line
- New U occupancies ≤ 3,600 sf inside Urban Limit Line
- Tenant improvements where fire protection system already installed
- Change of occupancy equal to or less hazardous

**FPC-Major** ($536 per Sec. 15-101(c)):
- All new occupancies other than R-3 or U
- New R-3 > 3,600 sf inside Urban Limit Line, or any size outside Urban Limit Line
- Additions ≥ 500 sf to non-R-3/U occupancies
- Change of occupancy deemed more hazardous
