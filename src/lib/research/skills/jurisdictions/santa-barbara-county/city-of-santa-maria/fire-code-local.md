## Adopted Edition and Enabling Ordinance

- **Adopted edition**: California Fire Code (CFC), 2025 Edition (based on IFC 2024 with California amendments).
- **Adopting ordinance**: Ordinance No. 2025-05 — "An Ordinance of the City Council of the City of Santa Maria, California, Amending Portions of Chapters 9-04 through 9-76 of Title 9 of the City of Santa Maria Municipal Code Concerning Building and Fire Regulations."
- **Effective date**: January 1, 2026 (aligned with statewide Title 24 cycle).
- **Municipal code location**: Title 9 (Building Regulations), Chapter 9-28 (Fire Prevention Code).
- **Section renumbering**: Each amended CFC section is renumbered with the prefix "9-28." preceding the CFC citation (e.g., § 9-28.040 for Chapter 3 amendments).

## Chapter 9-28 Structure

Per § 9-28.020: Chapters 1, 3, 4, 11, and 56 are adopted in their entirety. Local additions, deletions, and/or amendments have been applied to Chapters 1, 3, 5, 9, and 56.

CFC Appendices B, C, D, H, K, and O are adopted in their entirety, inclusive of the Santa Maria Fire Department Development Guidelines (§ 9-28.030.b).

## Local Amendments — Key Provisions

### Chapter 3: Open Burning (§ 9-28.040.a)
Section 307.1.1 replaced in entirety:
> "Open burning is prohibited within City limits, except as provided in CFC Section 307.4.2, 'Recreational fires', CFC Section 307.4.3, 'Portable outdoor fireplaces', or by a single use permit issued by the Fire Chief."

Fire Chief or designee may require discontinuance of any open flame activity when determined hazardous. Justified locally by Santa Barbara County's high fire-risk designation and regular high-wind conditions (cited in Ordinance findings).

### Chapter 5: Fire Lane and Access Road Standards (§ 9-28.050)
- § 503.2 replaced: Fire lanes shall be ≥24 ft unobstructed width when building height ≤30 ft; buildings >30 ft shall comply with CFC Appendix D (aerial access roads).
- Multi-lane fire access: each lane ≥14 ft; a 14-ft lane requires 30-ft approach + 30-ft departure (60 ft straight line minimum).
- Grade limit: ≤12% grade; no overhead utilities within 30 ft horizontally of fire lane edge.
- § 503.5: All electrically operated gates across fire access roads require Fire Chief-approved strobe device and manual override in case of power failure.
- § 505.1: Commercial/industrial/multi-family address numbers: minimum 12" height, 1.5" stroke width; residential standard 6" height, 1" stroke width (increased from CFC default of 4"/0.5").

### Chapter 9: Fire Sprinkler Thresholds (§ 9-28.60)
All CFC § 903.2 sprinkler area thresholds reduced from 12,000 sq ft to **5,000 sq ft** for the following occupancy groups:
- § 903.2.1.1 Item 1 (Group A-1): 5,000 sq ft
- § 903.2.1.3 Item 1 (Group A-3): 5,000 sq ft
- § 903.2.1.4 Item 1 (Group A-4): 5,000 sq ft
- § 903.2.2.1 (added): All Group B occupancies >5,000 sq ft gross floor area require automatic fire sprinklers throughout.
- § 903.2.3 Item 1 (Group E): 5,000 sq ft
- § 903.2.4 Item 1 (Group F): 5,000 sq ft
- § 903.2.7 Item 1 (Group M): 5,000 sq ft
- § 903.2.9 Item 1 (Group S-1): 5,000 sq ft
- § 903.2.10 Item 1: 5,000 sq ft; R-3 exception deleted.

**Note**: No local amendments were made to CFC Chapter 50 (Hazardous Materials) or Chapter 32 (High-Piled Combustible Storage) — those chapters apply at state CFC thresholds.

### Chapter 56: Fireworks (§ 9-28 Chapter 56)
- § 5601.1.3 amended: "Safe and sane" fireworks (as defined by California Health & Safety Code § 12529) may be sold and possessed from noon June 28 through 11 PM July 4; may be ignited between 11 AM and 11 PM on July 4 only.
- § 5601.2.3: Fireworks stand permits issued per SMFD requirements and CCR Title 19, Division 1, Chapter 6, Article 13.

## Fire Investigation Unit
§ 9-28.030.d: A Fire Investigation unit is established within SMFD. Members, upon completion of California Penal Code § 832 requirements, have full California peace officer authority per Penal Code § 830.37.

## Fees
§ 9-28.030.f (replacing CFC § 108.2): Fire Chief or designee collects fees as established by City Council resolution, reviewed at least annually. Third-party plan review fees paid directly by applicant per contractual agreements between SMFD and vendor.

Penalties for violations: fine up to $1,000 and/or imprisonment up to 180 calendar days per violation; each continuing day is a separate offense (§ 9-28.030.k, replacing CFC § 113.4).
