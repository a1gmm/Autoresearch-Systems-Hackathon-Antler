## CUPA Structure

- **CUPA type**: County-administered — the City of Santa Maria is NOT its own CUPA. Santa Barbara County serves as the sole Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all incorporated cities within the county, including Santa Maria.
- **Certifying authority**: California Environmental Protection Agency (CalEPA) has certified Santa Barbara County as the CUPA for the County of Santa Barbara.
- **Administering agency**: Santa Barbara County Public Health Department, Environmental Health Services (EHS), Hazardous Materials Division.
- **Also known as**: The Department of Environmental Health Services, Hazardous Materials Division is operationally synonymous with the CUPA for the County of Santa Barbara.

## Local Ordinance

- **County ordinance chapter**: Santa Barbara County Code of Ordinances, Chapter 18C (Environmental Health Services) — governs local CUPA implementation, program requirements, and fees.
- **Key section**: Chapter 18C, § 18C-52 — CUPA Fee Schedule (amended by Ordinance No. 5079, adopted April 9, 2019 at second reading, repealing the prior CUPA Fee Schedule from Chapter 15, Fire Prevention, Article V, § 15-104).
- **Fee authority**: Fees revised per MGT Cost of Services Report findings and updated by county resolution.

## Programs Administered

The county CUPA administers all six unified programs for businesses in Santa Maria:
1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — required via CERS for facilities with hazardous materials above reporting thresholds; Business Plan levels 1–5 (see fee schedule).
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator** — annual permits by waste generation tier (0–0.99 tons through ≥20 tons/year).
3. **Underground Storage Tank (UST)** — permits per tank; plan review for new or modified systems.
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — annual permits by storage tier (<1,320 gal through ≥100,001 gal).
5. **CalARP (Risk Management Plan / RMP)** — three-tier base fee structure; hourly consulting rate for RMP services.
6. **Site Mitigation Unit (SMU)** — separate from CUPA proper; Phase I/II ESA review, project development review.

All HMBP, UST, and CalARP submissions are made through **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System): https://cers.calepa.ca.gov

## Notes

- Santa Maria businesses submit all CUPA documents through CERS, not through City of Santa Maria portals.
- The City of Santa Maria has no separate hazardous materials business license or local CUPA overlay ordinance.
- For releases/spills: Call 9-1-1 (emergency); county CUPA has release reporting guidelines published separately.
