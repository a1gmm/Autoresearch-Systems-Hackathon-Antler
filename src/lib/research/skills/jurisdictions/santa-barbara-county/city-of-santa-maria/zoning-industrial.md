## Industrial Zone Districts

Santa Maria has three industrial zone districts plus a planned development overlay applicable to each:

| District | Chapter | Minimum Lot | Height Limit | Primary Character |
|----------|---------|-------------|--------------|-------------------|
| M-1 Light Manufacturing | 12-15 | 15,000 sq ft / 100-ft frontage | 35 ft | Self-contained light industrial, indoor operations |
| CM Commercial/Manufacturing | 12-16 | 15,000 sq ft / 80-ft frontage | 40 ft | Light industrial + limited wholesale commercial |
| M-2 General Manufacturing | 12-17 | 15,000 sq ft / 80-ft frontage | 40 ft | Heavy/general manufacturing; may not suit residential proximity |
| PD/M-1, PD/M-2 | Chapter 12 PD | Varies | Varies | Planned development overlay on M-1 or M-2 base |

## M-1 Light Manufacturing District (Chapter 12-15)

### Purpose (§ 12-15.02)
Accommodates "light industrial and design-research facilities which are self-contained" whose processes are characterized by low generation of adverse impacts, plus non-public-oriented offices serving no general clientele.

### Permitted Uses By Right (§ 12-15.03)
All operations must be conducted entirely indoors:
- Administrative, executive, and data processing offices incidental/accessory to primary industrial or manufacturing uses
- Non-public-oriented offices (no general clientele)
- Scientific research and development laboratories
- Architectural and engineering design offices
- Light assembly operations
- Manufacturing, processing, and packaging of scientific, optical, medical, dental, and precision instruments
- Pharmaceutical manufacturing
- Printing and publishing
- Warehousing and wholesale distribution
- Limited retail sales where the product sold is manufactured, fabricated, or assembled on-site
- Wholesale bakeries
- Underground flammable liquid storage up to 50 gallons (with Fire Marshal approval)
- Wholesale/retail sales within 1,000 feet of US-101 and Stowell Road interchange

### Prohibited Uses (§ 12-15.04)
- Professional offices serving the public (accountants, attorneys, doctors, dentists, real estate brokers, investment/insurance brokers)
- All residential uses

### Conditional Uses — CUP Required (§ 12-15.05)
- Screened outdoor storage of materials/products (≤15% of lot area, on side or rear)
- Mini-warehouses
- Explosive materials and aboveground flammable liquid storage tanks
- Pipeline capacity increases
- Truck terminals with screened facilities
- Integrated child care facilities
- Tractor/heavy equipment sales near US-101
- Churches (in existing buildings only)
- Recreational vehicle storage yards

### Development Standards (§§ 12-15.07 – 12-15.09)
- **Lot size**: Minimum 15,000 sq ft; 100-ft minimum frontage on public street or accessway.
- **Height**: Maximum 35 ft; CUP required for structures exceeding one story or 18 ft when adjacent to residential zones.
- **Front setback**: 20 ft minimum; 10-ft landscaped strip required between front parking and public right-of-way.
- **Side setback**: 10 ft when street-facing or adjacent to residential zones.
- **Rear setback**: 10 ft when adjacent to residential zones.
- **Landscaping**: Landscaped strip required along all street frontages and along property lines adjacent to residentially zoned property.

## CM Commercial/Manufacturing District (Chapter 12-16)

### Purpose (§ 12-16.02)
Provides for "light, industrial and limited commercial uses (primarily of a wholesale nature)" with operations compatible with adjacent commercial areas.

### Permitted Uses By Right (§ 12-16.03)
- Wholesale bakeries, cabinet shops
- Blueprinting, carpet cleaning plants, bottling plants, medical/scientific laboratories
- Wholesale/warehousing facilities not open to public; van storage; feed and fuel; underground flammable liquid storage (with Fire Marshal approval)
- Distribution plants, utility substations
- Automotive repair/service (standard)
- Equipment rental and service
- Heavy retail conducted wholly within buildings
- Administrative/executive offices accessory to primary industrial use

### Prohibited Uses (§ 12-16.04)
- Residential uses
- Professional service offices (architects, accountants, attorneys, doctors, dentists, real estate brokers, investment brokers)
- Outdoor auto repair/service (one outdoor RV lift allowed by CUP only)

### Conditional Uses — CUP Required (§ 12-16.05)
Screened storage yards, mini-warehouses, kennels, commercial vehicle sales/service, automotive body repair/painting, tire sales, auto detailing/wash, transmission repair, trucking terminals, child day care centers, recycling facilities, physical fitness centers, camper sales/service, churches, pipeline capacity increases.

### Development Standards
- Lot: 15,000 sq ft minimum; 80-ft frontage.
- Height: 40 ft maximum; CUP required over 18 ft near residential zones.
- Setbacks: 20-ft front; no side/rear except 10 ft minimum adjacent to residential.
- Screening: 6-ft masonry wall required abutting residential zones; solid fencing for open storage.

## M-2 General Manufacturing District (Chapter 12-17)

### Purpose (§ 12-17.02)
"Provide a district which will ensure an environment conducive to the development and protection of modern industrial processes" that may not be compatible with proximate residential or commercial uses but can work alongside light manufacturing zones.

### Permitted Uses By Right (§ 12-17.03)
- Boat manufacture
- Heavy equipment assembly
- Welding operations
- Sheet metal fabrication
- Underground flammable liquid storage (with Fire Marshal approval)
- Administrative/executive offices incidental to primary industrial uses
- Other uses deemed compatible by Planning Commission (case-by-case)

### Prohibited Uses (§ 12-17.04)
- Professional offices serving the public (architects, accountants, attorneys, doctors, dentists, real estate brokers, investment brokers)
- Residential uses

### Conditional Uses — CUP Required (§ 12-17.05)
- Manufacturing enterprises with inherent qualities making them potentially incompatible or inharmonious with adjacent uses (Planning Commission determination by resolution)
- Storage/handling of explosives, blasting agents, or aboveground flammable liquids
- Food processing plants
- Salvage yards
- Mini-warehouses
- Pipeline capacity increases
- Pallet making
- Greenhouses
- Child daycare facilities (accessory)
- Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) — added by Zoning Text Amendment Z2025-0002 (March 2025)

### Development Standards (§§ 12-17.08 – 12-17.10)
- **Lot size**: Minimum 15,000 sq ft; 80-ft minimum frontage width on public street or accessway.
- **Height**: Maximum 40 ft; CUP required for structures exceeding one story or 18 ft when adjacent to residential districts within 100 ft.
- **Front setback**: 20 ft minimum; 10-ft landscaped strip required if parking is provided in front yard.
- **Side/rear setback**: None required except 10 ft minimum when adjacent to "R" (residential) districts.
- **Corner lots**: 10-ft minimum landscaped side yard.

**Mixed-use in M-2**: Mixed-use projects considered on a case-by-case basis; pre-application required before CUP filing (Chapter 12-49).

## CUP Process (Chapter 12-35)

- **Filing**: Application filed with Community Development Department, Planning Division; filing fee required.
- **Decision body**: Planning Commission for standard CUPs; City Council where land use or zone designation amendment is also required.
- **Deviation thresholds** (Chapter 12-35): Deviations up to 10% — Administrative Use Permit; 10–25% deviation — Conditional Use Permit; >25% deviation — City Council approval.
- **Required findings**: Site adequate in size and topography; yards/spaces adequate; adequate street access for traffic generated; use will not be detrimental to health, safety, morals, or welfare.
- **Conditions**: All conditions run with the land and bind owner and successors; right to use permit is contingent on fulfillment of all conditions.
- **Appeals**: From Planning Commission to City Council.

## PD (Planned Development) Overlay
PD/M-1 and PD/M-2 overlay zones are layered on M-1/M-2 base districts; pre-application required before CUP filing for mixed-use or significant development projects. Governed by Chapter 12 Planned Development provisions.
