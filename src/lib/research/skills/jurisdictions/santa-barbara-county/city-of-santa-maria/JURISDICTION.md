## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA / Hazardous Materials (HMBP · UST · APSA · Hazardous Waste)

- **CUPA agency**: County of Santa Barbara Public Health Department, Environmental Health Services (EHS) — Hazardous Materials Unit. Santa Maria is NOT its own CUPA; the county-wide CUPA covers all incorporated cities including Santa Maria.
- **Programs administered**: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), Hazardous Waste Generator, CalARP.
- **Santa Maria area contact**: 805-346-8460 (main Santa Barbara office); 805-681-4900 (county north county line). Hours: Mon–Fri 8 am–5 pm.
- **Online reporting portal**: CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) — cers.calepa.ca.gov — required for HMBP, UST, and CalARP submissions.
- **County CUPA ordinance chapter**: Santa Barbara County Code of Ordinances, Chapter 18C (Environmental Health Services) governs local CUPA implementation.
- **Fees**: Set annually by county resolution; call 805-346-8460 or check countyofsb.org/2171/Hazardous-Materials for current schedule. [VERIFY - fee schedule not published on fetched pages]

### 2. Fire AHJ / California Fire Code (CFC)

- **AHJ**: Santa Maria Fire Department — career municipal department; member of the North County multi-agency Hazardous Materials Team.
- **Fire Prevention Bureau**: Fire Prevention / Emergency Management Division — https://www.cityofsantamaria.org/services/departments/fire-services/fire-prevention-emergency-management
- **Adopted CFC edition**: **2025 California Fire Code**, adopted by Ordinance No. 2025-05, amending Title 9, Chapter 9-28 of the Santa Maria Municipal Code, effective 2026-01-01. Supersedes the previously-adopted 2022 CFC edition.
- **Municipal code location**: Title 9 (Building Regulations), Chapter 9-28 (Fire Prevention Code). Amended CFC sections are renumbered with the "9-28." prefix (e.g., § 9-28.040 for CFC Chapter 3).
- **Local amendments include**: open burning prohibited within city limits except CFC §§ 307.4.2–307.4.3 or single-use permit from the Fire Chief; wildland-urban interface provisions apply given county high fire-risk designation and high wind conditions.
- **Permit fees**: Established by City Council resolution (Schedule of Fees and Charges). Fire Chief authorized to collect fees and may contract third-party plan reviewers; third-party plan review fees paid directly by applicant per contract terms.
- **General use permits**: Required for any operation not in CFC § 105.6 that the Fire Chief determines could create hazardous conditions.

### 3. POTW / Industrial Pretreatment (Sewer Use Ordinance)

- **POTW operator**: City of Santa Maria, Department of Utilities — Wastewater Services Division.
- **Sewer use ordinance**: Title 8, Chapter 8-12 (Wastewater Collection, Treatment and Disposal) of the Santa Maria Municipal Code.
- **Industrial user classes**:
  - Class I — Residential/hotel flat-rate users; no discharge permit required.
  - Class II — General commercial; volume-based charges; no individual permit.
  - Class III — Industrial/commercial users with SIC-classified discharges; must obtain a **Wastewater Discharge Permit** before connecting; volume + constituent-based charges per § 8-12.1005(c).
  - Class IV — Significant industrial users with BOD/TSS/TDS/Na/Cl loadings; permit required; charges calculated by formula (Vc×Vu + Bc×Bu + Sc×Su + special assessments) per § 8-12.1005(d).
- **Discharge permit requirement**: Class III and Class IV users must obtain permit before connecting; existing users must have obtained permit within one year of ordinance effective date (§ 8-12 Article 10).
- **Federal pretreatment**: § 8-12.412 — federal categorical pretreatment standards supersede local limits when more stringent; dilution as substitute for treatment is prohibited.
- **Self-monitoring**: Permits may require self-monitoring programs with specified sampling locations, frequency, methods, and reporting schedules.
- **Permit process**: Submit discharge report (process description, flow, BOD, TSS, TDS, Na, Cl, SIC, hours, employees) to Director of Utilities; permit terms set by Director.
- **Portal**: https://www.cityofsantamaria.org/services/departments/utilities-department/wastewater-services
- Source: https://ecode360.com/43656946 (§ 8-12 Wastewater Discharge Permit)
- Source: https://pub-santamaria.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=6479 (Ordinance No. 2024 amending Chapter 8-12)

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater

### 5. Zoning / Industrial Districts / CUP Process

- **Zoning code location**: Title 12 of the Santa Maria Municipal Code (Zoning Ordinance).
- **Industrial districts**:
  - **M-1 (Light Manufacturing)** — governed by Chapter 12-15; allows certain mixed-use combinations as of-right with M-1 as primary zone and OS as secondary; other secondary uses require Conditional Use Permit.
  - **M-2 (General Manufacturing)** — mixed-use projects considered case-by-case; pre-application required before CUP filing.
  - **CM (Commercial/Manufacturing)** — hybrid district; restricted mixed-use allowances.
  - **PD/M-1 and PD/M-2** — planned development overlays layered on M-1/M-2 base districts (Chapter 12 Planned Development provisions).
- **CUP process**: Chapter 12-35 (permit procedures); deviations up to 10% — Administrative Use Permit; 10–25% — Conditional Use Permit; greater than 25% — City Council approval. Mixed-use regulations in Chapter 12-49.
- **Zoning information contact**: Community Development Department, Planning Division — (805) 925-0951 ext. 2244.
- **Zoning map**: https://www.cityofsantamaria.org/services/zoning-map
- Source: https://www.zoneomics.com/code/santa-maria-CA/chapter_56
- Source: https://www.cityofsantamaria.org/services/zoning-information

### 6. Building Authority

- **AHJ**: City of Santa Maria, Community Development Department — Building Division.
- **Adopted codes**: 2025 California Building Code (with Appendices C, F, G, H, I, J), 2025 California Fire Code, and companion 2025 Title 24 codes — all adopted by Ordinance No. 2025-05, effective 2026-01-01 (Title 9, Chapters 9-04 through 9-76 of municipal code).
- **Local building amendments**: Permit expiration fixed at 365 days (§ 9-04.030); SMIP fees 0.013% (residential) / 0.028% (non-residential) of valuation; WUI and high-wind roofing controls; seismic/liquefaction provisions due to active faults in Santa Maria area.
- **Portal**: https://www.cityofsantamaria.org/services/departments/community-development/building-division-homepage
- Source: https://pub-santamaria.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=12165 (Ordinance No. 2025-05)

## Notes

- Largest city in Santa Barbara County (~110,000 population). Operates its own municipal fire department, but is NOT its own CUPA — all hazmat permitting flows through Santa Barbara County EHS.
- High fire-risk county designation and regular high-wind conditions are cited local conditions justifying local CFC amendments.
- The City's groundwater has high mineral/solids content, driving tighter wastewater return-flow controls (cited in Ordinance 2025-05 findings).
