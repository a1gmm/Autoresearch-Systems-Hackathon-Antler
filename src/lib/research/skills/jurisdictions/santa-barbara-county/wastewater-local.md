## GOTCHA — No County-Wide POTW

Santa Barbara County has **no single county-wide sewer utility**. The applicable Pretreatment Program depends entirely on which sanitation district serves the parcel. Identify the correct POTW from the county wastewater districts map before drafting any Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit (IWDP) application: https://www.countyofsb.org/2547/Wastewater-Districts-County-Service-Area

---

## POTW 1: City of Santa Barbara — El Estero Water Resource Center

- **Operator:** City of Santa Barbara Public Works, Water Resources Division
- **Service area:** City of Santa Barbara limits (not unincorporated county)
- **Governing ordinance:** **Santa Barbara Municipal Code Chapter 16** (Sewer Use and Pretreatment Ordinance)
- **NPDES permit authority:** Central Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board (CCRWQCB)

### SIU Thresholds
A Significant Industrial User (SIU) permit (Tier I) is required if the facility:
- Discharges ≥ **10,000 gallons/day** of processed wastewater, OR
- Has reasonable potential to adversely affect POTW operations or violate pretreatment standards

### Permit Types
| Tier | Permit Type |
|------|------------|
| I | Significant Industrial User (SIU) Permit |
| II | Non-Significant Industrial User Permit |
| III | Groundwater Discharge Permit |
| — | Temporary Discharge Permit (min. 10 working days notice) |
| — | Direct Discharge Permit |

### Local Discharge Limits (Santa Barbara Municipal Code §16.04.100)

**Prohibited discharge (§16.04.100(B)):**
- Oil or grease of animal/vegetable origin: > **100 mg/L**
- Oil or grease of mineral/petroleum origin: > **100 mg/L**
- pH outside range: **< 6.0 or > 10.0 standard units**

**Local metal limits (§16.04.100(A)) — maximum concentrations:**

| Pollutant | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Arsenic | 0.27 mg/L |
| Cadmium | 0.09 mg/L |
| Chromium (total) | 2.64 mg/L |
| Copper | 1.1 mg/L |
| Cyanide | 0.97 mg/L |
| Lead | 2.0 mg/L |
| Mercury | 0.032 mg/L |
| Nickel | 1.86 mg/L |
| PCBs | 0.222 mg/L |
| Selenium | 9.37 mg/L |
| Silver | 0.59 mg/L |
| Zinc | 7.11 mg/L |

### Permit Process
1. Submit **Industrial Wastewater Discharge Application** at least **90 days** before new discharge begins.
2. Permits valid up to **5 years**.
3. Permit conditions include monitoring schedules and reporting.

## POTW 2: Goleta Sanitary District (GSD)

- **Operator:** Goleta Sanitary District (independent special district)
- **Address:** 1 William Moffett Place, Goleta, CA 93117
- **Service area:** Goleta area (City of Goleta and adjacent unincorporated areas)
- **Governing ordinance:** **GSD Ordinance #92** (Pretreatment and Pollution Prevention Ordinance)
- **Regulatory framework:** 40 CFR Part 403 (EPA National Pretreatment Standards) + state regulations

### Program Description
GSD operates one of California's first pretreatment programs. Industries must remove contaminants on-site before discharge. District conducts education, inspections, effluent testing, and enforcement. Discharge of hazardous substances to the collection system is prohibited.

### Contact
- **Phone:** (805) 967-4519
- **Hours:** M–Th 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM; Fri 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM
- **Program page:** https://goletasanitary.org/environmental-protection/pretreatment-and-pollution-prevention/industrial-waste-control

*Note: Specific numerical discharge limits and permit process details are in Ordinance #92 (downloadable from GSD website). Verify directly with GSD for current limits.*

## POTW 3: Laguna County Sanitation District (LCSD)

- **Operator:** County of Santa Barbara, Public Works — Laguna County Sanitation District (dependent special district; county's largest sanitation utility)
- **Address:** 620 West Foster Road, Santa Maria, CA 93455
- **Phone:** (805) 739-8750
- **Service area:** Santa Maria Valley / north county area

### Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit (IWDP)
LCSD requires an **Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit Application** (revised 3/8/16) for facilities whose process discharges include:
- Food preparation / processing / packaging
- Laboratory operations
- Dry cleaning or laundry
- Equipment washdown
- Printing
- Photographic processing
- Any process using chemicals that may enter the sewer system via floor drains, rinse water, or cleanup

Application requires: process flow diagram, plumbing layout, list of all chemicals/fuels stored, pretreatment device descriptions, list of current environmental permits, water consumption breakdown.

*Note: LCSD's specific discharge limits and ordinance citation are not published on the public-facing county website. Request the governing sewer use ordinance from LCSD directly at (805) 739-8750.*

### County Code Reference
Article III of Santa Barbara County Code **Chapter 29** (Storm Drains and Sanitary Sewers) — "Discharge into Laguna County Sanitation District Treatment System" — governs sewer connection requirements including installation of traps, interceptors, and other pretreatment control devices on private property (§29-27.1 et seq.).
