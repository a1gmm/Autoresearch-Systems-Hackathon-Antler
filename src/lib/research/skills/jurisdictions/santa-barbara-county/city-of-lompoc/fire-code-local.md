> Local fire code reference for facilities within City of Lompoc limits. Fire prevention and permitting is administered by the Lompoc Fire Department through the Building & Safety Services Division — not a standalone fire prevention bureau.

## Adopted Fire Code

- **Edition adopted:** 2022 California Fire Code (CFC), based on the 2021 International Fire Code (IFC)
- **Adopting chapter:** Lompoc Municipal Code (LMC) **Chapter 15.03** (General Provisions) — the code adopted in § 15.03.010 is referred to as the "Lompoc Fire Code"
- **Local amendments chapter:** LMC **Chapter 15.04** (Construction Regulations) — § 15.04.090 contains the California Fire Code local amendments
- **Code family:** LMC Title 15 (Buildings and Construction) — encompasses both Chapter 15.03 (adoption) and Chapter 15.04 (amendments)
- **Ordinance number:** [VERIFY — specific adopting ordinance number for the 2022 CFC edition not confirmed online; contact City Clerk at building@ci.lompoc.ca.us or check https://ecode360.com/43253300 (Ordinance List)]
- **Note on 2025 CFC:** The 2025 California Building Standards Code became the statewide standard effective 2026-01-01; confirm whether Lompoc has re-adopted to the 2025 CFC edition at https://www.cityoflompoc.com/government/departments/building-safety-services/building-and-fire-codes

## Local Amendments (LMC § 15.04.090)

The following are confirmed local changes extracted from search-indexed text of Chapter 15.04:

**Deleted Appendices:** Appendices A, E, G, K, L, M, and N are deleted from the adopted California Fire Code.

**Fire Chief as Code Official:** The Fire Chief is designated as the Fire Code Official for the City; where "Enforcing Agency" is used in the CFC it means the Fire Code Official (LMC § 15.03).

**Automatic Fire Sprinklers — Cannabis:** Each cannabis business conducting commercial cannabis activity (manufacturing, farming, processing, distribution, or sales) must provide automatic fire sprinklers in accordance with NFPA 13 and the California Fire Code (LMC § 15.04, referencing CFC § 903.2.11.7).

**Sprinkler Modification Triggers:** Modifications requiring addition or extension of an automatic sprinkler system include removal, combining, or rearrangement of demising/tenant space walls creating a single occupancy ≥ 5,000 sq ft, or any change in occupancy/use resulting in a requirement for sprinklers under CFC § 903 (including increased occupant load, multiple fire areas, use of hazardous materials, and other increased hazards).

**Hazmat Permit Amounts / High-Piled Storage:** Specific locally-modified permit thresholds for hazardous materials or high-piled storage beyond the CFC baseline are NOT confirmed from publicly available web text. Contact the Building & Safety Services Division to verify whether local tables modify CFC Table 5003.1.1 or Chapter 32 high-piled storage thresholds.

## Fire Prevention Permits

Fire prevention permits are administered through the **Building & Safety Services Division**. Permit categories include operational permits as defined by CFC Chapter 1.

Confirmed fee categories from search-indexed Master Fee Schedule (effective February 5, 2024, amended March 8, 2024):
- Low hazard storage permits
- Medium hazard storage permits
- High hazard storage permits
- Annual LPG / propane tank operation
- Non-compliant facility re-inspection fees (up to 3 inspections)

**Specific dollar amounts** from the 2024 Master Fee Schedule were not retrievable (city website blocks automated access). Access fees at:
- https://www.cityoflompoc.com/government/departments/management-services/city-fees
