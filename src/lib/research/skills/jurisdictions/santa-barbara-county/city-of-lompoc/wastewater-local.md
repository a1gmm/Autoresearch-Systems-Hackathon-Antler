## Discharge Prohibitions (LMC § 13.16.240–13.16.340)

No user may discharge any pollutants — including oxygen demanding pollutants (BOD) — at a flow rate or concentration that:
- Causes pass-through or interference with the wastewater system; or
- Causes or significantly contributes to an exceedance of the calculated maximum allowable headworks loading for any given pollutant.

Discharge of any hazardous substance is prohibited.

The Director develops discharge limitations as necessary to ensure compliance with the ordinance; these may be adopted by ordinance or resolution of the City Council, or incorporated directly into the user's wastewater discharge permit.

## Industrial Wastewater Permit Process

- **Permit type:** General Wastewater Discharge Permit (required for industrial and commercial facilities discharging to city sewer that may impact the pretreatment program)
- **Significant change trigger:** Any change in discharge ≥ 20% of long-term average flow or pollutant concentration must be approved in writing by the Director before implementation
- **Pretreatment devices:** Grease interceptors (and similar onsite devices) are required where applicable; FOG program governed by LMC § 13.16.520–13.16.610
- **Inspections:** City conducts inspections, sampling, and records review at permitted sites
- **No confirmed online portal:** No dedicated Industrial Wastewater Permit online portal was found; contact the Wastewater Division directly

## Pretreatment Program Obligations (Per NPDES Order R3-2022-0004 § 6.3.5.2)

The city is required under federal law (40 CFR Part 403) and NPDES Order R3-2022-0004 to:
- Implement and enforce its approved POTW Pretreatment Program
- Enforce federal categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR §§ 403.5, 403.6)
- Cause new industrial users to achieve compliance upon commencement of discharge
- Submit an annual Pretreatment Report to the Central Coast RWQCB (due February 1 of the following year)
- Maintain an updated list of industrial users (SIUs, CIUs, and non-categorical IUs)
