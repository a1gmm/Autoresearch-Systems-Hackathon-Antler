## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA (Hazardous Materials Unified Program)

- **CUPA agency:** County of Santa Barbara, Public Health Department, Environmental Health Services (EHS) — the county-wide CUPA. City of Lompoc is **not** its own CUPA and has no documented Participating Agency; all CUPA programs for facilities in Lompoc city limits are administered by the county.
- **CUPA programs covered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generator, Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), Risk Management Plan (RMP/CalARP), and Permit by Rule.
- **Fee schedule (effective 2025-09-27):**
  - Business Plan Level 1: $421/yr; Level 3: $456/yr; Level 5: $509/yr
  - Hazardous Waste SQG: $454/yr; RCRA LQG: $1,303/yr
  - APSA Tier I (≤10,000 gal): $844/yr; Non-Qualified (>10,000 gal): $1,195/yr
  - UST, 1st tank: $2,008/yr; each additional tank: $497/yr
  - RMP/CalARP Level 1: $1,582/yr; Level 2: $2,960/yr; Level 3: $4,748/yr; Level 4: $5,136/yr
  - Violation re-inspection / consultation: $214/hr; emergency/incident response: $321/hr
- **Portal / contact:** www.countyofsb.org/phd/ehs

### 2. Fire AHJ — Lompoc Fire Department / Building & Safety Services

- **AHJ:** Lompoc Fire Department (municipal); Fire Chief designated as Fire Code Official per LMC § 15.03.
- **Adopted fire code:** 2022 California Fire Code, adopted in **Lompoc Municipal Code (LMC) Chapter 15.03** (General Provisions) with local amendments — known collectively as the "Lompoc Fire Code." Appendices A, E, G, K, L, M, and N are deleted from the adopted code. Fire prevention permits and plan review are administered through the **Building & Safety Services Division**.
- **Note on 2025 CFC:** The 2025 California Building Standards Code became the statewide standard effective 2026-01-01; verify whether Lompoc has re-adopted to the 2025 CFC edition on the live Building and Fire Codes page.
- **Hazmat team:** LFD participates in the North Santa Barbara County multi-agency Hazardous Materials Team (with Santa Barbara County and Santa Maria FDs).
- **Contact:** 805-875-8220; building@ci.lompoc.ca.us; counter hours Mon–Fri 8 AM–5 PM.

### 3. POTW / Industrial Wastewater Pretreatment

- **POTW operator:** City of Lompoc, Utilities Department — Wastewater Division.
- **Sewer use ordinance:** **LMC Chapter 13.16** implements the federal General Pretreatment Program (40 CFR Part 403). Current through **Ordinance No. 1740(25)**, adopted December 16, 2025.
- **IWP process:** Industrial and commercial facilities that discharge to the city sewer may be required to obtain a **General Wastewater Discharge Permit**. Any significant change in discharge (≥20% change in long-term average flow or pollutant concentration) must be approved in writing by the Director. The city conducts inspections, sampling, and records review at permitted sites.
- **Pretreatment devices:** Grease interceptors and similar onsite pretreatment devices are required where applicable.

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater

- **Ordinance:** **LMC Chapter 13.32** — Stormwater Quality Management. The city is a **small MS4** subject to NPDES permit requirements administered by the Central Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 3).
- **BMP requirements:** The city may require Best Management Practices (BMPs) for any activities, operations, or facilities that may cause or contribute to pollution of stormwater or the storm drain system. Non-compliant discharges must be remediated at the discharger's expense.
- **Stormwater Division:** Implements the City's adopted Stormwater Management Ordinance and Municipal and Industrial Permit programs; sits within Economic & Community Development.

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts and CUP Process

- **Zoning code:** **LMC Title 17** — Zoning Code. Industrial districts codified in **Chapter 17.216**.
- **Industrial zone designations:**
  - **I (Industrial) Zone:** Light industrial, manufacturing, and limited accessory uses including outdoor uses. Allows industrial uses listed in Table 17.216.030.A subject to applicable permit requirements.
  - **BP (Business Park) Zone:** Clean, planned industrial centers on large integrated parcels where all activities are conducted indoors; intended for mix of business facilities with shared building complexes, common sign programs, and common landscape areas.
- **Outdoor storage:** In industrial zones, permanent outdoor storage must be screened by walls and landscaping; no material stored higher than the required wall/fence height; storage may not exceed 50% of site area and must be on the rear portion of the parcel.
- **CUP process:** Certain uses in industrial zones require a **Conditional Use Permit (CUP)** or **Minor Use Permit (MUP)** per **LMC Chapter 17 (Conditional and Minor Use Permits)**. CUPs allow discretionary review with conditions to mitigate use impacts.
- **Planning Division contact:** 805-875-8275; planning@ci.lompoc.ca.us.

## Notes

- Lompoc runs its own municipal fire department but is **NOT** its own CUPA — all CUPA/hazmat business plan permitting is handled by Santa Barbara County EHS.
- Fire Prevention function sits within the city's Building & Safety Services Division (not a standalone fire prevention bureau).
- Federal-land note: LFD's response area includes the United States Penitentiary, Lompoc — federal facilities may fall outside local CUPA/AHJ jurisdiction for some programs.
- The Lompoc Municipal Code is published on eCode360 (https://ecode360.com/43247427) and qcode.us (https://qcode.us/codes/lompoc/).
