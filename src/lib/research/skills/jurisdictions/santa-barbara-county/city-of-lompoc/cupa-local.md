> Local CUPA reference for facilities within City of Lompoc limits. Lompoc is NOT its own CUPA — the county-wide CUPA administers all six unified programs inside city limits. There is no Participating Agency (PA) agreement documented for Lompoc.

## CUPA Identity

- **CUPA agency:** County of Santa Barbara, Public Health Department, Environmental Health Services (EHS) Division
- **Structure:** County-wide CUPA — City of Lompoc has NO separate CUPA or Participating Agency designation. All CUPA functions for facilities in Lompoc city limits are performed by the county.
- **Web:** https://www.countyofsb.org/2171/Hazardous-Materials
- **Contact:** www.countyofsb.org/phd/ehs

## Programs Administered

The SB County CUPA implements all six CalEPA unified programs for facilities in Lompoc:

| # | Program | Acronym |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan | HMBP |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator Program | HWG |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tanks | UST |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act | APSA |
| 5 | California Accidental Release Prevention Program | CalARP / RMP |
| 6 | Permit by Rule / Conditionally Exempt Treatment | PBR / CE |

## HMBP Filing Portal

- **CERS (state portal):** https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business — required for initial filing and annual updates
- **Thresholds:** H&SC §25507 — generally 55 gallons liquid, 500 lbs solid, 200 cubic feet gas
- **Update triggers:** Quantity increase ≥ 100%, new material, name/ownership/address changes

## Local Ordinance / Municipal Code Reference

- No Lompoc city hazardous-materials ordinance found that supplements the county CUPA — the county directly administers CUPA for city facilities.
- Fire Code hazardous materials operational permits (CFC Chapter 50) are a separate city-level permit; see `fire-code-local.md`.

## Notes

- Fire Prevention function in Lompoc sits within Building & Safety Services Division, not a standalone fire prevention bureau.
- Lompoc Fire Department participates in the North Santa Barbara County multi-agency Hazardous Materials Team (with SB County and Santa Maria FDs).
- Federal facilities (USP Lompoc / Vandenberg Space Force Base) may fall outside local CUPA jurisdiction for some programs.
