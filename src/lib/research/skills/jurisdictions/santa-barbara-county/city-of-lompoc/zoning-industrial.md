## Industrial Zone Districts

Lompoc has two industrial zone designations (LMC Chapter 17.216):

### I — Industrial Zone

**Purpose:** Areas appropriate for light industrial, manufacturing, and limited accessory uses, including outdoor uses.

**Accessory uses allowed** (incidental and secondary to primary use only):
- Office, showroom, retail, and similar uses — not to exceed **49% of gross floor area** of the primary industrial use
- Wine tasting rooms — may occupy up to **100% of floor area** if no wine production on-site

**Outdoor storage standards:**
- Permanent outdoor storage must be screened by walls and landscaping
- No material stored higher than required wall/fence height
- Storage may not exceed **50% of site area**
- Storage must be on the rear portion of the parcel

**Landscaping:** Minimum landscape coverage of **10% of lot area** in the I Zone.

**Screening adjacent to residential:** Minimum 10 feet of landscaping at 75% opacity when new industrial construction adjoins a residential zone.

### BP — Business Park Zone

**Purpose:** Clean, planned industrial centers on large integrated parcels where all activities are conducted indoors. Intended for a mix of business facilities with shared building complexes, common sign programs, and common landscape areas.

**Key distinction from I Zone:** All activities conducted indoors (no outdoor storage or outdoor industrial operations).

**Also used as base zone for overlays:** The Special Event Overlay (SEO) Zone applies to areas that have a base zone of I or BP.

## Allowed Uses — Table 17.216.030.A

LMC Table 17.216.030.A (Industrial Zones Allowed Uses) governs permit requirements for specific uses. Confirmed structure from code text:

- Any one or more land uses in Table 17.216.030.A may be established on any lot in the industrial zone, subject to the planning permit requirement listed in the Table
- Where a single parcel is proposed for development with two or more land uses simultaneously, the project is subject to the highest permit level required by the Table for any individual use

Confirmed use categories in the Table (specific permit level — Permitted vs. CUP vs. MUP — per use requires direct code access):

The complete Table 17.216.030.A with specific permit levels (P = Permitted by right, CUP = Conditional Use Permit, MUP = Minor Use Permit, — = Not allowed) is accessible via eCode360 or by contacting the Planning Division.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

- **Governing chapter:** LMC Chapter 17.520 (Conditional and Minor Use Permits)
- **Review authority:** Planning Commission for full CUPs; staff-level approval possible for Minor Use Permits (MUPs) where listed in the Table
- **Standard:** Review authority shall approve (with or without conditions) only after making specific findings
- **Conditions:** Review authority may impose conditions to mitigate the effects of the proposed use
- **Fees:** Planning Division works on a **time-and-materials basis** for most applications (applicant submits a deposit; monthly billings for work performed). A few minor permits are fixed fees per the Master Fee Schedule (effective February 5, 2024, amended March 8, 2024).
- **Deposit and billing:** Submit deposit with application; monthly invoices for actual staff time
