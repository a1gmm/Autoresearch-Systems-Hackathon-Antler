## EHS Local Rules by Family

### CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **Agency:** City of Santa Barbara is **NOT its own CUPA**. Served by Santa Barbara County Public Health Department — Environmental Health Services, Hazardous Materials Unit (county-wide CUPA). No City of Santa Barbara Participating Agency is documented.
- **Programs covered:** HMBP (Hazardous Materials Business Plans), UST (Underground Storage Tanks), APSA (Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act), CalARP (California Accidental Release Prevention), hazardous waste generator oversight.
- **CERS reporting:** All regulated businesses must submit to California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) electronically through the county CUPA.
- **Contact portal:** https://www.countyofsb.org/2171/Hazardous-Materials
- Source: https://www.countyofsb.org/2171/Hazardous-Materials

### Fire AHJ — California Fire Code (CFC)

- **Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ):** Santa Barbara City Fire Department (SBFD) — career municipal department, 8 stations.
- **Adopted fire code:** Municipal Code **Chapter 8.04** — adopts the **2022 California Fire Code** (Title 24, Part 9) and the **2021 International Fire Code** (IFC), including Appendices B, BB, C, CC, H, and Chapter 4, with local amendments for climatic/topographical conditions (Sundowner winds, high fire hazard areas, wildland-urban interface).
- **Adopting ordinance:** **Ordinance No. 6094** (effective 2023).
- **Fire Prevention Bureau:** Ryan DiGuilio, Fire Marshal — (805) 564-5702 — RDiGuilio@SantaBarbaraCA.gov — 925 Chapala St., open M–Th + alt Fri 8 AM–5 PM.
- **Fire permit online portal:** https://permits.santabarbaraca.gov/Fire
- **Key permit fees (FY2025 Combined Fee Schedule, amended 07-02-2024):**
  - Specialty Permits (Place of Assembly, Pyrotechnics, Open Flames, Open Burning, Special Events): $276 first permit; $110 each additional
  - Fireworks Aerial/Ground Display: $441 + standby time
  - Fire Detection/Alarm Systems: $502 + $6.62 per initiating device
  - Special Fire Suppression System: $386 + $4.41/head + $6.62/device
  - Sprinkler (NFPA 13, new, 20–100 heads): $613 + $0.74/head
  - Underground/Aboveground Flammable Liquid Storage Tank removal: $386 first + $93 each; installation: $276 first + $93 each
  - LPG Tank installation: $331/tank
  - Hazardous Material Response: fully allocated hourly rate (Captain $132/hr, Engineer $116/hr, Firefighter $101/hr)
  - HazMat vehicle standby: $261/hr
- Source: https://santabarbaraca.gov/sites/default/files/2024-07/Adopted%20FY2025%20Combined%20Fee%20Schedule%20-Amended%2007-02-2024.pdf

### Wastewater / Pretreatment (POTW)

- **POTW:** El Estero Water Resource Center (city-owned and operated).
- **Local ordinance:** Municipal Code **Chapter 16** — Sewer Connections and Use / Wastewater Pretreatment Program; grants authority to issue permits, set pretreatment standards, monitor and enforce discharge limits.
- **Permit tiers:**
  - Tier I — Significant Industrial User (SIU): subject to categorical pretreatment standards OR discharges ≥25,000 gpd average process wastewater OR contributes ≥5% of POTW capacity
  - Tier II — Non-significant Industrial User
  - Tier III — Groundwater Discharge
  - Temporary Discharge Permits
  - Direct Discharge Permits
- **High-strength surcharges** apply when wastewater exceeds BOD, TSS, or Ammonia thresholds; fees per FY23 Sewer Rate and Fee Schedule.
- **Contact:** Pretreatment@SantaBarbaraCA.gov — (805) 568-1093
- Source: https://santabarbaraca.gov/government/departments/public-works/water-resources/wastewater-system/wastewater-pretreatment

### MS4 / Stormwater

- **Program:** City of Santa Barbara Storm Water Management Program (SWMP) — Phase II Small MS4 under federal NPDES regulations; implemented since 2006 under Central Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board permit.
- **Administrator:** Public Works Department, Creeks Division.
- **Local code:** Stormwater management and BMP requirements codified in the City Municipal Code (eCode360 titles reference storm water management and installation/maintenance of runoff requirements); BMP Guidance Manual (December 2020) is the operative technical standard.
- **Requirements trigger:** Post-construction stormwater treatment for new development and redevelopment; peak runoff discharge management, runoff volume reduction, and water quality treatment per BMP Guidance Manual.
- **Contact:** SWMP@SantaBarbaraCA.gov — (805) 897-2658; enforcement hotline (805) 564-5558 — 801 Garden St., Suite 200.
- Source: https://sustainability.santabarbaraca.gov/programs/storm-water-management-program-swmp

### Zoning — Industrial / Manufacturing Districts

- **Zoning code structure:** City of Santa Barbara Municipal Code **Title 28 (Zoning — Coastal)** and **Title 30 (Zoning — Inland)**. New Zoning Ordinance (NZO) for inland areas adopted July 25, 2017.
- **Manufacturing zones (Title 30, Chapter 30.30):**
  - **M-1 Light Manufacturing Zone** — principal industrial district; narrowed range of uses under 2017 NZO. Permits manufacturing, R&D, service commercial, and flexible commercial uses. C-M (Commercial Manufacturing) is a transitional zone below M-1.
  - **OM-1 Ocean-Oriented Light Manufacturing Zone** — harbor-adjacent manufacturing district.
  - **C-M Commercial Manufacturing Zone** — mixed commercial/light manufacturing.
- **CUP process:** Conditional Use Permits for industrial uses requiring discretionary review are processed through the Planning Commission; minor CUP fee $15,666 (non-residential per FY2025 fee schedule).
- **Planning counter:** 630 Garden St., (805) 564-5578.
