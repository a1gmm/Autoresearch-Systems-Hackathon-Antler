## Zoning Code Structure

The City of Santa Barbara uses two parallel zoning titles depending on coastal vs. inland location:

- **Title 28 (Zoning — Coastal Zone):** Applies to properties within the California Coastal Zone boundary. Manufacturing chapter: SBMC §§ 28.72–28.73.
- **Title 30 (Zoning — Inland):** New Zoning Ordinance (NZO) adopted July 25, 2017. Manufacturing chapter: SBMC Chapter 30.30.

> The "Uses Permitted in the Various Zones" PDF published by the city (last known version Ord. 5459 / 2008 base, with later amendments) documents Title 28 zone uses. Title 30 provisions are accessible via eCode360 (Chapter 30.30; note: ecode360.com returns HTTP 403 for direct API calls — access through city website or contact Planning Counter).

## Industrial / Manufacturing Zone Districts

### M-1 — Light Manufacturing Zone (SBMC § 28.72 / Title 30 Chapter 30.30)

**Purpose:** The principal industrial district; permits manufacturing, assembly, processing, distribution, and related service uses, provided operations are not obnoxious or offensive by reason of odor, dust, gas, fumes, smoke, liquids, wastes, noise, or vibration imposing a hazard to life or property.

**Includes all uses permitted in C-M (Commercial Manufacturing) zone** plus additional manufacturing uses listed below.

**Residential restriction:** No dwelling unit permitted except a caretaker/night watchperson's residence ≤ 400 sq ft net floor area.

**Permitted by Right (SBMC § 28.72.030.B — representative list extracted from city PDF):**
- Agricultural equipment rental
- Alcohol / alcoholic beverages manufacture
- Assembly plant
- Automobile body/fender/paint/upholstery; automobile laundry
- Awning manufacturing
- Battery manufacturing and rebuilding
- Bookbinding / book manufacturing
- Bottling plant; brewery; creamery; milk pasteurization
- Building materials (new and used)
- Candy, candle, cosmetics, drug, medicine, perfume manufacturing
- Cannery (except fish and meat products)
- Cement products, clay tile, pottery, statuary manufacturing
- Clothing, fiber, felt, knitting mill, rug, rubber products manufacturing
- Cold storage plant; ice manufacturing and storage
- Contractor / farming equipment yard
- Distribution plant; produce yard or terminal; draying and truck yard/terminal
- Dog kennel (boarding, breeding, training)
- Drug / medicine / insecticide manufacturing
- Electrical appliance / electrical equipment / electronic instruments manufacturing
- Furniture manufacturing
- Ink manufacturing; paint mixing (no boiling)
- Iron works, ornamental (no casting); metal spinning; sheet metal products
- Laboratory for research, testing, and experimental purposes
- Leather products manufacturing (no tanning)
- Machinery, farm and repair
- Novelty manufacturing; toy manufacturing; Venetian blind manufacturing
- Paper products; plastic products; sandpaper; wood products manufacturing
- Phonograph manufacturing; clock factory
- Plating
- Pumping plant; refrigerating plant
- Stone grinding, cutting, and dressing
- Tool manufacturing (no drop hammer or punch presses)
- Other substantially similar uses as determined by the Community Development Director (§ 28.72.030.C)

**Requires Conditional Use Permit (CUP — Chapter 28.94):**
- Covenant or monastery
- Educational facility
- Dog kennel (if discretionary review triggered)
- Any use not expressly listed but deemed substantially similar requires a Community Development Director determination; some trigger CUP

### OM-1 — Ocean-Oriented Light Manufacturing Zone (SBMC § 28.73)

**Location:** Harbor-adjacent manufacturing district (Santa Barbara Harbor area).

**Permitted by Right:**
1. Boat sales, storage, construction, and/or repair
2. Marine storage
3. Public parking lots
4. Sail manufacturing and repair
5. Seafood processing and wholesaling
6. Household hazardous waste collection facility (as defined in § 28.04.405)
7. Other ocean-related uses deemed appropriate by Planning Commission

**All other M-1 uses require a CUP** (Chapter 28.94), with additional Planning Commission findings:
- The use is compatible with ocean-dependent or ocean-related uses; AND
- The property has no feasible economic value if limited to ocean-dependent/ocean-related uses (substantiated by objective evidence).

**Exception:** Structures or developments with a valid, unexpired Coastal Commission approval as of OM-1's effective date may be used for all M-1-permitted uses without a CUP.

### C-M — Commercial Manufacturing Zone (SBMC § 28.71)

Transitional zone between commercial and light industrial. Permits all uses from C-2 (Commercial) zone plus limited light manufacturing. C-M is a prerequisite zone for M-1 (M-1 includes all C-M uses).

### C-X — Research and Development and Administrative Office Zone

R&D establishments with administrative operations; governed by §§ 28.60.005, 28.60.030, and 28.60.040.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

- **Authority:** Planning Commission (first-instance); City Council on appeal.
- **Governing chapter:** SBMC Chapter 28.94 (Title 28 Coastal) / Chapter 30.215 (Title 30 Inland).
- **CUP fee (FY2025 fee schedule):** Minor CUP for non-residential use: **$15,666**.
- **Planning Counter:** 630 Garden St., Santa Barbara, CA 93101 — (805) 564-5578.
- **Permit portal:** https://permits.santabarbaraca.gov (Community Development online portal).

## Title 30 (Inland NZO) Manufacturing Chapter

- **Chapter 30.30 — Manufacturing Zones** (adopted July 25, 2017): Prescribes land use regulations via **Table 30.30.020** for M-1 and related manufacturing zones under the 2017 New Zoning Ordinance.
- Use classifications defined in Chapter 30.295.
