## POTW

**El Estero Water Resource Center** — city-owned and operated POTW.

## Local Pretreatment Ordinance

- **Enabling ordinance:** Ordinance No. 5675 — repealed and replaced the prior Title 16 in its entirety.
- **Codified:** Santa Barbara Municipal Code **Title 16** — "Liquid and Industrial Waste Disposal."
- **Key permit-authority section:** SBMC § 16.02.040 (definitions of user tiers); SBMC § 16.02.020 (scope and general applicability); SBMC § 16.04.040 (groundwater discharge prohibition).
- **Pretreatment standards cited:** SBMC § 16.04.120 (Local Limitations on Wastewater Strength — see discharge-limit table below).
- **Fees and charges:** Established by resolution of the City Council; current reference is the FY23 Sewer Rate and Fee Schedule.

Full Title 16 PDF: https://santabarbaraca.gov/sites/default/files/documents/Public%20Works/Lab/Title%2016.pdf

## Permit Tiers (SBMC § 16.02.040)

| Tier | Name | Threshold / Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Tier I | Significant Industrial User (SIU) Permit | ≥ 25,000 gpd average process wastewater to POTW, OR categorical pretreatment standard applies, OR reasonable potential to adversely affect POTW |
| Tier II | Non-Significant Industrial User Permit | Wastewater characteristics exceed typical domestic discharge |
| Tier III | Groundwater Discharge Permit | Discharge of groundwater to POTW (typically remediation projects) |
| — | Temporary Discharge Permit | Construction dewatering or other short-term discharge |
| — | Direct Discharge Permit | Direct-discharge operations (e.g., pressure washing) |

> Note: The city pretreatment page describes the Tier I SIU threshold as "10,000 gpd or more of processed wastewater or with potential to adversely affect POTW operations" in its summary language, but the federal SIU definition (40 CFR § 403.3) sets 25,000 gpd as the process-wastewater threshold. Both the federal standard and § 16.02.040 apply; the more stringent threshold governs.

## Permit Application Timeline

- **New discharge (Tier I/II):** Submit application **at least 90 days** before initiating discharge.
- **Temporary discharge permit:** At least **10 working days** before requested start date (up to 30 days may be required).
- **Permit validity:** Up to five years.
- **Application form:** Industrial Wastewater Discharge Application (available from city Public Works).

### General Discharge Prohibitions (SBMC § 16.04)
- pH: must be **≥ 6.0 and ≤ 10.0** standard units (§ 16.04 prohibition narrative).
- Temperature: discharge may not cause POTW influent to exceed **104°F (40°C)**.
- Flammable/explosive materials (gasoline, naphtha, petroleum oils, volatile liquids) at hazardous concentrations: prohibited.
- Solids or viscous substances causing POTW obstruction: prohibited.
- Stormwater, surface water, groundwater, uncontaminated cooling water, boiler blow-off: prohibited from Community Sewer unless a specific permit has been issued.

### High-Strength Surcharges
Assessed when monitoring data show wastewater exceeding local thresholds for **BOD, TSS, or Ammonia**. Specific surcharge rates are set in the FY23 Sewer Rate and Fee Schedule (available from Public Works).

## Priority Pollutant List

Title 16 incorporates the EPA 129 Priority Pollutants list by reference (§ 16.02.040, Appendix), which includes organics such as benzene, toluene, chloroform, PCBs, PAHs, and pesticides. Specific categorical limits (40 CFR Parts 405–471) apply to regulated industry categories.
