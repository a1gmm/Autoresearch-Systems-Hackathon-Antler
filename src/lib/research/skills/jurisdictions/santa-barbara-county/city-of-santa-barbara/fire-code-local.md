## Adopting Ordinance

**Ordinance No. 6094** — adopted by the City Council of the City of Santa Barbara.
**Effective date:** 2023 (exact effective date in ordinance text on file with City Clerk).
**Municipal Code chapter:** Title 8, **Chapter 8.04** (Fire Code).

## Codes Adopted by Reference (§ 8.04.010)

- **2021 International Fire Code (IFC)** — including Appendix Chapter 4 and Appendices B, BB, C, CC, and H.
- **2022 California Fire Code (CFC)** — Title 24, Part 9 of the California Code of Regulations.
- All standards and secondary codes referenced in those codes.
- The combined set is known as the "City of Santa Barbara Fire Code."

> **2025 CFC status (VERIFY):** Santa Barbara County adopted the 2025 CFC via a draft ordinance (first reading Nov 18, 2025; projected effective Jan 1, 2026) for unincorporated county areas. ## Local Amendments (§ 8.04.020)

Justified by local climatic (Sundowner winds ≥ 60 mph, extended drought), topographical (steep foothill roads, limited Highway 101 corridor), and geological (known fault network) conditions:

### Administration / Enforcement
- § 103 renamed "Fire Prevention Bureau"; bureau personnel have police powers while on duty.
- § 104.11 Fire investigations expanded to cover unauthorized hazardous-material releases.
- § 111 Board of Appeals deleted and replaced by § 115 Building and Fire Code Board of Appeals (7 members appointed by City Council, 4-year terms; cannot waive Title 24 requirements).
- § 112.4 Violation penalties: violations are a misdemeanor; each day a continuing violation is a separate offense.

### Open Burning / Outdoor Hazards
- § 307.1.1: Open burning that causes objectionable smoke or is made hazardous by atmospheric conditions is prohibited.
- § 308.1.4.1: LP-gas cooking devices with containers > 25 lbs (5-gallon) water capacity prohibited on combustible balconies or within 10 ft of combustible construction (exception: 1- and 2-family dwellings).
- § 317.1: Rooftop gardens and landscaped (vegetated) roofs are **prohibited in High Fire Hazard areas**.

### Chapter 4 — Emergency Planning
- Chapter 4 of the IFC is **deleted in its entirety** without replacement.

### Fire Apparatus Access Roads (§ 503 — fully deleted and re-adopted)
- Minimum unobstructed width: **20 ft** (16 ft for roads serving ≤ 3 single-family units, with fire code official approval).
- Road surface must support **60,000 lbs**.
- Maximum grade: **16%**; cross-slope ≤ 6%.
- Turning radius: ≥ **70 ft** (outer edge to outer edge).
- Dead-end roads > 300 ft: approved turnaround required.
- High-piled storage buildings: vehicle access per Chapter 32 (§ 503.1.3 retained).

### Explosives / Fireworks (§ 5601)
- § 5601.2: Storage of explosives and blasting agents restricted to the A-I (Airport Industrial) zone.
- § 5601.1.3: Manufacturing, possession, storage, sale, use, and handling of fireworks **prohibited** in the city (with narrow exceptions for licensed displays, pyrotechnic special effects, and Health & Safety Code Division 11 manufacturing).
- § 5601.7: Fire code official may seize and dispose of illegal fireworks/explosives at owner's expense.

### Fire Zone 2 Development Standards (§ 8.04.030)
- Buildings in Fire Zone 2 (mapped by the fire code official; includes High Fire Hazard and foothill areas) must provide a **10,000-gallon on-site water tank** for fire protection, plus:
  - Fire apparatus access compliant with § 503.
  - Landscaping within 150 ft of any structure must be fire-resistant.
  - Native brush, shrubs, and grasses cleared within 150 ft of any structure.
  - Residential fire sprinklers installed in any building used for sleeping or cooking (per NFPA Residential Standards).

### Mixed-Use Occupancy Notification
- Signs indicating residential units in mixed-use buildings required; format: "R-[floor number(s)]" — minimum 4-inch letters with ½-inch stroke, contrasting color, reflective tape on glass.
