## CUPA Agency

- **Agency:** County of Santa Barbara, **Public Health Department — Environmental Health Services (EHS), Hazardous Materials Unit**
- **Structure:** Single county-wide **own-CUPA** — certified by CalEPA; covers all unincorporated county land and all eight incorporated cities (Carpinteria, Goleta, Buellton, Solvang, Santa Barbara, Santa Maria, Lompoc, and the unincorporated areas). This is NOT administered by the Fire Department.
- **CUPA type:** Own-CUPA (not under county Fire Dept; administered by Public Health / EHS).

## Programs Administered

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — submitted via CERS (cers.calepa.ca.gov)
2. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — RMP equivalent
3. **Hazardous Waste Generator / Tiered Permitting** — covers Conditionally Exempt, Conditional Authorization, Permit-by-Rule (PBR), and Full Permit tiers (enforced per DTSC delegation)
4. **Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program** — installation, closure, monitoring, leak response
5. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA/AST)** — tanks ≥ 1,320 gallons aggregate

## Governing Local Ordinance

- **Primary chapter:** Santa Barbara County Code **Chapter 18C — Environmental Health Services**
- **CUPA Fee Schedule:** Chapter 18C, **Section 52** (amended by **Ordinance 5079**, adopted April 9, 2019, Board of Supervisors second reading)
  - Prior fee schedule was at Chapter 15, Article V, Section 15-104 (repealed by Ord. 5079)
- **Site Mitigation Fee Schedule:** Amended by **Ordinance 5078**, also adopted April 9, 2019
- **Delinquent invoice penalty:** 10% after 30 days
- **CEQA exemption basis for Ord. 5078/5079:** Fees used for operating expenses and purchase of supplies/equipment/materials

## CUPA Fee Schedule Structure

The actual dollar amounts in the fee schedule adopted by Ord. 5079 are codified at **Chapter 18C, §52** and were derived from an MGT Cost of Services analysis. Specific line-item amounts are in the schedule attachment (not reproduced in the legislative text). Contact EHS for the current fee schedule or access via countyofsb.org.

Key fee-triggering programs by CERS program element:
- HMBP (Hazardous Materials Business Plan) — annual permit fee by chemical quantity tier
- Hazardous Waste Generator — fee by tier (CE, CA, PBR, Full Permit)
- UST — fee per tank and per inspection cycle
- CalARP — fee based on Program level (Program 1/2/3)
- APSA — fee per facility

## Interaction with Fire Dept Permits

The Santa Barbara County Fire Department (Chapter 15) issues **separate** fire code operational permits for hazardous materials (Groups 1–4 by quantity) and for UST/AST operations. Where both apply, fire permit fees and CUPA fees are owed independently — the fire ordinance notes "CUPA fees apply" for UST-related permits (operational and construction), meaning the fire dept does not collect a separate fee for those.
