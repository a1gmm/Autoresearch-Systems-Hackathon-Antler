## Industrial Zone Districts — Chapter 18.16

Industrial zones are codified at **Title 18, Chapter 18.16 — Industrial Zones (B-P, M-1, M-2)**. Three districts exist:

### B-P — Business Park Zone
**Purpose:** Low-impact industrial and professional uses in campus-like settings.
Typical uses: research and development facilities, professional/executive offices, light manufacturing, warehouse and distribution centers, fabrication.

### M-1 — Light Manufacturing Zone
**Purpose:** Warehousing, assembly, manufacturing, wholesaling, R&D, commercial offices, and limited accessory retail sales.

### M-2 — General Manufacturing Zone
**Purpose:** Broader manufacturing and heavier industrial uses. [VERIFY — specific use list and standards for M-2 not captured in fetched online text; review Chapter 18.16 full text for M-2 provisions.]

## Allowable Uses and Permit Requirements

Allowable uses and permit triggers for all three zones are tabulated in **Table 2-7** of the UDO (Article 2: Zones, Allowable Uses, and Development Standards). Uses are categorized as:
- Permitted by right
- Subject to Administrative Permit
- Subject to **Conditional Use Permit (CUP)**

CUP authority rests with the Planning Commission (or Director for administrative permits). Setbacks may be increased for CUP uses when the Director or Commission determines additional buffering is necessary for compatibility with surrounding properties.

Permit processing procedures are governed by **Article 6** of Title 18 (Permit Processing Procedures) and **Article 7** (Zoning Code Administration).
