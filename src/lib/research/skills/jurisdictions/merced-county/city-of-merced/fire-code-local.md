> Research reference for the City of Merced fire code adoption and local AHJ.
> The City of Merced maintains its own fire department and acts as the AHJ within city limits.
> Ordinance 2583 was introduced at the December 1, 2025 City Council meeting and adopts the 2025 California Fire Code; confirm passage/effective date with the City Clerk or Municode.

## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

- **AHJ:** City of Merced Fire Department (career/municipal department; multiple stations)
- **Division:** Fire Prevention / Community Risk Reduction
- **Web:** https://www.cityofmerced.gov/public-safety/fire/fire-prevention-community-risk-reduction
- **Email:** Fireweb@cityofmerced.gov
- **City Hall / General:** (209) 388-7000

## Adopted Fire Code

- **Code edition:** 2025 California Fire Code (CFC), which incorporates the 2024 International Fire Code as amended by California — introduced via Ordinance 2583 at the December 1, 2025 City Council meeting
- **Municipal code chapter:** Chapter 17.32 "Fire Prevention Code" of the Merced Municipal Code (Title 17 — Buildings and Construction)
- **Adopting ordinance:** Ordinance 2583 (introduced December 1, 2025); amends Chapter 17.32 of the Merced Municipal Code
- **Companion ordinance:** Ordinance 2582 (same meeting) — amends multiple Title 17 sections (17.04.010, 17.05.010, 17.06.010, 17.07.010, 17.09.010, 17.10.010, 17.11.010, 17.12.010, 17.20.010, 17.24.010, 17.28.020, 17.34.010) to adopt the 2025 California Building Standards Code (Title 24, Parts 1–12)
- **Effective date:** [VERIFY — confirm second reading adoption date and effective date with City Clerk; introduced December 1, 2025, second reading/adoption likely December 15, 2025 or January 2026]
- **Prior cycle:** 2022 CFC was the prior edition; confirm whether Ord. 2545 (2022) was the prior adopting ordinance [VERIFY]

## Local Amendments

The December 1, 2025 staff report identified the following subject areas for local amendments (specific text not published online; [VERIFY] against adopted ordinance):

- Electric vehicles and EV charging infrastructure
- Lithium-ion battery energy storage systems (permitting and life-safety provisions)
- Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI) provisions — the City has published a Fire Hazard Severity Zone map: https://www.cityofmerced.gov/public-safety/fire/fire-hazard-severity-zone-maps
- Fire flow requirements for community safety

## Note on County Fire

The City of Merced operates its own municipal fire department (City of Merced Fire Department) and is not served by the Merced County Fire Department or CAL FIRE for fire prevention/AHJ purposes within city limits. Merced County Fire serves unincorporated county areas.
