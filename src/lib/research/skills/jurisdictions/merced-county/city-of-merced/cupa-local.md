> Research reference for the CUPA / Unified Program authority serving the City of Merced.
> The City of Merced is NOT its own CUPA — all six Unified Program elements are administered by the Merced County Division of Environmental Health (MCDEH) as the countywide CUPA, including facilities inside city limits.

## Programs Administered

MCDEH administers all six CalEPA Unified Program elements for all businesses in Merced County, including within City of Merced limits:

| # | Program | Acronym |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan | HMBP |
| 2 | California Accidental Release Prevention Program | CalARP |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tank Program | UST |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act / Aboveground Storage Tanks | APSA |
| 5 | Hazardous Waste Generator Program | HWG |
| 6 | Tiered Permitting Program (onsite hazardous waste treatment) | TPP |

## HMBP Program Details

- **Submission system:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business
- **Who must comply:** Any business storing, using, or handling hazardous materials at or above statutory threshold quantities (generally 55 gallons liquid, 500 lbs solid, 200 cubic feet compressed gas — per Health & Safety Code §25507)
- **Filing:** Annual via CERS; MCDEH is the reviewing agency
- **Inspections:** MCDEH CUPA team conducts routine, education-based inspections of regulated businesses; frequency [VERIFY with MCDEH]

## Relationship to City of Merced Fire Department

- The City of Merced Fire Department is the fire code AHJ within city limits (see fire-code-local.md) but is NOT the CUPA.
- HMBP filings, hazardous waste generator compliance, UST/APSA oversight, and CalARP review all go to MCDEH, not the city fire department.
- CFC hazmat operational permits (above CFC threshold quantities) are issued by the City of Merced Fire Department as AHJ, while the HMBP program is with MCDEH. These are parallel requirements.

## CalARP

- MCDEH serves as the Local Implementing Agency (LIA) for CalARP for facilities in Merced County including within city limits.
- Facilities with regulated substances above threshold quantities must submit a Risk Management Plan (RMP) to USEPA; MCDEH reviews the local CalARP portion.
