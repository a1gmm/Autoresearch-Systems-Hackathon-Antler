## Industrial Zoning Districts

The City of Merced Zoning Ordinance (Title 20, Article 2) includes the following industrial and employment districts:

### B-P — Business Park Zone

- **Purpose:** Employment-intensive uses in a campus-like setting; primarily "back-office," research and development, and limited commercial retail to serve employees
- **Typical uses:** Research and development facilities, professional and executive offices, light industrial fabrication, warehouse and distribution, limited retail and service uses for employees
- **Note:** Lower-impact industrial uses; not intended for heavy manufacturing or outdoor industrial operations

### M-1 — Light Manufacturing Zone

- **Purpose:** Warehousing, assembling, manufacturing, wholesaling, research and development, commercial offices, and limited accessory retail sales
- **Typical uses:** Light industrial fabrication, warehousing, R&D, wholesale distribution

### M-2 — Heavy Manufacturing Zone

- **Purpose:** Higher-impact manufacturing and industrial operations
- **Note:** Specific permitted uses and development standards are in Title 20, Article 2, Table 2-7 (Industrial Zone Allowed Uses and Permit Requirements) [VERIFY — navigate Municode to confirm M-2 purpose statement and use table]

## Allowed Uses and Permit Requirements

Per Title 20, Article 2, Table 2-7 documents the uses allowed in each industrial zone and the permit required:

- **By-right (P):** Use permitted without a discretionary permit; typically requires building permit only
- **Administrative Use Permit (AUP):** Ministerial review by Planning Director; used for uses that may need minor conditions but are generally compatible
- **Conditional Use Permit (CUP):** Discretionary review by Planning Commission; required for uses that have higher potential for impacts on surrounding properties
- **Not permitted (–):** Use not allowed in the zone

[VERIFY] The specific permit tier (P / AUP / CUP) for a given industrial use must be confirmed against the current Title 20 Table 2-7 via Municode or by contacting the Planning Division.

## CUP Process

- **Review body:** City of Merced Planning Commission (for CUPs); Planning Director (for AUPs)
- **Article reference:** Article 6 (Permit Processing Procedures) and Article 7 (Zoning Code Administration) of the Merced Zoning Ordinance
- **Setbacks:** Setbacks for uses requiring an AUP or CUP may be increased by the Director or Commission to ensure compatibility with surrounding properties
- **Online permitting:** Civic Access portal — civicaccess@cityofmerced.gov / https://www.cityofmerced.gov/business-and-development/permits-entitlements-and-licensing-portal
