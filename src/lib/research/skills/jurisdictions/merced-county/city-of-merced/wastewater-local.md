> The City of Merced operates its own POTW and a federally-required Industrial Pretreatment Program. This is not a county-run program.

- **Operator:** City of Merced (Water Quality Control Division / Utilities & Services)

### Who Must Obtain an Industrial Pretreatment Permit

Under 40 CFR Part 403:
- **Significant Industrial Users (SIUs):** Categorical industrial users (subject to EPA categorical pretreatment standards at 40 CFR Parts 405–471) and non-categorical dischargers exceeding 25,000 gpd or with pass-through/interference potential
- **Other industrial users** as determined by the Control Authority (City of Merced) based on discharge characteristics

### Permit Process

1. Contact City of Merced Water Quality Control Division to determine permit tier and applicability.
2. Submit application with facility information, SIC code, process description, and wastewater characterization data.
3. City reviews application and may conduct a site inspection.
4. If permit is required, City issues an Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit with facility-specific limits and monitoring requirements.
5. Annual permit renewal; compliance monitoring reports required throughout permit term.

### Prohibited Discharges (Federal Baseline — 40 CFR 403.5)

Per NPDES Order R5-2020-0014, the City must enforce at minimum these prohibitions against all industrial users:
- Wastes creating fire or explosion hazard in the treatment works
- Wastes with pH lower than 5.0 (corrosive damage)
- Solid or viscous wastes causing obstruction to flow
- Any pollutant introduced at a flow rate or concentration causing interference or pass-through
- Heat in excess of amounts that will inhibit biological activity (generally, discharge temp < 40°C / 104°F)
- Petroleum oil, non-biodegradable cutting oil, or mineral oil products causing interference or pass-through

## NPDES Order Key Pretreatment Obligations (from Order R5-2020-0014)

Per NPDES Order R5-2020-0014, Section VI.C.5 (Special Provisions for POTWs), the City of Merced as Control Authority must:
- Implement legal authorities per 40 CFR 403.8(f)(1)
- Enforce prohibited discharge standards under 40 CFR 403.5 and 403.6
- Conduct pretreatment programmatic functions per 40 CFR 403.8(f)(2)
- Submit annual pretreatment reports to the Central Valley Water Board, USEPA Region 9, and State Water Board by the due date in Table E-14 of the permit's MRP
