## POTW / Sanitation Structure

Merced County (unincorporated) does **not** operate a single county-wide sewer utility. Wastewater service in unincorporated areas is fragmented across multiple independent water/sewer districts and community services districts. There is no unified county POTW serving general unincorporated industrial areas.

Properties outside district boundaries are typically served by **on-site wastewater treatment systems (septic)** regulated under **Merced County Code Chapter 9.54** (Regulation of On-Site Wastewater Treatment Systems, adopted as Ordinance 1947 on December 6, 2016 by the Board of Supervisors, administered by MCDEH Environmental Health Division).

## Castle Commerce Center — Special Sewer Ordinance

The **Castle Commerce Center** (former Castle Air Force Base redevelopment area, unincorporated Merced County) has its own dedicated sewer use ordinance codified at **Merced County Code — Operation of Water and Sewer Systems at Castle Commerce Center** (eCode360 reference: Title 7 or associated special district chapter — [VERIFY exact chapter number]).

Key provisions of the Castle Commerce Center sewer ordinance:
- Connection requires prior application to the county director (referenced as "CAED director") for a sewer connection permit.
- The county may inspect any facility within Castle Commerce Center that discharges wastewater to the sewer system, including sewers, pumping plants, industrial processes, and pretreatment facilities.
- **Significant change notification**: Users must notify the county at least **90 days before** planned changes to operations or pretreatment systems, including flow increases of ≥20%, discharge of previously unreported pollutants, or introduction of new raw materials.
- **Civil penalty**: Intentional or negligent violations, or discharges causing pollution, can result in civil liability up to **$6,000 per day of violation**.

## Industrial Pretreatment Program

No county-run industrial pretreatment program (IWP/IPP) was found for general unincorporated Merced County. The Castle Commerce Center sewer ordinance contains pretreatment inspection authority, but it is site-specific to that redevelopment zone. For facilities in incorporated cities, see the relevant city file (City of Merced has an active Industrial Pretreatment Program under 40 CFR 403).

Most unincorporated Merced County industrial facilities rely on:
- On-site septic/OWTS (Chapter 9.54) for domestic waste, plus
- Site-specific permits or RWQCB waste discharge requirements for process wastewater

## Sewage Sludge

Land application of sewage sludge in unincorporated areas is regulated by **Merced County Code Chapter 9.52** (Regulation of Sewage Sludge), enforced by MCDEH.
