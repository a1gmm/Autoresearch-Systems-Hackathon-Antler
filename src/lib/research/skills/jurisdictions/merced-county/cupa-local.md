## CUPA Agency

**Merced County Division of Environmental Health (MCDEH)** is the sole **countywide CUPA** for all of Merced County — including all incorporated cities. No city in Merced County operates its own CUPA or is a CalEPA-designated Participating Agency.

> GOTCHA: Despite the county having a CAL FIRE contract fire department and three municipal fire departments, **none** of those fire agencies administer the CUPA. HMBP, CalARP, UST, APSA, hazardous waste, and tiered permitting are all exclusively under MCDEH.

## Programs Administered (Six Unified Program Elements)

1. Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)
2. California Accidental Release Program (CalARP)
3. Underground Storage Tank Program (UST)
4. Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act Program (APSA)
5. Hazardous Waste Generator Program
6. Tiered Permitting Program (on-site hazardous waste treatment)

## Electronic Reporting

All HMBP submissions and program reporting must be done through the **California Environmental Reporting System (CERS)**: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov

## Inspection Approach

MCDEH describes its methodology as "education-based and focused on customer service." Businesses in any of the six program elements are subject to routine inspections.
