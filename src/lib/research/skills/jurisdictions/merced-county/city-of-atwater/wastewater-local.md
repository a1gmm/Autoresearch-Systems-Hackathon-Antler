## POTW

**Atwater Regional Wastewater Treatment Facility**
- Owner: City of Atwater
- Operator (contracted): **Veolia Water West Operating Services Inc.**
- Address: 530 South Bert Crane Road, Atwater, CA 95301
- Design capacity: **6.0 MGD** (average dry weather flow)
- Recent actual flow: ~3.3 MGD (2021 annual average)
- NPDES Permit: **CAG585001** (Municipal General Order R5-2023-0025, enrollee R5-2023-0025-005); issued by Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 5), effective **1 August 2024**
- Previous permit: R5-2017-0085-02 (enrollee R5-2017-0085-001)
- WDID: 5B24NP00017
- Receiving water: Peck/Atwater Drain (flows to San Joaquin River)
- Treatment train: headworks screening/grit removal → oxidation ditches (nitrification/denitrification) → secondary clarifiers → cloth-media tertiary filters → UV disinfection

**Service area:** City of Atwater (pop. ~31,970), unincorporated Winton (pop. ~11,709), Federal Bureau of Prisons – Atwater, and Castle Airport Aviation and Development Center (~1,500 combined).

## Pretreatment Program

**Pretreatment Program: YES** — confirmed by Central Valley Regional Water Board NOA (R5-2023-0025-005, issued 17 July 2024).

The City maintains an **Industrial Pretreatment Program (IPP)** as required by 40 CFR Part 403 (federal pretreatment regulations) and its NPDES permit. Industrial users discharging to the Atwater sewer system must obtain an Industrial Waste Permit (IWP) from the City if they meet Significant Industrial User (SIU) thresholds.

> [VERIFY — The specific local discharge limits, IWP application form, and SIU list are not published online. Contact Public Works at (209) 357-6310 or Jvinson@atwater.org for the current pretreatment program document and IWP requirements.]
