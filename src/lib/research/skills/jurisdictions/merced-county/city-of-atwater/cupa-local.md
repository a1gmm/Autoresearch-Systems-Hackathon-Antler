## Controlling CUPA

The City of Atwater is **not its own CUPA**. All six state-mandated Unified Program elements are administered countywide by:

**Merced County Division of Environmental Health (MCDEH)**
- Role: Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all of Merced County, including all incorporated cities
- Address: 2222 M Street, Second Floor, Merced, CA 95340
- Phone: (209) 381-1100
- Email: [email protected]
- Web: https://www.countyofmerced.com/1830/CUPA-Hazardous-Materials-Waste-Informati

## Six Programs Administered by MCDEH

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — electronic submission via CERS (California Environmental Reporting System)
2. **California Accidental Release Program (CalARP)**
3. **Underground Storage Tank Program (UST)**
4. **Aboveground Storage Tank Program (APSA)**
5. **Hazardous Waste Generator Program** — requires state or federal EPA ID number depending on volume
6. **Tiered Permitting Program**

## Reporting and Compliance

- HMBPs must be current and submitted electronically through **CERS** (cers.calepa.ca.gov)
- Hazardous materials spills: report to Cal OES at **1-800-852-7550**
- Agency self-describes as "education-based" with customer service focus

## Local Hazmat Ordinance

No city-level hazardous materials ordinance has been identified for Atwater. The CUPA function and hazmat requirements operate solely under Merced County/MCDEH authority and California state law.

## Hazmat Emergency Response

The **Merced County Type 1 Hazmat Team** (a regional mutual-aid team) responds within Atwater. The team operates under a Memorandum of Understanding among Merced County Fire, Merced City Fire, Atwater City Fire, and Los Banos Fire.
