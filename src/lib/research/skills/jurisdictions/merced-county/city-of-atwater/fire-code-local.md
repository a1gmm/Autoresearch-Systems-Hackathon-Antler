## Adopted Fire Code

**Municipal code chapter:** Title 8 (Health and Safety), **Chapter 8.40** — Fire Prevention and Automatic Fire Sprinkler Systems

Conditions of approval issued by the City (e.g., Ordinance CS 1060) cite "the California Fire Code" and "City of Atwater Fire Department requirements" as the controlling standard. ## Key Local Standards (from conditions of approval)

- Fire hydrants must meet minimum 1,500 gpm flow; placement per California Fire Code and Atwater Fire Department requirements; approved by Fire Chief.
- Fire protection lines must be separate from domestic service lines with detector check meter installations.
- Aboveground storage tanks (ASTs) governed by California Building Code, Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), and design requirements of Merced County and Cal Fire.
- Roof sheathing nailing per "2023 Building Standards" (per Building Division handout, referencing latest CA Building Standards Code).

## Note on CAL FIRE Relationship

Atwater's fire department is a city department (policy = City Council), but is staffed and administered by CAL FIRE under contract — distinct from the Merced County Fire Department (a separate county agency also under a CAL FIRE cooperative agreement). For hazmat first response, the Merced County Technical Rescue / Type 1 Hazmat Team operates under an MOU with Atwater City Fire, Merced City Fire, and Los Banos Fire.
