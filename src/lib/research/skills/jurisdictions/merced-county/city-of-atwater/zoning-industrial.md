## Industrial Zoning Districts

Atwater has three zones that accommodate industrial and business park uses:

| Zone | Common Name | Municipal Code Chapter |
|------|-------------|----------------------|
| M-1 | Light Industrial | Chapter 17.40 |
| M-2 | Heavy Industrial | Chapter 17.42 |
| BP Overlay | Business Park Overlay | Chapter 17.39 |

### M-1 — Light Industrial (Chapter 17.40)
Implements the General Plan Light Industrial land use designation. Typical permitted uses include manufacturing, warehousing, and industrial services. Certain uses within M-1 (e.g., public utility buildings/substations, machine and welding shops, carpenter shops, furniture manufacturers, and uses the Community Development and Resources Commission (CDRC) determines are not obnoxious) require a **Conditional Use Permit**.

### M-2 — Heavy Industrial (Chapter 17.42)
Implements the General Plan Heavy Industrial land use designation. Allows a broader range of industrial manufacturing uses. CUP required for specific uses as listed in Chapter 17.42.

### Business Park Overlay (Chapter 17.39)
An overlay zone applied to areas intended for business park development; compatible with lighter industrial and office/R&D uses. Refer to Chapter 17.39 for overlay standards.

> [VERIFY — Full permitted-use tables and development standards for Chapters 17.40 and 17.42 are not publicly accessible online (Municode requires authentication). Confirm specific use classifications and setback/height standards with the Planning Division or via the municipal code.]

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

**Chapter 17.71** — Conditional Use Permits

- **Approving body:** Planning Commission (CDRC in some contexts; City Council on appeal)
- **Process timeline:** Typically 60–90 days
- **Environmental review:** CEQA analysis required; Environmental Review form submitted with CUP application
- **Approval authority for minor matters:** City Manager or designee handles Lot Line Adjustments, Lot Mergers, Home Occupation Permits, and Minor Site Plan/Development Plan Amendments

Conditions of approval for industrial projects cite compliance with the California Fire Code, California Building Code (CBC), California Energy Code, ADA/CBC accessibility requirements, and Atwater Municipal Code.
