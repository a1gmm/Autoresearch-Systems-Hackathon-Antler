## Adopted Code Edition

Merced County has adopted the **2022 California Fire Code (CFC)** (Title 24, Part 9), which is based on the 2021 International Fire Code (IFC) as amended by the California Building Standards Commission. Adoption is codified at **Merced County Code Chapter 16.30** (Title 16 — Buildings and Construction).

The code applies to all construction and fire-prevention activities within the **unincorporated areas** of Merced County.

## Adopting Ordinance

The 2022 CFC adoption ordinance amended Chapter 16.30 of the Merced County Code. A prior adoption ordinance (second reading conducted **November 19, 2019**) had adopted the 2019 California Fire Code by amending **Section 9.24.010 of Chapter 9.24, Title 9**. The county's eCode360 index shows Chapter 16.30 as the current home for the fire code (the Title 16 reorganization consolidated building and fire standards). ## Local Amendments

Per county eCode360 records: *"The following sections of the 2022 California Fire Code are deleted, amended, or superseded by other provisions: None."* Merced County adopted the 2022 CFC without local modifications.

A separate **Chapter 9.24 (Fire Prevention)** addresses nuisance/abatement rules applicable countywide.

## Fire AHJ

**Merced County Fire Department**, operated under a CAL FIRE contract (in place since 1988). This arrangement makes Merced County a **CAL FIRE contract county** — the county fire chief reports through the CAL FIRE chain of command. The department covers all **unincorporated Merced County** and provides fire protection to several smaller incorporated cities by contract.

## Incorporated Cities — Separate Fire AHJs

- **City of Merced** — has its own municipal fire department (separate AHJ)
- **City of Los Banos** — has its own municipal fire department (separate AHJ)
- **City of Atwater** — city fire department staffed by CAL FIRE under a separate cooperative agreement (distinct from the county contract)
