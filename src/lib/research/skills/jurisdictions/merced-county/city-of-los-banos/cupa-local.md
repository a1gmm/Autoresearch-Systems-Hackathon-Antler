> Research reference for CUPA program administration in Los Banos. The City of Los Banos is NOT its own CUPA. All six CalEPA unified programs inside city limits are administered by Merced County Division of Environmental Health (MCDEH), the countywide CUPA.

## CUPA Identity

- **CUPA:** Merced County Division of Environmental Health (MCDEH)
- **Role:** Countywide CUPA — covers the City of Los Banos and all unincorporated Merced County
- **Address:** 2222 M Street, Merced, CA 95340
- **Phone:** (209) 381-1100

## Programs Administered

MCDEH administers all six state-mandated CalEPA unified programs countywide, including within Los Banos city limits:

| # | Program | Acronym |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan | HMBP |
| 2 | California Accidental Release Prevention Program | CalARP |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tank Program | UST |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act / Aboveground Storage Tanks | APSA |
| 5 | Hazardous Waste Generator Program | HWG |
| 6 | Tiered Permitting (onsite hazardous waste treatment) | TP |

## HMBP / CERS Filing

- Businesses storing, using, or handling hazardous materials at or above Health & Safety Code §25507 threshold quantities (generally ≥55 gallons liquid, ≥500 lbs solid, ≥200 cu ft gas) must file a Hazardous Materials Business Plan via CERS
- MCDEH is the reviewing and inspecting agency for Los Banos facilities
- Inspections: MCDEH describes its approach as education-based; routine inspection frequency [VERIFY with MCDEH]
- Accidental release / spill reporting: contact Cal OES Warning Center at 1-800-852-7550 for spills to waters or the environment

## Relationship to Fire Department

- Los Banos City Fire Department handles fire code operational permits (CFC Chapter 50+) for hazardous materials at the local level. This is separate from the CUPA HMBP program administered by MCDEH.
- Coordinate with both MCDEH (CUPA/HMBP) and Los Banos Fire (CFC hazmat operational permit) for hazardous materials operations above permit thresholds.
