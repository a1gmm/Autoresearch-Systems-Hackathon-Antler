> Research reference for the Los Banos fire AHJ and locally adopted fire code. Los Banos operates its own municipal fire department — it is NOT served by CAL FIRE or a county fire district. The 2022 California Fire Code was in effect at time of fetch; the city council introduced ordinances in November 2025 to adopt the 2025 CFC (effective statewide 2026-01-01) — confirm current adopted edition before quoting.

## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

- **AHJ:** Los Banos City Fire Department (municipal, paid + volunteer)
- **Stations:** Two stations within city limits; Station 1 at 333 7th Street, Los Banos, CA 93635
- **Phone:** (209) 827-7025
- **Email:** fire@losbanos.org
- **Web:** https://www.fire.losbanos.org/
- **Hours:** Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.

## Adopted Fire Code

- **Edition confirmed in code:** California Fire Code (CFC) **2022 Edition** (Title 24, Part 9, CCR), adopted by reference
- **Municipal code location:** Los Banos Municipal Code **Title 4 (Public Safety), Chapter 3 (Fire Prevention Code)**, § 4-3.01 et seq.
- **Prior ordinance history:** Multiple adoption ordinances (Ord. Nos. 1099, 1117, 1149, 1180, 1200 confirmed in code history); Ord. No. 1200 is the most recent confirmed adoption ordinance in the current code
- **2025 CFC pending adoption:** November 2025 City Council meeting introduced ordinances to adopt the 2025 California Fire Code. [VERIFY — confirm whether the 2025 CFC has been fully adopted and effective date]
- **Appendices:** Included by reference per standard California adoption; local appendices not separately confirmed online
