### Other Relevant Districts

- **R-C — Rail Corridor (Article 5):** Addresses development adjacent to rail; relevant for rail-served industrial facilities
- **PF — Public Facilities (Article 16):** Government and utility uses
- **U — Unclassified (Article 31):** Catch-all for uses not clearly fitting another district

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) / Use Permit Process

- **Governing article:** Article 23 — Application Processing, Site Plan Review Procedure, Administrative Permits, Use Permits, Variances and Appeals
- **Decision maker:** Planning Commission (for use permits); staff for administrative permits
- **Process:**
  1. Pre-application consultation recommended — contact a Planner before signing a lease or finalizing a location
  2. Submit: Uniform Application, Environmental Information form, Cost Recovery Contract, and application-specific packet
  3. CEQA environmental review (staff-led)
  4. Planning Commission hearing and decision
  5. Conditions of approval attached to Use Permit

## Industrial Land Market Context

- 800+ acres zoned light to heavy industrial within city limits; >40% vacant, parcels from 1–200 acres
- Rail corridor (R-C district) supports rail-served industrial development
- City is located at the intersection of Hwy 152 and I-5, with direct access to the San Joaquin Valley logistics corridor
