> Research reference for City of Los Banos wastewater discharge, sewer-use ordinance, and industrial pretreatment. Los Banos operates its own POTW (Wastewater Treatment Plant) and has a functioning industrial pretreatment program, consistent with a community that hosts food processors and an abattoir. The POTW is a relatively small facility (existing average day flow ~3.55 MGD as of 2023 SSMP).

## POTW / Operator

- **Operator:** City of Los Banos Public Works Department
- **WWTP location:** Northeast side of the city (specific address not published online)
- **Design flow / existing flow:** ~3.55 MGD average day (2023 SSMP); projected to grow to ~7.48 MGD by 2025 planning year
- **Discharge receiver:** Receives discharge via collection system; canals cross path (Grasslands Water District, CCID)
- **NPDES permit number:** Not confirmed via web fetch — [VERIFY via CIWQS database: https://ciwqs.waterboards.ca.gov/ciwqs/readOnly/CiwqsReportServlet?inCommand=reset&reportName=RegulatedFacility — filter by City: Los Banos]
- **Regulatory oversight:** California Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (Region 5)
- **Applicable state order:** General Order WQ 2022-0103-DWQ (SSS Order, governing the SSMP)

## Key Discharge Prohibitions (§ 6-5.04)

From the 2023 SSMP legal authority summary (referencing the municipal code):

- **Storm water / clear water infiltration** — prohibited (§ 6-5.04(a))
- **Chemical dumping** — gasoline, benzene, naphtha, fuel oil, and similar flammable/explosive substances prohibited (§ 6-5.04(c))
- **Fats, oils, and grease (FOG)** — discharges containing fats, wax, grease, or oils of animal origin exceeding **100 mg/L**, or substances that solidify between 32°F and 150°F, are prohibited (§ 6-5.04(e)(2))
- **Solids and viscous substances** — including ashes, cinders, glue, sand, mud, rags, metal, glass, tar, plastics, whole blood, hair, entrails, etc. (§ 6-5.04(c)(4))
- **Prohibited substances** — materials not amenable to treatment by sewage treatment processes
- **Dilution** — no water may be added to dilute wastes that would otherwise exceed applicable maximum concentration limits (federal categorical pretreatment standards apply where more stringent)

## Interceptors and Separators (§ 6-5.04(f))

- Grease traps/interceptors required where needed to comply with §§ 6-5.04(c) and (e)
- Must comply with the most recently adopted edition of the Uniform Plumbing Code

## Industrial Pretreatment Program (§ 6-5.06)

- **Program status:** Active — the City runs a pretreatment program that educates, inspects, and permits industrial users who discharge industrial process wastewater to the collection system and WWTP (per SSMP § 11.4.4)
- **Industrial users present:** Multiple food processors and an abattoir (slaughterhouse) discharge to the city system (SSMP § 1.7)
- **Permit requirement (§ 6-5.06(a)):** All non-residential dischargers whose wastewater requires special regulation or contains industrial wastes requiring source control, and all dischargers requiring flow estimation, must secure a **wastewater discharge permit** from the City
- **Permit conditions may include:** Flow rate and time-of-discharge limits, flow equalization requirements, other pretreatment provisions
- **Federal pretreatment standards:** Apply where more stringent than local limits (40 CFR Part 403 categorical standards)
- **State requirements:** Apply where more stringent than federal or local limits

## Inspection Authority (§ 6-5.07(d))

- City may inspect any discharger's facilities to verify compliance with discharge limits
- Inspection requires owner/occupant consent or a warrant; emergency inspections may proceed without consent
- City may install, maintain, and operate sampling and measuring equipment on discharger premises

## Enforcement (§ 6-5.08)

- Cease and desist orders may be issued for violations
- Industrial users in violation may be required to submit a compliance schedule

## FOG Control Program

- Food Service Establishments (FSEs) receive annual letters on correct FOG disposal (since 2009)
- Annual insert in city water bills on wipes/rags and FOG disposal
