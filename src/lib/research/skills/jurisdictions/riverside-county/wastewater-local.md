## Critical Routing Note

Riverside County has **no single county POTW**. Unincorporated areas are served by multiple independent special districts. The correct agency and ordinance depend entirely on the facility's address. Determine the district BEFORE applying any local limit or permit process.

## EMWD — Eastern Municipal Water District

**Governing ordinance:** Ordinance No. **59.7** — "Regulations for Waste Discharge and Sewer Use," adopted by EMWD Board of Directors **June 15, 2022** (superseding Ord. 59.6).

**Discharge limits:** Local Limits are set by **EMWD Resolution No. 3003** (as amended; current amendment 3003.3, adopted January 16, 2013). Limits are expressed in concentration (mg/L) or mass (lbs/day). The General Manager may impose mass limitations when concentration-based limits are insufficient.

### Permit Types (Ord. 59.7, Article 4)

| Permit Category | Trigger | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Waste Discharge Permit (WDP)** | Non-domestic wastewater with potential adverse impact on POTW | Full permit; includes effluent limits, monitoring, reporting |
| **Categorical Industrial User (CIU)** | Subject to federal categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR 403, 405–471) | Must meet categorical standards plus any more-stringent local limits |
| **Significant Industrial User (SIU)** | ≥ 25,000 gpd process wastewater, OR subject to categorical standards, OR determined to have significant potential for impact | Enhanced monitoring and reporting requirements |
| **Waste Discharge Authorization (WDA)** | Smaller non-domestic dischargers not requiring a full WDP | Streamlined authorization per Ord. 59.7, Article 5 |

### Local Limits Framework (Ord. 59.7, Section 3.3)

- No user shall discharge a quantity or quality exceeding the Local Limits established in **Resolution No. 3003** (as amended).
- Local Limits apply at the point of discharge to the POTW (except BMPs, which apply at end of process).
- General Manager may impose concentration OR mass limits (lbs/day).
- When categorical wastewater mixes with non-regulated wastewater, the Combined Waste Stream Formula (40 CFR 403.6) is applied.
- TDS limitations may be imposed separately to protect recycled water beneficial use (Ord. 59.7, Section 3.6).

**NOTE:** The specific numeric Local Limits table (pollutant-by-pollutant mg/L values) is in Resolution 3003 as amended. The resolution references plan check fees set in Resolution No. 2963. Obtain current numeric limits directly from EMWD Source Control Division — the resolution is not directly accessible online. Do not quote specific mg/L values without confirming from EMWD.

### Permit Process (Ord. 59.7, Article 4)

1. Submit permit application to EMWD Source Control Division (form + plan check fee per Resolution 2963).
2. EMWD reviews; may require installation of pretreatment equipment, monitoring equipment, or a Spill Containment Plan.
3. EMWD issues WDP or WDA with effluent limits, monitoring schedule, and reporting requirements.
4. Annual reporting; periodic compliance monitoring inspections.
5. Baseline monitoring report (BMR) required within 180 days of permit issuance for CIUs.

**Contact:** EMWD Source Control Division — 2270 Trumble Road, Perris, CA 92570 — (951) 928-3777 ext. 6216
**Ordinance PDF:** https://content.emwd.org/sites/default/files/migrate-documents/ordinance596.pdf

## WRCRWA — Western Riverside County Regional Wastewater Authority

**Governing ordinance:** **Ordinance No. 387** (WMWD Code, Title 6 — Sewer Use Regulations), adopted 2017. Codified in Western Municipal Water District Code (via codepublishing.com).

**Treatment plant:** 14634 River Road, Eastvale, CA 92880 — (951) 789-5189 (after-hours emergencies: (951) 789-5109).

### Permit Types (Ord. 387 / WMWDC Title 6)

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| **Individual Wastewater Discharge Permit** | Issued for up to 5 years; required for Industrial Users (IU) with potential adverse impact on POTW |
| **Categorical Industrial Users (CIU)** | Federally regulated processes subject to 40 CFR 405–471 categorical pretreatment standards |
| **Significant Industrial Users (SIU)** | Discharge > 25,000 gpd process wastewater; or categorical; or significant potential to impact POTW |
| **Temporary Permit** | Maximum 180 days |

Permit includes: effluent limits based on pretreatment standards, monitoring/sampling/reporting schedule, slug control plan (if required), reference to user charge rate resolution.

### Permit Process (WMWDC 6.12)

1. Submit new permit application (with appropriate fees) **180 days before** existing permit expires.
2. WRCRWA/WMWD General Manager reviews; may modify conditions for new/revised federal or local standards.
3. Permit transfer requires 90 days advance notice and General Manager approval.
4. Permit duration not to exceed 5 years from effective date.

**Source Control Program Manager:** Ben Burgett — bburgett@wmwd.com — (951) 571-7228
**Pretreatment page:** https://wrcrwa.org/158/Pretreatment
**Discharge limits:** https://westernwaterca.gov/385/Wastewater-Control-OrdinanceDischarge-Li

**NOTE:** WRCRWA has developed local limitations for specific industrial processes. The actual numeric local limits table is in a separate Board resolution (Board Resolution 00-66 and amendments). Obtain current limits from WRCRWA Source Control. Do not quote specific mg/L values without confirming from WRCRWA.

## County Sewer Ordinance (Unincorporated Fringe Areas)

For unincorporated areas with county-operated sewer systems (not under a special district), the baseline rule is:

- **Ordinance No. 592** (as amended through 592.1) — "Sewer Use, Sewer Construction and Industrial Wastewater Discharges."
- Codified at County Code **Title 13** (Public Services); Chapter 13.12 addresses stormwater/drain system protection.
