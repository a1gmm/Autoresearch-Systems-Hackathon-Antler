## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **Agency:** Riverside County Department of Environmental Health (DEH) — countywide CUPA for unincorporated areas and most incorporated cities (City of Riverside Fire and City of Corona Fire are Participating Agencies under the county CUPA for HMBP only, not independent CUPAs).
- **Local enabling ordinance:** Ordinance No. 651.5 (as amended through 651.5) — "Permitting of Businesses Handling Hazardous Materials." Codified in County Code **Title 8, Chapter 8.64** (Hazardous Materials Disclosure and Business Emergency Plans).
- **Programs administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generator, Underground Storage Tank (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP), Uniform Fire Code hazmat inspections.
- **Permit portal:** CalEHSA statewide portal (https://calepa.ca.gov/cupa/); DEH accepts HMBP submissions through the Statewide Information Management System (CERS/CalEHSA). Annual permits expire on the one-year anniversary of program qualification; renewal due within 30 calendar days of expiration.
- **Contact:** (951) 782-2968 (after-hours); main DEH office at https://rivcoeh.org/hazardous-materials

  - Desert: 77-933 Las Montanas Rd. #201, Palm Desert, CA 92211 — (760) 863-8886

Amendments authorized under Health & Safety Code §§ 17958, 17958.5, 17958.7, 18941.5 for local climatic, geological, and topographical conditions.

### 3. Industrial Wastewater — Pretreatment / IWP

- **No single county POTW** — unincorporated Riverside County is served by multiple special districts depending on location:
  - **Eastern Municipal Water District (EMWD):** Western Riverside County including Perris/Hemet/Temecula areas. Address: 2270 Trumble Road, Perris, CA 92570 — https://www.emwd.org/
  - **Western Riverside County Regional Wastewater Authority (WRCRWA):** Corona/Norco/Jurupa/Western MWD service area. Issues IU/SIU/CIU permits. Contact: Ben Burgett, Source Control Program Manager — bburgett@wmwd.com — (951) 571-7228. Treatment plant: (951) 789-5189. Wastewater Control Ordinance available at https://wrcrwa.org/158/Pretreatment
  - **Inland Empire Utilities Agency (IEUA):** Northwestern fringe areas (Ontario/Chino Valley area). Ordinance No. 97 governs industrial users. Pretreatment & Source Control Team: (909) 993-1600 — https://www.ieua.org/everything-water/pretreatment-source-control/wastewater-discharge-permits/
- **County sewer ordinance (unincorporated service areas):** County Code **Title 13** (Public Services); **Ordinance No. 592** (as amended through 592.1) — "Sewer Use, Sewer Construction and Industrial Wastewater Discharges." Chapter 13.12 addresses stormwater/drain system protection requirements.
- GOTCHA: Determine the specific district before routing — ask facility address to identify the correct POTW.

### 4. Stormwater — MS4 / Industrial General Permit

- **MS4 permittee:** Riverside County Flood Control & Water Conservation District (co-permittee with county and cities) under Santa Ana Regional Water Quality Control Board NPDES Permit Order No. R8-2010-0033.
- **Local ordinance:** **Ordinance No. 857** (as amended through 857.1) — stormwater/business registration and inspection. County Code **Title 13, Chapter 13.12** (Storm Drain System Protection Requirements).
- **Industrial General Permit (IGP):** State-level permit; registration and compliance filings through SMARTS — https://smarts.waterboards.ca.gov/smarts/faces/SwSmartsLogin.xhtml
- **Program contact:** Riverside County Watershed Protection, 1995 Market St., Riverside, CA 92501 — https://rcwatershed.org/programs/industrial-permits/

### 5. Zoning / Land Use — Industrial Districts

- **Governing code:** County Code **Title 17 — ZONING** (Ordinance No. 348 base, as amended). Administered by Riverside County Planning Department (TLMA — Transportation & Land Management Agency).
- **Industrial zone designations** (Chapter 17.12 zone classifications):
  - **I-P** — Industrial Park Zone (Chapter 17.96): light industrial/R&D, restricted outdoor storage
  - **M-SC** — Manufacturing-Service Commercial Zone (Chapter 17.100): mixed light mfg + commercial
  - **M-M** — Manufacturing-Medium Zone (Chapter 17.104): general manufacturing
  - **M-H** — Manufacturing-Heavy Zone (Chapter 17.108): heavy industrial, more permissive on outdoor operations
- **CUP process:** Conditional Use Permits required for certain industrial uses within these zones. Applications submitted to TLMA Planning Department.
  - Portal: https://planning.rctlma.org/Development-Process/Applications
