## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **CUPA**: City of Riverside is **NOT** its own CUPA. The certified CUPA is **Riverside County Department of Environmental Health (DEH)**.
- **City role**: City of Riverside Fire Prevention Division is a **Participating Agency (PA)** — administers Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP) within city limits only; county DEH retains CUPA certification.
- **Participating agencies** under County DEH CUPA: City of Riverside and City of Corona only.
- **Local ordinance**: RMC Chapter 9.48 establishes city authority to implement unified hazardous materials programs (HMBP, Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act, CFC provisions).
- **HMBP submission**: CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) is the only approved filing method — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/. Business plans must be reviewed and submitted annually; due dates align with County DEH permit expiration.
- **Contact (city PA)**: City of Riverside Fire Prevention Division — (951) 826-5737 — [email protected]
- **County DEH CUPA contact**: https://rivcoeh.org/hazardous-materials
- Source: https://riversideca.gov/fire/divisions/prevention/hazardous-materials-business-plans
- Source: https://rivcoeh.org/hazardous-materials-disclosure

### 4. Stormwater — MS4 & Industrial

- **Governing ordinance**: **RMC Chapter 14.12** (same chapter as wastewater) — covers discharge of pollutants into the storm drain system; implements the city's MS4 NPDES permit obligations.
- **MS4 permit**: Issued by the California Regional Water Quality Control Board (Santa Ana Region); specific NPDES permit number [VERIFY — not stated on city website].
- **LID/development requirements**: New development must infiltrate, harvest/use, evapotranspire, and/or bio-treat stormwater on-site per the city's MS4 permit.
- **LID BMP Design Handbook**: https://rcwatershed.org/permittees/riverside-county-lid-bmp-handbook/ (Riverside County Flood Control and Water Conservation District resource used by city)
- **Industrial stormwater**: Facilities needing a State Industrial General Permit (IGP) apply through State Water Board (SMARTS portal). City enforcement of local RMC Chapter 14.12 runs concurrently.
- **Stormwater development contact**: https://www.riversideca.gov/publicworks/engineering/stormwater/development
- Source: https://www.riversideca.gov/publicworks/engineering/stormwater/development
