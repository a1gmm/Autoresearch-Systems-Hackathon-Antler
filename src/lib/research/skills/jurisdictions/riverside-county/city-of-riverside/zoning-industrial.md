## Industrial Zone Districts — Chapter 19.130

**Chapter 19.130 — Industrial Zones (BMP, I, AI and AIR)**
(Ord. 7331 §7, 2016; Ord. 6966 §1, 2007)

The City of Riverside does **NOT** use traditional M-1/M-2 designations. Industrial zones are:

| Zone | Full Name | Primary Character |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| **BMP** | Business and Manufacturing Park | Low-intensity, low-impact industrial, office, R&D, small-scale warehouse, light manufacturing, support commercial |
| **I** | General Industrial | General manufacturing, wholesaling, limited warehousing and distribution, support commercial |
| **AI** | Air Industrial | Four subzones (AI-1, AI-2, AI-3, AI-4) protecting Riverside Municipal Airport environs; aviation, industrial, office, commercial |
| **AIR** | Airport Zone | Aviation, industrial, service and commercial uses related to or compatible with airport operations |

AI subzones:
- AI-1: Large-scale, high-visibility businesses
- AI-2: Airport/industrial/office, moderate-scale, at or adjacent to Riverside Municipal Airport
- AI-3: Moderate-size uses, varying locations
- AI-4: Small-size uses, limited visibility

## Development Standards Summary (Ch. 19.130.030)

**Table 19.130.030 A — BMP, I, and AIR Zones:**

|----------|-----|---|-----|
| Max FAR | 1.50 | 0.60 | 0.60 |
| Min. Lot Area | 40,000 sq. ft. | 10,000 sq. ft. | 8,000 sq. ft. |
| Min. Lot Width | 140 ft. | 60 ft. | 60 ft. |
| Min. Lot Depth | 100 ft. | 100 ft. | 100 ft. |
| Max. Building Height | 45 ft. | 45 ft. | 45 ft. |
| Front Setback (>30 ft. bldg on arterial) | 50 or 40 ft.* | — | — |
| Front Setback (≤30 ft. bldg, non-arterial) | 20 ft.* | — | — |
| Interior Side | 0 ft. | 0 ft. | 0 ft. |
| Street-side | Same as front | 20 ft.⁷ | 15 ft.⁷ |
| Rear | 0 ft. | 0 ft. | 0 ft. |
| Rear adjacent to street | Same as front | 20 ft.⁷ | 20 ft.⁷ |

*BMP front yard: 20 ft. of the 50 ft. setback must be landscaped; 40 ft. setback permitted only if fully landscaped.
⁷ Minimum 10-ft on-site landscape planter required along street-side and rear yards of I and AIR zones.
Adjacency to residential zone: minimum 50-ft. side or rear yard setback with 15 ft. nearest the residential use fully landscaped.

**AI Zone lot areas:** AI-1: 5 acres minimum; AI-2/AI-3: 40,000 or 20,000 sq. ft. (by arterial); AI-4: 14,000 sq. ft. minimum.

## Uses Specifically Prohibited (All Industrial Zones — Sec. 19.130.025)

Section 19.130.025 lists 24 specifically prohibited uses, including:
- Residential uses (except permitted caretaker housing)
- Ammonia, chlorine or bleaching powder manufacture
- Animal slaughtering; fat rendering; tallow/grease/lard manufacture
- Asphalt batching plants and rock/sand/gravel pit crushing
- Explosive manufacture or storage
- Foundry or smelting of ferrous metals; steel mills; boiler works
- Garbage, offal, dead animal or refuse incineration, reduction or dumping
- Hydrochloric, nitric, or sulfuric acid manufacture
- Junk, rag, or scrap iron storage yards or baling
- Petroleum refining or petroleum products manufacture
- Rubber or gutta-percha manufacture; soap manufacture
- Tanning, curing or storing of raw hides or skins
- Other uses determined by the CEDD Director to be detrimental by reason of odor, dust, smoke, gas, noise, vibration, or other causes

## Permitted vs. Conditional Uses (Ch. 19.150)

Permitted land uses, incidental uses, and temporary uses are set out in three tables in **Chapter 19.150 — Base Zones Permitted Land Uses** (Ord. 7630 §3–4, 2023; Ord. 7573, 2021; Ord. 7331 §12, 2016):

**Table 19.150.020 A — Permitted Uses Table** — indicates for each use and zone:
- **P** = Permitted by right
- **MC** = Minor Conditional Use Permit required (Chapter 19.730)
- **C** = Conditional Use Permit required (Chapter 19.760)
- **SP** = Site Plan Review Permit (Chapter 19.770)
- **X** = Prohibited

Example industrial entries:
- Warehousing & Distribution ≤10,000 sq. ft.: generally permitted
- Warehousing & Distribution 10,000–100,000 sq. ft.: permitted (P) or Minor CUP (MC) in I/AIR
- Warehousing & Distribution 100,000–400,000 sq. ft.: Minor CUP or CUP (MC/C) in I/AIR
- Warehousing & Distribution >400,000 sq. ft.: CUP (C) in I/AIR
- Vehicle Repair (Minor, Indoor): CUP (C) in BMP
- Commercial Storage (with overlay zone): see Ch. 19.190

Unlisted uses are prohibited unless the CEDD Director determines under **Chapter 19.060 (Interpretation of Code)** that the use is similar to and no more detrimental than a listed use.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

**Chapter 19.760 — Conditional Use Permit** (Article IX of Zoning Code)

Key procedural points (from City CUP Information Sheet):
- Approving authority: **Planning Commission** (empowers to grant, deny, and impose conditions)
- Application submitted to: Planning Division, CEDD
- Application must be **deemed complete** before scheduling for public hearing
- Required submittals include: plot plan, floor plans, parking analysis, Preliminary Water Quality Management Plan (WQMP) approved by Public Works, conceptual landscape plan, traffic lane/striping plan

**Minor Conditional Use Permit: Chapter 19.730** — applies to uses with MC designation; lower-intensity review.

**Findings required for CUP** (per CUP Information Sheet):
1. Proposed use is substantially compatible with other existing and proposed uses in the area
2. Proposed use will not be materially detrimental to health, safety, and general welfare
3. Proposed use is consistent with the purposes of the Zoning Code

Fees: Current fee schedule available at Planning Division — (951) 826-5371. Additional fees may apply for CEQA, County recording, Airport Land Use Commission review, and LAFCO.

**Airport overlay:** Sites in airport compatibility zones require additional review under **Chapter 19.149 — Airport Land Use Compatibility**. No structure or tree may penetrate FAA FAR Part 77.25 imaginary surfaces.
