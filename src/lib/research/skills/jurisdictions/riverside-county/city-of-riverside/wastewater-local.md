| II | 10,000–24,999 gpd | |
| III | 1–9,999 gpd | Only where discharge has potential to adversely affect POTW operations or violate Ch. 14.12 |

| V | Temporary (1–180 days) | |

Non-Significant Industrial Users (NSIU): industries not requiring a Class I–VI permit and not restaurants. Inspected annually.

## Application Requirements

- Significant Industrial Users must submit at least **45 days before** beginning discharge.
- Forms available:
  - Environmental Compliance Section – Permit Application Class I (PDF)
  - Environmental Compliance Section – Permit Application Class II (PDF)
- Permitted users must report proposed changes in operations in writing **30 days before implementation**, including:
  - ≥20% sustained increase or decrease in production/discharge
  - Process additions, deletions, or equipment changes
  - New processes, materials, or chemicals that may affect wastewater

## Prohibited Discharges

RMC Chapter 14.12 establishes absolute prohibition against discharge of specified substances that interfere with POTW operations, cause pass-through, create hazards, or violate pretreatment standards. Conventional pollutants addressed include BOD, COD, TSS, pH, fecal coliform, oil and grease, and total nitrogen.

Best Management Practices (BMPs) required by the city constitute local limits and pretreatment standards under the ordinance.
