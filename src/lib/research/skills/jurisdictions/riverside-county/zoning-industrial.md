## Industrial Zone Districts

### Article X — I-P Zone (Industrial Park)

**Full name:** Industrial Park Zone
**Ord. 348 Articles:** Sections 10.1–10.4
**Municode chapter:** Chapter 17.96 (as reflected in Title 17 cross-reference)

**Intent:** Light industrial and R&D-type uses; restricted outdoor storage; compatible with adjacent non-industrial uses.

**Permitted by right with Industrial Park Plot Plan (Section 18.30):**

*Industrial and manufacturing uses:*
- Food, lumber, wood, paper products: grain/bakery products, sugar/confectionery, nonalcoholic beverages, ice, furniture/fixture manufacture, printing/publishing, book binding
- Textile/leather: wearing apparel and accessories, handbags, luggage, footwear
- Chemical/glass: pharmaceutical research and manufacture; glassblowing, pressing, cutting
- Metal/machinery/electrical: jewelry manufacture; manufacture/assembly/testing/repair of electrical/electronic/electromechanical components (TV/radio, metering instruments, radar/IR/UV systems, coils/tubes/semiconductors, scientific/mechanical instruments, data processing, communication/navigation equipment, musical/recording equipment); office/computing machines; control devices; appliance manufacture; lighting fixtures
- Transportation: vehicle storage and impoundment (enclosed building); trailer/RV/boat storage (enclosed building)
- Engineering/scientific instruments: measuring devices, optical goods, medical/dental instruments, photographic equipment
- Industrial uses: public utility substations and storage buildings, warehousing and distribution (including mini-warehouses), communications/microwave installations, cold storage facilities, telephone exchanges, post offices, fire/police stations, water/gas service facilities, parcel delivery services, recycling collection facilities

*Service and commercial uses:* Banks, blueprint/duplicating services, laboratories (film/medical/research/testing), office equipment sales/service, professional offices (law/medical/dental/engineering), parking lots/structures, restaurants, barber/beauty shops, day care centers, health/exercise centers, mobilehomes (construction offices/caretaker quarters), one-family dwellings (proprietor/caretaker), on-site advertising signs, automobile service stations, motels, churches/temples.

**Permitted by Conditional Use Permit (Section 18.28):**
- Airports
- Heliports
- Recycling processing facilities
- Solar power plants (lot ≥ 10 acres)
- Parolee-Probationer Homes

### Article XI — M-SC Zone (Manufacturing – Service Commercial)

**Full name:** Manufacturing-Service Commercial Zone
**Ord. 348 Articles:** Sections 11.1–11.6 and Article XIa (Sections 11.25–11.30 for M-M)

**Intent:** Mixed light manufacturing and commercial; attracts industrial jobs; protects industrial areas from incompatible use encroachment.

**Permitted by right with Plot Plan (Section 18.30):**

*Industrial and manufacturing uses (broader than I-P):*
- Food products: meat/poultry (not slaughtering), dairy (not dairies), canning/preserving, grain/bakery, sugar, beverages, ice
- Textiles: weaving/finishing mills, wearing apparel, knitting mills, floor covering, yarn/thread mills
- Lumber/wood: saw and planing mills, containers/crates, fabricated wood buildings, lumber yards, furniture/fixtures, manufactured housing
- Paper products: paper/paperboard mills, containers/boxes, printing/publishing, binding
- Chemicals (non-hazardous): organic/inorganic compounds (not hazardous), drugs/pharmaceuticals, soaps/cleaners/toiletries, agricultural chemicals (not pesticides/fertilizers)
- Rubber, plastic, synthetic products (I-P does not include these)
- Leather products: tanning and finishing, handbags/luggage/footwear
- Stone/clay/glass/concrete: stone cutting, pottery, glassware, concrete/gypsum/plaster/mineral products
- Metal products: cans/containers, cutlery/tools/hardware, plumbing/heating, wrought iron, fencing, machine/welding/blacksmith shops, metal stamps/forged products, metal buildings, ordnance/firearms (no explosives), jewelry
- Machinery: engines/turbines, farm/garden/construction/industrial machinery, office/computing machines, refrigeration/heating equipment, equipment sales/rental/storage
- Electrical: electrical/electronic apparatus, appliances, lighting/wiring, radio/TV/communications, musical/recording
- Transportation: vehicles/aircraft/boats/parts manufacture, railroad equipment, motorcycles/bicycles, travel trailers/RVs, draying/freighting/trucking, railroad yards/stations, vehicle storage/impoundment, trailer/boat storage
- Engineering/scientific instruments: measuring devices, optical goods, medical instruments
- Industrial uses: cotton ginning, public utility substations/storage yards, heliports, building movers storage yard, mini-warehouses, warehousing/distribution, communications/microwave, cold storage plants, contractor storage yards

**CUP uses in M-SC** include the typical I-P CUP list plus heavier uses such as concrete batch plants, asphalt plants, disposal service operations, sand blasting, sewage treatment plants, smelting/foundries, gas/steam/oil drilling, swap meets, recycling processing facilities, solar power plants.

### Article XIa — M-M Zone (Manufacturing – Medium)

**Full name:** Manufacturing-Medium Zone
**Ord. 348 Articles:** Sections 11.25–11.30

**Intent:** General manufacturing; permits heavier operations than M-SC but below heavy industrial.

**Permitted by right with Plot Plan (Section 18.30):**
- All uses permitted in M-SC zone, PLUS:
- Rubber/plastic/synthetic products manufacture (tires/tubes, fabricated rubber/plastic/synthetic products)
- Beverages including alcoholic beverages, wineries, distilleries, and breweries
- Meat packing (not slaughtering)
- Paper shredding (listed in lumber/wood products)

**CUP uses in M-M** (Section 11.26.C) include:
- Concrete batch plants, asphalt plants
- Disposal service operations
- Drive-in theaters
- Airports
- Dump sites
- Sand blasting
- Gas/steam/oil drilling operations
- Sewerage treatment plants
- Swap meets
- Smelting metals and foundries
- Recycling processing facilities
- Solar power plants (≥ 10 acres)
- Mining operations subject to California Surface Mining and Reclamation Act

**Development standards (M-M — Section 11.28):**
- Minimum lot size: 10,000 sq ft (min. avg. width 75 ft); reduced to 7,000 sq ft / 65 ft width when sewers available
- Setbacks: 25 ft from residential zone boundaries; no minimum from non-residential zones
- Height: Buildings ≤ 50 ft (up to 75 ft with Section 18.34 approval); structures ≤ 50 ft (up to 105 ft with 18.34 approval)
- 6-ft solid masonry wall or landscaped earthen berm/masonry combination on property lines adjoining residential zones

### Article XII — M-H Zone (Manufacturing – Heavy)

**Full name:** Manufacturing-Heavy Zone
**Ord. 348 Articles:** Sections 12.1–12.6

**Intent:** Heavy industrial; most permissive on outdoor operations and hazardous processes.

**Permitted by right with Plot Plan (Section 18.30):**
- All uses permitted in M-M zone, PLUS:
- **Primary metal industries:** Foundries, rolling and drawing metals, casting metals, blast furnaces, smelting of metals (these are NOT permitted in M-SC or M-M by right)
- Paints and varnishes manufacturing
- Alcoholic beverages (wineries, distilleries, breweries)
- Natural gas — above ground storage
- All typical commercial/service uses including body/fender shops and spray painting, building materials sales yards, hardware/home improvement centers, nurseries, trailer/truck sales, feed/grain sales, mobilehome sales lots

**CUP uses in M-H** (Section 12.2.C):
- Auto wrecking and junk yards
- Abattoirs (slaughterhouses)
- Petroleum refineries
- (Additional uses by analogy under general CUP standards)

**Development standards (M-H — Section 12.5):**
- No minimum setback where yards adjoin commercial or industrial zones
- 25 ft minimum setback where adjoining residential zones
- Height: Buildings ≤ 50 ft (up to 75 ft with approval); structures ≤ 50 ft (up to 105 ft)

## CUP Process (Section 18.28)

1. Written application to the Planning Director on TLMA forms — accompanied by an initial deposit-based fee per **Ordinance No. 671**.
2. Public hearing held per Section 18.26 or 18.26.a.
3. CUP granted only if the applicant demonstrates the use will not be detrimental to health, safety, or general welfare.
4. Conditions imposed as necessary to protect community health/safety/welfare.
5. CUP may be revoked per Section 18.31 findings.
6. Applications requiring a General Plan amendment, specific plan amendment, change of zone, or development agreement are heard under Section 2.5, 2.6, 20.3.a., or 18.26b.

**Industrial Park Plot Plan (Section 18.30):** Required for I-P uses listed in Subsection B; submitted to TLMA Planning for review.
