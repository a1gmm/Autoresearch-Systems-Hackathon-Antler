> Research reference for industrial land use in Trinity County. Trinity County has no incorporated cities; all zoning authority is county-level. The county is in the process of a comprehensive zoning code update (Title 17 rewrite, Public Review Draft April 2026); the current adopted code is Zoning Ordinance 315. ## Industrial Zone — Draft Title 17 (April 2026)

The April 2026 draft consolidates all industrial uses into a **single "Industrial Zone (I)"** (Chapter 17.30). There is no separate light/heavy industrial split (no M-1/M-2 designations) in the draft.

### Purpose and Intent (§17.30.010)

> "The intent of this Chapter is to provide locations for manufacturing and industrial uses in a manner which is compatible with neighboring uses, protects the environment of the county, and provides economic opportunities within Trinity County."

### Performance Exceptions (§17.30.030.A)

By-right (P) uses are upgraded to a Director's Use Permit if they:
1. Involve water quantity uses over 3 gallons/minute in a Critical Water Resources Overlay area
2. Require an Authority to Construct permit from the **North Coast Unified Air Quality Management Board** (via new source review)
3. Exceed **65 dBA** at the property line
4. Require a **Regional Water Quality Control Board discharge permit**
5. Generate air emissions, liquid, solid, or hazardous wastes, noise, offensive odors, smoke, dust, or glare in a manner detrimental to public health, safety, or welfare

### Additional Requirements (§17.30.050)

- **Drainage plan** required before Building Permit or Use Permit for industrial zone projects
- **Encroachment permit** required from Trinity County Dept of Transportation for new County road access
- No vehicular access to local streets from industrial through-lots with frontage on a highway or collector road

### Overlay Zones Potentially Applicable to Industrial Sites

- **Critical Water Resource Overlay Zone (-CWR):** Triggers Director's Use Permit upgrade if water use > 3 gal/min
- **Flood Hazard Overlay Zone (-FHO):** Applies to FEMA-mapped flood areas
- **Airport Compatibility Overlay Zone (-ACO):** Applies near Weaverville Airport
- **Scenic Conservation Overlay Zone (-SCO):** May restrict industrial aesthetics near scenic corridors

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

CUPs are required for the most intensive or sensitive industrial uses (hazardous materials storage, mining, slaughterhouses, septage disposal, etc.).

**CUP application:** Submit to Trinity County Planning Division
- **Form:** "Conditional Use Permit, Variance, Rezone, and General Plan Amendment" application
- **Address:** 530 Main Street, Weaverville, CA 96093 / P.O. Box 2819, Weaverville, CA 96093
- **Phone:** 530-623-1351 Ext. 2
- **Email:** info.planning@trinitycounty.org
- **Counter hours:** Monday–Thursday, 8 am – 2 pm
- **Application documents:** https://www.trinitycounty.org/280/Planning-Documents-Forms

CUP applications go before the **Trinity County Planning Commission** in a public hearing. [VERIFY] current processing time and fee schedule with the Planning Division (fees have recently increased per the county website).

## Director's Use Permit (DUP) Process

Lower-intensity uses requiring discretionary review use a Director's Use Permit (DUP), reviewed administratively by the Community Development Director without a public hearing. Same Planning Division contact as CUP.

## Air Quality Note

The Air Quality AHJ for Trinity County is the **North Coast Unified Air Quality Management District (NCUAQMD)**, not a local county district. Any industrial use triggering an Authority to Construct automatically requires Director's Use Permit level review under the draft zoning code.
- NCUAQMD: https://www.ncuaqmd.org/
