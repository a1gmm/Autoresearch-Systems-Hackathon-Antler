## Programs Administered

Trinity County CUPA implements these CalEPA unified programs for the entire county (no city CUPAs exist):

| # | Program | Acronym | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan | HMBP | Filed via CERS; county reviews and inspects |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator and Tiered Permitting | HWG | Generator ID, manifest, tiered permit for onsite treatment |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tanks | UST | Installation, operation, closure, unauthorized release response |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act | APSA | Aboveground petroleum storage above threshold |
| 5 | California Accidental Release Prevention | CalARP | RMP coordination; county is the local implementing agency |

> NOTE: The county website lists five programs; CalARP is included. No sixth program (CFC hazmat operational permits) is listed separately — consistent with no unified county fire department.

## HMBP Program Details

- **Submission system:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/
- **Annual fee (confirmed):** $809/year including state surcharges (as of 2026; verify current amount with county)
- **State threshold quantities apply:** 55 gallons liquid / 500 lbs solid / 200 cubic feet gas (Health & Safety Code §25507)
- **Who must file:** Any business storing, handling, or using hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities
- **No confirmed local ordinance number for HMBP:** The county website references "California State Law" as legal authority; no local hazardous materials ordinance (HMO) number distinct from the state CUPA program was found online
- **Required CERS content:** Facility information, hazardous materials inventory, emergency response/contingency plan, site map, employee training records

## CalARP

Trinity County Environmental Health Division is the local implementing agency (LIA) for CalARP within the county. Facilities with regulated substances above federal RMP threshold quantities must:
1. Submit a Risk Management Plan (RMP) to U.S. EPA
2. Register with the county CUPA
3. Comply with CalARP (Health & Safety Code §25531 et seq.)
