> Research reference for wastewater/sewer service in Trinity County. Trinity County has no incorporated cities. Most of the county relies on individual septic/onsite wastewater systems. The only confirmed centralized POTW serving a community in the county is the Weaverville Sanitary District (WSD). Other communities (Hayfork, Trinity Center, etc.) have small local systems or rely on onsite treatment — verify which system, if any, serves the project address.

## Onsite Wastewater (Most of Trinity County)

The majority of Trinity County parcels — including all rural and most unincorporated communities outside Weaverville — are served by **onsite sewage disposal systems** (septic / OWTS), not a centralized sewer.

**Permit authority for OWTS:** Trinity County Environmental Health Division
- Phone: 530-623-1459
- Email: cupa@trinitycounty.org (general; confirm specific OWTS contact)
- Program: Environmental Health Division "Sewage Disposal" program
- https://www.trinitycounty.org/255/Environmental-Health-Division

Applicable state standard for OWTS: California State Water Resources Control Board Water Quality Order 2018-002-DWQ (OWTS Policy).

## Centralized POTW — Weaverville Sanitary District

The **Weaverville Sanitary District (WSD)** operates the only confirmed centralized POTW in Trinity County.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Utility name | Weaverville Sanitary District |
| Service area | Weaverville community (unincorporated county seat) |
| Treatment plant location | 630 Mountain View St., Weaverville, CA 96093 |
| Treatment process | Activated sludge with secondary clarification |
| Average daily flow | ~300,000 gallons/day (0.3 MGD) |
| Collection system | ~50 miles of pipeline (transite and plastic pipe, 4–15 inches diameter); one lift station |
| RWQCB permit | WDR Permit R1-2018-0004, issued by Regional Water Quality Control Board, Region 1 (North Coast) |
| Website | https://www.weavervillesd.com/ (confirmed but returned timeouts during fetch) |

### Industrial Pretreatment Program

**[VERIFY — no industrial pretreatment program (IPP) was found online for Weaverville Sanitary District.]**

At 0.3 MGD average daily flow, WSD is far below the 5 MGD federal pretreatment program trigger threshold (40 CFR 403.8). Federal regulations do not require WSD to have a formal pretreatment program. It is very unlikely WSD has a USEPA-approved pretreatment program with local limits.

- **Practical implication:** Industrial dischargers to the WSD sewer system are subject to federal categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR 400–471) directly through the Regional Water Board, not through a local pretreatment ordinance.
- Any significant industrial discharge should be coordinated with the RWQCB Region 1 (North Coast) and WSD directly.

## Trinity County Waterworks District No. 1 (Hayfork — Water Only)

Trinity County Waterworks District No. 1 provides **water service** to the Hayfork community and surrounding areas. It is a water-only utility and does not provide centralized sewer/wastewater treatment.

## RWQCB Region 1 (North Coast)

All NPDES/WDR permits for wastewater discharges in Trinity County are issued by the **State Water Resources Control Board, Regional Water Quality Control Board, Region 1 (North Coast)**.

- For discharge inquiries (including any industrial discharge to surface water or land): https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/northcoast/
- Weaverville SD WDR Permit: R1-2018-0004
