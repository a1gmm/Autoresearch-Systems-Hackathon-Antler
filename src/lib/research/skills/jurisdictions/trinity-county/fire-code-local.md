> Research reference for fire code authority in Trinity County. Trinity County has no incorporated cities; all local fire code authority is at the county level. Structural fire protection is fragmented across independent fire protection districts and volunteer fire departments — verify the specific AHJ for the project address.

## Fire Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

Trinity County has **no single countywide fire department** and **no countywide fire code adoption** on record online. Fire authority is split:

| Area | AHJ |
|---|---|
| Wildland / State Responsibility Area (SRA) | CAL FIRE — Trinity Management Unit; covers the vast majority of county land |
| Structural fire — Weaverville / Hayfork / Trinity Center etc. | Local fire protection districts and volunteer fire departments (e.g., Weaverville Fire Protection District, Hayfork Fire Protection District) |
| Federal lands (Six Rivers and Shasta-Trinity National Forests) | U.S. Forest Service fire staff |

**CAL FIRE Trinity Unit (TUEU):** https://www.fire.ca.gov/

**Weaverville FPD:** Confirm address/contact directly — a small volunteer district; no dedicated public-facing website confirmed.

## Fire Safe Ordinance (WUI — Chapter 8.30)

Trinity County **has adopted a Fire Safe Ordinance** codified at **Chapter 8.30** of the County Code of Ordinances (Title 8 — Health and Safety). This is the primary local fire-life-safety rule with direct EHS relevance.

**Key provisions confirmed from search index (Chapter 8.30):**

- Applies to all structures and development in wildland-urban interface (WUI) conditions — applicable to nearly all of Trinity County given its SRA land classification.
- **Driveway / access roads:** Turnaround area required for driveways 300 feet or longer; turnaround must be within 50 feet of the structure.
- **Gates:** Must be at least 2 feet wider than the road or driveway served; must be set back 30 feet from the roadway.
- **Road grade:** Roadways and driveways must not exceed 16 percent grade.
- **Additional standards:** Enforced by CAL FIRE and local FPDs as applicable AHJ.

## Hazardous Materials Operational Permits (CFC Operational Permits)

Because there is no unified county fire department:
- CFC hazmat operational permits (CFC Chapter 50+) would be issued by the local fire protection district or CAL FIRE for SRA parcels.
- For Weaverville-area industrial sites, contact Weaverville FPD.
