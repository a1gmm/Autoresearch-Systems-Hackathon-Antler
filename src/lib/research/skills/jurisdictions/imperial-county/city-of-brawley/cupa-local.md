## CUPA Agency

**DTSC Imperial County CUPA** — the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) was designated by CalEPA as the Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all of Imperial County, effective **January 1, 2005**. The City of Brawley is not its own CUPA and has no sub-delegated local unified program authority.

CUPA Office: 627 Wake Avenue, El Centro, CA 92243
Phone: (760) 352-0381 | Toll-free: 1 (866) 357-3990

Source: https://dtsc.ca.gov/imperial-county-cupa/

## Programs Administered

The DTSC Imperial CUPA administers the six unified program elements for all businesses in Brawley city limits:

| Program | Notes |
|---------|-------|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) | Required for facilities storing/handling hazardous materials above threshold quantities |
| Hazardous Waste Generator & Tiered Permitting | Includes Conditionally Exempt, Small Quantity, and Large Quantity generators; tiered permitting for treatment |
| Underground Storage Tanks (UST) | Expanded DTSC administration effective January 1, 2024 |
| Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) | Participating agency effective July 1, 2021 |
| California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) | For regulated substances above threshold quantities |
| Unified Fire Code Hazardous Materials | [VERIFY — confirm whether BFD plays a PA role or DTSC handles all] |

Source: https://dtsc.ca.gov/imperial-county-cupa/imperial-cupa-hazardous-waste-generator-and-tiered-permitting-program/

## Local Hazmat Ordinance / Chapter

- The City of Brawley **does not have its own hazardous materials ordinance** distinct from state law; DTSC implements the unified program under state statute (Health & Safety Code Chapter 6.11).
- Imperial County Public Health Department Environmental Health Division (EHD) coordinates on county-level hazmat planning:
  - Address: 1221 W. State St., Suite B, El Centro, CA 92243
  - Phone: (442) 265-1888
  - Email: hazmathealth@co.imperial.ca.us
  - Hours: Mon–Fri 8am–12pm and 1pm–5pm
- Source: https://www.icphd.org/environmental-health/hazardous-materials

## HMBP Submission

Hazardous Materials Business Plans are submitted through **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System) at https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/. The DTSC Imperial CUPA reviews and approves plans for all Brawley facilities.
