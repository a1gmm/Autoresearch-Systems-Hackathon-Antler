## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air
- **Air District: Imperial County Air Pollution Control District (ICAPCD)** — covers all stationary sources in Brawley and all of Imperial County. City has no separate air program.
- Permit to Operate required before construction/operation of any equipment that may emit air contaminants (Rule 100 series).
- Engineering Division reviews and inspects stationary point sources (factories, processing plants) and indirect sources (roads, construction).
- Source: https://apcd.imperialcounty.org/engineering/

### CUPA (Unified Program)
- **Not its own CUPA** — the entire county is served by the **DTSC Imperial County CUPA**, designated by CalEPA effective January 1, 2005.
- DTSC administers: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generator & Tiered Permitting, Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) (participating agency, effective July 1, 2021), and CalARP.
- Office: 627 Wake Avenue, El Centro, CA 92243. Phone: (760) 352-0381.
- For county-level HMBP coordination, Imperial County Public Health Department Environmental Health Division also has a role: (442) 265-1888, hazmathealth@co.imperial.ca.us.
- Source: https://dtsc.ca.gov/imperial-county-cupa/

### Fire
- **Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ): Brawley Fire Department** — city's own municipal fire department.
- Adopted code: **2025 California Fire Code** (Title 24, Part 9), based on IFC 2024, effective statewide January 1, 2026. City adopted with **local amendments** including **Fire Code Appendices A–Q** (first reading March 17, 2026; final adoption targeted April 7, 2026).
- Municipal code fire chapter: **Chapter 12** (Fire Department) in the Brawley Code of Ordinances (Municode).
- Source: https://library.municode.com/ca/brawley/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CH12FIDE
- Source: https://www.ivpressonline.com/news/brawley-adopts-building-standards-code-approves-home-program/article_d3601462-8c2d-43dd-a393-490b41e585c2.html

### Wastewater / Pretreatment
- **POTW: City of Brawley Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP)**, 5015 Best Road, Imperial County.
- **NPDES Permit No. CA0104523**, Order R7-2021-0005 (adopted June 15, 2021; effective July 1, 2021; expires June 30, 2026). Issued by RWQCB Colorado River Basin Region (Region 7).
- Design capacity: **5.9 MGD**. Discharge point: New River.
- **Industrial Pretreatment Program (IPP)** approved March 20, 2014 via Resolution R7-2014-0020. City is the Control Authority for its service area.
- Sewer Use Ordinance (SUO): **Chapter 22** of the Brawley Code of Ordinances (enacted 2002; revised June 18, 2013). Permit requirement: Section 22.31; application: Section 22.34.
- Key Significant Industrial User (SIU): **National Beef** (meat packing, ~2,400 head/day, ~1.61 MGD discharge).
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/rwqcb7/board_decisions/adopted_orders/orders/2021/2021_0005brawley_npdes_adopted.pdf
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/rwqcb7/water_issues/programs/pretreatment/docs/pretrconf_prgm_example/pretreatment_program_submittal.pdf

### Zoning — Industrial
- Zoning ordinance: **Chapter 27** of the Brawley Code of Ordinances (last updated September 2008, subsequent ordinances through Ord. 2025-06).
- Two industrial zones: **M-1** (Light Manufacturing and Industrial) and **M-2** (Heavy Manufacturing and Industrial).
- Discretionary review via Conditional Use Permit (CUP): Planning Commission (Section 27.270).
