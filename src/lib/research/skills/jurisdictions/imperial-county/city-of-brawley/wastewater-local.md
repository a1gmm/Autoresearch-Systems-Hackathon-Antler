Service area: ~5,400 connections (approx. 4,900 residential; remainder industrial and commercial)

## Significant Industrial Users (SIUs)

- **National Beef** — meat packing facility, ~2,400 head of cattle/day, discharges ~1.61 MGD of partially treated wastewater from pretreatment system (DAF units, belt press, suspended air flotation). Largest SIU in the system.
- **Pioneers Memorial Hospital (PMH)** — identified as non-domestic user.
- Other commercial users identified via water/sewer billing records and Industrial Waste Survey.

## Permit Process

1. SIU submits wastewater discharge permit application per Section 22.34 (with process description, flow, pollutant data, sampling locations).
2. Superintendent evaluates and may require additional info; issues permit within 30 days of complete application (Section 22.36).
3. Permit duration and reissuance per Sections 22.41 and 22.43.
4. Grab samples for pH, temperature, cyanide, sulfide, oil & grease, phenol, VOCs; 24-hour composites for all other pollutants.
