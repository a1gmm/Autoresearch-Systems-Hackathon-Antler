## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

**Brawley Fire Department** — the city's own municipal fire department serves as AHJ for fire code enforcement within city limits.

Source: https://www.brawley-ca.gov/departments/fire-department

## Adopted California Fire Code Edition

- **Adopted edition: 2025 California Fire Code** (Title 24, Part 9), which is based on the International Fire Code (IFC) 2024. Became effective statewide January 1, 2026.
- City Council first reading: **March 17, 2026**; final adoption targeted **April 7, 2026**.
- The City of Brawley updates its fire/building codes on the standard California three-year cycle.
- Prior edition (2022 CFC) was in effect until January 1, 2026.

Source: https://www.ivpressonline.com/news/brawley-adopts-building-standards-code-approves-home-program/article_d3601462-8c2d-43dd-a393-490b41e585c2.html

## Additional Notes

- Brawley is a small city (~26,000 population); the fire department handles inspections and permit issuance for occupancies within city limits.
- For unincorporated areas adjacent to Brawley, the Imperial County Fire Department provides contract service — these areas are under a separate AHJ.
- CalARP review (for facilities with regulated substances) is handled by DTSC Imperial CUPA, not the fire department.
