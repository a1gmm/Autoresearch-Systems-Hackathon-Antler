## Industrial Districts — Article VI (Sections 27.90–27.99)

### Zone M-1 — Light Manufacturing and Industrial (Section 27.90.1)

> Provides for industrial uses in close proximity to residential and commercial zones, including necessary public service facilities. Standards control intensity and limit performance of activities to levels permitting **no objectionable or obnoxious influences** such as smoke, noise, fumes, vibration, radiation, light or glare that might adversely affect adjacent zones.

**Minimum lot size:** 5,000 sf
**Minimum lot width:** 50 ft (standard/cul-de-sac/flag); 30 ft (flag access extension)
**Minimum front yard setback:** 20 ft from ultimate street R-O-W
**Minimum side yard setback:** 10 ft (interior); 10 ft (street side)
**Minimum rear yard setback:** 10 ft
**Maximum building coverage:** 70%
**Maximum floor area ratio (FAR):** 0.7:1
**Maximum height:** 40 ft (primary structure)
**Outdoor storage:** Must be enclosed by masonry wall 6–8 ft high
**Wall requirement:** Solid masonry wall (5–6 ft) required along side/rear lot lines adjoining residential or agricultural zones

### Zone M-2 — Heavy Manufacturing and Industrial (Section 27.90.2)

> Provides areas for **intensive industrial activities** with necessary transportation facilities for movement of completed products. Some M-2 uses may cause objectionable smoke, noise, fumes, vibration, radiation, light or glare — performance standards protect neighboring residential areas. Buffers required between M-2 and other zones where possible.

**Minimum lot size:** 10,000 sf
**Minimum lot width:** 60 ft (standard/cul-de-sac); 50 ft (flag); 30 ft (flag access extension)
**Minimum front yard setback:** 20 ft
**Minimum side yard setback:** 10 ft
**Minimum rear yard setback:** 10 ft
**Maximum building coverage:** 70%
**Maximum floor area ratio (FAR):** 0.7:1
**Maximum height:** 40 ft (primary structure)

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

- **Planning Commission CUP (C):** Required for uses listed as "C" in Table 27.92. Process governed by Sections 27.271–27.289.
- **Planning Director CUP (C*):** Administrative CUP for uses listed as "C*" — lower-intensity discretionary review.
- The Planning Commission may also approve uses similar to permitted-by-right uses if they find the use falls within the zone's intent, is not more obnoxious or detrimental, and is of a comparable nature and class (Section 27.92(c)).

Planning/Zoning contact: City of Brawley Development Services
Source: https://www.brawley-ca.gov/departments/development-services/planning/zoning-ordinances

## Special Development Standards (Section 27.94)

1. **Outdoor storage** — any outdoor area used for storage must be completely enclosed by a masonry wall, not less than 6 ft or more than 8 ft in height.
2. **Fences and walls** — per Article XII (Special Development Standards and Uses).
3. **Residential adjacency walls** — M-1 and M-2 lots with side or rear lot lines adjoining residential or agricultural zones must have solid masonry wall 5–6 ft high along those lot lines.
