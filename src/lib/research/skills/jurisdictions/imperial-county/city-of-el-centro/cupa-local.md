> Research reference for the CUPA serving the City of El Centro.
> El Centro does NOT have its own city CUPA — the DTSC Imperial County CUPA is a state-run CUPA certified by CalEPA that covers all of Imperial County, including El Centro.
> The Imperial County Public Health Department (ICPHD) Environmental Health Division has assumed the APSA and expanded UST programs under separate agreements with DTSC.

## HMBP Program Details

- **Submission system:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business
- **Who must comply:** Any business storing, using, or handling hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities (Health & Safety Code §25507 — generally 55 gallons liquid, 500 lbs solid, 200 cubic feet gas)
- **Reviewing agency:** DTSC Imperial CUPA (627 Wake Avenue, El Centro)
- **Annual review/resubmission:** Required every year via CERS
- **Update triggers:** Material quantities increase ≥100%; new previously-undisclosed materials; address/ownership/name changes; significant operational changes
- **Required CERS content:** Hazardous materials inventory, emergency response/contingency plan, updated site map, employee training records

## Hazardous Waste Generator Program

- Generator compliance (ID, manifest, labeling, storage time limits) inspected by DTSC Imperial CUPA inspectors
- Tiered permitting for treatment of hazardous waste on-site: see https://dtsc.ca.gov/imperial-county-cupa/imperial-cupa-hazardous-waste-generator-and-tiered-permitting-program/

## CalARP Program

- Facilities with regulated substances above threshold quantities must submit a Risk Management Plan (RMP) to USEPA
- DTSC Imperial CUPA is the local implementing agency (LIA) for CalARP within Imperial County (including El Centro)
- Program 2 and Program 3 inspections conducted by DTSC inspectors

## APSA / Aboveground Storage Tanks

- Facilities storing petroleum above APSA thresholds (≥1,320 gallons aggregate aboveground, or single tank >660 gallons) must maintain a Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure (SPCC) plan
- Contact ICPHD Environmental Health Division for APSA compliance and inspections: (442) 265-1888 | hazmathealth@co.imperial.ca.us

## UST Program

- Contact ICPHD Environmental Health Division for UST permits, inspections, and leak response under the expanded oversight structure (effective January 1, 2024)
- Tank installation, modification, closure, and leak reporting handled through ICPHD
