## Industrial Zone Designations

El Centro's Chapter 29 Article II establishes two manufacturing/industrial zone categories:

### ML — Light Manufacturing Zone

- **Purpose (verbatim per ordinance):** Provides for "fabrication, manufacturing, assembly or processing of materials in refined form" — operations "conducted within enclosed buildings" that do not create smoke, gas, odor, dust, noise, vibration, soot, or lighting "to a degree that is offensive when measured at the property line."
- **General character:** Enclosed-building operations only; light industrial; no detectable offensive conditions at or beyond the property line.
- **General plan implementation:** Implements the Light Manufacturing / General Industrial General Plan land use designation.

### MG — General Manufacturing Zone

- **Purpose (verbatim per ordinance):** Accommodates "manufacturing, processing, fabrication, and assembly of goods and materials" where operations do not create "offensive, obnoxious, or dangerous conditions that are detectable beyond the boundary of the zone." Permits "certain outdoor operations."
- **General character:** Broader manufacturing scope than ML; selected outdoor processing and outdoor storage allowed; wholesale trade and R&D facilities also permitted.
- **General plan implementation:** Implements the General Manufacturing / General Industrial General Plan land use designation.

## Use Regulations — Table 29-69.1

Uses in ML and MG zones are governed by **Table 29-69.1 Manufacturing Zones Use Regulations** in Chapter 29 Article II (Section 29-69).

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| P | Permitted use — by right |
| C | Conditional Use Permit required (per Article V, Division 6) |
| X | Prohibited use |

**Known permitted uses (ML and/or MG):**
- General manufacturing plants
- Assembly and fabrication operations
- Processing facilities (materials in refined form)
- Wholesale trade operations (MG)
- Service and research/development facilities (MG)
- Outdoor processing (MG only; prohibited in ML)

**Known conditional uses (requiring CUP in either ML and/or MG):**
- Communication facilities and collocation facilities (CUP required in both zones per confirmed ordinance text)
- Commercial cannabis activity is prohibited in both ML and MG zones

## Development Standards

Development standards for ML and MG zones are referenced in **Table 29-70.1** (Chapter 29 Article II, Section 29-70).

**Confirmed standards applicable to Manufacturing Zones:**
- **Parking:** 1 space per 500 sq ft of gross floor area (industrial) vs. 1 space per 250 sq ft (commercial zone — for reference)
- **Noise:** Maximum 75 dB daytime, 70 dB nighttime (performance standards apply to manufacturing zones per Article III, Division 8)
- **Landscaping:** Minimum 10% of building site
- **Screening:** Six (6) foot high solid masonry wall required where manufacturing zone abuts a residential zone
- **Loading spaces:** Required for industrial uses (dimensions per ordinance)

**Site plan review:** Required for development in manufacturing zones.
