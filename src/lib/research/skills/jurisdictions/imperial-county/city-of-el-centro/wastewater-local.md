> Research reference for the City of El Centro's POTW and industrial wastewater pretreatment program.
> El Centro operates its own Publicly Owned Treatment Works (POTW). As of the 2024 NPDES permit, a formal pretreatment program is conditional — required only if industrial users cause pass-through or interference. Verify current pretreatment program status and local discharge limits directly with El Centro Public Works before quoting numerical limits.

## Industrial Pretreatment Program Status

Per Order R7-2024-0041, Section 6.C.5.b ("Source Control and Pretreatment Provisions"):

- The POTW must have and enforce an adequate pretreatment program (40 CFR Part 403) **if** the Facility receives influent from Industrial Users that cause Pass Through or Interference or are otherwise subject to National Pretreatment Standards.
- If notified that a pretreatment program is required, the Discharger must submit a formal pretreatment program to the Colorado River Basin Water Board within one year.
- As of the permit effective date (January 2, 2025), the POTW is not confirmed to be operating a formal federally-approved pretreatment program. [VERIFY current status with El Centro Public Works — program may have been triggered by subsequent industrial user activity.]

## Water Supply

Water for the City of El Centro is supplied by the Imperial Irrigation District via the Colorado River canal system (no groundwater wells within 1 mile of the WWTP — groundwater in the area is too saline for municipal use).
