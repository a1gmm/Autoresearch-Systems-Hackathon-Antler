### Stormwater — Phase II Small MS4

- **Program:** El Centro is a Principal Permittee under the State Water Board Phase II Small MS4 General Permit No. 2013-0001 DWQ, administered by the Colorado River Basin Regional Water Quality Control Board.
- Joint permittee: City of Imperial (co-principal permittee per the shared Stormwater Management Plan).
- The SWMP includes: public education, illicit discharge detection and elimination (IDDE), construction site runoff controls, new development/redevelopment, and municipal operations BMPs.
- **Industrial General Permit:** The El Centro WWTP is enrolled under NPDES Industrial General Permit 2014-0057-DWQ (WDID No. 713I005796) for stormwater associated with sewage treatment facilities.
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/water_issues/programs/stormwater/swmp/elcentro_imperial_swmp.pdf | https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/coloradoriver/water_issues/programs/stormwater/

### Zoning — City of El Centro Chapter 29

- **Zoning code:** Chapter 29 ("Zoning") of the El Centro Municipal Code — https://library.municode.com/ca/el_centro/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CH29ZO
- **Industrial districts:** Two manufacturing zones — **ML (Light Manufacturing)** and **MG (General Manufacturing)** — defined in Article II, Section 29-69 and Table 29-69.1.
  - ML: enclosed-building operations only; fabrication, assembly, processing of refined-form materials; no off-site detectable offensive conditions.
  - MG: broader manufacturing and outdoor operations permitted; wholesale trade, R&D also allowed.
- **Use permit process:** Conditional uses in both zones require a Conditional Use Permit (CUP) per Article V, Division 6.
- **Building/Zoning contact:** City of El Centro Community Development Department — https://www.cityofelcentro.org/1180/Building-Zoning-Codes
