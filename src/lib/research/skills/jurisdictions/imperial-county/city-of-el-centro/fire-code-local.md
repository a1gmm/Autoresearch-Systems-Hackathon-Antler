## Fire Prevention Programs

### Engine Company Inspection Program

- Annual inspections conducted by Fire Captains for all occupancies requiring fire code permits or posing higher life-safety risk.
- Categories requiring operational permits / triggering Engine Company inspection include: hazardous materials storage or processing, places of assembly, combustible dust producing processes, flammable/combustible liquid use and storage.
- Inspection fees apply when a fire department inspection is required.

### Business Self-Inspection Program

- Available to lower-risk businesses that do not require fire code permits.
- Business completes and submits the self-inspection checklist provided by the Fire Department.
- Not a substitute for fire code permit where one is required.

### New Construction and Tenant Improvements

- El Centro Fire Department assists the Community Development Department with plan review and inspections for all new construction and tenant improvement projects.
- Fire plan review submittal goes to Community Development / Building Division with Fire Department review coordination.

## Hazardous Materials Operational Permits (CFC Chapter 50+)

Activities requiring a fire operational permit under the California Fire Code include (state CFC baseline; local amendments [VERIFY]):
- Storage, use, or handling of hazardous materials above CFC permit threshold quantities
- Compressed gas storage (CFC Chapter 53)
- Flammable/combustible liquid storage and use (CFC Chapter 57)
- High-piled combustible storage exceeding CFC thresholds (CFC Chapter 32)
- Aerosol products (CFC Chapter 51)
- Explosives (CFC Chapter 56)
- Cryogenic fluids (CFC Chapter 55)

## Hazardous Material Response Unit

El Centro Fire Department hosts the **countywide Hazardous Material Response Unit**, which responds to hazmat incidents throughout Imperial County.
