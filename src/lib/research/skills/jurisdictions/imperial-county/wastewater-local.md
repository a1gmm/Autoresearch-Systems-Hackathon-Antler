## Structure: No County-Level POTW or Pretreatment Program

Imperial County does **not** operate a single county-wide wastewater treatment plant or industrial pretreatment (IWP) program. Wastewater service in Imperial County is fragmented across:

1. **Seven incorporated city POTWs** — each city has its own treatment plant, sewer use ordinance, and (where applicable) industrial pretreatment program.
2. **Community Service Districts (CSDs) / Special Districts** — administered by the Imperial County Department of Public Works, Special Districts Division.

For an industrial facility in **unincorporated Imperial County**, the applicable wastewater authority depends on which CSD or city system serves the area. There is no single county IWP permit application.

## Regional Regulatory Authority: Colorado River Basin Water Board (Region 7)

The **Colorado River Basin Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB Region 7)** is the regional authority for all wastewater NPDES permits and pretreatment oversight in Imperial County:

- All city POTW NPDES permits are issued by Region 7.
- Industrial dischargers seeking indirect discharge permits (IWP) into a POTW must comply with the applicable local limits set by the POTW operator and federal categorical pretreatment standards.
- **Direct discharges** to surface water from industrial facilities require an NPDES permit from Region 7 (or State Water Board for general permits).
- Contact: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/coloradoriver/

## On-Site Wastewater / OWTS (Unincorporated Areas)

For facilities in unincorporated areas not connected to a sewer system, **On-Site Wastewater Treatment Systems (OWTS)** are regulated by the Imperial County Environmental Health Division under **Title 9, Division 10, Chapter 14** of the Imperial County Code of Ordinances (Local Agency Management Program — LAMP):
- LAMP document: https://www.icphd.org/media/managed/environmentalhealth/Imperial_County_Local_Agency_Management_Program.pdf
- Contact: Imperial County Environmental Health, (442) 265-1888

## Notes for Research Subagent

- Always determine whether the facility parcel is inside an incorporated city or in unincorporated Imperial County — this determines which POTW/pretreatment authority applies.
- For industrial IWP in city systems, verify the city's current local limits table and sewer use ordinance chapter; these vary by city and are subject to periodic revision under their NPDES permits.
- Brawley is the only city confirmed to have a fully documented Industrial Pretreatment Program on record with Region 7 (2013 submittal). Other cities' programs [VERIFY with Region 7 or directly with city].
