## Industrial Zone Districts — Overview

Imperial County defines four primary industrial base zones and two agricultural-related industrial overlay zones:

| Zone | Code | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| M-1 | Light Industrial | Wholesale, storage, trucking, assembly manufacturing; processing entirely within building |
| M-2 | Medium Industrial | General manufacturing, medium-intensity fabrication; within building or fenced area |
| M-3 | Heavy Industrial | Heaviest manufacturing, processing, and fabrication; outdoor operations allowed |
| AM-1 | Agricultural-Related Light Industrial | Agri-compatible light industrial; must not convert to non-agricultural use for 7 years from CO |
| AM-2 | Agricultural-Related Medium Industrial | Agri-compatible medium industrial; same 7-year restriction |

**Overlay zones applicable to industrial parcels:**
- G = Geothermal Overlay
- RE = Renewable Energy / Geothermal Overlay
- GH = Geological Hazard Area
- FP = Flood Plain Hazard Area
- H = Airport Approach Height Restriction
- U = Urban Areas (triggers additional urban standards on development)
- SP = Specific Plan Area
- C = Conditional Zone Change

## M-1 (Light Industrial) — §§ 90515.00–90515.11

**Purpose:** Designate areas for wholesale commercial, storage, trucking, assembly-type manufacturing, and similar light industrial uses. Processing or fabrication is limited to activities conducted **entirely within a building** that does not emit fumes, odor, dust, smoke, or gas beyond the building perimeter and produces no significant noise or vibration beyond the building perimeter.

### Permitted Uses (By Right) — Selected Examples (§ 90515.01)
- Ambulance station, auto service/repair (enclosed), auto wash, auto dealership
- Bakery, cold storage, computer sales/repair/manufacturing
- Contractor storage yard, data center (enclosed), drug/pharmaceutical sales and manufacturing
- Educational institutions, electronic equipment assembly (enclosed)
- EV Charging Stations (primary use), emergency shelters
- Financial institution, freight storage yard, furniture store/cleaning/refinishing
- Industrial Hemp manufacturing (semi-finished/finished products) per Div. 4 Ch. 6 and Title 14
- Industrial storage, laboratory (medical/dental/optical/biological/testing)
- Light machine shop fabrication (enclosed), lumber yard
- Mini warehouse (no outside storage), mini storage, moving/storage warehouse
- Newspaper/magazine printing, nursery, pest control, photographic processing
- Research and development office/laboratory, resource extraction and energy development
- Restaurant, sheet metal shop (enclosed), sign fabrication
- Solar energy generation (on-site consumption only), trade schools
- Tire re-treading (<10 employees), toy manufacturing, truck fueling station
- Utility substations, transmission lines, veterinary hospital (indoor only)
- Welding or blacksmithing (adequately fenced with obscured fencing)
- Wholesale distribution, wind-driven electrical generator (≤15 kW)
- **Pre-Schools, Elementary, Junior High, and High Schools are NOT permitted.**

### Uses Requiring Conditional Use Permit (CUP) — Selected Examples (§ 90515.02)
- Airports/aircraft landing fields
- Asphalt/concrete batch plant
- Auto wrecking, auto towing/dismantling
- Bulk fuel storage facilities
- Call centers, cemeteries/columbariums/crematories
- Chemical storage (non-toxic, non-explosive)
- Commercial Cannabis (all forms) per Div. 4 Ch. 6 and Title 14
- Communication towers (cell, TV, radio, satellite)
- Cotton gins, dairies, dehydration mills
- Electrical power generation plants, geothermal test/intermediate/major well facilities
- Golf course, heliport, hospitals/rest homes
- Labor camp, railroad station, recycling facility
- Race track, shooting range (indoor only), solid waste facilities
- Small ethanol plants (≤1 million gal/yr capacity)
- Surface mining, swap meets, trucking terminals and firms
- Waste energy facility

### Development Standards (§§ 90515.04–90515.11)
| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Minimum lot size | 10,000 sq ft |
| Minimum lot area per dwelling unit | N/A (no dwelling units except accessory caretaker) |
| Front yard setback | 10 ft from property line / ROW (0 ft if approved by Planning Director + PW + Fire Marshal concurrence) |
| Side yard | None required |
| Rear yard | None required |
| Height limit | 6 stories or 80 feet |
| Min. distance between structures | None except as required by California Building Code |
| Parking | Per § 90402.01(f) |
| Landscaping | Per § 90302.03 |

## M-2 (Medium Industrial) — §§ 90516.00–90516.11

**Purpose:** Designate areas for wholesale commercial, storage, trucking, assembly manufacturing, **general manufacturing**, research and development, **medium-intensity fabrication**. Processing within a building or within securely fenced (obscured fencing) areas; no fumes, odor, dust, smoke, or gas beyond the property line.

### Permitted Uses (By Right) — Selected Examples (§ 90516.01)
- **All M-1 uses permitted under § 90515.01** (plus the following additional):
- Alcohol and alcoholic beverage manufacturer
- Asphalt and asphalt products manufacturing
- Automobile assembly, body/fender works, dismantling (within building), painting, upholstering
- Boiler or tank works, brick/tile/terra cotta
- Carbon manufacturing, celluloid/pyroxylin manufacturing
- Cement and cement product manufacturing
- Cargo containers (with approved building permit)
- Data centers (yard or enclosed)
- Disinfectant manufacturing, feed mills, fertilizer and insecticide manufacturing
- Fish and meat packing plant, grain elevators, graphite manufacturing, gypsum manufacturing
- Industrial Hemp manufacturing per Div. 4 Ch. 6 and Title 14
- Insulation materials manufacturing, mini storage (outside storage allowed if screened)
- Oil reclamation plant, petroleum products storage
- Railroad repair shop, railroad yard, seed mill
- Solar energy generation (on-site consumption only)
- Wool pulling and scouring

### Uses Requiring CUP (§ 90516.02)
- Acid manufacturing, ammonia/chlorine/bleaching powder manufacturing
- Animal sales yards, slaughter plant, stockyards
- Auto wrecking yard (entirely fenced, obscured from adjacent parcels)
- Blast furnace, chemical manufacturing
- Commercial Cannabis (all forms) per Div. 4 Ch. 6 and Title 14
- Communication towers
- Distillation of coal/wood/tar, fat rendering, gelatin manufacturing
- Glass manufacturing, incinerators, junk yards, labor camps
- Major electrical generation/transmission facilities (coordinated with Imperial Irrigation District)
- Recycling facility, rubber and rubber products manufacturing
- Smelting of tin/copper/iron ore, solid waste facilities
- Storage and handling of radioactive materials, surface mining
- Transportation treatment units (TTUs), trucking firms/storage yards

### Development Standards (§§ 90516.04–90516.11)
| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Minimum lot size | 20,000 sq ft |
| Front yard setback | 10 ft from property line / ROW (0 ft with Planning Director + PW + Fire Marshal approval) |
| Side yard | None required |
| Rear yard | None required |
| Height limit | 6 stories or 80 feet |
| Min. distance between structures | None except as required by California Building Code |
| Parking | Per § 90402.01(F) |

## M-3 (Heavy Industrial) — §§ 90517.00–90517.11

**Purpose:** Heaviest manufacturing, processing, or fabrication. May be conducted **inside or outside** a building provided no fumes, odors, dust, smoke, or gas beyond property line and no significant noise or vibration beyond site perimeter.

### Permitted Uses (By Right) — Selected Examples (§ 90517.01)
- **All M-2 uses permitted under § 90516.01** (excluding M-1 uses not already in M-2)
- Abattoir / animal slaughter house, acid manufacturing
- Ammunition manufacturing, automobile assembly plants
- Automobile wrecking yard (entirely fenced, obscured from adjacent parcels)
- Creosote manufacturing, curing/tanning/storage of raw hides or skins
- Distillation of bones, coal, wood, or tar
- Drop forge industries, explosive manufacturing and storage
- Fat rendering, gas manufacturing, graphite manufacturing
- Incinerators, iron/steel/brass/copper foundries or fabrication plants
- Lumber mills
- Major electrical generation/transmission facilities (coordinated with IID)
- MRF (Material Recovery Facility)
- Ore reduction plants, petroleum refineries
- Quarry or stone mills, recycling facility, regional landfill
- Rolling mills, rubber and rubber products manufacturing
- Smelting of tin/copper/zinc/iron ore
- Solar energy generation (on-site consumption only)
- Transformation facility

### Uses Requiring CUP (§ 90517.02)
- Communication towers

## AM-1 (Agricultural-Related Light Industrial) — §§ 90510.00–90510.11

**Purpose:** Provide areas for agricultural-related light industrial uses compatible with adjacent agricultural land. Existing M-1/M-1-N zones may be converted to AM-1 for General Plan consistency. **Key restriction:** Any AM-1 use must not convert to a non-agricultural use for a minimum of **7 years** from Certificate of Occupancy.

### Representative Permitted Uses (§ 90510.01)
- Agricultural chemical (fertilizer, pesticide) sales and shipping (not manufactured)
- Agricultural accessories, equipment rental/repair/sales, farm implement manufacturing (<20 employees)
- Cold storage for agricultural products, contract harvesting business
- Equestrian establishment, farmers market facility, feed stores
- Fuel (bulk) sales, industrial hemp (cultivation, harvesting, light processing and manufacturing) per Div. 4 Ch. 6 and Title 14
- Packaging and processing facilities for agricultural products, poultry butchering/processing/shipping
- Seed stores, seed processing, solar energy generation (on-site)

### Development Standards (§ 90510.03–90510.10)
- Minimum lot size: 1 acre net (§ 90510.04)
- Traffic study required if use generates >100 ADT
- Air quality permits from ICAPCD required before commencing AM-1 use (§ 90510.05 cross-reference)
- Parking: same requirements as M-1 zone (§ 90510.09)
- Landscaping: same as M-1 zone (§ 90510.10)

## Notes for Research Subagent

- The M-1 permitted use list is broad; many uses a practitioner might expect to require a CUP are by-right in M-1.
- Hazardous materials uses (bulk fuel, chemical storage) require a CUP even in M-2/M-3.
- Commercial Cannabis requires a CUP in both M-1 and M-2 under Division 4, Chapter 6.
- All AM-1 uses with air emissions must also obtain permits from ICAPCD (§ 90510.05 cross-reference pattern).
- Zone change applications (including to/from industrial zones) require Planning Commission approval.
