## CUPA Agency — DTSC (Not the County)

**CRITICAL GOTCHA:** Imperial County is one of the few California counties where the **California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)** is the Certified Unified Program Agency, not any county agency. CalEPA authorized DTSC as the Imperial County CUPA effective **January 1, 2005**.

## Programs Administered by DTSC Imperial CUPA

| Program | Admin | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) | DTSC | Release response plans and inventory; Cal HSC §25500 et seq. |
| Hazardous Waste Generator / Tiered Permitting | DTSC | Generator registration and on-site treatment tiers (PBR, CA, CESQT, etc.) |
| California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) | DTSC | Risk Management Plan for regulated substances |
| Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) | ICPHD-EH (PA) | SPCC Plan review and inspection; tanks ≥1,320 gal petroleum |
| Underground Storage Tank (UST) | ICPHD-EH (PA) | Permit, inspection, closure certification |

**Note:** HMMP/HMIS (Hazardous Materials Management Plan / Inventory Statement) under the California Fire Code is a separate program enforced by the Imperial County Fire Department.

## Reporting Thresholds

- **HMBP trigger:** ≥55 gallons liquid, ≥500 lbs solid, or ≥200 cubic feet compressed gas of any hazardous material handled at any one time during the reporting year (Cal HSC §25507).
- **CalARP trigger:** Any regulated substance (RCRA EHS or state AHM) present at or above threshold planning quantity.
- **UST trigger:** Any underground tank storing hazardous materials including motor fuels.
- **APSA trigger:** Single AST ≥1,320 gallons of liquid petroleum, or cumulative ASTs ≥1,320 gallons where each individual tank ≥55 gallons. Agricultural/nursery/logging/construction site exemptions apply if no single tank >20,000 gal and cumulative ≤100,000 gal.
- **Hazardous Waste Generator:** Any quantity of RCRA or California-listed hazardous waste triggers generator registration.

## Submission / Reporting Portal

All HMBP, CalARP, hazardous waste generator, UST, and APSA submittals for Imperial County route through the **California Environmental Reporting System (CERS)**:
- Portal: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/

Paper submittals may be accepted by DTSC Imperial CUPA at 627 Wake Avenue; confirm with DTSC for current procedure.
