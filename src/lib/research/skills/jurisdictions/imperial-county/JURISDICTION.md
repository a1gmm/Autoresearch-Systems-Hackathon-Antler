## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air — Imperial County Air Pollution Control District (ICAPCD)

Imperial County has its **own standalone Air Pollution Control District**, not an AQMD. All stationary-source permits for industrial facilities in unincorporated Imperial County are issued by the ICAPCD.

- **Agency:** Imperial County Air Pollution Control District (ICAPCD)
- **Address:** 150 S. 9th Street, El Centro, CA 92243-2839
- **Phone:** (442) 265-1800 or (888) 547-2876
- **APCO:** Belen Leon-Lopez (belenleon-lopez@co.imperial.ca.us)
- **Engineering Division Manager:** Jesus Ramirez (jesusramirez@co.imperial.ca.us)
- ICAPCD enforces its own Rules and Regulations including Rule 310 (Permits Required); industrial facilities must obtain an Authority to Construct (ATC) and Permit to Operate (PTO) from ICAPCD before commencing any new or modified stationary source of air emissions.
- Notable programs: AB 617 North-End Community (Brawley, Westmorland, Calipatria), High Wind Fugitive Dust Exceptional Events Mitigation Plan, Prescribed Burn Program.
- Source: https://apcd.imperialcounty.org/

### CUPA — DTSC is the CUPA (not the County)

**GOTCHA:** Imperial County is one of the few California counties where the **state (DTSC) is the CUPA directly.** CalEPA authorized DTSC as the Imperial County CUPA effective **January 1, 2005**.

- **CUPA agency:** California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), Imperial CUPA Office
- **Address:** 627 Wake Avenue, El Centro, CA 92243
- **Phone:** (760) 352-0381 | Toll-free: (866) 357-3990 | Fax: (760) 352-1641
- **Programs administered by DTSC:** HMBP, Hazardous Waste Generator / Tiered Permitting, CalARP
- **Participating Agency (PA):** Imperial County Division of Environmental Health (ICPHD-EH) administers **APSA** (since 2021-07-01) and **UST** (since 2024-01-01) as a PA under the DTSC CUPA.
  - PA address: 1221 W. State St., Suite B, El Centro, CA 92243 | Phone: (442) 265-1888 | Email: hazmathealth@co.imperial.ca.us
- CUPA oversight surcharge: $67/year per facility (increased from $57 effective 2023-10-13). Additional inspection hours billed at $165/hour beyond initial fee.
- HMBP/CalARP/hazwaste submittals route to DTSC. UST and APSA submittals route to ICPHD-EH.
- Submissions via: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/ (California Environmental Reporting System)
- Source: https://dtsc.ca.gov/imperial-county-cupa/ | https://www.icphd.org/environmental-health/hazardous-materials

### Wastewater — No County-Level Pretreatment Program; Served by City POTWs and CSDs

Imperial County's unincorporated areas are served by **city-operated wastewater systems and Community Service Districts (CSDs)**, not a county POTW. There is no single county-run sewer authority with industrial pretreatment jurisdiction.

- Incorporated cities each operate their own POTW: Brawley (5.9 MGD; Industrial Pretreatment Program adopted 2002, Sewer Use Ordinance adopted 2002), El Centro, Calexico, Holtville, Imperial, Calipatria, Westmorland.
- CSD-served unincorporated communities: Salton City, Winterhaven, Desert Shores, Bombay Beach, Heber, Seeley, Niland — served by Special Districts administered by Imperial County Public Works.
- For industrial wastewater discharge permits (IWP) in unincorporated areas served by CSDs, contact: **Imperial County Public Works — Special Districts Division**, 1002 W. State St., El Centro, CA 92243 | Phone: (442) 265-1818.
- Regional water quality regulator: **Colorado River Basin Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 7)**, which issues NPDES permits for all discharges in Imperial County. Contact for pretreatment oversight.
- Source: https://publicworks.imperialcounty.org/ | https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/coloradoriver/

### Stormwater — Phase II MS4 / Colorado River Basin Water Board

- **Imperial County** (unincorporated areas) and the Cities of El Centro, Calexico, Brawley, and Imperial are all enrolled as permittees under the **State Water Board Phase II MS4 General Permit (Order 2013-0001-DWQ)**, administered by the Colorado River Basin Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 7).
- County stormwater compliance is managed by **Imperial County Public Works — Storm Water Division**.
- Local regulatory anchor: Title 9, Division 31 (Storm Water Control Ordinance) of the Imperial County Code of Ordinances. Municode: https://library.municode.com/ca/imperial_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT9LAUSCO_DIV31STCO
- Industrial facilities subject to the statewide **Industrial General Permit (IGP, Order 2014-0057-DWQ)** — apply through California State Water Board SMARTS portal.
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/coloradoriver/water_issues/programs/stormwater/

## CUPA (Unified Program) — Detail

## Notes

- GOTCHA: Imperial County is one of the few California counties where the **state (DTSC) is the CUPA directly** rather than a county environmental health or county fire hazmat division. HMBP/hazwaste/CalARP submittals route to DTSC; only UST and APSA are handled by the county Environmental Health PA.
- The City of Imperial does not run its own fire suppression department — it is served by the Imperial County Fire Department.
- El Centro, Calexico, and Brawley each operate their own municipal fire departments (see city files), but none is its own CUPA — all are served by the DTSC Imperial CUPA.
- Air permits for all stationary sources route to ICAPCD, not SCAQMD or any other district.
