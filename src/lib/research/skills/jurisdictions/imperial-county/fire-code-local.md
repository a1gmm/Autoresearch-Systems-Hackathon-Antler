## AHJ: Imperial County Fire Department

Imperial County has a **standalone county fire department** — it is NOT a CAL FIRE contract jurisdiction. The Imperial County Fire Department serves all unincorporated areas plus the City of Imperial (which lacks its own fire suppression department). It also provides contract services to areas near Holtville, Brawley, and Calipatria.

- **Fire Prevention Bureau:** 2514 La Brucherie Road, Imperial, CA 92251 (Station 1)
- **Main department office:** 1078 Dogwood Rd., Heber, CA 92249
- **Phone:** (442) 265-6000
- **Email:** FireDepartment@co.imperial.ca.us
- **Hours:** Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m. and 1:00 p.m.–5:00 p.m.
- The Fire Chief also serves as the county Office of Emergency Services (OES) Coordinator.

## Inspection Program

- **Annual inspections** for all commercial businesses in unincorporated areas.
- **Property surveys** conducted in unincorporated areas and contract cities upon receipt of complaint.
- The Fire Prevention Bureau conducts fire code enforcement, plan review, and state-mandated inspections.

## Permit Fees and Abatement

- Fees and citations issued per the **Imperial County Fire Department fee schedule** [VERIFY — not published online as of 2026-06-03; contact Prevention Bureau at (442) 265-6000].
- **7-step abatement process** for violations:
  1. Initial property survey
  2. Notice of Code Violation issued
  3. 30-day compliance notice (sent via certified mail)
  4. 15-day compliance notice (if needed after 30-day notice)
  5. Violation forwarded to Planning and Development Services
  6. Legal Counsel involvement for non-compliance
  7. Fees and citations per department schedule

## Fire Hazard Area / HMMP

- Hazardous Materials Management Plan (HMMP) and Inventory Statement (HMIS) requirements under the CFC are enforced by the Fire Prevention Bureau.
- Weed abatement complaints: andrewloper@co.imperial.ca.us
