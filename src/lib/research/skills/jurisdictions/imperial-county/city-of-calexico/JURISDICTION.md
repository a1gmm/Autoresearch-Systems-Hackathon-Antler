## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air

- **Imperial County Air Pollution Control District (ICAPCD)** is the local air district for all of Imperial County, including Calexico. The city has no independent air authority.
- ICAPCD issues Authority to Construct (ATC) and Permit to Operate (PTO) for stationary sources. Application processing fee: **$226.50**; additional fees apply per Rule 301 and Rule 302 fee schedules. [VERIFY current fee schedule year]
- Calexico is designated a disadvantaged community and is part of the CARB AB 617 community air monitoring program (Calexico/El Centro/Heber community).
- ICAPCD address: 150 S. 9th Street, El Centro, CA 92243; Phone: (442) 265-1800 / (888) 547-2876.

### CUPA (Hazardous Materials / Unified Program)

- **Not its own CUPA.** Calexico is served by the **DTSC Imperial County CUPA** — DTSC was certified as CUPA for Imperial County effective January 1, 2005.
- DTSC Imperial CUPA maintains a **field office at 301 Heber Avenue, Calexico, CA 92231** (main office: 627 Wake Avenue, El Centro, CA 92243); phone: (760) 339-2777.
- Programs administered: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP), Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), Hazardous Waste Generator and Tiered Permitting (HWGTP).
- The **Imperial County Public Health Department Environmental Health Division** (1221 W. State St., Suite B, El Centro; (442) 265-1888) is a Participating Agency since July 1, 2021, handling APSA and UST programs.
- Fee schedule: see https://dtsc.ca.gov/imperial-county-cupa/imperial-cupa-fees/ [VERIFY — DTSC page 403 at time of fetch]

## Notes

- Calexico runs its own fire department and is a HEAT JPA partner; it relies on DTSC Imperial CUPA for HMBP / hazwaste / UST / APSA / CalARP.
- Air permitting is entirely under ICAPCD — city has no local air authority.
- The city operates its own POTW and is the industrial pretreatment control authority for dischargers to its sewer system.
- Stormwater is under state Phase II MS4 general permit, not a city-specific NPDES permit.
- For building code adoption (California Building Code, Energy Code, etc.) contact the Building Division: (760) 768-2105 / building@calexico.ca.gov; Building Official: Cuauhtémoc Cortez.
