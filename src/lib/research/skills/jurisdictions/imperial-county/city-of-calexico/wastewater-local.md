## Plant Capacity and Treatment

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Average daily flow capacity | 4.3 MGD |
| Peak daily flow capacity | 5.5 MGD |
| Treatment process | Activated sludge (headworks, primary clarifier, aeration tanks, secondary clarifiers, sludge drying beds) |
| Effluent discharge point | New River (flows to Salton Sea) |

Collection system: pipe sizes 6–30 inches; two major interceptor mains (South and North Interceptors); multiple small pump stations for outlying developments.

## Industrial Pretreatment Program

- As the POTW, the City of Calexico is the **industrial pretreatment control authority** for indirect dischargers into its sewer system.
- Specific discharge limits (local limits) were **not publicly accessible** at time of fetch. [VERIFY local limits by requesting the Industrial Pretreatment Program document from the Public Works Department]
