## Industrial Zone Designations

Calexico uses **three industrial zone designations** (not M-1/M-2). These are established in Section 17.01.140(A)(3) of the General Provisions chapter:

| Zone Code | Zone Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | Industrial Zone | Standard industrial district |
| **IR** | Industrial Rail Served Zone | Industrial uses with rail access |
| **BP** | Business Park | Light industrial / office-industrial campus |

A Zoning Map (2021 Revision A) is available via the Applications & Forms page and shows current industrial zone boundaries.

## CEQA

Industrial development projects may be subject to CEQA review. A CEQA Process Flowchart is available on the city's Applications & Forms page. The city coordinates CEQA review with the County Health Department, Imperial Valley Regional Medical Authority (IVRMA), the school district, and ICAPCD (Air Quality).
