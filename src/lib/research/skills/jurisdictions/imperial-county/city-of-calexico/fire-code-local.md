## Municipal Code Chapters

Fire code is contained in **Title 15 – Buildings and Construction** of the Calexico Municipal Code:

- **Chapter 15.20 – Fire Prevention Code** — https://library.municode.com/ca/calexico/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT15BUCO_CH15.20FIPRCO
- **Chapter 15.22 – Uniform Fire Code** — https://library.municode.com/ca/calexico/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT15BUCO_CH15.22UNFICO

Note: The chapter titles "Fire Prevention Code" and "Uniform Fire Code" reflect legacy Municode headings. California adopted the **California Fire Code (CFC)** as part of Title 24, Part 9. Statewide, the 2022 CFC was effective January 1, 2023; the 2025 CFC became effective January 1, 2026. Calexico's operative edition should be confirmed against the city's most recent building code adoption ordinance [VERIFY with Building Division at (760) 768-2105 or building@calexico.ca.gov].

## Fire Prevention Activities

The Fire Prevention & Inspections division (operating from Station #1) conducts:
- Regular fire prevention inspections of commercial and industrial facilities
- Plan review for fire sprinkler systems, suppression systems, and fire protection equipment
- Coordination with architects, engineers, and contractors during design phases
- Code enforcement and vegetation management
- Public education (schools, health fairs, community events)

Source: https://www.calexico.ca.gov/index.asp?SEC=955CA7D8-4E58-4ADF-9F07-BFAA69E61C96

## Hazmat Response

The Calexico Fire Department participates in the **Imperial County Hazardous Emergency Response Team (HEAT)** under a Joint Powers Agreement (JPA) with Imperial County. HEAT provides countywide hazmat emergency response.
