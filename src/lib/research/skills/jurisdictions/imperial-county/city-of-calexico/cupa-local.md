## CUPA Agency

Calexico is **not its own CUPA**. The Certified Unified Program Agency for all of Imperial County is the **California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) Imperial County CUPA**, certified by CalEPA effective **January 1, 2005**.

- DTSC Imperial CUPA — field office: **301 Heber Avenue, Calexico, CA 92231**; Phone: (760) 339-2777; Fax: (760) 352-1641
- DTSC Imperial CUPA — main office: 627 Wake Avenue, El Centro, CA 92243
- Main program portal: https://dtsc.ca.gov/imperial-county-cupa/

## Participating Agency

The **Imperial County Public Health Department Environmental Health Division** is a Participating Agency (PA) under the DTSC Imperial CUPA since July 1, 2021. The PA administers selected delegated programs:

- **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** program
- **Underground Storage Tank (UST)** program (delegated as of January 1, 2024)

ICPHD Environmental Health office:
- Address: 1221 W. State St., Suite B, El Centro, CA 92243
- Phone: (442) 265-1888
- Email: hazmathealth@co.imperial.ca.us
- Hours: Monday–Friday, 8 am–12 pm and 1 pm–5 pm

## Programs Administered (Full CUPA Scope — DTSC)

| Program | Regulatory Authority |
|---|---|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) | HSC §§ 25500–25545 |
| California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) | HSC §§ 25531–25543 |
| Underground Storage Tanks (UST) | HSC Ch. 6.7 |
| Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) | HSC Ch. 6.67 |
| Hazardous Waste Generator & Tiered Permitting (HWGTP) | HSC Ch. 6.5 |

Note: As of January 1, 2024, UST oversight was transferred to ICPHD as PA.

## HMBP / CalEPA CERS Submission

Businesses submit Hazardous Materials Business Plans and other Unified Program submittals through CalEPA's California Environmental Reporting System (CERS): https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/

- Air permits for stationary sources are separate and administered by ICAPCD (not the CUPA).
