## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **Agency:** Napa County Division of Environmental Health (DEH), under Planning, Building & Environmental Services (PBES). Single countywide CUPA covering all incorporated cities (Napa, American Canyon, St. Helena, Calistoga, Yountville) — no city runs its own CUPA, no Participating Agencies.
- **Address / Phone:** 1195 Third Street, Suite 210, Napa, CA 94559 · 707-253-4417
- **Key staff (2025):** W. Douglas Calhoun (Environmental Management Coordinator); Gennina Bautista, Stephen Kim, Christian Martinez, Nicole Price, Joel Morgan
- **Programs administered:** HMBP (~1,250 facilities, triennial inspections), Hazardous Waste Generator / Tiered Permitting (~400 facilities), UST (44 facilities), APSA, CalARP (~9 RMP facilities; most common EHS substance: anhydrous ammonia), Methamphetamine-Contaminated Property Cleanup, Stormwater inspection (coordinated with Ch. 16.28)
- **Enabling state law:** Cal. HSC Division 20, Chapters 6.5, 6.7, 6.67, 6.95; CCR Titles 19, 22, 23
- **Local code:** Napa County Code Chapter 13.15 (Groundwater Conservation fees tied here); stormwater duties under Ch. 16.28
- **Fee schedule (PBES Fee Policy Manual Part 110, eff. 07/07/2025):**
  - HMBP Annual Permit — UST Facility <40,000 gal: **$677**
  - HMBP Annual Permit — 1,000–20,000 units: **$403** · >20,000 units: **$450** · Above-Ground Fuel Tanks Only: **$403**
  - UST Annual Operating Permit (per tank): **$477** · New Plan Check: **$914** · Closure Plan Check: **$538** (layout fee)
  - APSA Tier 1 Annual: **$185** · Tier 2 Annual: **$212** · Conditionally Exempt Farm: **$93**
  - CalARP — EHS Above Federal Qty: **$319** · Below Federal/Above CA Qty: **$319** · Conditionally Exempt: **$106** · Initial Submittal (10-hr min): **$1,590**
  - Hazardous Waste Generator Annual (Conditionally Exempt Treatment): **$239** · 1,000 kg RCRA/Non-RCRA/month: **$318**
  - Stormwater CUPA HazMat/Waste Storage Annual Permit: **$159** · Industrial/SWPPP Annual: **$200**
  - Hourly rate (as of 07/07/2025): **$159/hr** (+ 7.5% General Plan Surcharge where applicable)

### 2. Fire AHJ — Code Adoption and Permits

- **AHJ (unincorporated county + Town of Yountville):** CAL FIRE / Napa County Fire Department (CAL FIRE Sonoma-Lake-Napa Unit). County contracts with CAL FIRE for unincorporated areas. Fire Marshal's Office: 951 California Blvd, Napa, CA 94559 · 707-299-1464.
- **Separate city AHJs:** City of Napa FD · St. Helena FD · Calistoga FD · American Canyon Fire Protection District (see city-level files).
- **Adopted fire code (unincorporated county):** Napa County Code **Title 15, Chapter 15.32** (Fire Code). The Board of Supervisors held a public hearing December 9, 2025, to adopt the **2025 California Fire Code** (Title 24, Part 9) with local amendments, effective January 1, 2026 for applications filed on or after that date. Applications complete by December 31, 2025 were reviewed under the 2022 CFC. The 2025 CFC is based on the International Fire Code 2024 (IFC 2024).
- **Key local amendment confirmed in Ch. 15.32:** § 15.32.250 adds Table B105.2 — Minimum Fire Flow requirements (site-specific rural fire flow standards).
- **Plan submittal process:** Minimum 3 plan sets submitted to PBES Building Division (1195 Third Street), which forwards fire-related plans to the Fire Marshal's Office. Corrected plans go directly to Fire Marshal at 1125 3rd Street, Napa, CA 94559.
- **Adopted NFPA standards (per CAL FIRE/NCFD Development Guidelines, rev. Sept. 2016):** NFPA 13D/13R (2013 ed.), NFPA 1142-2012, NFPA-22 (2013 ed.). Confirm current editions with Fire Marshal.
- **Permit fee schedule:** PBES Fee Policy Manual Part 85 (Fire Marshal fees). Contact Fire Marshal's Office for current amounts.

### 3. POTW / Industrial Wastewater — Napa Sanitation District

- **POTW:** Napa Sanitation District (NapaSan) — independent special district; not a county agency. Soscol Water Recycling Facility, 1515 Soscol Ferry Road, Napa, CA 94558 · 707-258-6000 · info@napasan.com. Office: M–F 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m.
- **Service area:** City of Napa and portions of unincorporated Napa County. American Canyon has its own wastewater services; rural unincorporated areas may use septic.
- **Local sewer-use code:** NapaSan District Code **Title 4 — Sewer Use** (available at napasan.com). Key sections:
  - § 4.01.070 — User Permit required before any contributory industry may discharge
  - § 4.04.020 — POTW legal authority over Industrial Users (IUs), incorporating 40 CFR 403.8
  - § 4.04.030 — Industrial User Permit Program and issuance procedures
  - § 4.04.040 — Discharge Permit elements; SIU permit duration max 5 years; must include effluent limits, monitoring requirements, reporting, civil/criminal penalty statement
- **Permit categories (per NapaSan Discharge Permits page):**
  1. Categorical Industrial Users (CIUs) — ≥25,000 gpd process wastewater or categorical standard applies; full pretreatment program required
  2. Significant Industrial Users (SIUs) — requires individual IU discharge permit; monitoring, reporting
  3. Industrial Users (general) — non-domestic discharge; BMPs may suffice
  4. Wineries & Breweries — flow meter + weekly BOD/TSS/pH sampling during processing season
  5. Groundwater/Surface Cleaning — temporary permit up to 1 year; lab testing required
  6. Special Discharge / Waste Haulers — separate permit categories
- **Application process:** Contact NapaSan Engineering Dept at 707-258-6000 or download application from napasan.com. No online e-permit portal confirmed.
- **Napa County Code crossover:** County Code Title 13 (Water Service and Public Utilities) references sewer matters; Ch. 13.16 covers general provisions and discharge.

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater — County Ordinance

- **County MS4 program:** Napa County participates in a Phase II NPDES MS4 permit as co-permittee with the Cities of Napa, St. Helena, American Canyon, Calistoga, and Town of Yountville. Program administered through the Napa County Stormwater Management Program (NCSWMP) with coordination via the Napa County Watershed Information Center (707-253-4417 — same number as DEH).
- **Local ordinance:** Napa County Code **Chapter 16.28** (Stormwater Management and Discharge Control) — the countywide stormwater and runoff pollution control ordinance implementing NPDES obligations. DEH enforces the industrial/hazmat component under CUPA authority (fee: $159/yr for CUPA HazMat facilities; $200/yr for facilities also subject to Industrial General Permit/SWPPP).
- **Industrial facility requirements under Ch. 16.28 / State IGP:** Facilities with an HMBP or hazardous waste generator status must obtain the CUPA Stormwater Annual Permit from DEH. Facilities subject to the State Industrial General Permit (IGP, NPDES No. CAS000001) must maintain a SWPPP and obtain a separate DEH Industrial/SWPP Plan annual permit.
- **Napa County Stormwater Management Program (NCSWMP):** Available at California State Water Resources Control Board website.
- Source: https://library.municode.com/ca/napa_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT16EN_CH16.28STMADICO
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/water_issues/programs/stormwater/swmp/napa_swmp.pdf

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts and CUP Process

- **Zoning code:** Napa County Code **Title 18 — Zoning** (administered by PBES Planning Division). Unincorporated county only; cities have separate zoning codes.
- **Industrial zoning districts** (per Ch. 18.12 Establishment of Districts and Ch. 18.40):
  - **IP — Industrial Park** (Ch. 18.40): Light industrial, office, research/development uses. Manufacturing and warehouse uses generally allowed; some uses require Use Permit (U.P.).
  - **I — Industrial**: General industrial uses permitted.
  - **GI — General Industrial**: Broader industrial uses; many operations allowed with or without U.P. per Ch. 18.120.
  - Also: **AV — Airport** combining district (near Napa County Airport); **:FR — Fire Risk** and **:GR — Geologic Risk** combining districts trigger additional EHS review.
- **Use Permit (CUP) process:** Chapter 18.124 governs Use Permits. Hearing body is the Planning Commission or Zoning Administrator depending on project scale. Chapter 18.120 lists uses allowed with and without a U.P. in each district. Administrative Permits available for lower-impact uses (§ 18.120 also).
- **PBES contact:** Conservation, Development and Planning Department, 1195 Third Street, Napa, CA 94559. Pre-application conferences available (DEH referral fee $197–$318 for PLN pre-app meeting/site visit).

## Notes / GOTCHAs

- GOTCHA (single countywide CUPA): DEH under PBES is the sole CUPA for all of Napa County including all five incorporated jurisdictions. No city runs its own CUPA.
- GOTCHA (fire AHJ is fragmented): City of Napa FD, St. Helena FD, Calistoga FD, and American Canyon FPD each operate independently of the county CAL FIRE contract. Do not assume "Napa County Fire" is the AHJ inside city limits.
- GOTCHA (NapaSan is a separate district): Industrial wastewater is regulated by Napa Sanitation District, not by the County. NapaSan's service area does not cover American Canyon or most rural/unincorporated areas.
- GOTCHA (2025 CFC adoption): As of January 1, 2026, the 2025 CFC applies to new applications in unincorporated Napa County. Prior applications filed before year-end 2025 were reviewed under the 2022 CFC. Confirm current edition with Fire Marshal before quoting.
- GOTCHA (winery-specific EHS): Anhydrous ammonia (refrigeration) is the dominant CalARP substance (~9 facilities); winery wastewater discharge to NapaSan requires specialized Winery/Brewery permit with weekly BOD/TSS/pH sampling during crush season.
