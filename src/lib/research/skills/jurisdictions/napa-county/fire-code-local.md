## AHJ and Scope

**AHJ (unincorporated county + Town of Yountville):** CAL FIRE / Napa County Fire Department (CAL FIRE Sonoma-Lake-Napa Unit). The county contracts with CAL FIRE for fire protection and Fire Marshal services in unincorporated areas and Yountville. Incorporated cities have separate AHJs: City of Napa Fire Department, St. Helena Fire Department, Calistoga Fire Department, American Canyon Fire Protection District.

Fire Marshal's Office: 951 California Blvd, Napa, CA 94559 · 707-299-1464
Plan submittal: minimum 3 sets to PBES Building Division, 1195 Third Street, Napa, CA 94559 (forwarded to Fire Marshal)

## Adopted CFC Edition

- **Currently adopted (new applications on/after January 1, 2026):** 2025 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9), which is based on the International Fire Code 2024 (IFC 2024). ## Confirmed Local Amendments to Chapter 15.32

### § 15.32.250 — Table B105.2 Added: Minimum Fire Flow
Section 15.32.250 adds Table B105.2 establishing site-specific minimum fire flow requirements for rural/unincorporated areas. These are local standards beyond the base CFC Appendix B requirements.

### § 15.32.260 — Table B105.3 Added: Minimum Fire Flow
Section 15.32.260 adds Table B105.3 (additional minimum fire flow table). Confirm current values with Fire Marshal's Office before relying on specific GPM figures; the tables are calibrated to local hydrant availability and rural conditions.

### Development Guidelines Local Standards (CAL FIRE/NCFD, rev. Sept. 2016)
The CAL FIRE/Napa County Fire Marshal Development Guidelines (current revision September 2016) establish the following local requirements for unincorporated areas:
- **Residential sprinklers:** Required in all new residential occupancies since January 2008; designed to NFPA 13D, 2013 ed.
- **Water storage (residential, <6,000 sq ft):** Minimum 2,500 gallons dedicated to fire protection, plus separate sprinkler water storage.
- **Fire hydrant placement:** No closer than 30 ft and no farther than 150 ft from structure; centered in a turnout area.
- **Minimum water delivery (pressurized system):** 200 GPM at 20 PSI, certified by a registered engineer.
- **Pipe size:** Minimum 4 inches, C-900 or ductile iron.
- **Bridge certification:** Must carry 75,000 lbs; weight limit posted on all-weather sign.
- **Driveway vertical clearance:** Minimum 15 feet throughout.
- **Turnaround:** Within 50 feet of building, sized for large fire apparatus.
- **Defensible space:** Minimum 100 feet from structures (per PRC 4291); minimum 10 feet each side of driveway.
- **Address posting:** 4-inch reflective numbers at public roadway.

### WUI/Wildland Standards
Structures in fire hazard severity zones must comply with Chapter 7A ignition-resistant construction (enforced by Building Department). CalFire/NCFD enforces PRC 4290/4291 and CFC Section 4907 for new SRA construction:
- Parcels ≥ 1 acre: minimum 30-foot setback from all property lines/road center.
- Parcels < 1 acre: equivalent practical effect required.

## Adopted NFPA Standards (per Development Guidelines, 2016)
- NFPA 13D / 13R, 2013 edition
- NFPA 1142-2012 (Water Supplies, Suburban and Rural Fire Fighting)
- NFPA 22, 2013 edition (Water Tanks for Private Fire Protection)

Confirm current adopted editions with Fire Marshal's Office; the 2025 CFC references updated NFPA editions.
