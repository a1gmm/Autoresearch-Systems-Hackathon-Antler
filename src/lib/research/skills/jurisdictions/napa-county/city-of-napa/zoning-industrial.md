## Industrial Zone Districts

The City of Napa has two industrial zoning districts under Chapter 17.14:

### IP — Industrial Park District

**General Plan Implementation:** Implements the "Corporate Park" General Plan land use category.  
**Primary Location:** Applies to the **Napa Valley Corporate Park** and related campus-style development areas.  
**Character:** Campus-like setting with design requirements; intensive industrial uses permitted in certain sub-areas subject to special design criteria adopted for the Corporate Park.

**Minimum Lot Area:** 10,000 sq ft (from zoning ordinance consistency table)  
**Height Limit:** 40 ft  
**Floor Area Ratio:** See General Plan (non-residential max 0.7; up to 1.0 with discretionary review and approval)

**Areas A and B (IP District Internal Sub-Zones):**
- Area A & B: Professional/business offices, R&D, certain light industrial uses in enclosed buildings; limited hotel accommodations; retail and service uses (restaurants, convenience markets, banks, service stations) primarily serving area workers/businesses.

**Permitted Uses (IP) — General:**
- Manufacturing, warehousing, research and development, office
- Public and quasi-public uses
- Intensive industrial uses in designated sub-areas subject to Corporate Park design criteria
- Convenience retail and service uses (for area businesses)

### IL — Light Industrial District

**General Plan Implementation:** Implements the "Light Industrial" General Plan land use category.  
**Character:** Small-scale manufacturing and industrial service uses; operations must be compatible with adjacent uses and not create nuisances beyond the parcel.

**Minimum Lot Area:** 10,000 sq ft  
**Height Limit:** 40 ft  
**Floor Area Ratio:** See General Plan (non-residential max 0.7; up to 1.0 with discretionary review and approval)

**Permitted Uses (IL) — General:**
- Small-scale manufacturing, fabrication, packaging
- Storage and warehousing
- Equipment repair
- Wholesale establishments
- Construction and maintenance yards
- Trade and technical training facilities
- Utility plants
- Recycling centers and similar facilities

## EHS Performance Standards (NMC § 17.14)

Industrial development in IP and IL districts must not cause:

1. **Air quality:** Noxious, toxic, corrosive, or polluting fumes or gases (as determined by air quality authorities — BAAQMD or SJVAPCD)
2. **Wastewater:** Sewage or liquid wastes difficult to treat (as determined by NapaSan)
3. **Fire/explosion:** Unacceptable fire or explosion hazard (as determined by Napa Fire Department)

From General Plan Policy LUCD 12-4: "Ensure that industrial development is designed and operated to minimize — to the extent feasible — noise, heat, glare, dust, unscreened storage yards, air emissions, hazardous materials generation, and other negative effects."  
Related code sections: § 17.52.170 (Fences, Walls and Hedges), § 17.52.310 (Noise Standards).

## Design Review

Per Chapter 17.14 and Chapter 17.62, the following trigger design review in industrial districts:
- New nonresidential structures
- Additions
- Exterior remodels
- Lot subdivisions

## CUP (Conditional Use Permit / Use Permit) Process

Conditional uses in industrial districts require a **Use Permit** per **Chapter 17.60 – Use Permits** (confirmed chapter from eCode360 search results).

Uses identified as "conditional" in the district's land use regulation table require a use permit because they may be suitable only in specific locations, or require special consideration in design, operation, or layout to ensure compatibility with surrounding uses.

**Approving body:** Not confirmed from fetched content; contact Planning Division at 707-257-9530 to confirm whether the Planning Commission or Zoning Administrator handles industrial CUPs.  
**CUP Portal:** Contact Planning Division directly — no online submission portal identified.

## Parking Standards Note

Per General Plan Consistency Analysis (Attachment A, January 2025): parking standards for industrial districts (Chapter 17.54) need updating to foster maker spaces, R&D, and startup spaces. Verify current parking standards against Ch. 17.54 for industrial proposals.

## GOTCHA

Napa County Zoning (Title 18 at Municode) applies only to **unincorporated** county areas. For facilities within City of Napa limits, use NMC Title 17. The County has its own "IP Industrial Park" district (Napa County Code Chapter 18.40) which is entirely separate from the City's IP district.
