## Adopted Fire Code Edition

The City of Napa has adopted the **2022 California Fire Code** (Part 9 of California Code of Regulations Title 24, based on the 2021 International Fire Code published by the International Code Council). The adopting ordinance is **Ordinance O2022-013**, effective **November 15, 2022**, which amended Napa Municipal Code Chapter 15.04, including Section 15.04.080 (Amendments to California Fire Code).

Prior edition: **2019 California Fire Code** was adopted by **Ordinance O2019-007** (second reading December 3, 2019, effective January 1, 2020), amending NMC Chapter 15.04.

## Local Amendments to the California Fire Code (NMC § 15.04.080)

The following local amendments are confirmed from Ordinance O2019-007 (Exhibit A) and carried forward into O2022-013:

### Administrative
- **§ 1.11.2.1.1 (Enforcement):** City Council delegates fire/panic safety enforcement to the Fire Code Official. [O2019-007, item A]
- **§ 103.2 (Appointment):** Deleted; City Manager designates Fire Code Official. [item B]
- **§ 105.4.4.1 (Phased Approval):** Deleted. [item C]
- **§ 109.1 (Board of Appeals):** Deleted; appeals governed by NMC § 15.04.100. [item D]
- **§§ 110.3.1, 110.3.2, 110.3.3 (Prosecution of Violations):** Deleted; enforcement under NMC Ch. 1.16 and § 15.04.020. [item E]
- **§ 112.4 (Failure to Comply):** Deleted; enforcement under NMC Ch. 1.16 and § 15.04.020. [item F]

### Open Burning
- **§ 307.1 (General):** Open burning requires a permit from the Fire Code Official and must be for agricultural operations. [item G]
- **§ 307.4.1 (Bonfires):** Bonfires are **prohibited** within the City of Napa. [item H]
- **§ 307.4.2 (Recreational fires):** Recreational fires are **prohibited** within the City of Napa. [item I]

### Notification and Access
- **§ 401.3.3 (Delayed Notification):** Alarm companies must notify fire department dispatch **first** before contacting subscriber upon alarm signal. [item J]
- **§ 501.3 (Construction Documents):** Fire apparatus access, hydraulic calcs, and all fire protection system plans must be submitted and approved **prior to building permit issuance**. [item K]
- **§ 503.6 (Security Gates):** Permit required before installing any security gate on a fire access roadway. [item L]

### Water Supply
- **§ 507.2 (Type of Water Supply):** Fire flow must be from reservoirs, pressure tanks, elevated tanks, or water mains connected to the **municipal water system**. Private water systems may not be used for required fire flow. [item R]

### Incinerators and Hoods
- **§ 603.8 (Incinerators):** Commercial, industrial, and residential-type incinerators and chimneys are **prohibited** within the City of Napa. [item S]
- **§ 607.2 (Type I Hoods — Where Required):** Type I hood required at or above **all commercial cooking appliances and domestic appliances used for commercial purposes** that produce grease-laden vapors. Cooking in assembly occupancies and congregate residences is treated as commercial. [item T]
  - Local amendment adds: Type I hood systems must have horn strobe devices for notification and must connect to fire alarm systems when applicable.

### Automatic Sprinklers (§ 903.2 local expansion)
- **§ 903.2 (Where Required):** Automatic sprinkler systems required in new and existing buildings per expanded local scope. [items V–EE]
- **Existing commercial buildings:** Sprinkler system required when addition exceeds **50% of existing floor area** or when an additional story is added. [item O2019-007 recital C rationale]
- **New construction:** All newly constructed buildings or structures must have an automatic sprinkler system installed. [§ 903.2.2]
- **Manufactured homes:** Fire sprinkler systems required in new manufactured homes.
- **Residential (R-3):** Automatic residential fire sprinkler systems required in one- and two-family dwellings (new construction). Not required in existing dwellings except addition > 50% of floor area.

### Spare Sprinkler Cabinet (§ 903.3.1.5 — added)
- Spare sprinkler head cabinet with matching spare heads, sprinkler wrench, and weather/corrosion-resistant construction required on premises.

### Construction Documents (§ 901.2)
- Fire Code Official has authority to require construction documents and calculations for all fire protection systems; permits required for installation, rehabilitation, or modification. [item U]

## Key Local Justifications for Amendments

Per Ordinance O2019-007 Findings A–E (carried forward in O2022-013):
- High temperatures, low humidity, and seasonal high winds increase fire spread risk.
- Semi-arid Mediterranean climate with rapid ignition risk from all fuels.
- Three active fault zones (San Andreas, Hayward, Calaveras, Healdsburg-Rogers Creek) create simultaneous multi-fire and hazmat risk after seismic events.
- Traffic congestion and Napa River / SR-29 geographic barriers impede fire response.
- Steep hillside development on east and west flanks extends response times.

- Fire flow requests
- Fire protection system plan check + inspection
- Solar plan submittal
- High-pile storage questionnaire
- Security gate permits (fire access roadways)
- Open burning (agricultural use only; bonfires and recreational fires prohibited)
