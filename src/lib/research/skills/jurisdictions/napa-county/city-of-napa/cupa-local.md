The City of Napa is **NOT its own CUPA**. Napa County DEH is the single countywide CUPA, certified by CalEPA, serving the City of Napa and all other cities within Napa County. There is no separate city-level CUPA ordinance.

## Governing Authority

DEH operates under the California Health and Safety Code Chapter 6.95 et seq. (Hazardous Materials Business Plan program) and Chapter 6.5 et seq. (Hazardous Waste Control Law), implemented countywide. The county-level CUPA programs are codified in **Napa County Code** (not City of Napa Municipal Code) — the relevant county chapters include environmental health provisions administered by PBES (Planning, Building and Environmental Services).

HMBP filing is through **California CERS** (statewide portal): cers.calepa.ca.gov.

## Reporting Thresholds

A Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) is required when a facility stores at any time:
- ≥ **55 gallons** of liquid hazardous material
- ≥ **500 pounds** of solid hazardous material
- ≥ **200 cubic feet** of compressed gas hazardous material

## CUPA Portal

File HMBP, hazardous waste, UST, and CalARP records through **California CERS**: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov

## Delinquency and Late Fees

Per Sec. 110.020: any fee not paid by the delinquency date is increased by **25%**. Failure to file an initial operating permit (HMBP, UST operating permit) within 30 days of commencing operations or change of ownership may result in **double fees** or Administrative Enforcement Orders.
