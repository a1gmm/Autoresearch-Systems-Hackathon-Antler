## Pretreatment Program

NapaSan operates a **federally-approved pretreatment program** under 40 C.F.R. Part 403. NapaSan has authority over all Industrial Users (IUs) contributing wastewater to the POTW, including the authority to issue control mechanisms, conduct compliance monitoring, and take enforcement action (NSD Code § 4.04.030).

### Who Needs a Permit

Any **Significant Industrial User (SIU)** and any other IU deemed necessary by NapaSan must be issued a permit (§ 4.04.040). Any sewer user discharging **non-domestic wastes** to the sewer system must obtain an Industrial User Discharge Permit from NapaSan Engineering.

### Permit Application Process

1. Download Industrial User Discharge Permit Application from napasan.com, or contact Engineering at 707-258-6000.
2. Submit completed application to NapaSan Engineering Department.
3. NapaSan staff conducts facility inspection.
4. Permit issued with effluent limits, BMPs, monitoring/reporting conditions, compliance schedule.
5. Permit duration: maximum 5 years (§ 4.04.040(A)).
6. Renewal: Submit complete reissuance application minimum 90 days before expiration (§ 4.04.040(I)).

### Permit Minimum Elements (40 CFR 403.8 / § 4.04.040)

- Duration statement (≤ 5 years)
- Non-transferability clause (prior notification required on ownership change)
- Effluent limits based on general pretreatment standards, categorical standards, local limits, and State/local law
- Best Management Practices (BMPs)
- Self-monitoring, sampling, reporting, notification, and recordkeeping requirements (pollutants to monitor, sample location, frequency, type)
- Civil and criminal penalty statement
- Slug discharge controls (if determined necessary)
- Monitoring waiver terms if granted

### Special Requirements by User Type

**Wineries and Breweries:**
- Install flow meter
- Conduct weekly sampling for BOD, TSS, and pH

**Categorical Industrial Users (CIUs):**
- Must implement pretreatment programs meeting categorical standards in applicable 40 CFR parts
- Submit Baseline Monitoring Reports, Final Compliance Reports, Periodic Compliance Reports, and Monitoring/Analysis Reports per § 4.04.190

**Hauled Waste / FOG:**
- Fats, oil and grease from food service establishments may be accepted at NapaSan's FOG receiving station by permitted waste haulers
- Hauled waste pH must be 6.0–9.0 (§ 4.04.130)
- Oil & grease concentration limit: none for polar only (FOG from food service via permitted hauler only)

## Local Prohibitions (§ 4.04.110(G))

- No stormwater, groundwater, rainwater leaders/downspouts, or uncontaminated water to sanitary sewer (§ G.1)
- Groundwater/surface cleanup (LUST, monitoring wells, surface cleaning) requires a temporary discharge permit from NapaSan (§ G.2)
- No chlorinated hydrocarbon, organophosphate, or similar pesticide/herbicide/insecticide in any concentration (§ G.9)
- No non-degradable wastes (§ G.8)
- No food/beverage preparation grease to sewer except via permitted FOG hauler to FOG receiving station

## General Prohibitions (§ 4.04.110(A) — 40 CFR 403.5)

- pH lower than 6.0 or greater than 9.0
- Temperature causing POTW influent to exceed 40°C (104°F) at treatment plant
- Petroleum oil, non-biodegradable cutting oil, or mineral-origin products in amounts causing interference or pass-through
- Any pollutant (including BOD) released at flow rate/concentration causing interference with POTW
- Toxic gases, vapors, or fumes in quantities causing acute health hazard to personnel
