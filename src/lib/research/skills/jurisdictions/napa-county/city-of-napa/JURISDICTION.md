## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **Agency:** Napa County Division of Environmental Health (DEH) — the single countywide CUPA. The City of Napa is NOT its own CUPA; DEH serves all cities within the county.
- **Thresholds:** ≥ 55 gal liquid / ≥ 500 lb solid / ≥ 200 cu ft compressed gas triggers HMBP filing.
- **Regulated programs:** Hazardous Waste Generators (~400 facilities, triennial inspections); Underground Storage Tanks (~44 facilities, annual inspections); Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act; Hazardous Materials Business Plans (~1,250 county-wide facilities, triennial); CalARP (~9 facilities, triennial); Stormwater Management (~500 facilities, triennial).
- **Contact:** 1195 Third Street, Suite 210, Napa CA 94559 | 707-253-4417 | Coordinator: W. Douglas Calhoun
- **Fees:** DEH fee schedule published in Fee Resolution Part 110 – Environmental Health (adopted by Board of Supervisors, last updated November 5, 2024, effective July 7, 2025); pay by phone/check to PBES — no online payment for hazmat invoices.
- **HMBP portal:** California CERS (statewide) — file at cers.calepa.ca.gov.
Source: https://www.napacounty.gov/1919/CUPA | https://www.napacounty.gov/1726/Fees-Payments

### 2. Fire AHJ — City of Napa Fire Department

- **AHJ:** City of Napa Fire Department (established 1906). The City of Napa FD is the only Napa-County fire agency NOT dispatched by the CAL FIRE ECC in St. Helena; it runs its own dispatch.
- **Fire Prevention Division:** 1600 First Street, Napa CA 94559 | 707-257-9590
- **Adopted code:** 2022 California Fire Code (Part 9 of Title 24, based on the 2021 International Fire Code), adopted via **Ordinance O2022-013** (effective November 15, 2022), codified in Napa Municipal Code Chapter 15.08 (fire-related provisions within Title 15, Buildings and Construction).
- **Local amendments:** City of Napa Code Amendments (2019 base document updated by O2022-013); violations subject to enforcement under NMC Chapter 1.16 and Section 15.04.020.
- **Fire permit types:** Temporary tent permits; bonfire/recreational fire/incinerator permits; fire flow requests; fire protection system plan check + inspection; solar plan submittal; high-pile storage questionnaire; security gate permits for fire access roadways.
- **Fee schedule:** Fees set by resolution under NMC Chapter 3.04 — see City of Napa Master Fee Schedule (available from Fire Prevention Division or cityofnapa.org).
- **Design standards:** Fire apparatus turn specs, fire lane sign placement, addressing standards, underground piping standards — published in Fire Department Standards and Specifications document.
Source: https://www.cityofnapa.org/665/Prevention | https://www.cityofnapa.org/QuickLinks.aspx?CID=76

### 3. POTW / Industrial Wastewater — Napa Sanitation District (NapaSan)

- **POTW:** Napa Sanitation District (NapaSan) — independent special district, not a city department. Service area covers City of Napa and surrounding areas.
- **Governing code:** NapaSan District Code **Title 4 – Sewer Use** (§§ 4.01 General Provisions, 4.02 Use of Public Sewers Required, 4.03 Private Sewage Disposal, 4.04 Sewer Use Regulations). Full code at napasan.com/DocumentCenter/View/1575.
- **Pretreatment authority:** NapaSan operates a federally-approved pretreatment program under 40 C.F.R. § 403 et seq. Industrial Users discharging non-domestic waste must obtain an **Industrial User Discharge Permit** from NapaSan Engineering.
- **IWP process:** Download application from napasan.com → submit to Engineering Department → NapaSan staff inspection → permit issuance + monitoring/reporting conditions. Wineries/breweries: flow meter + weekly sampling for BOD, TSS, pH required.
- **Contact:** 1515 Soscol Ferry Road, Napa CA 94558 | 707-258-6000 | info@napasan.com | M–F 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m.
- **Rates/fees:** See NapaSan Rates & Fees page (napasan.com).
Source: https://napasan.com/161/Discharge-Permits | https://napasan.com/171/Napa-Sanitation-District-Code

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater

- **MS4 permit:** City of Napa operates under California Phase II MS4 General Permit **Order No. 2013-001 DWQ** (effective July 1, 2013) under the Federal Clean Water Act § 402(p)(3) and Porter-Cologne Water Quality Control Act.
- **Local ordinance:** City of Napa Storm Water Quality Control Ordinance, codified in the Napa Municipal Code (Title 13 or separate chapter — [VERIFY exact NMC chapter number]). Ordinance implements NPDES permit requirements; prohibits illicit discharges to the MS4.
- **Construction trigger:** Projects disturbing ≥ 1 acre must comply with the State Construction General Permit and implement erosion/sediment BMPs. Post-construction: BASMAA Post-Construction Manual (January 2019).
- **Industrial stormwater:** Facilities with process water or exposed materials may need a State Industrial General Permit (IGP) separate from the city MS4 permit — contact Regional Water Board.
- **Illicit discharge hotline:** 707-257-9600
- **Contacts:** Stormwater Program Coordinator – Stephanie Emmons; Senior Civil Engineer – Tim Wood
Source: https://www.cityofnapa.org/574/Stormwater-Quality

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts & CUP Process

- **Code:** City of Napa Municipal Code **Title 17 – Zoning**, Chapter 17.14 (Industrial Districts). City zoning is separate from Napa County's Title 18 (county unincorporated only).
- **Industrial districts:**
  - **IP (Industrial Park):** Implements the "Corporate Park" General Plan category; applies to Napa Valley Corporate Park; allows manufacturing, warehousing, R&D, office, public/quasi-public uses in a campus setting.
  - **IL (Light Industrial):** Implements "Light Industrial" GP category; allows small-scale manufacturing, fabrication, packaging, storage, equipment repair, wholesale, construction/maintenance yards, trade/technical training, utility plants, recycling centers.
- **EHS performance standards (§ 17.14):** Development must not cause: noxious/toxic/corrosive/polluting fumes or gases (as determined by air quality authorities); sewage difficult to treat (as determined by NapaSan); or unacceptable fire/explosion hazard (as determined by Napa Fire Department).
- **Design review:** New nonresidential structures, additions, exterior remodels, or lot subdivisions in industrial districts require design review per Chapter 17.62.
- **CUP process:** Conditional Use Permit procedures — [VERIFY - chapter 17.52 or 17.60 — not confirmed online]; contact Planning Division 707-257-9530.

## Notes / GOTCHAs

- CUPA is county-administered (DEH), not city — use Napa County DEH, not a city office, for HMBP/hazwaste/UST/CalARP filings.
- Fire AHJ is the City of Napa FD, not CAL FIRE or the county Fire Marshal (who covers unincorporated areas only).
- Wastewater/pretreatment goes to NapaSan (independent district), not a city department.
- Stormwater MS4 is city-operated; industrial facilities may also need a separate State IGP from the San Francisco Bay RWQCB (Region 2).
- NMC Title 17 is the City of Napa zoning code; NMC Title 18 (Municode) is Napa County unincorporated — do not confuse.
