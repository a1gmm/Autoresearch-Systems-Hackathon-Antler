## Agency

**Napa County Division of Environmental Health (DEH)** — the single countywide Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all of Napa County, covering all five incorporated cities (Napa, American Canyon, St. Helena, Calistoga, Yountville) and all unincorporated areas. No city operates its own CUPA; there are no Participating Agencies.

DEH is a division of Planning, Building & Environmental Services (PBES).

**Address:** 1195 Third Street, Suite 210, Napa, CA 94559
**Phone:** 707-253-4417 · **Fax:** 707-253-4545
**Enabling state law:** Cal. HSC Division 20, Chapters 6.5, 6.7, 6.67, 6.95; CCR Titles 19, 22, 23

## Programs Administered

DEH administers all six unified program elements:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — ~1,250 facilities, triennial inspections; threshold: 55 gal liquid, 500 lb solid, 200 cu ft compressed gas at any time
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator / Tiered Permitting** — ~400 facilities (RCRA and non-RCRA generators)
3. **Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program** — 44 facilities; includes plan check, installation inspection, closure
4. **Above-Ground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — Tier 1 and Tier 2 annual permits; conditionally exempt farms (>6,000 gal agricultural)
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — ~9 facilities; most common EHS substance is anhydrous ammonia (winery refrigeration); program covers both above-federal-quantity and above-CA-quantity facilities
6. **Stormwater Inspection** — CUPA-coordinated under Ch. 16.28; annual permits required for HMBP facilities and IGP/SWPPP facilities

Additional programs: Methamphetamine-Contaminated Property Cleanup; Remediation Oversight of Contaminated Properties; Abandoned Vehicle Abatement.

## Local Municipal Code

- **Stormwater:** Napa County Code **Chapter 16.28** (Stormwater Management and Discharge Control) — CUPA enforces the industrial/hazmat stormwater component
- **Abandoned Vehicle Abatement:** Napa County Code **Chapter 8.20**
- **Groundwater Conservation fees:** Napa County Code **Chapter 13.15**
- No separate county hazmat ordinance chapter beyond the state-enabling HSC framework; CUPA authority flows directly from HSC Ch. 6.95 and CCR Title 19

## Application / Portal
No online e-permit portal confirmed. Applications submitted in person or by mail to DEH at 1195 Third Street, Suite 210, Napa, CA 94559. Inspections are triennial for most HMBP facilities.

## GOTCHA
- DEH is the CUPA for the entire county including incorporated cities; do not seek a separate city-level HMBP.
- Anhydrous ammonia (winery refrigeration) is the dominant CalARP substance; ~9 facilities in the county.
- HMBP threshold quantities are statutory: 55 gal liquid / 500 lb solid / 200 cu ft compressed gas at any time.
