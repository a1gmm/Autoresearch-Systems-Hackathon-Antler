## Zoning Code Authority

**AMC Title 19 — ZONING**

Industrial districts are governed by:
- **Chapter 19.14** — INDUSTRIAL DISTRICTS (district purposes, permitted uses, development standards)
- **Chapter 19.20** — PAOLI LIGHT INDUSTRIAL COMMERCIAL OVERLAY DISTRICT (highway-fronting commercial overlay on PLI base zone)

Amended through at least Ord. 2024-01.

---

## Industrial Zone Districts (Three Districts)

### PLI — Paoli Light Industrial

**Purpose:** Research & development, laboratory, and limited industry; designed for lower-intensity industrial uses in the Paoli business park area; coexists with the Chapter 19.20 commercial overlay along Highway 29 / Newell Drive frontage.

**Chapter 19.20 Overlay Purpose:** "Accommodate commercial and commercially related uses that capitalize on vehicle access and visibility from Highway 29 and Newell Drive and serve industrial uses in the City's Industrial Parks." Overlay permits retail, personal services, restaurants, and similar uses that are not part of the base PLI entitlement.

### LI — Light Industrial

**Purpose:** Laboratory, limited manufacturing, R&D, and select commercial support uses; lower-impact industrial operations; does not permit general industry as-of-right.

### GI — General Industrial

**Purpose:** Broadest industrial district; permits general industry as-of-right and is the only district where hazardous use is conditionally permitted. Allows vehicle/equipment repair, trucking terminals, and mineral extraction (all as CUPs).

## Permitted and Conditional Uses Table (AMC §19.14.050)

**Legend:** P = Permitted (by right); C = Conditional Use Permit required; — = Not permitted

| Use | PLI | LI | GI |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Research & Development** | P | P | P |
| **Laboratory** | P | P | P |
| **Industry, limited** | P | P | P |
| **Industry, general** | C | — | P |
| **Hazardous use** | — | — | C |
| **Storage tank(s)** | C | C | C |
| **Mineral extraction** | — | — | C |
| **Recycling center** | — | — | C |
| **Vehicle/equipment repair** | — | — | P |
| **Trucking terminal** | C | — | P |
| **Wholesaling/distribution** | C/P | C/P | P |

EHS-critical observations:
- **Hazardous use** is only conditionally permitted in GI; not permitted in PLI or LI.
- **Storage tanks** require a CUP in all three industrial districts.
- **General industry** (which may include processes requiring hazmat handling) is a CUP in PLI and not permitted in LI; it is permitted by-right only in GI.

## Chapter 19.20 Overlay — Allowable Uses (AMC §19.20.020)

The Paoli Light Industrial Commercial Overlay District adds the following uses to the PLI base zone for parcels with Highway 29 or Newell Drive frontage:

### Permitted (P) under Chapter 19.20

- Charitable uses / public information center / minor utilities
- Ambulance services
- Banks and savings & loans (with walk-up service)
- Bookstore, drugstore, food sales
- Catering, take-out restaurant, restaurant (full service)
- Lodging services
- Maintenance and repair services
- Personal services, retail sales
- Zero emission vehicle service station

### Conditional (C) under Chapter 19.20

- Automobile rental
- Indoor entertainment
- On-premises liquor consumption / tasting room
- Personal improvement services
- Vehicle and equipment sales and service

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

- **Approval authority:** Planning Commission
- **Trigger:** Any use marked "C" in AMC §19.14.050 or §19.20.020
- **Code reference:** AMC §19.14.050 (use permit required for all "C" uses)
- **EHS-specific CUP triggers:**
  - Storage tanks (PLI, LI, GI)
  - Hazardous use (GI only)
  - General industry requiring hazardous materials handling (C in PLI; by-right in GI, but hazardous use within GI still requires CUP)
  - Trucking terminal (PLI)
  - Recycling center (GI)
  - Mineral extraction (GI)

---

## Notes / Gotchas

- A business operating in GI with "general industry" permit (P) that also qualifies as a "hazardous use" still requires a separate CUP for the hazardous use component.
- The Chapter 19.20 overlay does not add any industrial or hazmat-related uses — it exclusively adds commercial/service uses for highway-visible parcels.
- CUPA/HMBP permits (hazardous materials business plans, UST, etc.) are administered by **Napa County DEH**, not by the City's planning department; zoning CUP and CUPA are separate parallel tracks.
