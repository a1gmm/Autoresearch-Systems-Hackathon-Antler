## Governing Bodies

Two separate ordinances cover fire code for American Canyon:

1. **American Canyon Fire Protection District (ACFPD)** — the AHJ for operational fire permits and inspections; adopts and enforces the California Fire Code via its own board ordinance.
2. **City of American Canyon** — adopts the California Building Standards Code (Title 24) including fire-protection-related building provisions; City Ord. 2022-05 governs Chapter 16 of the AMC.

---

## Operative Fire Code Edition

**2022 California Fire Code (CFC)**, California Code of Regulations Title 24, Part 9, adopting by reference the 2021 International Fire Code (IFC), with locally-amended appendices (Chapters 4, A, B, BB, C, CC, E, F, G, H, and K).

## Local Amendments (Findings of Local Necessity)

ACFPD 2022-01 cites three local conditions justifying amendments stricter than the state baseline:

### Climatic
- High temperatures, low humidity, and seasonal high winds create extreme wildland fire conditions. Limited resources may not control fires in unprotected structures during such events.

### Geologic
- District lies near active fault zones: San Andreas, Hayward, Calaveras, and Healdsburg-Rogers Creek; within Napa County: Cordelia, Green Valley, and West Napa faults. Post-earthquake multiple simultaneous fires would overwhelm resources; built-in protection compensates.

### Topographic
- State Highway 29 divides the city into three geographic parts. East and west hillsides with residential development create natural and man-made barriers that delay response times.

### Resulting Local Amendments

| Subject | Local Amendment |
|---|---|
| Automatic sprinklers | All buildings shall have an approved fully automatic fire sprinkler system (with limited exceptions for detached structures under 1,000 sq ft and specific occupancy types) |
| Roof coverings | Minimum **Class A** roof covering required for all construction types |
| Spark arrestors | Required on fireplace chimneys during all reroofing projects |
| High-piled storage | Governed by 2022 CFC Chapter 32; no locally-modified thresholds found published |
| Hazmat operational permits | Governed by 2022 CFC; no locally-published modified threshold quantities found — contact ACFPD Fire Prevention Bureau |

## Fire Capital Facilities Fee (New Construction)

- **Fee:** $0.98 per square foot of gross floor area (all development types: residential, commercial, industrial)
- **Established by:** City Ord. 2023-02, effective March 21, 2023
- **Annual adjustment:** CPI for San Francisco / Oakland / San Jose area, applied July 1 each year
- **Collection:** City collects and remits to ACFPD; due prior to building permit issuance
- **Exemptions:** Replacement structures at or below original square footage; structures where a prior fire mitigation fee was paid under District Resolution 86-5
