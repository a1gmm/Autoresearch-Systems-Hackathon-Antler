## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **CUPA agency:** Napa County Division of Environmental Health (DEH) — the single countywide CUPA; American Canyon is **not** its own CUPA.
- **Address / phone:** 1195 Third Street, Suite 210, Napa, CA 94559 · 707-253-4417 · Fax 707-253-4545
- **Programs administered (six unified-program elements):** Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP, ~1,250 facilities countywide), Hazardous Waste Generators (~400 facilities), Underground Storage Tanks (UST, ~44 facilities), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), CalARP (9 facilities), area emergency planning.
- **Online portal / fee schedule:** not published as a direct link on the CUPA page — contact DEH at the number above.

### 2. Fire AHJ — American Canyon Fire Protection District (ACFPD)

- **AHJ:** American Canyon Fire Protection District (ACFPD), a subsidiary special district of the City of American Canyon; independent board of directors. Two stations; covers City limits plus adjoining unincorporated southern Napa County.
- **Adopted fire code:** 2022 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9 / 2021 IFC by reference), adopted by ACFPD Board **Ordinance 2022-01**, effective October 25, 2022. City of American Canyon also adopted the 2022 Title 24 codes via **City Ord. 2022-05** (enacted November 15, 2022, effective January 1, 2023) — Chapters 16.01–16.09 and 16.13 repealed and replaced.
- **Local amendments (fire):** High/very-high fire-severity-zone provisions require minimum Class A roof covering, spark arrestors, and expanded automatic fire-sprinkler requirements (§903.2 amendments).
- **City municipal code fire chapter:** AMC Chapter 8.08 — "Fire Protection District Regulations — Adopted by Reference" (Ord. 93-07 §1, 1993). [VERIFY – specific fee schedule for operational permits not found online; contact ACFPD Fire Prevention Bureau.]

### 3. POTW / Sewer Use — Industrial Wastewater Pretreatment

- **POTW operator:** City of American Canyon Public Works — Water Reclamation Facility; administers the commercial/industrial pretreatment program directly.
- **Local sewer-use ordinance:** AMC **Title 14 SEWER AND SANITATION SERVICES** — Chapter 14.04 (Wastewater Rates and Charges; amended by Ord. 2024-05, effective January 1, 2025) and **Chapter 14.16 SEWER USE REGULATIONS** (contains local limits and IWP provisions).
- **Industrial/commercial permit:** "Commercial Business Wastewater Discharge Permit" — required for non-domestic dischargers to the City sanitary sewer; Environmental Compliance staff inspect permitted facilities.
- **Local limits (AMC §14.16.220):** Selected examples — Mercury 0.00020 mg/L; Arsenic 0.2 mg/L; Cyanide 1.0 mg/L; Lead 0.45 mg/L; Copper 0.87 mg/L; Oil & grease (petroleum) 50 mg/L; pH 6.0–9.0. Bioassay: no discharge with lethal toxicity <25% concentration (96-hr static, threespine stickleback). Unlisted substances determined on a case-by-case basis.
- **Non-residential wastewater rates (Ord. 2024-05):** Monthly service charge $115.21 + $1.92/HCF volumetric (2025 rates; escalating to $173.42 + $2.89/HCF by 2029; CPI-adjusted from 2030 onward).
- **Industrial connection charge:** AMC §14.04.080 — calculated on BOD and TSS loading at time of building permit; values subject to approval by city engineer.
- **Application / portal:** https://www.americancanyon.gov/Live/Utilities-Services/Water/Commercial-Wastewater-Discharge (page returned 403 during fetch — contact Public Works directly)

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater

- **Program:** AMC **Chapter 14.28 STORMWATER AND POLLUTION DISCHARGE CONTROL PROGRAM** — implements the Phase II MS4 NPDES General Permit (Water Quality Order No. 2013-0001-DWQ, General Permit No. CAS000004) and the Napa Countywide Stormwater Pollution Prevention Program (NCSPPP).
- **Establishing ordinances:** Ord. 2003-08 §3 (2003); Ord. 2005-06 §1 (2005); Ord. 2016-02 §3 (2016).
- **Enforcement authority:** Public Works Director or designee.
- **Industrial discharger obligations:** Illicit discharges prohibited; industrial facilities must implement BMPs and maintain a Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP). Definitions follow federal Clean Water Act and EPA stormwater regulations (Nov. 16, 1991).
- **Post-construction standard:** New development must route stormwater runoff from impervious surfaces through water-quality detention/treatment basins (AMC §19.51.050 Sustainability Standards; BASMAA Post Construction Manual).
- Source (§14.28.030): https://law.cityofamericancanyon.org/us/ca/cities/american-canyon/code/14.28.030

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts & CUP Process

- **Zoning code:** AMC **Title 19 ZONING** — industrial districts governed by **Chapter 19.14 INDUSTRIAL DISTRICTS** and the **Chapter 19.20 PAOLI LIGHT INDUSTRIAL COMMERCIAL OVERLAY DISTRICT**.
- **Industrial districts (three):**
  - **PLI** — Paoli Light Industrial: R&D, lab, limited industry permitted (P); general industry, storage tanks, hazardous-use all require CUP (C).
  - **LI** — Light Industrial: laboratory, limited industry, R&D permitted (P); storage tanks, wholesale/distribution, restaurant require CUP (C).
  - **GI** — General Industrial: limited industry, R&D, lab, vehicle/equipment repair, trucking terminal permitted (P); **hazardous use** conditionally permitted (C — requires Planning Commission use permit); storage tanks (C); mineral extraction (C).
- **EHS-relevant CUP triggers:** Storage tanks (all three districts); hazardous use (GI only); general industry requiring hazardous materials handling (C in PLI/LI; P in GI with CUP for hazardous use).
- **CUP process:** Planning Commission approval required; "C" uses subject to use permit per AMC §19.14.050.

## Notes / Gotchas

- Fire service is **ACFPD** (subsidiary district of the City), not Napa County CAL FIRE and not a typical city fire department. ACFPD is the AHJ for fire permits and inspections.
- The 2022 CFC was the operative edition as of early 2026; **statewide 2025 Title 24 adoption took effect 2026-01-01** — verify with ACFPD whether their 2022-01 board ordinance has been updated to the 2025 CFC.
- CUPA filings (HMBP, hazwaste generator, UST, CalARP) go to **Napa County DEH**, not the City.
- Pretreatment/IWP permit goes to the **City's own Water Reclamation Facility** (not county); contact Public Works Environmental Services.
- MS4 compliance is under the Napa Countywide program (NCSPPP); the City implements Chapter 14.28 locally.
