## Agency

**Napa County Division of Environmental Health (DEH)** is the Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all of Napa County, including the City of American Canyon. American Canyon is **not** its own CUPA and has no separate city-level hazardous materials ordinance that modifies state CUPA thresholds.

- **Address:** 1195 Third Street, Suite 210, Napa, CA 94559
- **Phone:** 707-253-4417
- **Fax:** 707-253-4545
- **Environmental Management Coordinator:** W. Douglas Calhoun (and four named specialists as of the fetched page)
- **Website:** https://www.napacounty.gov/1919/CUPA

## Six Unified Program Elements Administered

| Element | Scale (countywide) |
|---|---|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP) | ~1,250 facilities; inspected triennially |
| Hazardous Waste Generators | ~400 permitted facilities |
| Underground Storage Tanks (UST) | ~44 facilities |
| Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) | included |
| California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) | ~9 facilities |
| Area Emergency Plan (coordination) | countywide |

Additional programs: methamphetamine contamination cleanup, stormwater CUPA permit, contaminated property remediation oversight.

---

## HMBP Threshold Quantities (State-Standard; no local modification found)

Per state law (HSC §25503.5) and as stated on the CUPA page:
- **Liquids:** ≥ 55 gallons at any time
- **Solids:** ≥ 500 pounds at any time
- **Compressed gases:** ≥ 200 cubic feet at any time

Facilities meeting or exceeding any threshold must file an HMBP.

## Delinquency / Late-Filing Penalty

Any fee not paid by the delinquency date increases **25%**. Commencing operations without a permit (including failure to file a new HMBP within 30 days of commencing operations or change of ownership) results in **double the permit fee**.
