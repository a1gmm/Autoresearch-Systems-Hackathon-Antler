## POTW Operator

**City of American Canyon Public Works — Water Reclamation Facility**

The City administers the commercial and industrial pretreatment program directly. There is no county or regional pretreatment authority — the City's own Water Reclamation Facility is the Control Authority.

- **Contact:** City of American Canyon Public Works / Environmental Compliance
- **Permit application portal:** https://www.americancanyon.gov/Live/Utilities-Services/Water/Commercial-Wastewater-Discharge (returned HTTP 403 as of fetch; contact Public Works directly)

### Other Limits (AMC §14.16.160, §14.16.100)

- **Temperature:** Discharge shall not raise sewer temperature above levels that would interfere with biological treatment (§14.16.160 — specific prohibitions — temperature).
- **Bioassay:** No discharge with lethal toxicity at <25% concentration (96-hour static bioassay, threespine stickleback).
- **Unlisted substances:** Evaluated on a case-by-case basis per §14.16.220(B).
- **VOC:** Not to exceed 10% of the lower explosive limit (LEL).

## Industrial Connection Charge (AMC §14.04.080)

Connection fees for industrial users are calculated based on BOD and TSS loading at the time of the building permit application:

**Formula:**
```
Fee = $35.11 × [flow (gpd) × (0.85 + (0.09 × BOD (mg/L) ÷ 236) + (0.6 × TSS (mg/L) ÷ 238))]
```

- **Base rate:** $35.11 per gallon per day
- BOD and TSS values require approval by the City Engineer
- If the discharger later exceeds approved flow or pollutant levels, revised charges are due within 60 days of demand
