## Critical User Definition (SHMC 13.24)

A **critical user** is any user in SIC Divisions A, B, D, E, or I that meets **any** of the following:
- Discharges ≥ 50,000 gallons per average work day, OR
- Contributes > 5% of the agency's average dry weather hydraulic or organic capacity, OR
- Discharges toxic pollutants in toxic amounts (as defined in 40 CFR 401.15 and 129.4), OR
- Has a significant impact on the wastewater collection or treatment system.

Critical users must pretreat wastewater to acceptable limits before discharge to the community sewer. Detailed plans for pretreatment facilities must be submitted to and approved by the City before construction.

**Secondary treatment requirements (SHMC 13.24 / 40 CFR Part 133):**
- Monthly average BOD ≤ 15 mg/L; weekly average ≤ 25 mg/L
- Monthly average TSS ≤ 15 mg/L; weekly average ≤ 20 mg/L
- Monthly average percent removal ≥ 85% for both BOD and TSS

**Additional discharge prohibitions (Order R2-2021-00XX):**
- Discharge to Napa River prohibited unless river:effluent flow ratio is at least **19:1**.
- **Dry-season ban (June 1–October 31):** Discharge to Napa River at Point 001 is prohibited during dry season, except when effluent exceeds recycled water system storage/distribution capacity.

## Pretreatment Permit Process

1. Identify if your process qualifies as a **critical user** (see definition above).
2. If yes, prepare pretreatment facility design plans and submit to City of St. Helena Public Works for approval before construction.
3. Apply for wastewater discharge permit with City Manager/Public Works.
4. Submit lab-certified wastewater characterization data (constituents, flow rate, discharge schedule).
5. **No online portal identified** — contact Public Works directly.
