## CUPA Structure

**City of St. Helena has NO separate municipal CUPA.** All hazardous-materials unified programs within city limits are administered countywide by:

**Napa County Division of Environmental Health (DEH)**  
CUPA designation: Napa County is the single Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all incorporated cities in the county, including St. Helena. Certification granted by CalEPA under California Health and Safety Code § 25404.

**Physical address:** 1195 Third Street, Suite 210, Napa, CA 94559  
**Phone:** 707-253-4417 | **Fax:** 707-253-4545  
**Web:** https://www.napacounty.gov/1919/CUPA

## Local Ordinance / County Code Authority

| Authority | Code Citation |
|-----------|--------------|
| Underground Storage Tanks | Napa County Code **Chapter 16.20** |
| Stormwater (CUPA-administered add-on) | Napa County Code **Chapter 16.28** |
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan (state HSC §25500 et seq.) | Implemented via DEH permit |

Note: There is no separate St. Helena municipal hazardous materials ordinance chapter beyond SHMC **17.28.080**, which requires compliance with state hazardous materials regulations and directs businesses to file annual HMBP with Napa County DEH.

**SHMC 17.28.080 (verbatim extract):** "The use, handling, storage and transportation of hazardous and extremely hazardous materials shall comply with the provisions of the California Hazardous Materials Regulations and the California Fire and Building Codes, as well as the laws and regulations of the California Department of Toxic Substances Control and the County Environmental Health Agency. A business plan shall be filed annually with the County of Napa Hazardous Material Section of the Department of Environmental Management, and a copy of the approval by the county shall be submitted to the community development director."

## Programs Administered

| Program | Countywide Scale |
|---------|-----------------|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan / Inventory Statement (HMBP) | ~1,250 facilities; triennial inspections |
| Hazardous Waste Generator | ~400 permitted facilities |
| Underground Storage Tank (UST) — Chapter 16.20 | 44 monitored facilities |
| Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) | Included in HMBP permit |
| California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) | 9 facilities countywide |
| Methamphetamine Contaminated Property Cleanup | As needed |
| Stormwater Management & Control (CUPA add-on) | ~500 facilities |

**HMBP Trigger Quantities (state thresholds applied by DEH):**
- ≥ 55 gallons of liquid hazardous material at any time, OR
- ≥ 500 lbs of solid hazardous material at any time, OR
- ≥ 200 cu ft of compressed gas at any time
