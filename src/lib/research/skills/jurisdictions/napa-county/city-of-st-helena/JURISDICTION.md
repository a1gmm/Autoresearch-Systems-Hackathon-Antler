## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **CUPA agency:** Napa County Division of Environmental Health (DEH) — the single countywide CUPA covering all incorporated cities including St. Helena. The City has no separate CUPA.
- **Address:** 1195 Third Street, Suite 210, Napa, CA 94559 | Phone: 707-253-4417
- **Programs administered (7 unified programs):** Hazardous Materials Business Plan / Inventory Statement (~1,250 facilities countywide); Hazardous Waste Generator; Underground Storage Tank (UST); Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA); California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP); Methamphetamine Contaminated Property Cleanup; Stormwater Management & Control (~500 facilities). All inspections are triennial.
- **HMBP permit fees (Napa County Fee Resolution, Sec. 110.150, effective 07/07/2025):** Annual permit for UST facility <40,000 gal — $637; Annual permit 1,000–20,000 units — $477; Annual permit >20,000 units — $403; Above-ground fuel tanks only — $403; Propane outdoor LPG <500 gal — $212; Re-inspection/consultation — flat fee.
- **Hazardous Waste Generator fees (Sec. 110.200, effective 07/07/2025):** <27 gal/mo — $239; ≥27 gal/mo — $239; Conditionally Exempt Treatment — $239; 1,000 kg RCRA/month — $318.
- **UST fees (Sec. 110.130):** Annual operating permit per tank — $477; Installation plan check (1 tank) — $914; Closure/abandonment plan check — $557.
- **APSA fees (Sec. 110.135):** Tier 1 annual permit — $185; Tier 2 annual permit — $212; Conditionally Exempt Farm — $93.
- **CalARP fees (Sec. 110.170):** Initial submittal — hourly (10-hr minimum); Permit above federal threshold — $1,590; Permit below federal / above CA — $319; Conditionally Exempt — $106.
- **Stormwater CUPA add-on fees (Sec. 110.1xx):** CUPA HazMat/Waste Storage annual permit — $159; Industrial Permit/SWPP Plan — $200.
- **Delinquency penalty:** 25% surcharge; failure to file on time — 200% of permit fee (Sec. 110.020).

### 2. Fire AHJ — Fire Code & Permits

- **AHJ:** St. Helena Fire Department (municipal; NOT served by County CAL FIRE contract — reverse contract: the County contracts WITH the City to cover adjoining unincorporated areas).
- **Fire Chief:** John Sorensen | Phone: 707-486-2882 | firechief@cityofsthelena.gov
- **Station:** 1500 Main Street, St. Helena, CA 94574 | Department line: 707-967-2880
- **Adopted fire code:** 2022 California Fire Code (CFC), adopted under St. Helena Municipal Code **Chapter 15.36** with local amendments.
- **Local amendment basis (SHMC 15.36.030):** Amendments justified by St. Helena's steep terrain with wildland-fire hazard zones; amendments affect fire apparatus access roads, residential development access, and fire protection system requirements.
- **Key local amendment (15.36.100):** Fire apparatus access road provisions. NOTE: in early 2026 the City adopted an ordinance to remove the two-road access requirement for new developments; a community referendum was organized but did not proceed to ballot.
- **Fire inspection types:** Fire and Life Safety inspections (all commercial/public buildings); bi-annual/annual business inspections (Main Street locations on alternate schedule; schools, hotels, multi-family, daycare annually); fire sprinkler/alarm/fire-line inspections; short-term rental inspections (annual).
- **Inspector contact:** Alec Vidler, 707-967-2880 (schedule inspections)
- **Permit types offered by SHFD:** Burn permits (for agricultural burns — contact Bay Area AQMD and CalFire; SHFD does not issue burn permits itself); Defensible Space Inspection & Certification; Fireworks permits; Fire Inspections; Weed Abatement.

### 3. POTW / Sewer Use & Industrial Pretreatment

- **POTW:** City of St. Helena Wastewater Treatment and Reclamation Plant, 1 Chaix/Thomann Lane, St. Helena, CA 94574.
- **NPDES permit:** No. **CA0038016**, Order No. R2-2016-XXXX (adopted March 1, 2016; expired February 28, 2021 — reissuance in progress as of 2021). Classified as a Minor POTW by US EPA and San Francisco Bay RWQCB (Region 2). Receiving water: **Napa River**. Average dry-weather design flow: 0.5 MGD.
- **Sewer use ordinance:** St. Helena Municipal Code **Chapter 13.24** (Wastewater Discharge). Sets discharge prohibitions, critical-user classification, pretreatment requirements, and permit obligations.
- **Critical user definition (SHMC 13.24):** Any user in SIC Divisions A, B, D, E, or I with discharge ≥50,000 gpd average work day, or >5% of agency's flow, or toxic pollutants in toxic amounts, or significant impact on collection/treatment system.
- **Pretreatment requirement:** Critical users must pretreat wastewater to acceptable limits before discharge; detailed plans for pretreatment facilities must be submitted to and approved by the City before construction.
- **Sewer service system:** SHMC **Chapter 13.20** (Sewer Service System). Sewer impact fees: SHMC 3.32.080.

### 4. Stormwater / MS4

- **Local ordinance:** St. Helena Municipal Code **Chapter 13.32** (Stormwater and Runoff Pollution Control). Prohibits discharge of pollutants to the storm sewer system; industrial/construction activity NPDES permit holders must prove compliance.
- **NPDES general permit reference in ordinance:** Water Quality Order No. 2013-0001-DWQ, General Permit No. CAS000004 (statewide industrial stormwater general permit).
- **Enforcement (SHMC 13.32):** Violations are misdemeanors (Sec. 13.32.140); civil penalties and special assessments authorized (Sec. 13.32.160); inspection authority in Sec. 13.32.110.
- **Administering agency:** City of St. Helena Public Works (stormwater division); Napa County DEH also runs a parallel stormwater inspection program for CUPA-covered facilities (fee: $159–$200/yr — see CUPA section above).

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts & CUP Process

- **Zoning code:** Title 17 of the St. Helena Municipal Code (comprehensive update adopted October 24, 2023; current through Ordinance 26-3, passed February 24, 2026).
- **Base zoning districts:** Established in SHMC **17.15.020**, Table 17.15.020(A). Business and industrial districts are regulated under **Chapter 17.18**.
- **Industrial / business zoning districts:** Use regulations in Table 17.18.020(A) (Business and Industrial Zoning Districts) list uses as Permitted (P), Minor Use Permit (MUP — staff-level review), or Conditional Use Permit (CUP — public hearing before Planning Commission). Identified districts include Industrial (I) and Service Commercial (SC).
- **CUP process:** Public hearing before Planning Commission required for CUP uses in industrial districts. Formula businesses in CB and MU districts require CUP if more than four are approved; in SC district up to four allowed without CUP.
- **Industrial district note:** Emergency shelters are a permitted use in the I and SC zoning districts (adopted 2015 ordinance).
- **Zoning map:** St. Helena Zoning Map (revised May 2024) — available via https://www.cityofsthelena.gov/243/Zoning-Information
- **Building Division (permit authority within city limits):** https://www.cityofsthelena.gov/245/Building-Division

## GOTCHA Notes

- **Reverse fire contract:** St. Helena runs its OWN municipal fire department. The County of Napa contracts WITH St. Helena (not the other way) to cover adjoining unincorporated areas.
- **CUPA is county-run:** No city-level CUPA; all hazmat/hazwaste permits go through Napa County DEH at 707-253-4417.
- **NPDES permit lapsed:** The WWTRP NPDES permit (CA0038016) expired February 2021; a tentative reissuance order was posted April 2021. Verify current permit status with San Francisco Bay RWQCB before quoting discharge limits.
- **2026 fire access change:** The two-road access requirement ordinance amendment (early 2026) may affect industrial/commercial development fire-permit conditions — verify current SHMC 15.36.100 text before advising on site design.
