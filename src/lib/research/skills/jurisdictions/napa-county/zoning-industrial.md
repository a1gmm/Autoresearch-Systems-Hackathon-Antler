## Industrial Zone Districts

Three industrial base districts exist in unincorporated Napa County (confirmed in the county's Summary of Allowable Uses by Zoning District and Title 18 table of contents):

### IP — Industrial Park Zoning District (Chapter 18.40)
- **Character:** Light industrial, office, research and development. Emphasis on compatibility with adjacent uses.
- **Uses allowed without a Use Permit (18.40.020):** Manufacturing, warehouse, and assembly uses generally allowed; office and R&D uses; winery support uses with restrictions; satellite earth stations (if not feasible in agricultural zones).
- **Uses allowed with a Use Permit (18.40.030):** Higher-impact manufacturing or uses generating significant noise, odor, or traffic; certain winery commercial industrial uses.
- **Performance standards:** Article II of Chapter 18.40 (§ 18.40.200 et seq.) governs nuisances (noise, odor, vibration, glare, etc.).
- **Exterior storage:** Subject to screening/enclosure requirements.
- Municode: https://library.municode.com/ca/napa_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT18ZO_CH18.40IPINPAZODI

### I — Industrial District (Chapter 18.36)
- **Character:** General industrial uses.
- **Uses allowed without a Use Permit (§ 18.36.020):** General manufacturing, warehousing, storage, service and repair operations.
- **Uses allowed with a Use Permit (§ 18.36.030):** Uses with greater off-site impacts.
- **Exterior storage (§ 18.36.040):** Exterior storage of materials and equipment is regulated; screening may be required.
- Municode: https://library.municode.com/ca/napa_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT18ZO_CH18.36IINDI_18.36.040EXST

### GI — General Industrial Zoning District (Chapter 18.44)
- **Character:** Broader industrial uses including operations with outdoor components, heavier manufacturing, contractor yards.
- **Uses allowed without a Use Permit:** Manufacturing, warehouse, outdoor storage yards, contractor storage (confirmed as a use in the GI column in the county's Summary of Allowable Uses table — "Yes w/SPA" for contractor's storage).
- **Uses allowed with a Use Permit:** Heavier or more impactful operations; certain outdoor uses require Site Plan Approval (SPA).
- **Development standards (Article I, Ch. 18.44):** Setbacks (§ 18.44.060), parking (§ 18.44.080), signs and lighting (§ 18.44.120).
- Municode: https://library.municode.com/ca/napa_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT18ZO_CH18.44GIGEINZODI

## Uses Allowed in All or Specific Industrial Districts (Chapter 18.120)

Chapter 18.120 lists uses that may be allowed with and without Use Permit approval in any or certain specified zoning districts, and uses allowed upon grant of an Administrative Permit. Satellite earth stations that cannot technically be located in an agricultural zone may be permitted in I, IP, or GI districts (per § 18.16.030(J) cross-reference).

## Combining Districts Applicable to Industrial Sites

- **:AV — Airport Combining District:** Near Napa County Airport; additional compatibility standards.
- **:FR — Fire Risk Combining District:** Triggers additional fire-hazard review; relevant to EHS projects.
- **:GR — Geologic Risk Combining District:** Additional geologic hazard review.
- **:UR — Urban Reserve:** Uses require annexation application before proceeding.

## Use Permit (CUP) Process — Chapter 18.124

**Governing chapter:** Chapter 18.124 — USE PERMITS
Source: https://library.municode.com/ca/napa_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT18ZO_CH18.124USPE

- **Hearing body:** Planning Commission or Zoning Administrator, depending on project scale.
- **Administrative Permit:** Available for lower-impact uses per Chapter 18.120.
- **Pre-application:** Pre-application conferences available through PBES; DEH referral fee for environmental health review: $318 (pre-app meeting/site visit as of 07/07/2025 fee schedule).
- **Process:** Application to PBES Planning Division → public notice → hearing → conditions of approval.

## Development Standards Reference

For development standards in IP, I, or GI districts, see:
- **Schedule of Zoning District Regulations:** § 18.104.010 consolidates standards across districts.
- Specific standards: minimum lot size, maximum lot coverage, building height, setbacks, parking (Chapter 18.104 and within each district chapter).

## GOTCHA

- Napa County Title 18 applies ONLY to unincorporated county land. Industrial parcels within City of Napa, St. Helena, Calistoga, American Canyon, or Yountville are governed by their own zoning codes.
- The county uses-by-district summary table is from 2006; confirm current allowed uses against live Municode ordinance text.
- Wineries in industrial districts still require a Use Permit for most commercial/industrial winery operations.
- The :FR (Fire Risk) and :GR (Geologic Risk) combining districts require additional EHS review beyond standard zoning — flag these when assessing industrial sites near hillsides or in fire hazard severity zones.
