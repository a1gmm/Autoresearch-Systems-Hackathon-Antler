## POTW Agency

**Napa Sanitation District (NapaSan)** — independent special district, not a county agency.
- **Treatment plant:** Soscol Water Recycling Facility, 1515 Soscol Ferry Road, Napa, CA 94558
- **Office:** 707-258-6000 · info@napasan.com · M–F 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m.
- **Engineering contact (pretreatment/discharge permits):** 707-258-6000
- **Service area:** City of Napa and portions of unincorporated Napa County. American Canyon has separate wastewater services; rural unincorporated areas may use septic.

## Permit Categories (§ 4.04.030 / Discharge Permits page)

1. **Categorical Industrial Users (CIUs)** — Industries subject to federal categorical pretreatment standards or averaging ≥25,000 gpd of process wastewater. Full pretreatment program, monitoring, and reporting required. Examples: circuit board manufacturers, electroplating shops, pharmaceutical facilities.
2. **Significant Industrial Users (SIUs)** — Individual IU discharge permit required; ongoing monitoring and periodic compliance reporting.
3. **Industrial Users (general)** — Non-domestic discharge; BMPs or other NapaSan Code provisions may suffice.
4. **Wineries & Breweries** — Flow meter + weekly BOD, TSS, and pH sampling during processing/crush season; additional fees for exceeding water quality limits.
5. **Groundwater / Surface Cleaning** — Temporary permit up to 1 year; independent lab water sample testing required to verify compliance with local limits.
6. **Special Discharge** — One-time permits (pool draining, construction dewatering, etc.); specifies discharge rate and location.
7. **Waste Haulers** — Licensed haulers disposing of domestic waste, FOG, portable toilet waste, treated waste; pH must be 6.0–9.0; discharge at Soscol Water Recycling Facility.

## Specific Prohibitions (§ 4.04.110(C))

- Pollutants with closed-cup flashpoint < 140°F / 60°C (fire/explosion hazard)
- pH < 6.0 or > 9.0 (unless works specifically designed)
- Solid/viscous pollutants causing obstruction (includes fats, oil, grease, tar, sand, etc.)
- Heat causing POTW temperature > 40°C (104°F) at plant
- Petroleum oil, non-biodegradable cutting oil in amounts causing interference or pass-through
- Trucked/hauled waste except at designated discharge points

## Application Process

- Contact NapaSan Engineering at 707-258-6000
- Download permit application forms (Groundwater, Industrial User, FOG/Septic/Waste Hauler) from napasan.com
- No confirmed online e-permit portal
- SIU/CIU permits: duration ≤ 5 years; reapplication required minimum 90 days before expiration
- BMR (Baseline Monitoring Report) due within 180 days after applicable categorical standard effective date

## GOTCHA

- NapaSan is an independent special district; it is NOT a county agency and does not cover American Canyon or most rural unincorporated county areas.
- Winery/brewery dischargers must install flow meters and conduct weekly BOD, TSS, and pH sampling during the crush season.
- Oil & Grease limit applies polar/non-polar: 75 mg/L polar; 50 mg/L non-polar. FOG from food service establishments accepted at the FOG receiving station under separate waste hauler permit.
