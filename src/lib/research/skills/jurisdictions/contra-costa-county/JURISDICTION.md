## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **CUPA administrator:** Contra Costa Health, Hazardous Materials Programs (CCHHMP) — countywide CUPA for all unincorporated areas and participating cities.
- **Programs covered:** Business Plan / HMBP, CalARP, Industrial Safety Ordinance (ISO), Hazardous Waste Generator, Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA).
- **Industrial Safety Ordinance (ISO):** County Ordinance Code **Chapter 450-8** — a local layer stricter than state CalARP, covering petroleum refineries and chemical plants that submit an RMP to US EPA. A separate ISO applies to the City of Richmond. Research Chapter 450-8 *in addition to* CalARP for any major chemical/refinery facility.
- **CUPA permit fees:** Fee schedule (PDF) posted at https://www.cchealth.org/health-and-safety-information/hazmat-programs/cupa-fees — restructured rates effective July 1, 2024.
- **Online payments:** hsdmobile.cchealth.org

### 2. Fire Code — AHJ: Contra Costa County Fire Protection District (Con Fire)

- **Adopted edition:** 2022 California Fire Code (CFC), as amended by **CCCFPD Ordinance No. 2022-34**, codified at County Ordinance Code **Division 722**.
- **Note on annexation:** Con Fire annexed East Contra Costa Fire Protection District on 2022-07-01 — jurisdiction now covers Brentwood, Oakley, and far East County. Do not list ECCFPD separately.
- **Operational permits required** (CFC Section 105.6 as amended): hazardous materials handling/storage, high-piled combustible storage, flammable/combustible liquids, compressed gases, aerosol products, explosives, LP-gas, and others. ~8,000 occupancies under annual inspection.
- **Construction permits** (CFC Section 105.7 as amended): new construction, tenant improvements, fire suppression/life-safety systems, high-piled storage systems, hazardous materials process piping.
- **Online permit submittal portal:** confire.vision33cloud.com/citizenportal
- **Engineering fee schedule:** https://www.cccfpd.org/DocumentCenter/View/183/Engineering-Fee-Schedule-PDF (fees effective July 18, 2021 — verify for current schedule)
- **Operational permit fee schedule:** https://cccfpd.org/DocumentCenter/View/323/Fee-Schedule-PDF

### 3. Industrial Wastewater — Pretreatment / IWP

- **Primary POTW:** Central Contra Costa Sanitary District (Central San) — serves the central county (Concord, Walnut Creek, Pleasant Hill, Lafayette, Danville, and surrounding areas).
- **Sewer use / source control ordinance:** Central San **District Code Title 10** — Source Control Ordinance. Key chapters: 10.04 (General Provisions), 10.08 (Regulations), 10.12 (Administration), 10.16 (Enforcement), 10.24 (Fees), 10.28 (Waste Hauler), 10.32 (Grease/Oil/Sand Interceptors).
- **Industrial User (IU) Permit:** Required for significant industrial users (SIUs) — e.g., electroplating, circuit-board manufacturing, and other federal categorical industries. IU Permits require O&M records, spill response plans, compliance certification, and discharge monitoring.
- **Special Discharge Permit:** Issued for temporary or non-standard discharges; may require pretreatment system and sampling.
- **GOTCHA:** East County (Brentwood, Antioch, Pittsburg area) is served by Delta Diablo Sanitation District, not Central San — verify the correct POTW by facility address.
- **Contact Central San Environmental Compliance:** www.centralsan.org/environmental-compliance
- Source: https://www.centralsan.org/environmental-compliance
- Source: https://www.centralsan.org/post/permitted-industrial-user

### 4. Stormwater — MS4 / Industrial Stormwater

- **MS4 program:** Contra Costa County Watershed Program (CWP) administers the Municipal Regional Stormwater NPDES Permit (MRP) **No. CAS612008** (MRP 3.0, reissued May 11, 2022, by SF Bay Regional Water Quality Control Board) for unincorporated county areas.
- **Local stormwater ordinance:** County Ordinance Code **Title 10, Division 1014** — Stormwater Management and Discharge Control. Section 1014-4.006 enumerates prohibited discharges.
- **Industrial operators** must also hold a separate state Industrial General Permit (IGP) from the State Water Board (SMARTS portal) — the county MS4 program conducts business inspections and illicit-discharge investigations but is separate from the IGP.
- **Illicit discharge reporting (unincorporated areas):** 925-313-2236
- **Contact CWP:** 255 Glacier Dr, Martinez CA 94553 | 925-313-2360 | cleanwater@pw.cccounty.us
- **Program website:** cccleanwater.org

### 5. Zoning / Land Use — Unincorporated County

- **Zoning authority:** Dept of Conservation & Development (DCD), Application & Permit Center.
- **Zoning code:** County Ordinance Code **Title 8 — Zoning**, Division 84 (Land Use Districts). Industrial district chapters:
  - **Ch. 84-58 — L-I (Light Industrial District):** warehousing, light manufacturing, research/lab uses.
  - **Ch. 84-62 — H-I (Heavy Industrial District):** heavy manufacturing, chemical processing, refinery-related uses.
  - **C-M (Commercial-Manufacturing)** district also permits many light-industrial and service-industrial uses.
  - Note: County zoning does not use M-1/M-2 — the local designations are L-I and H-I.
- **Land Use Permit (CUP equivalent):** Called a "Land Use Permit" (LUP) in Contra Costa County, governed by **Ch. 82-6**. Zoning Administrator hears and decides LUP applications. Required for many industrial uses in L-I and H-I zones.

### GOTCHAs — Unusual Local Rules

- **Industrial Safety Ordinance (ISO):** Ch. 450-8 is a local CalARP overlay for petroleum refineries and chemical plants — stricter than state CalARP. A separate Richmond ISO also exists. Always research Ch. 450-8 in addition to CalARP for major chemical/refinery facilities.
- **Con Fire annexation:** ECCFPD dissolved into Con Fire on 2022-07-01; do not treat East County as a separate fire district.
- **Two POTWs:** Central San (central county) vs. Delta Diablo (east county) — always confirm by facility address before routing wastewater questions.
- **CUP naming:** Contra Costa County calls its conditional-use approval a "Land Use Permit" (LUP), not a CUP. The code chapter is Ch. 82-6.
