## CUPA Identity

**Tuolumne County Division of Environmental Health** (a division of the Tuolumne County Community Development Department) is the **Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for the entire county**, including all unincorporated communities and the City of Sonora. There is no separate city-run CUPA in Tuolumne County.

CalEPA certified this CUPA. The October 2024 CalEPA Unified Program Performance Evaluation (Final Summary of Findings) rates the CUPA's implementation as satisfactory with improvement needed; no participating agencies were identified.

## Programs Administered

The Environmental Health Division administers all six unified program elements under Health and Safety Code Chapter 6.11:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — reported via CERS (California Environmental Reporting System)
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator Program** (including tiered permitting)
3. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — installation, operation, closure
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure (SPCC)
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — [UNVERIFIED — JURISDICTION.md notes CalARP locally unverified; confirm with CUPA]
6. **Tiered Permitting (TP)** — conditionally exempt, tiered permit activities

## HMBP Reporting Thresholds (State Standard)

A Hazardous Materials Business Plan is required for any facility storing:
- **Liquids:** > 55 gallons
- **Solids:** > 500 pounds
- **Gases:** > 200 cubic feet

All HMBPs are submitted electronically through CERS: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/

## Local Hazmat Ordinance

No dedicated standalone hazardous materials ordinance was found online for Tuolumne County beyond the state Unified Program framework administered by the Environmental Health Division. A 2022 Granicus item (Ord. amendment of Sections 15.20.060) addressed hazardous vegetation/defensible space — that is a fire safety provision, not a CUPA hazmat ordinance.
