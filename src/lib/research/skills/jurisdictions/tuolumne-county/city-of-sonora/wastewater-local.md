## Controlling Authority — POTW

Wastewater collection and treatment for the City of Sonora is provided by the:

**Tuolumne Utilities District (TUD)**
- Address: 18885 Nugget Blvd., Sonora, CA 95370
- Phone: (209) 532-5536
- Email: [email protected]
- Hours: Monday–Friday, 7:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.
- Website: https://tudwater.com

The City of Sonora does **not** operate its own POTW. The city's sewer collection connects to TUD's regional system.

**Note:** Title 13 (Water and Sewers) of the Sonora Municipal Code is listed as **(RESERVED)** — the city has not adopted its own sewer-use ordinance. TUD's Wastewater Ordinance is the governing document for sewer use and discharge within the district service area (which includes the city).

## Treatment Plant

**Sonora Regional Wastewater Treatment Plant (RWWTP)**
- Operated by TUD
- Design capacity: **2.6 MGD** (average dry weather flow basis)
- Current connections: ~9,488 equivalent single-family residential units (ESFRs) directly connected, plus septage
- Constructed late 1970s; ongoing capital improvements (new $42.2 million regional WWTP facility near completion as of 2023/2024)
- Service area includes Sonora, Columbia, East Sonora, Mono Village, Mono Vista, Ranchos Poquitos, Soulsbyville, Twain Harte, Willow Springs

## Discharge Permit

TUD operates under **Waste Discharge Requirements (WDRs)** issued by the **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 5)**. TUD has historically used spray-field land application of treated effluent and recycled water on pasture lands near Jamestown, which eliminated the need for a traditional NPDES permit. The District is regulated under WDRs per California Water Code.

Reclamation is to **Title 22** (California Code of Regulations) disinfected tertiary treated water standards.

## Sewer Use Ordinance

**TUD Wastewater Ordinance** — originally adopted August 24, 1993; most recently amended April 28, 2015.

Key provisions:
- **Chapter 2 — Prohibitions:** Categorical discharge prohibitions including no storm drainage/groundwater, no radioactive waste above permitted levels, and numeric strength limitations on conventional pollutants (§ 2.08)
- **Chapter 3 — Extension, Connection, and Discharge Permits:** Users are required to obtain a **Wastewater Discharge Permit** for discharges outside the normal residential/commercial classification (§ 3.03)
- **Chapter 3.07 — Pretreatment:** Industrial and commercial users must pretreat wastewater to meet District standards before discharge. Pretreatment facilities must be designed and approved by TUD. However, TUD's ordinance does not establish a formal EPA-approved Industrial Pretreatment Program (IWP) with local limits study or categorical standards — this is a **general pretreatment obligation** rather than a 40 CFR Part 403 approved program. [VERIFY — confirm with TUD whether an approved IWP has since been established]
- **Chapter 4 — Fees and Charges:** Connection fees, service charges, and special surcharges (rates updated per 2022 Board action, effective August 2023 through July 2027)
- **Chapter 6 — Enforcement / Chapter 7 — Penalties:** Civil and criminal penalties; cease and desist authority
- **Chapter 9 — Private Sewer Laterals:** Owner responsibility for maintenance and repair

## Industrial Pretreatment Program (IWP)

TUD does **not** appear to have a formal EPA-approved Industrial Pretreatment Program (40 CFR Part 403). The RWWTP design capacity is 2.6 MGD, which is below the 5 MGD threshold that typically triggers mandatory IWP requirements under federal regulations; however, EPA may still require an IWP if the POTW receives significant industrial flows. The TUD SSMP (2025) and wastewater ordinance contain general pretreatment language but no categorical local limits or SIU permit framework was found online.
