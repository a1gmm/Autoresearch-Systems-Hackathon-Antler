## Controlling Authority

The City of Sonora is **NOT its own CUPA**. All Unified Program elements for facilities within city limits are administered by the:

**Tuolumne County Division of Environmental Health — CUPA**
- Physical address: A.N. Francisco Building, 4th Floor, 48 W. Yaney Ave., Sonora, CA 95370
- Also listed as: 2 S. Green Street, Sonora, CA 95370-4618
- County website: https://www.tuolumnecounty.ca.gov/1555/Hazardous-Materials
- Parent department: Tuolumne County Environmental Health / Community Development

The Tuolumne County Division of Environmental Health is certified by CalEPA as the **Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA)** for all of Tuolumne County, including the City of Sonora.

## Programs Administered

The county CUPA administers the following Unified Program elements (per CalEPA CUPA framework and 2024 FSOF performance evaluation):

| Program Element | Acronym |
|---|---|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan | HMBP |
| Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act | APSA |
| Underground Storage Tanks | UST |
| Hazardous Waste Generator (small/large) | HWGP |
| Hazardous Waste On-Site Treatment (Tiered Permits) | HWOT |
| Spill Prevention, Control & Countermeasure | SPCC (coordination) |

**HMBP threshold:** A business plan is required for any facility that stores hazardous materials above: 55 gallons (liquid), 500 pounds (solid), or 200 cubic feet (gas) at any one time.

## Performance Evaluation (2024)

CalEPA and state Unified Program agencies conducted a performance evaluation of the Tuolumne County CUPA during October 2024 through July 2025. Findings included corrective actions related to UST inspection procedures and enforcement practices. The 1st Evaluation Progress Report was due December 29, 2025.
