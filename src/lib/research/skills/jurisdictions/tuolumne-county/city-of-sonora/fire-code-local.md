## Adopted Fire Code

The City of Sonora adopts the California Fire Code (CFC) — which is Part 9 of the California Building Standards Code (Title 24) — by ordinance. Fire code provisions are codified in **Title 15 (Buildings and Construction)** of the Sonora Municipal Code, including:
- **Chapter 15.12** — California Fire Code adoption (fire-code-chapter designation confirmed by Municode table of contents; specific section text not accessible online)
- **Chapter 15.16** — Fire Zones (fire district and fire-zone designations within city limits)
- **Chapter 8.32** — Unlawful Open Burning (Title 8 Health and Safety)
- **Chapter 8.40** — Fire Hazard Severity Zones

**Key adopting ordinances:**
- **Ordinance 825** (adopted 03-02-2015): "Building and Fire Code Adoption" — repealed prior uniform code chapters and adopted California codes. [VERIFY — specific CFC edition adopted (2013 CFC was current at that time) not confirmed from online text; ordinance PDF is scanned image]
- **Ordinance 853** (adopted 05-20-2019): Comprehensive Title 15 revision — Building and Construction. [VERIFY — whether this updated the CFC edition; ordinance PDF is scanned image]

The 2024 affidavit form published by Sonora Fire Department references the **2016 California Residential Code (CRC)** and **2016 California Building Code (CBC)** for residential occupancies, suggesting the city was on 2016-edition codes. As of 2026-01-01, the statewide 2025 California Building Standards Code (25th edition) took effect; **confirm with the fire department which CFC edition is currently enforced.**

## Defensible Space

- The city adopted a separate **Defensible Space Ordinance** (**Ordinance 835**, adopted per a Tuolumne County collaboration). See: https://tuolumnecounty.ca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/20188/Sonora-Defensible-Space-Ordinance
- Cal Fire / State Responsibility Area (SRA) defensible space rules (PRC §4291) also apply to properties in or adjacent to wildland-urban interface areas.

## Fire Prevention Services

The department provides fire prevention inspections, public education, and non-emergency public services. A **Smoke Detector, CO & Fire Extinguisher Self-Certification Form** is required annually for multi-dwelling complexes (apartments, R-1/R-2/R-2.1/R-4 occupancies).

## Notes

- Sonora is a member of the Tuolumne County Fire Authority JPA for mutual aid coordination, but the city operates its own municipal fire department and is sole AHJ within city limits.
- The surrounding unincorporated county (outside Sonora) is served by Tuolumne County Fire District / CAL FIRE.
- Specific fire permit fees are not published online; contact SFD directly.
