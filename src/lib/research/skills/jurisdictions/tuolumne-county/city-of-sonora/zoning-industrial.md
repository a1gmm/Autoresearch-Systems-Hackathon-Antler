## Governing Code

Zoning is codified in **Title 17 (Zoning)** of the Sonora Municipal Code.

- Online via Municode: https://library.municode.com/ca/sonora/codes/municipal_code
- City's own code site: https://sonora.municipalcodeonline.com/

Title 17 was comprehensively updated by **Ordinance 832** (adopted 11-16-2015, "2015 Title 17, Zoning Ordinance, Text Amendments"). Subsequent amendments include Ordinance 857 (07-15-2019, commercial uses text amendment) and Ordinance 874 (11-02-2020, cannabis businesses).

## Industrial Zone

The City of Sonora has one industrial/manufacturing zone:

**ML — Limited Manufacturing Zone** (Chapter 17.28 of the municipal code)
- Allows all uses permitted in nonresidential zones plus light manufacturing uses
- Light manufacturing is a permitted use by right in the ML zone (§ 17.28.020)
- Uses requiring a **Use Permit (Conditional Use Permit / CUP)** are listed in § 17.60.030 of the zoning ordinance
- [VERIFY — confirm exact list of ML permitted-by-right vs. CUP uses and development standards; Title 17 PDF is scanned image and full text was not accessible online]

Sonora is a small city (~4,500 residents) with limited industrial land. The ML zone is the only non-commercial/non-residential zone for manufacturing/industrial uses. There are no M-1 Heavy Industrial or M-2 districts identified in the code structure.

## Other Non-Residential Zones

Based on search results and the ordinances page, Sonora's non-residential zones include (all under Title 17):
- **CG** — General Commercial
- **CO** — Commercial Office
- **C** — Commercial
- **AR** — Agricultural Residential
- **PD** — Planned Development (§ 17.30)
- **ML** — Limited Manufacturing (the only industrial/manufacturing zone)

A **Commercial Specific Plan Area** exists under § 17.33, governing the Washington Street / downtown corridor.

## Notes

- Sonora is a small, historic Gold Rush–era city. Industrial land area is very limited.
- New industrial projects in the ML zone likely require environmental review under CEQA.
- The surrounding unincorporated county uses Tuolumne County's own zoning ordinance (not Sonora's Title 17).
