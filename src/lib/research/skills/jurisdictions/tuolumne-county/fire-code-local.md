## Adopted Fire Code Edition

**California Fire Code (CFC), 2016 Edition**, adopted via **Tuolumne County Ordinance Code Chapter 15.20**, Section 15.20.110. The adopting ordinance chain for the 2016 edition is **Ord. 3305 (2016)**, as last amended by **Ord. 3309 (2017)**.

The National Fire Codes, 2016 Edition (NFPA) were also adopted concurrently under Section 15.20.110(B) via Ord. 3305 (2016).

> [VERIFY — CFC edition currency] The PDF copy of Chapter 15.20 available online is dated 2017 (Ord. 3309 is the most recent ordinance cited). Tuolumne County's live Document Center blocks automated retrieval. The 2022 CFC became the statewide default in January 2023 per the California Building Standards Code update cycle. Confirm with the Fire Prevention Division whether Chapter 15.20 has been updated to adopt the 2019 or 2022 CFC.

## Local Amendments to the CFC (confirmed in 2017 text)

- **Section 505.2 (Street/Road Signs)** — amended by Section 15.20.045(A) of the Ordinance Code.
- **Section 505.1 (Address Identification)** — amended by Section 15.20.040 of the Ordinance Code.
- **Commercial/industrial fire flow** — must meet CFC 2016 Edition standards; two-hour duration at required rate; system residual pressure ≥20 psi (Section 15.20.010).
- **Building height limit** — max 30 ft / 2 stories for commercial/industrial unless automatic sprinkler + alarm system installed (1-story increase allowance) (Section 15.20.010(F)).
- **Defensible space** — 30–100 ft clearance from structure eaves to property line in wildland settings (Section 15.20.060); Chapter 8.14 addresses hazardous vegetation management countywide.

## Municipal Code Chapter

**Tuolumne County Ordinance Code, Chapter 15.20** (Fire Safety Standards). Sits in Title 15 (Buildings and Construction). The broader fire-related framework also includes Chapter 8.14 (Hazardous Vegetation Management).

## Fire AHJ

- **Unincorporated county:** Tuolumne County Fire Department (TCFD), Fire Prevention Division. The "Inspection Authority" under Section 15.20.005(F) is the **Fire Prevention Division of the Tuolumne County Fire Department**; "Chief" means the **County Fire Marshal** or authorized representative.
- TCFD operates under a cooperative agreement with **CAL FIRE Tuolumne-Calaveras Unit (TCU)** for state-responsibility-area (SRA) wildland protection.
- Separate independent fire districts (Columbia FPD, Jamestown FPD, Tuolumne Fire District, Groveland CSD Fire) serve portions of the unincorporated county — their AHJ boundaries are per-district and should be verified for any specific site.
- **City of Sonora:** served by the City of Sonora Fire Department (see city-of-sonora subfolder).
