## Industrial District Names

Tuolumne County's zoning code contains two industrial districts under Chapter 17.16:

| District | Abbreviation | General Description |
|---|---|---|
| Light Industrial | **LI** | Light manufacturing, processing, storage, and service industrial uses with limited off-site impacts |
| Heavy Industrial | **HI** | More intensive manufacturing, processing, refining, extraction; may allow uses not compatible with residential proximity |

General-plan land use designations mirror these zoning classes. Specific permitted (P) and conditionally permitted (CUP) uses are listed in **Table 17.16.1** of Chapter 17.16 (not reproduced here — fetch from county site or contact Planning Division).

Common industrial uses noted in county general plan and zoning documentation include:
- General manufacturing, processing and refining
- Mills, plants, and other processing facilities
- Storage and warehouses
- Mining, excavation and resource extraction
- General industrial operations

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

Many industrial uses in both LI and HI districts, and virtually all uses that generate significant off-site impacts (noise, odor, traffic, hazardous materials), require a **Conditional Use Permit**.

Decision-making body: **Tuolumne County Planning Commission** (public hearing required).

CUP application requirements include:
- Site plan (north arrow, scale, dimensions, property area, slopes, watercourses, existing/proposed improvements)
- Application fee (confirm current fee schedule with Planning Division)
- Environmental review per CEQA

Instructions and application form: https://www.tuolumnecounty.ca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/2342/Conditional-Use-Permit-Instructions  
Permit applications portal: https://www.tuolumnecounty.ca.gov/1301/Permit-Applications-and-Forms

## Special Purpose / Overlay Zones

Chapter 17.18 addresses Special Purpose Zoning Districts. Certain overlay or combining districts may apply to industrial parcels (e.g., airport overlay, flood zone, wildland-urban interface). Verify applicable overlays for any specific site.
