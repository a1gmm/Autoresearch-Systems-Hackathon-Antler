## POTW and Control Authority

**Facility:** San José–Santa Clara Regional Wastewater Facility (Water Pollution Control Plant)
**Location:** San José, CA
**Lead Control Authority:** City of San José Environmental Services Department

The City of San José is the control authority for the regional pretreatment program,
administering on behalf of eight co-permittee jurisdictions whose sanitary sewer
systems discharge to the facility:

**Unincorporated areas** — Contact the applicable sanitation district for the
receiving POTW. County Ordinance Code **Title B, Division B11, Chapter IV**
governs on-site wastewater treatment for unincorporated areas.

## Sewer Use Ordinance

**City of San José Municipal Code, Title 15, Chapter 15.14 — Sewer Use Regulations**

This is the primary legal authority governing industrial discharges to the
SJ/SC Regional Wastewater Facility. An early substantive amendment (Ordinance
No. 28179, §1) was adopted December 4, 2007, which updated Part 2 of Chapter 15.14.
Current version maintained at:
https://library.municode.com/ca/san_jose/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT15PUUT_CH15.14SEUSRE

Chapter 15.12 (Sewers) addresses general sewer connection and collection system rules.

## NPDES Permit / Pretreatment Program Basis

The facility operates under **NPDES Permit No. CA0037842** issued by the SF Bay
Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 2). The most recent permit cycle is
governed by a tentative order circulated in August 2025; the prior adopted order
(Order No. R2-2009-XXXX series) established the pretreatment program framework.

**Pretreatment legal authority:** 40 CFR Part 403 (General Pretreatment Regulations)
and federal categorical standards at 40 CFR §§405–471.

New local limits were adopted by the co-permittees (San José, Santa Clara, Milpitas,
West Valley Sanitation District, Cupertino Sanitation District) on varying dates
between **December 2007 and June 2008**, covering: chromium, copper, nickel, and
selenium. Those limits are approved as part of the pretreatment program under the
current NPDES order.

## Local Discharge Limits

The City of San José publishes local limits through Chapter 15.14 but does **not**
post a numeric limits table on public-facing web pages. The NPDES permit requires
annual evaluation of local limits under 40 CFR §403.5(c)(1). Key pollutants of
concern for the Santa Clara Valley semiconductor/electronics sector include:

| Pollutant | Regulatory Note |
|---|---|
| Copper | Specific copper action plan; antidegradation — local limits for copper cannot be eliminated |
| Nickel | Local limit adopted Dec 2007–Jun 2008 |
| Chromium (total / hexavalent) | Local limit adopted Dec 2007–Jun 2008 |
| Selenium | Local limit adopted Dec 2007–Jun 2008 |
| Cyanide | Cyanide action plan — monitoring, source control, pollution prevention |
| Dioxin-TEQ | Compliance schedule under NPDES order |
| Mercury | Effluent monitoring constituent |
| Fluoride | Significant semiconductor industry concern; verify current local limit with SJ |

**For current numeric mg/L limits:** Contact San José Environmental Services,
Source Control Section. Numerical limits are set via Chapter 15.14 and updated by
amendment; they are not reproduced here to avoid quoting stale values.

## County Ordinance (Unincorporated Areas)

For facilities in unincorporated Santa Clara County:
**Title B, Division B11, Chapter IV — On-Site Wastewater Treatment**
Governs septic systems and on-site treatment; does not establish industrial pretreatment
limits (unincorporated industrial facilities must connect to the applicable sanitation
district POTW and comply with that district's sewer use ordinance).
