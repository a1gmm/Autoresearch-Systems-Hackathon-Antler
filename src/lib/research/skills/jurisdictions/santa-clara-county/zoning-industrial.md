## Zoning Code Reference

**Santa Clara County Ordinance Code, Title C — Code of Land Use and Zoning,
Appendix I — Zoning Ordinance**

The Zoning Ordinance is organized into five Articles:
- Article 1: General Provisions (Chapters 1.10–1.30)
- Article 2: Base Districts (Chapters 2.10–2.50)
- Article 3: Combining Districts (Chapters 3.10–3.95)
- Article 4: Supplemental Regulations and Standards (Chapters 4.10–4.50)
- Article 5: Procedures and Administration (Chapters 5.10–5.80)

## Industrial Base Districts — Chapter 2.40

Industrial uses in unincorporated Santa Clara County are governed by
**Chapter 2.40: Commercial and Industrial Base Districts** (§§ 2.40.010–2.40.040).

The county uses two industrial district designations — **not** the generic M-1/M-2 naming:

### ML — Light Industrial
> "The purpose of the Light Industrial district (ML) is to provide for certain 'heavy'
> commercial and 'light' industrial uses which are generally incompatible with commercial
> areas but which perform important storage, manufacturing, or servicing functions.
> The uses permitted in this district are often associated with impacts such as noise
> and large volumes of truck traffic. The lands in this district should be located near
> commercial areas, near arterial traffic routes, along railroad lines."
> (§2.40.010(D), rev. April 2024)

### MH — Heavy Industrial
> "The purpose of the Heavy Industrial district (MH) is to provide for essential heavy
> industrial and commercial uses that are likely to impact the surrounding area.
> This district should be located so as to minimize adverse effects on adjoining areas."
> (§2.40.010(E), rev. April 2024)

## Supplemental Standards for ML and MH (§2.40.040)

A. **Landscaping** — All required yards shall be maintained as landscaped planting strips
   (except access/parking areas).

B. **Outdoor Storage** — Permitted in ML and MH if screened from adjoining property by
   wall, dense evergreen hedge, or fence. Materials shall not project above the screen.

C. **Increased Setback (Adjacent to R Districts)** — For ML or MH parcels adjacent to or
   across a street from R1, R2, or R3 districts, setbacks may be increased by the
   Zoning Administrator during ASA review.

D. **Off-Street Loading (Adjacent to R Districts)**:
   - ML district: loading areas not less than **50 feet** from any R1/R2/R3 boundary
   - MH district: loading areas not less than **200 feet** from any R1/R2/R3 boundary

## Use Permit Process (Chapter 5.65)

Uses designated "U" in Table 2.40-1 require a **Use Permit + Architecture and Site
Approval (ASA)** — a discretionary approval process.

**Review authority:** The **Planning Commission** hears and decides all Use Permit
applications (§5.65.020).

**Required findings (§5.65.030):**
1. Conforms with General Plan, Zoning Ordinance, and all applicable standards.
2. Site is adequate in size/shape for facilities plus any necessary buffers.
3. Use will not impair the integrity/character of the district.
4. Use will not be detrimental to public health, safety, or general welfare (parking,
   emergency access, water quality, noise, odor, traffic, drainage all considered).

**Pre-application meeting:** Required before submittal for new or major-modification
Use Permits (§5.65.060 / §5.20.020).

**Permit term:** A Use Permit with no time-limit condition is revoked automatically
if the use ceases for 12 consecutive months (§5.65.050).
