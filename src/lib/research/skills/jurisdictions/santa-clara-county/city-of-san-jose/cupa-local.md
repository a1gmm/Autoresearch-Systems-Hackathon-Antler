## CUPA Identity and Structure

- **San José has NO city-level CUPA.** The City of San José is NOT a CUPA and is NOT a Participating Agency.
- **Administering CUPA:** County of Santa Clara, Department of Environmental Health (DEH), **Hazardous Materials Compliance Division (HMCD)**
- **Structure:** Santa Clara County operates multiple CUPAs countywide; HMCD is the CUPA of jurisdiction for San José across all six CalEPA unified program elements
- **HMCD web:** https://deh.santaclaracounty.gov/hazardous-materials-and-waste-facility-compliance-and-permits
- **HMCD hazmat main:** https://deh.santaclaracounty.gov/hazardous-materials
- **Alternate HMCD URL (legacy):** https://hazmat.sccgov.org

## HMBP Program Details

- **Submission system:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business (HMCD is the reviewing agency for San José facilities)
- **Threshold quantities** (state baseline — no confirmed HMCD reduction below these):
  - Liquids: ≥ 55 gallons
  - Solids: ≥ 500 pounds
  - Gases: ≥ 200 cubic feet at NTP
- **Who must comply:** Any business storing, using, or handling hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities (Health & Safety Code §25507)
- **Initial filing:** Within 30 days of bringing threshold materials onsite
- **Annual review/resubmission:** Required every year via CERS
- **Update triggers:** Material quantities increase ≥100%; new undisclosed materials; address/ownership/name changes; significant operational changes (each triggers 30-day filing)
- **HMCD inspection program:** HMCD conducts routine compliance inspections; facilities in San José are inspected by HMCD staff (not city staff)

## CalARP Program

- HMCD is the local implementing agency (LIA) for CalARP in San José
- Regulated substances above threshold quantities require RMP submission to USEPA and CalARP registration with HMCD via CERS
- HMCD reviews local CalARP component and coordinates with OES for worst-case scenario planning
- **FY 2023-24 note:** CalARP surcharge to facilities increased effective October 13, 2023 (per Budget Act authorization for CalEPA)

## UST Program

- HMCD permits UST installation, modification, repair, and closure for facilities in San José
- Unauthorized releases: report to HMCD
- Leak detection systems require certification
- Submit permit applications via CERS

## APSA

- Facilities storing petroleum aboveground above APSA thresholds (≥1,320 gallons aggregate OR single tank >660 gallons) must maintain a Spill Prevention, Control and Countermeasure (SPCC) plan
- HMCD inspects for APSA compliance; CERS filing required

## Local Ordinance Authority

- **County Ordinance:** Santa Clara County Ordinance Code, Division B11 Chapter XIV (Toxic Gas Ordinance) — referenced for facilities storing above-threshold toxic gases
- No City of San José municipal code chapter governs CUPA programs — HMCD's authority derives from the County ordinance code and State Health & Safety Code Chapter 6.95
- [VERIFY] Full County ordinance chapter text: contact HMCD or search Santa Clara County Ordinance Code via county law library

## Closures Program

- HMCD administers a facility closures program: https://hazmat.sccgov.org/programs-and-services/closures
- Facilities permanently discontinuing hazardous materials storage must notify HMCD and close out CERS records

## Relationship to SJFD Fire Permit Track

SJFD/BFP enforces fire-code operational permits for hazmat occupancies under CFC Chapter 50+ and SJMC Chapter 17.12 — this is a **separate permit track** from HMCD's CUPA/HMBP program. Many industrial facilities in San José must satisfy both:
1. CERS HMBP filing reviewed by HMCD (CUPA track)
2. SJFD fire-code operational permit (fire track)
See fire-code-local.md for SJFD fire permit details.
