### 1. CUPA (Certified Unified Program Agency)

**San José has NO city-level CUPA.** CUPA authority for San José sits with the County of Santa Clara Department of Environmental Health, Hazardous Materials Compliance Division (HMCD).

- **Administering agency:** Santa Clara County DEH — Hazardous Materials Compliance Division (HMCD)
- **Programs HMCD runs for San José:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), Underground Storage Tank (UST) installation/closure, California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP), Hazardous Waste Generator, Onsite Hazardous Waste Treatment
- **Caveat:** Santa Clara County has 4 CUPAs and 3 Participating Agencies countywide; HMCD is the CUPA of jurisdiction for San José across all six program elements — there is no City of San José Participating Agency for CUPA programs
- **Submit HMBP & permits via:** County DEH portal (California Environmental Reporting System, CERS — cers.calepa.ca.gov)
- **Contact:** County DEH / HMCD — see https://deh.santaclaracounty.gov/hazardous-materials-and-waste-facility-compliance-and-permits
- **Fee schedule:** [VERIFY — fee schedule PDF not publicly indexed; contact HMCD directly]
- **Local ordinance:** Santa Clara County Ordinance Code, Division B11 Chapter XIV (Toxic Gas Ordinance referenced for above-threshold toxic gas storage)

### 2. Fire Code (AHJ: San José Fire Department)

- **AHJ:** San José Fire Department (SJFD), Bureau of Fire Prevention (BFP)
- **Adopted code:** 2022 California Fire Code (CFC), adopted with local amendments via **Ordinance No. 4615** (effective December 2, 2022); codified at **Municipal Code Chapter 17.12** (City of San José Fire Code)
- **Next code cycle:** CFC 2025 edition (effective statewide January 1, 2026); confirm whether SJ has adopted locally
- **Operational/Annual Permits:** Required for businesses that store, handle, or use hazardous materials, or conduct processes hazardous to life or property (per CFC Section 105 and SJMC Chapter 17.12). Includes hazardous materials (flammables, compressed gases, toxics), high-pile combustible storage, aerosols, and other listed occupancies
- **Permit portal (online):** https://www.sanjoseca.gov/business/development-services-permit-center/online-permits-at-sjpermits-org/fire-online-permits
- **Operational permit requests:** Email SJFD.billing@sanjoseca.gov or call 408-795-1869
- **Hazmat program inquiries:** 408-535-7750 (BFP HazMat line) or sjfd.bfpsolutions@sanjoseca.gov
- **Fee schedule:** [VERIFY — set by resolution; not publicly indexed as standalone PDF; contact BFP or see sjpermits.org]
- **2022 Fire Code Policies page:** https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/fire-department/fire-prevention-permits/development-construction/policies-based-on-2022-code

### 3. Industrial Wastewater / Pretreatment

- **POTW:** San José–Santa Clara Regional Wastewater Facility (RWF)
- **Lead agency:** City of San José Environmental Services Department (ESD), Water Quality Control Division — administers pretreatment on behalf of 8 tributary jurisdictions
- **Permit name:** Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit (IWP)
- **Who needs it:** Industrial users discharging process wastewater to the sanitary sewer system draining to the RWF; Significant Industrial Users (SIU) and Categorical Industrial Users (CIU per 40 CFR 405–471) must hold IWP
- **Local ordinance:** Municipal Code **Title 15, Chapter 15.14** (Sewer Use Regulations) — primary pretreatment authority
- **Application timeline:** Submit 90 days before planned new discharge or permit expiration; 30 days before any significant process change
- **Pretreatment contact:** Source Control Inspector line — (408) 945-3000
- **Forms/portal:** https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/environmental-services/water-utilities/stormwater-wastewater/businesses/industrial-commercial-facilities/pretreatment-forms
- **Wastewater discharge ordinances page:** https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/environmental-services/water-utilities/stormwater-wastewater/businesses/industrial-commercial-facilities/wastewater-discharge-ordinances
- **Fee schedule:** [VERIFY — fee schedule not publicly indexed; contact ESD Water Quality Control at (408) 945-3000]

### 4. Stormwater (MS4)

- **Permit authority:** San Francisco Bay Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 2)
- **Permit:** Municipal Regional Stormwater NPDES Permit, **No. CAS612008** (MRP) — San José is one of 76 Bay Area co-permittees; current version is MRP 3.0 (R2-2022-0018, changes effective July 1, 2023)
- **City implementation:** City of San José Environmental Services Department implements MRP requirements locally via:
  - City Council Policy 6-29: Post-Construction Urban Runoff Management (Provision C.3)
  - City Council Policy 8-14: Post-Construction Hydromodification Management
- **Industrial facilities:** Businesses with outdoor industrial activity or significant materials exposure must comply with the CA Industrial General Permit (IGP, NPDES No. CAS000001) filed with State Water Board via SMARTS system — separate from the city MS4
- **Stormwater contact:** City of San José ESD, Stormwater & Wastewater division — https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/environmental-services/water-utilities/stormwater-wastewater

### 5. Zoning / Land Use

- **Zoning code:** Municipal Code **Title 20** (Zoning Ordinance) — https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/planning-building-code-enforcement/planning-division/ordinances-proposed-updates/zoning-code-title-20
- **Municode:** https://library.municode.com/ca/san_jose/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT20ZO
- **Industrial district designations** (Chapter 20.50):
  - **IP** — Industrial Park (lighter industrial, high-quality setting, compatible with adjacent non-industrial)
  - **LI** — Light Industrial
  - **HI** — Heavy Industrial
  - Note: older M-1/M-2 nomenclature is NOT used in current Title 20; districts are IP/LI/HI
- **Conditional Use Permit (CUP):** CUPs in industrial zones processed under **Chapter 20.100**; some uses require City Council approval (designated "CC" in use tables); standard CUPs go to Planning Director
- **Zoning lookup / permits portal:** https://www.sjpermits.org (enter address or APN)
- **Planning contact:** Development Services Permit Center — (408) 535-3555
- **Zoning map:** https://www.zoneomics.com/zoning-maps/california/san-jose (third-party; verify against official GIS)
