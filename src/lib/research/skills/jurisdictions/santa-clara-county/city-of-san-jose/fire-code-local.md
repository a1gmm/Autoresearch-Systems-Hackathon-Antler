## Local Amendments — Confirmed

The following are confirmed local San José amendments, derived from the UpCodes published San José Fire Code 2022 and prior ordinance text:

### Hazardous Materials — Piping Identification (CFC §5003.2.2.1)
Section 5003.2.2.1(2) is amended to add:
> "Piping and tubing shall be identified in accordance with ASME A13.1 **and the Santa Clara County Fire Chiefs Marking Requirements and Guidelines for Hazardous Materials and Hazardous Waste**."
This requires compliance with county-level marking standards beyond the baseline ASME requirement.

### Sprinklers on Change of Use (§102.3.1 — local addition)
> "An approved automatic sprinkler system shall be provided throughout buildings or structures when an automatic sprinkler system is required per the California Fire Code due to a change of use or occupancy."

### Inspection Authority (§106.1 amendment)
The Bureau of Fire Prevention (BFP) has authority to inspect as often as necessary, including for unauthorized hazardous materials discharges.

### HMMP / HMIS (CFC Appendix H — §§5001.5.1, 5001.5.2)
San José adopts state-level requirements for Hazardous Materials Management Plans (HMMP) and Hazardous Materials Inventory Statements (HMIS) per Health & Safety Code Chapter 6.95 and Title 19 CCR Chapter 4. No confirmed local reduction below state thresholds.

## Hazmat Operational Permits

- Operational permits are required per CFC §105 and SJMC Chapter 17.12 for any business that stores, handles, or uses hazardous materials above CFC permit thresholds, or that conducts operations listed in CFC §105.5/§105.6
- **Minimum storage threshold** (state baseline — no confirmed San José reduction): 500 lb solid / 55 gal liquid / 200 cu ft at NTP for compressed gas (Health & Safety Code §25507 thresholds also apply via CUPA track — see cupa-local.md)
- SJFD/BFP enforces **fire-code operational permits** (CFC hazmat); CUPA/HMBP track is separate and handled by Santa Clara County HMCD — **two separate permit tracks may apply to the same facility**
- Permits apply to: flammables/combustibles (CFC Ch. 57), compressed gases (Ch. 53), cryogenic fluids (Ch. 55), explosives (Ch. 56), toxic/highly toxic (Ch. 60), oxidizers (Ch. 62), organic peroxides (Ch. 61), pyrophorics (Ch. 64), water-reactive solids/liquids (Ch. 66), aerosols (Ch. 51)

## High-Piled Combustible Storage (CFC Chapter 32)

- CFC 2022 Chapter 32 adopted with amendments; UpCodes matrix shows San José amends §3203.8 (Commodity Classification examples) and Table 3206.2 (General Fire Protection requirements)
- **State baseline permit trigger:** >500 sq ft of high-piled storage (Class I–IV commodities or Group A plastics stored >12 ft high or on solid shelving >6 ft high); >2,500 sq ft for public access retail storage

## GOTCHA

SJFD/BFP issues fire-code operational permits for hazmat operations. The CUPA/HMBP track (including business plan filing, UST, CalARP) is administered by **Santa Clara County DEH — HMCD**, not the City. Both may apply to the same facility. See cupa-local.md.
