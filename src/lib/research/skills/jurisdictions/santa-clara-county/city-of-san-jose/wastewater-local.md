## Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit (IWP)

- **Permit name:** Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit (IWP), also referred to as Industrial Wastewater Permit (IWP)
- **Who must hold one:**
  - **Significant Industrial Users (SIU):** Facilities with categorical processes (subject to EPA categorical pretreatment standards per 40 CFR §405–471), OR non-categorical users discharging >25,000 gpd process wastewater, OR users with reasonable potential to adversely affect the POTW
  - **Critical Users:** Dischargers whose wastewater contains Priority Pollutants (Pollutants of Concern or corrosive matter), or discharge in excess of 100,000 gpd
  - **Zero-Discharge Categorical Users:** Facilities with categorical processes but claiming no discharge to sewer — still must hold an IWP to document no discharge
- **Application timeline:** Submit 90 days before planned new discharge or permit expiration; 30 days before any significant process change (per SJMC 15.14 and 40 CFR Part 403)
- **Permit forms:** https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/environmental-services/water-utilities/stormwater-wastewater/businesses/industrial-commercial-facilities/pretreatment-forms

## Pretreatment Compliance Requirements

- **Self-monitoring:** Monthly reports for SIU/CIU; frequency varies by permit tier for smaller users
- **Baseline monitoring reports:** Required when permits are first issued
- **Periodic compliance reports:** Required for CIUs per 40 CFR §403.12
- **24-hour slug discharge notification:** Required immediately; written follow-up within applicable timeline
- **Bypassing treatment prohibition:** 40 CFR §403.17(d) — IUs may not bypass pretreatment systems
- **Analytical methods:** IUs must use correct analytical methods per 40 CFR §403.12(g)(4)

## Inspections

- ESD Source Control inspectors conduct routine IU compliance inspections
- Pre-permit site inspection may be required for new IU applications
- Inspection findings and violations are reportable in the annual pretreatment report to EPA Region 9 and RWQCB (due February 28 each year)
- Pretreatment Inspections page: https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/environmental-services/water-utilities/stormwater-wastewater/businesses/industrial-commercial-facilities/pretreatment-inspections
