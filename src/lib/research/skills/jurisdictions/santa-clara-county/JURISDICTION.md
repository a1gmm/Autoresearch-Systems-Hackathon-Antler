## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA / Hazardous Materials

**Agency:** Santa Clara County Dept of Environmental Health — Hazardous Materials Compliance Division (HMCD)

**Jurisdiction:** Unincorporated county + City of San José, Los Altos, Los Altos Hills, Milpitas, Monte Sereno, Morgan Hill, Saratoga.
(Gilroy, City of Santa Clara, and City of Sunnyvale each operate their own separate CUPA.)

**Local code:** Santa Clara County Ordinance Code, Title B, Division B11, Chapter XIII — Hazardous Materials Storage Ordinance (HMSO).

**Programs covered:** HMBP, HWG/TE, UST, APSA, Tiered Permitting, CalARP, Stormwater/urban-runoff compliance inspections.

**Reporting portal:** CERS — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov (state-mandated for all regulated businesses since 2013)

**Contact:** (408) 918-3400 | https://hazmat.sccgov.org/home | https://deh.santaclaracounty.gov/hazardous-materials-and-waste-facility-compliance-and-permits

**Adopted code:** 2022 California Fire Code (CFC) with local amendments, codified in County Ordinance Code **Title B, Division B7** (Fire Protection).

### 3. Industrial Wastewater / Pretreatment

**POTW:** San José/Santa Clara Regional Wastewater Facility (Water Pollution Control Plant) — City of San José is the lead Control Authority for the eight co-permittee jurisdictions.

**Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permits (IWDPs):** Required for facilities subject to EPA categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR §§405–471) and for facilities with significant non-domestic discharge.

**Sewer Use Ordinance — San José:** San José Municipal Code, **Title 15, Chapter 15.14** (Sewer Use Regulations). Governs discharges to the SJ/SC Regional WRF.

**Permit portal / forms:** https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/environmental-services/water-utilities/stormwater-wastewater/businesses/industrial-commercial-facilities/industrial-wastewater-discharge-permits

**Pretreatment forms:** https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/environmental-services/water-utilities/stormwater-wastewater/businesses/industrial-commercial-facilities/pretreatment-forms

**Unincorporated areas / other cities:** Contact the applicable sanitation district (e.g., Cupertino Sanitary District, West Valley Sanitation District). County Ordinance Code Title B, Division B11, Chapter IV covers on-site wastewater treatment for unincorporated areas.

**Sector note:** Significant semiconductor/electronics industry — local limits include copper, fluoride, and solvents; verify current local limits with SJ Environmental Services.

### 5. Zoning / Land Use (Unincorporated County)

**Zoning code:** Santa Clara County Ordinance Code, **Title C** (Code of Land Use and Zoning) — Appendix I contains the Zoning Ordinance. Organized into five Articles; Article 2 (Base Districts) defines district names and use tables.

**Industrial districts:** The county zoning ordinance uses its own district nomenclature (not the generic M-1/M-2 names). Industrial/manufacturing uses are regulated under base districts in Chapter 2.20 of Article 2. ## GOTCHA Notes

- **Four CUPAs in the county.** County DEH HMCD, City of Gilroy, City of Santa Clara, and City of Sunnyvale each operate independent CUPAs. Always confirm which CUPA has jurisdiction for the facility's address before filing.
- **San José is under County CUPA.** Despite being a major city, San José does not operate its own CUPA — HMCD is the CUPA of record.
- **Wastewater lead authority is the City of San José**, not the county, for the SJ/SC Regional WRF service area. Unincorporated facilities may fall under a sanitation district instead — identify the receiving POTW first.
- **SCCFD does not serve San José** for fire code purposes — San José Fire Dept is the AHJ within city limits, (408) 535-7770.
