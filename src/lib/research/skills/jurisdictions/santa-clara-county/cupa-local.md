## CUPA Agency

**Santa Clara County Department of Environmental Health (DEH) —
Hazardous Materials Compliance Division (HMCD)**

HMCD is the **own-CUPA** for most of Santa Clara County — certified directly by
CalEPA/CUPA Forum; not operating under another agency.

**Physical address:** 1555 Berger Drive, Suite 300, Floor 3, San José, CA 95112
**Phone:** (408) 918-3400
**Email:** hmcd@deh.sccgov.org
**Portal:** https://hazmat.sccgov.org/home | https://deh.santaclaracounty.gov/hazardous-materials
**Hours:** Monday–Friday 7:30 am – 5:00 pm

## Jurisdiction Coverage

HMCD serves as CUPA for:
- All **unincorporated** county areas (including Moffett Field, San Martin, Stanford)
- **City of San José** (does not operate its own CUPA)
- **Los Altos**
- **Los Altos Hills**
- **Milpitas**
- **Monte Sereno**
- **Morgan Hill**
- **Saratoga**

**Separate CUPAs (not HMCD):**
| City | Their Own CUPA |
|---|---|
| City of Gilroy | Gilroy Fire Dept / Environmental Programs |
| City of Santa Clara | Santa Clara Fire Department |
| City of Sunnyvale | Sunnyvale Environmental Services |

Always confirm the facility address against the city boundary before filing.
San José facilities go to HMCD despite San José's size.

## Local Ordinances

| Ordinance | Ordinance Code Citation | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hazardous Materials Storage Ordinance (HMSO) | Title B, Division B11, Chapter XIII | Primary permit trigger; storage quantities; emergency response; HMBP |
| Toxic Gas Ordinance (TGO) | Title B, Division B11, Chapter XIV | Additional controls for acutely toxic/highly toxic gases (sub-set of facilities) |

The HMSO applies to any facility that stores hazardous materials in HMCD's
jurisdiction. The TGO imposes stricter controls for acutely hazardous materials
(e.g., ammonia, chlorine, highly toxic gases); TGO jurisdiction is limited to
unincorporated areas, Los Altos Hills, and Monte Sereno (not the full HMCD footprint).

## Programs Administered (Unified Program Elements)

HMCD administers all six state Unified Program elements for its jurisdiction:

1. **HMBP** — Hazardous Materials Business Plan (Cal OES / CERS)
2. **HWG** — Hazardous Waste Generator / Tiered Permitting (DTSC)
3. **UST** — Underground Storage Tank Permit Program (State Water Board)
4. **APSA** — Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (State Water Board)
5. **CalARP** — California Accidental Release Prevention Program (CalEPA)
6. **HMSO/TGO** — Local hazmat storage & toxic gas permits (county ordinance)

Stormwater/urban-runoff compliance inspections are also coordinated through HMCD
for HMSO-regulated facilities.

## Electronic Reporting

All regulated businesses must use **CERS (California Environmental Reporting
System)** — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov — for HMBP, UST, APSA, and CalARP filings.
This has been mandatory since January 1, 2013.

HMCD participates in **UNIDOCS** to maintain consistency across local agency
implementations: http://www.unidocs.org/members/Santa_Clara_County_CUPA.html

## Permit Fees

HMCD invoices permit holders annually. The current fee schedule is designated
**DEH FY25 Fee Schedule** (Fiscal Year 2025). A PDF version is referenced at:
https://files.santaclaracounty.gov/migrated/DEH_FY25_Fee.pdf

Fees vary by program, permit type, and facility tier. **Specific dollar amounts are
not published on public-facing web pages** — the PDF link requires direct retrieval.
Call (408) 918-3400 or see: https://ehinfo.sccgov.org/fee-and-payment-information

The Environmental Health Permits and Fees ordinance chapter is:
**Title B, Division B11, Chapter II** (SCC Ordinance Code §§B11-25 et seq.)

## Plan Review

Facilities handling hazardous materials above HMSO threshold quantities must obtain
a **Plan Review** from HMCD before construction or modification.
Apply at: https://deh.santaclaracounty.gov/hazardous-materials/apply-plan-review-hazardous-materials-handling-facilities
