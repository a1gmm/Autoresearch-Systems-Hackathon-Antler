> AHJ is the Long Beach Fire Department, Fire Prevention Bureau. The city adopts the California Fire Code via LBMC Title 18, Chapter 18.48, with local amendments that add permit categories and tighten operational requirements. Chapter 18.48 is restated in its entirety each code cycle.

## Adopted Code Edition

- **Adopted code:** 2025 California Fire Code (CFC), Part 9 of the 2025 California Building Standards Code (Title 24, CCR).
- **Effective date:** January 1, 2026 (all new submissions must comply).
- **LBMC chapter:** Title 18 (Long Beach Building Standards Code), **Chapter 18.48** — Fire Code.
- **Adopting ordinance:** **ORD-25-0019** (adopted 2025, effective January 1, 2026) — restated Title 18 in its entirety to adopt the 2025 edition. Prior adoption: **ORD-22-0033** (adopted November 15, 2022) — adopted 2022 California Fire Code.
- **Model code basis:** The 2025 CFC is based on the 2024 International Fire Code with California amendments.

## Chapter 18.48 Structure

LBMC Chapter 18.48 adopts the CFC by reference and layers local amendments on top. Sections deleted from the CFC include 105.5.34, 112, 903.4.1 exceptions 4 and 5, 907.3.1 exception 1, and 913.4 methods 3 and 4. Appendix B is included and amended (fire flow tables).

## Key Local Amendments (ORD-25-0019 Exhibit A)

### Permit Types — §18.48.080/090
A third permit type — **Inspection Permit** — is added locally (§105.1.2 subsection 3). It covers:
- Construction, enlargement, alteration, demolition, or change of occupancy
- Fire access roadways, smoke control systems
- **High-piled storage** (non-H occupancy)
- **Hazardous materials** when not in H occupancies
- Special systems (see §105.6.31)

### Operational Permits — §18.48.120/130
Local additions to CFC §105.5 (sections 105.5.60 through 105.5.74) include:
- **105.5.61 Bulk storage facility:** operational permit required for aboveground bulk storage of flammable/combustible liquids, each 225,000 BBL or major fraction thereof.
- **105.5.64 General use permit:** catches any process or material hazardous to life/property not specifically listed elsewhere.
- **105.5.68 Marijuana facility:** operational permit for dispensary, cultivation, manufacturing, distribution.
- **105.5.71 Radioactive material:** operational permit to store or handle.

### Flammable/Combustible Liquids Threshold — §18.48.140
CFC §105.5.18 subsection (3) is amended locally to require an operational permit to store, handle, or use:
- Class II or Class IIIA liquids in excess of **25 gallons** inside a building, or **60 gallons** outside a building.
(This is a locally tightened threshold below the standard CFC provision.)

### Construction and Inspection Permits — §18.48.160
Local additions to CFC §105.6 (sections 105.6.26 through 105.6.31):
- **105.6.29 High-piled storage:** construction permit required for construction or modification of any high-piled storage area inside or outside any building or structure.
- **105.6.30 Hazardous materials (non-H occupancy):** construction permit required for installation or modification of hazardous materials when not in H occupancies.
- **105.6.31 Special systems:** construction permit required for vapor recovery systems, dust collection systems, compressed or liquefied gas manifolds, and other special systems requiring Fire Department approval.

### Declaration of Intended Use — §18.48.100
Locally added CFC §105.2.5: Any owner of any occupancy may be required to record with the LA County Recorder a **Declaration of Intended Use** stating occupancy classification of all portions of the facility. Binding on present and future owners. Unapproved changes of use can trigger immediate hearing and revocation of Certificate of Occupancy and permits.

### Fire Apparatus Access — §18.48.290/300/310
- Unobstructed width: **26 feet** (vs CFC baseline 20 feet) — §503.2.1.
- Surface: All-weather, no permeable pavers — §503.2.3.
- Turning radius: minimum **28-foot inside radius** — §503.2.4.

### Vessel/Port Jurisdiction — §18.48.030/060
CFC §101.2 is amended to extend the Fire Code to vessels moored, anchored, or berthed in waters under City jurisdiction and within Port of Long Beach boundaries.

### Records Submission — §18.48.180
All inspection, test, and maintenance reports for fire protection systems must be **electronically submitted** to LBFD via a method approved by the Fire Code Official (CFC §110.2.2).

### Fees — §18.48.170
Fees set by City Council resolution (CFC §108 as amended). Fee schedule effective October 1, 2025.

## Notes for EHS Research

- **CUPA hazmat operational permits** (HMBP/CERS) are separate from Fire Code operational permits and go through the Health Department CUPA — see cupa-local.md.
- **CalARP** is coordinated through the Fire Department (Fire Prevention Bureau) per the CUPA split structure.
- **High-piled storage** triggers both a construction permit (Fire) and potentially a building permit (Community Development).
- The locally lowered flammable-liquid threshold (25 gal inside / 60 gal outside for Class II/IIIA) is more restrictive than the default CFC table — confirm during permit scoping.
