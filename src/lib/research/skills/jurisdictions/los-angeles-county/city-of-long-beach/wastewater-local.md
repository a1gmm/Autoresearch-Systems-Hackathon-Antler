> Long Beach has its own municipal wastewater utility (Long Beach Water Department / LB Utilities). The industrial pretreatment framework operates under LBMC Title 15 (Sewers) and the LBU Rules & Regulations. The POTW issues a local sewer permit; LACSD issues the pretreatment/IWP permit — both are required.

## Rules & Regulations Framework

The LBU Rules, Regulations and Charges (effective August 1, 2023, Board Resolution) govern potable water, reclaimed water, sewer service, and the water shortage contingency plan. The industrial wastewater provisions are in **Part 13** of those Rules.

### Governing authority
Part 13 is codified in LBMC Chapter 15.20 and the LBU Rules. Board of Utilities Commissioners may change Rules by resolution; the General Manager administers.

## Local Discharge Limits — Part 13, Section 1301

The following limits apply to any person discharging industrial wastewater into a Long Beach Main Line Sewer (verbatim from Rules, Part 13, Section 1301):

| Parameter | Local Limit |
|---|---|
| Total oil and grease | ≤ 600 mg/L |
| Floatable oil and grease | ≤ 25 mg/L |
| Floatable hydrocarbon oil | ≤ 10 mg/L |
| Five-day biochemical oxygen demand (BOD5) | ≤ 1,000 mg/L |
| Suspended solids (SS) | ≤ 1,000 mg/L |
| Dissolved sulfide | ≤ 0.1 mg/L |
| pH | ≥ 6.0 (no upper limit stated in §1301) |

**Additional LBMC Chapter 15.20 prohibitions** (derived from search of §15.20.140):
- Earth, sand, rocks, ashes, gravel, plaster, concrete, glass, metal filings or objects that obstruct sewage flow
- Unshredded garbage or garbage containing broken glass
- Solid or semisolid waste from food processing (meat, fruit, vegetable) unless pretreated
- Materials that cause abnormal sulfide generation, abnormal maintenance costs, or objectionable conditions at final disposal
- Unpolluted water (refrigeration, air conditioning, industrial cooling, swimming pools) except as authorized by the General Manager

[VERIFY] — Long Beach utilities local limits for heavy metals (arsenic, cadmium, chromium, copper, lead, mercury, nickel, silver, zinc) are set by the LACSD pretreatment program (see below). LBU Rules §1301 lists the city-level general standards; LACSD establishes category-specific pretreatment limits for significant industrial users (SIUs).

## Industrial Wastewater Permit (IWP) Process

### Dual-permit requirement
Industrial users discharging to the Long Beach sewer system need BOTH:
1. **Long Beach Utilities local sewer permit** (revocable permit issued by LBU General Manager)
2. **LACSD Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit** — issued by the Sanitation Districts of Los Angeles County

### LBU local sewer permit — Section 1302

Before any person discharges industrial wastewater, they shall apply to LBU for an IWP from LACSD and submit the application to the Department along with a processing fee (per Appendix B of the Rules).

Application must include:
- Nature and scope of the industrial operation
- Quantity and characteristics of wastewater
- Detailed plans for any pretreatment facilities

LBU issues a revocable permit if the wastewater quality conforms to Parts 10–18 of the Rules and applicable statutes.

### Application materials

Applicant must submit:
- Completed Discharge Permit Application Forms A–D (available from LACSD website: https://www.lacsd.org/services/wastewater-programs-permits/industrial-waste-pretreatment-program/industrial-wastewater-discharge-permits)
- Engineering plans for any pretreatment facilities
- Processing fee per LBU Appendix B

### LACSD IWP
The LACSD Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit is issued through the LA County Sanitation Districts pretreatment program. LACSD sets federal categorical pretreatment standards plus local limits.

### Sampling and Monitoring — Section 1303

LBU may require:
- Installation of a **manhole or sampling compartment** upstream of the public sewer connection
- **Flow measuring device** and/or pH recording equipment
- Third-party inspection reports on wastewater quality or pretreatment facility performance
- Routine reports on materials processed

### Change in Permit — Section 1304

A new permit must be obtained if there are process modifications including:
- Alterations in pretreatment facilities
- Altered connections to the sewer
- Discharging additional streams or different kinds of industrial wastewater

### Proof of LACSD IWP Required for Meter Work
Before LBU installs metering or flow measurement equipment for industrial connections, the industrial customer must:
- Provide proof of an IWP through LACSD
- Submit written notification and calibration reports on a monthly basis after installation
