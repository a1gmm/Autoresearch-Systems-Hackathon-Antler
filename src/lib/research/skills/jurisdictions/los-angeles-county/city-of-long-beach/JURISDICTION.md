## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA (Unified Program)

Long Beach operates its **own city-level CUPA** — it is NOT under LA County CUPA.
The program is **split across two city departments**:

- **Health Dept — Environmental Health Bureau** handles: Hazardous Waste Generator, Hazardous Waste Tiered Permitting, Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), Underground Storage Tank (UST).
  - Address: 2525 Grand Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90815
  - Phone: (562) 570-4131 · Email: lbcupa@longbeach.gov
  - CERS filing portal: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/
  - Source: https://www.longbeach.gov/health/inspections-and-reporting/inspections/hazardous-materials-management/

- **Fire Dept — Fire Prevention Bureau** handles: California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) and emergency response coordination.
  - Address: 3205 Lakewood Blvd., Long Beach, CA 90808
  - Phone: (562) 570-2563
  - Source: https://www.longbeach.gov/health/inspections-and-reporting/inspections/hazardous-materials-management/

- CalEPA CUPA Directory entry (ID 1030): https://cersapps.calepa.ca.gov/Public/Directory/RegulatorDetails/1030

**GOTCHA:** A single facility may need to coordinate with BOTH departments — Health for HazMat/UST permitting and Fire for CalARP risk management plans.

### 2. Fire Code (AHJ: Long Beach Fire Department)

- **Adopted code:** 2025 California Fire Code (CFC), Part 9 of the 2025 California Building Standards Code.
  Effective **January 1, 2026** for new submissions. Municipal code authority: **LBMC Title 18, Chapter 18.48**.
- **Current adoption ordinance:** ORD-25-0019 (2025) — amended Ch. 18.48 to adopt 2025 CFC with local amendments.
  Prior full rewrite: ORD-22-0033 § 1(Exh. A) adopted November 15, 2022 (adopted 2022 CFC).
- **Permit types requiring Fire Prevention Bureau approval:**
  - High-piled combustible storage (construction permit — any new or modified high-pile storage area)
  - Hazardous materials storage/use/handling (construction permit for installation or modification; operational permit ongoing)
  - Spray booths, flammable/combustible liquid tanks, fire suppression systems
  - CalARP Risk Management Plans (coordinated via Fire Bureau)
  - Cannabis extraction/enrichment operations (special permit — contact Vince, 562-570-2569)
- **Fee schedule:** Fire Permit & Plan Check Fees (effective October 1, 2025); note 6% Technology Surcharge + 6% General Plan Surcharge + $115 processing fee per application.
  - Source: https://longbeach.gov/lbcd/fee-schedules/
  - Fee PDF: https://www.longbeach.gov/globalassets/lbcd/media-library/documents/building--safety/fee-schedules/fire-permit--plan-check-fees-october-1--2025
- **AHJ contact:**
  - Fire Prevention Bureau general plan review: (562) 570-6208, Mon–Fri 7:30 AM–4:30 PM (Wed opens 8:30 AM)
  - Email: LBFDrequests@longbeach.gov
  - Source: https://www.longbeach.gov/fire/fire-prevention/code-enforcement/

### 3. Industrial Wastewater / Pretreatment

- **POTW operator:** Long Beach Utilities (city-owned water/wastewater utility).
- **Local sewer use code:** LBMC **Title 15 (Public Utilities)** — sewer use regulations including industrial discharge requirements are in Chapter 15.20 (Sewers — Use Regulations) and Chapter 15.04 (Sewers — Definitions).
  - Source (Municode reference): https://library.municode.com/ca/long_beach/codes/municipal_code?nodeId=TIT15PUUT_CH15.20SEUSRE_15.20.140DASEPR
- **Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit (IWP):** Required for industrial users discharging to the city sewer system. Application includes Forms A–D from the LA County Sanitation Districts (LACSD) website plus engineering plans.
  - Submit to: Long Beach Utilities, 411 W. Ocean Blvd., Lobby Level, Long Beach, CA 90802
  - Phone: (562) 570-5700 · Hours: 7:30 AM–4:30 PM
  - IWP request page: https://www.lbutilities.org/Development-Services/Sewer-Development-Services/industrial-wastewater-discharge-permit-request
  - Rules & Regulations (2024 PDF): https://www.lbutilities.org/files/sharedassets/utilities/v/4/about-us/documents/rules-and-regs-2024.pdf
- **NPDES (receiving water):** Long Beach Water Reclamation Plant (WRP) discharges under Order R4-2022-0032 (NPDES).
  - Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/losangeles/board_decisions/adopted_orders/docs/5662_R4-2022-0032_WDR.pdf
- VERIFY — specific pretreatment local limits and surcharge thresholds: consult Rules & Regulations PDF above.

### 4. Stormwater (MS4)

- **Long Beach has its OWN individual MS4 permit** — separate from LA County's general MS4.
- **Order:** R4-2014-0024 (NPDES No. CAS004003), issued March 11, 2014 by the LA Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 4). Amended by Order R4-2014-0024-A01 (November 23, 2016).
- **Local ordinance:** LBMC Chapter 8.96 (Stormwater and Runoff Pollution Control) — requires pre-construction stormwater evaluation for new development and redevelopment subject to MS4 permit thresholds; LID regulations adopted via Ordinance ORD-10-035.
- **Regulator contact (RWQCB Region 4):**
  - Program Supervisor: Erum Razzak, Senior Environmental Scientist
  - Phone: (213) 620-2095 · Email: MS4StormwaterRB4@waterboards.ca.gov
- **City stormwater contact:** Long Beach Public Works Department
  - Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/losangeles/water_issues/programs/stormwater/municipal/longbeach.html

### 5. Zoning / Land Use (Industrial Siting)

- **Zoning code:** LBMC **Title 21 (Zoning)** — industrial districts in **Chapter 21.33**.
  Note: Title 22 (Transitional Zoning Code, adopted 2020) applies to certain areas (city core); verify which title governs the subject parcel.
- **Industrial district names (Title 21):**
  - **IL** — Light Industrial
  - **IM** — Medium Industrial
  - **IG** — General Industrial
  - **IP** — Port-related Industrial
  - Neo-industrial (Title 22): **NI-1**, **NI-2**
- **CUP:** Outdoor storage in any industrial zone requires a Conditional Use Permit (CUP) per LBMC Ch. 21.33. CUPs processed through the Planning Bureau.
- **Planning Bureau contact:**
  - Phone: (562) 570-6194
  - Address: 411 W. Ocean Blvd., Long Beach, CA 90802
  - Hours: Mon–Fri 8 AM–4 PM (Wed 9 AM–4 PM); closed noon–1 PM daily
  - Zoning map / districts: https://longbeach.gov/lbcd/planning/current/zoning/established/
  - Zoning code (Municode): https://library.municode.com/ca/long_beach/codes/municipal_code?nodeId=TIT21ZO_CH21.33INDI
  - Source: https://longbeach.gov/lbcd/planning/current/zoning/established/
