> Two zoning codes exist in parallel. Title 21 is the primary industrial code (Chapter 21.33); Title 22 (Transitional Zoning Code, adopted 2020) applies to the city core. Verify which title governs the subject parcel before researching use permissions. All outdoor storage in any industrial zone requires a CUP.

## Governing Zoning Code

- **Primary code:** LBMC **Title 21 — Zoning Regulations**; industrial districts in **Chapter 21.33**
- **Secondary/transitional code:** LBMC **Title 22 — Transitional Zoning Code** (adopted 2020) — applies to city core areas; industrial equivalent districts are NI-1 and NI-2 (Neo-Industrial zones). See Chapter 22.41 (NI Zone Development Standards).
- **Zoning map / parcel lookup:** https://longbeach.gov/lbcd/planning/current/zoning/established/
- **Chapter 21.33 on Municode:** https://library.municode.com/ca/long_beach/codes/municipal_code?nodeId=TIT21ZO_CH21.33INDI

## Industrial Zone Districts (Title 21, Chapter 21.33)

| District Code | Name | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **IL** | Light Industrial | Minimal environmental impact manufacturing and related operations |
| **IM** | Medium Industrial | More intensive manufacturing and processing than Light Industrial |
| **IG** | General Industrial | Comprehensive industrial uses including heavy manufacturing and processing |
| **IP** | Port-related Industrial | Maritime operations and logistics; Port of Long Beach activities |

Title 22 neo-industrial zones: **NI-1**, **NI-2** (city core areas). Standards at LBMC Chapter 22.41.

### Outdoor Storage (all industrial zones)
**All outdoor storage in any industrial zone (IL, IM, IG, IP) requires a Conditional Use Permit** per LBMC Chapter 21.33. This applies to containers, raw materials, finished goods, and equipment stored outdoors. Source: Information Bulletin IB-030 (Rev. 01-24-2025), Community Development Department.

### Temporary Activating Use (Use Category 16 — added ORD-20-0018)
Permitted as a temporary use (T) in all industrial zones: IL, IM, IG, IP.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

- **Hearing body:** Zoning Administrator (initial); appeals to Planning Commission; further appeals to City Council (per Table 21-1, ORD-20-0018).
- **Application filing:** Planning Bureau, 411 W. Ocean Blvd., 3rd Floor, Long Beach, CA 90802
  - Phone: (562) 570-6194
  - Hours: Mon–Fri 8 AM–4 PM (Wed 9 AM–4 PM); closed noon–1 PM daily
- **Required notice:** Yes (published for CUP applications — see LBMC §21.21.302)
- **Procedure chapter:** LBMC Chapter 21.52 — Conditional Uses (Municode: https://library.municode.com/ca/long_beach/codes/municipal_code?nodeId=TIT21ZO_CH21.52COUS)
- **Planning inquiry portal:** https://longbeach.gov/lbcd/planning/current/zoning/
- **CUP application procedures:** LBMC Chapter 21.21 (§21.21.201 — Application)

## Administrative Use Permit (AP)

Some industrial uses require an Administrative Use Permit (AP) instead of a full CUP. The AP is heard by the Zoning Administrator without Planning Commission involvement. Appeals go to the Zoning Administrator, then to City Council (see Table 21-1).

## Intermodal Shipping Containers as Accessory Storage (IB-030)

A specific policy governs intermodal containers used as storage in industrial zones:
- **All outdoor storage** (including containers) in industrial zones requires a CUP per Chapter 21.33 — no exception.
- Containers may NOT contain hazardous materials unless approved by the Fire Code Official.
- Containers may NOT contain food products unless approved by the Health Official.
- Containers may NOT be stacked, modified with utilities, exceed 9'-6" H × 8'-0" W × 40'-0" L, or be permanently anchored.
- Planning Bureau approval required; Building & Safety Bureau permit NOT required if all IB-030 criteria met.
- Source: IB-030, Community Development Department (effective 02-18-2008, revised 01-24-2025).

## Title 22 Neo-Industrial Zones (City Core)

Title 22 applies to parcels in the city core where the city has initiated zone-in procedures. NI-1 and NI-2 districts are the neo-industrial equivalent of Title 21's industrial districts. Permitted uses and development standards are at LBMC Chapter 22.41 (NI Zone Development Standards). Verify the applicable title via the zoning map before researching permitted uses.
