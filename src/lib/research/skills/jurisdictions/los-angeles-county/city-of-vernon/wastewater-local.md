## Summary

Industrial wastewater from Vernon facilities discharges to the **Sanitation Districts of Los Angeles County (LACSD)** regional sewer system. Two levels of authority apply: (1) **Vernon VMC Chapter 13.24** (Sewer System) governs local permit requirements and illicit-discharge prohibitions; and (2) **LACSD's Wastewater Ordinance** (the regional pretreatment ordinance) sets the discharge limits and pretreatment standards that permittees must meet. An industrial wastewater discharge permit requires approval from **both** the City of Vernon and LACSD.

## LACSD Wastewater Ordinance (Regional Pretreatment)

LACSD's Wastewater Ordinance (originally enacted April 1, 1972; major amendment July 1, 1998) provides the regulatory backbone for industrial pretreatment requirements applicable to Vernon dischargers. Key provisions:

- **Section 305**: Stormwater/groundwater disposal — permitted by Chief Engineer only in limited situations.
- **Section 406**: Comprehensive list of **prohibited wastes** — must not be discharged in any amount.
- **Section 410 / 422**: Wastewater treatment surcharge formula: `Surcharge = a(V) + b(COD) + c(SS) + d(M)(P)`, where V = annual flow (million gallons), COD = chemical oxygen demand (1,000 lb), SS = suspended solids (1,000 lb), P = peak flow rate (gpm, 8am–10pm window).

## Local Discharge Limits (Phase I — Universal)

LACSD enforces a set of Phase I local limits applicable to **all** dischargers (not just permit holders). These may not be exceeded at any time:

| Parameter | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| pH (lower bound) | **≥ 6.0** at all times |
| Dissolved sulfide | **≤ 0.1 mg/L** at all times |
| Temperature | **≤ 140°F** at point of introduction to collection system; **≤ 104°F** at POTW treatment plant influent |

### Prohibited Waste Categories (§ 406 — Partial List)

The following are prohibited in any amount or in excess of Chief Engineer-defined quantities:

- Gasoline, benzene, naphtha, solvent, fuel oil, or any liquid/solid/gas creating flammable/explosive conditions (exceeding the lower explosive limit at the approved monitoring location).
- Wastewater with pH **< 6.0** or with corrosive characteristics injurious to personnel or infrastructure.
- Solids or viscous substances causing flow obstruction (includes asphalt, tar, plastics, metal, glass, ashes, sand, mud, paunch manure, hair/fleshings, and materials that solidify in sewer).
- Cyanide in excessive quantities (as defined by Chief Engineer).
- Excessive BOD, COD, or other oxygen-demanding substances.
- Mercaptans, sulfides, phenols, or strongly odorous materials.
- Dissolved sulfides above **0.1 mg/L** or wastes contributing to excessive sulfide production.
- Dissolved silica, aluminum, or high-pH substances causing incrustation or scale.
- Wastes at ≥ **140°F** or causing plant influent to exceed **104°F**.
- Toxic organics, chlorinated hydrocarbons, or organic phosphorus-type compounds.
- Excessive deionized water, steam condensate, or distilled water.
- Iron, manganese, boron, chromium, phenols, plastic resins, copper, nickel, zinc, lead, mercury, cadmium, selenium, silver, arsenic, or other materials toxic to humans, animals, or biological treatment processes.
- Cooling tower blowdown exceeding one-third of makeup water.

**Note**: LACSD's full Phase I numerical local limits table (metals in mg/L) is published on the LACSD Discharge Limits page (https://www.lacsd.org/services/wastewater-programs-permits/industrial-waste-pretreatment-program/industrial-waste-policies/discharge-limits) but is not reproduced in public-facing documents; contact LACSD Industrial Waste Section for the current table or for permit-specific limits.

## Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit (IWP) Process

1. **Start with Vernon**: Submit initial application to the **City of Vernon Public Works / City Engineer** for approval.
2. **LACSD review**: Application forwarded to LACSD; both City Engineer and LACSD review and may impose conditions and fees.
3. **Permit issued by LACSD**: The Permit for Industrial Wastewater Discharge is issued by LACSD after both approvals. Permit may include monitoring requirements, self-reporting obligations, and pretreatment conditions.
4. **Ongoing**: Annual wastewater treatment surcharge billed by LACSD based on flow, COD, and suspended solids measurements.

**LACSD online application status**: As of research date, verify current portal availability directly with LACSD — online versus paper submission status should be confirmed before applying.
