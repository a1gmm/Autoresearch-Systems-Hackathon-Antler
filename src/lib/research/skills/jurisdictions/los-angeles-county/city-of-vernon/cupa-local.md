## Summary

Vernon operates one of only **four municipal CUPAs in California**. CalEPA certified the **Department of Health & Environmental Control (DHEC)** as Vernon's CUPA on **March 1, 1997**. All six unified program elements are administered directly by DHEC — not by LACoFD, not by LA County. Despite LACoFD serving as the fire AHJ, Vernon DHEC retains CUPA authority independently.

## Programs Administered by DHEC

DHEC administers all six unified program elements under SB 1082:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — Business plans for facilities handling hazardous materials above reporting thresholds. Submitted electronically via CERS.
2. **California Accidental Release Prevention (Cal-ARP)** — For facilities handling regulated substances above federal/state threshold quantities.
3. **Hazardous Waste Generator / Tiered Permitting** — For any business generating hazardous waste; tiered permit based on treatment type.
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — For facilities storing petroleum or fractions thereof at ≥ 1,320 gallons in aboveground tanks/containers.
5. **Underground Storage Tank (UST)** — Permitting, installation, modification, operation, and closure of USTs.
6. **Hazardous Materials (Unified Fire Code)** — Fire-code-based hazardous materials permit component (note: fire inspection AHJ is LACoFD, but the CUPA hazmat permit program stays with DHEC).

## Municipal Code Chapters

- **VMC Chapter 8.36** — Hazardous Materials: establishes a monitoring program for establishments producing, storing, handling, disposing, treating, emitting, discharging, or recycling hazardous materials.
- **VMC Chapter 8.40** — Underground Storage of Hazardous Substances: covers construction, operation, maintenance, monitoring, and testing of USTs.
- **VMC Chapter 8 (Business License Tax provision)** — Hazardous waste treatment/storage/disposal facilities: fee of 4% of annual gross receipts for inorganic hazardous waste; 1% for organic hazardous waste and infectious waste; minimum $5,000 total per calendar year.

Source for Chapter 8.36/8.40: https://ecode360.com/44470370 (VMC Hazardous Materials, eCode360 — access may require direct browser session)

## HMBP Reporting Thresholds

State thresholds apply (Vernon has not published lower local thresholds in available online materials):

| Phase | Solid | Liquid | Gas |
|-------|-------|--------|-----|
| State minimum | 500 lb | 55 gal | 200 cu ft |

Businesses must file in **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System) at https://cers.calepa.ca.gov before operating with threshold quantities on-site. DHEC receives and reviews Vernon submissions through CERS.

## Fee Schedule

Vernon DHEC's CUPA fee schedule is **not published online** as of the research date. The business license tax provision (4%/1% of gross receipts for hazardous waste TSD) is codified in VMC Chapter 8. For current annual CUPA program fees (HMBP, Cal-ARP, UST, APSA, hazardous waste generator), contact DHEC directly.

- **Contact for fees**: DHEC, (310) 583-8811 ext. 280
- **State surcharges** (billed by CalEPA, apply to all CUPAs):
  - Oversight surcharge: $94/year
  - Aboveground tank facility: $26/year
  - Underground storage tank: $20/year
  - Regulated Cal-ARP program: $370/year

## CERS Filing Portal

---

## Key Gotcha

Vernon DHEC is the CUPA for ALL six program elements. Even though LACoFD handles fire inspections, the CUPA-issued **Unified Program Facility Permit** comes from DHEC, not from LACoFD. Facilities in Vernon should direct CUPA inquiries (HMBP filings, hazardous waste generator permits, UST permits, Cal-ARP permits) exclusively to DHEC.
