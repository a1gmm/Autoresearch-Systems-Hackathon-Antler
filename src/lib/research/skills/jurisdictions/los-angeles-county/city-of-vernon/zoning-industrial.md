## Summary

Vernon's zoning is codified in **VMC Title 17** (Zoning Ordinance). The city is essentially a mono-zone industrial city: the primary base zone is the **General Industry (I) Zone**, which covers the vast majority of the ~5 sq-mi city. Several overlay zones and two mixed-use district zones account for the remainder. There is no residential zone; no planning commission — all CUP decisions go directly to **City Council**.

## General Industry (I) Zone — VMC § 17.22

### Permitted Uses (by right — § 17.22.020, partial list)

- Most industrial uses
- Data centers
- Cold storage warehouses
- Industrial gas manufacturing
- Telecommunications antenna/cell towers
- Warehouse use (other than cold storage) on lots **< 150,000 sq ft**

### Conditional Uses (CUP required — § 17.22.030, partial list)

- Refineries
- Generating facilities, power plants, cogeneration facilities
- Trash-to-energy facilities
- Petroleum-related uses and petroleum storage facilities
- Recycling facilities
- Trade schools
- Public utilities
- All uses not specifically permitted and not specifically prohibited (catch-all CUP requirement per Title 17)

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

**Decision-maker**: Vernon **City Council** (no planning commission; Council acts directly).

**Application package** (per § 17 CUP provisions and Planning Division guidance):

1. Operations plan describing the use, hours, equipment, and processes
2. Hazardous waste inventory plan (for any hazmat-generating operations)
3. Traffic report
4. CEQA application and environmental checklist (Initial Study or CEQA exemption as applicable)

**Process steps**:

1. Submit application to **Planning Division**, 2724 Leonis Blvd, Vernon, CA 90058.
2. Planning Division staff evaluates application, prepares staff report.
3. Public hearing scheduled before City Council.
4. City Council renders decision: **approve / approve with conditions / deny**.

**Online portal**: As of research date, no eTRAKiT or dedicated online CUP portal was confirmed for Vernon. Applications are submitted directly to the Planning Division. Confirm current submission method with the division before applying.

**Planning Division contact**: (323) 583-3313 | 2724 Leonis Blvd, Vernon, CA 90058

## Santa Fe South (MU-S) Zone — Industrial Uses (§ 17.24)

The MU-S zone explicitly allows a broad range of industrial uses by right (no CUP needed), including:

- R&D laboratories and commercial testing labs
- Computer, circuit board, and semiconductor manufacturing
- Research hospitals/labs (with medical waste)
- EV recharge facilities
- Auto body shops
- Gas dispensing facilities
- Metal plating shops
- Materials manufacturing
- Large manufacturing and assembly plants
- Industrial machinery
- Energy and utility operations
- Warehousing, logistics, mini-distribution centers

This makes the MU-S zone one of the most industrially permissive areas in Vernon while accommodating creative/studio uses alongside heavy industry.

---

## Gotchas

- **No planning commission**: CUP decisions skip the commission step — it's staff report → City Council hearing → Council vote. Timeline can be faster than typical city processes, but requires direct Council approval.
- **Warehouse size break**: Warehouse use is permitted by right in the I Zone only on lots **< 150,000 sq ft**. Larger warehouse lots likely require a CUP or different entitlement.
- **Hazmat inventory plan required**: Any CUP application involving hazardous materials must include a hazardous waste inventory plan as part of the application package.
