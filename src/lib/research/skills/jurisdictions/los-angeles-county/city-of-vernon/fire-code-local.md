## Summary

Vernon disbanded its own fire department on **October 21, 2020**; fire prevention and code enforcement authority (AHJ) transferred to the **LA County Consolidated Fire Protection District (LACoFD)**. The operative fire code is **LACoFD Title 32**, not a Vernon-specific adoption. Vernon's own VMC Chapter 7 adopted the 2016 CFC via **Ordinance No. 1237** — that chapter is superseded in practice by the LACoFD code for any facility in the District.

---

## Operative Code: LA County Title 32 (2023 County Fire Code)

- **Base edition**: 2022 California Fire Code (CCR Title 24, Part 9), incorporating by reference the 2021 International Fire Code.
- **Adopting action**: LA County Board of Supervisors introduced the ordinance January 10, 2023; operative date 30 days after adoption (effective **~March 2023**). The previous Title 32 was based on the 2019 CFC.
- **Title 32 text**: https://library.municode.com/ca/los_angeles_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT32FICO
- **UpCodes viewer**: https://up.codes/viewer/los-angeles-county/ca-fire-code-2022

### Adopted Appendices (Title 32 § 101.2.1)

From 2022 CFC: **Appendix B, BB, C, CC, H**.
LA County additions: **Appendix O, PP, QQ, RR**.

- Appendix QQ = **Fire-Code Fee Schedule** (fees referenced at § 105.1.1.1).

| Section | Topic | LA County Amendment |

### Hazardous Materials Permit (§ 105.5.22 / Table 105.5.22)

An **operational permit** is required to store, transport on-site, dispense, use, or handle hazardous materials in **excess of the amounts in Table 105.5.22** of the 2022 CFC. The table includes thresholds by hazard category (combustible liquids, corrosives, explosives, flammable liquids/gases/solids, highly toxic, organic peroxides, oxidizers, pyrophorics, reactives, water-reactives). Permits are issued by LACoFD Prevention.

In addition, CUPA unified program facility permits (HMBP) are separately required by Title 12 — those are administered by **Vernon DHEC** (not LACoFD), since Vernon is its own CUPA. See `cupa-local.md`.

### High-Piled Storage Permit (§ 105.5.24)

- Threshold: **> 500 sq ft** of high-piled combustible storage area (including aisles).
- Submission: Letter (material type, amount, storage method) + floor plan with stockpile/aisle locations.
- Form: LACoFD **Form 194** — Owner's Statement of Intended Use, High-Piled Combustible Storage.

## Vernon's Own Ordinance (Superseded for Fire AHJ Purposes)

- **Ordinance No. 1237** (adopted 2017): Amends VMC Chapters 7 (Fire Regulations) and 24 (Building and Construction); adopts by reference the **2016 California Fire Code** including Appendices A, B, BB, D, and H.
- **VMC Chapter 7** is the local codification, but enforcement authority passed to LACoFD in October 2020. Building permits for new construction in Vernon still reference the city's adopted code edition, which as of this research remains referenced to Ord. 1237. Verify current edition with Vernon Building Division.
- Source: https://www.cityofvernonca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/62/637606896069100000 (access blocked; confirmed via city building permit page)
