> Local EHS authority reference for **City of Vernon**. Vernon is a ~5 sq-mi all-industrial city in SE Los Angeles County. It runs its own CUPA and Phase I MS4 program; fire AHJ transferred to LA County Fire in Oct 2020. Verify live pages before quoting — local code editions update on 3-year cycles.

## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA (Hazardous Materials Unified Program)

- **Own CUPA** — Vernon DHEC (Dept. of Health & Environmental Control) has been the city's CUPA since March 1, 1997; one of only four municipal CUPAs in California.
- **Programs administered locally by DHEC**: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generator, Cal-ARP, Aboveground Storage Tank (AST), Underground Storage Tank (UST).
- **HMBP filing**: Electronic submission via CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) at cers.calepa.ca.gov — required before operating with threshold quantities.
- **DHEC contact**: 4305 S. Santa Fe Ave, Vernon, CA 90058 | (310) 583-8811 ext. 280
- **Fee info**: [VERIFY — fee schedule not found online; contact DHEC directly]

### 2. Fire Code & Fire Permits (AHJ: LA County Fire)

- **AHJ**: Los Angeles County Fire Department — Vernon Fire Department was disbanded **October 21, 2020** and service transferred to LACoFD. Do NOT reference a Vernon FD.
- **Vernon station**: Station 52/3 at 4305 S. Santa Fe Ave area; Vernon Fire District is now an LACoFD station.
- **Adopted code**: Vernon Municipal Code **Chapter 7** adopts the California Fire Code by reference. LACoFD enforces **Title 32** of the LA County Code (2023 County Fire Code, based on 2022 CFC edition) with local amendments.
- **Permit types**: Hazardous materials operations, Cal-ARP facilities, aboveground/underground tank installations, high-piled combustible storage (Form 194 — Owner's Statement of Intended Use).
- **Industrial/hazmat permit contact**: LACoFD Petroleum/Chemical Unit (East Regional Operations), 5200 Irwindale Ave, Irwindale, CA 91706 | (626) 430-3040
- **Fee schedule**: https://fire.lacounty.gov/fire-prevention-fees/

### 3. Industrial Wastewater / Pretreatment (POTW: LACSD)

- **POTW/receiving agency**: Sanitation Districts of Los Angeles County (LACSD) — Vernon discharges to the LACSD regional sewer system.
- **Local ordinance**: Vernon Municipal Code **Chapter 13.24** (Sewer System) — requires an industrial wastewater discharge permit before any industrial wastewater discharge; prohibits excessive flow rates and illicit discharges.
- **IWP process**: Application submitted to the City of Vernon first for approval, then forwarded to LACSD; both City Engineer and LACSD review and may impose conditions and fees.
- **LACSD contact for IWP**: (562) 699-7411 | https://www.lacsd.org/services/wastewater-programs-permits/industrial-waste-pretreatment-program/industrial-wastewater-discharge-permits

### 4. Stormwater (MS4 / NPDES)

- **MS4 type**: Vernon holds its own **Phase I MS4** NPDES permit — not covered under the LA County-wide Phase II general permit.
- **NPDES permit**: LA Regional Water Quality Control Board Order No. **R4-2012-0175**, NPDES Permit No. **CAS004001** (Coastal Watersheds of Los Angeles County MS4 permit; Vernon is a co-permittee).
- **Local ordinance**: Stormwater illicit-discharge prohibition and industrial/commercial BMP requirements codified in Vernon Municipal Code **Chapter 13.24** (Sewer System — illicit discharge, non-stormwater discharge, and pollutant control provisions).
- **Administering dept**: Vernon DHEC handles stormwater compliance enforcement for the city.
- **Contact**: DHEC, 4305 S. Santa Fe Ave, Vernon, CA 90058 | (323) 583-3313 (City Hall general)
- Source: https://www.cityofvernonca.gov/government/health-and-environmental-control/stormwater/ms4-permit
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/losangeles/water_issues/programs/stormwater/municipal/

### 5. Zoning / Land Use (Planning Division)

- **Zoning code**: Vernon Municipal Code **Title 17** (Zoning Ordinance).
- **Primary industrial zone**: **General Industry (I) Zone** — the only primary zone; covers effectively the entire city. Designed to accommodate most industrial uses including those incompatible with adjacent jurisdictions.
- **Overlay zones**: T — Truck & Freight Terminal Overlay; C-1 and C-2 Commercial Overlays; MU-S — Santa Fe South Mixed-Use District (allows industrial by right).
- **CUP process**: Uses not permitted by right require a **Conditional Use Permit (CUP)**. Application includes: operations plan, hazardous waste inventory plan, traffic report, CEQA checklist. Staff prepares report; public hearing held; **Vernon City Council** makes the final decision (approve / approve with conditions / deny). [No planning commission — goes directly to Council.]
- **Planning Division contact**: 2724 Leonis Blvd, Vernon, CA 90058 | (323) 583-3313
- **Application portal**: [VERIFY — check https://www.cityofvernonca.gov/government/planning-division for current application forms and eTRAKiT/portal status]
