## Governing Code

**Title:** Los Angeles County Code **Title 22 — Planning and Zoning** (also called the "Zoning Ordinance" or "Zoning Code")
**Administering agency:** LA County Department of Regional Planning (DRP)
**Address:** 320 West Temple Street, Los Angeles CA 90012
**Phone:** (213) 974-6411 | **Email:** info@planning.lacounty.gov
**Municode:** https://library.municode.com/ca/los_angeles_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT22PLZO

Industrial zones are governed by **Chapter 22.22 — Industrial Zones** (within Division 2 of Title 22).

## Industrial Zone Districts

Defined in **Section 22.22.020** (Table 22.22.020-A):

| Abbreviation | Full Name | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **M-1** | Light Manufacturing | Light industrial, assembly, limited outdoor nuisance |
| **M-1.5** | Restricted Heavy Manufacturing | Intermediate; more intensive than M-1 |
| **M-2** | Heavy Manufacturing | Heavy industrial operations |
| **M-2.5** | Aircraft, Heavy Industrial | Airport/aircraft manufacturing; loud noise/heavy vibration |
| **M-3** | Unclassified | Most permissive; allows nearly any use except explicitly prohibited |
| **D-2** | (Warehouse Zone) | ≥ 80% floor area for storage/warehousing |

## Land Use Regulations — Zones M-1, M-1.5, M-2, M-2.5 (Section 22.22.030)

**Permit/Review Categories** (Table 22.22.030-A):
- **P** — Permitted use (by right)
- **SPR** — Site Plan Review (ministerial)
- **DSP** — Discretionary Site Plan Review
- **CUP** — Conditional Use Permit (Chapter 22.158)

**Key industrial permitted/SPR uses in M-1 through M-2** (Table 22.22.030-B, selected):
- Assembly plants — P
- Plastic products including molding — SPR in M-1 (footnote 9 applies)
- Stone, marble, granite grinding/dressing/cutting — P in M-2+
- Canneries (excluding meat/fish) — SPR
- Fur products — SPR
- Lubricating oil canning/packaging (≤ 100 barrels above-ground) — P
- Automobile wrecking yards — requires CUP or DSP depending on zone
- Billboards — SPR
- Laboratories for testing experimental motion picture film — P

**Accessory uses** (Table 22.22.030-C):
- Access to property lawfully used for non-M-zone purpose — DSP in M-1; P in M-1.5/M-2
- Automobile washing/waxing accessory to auto service stations — P

**Prohibited uses in M-1, M-1.5, M-2, M-2.5** (Section 22.22.030.E):
1. Adult day care centers
2. Business and professional schools (art, barber, beauty, dance, drama, music)
3. Colleges and universities
4. Community gardens
5. Family child care homes (large and small)
6. Foster family homes
7. Hospitals
8. Hotels

## Zone M-3 — Unclassified (Section 22.22.040)

**Permitted uses:** Any use, including signs per Chapter 22.114, EXCEPT uses listed below.

**Requires Discretionary Site Plan Review (Chapter 22.190):**
- Automobile dismantling yards
- Boiler works
- Grading projects (off-site transport up to 100,000 cubic yards)

**Requires CUP (Chapter 22.158):**
- Any M-2 use that required CUP (same limitations apply)
- Institutions for care of alcoholic/mental patients
- Mobilehomes used as caretaker residences (per Section 22.140.140)

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

**Governing chapter:** Title 22, **Chapter 22.158 — Conditional Use Permits**
**Definition (from Title 22):** "Uses which because of characteristics peculiar to them, or because of size, technological processes, or types of equipment, or because of their location with reference to surroundings, street or highway width, traffic generation, or other demands on public services, require discretionary consideration relative to placement at specific locations in the zones where classified to ensure proper integration with other existing or permitted uses."

**Approval body:**
- Director of Regional Planning for most CUPs in industrial zones.
- Regional Planning Commission for larger or more complex projects (scope-dependent under Title 22).

**Application filing:** https://planning.lacounty.gov/how-do-i/file-an-application/
**Find zoning:** https://planning.lacounty.gov/how-do-i/find-my-zoning/
**Connect with a planner:** https://planning.lacounty.gov/connect-with-a-planner/

**Conditions of approval:** Governed by Section 22.158.060. Conditions of use are mandatory prerequisites and are binding on the property.

**Special/Temporary Uses:** "Special Use Permit" and "Special permit" references in Title 22 are construed to mean a Conditional Use Permit (per Title 22 definitions).

## Zone Change / General Plan Consistency

Zone changes resulting from the 2015 General Plan Update zoning consistency program are governed by Section 22.16.225 — applicants may choose whether pending applications are reviewed under the old or new zone/regulations.

## Development Standards

Governed by Division 6 of Title 22 (Development Standards). Key measurement rules:
- Heights measured as plumb-line distance from point to grade.
- Floor area ratio (FAR) = above-ground gross floor area / total lot area, rounded to tenths.
- Lot coverage includes footprints of all principal and accessory buildings; excludes uncovered walkways, driveways, landscaping, and swimming pools not in enclosed structures.

Specific setback and height limits for industrial zones are in Chapter 22.22 sections following 22.22.030 (not fully extracted here — consult Municode or contact DRP).
