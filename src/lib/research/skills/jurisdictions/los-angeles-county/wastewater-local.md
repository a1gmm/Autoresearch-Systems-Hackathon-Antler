## POTW / Sanitation Agency

**Agency:** Los Angeles County Sanitation Districts (LACSD) — 24 separate but jointly administered sanitation districts covering most of unincorporated LA County and many incorporated cities.
**Administrative District:** District No. 2 (District No. 2 holds sole authority to initiate civil enforcement actions on behalf of all districts.)
**Address:** 1955 Workman Mill Road, Whittier, CA 90601-1400 (P.O. Box 4998, Whittier, CA 90607-4998)
**Industrial Waste Section:** (562) 699-7411 | Fax: (562) 699-5422
**Program page:** https://www.lacsd.org/services/wastewater-programs-permits/industrial-waste-pretreatment-program

Note: The City of Los Angeles has a separate POTW (LA Sanitation / LASAN) with its own Industrial Waste Management Division — LACSD does not serve the City of LA proper.

## Governing Ordinance

**Short title:** "Wastewater Ordinance"

**Full formal title:** "An Ordinance Providing for the Administration of an Industrial Wastewater Control System; for the Regulation of Sewer Construction and Sewer Use; for the Imposition of Permit Requirements for Industrial Wastewater Dischargers; for the Prohibition, Regulation and Pretreatment of Industrial Wastewaters; for the Imposition of Fees and Charges; for the Distribution of Revenue; for the Implementation of Federal and State Pollution Control Regulations and for the Implementation of Other Methods of Controlling and Regulating the Discharge of Wastewaters"

**Amendment history (as stated in Section 104):**
- Original ordinance: April 1, 1972
- Amended: July 1, 1975; July 1, 1980; July 1, 1983; November 1, 1989
- Current codification: **as amended July 1, 1998** (supersedes all prior versions)
- The ordinance is not assigned a traditional municipal-code chapter number by LACSD — it is enacted directly by each District's Board of Directors under California Health & Safety Code §§ 4700–4859 and Government Code §§ 54725–54740.

## Section 406 — Prohibited Discharge Categories (Verbatim from Ordinance)

No discharge of:
- Any gasoline, benzene, naphtha, solvent, fuel oil, or any liquid/solid/gas causing or tending to cause flammable or explosive conditions at the approved monitoring location or in the sewerage system.
- Wastes adversely affecting sewers, maintenance personnel, treatment equipment, treatment processes, or quality of plant effluent or residue.
- Wastes causing or contributing to NPDES permit violations, New Source Performance Standards (40 CFR Part 60) violations, National Emissions Standards for Hazardous Air Pollutants (40 CFR Part 61) violations, or SCAQMD standards violations.
- Wastes adversely affecting water reclamation processes or quality of reclaimed water.

## Wastewater Treatment Surcharge

Quarterly estimated payments due: September 30, December 31, March 31; final true-up due August 15 each year.
Late penalty: 1% per day on original amount, not to exceed 10%; additional interest at 3% over prime rate.

Surcharge/connection fee information: https://www.lacsd.org/services/wastewater-programs-permits/industrial-waste-pretreatment-program/surcharge-and-connection-fee-programs

## Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit (IWP) Process

1. Obtain application from LACSD offices or local sewering agency (lateral sewer agency having jurisdiction in discharge area).
2. Local sewering agency reviews and forwards application plus plans/data to LACSD.
3. LACSD reviews; may require additional information; determines connection fee obligation.
4. Connection fee paid to LACSD before permit issued.
5. Application signed by both local sewering agency and LACSD; copy returned to applicant — constitutes the valid Permit.
6. Permit is non-transferable (location or ownership change requires new permit).
7. Significant flow/quality changes trigger immediate Permit revision application.
