## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials / Hazardous Waste / UST / APSA / CalARP

**AHJ:** Los Angeles Fire Department (LAFD) — the City of Los Angeles has its own CUPA (distinct from LA County Fire HHMD, which covers unincorporated areas and some contract cities).

**Legal authority:** CA Health & Safety Code § 6.95, Division 20; LAMC Article 7, Chapter V, §§ 120 and 120.1.4; CA Code of Regulations Title 19 §§ 2620-2732 and Title 24 Part 9 § 80.115.

**Programs consolidated under Annual CUPA Permit:**
- Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) — solids ≥ 500 lb, liquids ≥ 55 gal, gases ≥ 200 cu ft
- Hazardous Waste Generator
- Underground Storage Tanks (UST)
- Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)
- CalARP (California Accidental Release Prevention)

**Application portal:** CERS — https://cersbusiness3.calepa.ca.gov/Account/SignIn  
Annual HMBP certification window: January 1 – March 1 each year.

**Two permit types:**
1. Annual Consolidated CUPA Permit — all ongoing CUPA programs; invoice issued after CERS HMBP submission.
2. CUPA Project Permit — UST installation/modification/removal/abandonment; flammable/combustible liquid fuel transfers.

**FY 2025/2026 Unified Program Fee Schedule** (effective date updated January 5, 2026):
- HMBP — tiered by chemical inventory count: 1–3 chemicals $489 → 26+ chemicals $2,095
- UST annual permit: $1,257/tank; installation plan check $4,548 + $379/hr
- APSA (non-qualified ≥ 10,000 gal): $1,497
- CalARP administrative fee: $1,947 + $18.60/risk unit; inspection/review $375/hr
- Hazardous Waste Generator — tiered by employees: 0–2 $693 / 3–5 $1,010 / 6–19 $1,443 / 20–100 $1,948 / 101–500 $2,886 / 500+ $4,906

### 2. Fire Code / Operational Fire Permits

**AHJ:** Los Angeles Fire Department (LAFD), Fire Prevention & Public Safety Division.

**Adopted code:** 2023 Los Angeles Fire Code, based on the 2022 California Fire Code (CFC 2022) with LA-specific amendments. Effective: October 4, 2023 (Ordinance 187994). Codified in LAMC Article 7, Chapter V (LAFC).

**Key local additions:** LAFC Chapter 47 (Occupancy-Based Requirements); Chapter 48 (Motion Picture & Television Production Studio Sound Stages) — LA-specific chapters with no CFC equivalent.

**Hazmat operational permits:** Separate operational fire permit required for hazardous materials storage/use at regulated facilities; obtained through the LAFD Fire Prevention Bureau. CUPA Annual Permit and operational fire permits are distinct; some facilities need both.

**High-piled storage permits:** Issued by LAFD Fire Prevention Bureau for storage > 12 ft in height (per CFC Chapter 32).

**Payment/fee portal:** https://epay.lacity.org/lafd/  
**Full code text (UpCodes):** https://up.codes/viewer/los_angeles/ca-fire-code-2022  
**LAFD Fire Code page:** https://lafd.org/fire-prevention/fire-code

### 3. Industrial Wastewater / Pretreatment

**POTW/agency:** LA Sanitation (LASAN) — Industrial Waste Management Division (IWMD).  
**Authority:** LAMC § 64.30 (Industrial Wastewater Disposal) — the City's sewer-use ordinance. Discharge to the City POTW is a privilege, not a right (§ 64.30 policy statement).

**Permit requirement:** No discharge of industrial wastewater to the City POTW without an Industrial Wastewater Permit (IWP). Significant Industrial Users (SIUs) must obtain a full IWP; Local Industrial Users (LIUs) may qualify for a simplified permit.

**Regulated parameters (common):** BOD, TSS, oil & grease, pH, metals (Cd, Cr, Cu, Pb, Ni, Zn), cyanide; specific limits set per permit.

**Application portals:**
- IWP application: https://sanitation.lacity.gov/san/faces/home/portal/s-lsh-wwd/s-lsh-wwd-cw/s-lsh-wwd-cw-iwm/iwp

### 4. Stormwater — MS4 / Urban Runoff

**MS4 Permit:** NPDES Permit No. CAS004001 (Order No. R4-2012-0175), issued by the LA Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 4). Co-permittees include the City of Los Angeles, LA County, and LA County Flood Control District. Effective December 5, 2012; amended 2015, 2016, 2018.

**Local ordinance:** LAMC Article 4.4, §§ 64.70–64.72 — Stormwater and Urban Runoff Pollution Control Ordinance. Prohibits discharge of pollutants to the storm drain system. Ord. 181899 (2011) expanded LID/SUSMP requirements.

**Industrial facilities:** State Industrial General Permit (IGP) applies for facilities with SIC codes triggering industrial stormwater coverage; City enforces local prohibition (LAMC § 64.70.03) on illicit discharges.

**LID/development:** LAMC § 64.72 requires Low Impact Development practices for projects requiring building permits.

**RWQCB Region 4 contact:** Erum Razzak, (213) 620-2095 | MS4StormwaterRB4@waterboards.ca.gov

### 5. Zoning / Land Use — Industrial Districts

**Authority:** Los Angeles Department of City Planning (LADCP).  
**Code:** LAMC Chapter 1, Article 2 (Zoning); also Chapter 1A (new Zoning Code framework, currently parallel adoption).

**Industrial zone designations (LAMC Chapter 1):**
- **MR1** — Restricted Industrial Zone (lightest industrial; LAMC § 12.17.5)
- **MR2** — [VERIFY — see LAMC §§ 12.17.x series]
- **M1** — Limited Industrial Zone (manufacturing, warehousing, enclosed C2 uses; outdoor storage as accessory only; LAMC § 12.17)
- **M2** — Light Industrial Zone (M1 + MR2 uses, storage yards, animal keeping; LAMC § 12.18)
- **M3** — Heavy Industrial Zone (M2 uses + any industrial use; nuisance uses allowed ≥ 500 ft from other zones; LAMC § 12.20)

**Conditional Use Permit (CUP):**
- Governed by LAMC § 12.24 (CUP and quasi-judicial approvals)
- Class 2 CUP (LAMC Chapter 1A, § 13B.2.2) and Class 3 CUP (§ 13B.2.3) apply to different use categories
- Applications filed with LADCP; form and instructions at planning.lacity.gov

**Planning contacts:**  
- Department of City Planning Commission Office: (213) 978-1300  
- Planning portal: https://planning.lacity.gov  
- Zoning information: https://planning.lacity.gov (zoning counter or online lookup)
