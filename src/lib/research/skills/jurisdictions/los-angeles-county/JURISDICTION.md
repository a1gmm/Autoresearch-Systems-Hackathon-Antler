### 1. Hazardous Materials / CUPA

**Agency:** LA County Fire Department — Health Hazardous Materials Division (HHMD)
**Role:** HHMD is the CUPA for all unincorporated LA County areas (appointed under SB 1082).

**Programs administered:**
- Hazardous Waste Generator Program
- Hazardous Materials Release Response Plans & Inventory (Business Plan / HMBP)
- California Accidental Release Prevention (Cal-ARP)
- Aboveground Storage Tank (AST) Program
- Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program

**Participating Agencies (8 total):** LA County Dept of Public Works, LA County Agricultural Commissioner, and 6 others — each may hold inspection/enforcement records for sub-programs (e.g., UST inspections by Public Works).

**Reporting portal:** CERS (CalEPA statewide) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov

**HHMD contacts:**
- Petroleum Chemical Unit: 5200 Irwindale Ave, Irwindale CA 91706 | (626) 430-3040
- General hazmat inquiries: https://fire.lacounty.gov/hazmat-contact-us/
- Payment options: https://fire.lacounty.gov/payment-options/

**Fee document:** FY 24-25 CUPA fee schedule — https://fire.lacounty.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/CUPA-FY-24-25-Dcoument_v2.pdf

Source: https://fire.lacounty.gov/health-hazardous-materials-division/
Source: https://fire.lacounty.gov/cupa-programs/

### 2. Fire Code (AHJ)

**AHJ:** LA County Fire Department — Consolidated Fire Protection District of Los Angeles County
**Fire Prevention Division contact:** (323) 890-4243

**Adopted code:** 2023 County of Los Angeles Fire Code — based on **CFC 2022 edition** (which is based on the 2021 IFC), with local amendments.
**County Code:** Title 32 (Fire Code) — https://lacounty-ca.elaws.us/code/coor_title32
**Key local appendices added:**
- Appendix PP — Local Agency Fire Hazard Severity Zones
- Appendix QQ — LAC Fire Code Fee Schedule (effective January 11, 2024)
- Appendix RR — Rifle Range
- Chapter 81 — Automobile Wrecking Yards
- Chapter 82 — Infractions
- Chapter 83 — Consolidated Fire Protection District of Los Angeles County Fire Code

**Effective date of current edition:** January 11, 2024 (Appendix QQ fee schedule certification)

**Permit fee schedule:** 2023 LACFC Appendix QQ — https://lacounty.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/2023-LACFC-Appendix-QQ-Certified-Ord-Eff.-2024-01-11-Secured.pdf

**Payment locations:** Financial Management Division (Commerce); Irwindale Fire Prevention Office; LACoFD Film Desk (Sylmar). Online credit card accepted.

**Legal authority for fees:** CA Health & Safety Code §§ 13217, 13235, 13143.5, 13145(f)(2), 13146(f); Govt Code § 66014

**Hazmat / high-pile permits:** Applied for through Fire Prevention Division; fees in Appendix QQ. Contact regional Fire Prevention office (North/Central/East regions).

Source: https://fire.lacounty.gov/fire-prevention-fees/

### 3. Industrial Wastewater / Pretreatment

**POTW/Agency:** Los Angeles County Sanitation Districts (LACSD)
**Industrial Waste Section:** (562) 699-7411
**Program page:** https://www.lacsd.org/services/wastewater-programs-permits/industrial-waste-pretreatment-program

**Governing ordinance:** LACSD Wastewater Ordinance — enacted to regulate sewer construction, sewer use, and industrial wastewater discharges; imposes pretreatment requirements; provides for Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permits (IWP).
**Ordinance text:** https://www.lacsd.org/wastewater/industrial_waste/iwordinances/wastewater_ordinance.asp
[VERIFY — ordinance number/code chapter not confirmed online; LACSD does not publish it prominently by number]

**IWP process:**
1. Submit application; engineering staff review proposed pretreatment equipment.
2. Compliance checked against: local limits, federal categorical standards, spill containment, flow monitoring, rainwater diversion, and combustible gas monitoring policies.
3. Permit issued per discharge volume/type category.

**Forms:** https://www.lacsd.org/services/wastewater-programs-permits/industrial-waste-pretreatment-program/industrial-waste-forms
**Discharge limits:** https://www.lacsd.org/services/wastewater-programs-permits/industrial-waste-pretreatment-program/industrial-waste-policies/discharge-limits

### 4. Stormwater (MS4)

**Principal permittee:** County of Los Angeles / LA County Flood Control District
**Administered by:** LA County Dept of Public Works — Watershed Management Division
**Local ordinance:** LA County Code Title 12, Chapter 12.80 (Stormwater/Urban Runoff)

**NPDES permit:** Order No. R4-2012-0175 / NPDES No. CAS004001
**Issuing board:** LA Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 4)
**Permit scope:** Municipal Separate Storm Sewer System (MS4) discharges within coastal watersheds of LA County (excludes City of Long Beach)
**Amendments:** WQ 2015-0075 (June 16, 2015); R4-2012-0175-A01 (November 23, 2016); Attachment E modified July 9, 2018

**Industrial facilities:** Must comply with State IGP (Industrial General Permit) + Chapter 12.80 local requirements. County has authority to inspect and issue NOVs.

Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/rwqcb4/water_issues/programs/stormwater/municipal/losangeles.html

### Participating Agencies Note

Under the CUPA umbrella, LA County Public Works and the Agricultural Commissioner are Participating Agencies that may hold separate inspection/enforcement records for specific CUPA sub-programs (e.g., UST inspections). Always cross-check CERS records against both HHMD and Public Works for UST compliance history.
