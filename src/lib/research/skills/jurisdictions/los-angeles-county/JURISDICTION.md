> Local EHS authority reference for **Los Angeles County (unincorporated)**. Orientation for the research subagent: who administers the local programs and where their adopted codes live. Verify the live page before quoting; local code editions change on 3-year cycles.

## CUPA (Unified Program)

- LA County Fire Department — Health Hazardous Materials Division (HHMD)
- https://fire.lacounty.gov/cupa-programs/

## Fire authority (AHJ)

- LA County Fire Department (Consolidated Fire Protection District) — https://fire.lacounty.gov/

## Building authority

- LA County Public Works — Building & Safety Division

## Local code amendments

- Adopts CA Building Code (County Code Title 26) and CA Fire Code (Title 32) with local amendments — https://library.municode.com/ca/los_angeles_county/codes/code_of_ordinances

## Notes

- CUPA is specifically the Fire Dept's HHMD; Public Works and Ag Commissioner are Participating Agencies (e.g. UST).
