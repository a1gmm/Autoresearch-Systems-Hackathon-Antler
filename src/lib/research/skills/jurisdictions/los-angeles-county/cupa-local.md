## CUPA Identity

**Agency:** Los Angeles County Fire Department — Health Hazardous Materials Division (HHMD)
**Designation:** Own-CUPA (LA County Fire Department applied for and was appointed CUPA under SB 1082; not operating under another county's CUPA umbrella).
**Jurisdiction:** All unincorporated LA County areas plus those incorporated cities that contract with LACoFD. Cities with their own Participating Agencies (see below) administer sub-programs independently.

## Municipal Code Authority

Annual program fees are established per **Los Angeles County Ordinance Title 12, Chapters 12.50, 12.52, 12.64, and 12.70**.

SB 1082 (Senate Bill 1082) provides the state-level enabling authority for local cost recovery.

## Programs Administered (Six Unified Programs)

| # | Program | Trigger |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Handler (Business Plans / HMBP) | Any material above State or local reporting thresholds |
| 2 | California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) | Regulated substance above State/Federal threshold quantities |
| 3 | Hazardous Waste Generator & Tiered Permitting | Any amount of hazardous waste generated |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) | Petroleum stored ≥ 1,320 gallons above-ground |
| 5 | Underground Storage Tank (UST) | Any UST facility |
| 6 | California Uniform Fire Code — Hazardous Materials | Hazmat permits under Title 32 |

## Permit Fee Tiers — Hazardous Materials Handler

Fee tier is based on maximum quantity of regulated substance stored/handled onsite:

| Tier | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Small Quantity Handler | Only one hazardous material, below minor thresholds |
| Minor Handler | Two or more hazardous materials within threshold ranges |
| Moderate Handler | Liquids 501–2,750 gallons (or equivalent solids/gases) |
| Major Handler | Liquids 2,751–50,000 gallons (or equivalent) |
| Major Handler (Large Volume) | Liquids ≥ 50,001 gallons |
| Major Handler (Complex) | Refineries, chemical plants, distilleries ≥ 175,001 gallons |

Specific dollar amounts for each tier are published in the annual fee schedule document (not printed in the CUPA FAQ brochure). Fee schedule PDF for FY 24-25: https://fire.lacounty.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/CUPA-FY-24-25-Dcoument_v2.pdf
FY 25-26 fee schedule: contact CUPA at (323) 890-4000 / see https://fire.lacounty.gov/cupa-updates/

## Reporting Portal

CERS (California Environmental Reporting System): https://cers.calepa.ca.gov
All Business Plans (HMBP), CalARP RMPs, and inventory data submitted here.

## Key Compliance Notes

- Payment due **60 days from invoice date**; late payment incurs **40% penalty** on total fee (state surcharges excluded from penalty).
- Permit issued approximately 90 days after payment received in full; must be displayed conspicuously on-site.
- **Carbon dioxide (CO₂):** Businesses handling ≥ 1,000 cubic feet must obtain permit and submit plans to CERS (per AB 1716/SB 740/AB 2059 updates).
- Duplicate permit: $15.00.
