## Overview

Inyo County has **no countywide POTW or sewer use ordinance**. Wastewater is managed by independent water/sewer districts and the City of Bishop. Most of the county relies on on-site septic systems (regulated by Inyo County Environmental Health under Chapter C of the county fee schedule). All POTWs in the county are regulated by the **Lahontan Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB Region 6)** through Waste Discharge Requirements (WDRs).

## City of Bishop POTW

- **Operator:** City of Bishop Public Works Department
- **System:** 16 miles of collection pipe + one lift station
- **Design capacity:** 1.6 MGD
- **Current average flow:** ~800,000 GPD (approximately 50% of capacity)
- **Treatment:** Secondary treatment; treated effluent disposed via **land application** on ~125 acres of leased pasture (40 acres owned by City of Bishop + 85 acres owned by City of Los Angeles/DWP). No surface discharge to Owens River or other receiving water.

## Big Pine Community Services District POTW

- **Operator:** Big Pine Community Services District (CSD)
- **Office:** 180 North Main Street, Big Pine, CA 93513
- **Design capacity:** 0.150 MGD (150,000 GPD)
- **Current average flow:** ~27,000 GPD (winter); ~40,000 GPD (summer) — well below capacity
- **Treatment process:** Collection/headworks → oxidation ditch (165,600-gal capacity, extended aeration activated sludge) → secondary clarifier → two passive oxidation ponds → five percolation basins (4 gravity-flow 19,000-sq-ft basins + 1 new 42,500-sq-ft basin constructed at higher elevation) → sludge drying beds
- **Discharge method:** Land disposal via percolation to groundwater (no surface discharge). The facility is located on land owned by the City of Los Angeles/DWP.
- **Regulator/permit:** Lahontan RWQCB
  - WDID No.: **6B140100001**
  - Current order: **Board Order No. 6-95-35** (being revised; Tentative Order R6-2025-TENTATIVE adopted January 28, 2025)
  - Order history: 6-70-42 (1970) → 6-78-16 (1978) → 6-87-9 (1987) → 6-95-35 (1995) → R6-2025-TENTATIVE (pending)
- **New effluent limitations added in 2025 revision:** nitrate and total dissolved solids (TDS) effluent limitations established for the first time; minimum 50% nitrogen reduction at effluent discharge point required when compared to influent.
- **Industrial pretreatment:** Not required; small domestic-only POTW well below 5 MGD threshold.
- **Sludge:** Dried and disposed offsite per an approved Sludge Management Plan.

Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/lahontan/board_decisions/tentative_orders/2025/wdr-tntv-big-pine-csd.pdf

## On-Site Sewage Systems (Septic)

For facilities not served by a POTW, on-site sewage disposal systems are permitted by **Inyo County Environmental Health Services** under Inyo County Code.

2026 permit fees:
- New conventional septic system: $452
- New alternative design system: $634
- Existing leachline repair/replacement: $328
- Septic tank repair/replacement: $94

Source: https://www.inyocounty.us/services/environmental-health/fee-schedule
