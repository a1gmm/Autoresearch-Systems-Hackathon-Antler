## CUPA Agency

**Inyo County Department of Environmental Health Services (ICDEHS)**
168 N. Edwards St, Independence, CA 93526
Phone: (760) 878-0238
https://www.inyocounty.us/services/environmental-health

CalEPA-certified as the sole Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all of Inyo County, including the City of Bishop. There is no city-run CUPA and no participating agency split. Agricultural operations are regulated separately by the Inyo County Agricultural Commissioner.

## Programs Administered

| Program | Authority |
|---|---|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) | H&S Code § 25500 et seq. |
| Hazardous Waste Generator | H&S Code § 25100 et seq. |
| Underground Storage Tanks (UST) | H&S Code § 25280 et seq. |
| Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) | H&S Code § 25270 et seq. |
| California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) | H&S Code § 25531 et seq. |

Inyo County does not administer a Unified Program for the Tiered Permitting program (that is a DTSC function) as it is rarely applicable in this rural jurisdiction.

## HMBP Filing Threshold

Businesses storing or handling hazardous materials in quantities at any one time exceeding:
- **55 gallons** (liquids)
- **500 pounds** (solids)
- **200 cubic feet** (compressed gases)

must submit a Hazardous Materials Business Plan via CERS: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/

## Local Hazmat Area Plan

The Inyo County Hazardous Materials Area Plan (HMAP) is maintained by the CUPA/Environmental Health Services Department in coordination with the county's fire departments and public safety agencies. The plan serves as a planning guide for hazardous materials releases and provides chemical inventory data to county fire departments.

HMAP (2019 version): https://www.inyocounty.us/sites/default/files/2019-07/FINALInyoHMAP.pdf

|---|---|
| Base fee — CUPA facility (no UST, LQG, or treatment) | $139/facility/year |
| CUPA facility with UST(s) | $417/facility/year |
| CUPA facility — large quantity generator (LQG) | $256/facility/year |
| CUPA facility with treatment | $256/facility/year |
| Any two of: UST, LQG, or treatment | $387/facility/year |
| All three: UST + LQG + treatment | $525/facility/year |
| CalARP facility | $740/facility/year |

Additional service fees (plan check, reinspection, enforcement actions): $94/hour.

Fee schedule PDF: https://www.inyocounty.us/sites/default/files/2025-10/Environmental%20Health%202026%20Fee%20Schedule.pdf

**Fee authority:** Health and Safety Code §§ 25287(e) and 25404.5 authorize CUPA fee collection; fees are annually adjusted by resolution of the Inyo County Board of Supervisors.
