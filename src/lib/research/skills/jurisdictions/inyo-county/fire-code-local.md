## Adopted Fire Code Edition

**California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9)** — adopted by reference via Inyo County Code **Chapter 14.08 (Building Regulations)**, which states:

> "All parts of the most recent version of the California Code of Regulations, Title 24, are hereby adopted in the county of Inyo as a matter of state law, subject to the modifications and amendments contained in the chapter."

The county may adopt local amendments that are **more restrictive** than state minimums, based on local climatic, geological, or topographical conditions.

## Fire Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

Inyo County has **no single countywide AHJ for structural fire**. Jurisdiction depends on location:

| Area | AHJ |
|---|---|
| Southeastern Inyo (Shoshone, Tecopa, Death Valley Junction, Charleston View, Sandy Valley) | Southern Inyo Fire Protection District (SIFPD), (760) 852-4130 |
| Lone Pine area | Lone Pine Fire Protection District |
| Independence area | Independence Fire Department |
| Big Pine area | Paradise FPD |
| Olancha/Cartago | Olancha-Cartago FD |
| Bishop / unincorporated Bishop area | Bishop Fire Department (joint City of Bishop + Bishop Rural FPD) |
| State Responsibility Area (wildland) | CAL FIRE, San Bernardino Unit (BDU) |
| Federal lands (Death Valley NP, Inyo NF, BLM) | NPS / USFS / BLM fire programs |

**Bishop Fire Department note:** Joint operation between the City of Bishop and the Bishop (Rural) Fire Protection District; also serves Bishop Paiute Reservation under contract.

## Permits

Fire construction and operational permits are issued by the applicable local fire protection district or, for unincorporated areas without a structural FPD, through the county Building and Safety Department.

Permit fee schedule is set by resolution of each fire protection district board. Building-related permit fees for the county building department are set by Board of Supervisors resolution (no public fee schedule found online — [VERIFY at Building & Safety]).

**Southern Inyo FPD:** Fee Schedule Ordinance 05-01 (revised February 2024) governs district fees. Full text: https://www.sifpd.org/district-ordinances
