## Industrial District

### M1 — Manufacturing District (sole industrial zone)

Bishop uses a **single industrial zone designation: M1**. No M-2 or heavy industrial zone designation was found.

**General land uses allowed in M1:** Manufacturing, warehousing, and processing.

Source: City of Bishop 6th Cycle Housing Element, Table C (adopted August 26, 2021): https://www.hcd.ca.gov/housing-elements/docs/bishop-6th-adopted082621.pdf

## Overlay / Special Districts

- **Downtown Specific Plan (DTSP)** — mixed-use overlay in the downtown core (adopted/in development). References underlying districts including C-1, R-3, and R-3-P. Ordinance 544 governs supportive/transitional housing in this overlay.
- **Mixed-Use Overlay** — applied along portions of Line Street for commercial-to-residential conversion.
- **O-S (Open Space)** — Chapter 17.72 in Municode.
- **Mini Warehouse Development** — Chapter 17.86 in Municode (specific use standards).
- **Short-Term Rental (STR)** regulations apply citywide. Annual STR application fee: $150.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP)

- Use permit review and processing fee: **$500** (Resolution No. 2022-09, PL-5)
- Zone change review: **$1,500** (PL-1)
- Variance review: **$1,500** (PL-2)
- Zoning ordinance amendment review: **$1,500** (PL-3)
- Appeals: **$300** (PL-4)
- Environmental initial study: **$1,500** (PL-9)
- EIR staff review: **$2,500** (PL-10)

## EHS-Relevant Land Use Notes

- Manufacturing, warehousing, and processing are permitted in M1 outright (no CUP noted in housing element table, but [VERIFY in Title 17 for any conditional use triggers]).
- Light manufacturing and wholesale are listed as permitted in C-2 — check Title 17 for whether hazardous materials use or specific industrial processes require a CUP in C-2.
- Airport proximity: Bishop Airport (BIH) is nearby; some industrial land near the airport is zoned for uses compatible with airport operations per the Airport Land Use Compatibility Plan.
- Cannabis: City has a separate cannabis ordinance and regulations within the Planning Department. https://cityofbishop.ca.gov/departments/planning/ [cannabis link]
