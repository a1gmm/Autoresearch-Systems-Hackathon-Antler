## Adopting Jurisdiction / AHJ

**Bishop Fire Department** — a cooperative (joint operation) between the City of Bishop and the Bishop Rural Fire Protection District (BRFPD). Governed jointly by the City Council and the BRFPD Board of Commissioners.

- Fire Chief: Bret Russell — brussell@cityofbishop.ca.gov, ext. 401
- Deputy Fire Chief: Adam Marcellin — amarcellin@cityofbishop.ca.gov, ext. 403
- Station address: 377 West Line Street, Bishop, CA 93514
- Main line: (760) 873-5863 | Fire direct: (760) 873-5485
- Primarily volunteer staffing with some career personnel.

## Wildland Urban Interface (WUI)

- CAL FIRE issued updated Fire Hazard Severity Zone (FHSZ) maps for the City of Bishop Local Responsibility Area as of March 24, 2025. The City is required to adopt these maps by ordinance within 120 days.
- Wildland SRA protection provided by CAL FIRE San Bernardino Unit (BDU).
- WUI inspection fee: $100 (see fee schedule below).
- Source: https://cityofbishop.ca.gov/departments/fire/wildand_urban_interface.php
