## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air

- **District: Great Basin Unified Air Pollution Control District (GBUAPCD)** — formed 1974 as a joint powers agreement of Alpine, Mono, and Inyo Counties. GBUAPCD is the sole air permitting authority for all stationary sources within the City of Bishop. No separate city air ordinance exists.
- District HQ is located in Bishop at 157 Short Street, Suite 6, Bishop, CA 93514; phone (760) 872-8211.
- Primary pollutant concern is particulate matter (PM10); district operates 9 monitoring locations in Inyo County.
- Stationary source permit-to-operate and authority-to-construct applications go to GBUAPCD, not the City.
- Source: https://www.gbuapcd.org/ and https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/great-basin-unified-air-pollution-control-district

### CUPA

- **Not its own CUPA.** The countywide CUPA is the **Inyo County Environmental Health Services Department (ICDEHS)**, operating since 2002. City of Bishop businesses regulated under the county CUPA.
- Programs administered: Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generator (HWG), Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP).
- Threshold quantities triggering HMBP: 55 gallons (liquid), 500 pounds (solid), 200 cubic feet (compressed gas).
- All HMBPs must be filed electronically via CERS (cers.calepa.ca.gov).
- ICDEHS mailing address: 168 N. Edwards St., Independence, CA 93526; tel (760) 878-0238.
- **2026 CUPA Fee Schedule (effective January 1, 2026):**
  - Base fee (no UST, LQG, or treatment): $139/facility/year
  - CUPA facility with UST(s): $417/facility/year
  - CUPA facility — large quantity generator: $256/facility/year
  - CUPA facility with treatment: $256/facility/year
  - Combination of any two (UST, LQG, or treatment): $387/facility/year
  - All three activities: $525/facility/year
  - CUPA facility with CalARP: $740/facility/year
  - Additional service fees (reinspection, plan check, enforcement): $94/hour
- Source: https://www.inyocounty.us/services/environmental-health/environmental-health-programs/hazardous-materials-cupa and https://www.inyocounty.us/sites/default/files/2025-10/Environmental%20Health%202026%20Fee%20Schedule.pdf

### Wastewater

- **POTW operator: Bishop Area Wastewater Authority (BAWA)** — a Joint Powers Authority formed June 2020 by the City of Bishop and Eastern Sierra Community Services District (ESCSD). The City's plant has permitted capacity of 1.6 MGD; current average flow ~800,000 gpd.
- Treatment: lagoon-based primary and secondary treatment; effluent land-applied (flood irrigation) on ~40 acres (City) + ~85 acres (Los Angeles-leased).
- **Discharge permits** issued by Lahontan Regional Water Quality Control Board (LRWQCB):
  - City of Bishop plant: WDID-6B140101001
  - ESCSD plant: WDID-6B140108001
- **Current effluent permit limit:** BOD₅ ≤ 50 mg/L. TSS not currently regulated. Total Nitrogen not currently regulated (though monitored monthly). Projected future consolidated permit limit: BOD₅ 30 mg/L, TSS 30 mg/L, TN 10 mg/L.
- No local industrial pretreatment ordinance was found online. For industrial discharge questions contact BAWA/City Public Works at (760) 873-5863. [VERIFY whether a local sewer use ordinance with IWP provisions exists in Bishop Municipal Code Title 13.]
- **Monthly sewer rates (as of July 2023):** Single-family residence $42.00; Multi-family detached $31.50/unit; Brewery (with pretreatment) $8.40/1KBl/year; General commercial $39.90/toilet; Restaurant $4.20/seat; Car wash $126.00/stall.
- One-time sewer service permit: $50; Sewer development impact fee: $2,000/EDU; Grease interceptor inspection: actual cost.
- Source: https://cityofbishop.ca.gov/departments/public_works/sewer.php and https://bawa.ca.gov/ and BAWA Preliminary Engineering Report (Lumos & Associates, June 2025, WDID references)

### Zoning

- **Zoning Code: Title 17 of the Bishop Municipal Code** (Municode). Planning contact: Principal City Planner Anastasiia Budnyk, City of Bishop Planning Department, 377 West Line Street.
- **Industrial/Manufacturing zone designation: M1** (single tier; no M-2 found). The M1 district allows "manufacturing, warehousing, and processing" per the 6th Cycle Housing Element.
- Other key zones: C-2 (General Commercial, "light manufacturing and wholesale facilities" also permitted), C-H (Highway Commercial), C-1 (General commercial/retail), O-P (Offices/professional services), O-S (Open space), R-1/R-2000/R-3/R-3-P/R-M (residential).
- CUP/use permit review fee: $500 (Resolution No. 2022-09 PL-5).
- Zone change or variance review: $1,500 each.
- Source: https://library.municode.com/ca/bishop/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=COOR_TIT17ZO and https://www.hcd.ca.gov/housing-elements/docs/bishop-6th-adopted082621.pdf
