## CUPA Agency

**Inyo County Environmental Health Services Department (ICDEHS)**
City of Bishop is NOT its own CUPA. Inyo County is the certified CUPA for all of Inyo County including incorporated Bishop.
- ICDEHS has operated the CUPA program since 2002.
- Mailing address: 168 N. Edwards St., Independence, CA 93526
- Phone: (760) 878-0238
- Contact form: https://www.inyocounty.us/services/environmental-health

## Programs Administered

All six California unified hazardous materials programs are covered by ICDEHS:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP)** — required for facilities storing/handling above threshold quantities.
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator (HWG)** — covers facilities generating hazardous waste (LQG, SQG, VSQG).
3. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — permit, inspection, and closure.
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — tanks ≥1,320 gallons aggregate aboveground petroleum.
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — Risk Management Plans for Program 2 and 3 processes.
6. **Hazardous Waste Onsite Treatment** — covered under HWG program.

## Electronic Reporting

All HMBPs must be submitted electronically through:
- **CERS** (California Electronic Reporting Site): https://cers.calepa.ca.gov
- Inventory must be updated annually.
- Emergency responders access data through CERS; public can request non-confidential portions.

## Hazardous Materials Area Plan

The Inyo County EHSD maintains a Hazardous Materials Area Plan (HMAP) for emergency response under Health & Safety Code §25503.
- Current version: September 2008 (revision status [VERIFY]).
- Available: https://www.inyocounty.us/sites/default/files/2019-07/FINALInyoHMAP.pdf
