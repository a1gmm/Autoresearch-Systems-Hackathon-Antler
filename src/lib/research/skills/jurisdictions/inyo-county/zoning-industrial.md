## Industrial Zone Districts

Inyo County has **one industrial zone designation** in Title 18:

| Symbol | Name | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| **M-1** | General Industrial & Extractive | Chapter 18.57 |

There is no M-2 (heavy industrial), no I-1/I-2, and no separate light-industrial zone. M-1 is the sole industrial district.

## M-1 District — General Industrial & Extractive (Chapter 18.57)

### Purpose and Intent

The M-1 district is intended to provide space in suitable locations in Inyo County for all types of manufacturing, warehousing, processing, mining, ore reduction, and mineral development activities, provided such activity **does not cause pollution of any human or natural resource**.

### Operational Standards (Pollution Control)

All uses in the M-1 district must be planned, developed, conducted, and operated so that:
- Smoke, fumes, dust, odors, liquids, and other waste of any kind is **confined and/or purified** to control pollution of air, soil, or water to meet Planning Commission standards
- No threat to public health and welfare or the aesthetic value of the county

### Permitted Uses (By-Right)

- Manufacturing and industrial plants, factories
- Warehouses and storage facilities
- Mining and processing of natural resources, including borrow pits (subject to California Surface Mining and Reclamation Act)
- Retail and wholesale establishments for the **sale of products manufactured or produced on the same site**
- Beverage industries including retail/wholesale distribution of beverages manufactured on the same site
- Dwellings limited to caretaker/superintendent/bona fide employee use on the same parcel as the industrial use (mobilehomes/trailers permitted in rural areas)
- Emergency housing (per General Plan housing element policies)
- Public/quasi-public buildings and uses of recreational, religious, cultural, or public service nature
- Signs in compliance with Chapter 18.75 (sign regulations)

### Conditional Uses (CUP Required)

- Commercial cannabis cultivation (indoors only, with mitigation measures)
- Non-volatile and volatile cannabis manufacturing
- Cannabis transportation/distribution facilities
- Cannabis microbusinesses
- Any use similar to the by-right list if the use, location, and development plan is approved by the Planning Commission
- Floor area ratio exceeding 0.5 (up to maximum 1.2) for light industrial or general industrial GP designations

### CUP Process

Conditional Use Permits are issued by the **Inyo County Planning Commission**. Applications are filed with the Planning Department. The Board of Supervisors hears appeals from Planning Commission decisions.
