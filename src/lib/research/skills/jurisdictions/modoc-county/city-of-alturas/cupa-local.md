> Research reference for Unified Program (CUPA) administration in the City of Alturas. Alturas is NOT its own CUPA — it is fully served by the Modoc County Environmental Health Division, which is the countywide CUPA. All six CalEPA unified programs for facilities within city limits are administered by the county.

## CUPA Identity

- **CUPA:** Modoc County Environmental Health Division (countywide CUPA; covers all of Modoc County including the City of Alturas)
- **Address:** 202 W. 4th St., Alturas, CA 96101
- **Phone:** 530-233-6310
- **Web:** https://environmentalhealth.co.modoc.ca.us/nav/certified_unified_program_agency_(cupa).php

## Programs Administered

Modoc County Environmental Health implements all six CalEPA unified programs for facilities within the City of Alturas:

| # | Program | Acronym | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan | HMBP | Submitted via CERS; county EH reviews and inspects |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator Program | HWG | Generator ID, manifest compliance, inspection |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tanks | UST | Installation, operation, modification, closure, unauthorized release |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act | APSA | Spill prevention for above-threshold petroleum aboveground storage |
| 5 | Medical Waste | MW | Medical waste generator program |
| 6 | Used Oil Management | UO | Used oil collection and management |

> Note: California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) is listed as a standard unified program element. [VERIFY whether Modoc County EH administers CalARP locally or whether it is handled through state channels given the very small industrial base in the county.]

## HMBP Program Details

- **Submission system:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business
- **Who must comply:** Any business storing, using, or handling hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities (Health & Safety Code §25507 — generally 55 gallons liquid, 500 lbs solid, 200 cubic feet gas)
- **CERS submission:** File through CERS; the reviewing CUPA agency is Modoc County Environmental Health
- Resources available from Modoc County EH: Minor Repair Application for Hazardous Materials, UST Removal Application, Unauthorized Release Form, Business Plan guidance, Generator requirements documentation

## Relationship to City Fire Department

- The City of Alturas Fire Department is NOT the CUPA. The fire department handles CFC operational permits and fire marshal duties within city limits, but HMBP, UST, APSA, hazardous waste generator compliance, and related Unified Program items are the exclusive jurisdiction of Modoc County Environmental Health.
