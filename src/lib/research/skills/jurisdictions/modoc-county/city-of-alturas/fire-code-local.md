## Relationship to County / State Programs

- The City of Alturas Fire Department is the sole fire AHJ within city limits. The fire department does NOT delegate fire marshal duties to the county.
- CAL FIRE Lassen-Modoc Unit provides State Responsibility Area (SRA) coverage for the rural areas surrounding Alturas. For wildland-urban interface (WUI) concerns just outside city limits, CAL FIRE is the relevant contact.
- Modoc County Environmental Health (the countywide CUPA) — not the fire department — administers hazardous materials business plans and related Unified Program permits within city limits. See cupa-local.md.
