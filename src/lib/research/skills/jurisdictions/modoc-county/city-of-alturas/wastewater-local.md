> Research reference for wastewater/sewer regulation in the City of Alturas. Alturas operates its own municipal wastewater treatment plant (POTW) regulated by the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 5). The city's sewer use ordinance is in the municipal code; direct web fetch of the relevant chapter returned 403 from Municode. No industrial pretreatment program (IWP) was identified — consistent with a small rural POTW with no significant industrial user base. Confirm directly with Public Works before assuming an industrial discharge is exempt.

- **Regulatory body:** California Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (Region 5)
  - Region 5 — "Redding" sub-office covers far-northern California including Modoc County
  - Central Valley Water Board: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/centralvalley/

## Industrial Pretreatment Program (IWP)

- **No industrial pretreatment program (IWP) was identified** for the City of Alturas POTW. This is consistent with a very small rural system (~0.33 MGD) with no significant industrial user base. Under 40 CFR Part 403, a POTW is required to develop a pretreatment program only if it receives pollutants from industrial users that pass through or interfere with the POTW or are present in sewage sludge in quantities that violate applicable standards — conditions unlikely for Alturas given its rural character.
- [VERIFY — confirm with Public Works at 530-233-2052 and with Region 5 Water Board that no approved pretreatment program exists before concluding any industrial discharge is unregulated. The NPDES permit (R5-2020-0004) may impose local limits or pretreatment conditions.]

## Practical Guidance for Industrial/Commercial Dischargers

- Any non-domestic discharge to the Alturas sewer system should be pre-cleared with the Public Works Department before discharge.
- Even absent a formal IWP, general prohibitions (no flammable/explosive materials, no corrosives, no materials that may cause interference) apply under state law and the city's sewer use ordinance.
- Contact Modoc County Environmental Health (530-233-6310) for hazardous waste disposal questions — do not discharge hazardous waste to the sewer.
