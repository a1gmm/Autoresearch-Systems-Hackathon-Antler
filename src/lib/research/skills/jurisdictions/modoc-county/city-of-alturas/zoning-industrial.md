## Industrial Zone Districts

The zoning ordinance establishes two primary industrial zone districts (Section 28.12.010 and 28.21.030):

### M1 — Light Industrial/Manufacturing

- **Intent:** Accommodate low-intensity industrial uses close to commercial and residential areas with minimum environmental conflicts. Serves as a buffer zone between commercial/residential and M2 heavy industrial areas.
- **Primary use:** Light manufacturing and/or storage; some commercial uses (product sales and services) are allowed incidentally.
- **General Plan land use designation:** Light Industrial (LI)

### M2 — Heavy Industrial/Manufacturing

- **Intent:** Accommodate a full range of industrial uses. Areas zoned M2 should be "substantially removed from sensitive land uses, including residential areas, hospitals and schools."
- **Primary use:** Heavy manufacturing and processing uses with the greatest potential for adverse impacts (traffic, noise, dust, odors, vibrations).
- **General Plan land use designation:** Heavy Industrial (HI)

## Industrial Use Types (Section 28.35)

The following industrial use type categories apply in M1/M2 zones (defined in Article 8 and regulated in Section 28.35):

- Construction, Maintenance and Repair Services
- Industrial, General (manufacturing, processing, assembling — heaviest industrial)
- Light Manufacturing (manufacture, assembly, or packaging — lighter processes)
- Printing and Publishing
- Recycling Facilities
- Salvage and Wrecking Yards
- Storage, Self (self-storage facilities)
- Storage Yards: Equipment and Material
- Temporary Equipment and Material Storage Yard

## Key Use Standards for Industrial Activities (Section 28.35.010)

Applicable to all Industrial Use Types in M1 and M2 (unless otherwise stated):

1. **Outdoor storage:** Operable trucks and heavy equipment permitted by right on the back half of a fenced lot; all other outdoor equipment/material storage requires a **Use Permit**.
2. **Shipping containers:** Prohibited unless approved as part of a Use Permit.
3. **Outdoor work areas:** All manufacturing, packaging, assembly, processing, crushing, dismantling, and fabricating must be done **indoors** unless an outdoor work area is approved by a Use Permit.
4. **Screening:** All outdoor storage areas must be screened along side/rear property lines adjacent to residentially zoned property.
5. **Surfacing:** Outdoor storage and parking areas must be concrete, asphalt, chip seal, gravel, or other dust-free material.
6. **Incidental retail sales:** Permitted by right in M1 and M2 for all Industrial Use Types except General Industrial, where product sales must be directly related to and a small portion of site activity.
7. **Property maintenance:** All equipment storage areas and grounds maintained free of weeds and debris.

## Permit Process

### Permitted by Right
Some industrial uses in M1/M2 zones are permitted by right. Review the Land Use Matrix in Section 28.22.020 for specific use/zone combinations.

### Use Permit (UP) — Section 28.52.040
A **Use Permit** is required for:
- Outdoor storage beyond operable trucks/heavy equipment
- Outdoor work areas (manufacturing/processing outside)
- Shipping container use for work/storage
- Higher-intensity industrial uses in M1 (where M2 would normally be required)

**UP process:**
1. Submit application to Planning Department (City Hall, 200 W. North St.)
2. Application must include forms, site plan, and fees (Section 28.51.020)
3. Public notice per Section 28.51.030
4. Planning Commission hears and decides the Use Permit (Section 28.50.020)
5. Conditions of approval issued per Section 28.51.040; post-decision notice within time limits
6. Appeals to City Council per Section 28.52.090

### Administrative Permit (AD) — Section 28.52.010
Some lower-intensity uses require only an Administrative Permit, decided by the Director without a public hearing.

### Site Plan Review (SP) — Section 28.52.030
Required for Commercial, Industrial, and High Density Residential projects including government facilities. Site plan approval is a prerequisite for industrial development.

## Overlay Zones

The following overlay zones may apply to industrial properties:
- **-AS Airport Safety Overlay:** Reserved for future use (not yet active as of 2014)
- **-FC Flood Control Overlay:** Reserved for future use (not yet active as of 2014)
- **-DH Downtown Historic District Overlay:** Applied to the historic downtown area; unlikely to affect industrial parcels
