> EHS routing reference. Modoc County has no single countywide structural fire department. Fire code enforcement in the unincorporated county is fragmented across independent fire protection districts, CAL FIRE (for SRA wildland), and the county Building and Safety Division for building-code fire-safety provisions. There is no county fire marshal office with countywide structural-fire-code authority over commercial/industrial facilities. Identify the correct AHJ by facility address.

## Adopted Code Edition

- **Code adopted:** California Fire Code (CFC) 2022 Edition, Title 24 CCR Part 9, based on the 2021 International Fire Code (IFC) — adopted by **Modoc County Ordinance No. 221-J** as part of the comprehensive 2022 California Building Standards Code adoption.
- **Ordinance effective date:** August 1, 2024 (Ordinance No. 221-J adopted 8-1-24)
- **Adopting chapter:** Modoc County Code Title 15, Chapter 15.01 (Construction Codes, Administration), Section 15.01.020(H)
- **Local amendments to CFC:** Section 15.01.020(H) contains one structural delegation amendment — where the CFC requires delegating enforcement of building standards relating to fire and panic safety for Group R, Division 3 Dwellings to a building official or fire chief, that responsibility is assigned to the **building official** (Modoc County Building and Safety). No other CFC-specific local amendments were identified in the publicly available ordinance text.
- **Prior code:** The 2022 adoption via Ordinance 221-J expressly repealed prior Modoc County Building and Safety Code and conflicting ordinances.

## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) — Structural Fire Code

Modoc County does NOT have a single countywide fire code AHJ for commercial/industrial facilities. Determine AHJ by location:

| Area | AHJ |
|---|---|
| Unincorporated county — building permit fire-safety provisions (Group R-3 dwellings) | Modoc County Building and Safety Division (building official) |
| City of Alturas | City of Alturas Fire Department (Fire Marshal) — see city-of-alturas subfolder |
| State Responsibility Area (SRA) — wildland fire | CAL FIRE Lassen-Modoc Unit |
| Alturas Rural FPD service area (unincorporated fringe around Alturas) | Alturas Rural Fire Protection District |
| California Pines area | California Pines Community Services District / Cal Pines Volunteer FD |
| Adin, Canby, Cedarville, Davis Creek, Eagleville, Fort Bidwell, Lake City, Likely, Lookout, Willow Ranch areas | Respective local fire protection districts (volunteer) |
| Tulelake/Newell area | Tulelake Multi-County Fire Protection District |
| Modoc National Forest lands | USFS / Modoc National Forest |

> There is no county fire marshal or county fire department with structural fire code (CFC) inspection/enforcement authority for commercial/industrial facilities in unincorporated Modoc County. The 2018 General Plan directed the county to "collaborate with CalFire and other fire agencies to develop a County Fire Code" — indicating the county recognized it lacked a coordinated countywide structural fire code enforcement program as of that writing.

## Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI)

- Modoc County is substantially in Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone (VHFHSZ) territory; the 2018 General Plan identifies wildland fire as a major hazard
- Fire-safe construction requirements under CFC Chapter 7A / CBC apply in designated WUI/FHSZ areas
- The 2024 Modoc County Community Wildfire Protection Plan (CWPP) is maintained by the Modoc County Fire Safe Council: https://www.modocfiresafecouncil.org/
