> EHS routing reference. Modoc County is mostly served by individual septic systems (OWTS) rather than centralized sewers. The only municipal POTW is operated by the City of Alturas. A few small community systems exist (California Pines CSD). No formal industrial pretreatment program has been identified for the Alturas POTW, consistent with its small design flow (0.5 MGD). Unincorporated areas outside the City of Alturas and California Pines generally have no municipal sewer access.

## City of Alturas — POTW (Primary Sewered Area)

The City of Alturas operates the only full municipal wastewater treatment plant (POTW) in Modoc County.

- **Operator:** City of Alturas Public Works Department
- **Address (Water/Sewer Dept.):** 200 West North Street, Alturas, CA 96101
- **Phone:** (530) 233-2052
- **Hours:** Monday–Friday, 9:00 am–5:00 pm
- **Emergency:** Modoc County Sheriff's dispatch at (530) 233-4416
- **Web:** https://www.cityofalturas.us/department/public_works/water_sewer/index.php

### WWTP — Technical Profile

- **Design capacity:** 0.5 million gallons per day (MGD) average dry weather flow (secondary treatment)
- **Treatment type:** Secondary treatment with disinfection
- **Discharge point:** Pit River (a Water of the United States)
- **NPDES Permit:** Order No. R5-2020-0004, NPDES Permit No. CA0078921, issued by the California Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (RWQCB5)
- **Regulator:** Central Valley RWQCB (Region 5) — Modoc County falls under RWQCB5 jurisdiction for wastewater
- **Improvement project:** The City has approved a major WWTP improvement project (CEQA NOD filed 2021): decommission the existing WWTP, construct new offsite aeration ponds with land discharge via evaporation and percolation ponds along County Road 54. ### Industrial Pretreatment Program

- **No formal industrial pretreatment program identified.** Federal pretreatment regulations (40 CFR Part 403) require POTWs with design flows ≥5 MGD receiving industrial pollutants to develop a local pretreatment program. The Alturas POTW design flow is 0.5 MGD — well below the 5 MGD threshold — so no federal pretreatment program is required or expected.
- No local sewer-use ordinance with industrial discharge limits was found in publicly available sources for the City of Alturas. [VERIFY — local ordinance not found online; contact City of Alturas Public Works at (530) 233-2052]
- Industrial dischargers connecting to the Alturas sewer system should contact City Public Works to determine applicable local discharge standards and connection requirements.

## California Pines Community Services District — Sewer

- Serves portions of the California Pines residential community (Lake Units 1B, 3, 4, Mobile Home Park, limited area in Unit 2, Castle Rock Estates)
- **Sewer connection fee:** $1,500 (paid in full before connection)
- **Requirement:** Valid Building Permit from Modoc County Building Department required before CSD will connect a property
- **Contact:** California Pines CSD, 376 County Road 71, Alturas CA 96101; (530) 233-2766; contact@calpinescsd-fire.net
- **Web:** https://cpcsd.specialdistrict.org/water-sewer
- Industrial pretreatment: Not applicable — residential community services district; no industrial pretreatment program.

## Unincorporated Communities — No Central Sewer

The 2018 Modoc County General Plan and the 2010 LAFCO Fire MSR confirm that most unincorporated communities have **no community wastewater collection or treatment system**. Communities lacking central sewer include:

- Adin (has a small wastewater facility but minimum lot size constraints remain)
- Canby, Cedarville, Davis Creek, Eagleville, Fort Bidwell, Lake City, Likely, Lookout, New Pine Creek, Tulelake/Newell area — [most lack community water and wastewater systems per 2010 LAFCO data; verify current status]

Facilities in unincorporated Modoc County without access to municipal sewer are served by **Onsite Wastewater Treatment Systems (OWTS / septic)**. OWTS oversight is administered by Modoc County Environmental Health under the county OWTS Local Agency Management Plan (LAMP):
- LAMP document: https://webgen1files.revize.com/modocenvironmental/LAMP%20PDF.pdf
- MOU with RWQCB6 (Lahontan): https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/rwqcb6/water_issues/programs/owts/docs/mou/county_of_modoc.pdf

## Regional Regulator

- RWQCB5 (Central Valley Region) regulates the City of Alturas POTW NPDES permit (Order R5-2020-0004 / NPDES CA0078921)
- RWQCB6 (Lahontan Region) handles some northeastern California OWTS MOU oversight for Modoc County
