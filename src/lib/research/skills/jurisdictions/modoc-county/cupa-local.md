> EHS routing reference. Modoc County Environmental Health is the sole CUPA for the entire county, including the City of Alturas (the only incorporated city). There is no separate city-level CUPA. All six CalEPA unified programs are administered by the county CUPA.

## CUPA Identity

- **CUPA:** Modoc County Environmental Health (full CUPA, certified by CalEPA)
- **Address:** 202 West 4th Street, Alturas, CA 96101
- **Phone:** (530) 233-6310
- **Web:** https://environmentalhealth.co.modoc.ca.us/nav/certified_unified_program_agency_(cupa).php
- **Jurisdiction served:** All of Modoc County, including the City of Alturas and all unincorporated communities (Cedarville, Adin, Canby, California Pines, Tulelake/Newell, etc.)

> The City of Alturas has no participating-agency (PA) arrangement; it is served directly by the county CUPA. Tribal lands (Alturas Indian Rancheria) may involve federal jurisdiction overlap — verify with the CUPA and BIA.

## HMBP Program

- **Submission system:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business
- **Who must file:** Businesses storing, using, or handling hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities (generally: ≥55 gallons liquid, ≥500 lbs solid, ≥200 cubic feet compressed gas — per Health & Safety Code §25507)
- **Forms available from Modoc CUPA:** Application for minor hazardous materials repairs; UST removal applications; unauthorized release forms; business plan submission guidance; aboveground storage tank (AST) facts; generator requirements for hazardous waste and used oil
- **Local ordinance:** No local hazmat ordinance number was identified in publicly available sources — CUPA appears to operate under state statutory authority (Health & Safety Code Division 20, Chapter 6.95 and related). [VERIFY — local ordinance not found online]

## UST Program

- Applications for UST installation, modification, removal available through Modoc County Environmental Health
- Unauthorized release reporting: contact the county CUPA at (530) 233-6310
- Documents: unauthorized release forms available on the CUPA webpage

## APSA / Aboveground Storage Tanks

- Facilities storing petroleum above APSA thresholds (≥1,320 gallons aggregate aboveground, or single tank >660 gallons) must comply with APSA and CERS filing
- Modoc CUPA inspects for APSA compliance

## Notes for Rural/Small Jurisdiction

- Modoc County is very rural (~9,500 residents, ~4,000 sq mi). Industrial hazmat activity is limited; primary sectors are agriculture, timber, and rangeland operations.
- Federal and tribal land carve-outs: The large federal footprint (Modoc National Forest, BLM, Alturas Indian Rancheria, Alturas Rancheria) means USFS, BLM, or tribal authority may be the applicable authority on those lands rather than the county CUPA. Verify with the CUPA for sites on or adjacent to federal/tribal lands.
