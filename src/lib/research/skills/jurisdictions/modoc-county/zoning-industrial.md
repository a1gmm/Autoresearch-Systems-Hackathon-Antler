## Industrial Zoning Districts

The 2018 Modoc County General Plan identifies two industrial zoning categories in the unincorporated county:

### 1. Industrial (I) Zone — Chapter 18.54

- **Municode reference:** Chapter 18.54 — INDUSTRIAL (I) ZONE
- **URL:** https://library.municode.com/ca/modoc_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT18ZO_CH18.54INIZO_18.54.010PU
- **Description (from General Plan):** The full Industrial (I) zone is intended for heavier industrial uses. Compatible land uses include the broadest range of industrial operations.
- **Permitted uses (18.54.010):** Industrial uses permitted by right — full text not retrievable via web fetch (Municode returned 403). [VERIFY — review Chapter 18.54 directly via Municode or contact Planning Dept.]
- **Use permit (conditional) uses:** Certain uses in the Industrial zone require a Use Permit under Chapter 18.128. Any use in the I zone that is not listed as a permitted use by right requires a Use Permit.
- **Approximate acreage:** ~100 acres developed industrial land countywide (unincorporated Modoc County), per 2018 General Plan

### 2. Industrial Light (IL) Zone

- **Description (from General Plan):** The Industrial Light (IL) zone is compatible with commercial, higher-density residential, and agricultural adjacent land uses — intended for lighter industrial operations with less off-site impact.
- **Municode chapter:** Chapter number for IL zone not confirmed via web fetch — [VERIFY — contact Planning Dept. or access Municode Title 18 directly]
- Compatible uses include commercial and light industrial uses lacking significant air, noise, or hazmat impacts.

> Note: The City of Alturas also has approximately 31 acres of industrially zoned land within city limits (per 2018 General Plan). Those parcels are governed by the City of Alturas zoning code, not the county code.

## Use Permit (Conditional Use Permit) Process

The Modoc County Use Permit process is governed by **Title 18, Chapter 18.128** of the Modoc County Code. Key procedural requirements:

### Application Filing

Contact the Planning Department before filing. Submit concurrently:

| Item | Description |
|---|---|
| Planning Application + Supplemental Information | Property owner must sign or provide written authorization |
| Fees (payable to Modoc County Planning Department) | Minor: $700 + Environmental Review ($600) + recording fees; Intermediate: $1,000 + ER + recording; Major/Complex: $2,000 + ER + Time-and-Materials + recording |
| Description of Current Uses | Clear description of existing uses and activities |
| Description of Proposed Project | Type of operation, hours, employee count, types of hazardous materials or vapors, solid waste, sewer and water needs, special features |
| Plans/maps | Reproducible, drawn to scale; project location and facility details |
| Compliance demonstration | Show consistency with Title 18 and applicable zone standards |
| Fish and Game Filing Fees (post-decision, for CEQA projects) | $2,044 (payable to Modoc County) + $50 (payable to Modoc County Clerk) |

### Processing / Decision

- **Public Hearing:** Planning Commission holds a public hearing after the application is accepted as complete (notice per §18.140.050)
- **Completeness review:** County provides written notice of deficiencies within 30 days of submission
- **Decision:** Planning Commission may approve, approve with conditions, or deny
- **Approval findings (all four required):**
  1. Will not be detrimental to health, safety, peace, morals, comfort, or general welfare of persons in the vicinity
  2. Will not be detrimental or injurious to nearby property or the general welfare of the County
  3. The purposes of Title 18 would not be better achieved by rezoning
  4. The proposed use at the proposed location is consistent with the purpose of the zone
- **Conditions:** Commission may impose conditions including dedications, bonding, recorded notices, security, or limitations on administrative/permitted uses
- **Appeal:** Any interested person may appeal Planning Commission decisions per Chapter 18.144
- **Waiting period:** No building permit or other entitlement may issue until the appeal period has expired and any appeal is resolved in favor of granting the use permit

### Commencement and Expiration

- Use permit expires if the activity has not been actively and substantially commenced within **one year** of approval (or affirmation on appeal)
- Planning Director may grant an initial one-year extension for circumstances beyond permittee's control (administrative, no public notice)
- Planning Commission may grant extensions beyond one year (requires public hearing)

## General Plan Industrial Land Use Guidance (2018)

The 2018 General Plan identifies the following as critical issues for industrial land use in Modoc County:
- Amount of suitable industrial land
- Location relative to infrastructure (sewer, water, fire protection)
- Zoning consistency
- Obstacles to development (infrastructure, utilities)
- Industrial parks concept limited by sparse population and lack of utilities

Industrial development in unincorporated areas must demonstrate adequate water supply and fire protection; development is discouraged in areas lacking these services.
