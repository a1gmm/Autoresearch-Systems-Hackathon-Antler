## Fire AHJ

**Primary AHJ**: CAL FIRE Madera-Mariposa-Merced Unit (MMU) — provides wildland and structural fire protection for state-responsibility land throughout Mariposa County.
**County fire agency**: Mariposa County Fire Department — county agency organized under Mariposa County Code Chapter 2.96; operates cooperatively with CAL FIRE MMU. No independent municipal fire departments exist (no incorporated cities in county).

**Fire Prevention contact**: FirePrevention@mariposacounty.gov
**Fire Department main**: (209) 966-4330
**ECC/Dispatch**: (209) 966-3615 / 9-1-1
**Burn Day Hotline**: (209) 966-1200 or (888) 440-2876

Source: https://www.mariposacounty.gov/76/Fire-Department

## Adopted Code Edition

The Mariposa County Building Department enforces the **2025 California Building Standards Code (Title 24)** as the current edition statewide (effective 2026-01-01). The specific **California Fire Code (CFC) edition** formally adopted by ordinance into the Mariposa County Code (Title 15) was **not confirmed from online sources** — the county Building Department page references "current edition of the California Building Codes and local amendments" without specifying the CFC year. **[VERIFY: contact Building Dept at (209) 966-3934 or building@mariposacounty.gov to confirm adopted CFC edition and adopting ordinance number; check Title 15 at mariposa.municipalcodeonline.com]**

Source: http://www.mariposacounty.gov/67/Building

## Municipal Code Chapter

- **County Code**: Mariposa County Code, hosted at https://mariposa.municipalcodeonline.com/
- **Chapter 2.96**: Mariposa County Fire Department organizational structure (amended by Ordinance 818; state fire marshal duties assigned to county fire chief by Ordinance 930, March 3, 1998)
- **Title 15**: Building and construction codes (adopts California Building Standards Code with local amendments)
- **Section 15.10.260**: Building code violation enforcement procedures (added by Ordinance 1173, adopted May 10, 2022)
- **Fire Hazard Severity Zones**: Ordinance adopted by Board of Supervisors at September 2, 2025 meeting (ordinance number not yet recorded in the public ordinance index at time of fetch) — adds FHSZ provisions to county code **[VERIFY: ordinance number once codified]**

## Permit Types and Fees

The fire department page does not publish a standalone fire permit fee schedule online. Planning application fees that include fire review (2025 fee schedule):

| Application Type | County Fire Component (2025) |
|---|---|
| Administrative Use Permit | $357 (County Fire review) |
| Conditional Use Permit | $100 (County Fire) |
| Commercial/Industrial/Manufacturing Site Plan | $100 (County Fire) |

These are fire-review components of planning applications, not standalone fire permits. **[VERIFY: whether separate operational fire permits (hot work, high-piled storage, hazmat use) are issued by Mariposa County Fire Prevention and what the fees are — contact FirePrevention@mariposacounty.gov]**

## Burn Permits

- **CAL FIRE seasonal burn permits**: Required annually beginning May 1 in Mariposa County (typically through fall fire season); obtained free online at **burnpermit.fire.ca.gov**.
- **APCD burn permits**: Separate permit required from Mariposa County APCD for open burning; contact APCD at (209) 966-2220.
- **Open burning restrictions**: Burn Day status information at Burn Day Hotline; all burning suspended during Red Flag conditions.
