## Inspection Frequencies

- **UST facilities**: Inspected **annually**
- **All other regulated facilities** (HMBP, HWG, APSA): Inspected at a minimum **once every three years**
- **New businesses**: Located by EHU via newspaper monitoring or field surveys
- Results of compliance inspections become part of the business file and are available to emergency response agencies and for public review

Source: Mariposa County Area Plan for Emergency Response to Hazardous Materials Incidents, January 2025, p. 11–12

## CERS Reporting

All HMBP and CUPA program documentation submitted electronically via the **California Environmental Reporting System (CERS)**:
- Regulator portal: https://cersregulator2.calepa.ca.gov/
- Public portal: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/

Emergency response agencies (fire, police) may request read access to CERS facility data through EHU (email EH@mariposacounty.gov with subject line "Urgent CUPA"); each agency may have up to two Lead Users.

## Area Plan

**Mariposa County Area Plan for Emergency Response to Hazardous Materials Incidents**, January 2025
- Prepared by EHU per HSC §25500 et seq. and 19 CCR §5020.1–5020.8
- Revised on 3-year cycle (prior editions: 1991, 1995, 2001, 2003, 2009, 2016, 2025)
- Covers all state routes through the county (SR-49, SR-140, portions of SR-132, SR-120, SR-41)
- Hazmat classes present in county: explosives, toxics, corrosives, flammables, oxidizers, cryogenics, compressed gases, radioactive materials, agricultural pesticides

Source: https://www.mariposacounty.gov/DocumentCenter/View/100511/Mariposa-County-Area-Plan-January-2025

## CalARP Status

As of January 2025 Area Plan: **no CalARP-regulated facilities** exist in Mariposa County. If a facility meets CalARP thresholds, EHU as CUPA would administer the program.
