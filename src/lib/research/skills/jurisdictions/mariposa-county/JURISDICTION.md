## GOTCHA: No incorporated cities; no POTW; no industrial zoning

Mariposa County has **no incorporated cities** — it is one of only three California counties (with Alpine and Trinity) that contains zero incorporated municipalities. The town of Mariposa (county seat) is unincorporated. Consequently there are no city-level CUPAs, no municipal fire departments, no city building departments, and no city-level pretreatment programs; the county agencies below are the sole local AHJs countywide. Land use is organized around 11 "Town Planning Areas."

**FEDERAL-LAND CARVE-OUT**: A large share of Mariposa County is Yosemite National Park (NPS) and Sierra/Stanislaus National Forest (USFS) federal land, where federal authorities — not county EHS/fire/building — hold jurisdiction. Confirm whether a regulated facility sits on federal land before applying county programs.

## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air — Mariposa County APCD (own independent district)

- Mariposa County is served by its **own independent Air Pollution Control District** — the **Mariposa County APCD** — not SCAQMD, SJVAPCD, or any neighboring district. The APCD is a county agency co-located with Environmental Health at 5100 Bullion St, Mariposa, CA 95338; phone (209) 966-2220.
- **Permit requirement**: APCD Rule 12 (in the EPA-compiled ruleset) requires that no person shall build, alter, or replace any equipment whose use may cause air contaminants without first obtaining a **special use permit from the Mariposa County Planning Commission**. The Planning Commission may not approve the permit until the applicant demonstrates compliance with California Air Resources Board and U.S. air quality standards plus local APCD rules.
- **Key rules** (EPA-compiled ruleset, last updated in SIP September 2022): Rule 202 (visible emissions — no source may discharge opacity >3 minutes in any 1 hour); Rule 205 (particulate matter); Rule 6.1 (NOx limit 200 ppm from combustion); Rule 6.3 (combustion contaminants ≤0.1 grain/ft³ at 12% CO₂); Rule 6.4 (fuel burning equipment thresholds: ≤200 lb/hr SO₂, ≤140 lb/hr NOx, ≤10 lb/hr combustion contaminants); Rule 8 (petroleum product storage in tanks >40,000 gal requires permit); Rule 11 (general nuisance prohibition); Rule 11.1 (open burning of petroleum waste, tires, demolition debris, auto bodies prohibited after Dec 31, 1971).
- **No CalARP facilities** currently exist in Mariposa County as of the January 2025 Area Plan.
- Burn permits: CAL FIRE MMU issues seasonal burn permits (burnpermit.fire.ca.gov); APCD also issues open burning permits; both are required for hazard-reduction burning seasonally.

### Wastewater — No POTW; OWTS (septic) county-wide

- **No POTW/pretreatment program**: Mariposa County has **no industrial pretreatment program** and no significant POTW. The county is almost entirely served by on-site wastewater treatment systems (OWTS/septic). A few densely populated areas have small public sewer systems, but none operate a formal pretreatment program.
- **Governing code**:
  - Title 13.08 (Sewage Disposal) — siting, design, construction, O&M of OWTS
  - Title 13.06 (Sewers) — connections to sewer systems
  - Title 13.10 — gray water ordinance
  - Title 13.12 (Cleaning of Sewage Disposal Systems) — standards for liquid waste pumping businesses
- **LAMP**: Mariposa County Local Agency Management Program (LAMP) for OWTS, adopted January 24, 2017 (per State Water Board OWTS Policy, July 2012). EHU administers; systems discharging >3,500 gpd referred to Central Valley RWQCB (Fresno office) on case-by-case basis; systems >10,000 gpd go directly to RWQCB.
- **Industrial/commercial wastewater exclusions**: The LAMP explicitly does NOT apply to individual OWTS designed for commercial/industrial wastewater, food processing wastewater, or winery/brewery wastewater from commercial development in areas not served by public sewer — those require specific RWQCB authorization (WDR or NPDES permit from Region 5, Central Valley).
- **WDR**: The county operates the Mariposa County Landfill under WDR Order R5-2023-0010 (Title 27 landfill discharges to land, adopted April 27, 2023) — this is a solid waste facility, not a POTW.

### Zoning — Title 17 Development Code (no industrial zone)

- **Governing code**: Mariposa County Title 17 (Development Code — Zoning); Title 16 (Subdivisions). Administered by Mariposa County Planning Department, 5100 Bullion St, Mariposa, CA 95338; Phone: (209) 966-5151; Email: planningdept@mariposacounty.org.
- **No M-1/M-2 industrial zones exist**: The September 2024 revised preliminary draft of Title 17 Article 2 contains **no standalone industrial zone**. Zone families are: Agricultural/Resource (AE, GF, MP, TE), Residential (MG, MH, MT, RR), Commercial (CN, CR), Area Plan Zone (AP), Overlay Zones (AP, DR, HDR, MU, OW, SH), and Public Domain Zone (PDZ).
- **Industrial-type uses**: Mining (quarries, gravel pits, mineral processing) allowed via CUP in AE, GF, TE zones (Ch. 17.44); small-scale mining via Administrative Use Permit (AUP); wineries allowed by right (P) in AE; commercial/industrial/manufacturing site plan (CIM) review process exists for applicable uses.
- **Permit types**: P = by right; AUP = Administrative Use Permit (Ch. 17.104); CUP = Conditional Use Permit (Ch. 17.104); TUP = Temporary Use Permit (Ch. 17.112).
- **Development Code update**: Active update ongoing through Planning Commission workshops; most recent working draft (Sept 2024) in Planning Commission agenda packets; next public meeting January 9, 2025. Industrial zone structure may change in adopted code.
- **2025 planning fees** (from fee schedule, effective 2025): Commercial/Industrial/Manufacturing Site Plan (CIM) deposit $5,005; CUP deposit $2,984; AUP deposit $725.
