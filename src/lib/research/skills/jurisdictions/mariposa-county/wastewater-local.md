## KEY FINDING: No POTW, No Pretreatment Program

Mariposa County has **no industrial pretreatment program** and operates **no significant POTW** (publicly owned treatment works). The county is served almost entirely by on-site wastewater treatment systems (OWTS/septic). A few densely populated areas have small public sewer systems, but none operate under a formal pretreatment or industrial wastewater permit program.

- **Industrial/commercial wastewater** from commercial development not served by public sewer requires specific authorization from the **Central Valley RWQCB** (Region 5, Fresno office) via a Waste Discharge Requirements (WDR) order or NPDES permit — not from the county.
- LAMP explicitly excludes from county OWTS jurisdiction: commercial/industrial wastewater, food processing wastewater, and winery/brewery wastewater unless specifically authorized by RWQCB.

## Local Agency Management Program (LAMP) for OWTS

**LAMP adopted**: January 24, 2017
**Authority**: Per State Water Board Water Quality Control Policy for Siting, Design, Operation, and Maintenance of OWTS (July 2012) and Central Valley Basin Plan
**Administrator**: Mariposa County Health Department, Environmental Health Services Division

### OWTS Types Covered

1. **Standard systems**: Gravity-fed septic/leach field; no pretreatment device
2. **Alternative systems**: Advanced treatment/distribution; must be designed by licensed PE (Civil), Professional Geologist, CEG, REHS, or Certified Professional Soil Scientist; used where standard systems are not feasible

### Thresholds for RWQCB Referral

- Systems discharging **3,500–10,000 gpd**: Collaborative review with RWQCB Fresno office, on case-by-case basis (per Health Department Policy 16-04)
- Systems discharging **>3,500 gpd**: Referred to RWQCB
- Systems discharging **>10,000 gpd**: Go directly to RWQCB for WDR/NPDES

### Key Prohibitions (OWTS)

- No leach pits wider than 3 ft or deeper than 5 ft
- No waste disposal wells or cesspools
- No discharge to surface
- No installation in areas with: erosion/landslide risk, permanent/intermittent springs, groundwater within 2 ft of surface, standing/flood-prone areas, ledge rock/hardpan/soils >120 mpi within 2 ft, fractured rock, slopes with breakout risk

## LOCAL DISCHARGE LIMITS TABLE

**Not applicable** — no POTW, no pretreatment program, no local discharge limits table. Numeric effluent limits for any industrial discharge to receiving waters are set by the Central Valley RWQCB in facility-specific WDR or NPDES orders.

## Industrial/Commercial Discharge Pathway

A facility generating industrial wastewater in Mariposa County must:
1. Determine if located on federal land (Yosemite NP or National Forest) → federal jurisdiction
2. If not on federal land and no public sewer available → obtain WDR or NPDES permit from **Central Valley RWQCB**, Region 5, Fresno office: 1685 "E" Street, Fresno, CA 93706; (559) 445-5116; https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/centralvalley/
3. If a small public sewer system exists nearby → contact county EHU or the sewer system operator for connection requirements

## Landfill WDR (Not a POTW)

The county operates the **Mariposa County Landfill** under WDR Order R5-2023-0010 (Title 27 Discharges to Land, adopted April 27, 2023) — this is a solid waste disposal facility, not a wastewater treatment plant.
- Facility: Mariposa County Landfill, 5593 CA-49, Mariposa, CA 95338; Parcel 012-120-005-0; GeoTracker ID L10008183603
- Prior order: R5-2003-0094
