## KEY FINDING: No Industrial Zone (M-1/M-2) Exists

The Mariposa County Title 17 Development Code contains **no standalone industrial zone** (no M-1, M-2, or equivalent). The September 2024 revised preliminary draft of Article 2 (Zones, Allowable Uses, and Development Standards) establishes only these zone families:

| Zone Family | Zone Abbreviations |
|---|---|
| Agricultural and Resource | AE, GF, MP, TE |
| Residential | MG, MH, MT, RR |
| Commercial | CN, CR |
| Area Plan Zone | AP |
| Overlay Zones | AP, DR, HDR, MU, OW, SH |
| Public Domain Zone | PDZ |

**No industrial zone exists.** Industrial-type uses (mining, processing, manufacturing) are accommodated through conditional or administrative use permit processes within agricultural/resource zones, or via the Commercial/Industrial/Manufacturing (CIM) site plan process.

Source: Title 17 Article 2, Revised Preliminary Draft, September 2024 — https://ca-mariposacounty.civicplus.com/DocumentCenter/View/99107/Article-2-Revised-Preliminary-Draft-

| Mines, Quarries, and Gravel Pits | CUP | CUP | CUP | CUP | Ch. 17.44; Ch. 17.52.170 |
| Small Scale Mining | AUP | AUP | AUP | AUP | Ch. 17.44; Ch. 17.52.170 |
| Timber Processing Facilities | — | — | — | CUP | Ch. 17.10.040(B)(5) |
| Wineries | P (by right) | — | — | — | Products sold must be grown on-site |
| Utility Facilities and Infrastructure | P | P | P | P | — |
| Utilities Substations | AUP | AUP | AUP | AUP | — |
| Wireless Telecommunications Towers | P | P | P | P | Ch. 17.58 |
| Firewood Harvesting and Sales | AUP | AUP | AUP | AUP | Ch. 17.10.040(B)(6) |

**P** = Permitted by right (zoning clearance required if construction involved)
**AUP** = Administrative Use Permit (Ch. 17.104)
**CUP** = Conditional Use Permit (Ch. 17.104)
**—** = Not allowed

Source: Table 2-1, Title 17 Article 2 Revised Preliminary Draft, September 2024

### Commercial Zones (CN, CR) — Industrial-Adjacent Uses

Commercial zones (CN = Neighborhood Commercial, CR = Regional Commercial) do not permit heavy industrial uses. Manufacturing, warehousing, and processing are generally not listed in commercial zones. **[VERIFY: check current adopted Title 17 for any commercial-industrial hybrid uses in CN/CR]**

## Permit Processes

### Conditional Use Permit (CUP) — Chapter 17.104

Required for mining, quarries, mineral/construction material processing in all zones. Process involves:
- Planning Department application
- Public notice (posted + affected property owners)
- Planning Commission hearing
- Board of Supervisors appeal available

**2025 CUP application fees**: Deposit $2,984; Public Noticing (posted) $65; Public Noticing (affected property owners) $65; Public Works review $357; County Fire review $100; Health Dept review $198

### Administrative Use Permit (AUP) — Chapter 17.104

Used for small-scale mining and some other moderate-impact uses. Lower threshold than CUP.
**2025 AUP deposit**: $725

### Commercial/Industrial/Manufacturing Site Plan (CIM)

Specific site plan review for commercial, industrial, and manufacturing development:
**2025 CIM deposit**: $5,005; Public Noticing (posted) $65; Public Noticing (affected property owners) $65; Public Works $357; County Fire $100; Health Dept $198

Source: https://mariposacounty.gov/DocumentCenter/View/99623/2025-Fee-Schedule-by-Application

## Area Plans and Town Planning Areas

Many commercial and residential uses are governed not by base zones but by **Area Plans** for the 11 Town Planning Areas (TPAs) — e.g., Mariposa TPA, Coulterville TPA, Wawona TPA, etc. Industrial uses within TPAs may be controlled by TPA-specific Area Plans rather than the countywide base zones. **[VERIFY: check Area Plan for relevant TPA if facility is within a Town Planning Area]**

## Williamson Act Note

Approximately 50% of privately owned lands in the county are under Williamson Act contracts, prohibiting non-agricultural development for contract term. Confirm whether a parcel is under Williamson Act contract before pursuing industrial land use permits. Lands under contract are also subject to contract terms per Title 17 Table 2-1, §F.
