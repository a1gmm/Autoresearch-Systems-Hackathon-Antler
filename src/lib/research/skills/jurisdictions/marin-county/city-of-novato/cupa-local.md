## CUPA Structure

The City of Novato is **not** its own CUPA. The countywide CUPA — operated by **Marin County Public
Works** — serves all incorporated cities and unincorporated areas of Marin County, including Novato.
Approximately 850 Marin County businesses are regulated under this program.

## Programs Administered

Marin County CUPA administers all six California Unified Program elements:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — facilities above HSC § 25507 thresholds must file electronically via CERS (California Environmental Reporting System).
2. **Hazardous Materials Management Plan / Inventory Statement**
3. **Hazardous Waste Generator / Tiered Permitting Program**
4. **Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program** — permits, inspections, and enforcement.
5. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Tank (APSA) Program**
6. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) Program**

## HMBP Filing

All facilities meeting HSC § 25507 reporting thresholds must file via **CERS** (California
Environmental Reporting System). There is no separate Novato-specific filing portal.
