## Governing Code

**Novato Municipal Code (NMC) Chapter XIX — Zoning** (adopted and codified; current through at least
Ordinance No. 1730, November 18, 2025). Industrial and commercial-industrial uses are governed by
**Division 19.12** (Commercial/Industrial Zoning Districts).

Municode link: https://library.municode.com/ca/novato/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CHXIXZO

## Industrial & Commercial-Industrial Zone Districts (Division 19.12)

NMC Table 2-7 (in § 19.12.030) sets permitted vs. conditional uses per district. Two districts are
directly relevant to EHS industrial operations:

### CI — Commercial/Industrial District

- **Purpose (NMC 19.12.020):** Intended for "intensive commercial land uses, including automobile
  repair shops, construction[-related] uses" and similar commercial-industrial activities.
- **Key permitted uses:** Metal fabrication, laundries, warehousing, assembly operations, recycling
  facilities, equipment rental, auto repair, construction material yards.
- **Development standards (Table 2-8):**
  - Maximum FAR: **1.0**
  - Maximum building coverage: **40%**
  - Maximum height: **35 ft**
  - Setbacks: vary by adjacency to residential zones (typically 6–25 ft)

### LIO — Light Industrial/Office District

- **Purpose (NMC 19.12.020):** Intended for "light industrial and manufacturing uses" with "limited
  generation of nuisances" — intended to be compatible with nearby non-industrial uses.
- **Key permitted uses:** Light manufacturing, assembly, R&D, office, warehousing, recycling
  collection, equipment/vehicle storage.
- **Development standards (Table 2-8):**
  - Maximum FAR: **0.40**
  - Maximum building coverage: **60%**
  - Height: not specified in Table 2-8 (defer to general NMC standards)
  - Setbacks: vary by adjacency

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

CUPs are governed by **NMC Division 19.42**, specifically **NMC § 19.42.050** (Use Permits). General
process:

1. Pre-application meeting with Planning Division (recommended).
2. Application submittal — includes project description, site plan, environmental review materials.
3. Public notice and hearing before the **Planning Commission** (for discretionary CUPs).
4. Decision by Planning Commission; appeal to City Council available.
5. Conditions of approval recorded against property.

Uses requiring a CUP in CI and LIO districts (examples from Table 2-7): auto sales, bars/taverns,
hotels/motels, veterinary facilities, drive-through services, and certain recycling operations — the
exact list is in Table 2-7 of NMC 19.12.030.

## Overlay Districts Relevant to Industrial Operations

**NMC Division 19.16** contains overlay districts that can affect industrial sites:

- **§ 19.16.070** — Affordable Housing Overlay, Planned Hazardous Occupancy, and related overlays
  (confirm applicability for specific parcel at zoning map)
- **Baylands Overlay, Flood Hazard Overlay, Historic Overlay** — may impose additional requirements
  on parcels within those mapped boundaries.
