## POTW Operator

**Novato Sanitary District (NSD)** — an independent special district; the City of Novato does not
operate its own wastewater system. NSD is the designated POTW (Publicly Owned Treatment Works) and
the pretreatment control authority for all industrial and non-domestic users discharging within the
Novato service area.

## Governing Ordinance

**NSD Ordinance No. 70** — *Sanitary Code of Novato Sanitary District* — as amended through August
2022 (Ordinance No. 124). Full title: *"An Ordinance Regulating the Use of Public and Private Sewers
and Drains, the Installation and Connection of Building Sewers…Regulating the Discharge of Waters
and Wastes into the Public Sewer System and Providing Penalties for the Violation Thereof."*

## NPDES Permit

NSD's wastewater treatment plant holds an NPDES permit issued by the **San Francisco Bay Regional
Water Quality Control Board**. A tentative order for reissuance was publicly noticed in 2020.
(See: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/sanfranciscobay/board_decisions/tentative_orders/novato/tentative-order.pdf)

## Article VIII — Industrial / Non-Domestic Discharge Rules

**Ordinance No. 70, Article VIII** (Sections 801–832) governs all non-domestic discharges. Key
operative sections:

- **Sec. 802** — Unlawful to discharge non-domestic wastewater without a permit; District reserves
  right to refuse permit.
- **Sec. 804** — Federal Categorical Pretreatment Standards (40 CFR Part 400 et seq.) apply; more
  stringent federal limits supersede local limits immediately upon promulgation.
- **Sec. 805** — District may grant "fundamentally different factors" modifications to categorical
  standards if conditions justify.
- **Sec. 806** — State requirements apply where more stringent than federal or local limits.
- **Sec. 807** — District reserves right to impose more stringent limits by ordinance if needed to
  comply with NPDES permit.
- **Sec. 808** — General discharge prohibitions (flammables, petroleum greases, corrosives, toxics
  causing pass-through or interference, solids/viscous substances).

## Section 809 — Specific Pollutant Limitations (Local Discharge Limits)

Extracted verbatim from Ordinance No. 70 (as amended through Ord. 124, August 2022):

**(b)** Fats, oils, or grease (petroleum ether soluble): **200 mg/l maximum**

**(c)** pH: **5.5 minimum — 8.5 maximum**

**(d)** TDS: **2,420 mg/l maximum**; Chloride: **480 mg/l maximum** (where total daily discharge
≤ 5,000 gal/day; excess quantities evaluated case-by-case)

**(e)** BOD or TSS: **400 mg/l** without special agreement; **1,200 mg/l** absolute maximum (with
agreement and payment for additional plant capacity)

**(f) Metals and Priority Pollutants — Maximum Concentrations (mg/l):**

| Pollutant | Max Concentration (mg/l) |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| Ammonia | 125.0 |
| Arsenic | 0.5 |
| Boron | 1.0 |
| Cadmium | 0.11 |
| Chromium (total) | 1.0 |
| Copper | 1.5 |
| Lead | 0.4 |
| Mercury | 0.1 |
| Nickel | 1.0 |
| Silver | 0.43 |
| Zinc | 2.6 |
| Cyanide | 1.0 |
| Phenols | 5.0 |
| PAHs (polyaromatic hydrocarbons) | 1.0 |
| TICH (total identifiable chlorinated hydrocarbons) | 0.15 |
| TTO (total toxic organics) | case-by-case* |

\* TTO limit set on a case-by-case basis in the Non-Domestic Sewer Use Permit.

Other pollutants not listed, and upward/downward adjustments, determined case-by-case and incorporated
into individual Non-Domestic Sewer Use Permits (Sec. 809, last paragraph).

**(g)** Objectionable color: prohibited unless discharge shows <60% light transmissibility loss vs.
distilled water (measured at 400–800 nm, pH 5.0–9.0, 0.45 µm membrane filter).

**(h)** Sulfide-forming pollutants: prohibited where they would generate sewer sulfide >**1.0 mg/l**.

**Sec. 810** — Grease traps required at restaurants, commercial food prep; oil/sand interceptors at
gas stations, auto repair, vehicle washes. Chemical dissolution of grease specifically prohibited.

## Non-Domestic Sewer Use Permit (Sec. 817)

All non-domestic users must obtain a permit before connecting. Three classes:

| Class | Who Needs It | Trigger |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Class I** | Significant Non-Domestic Users (SNDUs) | Categorical user (40 CFR); OR discharge ≥25,000 gal/day process water; OR ≥5% of plant hydraulic/organic capacity; OR adverse-effect potential per District judgment |
| **Class II** | All other non-domestic users not specifically exempted | Default permit for non-Class-I industrial/commercial users |
| **Class III** | Operations with significant accidental discharge potential | Issued at District discretion; supplements Class I/II obligations |

Exempted from Class I/II (unless determined otherwise): restaurants, mortuaries, markets with garbage
grinders (per Sec. 817).

## Application Process (Sec. 819)

Applications for Class I or Class II permits must include:
- SIC code; facility/process description; site plans and schematics
- Estimated wastewater flow; constituents and characteristics
- Pre-treatment facility locations and sampling points
- Pre-application walk-through inspection by District representative
