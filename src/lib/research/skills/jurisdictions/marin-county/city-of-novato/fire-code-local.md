## AHJ

**Novato Fire Protection District (NFPD)** — an independent special district (not a City department).
Service area: ~71 sq mi encompassing all of the City of Novato, bounded by Sonoma County (N),
Pacheco Grade/Marinwood (S), Petaluma River/San Pablo Bay (E), ~3 miles west of Stafford Lake (W).
Estimated service population: 65,000.

## Adopted Fire Code

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Adopted code | **2022 California Fire Code (CFC)** |
| Adopting ordinance | **NFPD Ordinance 2022-1** |
| Also adopts | Appendix A of the **2021 International Wildland-Urban Interface Code (IWUIC)** |
| Repeals | NFPD Ordinance 2019-2 (prior adoption) |

Ordinance 2022-1 is titled: *"An Ordinance of the Novato Fire Protection District Adopting and Making
Local Amendments to the 2022 Edition of the California Fire Code and Appendix A of the 2021
International Wildland-Urban Interface Code, Regulating and Governing the Safeguarding of Life and
Property from Fire and Explosion Hazards…"*

No municipal code chapter number — NFPD is an independent special district and does not appear in the
Novato Municipal Code (NMC). Its ordinances are published directly on the NFPD website.

## Local Amendments

The full text of Ordinance 2022-1's local amendments is on the NFPD ordinances page (PDF behind the
district's document management system, which blocks direct fetch). Based on publicly available
summaries, the ordinance:

- Adopts the 2022 CFC in its entirety with local amendments.
- Incorporates Appendix A of the 2021 IWUIC (Wildland-Urban Interface provisions); this is
  significant given Novato's WUI exposure in the Stafford Lake / Bel Marin Keys area.
- Provides for issuance of permits and collection of fees (fee amounts set separately by
  **Ordinance 2025-02**, which repealed Resolution No. 2008-05 and established a new master fee
  schedule).

> **Note:** The specific local amendments to CFC chapters (e.g., modified hazmat MAQs, high-piled
> storage thresholds, or Chapter 1 administrative changes) are in the ordinance PDF. NFPD's
> ordinances page was not directly fetchable (HTTP 403). Contact the Prevention Division to obtain a
> copy or review it on-site.

## Permit Types Requiring Approved Plans

Per NFPD published guidance, permits with plan review are required for:

- Fire protection systems (sprinklers, suppression)
- Fire service underground (hydrant leads, risers)
- Pre-engineered systems (dry chem, clean-agent)
- Tent and canopy installations

Operations-based permits (hazmat storage, high-piled combustible storage, etc.) follow the 2022 CFC
Chapter 1 and Chapter 50 thresholds unless modified by Ordinance 2022-1 local amendments.

## Fee Schedule

**Ordinance 2025-02** — adopted by NFPD Board — repeals Resolution 2008-05 and sets the current
master fee schedule for all Prevention Division services. Specific fee amounts: contact NFPD
Prevention Division or download Ordinance 2025-02 from the NFPD ordinances page.
