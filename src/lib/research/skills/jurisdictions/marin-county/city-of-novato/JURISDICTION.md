## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **CUPA agency:** Marin County Public Works — Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA). The City of Novato is **not** its own CUPA; the countywide CUPA serves all incorporated cities in Marin, including Novato.
- **Programs administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Underground Storage Tank (UST) permits/inspections, hazardous waste generator oversight, and related Unified Program elements. Facilities must file HMBP electronically to CERS (California Environmental Reporting System).
- **Contact:** Julia Barnes, Supervising Hazardous Materials Specialist — (415) 473-6530 — Marin Commons, 1600 Los Gamos Drive, San Rafael, CA 94903

### 2. Fire AHJ — Novato Fire Protection District (NFPD)

- **AHJ:** **Novato Fire Protection District (NFPD)** — an independent special district (not a City department) covering ~71 sq mi including the entire City of Novato, bounded by Sonoma County (N), Pacheco Grade/Marinwood (S), Petaluma River/San Pablo Bay (E), ~3 miles past Stafford Lake (W).
- **Adopted fire code:** **2022 California Fire Code**, adopted via **NFPD Ordinance 2022-1**. The ordinance also adopts Appendix A of the 2021 International Wildland-Urban Interface Code. It repealed the prior Ordinance 2019-2.
- **Fee schedule:** NFPD adopted a new master fee schedule via **Ordinance 2025-02**. Permit types requiring approved plans include: fire protection systems, fire service underground, pre-engineered systems, tent and canopy installation.
- **Contact/portal:** Prevention Division — (415) 878-2623 — prevention@novatofire.org — fire permit applications via https://www.novatofire.org/how-do-i/submit-prevention-forms/permits-plan-review
- Source (ordinances): https://www.novatofire.org/prevention/ordinances
- Source (permits/fees): https://www.novatofire.org/how-do-i/submit-prevention-forms/permits-plan-review
- Source (district boundaries): https://www.marinlafco.org/novato-fire-protection-district

### 3. Building Authority

- **AHJ:** City of Novato — Building Division (Community Development Department). Issues building permits, conducts plan review and inspections.
- **Adopted code:** California Building Standards Code (Title 24); Novato Municipal Code **Chapter IV** (Building and Housing) contains local adopting ordinances. The 2025 edition of Title 24 took effect statewide 2026-01-01; confirm current local adopting ordinance via Municode.
- **Permit portal:** "Novato Permits" online portal — https://www.novato.gov/government/community-development/building-division
- **Fees:** City Master Fee Schedule — https://www.novato.gov/government/finance/master-fee-schedule

### 4. POTW / Sewer Use — Novato Sanitary District (NSD)

- **POTW operator:** **Novato Sanitary District** — an independent special district; the City of Novato does not operate its own wastewater system.
- **Sewer Use Ordinance:** **NSD Ordinance No. 70** (revised August 2022). Regulates use of public/private sewers and drains, connection of building sewers, and discharge of waters and wastes. Contains Article VIII on Non-Domestic Wastewater Discharge, Section 804 (Federal Categorical Pretreatment Standards), and Section 805 (Modification of Federal Categorical Pretreatment Standards).
- **Industrial discharge:** Regulated under Ordinance 70 consistent with federal pretreatment regulations (40 CFR 403). Industrial users subject to categorical standards must obtain pretreatment authorization. [VERIFY - NSD does not appear to list an online IWP application portal; contact district directly.]
- **NPDES permit:** NSD's wastewater treatment plant holds an NPDES permit reissued by the San Francisco Bay Regional Water Quality Control Board (reissuance noticed 2020).
- **Contact:** 500 Davidson Street, Novato, CA 94945 — (415) 892-1694 — info@novatosan.com — M–F 8 am–5 pm (closed alternate Fridays)
- Source (ordinance): https://novatosan.com/doc/1303/
- Source (sewer regulations page): https://novatosan.com/doing-business/sewer-use-regulations/

### 5. MS4 / Stormwater

- **Program:** City of Novato is a co-permittee under the **Marin Countywide Stormwater Pollution Prevention Program (MCSTOPPP)**, a joint program formed in 1993 by Marin County, its 11 cities/towns, and the Marin County Flood Control and Water Conservation District.
- **NPDES permit:** Phase II MS4 General Permit No. **CAS000004** (Water Quality Order No. 2013-0001-DWQ), issued by the State Water Resources Control Board.
- **Local ordinance:** Novato Municipal Code **§ 5-27** (within Chapter V — Development Standards) contains stormwater/NPDES illicit-discharge controls. Illicit discharges (silt, oil, road debris, floatables) from residential, commercial, or construction areas are prohibited.
- **City contact:** Public Works Department — (415) 899-8246
- Source (NPDES program page): https://www.novato.org/government/public-works/maintenance/drainage-pump-station-maintenance/npdes-storm-water-program
- Source (MCSTOPPP): https://mcstoppp.org/
- Source (NMC § 5-27): https://library.municode.com/ca/novato/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CHVDEST_5-27IMPL

### 6. Zoning — Industrial / Commercial-Industrial Districts

- **Zoning code:** Novato Municipal Code **Chapter XIX** (Zoning). Industrial and commercial-industrial land uses are governed by **Division 19.12** (Commercial/Industrial Zoning Districts).
  - NMC 19.12.010 — Purpose
  - NMC 19.12.020 — Purposes of Commercial/Industrial Districts
  - NMC 19.12.030 — Commercial/Industrial District Land Uses and Permit Requirements (Table 2-7)
  - NMC 19.12.040 — Commercial/Industrial District General Development Standards
- **Conditional Use Permits (CUPs):** Permit approval procedures are in **Division 19.42**, specifically NMC 19.42.050 (Use Permits). Allowable uses and permit tier (permitted vs. CUP) are established in NMC 19.06.030 and Table 2-7.
- **Special Purpose Districts:** NMC 19.14.030 (Table 2-9) governs Special Purpose District land uses and permit requirements.
