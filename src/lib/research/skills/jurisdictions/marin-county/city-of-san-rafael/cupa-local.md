> Research reference for the CUPA program serving the City of San Rafael.
> San Rafael is NOT its own CUPA. All CUPA programs within San Rafael city limits are administered by Marin County Public Works — Certified Unified Program Agency, serving all of Marin County (approximately 850 regulated businesses countywide).

## CUPA Identity

- **CUPA:** Marin County Public Works — Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA)
- **Administers:** Countywide, including City of San Rafael city limits
- **CUPA type:** County-administered (San Rafael is not a Participating Agency with its own sub-CUPA)
- **Administrator:** Julia Barnes, Supervising Hazardous Materials Specialist
- **Phone:** (415) 473-6530
- **Address:** Marin Commons, 1600 Los Gamos Drive, San Rafael, CA 94903
- **Agency page:** https://publicworks.marincounty.gov/agencies/certified-unified-program-agency-cupa/
- **CUPA main page:** https://publicworks.marincounty.gov/cupa/
- **Fee schedule page:** https://publicworks.marincounty.gov/certified-unified-program-agency-cupa-fees/

## Programs Administered

Marin County CUPA administers all six CalEPA unified programs for businesses within San Rafael:

| # | Program | Acronym |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan | HMBP |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator / Tiered Permitting Program | HWG/TP |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tank Program | UST |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act / Aboveground Storage Tanks | APSA/AST |
| 5 | California Accidental Release Prevention Program | CalARP |
| 6 | Hazardous Materials Management Plan / Inventory Statement | HMMP/IS |

- **Reporting system:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://publicworks.marincounty.gov/calepas-california-environmental-reporting-system-cers/
- **CUPA file review:** CUPA File Review Request Form available at marincounty.gov (2025 version)

## Local Ordinance / Municipal Code Chapter

- **City of San Rafael local hazmat ordinance:** None identified. No separate City of San Rafael hazmat or CUPA ordinance was found online. All HMBP/CUPA duties flow through Marin County CUPA under California Health & Safety Code §25500 et seq. and the county-wide CUPA authority.
- **Marin County CUPA fee ordinance:** Ordinance No. 3490 (Public Services Fees, effective July 1, 2017). Project fees schedule effective December 6, 2012. Fees are set annually.

### Delinquency Penalties

Fees delinquent for 30+ days: 25% penalty, plus additional monthly penalties; total penalty capped at 100% of original fee.

## HMBP Thresholds

State Health & Safety Code §25507 baseline thresholds apply (Marin County CUPA has not confirmed locally lower thresholds):
- Liquids: ≥55 gallons
- Solids: ≥500 lbs
- Gases: ≥200 cubic feet
