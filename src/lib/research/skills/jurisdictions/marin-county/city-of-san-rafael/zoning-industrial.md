## Industrial Zone Districts (SRMC Chapter 14.06)

Chapter 14.06 (Industrial Districts) is located within Division II — Base District Regulations. Section 14.06.010 establishes the specific purposes; Section 14.06.020 contains Table 14.06.020 (Land Use Standards); Section 14.06.030 contains Development Standards.

San Rafael has four industrial/light-industrial zone districts:

| District Symbol | Full Name | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | Industrial | Heavy/general industrial uses; manufacturing, warehousing, distribution |
| **LI/O** | Light Industrial / Office | Light industrial and office mix; lower-impact industrial uses |
| **CCI/O** | Cottage/Commercial Industrial / Office (Core Canal Industrial/Office) | Light industrial, automotive service, small-scale office; specialty retail consistent with industrial uses allowed if FAR and trip allocation standards met; general retail, personal service, and food service NOT permitted (severe parking congestion and small lot sizes) |
| **LMU** | Light Manufacturing Use | Light manufacturing uses |

### Chapter 14.06 Specific Purposes (§ 14.06.010)

Each district has stated purposes:
- **I:** Provide for and protect sites for industrial activity including manufacturing, processing, warehousing, and distribution that may generate noise, odors, or truck traffic incompatible with residential/commercial areas.
- **LI/O:** Provide for light industrial activities and compatible office uses in locations transitional between heavier industrial and commercial/residential zones.
- **CCI/O (Core Canal Industrial/Office):** Provide for light industrial, automotive service, and small-scale office uses; specialty retail consistent with industrial uses allowed subject to FAR requirements and trip allocation standards; general retail/personal service/food service restricted.
- **LMU:** Provide for light manufacturing uses.

## Land Use Permissions (Table 14.06.020)

Full Table 14.06.020 is in SRMC § 14.06.020. Key patterns confirmed:

- The table lists land use classifications (permitted, conditionally permitted, not permitted) across I, LI/O, CCI/O, and LMU.
- Uses may be permitted by right (P), conditionally permitted requiring a Use Permit (CUP / UP), or not permitted (—).
- Additional use regulations in supplemental sections may apply to specific uses regardless of the base district designation.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process (SRMC Chapter 14.22)

Chapter 14.22 (Use Permit) governs the CUP process. Three levels of review authority based on use type and zoning district:

| Level | Decision Maker | Hearing Required? | Processing Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Administrative | Planning Staff | No public hearing | 3–6 weeks total |
| Zoning Administrator | Zoning Administrator | Yes — public hearing | 6–32+ weeks |
| Planning Commission | Planning Commission | Yes — public hearing | 6–32+ weeks |

- **Public notice:** Mailed at least 15 days before hearings to properties within 300 feet of the site.
- **CEQA adds time:** Projects requiring Negative Declaration or EIR: add 6–12+ months.
- **Completeness review:** 30 days after application submittal.

## Development Standards (§ 14.06.030)

Development standards governing industrial districts include setbacks, height limits, floor-area ratio (FAR), and lot coverage. Specific numeric standards are in § 14.06.030 Table — [VERIFY exact figures via https://library.municode.com/ca/san_rafael/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT14ZO].

Key notes:
- **CCI/O district:** General retail, personal service, and food service uses are NOT permitted due to parking congestion and small lot sizes. Specialty retail consistent with industrial uses is allowed subject to FAR and trip allocation standards.
- **FAR transfers:** Ordinance No. 1964 (adopted November 19, 2018) codified a process for evaluating FAR transfers among properties within downtown and other zoning districts (per SRMC General Plan Policy LU-9).
