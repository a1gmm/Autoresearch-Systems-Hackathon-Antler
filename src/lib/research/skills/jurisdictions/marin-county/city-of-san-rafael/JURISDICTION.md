## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **CUPA agency:** Marin County Public Works — Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA). San Rafael is **not** its own CUPA; it is served county-wide by Marin County CUPA.
- **CUPA administrator:** Julia Barnes, Supervising Hazardous Materials Specialist.
- **Contact:** (415) 473-6530 | 1600 Los Gamos Drive, San Rafael, CA 94903.
- **Programs covered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generator, CalARP, UST, APSA (aboveground petroleum storage), and Tiered Permitting.
- **CUPA portal:** No standalone portal cited on CUPA page; contact agency directly or see Cal e-PETS statewide portal.
- **Local ordinance:** No separate City of San Rafael hazmat ordinance found — all HMBP/CUPA duties flow through Marin County CUPA.
- Source: https://publicworks.marincounty.gov/agencies/certified-unified-program-agency-cupa/

### 4. POTW / Industrial Wastewater Pretreatment

- **Collection system:** San Rafael Sanitation District (SRSD) — serves central San Rafael and adjacent unincorporated areas; operates ~132 miles of pipeline and 32 pump stations. Address: 111 Morphew Street, San Rafael, CA 94901 | (415) 454-4001 | srsd@cityofsanrafael.org.
- **Treatment plant:** Central Marin Sanitation Agency (CMSA) — receives and treats wastewater from SRSD, Ross Valley Sanitary District, and Sanitary District No. 2 of Marin County. NPDES Permit No. CA0038628 (Order R2-2023-0006).
- **Pretreatment program:** Administered by CMSA, not SRSD. Industrial/commercial dischargers into the collection system must obtain an Industrial User Permit from CMSA. CMSA Permit classes: **Class I** (Significant/Categorical Industrial Users) and **Class II** (manufacturing and mass transit facilities).
- **CMSA Sewer Use Ordinance:** CMSA Ordinance No. 2009-2 governs pretreatment, discharge prohibitions, and local limits. FOG (fats, oils, grease) is covered under CMSA Ordinance No. 2021-1.
- **Permits portal:** https://www.cmsa.us/services/permits
- **Local sewer code:** SRMC Title 15 (Utilities), Chapter 15.06 (Sewer District — Standards and Minimum Requirements) governs connections and use within city limits. Industrial pretreatment limits/IWP flow through CMSA.
- Source: https://www.cityofsanrafael.org/departments/sanitation-district/
- Source: https://www.cmsa.us/services/permits
- Source: https://www.cmsa.us/assets/documents/permits/NPDES.pdf (NPDES Order R2-2023-0006)

### 5. Stormwater / MS4

- **MS4 permittee:** City of San Rafael is a co-permittee under the San Francisco Bay Regional Water Quality Control Board Phase II MS4 permit (Small Municipal Separate Storm Sewer System). Stormwater program administered through Public Works Department.
- **Local stormwater ordinance:** SRMC Title 9 (Health and Safety), Chapter 9.40 covers refuse/cleanup/stormwater actions; Title 11 (Public Works), Chapter 11.04 covers environmental public rights-of-way and stormwater. [VERIFY — Chapter 11.04 content not accessible online; confirm chapter title and MS4 citation.]
- **Regulated projects:** Stormwater Control Plans required for regulated development projects (SRMC and Bay Area-wide C.3/C.6 post-construction requirements). Applications go through eTRAKiT: https://epermits.cityofsanrafael.org/
- **SB 205 compliance:** Business license renewals for certain industrial/commercial sectors require stormwater discharge compliance certification (Marin County administers SB 205 form).

### 6. Zoning — Industrial Districts (SRMC Title 14)

- **Zoning code:** SRMC Title 14 (Zoning). Industrial districts in San Rafael include: **I** (Industrial), **LI/O** (Light Industrial/Office), **CCI/O** (Cottage/Commercial Industrial/Office), **LMU** (Light Manufacturing Use).
- **Land use regulations:** Chapter 14.06 governs industrial district land use and development standards (§ 14.06.020 Land Use Standards; § 14.06.030 Development Standards). Land use tables span SRMC Chapters 14.04–14.11.
- **CUP process:** Chapter 14.22 (Use Permit). Administrative, Zoning Administrator, or Planning Commission level depending on use/district. Processing: 3–6 weeks (administrative) to 6–32+ weeks (Planning Commission with CEQA). Application submitted to Planning Division with General Application Form.
- **CUP portal / application:** https://cityofsanrafael.org/conditional-use-permit/
- **Planning Division contact:** (415) 485-3085 | community.development@cityofsanrafael.org | 1400 Fifth Avenue, Top Floor, San Rafael, CA 94901.
