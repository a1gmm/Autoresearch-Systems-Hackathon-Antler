## Adopted Fire Code

- **Municipal code title:** SRMC Title 4 (Fire)
- **Current CFC edition in effect (pre-2026):** 2022 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9). The statewide 2025 CFC cycle took effect 2026-01-01; verify whether San Rafael has passed a separate Title 4 ordinance adopting the 2025 CFC.
- **Building code adoption (for reference):** Ordinance No. 2052, adopted September 15, 2025, effective January 1, 2026, adopts 2025 California Building Code and companion codes under SRMC Title 12. Title 12 Chapter 12.105 is the permit authority for construction fire-code-related plan check.
- **Building permit applications filed before 2026-01-01** remain subject to the 2022 code cycle.

## Hazardous Materials Permit Thresholds

Hazardous materials operational permits are required under the California Fire Code for storage/handling above CFC threshold quantities (CFC 2022 Chapter 50). No separate San Rafael local lowering of thresholds was confirmed online; thresholds follow state CFC baseline. [VERIFY local amendments in SRMC Title 4 with Fire Prevention Bureau.]

State CFC baseline HMBP trigger thresholds (for reference; CUPA program administered by Marin County CUPA, not San Rafael FD — see cupa-local.md):
- Liquids: ≥55 gallons
- Solids: ≥500 lbs
- Gases: ≥200 cubic feet

## High-Piled Combustible Storage Permits

A High-piled Storage operational permit ($264) is required for high-piled combustible storage under CFC 2022 §3204. State baseline thresholds apply:
- Class I–IV commodities stored > 12 ft high (or on solid shelving > 6 ft), in areas > 500 sq ft
- Group A plastics and similar high-hazard commodities: > 500 sq ft
- Public access storage (retail): > 2,500 sq ft
