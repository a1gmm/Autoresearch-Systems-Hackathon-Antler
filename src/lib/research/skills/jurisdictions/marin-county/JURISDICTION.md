## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **Agency:** Marin County Public Works — Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA). Single countywide CUPA covering both unincorporated areas and incorporated cities (~850 regulated businesses). Housed in **Public Works**, not Environmental Health or a fire-department hazmat division.
- **Six program elements administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Materials Management Plan/Inventory Statement, Hazardous Waste Generator/Tiered Permitting, Underground Storage Tank (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), and California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP).
- **Online reporting portal:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — businesses file HMBP, hazardous waste, and other elements here.
- **Contact:** Julia Barnes, Supervising Hazardous Materials Specialist — (415) 473-6530 — 1600 Los Gamos Drive, San Rafael, CA 94903.
- **Fee schedule** (effective July 1, 2017, Ordinance No. 3490 — verify currency):
  - HMBP non-tank materials: R1 (0–55 gal) $53; R2 (56–165 gal) $438; R3 (166–550 gal) $467; R4 (551–1,100 gal) $496; R5 (>1,101 gal) $526.
  - HMBP tanks: T1 (≤500 gal) $263; T2 (501–1,500 gal) $292; T3 (1,501–12,000 gal) $321; T4 (12,001–40,000 gal) $350; T5 (>40,000 gal) $379.
  - UST annual permit per tank: $1,061; installation/plan check per tank (5 hrs): $530; tank removal per tank: $530; consultation/oversight: $106/hr.
  - CalARP annual fee: $2,652.
  - Hazardous Waste Generator standalone: $212; combined with other programs: $451; large quantity generator: $637; onsite treatment/tiered permitting: $583.
  - APSA: 1,320–<10,000 gal $796; 10,000–<100,000 gal $1,591; ≥100,000 gal $2,652; plan check/inspection per tank: $530.
  - Penalty: fees delinquent 30+ days incur a 25% surcharge.

### 2. Fire AHJ — Fire Code & Operational Permits

- **County AHJ (unincorporated/West Marin):** Marin County Fire Department (MCFD) — county-operated, CSA 31, six stations serving Woodacre, San Geronimo, Lagunitas, Nicasio, Point Reyes, Inverness, Tomales. Not a CAL FIRE structural-service contract.
- **Adopted fire code:** **Ordinance No. 3775** (adopted November 2024, filed December 2024) repeals prior Chapter 16.16 and re-establishes **Chapter 16.16** of the Marin County Code adopting the **2022 California Fire Code (CFC)** with Marin County local amendments, including wildland-urban interface provisions extending CFC Section 4907.2 requirements to all Fire Hazard Severity Zones (FHSZs) and designated WUI areas. (Verify with MCFD if the 2025 CFC statewide adoption has triggered a further update.)
- **Prior ordinance** (superseded): Ordinance No. 3659 (2016 CFC adoption); Ordinance No. 3550 (earlier edition).
- **Permit applications:** Apply through MCFD Fire Prevention & Investigation Division — operational permits (hazmat storage, spray booths, compressed gases, high-piled combustibles, etc.) and construction permits (sprinklers, fire alarms, generators).
- **Fire permit fees:** [VERIFY — no Marin-specific operational permit fee schedule found online; contact MCFD Fire Prevention at (415) 499-6654 or visit marincounty.gov/departments/fire.]
- **Incorporated districts:** Many cities served by independent FPDs (Novato FPD, Southern Marin FPD, Kentfield FPD, Tiburon FPD, Bolinas FPD, Stinson Beach FPD, Sleepy Hollow FPD) and municipal/JPA departments (San Rafael FD; Central Marin FD — JPA for Larkspur and Corte Madera). Each adopts CFC locally; verify edition per district.

### 3. Wastewater / Pretreatment (POTW & Sewer Use)

- **No single countywide POTW.** Marin's wastewater is handled by a patchwork of independent sanitary districts; each has its own sewer use ordinance and, where applicable, an industrial pretreatment program.
- **Novato Sanitary District (NSD):** Serves City of Novato and surrounding unincorporated areas, ~60,000 residents; WWTP avg. dry-weather flow up to 7.0 MGD. Sewer use governed by **NSD Sewer Ordinance No. 70** (revised August 2022) and amendments (Ord. #75, #78, #101, #115, #119, #124). NSD operates a pretreatment program (Laboratory Technician/Pretreatment Programs Inspector on staff). Contact: 500 Davidson St., Novato CA 94945 — (415) 892-1694. Source: https://novatosan.com/doing-business/sewer-use-regulations/
- **Sanitary District No. 5 of Marin County (Sani5):** Serves City of Belvedere and Town of Tiburon (east of Gilmartin Drive). Sewer use governed by **Ordinance No. 2014-02a** (eff. March 4, 2015), updated by Ordinances 2024-01 and 2024-02 (September 2024). Lateral inspection required at property sale, remodel >$50K, or during sewer main/road work. Connection fee: $914/fixture unit; lateral permit: $235 (residential). Contact: 2001 Paradise Drive, Tiburon CA 94920 — (415) 435-1501. Source: https://www.sani5.org/permits-and-sewer-lateral-inspections
- **Marshall Onsite Wastewater Disposal Zone #1:** County-regulated onsite systems; Marin County Ordinance No. 3512 governs. Source: https://www.marincounty.gov/sites/g/files/fdkgoe241/files/2024-05/wastewater-regulations-ordinance-3512.pdf
- **IWP process:** For industrial users discharging to an NSD or Sani5 sewer, contact the applicable district's pretreatment coordinator directly for a Significant Industrial User (SIU) determination and IWP permit application. No unified county IWP portal found. [VERIFY - IWP portal/form for each district not confirmed online.]

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater

- **Program:** Marin County Stormwater Pollution Prevention Program (MCSTOPPP) — formed 1993 as a Joint Exercise of Powers Agreement (JEPA) between the Marin County Flood Control and Water Conservation District and 11 member cities/towns plus unincorporated county.
- **NPDES Permit:** Phase II Municipal (MS4) NPDES General Permit, Water Quality Order No. 2013-0001-DWQ, General Permit No. CAS000004 (State Water Board; eff. July 1, 2013). County and all Marin municipalities covered.
- **Local ordinance:** Marin County Municipal Code **Title 23, Chapter 23.18** — Urban Runoff Pollution Prevention ("Local clean water laws"). [Verify current ordinance number — a Board of Supervisors draft ordinance for stormwater was circulated but specific enacting ordinance # not confirmed online.]
- **Construction/industrial requirements:** Construction site stormwater runoff controls and post-construction stormwater management for development projects per MCSTOPPP guidance.
- **Contact:** mcstoppp@marincounty.gov; illegal discharge reporting at mcstoppp.org/illegal-discharge-contacts/; phone (415) 473-4381.

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts & CUP Process

- **Zoning authority (unincorporated):** Marin County Community Development Agency (CDA) — Planning Division, 3501 Civic Center Drive, Suite 308, San Rafael.
- **Governing code:** Marin County Municipal Code **Title 22 — Development Code** (adopted June 24, 2003; amended through 2024). Industrial districts are in **Article II, Chapter 22.12 — Commercial/Mixed Use and Industrial Districts**.
- **Key industrial district:**
  - **IP (Industrial, Planned):** Intended for business park, light industrial, and manufacturing uses in campus-like settings. Subject to Master Plan requirements due to "Planned" designation. Land uses and permit requirements: § 22.12.030.
  - No "heavy industrial" or separate standalone "I" (General Industrial) district confirmed in unincorporated Marin's current code — IP is the primary industrial designation. [VERIFY — confirm with CDA if additional industrial district types apply to a specific parcel.]
- **CUP process:** Chapter 22.48 — Use Permits governs conditional use permits (CUPs) and use permit approvals in all districts, including IP. Industrial uses requiring a CUP are identified in the land use table at § 22.12.030.
- **Planning permit fees:** Last updated March 1, 2024 — see CDA Planning Division fee schedule.

## Notes & GOTCHAs

- **CUPA placement:** Marin's CUPA is in **Public Works**, not Environmental Health or a fire-hazmat division. Single countywide CUPA — no separate city-run CUPAs verified; incorporated cities including San Rafael and Novato are served by the county Public Works CUPA.
- **No single POTW:** Wastewater is fragmented across independent sanitary districts (NSD, Sani5, Central Marin Sanitation Agency, Las Gallinas Valley SD, etc.). Always identify the correct district for the parcel before pursuing an IWP.
- **Fire consolidation:** Central Marin Fire Department is a JPA serving Larkspur and Corte Madera — do not list as standalone city departments. Source: https://www.centralmarinfire.org/
- **Ordinance 3775 / CFC edition:** The most recent fire code ordinance (No. 3775, December 2024) adopts the **2022 CFC**. The statewide 2025 California Building Standards Code took effect 2026-01-01; confirm whether a subsequent Marin ordinance has adopted the 2025 CFC before citing the edition.
