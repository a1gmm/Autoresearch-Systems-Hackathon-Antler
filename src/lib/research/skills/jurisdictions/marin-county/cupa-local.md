## Agency

**Marin County Public Works — Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA)**
Single countywide CUPA. Covers both unincorporated Marin County and all incorporated cities and towns (including San Rafael and Novato). Approximately 850 regulated businesses. Housed in the **Public Works** department — not Environmental Health or a fire-hazmat division.

## Authority & Ordinance

Marin County is a **CalEPA-certified CUPA** under Health and Safety Code Division 20, Chapter 6.11, § 25404 et seq. The county's single-fee system was established by **Ordinance No. 3490** (fee schedule effective **July 1, 2017**). No separate local hazardous materials ordinance chapter in the Marin County Code (Title 6 or similar) was identified online; the CUPA operates under state Health and Safety Code authority as certified.

## Six Program Elements Administered

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — per HSC § 25500 et seq.; online filing through CERS.
2. **Hazardous Materials Management Plan / Inventory Statement (HMMP/HMIS)** — for facilities with regulated quantities.
3. **Hazardous Waste Generator / Tiered Permitting (HWG/TP)** — per HSC § 25100 et seq.
4. **Underground Storage Tank (UST)** — per HSC § 25280 et seq.
5. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — per HSC § 25270 et seq.
6. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — per HSC § 25531 et seq.

## Online Reporting Portal

California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov  
Businesses file HMBP, hazardous waste generator, UST, and other elements through CERS.

## Notes

- No separate city-level CUPAs in Marin County confirmed; all incorporated cities are served by this county CUPA.
- Murray Park and San Quentin Village sewer districts: $472/year per living unit (separate from CUPA fees).
- State ID number applications: Use DTSC Form 1358; processing takes 3–4 weeks.
