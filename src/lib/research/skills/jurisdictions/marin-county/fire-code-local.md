## AHJ Scope

**Marin County Fire Department (MCFD)** is the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) for unincorporated Marin County and West Marin (CSA 31). Incorporated cities and towns are served by independent fire protection districts (Novato FPD, Southern Marin FPD, Kentfield FPD, Tiburon FPD, Stinson Beach FPD, Bolinas FPD, Sleepy Hollow FPD) and the Central Marin Fire Department JPA (Larkspur + Corte Madera). Each district adopts the CFC locally — verify edition per district.

## Adopted Fire Code Edition

**2022 California Fire Code (CFC)** — adopted by **Ordinance No. 3775**, passed by the Marin County Board of Supervisors November 2022, filed December 2024.

- **Municipal Code chapter established:** Marin County Code **Title 16, Chapter 16.16** — "Marin County Amendments to the International Fire Code and the California Fire Code."
- **Prior chapter repealed:** Former Chapter 16.16 (2016 CFC, Ordinance No. 3659) and associated WUI chapter (16.17, Ordinance No. 3453).
- **Wildland-Urban Interface code:** 2021 International Wildland-Urban Interface Code and 2006 IWUIC portions were also in effect under Ord. 3775 until the proposed 2025 update.

## Pending 2025 CFC Adoption

A new ordinance was presented for first reading to the Board of Supervisors on **November 4, 2025** (first reading) and placed on the November 18, 2025 consent calendar to adopt the **2025 California Fire Code** (CCR Title 24) and the **2025 California Wildland-Urban Interface Code**, effective January 1, 2026. The 2025 ordinance integrates the separate WUI chapter (16.17) into Chapter 16.16, and extends WUI provisions to all Fire Hazard Severity Zones (Local Responsibility Areas + State Responsibility Areas) and all designated WUI areas. As of the research date (June 2026), verify with MCFD whether this ordinance has been enrolled as the current operative edition.

## Key Local Amendments (Ord. 3775 / Chapter 16.16)

Marin County local amendments to the 2022 CFC include:

### § 401.1.1 — Hazardous Occupancies (Local Amendment)
In occupancies of a hazardous nature where fire apparatus access is unduly difficult, or where special life/fire safety hazards exist as determined by MCFD standards, facility management must:
- Develop and implement an **Emergency Response Plan**
- Provide an on-site **Emergency Response Team** and **Emergency Liaison Officer**
- Conduct staff training and fire drills per CFC Sections 405 and 406

### § 105.7.26 — Vegetation Management Plans
Operational permit required for vegetation management as defined by MCFD.

### Key Entry Systems — HMBP Facilities
In commercial structures with a **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** on file, the Fire Code Official is authorized to require installation of a key-entry system (Knox box or equivalent) at an approved location.

### Public Storage Facilities — Hazmat Prohibition
Storage of hazardous materials or flammable/combustible liquids in **public storage facilities** is prohibited. Public storage facilities must comply with minimum setback/fire-resistive construction requirements per Table 602 of the Building Code (Group S, Division 1 occupancies). Fire apparatus access roads: minimum 20 ft unobstructed width, 15 ft vertical clearance.

### Wildland-Urban Interface Provisions
CFC Section 4907.2 (vegetation management/defensible space) requirements extended to **all Fire Hazard Severity Zones (FHSZs)** and all designated WUI areas within MCFD jurisdiction — more stringent than the state baseline.

## Permit Types Administered

MCFD Fire Prevention and Investigation Division issues:
- **Operational permits:** hazmat storage/handling, spray booths, compressed gases, high-piled combustible storage, LP-gas, open burning, pyrotechnics, tire storage, hot work.
- **Construction permits:** fire sprinkler systems, fire alarm systems, standpipes, generators, kitchen suppression.

No Marin-specific operational permit fee schedule was found online; contact MCFD Fire Prevention at (415) 499-6654 or the Division offices at 1600 Los Gamos Drive, San Rafael, CA 94903.
