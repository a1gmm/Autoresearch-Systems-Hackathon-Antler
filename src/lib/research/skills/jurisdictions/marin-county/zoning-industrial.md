## Article II — Zoning Districts Structure

Article II of Title 22 ("Zoning Districts and Allowable Land Uses") covers all district types. Industrial districts are in:

- **Chapter 22.12 — Commercial/Mixed Use and Industrial Districts** (§ 22.12.020 Applicability; § 22.12.030 Land Uses and Permit Requirements)

Residential districts: Chapter 22.10  
Agricultural/Resource/Production districts: Chapter 22.08  
Special purpose districts: Chapter 22.14 and Chapter 22.16  
Coastal Zone: Title 20 (separate code), Title 21

## Industrial Zone Districts

### IP — Industrial, Planned (Primary Industrial District)

**Purpose (§ 22.12.020):** The IP (Industrial, Planned) District is intended for areas of the county appropriate for **business park, light industrial, and manufacturing uses in campus-like settings.**

**"Planned" designation:** Because IP contains the term "Planned," it is a Planned District subject to **Master Plan (MP) requirements**. All uses in Planned districts require Master Plan approval as a baseline, per Chapter 22.44 (Master Plans and Precise Development Plans). The Master Plan may authorize lesser entitlements or waivers for specific uses (§ 22.44.040 — waiver of Master Plan amendment/Precise Development Plan amendment).

**No separate heavy-industrial or general-industrial district** has been confirmed in unincorporated Marin County's current code. IP is the sole primary industrial district designation. [VERIFY with CDA if any parcel-specific overlay or district type applies to a specific location.]

### Other Relevant Designations

- **C-RP (Commercial, Retail-Professional)** and **C-OT (Commercial, Old Town)** — commercial; not industrial.
- **ARP (Agricultural, Resource Production)** — rural resource production; some agricultural industry uses.
- Coastal Zone industrial uses are governed by Title 20 (LCP Implementation Plan / Coastal Zoning Code).

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process — Chapter 22.48

**Chapter 22.48 — Use Permits** governs conditional use permits in all districts including IP.

Key steps:
1. Pre-application conference with CDA Planning Division (recommended).
2. Submit Use Permit application with project description, site plans, and applicable environmental review.
3. Planning staff review; CEQA determination (Categorical Exemption, Negative Declaration, or EIR).
4. Public notice to adjacent owners and occupants.
5. Hearing before **Zoning Administrator** (minor CUP) or **Planning Commission** (major CUP) depending on project type and location.
6. Decision with findings; appeal to Planning Commission or Board of Supervisors.

Processing time: 3–6 months typical; major projects with EIR may take 12+ months.

## Master Plan Process — Chapter 22.44 (Planned Districts Including IP)

Because IP is a Planned District, a Master Plan (MP) must be adopted or amended before new uses are established:
- MP establishes the permitted land uses, development standards, and design criteria for a specific IP-designated property.
- **Precise Development Plan (PDP)** implements an approved MP for specific buildings or phases.
- MP/PDP amendment may be waived (§ 22.44.040) for minor changes at the Zoning Administrator's discretion.
