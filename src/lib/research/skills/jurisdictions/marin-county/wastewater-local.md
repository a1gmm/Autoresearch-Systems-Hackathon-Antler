## GOTCHA — No Single Countywide POTW

Marin County has no unified POTW. Wastewater is managed by independent sanitary districts; each has its own sewer use ordinance and, where applicable, an industrial pretreatment program. Always identify the correct district for the specific parcel before pursuing any industrial waste permit (IWP).

Principal districts with industrial-relevance:

| District | Service Area | Pretreatment Program | Key Ordinance |
|----------|-------------|---------------------|---------------|
| **Novato Sanitary District (NSD)** | City of Novato + surrounding uninc. areas (~60,000 residents) | Yes (active) | Ord. No. 70 (rev. Aug 2022 per Ord. 124) |
| **Sanitary District No. 5 (Sani5)** | Belvedere + Tiburon (east of Gilmartin Dr.) | Limited | Ord. No. 2014-02a (eff. March 4, 2015); updated Ord. 2024-01 and 2024-02 |
| **Central Marin Sanitation Agency** | Larkspur, Corte Madera, San Anselmo, Fairfax, parts of Ross | Verify separately | — |
| **Las Gallinas Valley SD** | North San Rafael area | Verify separately | — |

### Pretreatment Program Structure (Article VIII)

NSD operates a federally-delegated pretreatment program consistent with 40 CFR Part 403. Industrial users discharging non-domestic wastewater must obtain a **Non-Domestic Sewer Use Permit** before connecting or discharging.

#### Permit Classes (§ 817)

| Class | Who Qualifies |
|-------|--------------|
| **Class I** (Significant Industrial User / SIU) | EPA categorical users; discharge >25,000 gal/day process water; discharge ≥5% of plant hydraulic/organic capacity; reasonable potential to adversely affect WWTP |
| **Class II** | All other non-domestic users not specifically exempted |
| **Class III** | Commercial/industrial/institutional with significant potential for accidental discharge of prohibited materials |

Exempted without permit (unless determined otherwise): restaurants, mortuaries, markets with garbage grinders.

Federal Categorical Pretreatment Standards apply per 40 CFR where more stringent (§ 804).

#### General Discharge Prohibitions — § 808 (selected)
- Flammable/explosive liquids, solids, or gases that create fire/explosion hazard
- Solid or viscous pollutants capable of causing obstruction
- Radioactive wastes or isotopes (above allowable limits)
- Storm water, surface runoff, or unpolluted drainage (must go to storm sewer per § 814)
- Wastewater from cleanup projects (UST, contaminated soil/groundwater) requires separate temporary discharge permit (§ 811)

### Permit Application Process (§ 819)

Class I and Class II applicants must submit:
- Completed application form + application fee
- SIC number (Standard Industrial Classification)
- Site plans, process descriptions, raw materials list
- Estimated wastewater flow, constituents, peak/average discharge rates
- Location of lateral service sewers and pretreatment facilities
- Source, volume, and chemical characteristics of each tank
- Walk-through site inspection by District representative required prior to permit finalization

Permit duration: up to 3 years; renewal application 90 days before expiration (§ 822).

Waste haulers pay a per-load dump fee per separate District regulations (§ 818).

### Additional Requirements
- **Grease traps / oil-sand interceptors** required for restaurants, gas stations, auto repair shops with floor drains, vehicle wash facilities (§ 810).
- **Accidental discharge prevention plan** required for Class I/II permittees, including spill containment and employee training (§ 816).
- **Monitoring manhole** may be required at user's expense (§ 827).
