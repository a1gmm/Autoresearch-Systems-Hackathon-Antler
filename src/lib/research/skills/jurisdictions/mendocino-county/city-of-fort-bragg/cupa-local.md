## Programs Administered

As a CUPA, Mendocino County Environmental Health administers the six Unified Program elements for all businesses in Mendocino County (including the City of Fort Bragg):

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — inventory reporting and emergency response planning for businesses that handle hazardous materials above thresholds
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator Program / Onsite Tiered Permit** — oversight of businesses generating hazardous waste
3. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — spill prevention plans for petroleum storage ≥ 1,320 gallons
4. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — risk management plans for regulated substances
5. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — permits, inspections, and closure for underground tanks
6. **Hazardous Materials Release Response Plans and Inventory** — community right-to-know reporting

Source: https://calepa.ca.gov/cupa/about/ (CalEPA Unified Program description)
