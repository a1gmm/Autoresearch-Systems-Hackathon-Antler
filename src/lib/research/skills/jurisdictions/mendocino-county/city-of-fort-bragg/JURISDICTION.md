## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air
- **District: Mendocino County Air Quality Management District (MCAQMD)** — Fort Bragg (Mendocino County) is **not** in the North Coast Unified AQMD (which covers Humboldt, Del Norte, and Trinity). The North Coast Air Basin is split among three districts; MCAQMD covers all of Mendocino County including Fort Bragg.
- MCAQMD issues two permits for non-vehicular stationary sources: **Authority to Construct** (before construction) and **Permit to Operate** (continued operation after compliance demonstration). Required for industrial plants, auto body shops, dry cleaners, gasoline stations, large grading operations, and activities releasing air toxics.
- Mendocino County is an **attainment area for all federal standards**; Emission Reduction Credits (ERCs) are NOT required for new large sources.
- MCAQMD office: 1100A Hastings Road (new location as of 2025-2026). Source: https://www.co.mendocino.ca.us/aqmd/

### CUPA
- **Not its own CUPA** — Fort Bragg is served by the **Mendocino County Division of Environmental Health**, which is the CUPA for the county. The CUPA administers the six Unified Program elements: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generator/Onsite Tiered Permit, Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP), Underground Storage Tanks (UST), and Hazardous Materials Release Response Plans.
- Source: https://www.mendocinocounty.gov/departments/public-health/environmental-health/hazardous-materials [VERIFY — site blocks automated access; confirm programs list and fee schedule directly]

### Fire
- **AHJ: Fort Bragg Fire Department** — operated under a Joint Powers Authority formed in 1990 between the **City of Fort Bragg** and the **Fort Bragg Rural Fire Protection District**. Predominantly volunteer.
- **Adopted fire code: California Fire Code, Cal. Code of Regulations, Title 24, Part 9, 2022 Edition**, and its appendices — adopted by Fort Bragg Municipal Code **Chapter 15.05**, enacted by **Ordinance 994, § 3, passed 05-13-2024**.
- The Fort Bragg Fire Code applies to all buildings, structures, areas, and occupancies within the City of Fort Bragg (Health & Safety Code §§ 13145 and 13146).
- Appeals go to the **Fire Appeals Board**, appointed by the Fort Bragg Fire Protection Authority, within 15 days of a refusal.
- Source: https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/FortBragg/html/FortBragg15/FortBragg1505.html

### Wastewater
- **POTW operator: City of Fort Bragg Public Works — Treatment Division** (the city owns and operates its own wastewater treatment facility, referred to as "the WWTF" and "sanitary sewer works of the District").
- **Sewer use ordinance: FBMC Chapter 14.16 "Sanitary Code – Use of Public Sewers"**, enacted by **Ordinance 911, § 2, passed 08-24-2015**.
- Local discharge limits are set out in Chapter 14.16.090 (see wastewater-local.md for the full table). Key limits: pH 6.0–8.5, FOG ≤ 100 mg/L, temperature < 150°F influent (≤ 104°F at WWTF introduction), flashpoint ≥ 140°F; full toxicant table including metals.
- FOG program: Chapter 14.16.100 — all food service establishments must install grease interceptors and comply with FOG control requirements.
- Source: https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/FortBragg/html/FortBragg14/FortBragg1416.html

### Stormwater
- **Permit authority: North Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), Region 1.**
- City of Fort Bragg is regulated as a small **MS4 (Phase II)** under State Water Board Order No. 2013-0001. The city manages a Stormwater Management Program.
- Industrial facilities within city limits with SIC codes triggering industrial stormwater coverage must obtain the statewide **Industrial General Permit (IGP)** from the State Water Board.
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/northcoast/water_issues/programs/npdes_stormwater/

### Zoning
- **Two separate land use codes** apply depending on location:
  - **Inland**: Inland Land Use and Development Code (ILUDC) — FBMC **Chapter 18** (Title 18), enacted by Ord. 930 (2017), most recently amended by Ord. 979 (2022) and current through Ord. 1021 (Jan 2026).
  - **Coastal**: Coastal Land Use and Development Code — FBMC **Chapter 17** (Title 17), for parcels within the California Coastal Zone.
- Industrial districts (Inland code): **IL (Light Industrial)** and **IH (Heavy Industrial)** — no M-1/M-2 nomenclature. See zoning-industrial.md for full details.
- Source (Inland industrial chapter): https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/FortBragg/LUC18/FortBraggLUC182/FortBraggLUC1824.html

## CUPA (Unified Program)
- **Not its own CUPA** — served by the **Mendocino County Division of Environmental Health (CUPA)**. — https://www.mendocinocounty.gov/departments/public-health/environmental-health/hazardous-materials

## Fire authority (AHJ)
- **Fort Bragg Fire Department** — operated under a Joint Powers Authority formed in 1990 between the **City of Fort Bragg** and the **Fort Bragg Rural Fire Protection District**; jointly provides fire service to the city and outlying rural areas. Predominantly volunteer. — https://www.city.fortbragg.com/services/fire-department

## Building authority
- **Not its own building department** — building permits, plan review, and inspections within the City of Fort Bragg are administered by the **Mendocino County Department of Planning & Building Services — Building Division** (which has a Fort Bragg office). — https://www.mendocinocounty.gov/departments/planning-building-services/building-division

## Notes
- GOTCHA (building handled by county): Although Fort Bragg is an incorporated city, its building/construction permitting is handled by the **county** Building Division, not by a city building department.
- GOTCHA (fire JPA): The AHJ is the **Fort Bragg Fire Department** JPA (City + Fort Bragg Rural FPD), not a county or CAL FIRE structural department.
- GOTCHA (air district): Fort Bragg is under **MCAQMD** (Mendocino County), not the North Coast Unified AQMD (which is Humboldt/Del Norte/Trinity).
- GOTCHA (dual zoning codes): Different land use codes apply inside vs. outside the Coastal Zone. Most industrial land is in the Inland area (Chapter 18); verify which code applies for any given parcel.
