## Applicable Zoning Code

Fort Bragg has **two separate land use codes** depending on coastal zone location:

- **Inland Area** (most industrial land): **Inland Land Use and Development Code (ILUDC)** — FBMC Title 18 / Chapter 18. Enacted by Ord. 930, passed 06-12-2017; amended by Ord. 952 (2019) and Ord. 979 (2022). Code current through Ord. 1021, passed January 12, 2026.
- **Coastal Zone**: **Coastal Land Use and Development Code** — FBMC Title 17 / Chapter 17. Separate code for parcels within the California Coastal Zone (see Chapter 17.22 for commercial districts; check Chapter 17 for coastal industrial provisions). [VERIFY coastal industrial chapter]

Industrial zoning chapter references in this document are from the **Inland LUC (Chapter 18)** unless noted.

## Industrial Districts

Fort Bragg uses **IL / IH** nomenclature — there are no M-1 or M-2 districts. Established by FBMC § 18.14.020 and Chapter 18.24.

### IL — Light Industrial

**Purpose (§ 18.24.020.A):** Applied to areas appropriate for a variety of commercial, manufacturing, wholesale and distribution, and industrial uses that do not generate significant customer traffic or high levels of noise, dust, odors, or other potential off-site nuisance characteristics. Allowable manufacturing uses and activities must be **entirely within enclosed structures**.

- Maximum Floor Area Ratio (FAR): **0.40**
- Maximum building height: **35 ft** (45 ft with Use Permit)

### IH — Heavy Industrial

**Purpose (§ 18.24.020.B):** Applied to areas appropriate for a range of heavy industrial uses including manufacturing, assembly and processing, the storage and distribution of raw materials, aggregate plants, and related industrial uses that are generally compatible with and require locations removed from residential and visitor-serving uses.

- Maximum Floor Area Ratio (FAR): **0.40**
- Maximum building height: **40 ft** (60 ft with Use Permit)

## Use Permit / CUP Process

- **Minor Use Permit (MUP):** § 18.71.060 — lower-level discretionary review
- **Use Permit (UP):** § 18.71.060 — standard discretionary review (equivalent to CUP in other jurisdictions)
- Applications handled by the City of Fort Bragg Community Development Department

[VERIFY — contact Community Development at (707) 961-2823 or https://www.city.fortbragg.com/departments/community-development for current fee schedule and application process]

## Cannabis Note

Cannabis businesses in industrial zones require separate compliance with FBMC Chapter 9.30 in addition to zoning clearances/permits. Both MUP and zoning clearance routes are available depending on use type.
