## Adopted Code Edition

The City of Fort Bragg has adopted the **California Fire Code, California Code of Regulations, Title 24, Part 9, 2022 Edition**, and its appendices, subject to local amendments set forth in Fort Bragg Municipal Code Chapter 15.05. This chapter is known as the "Fort Bragg Fire Code."

- **Adopting ordinance:** Ord. 994, § 3, passed 05-13-2024
- **Municipal code chapter:** FBMC Chapter 15.05
- **Sections:** 15.05.010 (Edition Adopted), 15.05.020 (Application), 15.05.030 (Appeals)

Where no applicable standards are set forth in the CFC, compliance with applicable NFPA standards or other nationally recognized fire safety standards approved by the AHJ is deemed prima facie evidence of compliance.

Source: https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/FortBragg/html/FortBragg15/FortBragg1505.html

## Fire Authority (AHJ)

**Fort Bragg Fire Department** — Joint Powers Authority formed in 1990 between the **City of Fort Bragg** and the **Fort Bragg Rural Fire Protection District**. Predominantly volunteer. Provides fire service within city limits and outlying rural areas.

The **Fire Chief** (or authorized representative) enforces the Fort Bragg Fire Code and all building standards relating to fire and panic safety, pursuant to Cal. Health & Safety Code §§ 13145 and 13146.

Source: https://www.city.fortbragg.com/services/fire-department

## Scope

Applies to all buildings, structures, areas, and occupancies within the City of Fort Bragg.

## Appeals

Permit refusals or code-applicability disputes may be appealed to the **Fire Appeals Board**, appointed by the Fort Bragg Fire Protection Authority. Appeal must be filed no later than **15 days** after the refusal or claim. (FBMC § 15.05.030)
