## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air — Mendocino County AQMD (own district, not NCUAQMD)

- **Mendocino County Air Quality Management District (MCAQMD)** is a stand-alone district; it is **not** part of the North Coast Unified AQMD (which covers Del Norte, Humboldt, Trinity). MCAQMD covers all of Mendocino County, including incorporated cities.
- Mendocino County is an **Attainment Area for all Federal Standards**; Emission Reduction Credits (ERCs) are NOT required for new large sources.
- **Authority to Construct (ATC)** required before construction of any new or modified equipment with potential to emit air contaminants. **Permit to Operate** follows after compliance demonstration.
- Application: submit completed form + fee + detailed equipment description + process and materials description. — https://www.co.mendocino.ca.us/aqmd/permitting.html
- Source: https://www.co.mendocino.ca.us/aqmd/

### CUPA — Mendocino County Division of Environmental Health

- **Mendocino County Division of Environmental Health** is the countywide CUPA, housed in the Department of Public Health; office at 501 Low Gap Road, Ukiah, CA 95482.
- Single countywide CUPA covering both unincorporated areas and incorporated cities; no city in Mendocino County operates a separate CUPA or Participating Agency [VERIFY whether any city holds PA status].
- Programs administered: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generator program, Underground Storage Tank (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP).
- Electronic reporting via CERS (California Environmental Reporting System). Fee schedule adopted by Board of Supervisors; check payment page for current schedule (a fee schedule effective June 23, 2024 was referenced in county records). — https://www.mendocinocounty.gov/departments/public-health/environmental-health/hazardous-materials
- Source: https://www.mendocinocounty.gov/departments/public-health/environmental-health/payment

### Fire — Fragmented AHJ; CFC 2013 adopted in county code; no county fire department

- **No single county fire department.** Structural fire in unincorporated areas is provided by numerous independent volunteer **fire protection districts**; wildland/SRA is covered by **CAL FIRE Mendocino Unit (MEU)**. AHJ varies by project location.
- For areas **not located in any fire district jurisdiction**, Section 18.04.060 of the county code substitutes the **Building Official** as the fire authority.
- County building code (Title 18, Chapter 18.04) adopts the **California Fire Code, 2013 Edition** (Part 9 of Title 24) with local amendment: Section 202 definition of FIRE CHIEF amended to mean the chief officer of the fire department serving the jurisdiction, **or the Building Official** for areas not in a fire district.
- **GOTCHA**: The 2013 CFC adoption ordinance is the most recent publicly extractable ordinance found; the 2022 CFC adoption for geographic limits on aboveground tanks (Class I, II) in residential/congested commercial areas is referenced in state records but the specific adopting ordinance number was not confirmed. - Incorporated cities have their own fire AHJs: Ukiah Valley Fire Authority, Fort Bragg Fire Department, Little Lake Fire Protection District (Willits). — https://www.firesafemendocino.org/fire-agencies

### Wastewater — County is not the POTW; City of Ukiah POTW; on-site systems regulated by Title 16

- Mendocino County does **not** own or operate a regional POTW. Sewer service in incorporated areas is municipal:
  - **City of Ukiah** owns and operates the main wastewater treatment plant serving ~17,000 people in the city and the **Ukiah Valley Sanitation District (UVSD)** — an adjacent county sanitation district. Industrial dischargers connecting to Ukiah's system are subject to Ukiah's sewer use/pretreatment requirements, not county code.
  - Fort Bragg and Willits operate their own wastewater systems.
- **County jurisdiction is limited to on-site wastewater (septic/OWTS)** in unincorporated areas: governed by Title 16, Chapter 16.08 (On-Site Sewage Systems) and the county LAMP (Local Agency Management Plan) for OWTS.
- County Code § 16.12 / § 16.16 prohibit subdivisions within Ukiah and Meadowbrook Manor Sanitation District service areas from using on-site septic when public sewer connection is available.
- No county-level industrial pretreatment / IWP program found — direct to applicable city. [VERIFY if any county sanitation district (e.g., UVSD) has its own sewer use ordinance with industrial discharge limits].

### Stormwater — MS4 Phase II in Ukiah and Fort Bragg urbanized areas; Title 16, Ch. 16.30

- Mendocino County is a regulated Small MS4 operator under NPDES Phase II (WQO 2003-0005-DWQ) for the **unincorporated urbanized areas** of **Ukiah** and **Fort Bragg** only.
- **Mendocino County Storm Water Management Program (MCSWMP)** administered by Mendocino County Water Agency (890 North Bush St, Ukiah).
- County Code Title 16, **Chapter 16.30** (Stormwater Runoff Pollution Prevention Procedure, Ord. No. 4270): applies to all water entering the County storm drainage system or waters of the U.S. from developed/undeveloped lands within the unincorporated urban boundary areas.
- Industrial or construction NPDES permittees anywhere in the county must comply with their individual permit; projects in the Ukiah or Fort Bragg MS4 area must submit a **Water Pollution Control Plan** as part of encroachment permit applications.
- State Regional Board for stormwater: **North Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board** (Region 1). — https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/northcoast/
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/water_issues/programs/stormwater/swmp/mendocino_swmp.pdf

### Zoning — Title 20; industrial districts I-1, I-2, I (Coastal), P-I (Pinoleville)

- County zoning is in **Title 20** of the Mendocino County Code, split into Division I (Inland Zoning Code) and Division II (Coastal Zoning Code).
- **Industrial districts in Division I (Inland)**:
  - **I-1 Limited Industrial** (Ch. 20.096): low-nuisance manufacturing and industrial uses; building height limit 50 ft; no minimum lot area.
  - **I-2 General Industrial** (Ch. 20.100): full range of industrial including moderate-to-high nuisance; building height limit 65 ft; no minimum lot area; heavy industrial and explosives storage by Major Use Permit.
  - **P-I Pinoleville Industrial** (Ch. 20.102): applied to lands within Pinoleville Rancheria; MUP required for uses incompatible with adjacent residential.
- **Industrial district in Division II (Coastal)**:
  - **I — Coastal Industrial** (Ch. 20.400): coastal-related and coastal-dependent industrial by right; general and heavy industrial by conditional use permit.
- Industrial use types defined in Chapter 20.028: Custom Manufacturing, General Industrial, Heavy Industrial, Explosives Storage.
- **Right to Industry Ordinance**: Chapter 6.35 of county code protects established industrial operations from nuisance complaints after 3 years of operation; applies within I-1, I-2, I (Coastal), and P-I zones.
- Source: https://www.co.mendocino.ca.us/bos/cgi-bin/meetings/28304/28334/28514/28554/ZoningDistricts28554.pdf

## Building authority

- **Mendocino County Department of Planning & Building Services — Building Division** administers Title 18 for the unincorporated county and for the cities of **Fort Bragg and Point Arena** (those cities do not run their own building authority). Cities of **Ukiah and Willits** run their own building departments.
- Title 18, Chapter 18.04 adopts all Title 24 California codes; California Building Code 2013 edition is the reference in the publicly extractable ordinance; [VERIFY current adopted CBC edition — 2022 or 2025 post-2026-01-01 state update].
- Building permit fees are adopted by Board of Supervisors resolution; see Planning & Building Services. — https://www.mendocinocounty.gov/departments/planning-building-services/building-division
- Source: https://library.municode.com/ca/mendocino_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=MECOCO_TIT18BURE
