## Key Structural Fact: County Is Not a POTW Operator

Mendocino County does **not** own or operate a regional Publicly Owned Treatment Works (POTW). There is no county-level industrial wastewater pretreatment (IWP) program. Industrial dischargers connecting to a municipal sewer must comply with the **city's** sewer use ordinance, not county code.

## County Code: Title 16 — Water and Sewage

County Code Title 16 covers water and sewage in unincorporated areas; the chapters relevant to EHS are:

### Chapter 16.08 — On-Site Sewage Systems (OWTS)
- Governs installation and alteration of on-site wastewater systems (septic) in unincorporated areas.
- Administered by the Division of Environmental Health.
- Source: https://library.municode.com/ca/mendocino_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=MECOCO_TIT16WASE

### Chapter 16.12 / 16.16 — Connection Requirements
- Prohibits subdivisions within the Ukiah Sanitation District and Meadowbrook Manor Sanitation District service areas from using on-site septic systems when a public sewer connection is available.

### Chapter 16.30 — Stormwater Runoff Pollution Prevention Procedure (Ord. No. 4270)
- Applies to all water entering the County storm drainage system or waters of the U.S. from unincorporated **urbanized boundary areas** of Mendocino County (Ukiah and Fort Bragg urbanized areas).
- Any person subject to an Industrial or Construction Activity NPDES stormwater discharge permit **anywhere in the county** must comply with all provisions of that permit.
- Projects in Ukiah or Fort Bragg MS4 areas must submit a **Water Pollution Control Plan** with encroachment permit applications.
- Administered by Mendocino County Water Agency (890 North Bush Street, Ukiah; (707) 463-4589).
- Source: http://mendocinoco-ca.elaws.us/code/coor_title16_ch16.30_sec16.30.030

## OWTS / Septic for Non-Sewer Sites

- Onsite wastewater systems (OWTS) in unincorporated areas are regulated under the **Mendocino County Local Agency Management Plan (LAMP)** and associated **Technical Standards Manual**, adopted pursuant to the State Water Board's OWTS Policy.
- Administered by Mendocino County Division of Environmental Health.
- LAMP: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/northcoast/water_issues/programs/owts/190415/LAMP_and_TechStdsManual.pdf
