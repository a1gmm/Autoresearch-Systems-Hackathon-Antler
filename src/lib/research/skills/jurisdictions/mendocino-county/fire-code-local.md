## Local Amendments to the CFC

### Section 18.04.060 — Only Amendment to the 2013 CFC

The CFC 2013 Edition is adopted with **one local amendment** in Section 18.04.060:

> **Section 202 definition of FIRE CHIEF** is amended to read:
> "FIRE CHIEF. The chief officer of the fire department serving the jurisdiction, or a duly authorized representative; **areas not located in the jurisdiction of a fire district shall be under the authority of the Building Official.**"

This amendment addresses the fragmented fire authority structure in Mendocino County: where no fire protection district has jurisdiction, the county Building Official steps into the fire authority role.

### Geographic Limits for Aboveground Tanks (2022 CFC Reference)

The 2022 CFC adoption (if confirmed) establishes geographic limits restricting storage of Class I, Class II, and flammable liquids in outside aboveground tanks in:
- All residential areas
- All heavily populated or congested commercial areas as established by the County of Mendocino
- Agricultural land of less than two acres

### Board of Appeals

All references to "Board of Appeals" in the CFC are amended to mean the **Board of Building and Housing Appeals** established by Mendocino County Code § 2.24.030.

## Fire Authority / AHJ

- **No single county fire department.** Structural fire protection in unincorporated areas is fragmented across independent volunteer fire protection districts.
- **CAL FIRE Mendocino Unit (MEU)** covers State Responsibility Area (SRA) / wildland fire.
- For areas outside any fire district: Building Official acts as fire authority per the § 18.04.060 amendment.
- Incorporated cities have their own AHJs: Ukiah Valley Fire Authority, Fort Bragg Fire Department, Little Lake Fire Protection District (Willits).
- Determine specific district AHJ by project address before issuing any fire-code determination.
- Fire agency directory: https://www.firesafemendocino.org/fire-agencies

## Permit Fees

- Fire code permit fees are not separately scheduled by the county; construction permit fees (building, including fire code compliance) are set by Board of Supervisors resolution and collected by the Planning & Building Services Department.
- Fee schedule referenced in § 18.08.010; adopted from time to time by the Board of Supervisors.
- Extension fees for expired applications are set by Board of Supervisors resolution.
