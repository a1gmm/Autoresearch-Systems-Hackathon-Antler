## POTW / Facility

**City of Ukiah Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP)**
- Address: 1320 Airport Rd, Ukiah, CA
- Operated by: **City of Ukiah** (Public Works / Water Resources Department)
- Contact: Michelle Wagenet — (707) 467-2842
- Design capacity: 2.8 MGD dry weather; 20 MGD peak wet weather
- Operational since 1958; $56.5M improvement project completed 2009
- NPDES Permit No.: **CA0022888** (North Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board — Region 1)

## Dual-Service Area

The WWTP serves both the **City of Ukiah** (collection system owner) and the **Ukiah Valley Sanitation District (UVSD)**, which contracts with the City via a Participation Agreement for plant access and collection system maintenance. UVSD covers unincorporated Ukiah Valley.

UVSD contact: 151 Laws Avenue, Ukiah, CA 95482 — (707) 462-4429 — https://www.uvsd.org/
