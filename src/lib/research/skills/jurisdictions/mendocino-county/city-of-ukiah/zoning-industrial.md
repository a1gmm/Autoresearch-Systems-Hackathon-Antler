## Industrial District

The City of Ukiah has a single general **Manufacturing (M)** zoning district for industrial and major employment uses. The code uses "M" (not I-1/I-2) as the designation.

### Definitions (Div. 9, Ch. 2, Art. 21)
- **Light Industrial** — low-intensity industrial and related manufacturing activities that do not involve the use or storage of highly flammable or explosive materials, or the processing of raw materials. Typical uses: electronics assembly, furniture manufacturing, bottling plants, garment manufacturing.
- **Basic Industry** — processing of raw materials, or manufacturing involving flammable/explosive materials. Typical uses: rock/sand/gravel processing, lumber mills.

### Manufacturing (M) District — Key Points
- Primary district for manufacturing and major employment uses within city limits.
- Cannabis testing laboratories are classified as a **minor use** in M zones.
- Outdoor sales establishments in M zones require a **Use Permit**.
- Specific permitted-by-right vs. conditionally-permitted uses are enumerated in the applicable article of Div. 9, Ch. 2.

## Airport Industrial Park

A distinct area designated as **Airport Industrial Park (AIP)** operates under separate "Planned Development Regulations" adopted by City Council. The AIP is located near Ukiah Municipal Airport and has its own development standards.

## CUP / Use Permit Process

Use Permits (functionally equivalent to CUPs) are processed per **Div. 9, Ch. 2, Art. 20** (Administration and Procedures):

- **Zoning Administrator** — reviews and decides minor use permit applications; may refer any application to the Planning Commission at discretion.
- **Planning Commission** — five-member body appointed by City Council; reviews and decides **Major Use Permits**, Site Development Permits, and Variances.

Conditions of approval may include measures to preserve zoning district integrity, ensure compatibility with surrounding land uses, and promote safe/orderly use of property.
