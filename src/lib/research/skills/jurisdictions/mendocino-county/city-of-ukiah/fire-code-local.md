## Adopted Edition

**California Fire Code (CFC), 2022 Edition** — adopted by the City of Ukiah as the local Fire Code.
- Based on the International Fire Code (IFC) 2021.
- Adopted with appendices: Appendix Chapter 4 and Appendices B, BB, C, CC, D, E, F, G, H, I, K, N, and O.
- NFPA 1 Chapter 38 is also incorporated by reference.

## Fire AHJ

**Ukiah Valley Fire Authority (UVFA)** — Joint Powers Authority (effective July 1, 2017) formed between the **City of Ukiah** and the **Ukiah Valley Fire Protection District**. The UVFA serves both the City and the unincorporated Ukiah Valley.

Fire Prevention (plan review, fire permits, inspections) is administered through the City of Ukiah Fire Prevention Office located at the Civic Center, 300 Seminary Ave., Ukiah.

- Fire Prevention Office: (707) 463-5786
- Inspections Line: (707) 463-6739
- Permit submissions: CitizenServe online portal
- Fee schedule: "Fire Prevention Office Fee Schedule 2020 for Inspections and Permits" (PDF available from cityofukiah.com/uvfa/)

Note: This is **not** a CAL FIRE or county fire district jurisdiction — UVFA is the local AHJ for the city limits.
