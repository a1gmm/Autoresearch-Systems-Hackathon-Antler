## Controlling CUPA

The City of Ukiah is **not its own CUPA**. Hazardous materials unified program administration for businesses within city limits falls to:

**Mendocino County Department of Public Health — Environmental Health Division (CUPA)**
- Address: 501 Low Gap Road, Ukiah, CA 95482
- Program page: https://www.mendocinocounty.gov/departments/public-health/environmental-health/hazardous-materials
- Service directory: https://www.mendocinocounty.gov/Home/Components/ServiceDirectory/ServiceDirectory/331/26

The county CUPA is certified by CalEPA under Health & Safety Code §25404 to administer the six unified program elements within its jurisdiction, which includes all incorporated cities in Mendocino County that have not separately certified as CUPAs.

## Programs Administered (standard statewide unified program elements)

1. Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) — CalARP Tier II / CERS reporting
2. California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) program
3. Hazardous Waste Generator program (manifests, storage, inspections)
4. Underground Storage Tank (UST) program
5. Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) Spill Prevention, Control & Countermeasure (SPCC)
6. Certified Unified Program — Hazardous Waste Treatment (tiered permits)

## Local Hazmat Ordinance

No separate City of Ukiah hazmat ordinance was found online. The county CUPA ordinance and state H&SC Chapter 6.95 govern.

## Related JPA Note

The **Mendocino Solid Waste Management Authority (MSWMA)** is a JPA formed by the Cities of Willits, Fort Bragg, and Ukiah, and Mendocino County. MSWMA handles solid waste disposal but is distinct from the CUPA function.
