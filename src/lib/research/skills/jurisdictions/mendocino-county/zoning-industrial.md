## Zoning Code Structure

Mendocino County zoning is in **Title 20 — Zoning Ordinance** of the Mendocino County Code, split into:
- **Division I**: Mendocino County Inland Zoning Code (applies to unincorporated inland areas)
- **Division II**: Mendocino County Coastal Zoning Code (applies to Coastal Zone under LCP)

Available via Municode: https://library.municode.com/ca/mendocino_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=MECOCO_TIT20ZOOR

Industrial use types are defined in Chapter 20.028 (Division I) and Chapter 20.328 (Division II).

## Industrial Districts — Division I (Inland)

### I-1 Limited Industrial (Chapter 20.096)

**Intent**: Create and preserve areas for manufacturing and industrial uses with **no or very low nuisance characteristics**. Nonindustrial uses that support or are adjuncts to industrial uses are permitted (administrative, sales, service).

**Permitted by Right:**
- Industrial caretaker housing
- Religious assembly
- Wholesaling, storage and distribution — light
- General industrial (manufacturing, compounding, processing, assembling, packaging, treatment, fabrication)
- Custom manufacturing (hand-tool / home-shop scale)
- Tree crops
- Essential services; fire and police protection services

**Minor Use Permit (MUP) required:**
- Industrial employee housing
- General retail sales

**Major Use Permit (MUP) required:**
- Major impact services and utilities
- Wholesaling, storage and distribution — heavy
- Mining and processing

**Development Standards:**
- Minimum lot area: None required
- Minimum front yard: 10 feet
- Minimum side/rear yard: None required (except 5 ft side / 20 ft rear where contiguous to non-commercial/non-industrial district)
- Building height limit: **50 feet**

Source: https://www.co.mendocino.ca.us/bos/meetings/MG32913/AS32944/AI33098/DO33144/1.PDF (Ordinance No. 3639, adopted 1987; amendments through Ord. No. 4017, 1998)

### I-2 General Industrial (Chapter 20.100)

**Intent**: Create and preserve areas for a **full range of industrial uses with moderate to high nuisance characteristics** (unsightliness, noise, odor, traffic, hazards); applied to locations with large acreage away from residential/commercial areas.

**Permitted by Right (selected):**
- Industrial caretaker housing
- Religious assembly
- All commercial automotive and equipment types
- Building maintenance, business equipment, construction sales and services
- Recycling centers; scrap operations
- Wholesaling, storage and distribution — all types (light, heavy, mini-warehouses)
- Custom manufacturing; General industrial
- Animal raising — general agriculture; forest production and processing — all types; packing and processing — all types
- Research services; laundry services; mail order businesses
- Essential services; fire and police protection; lodge, fraternal and civic assembly

**Minor Use Permit required:**
- Industrial employee housing
- General retail sales

**Major Use Permit required:**
- Heavy industrial (radioactive materials compounding, petroleum refining, explosives manufacturing)
- Explosives storage
- Mining and processing
- Major impact facilities; major impact services and utilities
- Commercial recreation — outdoor entertainment/sports; stockyard

**Development Standards:**
- Minimum lot area: None required
- Minimum front yard: 10 feet
- Minimum side/rear yard: None required (except 5 ft side / 20 ft rear where contiguous to non-commercial/non-industrial district)
- Building height limit: **65 feet**

Source: https://www.co.mendocino.ca.us/bos/meetings/MG32913/AS32944/AI33098/DO33144/1.PDF (Ord. No. 3639 (part), adopted 1987; amendments through Ord. No. 4017 (part), 1998)

### P-I Pinoleville Industrial (Chapter 20.102)

**Intent**: Applied to all lands zoned I-1 within Pinoleville Rancheria. Industrial uses allowed but must be compatible with existing/future residential use of the Rancheria; Minor or Major Use Permit required for uses that may cause nuisance to Rancheria residents.

## Industrial District — Division II (Coastal Zone)

### I — Coastal Industrial (Chapter 20.400)

**Intent**: Provide suitable locations for necessary industrial and commercial service activities where transportation facilities and utilities exist or can be provided.

**Principal Permitted Uses (by right):**
- Coastal-Related Industrial (e.g., fish waste processing)
- Coastal-Dependent Industrial (docks, commercial fishing, fish processing, boat building/repair, aquaculture support)
- Custom Manufacturing: Light Industrial
- Mail order businesses; research services; passive recreation

**Conditional Uses (Coastal Development Use Permit required):**
- General Industrial; Heavy Industrial
- Employee Caretaker Housing
- Major impact facilities and services; alternative energy facilities
- Agricultural uses (animal waste processing, forest production, packing/processing, fisheries byproducts)
- Mining and processing; onshore oil and gas development
- Automotive uses (cleaning, fleet storage, gasoline sales, repairs, storage)
- Wholesaling (light and miniwarehouses)

**Development Standards:**
- Minimum lot area: 6,000 sq ft (with water AND sewer service); 12,000 sq ft (water OR sewer); 40,000 sq ft (no water or sewer service)
- Minimum front yard: 10 feet
- Minimum side/rear yard: None (except 5 ft / 20 ft contiguous to non-industrial; fencing or landscaping screening required)
- Building height limit: [VERIFY — not captured in extracted text]

Source: Ord. No. 3785 (part), adopted 1991; Ord. No. 4149 (part), adopted 2005

## Industrial Use Types (Chapter 20.028)

| Type | Definition |
|---|---|
| Custom Manufacturing | On-site production of individually crafted goods using hand tools or home-shop-scale equipment; may include incidental on-site retail sales |
| General Industrial | Manufacturing, compounding, processing, assembling, packaging, treatment, or fabrication; includes aggregate crushing/screening/washing |
| Heavy Industrial | All other industrial plants involving radioactive materials, petroleum refining, or explosives manufacturing |
| Explosives Storage | Storage of any quantity of explosives per Cal. H&S Code § 1200 |

Source: Ord. No. 3639 (part), adopted 1987

## Use Permit Process

- **Minor Use Permit (MUP)**: Administrative review; less intensive uses (employee housing, retail in industrial zones).
- **Major Use Permit (MUP)**: Discretionary review; high-impact uses (heavy industrial, mining, explosives). Approval conditioned on compatibility with surrounding uses.
- **Coastal Development Permit**: Required for development in the Coastal Zone (Division II areas); reviewed against Local Coastal Program (LCP).

## Right to Industry Protection (Chapter 6.35)

The **Mendocino County Right to Industry Ordinance** (Chapter 6.35 of the county code) protects industrial operations in I-1, I-2, I (Coastal), and P-I zones from nuisance complaints:
- No industrial operation consistent with applicable zoning and environmental laws shall be declared a nuisance after operating for **more than 3 years**, if it was not a nuisance when it began.
- Exception: negligent or improper operation.
- Noticing requirement: parcels within 300 feet of an industrial zone boundary must be notified of adjacent industrial zoning upon property transfer.
