> Research reference for CUPA program authority in the City of Willits.
> ROUTING NOTE: Willits is NOT its own CUPA and has no separate city hazmat program. All six CalEPA unified programs are administered county-wide by the **Mendocino County Division of Environmental Health (CUPA)**. This is standard for small California cities — Willits, Fort Bragg, Ukiah, and all unincorporated Mendocino County are served by the same county CUPA.

## CUPA Identity

- **CUPA:** Mendocino County Division of Environmental Health — Hazardous Materials Program (county-wide CUPA; not city-specific)
- **Address:** 501 Low Gap Road, Ukiah, CA 95482

## Programs Administered (State Baseline)

The Mendocino County CUPA implements all six CalEPA unified programs county-wide, including within Willits city limits:

| # | Program | Acronym |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan | HMBP |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator Program | HWG |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tanks | UST |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act | APSA |
| 5 | California Accidental Release Prevention Program | CalARP |
| 6 | California Fire Code Hazardous Materials Operational Permits | CFC HazMat |

## HMBP / CERS Reporting

- **Submission system:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business
- **Who must comply:** Any business storing, using, or handling hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities (generally: ≥55 gallons liquid, ≥500 lbs solid, ≥200 cubic feet gas; per Health & Safety Code §25507)
- **Annual renewal:** Required every year in CERS
- **Local hazmat ordinance:** [VERIFY — no Willits-specific local hazmat ordinance found online; state Health & Safety Code §25500 et seq. controls]

## Relationship to CalARP / OSFM

- Mendocino County CUPA is the Local Implementing Agency (LIA) for CalARP facilities in Willits.
- The California Office of the State Fire Marshal (OSFM) provides oversight of CUPA programs statewide: https://osfm.fire.ca.gov/what-we-do/pipeline-safety-and-cupa/certified-unified-program-agency
