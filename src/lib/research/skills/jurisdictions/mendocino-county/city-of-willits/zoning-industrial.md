> Research reference for industrial zoning in the City of Willits.
> Willits administers its own zoning code (unlike some Mendocino County cities that rely on county land use authority). Industrial uses are regulated under Title 17 (Zoning) of the Willits Municipal Code, with two industrial zone designations: ML (Limited Industrial) and MH (Heavy Industrial).

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

- CUPs for industrial uses in ML/MH zones are processed by the City of Willits Planning Division.
- Applications route through the Community Development Department (contact: Connor Stack, City Planner, 707-459-7168).
- CUPs require Planning Commission review and approval at a noticed public hearing.
- [VERIFY] The specific chapter of Title 17 governing the CUP application procedure, public notice requirements, appeal process, and fee schedule — these are typically in a general "Permits and Procedures" chapter (possibly Chapter 17.36 or similar); the full table of contents was not accessible via direct fetch.

## CEQA / Environmental Review

- Industrial projects in Willits are subject to California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) review, coordinated by the City of Willits Planning Division.
- A recent CEQA project of record: City of Willits Land Use Element & Sphere of Influence Updates (CEQA Net 2023030321): https://ceqanet.lci.ca.gov/2023030321/3

## Cannabis Activity

- The City of Willits has a separate Cannabis Activity Program regulating cannabis-related land uses; administered by the Planning Division. This may affect industrial zone uses involving cannabis cultivation or manufacturing.
