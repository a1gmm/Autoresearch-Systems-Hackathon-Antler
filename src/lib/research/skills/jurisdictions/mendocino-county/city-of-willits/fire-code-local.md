> Research reference for fire code authority in the City of Willits.
> CRITICAL ROUTING NOTE: Willits does NOT have a city fire department. The AHJ is the **Little Lake Fire Protection District**, an independent special district (formed 1974). All fire code compliance, plan checks, operational permits, and inspections route to that district — not to City Hall.

## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

- **AHJ:** Little Lake Fire Protection District (independent special district)
- **Address:** 74 E. Commercial Street, Willits, CA 95490
- **Phone:** (707) 459-6271
- **Hours:** Monday–Friday 8:00 AM–5:00 PM (closed 12:00–1:00 PM)
- **Web:** https://www.littlelakefire.org/
- **City of Willits info page:** https://www.cityofwillits.org/136/Fire-Protection-District

The District is primarily volunteer with some career staff. It serves the City of Willits and surrounding rural areas of the Little Lake Valley.

## Adopted Fire Code Edition

- The California Fire Code (CFC) is the statewide baseline for all California jurisdictions. As of 2026-01-01 the current statewide edition is the **2025 California Fire Code** (Title 24, Part 9, based on IFC 2024). The prior edition was the 2022 CFC (effective 2023-01-01 through 2025-12-31).
- **Specific adopting ordinance for Willits / Little Lake Fire Protection District:** [VERIFY — not found online]. The district's website (littlelakefire.org) does not publish an ordinance or code adoption page. The City of Willits Municipal Code Title 15 (Buildings and Construction) on Municode covers the building codes adopted by the City's Building Division but does not appear to separately address fire code adoption by the district.
- **Recommended action:** Contact Little Lake Fire District at (707) 459-6271 to confirm the current adopted CFC edition and any locally-adopted amendments.

## Fire Hazard Severity Zones

- The City of Willits is designated as a **Local Responsibility Area (LRA)** for fire hazard severity zones (as opposed to a State Responsibility Area served by CAL FIRE).
- Willits updated its LRA Fire Hazard Severity Zone map in 2025. Updated maps and Q&A are available at: https://www.cityofwillits.org/308/2025-Willits-LRA-Fire-Hazard-Severity-Zo
