> Research reference for sewer/wastewater discharge in the City of Willits.
> Willits operates its own POTW (city-owned, not a sanitation district). Industrial pretreatment program: **NOT required** under the current NPDES permit — the city is too small (permitted flow <5 mgd) — but a source control program IS required and enforced. Dischargers with categorical processes should still confirm compliance obligations with the NPDES permit and federal categorical standards.

## Industrial Pretreatment Program Status

The NPDES permit (Order R1-2021-0021, Special Provision 6.3.5.2) explicitly states:

> "…there is no industrial user subject to regulation under the NPDES Pretreatment Program being discharged to the Facility and the permitted flow of the Facility is less than five mgd; therefore, the Order does **not require the Permittee to develop a pretreatment program** that conforms to federal regulations."

**Conclusion: Willits does NOT operate a formal 40 CFR Part 403 industrial pretreatment program (IWP/IWD permit program) as of the 2021 NPDES permit.**

Instead, the city is required to implement a **source control program** (Special Provision 6.3.5.2.1), which includes:
- Maintain legal authority to monitor and enforce source control
- Prohibit introduction of pollutants that create fire/explosion hazards, corrosive conditions, or toxic gases in the WWTF
- File an annual source control summary in the Annual Report to the RWQCB (due March 1)
- If a categorical industrial user (CIU) begins discharging, notify the Regional Water Board within 30 days and begin implementing categorical pretreatment standards (Special Provision 6.3.5.2.2)

| Recycled Water (Discharge Point 004) | Groundwater recharge/irrigation under Master Recycling Permit (Order R1-2021-0021) |

## Discharge Prohibitions (Source Control — Federal/State Baseline)

Under NPDES Special Provision 6.3.5.2.1.6, the following are prohibited from introduction into the WWTF:
- Pollutants that create a fire or explosion hazard
- Pollutants that cause corrosive structural damage to the WWTF
- Solid or viscous pollutants causing obstruction to flow
- Any pollutant discharged in sufficient quantity to cause interference or pass through
