## Programs Administered

The Mendocino County CUPA administers all six unified program elements:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — required for any business that handles hazardous materials above thresholds (55 gallons liquid / 500 lbs solid / 200 cu ft compressed gas). Includes inventory, emergency response plan, and employee training plan.
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator (HW Generator)** — inspection and compliance program for all categories of HW generators (SQG, LQG, VSQG) operating in the county.
3. **Underground Storage Tank (UST)** — permit, inspection, and closure program for USTs storing petroleum, hazardous substances, or waste.
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — applies to facilities with aggregate aboveground petroleum storage ≥ 1,320 gallons; requires Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure (SPCC) plan.
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — Risk Management Plan (RMP) program for facilities handling regulated substances above threshold quantities.
6. **Hazardous Waste Treatment (tiered permits)** — Conditionally Exempt Small Quantity Treatment (CESQT) and other tiered permitting for on-site treatment.
