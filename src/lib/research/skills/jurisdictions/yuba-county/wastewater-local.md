## Overview — Fragmented District Structure

Yuba County has **no single county-wide sewer/POTW system**. Wastewater service in unincorporated areas is divided among several special districts and, for most rural parcels, on-site septic systems regulated by the county itself. Industrial dischargers must identify the specific district serving their parcel.

## On-Site Wastewater (Septic) — County Regulated

For parcels not served by a public sewer system (the majority of rural unincorporated Yuba County):

**Yuba County Environmental Health Division, Sewage Disposal Program**
- Regulates on-site wastewater treatment systems (OWTS) and greywater systems under **Yuba County Ordinance Code Chapter 7.07**
- Permits construction, repair, and maintenance of septic systems
- Program Contact: Chris Benedict, (530) 749-5469
- Address: 915 8th Street, Suite 123, Marysville, CA 95901
- No industrial pretreatment program (septic-served parcels are not POTWs)

## Olivehurst Public Utility District (OPUD) — Serves Olivehurst/Linda Area

**Service area:** Olivehurst and surrounding portions of unincorporated Yuba County (southern valley floor, including areas around 9th Avenue/Olivehurst area).

**Wastewater Treatment Facility:** Located at 1970 9th Avenue, Olivehurst, CA 95961. State-of-the-art activated sludge plant with membrane filtration and UV disinfection; permitted capacity: **3 million gallons per day (MGD)**.

**Sewer Use Ordinance:** OPUD **Ordinance No. 209** — Sewer Use Ordinance (the governing document for discharge to OPUD's collection system). Article VI prohibits illicit discharges; Section 602 lists prohibited waste types; Section 603 addresses regulated discharges and OPUD's authority to disallow harmful discharges.

**FOG Program:** OPUD operates a Fats, Oils, and Grease (FOG) program targeting commercial food-service establishments.

**Industrial Pretreatment Program:** OPUD does not appear to operate a formal federal pretreatment program (40 CFR Part 403) — it is likely below the threshold requiring a formal IWP program. [VERIFY — confirm with OPUD whether any Significant Industrial Users (SIUs) hold industrial waste discharge permits.]

OPUD contact: (530) 743-4357; https://www.opud.org/
Ordinances: https://www.opud.org/ordinances

## Linda County Water District (LCWD) — Serves Linda Area

**Service area:** Linda community (western Yuba County near Marysville).
**Address:** 1280 Scales Ave., Marysville, CA 95901; (530) 743-2043
Provides sanitary sewer service; rates and connection rules governed by district ordinances (sewer rate ordinances adopted by board action).

[VERIFY — LCWD website does not publish a sewer use ordinance or industrial pretreatment program details. Contact directly for discharge rules applicable to industrial users.]

## South Yuba County Regional Wastewater Project (in progress)

A major infrastructure project connecting the **City of Wheatland**, **OPUD**, and portions of **southern Yuba County** is underway (Phase 1: $155M pipeline between Wheatland and OPUD; Phase 2: $40.5M for 40 Mile Road corridor). When complete, Wheatland wastewater will be treated at OPUD's facility. Future connections for Beale Air Force Base are also planned.

Funded in part by State Water Resources Control Board ($75M) and Yuba Water Agency ($80M loan).

Project info: https://engage.yubawater.org/south-county-regional-wastewater

## City of Yuba City — Not in Yuba County

The City of Yuba City (Sutter County) operates an industrial pretreatment program for its POTW (10.5 MGD facility). This is **not** a Yuba County POTW. Do not confuse jurisdictions.

## Industrial Discharger Guidance

Industrial users in unincorporated Yuba County must:
1. Identify whether the parcel is connected to OPUD, LCWD, or is on septic.
2. Contact the applicable district for sewer connection and discharge permit requirements.
3. For OPUD-served parcels: review Ordinance No. 209 prohibited-substance list before discharge.
4. NPDES-regulated dischargers must coordinate with the **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 5)**, based in Rancho Cordova.
