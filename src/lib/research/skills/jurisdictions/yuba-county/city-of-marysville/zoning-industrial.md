## Industrial Zone Districts

Per **Article 3 (Zoning Districts and Map)** of the Marysville Zoning Ordinance, the city establishes the following districts. The two industrial districts are:

| District Code | District Name |
|---|---|
| IRO | Industrial Research Office District |
| M-1 | Light Industrial District |
| M-2 | General Industrial District |

## M-1: Light Industrial District (Article 12)

**Purpose:** Accommodate wholesale, warehouse, and industrial operations whose external physical effects are restricted to the district and do not detrimentally affect surrounding areas. Raw material processing is not permitted; only previously prepared materials.

**Principal Uses Permitted (Section 1210):**
- Basic research, design, and pilot/experimental product development (wholly enclosed)
- Manufacturing, compounding, processing, packaging, or treatment of: bakery goods, candy, cosmetics, food products, pharmaceuticals, seeds, toiletries; products from canvas, cloth, glass, paper, plastics, rubber, sheet metal, wood (excluding sawmills), wire, etc.
- Tool, die, gauge, and machine shops
- Manufacture of musical instruments, toys, novelties, metal/rubber stamps
- Manufacture/assembly of electrical appliances and electronic instruments
- Central dry-cleaning or laundry plants (no retail)
- Pottery/ceramics using electric or gas kilns
- Dental, surgical, optical goods manufacture
- Research, experimental, film, and testing laboratories
- Printing, engraving, bookbinding shops
- Warehouse, storage and transfer; utility buildings; freight terminals
- General automotive repair; trade or industrial schools
- Accessory buildings and uses customarily incidental to above

**Uses Permitted Subject to Special Conditions (Section 1220):**
- Commercial kennels (setback and separation requirements from residential districts)
- Storage facilities for building materials, gravel, sand, stone, lumber, and contractor's equipment (screening required)
- Commercial greenhouses (no retail)
- Mini-warehouse/self-storage (40-ft side/rear setbacks; buildings spaced ≥30 ft apart; fire access lanes required)

**Required Conditions (Section 1230):**
- All outdoor storage in rear yard; screened from residential areas and public thoroughfares.
- No outdoor storage in off-street parking or loading areas.
- Performance standards apply (Section 1675).

## M-2: General Industrial District (Article 13)

**Purpose:** Accommodate manufacturing, assembling, and fabrication activities including large-scale or specialized industrial operations requiring transportation network access, whose external effects may be felt in surrounding districts. Permits manufacture from raw materials as well as previously prepared materials.

**Principal Uses Permitted (Section 1310):**
- All uses permitted in the M-1 District (above)
- Heating and electric power generating plants
- Bottling or packaging of cleaning compounds and polishes
- Manufacture of furniture and upholstery, paper, hardware, cutlery, leather goods, luggage, wearing apparel, automotive accessories (not including tires)
- Metal plating, welding, polishing and buffing, machining, painting, varnishing, and other finishing
- Automobile repair including bumping, painting, refinishing, steam cleaning, rust-proofing, high-speed washing
- Railroad yards and terminals
- Assembly plants for automobiles or other machinery
- Brewing or distillation of malt beverages or liquors
- Canning factories
- Metal stamping and pressing plants
- Lumber or planing mills
- Manufacture of cement, lime, gypsum, or plaster of paris, corrosive acid or alkali
- Storage facilities for building materials, gravel, sand, stone, lumber, and contractor's equipment/supplies (screening required)
- Freestanding non-accessory signs
- Accessory structures and uses customarily incidental to above

**Uses Permitted After Special Approval (Section 1320) — Planning Commission approval required after public hearing:**
- Chemical plants
- Incineration of garbage or refuse
- Petroleum or other flammable liquids — production, refining, or storage
- Manufacture of tires
- Other uses of similar general character (as determined by City Council after Planning Commission recommendation)

The Planning Commission must find that the use: is not injurious to the district and environs; is not contrary to the spirit and purpose of the Ordinance; is not incompatible with existing uses; would not interfere with orderly development; and would not be detrimental to safety or traffic.

**Required Conditions (Section 1330):**
- All outdoor storage in rear yard or interior side yard; screened from residential areas and public thoroughfares.
- No outdoor storage in designated parking or loading areas.
- Lots within 1,000 ft of a residential district shall not be occupied for M-2 use unless separated by a major or secondary thoroughfare (Planning Commission may waive if residential district is used for non-residential purposes).
- Performance standards apply (Section 1675).
- Schedule of Regulations (Article 15) controls height, bulk, lot size, and yard setbacks.

## CUP / Special Use Permit Process

**Authority:** Planning Commission (with appeals to City Council).

**Chapter 18.72** — Types of Site Development Permits
**Chapter 18.80** — General Processing Procedures (common to use permits, variances, and zoning amendments)

Key procedural rules:
- Use permits require a **public hearing** before the Planning Commission.
- Approved use permits **expire and become null and void** if the use or construction has not substantially commenced within **two years** of approval (without further action by the city).
- Permit revocation or modification requires a public hearing by the Planning Commission; the Commission's decision may be appealed to the City Council.
