## POTW / Treatment Operator

**Collection system:** Owned and operated by the **City of Marysville** (Public Works Department). Approximately 63 miles of sanitary sewer mains and 6 pumping stations within city limits.

**Wastewater treatment (since ~2015):** The city decommissioned its own treatment plant and contracted treatment to the **Linda County Water District (LCWD) Wastewater Treatment Plant**:

- Facility address: 909 Myrna Avenue, Marysville, CA 95901
- LCWD contact: (530) 743-2043 | 1280 Scales Ave., Marysville, CA 95901
- Design capacity: 5.0 MGD (tertiary treatment)
- NPDES permit: CA0079651 (Order R5-2022-XXXX, adopted December 2022, effective February 1, 2023, expires January 31, 2028)
- Receiving water: Feather River (Discharge Point 001) and evaporation/percolation ponds in the Feather River floodplain (Discharge Point 002)
- Regulating board: Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB Region 5)

Billing for city sewer service is handled by **Utility Management Services (UMS)**: (530) 717-2602 or (866) 912-3692 (24-hr emergency).

## Sewer Use Ordinance

**Municipal Code Chapter:** 6.04 SEWERS AND SEWAGE DISPOSAL (City of Marysville)

Key provisions:
- Every user of the sanitary sewer system shall connect to the city sewer main by a sewer service lateral.
- Sewer service laterals in the public right-of-way or easement are owned and maintained by the **property owner** from the connection point at the city main to the building.
- New sewer service accounts and account transfers must be placed in the name of the **owner of record** (per current Yuba County Assessor's roll).

**Ordinance No. 1438** (effective February 4, 2022): amended the Municipal Code to improve collection of sewer service charges.

## Industrial Pretreatment Program (IWP)

The city's NPDES permit for the Linda County Water District treatment plant (Order R5-2022-XXXX) includes **Section VI.C.5 — Special Provisions for Publicly-Owned Treatment Works (POTWs)**, which requires the Discharger to develop and submit a **POTW Pretreatment Program** per 40 C.F.R. Part 403 to the Central Valley Water Board for approval.
