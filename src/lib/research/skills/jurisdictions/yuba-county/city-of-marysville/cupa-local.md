## Programs Administered (Countywide, Including City of Marysville)

Under California Health & Safety Code (SB 1082), the Yuba County CUPA consolidates the following programs for all facilities in Yuba County:

| Program | Details |
|---|---|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) | Annual certification; tri-annual inspections of facilities storing hazardous materials above threshold quantities |
| California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) | Risk Management Plans (RMP) for facilities storing extremely hazardous substances above thresholds; tri-annual audits |
| Hazardous Waste Management | RCRA compliance; tri-annual inspections of all facilities generating hazardous waste; proper handling, storage, transport, and disposal |
| Underground Storage Tanks (UST) | Annual inspections; permitting of all installations and repairs; compliance with Title 23 CCR and federal standards |
| Aboveground Storage Tanks (AST) | Spill Prevention Control and Countermeasure (SPCC) plan oversight |

Source: https://www.yuba.gov/departments/community_development/environmental_health/hazardous_materials_(cupa)/hazardous_materials_management/index.php

## Emergency Response

Yuba County Environmental Health (YCEH) coordinates with the **Yuba-Sutter Hazardous Materials Response Team (YSMRT)**, a regional team covering Yuba and Sutter Counties. The City of Marysville Fire Department also participates in YSMRT and maintains a hazardous materials response vehicle.

YCEH is responsible for maintaining the **Area Plan** for hazardous materials emergency response in Yuba County.

Source: https://www.yuba.gov/departments/community_development/environmental_health/hazardous_materials_(cupa)/emergency_response/index.php
