## Fire AHJ

**Marysville Fire Department** — independent municipal fire department (one of the few in Yuba County). Not served by a county fire district or CAL FIRE unit; the city operates its own department.

- Address: 107 9th Street, Marysville, CA 95901
- Phone: (530) 741-6622
- Fire Chief: Kyle Heggstrom (as of mid-2026; verify current)
- Email: kheggstrom@marysville.ca.us

The fire department also maintains a **Hazardous Materials Response Vehicle** serving Yuba and Sutter Counties and participates in the **Yuba-Sutter Hazardous Materials Response Team (YSMRT)**.

Source: https://www.marysville.ca.us/fire-department

## Adopted Fire Code

**Municipal Code Chapter:** 13.20 FIRE CODE

**Adopting ordinance:** Ordinance No. 1427 (adopted 2020)

**Adopted code:** California Fire Code — adopted by reference and incorporated as fully as if set out at length in Chapter 13.20. One copy is on file in the office of the City Building Official.

> The 2020 adoption (Ord. 1427) aligned with the **2019 California Fire Code** (CCR Title 19 / NFPA base), which was the state-current edition at that time. The 2022 CFC became effective statewide on January 1, 2023, and the 2025 California Building Standards Code (Title 24) took effect January 1, 2026. **Verify with the Fire Department which edition the city's local chapter currently references** — the municipal code text hosted on CodePublishing may not reflect the latest state cycle.

## Local Definitions / Amendments

Per the municipal code, the following local definitions apply within Chapter 13.20:

- **"Jurisdiction"** means the City of Marysville.
- **"Fire code official"** or **"Chief"** means the City Fire Chief.
- The California Fire Code is enforced by the fire department under the supervision of the Fire Chief.

## Fire Inspections & Permits

- **Annual inspections** required for all businesses for fire and life safety compliance.
- **Operational permits** required for operations that increase risk for residents or workers.
- **Plans review** required for new construction or modifications exceeding 3,000 sq ft or changing building use.
- **Pre-fire plans** required for certain facilities.
- The city's fire code page states: "For current requirements, please consult the latest edition of the California Fire Code."

Source: https://www.marysville.ca.us/business-fire-inspections
