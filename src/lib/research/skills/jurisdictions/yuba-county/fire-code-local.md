## Adopted Code Edition

The Yuba County Building Department has adopted the **2025 California Fire Code (CFC)** (Title 24, Part 9, based on the 2024 International Fire Code) effective **January 1, 2026**. All construction plans submitted on or after January 1, 2026 must comply with the 2025 CFC.

The adoption is implemented through **Yuba County Ordinance Code Title X, Chapter 10.05** ("Building, Structures, Construction Codes"), which adopts California Building Standards Code Title 24 by reference. A separate standalone fire code ordinance chapter was not found online; fire code adoption flows through the building-code chapter.

[VERIFY — a dedicated Yuba County fire prevention ordinance chapter (analogous to many counties' "Chapter 8.XX Fire Code") was not located in the publicly accessible ordinance code. Confirm with the Yuba County Building Department at (530) 749-5440 whether local fire code amendments exist beyond the base CFC adoption.]

## Prior Edition (for reference)

The 2019 CFC was effective January 1, 2020 through December 31, 2025 per a county notice dated 2020.

## Fire Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

**Unincorporated Yuba County — primary AHJ: CAL FIRE Nevada-Yuba-Placer Unit (NEU).** CAL FIRE NEU provides fire protection and emergency services throughout the unincorporated areas of Yuba County (along with Nevada, Placer, Sierra, and Sutter counties).

Multiple local fire protection districts and volunteer departments serve specific unincorporated communities within the county:
- Linda Fire Protection District — 1286 Scales Ave., Marysville
- Olivehurst Fire Department (OPUD) — 1962 9th Ave., Olivehurst
- Smartsville FPD, Dobbins/Oregon House FPD, Foothill VFD, and others for foothill/rural areas

The correct AHJ for a specific parcel must be confirmed by parcel address; CAL FIRE NEU is the backstop AHJ for areas not covered by a local district.

**Key exceptions:**
- **City of Marysville**: served by the independent Marysville Fire Department (not CAL FIRE).
- **City of Wheatland**: served by the Wheatland Fire Authority (JPA). See city-of-wheatland folder.
- **Beale Air Force Base**: federal land with its own DoD fire department; county/CAL FIRE AHJ does not apply.

## Fire Safe Regulations (Wildland Interface)

Yuba County Development Code, **Chapter 11.22 Fire Safe Regulations** and **Chapter 11.52 Fire Safe Regulations** address fire safety requirements in State Responsibility Areas (SRA) and Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zones (VHFHSZ). These apply in addition to the CFC for development in wildland-interface areas.
