## CUPA Agency

**Yuba County Environmental Health Division** (within the Community Development & Services Agency) is the **single Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for the entire county**, including the incorporated cities of Marysville and Wheatland. No city in Yuba County operates its own CUPA.

Listed in CalEPA's CERS Unified Program Regulator Directory as "Yuba County Environmental Health Department."

## Programs Administered

The CUPA consolidates six regulatory programs under a single permit/inspection framework:

1. **Hazardous Materials Management (HMBP/HMRRP)** — Annual HMBP certification submittal via CERS; tri-annual facility inspections.
2. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — Tri-annual audits of Risk Management Plans for facilities with designated extremely hazardous substances above threshold quantities.
3. **Hazardous Waste Generator** — Tri-annual inspections of generators to ensure RCRA compliance.
4. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — Permit issuance and annual inspections.
5. **Aboveground Storage Tanks (AST / APSA)** — Tri-annual inspections for facilities storing ≥1,320 gallons of petroleum or other hazardous substances; SPCC plan required.
6. **Emergency Response / Area Plan** — Yuba County Environmental Health maintains the Area Plan and participates in the regional Yuba-Sutter Hazardous Materials Response Team.

## CERS Reporting

All HMBP, CalARP, UST, and related submittals are made electronically through the California Environmental Reporting System (CERS): https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/

The CUPA's CERS regulator ID can be confirmed via: https://cersapps.calepa.ca.gov/public/directory/ (select "Yuba" county).

## Emergency Response

**Yuba-Sutter Hazardous Materials Response Team** — a regional team serving Yuba and Sutter counties; Yuba City Fire Department hosts a CAL OES Type II hazmat vehicle (acquired 2018-2019) capable of responding to tanker truck rollovers, railcar derailments, and other major releases in Northern California.
