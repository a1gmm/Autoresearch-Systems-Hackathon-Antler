## Industrial Zone Designations (§ 11.04.020 and § 11.09.010)

Three industrial base zone districts exist:

### IC — Industrial Commercial
Purpose: Applied to property designated industrial/commercial under **Measure R (Yuba County Raceway)** — a mix of light industrial and commercial uses compatible with adjacent sports/entertainment uses. Narrow application; site-specific.

### IG — General Industrial
Purpose: Reserve areas for industrial plants including operations that store large volumes of hazardous or unsightly materials, or produce dust, smoke, fumes, odors, or noise affecting surroundings. Protects industrial land from residential intrusion. Permits limited ancillary retail for sale/display of on-site manufactured products.

### IL — Light Industrial
Purpose: Compatible uses near residential and less-intense commercial areas. Allows light industrial and service-commercial uses with limited potential for noise, odor, vibration impacts. Discretionary approval pathway for uses with mitigable impacts. Campus-style setting; limited retail, services, restaurants, and quasi-public uses serving on-site employees.

**(A) Transitional Standards:** Where an industrial district is adjacent to a Residential or Neighborhood Mixed-Use District with existing residential uses, height is stepped down (30 ft within 40 ft; 40 ft within 50 ft; 50 ft within 100 ft of district boundary) and setbacks increase (15 ft interior side yard, 20 ft rear yard from residential boundary).

Parking & Loading governed by Chapter 11.25.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

Uses designated **C** require a **Conditional Use Permit** from the Yuba County Planning Commission. Uses designated **M** require a **Minor Conditional Use Permit** (lower-level discretionary review). Uses designated **A** require an **Administrative Use Permit**.

The Planning Commission acts as the approving authority for CUPs and variances; it also advises the Board of Supervisors on general plan amendments and rezones. Commission typically meets the third Wednesday of each month.

CUP application process:
- File "Planning Project Application Form" with the Planning Department
- Submit "Conditional Use Permit Checklist" documentation
- Pay applicable fees (see "Project Development Fees" schedule)
- CEQA review applies

Additional use-specific supplemental regulations are cross-referenced in Table 11.09.020 (e.g., §11.32.070 Caretaker Residences, §11.32.080 Entertainment, §11.32.090 Park & Recreation, §11.32.250 SRO, §11.32.300 Temporary Uses).

## Overlay Districts Affecting Industrial Sites

- **Airport Environs (AP) Overlay** (Ch. 11.13) — Height limitations and noise requirements near Yuba County Airport; FAA notification required.
- **Floodplain (FP) Overlay** (Ch. 11.14) — Development restrictions in FEMA floodplain.
- **NPDES Overlay** (Ch. 11.15) — Stormwater controls for development.
- **Fire Safe Regulations** (Ch. 11.22 / Ch. 11.52) — Additional fire safety requirements in SRA / VHFHSZ.
