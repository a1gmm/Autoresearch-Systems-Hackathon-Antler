> Zoning reference for industrial land use in the City of Wheatland. Wheatland has two industrial zoning districts (M-1 Light Industrial and M-2 Heavy Industrial) in Title 18 of the Municipal Code. The city administers its own planning/zoning; building permitting is contracted to Yuba County.

## Zoning Code Location

- **Municipal code:** Title 18 (Zoning), City of Wheatland Municipal Code
- **eCode360 index:** https://ecode360.com/42503344
- **Authority:** Government Code Title 7, Division 1 (State Planning and Zoning Law); local implementing ordinance in Title 18

## Industrial Districts

### M-1 — Light Industrial District (Chapter 18.38)

- **Purpose:** Provide areas for light manufacturing, wholesaling, storage, and transfer functions that do not create nuisances or adverse effects on nearby commercial or residential uses.
- **General plan consistency:** Light Intensity Industrial land use designation.
- **Permitted uses (illustrative, not exhaustive):** Manufacturing, processing, assembling, contracting, repair, office, research, transportation, transfer, storage, wholesale, administrative uses — except those uses specifically listed as prohibited or requiring a conditional use permit in §§18.42.030 and 18.42.040.
  - Small convenience grocery (≤3,000 sq ft gross floor area) permitted.
  - Research institutes permitted.
  - Waste collection / waste transfer stations: **NOT permitted** in M-1.
- **eCode360:** https://ecode360.com/42504103

### M-2 — Heavy Industrial District (Chapter 18.42)

- **Purpose:** Provide sites for manufacturing and processing uses that, by their nature, require locations buffered from other uses to avoid nuisances or deleterious effects on neighboring properties.
- **General plan consistency:** Heavy Intensity Industrial land use designation.
- **Permitted uses:** All uses permitted in M-1 are also permitted in M-2. Additional heavy industrial uses consistent with M-2 purpose.
- **eCode360:** https://ecode360.com/42504136

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

- **CUP chapter:** Chapter 18.76 (Conditional Use Permits) — [VERIFY exact chapter; confirmed by search index reference to "Chapter 18.76" for CUP procedures]
- **Decision-maker:** Planning Commission (public hearing required)
- **Purpose:** Ensure that proposed uses are consistent with City regulations and compatible with surrounding uses.
- **Appeal:** Planning Commission decisions may be appealed to the City Council within **10 calendar days** of the Planning Commission action date. Appeals are set forth in Zoning Ordinance Chapter 18.82.
- **Standards:** Planning Commission applies CUP standards and considerations to both principal and accessory uses as specified in the zoning ordinance.
