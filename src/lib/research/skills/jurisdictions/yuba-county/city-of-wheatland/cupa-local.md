> The City of Wheatland is NOT its own CUPA. All six CalEPA unified program elements for businesses located within Wheatland city limits are administered by the **Yuba County Environmental Health Division** (the countywide CUPA), under the Community Development & Services Agency.

## CUPA Identity

- **CUPA:** Yuba County Environmental Health Division (countywide CUPA — serves all of Yuba County including the City of Wheatland)

## Programs Administered

Yuba County Environmental Health CUPA administers all six CalEPA unified programs:

| # | Program | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) | Annual certification via CERS; tri-annual inspections |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator Program | RCRA and CA Hazardous Waste Control Law compliance; tri-annual inspections |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tanks (UST) | Permitting, installation, repair, annual inspections |
| 4 | Aboveground Storage Tanks (AST / APSA) | SPCC plan review for facilities storing ≥1,320 gallons aggregate aboveground hazardous substances |
| 5 | California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) | RMP review and tri-annual audits for facilities with extremely hazardous substances above threshold |
| 6 | CFC Hazardous Materials Operational Permits | [VERIFY] — confirm whether Yuba County CUPA or the Wheatland Fire Authority AHJ issues CFC Ch. 50+ operational permits in Wheatland city limits |

## HMBP / CERS Filing

- **Submission system:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business
- Businesses handling hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities (generally 55 gallons liquid / 500 lbs solid / 200 cu ft gas, per Health & Safety Code §25507) must file an HMBP.
- Yuba County Environmental Health is the CERS reviewing agency for Wheatland businesses.

## Emergency Response

- Yuba County Environmental Health is a member of the **Yuba-Sutter Hazardous Materials Response Team (YSMRT)**, providing regional hazmat emergency response for Yuba and Sutter Counties.
- YSMRT web: https://www.yuba.gov/departments/community_development/environmental_health/hazardous_materials_(cupa)/emergency_response/yuba_sutter_hazardous_materials_response_team/index.php
- The county CUPA develops and updates the Area Plan for hazmat emergency response within Yuba County.
