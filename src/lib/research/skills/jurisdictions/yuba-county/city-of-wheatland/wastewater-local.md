> The City of Wheatland owns and operates its own POTW (Publicly Owned Treatment Works) and sewer collection system. Wastewater is a city Public Works function — there is no regional sanitation district. The sewer use ordinance is codified in Title 13 of the Wheatland Municipal Code. Industrial pretreatment program status is uncertain for this small rural city — see note below.

### Enforcement Powers

- The city engineer may **disconnect premises from the sewer system** for violations after providing at least 5 days' written notice specifying the violation.
- Mechanic's lien or tax-assessment procedures are available for unremediated sewer lateral defects (per California Health & Safety Code §5463).

### FOG Program

- Chapter 13.22 establishes grease discharge rules for food service establishments (FSEs).
- Purpose: prevent sewer blockages from fats, oils, and grease discharges.

## Industrial Pretreatment Program (IWP)

- **Status: UNCERTAIN — small city, likely no formal pretreatment program [VERIFY]**
- The City of Wheatland is a small rural municipality (~4,000 population). Small POTWs that do not have significant industrial users are often not required by EPA/State to operate a formal local pretreatment program (40 CFR Part 403 requires a program only when EPA/State has determined one is needed).
- The municipal code structure includes industrial wastewater chapters, but no evidence was found of a formal EPA-approved local pretreatment program or an active Industrial Waste Permit (IWP) issuance program.
- **For industrial dischargers:** Contact City of Wheatland Public Works to determine whether an industrial wastewater permit is required, and what discharge limits apply. The applicable federal Categorical Pretreatment Standards (40 CFR Part 405 et seq.) apply directly to categorical industrial users regardless of local program status.
- [VERIFY] Contact the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 5, Rancho Cordova office) to confirm whether Wheatland's NPDES permit includes pretreatment program requirements.
