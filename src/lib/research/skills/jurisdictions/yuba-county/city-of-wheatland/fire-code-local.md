> Local fire code reference for the City of Wheatland. The fire AHJ is the Wheatland Fire Authority — a Joint Powers Agency (JPA), not a city department, not CAL FIRE, and not the county. Building plan-check is done by Yuba County under contract, but fire code enforcement/permits belong to the Wheatland Fire Authority.

## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

- **AHJ:** Wheatland Fire Authority (Joint Powers Agency — City of Wheatland + Plumas Brophy Fire Protection District)
- **Formed:** 2005 JPA agreement
- **Address:** 313 Main Street, Wheatland, CA 95692
- **Phone:** (530) 633-0861
- **Emergency:** 911
- **Web:** https://www.wheatlandfireauthority.org/
- **Service area:** City of Wheatland + Plumas Brophy Fire Protection District (surrounding rural area)

## Adopted Fire Code

- **Code edition:** 2022 California Fire Code (CFC), Part 9 of Title 24 of the California Code of Regulations, adopted by reference
- **Municipal code location:** Title 15 (Buildings and Construction), **Chapter 15.12** — California Fire Code
- **Adopting ordinance:** Ordinance No. 495, adopted January 9, 2024
- **Code current through:** Ordinance No. 503 (adopted June 10, 2025)

### Appendices Adopted

The following CFC appendices are expressly adopted:

- Appendix 4 (Referenced Standards)
- Appendix B (Fire-Flow Requirements)
- Appendix C (Fire Hydrant Locations and Distribution)
- Appendix I (Fire Protection Systems — Noncompliant Conditions)
- Appendix D, Sections D104, D105, and D106 (Fire Apparatus Access Roads — Secondary, Dead-End, and Aerial Access)

### Notable Local Amendments (confirmed via search index)

- **Aboveground tanks — Class I and II liquids:** Storage in aboveground tanks is **prohibited within City limits** except as authorized by the Fire Code Official; exception for existing agricultural operations.
- Copies of the adopted code and appendices are maintained at the office of the city's building official (Yuba County Building Department, 915 8th Street, Suite 123, Marysville — see Building authority note below).

> [VERIFY] Whether the City has adopted the 2025 California Fire Code (effective statewide 2026-01-01) since Ordinance 495 references the 2022 CFC. The code is current through Ord. 503 (June 2025), which post-dates the 2025 CFC adoption cycle. Confirm with Wheatland Fire Authority directly.

## Building Authority (Related)

- Building permitting and inspection are contracted to **Yuba County Building Department**, not a city department. Copies of fire-related building codes are kept there.
- Yuba County Building Dept: 915 8th Street, Suite 123, Marysville, CA 95901 — (530) 749-5440
- Web: https://www.yuba.gov/departments/community_development/building_department/index.php

## Fire Permits / Plan Check

- Fire operational permits and plan check are routed through the **Wheatland Fire Authority** (313 Main St, Wheatland; (530) 633-0861).
- For construction-related fire plan check, coordinate between Wheatland Fire Authority (fire code) and Yuba County Building Dept (building code).
- [VERIFY] Confirm fee schedule and permit application form with Wheatland Fire Authority — not published online.
