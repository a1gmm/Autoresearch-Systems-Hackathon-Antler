## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air Quality
- **District:** Sacramento Metropolitan Air Quality Management District (SMAQMD) does NOT cover Colusa County. The controlling air district is the **Colusa County Air Pollution Control District (CCAPCD)**.
- CCAPCD issues Authorities to Construct (ATCs) and Permits to Operate (PTOs) for stationary sources within city limits. All air permit applications go to CCAPCD, not the City.
- CCAPCD contact: 100 Sunrise Blvd, Suite E, Colusa, CA 95932 | (530) 458-0669
- Source: https://www.countyofcolusaca.gov/216/Air-Pollution-Control-District

### CUPA (Hazardous Materials / Unified Program)
- **Not its own CUPA.** The City of Colusa is served by the **Colusa County Environmental Health Division** (CUPA certified by CalEPA).
- Programs administered: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generator program, Underground Storage Tank (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP).
- Regulated facility inventory (per county CUPA profile): ~328 HMBP sites, ~139 hazardous waste generators, ~56 APSA sites, ~19 UST sites, ~9 CalARP facilities county-wide.
- All HMBP and hazmat forms submitted electronically through CERS: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov
- UST installation/closure permits: Colusa County ViewPoint Cloud portal.
- CUPA fee schedule (county-wide; applies to city facilities):
  - Business Plan: $168–$336
  - Hazardous Waste — Small generator: $196 | Medium: $252 | Large: $280
  - UST Operating (single tank): $448 | Installation: $560 | Closure/removal: $672
  - APSA: $224
  - CalARP: $1,120
  - Landfill: $3,360 | Transfer station: $2,016
  - State surcharges additional: UST +$15; Business Plan +$35; CalARP +$270
- Source: https://www.countyofcolusaca.gov/426/Hazardous-Materials-Waste-CUPA

### Fire (Fire Code / AHJ)
- **AHJ:** City of Colusa Fire Department — combination paid/volunteer department (4 paid staff, 25 volunteers). Fire Chief Logan Conley. 750 Market Street, Colusa, CA 95932 | (530) 458-7721.
- Fire prevention, commercial inspections, and permit issuance handled by this department within city limits.
- Fire code: Chapter 9 of the City of Colusa Code of Ordinances (Municode) adopts the California Fire Code (CFC) with local amendments. The elaws.us mirror references the **2013 CFC** as the adopted base; the current CFC edition (2022, effective until 2026-01-01 when superseded by 2025 CFC) should be verified with the department.   - Prohibition on driving vehicles over fire hoses without protection
  - Prohibition on obstructing fire/police access streets, roads, alleys

### Wastewater / Sewer
- **POTW:** City of Colusa Wastewater Treatment Plant, 2820 Will S. Green Road, Colusa, CA 95932. Operated by City Public Works / Utilities Division.
- NPDES Permit: **CA0078999**, Order **R5-2022-0020** (Central Valley RWQCB), effective 1 June 2022, expires 31 May 2027. Classified as a **Minor Discharge**.
- Design/permitted flow: 0.7 MGD average dry weather flow; tertiary treated (activated sludge + tertiary filtration + UV disinfection).
- Discharge point: Unnamed tributary to Powell Slough (tributary to Colusa Basin Drain, Sacramento River Watershed).
- **No formal federal pretreatment program** — the NPDES fact sheet shows "Pretreatment Program:" field blank, consistent with a Minor facility. Industrial users must nonetheless comply with federal categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR Part 403) and state law.
- Sewer use ordinance: **Chapter 20** of City of Colusa Code of Ordinances (Municode node CH20WA) [VERIFY chapter structure and local discharge limits — chapter text not publicly accessible without Municode login].

### Zoning (Industrial Districts)
- Zoning code: **Appendix A — Zoning Code** to the City of Colusa Code of Ordinances (Municode). Three industrial district types:
  - **M-1 Light Industrial** (Article 12): Wholesale/storage warehouses, manufacturing, fabricating, repairing, packaging, laundries, truck terminals, lumber yards. Dwellings/hotels require use permit. Building height max 100 ft; rear yard setback 5 ft; no minimum lot area.
  - **M-2 General Industrial** (Article 13): All M-1 uses plus lumber mills, grain elevators, pottery kilns, concrete batch plants, foundries, junkyards/auto wrecking (with 6-ft solid wall/fence enclosure). Building height max 100 ft; no minimum lot area.
  - **M-L Limited Manufacturing** (Article 14): Clean/quiet light manufacturing — offices, labs, electronics/plastics/instruments, printing, garment/paper products. Min. lot area 10,000 sq ft; 20-ft setbacks front/side/rear; max height 35 ft (2 stories).
- Use Permit (CUP): Article 33. Dwellings/hotels in M-1/M-2 require use permit. Unlisted uses in M-2 require major use permit. Planning Commission approval required for certain utility installations in M-L.
- Planning contact: planning@cityofcolusa.gov | (530) 458-4941 ext. 3103
- Source: https://www.zoneomics.com/code/colusa-CA/chapter_45 | https://library.municode.com/ca/colusa/codes/code_of_ordinances

## GOTCHA Notes
- The City of Colusa runs its OWN municipal fire department and its OWN building/planning department, but does NOT run its own CUPA — all hazmat/hazwaste/UST/APSA/CalARP regulated under the Colusa County Environmental Health CUPA.
- Air permits go to **CCAPCD**, not the City.
- The POTW is a Minor NPDES discharger with no formal pretreatment program; however, industrial dischargers must still meet federal categorical standards.
- Zoning code is an appendix to the municipal code; chapter/article numbering in the appendix differs from main code chapter numbering.
