## Industrial District Overview

The City of Colusa Zoning Code has three industrial/manufacturing district designations:

| District | Article | Name |
|---|---|---|
| M-1 | Article 12 | Light Industrial District |
| M-2 | Article 13 | General Industrial District |
| M-L | Article 14 | Limited Manufacturing District |

## M-1 — Light Industrial District (Article 12)

### Permitted Uses (by-right)
- All uses permitted in the C-G (General Commercial) District (except as noted below)
- Wholesale and storage warehouses
- Feed and fuel yards
- Manufacturing, processing, fabricating, repairing, and packaging operations (excluding fish fats/oils, bones, meat products)
- Dyeing, dry-cleaning, rug cleaning, laundries
- Veterinary hospitals
- Cabinet shops
- Construction materials yards (excluding rock/sand/gravel mixing or open lumber yards)
- Welding and sheet metal shops
- Bottling works
- Truck terminals
- Lumber yards (when conducted within a building or enclosed within a solid wall or fence not less than 6 feet in height)

### Uses Requiring a Use Permit
- Dwellings (residential uses)
- Hotels

### Development Standards

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Minimum Lot Area | None specified |
| Minimum Lot Width | None specified |
| Front Yard Setback | None specified |
| Side Yard Setback | None (except as required by building code) |
| Rear Yard Setback | **5 feet** |
| Maximum Building Height | **100 feet** |
| Lot Coverage | None specified |
| Off-Street Loading | Private off-street space for all materials and equipment handling |
| Off-Street Parking | Per Article 29 |

### Performance Standards
Operators must confine smoke, dust, noxious odors and fumes, and noise above ambient level within the premises. Flammable/explosive materials storage must comply with applicable safety regulations.

Source: https://www.zoneomics.com/code/colusa-CA/chapter_12

## M-2 — General Industrial District (Article 13)

### Permitted Uses (by-right)
All M-1 uses (subject to the same dwelling/hotel use-permit requirement), plus:
- Wholesale lumber yards and lumber mills
- Grain elevators and grain storage
- Pottery kilns and ceramic works (heavy industrial types)
- Concrete batch plants
- Blacksmith shops and casting foundries
- Building material storage yards and contractors storage yards (when enclosed by solid wall or fence, minimum 6 feet in height)
- Junkyards and auto wrecking yards (when enclosed by solid wall or fence, minimum 6 feet in height, with Planning Commission approval)

### Uses Requiring a Major Use Permit
- Any lawful use not otherwise provided for in the zoning ordinance

### Development Standards

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Minimum Lot Area | None specified |
| Minimum Lot Width | None specified |
| Front Yard Setback | None specified |
| Side Yard Setback | None (except as required by building code or other regulations) |
| Rear Yard Setback | None specified |
| Maximum Building Height | **100 feet** |
| Lot Coverage | None specified |

### Storage and Operations Provisions
Outdoor storage of materials, junkyards, and auto wrecking yards require:
- Solid wall or fence enclosure, minimum 6 feet in height
- Planning Commission approval

Source: https://www.zoneomics.com/code/colusa-CA/chapter_13

## M-L — Limited Manufacturing District (Article 14)

### Purpose
The M-L district accommodates a limited group of clean, quiet business, professional, research, and technical manufacturing uses that require unique spatial needs not compatible with commercial or general industrial zones.

### Permitted Uses (by-right)
- Commercial and professional offices
- Research institutes and laboratories
- Electrical, small electronic, plastics, and instrument manufacturing
- Bookbinding, printing, design, lithography, and cartography
- Garment and paper products manufacturing
- Underground and aboveground utility installations for local service

**Performance requirement:** All uses must confine noise, smoke, dust, and odors to the premises.

### Uses Requiring Planning Commission Approval
- Utility substations, generating plants, and gas holders

### Development Standards

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Minimum Lot Area | **10,000 sq ft** |
| Minimum Lot Width | Not specified |
| Front Yard Setback | **20 feet** |
| Side Yard Setback | **20 feet** |
| Rear Yard Setback | **20 feet** |
| Maximum Building Height | **35 feet (2 stories)** |
| Off-Street Parking | Per Article 29 |

All M-L uses must comply with the general regulations in Article 32.

Source: https://www.zoneomics.com/code/colusa-CA/chapter_14

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

Use permits (CUP) are governed by **Article 33 — Use Permits** of the Colusa Zoning Code. Per Article 33, the Planning Commission (or administrative designee for minor use permits) is the approval authority.

Key triggers in industrial zones:
- **M-1:** Dwellings and hotels → use permit required
- **M-2:** Any unlisted lawful use → major use permit required; outdoor junkyards/storage yards → Planning Commission approval for fence/wall
- **M-L:** Substations, generating plants, gas holders → Planning Commission approval
