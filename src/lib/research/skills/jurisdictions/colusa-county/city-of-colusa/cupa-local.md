## CUPA Agency

**Colusa County Environmental Health Division**  
(Community Development Department)  
547 Market Street, Colusa, CA 95932  

The City of Colusa is **not its own CUPA**. All Unified Program elements for businesses located within city limits are administered by the County-level CUPA.

CalEPA-certified as the single CUPA for all of Colusa County (unincorporated and incorporated areas, including the City of Colusa).

Source: https://www.countyofcolusaca.gov/426/Hazardous-Materials-Waste-CUPA

## Programs Administered

The Colusa County CUPA administers all six Unified Program elements:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP)** — required for businesses that handle hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities.
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator Program** — oversight of small, medium, and large hazardous waste generators.
3. **Underground Storage Tank (UST)** — permits for installation, operation, and closure of underground storage tanks.
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — oversight of aboveground petroleum storage (largely agricultural sites in this county).
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — Program 2 and Program 3 facilities handling acutely hazardous materials.
6. **Hazardous Waste Tiered Permitting** — (consolidated under generator program above).

Staffing: All inspectors are Registered Environmental Health Specialists (REHS). The CUPA has historically been ahead of mandated inspection frequency.

## Permit Applications and Reporting

- **HMBP / Hazmat reporting:** Electronic submission required via **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System): https://cers.calepa.ca.gov
- **UST installation permit:** Apply via Colusa County ViewPoint Cloud permit portal.
- **UST closure permit:** Apply via Colusa County ViewPoint Cloud permit portal.

## Air District Interaction

The Colusa County Air Pollution Control District (CCAPCD) administers air permits separately from the CUPA. CalARP facilities with toxic release scenarios may need coordination between CUPA and CCAPCD. CCAPCD: https://www.countyofcolusaca.gov/216/Air-Pollution-Control-District
