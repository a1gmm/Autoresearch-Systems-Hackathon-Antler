## Pretreatment Program

The City of Colusa Wastewater Treatment Plant is classified as a **Minor** NPDES discharger. The NPDES fact sheet (Order R5-2022-0020, Attachment F) lists "Pretreatment Program:" with no value — meaning the facility does **not** operate a formal federal pretreatment program under 40 CFR Part 403.

**Implication for industrial users:** Industrial dischargers to the city sewer must still comply with:
- Federal categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR Parts 405–471) applicable to their industrial category
- General and specific prohibitions under 40 CFR § 403.5 (pass-through, interference)
- Any local limits in the City's Sewer Use Ordinance (Chapter 20 of the municipal code)

Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/rwqcb5/board_decisions/adopted_orders/colusa/r5-2022-0020_npdes.pdf (Attachment F, Fact Sheet)
