## Local Amendments (§ 9-2)

The following local amendments/additions are codified in Section 9-2 of Chapter 9 (Article I). These are **in addition to** the adopted statewide CFC:

1. **Fire hose protection** — Prohibition on driving motor vehicles over fire hoses in use during an emergency without a protective device or authorization from the fire officer in charge.

2. **Access obstruction** — Prohibition on obstructing streets, roads, lanes, paths, or alleys needed for fire department or police access during emergencies.

3. **Class I and II flammable liquid storage** — Storage of Class I and II flammable/combustible liquids in aboveground tanks located outside of buildings is restricted within the city. Exceptions exist for certain zoning areas (details in the ordinance text).

4. **Fireworks ("Safe and Sane")** — "Safe and Sane" fireworks are permitted within city limits under specific conditions defined in the ordinance. (Note: this is a local relaxation from the default CFC prohibition.)

5. **Explosive manufacture** — Manufacture of explosives is **prohibited** within the City of Colusa.

6. **Explosive storage** — Storage of explosive materials is **prohibited** within the City of Colusa.

7. **Propane prohibition (residential)** — Use of propane in place of natural gas is **prohibited** in residential structures within city limits.

8. **Open burning** — No rubbish, garbage, brush, grass, tree trimmings, or other materials shall be burned outside of any building within the City.

## Permit Requirements

The Fire Department requires permits for the installation of certain systems (fire suppression, sprinklers, hazardous materials storage). The Fire Chief or designee determines whether an installation is safe before issuing a permit.

Commercial inspections are conducted by the Fire Prevention division. A "Business Fire Safety Packet" (PDF) is available from the Fire Department for businesses seeking pre-inspection guidance.

[VERIFY] Fee schedule for fire permits not found online. Contact Fire Department at (530) 458-7721 for current fee schedule.
