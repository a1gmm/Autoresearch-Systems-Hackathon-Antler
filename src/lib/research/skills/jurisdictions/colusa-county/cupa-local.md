> Research reference for the Colusa County CUPA program. Colusa County Environmental Health is the single CUPA for the entire county — there are no participating agencies or city-run CUPAs. Both cities (Colusa and Williams) are covered by this county CUPA.

## CUPA Identity

- **CUPA Agency:** Colusa County Environmental Health
- **Parent Department:** Development Services Department (also called Community Development Department), County of Colusa
- **Address:** 547 Market Street, Colusa, CA 95932
- **Online Permit Portal (UST applications):** https://colusacountyca.viewpointcloud.com
- **CERS (HMBP/UST reporting):** https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/
- **CalEPA CUPA certification:** Yes — certified by CalEPA as a full CUPA for Colusa County

## Geographic Scope

The Colusa County CUPA covers:
- All unincorporated areas of Colusa County
- **City of Colusa** (county seat) — no separate city CUPA
- **City of Williams** — no separate city CUPA

No Participating Agencies (PAs) exist within Colusa County.

## Programs Administered

| # | Program | Acronym | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan | HMBP | Submitted via CERS; county EH reviews and inspects |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator Program | HWG | Generator ID, manifest compliance, inspection |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tank | UST | Installation, operation, modification, closure, leak response; apply via viewpointcloud portal |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act / AST | APSA | Spill prevention for above-threshold petroleum storage; APSA sites heavily agricultural (many farm sites) |
| 5 | California Accidental Release Prevention Program | CalARP | Local implementing agency for RMP submissions |
| (6) | *Fire Code Hazmat Operational Permits — CFC Chapter 50+* | *see Notes* | *Administered by individual fire districts/departments as AHJ, not by county CUPA directly* |

> Note: Unified Program element 6 (CFC hazmat operational permits) is administered by the AHJ for fire code in each sub-area — the City of Colusa Fire Department within City of Colusa, Williams FPA within Williams, and the relevant independent fire protection district for unincorporated areas. The county CUPA does not directly issue CFC operational fire permits.

## HMBP Program Details

- **Who must comply:** Any business storing, using, or handling hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities (Health & Safety Code §25507 — generally 55 gallons liquid, 500 lbs solid, 200 cubic feet gas).
- **Submission system:** CERS at https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/; county Environmental Health is the reviewing agency.
- **Inspector qualifications:** All CUPA inspectors are Registered Environmental Health Specialists (REHSs) in a continuous education program.

## UST Program Details

- **Permit applications:** Submit via online portal at https://colusacountyca.viewpointcloud.com
- **Compliance deadline:** UST systems with single-walled components designed before January 1, 1984 must achieve permanent closure by **December 31, 2025** (state mandate; may already be overdue — verify current compliance status for legacy tanks).

## APSA Program Details

- Facilities storing petroleum above APSA thresholds (≥ 1,320 gallons aggregate aboveground, or single tank > 660 gallons) must have Spill Prevention plans.
- **Reporting requirement:** Notify county Environmental Health immediately upon discovery of petroleum spills of **42 gallons or more**.
- **Inspection frequency:** Daily visual tank inspections required at APSA-covered facilities.

## CalARP Program Details

- County Environmental Health is the local implementing agency (LIA) for CalARP.
- Facilities with regulated substances above threshold quantities must submit a Risk Management Plan (RMP) to the USEPA.
- County EH reviews the local CalARP portion and coordinates with OES for worst-case scenario planning.
