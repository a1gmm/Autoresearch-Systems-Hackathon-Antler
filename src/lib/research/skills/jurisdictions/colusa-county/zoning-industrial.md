## Industrial Zone Designations (Article 44-2, Section 44-2.50)

Three industrial zone classifications exist in the unincorporated county:

| Zone | Abbreviation | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Light Industrial | M-1 | Light industrial and service commercial uses with little potential for noise, odor, vibration, or other impacts to adjacent uses. |
| Heavy Industrial | M-2 | Full range of industrial uses, including operations requiring storage of large volumes of hazardous or unsightly materials, or that produce dust, smoke, fumes, odors, or noise affecting surrounding land uses. |
| Research and Development | RD | High technology, non-polluting manufacturing plants, research and development facilities, corporate and industrial offices, and support services in a rural or campus-like / business park setting. |

**Setback notes:**
- [a] 15-ft front setback required when adjacent to a Residential Zone.
- [b] 30-ft setback (front, rear, or side) required when adjacent to a Residential Zone; zero side lot line must include fireproof walls meeting all applicable building and fire code requirements.
- [c] 15-ft side setback required when adjacent to a Residential Zone; zero side lot line must include fireproof walls.

### Residential Uses

Residential uses are generally **not allowed** in industrial zones. Caretaker quarters (A) permitted in M-1 and M-2 for on-site security personnel. Agricultural worker housing may be allowed via administrative permit in limited circumstances.

## Use Permit (CUP) Process

Uses requiring a **Use Permit (U)** require a full Conditional Use Permit through the Planning Commission. The process is governed by Section 44-1 of the Zoning Code:

1. Pre-application meeting with Community Development / Planning Division recommended.
2. Submit Use Permit application and fee to Community Development Department, 1213 Market Street, Colusa CA 95932.
3. CEQA environmental review (Initial Study / Mitigated Negative Declaration or EIR) required for most industrial use permits.
4. Public notice and hearing before Planning Commission.
5. Findings must support compatibility with surrounding uses and General Plan consistency.

**Minor Use Permit (M):** Heard administratively by the Zoning Administrator rather than Planning Commission; still requires public notice. Generally used for lower-impact conditional uses.

**Online permit portal:** https://colusacountyca.viewpointcloud.com (planning applications)
**Phone:** (530) 458-0480

## Key EHS Intersections

- **M-2 zone + heavy manufacturing / hazmat storage:** Facilities in M-2 storing hazardous materials above CUPA thresholds must comply with both the Use Permit conditions and the CUPA HMBP program (County Environmental Health, separate from Planning).
- **Agricultural chemicals manufacturing (U in M-2):** A full Use Permit required; CEQA analysis; CUPA HMBP filing; likely CalARP if acutely hazardous materials above threshold; air permit from Colusa County APCD.
- **Setback note for hazmat storage near residential:** M-2 requires 30-ft setback from any Residential Zone; fire districts may impose additional separation distances for hazmat storage under the CFC.
