> Research reference for wastewater and industrial discharge in Colusa County. There are two separate POTWs (City of Colusa and City of Williams). The unincorporated county has no centralized sewer — it relies on on-site systems (OWTS) governed by County Code Chapter 36. Neither POTW is large enough to require a federally-mandated pretreatment program, but local sewer use ordinance provisions must be verified.

#### Pretreatment Program

- At 0.7 mgd design flow, City of Colusa is **well below the 5 mgd federal threshold** for a mandatory industrial pretreatment program under 40 CFR Part 403.
- [VERIFY] Whether the Central Valley RWQCB has required a local pretreatment program despite the small flow (RWQCB can require pretreatment for smaller POTWs if they receive industrial pollutants that could pass through or interfere). No pretreatment program requirement was found in the WDR orders reviewed.

## Unincorporated County — On-Site Wastewater

- **Governing code:** Colusa County Code **Chapter 36**, "On-Site Wastewater Treatment Systems" (OWTS) — applicable to all existing and new OWTS in the unincorporated area with design flows ≤ 10,000 gpd.
- Source: https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/ColusaCounty/html/ColusaCounty36.html (returned HTTP 403 on direct fetch; confirmed via search index)
- Industrial facilities in unincorporated areas generating wastewater above OWTS capacity must obtain a Waste Discharge Requirement (WDR) from the Central Valley RWQCB.

## Industrial Wastewater Permit Process (City of Colusa)

1. Contact City of Colusa Utilities (utilities@cityofcolusa.gov) to determine if the facility's discharge is compatible with the city sewer system and POTW treatment capability.
2. [VERIFY] Whether a formal Industrial Wastewater Permit (IWP) or Significant Industrial User (SIU) permit is required under the city's sewer use ordinance — no such formal program was confirmed given the POTW's small size and absence of a federal pretreatment requirement.
3. Regardless of local pretreatment program status, federal Categorical Pretreatment Standards (40 CFR Parts 405–471) apply directly to categorical industrial users discharging to any POTW.
4. Prohibited discharge standards (general pretreatment, 40 CFR §403.5) apply: no flammable/explosive materials, corrosives with pH < 5.0, interfering pollutants, or materials that could damage the collection system.
