## City of Colusa — Local Amendments (§ 9-2)

Section 9-2 of Chapter 9 amends the California Fire Code with the following confirmed local provisions:

1. **Prohibited open burning:** No rubbish, garbage, brush, grass, tree trimmings, or other materials shall be burned outside of any building within the City of Colusa. (Exception: agricultural waste burning under certain conditions.)

2. **Aboveground flammable/combustible liquid storage:** Storage of Class I and Class II liquids in aboveground tanks outside of buildings is prohibited within the city without prior written approval.

3. **Cryogenic flammable fluids:** Storage and use of flammable cryogenic fluids in stationary containers in any amount is prohibited within the City of Colusa without prior approval from the Fire Department.

4. **Residential sprinkler systems:** Installers of residential automatic sprinkler systems must hold a C-16 contractor's license. Residential automatic sprinkler systems must have an external visual notification appliance (strobe/light) readily visible from the street or road fronting the property.

5. **Emergency access:** No person shall obstruct any street, road, lane, path, or alley intended for fire or police response routes.

## Permit Requirements

Fire permits in the City of Colusa are issued under the California Fire Code (CFC) operational permit framework. Permit-required operations include:

- Hazardous materials storage/use above CFC threshold quantities (CFC Chapter 50+)
- Flammable/combustible liquid aboveground storage (requires prior written approval per local amendment — see above)
- Open burning (generally prohibited within city limits per local amendment)
- Explosives, compressed gases, cryogenic fluids (prior approval required)
- Construction of fire suppression systems (plan check by AHJ)

Contact: City of Colusa Fire Department for permit applications and inspections — https://cityofcolusa.com/fire-department/

## Unincorporated County — Fire Districts

The unincorporated area of Colusa County is served by several independent fire protection districts. Each district is its own AHJ for fire code:

- Sacramento River FPD
- Glenn-Colusa FPD (overlaps Glenn County)
- Arbuckle-College City FPD
- Maxwell FPD
- Princeton FPD
- Williams FPD (covers unincorporated area around City of Williams)

County Fire Districts page: https://www.countyofcolusaca.gov/239/Fire-Districts

> [VERIFY] Each FPD must be contacted individually to confirm: (a) which edition of the CFC they have formally adopted, (b) whether they issue fire code operational permits, and (c) their local amendments if any. Many rural FPDs adopt the CFC by reference from the state without formal local ordinance action — in that case the current statewide CFC edition applies by default.
