## POTW

**City of Williams Wastewater Treatment Plant**
Owned and operated by the City of Williams Public Works Department.
810 E Street, Williams, CA 95987
Public Works phone: (530) 473-2519
Utility billing / service connections: Finance Dept, (530) 473-2948

The POTW provides wastewater collection, treatment, and disposal services for the City of Williams. Treated effluent is discharged to **Salt Creek**, a water of the United States tributary to Powell Slough and the Colusa Basin Drain within the Sacramento River Watershed.

## 2024 Enforcement Action

**ACL Order R5-2024-0510** (Settlement, 2024):
- 57 serious violations + 7 non-serious violations of effluent limits, January 2021 – December 2023
- Violations primarily for BOD and ammonia exceedances
- Mandatory minimum penalties assessed: $192,000
- Resolved via **Compliance Project** (in lieu of cash penalty, per Water Code § 13385(k)) — POTW qualifies as "small community POTW" (population 5,538; Colusa County rural county; unemployment rate 15%)
- Compliance project components: investigation/monitoring, chemical treatment, tertiary filter upgrade, development of operating procedures and source control measures
- Expected project cost: >$192,000; must be completed within 5 years

Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/rwqcb5/board_decisions/adopted_orders/colusa/r5-2024-0510_settle.pdf

## Industrial Pretreatment Program (IWP)

No formal IWP program was found online for Williams. Under 40 CFR Part 403, a POTW must establish a pretreatment program only if it receives industrial discharges from Significant Industrial Users (SIUs) that could interfere with plant operations or pass through pollutants. Given Williams' small size and primarily residential/agricultural service area, a formal IWP may not be required.
