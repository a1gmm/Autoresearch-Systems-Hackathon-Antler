## CUPA Agency

The City of Williams is **not its own CUPA**. All Unified Program functions are administered by:

**Colusa County Environmental Health**
547 Market Street, Colusa, CA 95932
Hours: Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–12:00 PM and 1:00 PM–4:30 PM

The county was certified by CalEPA as the sole CUPA for all of Colusa County. There is no city-level CUPA or sub-CUPA for Williams.

Source: https://www.countyofcolusaca.gov/426/Hazardous-Materials-Waste-CUPA

## Programs Administered

All six consolidated Unified Program elements are handled by county inspectors (all Registered Environmental Health Specialists):

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — Health & Safety Code Chapter 6.95
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator Program** — Title 22, CCR
3. **Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program** — Health & Safety Code Chapter 6.7
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — Health & Safety Code Chapter 6.67
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — Health & Safety Code Chapter 6.95, Article 2; 40 CFR Part 68
6. **General environmental compliance oversight**

County-wide inventory (approximate): 328 HMBP sites, 139 hazardous waste generators, 56 APSA sites (many agricultural), 19 UST sites, 9 CalARP facilities.
