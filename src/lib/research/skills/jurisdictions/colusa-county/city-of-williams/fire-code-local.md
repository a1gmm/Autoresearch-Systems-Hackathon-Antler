## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

**Williams Fire Protection Authority**
810 E Street, Williams, CA 95987
Phone: (530) 473-2269
Fire Chief: Jeff Gilbert
Website: https://www.williamsfireprotectionauthority.org/

The Williams Fire Protection Authority is a combination (career/volunteer) department created in 1994 through a **Joint Powers Agreement (JPA)** between the City of Williams and the Williams Fire Protection District. It serves the city and surrounding unincorporated area. It is the AHJ for fire code, fire prevention permits, and fire inspections within city limits — not a city department.

## Municipal Code Chapter

Fire code adoption in Williams is done via Williams Fire Protection Authority ordinance (separate JPA agency), not a chapter of the City of Williams municipal code. The city code (Municode, library.municode.com/ca/williams) does not contain a fire code chapter — the authority's own ordinances govern.

## Fire Permits

Fire permits (including construction permits, operational permits for hazmat storage, spray booths, high-piled storage, etc.) are issued by the **Williams Fire Protection Authority**, not by the City building department. ## Key Notes

- GOTCHA: The AHJ is the Williams Fire Protection Authority (JPA entity), not a city fire department. All fire-code submittals and permit applications go to the authority, not City Hall.
- The authority issues both city and county resolutions under its JPA, so Ordinance 25-01 covers the unincorporated area served by the Williams FPD as well as the city.
