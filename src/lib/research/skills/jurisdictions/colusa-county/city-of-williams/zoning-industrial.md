## Code Architecture

The 2010-30 General Plan and implementing zoning code replaced the traditional M-1/M-2 industrial designation system with two industrial-character districts:

| Old System | New Character-Based District | Code Abbreviation |
|---|---|---|
| Light Industrial (M-1 equivalent) | Business Park | B-P |
| Heavy/General Industrial (M-2 equivalent) | Industrial | IN |

## Business Park District (B-P)

**Purpose:** Industrial and business campus development; planned environment with higher development standards.

**Intended uses:**
- Uses traditionally designated "light" industrial
- Offices and warehousing where operational activities occur mostly indoors
- Operations with a heightened appearance and quality development standard

**Development standards (from Design Review Manual 2016, adopted Sept 2012):**
- Building height: up to 3 stories
- Common green space set-aside: 20% minimum
- Floor area ratio (FAR): approximately 0.88 (example: ~172,500 sq ft building on a 4.5-acre site)
- Campus-like setting with ample landscaping and buffering
- Location: West and East Williams business park areas (per Design Review Manual)

**Notes:** Outdoor operational activities should be minimal; indoor-focused industrial uses preferred.

## Industrial District (IN)

**Purpose:** Larger-scale and more intensive industrial uses; accommodates a broad assortment of one-story industrial developments.

**Intended uses:**
- Manufacturing uses
- Outdoor operations
- Outdoor storage
- Larger footprint, more intensive industrial activities

**Development standards (from Design Review Manual 2016):**
- Building height: primarily one-story
- Green space: minimum 10% for perimeter bufferyard treatments
- Bufferyard and site design standards established based on visibility and proximity
- Location: East Williams industrial area

**Notes:** More permissive outdoor operations than B-P; bufferyard requirements scale with proximity to sensitive uses.
