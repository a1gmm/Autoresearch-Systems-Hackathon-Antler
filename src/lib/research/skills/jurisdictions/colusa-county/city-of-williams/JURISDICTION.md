## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air — Colusa County APCD controls

- **Air district:** Colusa County Air Pollution Control District (APCD) — the local AQM authority for all of Colusa County, including the City of Williams. No separate city-level air program.
- Authority to Construct (ATC) and Permit to Operate (PTO) required before installing or modifying any stationary source of air contaminants — Rule 400 governs permits.
- District office: 100 Sunrise Blvd #F, Colusa, CA 95932; (530) 458-0590.
- The City of Williams lies within the **Sacramento Valley Air Basin (SVAB)**.
- Source: https://www.countyofcolusaca.gov/461/Air-Pollution-Control-District
- Source: https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/colusa-county-air-pollution-control-district

### CUPA — Colusa County Environmental Health

- **Not its own CUPA.** The City of Williams is served by the **Colusa County Environmental Health** division, which holds the single county-wide CUPA certification.
- Programs: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generator, Underground Storage Tank (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Tank (APSA), California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP).
- Submissions via CERS: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/ and county portal: colusacountyca.viewpointcloud.com
- CUPA address: 547 Market Street, Colusa, CA 95932
- Selected fee schedule (from county EH fee schedule PDF):
  - Business Plan New Application: $336/facility
  - Business Plan Annual: $168/facility
  - Hazardous Generator Small (<500 lb): $196/year
  - Hazardous Generator Medium (501–2,000 lb): $252/year
  - Hazardous Generator Large (>2,000 lb): $280/year
  - UST Operating/Existing Permit (1 tank): $448/year
  - APSA/AST Permit (per site): $224/year
  - CalARP (state surcharge): see CalEPA fee schedule; county fee: $1,120
  - Farm Business Plans and farm HazWaste: local/state exemptions apply
- Source: https://www.countyofcolusaca.gov/426/Hazardous-Materials-Waste-CUPA
- Source: https://www.countyofcolusaca.gov/727/Environmental-Health-Fees

### Wastewater — City POTW; RWQCB Central Valley Region regulates

- **POTW:** City of Williams Wastewater Treatment Plant — owned and operated by the City of Williams Public Works Department (810 E Street; (530) 473-2519).
- Treated effluent discharged to **Salt Creek** (tributary to Powell Slough and Colusa Basin Drain, Sacramento River Watershed).
- Regulated under RWQCB Central Valley Region General Order R5-2017-0085-008 (NOA issued 19 June 2020, effective 1 August 2020). Replacement General Order R5-2023-0025 issued October 2023; new NOA for Williams not yet issued as of 2024.
- Key NPDES effluent limits (from NOA R5-2017-0085-008):
  - Ammonia Nitrogen (as N): 0.84 mg/L avg monthly / 1.9 mg/L avg weekly
  - BOD5: 10 mg/L avg monthly / 15 mg/L avg weekly
  - BOD5 and TSS percent removal: ≥85% average monthly
  - Total Coliform: ≤2.2 MPN/100 mL (7-day median); ≤23 MPN/100 mL (any 30-day period); ≤240 MPN/100 mL (any time)
- 2024 enforcement action: ACL Order R5-2024-0510 — 57 serious + 7 non-serious violations (Jan 2021–Dec 2023); $192,000 liability resolved via compliance project (WWTP upgrades: chemical treatment, tertiary filter upgrades, source control measures).
- Industrial pretreatment program: [VERIFY] — municipal code Chapter 13.08 governs sewer use. The city operates its own POTW; whether a formal IWP program is required depends on whether Significant Industrial Users (SIUs) are present. No SIU program evidence found online.
- Sewer/water service: Contact Finance Dept at City Hall, 810 E Street; (530) 473-2948.
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/rwqcb5/board_decisions/adopted_orders/colusa/r5-2024-0510_settle.pdf

### Zoning — Industrial districts B-P and IN

- **Zoning code:** Title 17, City of Williams Code of Ordinances (available via Municode). Updated with the 2010-30 General Plan adopted by Ordinance 194-12 (June 20, 2012).
- The 2010-30 General Plan introduced a **character-based code** replacing traditional M-1/M-2 designations with:
  - **B-P (Business Park Zoning District):** Light industrial / business campus uses; offices, warehousing, and indoor-operational uses; up to 3-story buildings; 20% common green space; FAR ~0.88; located in West and East Williams business park areas.
  - **IN (Industrial Zoning District):** Larger-scale and more intensive industrial uses; manufacturing, outdoor operations, and outdoor storage allowed; 1-story industrial developments; minimum 10% green space; located in East Williams industrial area.
- Conditional use permits (CUPs) administer uses that may have compatibility impacts in both zones. [VERIFY specific permitted vs. conditional use lists in Title 17 chapters]

## Notes

- GOTCHA: fire protection in Williams is provided through a JPA (Williams Fire Protection Authority) jointly run by the City and the Williams Fire Protection District, not a standalone city fire department.
- The City of Williams does NOT run its own CUPA — hazmat/hazwaste/UST/APSA/CalARP are handled by the Colusa County Environmental Health CUPA.
- GOTCHA: Williams WWTP received a 2024 ACL enforcement order (R5-2024-0510) for BOD/ammonia exceedances 2021–2023; WWTP upgrades are underway. Monitor compliance project progress.
- GOTCHA: Williams zoning uses B-P and IN district codes (not traditional M-1/M-2) under the 2010-30 character-based code.
- Air permitting: all stationary source permits go to Colusa County APCD, not any city agency.
