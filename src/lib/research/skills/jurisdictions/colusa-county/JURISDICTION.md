## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air

- **Colusa County Air Pollution Control District (CCAPCD)** is the sole local air district covering the entire county (unincorporated area plus Cities of Colusa and Williams).
- Address: 100 Sunrise Blvd., Suite F, Colusa, CA 95932 | Phone: (530) 458-0590 | Burn Line: (530) 458-0581 | Email: ccair@countyofcolusaca.gov
- Programs: stationary source permits, agricultural burn permits (paper and online via burnpermit.fire.ca.gov for SRA burns effective 2026-05-01), air toxics AB 2588 reporting, air quality monitoring.
- CCAPCD is an independent district; it does NOT share jurisdiction with YSAQMD or Sacramento AQMD.
- Source: https://www.countyofcolusaca.gov/461/Air-Pollution-Control-District | https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/colusa-county-air-pollution-control-district

### CUPA

- **Colusa County Environmental Health** (within Development Services Department) is the single CUPA for the **entire county including Cities of Colusa and Williams**. There are no participating agencies or city-run CUPAs.
- Programs: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generator, Underground Storage Tank (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP).
- Facility inventory (approximate): 328 HMBP sites, 139 hazardous waste generators, 56 APSA sites (many at farms), 19 UST sites, 9 CalARP facilities.
- HMBP and UST reporting via CERS: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/ | UST permit applications via online portal: colusacountyca.viewpointcloud.com
- Address: 547 Market Street, Colusa, CA 95932
- **Key fee examples** (from county CUPA page): UST Operating/Existing (one tank) $448; New Business Plan $336; Large Hazardous Generator $280; CalARP $1,120.
- Source: https://www.countyofcolusaca.gov/426/Hazardous-Materials-Waste-CUPA

### Fire

- **Unincorporated county:** No consolidated county fire department. Served by independent fire protection districts: Sacramento River FPD, Glenn-Colusa FPD (crosses into Glenn County), Arbuckle-College City FPD, Maxwell FPD, Princeton FPD, Williams FPD, and others. Each district is its own AHJ for fire code within its area.
- **City of Colusa:** City of Colusa Fire Department (combination paid/volunteer) — https://cityofcolusa.com/fire-department/. ### Zoning (Unincorporated County)

- **Governing code:** Colusa County Zoning Code, adopted August 26, 2014, codified as Chapter 44 of the County Code (Code Publishing: https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/ColusaCounty/#!/ColusaCounty44.html). Zoning maps updated October 24, 2024.
- **Industrial zones (Article 44-2, Section 44-2.50):** Three industrial zone designations — M-1 (Light Industrial), M-2 (Heavy Industrial), RD (Research and Development). FAR: M-1 and M-2 = 0.6; RD = 0.75. Primary structure max height: M-1/RD = 50 ft, M-2 = 100 ft.
- **Permits for industrial uses:** Manufacturing (Light) = P (permitted by right) in M-1, M-2, RD; Manufacturing (Heavy) = U (Use Permit) in M-1, P in M-2, U in RD; Warehousing/Wholesaling/Distribution = P in M-1 and M-2, M (Minor Use Permit) in RD.
- Zoning map and GIS: https://www.countyofcolusaca.gov/612/Zoning-Code
- Source: Colusa County Zoning Code PDF (DocumentCenter/View/5218) | https://www.countyofcolusaca.gov/612/Zoning-Code

## Notes

- Colusa County has only two incorporated cities: **Colusa** (county seat) and **Williams**. Arbuckle, Maxwell, Princeton, and Stonyford are unincorporated communities.
- GOTCHA: neither the City of Colusa nor the City of Williams runs its own CUPA — both are covered by the single Colusa County Environmental Health CUPA.
- GOTCHA: Colusa County has its own APCD (CCAPCD) — do not confuse with YSAQMD (Yolo-Solano) or SMAQMD (Sacramento) which do not cover Colusa County.
- There is no consolidated county fire department; the unincorporated county is a patchwork of independent fire protection districts (some, like Glenn-Colusa FPD, cross the county line into Glenn County).
- The City of Colusa WWTP at 0.7 mgd is a small system; it does not hold a federally-required pretreatment program (5 mgd threshold) but may have local sewer use ordinance provisions — [VERIFY with city utilities].
