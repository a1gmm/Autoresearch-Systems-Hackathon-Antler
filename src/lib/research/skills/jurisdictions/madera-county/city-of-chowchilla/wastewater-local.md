## POTW Operator

**City of Chowchilla Public Works Department, Wastewater Division** owns and operates the municipal sewer collection system and treatment plant.

- Contact: (559) 665-8615 ext. 789
- After-hours emergencies: (559) 647-9138

## Treatment Facility

- **Facility:** City of Chowchilla Wastewater Treatment Facility (WWTF)
- **Location:** 15750 Avenue 24½, Chowchilla, CA 93610
- **Design capacity:** 1.0 MGD
- **Infrastructure:** 4 lift stations; 37 miles of collection lines
- **Primary function:** Receives, treats, and disposes of residential and commercial wastewater. The facility is monitored by multiple State of California regulatory agencies.

## NPDES Permit

The WWTF operates under a Waste Discharge Requirements / NPDES permit issued by the **California Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (Region 5)**. The specific order number could not be confirmed through online sources.

## Industrial Pretreatment Program

The 1.0 MGD design capacity of the WWTF is **below the EPA threshold (5 MGD) that triggers a mandatory approved pretreatment program** under 40 CFR Part 403. Chowchilla is therefore not required to maintain an EPA-approved pretreatment program.

However, the city does apply **strength-based sewer billing** for commercial/industrial users, stratified into four groups based on wastewater strength (per FY 2025-26 Master Fee Schedule, Prop 218 Study, June 2025):

| Group | Strength Category | Rate (per 100 cf, based on 80% of water used) |
|---|---|---|
| Group 1 | Extra-Low Strength | $3.04 |
| Group 2 | Low Strength | $3.45 |
| Group 3 | Medium Strength | $3.73 |
| Group 4 | High Strength | $7.37 |

Debt service surcharges (same basis):
- Group 1: $0.09; Group 2: $0.11; Group 3: $0.11; Group 4: $0.31

**What this means for industrial users:** A facility discharging high-strength wastewater (Group 4) is billed at more than twice the Extra-Low rate. Whether a formal Industrial User Permit (IUP) or local discharge limits apply beyond billing classification is not confirmed online.
