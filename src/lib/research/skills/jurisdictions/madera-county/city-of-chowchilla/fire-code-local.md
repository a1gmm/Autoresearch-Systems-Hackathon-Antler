## Fire AHJ

**Chowchilla Fire Department** (municipal department) is the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) within city limits. The department operates as a combination volunteer corps with full-time Battalion Chief and Fire Inspector positions.

## Adopted Fire Code

Fire code provisions are contained in **Chowchilla Municipal Code Title 15** (Building and Construction Codes). The specific chapter within Title 15 that adopts the California Fire Code (CFC) edition and any local amendments is not machine-readable from the Municode platform; the adopted CFC edition and adopting ordinance number could not be confirmed online.

[VERIFY — Contact AHJ or City Clerk for the current adopted CFC edition (likely 2022 CFC, effective statewide 2023), adopting ordinance number, and local amendments chapter within Title 15.]

State minimum: California adopts the International Fire Code on a triennial cycle via the California Building Standards Commission. The 2022 California Fire Code (based on IFC 2021) became mandatory statewide on January 1, 2023.

## Fire Inspection Requirements

Per the FY 2025-26 Master Fee Schedule (Council Resolution #40-25, June 24, 2025):

- Annual fire inspections are required for all businesses located in Chowchilla (some exceptions apply — contact Fire Department).
- **Fire Inspection Fee:** $75.00 flat fee (includes initial inspection; additional $75.00/hour for inspections exceeding 2 hours).
- **Fire Sprinkler Permit fees:**
  - 0–2,749 sq ft: $275.00
  - 2,750–10,000 sq ft: $0.10/sq ft
  - 10,001–100,000 sq ft: $0.06/sq ft
  - Over 100,001 sq ft: $0.03/sq ft
- **Combined Fire Department & Community Development Annual Inspection:** $225.00
- **Fire Damage Repair Permit:** $491.00 minimum (worksheet-based calculation).
