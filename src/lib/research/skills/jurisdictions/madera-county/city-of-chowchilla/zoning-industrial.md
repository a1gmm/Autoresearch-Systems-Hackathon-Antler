## Industrial Zone Districts

Two industrial zone districts exist. Use permissions are governed by the Land Use Tables in Chapter 18.08, Section 18.08.030.

### I-L: Light Industrial Zone (Chapter 18.32)

**Purpose (§ 18.32.010):** Provide areas for manufacturing, wholesale, and storage activities primarily conducted inside buildings that will not be detrimental to adjacent commercial or residential neighborhoods by reason of noise, smoke, odor, dust, vibrations, fumes, glare or gas.

**Key development standards:**
- Minimum site area: 20,000 sq ft (smaller lots allowed via CUP per Ch. 18.80)
- Minimum lot frontage: 60 ft
- Front/street-side setback: 15 ft minimum
- Rear/interior side setback: 10 ft minimum
- Maximum height: 75 ft (up to 100 ft if set back ≥200 ft from any lot line)
- Block wall ≥7 ft required along any side/rear lot line abutting residential or PF zone
- No minimum open space requirement
- Landscaping required per Ch. 18.52; minimum 10 ft from street-frontage lot line
- Open storage must be screened by solid wall/fence ≥7 ft, not visible above fence

**Signage (I-L):** No limit on number of building signs; 1 freestanding sign per frontage maximum; freestanding sign area: 1 sf per lineal ft of street-frontage property line OR 100 sf/acre of site (whichever greater), max 600 sf; max height 20 ft.

**Discharge note (§ 18.32.150.E):** No solid or liquid waste shall be discharged into a natural watercourse, nor into a public or private sewage disposal system except in compliance with RWQCB Central Valley Region regulations and city ordinances.

### I-H: Heavy Industrial Zone (Chapter 18.34)

**Purpose (§ 18.34.010):** Provide areas for manufacturing, wholesale, and storage activities conducted inside and/or outside buildings that will not be detrimental to adjacent neighborhoods by reason of noise, smoke, odor, dust, vibrations, fumes, glare or gas.

**Key development standards:**
- Minimum site area: 20,000 sq ft (smaller lots allowed via CUP per Ch. 18.80)
- Minimum lot frontage: 100 ft
- Front/street-side setback: 15 ft minimum
- Rear/interior side setback: 10 ft minimum
- Maximum height: 75 ft; up to 100 ft if set back ≥200 ft from every lot line; up to 150 ft if set back ≥300 ft from every lot line
- Block wall ≥7 ft required along any side/rear lot line abutting residential or PF zone
- No minimum open space requirement
- Landscaping required per Ch. 18.52; minimum 15 ft from street-frontage lot line (vs. 10 ft in I-L)
- Open storage: same screened-fence requirement as I-L

**Signage (I-H):** Same standards as I-L.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process (Chapter 18.80)

CUPs are required for uses designated "C" in the land use tables, and for certain development standards variances (e.g., lot size below 20,000 sf minimum). The CUP chapter is at § 18.80 of Title 18.

**Planning authority:** Community & Economic Development Department / Planning Commission.

**CUP fee (FY 2025-26):** $1,350.00 (standard CUP application fee, per Master Fee Schedule). Modification/Revocation of CUP or Variance: $950.00.

**Deposit requirements (projects requiring public hearing, not CEQA-exempt):** $3,000 minimum deposit; projects not CEQA-exempt: $10,000 minimum deposit.

## Industrial Specific Plan (In Progress)

A major **Industrial Specific Plan (ISP)** covering approximately 2,893 acres is in progress. An NOP for the EIR was issued November 2024 (SCH# 2024110151). The ISP area is bounded by Mariposa Ave (N), SR 99 (E), SR 152 (S), Road 15 (W), and is planned for e-commerce, distribution, and manufacturing. Upon adoption, the ISP may establish additional or modified zone districts and development standards superseding Title 18 within the ISP area. Status: CEQA/EIR in preparation as of mid-2026.
