## CUPA Authority

**City of Chowchilla is NOT its own CUPA.** All Unified Hazardous Materials Program (UHMP) functions within Chowchilla city limits are administered by the **Madera County Environmental Health Division**, the CalEPA-designated countywide CUPA.

- CUPA Agency: Madera County Environmental Health Division
- Phone: (559) 675-7823
- County fee schedule effective 07/01/25 — contact for current fee amounts.

## Programs Administered by Madera County CUPA

The county CUPA administers all six unified program elements:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — Facilities meeting threshold quantities must submit via California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) at cers.calepa.ca.gov. Thresholds: 55 gal liquid / 500 lb solid / 200 cf compressed gas.
2. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — Permits, inspections, and release reporting.
3. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — Facilities storing ≥1,320 gal aggregate petroleum above ground.
4. **Hazardous Waste Generator & Onsite Treatment** — Including tiered permits for onsite treatment.
5. **CalARP (California Accidental Release Prevention)** — Risk management plans for facilities subject to 40 CFR Part 68 and Cal OES requirements.
6. **Hazardous Waste Storage & Disposal** coordination.

## Local Hazmat Ordinance

No separate City of Chowchilla hazardous materials ordinance has been identified online. The county CUPA's authority derives from Madera County's designation by CalEPA and the applicable state statutes (Health & Safety Code Chapters 6.95, 6.67, 6.7, 6.71).
