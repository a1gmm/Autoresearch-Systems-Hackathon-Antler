## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air

- **District:** San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution Control District (SJVAPCD / Valley Air District) — Madera County is one of the eight counties in the San Joaquin Valley Air Basin served by SJVAPCD. No separate city-level air authority.
- City does not adopt its own air rules; all Permits to Operate, BACT determinations, and New Source Review go through SJVAPCD.
- Source: https://www.valleyair.org/about

### CUPA (Unified Hazardous-Materials Program)

- **Not its own CUPA.** Chowchilla is served by the **Madera County Environmental Health Division** (CalEPA-designated countywide CUPA).
- Programs administered: Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), Hazardous Waste Generator & Onsite Treatment, CalARP (accidental release prevention for RMP-covered facilities).
- Businesses submit HMBPs through California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) at cers.calepa.ca.gov. Threshold quantities: 55 gal liquid / 500 lb solid / 200 cf compressed gas.
- County EH fee schedule effective 07/01/25 — contact (559) 675-7823 for fee amounts.
- Source: https://www.maderacounty.com/government/community-economic-development-department/divisions/environmental-health-division/certified-unified-program-agency-cupa
- Source (HMBP): https://www.maderacounty.com/government/community-economic-development-department/divisions/environmental-health-division/certified-unified-program-agency-cupa/hazardous-materials-business-plans-hmbp

### Stormwater

- **MS4 permit:** City of Chowchilla is a Phase II Small MS4 permittee under State Water Board Order No. 2013-0001-DWQ (NPDES General Permit No. S000004), applicable to the Chowchilla CA Urban Cluster (~18,720 population). Must implement six minimum control measures.
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/water_issues/programs/stormwater/phase_ii_municipal.html

### Zoning (Industrial)

- **Code:** Chowchilla Municipal Code Title 18 — Zoning Ordinance, adopted October 9, 2018, effective November 8, 2018.
- Two industrial zone districts: **I-L (Light Industrial)** Ch. 18.32 and **I-H (Heavy Industrial)** Ch. 18.34.
- Key industrial uses by right (I-L): light manufacturing facility (P), warehouse/distribution center (P), research & development facility (P), agricultural products packing/processing (C requires CUP).
- Key industrial uses by right (I-H): heavy manufacturing facility (P), light manufacturing (P), warehouse/distribution (P), trucking/transportation terminal (C requires CUP), junkyard/salvage (C requires CUP), meat packing/slaughterhouse (C), hazardous waste facility (C).
- CUP process governed by Chapter 18.80 (Conditional Use Permits); planning authority is Community & Economic Development Department.
- **Industrial Specific Plan (in progress):** ~2,893-acre expansion covering e-commerce, distribution, and manufacturing; NOP for EIR issued November 2024 (SCH 2024110151). Boundaries: Mariposa Ave (N), SR 99 (E), SR 152 (S), Road 15 (W).
- Source: https://cityofchowchilla.org/DocumentCenter/View/803/Chowchilla-Zoning-Ordinance
- Source (ISP CEQA): https://ceqanet.lci.ca.gov/2024110151

## Notes

- Chowchilla runs its own fire and building functions but relies on Madera County Environmental Health Division for all CUPA/hazardous-materials regulation.
- SJVAPCD controls all air permits — no city-level air rule.
- The Industrial Specific Plan (~2,893 acres) is a significant in-progress land-use action that may add new zone districts or modify I-L/I-H standards upon adoption.
- The 1.0 MGD WWTF is below the EPA mandatory pretreatment threshold; formal pretreatment program status should be [VERIFIED] with Region 5 RWQCB.
