## Overview: Two Regulatory Tracks

Madera County has no county-operated sewer collection system. Industrial wastewater falls into one of two tracks depending on location:

1. **Unincorporated county** — on-site wastewater treatment systems (OWTS / septic). Regulated by Madera County Environmental Health Division under county code.
2. **City of Madera limits** — industrial dischargers connect to the City of Madera POTW, regulated by the City's sewer use ordinance and the pretreatment program administered by the City of Madera Public Works Department.

## County Code (Unincorporated Areas)

**Title 13 (Water and Sewers), Part II (Sewage Disposal)** of the Madera County Code of Ordinances governs on-site sewage disposal in the unincorporated county. Chapter structure includes provisions for septic system standards, well standards (Ch. 13.52), and county-wide sewer area regulations (Ch. 13.45, 13.89).

Industrial dischargers in unincorporated areas typically rely on septic/OWTS approved by the Environmental Health Division, or connect to a city system if adjacent. No county sewer use ordinance with industrial pretreatment limits was found online [VERIFY via Municode Title 13 full text].

## Stormwater Note

Industrial facilities in unincorporated Madera County discharging stormwater must obtain coverage under the statewide **Industrial General Permit (IGP)** via the State Water Board's SMARTS system. Madera County is not a Phase I MS4 permittee; the county and small regulated municipalities operate under the statewide Phase II Small MS4 General Permit [VERIFY order number with CV RWQCB].
