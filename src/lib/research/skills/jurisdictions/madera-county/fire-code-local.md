## Fire Authority / AHJ

- **Madera County Fire Department** — operated under a cooperative agreement with **CAL FIRE** (career staffing from CAL FIRE). AHJ for all unincorporated county land.
- **County Fire Marshal's Office** (Fire Division, Community & Economic Development Department) — plan review and inspection for unincorporated areas; implements State Fire Marshal standards and county Board of Supervisors adoptions.
- Contact: firemarshal@maderacounty.com | (559) 377-4879
- Fire Division page: https://www.maderacounty.com/government/community-economic-development-department/divisions/fire-division

## Burn Permits

Hazard-reduction burning in the unincorporated county requires a **CAL FIRE burn permit** (not a county permit). Available exclusively online: https://burnpermit.fire.ca.gov/
