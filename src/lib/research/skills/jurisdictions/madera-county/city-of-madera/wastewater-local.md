## Regulatory Permit

The WWTF discharges treated effluent to **evaporation/percolation ponds** (land disposal), not to surface waters. It is therefore regulated under **Waste Discharge Requirements (WDRs)**, not a standard NPDES permit.

- **Order: R5-2022-0074** — Waste Discharge Requirements, adopted **9 December 2022** by the California Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (RWQCB Region 5 — Fresno Office).
- Prior orders: 95-046 (1995), 89-051 (1989), 72-272 (1972).
- Source PDF: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/centralvalley/board_decisions/adopted_orders/madera/r5-2022-0074_wdrs.pdf
- RWQCB Region 5 contact: 1685 "E" St., Fresno, CA 93706-2007

## Effluent Limits (Table 11, Order R5-2022-0074)

Limits apply to discharge at EFF-001 to the evaporation/percolation ponds:

| Constituent | Monthly Average | Daily Maximum | Monthly Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| BOD5 | 40 mg/L | 80 mg/L | — |
| TSS | 40 mg/L | 80 mg/L | — |
| Total Nitrogen | — | — | 10 mg/L |

Additional operational limits:
- **Flow**: ≤7.0 mgd monthly average daily dry weather flow at INF-001.
- **pH**: Wastewater in any unlined pond shall not be <6.0 or >10.0.
- **Dissolved Oxygen**: Upper 1 ft of any treatment/storage pond shall not be <1.0 mg/L for three consecutive sampling events.
- **TDS (Salinity) Action Level**: 500 mg/L annual average — triggers Salinity Action Level Report obligation if exceeded.

Prohibition categories in the WDRs pretreatment provisions (Order R5-2022-0074, Section I) that must be reflected in the local ordinance include:
- Wastes creating fire or explosion hazard in collection system or treatment works
- Wastes causing corrosive structural damage to treatment works
- Solid/viscous wastes causing obstruction to flow in sewers
- High-volume or high-strength BOD/pollutant loads that would cause inhibition or disruption of treatment
- Heat in amounts that inhibit biological activity in the treatment works
- Petroleum oil, non-biodegradable cutting oil, or mineral oil origin products causing interference or pass-through
- Trucked or hauled wastewater or septage (except at predesignated points)

## GOTCHA Notes

- The WWTF uses land disposal (evaporation/percolation ponds) — this is **not a surface water discharge**. There is no NPDES permit number. The facility does not hold a public-facing NPDES number; all permit language is in WDRs Order R5-2022-0074.
- Because the WWTF does not discharge to surface water, industrial users seeking to understand "what triggers NPDES?" should look at the state IGP for stormwater at their own facility, not the WWTF permit.
- The pretreatment program covers 7 non-categorical IUs. If a new industrial user falls under a federal categorical standard (e.g., metal finishers, electroplaters), the IPP must be updated — contact RWQCB Region 5 and the City.
