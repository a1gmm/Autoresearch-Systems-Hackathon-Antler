## Industrial Zone Designations

The City of Madera uses the **IP (Industrial Park)** designation as its primary industrial zone — confirmed in the city code at amlegal node 0-0-0-8077. The city does not use traditional "M-1 / M-2" designations based on available online evidence.

| Zone Code | Name | Code Reference |
|---|---|---|
| IP | Industrial Park | § 0-0-0-8077, Title 10, Chapter 3 |
| PD | Planned Development | § 0-0-0-7154, Title 10, Chapter 3 |

## IP (Industrial Park) Zone

- **Code location**: § 0-0-0-8077 in the City of Madera Code of Ordinances (American Legal Publishing).
- **Purpose**: The IP zone is designed for industrial park-type development, typically including light manufacturing, warehousing, research and development, and related uses conducted in a park-like setting.

## GOTCHA Notes

- The City of Madera code uses "IP" (Industrial Park) rather than M-1/M-2. Do not apply Madera County's M-1/M-2 zoning rules (from Title 18 of the Madera County Code) to city parcels.
