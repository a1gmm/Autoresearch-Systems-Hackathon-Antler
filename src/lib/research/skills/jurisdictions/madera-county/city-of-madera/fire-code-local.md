## AHJ — Authority Having Jurisdiction

**Madera City Fire Department**
- Operates under a CAL FIRE strategic partnership; city retains policy oversight via the City Council.
- Is the AHJ for all fire code enforcement, plan review, and fire prevention inspections within city limits.
- Prevention Bureau contact: fireinfo@madera.gov / Non-emergency: (559) 661-5499
- Source: https://www.madera.gov/emergency-protective-services/fire-department/

## Adopted Fire Code Edition

The City of Madera has adopted the **2022 California Fire Code (CFC)** by reference, codified at:

> **§ 9-1.06 ADOPTION OF THE CALIFORNIA FIRE CODE** — City of Madera Code of Ordinances, Article 1 of Chapter 9 (Building Regulations).

- The 2022 CFC incorporates the **2021 International Fire Code (IFC)** by reference with California amendments.
- Source: https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/madera/latest/madera_ca/0-0-0-10423

**Transition note (2025 CFC):** The City has adopted the 2025 California Building Standards Code effective January 1, 2026. Permit applications submitted on or after 2026-01-01 are subject to the 2025 CBS Codes (which include the 2025 CFC). [VERIFY with City Building Division that the 2025 CFC is fully in effect for fire permits — contact buildingpermits@madera.gov or (559) 661-5440.]
- Source: https://www.madera.gov/business-building-development/building-development/
