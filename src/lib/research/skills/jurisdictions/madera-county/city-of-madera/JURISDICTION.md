## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air

- **Controlling district: San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution Control District (SJVAPCD / Valley Air District)** — the APCD covers all of Madera County and seven other San Joaquin Valley counties. The City of Madera has no separate local air permitting authority.
- Stationary sources in city limits require an Authority to Construct (ATC) from the Valley Air District before construction, and a Title V Operating Permit if a "major source" under CAA.
- Permit-Exempt Equipment Registration (PEER) program available for qualifying small equipment.
- Source: https://www.valleyair.org/permitting
- Source: https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/san-joaquin-valley-air-pollution-control-district

### CUPA (Unified Program)

- **Not its own CUPA** — served by the **Madera County Environmental Health Division**, CalEPA-designated CUPA for all of Madera County (countywide; the City of Madera has no separate CUPA).
- Programs administered: Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generator (RCRA/non-RCRA), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), Underground Storage Tanks (UST), CalARP/Risk Management Plan (RMP), and Conditionally Exempt/Permit-by-Rule waste treatment.
- HMBP reporting thresholds: ≥55 gal liquid / ≥500 lb solid / ≥200 cu ft compressed gas. All HMBPs submitted via CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) since Jan 1, 2013.
- Fees paid to Madera County Environmental Health Division; see cupa-local.md for fee schedule.
- Source: https://www.maderacounty.com/government/community-economic-development-department/divisions/environmental-health-division/certified-unified-program-agency-cupa

- **AHJ: Madera City Fire Department** — municipal department operating under a CAL FIRE strategic partnership; City Council retains policy oversight. AHJ within all city limits.
- Adopted fire code: **2022 California Fire Code** (CFC) — codified at § 9-1.06 of the City of Madera Code of Ordinances (Title 9, Article 1). The 2022 CFC incorporates the 2021 International Fire Code by reference.

### Wastewater

- **POTW: City of Madera — Madera Wastewater Treatment Facility (WWTF)**, located at 13048 Road 21½, Madera, CA 93637 (approx. 5.4 miles southwest of city center).
- Regulated by the **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB Region 5)** under Waste Discharge Requirements Order **R5-2022-0074** (adopted 9 December 2022). The WWTF discharges to evaporation/percolation ponds (land disposal, not surface water), so it does not hold a standard NPDES permit.
- Facility-wide flow limit: **7.0 mgd** (monthly average dry weather). Expansion to 10.1 mgd requires a new RWD and CEQA compliance.
- **Industrial Pretreatment Program (IPP)** in place; approved 23 September 1983 (40 CFR Part 403). As of Order R5-2022-0074, seven industrial users (IUs) — all non-categorical — are permitted; current IU permits valid through 31 December 2025.
- Industrial sewer connection/discharge inquiries: Public Works / Utility Billing (559) 661-5459; utilitybilling@madera.gov.
- Sewer rates set by Resolution 22-102 (effective July 20, 2022; first rate adjustment August 1, 2022).
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/centralvalley/board_decisions/adopted_orders/madera/r5-2022-0074_wdrs.pdf
- Source: https://www.madera.gov/living-in-madera/utilities/sewer-water-and-solid-waste-rates/

### Stormwater

- City of Madera is an MS4 permittee subject to the Statewide Phase I MS4 General Permit (or individual MS4 permit) administered by the Central Valley RWQCB. Industrial facilities within city limits must also comply with the California Industrial General Permit (IGP) for stormwater discharges.
- GOTCHA: The WWTF uses evaporation/percolation ponds — stormwater management at the treatment facility is addressed in WDRs Order R5-2022-0074 (no surface water discharge). Industrial operators must independently enroll under the IGP via SMARTS.

## Notes

- GOTCHA: The Madera City Fire Department is **not a fully standalone municipal force** — personnel are managed by CAL FIRE under a strategic partnership, while the City Council keeps policy oversight. It is still the AHJ within city limits.
- GOTCHA: The City of Madera WWTF discharges to land (evaporation/percolation ponds), not to surface water. It is regulated under WDRs, not a standard NPDES permit. The pretreatment program applies to industrial users discharging to the sewer collection system.
