## CUPA Agency

The City of Madera is **not its own CUPA**. The CalEPA-designated Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all of Madera County — including the incorporated City of Madera — is the:

**Madera County Environmental Health Division**
Community and Economic Development Department

## Programs Administered

The Madera County CUPA administers all six Unified Program elements:

| Program | Description |
|---|---|
| HMBP (Hazardous Materials Business Plan) | Required if storing ≥55 gal liquid / ≥500 lb solid / ≥200 cu ft compressed gas |
| Hazardous Waste Generator | RCRA SQG, LQG, VSQG, non-RCRA LQG |
| APSA (Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act) | Aboveground tanks storing petroleum products |
| UST (Underground Storage Tank) | Underground fuel/chemical storage tanks |
| CalARP / RMP (Risk Management Plan) | Facilities with listed chemicals above threshold quantities |
| Conditionally Exempt / PBR / Tiered Permit | Small-quantity hazardous waste treatment on-site |

## HMBP Reporting

- All Business Plans must be submitted via **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System) — mandatory since January 1, 2013.
- CERS portal: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov
- Thresholds: ≥55 gallons (liquid), ≥500 pounds (solid), ≥200 cubic feet (compressed gas) of a hazardous material at any time during the year.
- Exemption available for beverage carbonation only (CO2 for carbonated beverages).
