## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air — San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution Control District (SJVAPCD)

- Madera County lies entirely within the **San Joaquin Valley Air Basin**. Air quality permitting, Title V major-source permits, and Rule enforcement are all under the **San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution Control District (SJVAPCD / Valley Air District)**, not the county.
- SJVAPCD is a nonattainment district for ozone (8-hr) and PM2.5. Over 670 rules govern stationary sources. Madera County is a "hot spot" county under SJVAPCD wood-burning curtailment rules: Level 1 PM2.5 curtailment threshold is 12 μg/m³; Level 2 threshold is 35 μg/m³ (lowered from 20 and 65 respectively).
- Any stationary source in unincorporated Madera County requiring an Authority to Construct or Permit to Operate files with SJVAPCD, not the county.
- Source: https://www.valleyair.org/ and https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/san-joaquin-valley-air-pollution-control-district

### CUPA — Madera County Environmental Health Division

- **Madera County Environmental Health Division** (within the Community & Economic Development Department) is the CalEPA-designated **CUPA for the entire county**, including the Cities of Madera and Chowchilla. No city runs its own CUPA.
- Six CUPA programs administered: Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generator and Onsite Treatment, Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP), and Tiered Permitting.
- HMBP thresholds: ≥55 gallons liquid, ≥500 lbs solid, ≥200 ft³ compressed gas (at STP). All HMBPs submitted via **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System) since January 1, 2013.
- CalARP: Facilities handling listed regulated substances above threshold quantities (CCR Title 19, Div. 2, Ch. 4.5 tables 1–3) must submit a Risk Management Plan; CUPA conducts inspections.

### Fire — Madera County Fire Department / CAL FIRE

- **Madera County Fire Department** is operated under a cooperative agreement with **CAL FIRE**; career staffing provided by CAL FIRE. The Fire Marshal's Office (Fire Division, under Community & Economic Development) is the AHJ for unincorporated county.
- Fire prevention code: **Title 14, Part II (Fire Prevention), Chapter 14.35** of the Madera County Code of Ordinances adopts the California Fire Code with local amendments. Adopting ordinance: **Ordinance No. 702**, effective December 13, 2022. The 2022 California Fire Code (based on the 2021 IFC) is the operative edition as of that adoption [VERIFY whether the 2025 CFC has been locally adopted since].

### Wastewater — City of Madera POTW (unincorporated county: septic/OWTS)

- The **unincorporated county** has no county-operated sewer system; on-site wastewater treatment systems (OWTS / septic) are regulated by the **Madera County Environmental Health Division** under Title 13 (Water and Sewers), Part II (Sewage Disposal) of the county code.
- **Industrial dischargers within city limits** connect to the **City of Madera Wastewater Treatment Facility** (13048 Road 21½, Madera, CA 93637), a land-discharge POTW regulated by the **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CV RWQCB, Region 5)** under WDR Order R5-2022-XXXX (tentative/adopted Dec 2022; prior orders: 95-046, 89-051, 72-272). The City's pretreatment program was approved September 23, 1983 under 40 C.F.R. Part 403.
- Industrial dischargers to the City of Madera POTW must obtain an **Industrial User Permit** from the City of Madera Public Works / Sewer Division. County-code sewer provisions are in Title 13, Part II (Sewage Disposal). Specific local discharge limits [VERIFY — not published online].
- City of Chowchilla operates its own POTW separately.
- Source: https://www.madera.gov/home/departments/public-works/sewage/ and https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/centralvalley

### Stormwater — CV RWQCB / Phase II General Permit

- Madera County is **not listed as a Phase I MS4 permittee** under the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board. Small regulated MS4s in the county (including county roads and City of Madera) operate under the statewide **Phase II Small MS4 General Permit** (NPDES). ### Zoning — Title 18 (Zoning Ordinance), Industrial Zones Chapter 18.20

- Madera County Code Title 18 (Zoning). The zoning ordinance underwent a comprehensive update (Public Review Draft January 2023).
- Industrial districts use **I-L (Industrial, Light)** and **I-H (Industrial, Heavy)** designations — *not* M-1/M-2.
  - **I-L** implements the General Plan "Light Industrial (LI)" land use designation; max FAR 0.20.
  - **I-H** implements the General Plan "Heavy Industrial (HI)" land use designation; max FAR 0.20; must be located away from residential zones.
- Industrial Major uses: **CUP** in I-L, **P** (by-right) in I-H. Industrial Minor: **P** in both. Junkyards, Refuse Disposal, Storage (Restricted): **CUP** in both.
- Development standards (both I-L and I-H): 1-acre min parcel; 80% max structure coverage; 60-ft max height; front setback 25 ft, side 10 ft, rear 20 ft.
- Source: Madera County Zoning Ordinance Update (January 2023 Public Review Draft), Chapter 18.20; https://library.municode.com/ca/madera_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT18ZO
