## Industrial District Designations

Madera County uses **I-L** and **I-H** — *not* M-1/M-2. The legacy M-zone designations do not appear in the updated ordinance.

| Zone Symbol | Full Name | General Plan Designation | Max FAR |
|------------|-----------|--------------------------|---------|
| I-L | Industrial, Light | Light Industrial (LI) | 0.20 |
| I-H | Industrial, Heavy | Heavy Industrial (HI) | 0.20 |

**Chapter: 18.20 — Industrial Zones** (within Article 2 — Zones, Allowable Uses, and Development Standards)

Source: Chapter 18.20, Madera County Zoning Ordinance Update (Jan. 2023)

## Zone Purposes

- **I-L**: Light fabrication, assembly-type manufacturing, processing, wholesale distributing, warehousing, office, and R&D facilities. Low-intensity, low-impact. Implements "Light Industrial (LI)" General Plan designation.
- **I-H**: Higher-intensity manufacturing, warehouse, and storage uses. Due to potential environmental impacts and land use conflicts, shall be located away from residential zones. Implements "Heavy Industrial (HI)" General Plan designation.

## Development Standards (Table 18.20-2)

Both I-L and I-H share the same base standards:

| Standard | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Minimum parcel area | 1 acre |
| Maximum dwelling structure coverage | 20% |
| Maximum total structure coverage | 80% |
| Maximum parcel width | 60 ft |
| Maximum building height | 120 ft |
| Length-to-width ratio (industrial uses) | 4:1 max |
| Length-to-width ratio (other uses) | 3:1 max |
| Front setback (min.) | 25 ft |
| Side setback (min.) | 10 ft |
| Rear setback (min.) | 20 ft |

Accessory structures in I-L and I-H: max height 60 ft.
Solar energy generation facilities: exempt from maximum height standards.
Non-industrial uses in industrial zones: max structure height 35 ft (unless Planning Director determines otherwise).

Source: Table 18.20-2, Chapter 18.20, Madera County Zoning Ordinance Update (Jan. 2023)

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

- CUP applications reviewed in compliance with Article 6 (Permit Processing Procedures) and Article 7 (Zoning Ordinance Administration) of Title 18.
- Uses not listed in Table 18.20-1 are reviewed by the Zoning Administrator under Section 18.04.040 (Uses Not Classified) to determine similarity and permit type; otherwise prohibited.
- By-right (P) uses require a **Zoning Clearance** (Chapter 18.74) before establishment; if construction is involved, the Building Permit serves as the Zoning Clearance.

## EHS-Relevant Performance Standards

Chapter 18.40 — Performance Standards applies to all industrial zones and covers:
- Air Emissions (Section 18.40.040)
- Compliance requirements (Section 18.40.030)

These performance standards establish operational limits for noise, odor, vibration, and air emissions from industrial uses in all zones.
