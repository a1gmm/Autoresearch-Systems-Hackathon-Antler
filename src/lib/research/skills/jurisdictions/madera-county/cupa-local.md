## Programs Administered (six statewide CUPA programs)

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP)** — businesses handling/storing hazardous materials at or above reportable quantities must submit a Business Plan via CERS.
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator and Onsite Hazardous Waste Treatment** — lower-tier regulation: Permit by Rule (PBR), Conditional Authorization (CA), and Conditionally Exempt (CE) generators regulated by the county.
3. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — installation, operation, and closure of USTs containing hazardous substances or petroleum.
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — facilities with aboveground petroleum storage above APSA thresholds.
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — facilities handling listed regulated substances (CCR Title 19, Div. 2, Ch. 4.5, tables 1–3) above threshold quantities must submit a Risk Management Plan; CUPA conducts RMP inspections.
6. **Tiered Permitting** (Hazardous Waste Onsite Treatment).

## HMBP Reporting Thresholds

| Phase | Threshold |
|-------|-----------|
| Liquids | ≥ 55 gallons |
| Solids | ≥ 500 pounds |
| Compressed gases | ≥ 200 cubic feet (at STP) |

All Business Plans submitted electronically through **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System, cers.calepa.ca.gov) — required since January 1, 2013.

## CalARP Specifics

Facilities must:
- Handle, manufacture, use, or store a listed regulated substance (CCR Title 19, Div. 2, Ch. 4.5, Tables 1–3) above threshold quantity.
- Submit a Risk Management Plan to the CUPA.
- Participate in CUPA-led inspections.

Contact for CalARP: same CUPA contact above.
