## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

Mono County has **no countywide fire department.** AHJ is fragmented across independent fire protection districts and state agency:

| Area | AHJ |
|---|---|
| Wildland / State Responsibility Area (SRA) | **CAL FIRE San Bernardino/Inyo/Mono Unit (BDU)** |
| Mammoth Lakes incorporated area | **Mammoth Lakes Fire Protection District (MLFPD)** |
| Unincorporated communities — by district | See district list below |

**Independent fire protection districts (unincorporated areas):**

| District | Service Community |
|---|---|
| Antelope Valley FPD | Antelope Valley area |
| Bridgeport FPD | Bridgeport (county seat) |
| Chalfant Valley Fire Department | Chalfant Valley |
| June Lake FPD | June Lake |
| Lee Vining FPD | Lee Vining |
| Long Valley FPD | Long Valley / Crowley Lake area |
| Mono City FPD | Mono City (near Mono Lake) |
| Paradise FPD | Paradise area |
| Wheeler Crest FPD | Wheeler Crest area |
| White Mountain FPD | White Mountain / Benton area |

Each district is its own AHJ within its service boundary. Building permit fire-code plan checks for unincorporated Mono County are handled by the Mono County Community Development Department — Building Division, coordinating with the applicable district.

Source: https://monocounty.ca.gov/sdsa · https://www.fire.ca.gov/who-we-are/units (CAL FIRE BDU)

## Adopted California Fire Code — Mammoth Lakes FPD (Primary Reference)

The **Mammoth Lakes Fire Protection District** is the largest structural fire authority in the county. It has adopted the California Fire Code through its own ordinance:

- **Ordinance No. 2022-01** — Adopted the **2022 California Fire Code** (Title 24, Part 9, based on IFC 2021), together with applicable sections of the 2022 California Building Code and 2022 California Residential Code, comprising the MLFPD Life Safety Code.

## Mono County Code — Fire-Safe Regulations (Unincorporated County)

The Mono County General Plan **Chapter 22** establishes Fire Safe Regulations for new construction within State Responsibility Areas (SRA). These supplement CAL FIRE enforcement under **Public Resources Code § 4290** and Title 14 CCR:

- **Applicability:** Construction within SRA approved after October 1, 1991.
- **Defensible space:** Reduced-fuel zones around occupied dwellings and structures.
- **Road access standards:** Road width and access specifications for emergency vehicles.
- **Emergency water supply:** References **NFPA 1142, Standard on Water Supplies for Suburban and Rural Fire Fighting, 2012 Edition** for rural areas without public water systems.
- **Roofing:** Class A roof covering requirements in SRA zones.
- **Building identification:** Signage and address standards for emergency response.

**Specific CFC adoption ordinance for unincorporated Mono County:** The Mono County Code Title 15, Chapter 15.04 covers building code adoption. A separate fire code adoption chapter has **not been confirmed** in the online code. Individual fire protection districts adopt the CFC as independent special districts. ## Permit Process — MLFPD

Fire construction permits and plan reviews are processed exclusively through:
- **Tyler Tech Mobile-Eyes portal:** https://www.mobile-eyes.com/pa_index.asp
- Permits valid for one year from issuance.
- Inspection requests submitted through the same portal.
- Renewals: prevention@mlfd.ca.gov
