## Overview

Mono County has **no countywide sewer system.** Wastewater is managed through two parallel frameworks:

1. **Onsite systems (OWTS/septic)** — predominant in the rural unincorporated county; regulated by Mono County Environmental Health under the Lahontan RWQCB-approved LAMP.
2. **Centralized POTWs** — limited to four communities with community sewer systems.

**Communities with community sewer systems:** Bridgeport, Lee Vining, June Lake, and Hilton Creek. All other areas (the vast majority of the county) rely on on-site septic systems.

Regional permitting authority: **Lahontan Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB)** — issues Waste Discharge Requirements (WDRs) to POTWs and oversees individual OWTS via the county LAMP.

### Sewer Code

MCWD's sewer regulations are codified in **MCWD Code — Chapter 11 (Sewer)**. Chapter 11 governs design, construction, alteration, use, and maintenance of public sewers, house laterals, industrial connections, liquid waste pretreatment plants, sewage pumping plants, sand and grease interceptors, and FOG (fats, oils, grease) control.

- Chapter 11 PDF (with table of contents and attachments): https://www.mcwd.dst.ca.us/files/53cc9a926/MCWD+Code+-+Chapter+11+Sewer+(with+TOC+and+Attachments).pdf

## Onsite Wastewater (OWTS / Septic) — Unincorporated County

The vast majority of Mono County parcels rely on individual septic systems. Oversight is shared:

- **Mono County Environmental Health Division** — issues OWTS permits for the unincorporated county under the county's LAMP (Local Agency Management Program) approved by the Lahontan RWQCB.
- **Lahontan RWQCB** — sets basin plan water quality objectives; may require individual WDRs for large or high-strength OWTS (flows > 10,000 gpd or problematic waste streams).
- **Contact for OWTS permits:** Mono County Environmental Health — Mammoth Lakes: (760) 924-1830; Bridgeport: (760) 932-5580.

Source: https://monohealth.com/environmental-health

## Industrial Pretreatment — Summary Guidance

For a facility considering discharge to any Mono County POTW:

1. **Mammoth Lakes area → MCWD:** Review Chapter 11 of MCWD Code; contact permits@mcwd.dst.ca.us for industrial connection and pretreatment requirements.
2. **Bridgeport → BPUD:** Contact BPUD directly; no published IWP program found.
3. **Lee Vining / June Lake / Hilton Creek:** Contact applicable district; no published IWP programs found online.
4. **All other locations:** On-site OWTS — Mono County EHD permit required; high-strength industrial waste streams may require individual Lahontan RWQCB WDR. [VERIFY — rural areas of Mono County have no POTW access]
