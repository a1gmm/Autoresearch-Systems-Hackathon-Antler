## Fire AHJ

**Mammoth Lakes Fire Protection District (MLFPD)** — an independent special district governed by an elected Board of Fire Commissioners. It is NOT a Town of Mammoth Lakes municipal department. The Prevention Division handles construction plan review, fire inspections, and operational permits.

- Website: https://mlfd.ca.gov/
- Prevention Division phone: (760) 934-2300
- Prevention Division email: prevention@mlfd.ca.gov
- Address: 3150 Main Street, Mammoth Lakes, CA 93546

## Adopted Code Edition

**Ordinance No. 2022-01** — adopted by the Board of Fire Commissioners on **November 15, 2022**; effective 30 days after passage (~December 15, 2022).

Codes adopted:

- **2022 California Fire Code** (CCR Title 24, Part 9)
- 2022 California Building Code Volumes 1 and 2 (CCR Title 24, Part 2)
- 2022 California Residential Code (CCR Title 24, Part 2.5)
- 2022 California Administrative Code (CCR Title 24, Part 1)
- 2022 California Electrical Code (CCR Title 24, Part 3)
- 2022 California Mechanical Code (CCR Title 24, Part 4)

## Key Local Amendments (Ordinance No. 2022-01, Section 3)

The MLFPD cites three local conditions justifying more restrictive standards:

- **Climatic**: Extreme cold, heavy snowfall, high winds in winter; hot/low-humidity/high-wind conditions in summer; surrounding wildland vegetation including drought-stressed timber.
- **Geological**: Seismically and volcanically active area; a significant earthquake could render the FD incapable of response — makes built-in fire protection essential.
- **Topographical**: Elevations 7,500–11,000 ft; slopes up to 60°; limited mutual aid availability; stair-climbing response time impacts.

Notable amendments include:

- **High-rise redefined at 55 feet** (rather than the state default of 75 ft) due to limited aerial equipment, inclement weather, and stair-climb response-time impacts. Mid-rise (4 stories to 55-ft floor level) also carries special requirements.
- Highway 395 designated as a CALTRANS hazmat transportation route passes through the district — hazmat incident provisions correspondingly emphasized.
- Fire investigation: fires presumed suspicious until proven otherwise; fire investigators authorized to take charge of physical evidence.
- Rescue air replenishment required for all high-rise structures (Appendix L amendment).

## Permit Process

All applications submitted through **Tyler Tech Mobile-Eyes portal**: https://www.mobile-eyes.com/pa_index.asp  
Counter, phone, and email submissions not accepted. Permits valid for one year.

Permit types include: residential/commercial construction, energy storage systems, commercial kitchen hood suppression, special events, recreational burns, wood burner/insert.

## Municipal Code Cross-Reference

The Town of Mammoth Lakes Municipal Code (Title 15 — Building and Construction) contains building-related code adoptions by the Town's own Building Division. Fire code authority rests with the independent MLFPD, not with Title 15.
