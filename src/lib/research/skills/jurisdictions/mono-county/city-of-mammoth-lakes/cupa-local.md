## Unified Program Elements Administered

Mono County Environmental Health administers all six Unified Program elements on behalf of CalEPA:

1. **HMBP** — Hazardous Materials Business Plan
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator** program (including inspections and episodic generation)
3. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)**
4. **Aboveground Storage Tanks (APSA)** — per Health & Safety Code §25270
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)**
6. **Hazardous Waste On-Site Treatment (Tiered Permitting)**

## Reporting

All HMBP and related submissions are electronic-only via **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System):

- CERS portal: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov
- Paper submissions rejected since January 1, 2012
- All HMBPs required via CERS since January 1, 2013
- UST data electronically required since January 1, 2014

## HMBP Reporting Thresholds

A business must file an HMBP if it handles, uses, or stores:
- ≥ 55 gallons of hazardous liquids
- ≥ 500 pounds of hazardous solids
- ≥ 200 cubic feet of compressed hazardous gases

HMBP must include: owner/operator information, business activities description, materials inventory, site map, and emergency contingency plan. Annual renewal required (every 365 days). Amendments due within 30 days of a major change, or within 60 days of commencing operations.
