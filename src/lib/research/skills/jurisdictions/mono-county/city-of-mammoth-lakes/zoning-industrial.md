## Industrial District Designation

A single **Industrial ("I") zone** exists in Mammoth Lakes. There is no separate heavy industrial or light industrial sub-zone; the single "I" district is intended for light industrial and limited service uses, and expressly includes provisions to facilitate relocation of nonconforming industrial/service uses from commercial zones into the designated industrial area.

**Zoning map symbol:** "I"

## Purpose / Intent (Chapter 17.28)

To reserve appropriately located areas for viable industrial uses while:
- Protecting adjacent residential and commercial areas from incompatible impacts
- Minimizing traffic congestion
- Preventing utility overloads
- Facilitating relocation of existing nonconforming industrial/service uses from commercial zones to designated industrial areas

All commercial and industrial activities (other than off-street parking) shall be conducted entirely within an enclosed structure, except as otherwise permitted by Chapter 17.28.

## Allowed Land Uses (Table 17.28.020)

| Permit Type | Example Uses |
|---|---|
| **Permitted (P)** — by right | Artisan shops, auto/vehicle sales & rental, nurseries/garden centers, food preparation, maintenance & repair services, personal services, research & development, large vehicle/equipment sales-service-rental, indoor warehousing & storage, outdoor storage, handicraft/custom manufacturing, reverse vending machines, small collection facilities |
| **Administrative Permit (A)** — staff-level approval | Caretaker housing, building materials & services, pet grooming, major vehicle repairs, construction & material yards, wholesaling & distribution, vehicle storage, large collection facilities, outdoor display & sales, parking |
| **Use Permit (U)** — Planning Commission approval | Parks & playgrounds (public), kennels & pet day care, general and heavy industry, cannabis cultivation/manufacturing/distribution/testing, transportation passenger facilities, major utilities |

## Development Standards (Chapter 17.28)

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Minimum lot area | 8,000 sq ft |
| Maximum building height | 35 feet |
| Maximum lot coverage | 100% |
| Required snow storage | 40% of parking/driveway area |
| Front setback | 20 ft (general); 0 ft on Commerce Circle |
| Side/rear setbacks | 0–10 ft depending on location |

## Use Permit (CUP) Process

Uses marked "U" in Table 17.28.020 require a **Use Permit** (functionally equivalent to a Conditional Use Permit) approved by the **Planning Commission**. Administrative permits ("A") are staff-level approvals. The permit procedures are governed by:

- **Chapter 17.84** — Administrative Permits
- **Article IV** — Land Use and Development Permit Procedures (Title 17)
