## City of San Luis Obispo as Participating Agency (PA)

The **City of San Luis Obispo Fire Department** serves as a **Unified Program Participating
Agency (PA)** for facilities within city limits. Businesses inside the city boundaries direct
HMBP/inventory submissions, hazardous-materials plan review requests, and permit
issuance for new construction / tenant improvements / hazardous-process installations to
the **City Fire Department's Hazardous Materials Coordinator** — not to the county CUPA
directly.

- City Fire Dept PA role covers: HMBP review/approval, plan review for new
  construction, tenant improvements, installation and closure of hazardous material
  processes, emergency responder training, and public information requests.  
Source: https://www.slocity.org/government/department-directory/fire-department/fire-prevention/hazardous-materials

## Programs Administered by the County CUPA

The following Unified Program elements are administered countywide (with the city Fire
Dept as PA for city-sited facilities):

| Program | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| HMBP / Hazardous Materials Handler | Review and inspection of facilities handling hazardous materials above threshold quantities |
| Cal ARP (Accidental Release Prevention) | Programs 1–4; fee varies by program tier |
| Hazardous Waste Generator | VSQG, SQG, LQG inspections; contingency plan review |
| Tiered Permitting | Permits for on-site hazardous waste treatment systems |
| Underground Storage Tanks (UST) | Facility permits, installation/modification, closure, repair |
| Aboveground Petroleum Storage Tanks (APSA) | Facility inspections; SPCC plan coordination |
| Household Hazardous Waste | Disposal program (via SLO County IWMA) |

Source: https://www.slocounty.ca.gov/departments/health-agency/public-health/environmental-health-services/all-environmental-health-services/cupa-program-hazardous-materials-waste

## Local Hazardous Materials Ordinance / Municipal Code

No city-specific hazardous materials ordinance chapter was found separate from state
law. The city Fire Department functions as a PA under the state Unified Program
(Health & Safety Code Division 20, Chapter 6.11). Local fire-prevention standards
applicable to hazardous materials storage, handling, and processes are codified in
**Municipal Code § 15.04.090** (local amendments to the California Fire Code, Part 9).  
Source: https://sanluisobispo.municipal.codes/Code/15.04.090

## CUPA Permit Portal / Submission

Hazardous Materials Business Plans and chemical inventory data are submitted through
the **California Environmental Reporting System (CERS)**:  
https://cers.calepa.ca.gov

For city-sited facilities, the submission flows to the City Fire Department PA for
initial review before county CUPA records it.

## GOTCHA

Businesses inside city limits must engage the **City Fire Department** (not the county
CUPA office) for HMBP permits, plan review, and hazmat process permits. The county
EHS remains the certified CUPA of record and handles LQG/SQG/UST/Cal ARP
inspections.
