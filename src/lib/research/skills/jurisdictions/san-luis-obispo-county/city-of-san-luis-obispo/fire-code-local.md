## Adopted Fire Code Edition

of the **San Luis Obispo Building Construction and Fire Prevention Code, 2026** via an
ordinance introduced November 18, 2025, with second reading scheduled December 2,
2025, and an effective date of **January 1, 2026**.

Prior cycle: **2022 CFC** adopted via **Ordinance No. 1723** (passed November 15, 2022),
effective January 1, 2023, as Part 9 of the Building Construction and Fire Prevention
Code, 2023. Ord. 1723 adopted CFC Appendix Chapters 4, B, and C.

## Key Local Amendments (§ 15.04.090)

### EV Charging Station Fire Sprinkler Upgrade (§ 15.04.090(Z))

Adopted with the 2025 CFC cycle to memorialize a requirement the Fire Department had
applied on a case-by-case basis for seven years:

- **Scope**: Applies to Group S-2 Occupancy parking garages that install Level 3 or
  Level 4 EV charging stations.
- **Required sprinkler density**: NFPA 13-2022 **Extra Hazard Group II (EH II)**
  design (0.40 GPM/SF) over parking spaces associated with Level 3 / Level 4
  chargers plus a **minimum 3-foot perimeter extension** beyond those spaces.
- **Hose allowance**: Areas < 1,500 SF require 250 GPM; larger areas require 500 GPM
  per EH II.
- **Fire alarm integration**: Level 3 charger installations must connect a sprinkler
  waterflow switch to the building fire alarm system or a sprinkler monitoring system.
- **Does not apply to**: Mechanical-Access enclosed parking garages (per 2022 CBC
  § 406.6.4); single-family residential; or Level 1 / Level 2 chargers.

### Wildland-Urban Interface (§ 15.04.090(NN) — repealed and replaced)

Former § 15.04.090(NN) defined WUI as "areas designated as Local Very High Fire
Hazard Severity Zones." That section was deleted with the 2025 cycle and replaced by
adoption of the **2025 California WUI Code** (Part 7 of Title 24). The new WUI Code
incorporates home-hardening (formerly CBC Ch. 7A), defensible space (Zones 0/1/2),
water supply, and fire access standards.

**FHSZ trigger**: City adopted updated Fire Hazard Severity Zone maps June 17, 2025
(effective July 17, 2025), which now include Moderate and High FHSZs in addition to
Very High FHSZs. WUI Code Chapter 6 (defensible space) applies only in the Very High
FHSZ per city interpretation.

### Sky Lanterns / Hazardous Fire Area / Tank Abandonment

Additional local amendments in § 15.04.090 (carried over from 2022 cycle):

- Sky lantern prohibition definition and regulations.
- Fire Chief authority to designate Hazardous Fire Areas.
- Tank abandonment procedures (local supplement to CFC requirements).

## Permit Types and Plan Review

Processed through the **Fire Prevention Bureau**:

- Fire sprinkler systems (new construction, tenant improvements, NFPA 13 upgrades)
- Fire alarm systems
- Hazardous materials storage / processes (coordinated with CUPA PA role — see
  cupa-local.md)
- High-piled storage (CFC Ch. 32 — operational permit required)
- Paint booths, hot work, industrial ovens, fuel dispensing (operational permits)

Fee codes from the City's **Comprehensive Fee Schedule** (FY 2025-26 edition):

- Fee #44 — Supplemental Fire Plan Review
- Fee #45 — Supplemental Fire Inspection
