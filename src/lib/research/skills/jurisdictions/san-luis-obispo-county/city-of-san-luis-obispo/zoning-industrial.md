## Industrial Zone District — Single M Zone

The City of San Luis Obispo uses **one Manufacturing zone designation** — **"M"** (not
M-1/M-2 sub-tiers). The Manufacturing (M) Zone is established in **Chapter 17.40**.

> The city does NOT have separate M-1 / M-2 zone tiers. Heavy vs. light manufacturing
> classification drives the permit-type determination (by-right vs. CUP) via Table 2-1.

- Chapter: **§§ 17.40.010 — 17.40.020**
- Development standards table: **Table 2-21: M Zone Development Standards**  
Source: https://sanluisobispo.municipal.codes/Code/17.40

## Use Classification (§ 17.156.028)

Use types relevant to industrial EHS permitting are defined in **Municipal Code
§ 17.156.028 ("M definitions")** and **Chapter 17.156** (Land Use Definitions):

**Manufacturing — Light** (§ 17.156.028):  
> "A use engaged in the manufacture, predominately from previously prepared materials,
> of finished products or parts, including processing, fabrication, assembly, and treatment
> packaging, taking place primarily within enclosed buildings and producing minimal
> impacts on nearby properties."  

Includes (by example): electronics, electronic instruments, equipment, and appliances;
brewery and alcohol production; pharmaceutical manufacturing; production apparel
manufacturing; food products manufacturing.

**Manufacturing — Heavy** (§ 17.156.028):  
> "Manufacturing of products from extracted or raw materials or recycled or secondary
> materials, or bulk storage and handling of such products and materials."

Includes (by example): biomass energy conversion; textile mills; leather and allied
products; wood products; paper manufacturing; **chemical manufacturing**; plastics and
rubber products; nonmetallic mineral products (sand, gravel, clay); **primary metal
manufacturing**; fabricated metal products; **petroleum refining**; automotive, ship,
aircraft, and heavy equipment manufacturing.

Source: https://sanluisobispo.municipal.codes/Code/17.156.028

## Use Regulations by Zone — Table 2-1

All land uses within the M zone are subject to **§ 17.10.020 — Use Regulations by Zone
(Table 2-1: Uses Allowed by Zone)**. The table uses permit-type symbols to indicate:

| Symbol | Permit Type |
|--------|------------|
| (blank / permitted) | Use allowed as a matter of right |
| DA | Director's Action (minor use permit, administrative) |
| PC | Planning Commission action (conditional use permit) |
| — | Use not allowed in this zone |

The manufacturing use classification (Light vs. Heavy) — determined from the § 17.156
definitions — identifies which row in Table 2-1 applies, which in turn specifies whether
a use is by-right, requires a Director's Action permit, or requires a Planning Commission
CUP.

> **Practical note**: Manufacturing—Light is generally the permitted / Director's Action
> category in the M zone. Manufacturing—Heavy is generally the CUP category. Exact
> symbol designations must be confirmed in Table 2-1 directly; the table was not
> available in accessible online form as of the fetch date.

Source: https://sanluisobispo.municipal.codes/Code/17.10.020

## Development Standards — M Zone (§ 17.40.020 / Table 2-21)

Section 17.40.020 and Table 2-21 set property development standards for the M zone.
Specific numeric values (setbacks, height limits, floor area ratio) are in Table 2-21 in
the municipal code. Additional regulations may be specified in the "Specific Use
Regulations" column of Table 2-1 for individual use categories.

Source: https://sanluisobispo.municipal.codes/Code/17.40.020

## CUP / Minor Use Permit Process (Chapter 17.110)

Conditional Use Permits and Minor Use Permits are governed by **Chapter 17.110**.  
Required findings for a CUP or MUP (§ 17.110.070):

1. The proposed use is consistent with the General Plan.
2. The proposed use is allowed or conditionally allowed within the applicable zone.
3. The proposed use complies with all other applicable provisions of the zoning
   regulations.
4. The design, location, size, and operating characteristics of the proposed use are
   compatible with the existing and future land uses in the vicinity.
5. The site is adequate in size and shape to accommodate the proposed use.
6. The location does not violate any applicable law or regulation.

Source: https://sanluisobispo.municipal.codes/Code/17.110  
Source (required findings): https://sanluisobispo.municipal.codes/Code/17.110.070

## Discretionary Review Levels (§ 17.102.020 / § 17.106.030)

Level of review depends on use classification and permit type from Table 2-1:

- **By-right / Zoning Clearance**: No public hearing; staff-level administrative review.
- **Minor Use Permit (MUP) / Director's Action**: Director's level; no public hearing.
- **Conditional Use Permit (CUP) / Planning Commission**: Public hearing before
  the Planning Commission.

Source: https://sanluisobispo.municipal.codes/Code/17.102.020

## GOTCHA Notes

- **Single M zone**: The city has ONE Manufacturing (M) zone — not M-1/M-2 sub-zones.
  The permit-type (by-right vs. CUP) is driven by the use classification (Light vs.
  Heavy), not by zone sub-tier.
- **WUI overlay risk**: New industrial development on parcels designated Moderate,
  High, or Very High FHSZ (maps adopted July 17, 2025) must comply with the 2025
  California WUI Code in addition to standard M-zone development standards.
- **Table 2-1 access**: The use-regulation table is only reliably readable in the live
  municipal code (sanluisobispo.municipal.codes) or the PDF version of Title 17 from
  the city's Community Development website; the table could not be scraped in full as
  of the fetch date.
