> Local EHS authority reference for the **City of San Luis Obispo** (county seat, largest city in San Luis Obispo County). ## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA / Hazardous Materials (HMBP, HW Generator, UST, Cal ARP, APSA)

- **Certified CUPA**: County of San Luis Obispo Environmental Health Services (EHS). The county is the CUPA of record for all facilities in the county, including inside city limits.
  Source: https://www.slocounty.ca.gov/departments/health-agency/public-health/environmental-health-services/cupa-program-(hazardous-materials-and-waste)
- **City Participating Agency (PA)**: City of San Luis Obispo Fire Department — Hazardous Materials program. The Fire Department is a Unified Program Participating Agency (PA) for facilities within city limits. Businesses inside the city submit HMBPs/inventories, obtain hazardous-materials permits, and request plan review for new construction/tenant improvements/hazardous-process installations through the City Fire Dept's **Hazardous Materials Coordinator**, not directly to the county CUPA. The Fire Dept also trains emergency responders and processes public information requests.
  Source: https://www.slocity.org/government/department-directory/fire-department/fire-prevention/hazardous-materials
- **County CUPA program contact**: Linnea Chandler, (805) 781-4917, M–F 8 am–5 pm.
  Source: https://www.slocounty.ca.gov/departments/health-agency/public-health/environmental-health-services/cupa-program-(hazardous-materials-and-waste)
- **CUPA program elements administered**: HMBP/inventory review and approval; plan review and permit issuance for new construction, tenant improvements, and installation/closure of hazardous material processes; Cal ARP; Hazardous Waste Generator; Aboveground Petroleum Storage Tank (APSA); Underground Storage Tank (UST); Tiered Permitting.
- **Fee schedule**: City of San Luis Obispo Comprehensive Fee Schedule (updated annually, FY 2025-26 edition current). Fire prevention and hazardous materials fees are listed in the Fire Department section.
  Source: https://www.slocity.org/government/department-directory/community-development/fees

### 2. Fire AHJ — Adopted Code & Permits

- **Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)**: City of San Luis Obispo Fire Department (municipal department).
  Source: https://www.slocity.org/government/department-directory/fire-department
- **Adopted fire code**: California Fire Code (CFC), 2022 Edition (Part 9, Title 24) — adopted via **Ordinance 1723** (2022) as Part 9 of the _San Luis Obispo Building Construction and Fire Prevention Code, 2023_. Effective for permit applications on/after January 1, 2023. Local fire-prevention amendments are in **Municipal Code § 15.04.090**.
  Source: https://sanluisobispo.municipal.codes/Code/15.04.090
- **2026 update note**: New Fire and Building codes went into effect January 1, 2026 (statewide 2025 Title 24 cycle); confirm whether the city has enacted an adopting ordinance superseding Ord. 1723 for the 2025 CFC.
  Source: https://www.slocity.org/Home/Components/News/News/11245/2359
- **Wildland-Urban Interface**: City adopted updated Fire Hazard Severity Zone (FHSZ) maps on June 17, 2025 (effective July 17, 2025). WUI Building Standards (CBC Ch. 7A) and CFC Ch. 49 apply to Moderate/High/Very High FHSZ parcels.
  Source: https://www.slocity.org/Home/Components/News/News/11245/2359
- **EV sprinkler addendum**: New buildings must design fire sprinkler systems per NFPA 13-2022 specifications for EV charging stations (per § 15.04.090 local amendment).
- **Plans/permits**: Fire prevention plan review and permits processed through the Fire Prevention Bureau.
  Source: https://www.slocity.org/government/department-directory/fire-department/fire-prevention/plans-inspections

### 3. POTW / Industrial Wastewater Pretreatment

- **POTW name**: City of San Luis Obispo **Water Resource Recovery Facility (WRRF)** — city-owned treatment works including collection, storage, treatment, recycling, and reclamation of sewage and industrial wastes.
  Source: https://www.slocity.org/government/department-directory/utilities-department/wastewater/water-resource-recovery-facility
- **Pretreatment Program established**: 1983. Administered by the City Utilities Department / Environmental Programs division.
  Source: https://www.slocity.org/government/department-directory/utilities-department/environmental-programs/pretreatment-program
- **Local sewer-use ordinance**: Municipal Code **Chapter 13.08** (Sewers). Key articles:
  - Art. V — Individual Wastewater Discharge Permits (§§ 13.08.ArtV): IWP application, issuance, conditions.
  - § 13.08.040 — Prohibited discharges.
  - Art. VI — Reporting requirements for Significant Industrial Users (SIUs).
  - Art. X — Charges and fees.
  Source: https://sanluisobispo.municipal.codes/Code/13.08
- **Significant Industrial User (SIU) classification**: Categorical Pretreatment Standards subjects (40 CFR Parts 405–471); dischargers ≥ 10,000 gpd process wastewater; Class I Industrial Users (materials/wastes that if discharged could negatively impact POTW).
- **Pretreatment standards**: Categorical standards per 40 CFR Ch. I, Subchapter N; permits may require installation of pretreatment technology, pollution control, or containment.
- **F.O.G. Program**: Separate fats/oils/grease control program for food-service establishments.
  Source: https://www.slocity.org/government/department-directory/utilities-department/environmental-programs/pretreatment-program/fog
- **WRRF contact**: Patrick McGrath (WRRF Supervisor), pmcgrath@slocity.org; general wastewater: (805) 781-7100, 990 Palm Street, San Luis Obispo, CA 93401.

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater

- **MS4 NPDES Permit**: Central Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 3) **Order No. R3-2024-0001** — the current Phase I/Phase II MS4 permit for the City of San Luis Obispo.
  Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/centralcoast/board_info/agendas/2024/jun/item07_att2.pdf
- **Local ordinance**: Municipal Code **Chapter 12.08** — Urban Stormwater Quality Management and Discharge Control.
  - § 12.08.140 — Compliance with industrial/construction activity NPDES stormwater discharge permit (NOI + SWPPP to RWQCB required for applicable activities).
  - § 12.08.150 — City authority to require BMPs for activities that may cause/contribute to stormwater pollution.
  Source: https://sanluisobispo.municipal.codes/Code/12.08
- **Post-construction requirements**: City Engineering/Development Review administers post-construction stormwater BMP standards for new development and redevelopment.
  Source: https://www.slocity.org/government/department-directory/community-development/engineering-development-review/stormwater

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts & CUP Process

- **Zoning code**: Municipal Code **Title 17** — Zoning Regulations. Use regulations by zone are set out in **§ 17.10.020** (Table 2-1: Uses Allowed by Zone).
  Source: https://sanluisobispo.municipal.codes/Code/17
- **Manufacturing (M) Zone**: Established in **Chapter 17.40**. The city uses a single Manufacturing (M) zone designation (not M-1/M-2 sub-zones). Development standards are in § 17.40.020.
  Source: https://sanluisobispo.municipal.codes/Code/17.40
- **Use classification** (defined in § 17.156.028 and Ch. 17.156):
  - _Manufacturing—Light_: finished-product manufacturing from previously prepared materials, primarily within enclosed buildings, minimal off-site impacts. Includes electronics, brewery/alcohol production, pharmaceuticals, apparel. Accessory wholesale/direct retail of on-site goods allowed.
  - _Manufacturing—Heavy_: products from raw/extracted/recycled materials or bulk storage/handling thereof. Includes chemical manufacturing, plastics/rubber, petroleum refining, primary metals, paper, automotive/aircraft/ship manufacturing.
  Source: https://sanluisobispo.municipal.codes/Code/17.156.028
- **CUP process**: Conditional Use Permits and Minor Use Permits are governed by **Chapter 17.110**. Once use category and required permit type are identified from Table 2-1, applicants submit a planning application with applicable checklist and fees. Levels of review are detailed in § 17.106.030.
  Source: https://sanluisobispo.municipal.codes/Code/17.110
- **Planning & Zoning contact**: Community Development Department, Planning Division.
  Source: https://www.slocity.org/government/department-directory/community-development/planning-zoning

- **Authority**: City of San Luis Obispo Community Development Department, Building & Safety Division.
  Source: https://www.slocity.org/government/department-directory/community-development/building-safety

  Source: https://sanluisobispo.municipal.codes/Code/15.04

## GOTCHA Notes

- **CUPA Participating Agency**: Businesses inside city limits go to the **City Fire Dept** for HMBP permits and hazardous-materials plan review — not directly to the county CUPA. The county (EHS) remains the certified CUPA of record.
- **Fire Code edition in flux**: Ord. 1723 adopted the 2022 CFC effective Jan 1, 2023. The 2025 CBC/CFC cycle took effect statewide Jan 1, 2026; verify whether a superseding city ordinance is in place.
- **FHSZ trigger**: SLO adopted new FHSZ maps July 17, 2025 — industrial parcels near the urban fringe may now carry new WUI requirements that were not previously applicable.
- **Single M zone**: The city has one Manufacturing (M) zone, not M-1/M-2 sub-tiers; heavy vs. light manufacturing classification drives permit-type (by-right vs. CUP) determination via Table 2-1.
