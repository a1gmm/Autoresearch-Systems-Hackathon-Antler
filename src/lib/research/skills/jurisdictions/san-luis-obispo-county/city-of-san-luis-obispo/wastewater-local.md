## Prohibited Discharges — § 13.08.040

The ordinance prohibits (among others):

- **Temperature**: Liquid or vapor > **150°F (66°C)** into the collection system; or any
  temperature that would inhibit biological activity; in no case > **104°F (40°C)** at
  point of introduction into the POTW.
- **pH**: Wastes with pH < **5.0** or pH > **9.0**, or with corrosive properties that could
  damage structures, equipment, or personnel.
- **Dilution prohibited**: Users may not increase process water use or dilute a
  discharge as a substitute for adequate treatment (unless expressly authorized by
  an applicable pretreatment standard).
- **FOG (non-petroleum)**: Fats, oils, and grease at concentrations causing stoppages,
  plugging, or damage to sewers.
- **Petroleum oil/grease**: Discharges causing interference, pass-through, or
  obstructions.
- **Oxygen-demanding pollutants**: BOD/similar at flow rates or concentrations that,
  alone or in combination, cause interference or pass-through.
- **Solids and viscous substances**: Causing obstruction to flow.
- **Medical/infectious wastes, noxious materials**: Prohibited categories per federal
  and state rules.

Source: https://sanluisobispo.municipal.codes/Code/13.08.040

## Categorical Pretreatment Standards

Industrial users in regulated categories must comply with categorical pretreatment
standards at **40 CFR Chapter I, Subchapter N, Parts 405–471**. The director is
authorized to establish local limits pursuant to **40 CFR 403.5(c)** that are more
stringent than federal categoricals where necessary to protect the POTW.

The WRRF's approved POTW Pretreatment Program is an **enforceable condition** of
NPDES Order R3-2024-0001 (see § 6.3.5.2 of that Order). Enforcement authority flows
from 40 CFR Part 403 and CWA §§ 307(b), (c), (d), and 402(b).

## Significant Industrial User (SIU) Classification

A facility is classified as an SIU if it meets any of these criteria (per § 13.08.020 and
40 CFR 403.3):

1. Subject to federal Categorical Pretreatment Standards (40 CFR Parts 405–471); or  
2. Discharges ≥ 10,000 gallons/day (gpd) of process wastewater to the POTW; or  
3. Contributes ≥ 5% of the average dry weather hydraulic or organic capacity of the
   POTW treatment plant; or  
4. Discharge that the director determines has, or has potential to have, a significant
   impact on the POTW, receiving water quality, or sludge disposal practices.

City classification: **Class I Industrial Users** — discharges of materials/wastes that if
discharged could negatively impact the POTW.

## Individual Wastewater Discharge Permit (IWP) Process

Governed by Municipal Code **Chapter 13.08, Article V**.

Key steps:

1. Applicant submits IWP application to the Environmental Programs Division.
2. City reviews for categorical applicability, discharge volumes, and pollutant profile.
3. Director issues permit with applicable effluent limits (numerical or BMP-based),
   monitoring requirements, and reporting schedule.
4. Permit conditions may require installation of pretreatment technology, pollution
   control systems, or containment systems.

**Portal**: No online portal identified. Contact Environmental Programs Division directly.
