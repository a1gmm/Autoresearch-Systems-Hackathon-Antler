## CRITICAL GOTCHA: No Single County POTW

There is **no county-level POTW or industrial pretreatment program** for unincorporated San Luis Obispo County. Pretreatment/IWP jurisdiction depends entirely on which sanitation district serves the facility's physical location. The two primary POTWs with formal pretreatment programs are:

1. **City of San Luis Obispo WRRF** — serves City of SLO and some adjacent areas
2. **South San Luis Obispo County Sanitation District (SSLOCSD)** — serves the Five Cities area (Arroyo Grande, Grover Beach, Oceano, Pismo Beach, and surrounding unincorporated areas)

Identify the correct district before any discharge permit inquiry.

## POTW 1: South San Luis Obispo County Sanitation District (SSLOCSD)

### Agency

- **District name:** South San Luis Obispo County Sanitation District (SSLOCSD)
- **Address:** 1600 Aloha Place, Oceano, CA 93445
- **Phone:** (805) 489-6666
- **Website:** https://sslocsd.org/

### Pretreatment Ordinance

**Ordinance No. 1994-1** — SSLOCSD Pretreatment Ordinance, adopted 1994.

- Implements federal General Pretreatment Regulations (40 CFR 403) and the Clean Water Act (33 USC § 1251 et seq.)
- Adopted to comply with NPDES/Waste Discharge Requirements (WDR) **Order No. R3-2009-0046** issued by the Central Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 3)
- Receiving water: Pacific Ocean (ocean outfall)

### Program Objectives (from Ordinance 1994-1)

1. Prevent introduction of pollutants into the POTW that will interfere with plant operations
2. Prevent pollutants from passing through to the receiving waters (Pacific Ocean)
3. Protect plant staff from hazardous chemical exposure

### Industrial User Permit Process

- Inspection required prior to permit issuance
- Annual compliance inspections thereafter
- Contact SSLOCSD at (805) 489-6666 to initiate the permitting process

### Local Discharge Limits

Specific numerical local limits (mg/L values for metals, BOD, TSS, etc.) are embedded in Ordinance 1994-1. The ordinance PDF (https://sslocsd.org/wp-content/uploads/bsk-pdf-manager/2016/10/Pre-T-1994.pdf) is a scanned image-only document and the numerical limits table could not be extracted by automated text extraction. Contact SSLOCSD directly for the current local limits table; limits may have been updated by amendment since 1994.

**Known general prohibitions from program description:**
- Discharges that interfere with POTW operations
- Discharges that would pass through to the ocean
- Substances hazardous to plant personnel

## POTW 2: City of San Luis Obispo Water Resource Recovery Facility (WRRF)

### Agency

- **POTW name:** City of San Luis Obispo Water Resource Recovery Facility (WRRF)
- **Administering department:** City of SLO Utilities Department, Environmental Programs Division
- **Contact:** https://www.slocity.org/government/department-directory/utilities-department/environmental-programs/pretreatment-program

### Pretreatment Ordinance

**City of San Luis Obispo Municipal Code, Chapter 13.08 (Sewers)**

- Pretreatment program established 1983
- Mandated by EPA and State Water Resources Control Board
- Implements 40 CFR 403

Relevant sub-articles within Chapter 13.08:
- **Article V** — Individual Wastewater Discharge Permits (IWDPs)
- **Article VI** — Reporting Requirements
- **Article X** — Charges and Fees

### Significant Industrial User (SIU) Thresholds

SIUs (requiring an Individual Wastewater Discharge Permit, IWDP) include facilities that:
- Discharge ≥ 10,000 gpd of process wastewater, OR
- Discharge ≥ 5 % of the POTW's hydraulic or organic capacity, OR
- Discharge priority pollutants

### Local Discharge Limits

Chapter 13.08 establishes local limits in addition to federal categorical standards. The code prohibits:
- pH < 5.0 or > 9.0 (§ 13.08.040)
- Oxygen-demanding pollutants (BOD) at flow rate/concentration causing interference or pass-through

Specific numerical local limits for metals and other parameters are published by the Environmental Programs Division but were not retrievable from the online municipal code pages at time of fetch (pages returned 403). Contact the Pretreatment Program directly for the current local limits table and best management practices (BMPs) that may be incorporated into individual permits.

### IWDP Permit Process

Administered under Chapter 13.08, Article V. New industrial dischargers must apply for an IWDP before discharging process wastewater. Contact the Environmental Programs Division at the link above.

## Stormwater Note

Industrial stormwater is regulated separately under the statewide NPDES Industrial General Permit (IGP). The county EHS may review IGP compliance as part of HMBP facility inspections. No county-level industrial stormwater discharge permit exists; go directly to the State Water Board IGP.
