## Adopted Code Edition

**2022 California Fire Code (CFC)** — Part 9 of Title 24 of the California Building Standards Code, including Chapter 4, Section 103, Section 503, and Appendices B, BB, C, CC, H, and P. All other Appendices are expressly **not** adopted. All annual supplements and state amendments/errata are adopted by reference.

> VERIFY: The 2025 California Fire Code became the statewide standard effective 2026-01-01. As of the date this file was created, the CAL FIRE SLU / County Fire Marshal website had not confirmed local adoption of the 2025 CFC to supersede Ord. 3481. Confirm with the Fire Marshal's office before quoting the adopted edition.

## AHJ

**San Luis Obispo County Fire Department** (operated by CAL FIRE San Luis Obispo Unit under contract since 1930) — unincorporated county areas and City of Pismo Beach by contract. Fire Marshal: (805) 503-5715; CAL FIRE SLU reception: (805) 543-4244.

"Jurisdiction" is defined in § 16.10.020(a) as: *"the unincorporated territory of the County of San Luis Obispo protected by the California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection/San Luis Obispo County Fire Department."*

## Local Amendments (Ord. 3481, § 16.10.060)

### Definitions (§ 101.1, §103.5)
- "Building Department" = SLO County Department of Planning and Building
- "Chief of Police" = Sheriff of San Luis Obispo County
- Fire Dept. personnel have peace-officer authority under Penal Code §§ 830.2(g), 830.7(d), 836.5(a)

### Storage Limits (§§ 16.10.030–16.10.050)
- **Flammable/combustible liquids** (CFC § 5704.2.9.6.1): Locations where above-ground tanks are prohibited are established by Titles 22 and 23 (zoning).
- **Liquefied petroleum gas** (CFC § 6104.2): Limits established by Titles 22 and 23.
- **Explosives/blasting agents** (CFC Ch. 56): Limits established by Titles 22 and 23; conflicts with County Code § 6.32 resolved in favor of the more restrictive provision.

### Occupancy Prohibition (§ 105.3.3)
Buildings may not be occupied prior to Fire Code Official issuing permit confirming all CFC provisions met.

### Board of Appeals (§ 108.1)
Fire Code Board of Appeals established October 9, 1990 (Consent Agenda Item A-15, on file with County Clerk-Recorder). Appeals of Fire Code Official orders go here.

### Floor Area Definition (Amendment No. 3)
Floor area includes all combustible areas attached to the structure — garages, patio covers, overhangs, covered walkways, workshops — and additions/remodels count cumulatively.

### Sky Lanterns (§ 308.1.6.3)
Prohibited except for permitted religious/cultural ceremonies when tethered and attended.

### Special Events (§ 403.2.2)
Written plan required 30 days prior to public assembly events; fire safety review mandatory; field inspection required before public occupancy.

### Fire Apparatus Access Roads — Dead-End Limits (§ 503.1.2.1)

| Parcel size | Max dead-end length |
|-------------|---------------------|
| < 1 acre | 800 ft |
| 1–4.99 acres | 1,320 ft |
| 5–19.99 acres | 2,640 ft |
| ≥ 20 acres | 5,280 ft |

Developments > 30 dwelling units in SRA or VHFHSZ require two separate approved access roads.

### Grade and Driveway (§§ 503.2.7, 503.2.9–503.2.9.5)
- Max road grade: 20 %; grades 12–20 % require non-skid asphalt/concrete.
- Driveways with proposed grade 16–20 % must be approved by the County Fire Marshal.
- Driveway width in High/Very High Fire Severity Zones: 12 ft for 0–199 ft; 14 ft for > 200 ft.
- Turnaround required if driveway > 300 ft (within 50 ft of building); turnouts every 400 ft for driveways > 800 ft.

### Address Identification (§ 505.1)
Characters must be reflective: 6 in. residential, 8 in. commercial, **10 in. industrial**; minimum ½ in. stroke width.

### Standpipe (§ 905.3.1)
Buildings > 20,000 sq ft floor area AND > 18 ft height shall have a dry or wet standpipe with 2½ in. outlet at roof near roof access.

### Fireworks (§ 5609.1)
All fireworks (including "Safe and Sane") prohibited; exceptions for properly permitted professional displays per Title 19 CCR Ch. 6.

### Fuel Modification (§ 4907.1.1)
10 ft of fuel modification required on both sides of driveways on private parcels.

### Explosives Conflict (Ch. 50)
Where Ch. 50/56 CFC conflicts with County Code § 6.32, § 6.32 controls (or the most restrictive provision for Ch. 56 conflicts).

### Water Tanks (§ 507.2.2.1)
Fire water tanks and supply lines of combustible/polyethylene materials require 30 ft vegetation clearance.

### Areas Without Water Supply (App. B103.3)
Where no service water company exists, NFPA 1142 (current edition) establishes on-site storage; minimum 2,500 gallons dedicated fire water required.

### Fire Alarm Monitoring (§ 907.6.6)
All required fire alarm systems must be monitored by a UL-listed supervising station; station must notify Fire Chief immediately on alarm.
