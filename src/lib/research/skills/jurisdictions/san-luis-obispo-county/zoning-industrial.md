## Title 22 — Inland Area Industrial Zone

### Industrial Land Use Category (IND)

The primary industrial designation in unincorporated SLO County (Inland Area) is the **Industrial (IND)** land use category. There is no M-1/M-2 numbering; the county uses named functional categories.

**Purpose (from Coastal Framework for Planning, which mirrors Inland LUE criteria):**
- Identify areas suited to industrial activities that will not adversely affect adjacent areas
- Concentrate industrial uses for efficient use of transportation, circulation, and energy facilities
- Protect adjacent land uses from harmful influences; prevent incompatible uses from intruding into industrial areas
- Residences are allowed only as caretaker or accessory uses
- Industrial uses outside urban/village reserve lines are limited to activities not employment-intensive and not requiring full urban services

**Character criteria:**
- Areas within urban service and reserve lines, with full urban services available or programmed
- Slopes less than 15 %; outside floodways, fault zones, and environmentally sensitive areas
- Areas adjacent to major transportation terminals and energy facilities
- Access via collector or arterial streets; industrial traffic not routed through residential areas
- Areas appropriate for industrial parks with unified landscaping, signing, building design, services, infrastructure, and circulation

### Allowable Uses Structure (Title 22)

**Chapter 22.06** — Allowable Land Uses and Permit Requirements by Land Use Category (Article 2)

**Table 2-2** (in § 22.06.030) maps land use categories to permit requirements for each use type. The permit types in the table are:

| Code | Permit type |
|------|------------|
| **A** | Allowed (Zoning Clearance only, issued ministerially with building permit) |
| **MUP** | Minor Use Permit (administrative approval by Planning Director) |
| **CUP** | Conditional Use Permit (Planning Commission or Zoning Administrator) |
| *(blank)* | Not allowed in that category |

**Manufacturing and Processing uses** (Article F of Table 2-2) that are principally permitted ("P") in the Industrial (IND) category per the Coastal Table O (which mirrors Inland Table 2-2 structure) include:

- Apparel Products
- Chemical Products
- Concrete, Gypsum & Plaster Products
- Electrical Equipment, Electronic & Scientific Instruments
- Food & Kindred Products
- Furniture & Fixture Products
- Glass Products
- Lumber & Wood Products
- Machinery Manufacturing
- Metal Industries, Fabricated
- Metal Industries, Primary (with S-1 special standards)
- Motor Vehicles & Transportation Equipment (with S-1 special standards)
- Paper Products
- Paving Materials
- Petroleum Refining & Related Industries (requires specific plan per § 23.08.094 for new/expanded Coastal uses; similar restrictions may apply Inland)
- Plastics & Rubber Products (with S-1 special standards)
- Printing & Publishing
- Recycling Collection Stations
- Recycling & Scrap
- Small Scale Manufacturing

### Standards for Specific Industrial Uses — Chapter 22.30

**Chapter 22.30** (Article 4 of Title 22) contains standards for specific land uses. Industrial uses often reference § 22.30.160 (Commercial/Industrial uses) and related subsections. Compliance with Chapter 22.30 standards is required in addition to the land-use permit.

### Commercial Services (CS) Category

**Commercial Services (CS)** is a second category relevant to industrial-adjacent facilities. Per the Coastal Framework:

> "To provide areas for commercial or industrial trade services and light manufacturing where the character is existing heavy commercial, service, and small-scale industrial uses."

This category accommodates light industrial and service-commercial activities that don't meet the full Industrial designation criteria.

## Title 23 — Coastal Zone Industrial Uses

### Section 23.08.080 — Industrial Uses (S-5)

In the Coastal Zone, industrial uses in the **Industrial** land use category are allowable as "S-5" uses — meaning they are subject to the special standards in § 23.08.080 of the Coastal Zone Land Use Ordinance.

For **new or expanded uses** within Petroleum Refining and Related Industries and Marine Terminals/Piers in the Coastal Zone, a **specific plan** is required prior to acceptance of land use permit(s), per § 23.08.094.

## CUP Process

Conditional Use Permits for industrial uses are processed by the SLO County Department of Planning & Building:

1. Pre-application conference recommended (call 805-781-5000)
2. Application submitted via PermitSLO portal: https://sanluisobispocountyca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/selfservice#/home
3. Environmental review (CEQA) as required
4. Public hearing before Planning Commission or Zoning Administrator depending on project type
5. Community Planning Standards (Article 9 of Title 22) apply additional requirements in designated community plan areas (e.g., Paso Robles, Templeton, Los Osos planning areas)

---

## Planning Area Overlay

**Article 9 and Article 10 of Title 22** contain community-specific and planning-area standards that can restrict or add requirements to uses otherwise allowed in the IND category. Always check the applicable planning area chapter for the specific location.

## How to Look Up a Parcel

Use the **Land Use View** mapping application to determine the land use category (= zone) for any unincorporated parcel by APN or address:
https://www.slocounty.ca.gov/departments/planning-building/how-to-apply-for-a-permit-in-unincorporated-slo-co/land-use,-subdivision,-zoning/zoning/allowable-uses/guide-to-zoning-and-property-information

Then cross-reference the land use category against Table 2-2 (Inland) or Coastal Table O (Coastal Zone) to determine allowed uses and permit types.
