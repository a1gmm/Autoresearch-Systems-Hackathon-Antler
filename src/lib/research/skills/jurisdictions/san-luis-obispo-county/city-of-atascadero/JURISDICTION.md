## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **CUPA agency:** County of San Luis Obispo Environmental Health Services (EHS) — the City of Atascadero is NOT its own CUPA. The county acts as the Certified Unified Program Agency for all unincorporated and incorporated areas including Atascadero.
- **Programs administered:** HMBP (Hazardous Materials Business Plan), hazardous waste generator registration, UST (Underground Storage Tank) installation/operation permits, APSA (Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act) inspections, California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP), and Tiered Permitting for waste treatment systems.
- **CUPA supervisor:** Linnea Chandler, Hazardous Materials (CUPA) Programs Supervisor — (805) 781-4917.
- **General EHS line:** (805) 781-5544, Mon–Fri 8 am–5 pm.
- **Atascadero Fire & Emergency Services** provides hazmat emergency response via the regional hazmat team but is NOT the permitting CUPA.
- Source: https://www.slocounty.ca.gov/departments/health-agency/public-health/environmental-health-services/all-environmental-health-services/cupa-program-hazardous-materials-waste

### 2. Fire Authority (AHJ) — Fire Code

- **AHJ:** City of Atascadero Fire & Emergency Services (municipal department). Fire Chief: Casey Bryson.
- **Adopted fire code:** 2022 Edition of the California Fire Code (CFC), including all appendices **except** Appendix J, plus the 2021 Edition of the International Wildland-Urban Interface Code (Appendices A and F only). Adopted by city ordinance (effective per state cycle).
- **Local amendments to 2022 CFC** include: fire access to solar facilities; driveway widths for fire access; automatic fire sprinklers requirements; solar panel provisions; fire access road width with fire hydrants.
- **2025 CFC transition:** The 2025 California Codes became effective statewide 2026-01-01. The city's 2025 local amendment package adds: (1) roof assemblies must meet ignition-resistance criteria to prevent ember ignition; (2) exterior walls/projections must use ignition-resistant materials; (3) decks/accessory structures subject to special ignition-resistance requirements.
- **Fire Hazard Severity Zones:** Parts of Atascadero are designated Moderate/High/Very High FHSZ; WUI standards apply per California Building Code Chapter 7A, California Residential Code Section R337, and CFC Chapter 49.
- **Municipal code location:** Title 5, Chapter 7 of the Atascadero Municipal Code (AMC) — fire code chapter (verify specific section at ecode360.com/AT4901).
- **Fire permit fees (2025 Adopted Fee Schedule, effective July 28, 2025):**
  - Fire Code Permit: $218 per permit
  - Fire Building Plan Check/Inspection: 30% of Building Plan Check Fees + 7.5% technology surcharge
  - Fire Sprinkler Plan Check — Commercial: $207 + actual cost of consultant
  - Fire Sprinkler Inspection — Residential: $302
  - Fire Alarm Plan Check: see fee schedule
  - Fire Code Position Letter: $336 per letter
  - Fire Flow Test: $274 per test
  - Fire Code Occupancy Letter: $246 per letter
  - Fire False Alarm Response: first 3 responses/year — no charge
- **Permit portal:** eTrakit at https://permits.atascadero.org/eTrakit/ (not all projects eligible)
- **Contact:** (805) 470-3300 (Fire Marshal); fire@atascadero.org; 6500 Palma Ave, Atascadero, CA 93422
- Source (fire dept): https://www.atascadero.org/department/fire-emergency-services

### 4. Wastewater / POTW — Sewer Use

- **POTW:** City of Atascadero Wastewater Division operates its own treatment facility (1.4 MGD design capacity) with aerobic, facultative, and polishing lagoons plus six percolation ponds. Serves ~50% of residents and majority of businesses; Class B biosolids.
- **NPDES wastewater permit:** Order No. R3-2021-0001, NPDES No. CA0047953, issued by the California Regional Water Quality Control Board — Central Coast Region (Region 3).

### 5. Stormwater / MS4

- **MS4 permit authority:** City operates under the State Water Resources Control Board General Permit for Small MS4s (Order No. 2013-0001-DWQ, NPDES General Permit No. CAS000004).
- **Local ordinance:** AMC Title 7, Chapter 13 — "Stormwater Pollution Prevention" (ecode360.com/43097152). Defines "prohibited discharge" (non-stormwater to storm drain system or natural creeks), NPDES industrial activity per 40 CFR §122.26(b)(14), and "Municipal Stormwater Permit."
- **Stormwater Management Plan (SWMP):** Originally adopted 2003, updated 2008; City is regulated by Central Coast RWQCB.
- **On-Site Stormwater Management Plan Check fee (2025):** Tier 1 & 2 included in grading plan check; Tier 3 — $739; Tier 4 — $1,143 (+ 7.5% tech surcharge).
- **SWPPP Plan Check/Inspection fee:** $403 plan check; $336/quarter per site.

### 6. Zoning — Industrial Districts & CUP Process

- **Zoning code:** AMC Title 9 — "Zoning Regulations." Chapter 3 governs zoning districts; Chapter 2 governs permit procedures including CUPs (AMC §9-2.108) and Administrative Use Permits (AUPs).
- **Industrial zoning districts:**
  - **IP (Industrial Park):** Light manufacturing and large lot service commercial needs.
  - **I (Industrial):** Heavy manufacturing and industrial uses.
- **CUP process:** Conditional Use Permits processed by Community Development Department per AMC §9-2.108; CUP approval valid 24 months from effective date (or life of tentative map if part of planned development). Table 3-2 (AMC §9-3.330) identifies permitted, AUP, CUP, and prohibited uses for each district.
- **Outdoor commercial/industrial sales and storage ≥10,000 sq ft** requires CUP even if the use is otherwise allowed (AMC §9-2.110).
- **Zoning ordinance history:** Ordinance No. 614 (adopted December 12, 2017) comprehensively amended Title 9 Chapters 1–4, 6, 9, and 15 (PLN 2017-1650 / ZCH 2017-0186).
- **Fee:** Administrative Use Permit review — see fee ref #25-001; CUP — see 2025 Adopted Fee Schedule.
