## POTW Operator

**City of Atascadero Wastewater Division** owns, operates, and maintains the municipal sewer collection system and the Water Reclamation Facility (WRF).

- **Contact:** (805) 470-3148; pwops@atascadero.org
- **FOG (Fats, Oils & Grease) program:** (805) 470-3140
- **Address:** 6500 Palma Ave, Atascadero, CA 93422

## Facility Description

- **Design capacity:** 1.4 million gallons per day (MGD) average daily flow
- **Current loading (2023):** ~1.38 MGD (approximately 99% of design capacity); facility upgrade/replacement in planning
- **Treatment process:** Aerobic lagoon → facultative lagoon → polishing lagoon → six percolation ponds (land-discharge; no surface water discharge)
- **Biosolids class:** Class B
- **Collection system:** 63+ miles of laterals, mains, trunks, and force mains (4–24 inches diameter); 15 lift stations; ~5,000 parcels served across ~2,000 acres
- **Service area:** Approximately 50% of city residents and majority of businesses within the Urban Service Line (USL); rural parcels west of USL use on-site septic systems

## Regulatory Permit

- **Permit type:** General Waste Discharge Requirements (WDR) — land discharge, not NPDES surface discharge
- **Governing order:** Central Coast RWQCB Order No. R3-2020-0020 (General Permit for Domestic Wastewater Systems >100,000 GPD)
- **Enrollment date:** June 6, 2023 (City enrolled in General Permit; previously operated under Order No. 01-014)
- **Issuing authority:** California Regional Water Quality Control Board — Central Coast Region (Region 3)
- **Key effluent limits under General Permit:**
  - Total Dissolved Solids (TDS): 550 mg/L (current WRF routinely exceeds at ~900+ mg/L — WRF upgrade required)
  - Chloride: 70 mg/L (current WRF routinely exceeds at ~230+ mg/L)
  - Total Nitrogen: 10 mg/L

Note: The JURISDICTION.md previously cited NPDES No. CA0047953 / Order No. R3-2021-0001. That permit is for the **City of El Paso de Robles** (Paso Robles), not Atascadero. The correct governing order for Atascadero is R3-2020-0020 (land-discharge General Permit).

## Sewer Use Ordinance (Municipal Code)

The Atascadero sewer use regulations are codified in **AMC Title 7 — Public Works**:

- **Chapter 3** (AMC §§7-3.001 et seq.) — Sewer Connection Required; Septic Tank Abandonment (ecode360.com/43096565)
  - Requires connection to public sewer when available (within 200 feet of structure or at property line in a public right-of-way)
  - Prohibits use of cesspools, privies, or septic tanks in sewer-served areas
- **Chapter 5** (AMC §§7-5.001 et seq.) — Public Sewer Extensions (ecode360.com/43096596)
  - Procedures for extending the public sewer system
- **Chapter 8** (AMC §7-8.001) — Storm water, etc. prohibited from sanitary sewers

## Industrial Pretreatment Program

The AMC Title 7 sewer use framework references industrial pretreatment requirements as administered by the Director of Public Works, but a separate codified industrial pretreatment chapter was not confirmed to be publicly accessible online as of the research date.

Key framework provisions confirmed from the Sewer System Management Plan (SSMP, adopted 2009; updated 2019):
- The SSMP implements requirements of both the Central Coast RWQCB and the Statewide General Waste Discharge Requirements (GWDR under Water Quality Order No. 2006-0003).
- Prohibited discharges include raw or chemically treated wastewater from industrial sources, hazardous waste (except as permitted), and any waste that could interfere with the POTW or cause pass-through.
- Fats, Oils, and Grease (FOG) Control Program is active: food service establishments and automotive/car wash facilities are identified as high-risk generators; grease interceptors required.

**Local discharge limits table:** Not published online. The City's WRF is a non-NPDES land-discharge system; categorical pretreatment standards under 40 CFR Part 403 apply to categorical industrial users. Contact the Wastewater Division for the current local limits analysis.

**Industrial/commercial pretreatment permit process:** Contact the Atascadero Wastewater Division directly — (805) 470-3148, pwops@atascadero.org. No online permit portal confirmed for industrial waste discharge permits.
