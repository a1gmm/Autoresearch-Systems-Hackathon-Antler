## CUPA Agency

The City of Atascadero does **not** operate its own CUPA. The **County of San Luis Obispo Environmental Health Services (EHS)** is the Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all incorporated and unincorporated areas of San Luis Obispo County, including the City of Atascadero.

- **Agency:** County of San Luis Obispo, Health Agency — Environmental Health Services Division
- **CUPA designation:** County-wide; no separate city CUPA
- **CalEPA CUPA directory ID:** 1058 (https://cersapps.calepa.ca.gov/Public/Directory/RegulatorDetails/1058)

## Programs Administered

The SLO County CUPA administers all six Unified Program elements for facilities in Atascadero:

1. **HMBP (Hazardous Materials Business Plan)** — facilities storing/handling hazardous materials above threshold quantities must submit/certify annually via CERS; routine inspections.
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator Registration & Inspection** — VSQG, SQG, and LQG facilities; annual fees and routine inspections.
3. **UST (Underground Storage Tank)** — installation/modification permits; operation permits; facility inspections; closure/removal permits.
4. **APSA (Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act)** — AST facility inspections for petroleum above thresholds.
5. **CalARP (California Accidental Release Prevention)** — Risk Management Plans for regulated substances above threshold quantity; Programs 1–4; 45-day public comment.
6. **Tiered Permitting** — hazardous waste treatment systems (Permit by Rule, Conditionally Authorized, Conditionally Exempt, CESQT).

## HMBP Threshold Quantities (trigger for regulation)

- Liquids: **55 gallons**
- Solids: **500 pounds**
- Compressed gases: **200 cubic feet**

## Local Ordinance / County Code

The SLO County CUPA program is authorized under California Health and Safety Code Chapter 6.11 (Unified Hazardous Waste and Hazardous Materials Management Regulatory Program). The County code for environmental health is accessible via Municode at https://library.municode.com/ca/san_luis_obispo_county/codes/county_code. A specific hazardous-materials chapter number was not confirmed from online sources; contact the County Administrative Office at (805) 781-5011 or EHS at (805) 781-5544 for the current county code chapter reference.

## Submission Portal

All annual HMBP and hazardous waste certifications are submitted through **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System): https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/
