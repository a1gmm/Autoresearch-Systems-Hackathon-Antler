## Industrial Zoning Districts

### IP — Industrial Park
**Purpose (AMC §9-3.320):** "Established to provide for the light manufacturing and large lot service commercial needs of the residents of the City and surrounding areas."

Typical uses: light manufacturing, small-lot service commercial, research and development, warehousing/distribution (see Table 3-2 below).

### I — Industrial
**Purpose (AMC §9-3.320):** "Established to provide suitable locations for heavy manufacturing and industrial uses within the City."

Typical uses: heavy/high-intensity manufacturing, recycling and scrap, vehicle and freight terminals, storage and dismantling.

## Development Standards (AMC §9-4 / Table 4.1)

- **Minimum lot size:** None specified (N/A for IP and I zones)
- **Front setback:** None required for IP and I zones (except ground-floor residential uses or where >50% of same-block lots are residential-zoned)
- **Side setback:** None required for IP and I zones
- **Rear setback:** None required, except:
  - Adjacent to alley: minimum 5 ft (or 10 ft if alley provides vehicular access)
  - Adjacent to residential zone: no setback for portions ≤12 ft height within 10 ft of rear line; minimum 10 ft for portions >12 ft height

## Outdoor Commercial/Industrial Storage

**AMC §9-2.110:** Outdoor commercial or industrial sales and storage **≥ 10,000 square feet** requires a CUP even if the underlying use is otherwise allowed.

## CUP Process (AMC §9-2.108)

- **Processed by:** Community Development Department
- **Decision body:** Planning Commission (standard CUP); City Council (major/controversial)
- **CUP approval validity:** 24 months from effective date (or life of tentative map if part of planned development)
- **Fees (2025 Adopted Fee Schedule):**
  - Minor CUP: $3,131 per application (ref 25-002)
  - Major CUP — standard (<5 acres): $6,670 per application (ref 25-003)
  - Major CUP — major (≥5 acres): $10,820 per application (ref 25-003)
  - AUP (other): $2,151 per application (ref 25-001)

## AUP Process

Administrative Use Permits are reviewed administratively by the Community Development Director (no public hearing required for qualifying uses). Fees: see above (ref 25-001).
