## Adopted Code Edition

The City of Atascadero adopted the **2022 Edition of the California Fire Code (CFC)**, including all appendices except Appendix J, and the **2021 Edition of the International Wildland-Urban Interface Code (IWUIC)**, Appendices A and F only, as the Fire Prevention Regulations of the City of Atascadero.

Effective statewide date for the 2025 California Codes: 2026-01-01. Confirm whether the city has yet adopted a local ordinance package for the 2025 CFC.

- **Chapter:** Chapter 7 — Fire Code

## Adopting Ordinance

The 2022 CFC adoption ordinance is **Ordinance No. 661** (introduced by the Atascadero City Council at the Public Hearing of February 14, 2023; adopted 2023). Draft Ordinance B at that hearing covered Title 8 Building Code; the companion fire-code ordinance adopted Title 4 amendments. Confirm the exact adoption date and effective date with the City Clerk (records.atascadero.org).

## Local Amendments (AMC §4-7.103)

The following local modifications to the 2022 CFC were codified in AMC §4-7.103. These are confirmed categories; fetch the live code text for the exact amendment language before quoting numbers:

1. **Fire access to solar facilities** — amends CFC provisions for roof-mounted and ground-mounted solar to require maintained fire access pathways.
2. **Driveway widths for fire access** — driveway turnarounds must conform to Atascadero Fire and Emergency Services Standards (City Engineering Standard Specifications and Drawings for Public Works Construction).
3. **Automatic fire sprinklers** — additional sprinkler requirements beyond state minimums.
4. **Solar panel provisions** — specific installation and setback requirements for solar panels affecting fire operations.
5. **Fire access road width with fire hydrants** — access road width meeting City Engineering Standard Specifications when fire hydrants are present.

## 2025 CFC Local Amendments (anticipated)

Per reporting on the 2025/2026 code cycle, the city's 2025 local amendment package was expected to add:
1. Roof assemblies must meet ignition-resistance criteria to prevent ember ignition.
2. Exterior walls and projections must use ignition-resistant materials.
3. Decks and accessory structures subject to special ignition-resistance requirements.

Verify adoption status: the 2025 CFC becomes effective statewide 2026-01-01; local amendment adoption may follow.

## Fire Hazard Severity Zones (FHSZ)

Portions of Atascadero are designated **Moderate, High, and Very High FHSZ**. WUI standards apply per:
- California Building Code Chapter 7A
- California Residential Code Section R337
- CFC Chapter 49
