## Own-CUPA vs. Under-County

**Own-CUPA.** SLO County EHS is the single county-wide CUPA (CalEPA Regulator ID 1058). However, the **City of San Luis Obispo Fire Department** acts as a **Participating Agency (PA)** under this CUPA for HMBP plan review and hazardous-materials permit functions for facilities within City of San Luis Obispo limits. Facilities inside the City of SLO deal with the City Fire Dept's Hazardous Materials Coordinator for those PA functions — not the County CUPA directly.

## Programs Administered

Per the county EHS webpage (fetched 2026-06-03):

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — facility inspections; plan reviews for businesses exceeding threshold quantities
2. **Hazardous Materials Facility Ownership Transfer** — authorization/permitting on change of ownership
3. **Hazardous Waste Generator Inspections** — contingency plans; annual self-certification for Small Quantity Generators (SQG)
4. **Tiered Permitting** — for hazardous waste treatment systems (Permit by Rule, Conditional Authorization, Conditional Exemption, Full Permit tiers per 22 CCR)
5. **Underground Storage Tank (UST)** — operations, installation, modification, and removal permitting and inspections
6. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Tank (APSA/AST)** — inspection and permitting for facilities ≥ 1,320-gallon aggregate aboveground petroleum storage
7. **CalARP (California Accidental Release Prevention)** — for facilities with regulated substances above Program 2/3 thresholds
8. **Household Hazardous Waste (HHW)** — disposal coordination (public drop-off program)

## HMBP Threshold Quantities

Facilities must submit a Hazardous Materials Business Plan in CERS when storing materials above:

- **Liquids:** ≥ 55 gallons
- **Solids:** ≥ 500 pounds
- **Compressed gases:** ≥ 200 cubic feet

Below thresholds, HMBP not required but CalARP thresholds may still trigger.

## Local Ordinance / Municipal Code Chapter

The County does not administer hazardous materials programs under a separate local municipal ordinance distinct from state law. The CUPA operates under authority of Cal. H&S Code §§ 25404–25404.5 (Unified Hazardous Waste and Hazardous Materials Management Regulatory Program). The EHS Inspection and Enforcement Policy is on file with the county — see reference document link below.

No standalone county hazardous-materials ordinance chapter number (analogous to a city code chapter) was identified in available online documents. The county code structure for environmental health is administered through the Health Agency, not through a titled county code chapter separate from state statutory authority.

## Fee Schedule

Annual fees are described as "included in the cost of the annual health fee" for HMBP-regulated facilities. Specific fee amounts are not published on the program webpage; contact EHS at (805) 781-4917 for the current fee schedule. Chargeable reinspections may apply after violation findings.

## Reporting Portal

**California Environmental Reporting System (CERS):** https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/

All HMBP submissions, UST registrations, and hazardous-waste tiered permit applications are filed in CERS. Annual HMBP certification is required in CERS.
