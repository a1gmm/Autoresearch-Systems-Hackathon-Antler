## EHS Local Rules by Family

### CUPA (Hazardous Materials Unified Program)

- **CUPA agency:** San Luis Obispo County Environmental Health Services (EHS), a division of the County Health Agency, Public Health Department. Single county-wide CUPA; CalEPA Regulator ID 1058.
- **Address/phone:** 2156 Sierra Way, San Luis Obispo, CA 93406 — phone (805) 781-5544 / fax (805) 781-4211 / email lchandler@co.slo.ca.us (CUPA Program Supervisor: Linnea Chandler). Business-hours contact: (805) 781-5000 or toll-free (800) 834-4636.
- **Program elements administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Tank (APSA/AST), Hazardous Waste Generator (including tiered permitting), and CalARP (California Accidental Release Prevention).
- **HMBP thresholds:** liquids ≥ 55 gal, solids ≥ 500 lbs, compressed gases ≥ 200 cu ft. Annual certification/inspection; fees described as "included in the cost of the annual health fee" — contact EHS for current fee schedule.
- **CUPA Participating Agency split:** The **City of San Luis Obispo Fire Department** is a Participating Agency (PA) under this CUPA for HMBP plan review and hazardous-materials permit functions inside the City of SLO. All other incorporated cities and unincorporated areas are handled directly by county EHS.
- **Reporting portal:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) at https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/ — all HMBP submissions, UST registrations, and hazardous-waste tiered permit applications are filed here.
- **Enforcement policy:** CUPA Inspection and Enforcement Policy on file with EHS — https://www.slocounty.ca.gov/departments/health-agency/public-health/environmental-health-services/forms-documents/reference-materials/hazardous-materials-program-reference-documents/cupa-inspection-and-enforcement-policy
- Source: https://www.slocounty.ca.gov/departments/health-agency/public-health/environmental-health-services/all-environmental-health-services/cupa-program-hazardous-materials-waste
- Source (CalEPA directory): https://cersapps.calepa.ca.gov/Public/Directory/RegulatorDetails/1058

### Fire AHJ and Fire Code

- **AHJ (unincorporated areas + City of Pismo Beach by contract):** San Luis Obispo County Fire Department, operated by CAL FIRE San Luis Obispo Unit (SLU) under a contract with the County (in place since 1930). Also serves Los Osos and Avila Beach Community Services Districts via cooperative agreements. Fire Marshal office: 100 Cross Street, San Luis Obispo, CA 93401 — phone (805) 543-4244, email slu.reception@fire.ca.gov.
- **Adopted fire code:** **2022 California Fire Code (CFC)** — Part 9 of Title 24 — adopted by **Ordinance No. 3481**, amending County Code Chapters 16.04 and 16.10. Board of Supervisors findings adopted December 6, 2022; effective January 1, 2023. Adopted appendices: Chapter 4, Section 103, Section 503, Appendices B, BB, C, CC, H, and P. All other appendices expressly not adopted. [VERIFY whether the 2025 CFC has been locally adopted to supersede Ord. 3481 — statewide effective 2026-01-01.]
- **Local fire code chapter:** County Code Chapter 16.10 (California Fire Code), Chapter 16.04 (Burn/Campfire/Blasting permits for unincorporated areas).
- **Local amendments (Ord. 3481 Section 16.10):** definitions of "jurisdiction" (= unincorporated territory of SLO County protected by CAL FIRE/SLO County Fire Dept), "Building Department" (= SLO County Dept of Planning & Building), "Chief of Police" (= County Sheriff); limits on flammable/combustible liquid storage above-ground tanks (cross-referenced to Titles 22 and 23); Board of Appeals established 10-9-1990.
- **Permit fee schedule:** 25-26 County Fire Fee Schedule available from the County Administrative Office; link via https://calfireslo.org/fire-marshal/
- **Wildland/SRA:** CAL FIRE SLU Unit handles State Responsibility Area (SRA) fire-safe regulations (2020 SRA Fire Safe Regulations: https://calfireslo.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/SRA-Fire-Safe-Regulations-2020.pdf). AB-38 defensible-space requirements apply in High/Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zones.
- Source: https://calfireslo.org/fire-marshal/ and Ordinance No. 3481 (https://mcclibraryfunctions.azurewebsites.us/api/ordinanceDownload/16608/1195821/pdf)

### Industrial Wastewater / Pretreatment (POTW)

- **There is no single county POTW.** The unincorporated county is served by several separate sanitation agencies depending on location:
  - **City of San Luis Obispo POTW** (serves the City and some adjacent areas): pretreatment ordinance codified in City Municipal Code Chapter 13.08. Individual Wastewater Discharge Permits (IWDPs) required for Significant Industrial Users (SIUs); SIU thresholds include ≥ 10,000 gpd process wastewater, ≥ 5% of POTW hydraulic capacity, or discharge of priority pollutants. Contact: City of SLO Utilities Dept, Environmental Programs — https://www.slocity.org/government/department-directory/utilities-department/environmental-programs/pretreatment-program
  - **South San Luis Obispo County Sanitation District (SSLOCSD)** (serves Arroyo Grande, Grover Beach, Oceano, Pismo Beach, and surrounding unincorporated areas): adopted Pretreatment Ordinance 1994-1. Pre-permit facility inspections; annual compliance inspections thereafter. Address: 1600 Aloha Place, Oceano CA 93445, phone (805) 489-6666. Ordinance text: https://sslocsd.org/wp-content/uploads/bsk-pdf-manager/2016/10/Pre-T-1994.pdf
  - Source: https://sslocsd.org/environmental-programs/pretreatment-program/
- **GOTCHA:** There is no county-level IWP program. Identify the correct sanitation district for the facility's physical location before filing a discharge permit application.

### Stormwater / MS4

- **Permit authority:** San Luis Obispo County is the Lead Planning Agency for the Phase II NPDES MS4 General Permit covering unincorporated communities. County Code **Title 19, Chapter 19.11** (Stormwater Management) is the local implementing ordinance — https://library.municode.com/ca/san_luis_obispo_county/codes/county_code?nodeId=TIT19BUCO_CH19.11STMA [VERIFY - Municode page returned 403; chapter title confirmed from search index but full text not retrieved].
- **Construction stormwater:** Regulated under the statewide NPDES Construction General Permit (CGP); county Building Dept coordinates with Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 3, Central Coast).
- **Industrial stormwater:** Regulated under the statewide NPDES Industrial General Permit; county EHS may require compliance as part of HMBP facility review.
- Source: https://library.municode.com/ca/san_luis_obispo_county/codes/county_code?nodeId=TIT19BUCO_CH19.11STMA

### Zoning / Land Use (Industrial Districts)

- **Zoning code titles:**
  - **Title 22** — Inland Land Use Ordinance (unincorporated inland areas, most of the county) — https://library.municode.com/ca/san_luis_obispo_county/codes/county_code?nodeId=TIT22LAUSOR
  - **Title 23** — Coastal Zone Land Use Ordinance (areas within the Coastal Zone) — https://library.municode.com/ca/san_luis_obispo_county/codes/county_code?nodeId=TIT23COZOLAUS
- **Industrial use zones (Title 22):** Land use designations come from the Land Use Element of the General Plan; allowable uses and permit requirements by category are in **Chapter 22.06** (Article 2). Industrial uses and service-commercial uses requiring a CUP are addressed in **Chapter 22.30** (Standards for Specific Land Uses). Table 2-1 in Chapter 22.06 maps land-use categories to permit-type requirements (permitted by right, Minor Use Permit, or Conditional Use Permit).
- **Coastal Zone industrial uses (Title 23):** Industrial uses in the Coastal Zone are addressed in **Chapter 23.08, Section 23.08.080** (Industrial Uses, Standard S-5) — https://library.municode.com/ca/san_luis_obispo_county/codes/county_code?nodeId=TIT23COZOLAUS_CH23.08SPUS_23.08.080INUS
- **CUP process:** Conditional Use Permits for industrial uses are processed by the SLO County Department of Planning & Building. Application portal: PermitSLO (https://sanluisobispocountyca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/selfservice#/home). Community Planning Standards (Article 9 of Title 22) apply additional requirements in designated community plan areas.
- Source: https://library.municode.com/ca/san_luis_obispo_county/codes/county_code?nodeId=TIT22LAUSOR and https://www.slocounty.ca.gov/departments/planning-building/grid-items/codes-and-ordinances/ordinances

## GOTCHA Notes

- **CUPA PA split:** The county EHS is the CUPA, but the **City of SLO Fire Department** acts as Participating Agency for HMBP/hazmat permit functions within the City of San Luis Obispo limits. Facilities inside city limits deal with the City Fire Dept's Hazardous Materials Coordinator for those PA functions.
- **County fire = CAL FIRE contract:** There is no standalone county-employee fire department. "San Luis Obispo County Fire Department" is CAL FIRE (SLU) under contract. Cities of SLO, Paso Robles, Atascadero, Arroyo Grande/Grover Beach (Five Cities Fire Authority), and Morro Bay operate their own municipal fire departments and are each their own fire AHJ.
- **No single county POTW:** Pretreatment/IWP jurisdiction depends on which sanitation district serves the facility's location — do not assume a county-level program exists.
- **Multi-agency hazmat response team:** A regional San Luis Obispo Hazardous Materials Team handles emergency response (CAL FIRE/County Fire, City SLO Fire, Five Cities Fire, Paso Robles Fire, Atascadero Fire, County EHS). This is emergency response, NOT CUPA permitting authority.
- **2025 CFC/CBC:** The 2025 California Building Standards Code became the statewide standard effective 2026-01-01. Verify whether SLO County has adopted it locally to supersede Ordinance No. 3481 (2022 CFC) and the 2022 Building Code Updates.
- **SSLOCSD Ordinance 1994-1 local limits:** The pretreatment ordinance PDF is a scanned image-only document; specific numerical local discharge limits (metals, BOD, TSS) could not be extracted automatically. Call SSLOCSD at (805) 489-6666 for current limits.
- **City SLO Chapter 13.08 local limits:** The municipal code pages for §§ 13.08.040/13.08.050 returned 403 at fetch time; numerical limits table was not retrievable. Contact the City SLO Environmental Programs Division directly.
- **Title 22 Table 2-2:** The Municode library returns 403 for direct chapter pages; the full Table 2-2 matrix (permitted vs. MUP vs. CUP by use in IND category) could not be extracted. Use the PermitSLO portal or call 805-781-5000 for a specific parcel determination.
