POTW operations or NPDES compliance so require (§ 14.10.070.F–G).

## Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit (IWP) — § 14.10.350

An Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit is required for:
- **Significant Industrial Users (SIUs):** Facilities discharging ≥ 25,000 gpd of process
  wastewater, or contributing ≥ 5% of POTW hydraulic or organic capacity, or subject
  to categorical pretreatment standards, or designated by the city as having significant
  potential to affect POTW operations.
- Other facilities the director designates based on risk to the POTW.

### Permit Process

1. **Wastewater Survey** (§ 14.10.360): City may require a survey of any discharger.
2. **Permit Application** (§ 14.10.370): Filed through the Wastewater Division; contact
   Matt Thompson at [email protected] or (805) 227-7239.
3. **Permit Conditions** (§ 14.10.380): Includes monitoring, reporting, pretreatment
   requirements, and local limits per § 14.10.070.
4. **Permit Duration** (§ 14.10.390): As stated in individual permit.

## Grease Control — § 14.10.370 / FOG Program

- Grease control devices required for Food Service Establishments (FSEs) and other
  oil-and-grease generators (§ 14.10.300–340).
- FOG Fats, Oils, and Grease Control Manual adopted per § 14.10.260.

## Prohibited Discharges — § 14.10.040

Prohibited substances include: pH <5 or >12 (immediate prohibition), explosive/flammable
materials, solid/viscous materials causing obstruction, steam or heat exceeding 104°F
(40°C), oils >100 mg/L (covered under local limit above), radioactive wastes, medical/
infectious wastes, trucked/hauled waste except at city-designated points, and stormwater
or groundwater.

## Enforcement

- **Civil penalties** available per § 14.10.750.
- **Criminal penalties** per § 14.10.760.
- **Enforcement Response Plan** required by § 14.10.660.
- **Noncompliance reports** published annually per § 14.10.610.
