## Adopted Fire Code Edition

The City of El Paso de Robles adopted the **2022 California Fire Code (CFC)**, including
Chapter 1 and **Appendix B** (Fire Flow Requirements for Buildings) and **Appendix D**
(Fire Apparatus Access Roads).

## Adopting Ordinance

- **Ordinance No. 1133 N.S.** — adopted on second reading by the City Council on
  **January 16, 2024** (first reading December 5, 2023).
- The ordinance amends **Municipal Code Title 17 (Building and Construction),
  Chapter 17.04 (Uniform Codes)**, specifically **§ 17.04.010** (codes adopted by reference)
  and **§ 17.04.030** (local amendments to the California Fire Code).
- Source: Council Agenda Report + Ordinance 1133, available at
  https://pub-pasorobles.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=2591

> **Note on Title 16:** Municipal Code Title 16 (Fire) / Chapter 16.10 predates the 2022
> code cycle. The operative fire-code adoption and all local amendments are now in
> **Title 17, Chapter 17.04**. Verify whether Title 16 has been repealed or still contains
> legacy operational provisions before citing it.

## Local Amendments to the 2022 CFC (§ 17.04.030)

All amendments are contained in Ordinance 1133, Attachment 1, Section 17.04.030:

### B. CFC § 311 — Vacant Premises
- **§ 311.1.3 Securing Premises.** Owner/occupant of fire-damaged property must board
  up, fence, or barricade all openings accessible to the public within **24 hours** of the
  incident, as directed by the Fire Chief.

### C. CFC § 505 — Premises Identification
- **§ 505.1.1 Address Identification.** Buildings with 5+ units served by an alley or
  interior driveway must display a directory/annunciator board at each driveway entrance,
  approved by the Fire Chief. Senior housing, hotel, and motel boards must be graphic type.
- **§ 505.1.2 Rear Door Address Numbers.** Buildings with alley access must display
  address numbers on the rear door of each tenant space.

### D. CFC § 507 — Fire Protection Water Supplies
- **§ 507.2.3 Installation Requirements.** Private fire service mains and water tank plans
  require Fire Chief review and field verification before Certificate of Occupancy. Fire
  suppression backflow devices must be freeze-protected (dedicated 120V receptacle within
  5 ft, OR insulated cover R-3 minimum, Class III flame spread, smoke density ≤ 450).

### E. CFC § 903.2 — Automatic Sprinkler Systems (expanded local threshold)
- Local amendment reads: automatic sprinkler systems required in **all new buildings**
  where total floor area (usable + unusable) exceeds **5,000 sq ft** (CFC baseline is
  higher for many occupancies).
- Shell buildings in **commercial zones**: hydraulically designed to NFPA 13 Ordinary
  Hazard Group III minimum.
- Shell buildings in **industrial/manufacturing zones**: NFPA hazard group determined by
  the Fire Chief.
- **Existing buildings:** Sprinklers required if occupancy group changes AND floor area
  exceeds 5,000 sq ft.

### F. CFC § 903.3.7 — Fire Department Connections
- Connections must be on the **addressed side** of the building and within **150 feet**
  of a fire hydrant (exact location specified by Fire Chief).

### G. CFC § 5608 — Fireworks
- **§ 5608.2 Fireworks Prohibited.** All fireworks (including "Safe and Sane") are banned
  within city limits except as allowed by the Fire Chief.

## Hazmat & High-Piled Storage

The 2022 CFC's Chapter 50 (Hazardous Materials) and Chapter 32 (High-Piled Combustible
Storage) apply at state defaults — **no local amendment to hazmat permit threshold
quantities or high-piled storage thresholds was found in Ordinance 1133**. CFC Chapter 1
operational permits for hazmat are enforced at standard CFC quantities.

## Fire Permit Types Requiring AHJ Approval

Per the March 2023 Fire Permit Application Packet (REV #1):
- Underground Fire Lines (NFPA 24)
- Fire Sprinkler Systems — Commercial/Industrial (NFPA 13)
- Fire Alarm Systems (NFPA 72; CFC § 907)
- Commercial Hood Systems (NFPA 96; CFC § 606)
- Chemical Extinguishing Systems (NFPA 17/17A; CFC § 904)
- Operational Permits (CFC Chapter 1): hazmat storage/handling, high-piled storage,
  flammable/combustible liquids, etc. — contact Fire Marshal.
