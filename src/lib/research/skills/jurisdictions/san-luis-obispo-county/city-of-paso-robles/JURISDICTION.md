## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA (Hazardous Materials Unified Program)

- **Not its own CUPA** — San Luis Obispo County Environmental Health Services (EHS) is the certified CUPA for all of SLO County, including Paso Robles. The county CUPA administers all six unified program elements: HMBP, Hazardous Waste Generator, UST, APSA/Aboveground Petroleum Storage Tanks, CalARP, and Tiered Permitting.
- County CUPA supervisor: Linnea Chandler, (805) 781-4917. Address: County Government Center, 1055 Monterey Street, San Luis Obispo, CA 93408. Hours: Mon–Fri 8am–5pm.
- Business Plans submitted via CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov
- Paso Robles Fire and Emergency Services participates in the regional SLO County HazMat Team (multi-agency, ~30 members) for emergency response only; the city fire department is not the CUPA.
- Source: https://www.slocounty.ca.gov/departments/health-agency/public-health/environmental-health-services/all-environmental-health-services/cupa-program-hazardous-materials-waste
- Source: https://calfireslo.org/haz-mat/

### 2. Fire AHJ (Authority Having Jurisdiction)

- **AHJ:** City of Paso Robles Fire and Emergency Services (municipal department).
  - Address: 900 Park Street, Paso Robles, CA 93446
  - Fire Marshal (permit/plan review): (805) 227-7560; email: firemarshal@prcity.com
  - Inspection scheduling: (805) 227-7221
- **Adopted fire code:** The **2022 California Fire Code** is the operative edition — confirmed in the March 2023 Fire Permit Application Packet (REV #1, 3-22-2023), which cites "2022 California Fire Code" for fire alarm (§907), commercial hood (§606), and chemical extinguishing (§904) system permits. (An earlier FAQ referenced the 2019 CFC; the 2023 packet supersedes this.)
- **Local fire code:** Municipal Code **Title 16** (Fire). Chapter 16.10 adopts/amends the CFC with local provisions.
  - Municode: https://library.municode.com/ca/el_paso_de_robles/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT16FI
- **Permit types requiring fire department approval:** Underground Fire Lines, Fire Sprinkler Systems (NFPA 13/24), Fire Alarm Systems (NFPA 72), Commercial Hood Systems (NFPA 96), Chemical Extinguishing Systems (NFPA 17/17A); also Operational Permits for hazmat storage/handling under CFC Chapter 1.
- **Permit portal:** PasoPermits (Accela Citizen Access) — https://aca-prod.accela.com/prcity/Default.aspx; or email firemarshal@prcity.com for fire system permits.
- **Fee schedule:** Comprehensive Fee Schedule (FY 2024-25, adopted June 18, 2024, effective July 1, 2024) available at https://www.prcity.com/159/Fees-for-City-Services — [VERIFY specific fire permit fee amounts from schedule PDF].

### 3. POTW / Industrial Wastewater (Pretreatment)

- **POTW operator:** City of Paso Robles Public Works & Utilities — Wastewater Division.
  - Contact: Wastewater Manager Matt Thompson, MThompson@prcity.com; (805) 227-7239
  - 1000 Spring Street, Paso Robles, CA 93446
- **Sewer Use Ordinance:** Municipal Code **Title 14 (Water and Sewers), Chapter 14.10** (Sewer Use). Key sections: §14.10.070(B) local discharge limits; §14.10.370 grease interceptor requirements; Article IX for permit conditions.
  - Municode: https://library.municode.com/ca/el_paso_de_robles/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT14WASE
- **Formal pretreatment program:** Approved April 17, 2023 by the Central Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board. NPDES Permit No. **CA0047953** (Order No. **R3-2021-0001**). Program meets 40 C.F.R. Part 403. City has had a voluntary pretreatment program since 2007; formal program submitted Oct. 2018 and approved 2023.
- **Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permits (IWP):** Required for significant industrial users under 40 C.F.R. Part 403 or facilities discharging pollutants harmful to the system. Apply through Wastewater Division.
- **Stormwater chapter:** Municipal Code **Title 14, Chapter 14.20** governs illicit discharges to storm drains; §14.20.100 lists allowable non-stormwater discharges. Storm Water Control Ordinance adopted July 16, 2013.
- Source: https://www.prcity.com/556/Industrial-Wastewater
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/centralcoast/public_notices/docs/2023/paso-robles-cover-letter-notice-of-approval-pretreatment.pdf
- Source: https://library.municode.com/ca/el_paso_de_robles/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT14WASE_CH14.10SEUS_ARTIXRERE_14.10.500PECORE

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater

- **MS4 permit:** City enrolled in SWRCB General Permit No. **CAS000004** (Phase II Small MS4). Central Coast RWQCB is the oversight board.
- **Local ordinance:** Title 14, Chapter 14.20 (Stormwater/Illicit Discharge); Stormwater Program Guidance Document dated July 1, 2013.
- **Program elements:** Illicit discharge detection and elimination, construction site runoff control, post-construction stormwater controls, municipal operations BMPs, public education.
- Stormwater Division: https://www.prcity.com/453/Stormwater
- Stormwater Guidance Document: https://www.prcity.com/455/Stormwater-Guidance-Document

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts & CUP Process

- **Zoning code:** Municipal Code **Title 21** (Zoning). Comprehensive update adopted by City Council with an effective date of October 31, 2024 (30 days after adoption on ~October 1, 2024).
  - Municode: https://library.municode.com/ca/el_paso_de_robles/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT21ZO
  - Current Zoning Code PDF: https://www.prcity.com/DocumentCenter/View/38795/Current-Zoning-Code
- **Industrial zoning districts:** **M** (Industrial/Manufacturing) and **PM** (Planned Industrial). Chapter 21.34 covers commercial, industrial, and airport zoning districts.
- **Permitted vs. conditional uses:** Zoning Matrix (PDF) lists all P (permitted by right) and C (conditional) uses by district. Many industrial operations in M/PM zones require a **Conditional Use Permit (CUP)** per Chapter **21.19** (Conditional Use Permits and Administrative Use Permits).
  - Zoning Matrix: https://www.prcity.com/DocumentCenter/View/14202/Zoning-Matrix-PDF
- **CUP process:** Apply through Planning Division, (805) 237-3970. Goes to Planning Commission for approval.
  - CUP page: https://www.prcity.com/279/Conditional-Use-Permit
- **Zoning map:** https://www.prcity.com/280/Zoning
- Source: https://prcity.com/351/Zoning-Code
- Source: https://www.prcity.com/1100/Comprehensive-Zoning-Code-Update
