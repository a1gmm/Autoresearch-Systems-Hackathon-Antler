## CUPA Agency

**San Luis Obispo County Environmental Health Services (EHS)** — the certified CUPA for
all of SLO County, including incorporated cities such as Paso Robles. The City does **not**
operate its own CUPA; it is fully under the county CUPA.

## Own-CUPA vs. County CUPA

Paso Robles is **NOT its own CUPA**. SLO County EHS is the sole CUPA for the county.
The Paso Robles Fire and Emergency Services participates in the SLO County regional
HazMat Team for **emergency response only** — the fire department is not a CUPA agent
and does not administer unified program elements.

## Programs Administered (Six Unified Program Elements)

SLO County EHS administers all six state-required unified program elements:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — required for facilities storing/handling
   hazardous materials above thresholds: liquids ≥ 55 gallons, solids ≥ 500 lb,
   compressed gases ≥ 200 cu ft. Annual self-certification via CERS.
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator (HWG)** — generator inspection and contingency plan
   review; small-quantity generator annual self-certification.
3. **Underground Storage Tank (UST)** — installation/modification permitting, removal
   permitting, and facility inspection.
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Tank (APSA/APST)** — facility inspection and
   installation permitting for tanks subject to SPCC thresholds.
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — Program 2 and Program 3
   processes for facilities with regulated substances above threshold quantities.
6. **Tiered Permitting (TP)** — hazardous waste treatment permitting (Permit by Rule,
   Conditional Authorization, Conditional Exemption).

Additional services:
- Hazardous Materials Facility Ownership Transfer Authorization/Permitting
- Household Hazardous Waste Disposal Information

## Submission / Reporting Portal

All HMBP submissions, annual certifications, and generator self-certifications are filed
through **CERS (California Environmental Reporting System)**:
- https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/

## Local Ordinance / Municipal Code Chapter

No Paso Robles–specific hazardous materials ordinance was identified. The county CUPA
operates under the state Unified Hazardous Waste and Hazardous Materials Management
Regulatory Program (California Health & Safety Code Division 20, Chapter 6.11). No local
SLO County hazmat ordinance chapter was published on the county CUPA web page.
