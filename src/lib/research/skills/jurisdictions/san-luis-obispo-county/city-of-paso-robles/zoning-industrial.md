## Industrial Zone Districts — Chapter 21.34

Land-use regulations for Commercial, Industrial, and Airport zones are in
**Chapter 21.34** (§ 21.34.010–21.34.090).

### M — Industrial Zoning District (§ 21.34.010.I)

> "The Industrial Zoning District is established to provide areas for general industrial uses,
> such as manufacturing and fabrication, industrial services, outside storage, vehicle
> repair, warehousing, and wholesale distribution."

### PM — Planned Industrial Zoning District (§ 21.34.010.J)

> "The Planned Industrial Zoning District is established to provide areas where innovation
> and flexibility in the design and function of industrial developments can be achieved."

### C-3 — Commercial/Light Industrial (§ 21.34.010.F)

C-3 is not a pure industrial district but accommodates **light industrial** and creative
business enterprises. Listed here because many light-industrial uses land in C-3.

## Development Standards — Table 21.34.030-1

| Standard | M (Industrial) | PM (Planned Industrial) |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum lot area | 5,000 sq ft | 10,000 sq ft |
| Minimum lot width | 50 ft | — |
| Height limit | 50 ft | 50 ft |
| Front setback | 25 ft (see § 21.34.070.A) | 25 ft (see § 21.34.070) |
| Side – interior setback | — | — |
| Rear setback | — | — |

Additional PM note (§ 21.34.070.B): Pavement in the front setback limited to **40 percent**
of the required front setback area.

**Adjacent to residential:** Where M or PM parcel abuts a residential zone, interior side
and rear setbacks increase to 5 ft and 10 ft respectively (§ 21.34.040.A); a 6-ft solid wall
or fence is required on interior lot lines (§ 21.34.040.B); and a minimum 5-ft landscaped
buffer with evergreen screening trees at 15-ft intervals is required (§ 21.34.040.C).

## Permitted vs. Conditional Uses — Chapter 21.32

Uses in all zones are regulated by **Chapter 21.32** (Land Use Regulations in Zoning
Districts). Permitted (P) and Conditional Use Permit (C) designations extracted from the
Land Use Table in the current zoning code (Title 21, Article 3):

### Industry, Manufacturing & Processing, Warehousing, and Wholesaling Uses

| Use Category | M | PM | C-3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Industrial — Heavy | C | C | — |
| Industrial — Light | P* | P* | P* |
| Industrial — Artisan | P | P | C |
| Food and Kindred Products Processing | C | C | — |
| Composting / Green Waste Facility | C | C | — |
| Crop Production and Processing | P | P | — |
| Resource Extraction | C | C | — |
| Recycling Collection Centers | C | — | C |
| Warehousing / Wholesale Distribution | P | P | — |

*P* = Permitted only in the Commerce Industrial Park (properties facing Sherwood Road,
Commerce Way, Fontana Road, and Linne Road); see § 21.69.090.

> These are the land-use categories as listed in the zoning table. "Industrial — Heavy"
> includes uses with significant process emissions, hazmat, or outdoor storage. "Industrial
> — Light" covers manufacturing with limited nuisance characteristics. Verify specific use
> against the Land Use Regulations before relying on this table.

**Development Plan review** (Planning Commission, public hearing) is required for all
industrial/commercial construction with **≥ 10,000 sq ft** of gross floor area, or smaller
buildings where infrastructure has not been installed (§ 21.16.010.B).

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process — Chapter 21.19

- **Review authority:** Planning Commission (§ 21.19.020.A).
- **Administrative Use Permit (AUP):** Zoning Administrator decides on lower-impact
  conditional uses; can escalate to Planning Commission (§ 21.19.020.B).
- **Application processing:** Per Chapter 21.09. Apply through the Planning Division,
  (805) 237-3970.
- **Notice and hearing (CUP):** Planning Commission holds a public hearing; notice per
  Chapter 21.26 (§ 21.19.040.A).
- **Notice (AUP):** Mailed notice to all owners/occupants within **300 feet**, minimum
  10 business days before Zoning Administrator decision date (§ 21.19.040.B).

### CUP Required Findings (§ 21.19.050)

1. Consistent with General Plan and any applicable specific plan; allowed in zoning district
   subject to CUP; complies with all Zoning Code and Municipal Code provisions.
2. Compatible with existing and future land uses in the vicinity.
3. Site is physically suitable (design, access, utilities, public protection).
4. Measure of site suitability: use will not adversely affect public health, safety, or welfare,
   or be materially injurious to surrounding improvements, persons, or uses.

## Zoning Map — Industrial Areas

Industrial (M) and Planned Industrial (PM) zoned areas are concentrated in the southeast
quadrant of the city (Commerce Industrial Park area) and near the Paso Robles Municipal
Airport (AP zone, also identified as a future industrial opportunity area per § 21.34.010.K).
