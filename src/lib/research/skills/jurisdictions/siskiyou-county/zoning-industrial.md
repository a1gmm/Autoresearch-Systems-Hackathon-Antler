# Zoning — Industrial Districts, Siskiyou County (Unincorporated)

## Governing Code

Zoning is codified in **Siskiyou County Code Title 10 — Planning and Zoning, Chapter 6 — Zoning**, beginning with Article 37 for use classifications.

- Municode (Title 10): https://library.municode.com/ca/siskiyou_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT10PLZO

- Fees governed by § 10-6.1601

## Industrial Zoning Districts

Siskiyou County's zoning ordinance includes both legacy and current industrial district designations:

| District Symbol | Name | Municode Article |
|----------------|------|-----------------|
| M-1 | Light Industrial (legacy) | Article 10 |
| M-2 | Heavy Industrial (legacy) | Article 11 |
| M-L | Limited Industrial District | Article 45 |
| M-M | Light Industrial District | Article 46 |
| M-H | Heavy Industrial District | Article 47 |

### M-M (Light Industrial — § 10-6.4601)
Permitted uses include: repair shops, animal hospitals, lumber yards, feed stores, assembly plants, bulk storage, fruit and vegetable freezing and processing operations.

### M-H (Heavy Industrial — § 10-6.4701)
Permitted uses include: creameries, canneries, fertilizer production, and any use permitted by right in M-L or M-M.  
Uses requiring a **Conditional Use Permit (CUP)** include: junk yards, heavy manufacturing, commercial excavation of stone or earth materials, airstrips, storage of flammables, concrete or asphalt batching plants.

### M-L (Limited Industrial)
Specific permitted uses not retrieved online. [VERIFY — review Article 45 in full code]

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

Called a **Use Permit** in Siskiyou County. Key process details (from Use Permit Application Guide, Revised 2025):

- **Deciding body**: Planning Commission (public hearing)
- **Timeline**: 90–120 days from accepted application; can extend when additional agency review or environmental studies are required
- **Hearing schedule**: Planning Commission meets the **third Wednesday of each month**
- **Notice**: All property owners within a minimum **300-foot radius** must be notified
- **Application submission**: Planning Division, 806 South Main Street, Yreka; or download at https://www.co.siskiyou.ca.us/planning/page/applications-and-permits
- **Applicant**: Property owner must sign; a Registered Civil Engineer, Licensed Land Surveyor, or Licensed Architect may prepare the application
- **Required materials**: Written statement (business type, major activities, employee count, hours, outdoor activities), Environmental Information Form, Water/Sewer Clearance Form, Development Plans, photographs

### Multi-Agency Fees
In addition to planning fees (see fee schedule at § 10-6.1601 and Application for Development Review form, page 5), applicants typically pay:
- CDFW filing fee (due at project approval, before Notice of Determination)
- Siskiyou County Clerk fee for Notice posting
- Environmental Health Division sewer/water clearance fee
- Public Works engineering review fee
- County Counsel review fee

Environmental Health clearance must be obtained **before** filing the planning application.

## Nonconforming Industrial Uses

A Conditional Use Permit is required before a legally established nonconforming industrial structure or use may be enlarged or expanded by more than **20 percent**.
