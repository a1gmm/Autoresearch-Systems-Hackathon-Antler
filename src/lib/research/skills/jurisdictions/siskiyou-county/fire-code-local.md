# Fire Code — Siskiyou County (Unincorporated)

## Adopted Edition

The County has adopted the **2025 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9)**, effective **January 1, 2026**. The 2025 CFC incorporates the 2021 International Fire Code with California amendments.

- Source page: https://www.siskiyoucounty.gov/building/page/current-building-codes
- Building code family codified in **Siskiyou County Code Title 9 — Building Regulations** (Chapter 1 covers uniform code adoptions; § 9-1.01 states the county enacts regulations "at least equal to those imposed by the State Building Standards and State Housing Laws").
- Municode Title 9: https://library.municode.com/ca/siskiyou_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT9BURE

## Fire AHJ by Territory

| Territory | Authority Having Jurisdiction |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Unincorporated county / State Responsibility Area (SRA) | **CAL FIRE — Siskiyou Unit**, 1809 Fairlane Road, Yreka, CA 96097; 530-842-3516 |
| City of Yreka | Yreka Fire Department |
| City of Mount Shasta | Mt. Shasta Fire (merged city dept + Mt. Shasta Fire Protection District) |
| City of Weed | City of Weed Fire Department |
| Other incorporated cities | Respective city fire departments or fire protection districts |
| Federal lands (Klamath NF, Shasta-Trinity NF, Lava Beds NM) | USFS / NPS — not CAL FIRE or the county |

**Note:** The vast majority of Siskiyou County's land area is in the SRA or on federal land. CAL FIRE Siskiyou Unit is the de facto fire prevention and code-inspection authority for most industrial facilities in unincorporated areas. CAL FIRE has four fire prevention specialists covering the entire county and typically inspects each community every other year.

## Fire Hazard Severity Zones

The County is in the process of adopting Local Responsibility Area (LRA) Fire Hazard Severity Zone designations per Government Code § 51179. The State Fire Marshal issued initial FHSZ maps in February 2025; public comment closed April 2, 2025. Final county ordinance has not yet been published online. ## Local Fire Hazard Regulations (Title 3)

County Code **Title 3 — Public Safety, Chapter 3** ("Siskiyou County Fire Control and Fire Hazard Regulations") addresses maintenance of flammable materials, firebreaks, and related enforcement. This is separate from the building-permit fire code adoption and covers operational fire-hazard maintenance.
