# Wastewater / Sewer — Siskiyou County

## County-Level Structure: Primarily Onsite Systems

Siskiyou County is a large, largely rural county with no county-operated POTW (publicly owned treatment works). The county's wastewater program at the county level covers **individual (onsite) sewage disposal systems** only:

- Regulated by **Siskiyou County Environmental Health Division** under County Code **Title 5, Chapter 2 — Sewage Disposal**
- Municode: https://library.municode.com/ca/siskiyou_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT5SAHE_CH2SEDI_S5-2.01APSC
- Governing policy document: **LAMP OWTS (Onsite Wastewater Treatment Systems) Policy** — governs permitting, percolation testing (wet-season testing January 1–April 30), and system installation

There is **no county-level industrial pretreatment program** because there is no county POTW to protect.

## Municipal POTWs Within the County

Industrial dischargers connecting to a city sewer system must deal with the **city's** sewer-use ordinance and pretreatment requirements, not the county's. Key city POTWs:

### City of Yreka
- Operates a POTW located between SR 263/North Main Street and Yreka Creek (~600 ft north of Montague Road/SR 3 & SR 263 intersection)
- Designed capacity: **1.3 million gallons per day** average dry weather flow
- Responsible for collection, treatment, and disposal of domestic and industrial sewage within city boundaries
- The city offers an **Industrial Wastewater Discharge Questionnaire** (PDF available on city website) — indicates some form of industrial discharge oversight exists

## Industrial Discharge Routing

For a facility in **unincorporated Siskiyou County**: no municipal sewer is typically available; onsite wastewater treatment (septic/OWTS) is the norm, permitted through the county Environmental Health Division.

For a facility **within a city**: contact that city's public works / utilities department for sewer-use ordinance requirements and any industrial discharge permit obligations.

## Relevant County Code

- **Title 5, Chapter 2** — Sewage Disposal (onsite systems): https://library.municode.com/ca/siskiyou_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT5SAHE_CH2SEDI_S5-2.21DRSP
- Any development requiring sewer/water must obtain **Environmental Health Division clearance** before a planning use-permit application can be filed (per Use Permit Application Guide, Revised 2025)
