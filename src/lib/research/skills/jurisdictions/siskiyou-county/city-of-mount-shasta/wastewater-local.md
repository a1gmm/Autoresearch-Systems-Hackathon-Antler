## Industrial Pretreatment Program (IWP)

The NPDES permit (Order R5-2023-0016, Fact Sheet) explicitly states:

> **Pretreatment Program: No**

This means the City of Mt. Shasta POTW does **not operate a formal federal pretreatment program** under 40 CFR Part 403 (i.e., it does not have EPA- or state-approved Pretreatment Program oversight obligations for Significant Industrial Users). The POTW's design flow of 0.8 MGD and its relatively small industrial load do not require it.

However, the City **does issue individual Industrial Waste Discharge Permits (IWPs)** on a case-by-case basis under MSMC 13.56.270 for non-categorical industrial dischargers. These are local permits, not federal-pretreatment categorical permits.

## Prohibited Discharges (MSMC 13.56.030 / 13.56.320)

Key prohibitions (verbatim from IWP standard conditions referencing MSMC):
- Fats, oils, or greases of animal or vegetable origin that will cause adverse effects on the POTW
- Petroleum oil or nonbiodegradable cutting oil that will cause interference or pass through
- Flammable wastes with closed-cup flashpoint < 140°F (60°C) (per 40 CFR 261.21)
- Wastewater > 120°F (49°C) or causing POTW influent > 104°F (40°C)
- Solids > 3/8 inch in any dimension
- Wastewater with pH < 6.5 or > 8.5
- Radioactive wastes (except as authorized)
- Stormwater, groundwater, and unpolluted water (unless authorized by Public Works Director)
- Wastewater causing toxicity test failure

## Enforcement / Civil Penalty

A permittee who violates the City of Mt. Shasta sewer use ordinance, any permit term, or any applicable pretreatment standard or requirement is liable to the City for a maximum civil penalty of **$6,000 per violation, per day** (per MSMC 13.56).

## Enforcement History

The City entered a $1.3 million settlement with the Central Valley RWQCB for collection-system overflows (October 2014 – April 2017) and NPDES effluent limit violations (February 2017 – May 2020) involving BOD, TSS, copper, and zinc.
