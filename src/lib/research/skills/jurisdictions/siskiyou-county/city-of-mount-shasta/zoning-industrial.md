## Zoning Code Location

Mount Shasta's zoning regulations are codified in **Title 18 of the Mt. Shasta Municipal Code (MSMC)**, also referred to as the "Land Development Code."

- **MSMC Chapter 18.16 — District Regulations** (primary chapter; land use tables organized by district)
- **MSMC Chapter 18.20 — General Regulations** (development standards applicable citywide)
- **MSMC Chapter 18.60 — Architectural Review**
- **MSMC Chapter 18.80 — Surface Mining and Reclamation**

Code current through Ordinance CCO-25-03 (July 28, 2025). Online via CodePublishing: https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/MtShasta/

## Zoning Districts

The City Planner maintains the official Zoning Map (last published 2017; confirm current version). Districts are established in Chapter 18.16. The following district types have been confirmed as existing in Mount Shasta's zoning framework:

**Residential:**
- R-1 (single-family residential and similar residential tiers)

**Commercial:**
- C-1, C-2 (commercial districts — all residential uses permitted in R-3 are also permitted in C-1 and C-2 per Ch. 18.16)

## Use Permit / CUP Process

Land use tables in Chapter 18.16 classify uses as:
- **Permitted by right** (P)
- **Conditional Use Permit required** (CUP)
- **Not permitted** (N)

The Planning Commission hears CUP applications. The Planning Department has recommended amendments to Chapters 18.08, 18.16, and 18.98 in recent years (confirming these are the active chapters for use regulation and procedures).

## Environmental Review

Projects in industrial zones that may have significant environmental effects are subject to **CEQA review** administered by the City Planning Department as lead agency.

## Note on Industrial Activity in Mount Shasta

Mount Shasta (~3,400 residents) is primarily a residential/commercial/tourism city at the base of Mount Shasta volcano. Significant heavy industrial land use is limited. The city is within a designated Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone (MSMC Ch. 7.60), which may impose additional development constraints. Confirm with the Planning Department whether any general industrial or light industrial zoning district is present and the extent of industrially-zoned land in the city.
