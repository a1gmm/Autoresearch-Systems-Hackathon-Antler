## Adopted Fire Code

The City of Mount Shasta adopts the California Fire Code (CFC) by reference as part of Title 15 of the Mt. Shasta Municipal Code (MSMC). The prior explicit adoption chapter — **Chapter 15.06 "Uniform Fire Code"** — was **repealed by Ordinance CCO-20-04** (adopted 2020). The repeal brings Mt. Shasta into alignment with the statewide mandatory adoption model: the current edition of Title 24, Part 9 (California Fire Code) applies by operation of state law. As of 2026-01-01 the operative edition statewide is the **2025 California Fire Code** (based on IFC 2024); confirm the city has not enacted local amendments superseding the state default via a post-CCO-20-04 ordinance.

- **Municipal code reference:** MSMC Title 15 (Buildings and Construction); former Ch. 15.06 (repealed CCO-20-04)
- **Fire hazard severity zones:** Chapter 7.60 (Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zones — the Director of Forestry and Fire Protection has designated a major portion of the City as VHFHSZ)
- **Burn permits:** Chapter 7.15 (Fire Prevention – Burn Permit Required; repealed and re-adopted by CCO-20-04 — confirm current codified status)
- **Code current through:** Ordinance CCO-25-03 (July 28, 2025)

## Fire AHJ

**Mt. Shasta Fire** — a merged operation of Mt. Shasta City Fire Department and the Mt. Shasta Fire Protection District, combined in 2016. The AHJ serves the city and the surrounding ~60-square-mile fire protection district (~7,000 population total). Staffed by a full-time Fire Chief, paid personnel, and volunteers.

- City fire page: https://www.mtshastaca.gov/fire-department
- Mt. Shasta Fire Protection District: https://mtshastafiredistrict.gov/

**GOTCHA:** Because Mt. Shasta Fire is a joint city + fire-protection-district operation, the AHJ for a given parcel may be the City or the District depending on location within the boundary. Confirm parcel-level jurisdiction before citing the AHJ.

**State Responsibility Area (SRA) / CAL FIRE:** Wildland areas outside city limits and in the surrounding SRA fall under CAL FIRE Siskiyou Unit for fire suppression. Facilities in the SRA may be subject to CAL FIRE's fire prevention fees and regulations in addition to or instead of the city AHJ.

## Local Amendments

No local amendments to the California Fire Code have been identified in the online municipal code beyond the fire hazard severity zone designations (Ch. 7.60) and the burn permit program (Ch. 7.15).
