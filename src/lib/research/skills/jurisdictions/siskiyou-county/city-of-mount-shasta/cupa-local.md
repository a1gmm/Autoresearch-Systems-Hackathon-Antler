## CUPA Jurisdiction

The City of Mount Shasta is **not its own CUPA**. All Unified Program elements inside city limits are administered by:

**Siskiyou County Environmental Health Division**
Community Development Department
806 South Main Street, Yreka, CA 96097
Phone: 530-841-2100 (main) | 530-842-8213 (CUPA permits)
Email: EHPermitTech@co.siskiyou.ca.us
Hours: 8:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m., Monday–Friday

The Division was **certified as the local CUPA on January 1, 1997** (established under Senate Bill 1082, Chapter 418, Statutes of 1993; authority under Health & Safety Code § 25404(a)(1)(A)).

Deputy Director: Rick Florendo

## Programs Administered

The Siskiyou County CUPA consolidates and administers the following programs for all facilities in Siskiyou County, including those within the City of Mount Shasta:

| Program | Authority |
|---|---|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) | H&S Code Ch. 6.95 |
| Hazardous Waste Generator Compliance Inspections | H&S Code Ch. 6.5; 22 CCR |
| Underground Storage Tanks (UST) | H&S Code Ch. 6.7 |
| Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) | H&S Code Ch. 6.67 |
| California Accidental Release Prevention Program (CalARP) | H&S Code Ch. 6.95, Art. 2 |
| Release Response / Spill Investigation | H&S Code § 25404 |

All reporting is through **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System).

## Hazardous Waste Generator Classification

Three generator tiers apply to facilities in Mt. Shasta:
- **CESQG** — Conditionally Exempt Small Quantity Generator
- **SQG** — Small Quantity Generator
- **LQG** — Large Quantity Generator

Compliance audits check accumulation time limits, container labeling, storage area standards, and emergency notification/planning requirements per 22 CCR § 66261.3 et seq.

## Local Hazmat Ordinance

No distinct City of Mount Shasta hazardous materials ordinance was found. The county-level regulatory framework applies directly to city facilities through the state Unified Program.
