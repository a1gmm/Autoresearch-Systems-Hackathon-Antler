## Fire AHJ

**Yreka Fire Department** (all-volunteer municipal department) is the fire authority having jurisdiction (AHJ) inside city limits. The department is entirely volunteer including the Chief Officer.

- Address: 401 W Miner Street, Yreka, CA 96097
- Non-emergency phone: 530-841-2383 (leave message; response within 2–3 business days)
- Emergency: 911
- Fire Chief (as of 2024): Jerry Lemos
- Business hours: by appointment only (volunteer department)

For State Responsibility Area (SRA) wildland surrounding the city, the AHJ is **CAL FIRE Siskiyou Unit**.

## Adopted Fire Code

The City of Yreka's building and fire code enforcement is administered by the **City Building Department** (Building Inspector: James McIntyre, 530-841-2322; 530-938-4441).

**Current statewide code in effect:** Per California state law, the **2025 California Fire Code** (based on the 2024 IFC) became effective **January 1, 2026**, superseding the 2022 CFC. Yreka's Building Department enforces fire code provisions as part of its plan review and inspection program.

**Specific local adopting ordinance number: [VERIFY — not found online].** The city's Municode-hosted code references building/fire provisions under **Title 11 — Building Code**. Known chapters include:
- Chapter 11.10 — Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zones (local adoption of CAL FIRE-designated FHSZ map)

## Fire Hazard Severity Zone Ordinance (2025)

In April 2025, the Yreka City Council introduced an ordinance updating the city's fire hazard severity zone map to align with the updated CAL FIRE LRA (Local Responsibility Area) FHSZ maps. The maps categorize city parcels into Moderate, High, and Very High fire severity zones based on vegetation, topography, and fire history. A second reading/final adoption was scheduled following the introduction.

- **Adopting ordinance number: [VERIFY — not confirmed in public documents]**
- Building requirements in Very High FHSZ zones are governed by Chapter 11.10 of the Yreka Municipal Code and state law (Government Code § 51175 et seq.).

## Notes
