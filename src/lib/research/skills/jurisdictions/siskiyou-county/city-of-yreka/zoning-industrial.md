### Chapter 16.42 — Heavy Industrial (M-2)
[VERIFY — chapter title confirmed ("HEAVY INDUSTRIAL M-2", Municode nodeId: TIT16ZO_CH16.42HEINM-); full permitted uses list not confirmed online.]

Typical heavy industrial uses in California M-2 zones: heavy manufacturing, outdoor storage, industrial processing, recycling operations. Uses generating significant noise, odors, or hazardous materials typically require a Conditional Use Permit.

**Storage containers** (cargo containers/truck trailers) in any industrial zone require a **Conditional Use Permit** as an accessory use under Chapter 16.46 (Special Provisions), § 16.46.140.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

Governed by **Chapter 16.44** of the Yreka Municipal Code (amended by **Ordinance No. 867**).

Key steps:
1. Submit CUP application to Planning Department with site plan, building plans, elevations, landscaping plan (per Chapter 16.52), and applicable fee (fee set by City Council resolution — [VERIFY current fee]).
2. Planning Department has **30 calendar days** to determine completeness; applicant has up to **90 days** to cure deficiencies.
3. Upon completeness, CEQA review is completed; then a **public hearing is scheduled with the Planning Commission** (within 15 days of completing environmental review).
4. Public hearing noticed per Government Code §§ 65090–65096.
5. Planning Commission makes findings and grants/denies CUP; appeals go to City Council.

Required CUP findings include: compatibility with surrounding uses; consistency with General Plan and Zoning Ordinance; adequate public facilities (water, sewer, utilities).

## Rezoning / Zoning Text Amendments

**Chapter 16.57** governs rezoning and zoning text amendments (confirmed via CEQA filing reference). Process requires Planning Commission hearing and City Council adoption.
