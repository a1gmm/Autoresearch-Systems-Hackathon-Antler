## Controlling CUPA Authority

The City of Yreka is **not its own CUPA**. All Unified Program elements for facilities inside Yreka city limits are administered by:

**Siskiyou County Environmental Health Division**
Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) — certified since January 1, 1997

- Address: 806 South Main Street, Yreka, CA 96097

- Deputy Director: Rick Florendo

## Programs Administered (Six Unified Program Elements)

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — inventory and emergency response plans; reported through CERS
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator** — inspection and compliance with the Hazardous Waste Control Act; accumulation limits, labeling, container/tank standards
3. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — installation, operation, closure, and repair permits
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — SPCC plan requirements for tanks ≥ 1,320 gallons
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — Risk Management Plan review for regulated substances
6. **CERS Reporting** — all regulated facilities submit electronically through the California Environmental Reporting System (cers.calepa.ca.gov)

## Permit / Application Process

Facilities in Yreka must submit the following to Siskiyou County Environmental Health (not the city):
- HMBP submitted via CERS (annual renewal)
- UST installation/closure permits — application to County EH
- APSA notification — County EH
- Hazardous waste permit — County EH

Available applications and permits: https://www.siskiyoucounty.gov/environmentalhealth/page/applications-and-permits

An **Environmental Health Fee Schedule** is maintained by the County (specific fees not posted on the public-facing page — contact EHPermitTech@co.siskiyou.ca.us for current schedule).

**Payment note:** As of June 26, 2026, the County will no longer accept annual CUPA permit payments through its prior third-party payment system; facilities must use the updated payment method.

## Local Hazmat Ordinance

No standalone City of Yreka hazardous materials ordinance has been identified online. The County CUPA program is the operative regulatory layer for city facilities. [VERIFY — contact Yreka City Manager's office if city adopted any supplemental hazmat ordinance.]
