## Regulatory Permit

**WDR Order No. R1-2021-0016** (WDID 1A84073OSIS)
Issued by: California Regional Water Quality Control Board, **North Coast Region (Region 1)**
Adopted: June 17, 2021 (signed July 8, 2021); replaces Order No. R1-2003-0047.

This is a **Waste Discharge Requirements (WDR)** order, not an NPDES permit. Treated effluent is discharged to land (groundwater recharge via percolation ponds and subsurface drip disposal fields), not directly to surface water.

## Facility Design

| Disposal | Subsurface drip disposal fields (22.4 acres) + 4 percolation ponds, north of facility adjacent to Yreka Creek |
| Receiving water (indirect) | Shasta Valley Hydrologic Area groundwater; Yreka Creek watershed |

Biosolids: aerobically digested, mechanically dewatered by centrifuge, disposed at a permitted landfill.

## Source Control and Pretreatment Program

### Current Status
WDR Order R1-2021-0016, Section VIII.F requires the City to develop a **source control program** and conduct an **Industrial Waste Survey (IWS)**. Specific requirements:

1. **Source control program technical report** — due to the North Coast RWQCB Executive Officer by June 15, 2023. Must describe staff/budget resources and a written plan for monitoring industrial, commercial, and residential discharges.

2. **Industrial Waste Survey (IWS)** — conducted during calendar year 2023 (January 1 – December 31, 2023), covering all industrial users (IUs) in the service area. Must determine: flow rates, SIC codes, pretreatment status, whether each IU qualifies as a Significant Industrial User (SIU), and whether the City must establish a formal pretreatment program under 40 C.F.R. Part 403.

3. **IWS report** — submitted to the RWQCB no later than June 15, 2024, with a certification on whether the facility receives pollutants from any IU requiring a formal pretreatment program.

### Known Industrial User
**Norcal Products** is identified in Order R1-2021-0016 as a **categorical industrial user (CIU)** connected to the city collection system. This triggered the IWS requirement.

### Formal Pretreatment Program
As of the 2021 permit order, the City does **not** operate a formally established NPDES-style pretreatment program under 40 C.F.R. Part 403. The WDR order requires the IWS to determine if one is needed.
