# CUPA — Siskiyou County

## Controlling CUPA

**Siskiyou County Environmental Health Division** is the sole countywide CUPA, certified by Cal/EPA effective **January 1, 1997**, under Health and Safety Code § 25404(a)(1)(A).

There is **no separate city CUPA** anywhere in Siskiyou County. All nine incorporated cities (Yreka, Mount Shasta, Weed, Dunsmuir, Montague, Etna, Fort Jones, Dorris, Tulelake) and all unincorporated areas are served by the County CUPA. No city is listed as a Participating Agency with its own CUPA certification.

## Programs Administered

The County CUPA administers all six Unified Program elements:

1. Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP) — reporting via CERS
2. Hazardous Waste Generator Program
3. Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program
4. Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)
5. California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) — RMP review and inspections
6. Medical Waste Management Program (also noted)

Hazardous waste response: SCEH responds to releases/threatened releases of hazardous materials; a Registered Environmental Health Specialist supervises remedial action.

## CERS Submissions

All HMBP, inventory, and emergency plan submissions go through the **California Environmental Reporting System (CERS)** at https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/. There is no separate local portal.

## CalARP Specifics

Facilities handling regulated substances above threshold quantities listed in CCR Title 19, Division 2, Chapter 4.5 must submit Risk Management Plans (RMPs). SCEH conducts:
- Routine facility inspections
- RMP completeness reviews
- RMP evaluation reviews
