## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air

Kern County is divided between **two air districts** — confirm the facility's geographic location before routing a permit:

| Area | District | Contact |
|---|---|---|
| San Joaquin Valley floor (Bakersfield, Delano, Shafter, Wasco, Taft, Tehachapi corridors) | **San Joaquin Valley Unified APCD (SJVAPCD/Valley Air District)** — https://www.valleyair.org | Title V, Authority to Construct (ATC), Permit to Operate (PTO) |
| Eastern / high-desert Kern (Mojave, Ridgecrest, California City, Tehachapi eastern slopes) | **Eastern Kern Air Pollution Control District (EKAPCD)** — http://www.kernair.org | ~3,704 sq mi; 2700 M St, Suite 302, Bakersfield, CA 93301; (661) 862-5250 |

Both districts issue Authority to Construct and Permit to Operate for stationary sources. SJVAPCD also administers portable equipment registration and Title V.

Source: https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/california-air-districts; http://www.kernair.org/Main_Pages/Subpages/Info_Sub/Boundary.html

### Fire

- **AHJ**: Kern County Fire Department — unincorporated county and contract cities
  - Fire Prevention Division: (661) 391-3310; fireprevention@kerncountyfire.org
  - Source: https://kerncountyfire.org
- **Adopted code**: California Fire Code (CFC) **2019 Edition** (CCR Title 24, Part 9) + IFC 2018 Edition (Appendices B, C, D)
- **Adopting ordinance**: **Ordinance No. 8866**, adopted **November 5, 2019**
- **Municipal code chapter**: **Chapter 17.32, Title 17** (Buildings and Construction) of the Kern County Ordinance Code
- Local amendments include: zone-based restrictions on cryogenic fluids (M-2 CUP / M-3 as-of-right), aboveground tanks (A, M-1/2/3, NR zones), LPG >2,000 gal (M-2 with CUP + setbacks; M-3 as-of-right), explosives (M-2 CUP; M-3 as-of-right), CNG (C-1/2/CH, M-2/M-3), cannabis operations (§ 321 added), wildland interface (Chapter 17.34)
- Permit fees established by Board of Supervisors; 2020 published schedule effective January 1, 2020
- Source: https://kerncountyfire.org/fire-prevention/building-codes/; https://kerncountyfire.org/wp-content/uploads/Fire-Code-Ordinance-Ordinance8866.pdf

### Wastewater

- **Unincorporated county sewer**: Kern County Public Works, Title 14 (Chapter 14.08) — county does not operate a POTW with an independent federal pretreatment program; industrial users connect through service agreements with adjacent POTWs
- **Primary POTW for most of county**: City of Bakersfield Wastewater Division — WWTP No. 2 (east Hwy 99) and No. 3 (west Hwy 99)
  - Governing ordinance: **Bakersfield Municipal Code (BMC) Chapter 14.12 — Sewers**
  - Pretreatment: 40 CFR Part 403 + BMC Chapter 14.12
  - Civil penalty for IWP violations: up to $10,000/day (BMC § 14.12.410)
  - Industrial discharge permit (IWP): apply through City Engineer's office
  - Source: https://www.bakersfieldcity.us/632/Wastewater; https://bakersfield.municipal.codes/Code/14.12
- **North of River Sanitation District (NORSD)**: serves northern Bakersfield area — https://www.norsd.com/
- **Local limits (mg/L)**: [VERIFY — BMC Chapter 14.12 full text not extractable at time of fetch; contact Bakersfield Wastewater Division directly]
- RWQCB: Region 5 (Central Valley) for San Joaquin Valley floor; Region 6 (Lahontan) for eastern Kern; Region 3 (Central Coast) for small Carrizo Plain portion

### Stormwater

- **MS4 Permit**: Kern County operates under a Phase I MS4 permit jointly with the City of Bakersfield — **Order No. R5-2016-0040-018**, NPDES Permit No. **CAS082597** (RWQCB Region 5 — Central Valley)
- Permit authorizes stormwater discharges from municipal storm sewer systems to Kern River and Tulare Lake Basin canals
- **Industrial General Permit (IGP)**: industrial facilities in regulated SIC categories (manufacturing, livestock, transportation, etc.) must enroll under the statewide Industrial General Permit (Order 2014-0057-DWQ, as amended)
- NPDES construction stormwater: applies to projects disturbing ≥1 acre
- NPDES applicability form: https://www.kernpublicworks.com/services/development/services/engineering-resources/grading/npdes-applicability-form
- Source: https://archive.epa.gov/region9/water/archive/web/pdf/20120828-kern-county-ms4report-final.pdf; https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/centralvalley/water_issues/storm_water/municipal_permits/

### Zoning (Unincorporated)

- **Governing code**: Title 19 — Zoning, Kern County Ordinance Code
- **Industrial districts**: M-1 Light Industrial (Ch. 19.36), M-2 Medium Industrial (Ch. 19.38), M-3 Heavy Industrial (Ch. 19.40)
- All industrial zones subject to Special Development Standards — **Chapter 19.80** (road dedication, utilities, fire protection, incompatible use separation, dust control)
- CUP required in M-2 for: cryogenic fluids, LPG >2,000 gal (with ½-mile residential setback), explosives, blasting agents; M-3 permits those uses as of right
- Planning & Natural Resources Dept: https://kernplanning.com; (661) 862-8600
- Source: https://library.municode.com/ca/kern_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT19ZO

See also: `zoning-industrial.md`

## Key Split-Jurisdiction Notes

- **CUPA split**: Bakersfield has its own CUPA (Bakersfield Fire Dept). County Public Health is CUPA everywhere else.
- **Air district split**: Valley Air (SJVAPCD) for the floor; Eastern Kern APCD for the desert/high-desert east (~3,704 sq mi). Verify by address before routing.
- **Wastewater split**: No single county POTW pretreatment program. Industrial users route through the POTW serving their location (Bakersfield, NORSD, or others).
- **Kern spans three water board regions** (3, 5, 6) — confirm watershed for the controlling RWQCB.
