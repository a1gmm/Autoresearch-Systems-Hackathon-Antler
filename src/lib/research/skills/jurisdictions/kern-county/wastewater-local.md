## Wastewater Authority — Unincorporated Kern County

Kern County Public Works manages sewer infrastructure for unincorporated county service areas. **The county does not operate a POTW with a federal pretreatment program of its own** — industrial users connecting to county-served areas discharge through service agreements with adjacent POTWs (City of Bakersfield, North of River Sanitation District, or others depending on location).

- Sewer information: https://www.kernpublicworks.com/services/solid-waste/sewer-information
- Development standards for sanitary sewers: https://www.kernpublicworks.com/services/development/services/engineering-resources/development-standards/div-3-sanitary-sewers

## Primary POTW — City of Bakersfield

For the vast majority of industrial facilities in Kern County (in or near Bakersfield), the relevant POTW is the **City of Bakersfield Wastewater Division**, which operates:

- **Wastewater Treatment Plant No. 2** (east of Hwy 99)
- **Wastewater Treatment Plant No. 3** (west of Hwy 99)

NPDES permit No.: CAS082597 (shared MS4 permit; WWTP NPDES: see CIWQS facility 273208 for Plant No. 2 — https://ciwqs.waterboards.ca.gov/ciwqs/readOnly/CiwqsReportServlet?reportName=facilityAtAGlance&placeID=273208)

### Sewer-Use Ordinance — City of Bakersfield

Governing ordinance for industrial discharge into City of Bakersfield sewer system: **Bakersfield Municipal Code (BMC) Chapter 14.12 — Sewers**

- Full chapter: https://bakersfield.municipal.codes/Code/14.12
- Pretreatment basis: 40 CFR Part 403 (National Pretreatment Standards)
- Categorical pretreatment standards: EPA rules (40 CFR Parts 405–471) apply to applicable SIC categories
- Civil penalty for permit violations: up to **$10,000 per day** (BMC § 14.12.410)
- Criminal penalty: fine up to **$2,500** and/or imprisonment up to 6 months (BMC § 14.12.410)

### Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit (IWP) — Bakersfield

- Application form: available from City of Bakersfield, Office of the City Engineer
- Approval conditions (BMC § 14.12.250): applicant must comply with all Chapter 14.12 requirements, furnish requested information, and city engineer determines adequate POTW capacity
- Reporting (BMC § 14.12.325): industrial users must submit results of sampling and analysis identifying nature and concentration of regulated pollutants (daily max and average)
- Confidential data: BMC § 14.12.130; all other reports available via public records request (40 CFR 403.14(b))

### Septage/Grease Hauler Discharge — Bakersfield

- Governed by **BMC Chapter 14.12** and the City's Standard Provisions (January 1, 2024)
- Permits issued by City's Wastewater Division; requires current Kern County Environmental Health (KCEHD) permit for renewal
- Prohibited discharges: pH < 5.0 or > 12.0; hazardous materials; brines exceeding city limits; any material that would harm treatment plant stability
- Liquid industrial waste: not permitted at septage/grease receiving stations; contact Wastewater Division separately (BMC § 14.12.370(B))
- Contact: City of Bakersfield Wastewater Division — https://www.bakersfieldcity.us/632/Wastewater

## Regional Water Board

Kern County spans multiple RWQCB regions — confirm the controlling board for your specific watershed:

| Area | Regional Board |
|---|---|
| San Joaquin Valley (Bakersfield, west Kern) | RWQCB Region 5 — Central Valley (https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/centralvalley/) |
