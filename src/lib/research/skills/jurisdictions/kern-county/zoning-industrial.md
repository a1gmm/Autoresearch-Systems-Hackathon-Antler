## Industrial Zoning Districts

Kern County Title 19 contains three industrial districts:

| District | Chapter | Name |
|---|---|---|
| M-1 | 19.36 | Light Industrial |
| M-2 | 19.38 | Medium Industrial |
| M-3 | 19.40 | Heavy Industrial |

### M-1: Light Industrial District (Chapter 19.36)

- Intended for light manufacturing, processing, and distribution uses with minimal off-site impacts
- **Aboveground flammable/combustible liquid tanks**: permitted (Kern County Fire Code § 17.32.003 exception)
- Compressed natural gas (CNG): [VERIFY whether expressly permitted as of right or requires CUP in M-1]
- Sources: http://kerncounty-ca.elaws.us/code/coor_title19_ch19.36; https://library.municode.com/ca/kern_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT19ZO_CH19.36LIINDI

### M-2: Medium Industrial District (Chapter 19.38)

- Broader range of industrial uses than M-1; hazardous-material-related uses require CUP
- Permitted by right: includes most manufacturing and warehousing uses
- **Conditional uses requiring CUP (Planning & Natural Resources Dept)**:
  - Flammable cryogenic fluids in stationary containers (Fire Code § 17.32.002)
  - LPG storage >2,000 gal aggregate (Fire Code § 17.32.004; must also be ≥½ mile from residential)
  - Explosives and blasting agents (Fire Code § 17.32.005)
  - Compressed natural gas storage (Fire Code § 17.32.006)
- Equipment storage yards: require site plan approval and a no-fee tracking permit (not a formal building permit)
- Sources: http://kerncounty-ca.elaws.us/code/coor_title19_ch19.38_sec19.38.030; https://library.municode.com/ca/kern_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT19ZO_CH19.38MEINDI

### M-3: Heavy Industrial District (Chapter 19.40)

- Broadest industrial uses; most hazardous-materials storage allowed as of right (no CUP)
- **Permitted uses include** (H&S Code cross-references via fire code zones):
  - Flammable cryogenic fluid storage in stationary containers (as of right — no CUP needed)
  - LPG storage >2,000 gal (as of right, subject to ½-mile separation from residential)
  - Explosives and blasting agents (as of right)
  - Compressed natural gas storage (as of right)
  - Aboveground flammable/combustible liquid tanks (as of right)
  - Fruit, vegetable and plant products processing (including packing, canning, preserving, shipping)
  - LPG bulk storage or distribution (>2,000 gal capacity with distance requirements)
- Source: http://kerncounty-ca.elaws.us/code/coor_title19_ch19.40_sec19.40.020

## Special Development Standards (Chapter 19.80)

All development in M-1, M-2, and M-3 zones is subject to Chapter 19.80 Special Development Standards, which impose:

- Dedication of land for road widening; paving of street frontage with curb, gutter, and sidewalk
- Underground utility placement
- Drainage water retention on-site
- Water supply and sewage system requirements
- Fire protection facilities
- Separation of incompatible land uses
- Screening of outside industrial storage areas
- Landscaping and parking provisions
- Dust control during grading and operation
- Trash receptacle screening

Review occurs automatically during building or grading permit applications.

Source: https://kernplanning.com/informational-guides/info-guide-special-development-standards/

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

- Issuing authority: Kern County Planning & Natural Resources Department
- CUP required in M-2 for hazardous-material-intensive uses (see above); M-3 typically permits those uses by right
- Fire Code zone-based restrictions (§§ 17.32.002–17.32.006) are the primary driver of CUP triggers for EHS-relevant industrial uses
- For uses not requiring formal building permits (e.g., equipment yards in M-2): obtain site plan approval and no-fee tracking permit before establishing use
- Planning fees: https://kernplanning.com/planning-fees/

## Oil and Gas

A focused 2025 amendment to Title 19 (CEQA tracking: 2013081079) addresses oil and gas local permitting. Oil and gas extraction is a significant land use in Kern County; related zoning provisions may be found in the 2025(A) revision package.
