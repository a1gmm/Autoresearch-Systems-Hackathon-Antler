## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air

- **Controlling district:** San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution Control District (SJVAPCD / Valley Air District). Bakersfield lies entirely within SJVAPCD jurisdiction. The city has no independent air permitting authority.
- SJVAPCD Southern Regional Office: 2700 M Street, Suite 275, Bakersfield, CA 93301-2370; (661) 326-6900
- Stationary sources (refineries, manufacturing) require SJVAPCD Authority to Construct + Permit to Operate; major sources also hold Title V operating permits.
- NOTE: Eastern Kern APCD (kernair.org) covers the eastern high-desert portion of Kern County; it does NOT cover the city of Bakersfield.
- Source: https://www.valleyair.org/

### CUPA (Unified Program)

- **CUPA Agency:** Bakersfield Fire Department, Fire Prevention Division — Environmental Services Section. Bakersfield has been a certified independent CUPA since 1996; it does NOT roll up to Kern County Public Health (which is the CUPA for the rest of the county).
- GOTCHA: Facilities inside Bakersfield city limits must file with BFD CUPA, NOT Kern County Environmental Health.
- Programs administered (all six statutory elements): Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Hazardous Waste Generator permits, Risk Management Prevention Plan (RMPP/CalARP), and Tiered Permitting for hazardous waste treatment.
- Approximately 1,600 regulated facilities. Staff: seven inspectors + one supervisor, all trained to Hazardous Materials Specialist level. Annual fire inspection is combined with CUPA inspection.
- California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) portal used for electronic submissions.
- Fee schedule: BMC Master Fee Schedule Section B-40. See cupa-local.md for detail.
- Source: https://www.bakersfieldcity.us/245/Fire-Prevention

### Fire

- **AHJ:** Bakersfield Fire Department — https://www.bakersfieldcity.us/233/Fire
- Fire Prevention Division phone: (661) 326-3979
- Adopted code: **2022 California Fire Code** (including Appendix Chapter 4 and Appendices B, BB, C, CC, D, E, F, G, H, I, J, K, L, and O), adopted by **Ordinance 5119** (2023). Codified in BMC Chapter 15.65.
- Local amendments exist in Chapter 15.65 (e.g., §15.65.080 amends CFC §105.5 required operational permits; §15.65.090 amends §105.6.1 automatic fire-extinguishing systems; §15.65.100 amends §105.6.6 fire alarm systems; §15.65.110 amends §907.1.5 fire alarm use; §15.65.160 adds CFC §116; §15.65.193 amends Appendix C §C102.1 fire hydrant minimums).
- See fire-code-local.md for detail.
- Source: https://bakersfield.municipal.codes/Code/15.65_Art1

### Wastewater

- **POTW Operator:** City of Bakersfield Public Works Department, Wastewater Division — (661) 326-3249; 6901 McCutchen Road, Bakersfield, CA 93313
- Two treatment plants:
  - **Wastewater Treatment Plant #2** — 1700 Planz, Bakersfield, CA 93307. Serves area east of Hwy 99. WDR Order R5-2009-0122. Also accepts septage/grease hauler discharge.
  - **Wastewater Treatment Plant #3** — 6901 McCutchen Rd, Bakersfield, CA 93313. Serves area west of Hwy 99. Expanded to 32 MGD capacity. Also accepts septage/grease hauler discharge.
- Sewer use ordinance: **BMC Chapter 14.12** (Sewers). Governs pretreatment, discharge permits (IWP), local limits, SIU reporting, FOG, and enforcement.
  - §14.12.220 — prohibited discharge standards + BMPs including FOG prohibition (§14.12.220 B.9)
  - §14.12.250 — industrial discharge permit application procedure and approval criteria
  - §14.12.310 — separation of domestic and industrial wastewaters required
  - §14.12.325 — reporting requirements for significant industrial users (SIUs)
  - §14.12.370 — septage/hauler discharge authorization
  - §14.12.410 — civil penalties: up to $10,000/day per violation
- Satellite collection system agencies discharging to city system: East Niles Community Services District, Kern Sanitation Authority (KSA), and County Service Area 71 (CSA71).
- IWP applications: available from the office of the City Engineer; contact Wastewater Division at (661) 326-3249.
- See wastewater-local.md for detail.
- Source: https://bakersfield.municipal.codes/Code/14.12 | https://www.bakersfieldcity.us/632/Wastewater

### Stormwater

- **Co-permittees:** City of Bakersfield AND County of Kern are co-permittees under **NPDES Permit No. CA00883399**, Order No. 5-01-130 (Central Valley RWQCB, originally adopted 24 June 1994, re-issued 2001). Phase I MS4 permit.
- Receiving waters: Kern River and various canals of the Tulare Lake Basin; roughly 200 retention basins capture most runoff.
- Local stormwater ordinance: **BMC Chapter 8.34** (Industrial Stormwater). §8.34.060 requires holders of NPDES industrial stormwater permits who discharge to the city's storm drainage system to comply with the city's MS4 permit requirements.
- Industrial facilities subject to the state Industrial General Permit (IGP) must also comply with BMC Ch. 8.34 local requirements.
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/water_issues/programs/stormwater/docs/ms4permit_bakersfield_5_01_130.pdf | https://bakersfield.municipal.codes/Code/8.34

### Zoning

- **Zoning code:** BMC Title 17 (Zoning Ordinance). Three industrial zone designations:
  - **M-1** (Light Manufacturing) — Chapter 17.28
  - **M-2** (General Manufacturing) — Chapter 17.30
  - **M-3** (Heavy Industrial) — Chapter 17.31
- All industrial zones require site plan review (Ch. 17.08) and conditional use permits (Ch. 17.64) for certain uses.
- See zoning-industrial.md for detail.
- Source: https://bakersfield.municipal.codes/Code/17 | https://docs.bakersfieldcity.us/weblink/0/edoc/1376464/17.28%20%20%20M-1%20Zone.pdf
