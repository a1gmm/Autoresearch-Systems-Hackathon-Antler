## Sewer Use Ordinance

**BMC Chapter 14.12** (Sewers) — Title 14 (Water and Sewers)
Link: https://bakersfield.municipal.codes/Code/14.12

This chapter is the city's sewer use ordinance (SUO) and pretreatment program legal authority. It implements federal pretreatment regulations (40 CFR Part 403).

## Significant Industrial Users (SIU) Definition

Under BMC 14.12.030 (consistent with 40 CFR 403 definitions):
- All industrial users subject to **categorical pretreatment standards**, OR
- Those discharging an average of **25,000 gallons per day or more** of process wastewater to the POTW, OR
- Those whose discharge constitutes 5% or more of the hydraulic or organic capacity of the POTW, OR
- Those determined to have significant impact on the POTW or receiving water quality.

## Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit (IWP)

**Authority:** BMC §14.12.250
**Application:** Submit completed City application form to the office of the City Engineer. The City may require additional characterization data beyond the standard form.
**Approval criteria:** Applicant must have complied with all applicable requirements of BMC Chapter 14.12, submitted all requested information, and the City Engineer must determine there is adequate capacity in city facilities to convey, treat, and dispose of the wastewaters.
**Contact for IWP inquiries:** Wastewater Division, (661) 326-3249

Certification requirement: Signing official must certify under penalty of law that the application is true, accurate, and complete (40 CFR Part 403 §403.14(b)).

Source: https://bakersfield.municipal.codes/Code/14.12.250

## Local Discharge Limits

The chapter defines "local limits" as one of three types of pretreatment standards (alongside prohibited discharge standards and categorical pretreatment standards). The city publishes local limits as part of its federally approved pretreatment program.

[VERIFY: The specific numerical local limits table (e.g., metals, BOD, TSS, pH, oil & grease mg/L thresholds) is not reproduced in publicly indexed web pages. Contact the Wastewater Division at (661) 326-3249 or request the Industrial Pretreatment Program document directly.]

Known general prohibitions from the ordinance:
- pH limits enforced at all times (hauled waste: pH must be 5.0–12.0)
- Total dissolved solids above 5,000 mg/L: flagged as characteristic requiring disclosure
- Dissolved sulfides above 0.1 mg/L: flagged
- Flammable materials, toxic gases, radioactive materials: prohibited

## FOG (Fats, Oils & Grease) Program

**Authority:** BMC §14.12.220 B.9
- BFD/KCEHD jointly distribute FOG BMPs to food establishments.
- Wastewater Industrial Inspection Staff inspect restaurants annually.
- WWTP #2 operates as a FOG/septage receiving station.
- "HOT SPOT" list maintained for recurring FOG problem locations; extra cleaning frequency applied.
- Enforcement: City Industrial Waste Inspectors conduct new construction inspections and enforce per BMC 14.12.

## Septage/Grease Hauler Discharge Permits

**Authority:** BMC §14.12.370
- Permits required for all haulers; issued by Wastewater Division.
- Permits are vehicle-specific and non-transferable on ownership change.
- Renewal application due at least 10 days before expiration.
- Discharge fees: rate per gallon set by resolution each fiscal year; billed monthly.
- Plant 2 connection: 6" male cam-lock fitting. Plant 3 septage: 4" male cam-lock; Plant 3 grease: 6" male cam-lock.
- Application form: City of Bakersfield Wastewater Division, 6901 McCutchen Rd., (661) 326-3249.
