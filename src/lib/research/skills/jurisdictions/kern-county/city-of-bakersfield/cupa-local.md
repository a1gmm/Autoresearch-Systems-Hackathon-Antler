## CUPA Agency

**Bakersfield Fire Department, Fire Prevention Division — Environmental Services Section**
- Bakersfield has operated as a **certified independent CUPA since 1996**.
- The city's CUPA is entirely separate from Kern County Public Health Services, which is the CUPA for all unincorporated Kern County and other cities in the county.
- Facilities located inside Bakersfield city limits must submit to BFD CUPA, NOT Kern County.

## Operational Scale

- Approximately **1,600 regulated facilities** within city limits.
- Staff: seven inspectors + one supervisor, all trained to Hazardous Materials Specialist level.
- BFD combines annual fire safety inspections with applicable CUPA program inspections (reduces business burden).
- Staff respond to chemical spills and hazmat incidents.
- Electronic reporting portal: **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System).

## Local Hazmat Ordinance

Hazmat ordinance authority flows from BMC Chapter 15.65 (Fire Code adoption) and the State Health & Safety Code unified program statutes. There is no separate standalone hazardous materials municipal code chapter identified in city sources; CUPA enforcement is conducted under the fire prevention ordinance authority and state HSC.
