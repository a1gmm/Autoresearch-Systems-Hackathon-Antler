## Zoning Code

**BMC Title 17** (Zoning Ordinance)
Current through Ordinance 5211, passed February 25, 2026.
Online: https://bakersfield.municipal.codes/Code/17

Three industrial zone designations:

| Zone | Name | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| M-1 | Light Manufacturing | 17.28 |
| M-2 | General Manufacturing | 17.30 |
| M-3 | Heavy Industrial | 17.31 |

All industrial development is also subject to:
- Site plan review: Chapter 17.08
- Landscaping: Chapter 17.61
- Off-street parking and loading: Chapter 17.58
- Signs: Chapter 17.60
- Conditional use permits (CUP): Chapter 17.64

## M-1 Zone — Light Manufacturing (Chapter 17.28)

**Purpose:** Allows light manufacturing uses that do not produce fumes, odor, dust, smoke, gas, noise, or vibration detrimentally impacting neighboring properties. Uses must meet applicable adopted city or public agency standards.

**Building height:** 6 stories, not to exceed 75 feet.

**Setbacks:**
- Front yard: minimum 10 feet from front property line
- Side yard: 20 feet where lot abuts R, E, or MH zone; 10 feet on corner lot street-side; none otherwise
- Rear yard: 20 feet where lot abuts R, E, or MH zone; none otherwise

**Permitted uses (by-right):** Includes all C-O, C-1, and C-2 zone uses (except residential dwelling use), plus:
- Animal hospitals, kennels, veterinaries
- Automobile/light truck parking and storage
- Automobile assembly, body/fender work, painting, upholstering, dismantling (wholly within a building)
- Bakeries, bottling plants, cabinet/carpenter shops
- Concrete batch plants (portable, not to exceed 2-yard capacity)
- Contractor's plants and storage yards
- Electric welding and electroplating
- Ice and cold storage plants
- Laboratories (experimental, research, testing)
- Laundries, cleaning and dyeing plants
- Lumberyards, machine shops (except punch presses over 20 tons, drop hammers, automatic screw machines)
- Paint mixing plants (no boiling process)
- Public utilities service yards, power plants, distributing stations
- Rubber fabrication; sheet metal shops; stone monument works
- Storage spaces for transit/transportation equipment
- Tool rental and equipment; truck repairing/overhauling; welding/metal fabricating/blacksmith shops
- Wholesale businesses, storage buildings, warehouses
- Manufacturing of: arts and crafts, billboards/electric signs, ceramic products, clothing, cosmetics/drugs/pharmaceuticals, electronic instruments, food products (except rendering/refining fats or oils), furniture, musical instruments/toys, prefabricated buildings, shoes, soap (cold mix only), textiles
- Manufacture from previously prepared materials: bone, cellophane, canvas, cloth, cork, feathers, felt, fiber, fur, glass, hair, horn, leather, paper, plastics, precious/semi-precious metals or stones, shell, textiles, tobacco, wood, yarns, paint (no boiling process)
- Adult entertainment establishments (subject to Ch. 17.69)
- Bakersfield Airpark property: additional aviation-related uses permitted (crop dusting, aircraft rental, repair, runways, aviation fuel sales, aviation manufacturing, flight training, freight/package delivery, hangars)

**Uses requiring CUP:** Agricultural packing plants; aircraft and automobile factories; automobile parts manufacturers; battery manufacturers; large breweries or distilleries; certain medical facilities (contagious/mental/drug/liquor); equestrian establishments; freighting/trucking yards or terminals; livestock slaughtering (wholly within a building); machine shops including punch presses and automatic screw machines; planing mills; residential dwellings; tire rebuilding/recapping/retreading plants; food/shelter service agencies.

**Open storage:** Must be screened by solid wall or fence not less than 6 feet high; materials shall not be stacked above screening height. Outside storage areas must use permanent dust binders or control measures consistent with air pollution control regulations.

Source: https://docs.bakersfieldcity.us/weblink/0/edoc/1376464/17.28%20%20%20M-1%20Zone.pdf (January 2018)

## M-2 Zone — General Manufacturing (Chapter 17.30)

**Purpose:** Allows all M-1 uses plus heavier manufacturing and processing operations.

**Building height:** 13 stories, not to exceed 150 feet.

**Setbacks:**
- Front yard: minimum 10 feet
- Side yard: 20 feet where lot abuts R, E, or MH zone; 10 feet on corner lot street-side; none otherwise
- Rear yard: 20 feet where lot abuts R, E, or MH zone; none otherwise

**Permitted uses (by-right):** All M-1 zone uses, plus:
- Acetylene gas manufacture or storage
- Aircraft and automobile factories
- Agricultural packing plants (vegetables and fruits)
- Alcohol and alcoholic beverages manufacture
- Ammonia, chlorine, and bleaching powder manufacture
- Automobile and truck manufacture
- Bag cleaning; blast furnaces; boiler or tank works
- Large breweries or distilleries
- Brick, tile, or terra cotta products manufacture
- Building materials manufacture; carpet and rug manufacture
- Cement and lime manufacturing (equipped to collect at least 97% of particulate matter from kiln gases)
- Clay product manufacture; coke ovens; cotton gins or oil mills
- Crematories; creosote treatment or manufacture
- Disinfectant manufacture; distillation of coal, wood, or tar; dyestuffs manufacture
- Exterminator or insect poison manufacture; feed, flour, and grain mills
- Firearms manufacture; forge plants; freighting and trucking yards and terminals
- Freight classification yards
- Glass and glass product manufacture; grain elevators
- Iron, steel, brass, or copper foundries or fabrication plants and heavy weight casting
- Lamp black manufacture; linoleum or oiled products manufacture
- Machine shops including punch presses and automatic screw machines
- Ore reduction; paint, oil, shellac, turpentine, or varnish manufacture
- Paper or pulp manufacture; petroleum refining and reclaiming plants
- Planing mills; plastic manufacture; potash works
- Railroad roundhouses and repair shops; rolling mills
- Rubber processing and manufacture; sawmills; soap manufacture
- Sodium compounds manufacture; starch manufacture
- Tar roofing/waterproofing or other tar products manufacture
- Automobile and truck parts manufacturer; battery manufacturer
- Creameries; metal container manufacturer
- Tire rebuilding, recapping, and retreading plants
- Adult entertainment establishments (subject to Ch. 17.69)
- Food/shelter service agencies (as defined in §17.04.285)

**Uses requiring CUP:** Acid manufacture; ammunition manufacture; cement/lime/gypsum/plaster of Paris manufacture; chemical manufacture; curing/tanning/storage of rawhide or skins; distillation of bones; drop forge industries; dumps and refuse disposal areas; explosives manufacture or storage; fat rendering; feed and fuel yards; fertilizer manufacture; garbage/offal/dead animal reduction or dumping; gas manufacture; gelatin or size manufacture; glucose or dextrin manufacture; glue manufacture; nonmineral oil extraction plants; sewer farms or sewage disposal plants; smelting of tin, copper, zinc, or iron ores; slaughterhouse; scrap metal yards/junkyards; wineries.

Source: https://docs.bakersfieldcity.us/weblink/0/edoc/1376465/17.30%20%20%20M-2%20Zone.pdf (January 2018)

## M-3 Zone — Heavy Industrial (Chapter 17.31)

**Purpose:** "Intended to provide areas suitable for the development of heavy manufacturing and industrial uses, processing of animals for food or byproducts, waste recycling or disposal processing, and processing or manufacture of undesirable products. Uses allowed in the M-3 zone are incompatible with other land uses and should be located in places substantially removed from uses which may be impacted from M-3 uses." (§17.31.010)

**Building height:** Not to exceed 210 feet (approximately 14 stories).

**Setbacks:**
- Front yard: minimum 10 feet
- Side yard: 20 feet where lot abuts R, E, or MH zone; 10 feet on corner lot street-side; none otherwise
- Rear yard: 20 feet where lot abuts R, E, or MH zone; none otherwise
- Minimum lot area: none

**Permitted uses (by-right):** Acetylene gas manufacture and storage; acid manufacture; alcohol and alcoholic beverage manufacturing; beef/swine/poultry/rabbit slaughter; blast furnaces; cement and lime manufacturing (97% particulate collection); chemical manufacture; clay product manufacture; coke ovens; cotton gins or oil mills; creosote treatment; curing/tanning/storage of rawhide or skins; disinfectant manufacture; distillation of coal, wood, bones, or tar; drop forge industries; explosives manufacture or storage; exterminator/insect poison manufacture; fat rendering; feed and fuel yards; fertilizer manufacture; forge plants; gelatin or size manufacture; glass/glass product manufacture; glucose or dextrin manufacture; glue manufacture; iron/steel/brass/copper foundries and heavy weight casting; nonmineral oil extracting plants; ore reduction; paint/oil/shellac/turpentine/varnish manufacture; paper or pulp manufacture; **petroleum refining, reclaiming plants, and associated uses**; rolling mills; rubber processing and manufacture; sawmills; smelting of tin, copper, zinc, or iron ores; scrap metal yards/junkyards; tar roofing/waterproofing/tar products; accessory buildings; caretaker/night security dwelling (accessory and incidental).

**Uses requiring CUP:** Coal-fired cogeneration facilities or steam generators; community septic disposal systems; electrical power generator plants; **hazardous waste disposal facilities**; mining and mineral extraction; nonhazardous oily waste disposal facilities; sanitary landfills; septage disposal sites; sewage treatment plants; transfer stations; waste-to-energy facilities.

Source: https://docs.bakersfieldcity.us/weblink/0/edoc/721695/17.31%20%20%20M-3%20Zone.pdf (November 2012)

## CUP Process

Conditional use permits for industrial zones are processed under **Chapter 17.64**. All industrial development (both permitted and CUP uses) requires site plan review under Chapter 17.08 prior to permit issuance.

Contact: City of Bakersfield Development Services Department (Planning Division)
Website: https://www.bakersfieldcity.us (search "Planning" or "Zoning")

## Additional Development Standards (All Industrial Zones)

- Residential-adjacent buffer: Solid masonry wall, minimum 6 feet (from highest grade), required where industrial development abuts residential-zoned or residential-designated property. Front yard walls capped at 4 feet; street frontages may use landscaping instead of or combined with wall.
- Open storage screening: Solid wall or fence, minimum 6 feet, solid gates at openings; materials may not be stacked above screening.
- Dust control: Outside storage areas must use permanent dust binders or dust control measures consistent with SJVAPCD air pollution control regulations.
- Rooftop screening: Required for rooftop equipment visible from adjacent residential property (new construction and remodels involving 50% or more of roof structure or floor area addition).
