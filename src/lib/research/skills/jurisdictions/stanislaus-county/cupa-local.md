## CUPA Agency

**Stanislaus County Department of Environmental Resources (DER) — Hazardous Materials Division** is the sole CUPA for the entire county. There is no separate city or fire-district CUPA anywhere in Stanislaus County; all regulated facilities (including those in Modesto, Turlock, Ceres, and all unincorporated areas) are served by the county DER.

- **Address:** 3800 Cornucopia Way, Suite C, Modesto, CA 95358
- **Phone:** (209) 525-6700
- **Hours:** Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM
- **Web:** https://www.stancounty.com/er/hazmat/cupa.shtm

> Note: Medical Waste Program and Household Hazardous Waste collection are also administered by DER but are not Unified Program elements.

## Submission Portal

All HMBP submittals, annual certifications, and updates must be made electronically through the **California Environmental Reporting System (CERS)**: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/

Annual certification: businesses must review and re-certify all HMBP elements in CERS each year.

## Local Hazmat Ordinance

No separate local hazmat ordinance number was found published online. The DER Hazardous Materials Division operates under state law (Health & Safety Code Chapter 6.95 et seq.) and the CalEPA Unified Program regulations (27 CCR Division 3). The Stanislaus County Code is codified on eCode360 but no county-specific hazmat ordinance chapter separate from the state statutory framework was identified.
