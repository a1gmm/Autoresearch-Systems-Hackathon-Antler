## Adopted Fire Code Edition

- **Primary code:** 2022 California Fire Code (CFC), including Appendix Chapter 4 and Appendices A, B, BB, C, CC, D, E, F, G, I, K and Division II Administration, as published by the International Code Council.
- **County Code chapter:** Title 16, **Chapter 16.55 — Fire Code** of the Stanislaus County Code.
- **Adopting ordinance:** Ordinance No. **CS 1338** (2022).
- **Code copy on file:** Stanislaus County Fire Prevention Division, 1010 Tenth Street Place, Modesto, CA — public examination copy maintained there.

> NOTE: The 2025 California Fire Code took effect statewide on 2026-01-01 per the California Building Standards Commission. ## Fire AHJ — Unincorporated County

Stanislaus County is a rural patchwork with **no single county fire department**. The fire authority having jurisdiction (AHJ) for fire code/prevention purposes in unincorporated areas is:

- **Stanislaus County Office of Emergency Services — Fire Warden / Fire Prevention Bureau**
  - Address: 1010 Tenth Street Place, Modesto, CA
  - Web: https://oes.stancounty.gov/divisions/fire-warden/fire-prevention-bureau
  - The Fire Warden is appointed by the Board of Supervisors pursuant to Government Code § 24008.
  - The Fire Prevention Bureau performs plan review, permitting, and inspections for unincorporated areas on behalf of approximately 14 independent fire protection districts.

**Incorporated cities** maintain their own fire AHJs (see city-level files):
- City of Modesto — Modesto Fire Department
- City of Turlock — Turlock Fire Department (administrative contract with Modesto FD through June 2026)
- City of Ceres — fire protection services provided by Modesto FD under contract
- Stanislaus Consolidated Fire Protection District — adopted its own ordinance (Ordinance No. 2022-13) tracking the 2022 CFC

## Key Local Amendments (Chapter 16.55.040)

The following amendments to the 2022 CFC are adopted in Stanislaus County Code § 16.55.040:

### Flammable/Combustible Liquids — Bulk Plants (§ 5705)
- Construction of **new bulk plants** for storage of flammable or combustible liquids is restricted to areas of Stanislaus County zoned **Heavy Industrial** (M district).
- Existing nonconforming bulk plants that substantially comply with the code may continue if the Fire Chief grants a permit.

### Ethanol and Biodiesel Manufacturing — Residential Prohibition
- § 5705.3.5.2 amended by adding subparagraph 7.2: **manufacture of ethanol** is prohibited in Residential Occupancies and in U Occupancies associated with Residential Occupancies.
- Similarly, **manufacture of biodiesel** is prohibited in Residential and associated U Occupancies.
