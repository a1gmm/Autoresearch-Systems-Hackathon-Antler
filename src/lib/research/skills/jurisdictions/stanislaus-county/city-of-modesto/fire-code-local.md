## Fire AHJ

**Modesto Fire Department** is the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) within City of Modesto limits.

- Fire Prevention Division: 209-577-5200
- Fire Administration: 209-572-9590
- Fire Prevention plan review: Deputy Fire Marshal reviews plans for compliance with fire protection and life-safety codes adopted at state and local level.
- Address: 1010 10th Street, Modesto, CA 95354 (City CED/Fire offices)

## Adopted Edition

The City of Modesto enforces the **2022 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9)**. The 2022 CFC is based on the 2021 International Fire Code (IFC) with California amendments.

- The state adopted the 2022 CFC effective January 1, 2023 (180-day rule per Health & Safety Code § 17958).
- Modesto's own fire code chapter is located in **Title 9 (Building Regulations)** of the Modesto Municipal Code (library.municode.com/ca/modesto). The specific chapter number within Title 9 was not accessible online at time of research — [VERIFY — Municode access blocked; contact Fire Prevention to confirm adopting ordinance number and any local city amendments].

## County Fire Code (Coordination Context)

The **Stanislaus County Fire Warden's Office** coordinates fire code adoption among local fire agencies in the county, including cities contributing to the Less Than Countywide Fire Tax (LTCW). On December 20, 2022, the Stanislaus County Board of Supervisors adopted **Ordinance C.S. 1338**, amending **Chapter 16.55 of the Stanislaus County Code** to adopt by reference the 2022 California Fire Code with local changes, additions, and modifications.

- County ordinance scope: unincorporated areas of Stanislaus County (Chapter 16.55 is the county fire code chapter; not directly applicable within Modesto city limits but used as coordination baseline).
- County Fire Prevention Bureau: 1010 10th Street, Suite 3538, Modesto, CA 95354; (209) 552-3700 (Fire Marshal Matt Jenkins).

### Key County Local Amendments (Ordinance C.S. 1338 / Chapter 16.55)

These amendments apply to the unincorporated county; Modesto may have adopted similar provisions — verify directly:

- Section 105.6.15 of the 2022 CFC deleted.
- Construction of new bulk plants for flammable/combustible liquid storage restricted to areas zoned Heavy Industrial.
- Heating, lighting and cooking appliances using Class I, II, and III liquids not permitted inside buildings/structures (except single-family dwellings).
- Outside storage of liquefied petroleum gas (LP-gas) restricted to areas zoned Commercial-Light Industrial, Heavy Industrial, and automotive service stations.
- Appendices adopted: Chapter 4, Appendices A, B, BB, C, CC, D, E, F, G, I, K, and Division II Administration.

## Notes

- Modesto Fire Department also provides contract fire services to Ceres and administrative/command services to Turlock — but the fire AHJ for each of those cities is separate.
- For Modesto city-specific fire code amendments, contact the Modesto Fire Prevention Division at 209-577-5200 or visit Title 9 of the Modesto Municipal Code on Municode.
