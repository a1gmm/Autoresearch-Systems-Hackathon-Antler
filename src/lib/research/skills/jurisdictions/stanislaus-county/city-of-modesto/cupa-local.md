## Controlling CUPA

The City of Modesto is **not its own CUPA**. All Unified Program elements within Modesto city limits are administered by:

**Stanislaus County Department of Environmental Resources (DER) — Hazardous Materials Division**
- Address: 3800 Cornucopia Way, Suite C, Modesto, CA 95358
- Phone: (209) 525-6700
- Hours: Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–4:30 PM
- Division Manager: Alvin Lal
- Website: https://www.stancounty.com/er/hazmat/cupa.shtm

This is the sole countywide CUPA serving all jurisdictions in Stanislaus County, including incorporated cities such as Modesto.

## Programs Administered by Stanislaus County CUPA

All six CUPA elements are administered by the county DER:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — businesses with hazardous materials at or above 55 gallons (liquids), 500 lbs (solids), or 200 cf (compressed gases) must file via CERS. Annual recertification required (AB 2286, effective Jan 1, 2013).
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator Program** — EPA/California ID number required; reporting via CERS.
3. **Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program** — ownership/operation, upgrade/install, and closure reporting.
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Tank (AST) Program** — facilities with any tank >660 gallons or total >1,320 gallons.
5. **CalARP (Chemical Accident Prevention Program)** — facilities with regulated substances above threshold quantities.
6. **Medical Waste Program** — medical waste generator registration.
7. **E-Waste Recycling** — administered via household hazardous waste/e-waste events.

## Permit Application

Businesses submit the **Consolidated Permit Application Package (CPAP)** — long form — to the DER. Starting January 1, 2013, electronic submission through the **California Environmental Reporting System (CERS)** is mandatory for most elements.

## Local Hazmat Ordinance

No separate City of Modesto hazmat storage ordinance was found online. The statutory authority is California Health and Safety Code Chapter 6.95 (Division 20), which establishes minimum statewide standards for Hazardous Materials Business Plans. The county CUPA operates under this state framework. [VERIFY — city-level hazmat ordinance may exist in Modesto Municipal Code Title 3 or Title 5; not confirmed online.]

## Fee Schedule

The current DER fee schedule is published as an annual PDF on the Stanislaus County DER website. Contact DER at (209) 525-6700 for current fees.
