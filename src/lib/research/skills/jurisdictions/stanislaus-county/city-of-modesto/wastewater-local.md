## POTW Operator

The City of Modesto operates its own **Publicly Owned Treatment Works (POTW)** through the Utilities Department — Wastewater Services Division. This is a large regional facility, not a county or district system.

### Treatment Facilities

**Sutter Campus (Primary/Secondary):**
- 1221 Sutter Avenue, Modesto, CA 95351
- Treats approximately 20 million gallons per day
- Also receives cannery process water (Can Seg Line) seasonally

**Jennings Campus (Secondary/Tertiary):**
- 7007 Jennings Road, Modesto, CA 95353
- Treats approximately 15 million gallons per day to recycled water standards via Biological Nutrient Removal (BNR) / membrane bioreactor / UV disinfection

The combined system serves the City of Modesto plus portions of Empire Sanitary District and City of Ceres — total service population over 224,000. The facility is one of the largest beneficial reuse facilities in California; tertiary effluent is reused for agricultural irrigation (Modesto Ranch).

## NPDES Permits

The POTW holds multiple NPDES permits regulated by the **California Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (Region 5)**:

- **NPDES No. CAG585001** (General WDRs Order R5-2017-0085 / NOA R5-2017-0085-020) — discharge to San Joaquin River; also the permit under which the **pretreatment program** is regulated and reported.
- **NPDES No. CA0085316** (WDRs Order R5-2022-0034) — discharge to Delta-Mendota Canal (tertiary effluent).
- **WDRs Order R5-2025-0059** (adopted December 12, 2025) — governs land discharge / water recycling operations at both campuses; replaces prior Orders 94-030, 99-112.

## Industrial Pretreatment Program

Modesto operates an **active Industrial Pretreatment Program** administered by the **Environmental Compliance Section** (part of Utilities Department). The program is federally required because the POTW receives industrial pollutants and has design flows well above 5 MGD.

- **Permit types**: Categorical Industrial Users (CIUs), Major SIUs, Minor SIUs, Dry permits — as indicated on the city's Wastewater Discharge Permit application form.
- **Federal categorical regulations**: 40 CFR Part 403 (National Pretreatment Standards); applicable categorical pretreatment standards (e.g., 40 CFR Parts 405–471) applied per industry type.
- **Industrial pretreatment reporting**: Baseline Monitoring Reports, Periodic Compliance Reports, and Slug Discharge Control Plans required for permitted industrial users.
- **Slug Control Plans**: All permitted industrial users are required to develop slug discharge control plans.
- **Annual Pretreatment Report**: Published by Environmental Compliance Section; most recently noted for 2018 program year (verify for current year).
- **Enforcement Response Plan**: Available for public review.

### Sewer Use Ordinance / Municipal Code

The sewer use ordinance is codified in **Title 5 (Sanitation and Health)** of the Modesto Municipal Code, Chapter 5 (Sewer/Wastewater), Article 1. The section numbering scheme is 5-5.xxx (e.g., § 5-5.109 references general requirements for wastewater and sewer discharge).

- Full ordinance text: https://library.municode.com/ca/modesto/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT5SAHE

### Wastewater Discharge Permit Application

The permit application form (Wastewater Discharge Permit Application — Baseline Monitoring Report, Permit No. issuance period 2017-2021) references the following parameters tracked for industrial dischargers:

- BOD5, Suspended Solids, pH, Temperature, TDS
- Metals: Aluminum, Antimony, Arsenic, Barium, Beryllium, Boron, Cadmium, Chromium, Cobalt, Copper, Iron, Lead, Manganese, Mercury, Molybdenum, Nickel, Selenium, Silver, Tin, Vanadium, Zinc
- Organics: Solvents, Pesticides, Toxic Organics (TTO), Volatile Acids, Phenols, Hydrocarbons
- Other: Oil & Grease, Fluoride, Cyanide, Chlorine, Sulfide, Surfactants (MBAS), Radioactive materials
