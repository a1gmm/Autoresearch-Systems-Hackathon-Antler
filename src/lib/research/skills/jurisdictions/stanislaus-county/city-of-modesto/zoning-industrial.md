## Zoning Code Location

Industrial zoning for the City of Modesto is governed by **Title 10 (Zoning Regulations)** of the Modesto Municipal Code (MMC).

- Full zoning code: https://library.municode.com/ca/modesto/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT10ZORE
- City zoning page: https://www.modestogov.com/925/Zoning-Code-MMC---Title-10
- The code sets forth allowed land uses, site development standards, parking, signage, and other regulatory provisions for each zoning district.

## Industrial Zoning Districts

Modesto has three industrial zone classifications:

### C-M Zone (Commercial-Manufacturing)
Intended to promote, facilitate, and maintain land use compatibility by providing land area for specified **commercial and light industrial uses**. Typically a transitional zone between commercial and industrial areas.

### M-1 Zone (Light Industrial)
Intended to accommodate **light industrial, assembly, storage, warehouse, and similar uses** in terms of industrial activity. These uses may be located relatively near residential areas. Less intensive manufacturing; suitable buffer zone.

### M-2 Zone (Heavy/General Industrial)
Intended to provide suitable sites for a **wide range of industrial, manufacturing, business park and other similar uses** that are situated farther from non-industrial and potentially incompatible uses. Highest-intensity industrial operations located here.

## Land Use Table

Permitted and conditional land uses for all zones are compiled in **Title 10, Chapter 3, Article 1 — Land Use Table** (§ 10-3.101). Uses are designated as:
- **P** — Permitted by right
- **CUP** — Conditional Use Permit required
- **Prohibited** — not allowed in district

For specific industrial process/manufacturing uses, review the Land Use Table at: http://modesto-ca.elaws.us/code/coor_title10_ch3_art1_sec10-3.101

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

A CUP is required in Modesto for industrial uses that may have significant impacts on surrounding land uses or require site-specific review. Key process elements (based on city documentation):

- Application filed with the **Planning Division**, Community & Economic Development Department.
- CUP applications reviewed by the Planning Commission for larger/more impactful projects; some minor CUPs may be approved administratively.
- Public notice and hearing required.
- Conditions of approval are project-specific and may address hours of operation, noise, traffic, hazardous materials handling, landscaping, and compatibility with adjacent uses.

## Environmental Review Note

Industrial projects in Modesto may require CEQA review (Categorical Exemption, Mitigated Negative Declaration, or EIR) coordinated through the Planning Division. Projects generating hazardous materials may also require review by Stanislaus County DER (CUPA) and coordination with the Modesto Fire Prevention Division.
