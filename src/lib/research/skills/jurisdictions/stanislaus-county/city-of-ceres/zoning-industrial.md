## Zoning Code Location

**Title 18 (Zoning)** of the Ceres Municipal Code — "Ceres Zoning Ordinance." Full PDF available at the mcclibrary.blob.core.usgovcloudapi.net CDN (linked from Municode). The zoning code is cited as Title 18.

## Industrial District Names and Designations

Ceres has two primary industrial zones (plus an industrial sub-zone in the Mitchell Road Corridor Specific Plan):

### M-1 — Light Industrial Zone (Chapter 18.19)

**Purpose (§ 18.19.010):** Intended to provide for and promote concentrations of light and specialized industrial uses and administrative and research establishments.

**Principal uses (§ 18.19.020):** Light manufacturing, machine shops, bottling plants, sheet metal shops, boat building/repairs, upholstery shops, C-3 commercial uses appropriate to the zone, and similar non-nuisance uses. All uses subject to performance standards in Chapter 18.38 (Material Effects/Nuisance Standards).

**Conditional uses in M-1 (§ 18.19.040, CUP required per Chapter 18.30):**
- Automobile service stations (subject to § 18.28.090)
- Convenience-oriented retail/service commercial uses for principal-use employees
- M-2 uses compatible with existing and contemplated M-1 uses
- Recycling centers > 500 sq ft
- Wireless towers (monopole/guyed/self-supporting)
- Adult businesses (per Chapter 5.15)
- Industrial recreational facilities

**Prohibited in M-1 (§ 18.19.050):** Residential uses (except security-guard quarters), general retail/service commercial, M-2 uses (unless specifically permitted), fortunetelling, pallet storage/fabrication.

**Development standards (§ 18.19.060):**
- Minimum lot area: 10,000 sq ft (principal uses); lot area for CUP uses as specified by Planning Commission
- Maximum lot coverage: 60%
- Building height: As approved by Planning Commission (Airport Overlay constraints apply where applicable)
- Site plan approval required before any building is erected; submitted to and approved by Planning Commission

### M-2 — General Industrial Zone (Chapter 18.20)

**Purpose (§ 18.20.010):** Intended to provide for and encourage sound industrial development in large, exclusive areas; regulated to protect nearby uses from hazards, noise, and nuisances.

**Principal uses (§ 18.20.020):** All M-1 uses plus manufacturing, processing, packaging, and assembly uses (certain uses listed in § 18.34.080 require a CUP).

**Conditional uses in M-2 (§ 18.20.040, CUP required per Chapter 18.30):**
- Automobile service stations
- [Additional uses per § 18.20.040 — see ordinance for complete list]

**Prohibited in M-2 (§ 18.20.050):** Residential uses (unless authorized as accessory), [additional prohibitions per § 18.20.050].

**Development standards (§ 18.20.060):**
- Minimum lot area: 20,000 sq ft (with exceptions for some conditional uses)
- Building height: As approved by Planning Commission upon site plan approval (Airport Overlay constraints apply)

### IP — Industrial Park Zone (Mitchell Road Corridor Specific Plan)

An "Industrial Park" zone (abbreviated IP) is established under the Mitchell Road Corridor Specific Plan and appears in the zoning maps. It is a sub-zone of the Specific Plan area and its standards are governed by that plan document rather than the general Title 18 chapters.

## Performance Standards — Chapter 18.38

All industrial uses in M-1 and M-2 must comply with Chapter 18.38 (Material Effects Performance Standards), which regulates nuisance-type impacts including noise, vibration, dust, smoke, glare, and odor.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process — Chapter 18.30

**Decision-maker:** Ceres Planning Commission (for standard CUPs). The Director of Community Development decides administrative CUPs.

**Application (§ 18.30.040):**
- Filed by property owner or authorized representative with the Planning Division
- Application on Planning Division forms; must be complete with all data prescribed by the Commission
- Administrative CUP: filed by business owner with written owner approval; no public hearing; circulated to all city departments

**Investigation (§ 18.30.060):** Planning Division investigates facts and prepares a staff report for the Commission.

**Public hearing (§§ 18.30.060–18.30.070):** Legal notice published; public hearing held not less than 10 days nor more than 30 days after publication. Planning Commission conducts the hearing.

**Findings and conditions (§ 18.30.080):** Commission may grant CUP only upon findings that the use is necessary to the City's development and is not detrimental to surrounding properties. Conditions may be imposed.

**Filing fee (§ 18.30.050):** Set by City Council resolution.

**Appeal (§§ 18.30.100–18.30.130):** Right of appeal to City Council; appeal fee set by resolution; public hearing required on appeal.

**Time limits (§ 18.30.090):** Development must commence within the time specified in the permit or the CUP becomes void.

## Note on Airport Overlay Zone

Portions of Ceres fall within the A-O (Airport Overlay Zone), which imposes height restrictions on buildings in M-1 and M-2 zones that overlap the overlay boundary. Any industrial project in or near the A-O area must comply with overlay height limits if more restrictive than M-1/M-2 base standards.
