## Fire AHJ

Since August 3, 2021, fire protection within Ceres is delivered by the **Modesto Fire Department** under a joint services agreement approved by the Ceres City Council on June 14, 2021. The **Modesto-Ceres Fire Protection Agency** (a JPA between the cities of Modesto and Ceres) governs the arrangement and also serves the Industrial Fire Protection District. Operational services — inspections, fire prevention, fire marshal functions — are performed by Modesto Fire under this five-year agreement (effective August 3, 2021 through June 30, 2026, with optional two-year extension).

Prior to August 2021, Ceres operated its own Fire Division, which also served the Ceres Fire Protection District and a portion of the Industrial Fire Protection District under a separate contract.

## Adopted Fire Code

The City adopts the **California Fire Code (CFC)** — Title 15, Chapter 08 of the Ceres Municipal Code. The city's building-code page states it uses "the current versions of the Building, Plumbing, Electrical and Mechanical Codes as adopted by the State of California," which includes the CFC.

- **Code edition currently in effect statewide:** 2022 CFC (effective January 1, 2023; part of the 2022 California Building Standards Code, Title 24).
- **Local amendments filed with CBSC for 2022 code cycle:** None — Ceres did not file local amendments with the California Building Standards Commission for the 2022 code cycle (confirmed via CBSC 2022-Ordinances list; no ordinance number appears next to Ceres).
- **Municipal Code location:** Title 15 (Building Codes), Chapter 08 — California Fire Code. Section numbering within Chapter 08 tracks CFC section numbers with a "15.08." prefix (e.g., § 15.08.170 corresponds to CFC § 603.8).

[VERIFY — the specific adopting ordinance number for the current CFC edition and any local amendments are not published online. Contact the City of Ceres Building Division or Modesto Fire for the exact ordinance reference.]

## Fire Prevention / Inspection Contact

Under the JPA, fire prevention inspections and plan review for Ceres are performed by Modesto Fire. The Ceres city website directs residents to the Modesto Fire Department website for fire services information.

- City of Ceres general line: (209) 538-5700
- City of Ceres Building Division (coordinates fire plan review): (209) 538-5753; 2220 Magnolia Street, Ceres CA 95307
- Modesto-Ceres Fire Protection Agency: https://www.modestoceresfire.org/
- Modesto Fire Department: https://www.modestogov.com/149/Fire-Department

## Notes

- The fire/hazmat CUPA authority is NOT held by Modesto Fire — it remains with the Stanislaus County DER (see cupa-local.md).
- Ordinance 2017-1040 amended Chapter 9.30 of the Ceres Municipal Code regarding fireworks sales and related fire-code fine amounts (the only fire-code ordinance number found in online records for Ceres).
