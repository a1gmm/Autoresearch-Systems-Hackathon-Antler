## POTW Operator

**City of Ceres — Wastewater Division** owns and operates the Ceres Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP), located on the south side of Service Road between Morgan Road and Blaker Road, Ceres CA 95307. Treatment capacity is 4.2 MGD; average daily flow is approximately 3.1 MGD.

A portion of effluent (currently up to 6.0 MGD capacity via a 12-mile force main) is conveyed to the **City of Turlock** for final disposal via land application. In addition, a separate North Ceres Sewer Service Area (NCSSA) — bounded roughly by S. 9th St., the Tuolumne River, Mitchell Road, and Hatch Road — has its wastewater collected by Ceres but treated at the **City of Modesto's WWTP** under a 1979 agreement (residential and commercial flows only; toxic/flammable discharges prohibited from the NCSSA system).

## Industrial Pretreatment Program (IWP/WDP)

Ceres operates a **local industrial pretreatment program** under Title 13. Key elements:

- **Significant Industrial User (SIU) threshold:** Users discharging ≥ 25,000 gpd of process wastewater to the POTW, or subject to federal categorical pretreatment standards.
- **Wastewater Discharge Permit (WDP):** Required for SIUs before discharge. The Director of Public Works/City Engineer is the permitting authority. Application filed per § 13.25; annual renewal process.
- **FOG WDP:** Separate permit required for food service establishments (FSEs) per Chapter 13.18.
- **Categorical pretreatment standards:** Federal categorical standards apply to industrial users in regulated categories; Ceres incorporates these by reference.

## Local Discharge Limits

**Local limits apply at the point of connection to the public sewer (instantaneous peak unless stated otherwise).** Specific numeric limits are codified in § 13.15.050 (all dischargers) and § 13.15.060 (additional commercial/industrial limits). The 2025 SSMP Appendix contains the full local limits table. Selected limits identified in the ordinance text include:

- **BOD:** 250 mg/L daily average; 400 mg/L peak
- **Arsenic:** 1.0 mg/L
- **Fats, oils, and grease (FOG — animal/vegetable):** 100 mg/L maximum (unless authorized by permit)
- **pH:** Less than 5.5 or greater than 9.0 prohibited
- Additional metals limits (Cadmium, Chromium, Copper, Lead, Mercury, Nickel, Silver, Zinc, Cyanide) are in the local limits table — exact values confirmed in § 13.15.050-B; [VERIFY exact values from the current adopted ordinance or contact the Wastewater Division]

## Key Prohibitions (§ 13.15.040 — Prohibited Discharge Standards)

- Pollutants creating fire/explosive hazard (including waste streams at > 5% LEL)
- Wastewater with pH < 5.5 or > 9.0
- Solid/viscous substances causing POTW obstruction
- Medical wastes (unless authorized by permit)
- FOG > 100 mg/L (animal/vegetable origin)
- Pollutants causing POTW interference or pass-through

## North Ceres Note

The NCSSA agreement (1979, Ceres–Modesto) permits only **residential and commercial** dischargers into the Modesto-treated system. No industrial users may discharge to the NCSSA. Industrial users in the NCSSA area must connect to the main Ceres sewer system or obtain individual arrangements.
