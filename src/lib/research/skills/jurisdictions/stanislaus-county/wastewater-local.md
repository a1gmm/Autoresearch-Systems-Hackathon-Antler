## Overview — No Single County POTW

Stanislaus County is a largely agricultural and rural county with **no county-operated POTW**. Wastewater service is fragmented across independent cities and special districts. The county government's role is:

1. **On-site systems (septic / aerobic):** regulated by the Stanislaus County Department of Environmental Resources — Environmental Health Division under County Code Title 16, Chapter 16.10 and the Aerobic Ordinance.
2. **Public sewer connection** in the Bret Harte neighborhood only: County Code Chapter 16.09 — PUBLIC SEWER CONNECTION (narrow geographic scope; not a general industrial pretreatment program).

## Industrial Pretreatment Programs

No county-level industrial pretreatment program exists. Individual POTWs administer their own programs in compliance with 40 CFR Part 403:

- **City of Modesto** operates the largest wastewater treatment plant in the county and maintains a federal pretreatment program. Significant Industrial Users (SIUs) discharging to the Modesto collection system must obtain an Industrial User Permit from Modesto's Utilities Department. [VERIFY — obtain Modesto's current sewer use ordinance and IWP local limits]
- Smaller cities (Turlock, Ceres, etc.) may or may not have formal IWP programs depending on their NPDES permit requirements and receiving-water conditions. Contact each POTW directly.

## On-Site Wastewater (Rural / Unincorporated Areas)

For facilities in unincorporated Stanislaus County not served by a public sewer:

- **Septic / conventional systems:** County Code § 16.10.040 and Environmental Health Regulations.
- **Aerobic systems:** Stanislaus County Aerobic Ordinance — https://www.stancounty.com/er/environmentalhealth/aerobicsordinance.shtm
- **Measure X:** Primary and Secondary Sewage Treatment Initiative — requires primary and secondary sewage treatment capacity to exist before urban development can connect. See: https://www.stancounty.com/er/environmentalhealth/measure-x.shtm
- **Contact:** DER Environmental Health Division — https://www.stancounty.com/er/environmentalhealth/

## Summary Assessment

No county-level sewer-use ordinance or industrial pretreatment program was found. The controlling authority for industrial wastewater discharge is the individual city/district POTW. For any facility in unincorporated areas lacking sewer service, the county DER Environmental Health Division is the permitting authority for on-site systems.
