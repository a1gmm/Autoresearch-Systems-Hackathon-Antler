## Sewer Use Ordinance

Codified at **TMC Chapter 6-4, "Sewage Disposal."** Key provisions found via search:

- **Pretreatment defined:** Reduction, elimination, or alteration of pollutant properties in wastewater prior to discharge into the POTW, by physical, chemical, or biological processes (consistent with 40 CFR 403 definitions).
- **General prohibition:** No user shall discharge (directly or indirectly) any pollutant or wastewater that, alone or by interaction, causes or threatens interference with POTW operation, sludge processes, or disposal practices.
- **Industrial pretreatment program:** Turlock operates a **formal pretreatment program** — the NPDES permit (Order R5-2021-0005, Special Provisions §V.5) requires the city to fulfill all 40 CFR Part 403 Control Authority obligations, including: implementing legal authorities (§403.8(f)(1)), enforcing categorical pretreatment standards (§403.5 and §403.6), programmatic functions (§403.8(f)(2)), and adequate funding/staffing.

## Significant Industrial Users (SIUs)

Definition in TMC Chapter 6-4 follows federal standard:
- Industrial users subject to categorical standards under 40 CFR 403.6; **or**
- Users discharging ≥ 25,000 gallons/day average process wastewater; **or**
- Users contributing a process wastestream comprising ≥ 5% of average POTW hydraulic or organic capacity.

SIUs are subject to individual industrial waste discharge permits and pretreatment reporting.

## Industrial Loading Context

The Turlock RWQCF receives substantial industrial loading: food processing and dairy industries account for approximately **50% of flow** and approximately **70% of organic loading**. This underscores an active, substantive pretreatment program — Turlock is not a "minimal industrial use" POTW.

## Enforcement

Industrial users exceeding monthly discharge amounts or capacities specified in their waste discharge permit are subject to enforcement proceedings and may be required to pay additional capacity charges (TMC Chapter 6-4).
