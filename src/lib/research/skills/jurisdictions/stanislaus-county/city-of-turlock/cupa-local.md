## CUPA Agency

The City of Turlock is **not its own CUPA**. All CUPA-regulated programs within Turlock city limits are administered by:

## Programs Administered by County CUPA

The Stanislaus County DER is the single Unified Program Agency for the entire county and administers all six CUPA program elements:

1. Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP) — via CERS
2. California Accidental Release Prevention Program (CalARP / RMP)
3. Aboveground Storage Tank (AST) Program
4. Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program
5. Hazardous Waste Generator Program (on-site treatment)
6. Medical Waste Management Program

## Reporting / CERS

All CUPA-regulated businesses must submit electronically through the **California Environmental Reporting System (CERS)** — paper forms no longer accepted (required since January 1, 2013). Facilities must recertify annually.

**Thresholds that trigger HMBP filing:**
- ≥ 500 lb solid hazardous material
- ≥ 55 gal liquid hazardous material
- ≥ 200 ft³ compressed gas
- Any quantity of hazardous waste
- Extremely hazardous substances (EHS) at or above threshold planning quantities
