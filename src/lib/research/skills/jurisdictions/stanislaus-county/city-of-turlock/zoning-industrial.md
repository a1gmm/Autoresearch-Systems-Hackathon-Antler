## Zoning Code Location

Turlock's zoning regulations are in **Title 9 of the Turlock Municipal Code (TMC)**:
- Chapter 9-1: General Provisions
- Chapter 9-2: Regulations Applying to All Districts
- **Chapter 9-3: Base District Regulations** (industrial districts are in Article 4 of this chapter)
- Chapter 9-4: Overlay District Regulations
- Chapter 9-5: Permits and Procedures

## Industrial Base Districts

Turlock uses two industrial base zoning districts (Article 4, Chapter 9-3):

### I — General Industrial District

**Purpose:** Provides for the full range of manufacturing, industrial processing, general service, and distribution uses deemed suitable for location in Turlock; protects industrial areas from displacement by incompatible uses.

Permitted-use designations in I district:
- **P** = permitted by right
- **MAA** = minor administrative approval
- **MDP** = minor discretionary permit
- **CUP** = conditional use permit required
- **NP** = not permitted

### I-BP — Industrial Business Park District

**Purpose:** Provides sites with high architectural and landscape standards for industrial office centers, limited manufacturing, warehousing, and large-scale single-destination retail. More restrictive than the I district in terms of use types and design standards.

Notable: Large-scale, single-destination retail uses in I-BP require a **CUP** (Article 6, Chapter 9-5 TMC).

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

- **Decision-maker:** Turlock Planning Commission (for standard CUPs).
- **Processing time:** 8–12 weeks, depending on project complexity and CEQA review required.
- **Public noticing:** All property owners within **500 feet** of the proposed project are notified of the application and public hearing.
- **Application submittal:** Development Services Department, 156 S. Broadway, Suite 120, Turlock, CA 95380.

Uses not listed in the permitted-use schedule may be allowed via CUP if found to be of similar nature and intensity as other permitted uses, at the discretion of the Development Services Director.

## Design Review

New or expanded uses involving an expansion of floor area of **5,000 sq ft** or **25% of existing building floor area** (whichever is less) are subject to design review and require a Minor Discretionary Permit (MDP) design review approval (Article 10, Chapter 9-5 TMC).
