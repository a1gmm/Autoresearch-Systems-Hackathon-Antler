## Adopted Edition

The City of Turlock adopted the **2022 California Fire Code (CFC), Title 24, Part 9** — which incorporates the International Fire Code (IFC) 2021 edition with errata — as the local fire code. The adoption is codified at **Turlock Municipal Code (TMC) Chapter 4-3, "Fire Codes and Administration."**

- **Adopting ordinance:** Ordinance No. 1295-CS, adopted **November 10, 2022**.

## Fire AHJ

- **Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ):** Turlock Fire Department (TFD), a municipal fire department.
- **Administrative note:** Since January 2022, administration/management/command services for TFD are provided under contract with the **Modesto Fire Department**; this is an operational arrangement — TFD remains the legal AHJ. The contract ran through June 2026; verify renewal status.
- **Fire Department address:** 244 N. Broadway, Turlock, CA 95380
