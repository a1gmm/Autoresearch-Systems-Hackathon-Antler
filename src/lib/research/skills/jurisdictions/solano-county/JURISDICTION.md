## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **Agency:** Solano County Department of Resource Management, Environmental Health Division (HazMat Program) — single countywide CUPA covering all unincorporated areas **and** all seven incorporated cities (Benicia, Dixon, Fairfield, Rio Vista, Suisun City, Vacaville, Vallejo). No city operates a separate CUPA or Participating Agency.
- **Programs administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP / inventory + emergency response), Hazardous Waste Generator + onsite treatment (Tiered Permitting), Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA / SPCC), California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP). Also: Environmental Monitoring Wells/Soil Borings, Fairfield-Suisun Stormwater Inspections, Trauma Scene Contractors, Waste Tires, Household Hazardous Waste.
- **Reporting portal:** CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) — http://siteportal.calepa.ca.gov

### 2. Fire AHJ — Adopted Code & Permits

- **No single county fire department.** Unincorporated areas are served by independent fire protection districts (Montezuma FPD, Cordelia FPD, Vacaville Fire Protection District, Dixon FPD, etc.); each incorporated city has its own municipal fire department (see city files for Vallejo FD, Fairfield FD, Vacaville FD, Benicia FD, Suisun City FD, Dixon FD, Rio Vista FD).
- **Adopted fire code (unincorporated county, building/plan-check AHJ):** Solano County Code **Chapter 6.3, Section 6.3-03** adopts the **California Fire Code, 2025 Edition** (Part 9 of Title 24, based on IFC 2024), together with Appendix Chapter 4 and Appendices B, BB, C, CC, D, E, F, G, I, and J. Adopted by Ordinance No. 2025-___ (draft circulated as "25-862"), passed at the Board of Supervisors' regular meeting of **December 2, 2025**; operative **January 1, 2026**.
- **Local fire ordinances also adopted:** 2025 Fire Hazard Severity Zone Maps Ordinance; Solano County Consensus Fire Ordinance (2025 version); Water Efficient Landscape Ordinance (MWELO 2015). PDFs available on county Building & Safety Services ordinances page.
- **Enforcement note (LRA):** County enforces Local Responsibility Area (LRA) fire-hazard-severity-zone construction requirements for new buildings in unincorporated areas beginning **July 2025**.

### 3. POTW / Industrial Wastewater Pretreatment

- **Primary POTW (central Solano County):** Fairfield-Suisun Sewer District (FSSD) — serves Fairfield, Suisun City, and Travis AFB; over 135,000 residential/commercial/industrial customers. Created by California Legislature act in 1951.
- **Sewer use ordinance:** **FSSD Wastewater Discharge Ordinance 1701** (current version 1701-1, PDF available on district site) — sets discharge requirements into the collection and treatment system; coordinates with the SF Regional Water Quality Control Board and EPA.
- **Industrial pretreatment — key local limits (IWP):** Copper ≤ 1.3 mg/L (daily max); Zinc ≤ 2.3 mg/L (daily max); Oil & Grease (Total) ≤ 300 mg/L; pH 6.0–11.0; Temperature < 130 °F. Contact Pretreatment Program Coordinator for full categorical/local limits.
- **IWP application:** Contact Pretreatment Program Coordinator at (707) 429-8930 or sourcecontrol@fssd.com before any non-domestic process discharge; short-term/groundwater discharge request form available as PDF on district site.
- **NPDES permit for FSSD WWTP:** Reissuance underway as of 2025 (CEQA notice filed 2025120584 with OPR); current permit number [VERIFY via SFBRWQCB or FSSD directly].
- **Other POTWs:** Vallejo Sanitation & Flood Control District (VSFCD) serves Vallejo/Benicia corridor; Vacaville sewer served by City of Vacaville Public Works — contact each for their sewer-use ordinance/IWP.
- Source: https://www.fairfieldsuisunsewer.ca.gov/wastewater-ordinances/

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater

- **Program:** Solano County is a Phase II NPDES MS4 permittee under the San Francisco Regional Water Quality Control Board (SFBRWQCB), designated Phase II community December 8, 1999; formally notified October 4, 2002. Program implemented per the county's Storm Water Management Plan (SWMP, adopted 2003).
- **Ordinance hooks for illicit discharge:** County Code includes stormwater-related provisions in **Chapter 9** (Drainage and Land Leveling), **Chapter 23** (Refuse/Garbage), **Chapter 24** (Roads/Streets), **Chapter 25** (Chemical Toilets/Sewage), and **Chapter 31** (Grading and Erosion Control) — all referenced as stormwater ordinance attachments in the SWMP.
- **Industrial stormwater:** Industrial facilities in the unincorporated county must also hold an Industrial General Permit (IGP) from SFBRWQCB/State Water Board where required by SIC code; county MS4 program enforces illicit discharge prohibition.
- **Illicit discharge reporting:** https://www.solanocounty.gov/government/resource-management/public-works/stormwater
- **Solano Stormwater Authority (SSA):** Multi-agency body coordinating C.3 post-construction requirements; publishes the Stormwater C.3 Guidebook (2024 edition, updated November 2024) — https://solanostormwater.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/SSA_StormwaterC3Guidebook_2024_Ed._2024-11-01.pdf
- Source: https://www.solanocounty.gov/government/resource-management/public-works/stormwater
- Source (SWMP archive): https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/water_issues/programs/stormwater/swmp/solano_swmp.pdf

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts & CUP Process

- **Code location:** Solano County Code **Chapter 28 (Zoning Regulations)** — current through Ordinance 1867 (passed February 3, 2026). Article II sets districts and allowable uses; Article III contains land use regulations including Section 28.77 (Industrial, Manufacturing, Processing and Wholesale Uses).
- **Industrial/manufacturing district categories** (confirmed in Ordinance 2025-XXXX / BESS ordinance): **Manufacturing and Industrial Districts** and **Industrial-Agricultural Service Districts** — specific district codes (e.g., M-L, M-G, I-AS) referenced in the code but full designation table requires review of Article II Table A directly.
- **CUP process:** Conditional Use Permits (CUPs) are heard and decided by the **Solano County Zoning Administrator** (Allan Calder, Planning Manager, (707) 784-6765). Zoning Administrator meets first and third Thursday of each month at 10:00 a.m., 675 Texas St, 5th floor. Adverse decisions appealable to the Solano County Planning Commission within 10 days.
- **BESS note:** Front-of-meter Battery Energy Storage Systems in Manufacturing and Industrial Districts were subject to interim prohibition (Ordinance 2024-1852-U) lifted August 26, 2025 for those districts with interim standards; permanent BESS ordinance (ZT-25-02) pending adoption as of early 2026.

## Structural Gotchas

- **Single countywide CUPA, fragmented fire:** The CUPA is consolidated under Environmental Health / Resource Management and is the **sole CUPA for the entire county including all seven cities** — no city is its own CUPA. Fire AHJ is highly fragmented: every city has its own municipal FD and the unincorporated county is split among multiple independent fire protection districts (no county fire department).
- **City FD vs same-name fire protection district:** Vacaville FD (municipal, city limits) is a SEPARATE entity from the Vacaville Fire Protection District (independent special district, ~135 sq mi surrounding unincorporated area, split from City in 1981). Dixon FD is a combination department serving both the City and the Dixon Fire Protection District by contract. — https://www.vfpd.net/
- **Travis AFB carve-out:** Travis Air Force Base (east of Fairfield) is federal land with its own Travis AFB Fire Department and is outside city/county fire AHJ and local building jurisdiction. — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Travis_Air_Force_Base
- **Title 24 edition:** County adopted 2025 Title 24 (operative 2026-01-01) via Ordinance 2025-___ amending Chapter 6.3. Confirm each incorporated city's currently operative edition on its live Municode/code page (Vacaville's posted ordinance still referenced 2022 edition as of mid-2025).
- **Wastewater: not all cities on FSSD:** Vallejo and Benicia are served by the Vallejo Sanitation & Flood Control District (VSFCD), not FSSD. Each POTW has its own sewer-use ordinance and IWP thresholds.
