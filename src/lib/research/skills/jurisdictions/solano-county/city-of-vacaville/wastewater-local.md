## Sewer-Use Ordinance

- **eCode360 (authoritative current text):** https://ecode360.com/VA4679 → Title 13

- **Scope:** Regulates direct and indirect contributors to the City's sewer collection system; requires Industrial Wastewater Permits (IWPs) for regulated non-domestic users

## Who Needs an IWP (Industrial Wastewater Permit)

A permit is required for any non-domestic discharger inside city limits, including:
- Industrial facilities discharging process wastewater
- Haulers of septage
- Fats, oil, and grease (FOG) dischargers (restaurants, food processing)
- Dischargers of remediated groundwater
- Any discharger that is a Significant Industrial User (SIU) under federal pretreatment definitions (>25,000 gpd process flow or potential for pass-through/interference)
- Any Categorical Industrial User (CIU) subject to EPA categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR Parts 405–471)

## IWP Application Process

1. Contact City of Vacaville Utilities Department, (707) 469-6400, to confirm permit applicability
2. Submit IWP application (form available through commercial/industrial portal above)
3. City reviews application; may conduct a site inspection before permit issuance
4. Permit issued; compliance monitoring and periodic reporting required throughout permit term
5. Annual renewal required; 11% pretreatment surcharge applied to sewer bill (see below)

## Fees and Surcharges

- **IWP permit fee (2025):** $647.89 per permit (annual; confirmed via City utilities search results — [VERIFY current year amount at https://www.cityofvacaville.gov/government/utilities/fees-and-rates])
- **Pretreatment surcharge:** 11% surcharge applied to the sewer portion of the monthly or bi-monthly water/sewer bill for businesses subject to the pretreatment program. This surcharge partially funds the City's Pretreatment Program, which is required by federal and state regulations.

## Prohibited Discharges (General)

Per VMC Ch. 13.08 and federal pretreatment regulations:
- Substances causing fire, explosion, or structural damage to the sewer system
- Materials that would cause interference or pass-through at the EWWTP
- Uncontrolled discharges of slug loads
- Stormwater to sanitary sewer (cross-connections prohibited)

## Pretreatment Program Status

Vacaville operates an active federally-approved pretreatment program under NPDES permit CA0077691. The program is required by 40 CFR Part 403 because the EWWTP discharges to surface waters that require protection. This is a fully active pretreatment program — not a de minimis program.
