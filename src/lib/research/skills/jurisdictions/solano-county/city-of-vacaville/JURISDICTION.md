## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA (Hazardous Materials Unified Program)

- **Agency:** Solano County Department of Resource Management, Environmental Health Division — HazMat Program. Vacaville is **not its own CUPA**; the countywide CUPA covers all Solano cities including Vacaville.
- **Jurisdiction confirmed:** CUPA has jurisdiction in Benicia, Dixon, Fairfield, Rio Vista, Suisun City, Vacaville, Vallejo, and surrounding unincorporated areas.
- **Programs administered:** HMBP (Hazardous Materials Business Plan), Hazardous Waste, USTs, APSA, CalARP, Household Hazardous Waste facilities.
- **Enabling state law:** California Health and Safety Code, Division 20, Chapter 6.95, Article 1, §§ 25500–25519.
- **Reporting portal:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov
- **Office:** 675 Texas St, Suite 5500, Fairfield, CA 94533
- **Phone:** (707) 784-6765 (8 AM–5 PM) | After-hours: (707) 421-7090
- **Email:** HazMat@SolanoCounty.gov
- Source: https://www.solanocounty.gov/government/resource-management/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-hazmat-program

### 2. Fire AHJ — City of Vacaville Fire Department

- **AHJ inside city limits:** City of Vacaville Fire Department (municipal department; ~100 personnel, five stations). NOT the Vacaville Fire Protection District (independent special district covering surrounding unincorporated area — split off 1981). Do not conflate.
- **Adopted fire code:** 2022 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9), as adopted at Vacaville Municipal Code (VMC) **Ch. 15.20.270–272** ("Fire Code and Administration of Fire Code" / "California Fire Code"). The VMC is current through Ordinance 2008 (adopted October 28, 2025); confirm whether the 2025 CFC has been locally adopted for work commencing after 2026-01-01.
- **Fire Code Official:** Fire Chief, per VMC Ch. 15.20.270.
- **Permit trigger:** CFC-based operational permits required for hazardous materials storage, spray booths, welding, tank installation, high-piled storage, fire alarm/sprinkler systems, and other regulated uses. Fire permits may be issued as proof of compliance per VMC Ch. 15.20.271.
- **Fee authority:** Fire Chief may establish fee schedule by resolution of City Council (VMC Ch. 15.20.270).
- **Permit application page:** https://www.cityofvacaville.gov/government/fire-department/permit-application-fees
- **Fire Prevention / Code Enforcement:** https://www.cityofvacaville.gov/government/fire-department/fire-prevention
- **VFPD (unincorporated area only):** https://www.vfpd.net/
- Source: https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/Vacaville/html/Vacaville15/Vacaville1520270.html (VMC Ch. 15.20.270)
- Source: https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/Vacaville/html/Vacaville15/Vacaville1520271.html (VMC Ch. 15.20.271)

### 3. POTW / Industrial Pretreatment Program

- **POTW:** City of Vacaville Easterly Wastewater Treatment Plant (EWWTP), treating ~7.5 MGD, discharging to Alamo Creek.
- **NPDES permit:** CA0077691, issued by the **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board** (RWQCB5). Permit renewal was under public notice as of March 2025 (public comment deadline April 11, 2025; board hearing June 19/20, 2025).
- **Pretreatment ordinance:** VMC **Chapter 13.08** — "Sewers." Regulates direct and indirect contributors; requires Industrial Wastewater Permits (IWPs) for regulated non-domestic users including industrial facilities, septage haulers, fats/oil/grease dischargers, and remediated groundwater dischargers.
- **Surcharge:** 11% surcharge applied to the sewer portion of the monthly/bi-monthly bill for businesses subject to the Pretreatment Program. Surcharge partially funds the program per federal/state requirements.
- **Utility contact:** City of Vacaville Utilities Department, (707) 469-6400.
- **Pretreatment/commercial-industrial portal:** https://www.cityofvacaville.gov/government/utilities/environmental-protection/commercial-and-industrial
- **RWQCB5 tentative order (2025):** https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/centralvalley/board_decisions/tentative_orders/2506/12_city_of_vacaville_npdes/
- Source: https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/Vacaville/html/Vacaville13/Vacaville1308.html (VMC Ch. 13.08)
- Source: https://www.cityofvacaville.gov/government/utilities/sewer/wastewater-treatment

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater

- **MS4 permit:** City of Vacaville operates under the State Water Resources Control Board **Phase II Small MS4 General Permit (NPDES No. CAS000004)**, administered by the Central Valley RWQCB. New development/redevelopment must demonstrate compliance with the City Stormwater Management Plan and implement Best Management Practices (BMPs); a separate Erosion Control Plan (SWPPP) must be approved by the City Engineer/Director of Public Works prior to project plan approval.
- **Stormwater code reference:** VMC **Ch. 14.26.030** (Regulations and Requirements) references NPDES BMP requirements for land development.
- **City Utilities — Environmental Protection:** https://www.cityofvacaville.gov/government/utilities/environmental-protection
- Source: https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/Vacaville/html/Vacaville14/Vacaville1426030.html (VMC Ch. 14.26.030)
- Source: https://www.cityofvacaville.gov/home/showpublisheddocument?id=779 (Storm Drain Design Standards)

### 5. Zoning / Land Use — Industrial and Employment Districts

- **Zoning code location:** VMC **Title 14 — Land Use and Development Code**, Division 14.09 — Zoning. The official current text is hosted on eCode360: https://ecode360.com/VA4679
- **Employment zoning districts** (VMC Ch. **14.09.080**): Vacaville uses "Employment" district designations for industrial and business-park land. District abbreviations confirmed as referenced in municipal code; typical Vacaville employment districts include Business Park (BP) and manufacturing/industrial tiers — confirm exact abbreviations in Ch. 14.09.080 as the eCode360 platform is the authoritative source.
- **Zoning districts and map:** VMC Ch. **14.09.040** — establishes zoning districts and the Official Zoning Map.
- **Conditional Use Permit (CUP) process:** VMC Ch. **14.09.300** — Use Permits. CUPs are decided by the **Planning Commission** following a public hearing. Appeals must be filed in writing within **10 calendar days** of the decision (5 calendar days for minor use permits). The Planning Commission also acts on zoning amendments, variances, and subdivisions.

## GOTCHA Notes

- **City FD vs. same-name district:** "Vacaville Fire Department" (municipal, city limits AHJ) is NOT the "Vacaville Fire Protection District" (independent special district, unincorporated surrounding area). Do not conflate when assigning the fire AHJ.
- **CUPA is county, not city:** Like all Solano cities, Vacaville is not its own CUPA. Solano County EH is the CUPA. HMBP/CalARP/UST filings go to the County, not the City.
- **Municipal code platform migration:** codepublishing.com URLs redirect to eCode360 (https://ecode360.com/VA4679). Use eCode360 for authoritative current text; the VMC is current through Ordinance 2008 (October 28, 2025).
- **2025 CFC:** State mandated adoption of the 2025 California Fire Code by 2026-01-01. Confirm whether Vacaville has locally adopted the 2025 CFC for work on projects permitted after that date.
