## Employment Zoning Districts (VMC Ch. 14.09.080)

Vacaville uses "Employment" district designations for industrial, warehouse, R&D, and business-park land uses. The specific district abbreviations and names are established in VMC Ch. 14.09.080 and Table 14.09.080.A (Land Use Regulations — Employment Zoning Districts).

Land-use categories in Table 14.09.080.A use:
- **P** = Permitted Use (by-right)
- **M** = Minor Use Permit required
- **C** = Conditional Use Permit (CUP) required
- **(blank)** = Not allowed in that district

> [VERIFY — district abbreviations not confirmed via direct code fetch (eCode360 and codepublishing.com both returned 403 on direct access)] Typical Vacaville employment district abbreviations referenced in planning commission staff reports and city materials include Business Park (BP) and associated sub-tiers. Confirm exact abbreviations and the full use table in VMC Ch. 14.09.080 via eCode360 or by contacting Community Development.

## Overlay Districts (VMC Ch. 14.09.040)

Confirmed overlay designations that may apply to industrial parcels:
- **AB** — Agricultural Buffer
- **AE** — Airport Environs (Nut Tree Airport influence area — relevant for industrial uses near airport)
- **H** — Hillside
- **HP** — Historic Preservation
- **LLV** — Lower Lagoon Valley
- **RMM** — Rice McMurtry
- **PD** — Planned Development
- Northeast Growth Area overlay

**AE (Airport Environs) note:** Industrial or manufacturing uses near Nut Tree Airport may be subject to height restrictions and land-use compatibility requirements under the Airport Environs overlay. Coordinate with Solano County Airport Land Use Commission if applicable.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

- **Governing chapter:** VMC Ch. **14.09.300** — Use Permits
- **Decision-maker:** Planning Commission (following a public hearing)
- **Appeal period:** Written appeal must be filed within **10 calendar days** of Planning Commission decision (5 calendar days for minor use permits)
- **Appeals to:** City Council [VERIFY appeal body in VMC Ch. 14.09.300]
- **Application portal (eTRAKiT):** https://permits.cityofvacaville.com
- **Planning Commission composition and calendar:** https://www.cityofvacaville.gov/government/city-commissions
