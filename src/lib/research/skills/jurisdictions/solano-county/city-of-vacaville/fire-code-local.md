> Research reference for the City of Vacaville fire code adoption and fire AHJ.
> The municipal fire department serves city limits; a separate special district (VFPD) serves the surrounding unincorporated area — do not conflate.
> VMC is current through Ordinance 2008 (October 28, 2025). Confirm whether the 2025 CFC has been locally adopted for projects permitted after 2026-01-01.

## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

- **AHJ inside city limits:** City of Vacaville Fire Department (municipal department; ~100 personnel, five stations)
- **Fire Code Official:** Fire Chief (designated by VMC Ch. 15.20.270)
- **Fire Prevention / Code Enforcement:** https://www.cityofvacaville.gov/government/fire-department/fire-prevention
- **Permit application / fees:** https://www.cityofvacaville.gov/government/fire-department/permit-application-fees

> IMPORTANT: Do NOT confuse with the **Vacaville Fire Protection District (VFPD)**, an independent special district that serves the unincorporated area surrounding the city (split off 1981). VFPD is at https://www.vfpd.net/ and has no jurisdiction inside city limits.

## Adopted Fire Code

- **Code edition:** 2022 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9) — adopted via VMC **Ch. 15.20.271**
- **Municipal code chapters:**
  - VMC Ch. **15.20.270** — Fire Code and Administration of Fire Code (establishes Fire Department as Code Compliance Agency, Fire Chief as Fire Code Official, fee authority by Council resolution)
  - VMC Ch. **15.20.271** — California Fire Code (adoption and local amendments)
  - VMC Ch. **15.20.272** — referenced companion chapter

## Excluded Chapters

The following CFC chapters are expressly **not adopted** by Vacaville:
- Chapter 25 (Fruit and Crop Ripening)
- Chapter 26 (Fumigation and Thermal Insecticidal Fogging)
- Chapter 49 (Requirements for Wildland-Urban Interface Fire Areas)
- Appendices A, H, and M

## Known Local Amendments

- **Sprinkler amendment (Group R occupancies):** Automatic sprinkler systems in Group R Occupancies of four stories or less designed per NFPA 13R shall have sprinklers installed in attics, balconies, porches, and open corridors (local amendment confirmed via search; full text in VMC Ch. 15.20.271 [VERIFY exact VMC section citation])

## Operational Permit Triggers

Under the adopted 2022 CFC, operational permits are required for (among others):
- Hazardous materials storage/use above CFC Chapter 50 threshold quantities
- High-piled combustible storage (>500 sq ft for Class I–IV; >500 sq ft for Group A plastics)
- Flammable/combustible liquids (CFC Chapter 57)
- Compressed gases (CFC Chapter 53)
- Spray booths (CFC Chapter 15)
- Tank installation (above- and below-ground)
- Fire alarm and automatic sprinkler systems (plan check + permit)
- Welding and cutting operations (CFC Chapter 35)
