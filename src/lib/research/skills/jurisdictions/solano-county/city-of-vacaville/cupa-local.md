> Research reference for CUPA programs applicable to businesses in Vacaville city limits.
> Vacaville is NOT its own CUPA. All six CalEPA unified programs are administered by the Solano County Department of Resource Management, Environmental Health Division — HazMat Program (the countywide CUPA).
> Route all HMBP, UST, CalARP, APSA, and Hazardous Waste filings to the County CUPA — never to City of Vacaville.

## Programs Administered

The Solano County CUPA implements all six CalEPA unified programs for Vacaville businesses:

| # | Program | Acronym | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan | HMBP | Filed via CERS; Solano County EH reviews and inspects |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator Program | HWG | Generator ID, manifest compliance, inspection |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tanks | UST | Installation, operation, modification, closure, leak response |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act | APSA | Spill prevention for above-threshold petroleum storage |
| 5 | California Accidental Release Prevention Program | CalARP | Local implementing agency for RMP submissions |

## HMBP Filing

- **Reporting portal:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov
- **Threshold quantities (state baseline):** Generally 55 gallons liquid, 500 lbs solid, 200 cubic feet gas (Health & Safety Code §25507)
- **Who must file:** Any business storing, using, or handling hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities
- **Initial filing:** Within 30 days of bringing threshold materials onsite
- **Annual update:** Required every year via CERS
- **Update triggers:** >100% increase in quantity of a material; new material not previously disclosed; address/ownership/name change; significant operational change

## CalARP (Accidental Release Prevention)

- Solano County EH is the Local Implementing Agency (LIA) for CalARP for Vacaville facilities
- Facilities with regulated substances above federal threshold quantities must submit a Risk Management Plan (RMP) to USEPA and coordinate local CalARP review with Solano County EH
- Enabling law: California Health and Safety Code §25531 et seq. (CalARP); federal counterpart: Clean Air Act §112(r)

## UST Program

- UST permits, modifications, closures, and release investigations for Vacaville are handled by Solano County EH HazMat
- State-database tracking: GeoTracker (https://geotracker.waterboards.ca.gov)
- Solano County also uses Envirostor and the Cortese List for contaminated site tracking
