## CUP Process — Zoning Administrator

All conditional use permit (CUP) applications for industrial and manufacturing uses in unincorporated Solano County are heard and decided by the **Solano County Zoning Administrator**.

### Zoning Administrator

- **Current Zoning Administrator:** Allan Calder, Planning Manager
- **Phone:** (707) 784-6765
- **Email:** Planning@solanocounty.gov
- **Office:** Department of Resource Management, 675 Texas St, 5th floor, Fairfield CA 94533
- **Web:** https://www.solanocounty.gov/government/resource-management/commissions-special-districts/solano-county-zoning-administrator

### Meeting Schedule

- **Frequency:** First and third Thursday of each month at **10:00 a.m.**
- **Location:** Department of Resource Management, 5th floor, 675 Texas St, Fairfield CA 94533

### CUP Appeal

- Any person adversely affected by a Zoning Administrator decision may appeal to the **Solano County Planning Commission** within **10 days** of the Zoning Administrator's action.
- Planning Commission contact: https://www.solanocounty.gov/government/resource-management/commissions-special-districts/solano-county-planning-commission

## BESS / Energy Storage Note

- Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) in front-of-meter configurations in Manufacturing and Industrial Districts were subject to an interim prohibition (Ordinance No. 2024-1852-U).
- The interim prohibition was **lifted August 26, 2025** for Manufacturing and Industrial Districts (with interim development standards).
- **Permanent BESS ordinance (ZT-25-02):** Pending adoption as of early 2026. [VERIFY current status with Planning Services]
- This is relevant for any industrial facility considering grid-scale energy storage.
