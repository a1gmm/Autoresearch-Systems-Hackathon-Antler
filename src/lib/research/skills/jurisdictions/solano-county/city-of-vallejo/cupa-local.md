## Programs Administered (All Six CalEPA Unified Program Elements)

| # | Program | Acronym | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan | HMBP | Filed via CERS; Solano County EH reviews and inspects |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator Program | HWG | Generator ID, manifest compliance, inspection |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tanks | UST | Installation, operation, modification, closure, leak response |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act | APSA | SPCC compliance; storage above threshold quantities |
| 5 | California Accidental Release Prevention Program | CalARP | Local implementing agency for RMP submissions |
| 6 | Tiered Permitting (Conditionally Exempt / Tiered Permit Generators) | TP | Hazardous waste generator tiers |

> Household Hazardous Waste (HHW) facilities are also listed as a County EH program element.

## HMBP Program

- **Submission system:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business
- **Who must comply:** Businesses storing, using, or handling hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities (HSC §25507 — generally 55 gallons liquid, 500 lbs solid, 200 cu ft gas)
- **Filing:** Initial filing within 30 days of bringing threshold materials on-site; annual update required
- **Reviewing agency:** Solano County EH HazMat Program (not Vallejo Fire Department)

## Regulatory Authority

- Operates under California Health and Safety Code §78000 et seq. (remediation/hazardous waste) and related statutes
- Enforcement: permit issuance, facility inspections, complaint investigations, enforcement actions
- **[VERIFY]** Confirm whether Solano County has enacted a local hazardous materials ordinance supplementing state HSC thresholds, and whether any Vallejo-specific provisions apply.

## Key Distinction from Fire Code Hazmat Permits

- The CUPA HMBP (filed with Solano County) and the California Fire Code operational permit for hazardous materials (issued by Vallejo Fire Prevention Division) are **separate, parallel requirements**.
- Facilities above CFC operational permit thresholds must obtain a fire permit from Vallejo Fire Prevention in addition to maintaining their CUPA HMBP with the County.
- See fire-code-local.md for fire permit details.
