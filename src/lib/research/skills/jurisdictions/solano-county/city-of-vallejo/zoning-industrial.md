> Research reference for industrial zoning and use permit process in City of Vallejo.
> Title 16 Zoning Code was comprehensively updated per Ordinance No. 1885 N.C. (2d), adopted December 16, 2025, aligned with General Plan 2040.
> A Major Use Permit (Planning Commission approval) is required for many conditional uses in industrial districts.

## Industrial Zoning Districts (VMC Chapter 16.102, Table 16.102-A)

| District | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **IL** | Light Industrial | Applied to areas appropriate for light industrial uses, commercial service uses, and activities that may be incompatible with residential, retail, and/or office uses. Business/Light Industrial; Business/Limited Residential overlay areas. |
| **IG** | General Industrial | Applied to areas appropriate for industrial and manufacturing activities, warehousing, wholesaling, and distribution uses. Uses may generate truck traffic and operate 24 hours. Includes buffering standards for adjacent residential uses. |

- Zoning districts and use standards are defined in **Part II** (Chapter 16.200, Districts and Development Types) and **Part III** (Chapter 16.300, Use Standards) of Title 16.
- Industrial districts chapter: **Chapter 16.102 — Establishment of Zoning Districts**

## Use Permit Process

### Major Use Permit (MUP)

- **Governing chapter:** VMC **Chapter 16.606 — Minor and Major Use Permits**
- **Decision-maker:** **Planning Commission** (public hearing required)
- **What it covers:** A conditional use permit allowing uses or activities not granted as a matter of right in a zoning district — evaluated on whether they are suitable only in certain locations or only if designed/operated in a particular manner
- **Examples of uses requiring MUP in industrial districts:** [VERIFY specific trigger list for IL/IG against Chapter 16.606 and Table 16.300 in the new 2025 zoning code — the pre-2025 code included service stations, 24-hour operations, certain commercial uses in industrial zones as conditional]

**Application process:**
1. Optional preliminary review / Technical Project Review Committee meeting (~3 weeks; recommended for complex projects)
2. Submit completed Major Use Permit application to Central Permit Center (555 Santa Clara Street) — include filing fee + notice fee
3. Planning staff reviews for completeness within 30 days (state law); typically 3 weeks
4. CEQA environmental review if required (coordinated by Planning staff)
5. Staff routes plans to affected departments: Public Works, Fire Prevention, VFWD (wastewater), others
6. Planning Commission public hearing (scheduled once application is complete); property owners within 500 feet notified at least 10 days prior (21 days if an Initial Study was prepared)
7. Commission may approve, conditionally approve, or deny
8. Decision may be appealed

### Minor Use Permit

- **Decision-maker:** Planning Director (administrative review, no public hearing)
- Used for lower-impact conditional uses; specific triggers defined in Chapter 16.606

## General Plan Context

- **General Plan 2040** governs long-range land use; Industrial land use designations map to IL and IG zoning districts.
- Industrial areas are concentrated around the Mare Island corridor, portions of the waterfront, and designated industrial zones north and east of downtown.
