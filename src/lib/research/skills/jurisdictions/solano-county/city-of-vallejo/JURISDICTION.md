## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **Agency:** Solano County Department of Resource Management, Environmental Health Division — HazMat Program. This is the countywide CUPA; Vallejo is **not** its own CUPA. All six unified-program elements for facilities inside Vallejo city limits are handled by the County:
  - Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP)
  - Hazardous Waste Generator program
  - Underground Storage Tanks (USTs)
  - Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)
  - California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)
  - Tiered Permitting (conditionally exempt / tiered permit generators)
- **Address:** 675 Texas St, Suite 5500, Fairfield, CA 94533
- **Phone:** (707) 784-6765 (Mon–Fri 8 AM–5 PM); After-hours emergency (707) 421-7090
- **Email:** HazMat@SolanoCounty.gov
- **Online permit payment portal:** https://paydirect.link2gov.com/SOLResourceManagementVT/ItemSelection/SelectItems
- Source: https://www.solanocounty.gov/government/resource-management/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-hazmat-program

### 3. POTW / Industrial Wastewater — Pretreatment

- **POTW operator:** **Vallejo Flood and Wastewater District (VFWD)** — an independent special district (not the City). Operates the wastewater treatment plant and sewer collection system serving Vallejo.
- **Address:** 450 Ryder Street, Vallejo, CA 94590; **Phone:** (707) 644-8949; **Email:** admin@vallejowastewater.org
- **Environmental Compliance (pretreatment contact):** (707) 652-7813
- **Sewer-use / pretreatment code:** VFWD District Code **Title 4 — Sanitary Sewer System** (codified at Municode under Vallejo Flood and Wastewater District). Includes Chapter 4.16 covering discharge standards, prohibited discharges, and industrial/commercial source-control requirements.
- **Enabling ordinance for sewer connections:** Ordinance No. **ORD2018-70A** — Establishing Facilities and Issuance of Permits to Connect to the Sanitary Sewer System.
- **Connection fees ordinance:** Ordinance No. **ORD2017-70A.1(2)** — Establishing Fees to Connect.
- **Upper lateral ordinance:** Ordinance No. **Ord 2025-73** (effective 7/1/2025) — Constructing, Evaluating, Repairing, Replacing, and Maintaining Upper Sewer Laterals.
- **Industrial Waste Permit (IWP) process:** Facilities with non-domestic process discharges must contact VFWD's Environmental Compliance Supervisor. Industrial permits are issued at **no charge** for routine issuance and inspection. A Pre-Treatment Questionnaire is required for food-service establishments or any business with potential non-domestic discharge before plan approval.
- **Septage hauler permits:** Available from Pretreatment Program Coordinator; fees subject to annual CPI adjustment.
- Source (industrial customers): https://www.vallejowastewater.org/208/Industrial-Customers
- Source (permitting): https://www.vallejowastewater.org/204/Permitting-Plan-Review
- Source (ordinances): https://www.vallejowastewater.org/188/Ordinances-Codes
- Source (Municode Title 4): https://library.municode.com/ca/vallejo_flood_and_wastewater_district/codes/flood_and_wastewater_district?nodeId=CD_TIT4SASESY

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts & CUP Process

- **Zoning code:** Vallejo Municipal Code **Title 16 — Zoning Code** (comprehensive update adopted per Ordinance No. 1885 N.C., December 16, 2025, aligned with General Plan 2040).
- **Industrial zoning districts (per Table 16.102-A, Chapter 16.102):**
  - **IL** — Light Industrial (Business/Light Industrial; Business/Limited Residential overlay areas)
  - **IG** — General Industrial (Industrial; heavy industrial activities with buffering standards for adjacent residential uses)
- **Use permit process:** Vallejo uses a **Major Use Permit** (MUP) and **Minor Use Permit** system under **Chapter 16.606** (Minor and Major Use Permits).
- **FY 2024-25 planning fees (with 12% surcharge applied):**
  - Major Use Permit: **~$5,455** (base $4,870.94 + surcharges)
  - Major Use Permit Amendment: 25% of original fee
  - Minor Use Permit: **~$1,780** (base $1,589.01 + surcharges)
  - Rezoning / Text Amendment: **~$10,843** (base $9,681.93 + surcharges)
  - General Plan Amendment: **~$9,223** (base $8,233.88 + surcharges)
- **Planning department:** City of Vallejo Planning & Development Services; Central Permit Center handles permit intake.
