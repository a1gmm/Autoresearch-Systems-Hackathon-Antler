> Research reference for the City of Vallejo fire code adoption and AHJ.
> Vallejo is NOT served by a county fire district — it has its own municipal fire department that is the AHJ within city limits.
> Verify the adopted CFC edition with Fire Prevention; the 2025 CFC became effective statewide 2026-01-01 and local adoption status must be confirmed.

## Adopted Fire Code

- **Municipal Code chapter:** VMC **Chapter 12.28 — Fire Code Adopted** (Title 12, Building Code)
- **Operative edition (last confirmed):** **2022 California Fire Code** (CFC, Title 24 Part 9, based on IFC 2021), adopted by reference via Chapter 12.28
- **[VERIFY]** The 2025 California Fire Code (CFC) became effective statewide 2026-01-01. Confirm with Vallejo Fire Prevention Division whether the City has adopted the 2025 CFC and the adopting ordinance number.
- **Prior adopting ordinance pattern:** The City's broader code update through Ordinance No. 1885 N.C. (2d) was passed December 16, 2025; confirm if this cycle included a fire code edition update.
- **Local amendments:** Specific local amendments to CFC beyond the state-adopted text were not available online at time of research — [VERIFY with Fire Prevention Bureau].
- **Permit intake:** Through the Central Permit Center, 555 Santa Clara Street, Vallejo, CA 94590; (707) 648-4374 (Building); planning/fire permit coordination at (707) 648-4326 / (707) 648-4565.

- Public education / community outreach

## Key Notes for Industrial Facilities

- Hazardous materials storage requiring a CUPA Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) is administered by **Solano County CUPA** (not Vallejo Fire) — see cupa-local.md.
- CFC operational permits (e.g., compressed gases, high-piled storage, spray booths) are issued by **Vallejo Fire Prevention Division**.
- These are parallel requirements: CUPA HMBP (county) + CFC operational permit (city fire dept).
- Fire Prevention Division conducts inspections of permitted operations; coordinate early for complex industrial projects.
