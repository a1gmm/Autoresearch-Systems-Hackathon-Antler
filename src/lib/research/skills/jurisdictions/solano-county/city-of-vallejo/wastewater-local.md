## Industrial Pretreatment Program (IWP)

### Who Must Obtain a Permit

Facilities with non-domestic process discharges must contact VFWD's Environmental Compliance Supervisor before connecting or commencing discharge. Categories include:
- Industrial / manufacturing facilities with process wastewater
- Food service facilities (restaurants, bakeries, delis, coffee houses — any facility preparing/serving food)
- Breweries, wineries, and distilleries
- Any business with potential non-domestic discharge

### Application Process

1. Complete the **VFWD Pretreatment Questionnaire** (7 pages + supplemental forms for food service and breweries/wineries/distilleries) — available at https://www.vallejowastewater.org/DocumentCenter/View/852
2. Submit completed questionnaire to VFWD Environmental Compliance Department, 450 Ryder Street
3. For food service: submit Supplementary Questionnaire for Food Service Facilities
4. For breweries/wineries/distilleries: submit Supplementary Questionnaire for Breweries, Wineries and Distilleries
5. Attach: Solano County Hazardous Materials Business Plan chemical inventory; facility plumbing diagram showing all wastewater flows, connections, and sampling points; copies of existing environmental permits
6. VFWD reviews and issues or denies the Industrial Waste Permit
7. **Fees:** Industrial permits issued at **no charge** for routine issuance and inspection (confirmed by VFWD)

### Key Information Captured in Questionnaire

- Nature of industry / SIC code / business description
- Water supply sources and quantities (including City of Vallejo water meter data)
- All wastewater discharge streams (process, sanitary, cooling, RO reject, stormwater) with flows in GPD
- Batch discharge schedules and seasonal variation
- Pretreatment methods in place (pH adjustment, oil/grease interception, filtration, flocculation, etc.)
- Wastewater characteristics (pollutant types present)
- Chemical and hazardous waste storage on-site
- EPA Hazardous Waste generator number (if applicable)
- Spill prevention, spill log, solvent management plan status

### Prohibited Discharges and Local Limits

VFWD Title 4, Chapter 4.16 establishes prohibited discharge categories and local limits consistent with federal pretreatment standards (40 CFR Part 403). Specific numerical local limits (mg/L) for metals, BOD, TSS, O&G, and other pollutants are set by VFWD and flow from the NPDES permit requirements.

- **[VERIFY]** Obtain VFWD's current local limits table (numerical mg/L values) directly from Environmental Compliance at (707) 652-7813 — numerical limits are not reproduced on the public website.
- Federal categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR Parts 405–471) apply to applicable industrial categories independently of local limits.

### Copper and Cyanide Action Plans

VFWD's NPDES Order contains specific Copper Action Plan and Cyanide Action Plan provisions (Tables 4 and 5 of Order R2-2023-00XX). Industrial users with copper- or cyanide-containing discharges should verify current local limits in light of these action plans.

## Septage Haulers

Septage hauler permits available from VFWD Pretreatment Program Coordinator; fees subject to annual CPI adjustment. [VERIFY] Current fee with VFWD.

## Stormwater Note

Storm drainage is a separate VFWD program under District Code Title 6. The City of Vallejo holds its own MS4 stormwater permit (Phase I MRP 3, SF Bay RWQCB Order R2-2022-0018, amended R2-2023-0019) — stormwater discharge to the sanitary sewer is prohibited.
