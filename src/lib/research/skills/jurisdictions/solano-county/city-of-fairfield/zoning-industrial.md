## Zoning Ordinance Reference

**Chapter 25 — Zoning Ordinance**, Fairfield Municipal Code (codified at codepublishing.com/CA/Fairfield). Chapter 25 reflects amendments through March 18, 2025.

## Industrial Zoning Districts (§ 25.24)

Three industrial zoning districts are established in Fairfield:

### IBP — Industrial Business Park
- **Character:** Master-planned campus settings.
- **Typical uses:** Administrative/professional offices, research and development parks, limited distribution, light manufacturing and assembly.
- **Commercial restrictions:** Commercial uses limited to business support services and accessory on-site sales.

### IL — Limited Industrial
- **Character:** Low-intensity light and medium industrial activities.
- **Typical uses:** Assembly and fabrication, warehousing, distribution centers, administrative offices, business support services.

### IG — General Industrial
- **Character:** Medium and heavy manufacturing.
- **Siting requirement:** Requires direct access to major transportation routes (arterials, freeways, or rail).
- **Typical uses:** Widest range of intense manufacturing and industrial uses.

## Use Table Mechanics

- **"P" (Permitted):** Use allowed by right, subject to all applicable ordinance provisions; no City discretion.
- **"C" (Conditionally Permitted):** Use allowed only with a Conditional Use Permit (§ 25.40.6); City has discretion to approve, approve with conditions, or deny.

Use tables for industrial districts are found in **Table 25-11** within Section 25.24.

## CUP Process (§ 25.40.6)

**Hearing body:** Fairfield Planning Commission.

**Process overview:**
1. Pre-application consultation with Planning Division staff recommended — confirm required supplemental materials and fees before submission.
2. Submit CUP application online via **Fairfield BUILD** portal with: completed Master Application Form, mailing labels (500 ft radius), and digital PDF copies of all plans and supporting documents.
3. Planning Division reviews for completeness and schedules public hearing before the Planning Commission.
4. Planning Commission acts on the application — may approve, approve with conditions, or deny based on § 25.40.6 criteria.

**Appeal:** Planning Commission decisions may be appealed per municipal code procedures.
