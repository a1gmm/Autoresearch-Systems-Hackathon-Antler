## Adopted Code Edition

**2022 California Fire Code** (California Code of Regulations, Title 24, Part 9 — 2022 California Building Standards Code), adopted in its entirety including Division II of Chapter 1 and Appendices 4, A, B, BB, C, CC, D, H, and I.

Additionally adopts (to the extent not inconsistent with the 2022 CFC): Chapters 3, 5, 8, and Appendix D of the **2021 International Fire Code** (IFC, International Code Council).

## Adopting Ordinance

**Ordinance No. 2022-15** — "Ordinance of the City Council of the City of Fairfield Repealing and Replacing Chapter 8 (Fire Prevention Code) of the Fairfield City Code, Adopting by Reference California Code of Regulations, Title 24, Part 9, Known as the 2022 California Fire Code, with Amendments to Reflect Local Conditions, and Adopting by Reference Certain Provisions of the 2021 International Fire Code."

Effective: 30 days after adoption or January 1, 2022, whichever is later.

## Key Local Amendments (§ 8.3, Ord. 2022-15)

### Sprinkler Requirements (§ 903)
- **§ 903.2 (amended):** All buildings greater than **3,600 sq ft** shall be equipped throughout with an approved fully automatic fire sprinkler system — regardless of any exception elsewhere in the CFC or CBC.
- **Retrofit trigger:** Existing buildings not previously required to have sprinklers must install NFPA 13 throughout when: (1) alteration/repair/remodel exceeds 50% of gross floor area; (2) addition increases gross floor area by 50%; or (3) change of occupancy increases life safety or fire risk as determined by Fire Chief.
- **Exception:** Non-combustible detached canopies open on four sides (not exceeding basic allowable sq ft per CBC Table 503) used exclusively for parking or non-combustible storage (including fuel islands).
- **§ 903.3.1.2 (deleted):** NFPA 13R sprinkler systems deleted in their entirety.
- **§ 903.3.5.1 (amended):** Domestic water services shall only be used to supply NFPA 13D automatic sprinkler systems.
- **§ 903.3.5.2 (deleted):** Residential combination services deleted in their entirety.
- **§ 903.4.2 (amended):** One exterior audible alarm and visual strobe required on each automatic sprinkler system; actuates building fire alarm system.

### Fire Alarm Monitoring (§ 907.6.6)
- Fire alarm systems required by Chapter 9 or CBC shall be monitored by a **Central Station Service (UUFX)** listed in the current UL Online Certifications Directory.
- Combination fire and burglar alarm systems are not permitted (Exception: Group R-3 and R-3.1).

### Fire Apparatus Access Roads (Appendix D)
- **D103.2:** Maximum grade 15%; steeper grades require fire code official approval.
- **D103.6:** Required NO PARKING – FIRE LANE signs must comply with **Fairfield Fire Prevention Standard 92-40 — Designated Fire Lanes**.
- **D107.1 (amended):** Developments of one- or two-family dwellings where the number of dwelling units **exceeds 30** shall be provided with **two separate and approved fire apparatus access roads**.
- § 105.6.11: Security gates obstructing emergency access require a construction permit.

### Fireworks (§ 5601.1.6)
- Sale, use, possession, and discharge of all fireworks (including "safe and sane") are prohibited within city limits. Exceptions: licensed public displays with fire official authorization; EPA-classified "exempt fireworks" for industrial/commercial/agricultural/religious use with permit.

### Administration
- **§ 102.7.3:** Fire Chief may issue Fairfield Fire Department Prevention Standards (supplemental rules and regulations) — made available to the public during normal business hours.
- **§ 103.3.1:** Fire Prevention Division personnel have police officer powers.
- **§ 103.3.2:** Fire Chief may designate members as peace officers under CA Penal Code § 830.37.
- **§ 104.2.1:** Fire Chief may establish fee schedule (approved by City Council) for plan check, inspections, and permits; fee schedule kept in City Clerk office and Office of the Fire Marshal.
- **§ 111.1 (amended):** Appeals go to City Manager within 30 days of Fire Chief decision; hearing within 30 days per FMC §§ 1.15–1.16.

### Deleted Appendices
Appendices E, G, J, K, L, M, N, O, and P are deleted in their entirety.

## Permit Program

Operational and construction permits required per CFC § 105.6.1–105.6.24 (hazardous-operations permit categories). Permit applications submitted via **Fairfield BUILD** online portal or Fire Prevention Division.
