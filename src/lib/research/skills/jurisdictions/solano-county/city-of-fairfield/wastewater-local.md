## POTW Operator

**City of Fairfield does not operate its own POTW.** The city is served by the **Fairfield-Suisun Sewer District (FSSD)** — a special district (not the City itself) that serves Fairfield, Suisun City, and Travis AFB.

## Controlling Ordinance

**FSSD Ordinance No. 2017-01 — Wastewater Discharge Ordinance** (adopted January 23, 2017 by the FSSD Board of Directors).

The Ordinance implements the General Pretreatment Regulations (40 CFR Part 403) and Clean Water Act (33 U.S.C. § 1251 et seq.). It is codified as Ordinance No. 1701 in District references.

- PDF: https://www.fairfieldsuisunsewer.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Ord-1701-1.pdf

## Who Needs a Permit

Any facility with a **non-domestic process flow**, or any discharge beyond bathrooms and sinks, must obtain an Individual Wastewater Discharge Permit from FSSD's Pretreatment Program. This includes:
- Significant Industrial Users (SIUs) — categorical industrial users or facilities discharging ≥25,000 gpd or having significant impact on the POTW.
- Non-Significant Categorical Industrial Users (NSCIUs).
- Short-term or ground water discharge sources.

## Permit Types

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Individual Wastewater Discharge Permit | Standard industrial pretreatment permit; duration set by District; includes specific effluent limits, monitoring requirements, and reporting obligations. |
| Short Term Discharge Permit | For temporary or one-time non-domestic discharges. |
| Ground Water Discharge Permit | For dewatering, remediation, or other ground water discharges to the sewer. |

## Local Discharge Limits (Ord. 2017-01, § 2.4)

The following Daily Maximum concentration limits apply at the point of discharge to the community sewer or POTW:

| Parameter | Daily Maximum |
|---|---|
| Arsenic | 0.1 mg/L |
| Cadmium | 0.05 mg/L |
| Chromium | 0.15 mg/L |
| Copper | 1.3 mg/L |
| Cyanide | 0.7 mg/L |
| Lead | 0.5 mg/L |
| Mercury | 0.01 mg/L |
| Nickel | 0.9 mg/L |
| Silver | 0.2 mg/L |
| Zinc | 2.3 mg/L |
| Total BTEX (benzene, toluene, ethylbenzene, xylenes) | 6.95 mg/L |
| Oil and/or Grease | 300 mg/L |
| pH | 6.0–11.0 (standard units) |
| Temperature | < 130°F (< 54.4°C) |

All metallic limits are for total metal unless otherwise indicated. The District may impose mass limitations in addition to concentration limits. Dilution of discharges to achieve compliance is prohibited (§ 2.6).

## Pretreatment Program Structure

- **§ 2.1:** Prohibited discharge standards (pass-through/interference pollutants).
- **§ 2.2:** National Categorical Pretreatment Standards (40 CFR Part 405 et seq.) apply to categorical industrial users.
- **§ 3.1:** Users must provide and maintain pretreatment facilities at their own expense; detailed plans must be submitted to the District Representative for review before construction.
- **§ 3.3:** Slug/accidental discharge control plans required for applicable facilities.
- **§ 4.2:** Individual Wastewater Discharge Permit required for all applicable industrial users.
- **§ 6.1–6.14:** Reporting requirements include Baseline Monitoring Reports, periodic compliance reports, reports of changed conditions, and certification statements.
- **§ 7.1:** District has right of entry for inspection and sampling.
- **§ 10.6–10.7:** Administrative fines and civil penalties for violations.

## IWP Application Process

1. Contact Pretreatment Program Coordinator: (707) 429-8930 or sourcecontrol@fssd.com.
2. Complete the appropriate application form (Standard Industrial, Short Term, or Ground Water Discharge) — available on FSSD website.
3. Submit completed application, wastewater analysis (§ 4.1), and required certifications (§ 4.6).
4. District Representative issues permit decision (§ 4.7) with permit conditions per § 5.2.

## NPDES Permit

FSSD's NPDES permit for the treatment plant discharge is under San Francisco Bay Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB Region 2). Order R2-2025 reissuance was pending before the SF Bay RWQCB as of 2025; CEQA notice posted 2025.

## Note on City Role

The City of Fairfield has no independent industrial pretreatment program; all IWP permitting, inspection, and enforcement within city limits is administered solely by FSSD.
