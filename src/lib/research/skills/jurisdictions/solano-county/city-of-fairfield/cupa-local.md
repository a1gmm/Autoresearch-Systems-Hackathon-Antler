## Controlling CUPA

**City of Fairfield is NOT its own CUPA.** The City is served by the **Solano County Department of Resource Management, Environmental Health Division — Hazardous Materials (HazMat) Program**, which is the Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all jurisdictions in Solano County.

The County CUPA office is physically located at the Fairfield address below; Fairfield businesses submit all CUPA filings directly to the County.

## CUPA Agency

## Jurisdictions Served

Benicia, Dixon, **Fairfield**, Rio Vista, Suisun City, Vacaville, Vallejo, and unincorporated Solano County areas.

## Programs Administered

The Solano County CUPA administers all six unified programs as required by California HSC § 25400 et seq.:

| Program | Abbrev. | Statutory Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Hazardous Materials Business Plan (Tier II reporting) | HMBP | HSC § 25500 et seq. |
| Hazardous Waste Generator Program | HW | HSC § 78000 et seq. |
| Underground Storage Tanks | UST | HSC § 25280 et seq. |
| Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act | APSA | HSC § 25270 et seq. |
| California Accidental Release Prevention Program | CalARP | HSC § 25531 et seq. |
| Household Hazardous Waste Facilities | HHW | HSC § 25218 et seq. |

Additional services: disaster response, environmental monitoring, stormwater inspections, and waste tire management.

## Statutory Basis

California Health & Safety Code § 25400 et seq. (HMBP/CUPA framework); HSC § 78000 et seq. (Division 45, Part 2, Ch 1, Art 1 — hazardous substance management/remediation).
