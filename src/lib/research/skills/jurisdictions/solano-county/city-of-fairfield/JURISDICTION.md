## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **CUPA agency:** Fairfield is NOT its own CUPA. The **Solano County Department of Resource Management, Environmental Health Division (HazMat Program)** is the countywide Certified Unified Program Agency serving Fairfield and all other Solano County jurisdictions (Benicia, Dixon, Rio Vista, Suisun City, Vacaville, Vallejo, plus unincorporated areas).
- **Programs administered:** HMBP (Hazardous Materials Business Plan / Tier II), Hazardous Waste, UST (Underground Storage Tanks), APSA (Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act), CalARP (California Accidental Release Prevention Program), HHW Facilities.
- **Office:** 675 Texas St, Suite 5500, Fairfield, CA 94533.
- **Phone:** (707) 784-6765 (8 AM–5 PM); after-hours: (707) 421-7090.
- **Email:** HazMat@SolanoCounty.gov
- **Statutory basis:** California Health & Safety Code § 25400 et seq. (HMBP); HSC § 78000 et seq. (hazardous waste remediation).

### 2. Fire AHJ — Fire Code Adoption & Permits

- **AHJ:** City of Fairfield Fire Department, Fire Prevention Division.
- **Contact:** (707) 399-5627; fireprevention@fairfield.ca.gov.
- **Adopted code:** **2022 California Fire Code** (2022 California Building Standards Code, Title 24, Part 9), adopted via **Ordinance No. 2022-15** — adopted in full including Division II of Chapter 1 and Appendices 4, A, B, BB, C, CC, D, H, and I. Additionally adopts Chapters 3, 5, 8, and Appendix D of the 2021 International Fire Code (IFC) to the extent not inconsistent with the 2022 CFC. Appendices E, G, J, K, L, M, N, O, and P are deleted in their entirety.
- **Key local amendments (Ord. 2022-15):**
  - All buildings > 3,600 sq ft must be equipped throughout with an approved fully automatic fire sprinkler system.
  - NFPA 13R sprinkler systems (§ 903.3.1.2) deleted in their entirety.
  - Domestic water services may only supply NFPA 13D automatic sprinkler systems.
  - Residential combination services (§ 903.3.5.2) deleted in their entirety.
  - Developments of 1- or 2-family dwellings exceeding 30 units require two separate approved fire apparatus access roads.
  - No-parking fire lane signs must comply with Fairfield Fire Prevention Standard 92-40.
- **Permit types:** Annual and temporary operational permits; Chapter 1, Division II of the CFC (§§ 105.6.1–105.6.24) specifies hazardous-operations permit categories.
- **Fee schedule:** Published effective 7/1/2024 and 7/1/2025 on City website; specific dollar amounts [VERIFY - fairfield.ca.gov blocked during fetch].
- **Municipal code chapter:** Chapter 8 (Fire Protection), Fairfield Municipal Code.

### 3. POTW / Industrial Wastewater Pretreatment

- **POTW operator:** **Fairfield-Suisun Sewer District (FSSD)** — a special district serving Fairfield, Suisun City, and Travis AFB (not the City of Fairfield directly). Address: 1010 Chadbourne Road, Fairfield, CA 94534.
- **Phone:** (707) 429-8930; email: contactus@fairfieldsuisunsewer.ca.gov; pretreatment email: sourcecontrol@fssd.com.
- **Controlling ordinance:** **Wastewater Discharge Ordinance No. 1701** (PDF available on District website).
- **Who needs a permit:** Any facility with non-domestic process flow, or any discharge beyond bathrooms and sinks, must obtain a permit through the District's Pretreatment Program.
- **Permit types:** Standard Industrial Pretreatment Permit; Short Term Discharge permit; Ground Water Discharge permit.
- **Local limits enforced:** Daily maximum concentrations for arsenic, cadmium, chromium, copper, lead, mercury, nickel, silver, zinc, BTEX, cyanide; plus pH, temperature, oil & grease.
- **Process:** Contact Pretreatment Program Coordinator at (707) 429-8930 or sourcecontrol@fssd.com; complete Short Term or Ground Water Discharge Request Form available on website.
- **NPDES permit for plant:** FSSD's NPDES permit reissuance pending before SF Bay RWQCB (Order R2-2025; CEQA notice posted 2025).
- Source (ordinances): https://www.fairfieldsuisunsewer.ca.gov/wastewater-ordinances/
- Source (discharge permits): https://www.fairfieldsuisunsewer.ca.gov/discharge-permits/
- Source (ordinance PDF): https://www.fairfieldsuisunsewer.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Ord-1701-1.pdf

### 4. Stormwater — MS4 / NPDES

- **MS4 permittee:** City of Fairfield Public Works Department.
- **Municipal ordinance:** **Chapter 22B — Stormwater Management and Discharge Control**, Fairfield Municipal Code. Purpose: regulate non-stormwater discharges to the public storm drain system and reduce pollutants to the maximum extent practicable (MEP), consistent with the Clean Water Act and Porter-Cologne Act.
- **Governing NPDES permit:** California Regional Water Quality Control Board, **San Francisco Bay Region** (RWQCB Region 2) Municipal Regional Stormwater NPDES Permit, **Order R2-2009-0074, NPDES Permit No. CAS612008** (and subsequent reissuances). Fairfield is one of three Solano County cities included in the SF Bay MRP, along with Suisun City and Vallejo.
- **Enforcement:** City of Fairfield Public Works Department — Storm Water Quality program.

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts & CUP Process

- **Zoning ordinance:** **Chapter 25 — Zoning Ordinance**, Fairfield Municipal Code (codified at codepublishing.com/CA/Fairfield).
- **Industrial zoning districts (Section 25.24):**
  - **IBP (Industrial Business Park):** Master-planned campus settings; suited for administrative/professional offices, R&D parks, limited distribution, light manufacturing and assembly. Commercial uses limited to business support services and accessory on-site sales.
  - **IL (Limited Industrial):** Low-intensity, light and medium industrial activities; typical uses include assembly/fabrication, warehousing, distribution centers, admin offices, business support services.
  - **IG (General Industrial):** Medium and heavy manufacturing; requires direct access to major transportation routes (arterials, freeways, rail); widest range of intense manufacturing and industrial uses.
- **CUP process:** Conditionally permitted uses ("C" on use tables) require a **Conditional Use Permit per Section 25.40.6** reviewed and acted on by the **Fairfield Planning Commission**. City has discretion to approve, condition, or deny based on individual case circumstances and criteria in § 25.40.6.
- **Zoning map:** Available via City of Fairfield Planning Division website.
