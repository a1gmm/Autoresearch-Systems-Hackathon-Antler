> Local fire code reference for **Solano County (unincorporated areas)**. Solano County has no single county fire department; fire AHJ is split between independent fire protection districts depending on location. All five districts named below adopted the same joint ordinance (No. 252-01) adopting the 2025 CFC.

## Adopted Code — County Building/Plan-Check

- **Code:** California Fire Code, **2025 Edition** (CFC 2025 = CCR Title 24, Part 9, based on IFC 2024)
- **County adopting ordinance:** Ordinance No. 2025-_____ (draft circulated as "25-862"), amending **Chapter 6.3, Section 6.3-03** of the Solano County Code. Passed and adopted by the Solano County Board of Supervisors at its regular meeting of **December 2, 2025**; operative **January 1, 2026**.
- **Appendices adopted (county building code chapter):** Appendix Chapter 4; Appendices B, BB, C, CC, D, E, F, G, I, and J.
- **Permit/plan-check portal:** https://aca-prod.accela.com/SOLANOCO/Default.aspx (online submittal required; plan check fees due at submittal, permit fees due at issuance)

## Fire Protection District AHJs — Unincorporated County

Solano County has **no county fire department**. Unincorporated parcels are served by one of five independent fire protection districts. Each district adopted the **joint ordinance (No. 252-01)** repealing and replacing Ordinance No. 1922-01, adopting all chapters of the **California Fire Code, 2025 Edition** (CCR Title 24, Part 9) with the local amendments described below.

| **Montezuma Fire Protection District** | ~300 sq mi eastern/rural (mostly farmland; Rio Vista vicinity) | 21 N 4th Street, Rio Vista CA 94571 · (707) 374-5962 · montezumafirepd@gmail.com |

> **Incorporated cities** have their own municipal fire departments (Fairfield FD, Vacaville FD, Vallejo FD, Benicia FD, Dixon FD, Rio Vista FD, Suisun City FD) and their own CFC adoption ordinances — see individual city files.

## Local Amendments (Joint Ordinance No. 252-01)

All five districts share these local amendments to the 2025 CFC:

- **§ 101.1 (Title):** Code is titled "Fire Code of the Dixon, Vacaville, Suisun, Cordelia and Montezuma Fire Protection Districts."
- **§ 102.1(5) (Applicability added):** Provisions apply to vehicles, ships, boats, and mobile vehicles when fixed in a specific location within Solano County boundaries.
- **§ 103.1 (Code Compliance Agency):** The Prevention Division of each district is the enforcement agency; the official in charge is the "fire code official."
- **§ 105.7 (CUPA Jurisdiction — local amendment):** The Solano County Environmental Health Division (acting as CUPA) has **exclusive authority** to implement, administer, and enforce state-mandated hazardous materials programs (HMBP, CalARP, UST, APSA, Hazardous Waste). Fire Code Officials shall **not** issue separate operational permits under CFC § 105.6.20 (Hazardous Materials) for activities already regulated under CUPA. The Fire Code Official retains authority for fire/life-safety enforcement, plan review, hazmat building requirements, and emergency response readiness inspections. Facilities subject to HSC § 25507 reporting thresholds must provide documentation of current CUPA permit coverage as a condition of fire clearance, occupancy, or business licensing.
- **§ 107.6 / 108.3–108.4 (Fees):** Board of Directors may establish fee schedule by resolution for plan review, inspections, permits, and false alarms. Work commencing before permit issuance is subject to an investigation fee equal to **three times the fire permit fee** in addition to regular fees.
- **§ 111.1.1 (Appeals — State Fire Marshal occupancies):** Written appeal to the State Fire Marshal is available where an alternate means of protection is denied in an SFM-regulated occupancy.

**Local climatic/topological justification for amendments:** Hot, dry summers (May–October, low humidity); north winds; freeway/rail rights-of-way that bisect surface roads impeding emergency access; natural creeks and drainage channels causing access problems in portions of the county.

## Fire Hazard Severity Zone (FHSZ) — LRA Enforcement

- **Effective July 2025:** County enforces Local Responsibility Area (LRA) FHSZ construction requirements for new buildings in unincorporated areas.
- **Basis:** 2025 Fire Hazard Severity Zone Maps Ordinance (adopted by Board of Supervisors) implementing updated State Fire Marshal FHSZ maps released February 24, 2025.
- **Solano County Consensus Fire Ordinance (2025 version):** Separate document available on the Building Safety Services ordinances page; governs construction standards in fire-hazard zones.
