> Wastewater reference for **Solano County**. There is no single countywide POTW — service is fragmented among three primary agencies depending on city/location. Industrial users must identify the correct POTW for their address. All three POTWs have active industrial pretreatment programs.

## 1. Fairfield-Suisun Sewer District (FSSD) — Primary POTW, Central Solano County

- **District:** Fairfield-Suisun Sewer District (FSSD), established 1951 by California Legislature act
- **Service area:** Fairfield, Suisun City, Travis AFB; approx. 135,000 residential/commercial/industrial customers
- **Plant:** Advanced secondary treatment WWTP; NPDES Permit No. CA0038024 (reissuance underway — Regional Water Board Order R2-2025-0028 as of August 2025)
- **Address:** 1010 Chadbourne Road, Fairfield CA 94534-9700
- **Pretreatment contact:** Pretreatment Program Coordinator — (707) 429-8930 · sourcecontrol@fssd.com
- **Wastewater Ordinances page:** https://www.fairfieldsuisunsewer.ca.gov/wastewater-ordinances/
- **Discharge Permits page:** https://www.fairfieldsuisunsewer.ca.gov/discharge-permits/

### Governing Ordinance

- **Wastewater Discharge Ordinance No. 1701** (current version 1701-1) — sets discharge requirements into the FSSD collection and treatment system; coordinated with SF Regional Water Quality Control Board (SFBRWQCB) and EPA.
- Local limits originally adopted January 28, 1991; last comprehensive evaluation 2015 (report submitted to SFBRWQCB October 14, 2015, with proposed revisions adopted ~January 2017).

### Industrial Pretreatment Program (IWP)

- **Active pretreatment program:** Yes — FSSD has an established pretreatment program with categorical industrial users (CIUs), non-significant categorical industrial users (NSCIUs), and general industrial users (IUs). As of the 2015 evaluation, seven industrial users were permitted.
- **Who must apply:** Any business with non-domestic process flows or discharges beyond standard bathroom/sink usage must contact the Pretreatment Coordinator before discharging.
- **Short-term/groundwater discharge:** Request form and Terms & Conditions available as PDF on FSSD discharge permits page.
- **NPDES context:** Effluent discharged to Boynton Slough, Suisun Marsh, and Ledgewood Creek (shallow tidal sloughs); FSSD operates under SFBRWQCB permit. Copper Control Program is an ongoing compliance obligation under the new permit (Order R2-2025-0028).

## 3. City of Vacaville — Easterly Wastewater Treatment Plant

- **Operator:** City of Vacaville Public Works / Utilities Department
- **Plant:** Easterly Wastewater Treatment Plant; also serves Elmira
- **Governing ordinance:** Vacaville Municipal Code **Chapter 13.08 (Sewers)** — https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/Vacaville/html/Vacaville13/Vacaville1308.html
- **Web:** https://www.cityofvacaville.gov/government/utilities/sewer
- **Contact:** (707) 469-6400

### Vacaville Pretreatment Program (VMC Chapter 13.08)

- **Active pretreatment program:** Yes — required by Federal and State regulations; funded in part by surcharges on permitted industrial users.
- **Who must permit:** Industrial facilities, haulers of septage/fats/oils/grease (FOG), and dischargers of remediated groundwater within city limits.
- **Pretreatment program covers:** Regulation of direct and indirect contributors to the municipal wastewater system; permit issuance, monitoring, enforcement, user reporting.
- **Capacity note:** Program "assumes that existing customer's capacity will not be preempted" (VMC § 13.08).
