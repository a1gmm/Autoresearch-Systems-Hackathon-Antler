> CUPA reference for **Solano County**. Solano County operates a **single countywide CUPA** administered by the Department of Resource Management, Environmental Health Division. This CUPA covers all unincorporated areas **and all seven incorporated cities** (Benicia, Dixon, Fairfield, Rio Vista, Suisun City, Vacaville, Vallejo). No city operates a separate CUPA or Participating Agency.

Single countywide CUPA — covers:
- All unincorporated Solano County
- City of Benicia
- City of Dixon
- City of Fairfield
- City of Rio Vista
- City of Suisun City
- City of Vacaville

**Key point:** There is no separate city-level CUPA anywhere in Solano County. The fire code amendment (Joint Ordinance No. 252-01, § 105.7) explicitly affirms the Environmental Health Division's exclusive authority for all CUPA-program elements countywide, and prohibits fire districts from issuing duplicative hazmat operational permits.

## HMBP / CERS Filing

- **Reporting portal:** CERS — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business (also referenced as http://siteportal.calepa.ca.gov)
- **Who must comply:** Businesses storing, using, or handling hazardous materials at or above CalEPA threshold quantities (generally 55 gallons liquid, 500 lbs solid, 200 cubic feet gas — per HSC § 25507)
- **Facilities above thresholds:** Must maintain current CUPA permit as a condition of fire clearance, occupancy, or business licensing (per fire code amendment § 105.7)

## Relationship to Fire Code / Fire Districts

The joint fire district ordinance (No. 252-01, § 105.7) expressly delineates CUPA vs. Fire Code Official authority:
- **CUPA:** Exclusive authority for HMBP, CalARP, UST, APSA, Hazardous Waste programs
- **Fire Code Official:** Retains authority for fire/life safety enforcement (CFC Chapters 50–67), plan review, hazmat building requirements (secondary containment, separation distances, signage), fire protection systems, and emergency response readiness inspections
- These are **complementary, not duplicative**: operators dealing with hazardous materials above thresholds need both CUPA compliance and CFC fire safety compliance
