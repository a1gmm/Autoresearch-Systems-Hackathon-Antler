## Industrial / Employment Zone Districts

Live Oak uses "Employment" zone district designations rather than traditional "Industrial" or "M" zone names. Two Employment zones have been identified:

### E1 — Employment / Office Zone

- **Intent:** Business office environment located along arterials or collectors
- **Primary uses:** Professional offices, business parks, research and development parks, related service uses in a campus-like setting
- **Supporting uses:** May include retail, service, or restaurant uses developed in conjunction with office use
- **General Plan consistency:** Employment land use designation
- **Residential:** [VERIFY — no explicit prohibition confirmed for E1; contact planning]

### E2 — Employment / Manufacturing Zone

- **Intent:** Medium and large lots for manufacturing and industrial uses
- **Permitted activity:** Manufacture, fabrication, assembly, or processing of raw and/or finished materials; industrial processing and assembly warehousing; auto service centers, research and development parks, construction industries, incidental wholesale and retail activity, outdoor storage
- **Residential:** **Prohibited** — residential uses of any kind are not allowed in the E2 district
- **General Plan consistency:** Employment land use designation

> [VERIFY] Whether uses such as hazardous materials storage, chemical processing, or food processing are permitted by right or require a Conditional Use Permit in E2. ## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

- **CUP authority:** Planning Commission (with City Council on appeal)
- **CUP procedure:** § 17.35.030, Title 17 — confirmed in search index
- **General processing procedures:** Chapter 17.37 — covers application processing timelines and procedures
- **Zoning amendments:** Chapter 17.36

The CUP process follows the standard California Government Code §65901 framework. Many industrial uses in the E2 district that involve elevated hazard levels, outdoor storage above specified quantities, or operations with significant off-site impacts likely require CUP approval rather than over-the-counter permits.

> [VERIFY] Obtain the use permit application and fee schedule from https://www.liveoakcity.org/departments/planning-department/useful-documents or by contacting the Planning Division at (530) 797-3026.
