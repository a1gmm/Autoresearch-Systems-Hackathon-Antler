> Research reference for Unified Hazardous Materials / Hazardous Waste program authority in the City of Live Oak, CA.
> Live Oak is NOT its own CUPA. It is served by the Sutter County Environmental Health Division (Development Services Department), the countywide CalEPA-designated CUPA. All six unified program elements are administered by the County.

## CUPA Identity

- **CUPA:** Sutter County Environmental Health Division (Development Services Department)
- **Designated by:** CalEPA (countywide CUPA covering all unincorporated areas and incorporated cities in Sutter County, including the City of Live Oak)

## Programs Administered

The Sutter County CUPA implements all six CalEPA unified program elements for businesses operating within the City of Live Oak:

| # | Program | Acronym | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan | HMBP | Submitted via CERS; Sutter County EH reviews and inspects |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator Program | HWG | Generator registration, manifest compliance, inspection |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tanks | UST | Permitting, operation, closure, leak response |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act | APSA | Spill prevention for above-threshold petroleum storage |
| 5 | California Accidental Release Prevention Program | CalARP | Local implementing agency for RMP submissions |
| 6 | CFC Hazardous Materials Operational Permits | CFC HazMat | Coordinated with fire AHJ (Sutter County Fire Dept.) |

## HMBP Program Details

- **Submission system:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business
- **Filing requirement:** All businesses storing, using, or handling hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities must file via CERS (mandated electronically since 1 January 2013 per AB 2286)
- **Thresholds (state baseline):** Generally 55 gallons liquid, 500 lbs solid, 200 cubic feet gas (Health & Safety Code §25507)
- **Inspection program:** Sutter County Environmental Health conducts inspections; frequency [VERIFY — contact Sutter County EH]

## Relationship to City

- City of Live Oak has **no independent CUPA authority** and no fire-based CUPA division.
- Sutter County Environmental Health is the sole CUPA for all regulated businesses in the city.
- Note: The Sutter County Fire Department (the fire AHJ for Live Oak) coordinates with the CUPA on CFC hazardous materials operational permits, but the CUPA itself is administered by Environmental Health (not fire).
