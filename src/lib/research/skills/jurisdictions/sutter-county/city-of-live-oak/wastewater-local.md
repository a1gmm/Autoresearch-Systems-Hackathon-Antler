## Facility Details

- **Design permitted flow:** 1.4 MGD (average dry weather flow)
- **Population served:** approximately 8,500
- **Facility type:** Major POTW (Threat to Water Quality: 1; Complexity: B)
- **Treatment:** Tertiary — headworks with odor control, selector basin, two oxidation ditches, two secondary clarifiers, two cloth media filters, UV disinfection (5-bank system); nitrification included
- **Receiving water:** Reclamation District 777 Lateral Drain No. 2, tributary to East Interceptor Canal, Wadsworth Canal, and Sutter Bypass (Sacramento watershed)
- **Beneficial uses protected:** AGR, GWR, FRSH, REC-1, WARM, WILD, MIGR, SPWN
- **Biosolids disposal:** Hauled to Recology Ostrom Road Landfill; regulated under 40 CFR Part 503

## Industrial Pretreatment Program

**Pretreatment Program: NO**

The Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board's Notice of Applicability (NOA) for enrollee R5-2017-0085-018 explicitly states:

> "Pretreatment Program: No"

There is no formal industrial pretreatment program (IPP) at the City of Live Oak WWTP. Industrial users discharging to the Live Oak sewer system are not subject to a locally-administered pretreatment permit program. Federal categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR categorical standards) still apply directly to industrial dischargers as a matter of federal law, but no local significant industrial user (SIU) permit program exists here.

For industrial process wastewater discharge questions, contact the Public Works Department directly. Extremely high-strength or hazardous waste streams may require hauling rather than sewer discharge.

### Key Chapter 13.32 Provisions (from search index — direct fetch blocked)

- Defines **industrial wastewater** as wastewater from industrial processes, trade, or business, as distinct from domestic or sanitary wastes
- Defines **industrial users** as nongovernmental, nonresidential users discharging more than the equivalent of 25,000 gallons per day
- Contains **unlawful discharge** prohibitions
- Defines **floatable fat / grease** and references pretreatment facilities for oil/fat/grease separation (gravity separation required for applicable dischargers)
- Article 7 covers permits, fees, and service charges for sewer connections
- Requires a permit from the Building Official for any installation, removal, alteration, repair, or replacement of plumbing drainage piping or fixtures
- Connections for properties outside the city require a permit and contract binding the applicant to all city sewer ordinances
