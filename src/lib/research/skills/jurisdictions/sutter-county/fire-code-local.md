## Fire AHJ

**Sutter County Fire Department** (administered under the Office of Emergency Services) is the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) for fire code enforcement in the **unincorporated county and the City of Live Oak** (by contract). The City of Yuba City has its own municipal fire department and is a separate AHJ — see the Yuba City jurisdiction file.

Contact: Sutter County Office of Emergency Services, 1130 Civic Center Blvd, Yuba City, CA 95993.
Web: https://www.sutter.gov/government/county-departments/office-of-emergency-services/sutter-county-fire-department

## Local Code Structure

Sutter County adopts and locally amends the California Fire Code (CFC) through **Title 600 (Fire Prevention)** of the Sutter County Code of Ordinances, hosted on Municode. The operative chapter is:

- **Chapter 620 — Fire Code**: adopts the California Fire Code by reference
  - **Section 620-030 — Amendments**: contains local amendments to the adopted CFC

## Related Building Code Chapter

Chapter 1360 of Title 1300 (Building) addresses fire-related building requirements and fee schedules for the unincorporated county. The county adopts Title 24 building codes through Title 1300.

## Yuba City Note (adjacent AHJ)

The City of Yuba City introduced an ordinance in late 2025 / early 2026 to adopt the **2025 California Fire Code**, repealing and reenacting Chapter 5 of Title 4 of the Yuba City Municipal Code. Local amendments carried forward include: sprinkler requirement for commercial buildings ≥ 3,600 sq ft; permitting requirements for certain events.
