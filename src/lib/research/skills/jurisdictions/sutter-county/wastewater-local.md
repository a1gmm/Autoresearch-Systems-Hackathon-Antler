## Controlling Authority

Wastewater service in the unincorporated county is administered by the **Sutter County Public Works Department, Water Resources Division**.

## POTW / Collection Systems

Sutter County operates **small rural wastewater systems** for specific unincorporated communities — there is no single county-wide POTW. Known systems:

- **Sutter County Waterworks District No. 1 (SCWWD #1)** — serves the Community of **Robbins** via a Septic Tank Effluent Pumping (STEP) system (constructed 1997). Treats ~10 million gallons/year. Governed by county-issued monthly wastewater rate ordinances (SCWWD#1 – 2009-01, SCWWD#1 – 16-01).
- **Rio Ramaza Community Services District (CSD)** — serves the Community of **Rio Ramaza** with sewer lines, a lift station, and two wastewater ponds (~10,000 gallons/day capacity).
- Most other rural unincorporated communities rely on **onsite septic systems** permitted by the Sutter County Environmental Health Division, not on central sewers.

**Cities:** Yuba City and Live Oak operate their own separate municipal wastewater systems with their own POTWs — see city-specific jurisdiction files.

## Industrial Pretreatment Program

**No formal Industrial Pretreatment Program (IPP) found for the county-operated systems.** The SCWWD #1 STEP system and Rio Ramaza ponds serve small rural residential communities; no industrial or significant commercial users have been identified in the publicly available documentation. An EPA-required IWP only applies to POTWs with design flow ≥ 5 MGD or those receiving significant industrial users — these county systems fall well below that threshold.

## NPDES / Stormwater Context

The urbanized portions of Sutter County hold a **Phase II Small MS4 General Permit** (effective July 2013, issued by the State Water Resources Control Board) for stormwater. This is separate from wastewater discharge.
