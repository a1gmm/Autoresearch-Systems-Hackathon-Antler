> Research reference for the City of Yuba City fire code adoption and local AHJ. Yuba City operates its own municipal fire department (not served by a county fire district or CAL FIRE). The Fire Prevention Bureau is the AHJ for fire code enforcement within city limits.

## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

- **AHJ:** City of Yuba City Fire Department — Fire Prevention Bureau
- **Address:** 824 Clark Avenue, Yuba City, CA 95991
- **Web:** https://www.yubacity.net/departments/fire_department/prevention/index.php
- **General Fire Dept web:** https://www.yubacity.net/departments/fire_department/index.php
- **Note:** Yuba City Fire is a municipal department separate from Sutter County Fire. It serves the incorporated city limits only.

## Adopted Fire Code

- **Governing municipal code:** Yuba City Municipal Code (YCMC) Title 4 (Public Safety), Chapter 5 (Fire Prevention)
- **Most recently confirmed adopted edition:** 2025 California Fire Code (CFC) — Yuba City introduced an ordinance to adopt the 2025 CFC and carried forward existing local amendments from the prior cycle. The 2025 CFC is based on the 2024 International Fire Code (IFC) and became effective statewide 2026-01-01.
- **Prior edition:** 2022 California Fire Code (previously in effect before the 2026-01-01 statewide transition)
- **Ordinance action:** City Council introduced an ordinance repealing and reenacting Chapter 5 of Title 4 of the YCMC to adopt the 2025 CFC; first reading waived per staff recommendation. [VERIFY — confirm ordinance number and final adoption date with City Clerk or Fire Prevention Bureau]

## Local Amendments (Carried Forward)

The following local amendments were confirmed as carried forward into the 2025 CFC adoption cycle:

1. **Commercial sprinkler threshold:** Fire protection (automatic sprinkler) required for commercial buildings of **3,600 square feet or larger** — a more stringent local threshold than the state CFC baseline. This has been a longstanding Yuba City local amendment.
2. **Security gates:** Permitting requirements for certain security gates (gates must allow emergency vehicle/personnel access).
3. **Event permits:** Permitting requirements for certain public events.
4. **2025 cycle additions (statewide):** New state provisions affecting battery storage (energy storage systems) and lithium-ion batteries were incorporated as part of the 2025 CFC adoption; staff proposed no additional new local amendments beyond carrying forward the above.

> [VERIFY] Obtain the adopting ordinance number and effective date from Yuba City Clerk or the Municode code update. Confirm whether the 3,600 sq ft sprinkler requirement applies to new construction only or also to change-of-occupancy and tenant improvements.

## Fire Prevention Bureau Operations

- The Fire Prevention Bureau is responsible for all state-mandated inspections, new construction plan review, tenant improvement projects, weed abatement, firework stands, and public education.
- Inspections are conducted using the California Fire Code, California Building Code, and NFPA standards.
- The bureau is staffed by a Fire Marshal supported by two Fire Safety Inspectors.
- **Operational permits** are issued on commercial occupancies to verify fire-safe conditions. Permit applications are managed through Accela (plan reviews and permit payments).

## Relationship to CUPA

- The City of Yuba City Fire Department is **not** the CUPA. Hazmat programs (HMBP, UST, APSA, hazardous waste) are administered by the **Sutter County Environmental Health Division (CUPA)** — see `cupa-local.md`.
- The Yuba-Sutter Hazardous Materials Response Team (YSHMRT) provides regional HazMat emergency response as mutual aid; this is separate from CUPA program administration.
