> Research reference for industrial dischargers to the Yuba City sewer system. Yuba City operates its own POTW and has an active Industrial Pretreatment Program (IPP) with industrial waste discharge permits. This is not a "no-program" rural jurisdiction — industrial users must obtain a local discharge permit.

## Industrial Pretreatment Program (IPP)

Yuba City operates an active Industrial Pretreatment Program administered by the Public Works / Utilities division Environmental Compliance section.

### Program Functions

- Issues industrial waste discharge permits to regulated industrial and commercial users
- Samples and monitors wastewater in the collection system to verify permit compliance
- Manages the Fats, Oils, and Grease (FOG) Control Program
- Provides public outreach and education
- Enforces discharge prohibitions under 40 CFR 403.5 and local limits

### Permit Categories

| Permit Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Industrial/Commercial Wastewater Discharge Permit | Required for significant industrial users (SIUs) and other regulated non-domestic dischargers |
| Treated Groundwater Discharge Permit | For groundwater remediation discharges to the sewer |
| Septic Waste Hauler Permit | For hauled septage discharge |

### Regulated Industries / Users

The program addresses discharge concerns from food service establishments, dry cleaners, and other commercial/industrial users. Significant Industrial Users (SIUs) subject to federal Categorical Pretreatment Standards (CPS) under 40 CFR Parts 405–471 must comply with applicable categorical standards in addition to local limits.

### Local Limits

- Local limits are established under YCMC Title 6 Chapter 5 and implement 40 CFR 403.5(c) requirements
- The NPDES permit (Order R5-2024-XXXX) requires Yuba City to provide a written technical evaluation of the need to revise local limits in accordance with 40 CFR 122.44(j)(2)(ii) by 1 March 2028
- [VERIFY — specific numerical local limits (e.g. mg/L for metals, pH range, temperature) are not publicly posted online; contact Environmental Compliance for current Local Limits document]

### Discharge Prohibitions

General prohibitions under 40 CFR 403.5 apply: no flammable/explosive materials, no materials causing obstruction, no materials causing interference with POTW operation, no hazardous concentrations of toxic pollutants.

## IWP Application Process

1. Contact Environmental Compliance (Kathy Willis or Ryan Armore) to determine if an Industrial Waste Discharge Permit is required for your discharge.
2. Submit permit application to Environmental Compliance section.
3. City evaluates discharge against local limits and categorical pretreatment standards.
4. Permit issued with monitoring requirements, reporting schedule, and local limit conditions.
5. Annual compliance reports and periodic self-monitoring are required for SIUs.

[VERIFY — obtain current permit application form and fee schedule directly from Environmental Compliance]

## FOG Program

The Fats, Oils, and Grease (FOG) Control Program applies to food service establishments. Grease interceptors/traps are required; maintenance and cleaning records must be maintained. Contact Environmental Compliance for FOG permit requirements.
