> Research reference for CUPA programs affecting businesses in Yuba City. The City of Yuba City is **not** its own CUPA. All CalEPA unified program functions (HMBP, UST, APSA, hazardous waste generator, CalARP) for businesses within Yuba City are administered by the **Sutter County Environmental Health Division**, which is the CalEPA-designated countywide CUPA covering all of Sutter County including incorporated cities.

## CUPA Identity

- **CUPA:** Sutter County Environmental Health Division (Development Services Department)
- **CalEPA designation:** Sutter County Environmental Health is the CalEPA-certified CUPA for all of Sutter County, including the City of Yuba City
- **Address:** 1130 Civic Center Blvd., Yuba City, CA 95993
- **Web:** https://www.sutter.gov/government/county-departments/development-services/environmental-health/cupa
- **HMBP forms:** https://www.sutter.gov/government/county-departments/development-services/environmental-health/cupa/hazardous-materials-business-plan-hmbp-forms

## Programs Administered

Sutter County Environmental Health administers all six CalEPA unified programs for businesses in Yuba City:

| # | Program | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) | Filed via CERS; county EH reviews and inspects |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator Program | Generator ID, manifest compliance, inspection |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tanks (UST) | Installation, operation, modification, closure, leak response |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) | Spill prevention for above-threshold petroleum storage |
| 5 | California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) | Local implementing agency for RMP submissions |
| 6 | CFC Hazardous Materials Operational Permits | Coordinated with Yuba City Fire Department as local fire AHJ |

## HMBP / CERS Filing

- **Submission system:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business
- **Who must comply:** Businesses storing, using, or handling hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities (generally 55 gallons liquid, 500 lbs solid, 200 cubic feet gas — per Health & Safety Code §25507)
- **Electronic filing required:** Mandatory electronic submission via CERS (required statewide since January 1, 2013)
- **Annual review:** HMBP information must be reviewed and re-certified annually in CERS
- **Update triggers:** Material quantity increases ≥100%; new previously-undisclosed materials; address/ownership/name changes; significant operational changes

## Aboveground and Underground Storage Tanks

- Sutter County Environmental Health administers both UST and APSA/aboveground tank programs countywide
- Tank permits, inspections, and leak reporting are handled through the county CUPA office at 1130 Civic Center Blvd

## Emergency Response

- The **Yuba-Sutter Hazardous Materials Response Team (YSHMRT)** provides regional HazMat emergency response for both Yuba and Sutter Counties. Yuba City Fire Department participates in this team as mutual aid. Contact: https://www.yuba.gov/departments/community_development/environmental_health/hazardous_materials_(cupa)/emergency_response/yuba_sutter_hazardous_materials_response_team/index.php
- YSHMRT is an emergency response function; it is not the CUPA regulatory program.

## Relationship to City Fire Department

- The **City of Yuba City Fire Department** is the local fire AHJ and issues CFC operational permits for hazmat storage/use within city limits (see `fire-code-local.md`), but it is not the CUPA.
- Businesses may need both a Sutter County CUPA filing (HMBP/UST/APSA via CERS) and a Yuba City Fire operational permit for hazmat operations above CFC permit thresholds — these are parallel, not substitute, requirements.
