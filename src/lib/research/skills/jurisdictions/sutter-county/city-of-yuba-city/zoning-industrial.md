## Industrial Zone Districts

### M-1 — Light Industrial District (Article 20)

**Purpose:** Provide suitable areas for low-intensity assembly, processing, or manufacturing activities, product distribution, and related activities that avoid creating excessive noise, dust, odor, smoke, or vibration nuisances on surrounding properties.

**Permitted Uses (representative list):**
- Auto body and painting shops
- Machine shops and welding shops
- Cabinet and sheet metal shops
- Wholesale businesses and distribution centers
- Storage warehouses and mini-warehouses
- Research and development laboratories
- Commercial laundries
- Truck terminals and contractor yards
- Indoor assembly and manufacturing meeting noise/odor restrictions
- Recycling facilities
- Support uses (e.g., coffee shops/cafeterias serving the industrial use)
- Accessory dwelling units (subject to specific standards)
- Retail sales limited to 15% of building square footage, products created on-site only

**Development Standards:**

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Minimum lot size | 20,000 sq ft |
| Maximum building height | 45 feet |
| Front yard setback | 10 feet (min) |
| Street side yard setback | 10 feet (min) |
| Interior side yard setback | None (15 ft when adjacent to residential) |
| Rear yard setback | None (15 ft when adjacent to residential or through lots) |

Additional requirements per referenced articles: parking, landscaping, lighting, signage, trash enclosures.

---

### M-2 — Industrial District (Article 21)

**Purpose:** Provide areas for a full range of industrial uses consistent with the Industrial General Plan designation.

**Permitted Uses:** All uses permitted in M-1, plus:
- Bulk petroleum storage and wholesale
- Bulk product storage (indoor)
- Cement and asphalt batch plants
- Dismantling yards
- Food processing operations
- Gravel crushing or processing
- Junk yards
- Lumber processing
- Recycling facilities
- Wood pulp processing
- Indoor recreational facilities
- Public and quasi-public uses

**Conditional Uses (Use Permit required):**
- Caretaker/night watchman residence (as part of industrial use only)
- Commercial coaches (various sizes)
- Emergency shelters
- Explosive or hazardous material handling
- Flea markets
- Heliports
- Outdoor assembly/processing with significant noise, dust, or odor
- Limited retail sales (max 15% of building sq ft, on-site products only)
- Outdoor storage and sales areas

**Development Standards:**

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Minimum lot size | 1 acre |
| Maximum building height | 60 feet |
| Front yard setback | 10 feet (min) |
| Street side yard setback | 10 feet (min) |
| Interior side yard setback | None (15 ft when adjacent to residential) |
| Rear yard setback | None (15 ft when adjacent to residential or through-lot conditions) |

## CUP / Use Permit Process

- Conditional uses in industrial districts require a **Use Permit** (Yuba City's term for a Conditional Use Permit)
- Review process is described in YCMC §8-5.7003 [VERIFY — Municode direct fetch returned 403; confirm procedure through Planning Division or Municode]
- Applications go to the **Planning Division**, Development Services Department

## Other Relevant Zone Districts

- **C-M (Heavy Commercial/Light Industrial):** Also permits some light industrial uses alongside heavy commercial; consider for operations at the commercial/industrial boundary
- **PD (Planned Development):** Can accommodate industrial uses with custom standards
