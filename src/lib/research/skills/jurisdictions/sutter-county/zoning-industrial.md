## Zoning Code Location

**Title 1500 — Zoning**, Sutter County Code of Ordinances (Municode). This is the operative zoning ordinance for the **unincorporated county only**; Yuba City and Live Oak both maintain their own zoning codes.

## Industrial Zoning Districts

The Zoning Code establishes three industrial / employment-tier designations in **Part 2 (District Specific Regulations)** and **Article 7 (Commercial and Employment Districts)**:

| District | Name | General Plan Implements |
|----------|------|------------------------|
| **M-1** | Light Industrial | Industrial, Industrial/Commercial, Employment Corridor |
| **M-2** | General Industrial | Industrial, Industrial/Commercial, Employment Corridor |
| **EC** | Employment Corridor | Employment Corridor (Highway 99 corridor and adjacent areas) |

**M-1 (Light Industrial)**: Full range of lower-intensity manufacturing, assembly, processing, fabrication, bulk handling, storage, and warehousing conducted in a manner that minimizes visual and operational impacts on adjoining uses.

**M-2 (General Industrial)**: Intensive manufacturing, assembly, processing, fabrication, bulk handling of products, storage, warehousing, and heavy trucking. Intended to locate away from residential and sensitive uses.

**EC (Employment Corridor)**: Mixed employment and revenue-generating land uses; primarily along the Highway 99 corridor north and south of Yuba City and adjacent areas.

## Approval Types

The Zoning Code uses a standardized approval matrix (Table 1500-07-1 for commercial/employment districts):

- **P** — Permitted outright (ministerial)
- **ZC** — Zoning Clearance (ministerial)
- **AP** — Administrative Permit (staff-level, no hearing)
- **UP** — Use Permit (requires public hearing before Planning Commission or Board of Supervisors)

Uses not specifically listed may be approved by the Planning Director under Section 1500-03-030.

## CUP / Use Permit Process

"Use Permit" (UP) is the Sutter County equivalent of a Conditional Use Permit. Applications are processed by **Sutter County Planning Services** (within Development Services). Uses requiring a UP go before the **Planning Commission** for a public hearing with findings of consistency with the General Plan and applicable development standards.

Variance, general plan amendments, and rezone applications follow similar Planning Commission → Board of Supervisors tracks.

## Overlay Districts

Title 1500 also contains overlay districts (Article 8 — Overlay/Combining Districts), which may impose additional requirements on industrial parcels (e.g., flood, agricultural buffer, highway corridor overlays). Confirm applicable overlays for any specific parcel.
