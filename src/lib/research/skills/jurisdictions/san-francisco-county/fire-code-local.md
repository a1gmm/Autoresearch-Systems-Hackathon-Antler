## Adopting Ordinance

- **Ordinance No. 248-22**, File No. 221103
- Approved December 15, 2022; effective and operative **January 15, 2023**
- Repeals the 2019 San Francisco Fire Code in its entirety and enacts the 2022 San Francisco Fire Code
- Adopted findings of local conditions per California Health and Safety Code § 17958.7
- Board of Supervisors sponsor: Mayor Breed; Supervisors Peskin, Chan

## Municipal Code Chapter

**San Francisco Fire Code, Chapter 1** — San Francisco General Code Provisions. Provisions marked "[For SF]" replace the corresponding CFC or IFC section.

## Local Conditions Findings (§ 17958.7)

San Francisco identified six local conditions justifying more-restrictive amendments:
1. Increased earthquake/fire risk from local microzones and liquefaction hazards
2. High-density buildings on very small lots, many built to property lines
3. Topography driving high-density development requiring special fire separation
4. Steep hills and narrow streets requiring special safety provisions
5. High building density and crowded occupancy
6. Surge in high-rise and super-high-rise development

## Hazardous Materials Permit Thresholds

The SFFC adopts the CFC 2022 Chapter 50 quantities without modification to the maximum allowable quantity (MAQ) tables. Key MAQ examples from Table 5003.1.1(1)–(4):
- Flammable liquids Class IA: 30 gallons per control area (storage)
- Combustible liquids Class II: 120 gallons per control area (storage)
- Flammable solids: 125 pounds per control area
- Corrosives: 500 gallons liquid / 5,000 pounds solid
- Highly toxic: 10 gallons liquid / 10 pounds solid
- MAQ may increase 100% in sprinklered buildings (per SFM Table note)

Quantities exceeding MAQ trigger an operational permit (Sec. 105.5, "Hazardous Materials, Regulated Activities").
