## POTW Operator

**San Francisco Public Utilities Commission (SFPUC)**
Wastewater Enterprise
Operates two treatment facilities:
- Southeast Water Pollution Control Plant (Hunters Point)
- Oceanside Water Pollution Control Plant (western SF)

Contact: SewerUseInquiries@sfwater.org | 311 or (415) 701-2311

## Sewer-Use / Pretreatment Ordinance

**SF Municipal Code, Article 4.1 (Industrial Waste Ordinance #19-92)**
Formally: "Chapter X (Public Works Code) of Part II of the San Francisco Municipal Code, Article 4.1"

- Ordinance adopted: **Ord. 19-92**, App. 1/23/92
- Amended Article 4.1 sections are codified at **Sec. 118–124** of the SF Municipal Code (Public Works Code, Chapter X, Part II)
- Additional pollutant limits: **SF Department of Public Works Order No. 158170** — "Industrial Waste Discharge Limits into City's Sewerage System" (adopted under authority of Article 4.1)

Federal overlay: Clean Water Act (33 U.S.C. § 1251 et seq.); 40 CFR Part 403 General Pretreatment Regulations; federal Categorical Pretreatment Standards at 40 CFR Parts 405–471.

NPDES permit: issued by **SF Bay Regional Water Quality Control Board** (Region 2).

## Local Discharge Limits Table

Per **DPW Order No. 158170**, the following concentration-based numerical limits apply to any 24-hour composite sample of wastewater discharged to the City's sewerage system:

| Pollutant | Limit (mg/L) |
|-----------|-------------|
| Arsenic (As), Total | 4.0 |
| Cadmium (Cd), Total | 0.5 |
| Chromium (Cr), Total | 5.0 |
| Copper (Cu), Total | 4.0 |
| Lead (Pb), Total | 1.5 |
| Mercury (Hg), Total | 0.05 |
| Nickel (Ni), Total | 2.0 |
| Silver (Ag), Total | 0.6 |
| Zinc (Zn), Total | 7.0 |
| Phenol | 23.0 |
| Cyanide (CN), Total | 1.0 |

All parameters defined per 40 CFR Part 136 (1991). Limits apply at the point of discharge into the sewerage system; dilution of a discharge to achieve compliance is prohibited.

**Additional grab-sample limits (Ord. 19-92, Sec. 123):**

| Pollutant Parameter | Limit |
|--------------------|-------|
| pH | 6.0 min – 9.5 max |
| Dissolved Sulfides | 0.5 mg/L |
| Temperature | 125°F (52°C) max |
| Hydrocarbon Oil and Grease | 100 mg/L (grab) |
| Total Recoverable Oil and Grease | 300 mg/L (production-week composite) |

**Categorical standards** (40 CFR Parts 405–471) apply on top of local limits where applicable (e.g., electroplating, metal finishing, dental amalgam).

## Prohibited Discharges (Ord. 19-92, Sec. 123(e)–(f))

No person shall discharge without a permit:
- Flammable or explosive substances
- Any substance causing obstruction, interference, or pass-through
- Bioaccumulative Toxic Substances exceeding Soluble Threshold Limit Concentration (STLC)
- Wastewater raising treatment plant influent above 104°F (40°C)
- Radioactive materials (require separate Director permit + NRC authorization)
- Groundwater / dewatering / remediation discharges (permit required; apply ≥45 days before commencement)

## Permit Types and Process

| Permit Type | Description | Application |
|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| **Industrial User (IU) Permit** | For regular or continuous discharges from commercial/industrial operations (laundries, food processors, hospitals, dental offices) | IU Permit Application Form + Instructions |
| **Significant Industrial User (SIU) Permit** | IU with elevated discharge volume/toxicity, includes Slug Discharge Control Plan | SIU Permit Application Form (revised 05/2022) + Slug Discharge Control Plan |
| **Batch Wastewater Discharge Permit** | Non-routine, occasional, or temporary discharges: construction dewatering, contaminated-site investigation, pressure-washing, well drilling | Batch Permit Application Form (revised 9/22); Instructions revised 3/31/25 |
| **Waste Hauler Permit** | Trucked waste disposal into City sewerage system | Waste Hauler Permit Application (revised 3/31/2025) |

All permit applications available at: https://www.sfpuc.gov/programs/pretreatment-program/wastewater-discharge-permits
