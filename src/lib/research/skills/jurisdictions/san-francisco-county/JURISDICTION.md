## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **CUPA agency:** City and County of San Francisco, Department of Public Health (SFDPH), Environmental Health Branch — Hazardous Materials and Waste Program. Single certified CUPA for the entire City and County; no Participating Agencies because SF is a consolidated city-county.
- **Program elements administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generator / Tiered Permitting, Underground Storage Tank (UST — installation, modification, closure), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA/AST), California Accidental Release Prevention / Risk Management Plan (CalARP/RMP).
- **Reporting threshold:** Businesses storing ≥55 gallons of liquids, ≥500 lb of solids, or ≥200 cu ft of compressed gases (or laboratory quantities above reportable quantities) must register.
- **Local ordinance chapters:**
  - SF Health Code Article 21 — Hazardous Materials (implements CA H&S Code Ch. 6.95 [HMBP] and Ch. 6.7/6.75 [UST])
  - SF Health Code Article 21A — Risk Management Plans (implements CA H&S Code Ch. 6.95 CalARP)
  - SF Health Code Article 22 — Hazardous Waste Management (implements CA H&S Code Ch. 6.5)
  - SF Health Code Article 24 — Chlorofluorocarbon Recycling Ordinance
- **Fee schedule:** SF Health Code Sec. 1176. Base annual fee and new-registration application fee each set at $172 (base year); Controller adjusts annually by CPI each April 15 for the following fiscal year. Source: https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/san_francisco/latest/sf_health/0-0-0-3882
- **Electronic reporting portal:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — statewide portal required for HMBP submittals.
- **Forms and resources:** https://www.sf.gov/resource--2023--hazardous-materials-and-waste-forms-and-information
- Source: https://www.sf.gov/topics/hazardous-materials-and-waste | https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/san_francisco/latest/sf_health/0-0-0-3423

### 2. Fire AHJ — San Francisco Fire Department (SFFD)

- **AHJ:** San Francisco Fire Department — Division of Fire Prevention & Investigation / Bureau of Fire Prevention. Single municipal fire department; covers the entire consolidated city-county.
- **Adopted fire code:** 2025 San Francisco Fire Code, which adopts with local amendments the **2025 California Fire Code** (Title 24, Part 9) and incorporates by reference portions of the 2024 International Fire Code not adopted by the California Building Standards Commission. Prefix "[For SF]" marks local SF amendments that replace state code provisions.
- **Local amendment process:** SFFD Chief must recommend more-restrictive standards to the Board of Supervisors (through the Fire Commission) within 180 days of each state code adoption, based on local climatic, topographical, or geological conditions.
- **Permit fees (Chapter 1, Division II, Section 113; also SF Business & Tax Regulations Code Article 2):** Most operational/temporary fire permits cost **$436**; conditional use permits $95; non-profit assembly permits waived. Inspection fee: **$153/hour**. Preliminary consultation fee: **$612**. Fees reviewed annually by the SF Controller and Board of Supervisors.
- **Online permit portal (PermitSF):** Fire alarm, fire sprinkler, and open flame permits — https://sf-fire.org/services/permits
- **Bureau of Fire Prevention:** 49 South Van Ness, Suite 560, San Francisco CA 94103 | (628) 652-3260 | Mon 9am–5pm, Tue–Fri 8am–5pm
- Source: https://sf-fire.org/services/permits | https://up.codes/viewer/san_francisco/ca-fire-code-2025/chapter/1/san-francisco-general-code-provisions | https://sf-fire.org/media/2941/download?inline= (SFFD FY2024-25 Operating Budget)

### 3. POTW / Industrial Wastewater — SFPUC Pretreatment Program

- **POTW operator:** San Francisco Public Utilities Commission (SFPUC) — operates the Southeast Water Pollution Control Plant and Oceanside Water Pollution Control Plant.
- **Local sewer-use ordinance:** SF Municipal Code, **Article 4.1, Chapter X, Part II** (sewer-use / pretreatment ordinance). Additional pollutant limits in Department of Public Works Order No. 158170.
- **Federal basis:** Clean Water Act + 40 CFR Part 403 General Pretreatment Regulations; NPDES permits issued by the SF Bay Regional Water Quality Control Board.
- **Permit types:**
  - Industrial User (IU) Permit — for regular/continuous discharges (laundries, food processors, hospitals, dental offices, etc.)
  - Significant Industrial User (SIU) Permit — with Slug Discharge Control Plan
  - Batch Wastewater Discharge Permit — for non-routine/temporary discharges (construction dewatering, well drilling, pressure washing)
  - Waste Hauler Permit — for trucked waste
- **Application:** Permit application forms available on SFPUC website; Batch and Waste Hauler forms revised 3/31/25.
- **Contact:** SewerUseInquiries@sfwater.org | 311 or (415) 701-2311
- **Fee info:** Rates listed under SFPUC Water, Power and Sewer Rates — https://www.sfpuc.gov/accounts-services/water-power-sewer-rates/rates [VERIFY - specific IWP permit fee amounts not published on the permits page]
- Source: https://www.sfpuc.gov/programs/pretreatment-program/wastewater-discharge-permits

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater

- **MS4 permittee:** SFPUC — covered under the SF Bay Regional Water Quality Control Board Municipal Regional NPDES Stormwater Permit (MRP Order No. R2-2022-0018, replacing prior permit).
- **Stormwater Management Ordinance (SMO):** Effective May 22, 2010; updated 2016. Codified at **SF Public Works Code, Article 4.2, Sections 147–147.6**.
- **Applicability:** New and redevelopment projects creating/replacing ≥5,000 sq ft of impervious surface in combined-sewer areas, or ≥2,500 sq ft in separate-sewer areas, must manage stormwater using green infrastructure maintained for the lifetime of the project.
- **Performance standards:** Combined sewer areas — match pre-development runoff or reduce by 25%; separate sewer areas — capture and treat 0.75-inch design storm.
- **Compliance steps:** (1) Preliminary Stormwater Control Plan (PSCP) approved before DBI permit; (2) Final SCP approved before Certificate of Final Completion; (3) Certification of Acceptable Construction during construction; (4) post-construction maintenance agreement recorded; (5) annual self-certification inspections.
- **Construction Site Runoff Ordinance:** Adopted 2013, Ordinance 260-13. Applies to all construction sites; SWPPP + NOI required for ≥1 acre; ESCP + project application for 5,000 sq ft–1 acre; BMPs-only for <5,000 sq ft. Civil penalties up to $25,000/day.
- **Contact / portal:** StormwaterReview@sfwater.org | stormwater credit lookup: https://stormwater.sfpuc.org/
- Source: https://www.sfpuc.gov/construction-contracts/design-guidelines-standards/stormwater-management | https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/san_francisco/latest/sf_publicworks/0-0-0-780 | https://www.sfpuc.gov/programs/pretreatment-program/construction-site-runoff

### 5. Zoning / Industrial Districts — SF Planning Code

- **Code authority:** SF Planning Code (Title I of the SF Municipal Code), enforced by the SF Planning Department. Zoning districts defined in Article 2 (Use Districts).
- **Industrial district types (Planning Code Section 210 / 210.3):**
  - **M-1** (Light Industrial) and **M-2** (Heavy Industrial) — traditional industrial/manufacturing zones permitting heavy industrial uses.
  - **PDR-1-D** (Design) — retains and encourages less-intensive PDR businesses, especially design-related clusters.
  - **PDR-1-G** (General) — retains and encourages existing PDR activities and promotes new business formation.
  - **PDR-2** (Core PDR) — encourages a wide range of light and contemporary industrial activities; non-residential only (housing, large office, and large-scale retail not permitted).
- **PDR = Production, Distribution, and Repair** — SF's primary industrial use classification. Industrial Protection Zones (IPZs) provide additional protection against displacement of PDR uses.
- **Conditional Use Authorization (CUA):** Governed by Planning Code Section 303 — Planning Commission hears and determines CUA applications. Required for uses not principally permitted in a zoning district. Criteria: necessary or desirable to the neighborhood, no undue negative effect, compliance with General Plan. Filing: CUA Supplemental Application + Project Application (PRJ). Contact: Planning Information Center, 628-652-7300 or pic@sfgov.org.
- **Property lookup:** SF Planning GIS Property Information Map — https://sfplanninggis.org/PIM/
- Source: https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/san_francisco/latest/sf_planning/0-0-0-20114 (Sec. 210) | https://sfbos.org/industrial-protection-zones-livework-projects-and-community-plans | https://www.sf.gov/information--zoning-businesses

## Notes

- **CONSOLIDATED CITY-COUNTY:** San Francisco is a single consolidated city and county. There are no separately incorporated cities within it; all local EHS authority (CUPA, fire AHJ, building, POTW, stormwater, zoning) is held by City and County of San Francisco departments. No city-level files apply.
- **Single CUPA, no Participating Agencies:** SFDPH Hazardous Materials and Waste Program is the sole certified CUPA for the entire jurisdiction.
- **Federal-land carve-outs:** The Presidio of San Francisco (Presidio Trust / NPS — federal), Port of San Francisco, and SFO Airport (physically in San Mateo County) operate under their own authorities — confirm jurisdiction case-by-case for facilities on these properties.
