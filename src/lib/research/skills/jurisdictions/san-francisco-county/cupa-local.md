## CUPA Agency

**San Francisco Department of Public Health (SFDPH)**
Environmental Health Branch — Hazardous Materials and Waste Program (HMUPA)
49 South Van Ness Avenue, Suite 600, San Francisco, CA 94103
Phone: (415) 252-3800 | Email: EnvHealth.DPH@sfdph.org

- **Own CUPA** — SFDPH is directly certified by CalEPA as the CUPA for the entire consolidated City and County of San Francisco.
- **No Participating Agencies** — SF is a single-jurisdiction consolidated city-county; all CUPA program elements are administered directly by SFDPH.

## Registration Thresholds

Under SF Health Code Article 21 (implementing H&S Code § 25501 et seq.), a business must register an HMBP if it handles at any one time at or above:
- **55 gallons** of a hazardous material liquid
- **500 pounds** of a hazardous material solid
- **200 cubic feet** of a compressed hazardous gas
- Any quantity of a federally listed extremely hazardous substance (EHS) at or above its threshold planning quantity (TPQ)

Hazardous waste generation: any amount of hazardous waste requires registration under Article 22.

## Electronic Reporting Portal

**California Environmental Reporting System (CERS):** https://cers.calepa.ca.gov
- Mandatory for all HMBP submittals
- Business plan annual certification, site maps, inventory, emergency response planning all submitted via CERS

## Fee Schedule

SF Health Code **Section 1176** — Hazardous Materials Fee Schedule:
- **Base annual fee:** $172 (base year; CPI-adjusted annually)
- **New-registration application fee:** $172 (base year; CPI-adjusted annually)
- Adjustment mechanism: Controller adjusts fees annually no later than **April 15** to reflect changes in the Consumer Price Index for the preceding calendar year. Fees may be rounded to nearest dollar, half-dollar, or quarter-dollar.
- Exception: Minimal Quantity Generators (as defined in Health Code) are exempt from the base annual fee.
- FY25-26 adjusted fee amounts: see current fee schedule linked from https://www.sf.gov/resource/2023/hazardous-materials-and-waste-forms-and-information
