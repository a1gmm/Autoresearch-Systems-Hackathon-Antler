## CUPA Authority

The City of Davis is **not its own CUPA**. Facilities within Davis city limits are regulated by:

**Yolo County Environmental Health Division — Hazardous Materials Programs**
(Certified Unified Program Agency for all of Yolo County)

## Programs Administered

Yolo County EH administers all six elements of the Unified Program for Davis facilities:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — annual reporting via CERS for facilities storing ≥500 lbs solid / ≥55 gal liquid / ≥200 cf gas of hazardous materials
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator Program** — onsite generation, accumulation, manifesting
3. **Underground Storage Tank (UST)** — permits, inspections, corrective action oversight
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — SPCC-equivalent for petroleum ASTs
5. **CalARP (Risk Management Plans)** — for facilities with listed acutely hazardous materials above threshold quantities
6. **Onsite Hazardous Waste Treatment (Tiered Permit)** — onsite treatment authorization

## Inspection Frequency

Facilities are inspected at minimum once every three years by a CUPA inspector. Inspection frequency may increase based on violation history or program tier.

## HazMat Emergency Response

The **Yolo County Multi-Agency HazMat Response Team** combines:
- Yolo County Environmental Health Division
- Davis Fire Department
- Woodland, West Sacramento Fire Departments
- UC Davis Fire Department
