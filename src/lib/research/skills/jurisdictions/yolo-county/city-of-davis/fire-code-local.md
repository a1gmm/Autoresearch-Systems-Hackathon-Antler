## Fire AHJ

**City of Davis Fire Department** — municipal fire department; serves the incorporated city limits.
- Fire Prevention Division: (530) 757-5682 | fireprevention@cityofdavis.org
- Non-emergency dispatch: (530) 747-5400
- Plan submittals (fire alarm, sprinkler, suppression systems): submit directly to fireprevention@cityofdavis.org

Note: the adjacent **UC Davis campus** is served by a separate **UC Davis Fire Department**; they share mutual aid but are distinct agencies with separate jurisdictions.

## Adopted Edition

**2025 California Fire Code** (Title 24, Part 9, CCR) adopted by reference under Davis Municipal Code **Chapter 13, Article 13.01, § 13.01.010**.

Prior adopting ordinances in the chapter history: Ords. 2302, 2371, 2427, 2490, 2568, 2636 (the current adopting ordinance number for the 2025 edition was not retrievable from public search; [VERIFY — current ordinance number not found online]).

One copy of the adopted code is on file with the Fire Chief for public inspection.

## Local Amendments

Local amendments, deletions, and additions are codified in **DMC § 13.01.040**. Specifically adopted with amendments are the following portions of the 2025 CFC:

- Chapter 1
- Section 308.1.7
- Section 311
- Section 403.11
- Section 503
- Section 510
- Section 1103.2
- Appendix B, C, D, and E
