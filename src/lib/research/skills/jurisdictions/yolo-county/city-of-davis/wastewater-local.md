## Industrial Pretreatment Program

Davis operates an **active Industrial Pretreatment Program** (the city is not a small system exempt from pretreatment requirements).

Key program features per DMC Art. 33.03 and city program materials:

- **Significant Industrial Users (SIU)** and **Categorical Industrial Users (CIU)** must obtain an **Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit (IWDP)** before connecting to the POTW (DMC § 33.03.170).
- New SIUs/CIUs: obtain IWDP before connection; existing facilities: within 90 days of ordinance effective date.
- Permit conditions may restrict: volume, flow rate, BOD, suspended solids, pH, and any other physical or chemical characteristic.

## Local Limits

- Local limits were originally developed in **1993**.
- Most recently modified and approved in **2024** — the State Water Board (on behalf of the Central Valley RWQCB) approved Davis's proposed changes on **December 28, 2023**, effective 2024.
- Specific numeric limits (mg/L) for metals (copper, zinc, lead, mercury), cyanide, BOD, TSS, and pH are **not publicly posted** in retrievable web text; the full local limits document must be obtained directly from the City.

Technical Review Criteria (TRC): violations flagged when ≥33% of measurements over a 6-month period exceed the permit limit × TRC factor (1.4 for BOD, TSS, fats/oil/grease; 1.2 for all other pollutants except pH).

## IWP Process (Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit)

1. Submit application to City of Davis Public Works — Utilities & Operations (pretreatment program).
2. City reviews proposed discharge characteristics.
3. Permit issued with effluent limits, monitoring schedule, reporting requirements, and pollution prevention provisions.
4. Sampling and compliance inspections ongoing.
