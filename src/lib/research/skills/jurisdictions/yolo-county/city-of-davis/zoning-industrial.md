## Industrial Zoning Districts

### 1. Industrial Administration and Research (I-R) District — Article 40.19

**Purpose:** Provide an environment exclusively for and conducive to modern, large-scale administrative facilities, research institutions, and specialized manufacturing organizations of a non-nuisance type.

**Principal Permitted Uses (§ 40.19.020):**
- Administrative, executive, and financial offices
- Laboratories (experimental, film, or testing)
- Manufacturing, assembly, or packaging of products from previously prepared materials (cloth, plastic, paper, leather, semi-precious metals or stones)
- Agriculture (excluding commercial fowl or animal raising)
- Public and semipublic uses
- Retail commercial uses necessary to serve the district (e.g., restaurants, auto service stations)

**Development Standards:**
- All uses must be conducted wholly within a completely enclosed building (exception: auto service stations, off-street parking/loading, utility substations).
- Manufacturing/industrial processes must use gas or electricity as fuel/power; oil-burning equipment permitted only as standby.

**Conditional Uses (§ 40.19.040):** All permitted uses except administrative/executive/financial offices and permitted retail commercial uses are subject to **performance standards review** before approval. ## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process — Article 40.30

**Governing article:** DMC Article 40.30 (§§ 40.30.010–40.30.110)

**Key procedural steps:**
- § 40.30.020 — CUP required for designated conditional uses
- § 40.30.030 — Findings the decision-maker must make
- § 40.30.040 — Application requirements
- § 40.30.050 — Application fee (see current Planning Fee Schedule effective July 1, 2025)
- § 40.30.060 — Required maps and drawings
- § 40.30.070 — Public hearing and notice
- § 40.30.080 — Issuance criteria (Planning Commission or City Council must find proposal meets approval findings)
- § 40.30.090 — Permit expiration
- § 40.30.100 — Effect of denial
- § 40.30.110 — Revocation

**Decision-maker:** Planning Commission (for most CUPs); City Council may hear appeals or certain categories.

**Administrative Use Permits (AUP):** A separate streamlined process exists under Article 40.30A for minor/less-impactful uses.

## Notes

- Davis is a university city with significant tech/biotech research presence; the I-R district is the primary zone for EHS-relevant industrial/lab facilities.
- The Planned Development (P-D) district (Article 40.22) is frequently used for large mixed or specialized developments, including research parks, and has its own conditional use review process (§ 40.22.140).
- No heavy industrial (M-1/M-2-equivalent) district was identified in Davis's zoning structure — manufacturing scope is limited to the I-R and I districts.
