## Programs Administered (All Six CalEPA Unified Programs)

| # | Program | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) | Annual CERS filing; Yolo County EH reviews and inspects |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator Program (HWG) | Generator ID, manifest compliance, tiered permitting (onsite treatment) |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tanks (UST) | Installation, operation, modification, closure, leak response |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act / APSA | Petroleum storage above APSA thresholds; CERS filing |
| 5 | California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) | Local implementing agency for RMP submissions; worst-case scenario coordination |
| 6 | CFC Hazardous Materials Operational Permits | Coordinated with fire AHJ (see fire-code-local.md for unincorporated area) |

## HMBP Program Details

- **Submission system:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business
- **Threshold quantities requiring HMBP filing (state baseline; no confirmed Yolo-specific lower thresholds):**
  - Solids: ≥ 500 lbs
  - Liquids: ≥ 55 gallons
  - Gases: ≥ 200 cubic feet
  - (Health & Safety Code §25507; confirm exceptions apply)
- **Filing frequency:** Annual update required in CERS; update within 30 days if material quantities increase ≥100%, new materials added, or significant operational changes.
- **Required CERS content:** Hazardous materials inventory, emergency response/contingency plan, facility site map, employee training records.
- **Inspection program:** Yolo County EH conducts routine facility inspections; frequency varies by facility risk tier. [VERIFY current inspection frequency schedule with Yolo County EH.]

## APSA / Aboveground Storage Tanks

- Facilities storing petroleum above APSA thresholds (generally ≥1,320 gallons aggregate aboveground, or single tank >660 gallons) must maintain an SPCC plan.
- CERS filing required for aboveground petroleum storage data; Yolo County EH is the reviewing agency.

## CalARP

- Yolo County EH is the local implementing agency (LIA) for CalARP in the unincorporated county and cities of Davis, Woodland, and Winters.
- Facilities with regulated substances above federal/state threshold quantities must file an RMP with USEPA; Yolo County EH reviews the local CalARP portion.

## Participating Agency — West Sacramento

- **City of West Sacramento Fire Department** operates as a Participating Agency (PA) under the Yolo County CUPA and administers all six unified programs within West Sacramento city limits.
- Facilities inside West Sacramento report to: City of West Sacramento Fire Department — Hazardous Materials Program
