> There is no county-level POTW or sewer authority for Yolo County. Wastewater service is fragmented by city and by unincorporated service areas. This file covers the controlling POTW and pretreatment authority for each sub-jurisdiction.
> For a specific facility, first determine which sewer service area it falls within, then contact the appropriate entity below.

## Unincorporated Yolo County — Onsite / Rural Areas

- Most unincorporated areas of Yolo County are **not served by a centralized sewer system**. Rural parcels use onsite wastewater treatment systems (OWTS — septic systems).
- **Regulatory authority for OWTS:** Yolo County Environmental Health Division — Land Use Programs (Onsite Wastewater Treatment System Program)
  - Web: https://www.yolocounty.gov/government/general-government-departments/community-services/environmental-health-division/land-use-programs/onsite-wastewater-treatment-system-program
  - Phone: (530) 666-8646 (Environmental Health main)
- **Industrial pretreatment program:** Not applicable for unincorporated rural areas (no POTW connection). Industrial or commercial dischargers in unincorporated areas must manage wastewater through approved onsite systems or haul to a licensed facility.

## City of Woodland — POTW and Pretreatment Program

- **POTW:** City of Woodland Water Pollution Control Facility (WPCF)
  - Address: 300 1st Street, Woodland, CA 95695
  - Phone: (530) 661-5800 | Fax: (530) 661-5813
  - Web: https://www.cityofwoodland.gov/741/Water-Pollution-Control-Facility
- **Discharge point:** Treated wastewater discharged to Tule Canal in the Yolo Bypass (~5 miles east of facility).
- **Facility history:** Originally built 1988; expanded 1997 and 1999; major upgrade 2007.
- **Industrial Pretreatment Program:**
  - Established 1995; required by EPA for POTWs with capacity >5 mgd receiving industrial wastewater.
  - Program page: https://www.cityofwoodland.gov/729/Industrial-Pretreatment-Program
  - **Significant Industrial Users (SIUs):** industries discharging ≥25,000 gallons of process wastewater per day; regulated via individual discharge permits.
  - **Pollution Prevention Program:** covers commercial/industrial dischargers below SIU threshold.
  - Contact: Environmental Compliance Specialist / Environmental Compliance Inspector — (530) 661-5800
- **Sewer use ordinance:** Woodland Municipal Code **Chapter 13.12 — Sewer and Storm Drainage Services** (Title 13 Public Services). Code current through Ordinance No. 1721, adopted May 21, 2024.
  - eCode360 reference: https://ecode360.com/WO5036 (Chapter 13.12, §13.12.010–§13.12.120)
  - Full local discharge limits and IWP permit application requirements **[VERIFY — numerical local limits not available on public web pages; request from Woodland Environmental Compliance at (530) 661-5800].**

## City of West Sacramento — POTW and Pretreatment Program

- **POTW:** West Sacramento sewage is conveyed to and treated by the **Sacramento Area Sewer District (SacSewer) / Sacramento Regional County Sanitation District** at the **EchoWater Resource Recovery Facility** (formerly Sacramento Regional Wastewater Treatment Plant), located in Elk Grove.
  - EchoWater treats an average of 135 million gallons per day from 1.6 million residents across Sacramento County and West Sacramento.
  - Regional San pretreatment program: https://www.regionalsan.com/wastewater-discharge-permits
- **Industrial Pretreatment Program:** Administered by Sacramento Area Sewer District (SacSewer) — Wastewater Source Control Section.
  - SIU threshold: ≥25,000 gallons/day of process wastewater, or >37.5 lbs/day BOD or TSS, or >8.3 lbs/day TKN, or categorical industrial users.
  - Discharge permits issued to both categorically regulated industries and industries discharging pollutants of local concern.
  - SacSewer pretreatment contact: https://www.sacsewer.com/pretreatment-program/
- **West Sacramento sewer use ordinance:** West Sacramento Municipal Code **Title 13, Chapter 13.08** — Sewer Service System (covers sanitary sewer use, industrial pretreatment standards, significant industrial user definitions, enforcement response).
  - qCode reference: https://library.qcode.us/lib/west_sacramento_ca/pub/municipal_code/item/title_13-chapter_13_08-article_i-13_08_040
  - Numerical local limits and specific IWP permit procedures **[VERIFY — full ordinance text not fetched; contact West Sacramento Public Works or SacSewer Source Control for current limits].**

## City of Davis — POTW and Pretreatment Program

- **POTW:** City of Davis Water Pollution Control Plant (WPCP), owned and operated by the City.
  - Located near the Yolo County Landfill.
  - Permitted capacity: 7.5 mgd.
  - Service area includes Davis city limits plus some unincorporated Yolo County areas (El Macero, North Davis Meadows, Davis Creek Mobile Home Park, Teichert Construction Complex).

## Key Notes for Industrial Dischargers

1. **Identify the sewer service area first** — unincorporated parcels are almost exclusively on septic; each city has its own POTW and pretreatment authority.
2. **Woodland and West Sacramento** are the cities most likely to have large-scale industrial dischargers; both have active pretreatment programs.
3. **West Sacramento industrial dischargers** are regulated by Regional San / SacSewer Source Control, not by City of West Sacramento directly.
4. **No county-level sewer use ordinance exists** — Yolo County does not operate a POTW.
