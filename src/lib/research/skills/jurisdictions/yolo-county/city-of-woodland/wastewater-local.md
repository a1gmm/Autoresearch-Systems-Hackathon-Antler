> Wastewater reference for City of Woodland industrial and commercial dischargers. The City operates its own POTW and a federally-required industrial pretreatment program. Dischargers connect to the city sewer system; there is no separate sanitation district for the incorporated city.

## POTW Identity

- **Address:** 42929 County Road 24, Woodland, CA 95776
- **Discharges to:** Tule Canal in the Yolo Bypass (approximately 5 miles east of facility)
- **NPDES permit:** CA0077950, Order R5-2026-0004 (California Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region)

## Industrial Pretreatment Program (IPP)

### Program Authority
- The City has operated an Industrial Pretreatment Program since **1995**, established as required by 40 C.F.R. Part 403 under its NPDES permit. The NPDES permit (Order R5-2026-0004, Provision VI.C.5) requires the City to fulfill all Control Authority pretreatment requirements.
- The program is implemented by the Wastewater Operations division (two dedicated staff: Environmental Compliance Specialist and Environmental Compliance Inspector, under a Laboratory Supervisor).

### Significant Industrial User (SIU) Threshold
- **SIU threshold:** Industries discharging at least **25,000 gallons per day** of process wastewater, or industries with reasonable potential to cause pass-through or interference at the WPCF (federal baseline per 40 C.F.R. §403.3).
- Categorical Industrial Users (CIUs) subject to EPA categorical pretreatment standards (40 C.F.R. Parts 405–471) are also regulated regardless of flow volume.

### Pollution Prevention Program (PPP)
- For commercial/industrial dischargers **below** the SIU threshold whose waste could impact the WPCF. Focused on:
  - Food service establishments (fats, oils, and grease / FOG)
  - Automotive repair (solvents, oils, hazardous waste fluids)
  - "Businesses of concern" with WPCF impact potential
- Program is education- and enforcement-based; does not issue individual discharge permits for sub-threshold users.
- Web: https://www.cityofwoodland.gov/736/Pollution-Prevention-Program

## Local Limits

- Specific numeric local limits (mg/L for metals, BOD, TSS, O&G, pH) are set in the sewer use ordinance and in the City's approved pretreatment program. [VERIFY — local limits table not available on public web pages; request directly from Wastewater Operations at (530) 661-5800]
- Dental offices must comply with EPA Dental Effluent Guidelines (confirmed by city program reference).
- Groundwater discharge (land application) from Discharge Point 002 is separately regulated under NPDES Order R5-2026-0004.

## No Industrial Sanitation District

- There is no separate sanitation district serving the City of Woodland. The city is the sole POTW operator for its incorporated limits.
- The Woodland-Davis Clean Water Agency (WDCWA) is a water **supply** agency only (surface water from Sacramento River treatment); it is not a wastewater authority.
