## Industrial Zone Districts (Chapter 17.40)

### Industrial Flex (IF)
- Intended for a mix of research and development, limited artisan/small-scale manufacturing, multi-tenant spaces with offices, agricultural processing, food producers, industrial technology, and other sensitive processing subject to performance standards.
- **Restrictions:** Larger and more intensive uses that may create odors, particulates, dust, vibration, etc. are restricted. General industrial uses with outdoor facilities are **not allowed** in IF.

### Industrial General (IG)
- The primary heavy industrial zone. Encourages sound industrial development in areas reserved exclusively for industrial use.
- Supports light, medium, and heavy industrial uses, including: manufacturing, processing, refining, and similar uses including those with outdoor facilities; warehousing, distribution, and logistics; supporting commercial services and office space.
- Heavy and outdoor industrial processes are permitted subject to performance standards.

### Business Park (BP)
- Accommodates master-planned office parks with research and development, green technology, and light manufacturing related to R&D.
- Also allows supporting uses: professional offices, hotels, and general retail serving industrial/office tenants.
- Not a heavy industrial zone; light manufacturing only.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

- **Approval authority:** Planning Commission (grants CUPs at a public hearing)
- **Application forms:** https://www.cityofwoodland.gov/565/Application-Forms-and-Common-Permit-Type
- **Planning applications portal:** https://www.cityofwoodland.gov/1702/Planning-Applications-and-Resources
- Planning Commission meets regularly; contact Planning Division for current meeting schedule and pre-application conference availability.
- A Zoning Administrator Permit (staff-level, no public hearing) covers a subset of uses below the CUP threshold.
