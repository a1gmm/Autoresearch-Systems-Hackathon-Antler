> CUPA reference for City of Woodland facilities. The City of Woodland is **not its own CUPA** — all six CalEPA unified program elements are administered county-wide by the Yolo County Environmental Health Division. City of Woodland facilities submit via CERS to Yolo County EH.

## CUPA Identity

- **CUPA:** Yolo County Environmental Health Division — Hazardous Materials Programs
- **Address:** 292 West Beamer Street, Woodland, CA 95695 (physically located within the City of Woodland, which is the Yolo County seat)
- **Phone:** (530) 666-8646
- **Fax:** (530) 669-1448
- **Email:** environmental.health@yolocounty.gov
- **Web:** https://www.yolocounty.gov/government/general-government-departments/community-services/environmental-health-division/hazardous-materials-programs
- **CERS regulator ID:** 1083 (Yolo County Environmental Health)

## Jurisdiction

- Yolo County Environmental Health is the CUPA for the **entire county**, including all incorporated cities: Woodland, Davis, West Sacramento, Winters, and Esparto.
- The City of Woodland does **not** operate a separate CUPA. City facilities are regulated by the county CUPA.
- Note: The City of West Sacramento Fire Department administers some local hazmat functions for West Sacramento; confirm for other cities if needed. For Woodland itself, Yolo County EH is the controlling CUPA.

## Programs Administered

Yolo County EH implements all six CalEPA unified program elements:

| # | Program | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) | Annual submission via CERS; inspection at least every 3 years |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator Program | Generator registration, manifest compliance, inspection |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tanks (UST) | Permitting, inspection, leak response |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) | Facilities at or above SPCC thresholds |
| 5 | California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) | Local implementing agency for Risk Management Plans |
| 6 | Onsite Hazardous Waste Treatment (Tiered Permitting) | At least once every 3 years inspection confirmed for onsite treatment permits |

## HMBP / CERS Filing

- **System:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business
- **Thresholds (state standard):** ≥ 55 gallons liquid, ≥ 500 lbs solid, ≥ 200 cu ft compressed gas (some exceptions apply)
- **Filing cadence:** Annual inventory update; Business Emergency Response Plan reviewed on a triennial basis
- **Who inspects:** Yolo County EH CUPA inspector; routine inspection frequency at least once every 3 years (confirmed from county program language)
- **No Woodland-specific lower thresholds confirmed** [VERIFY with county EH if any local threshold reductions apply]

## Local Hazmat Ordinance

- No separate City of Woodland local hazmat ordinance identified online. [VERIFY — Woodland Municipal Code does not appear to contain a standalone hazmat storage chapter beyond state-baseline CFC operational permit requirements administered by Woodland Fire Dept]
- CUPA enforcement authority derives from California Health & Safety Code Chapter 6.95 et seq. and CalEPA delegation.

## Relationship to Woodland Fire Department

- The Woodland Fire Department (Community Risk Reduction Division) administers **CFC Chapter 50+ hazmat operational fire permits** for facilities within city limits, separate from the county CUPA program.
- Facilities at or above CFC hazmat permit thresholds must obtain a fire permit from Woodland Fire AND file an HMBP via CERS with Yolo County EH.
- These are parallel requirements; the Fire Dept permit does not substitute for the CUPA HMBP filing.
