## Key Local Requirements (Derived from Technical Policies)

- **Fire lanes:** Minimum 20-ft unobstructed width, 13 ft 6 in vertical clearance; signs/markings every 50 ft. (TP-009, updated Feb 2026)
- **Private fire hydrants:** Clow 960 or approved equal; 8-in supply minimum; steamer outlet within 8 ft of fire dept access; commercial spacing 300 ft, residential 500 ft; hydrant within 40 ft of FDC for standpipe buildings. (TP-012, updated Feb 2026)
- **High-piled combustible storage:** Requirements per CRR-003 (guideline); refer to CFC Chapter 32 thresholds.
- **Dust hazard analysis:** CRR-009 provides dust hazard analysis procedures for industrial facilities.
- **Fire permits:** Division issues fire permits for temporary events, tent installations, fireworks booths, and operational uses. Fee schedule updated July 1, 2025. [VERIFY current fee schedule with CRR Division]

## Relationship to County / CAL FIRE

- The City of Woodland has its own municipal fire department. There is **no county fire district** serving the incorporated city.
- Yolo County and CAL FIRE serve unincorporated Yolo County areas only; Woodland city limits are wholly within Woodland Fire Department jurisdiction.
