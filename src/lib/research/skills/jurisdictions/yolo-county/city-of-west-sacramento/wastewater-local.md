> Research reference for wastewater discharge regulation in West Sacramento.
> West Sacramento's collection system discharges to Sacramento Regional County Sanitation District (Regional San / SacSewer), which operates the EchoWater Resource Recovery Facility. Industrial pretreatment is a split responsibility: the City's local sewer-use ordinance sets baseline prohibitions; Regional San's Wastewater Source Control Section issues industrial user permits and enforces federal pretreatment requirements.

## Local Sewer-Use Ordinance

- **Governing chapter:** City of West Sacramento Municipal Code, **Title 13, Chapter 13.08** — Sanitary Sewer System (general construction and use regulations)
- **Wastewater discharge regulations:** Title 13, **Chapter 13.09** — Wastewater Discharge and Regulations
  - Article II: Discharge Requirements
  - Article IV: Wastewater Discharge Permits
- **Code source:** https://ecode360.com/43203679 (Article II — Discharge Requirements); full Title 13 at https://ecode360.com/WE5032

### Core Prohibitions (Chapter 13.09, Article II)

- Unlawful to discharge any pollutant or wastewater at any flow rate or concentration that will cause interference with the wastewater collection system
- Unlawful to dump or discharge any solid, semi-solid, or liquid substance not defined as sewage, wastewater, or non-domestic wastewater into the collection system without permission or permit from the City or Regional San's Wastewater Source Control Section
- Users must provide pretreatment facilities deemed necessary by the City Manager or Regional San to protect the wastewater collection system

### Wastewater Discharge Permits (Chapter 13.09, Article IV)

- Regional San (SacSewer) may issue wastewater discharge permits to industrial/commercial users discharging to the West Sacramento collection system
- Such permits are subject to all provisions of Regional San's Consolidated Ordinance, sewer use charges and fees established by both the City and Regional San, and all applicable statutes and regulations

## Industrial Pretreatment Program — Regional San

West Sacramento connected to the Regional San system in 2007. Industrial pretreatment oversight for West Sacramento facilities is administered by Regional San's Wastewater Source Control Section (WSCS).

- **Administering agency:** Sacramento Area Sewer District (SacSewer) / Sacramento Regional County Sanitation District (Regional San) — Wastewater Source Control Section
- **Service territory includes:** West Sacramento (since 2007), plus Citrus Heights, Elk Grove, Folsom, Rancho Cordova, Sacramento, unincorporated Sacramento County
- **EchoWater Resource Recovery Facility:** the receiving POTW

### Who Needs an Industrial Discharge Permit

- Federally regulated categorical industrial users (CIUs) under 40 CFR Part 403 categorical standards
- Non-categorical facilities with high-flow or high-strength waste streams of local concern
- Any facility whose discharge may contain pollutants above local limits

### Permit Process

1. Contact Regional San Wastewater Source Control Section to determine if a permit is required
2. Submit permit application to Regional San WSCS
3. Regional San reviews; may require sampling or pretreatment facility installation
4. Permit issued with applicable local limits and monitoring requirements

## Note on Pretreatment Program Size

West Sacramento is not a large industrial city; significant industrial wastewater users may be limited. For most routine commercial discharges, the City's local prohibitions (Chapter 13.09) and Regional San's Consolidated Ordinance govern. Formal industrial pretreatment permits through Regional San WSCS are required for categorical industries and high-strength dischargers.
