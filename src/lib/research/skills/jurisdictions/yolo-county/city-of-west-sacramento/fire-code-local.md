> Research reference for the City of West Sacramento fire code adoption and local amendments.
> The City of West Sacramento has its own municipal fire department as AHJ; it is NOT served by a county fire district or CAL FIRE.

## Fire Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

- **AHJ:** City of West Sacramento Fire Department — Community Risk Reduction Division
- **Fire Marshal office:** under the Community Risk Reduction Division
- **Web:** https://www.cityofwestsacramento.org/government/departments/fire
- **Community Risk Reduction (CFC enforcement + CUPA):** https://www.cityofwestsacramento.org/government/departments/fire/community-risk-reduction

## Adopted Fire Code

- **Adopted code edition:** 2025 California Fire Code (CFC), Title 24, Part 9 of the California Code of Regulations, and Appendices Chapter 4, B, BB, C, CC, and D
- **Municipal Code chapter:** Title 15 (Buildings and Construction), **Chapter 15.14** — California Fire Code
- **Most recent adopting ordinance:** Ordinance No. **25-10** (effective November 5, 2025)
- **Prior adopting ordinance:** Ordinance No. **22-20** (2022 CFC cycle); Ordinance No. **16-5** (earlier cycle, now superseded)
- **Enforcement:** Community Risk Reduction Division of the West Sacramento Fire Department, under the supervision of the Fire Marshal

## Local Amendments (Chapter 15.14.040)

The following local amendments to the California Fire Code are codified in West Sacramento Municipal Code §15.14.040. These are confirmed from public search index summaries — fetch of the full text returned 403; verify directly with the municipal code.

| CFC Section | Amendment |
|---|---|
| §112.4 (Violation penalties) | Amended to specify violations constitute a misdemeanor |
| §503.2.3 (Fire apparatus access road surfaces) | Amended to require compliance with City of West Sacramento Community Development Department Engineering Division Standard Specifications |
| §903.2.22 (Automatic fire-extinguishing systems — local addition) | New section added requiring approved automatic fire-extinguishing systems in every new building exceeding 4,000 sq ft |

Additional local amendments address: liquefied petroleum gas storage capacity, definitions, explosives storage, and hazardous materials storage restrictions specific to West Sacramento zoning areas. Full text of §15.14.040 should be retrieved directly from the current municipal code for a complete amendment list.
