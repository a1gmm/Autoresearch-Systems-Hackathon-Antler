> Research reference for West Sacramento industrial zoning districts and the CUP process.
> The zoning ordinance is codified in Title 17 of the West Sacramento Municipal Code. Industrial and employment uses are primarily regulated under **Chapter 17.10 — Employment Zones**.

## Zoning Ordinance Structure

- **Title 17** — Zoning (West Sacramento Municipal Code)
- **Chapter 17.03** — Zones and Zoning Map (zone designations)
- **Chapter 17.10** — Employment Zones (industrial/manufacturing regulations)
- **Chapter 17.38** — Use Permits (CUP and Minor Use Permit process)
- **Online viewer:** http://online.encodeplus.com/regs/westsacramento-ca/doc-viewer.aspx
- **City zoning page:** https://www.cityofwestsacramento.org/government/departments/community-development/planning-division/zoning-ordinance

## Industrial / Employment Zone Names and Codes

West Sacramento uses "Employment Zone" terminology for its industrial districts. The following zone codes are established in the zoning map and Title 17:

| Zone Code | Zone Name | General Plan Designation |
|---|---|---|
| **M-L** | Limited Industrial | Mixed Commercial/Industrial |
| **M-1** | Light Industrial | Light Industrial |
| **M-2** | Heavy Industrial | Heavy Industrial |
| **M-3** | Water-Related Industrial | Water-Related Industrial |

In addition, a **BP** (Business Park) zone serves office/R&D-type employment uses.

### Employment Zone Purpose (Chapter 17.10)

Chapter 17.10 is intended to provide areas for a wide range of manufacturing, industrial processing, and service industrial uses and to protect areas where such uses already exist. Land use regulations are set in **Table 17.10.020** (permitted/conditional uses) and development standards in **Table 17.10.030**.

### Conditional Use Permit in Employment Zones

- Land uses not listed in the Employment Zones use table but substantially similar to listed uses may be allowed via Conditional Use Permit
- Industrial uses that present compatibility concerns with adjacent uses typically require CUP approval

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process — Chapter 17.38

### Review Authority

| Permit Type | Review Authority |
|---|---|
| Conditional Use Permit (CUP) | **Planning Commission** (public notice + hearing required) |
| Minor Use Permit | Zoning Administrator (may elevate to Planning Commission for controversial projects) |

### Approval Criteria

To approve a CUP, the Planning Commission must find that the proposed use:
1. Is allowed within the applicable zone and complies with all other applicable provisions of the zoning code
2. Will not be adverse to public health, safety, or general welfare
3. Complies with design or development standards applicable to the zone or use
4. Is compatible with existing and reasonably foreseeable future land uses and circulation in the vicinity (design, location, size, operating characteristics)

### Public Notice and Hearing

All CUP applications require public notice and hearing before the Planning Commission pursuant to Chapter 17.35 (Common Procedures).
