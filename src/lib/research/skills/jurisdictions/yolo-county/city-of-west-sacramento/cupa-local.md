> Research reference for the West Sacramento CUPA / Unified Program arrangement.
> CRITICAL ROUTING NOTE: Unlike most cities in Yolo County (Davis, Woodland, Winters — all served directly by Yolo County Environmental Health), **West Sacramento operates its own Unified Program as a Participating Agency (PA)** under the Yolo County CUPA umbrella. The facility's primary point of contact for HMBP, inspections, and hazmat permits is the **City of West Sacramento Fire Department**, not Yolo County EH.

## West Sacramento Fire Department — CUPA / Hazardous Materials Program

- **Division:** Community Risk Reduction
- **Program:** Hazardous Materials Program (CUPA / Participating Agency)
- **Web:** https://www.cityofwestsacramento.org/government/departments/fire/community-risk-reduction/hazardous-materials-program/cupa-program
- **Hazmat Program page:** https://www.cityofwestsacramento.org/government/departments/fire/community-risk-reduction/hazardous-materials-program

### Services and Functions

The West Sacramento Fire Department's Hazardous Materials Program (as a PA) provides:

- Facility inspections for HMBP/CERS compliance
- Hazardous materials emergency response
- Construction plan reviews (hazmat)
- Consulting on safe storage and use of hazardous materials
- CERS filing review and approval for West Sacramento facilities

### HMBP Filing

- **Submission system:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business
- **Who must file:** Any business storing, using, or handling hazardous materials at or above state threshold quantities (generally ≥55 gallons liquid, ≥500 lbs solid, ≥200 cu ft gas — per Health & Safety Code §25507)
- **Reviewing agency for West Sacramento facilities:** City of West Sacramento Fire Department (PA), NOT Yolo County EH directly
- **Initial filing:** Within 30 days of threshold quantities arriving on site
- **Annual renewal:** Required

## Yolo County CUPA (Umbrella Agency)

- **Agency:** Yolo County Environmental Health Division — Hazardous Materials Programs
- **Role:** Certified CUPA with statewide certification; PA oversight and program uniformity; primary CUPA contact for facilities in unincorporated Yolo County and cities NOT served by a PA (e.g., Davis, Woodland, Winters)
- **Web:** https://www.yolocounty.gov/government/general-government-departments/community-services/environmental-health-division/hazardous-materials-programs

## Key Routing Rule

For any facility **within West Sacramento city limits**: contact the **City of West Sacramento Fire Department** Community Risk Reduction Division for HMBP, hazmat permits, and CUPA inspections.

For facilities in unincorporated Yolo County or other Yolo cities: contact Yolo County Environmental Health.
