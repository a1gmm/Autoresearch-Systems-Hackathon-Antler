## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

Lassen County has **no single countywide structural fire department.** AHJ for fire code enforcement is fragmented:

| Area | AHJ |
|---|---|
| Unincorporated communities | Local independent fire protection district or volunteer department (see list below) |
| City of Susanville | Susanville Fire Department (municipal) — see city-of-susanville file |
| State Responsibility Area (SRA) wildland | CAL FIRE Lassen-Modoc Unit |

**Independent fire protection districts / departments (unincorporated areas):**
Susan River FPD, Janesville FPD, Doyle FPD, Standish-Litchfield FPD, Northwest Lassen County FPD, Madeline FPD, Milford FD, Big Valley FPD, Westwood FD.

Each district is its own AHJ within its service boundary. Building permit fire-code plan checks for unincorporated Lassen County are handled by the Lassen County Department of Planning and Building Services, which coordinates with the applicable district.

## Lassen County Code — Fire-Related Chapters

### Title 9 — Public Peace, Safety and Morals

**Chapter 9.16 — Fire Hazards ("Lassen County Fire Safe Regulations")**
- Adopts **Public Resources Code § 4290** and all regulations promulgated thereunder in the unincorporated areas of Lassen County.
- Establishes minimum wildfire protection standards per the California Board of Forestry for new development in fire-prone areas.
- Amended by **Ordinance No. 2020-03**.

**Chapter 9.17 — Regulations for Outdoor Burning**
- Adopted **October 22, 2024** (Ordinance No. 2024-XX — specific number [VERIFY]).
- Applies to unincorporated Lassen County areas designated as **Local Responsibility Area (LRA)** under Public Resources Code § 4125.
- Requires burn permits; regulates suspension and restriction of burn permits via proclamations issued by the **Lassen County Fire Warden**.
- CAL FIRE burn permits are separately required for State Responsibility Area (SRA) residents under CAL FIRE Lassen-Modoc Unit jurisdiction.

Source: https://ecode360.com/43552843 · https://susanvillestuff.com/cal-fire-burn-permits-now-required-in-lassen-modoc-and-plumas-counties/

### Title 12 — Buildings and Construction

**Ordinance No. 2022-03** (comprehensive building code revision):
- Repealed and reenacted: Chapters 12.08, 12.14, 12.15, 12.16, 12.18, 12.20, 12.24
- Added: Chapters 12.05, 12.09, 12.27
- Amended: Section 12.12.010; added Section 12.04.055
- The ordinance title references the "Lassen County Building Code" (Article I of Title 12).

**Chapter 12.27 — Limited Density Owner-Builder Rural Dwellings**
- Added by Ordinance No. 2022-03.
- Section 12.27.300 (Fire Safety Regulations) amended by **Ordinance No. 2025-03**.
- Applies to rural owner-builder residential construction; includes fire safety standards specific to that construction category.

## CAL FIRE / SRA Burn Permits

Starting May 2026 (and annually each fire season), CAL FIRE Lassen-Modoc Unit requires burn permits for hazard reduction burning within SRA in Lassen, Modoc, and Plumas Counties. Apply at: https://www.readyforwildfire.org/permits/
