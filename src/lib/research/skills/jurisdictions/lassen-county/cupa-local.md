## CERS Reporting

All regulated businesses must report via the California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) at https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/. The county EHD publishes CERS user guides and templates on its hazardous materials webpage.

## CalARP Program Structure

Three-tier system:
- **Program 1:** Worst-case release scenario would not reach public; no accidental release in past 5 years.
- **Program 2:** Processes not qualifying for Program 1 or 3; requires alternative release scenario analysis.
- **Program 3:** Facilities subject to OSHA Process Safety Management (29 CFR 1910.119) or in specified SIC codes (petroleum refining, chemical manufacturing).

Risk Management Plans (RMPs) are public records; available for review by contacting EHD at (530) 251-8269 or ehe@co.lassen.ca.us.
