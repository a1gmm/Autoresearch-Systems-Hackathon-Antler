## Programs Administered

Lassen County Environmental Health is the certified CUPA for the following six Unified Program elements, for all facilities in Lassen County including within the city limits of Susanville:

1. **HMBP** — Hazardous Materials Business Plan
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator / Tiered Treatment** — generator registration and tiered permit requirements
3. **UST** — Underground Storage Tank (installation, operation, closure)
4. **APSA** — Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (SPCC-equivalent state program)
5. **CalARP** — California Accidental Release Prevention Program (Risk Management Plans)
6. **CERS** — California Environmental Reporting System (electronic submission portal)

## Reporting Portal

All regulated facilities must submit HMBP, hazardous waste, UST, and other Unified Program data via **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System): https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/

## Air Permitting (Related)

Although not a CUPA function, stationary-source air permits in Susanville are handled by the **Lassen County Air Pollution Control District (LCAPCD)** — a joint city-county body staffed by City of Susanville personnel since 2012:

66 North Lassen Street, Susanville, CA 96130
Phone: (530) 252-4247
Website: https://www.lassenair.org/

LCAPCD permit application fee: **$56.75 + Evaluation Fee** (per District Rule 3:6). ATC expires 2 years from issuance.
