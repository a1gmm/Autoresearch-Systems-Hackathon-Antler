## Chapter 17.56 — General Industrial District (M)

**Purpose:** "To provide areas for all types of industrial uses. This district is consistent with the light industry or business park and the heavy or general industry general plan designations."

**Permitted uses include:**
- Combining, assembling, packaging, or manufacturing of materials in processed form that do not emit dust, odors, smoke, or unacceptable noise levels (including small equipment such as medical, dental, or optical equipment)
- Manufacture or assembly of: aircraft, automobiles, boilers, engines, motors and generators, mobile homes, railroad equipment, trucks, trailers, recreational vehicles, or other products requiring heavy machinery
- Manufacture of: acids, alcohol, ammonia, asphalt, cellulose, cement, dyes, fertilizer, film, fireworks, fuel briquettes, gelatin, glass, glue, tar, paint, plaster, plastics, rubber, soap, vinyl floor covering, hazardous chemical products (including acetylene, carbide, caustic soda, chlorine, cleaning and polishing preparations, creosote, exterminating agents, industrial gases, and explosives)

**Conditionally permitted uses (CUP required):**
- Adult entertainment businesses (Chapter 17.120) — CUP required; only permitted in C-M and M districts

**Development standards (§ 17.56):**
- Minimum lot size: **20,000 sq ft** (newly created lots; exceptions per § 17.96.010)
- Maximum structural height: **45 feet** — except within 40 feet of a residential district, limited to **one story, not to exceed 20 feet**
- Landscaped buffer: minimum **25 feet** in width along the edge of the industrial parcel wherever it abuts or is across the street from any non-industrial, non-agricultural property
- Zone walls: solid masonry or concrete wall (typically 6 ft) required where industrial use abuts R-1, R-2, R-3, R-4, or MHP districts (§ 17.96.060)
- Landscaping: commercial/industrial uses must landscape required yards adjoining public streets to a depth of 10 feet (§ 17.96.040)
- Lighting: all exterior lighting for nonresidential uses shall be downward directional with full cut-off fixtures (§ 17.96.050)

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

- CUP required for designated conditional uses in all industrial districts
- Appeals of permit denials or conditions: file with city council within **30 days** of the decision date
- Contact: City of Susanville Planning Division — https://www.cityofsusanville.gov/ (Planning & Zoning section)

## Design / Snow Load Requirements (Industrial Construction)

Per City Ordinance No. 99-860 (Plan Check Submittal Requirements):
- Ground snow load: **86 PSF** west of Roop Street / Maurino Manor Subdivision; **58 PSF** east of Roop Street
- Wind: Exposure B & C, 110 MPH (3-second gust)
- Seismic zone: D
- Climate zone: 16
- Frost depth: 20 inches
- If ground snow load exceeds 50 PSF, a licensed professional must submit construction documents
