## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air (District Control)

- **Lassen County Air Pollution Control District (LCAPCD)** is the local air district for all of Lassen County, including the City of Susanville. The LCAPCD is NOT a separate city agency — its board is a six-member joint body (three Lassen County Supervisors + three Susanville City Council members). Since 2012 the District is staffed by City of Susanville personnel.
- Address: 66 North Lassen Street, Susanville, CA 96130; Phone: (530) 252-4247
- Website: https://www.lassenair.org/
- Permits: Authority to Construct (ATC) and Permit to Operate (PTO) required for stationary sources; application fee = **$56.75 + Evaluation Fee** (Rule 3:6). ATC expires 2 years from issuance if equipment not installed. Title V applicability: sources ≥25 tpy must confirm compliance with all rules.
- CARB page: https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/lassen-county-air-pollution-control-district
- Source: https://www.cityofsusanville.gov/DocumentCenter/View/254/Permit-Application-PDF

### Wastewater / Industrial Pretreatment

- **Susanville Sanitary District (SSD)** is the POTW — a separate independent district (not the City). Formed 1948 as Susanville Consolidated Sanitary District; renamed SSD in 2005.
- Service area: ~6.2 square miles, largely coterminous with city limits; ~3,595 connections (3,199 residential, 387 commercial, **9 industrial**).
- Treatment plant capacity: 2.0 mgd average monthly / 3.1 mgd peak wet weather / 4.0 mgd hydraulic max. UV disinfection. Collection: 61 miles of pipe (60 gravity + 1 pressure sewer) plus 2 polishing ponds and wetland facility. Discharges to Jensen Slough via Outfall 001.
- NPDES permit: **Order No. R6T-2008-0022 (NPDES No. CA0102695)**, issued by Lahontan Regional Water Quality Control Board; amended by Order R6T-2008-0022-A01 (10/10/2012).
- Sewer Use Ordinance: **Ordinance No. 76** (available at susanvillesanitarydistrict.com — PDF; content could not be extracted from scan). Industrial pretreatment program details [VERIFY against Ord. 76 text].
- SSD contact: Phone (530) 257-5665; Emergency: (530) 310-3848; Hours: Mon–Fri 7:30 AM–4:30 PM
- City municipal code Title 13, Chapter 13.08 governs City water service; the code provides that if a customer lacks current sewer service (SSD), the City water division may discontinue water service after 24-hour notice from SSD.

### Zoning (Industrial Districts)

- Zoning code: **Title 17** of the Susanville Municipal Code (available via eCode360 at https://ecode360.com/SU5021).
- Industrial zone districts:
  - **M-L: Light Industrial District** — Chapter 17.52
  - **M: General Industrial District** — Chapter 17.56
  - **C-M: Commercial-Light Industrial District** — Chapter 17.48 (commercial-industrial hybrid)
- Key M-district standards (§ 17.56): min. lot 20,000 sq ft; max. height 45 ft (one story / 20 ft within 40 ft of a residential district); 25-ft landscaped buffer where parcel abuts non-industrial/non-agricultural property.
- Adult entertainment businesses are conditionally permitted only in C-M and M districts (Chapter 17.120).
- General development standards: Chapter 17.96 — applies to all commercial/industrial uses (yards, landscaping, lighting, parking, zone walls).
- CUP process: Conditional Use Permits required for certain uses in industrial zones; appeals to city council within 30 days.
- Source: https://ecode360.com/44219198; https://www.zoneomics.com/code/susanville-CA/chapter_25

## Notes

- Susanville is the only incorporated city in Lassen County. It operates its own municipal fire department (AHJ for fire permits) but is NOT its own CUPA — hazardous-materials/Unified Program oversight comes from the county Environmental Health CUPA.
- Air permitting is handled by LCAPCD, a joint city-county body staffed by City personnel.
- Wastewater collection and treatment is handled by the Susanville Sanitary District, a separate independent special district — not the City's Public Works department.
- Municipal code is hosted at: https://ecode360.com/SU5021
