## POTW Operator

The City of Susanville does **not** own or operate the wastewater treatment plant. Collection and treatment are handled by an independent special district:

**Susanville Sanitary District (SSD)**
Phone: (530) 257-5665
Emergency: (530) 310-3848
Hours: Mon–Fri, 7:30 AM–4:30 PM
Website: https://susanvillesanitarydistrict.com/

**History:** Formed December 14, 1948 as the Susanville Consolidated Sanitary District (SCSD) through consolidation of the Milwood Sanitary District and Susanville Sanitary District. Renamed to "Susanville Sanitary District" in 2005.

Source: https://susanvillesanitarydistrict.com/about_us.aspx

## Service Area and Connections

- Geographic area: approximately 6.2 square miles, largely coterminous with Susanville city limits
- Total connections: ~3,595
  - Residential: 3,199
  - Commercial: 387
  - **Industrial: 9**

## Treatment Plant

- Design capacity: 2.0 mgd average monthly / 3.1 mgd peak wet weather / 4.0 mgd max hydraulic
- Typical flows: ~1.0 mgd dry weather, ~1.2 mgd wet weather
- Disinfection: UV (upgraded from chemical treatment)
- Collection system: 61 miles of pipelines (60 gravity-fed + 1 pressure sewer)
- Receiving water: **Jensen Slough** via **Outfall 001**
- Other infrastructure: 2 polishing ponds + wetland facility

## City Municipal Code Cross-Reference

Susanville Municipal Code **Title 13, Chapter 13.08** (Water Service System) contains a provision that allows the City water division to discontinue water service to a customer if the Susanville Sanitary District notifies the City that the customer lacks current sewer service — 24-hour notice required before shutoff.

Source: https://ecode360.com/44217237 (eCode360 — Title 13 reference)
