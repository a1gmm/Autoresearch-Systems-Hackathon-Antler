## Adopted Fire Code Edition

**2022 California Fire Code** (Part 9 of Title 24, CCR) — adopted by reference via **Ordinance No. 22-1038 § 2 (2022)**, codified in Susanville Municipal Code **Title 15, Chapter 15.04 (Building Standards Code)**.

Adopted appendices: B, BB, C, CC, D, E, F, G, H, I, J, K, N, and Appendix Chapter 4 of the 2022 California Fire Code.

Section § 15.04.090 covers fire protection systems requirements.

Note: The Building Division page references "2019 California Building Code, Title 24" in its general description, but the Plan Check Submittal form (City Ordinance No. 99-860) and municipal code text confirm the 2022 editions are operative. Confirm current edition with the Building Division before quoting.

## Fire Permit Process

- Permits for buildings, structures, electrical, gas, mechanical, plumbing, and fire systems (including fire protection system alterations) require fees paid per the city's established fee schedule, adopted by city council resolution.
- When the fire department chief disapproves a permit application or refuses to grant a license or permit, applicants may appeal to the city council within **30 days** from the date of the decision.
