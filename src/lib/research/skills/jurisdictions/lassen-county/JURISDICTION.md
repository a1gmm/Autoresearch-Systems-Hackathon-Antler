## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air

- **District:** Lassen County Air Pollution Control District (LCAPCD) — independent local APCD covering all of Lassen County. Not SCAQMD, BAAQMD, or San Joaquin Valley; it is one of the smallest districts in California with its own Rules and Regulations.
- **Permit types:** Authority to Construct (ATC) and Permit to Operate (PTO) — required before building, erecting, altering, or replacing any equipment that may emit air contaminants (LCAPCD Regulation II, Rule 2:0). Permits must be displayed within 25 feet of the equipment.
- **Regulatory adoption date:** Rules effective October 1, 1971; EPA-compiled SIP regulations updated through 2022.
- **Office:** 66 N. Lassen Street, Susanville, CA 96130 · (530) 252-4247
- No Title V major-source permit program locally — large sources route through CARB/EPA Region 9.
- Source: https://www.lassenair.org/ · EPA compiled rules: https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2022-08/epa-compiled-lassen-county-apcd-rules-regulation-compilation.pdf · CARB district page: https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/lassen-county-air-pollution-control-district

### CUPA

- **Agency:** Lassen County Environmental Health Department — sole CUPA for all of Lassen County (CERS regulator ID 1028). No participating agencies; the only incorporated city (Susanville) is covered by the county CUPA.
- **Programs administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generator / Tiered Treatment, Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), California Accidental Release Program (CalARP) — consolidated into a single inspection per site.
- **CERS reporting:** All regulated businesses must report via CERS (California Environmental Reporting System).
- **CalARP structure:** Program 1 (low risk, no public impact zone), Program 2 (intermediate), Program 3 (OSHA PSM-covered or petroleum/chemical refining). Risk Management Plans available for public review on request.
- **Contact:** 707 Nevada Street, Suite 5, Susanville, CA 96130 · (530) 251-8269 · ehe@co.lassen.ca.us · Mon–Fri 8:00 am–4:00 pm

### Fire

- **AHJ (structural / fire code):** No countywide fire department. Unincorporated communities served by independent fire protection districts (Susan River FPD, Janesville FPD, Doyle FPD, Standish-Litchfield FPD, Northwest Lassen County FPD, Madeline FPD, Milford FD, Big Valley FPD, Westwood FD). Each district is the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) within its boundary. The City of Susanville has its own municipal fire department (see city file).
- **Wildland / SRA:** CAL FIRE Lassen-Modoc Unit is AHJ for State Responsibility Area (SRA) wildland fire. The Unit covers Lassen, Modoc, Plumas and parts of Shasta/Siskiyou counties.
- **Local fire code chapters (Lassen County Code):**
  - Chapter 9.16 (Fire Hazards) — adopts Public Resources Code § 4290 regulations as the "Lassen County Fire Safe Regulations" (California Board of Forestry minimum wildfire protection standards for new development). Amended by Ordinance No. 2020-03.
  - Chapter 9.17 (Regulations for Outdoor Burning) — adopted October 22, 2024; applies to unincorporated LRA areas; requires burn permits and regulates suspension/restriction of permits per proclamations by the Lassen County Fire Warden.

### Wastewater

- **Onsite systems (unincorporated):** Lassen County Environmental Health Department administers the Local Agency Management Program (LAMP) for all on-site wastewater treatment systems (OWTS/septic) under the State Water Board's 2012 OWTS Policy. LAMP adopted April 2020, covers both unincorporated and incorporated (Susanville) areas. OWTS with flows >10,000 gpd, or receiving high-strength wastewater above BOD 900 mg/L, require individual WDRs from RWQCB rather than county permit.
- **Regional RWQCB oversight:** Most of Lassen County falls under the **Lahontan Regional Water Quality Control Board**; a small southeastern portion may fall under Central Valley RWQCB. The applicable Regional Board issues WDRs to municipalities and special districts operating wastewater treatment plants.
- **Susanville Sanitary District (POTW):** The primary POTW serving the City of Susanville. Formed 1948 (renamed from Susanville Consolidated Sanitary District in 2005). Service area ~6.2 sq mi; ~3,595 connections (3,199 residential, 387 commercial, 9 industrial). WWTP capacity: 2.0 mgd average monthly flow, 3.1 mgd peak wet weather, 4.0 mgd hydraulic max. Governed by **Ordinance No. 76** (sewer use ordinance — scanned PDF, exact discharge limits not text-extractable). Additional ordinances: 74, 75, 81. Contact: (530) 257-5665.
- **Industrial pretreatment:** Only 9 industrial connections on Susanville Sanitary District system; a formal pretreatment program may not be required at this flow scale. [VERIFY whether an approved pretreatment program exists under 40 CFR Part 403.]
- **Lassen County Liquid Waste Program:** Handles septic/OWTS permitting — reviews percolation tests, design proposals, inspects installation. Not a sewer-use ordinance authority.

### Zoning

- **Zoning authority:** Lassen County Department of Planning and Building Services — administers Title 18 (Zoning) of the Lassen County Code for unincorporated areas.
- **Industrial district names:**
  - **M-1 Light Industrial District** (Chapter 18.42) — light manufacturing and less abrasive industrial activities; uses must occur within enclosed buildings.
  - **M-2 Heavy Industrial District** (Chapter 18.44) — permits normal operations of almost all industries; activities need not be confined to buildings (unlike M-1).
- **Use permit process:** Uses requiring discretionary approval go through either a Certificate of Conditional Use (ministerial, established standards, no public hearing required) under Chapter 18.114, or a Use Permit (requires planning commission or board of supervisors action) under Chapter 18.112.
- **Recent zoning amendments:** Ordinance No. 2019-12 — cannabis dispensaries allowed in industrial zones with reduced setbacks; Ordinance No. 2022-05 — architectural requirements repealed from Title 18; Ordinance No. 2023-04 — nonconforming uses (Ch. 18.120); Ordinance No. 2023-08 — kennel requirements in industrial zones.

## Notes

- GOTCHA (single incorporated city): Susanville is the **only** incorporated city in Lassen County. All other communities (Janesville, Doyle, Westwood, Bieber, Standish, etc.) are unincorporated and fall under county CUPA and building authority, with fire service fragmented across local fire protection districts.
- Large federal-land footprint (Lassen National Forest, BLM, parts of Lassen Volcanic National Park boundary nearby) — federal-land carve-outs apply where USFS/BLM/NPS is the land manager.
- APCD is very small; no Title V program locally — large industrial emitters must coordinate with CARB/EPA Region 9.
