## Zoning Authority

Lassen County Department of Planning and Building Services administers **Title 18 (Zoning)** of the Lassen County Code for all unincorporated areas. The only incorporated city (Susanville) has its own separate zoning code — see city-of-susanville file.

## Industrial Zoning Districts

### M-1 Light Industrial District (Chapter 18.42)

**Purpose:** Establish areas for light manufacturing and less abrasive industrial activities where heavy industry is not appropriate. Uses must be designed and constructed to **occur within confined (enclosed) areas** — operations must take place inside a building (with limited exceptions).

**Uses Permitted by Right (§ 18.42.030) — partial list confirmed from search results:**
- Animal hospitals and kennels
- Nurseries and greenhouses
- Saw shops (all operations within building)
- Vehicle repair services, garages, and body shops (all operations within building)
- Miscellaneous repair shops and related services (within building)
- Equipment rental and leasing (equipment screened by wall/fence/landscaping ≥6 ft high)
- Car washes
- Off-site construction, maintenance and contractors' yards (building, electrical, plumbing, heating, roofing, painting, landscaping, excavation, janitorial, fumigating, septic pumping, etc.)
- Welding shops, machine shops, sheet metal shops (within building)
- Commercial laundries

**Commercial uses permitted by right** are listed separately in **§ 18.42.035**.

**Uses Requiring Use Permit (§ 18.42.040):** Uses with greater potential for negative health/safety/welfare impacts; listed in § 18.42.040; subject to Chapter 18.112 Use Permit process.

**Development Standards — M-1 (§ 18.42.050):** [VERIFY exact M-1 lot size — eCode360 returned 403; M-2 minimum is 14,000 sq ft]
- Minimum front yard: None, except buildings ≥50 ft from centerline of any public roadway
- Minimum side yard: 25 ft when adjacent to R (residential) zones; increased 1 ft for each foot of building height over 35 ft
- Minimum rear yard: 10 ft; 25 ft when adjacent to R zones; increased 1 ft per foot of height over 35 ft

### M-2 Heavy Industrial District (Chapter 18.44)

**Purpose:** Permit the normal operations of almost all industries, subject only to regulations needed to control congestion, manage traffic circulation, provide fire and natural hazard protection, protect surrounding areas, and prevent intrusion by nonindustrial uses.

**Uses Permitted:** All uses permitted by right in M-1 (except commercial uses under § 18.42.035), PLUS:
- Manufacturing or assembly of aircraft, automobiles, boilers, engines, motors and generators, mobile homes, trailers, railroad equipment, and other products requiring heavy machinery
- Bulk storage of oil, gasoline, propane, and similar products
- Activities need **not** be confined to enclosed buildings (unlike M-1)

**Uses Requiring Use Permit:** Specified uses with greater potential impact; listed in M-2 chapter; subject to Chapter 18.112 Use Permit process.

**Development Standards — M-2 (§ 18.44.050):**
- Minimum lot size: **14,000 square feet**
- Minimum lot width: **110 feet** (no lot greater than 4× wider than deep, or 4× deeper than wide)
- Minimum front yard: None, except buildings **≥50 ft from centerline of any public roadway**
- Minimum side yard: None, except **25 ft when adjacent to R zones**; increased 1 ft per foot of height over 35 ft
- Minimum rear yard: **10 ft**; 25 ft when adjacent to R zones; increased 1 ft per foot of height over 35 ft

## Use Permit and Conditional Use Process

Two discretionary approval pathways:

| Pathway | Chapter | Hearing Required | Who Decides |
|---|---|---|---|
| Certificate of Conditional Use | 18.114 | No (ministerial, established standards) | Planning Department (administrative) |
| Use Permit | 18.112 | Yes (public hearing) | Planning Commission or Board of Supervisors |

**Certificate of Conditional Use:** Applied to uses that can be effectively regulated through ministerial application of established standards. Application made in writing on a prescribed form to the Planning Department.

**Use Permit:** Required when Planning Commission/Board determines a Conditional Use Certificate is inconsistent with the certificate process; also required for uses explicitly listed as use-permit-only in the applicable district chapter.

**Applications:** Submit to Lassen County Department of Planning and Building Services, 707 Nevada Street, Suite 5, Susanville, CA 96130 · landuse@co.lassen.ca.us · (530) 251-8269

## Air Quality Note for Industrial Siting

Any new stationary source in an M-1 or M-2 zone that may emit air contaminants must obtain an **Authority to Construct (ATC)** and **Permit to Operate (PTO)** from the Lassen County Air Pollution Control District (LCAPCD), 66 N. Lassen Street, Susanville · (530) 252-4247 · https://www.lassenair.org/ — prior to equipment installation or operation.
