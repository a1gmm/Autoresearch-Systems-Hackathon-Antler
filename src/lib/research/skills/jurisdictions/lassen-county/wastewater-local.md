## Overview

Lassen County has no countywide sewer system. Wastewater is managed through two parallel frameworks:
1. **Onsite systems (OWTS/septic)** — regulated by Lassen County Environmental Health under an approved LAMP for the vast majority of the unincorporated county.
2. **Centralized POTW** — primarily the Susanville Sanitary District, serving the City of Susanville and immediate surroundings. Other small communities may have their own district systems [VERIFY].

Regional permitting authority: **Lahontan Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB)** for most of Lassen County; a small portion may fall under Central Valley RWQCB (see LAMP § 1).

## Lahontan RWQCB WDR Oversight

The Lahontan Regional Water Quality Control Board issues Waste Discharge Requirements (WDRs) to the Susanville Sanitary District for its WWTP discharge. Notable enforcement history: February 2020 NOV identified 242 alleged violations (mostly treated effluent discharge violations); ACL settlement required $3,000 payment and suspended $723,000 contingent on completion of a $2.78 million compliance project (completed March 2012).

## Onsite Wastewater — Lassen County LAMP

**Lassen County LAMP (Local Agency Management Program)**
- Adopted: April 2020 (approved by Lahontan RWQCB)
- Administering agency: Lassen County Environmental Health Department
- Covers: All unincorporated areas AND City of Susanville (incorporated) for individual OWTS/septic
- Authority: State OWTS Policy (adopted June 2012 by SWRCB under AB 885)

**LAMP scope and exclusions:**
- Covers new, repair, modification, abandonment of conventional and engineered OWTS up to 10,000 gpd.
- **Excluded from LAMP (require individual RWQCB WDR):**
  - Any OWTS with projected flow > 10,000 gpd
  - Any OWTS receiving high-strength wastewater (except commercial food service with BOD ≤ 900 mg/L and functional grease interceptor)
  - Large or specialized systems per Regional Board Basin Plan

**Permit process key steps:**
1. Site evaluation / percolation test (per LAMP Appendix procedure)
2. System design proposal review
3. County EHD permit issuance
4. Construction inspection by EHD

**Contact for OWTS permits:**
- Lassen County Environmental Health: (530) 251-8269 · ehe@co.lassen.ca.us · 707 Nevada Street, Suite 5, Susanville, CA 96130

Source: https://www.lassencounty.org/dept/environmental-health/liquid-waste-program · https://www.lassencounty.org/sites/default/files/departments/environmental_health/LiquidWaste_Wells/Final_LassenLAMP_04.2020.pdf
