## Industrial Zone Districts (§ 82.06.020, Table 82-1)

The county uses **two** primary industrial zoning district symbols. Legacy M-1/M-2 designations are not used; verify current designation via LUS parcel lookup.

### IC — Community Industrial
**Purpose (§ 82.06.020(4)(A)):** Provides sites for light industrial uses such as light manufacturing, wholesale/warehouse services, contract/construction services, transportation services, agriculture support services, incidental commercial and accessory residential uses, and similar and compatible uses. These uses shall have **limited outside storage**.

**Minimum area for designation:** 5 acres (Table 82-16)  
**Minimum lot size (Valley Region):** 5 acres; min. width 60 ft, min. depth 100 ft; max. width-to-depth ratio 1:3 (Table 82-18A)

### IR — Regional Industrial
**Purpose (§ 82.06.020(4)(B)):** Provides sites for heavy industrial uses that have the potential to generate severe negative impacts, incidental commercial uses, agricultural support services, salvage operations, and similar and compatible uses.

**Minimum area for designation:** 30 acres (Table 82-16)  
**Minimum lot size (Valley Region):** 5 acres; min. width 60 ft, min. depth 100 ft; max. width-to-depth ratio 1:3 (Table 82-18A)

### Other Special Purpose Districts in Same Chapter
- **IN — Institutional:** Public and quasi-public facilities
- **SD — Special Development:** Mixed use (can have RES/COM/IND suffix)
- **SP — Specific Plan:** As determined by Specific Plan

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

**Chapter:** Development Code Chapter 85.06  
**Review authority:** Land Use Services (LUS) — Planning Director or Planning Commission depending on project size/complexity  
**Application portal:** EZOP (Electronic Zone/Ordinance Permitting) — wp.sbcounty.gov/ezop  
**Accepted file formats:** PDF, CSV, JPG, TIF, TIFF, XLS, XLSX (max 120 MB per file)

**Process steps:**
1. Pre-application meeting with LUS (recommended for complex projects)
2. Submit CUP application through EZOP portal with Letter of Intent, site plans, environmental documents
3. LUS reviews for completeness; CEQA determination made
4. Public notice / hearing (Planning Commission for most CUPs)
5. Conditions of Approval issued with CUP; recorded with County Recorder

**Key threshold:** Uses listed as P(2) in Table 82-17 require a CUP (not just Site Plan Permit) when building coverage exceeds 10,000 sq ft, the use will have more than 20 employees per shift, or the project is not CEQA-exempt.
