## Industrial Zone Districts

Three industrial zone districts exist in Victorville:

### IPD — Industrial Park District (§16-3.11.010(b)(1))

- **Purpose:** "Provide for and to encourage the grouping together of light industrial uses and certain compatible commercial-retail uses that can maintain high standards as to their appearance and which will not interfere with industrial operations or generate excessive traffic."
- **Character:** Highest-amenity industrial zone; light manufacturing and ancillary commercial/retail permitted

### M-1 — Light Industrial District (§16-3.11.010(b)(2))

- **Purpose:** "Provide appropriately located areas for the establishment of industrial uses and directly related activities which will foster a mutually beneficial and compatible pattern of industrial land uses."
- **Character:** Industrial uses free from nuisance or hazardous features that would adversely affect adjacent non-industrial districts; excludes uses generating excessive noise, smoke, dust, noxious gases, vibrations, glare, heat, fire hazard, or industrial waste discharge

### M-2 — Heavy Industrial District (§16-3.11.010(b)(3))

- **Purpose:** "Provide space in suitable locations for certain less restricted types of manufacturing and industrial uses."
- **Character:** Most permissive industrial zone; allows IPD and M-1 uses plus heavier/less-restricted manufacturing if the Planning Commission finds those uses do not adversely affect the ability to develop other heavy manufacturing uses in the M-2 zone

| Standard | IPD | M-1 | M-2 |

| Maximum FAR | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 |
| Front Setback | 30 ft | 10 ft | 10 ft |
| Maximum Height | 45 ft | 50 ft | 50 ft |

## Key Development Requirements (§16-3.11.030 – §16-3.11.060)

- **Site Plan Review** required for all development in industrial districts (§16-3.11.050)
- **Residential buffer walls:** 6-foot masonry walls required where industrial lots abut residential districts (§16-3.11.030)
- **Asphalt roof shingles:** Prohibited on new construction in industrial districts (§16-3.11.040(e))
- **Design guidelines:** §16-3.11.060 — comprehensive requirements covering landscaping, parking design, building design, and signage

## Land Use Permit Matrix — Industrial Districts (§16-3.07.010, Table 7-1)

Per the code structure, Table 7-1 in Article 7 governs all permitted and conditional uses across all zones. The following use-category framework applies:

- **P** = Permitted by right in the district (must meet all other zoning standards)
- **C** = Conditional Use Permit required (granted by Zoning Administrator or Planning Commission before use is allowed)
- **—** = Not permitted

General use categories applicable to industrial zones (from district purpose language and CEQA context):
- Light manufacturing: **P** in IPD, M-1, M-2
- Heavy / less-restricted manufacturing: **P or C** in M-2; restricted in IPD/M-1
- Warehousing / distribution: expected **P** in M-1, M-2
- Compatible commercial/retail: **P or C** in IPD only
- Outdoor storage: likely regulated by development standards; may be conditional

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

- **Decision-makers:** Zoning Administrator (minor CUPs) or Planning Commission (major CUPs), per Table 5-1 — Permit Approval Matrix in Chapter 3, Article 2
- **CUP required for:** Uses designated "C" in Table 7-1; exact list requires reviewing the full table
- **CUP application:** Submitted to City of Victorville Development Department — Planning Division
- **Portal / contact:** https://www.victorvilleca.gov/government/city-departments/development/planning

## Zoning Map and General Plan Designations

The City of Victorville General Plan 2030 designates industrial areas as "Light and Heavy Industrial" corresponding to M-1, M-2, and IPD zoning. The city's GIS zoning checker allows parcel-specific zoning lookups:
- https://www.victorvilleca.gov/government/city-departments/development/planning/check-your-zoning

## EHS-Relevant Notes for Industrial Zones

- **Hazardous materials uses** in industrial zones are subject to Victorville Fire Prevention Division CUPA review (see cupa-local.md) and CFC operational permits (see fire-code-local.md) independent of zoning approvals
- **Outdoor storage of hazardous materials** may require both a CUP from Planning and a CUPA permit from Fire Prevention
- **M-1 purpose language** explicitly excludes uses creating "fire hazard or the discharge of industrial wastes" — high-risk hazmat facilities are likely M-2 with a CUP, or require specific findings
- **VVWRA pretreatment** requirements apply to all industrial facilities in Victorville regardless of zone (see wastewater-local.md)
