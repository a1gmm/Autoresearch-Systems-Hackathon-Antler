## Wastewater / POTW

- **POTW:** Victor Valley Wastewater Reclamation Authority (VVWRA) — https://www.vvwraca.gov/
- City collection system flows to VVWRA Regional Wastewater Treatment Plant, 20111 Shay Road, Victorville
- VVWRA NPDES Permit: Order R6V-2020-0028 (RWQCB-Lahontan, 2020); discharge to Mojave River
- City Sewer Use Ordinance: VMC Title 10, Chapter 10.02 — https://library.municode.com/ca/victorville/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT10WASEUT_CH10.02SASEUSOR_ARTIPUDE
- Industrial pretreatment administered by VVWRA (VVWRA Pretreatment Ordinance 001)
- VMC Chapter 10.08 — Victorville Water District Industrial Wastewater Discharge Pretreatment Program
