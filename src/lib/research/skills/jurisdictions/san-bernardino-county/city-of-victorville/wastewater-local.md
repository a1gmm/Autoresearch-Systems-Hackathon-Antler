> Research reference for wastewater discharge, pretreatment, and industrial sewer use in Victorville.
> TWO layers of regulation apply: (1) City of Victorville Sanitary Sewer Use Ordinance (VMC Title 10, Chapter 10.02) governs connections to the city collection system; (2) Victor Valley Wastewater Reclamation Authority (VVWRA) administers the pretreatment program for industrial dischargers to the regional POTW. Industrial users typically need to deal with VVWRA for the pretreatment/industrial permit and with the City for sewer connection/use.

## POTW / Treatment Agency

- **POTW:** Victor Valley Wastewater Reclamation Authority (VVWRA)
- **Facility:** Victor Valley Regional Wastewater Treatment Plant
- **Address:** 20111 Shay Road, Victorville, CA 92394
- **Service area:** City of Victorville, Town of Apple Valley, City of Hesperia, portions of unincorporated San Bernardino County (Spring Valley Lake, Oro Grande) — approximately 400,000 residents
- **NPDES Permit:** Order No. R6V-2020-0028 / NPDES No. CA0102822 — issued by California Regional Water Quality Control Board, Lahontan Region (RWQCB-Lahontan); expired May 6, 2025 (renewal ROWD due November 7, 2024; permit remains effective pending renewal)
- **Discharge receiving water:** Mojave River (max 14.0 MGD annual average to river; up to 22 MGD recycled water delivery)
- **Environmental compliance contact:** https://www.vvwraca.gov/departments/environmental-compliance
- **VVWRA main:** https://www.vvwraca.gov/

## VVWRA — Industrial Pretreatment Program

Federal law (Clean Water Act §402; 40 CFR Part 403) requires VVWRA, as the Control Authority for its POTW, to implement an approved Industrial Wastewater Pretreatment Program Plan. VVWRA's plan dates from January 1, 1995, with subsequent modifications approved by RWQCB-Lahontan.

### Program Authority

- **VVWRA Pretreatment Ordinance:** Referenced as "Ordinance 001" in search indices; full text available via VVWRA Ordinances and Agreements page: https://www.vvwraca.gov/governance/ordinances-and-agreements
- **Environmental compliance page:** https://www.vvwraca.gov/departments/environmental-compliance/industrial-pretreatment-national-pretreatment
- **Regulatory requirement:** NPDES Permit R6V-2020-0028, Section VI.C.5 (Special Provisions for POTWs) — requires VVWRA to implement 40 CFR 403.8(f)(1)-(3) (legal authorities, programmatic functions, funding/personnel)
- **VVWRA regulatory documents:** https://www.vvwraca.gov/governance/regulatory-documents

### Industrial Pretreatment Permit Process

1. Businesses discharging non-domestic wastewater to the VVWRA collection system must obtain an **Industrial Waste Discharge Permit** from VVWRA's Environmental Compliance Department
2. VVWRA inspects industrial users and conducts monitoring to verify compliance with pretreatment standards
3. Applicable standards: 40 CFR Part 403 General Pretreatment Regulations + categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR Part 405–471) for applicable industrial categories
4. Annual pretreatment report filed by VVWRA with RWQCB-Lahontan by March 1 of each year

## Sewer Collection — City Responsibility

- The City of Victorville owns and operates the sanitary sewer collection system within city limits
- Wastewater flows to VVWRA's regional treatment plant
- City Engineering manages the Sewer System Management Plan (SSMP): https://www.victorvilleca.gov/government/city-departments/engineering/development-engineering/master-plans/sewer-system-management-plan-ssmp-875
- Sewer rates governed by VMC Chapter 10.01 — Sewer Rate Ordinance
