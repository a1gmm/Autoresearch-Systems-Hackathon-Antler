## Adopted Fire Code

- **CFC edition adopted:** 2025 California Fire Code (CCR Title 24, Part 9), based on IFC 2024
- **Adopting ordinance:** Ordinance No. 2460 — introduced at public hearing December 2, 2024; unanimously approved
- **Municipal code location:** Title 8 — FIRE of the Victorville Municipal Code
- **Key chapter:** Chapter 8.08 (fire code operational chapter — confirmed by Fire Prevention Standard A-1, rev. 11-5-2025, which cites "section 8.08.04(5) of the City of Victorville Municipal Code")
- **Appendices adopted:** Appendices B, BB, C, CC, D, and H adopted by reference (confirmed via search index; verify full list in Ord. 2460 text)
- **What the ordinance does:** Repeals and re-enacts chapters and sections of Title 8 to adopt the 2025 CFC with local calibrations; reflects Victorville Fire Department operational changes made since the city re-established its own fire department in March 2019

## Local Amendments — Confirmed

| Topic | Local Provision |
|---|---|
| Fire Code Official authority | §8.08.04(5) authorizes the fire code official to adopt policies, procedures, rules, and regulations to clarify application of the CFC and specify requirements not explicitly in the CFC (confirmed verbatim in Fire Prevention Standard A-1, rev. 11-5-2025) |
| Fire apparatus access roads | Fire Prevention Standard A-1 (rev. 11-5-2025) operationalizes CFC §§102.9, 103, 104.1.1 and 503.x; minimum 2 access points for commercial/industrial; single-access exception at 35 ft wide, max 62,000 sq ft, max 30 ft / 3 stories |
| Number of access points | Two separate fire apparatus access points required for all commercial and industrial development; points must be ≥ ½ diagonal distance of the development area apart |

## High-Piled Combustible Storage

State CFC 2025 baseline thresholds apply unless local amendment reduces them [VERIFY]:

| Storage type | Permit threshold |
|---|---|
| Class I–IV commodities | Storage area > 500 sq ft with piling > 12 ft (solid shelving > 6 ft) |
| Group A plastics / high-hazard | > 500 sq ft |
| Public access / retail | > 2,500 sq ft |

Annual operational permit required; submit a High-Piled Storage Plan (commodity analysis, rack drawings, sprinkler design) to Victorville Fire Prevention Division for plan check.

## Hazardous Materials Operational Permits (CFC)

CFC Chapter 50+ operational permits are required for:

- Hazardous materials stored/used above CFC permit threshold quantities
- Compressed gases (CFC Chapter 53)
- Flammable/combustible liquids above threshold (CFC Chapter 57)
- Explosives, cryogenic fluids, oxidizers, pyrophoric materials (CFC Chapters 55, 56, 62–66)

Annual operational permits are listed and issued by Victorville Fire Prevention Division; fee schedule at:
https://www.victorvilleca.gov/government/city-departments/development/permit-center/fire-prevention/annual-operational-permits
