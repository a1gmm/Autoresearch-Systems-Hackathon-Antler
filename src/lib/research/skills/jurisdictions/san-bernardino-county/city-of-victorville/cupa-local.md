> Research reference for the City of Victorville acting as its own CUPA.
> CRITICAL GOTCHA: Victorville is the single exception in San Bernardino County — it operates its own CUPA independently of the county. San Bernardino County Fire Protection District is CUPA for every other city/unincorporated area in the county; do NOT apply county CUPA guidance to Victorville facilities.
> The Victorville Fire Department administers all six CalEPA unified programs for city-limits locations.

## Programs Administered

Victorville Fire Department / Fire Prevention Division implements all six CalEPA unified programs within city limits:

| # | Program | Acronym | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan | HMBP | Filed via CERS; Victorville FPD is reviewing agency |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator Program | HWG | Generator registration, manifest compliance, inspection |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tanks | UST | Covered under VMC Title 6, Chapter 6.50 (see below) |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act | APSA | SPCC-plan facilities; CERS filing |
| 5 | California Accidental Release Prevention | CalARP | Local implementing agency for RMP facilities |
| 6 | CFC Hazardous Materials Operational Permits | CFC HazMat | CFC Chapter 50+ operational permits; annual renewal |

  - Confirms Section 6.50.090 (Removal of underground storage tanks) and related provisions

## HMBP Program Details

- **Submission portal:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business
- **Who must comply:** Businesses handling hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities per HSC §25507 — generally 55 gallons liquid, 500 lbs solid, 200 cubic feet gas
- **Annual review/resubmission deadline:** January 1 – March 1 each year
- **Required CERS content:** Hazardous materials inventory, emergency response/contingency plan, site map, employee training records
- **Inspection agency:** Victorville Fire Prevention Division inspects to verify CERS inventory accuracy

## UST Program (Chapter 6.50)

- Victorville Municipal Code **§6.50.090** addresses removal of underground storage tanks
- Victorville FPD, as CUPA, handles UST installation permits, modification, closure, and release response within city limits
- The county CUPA (SBCo Fire) handles USTs in all San Bernardino County cities except Victorville

## Fee Schedule

A 2022 User Fee Study (NBS) noted that during re-establishment of Victorville's own fire department (March 2019), the city removed "almost all fees for hazardous materials regulation such as CUPA program fees" from the legacy fee schedule, which was based on a copy of the county structure. A revised fee structure was being developed.
