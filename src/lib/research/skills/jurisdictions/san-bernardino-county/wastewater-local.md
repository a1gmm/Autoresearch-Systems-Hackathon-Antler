## POTW / Governing Agency

**Agency:** San Bernardino Municipal Water Department (SBMWD), Environmental Control Section  
**Governing document:** **Rule and Regulation No. 26 — Wastewater Facilities and Non-Domestic Discharge Permits**  
**Underlying authority:** Clean Water Act, 40 CFR Part 403 (General Pretreatment Regulations), Santa Ana RWQCB NPDES Permit for SBMWD's Water Reclamation Plant (WRP), and the San Bernardino Municipal Code and City Charter.

> SCOPE GOTCHA: SBMWD serves the City of San Bernardino and portions of its service territory. For facilities in **unincorporated San Bernardino County** not served by SBMWD, a different POTW applies (e.g., Victorville Water District, Big Bear Area Regional Wastewater Agency, or a County Special District). Confirm the serving POTW by parcel address before applying any limits below.

## Permit Types (Rule and Regulation No. 26, Section 5)

| Permit Type | When Required |
|---|---|
| **Industrial User (IU) Permit** | Any non-domestic facility whose discharge may affect POTW operations or NPDES compliance |
| **Significant Industrial User (SIU) Permit** | Facilities meeting federal SIU criteria (5+ kg/day regulated pollutant, or process-regulated category) |
| **Food Service User Permit** | Restaurants, commercial kitchens — grease interceptor/trap required |
| **Liquid Waste Hauler Permit** | Haulers disposing septic/chemical toilet waste at WRP septic collection station |
| **Inland Empire Brine Line (IEBL) Indirect Industrial User Permit** | Facilities discharging high-TDS waste to the Brine Line |

**When to apply:** Before commencing discharge. New facilities and those completing tenant improvements must also submit for **plan check review** before construction begins.

## Permit Application Process

1. Submit the Industrial User Permit Application to the Environmental Control Section (no online portal; applications submitted directly to Section).
2. Undergo plan check review if construction/TI is involved.
3. Install any required monitoring equipment, pretreatment equipment (interceptors, pH neutralization, etc.) as specified by the Director.
4. Facility Waste Management Plan may be required (Rule 26 § 4.16).
5. Permit issued; subject to periodic renewal, monitoring, and inspection.

**Application form:** https://sbmwd.org/DocumentCenter/View/311/Industrial-User-Permit-Application-PDF

## Discharge Prohibitions (Rule and Regulation No. 26, Section 3)

Key categorical prohibitions:
- pH below **5.0** or above **11.0** at point of discharge; influent to WRP must remain between 6.5 and 8.0.
- Flash point < **140°F (60°C)** (closed cup, per 40 CFR 261.21).
- Explosive/flammable substances exceeding **10% of LEL** at point of discharge or in collection system.
- Solids/debris that obstruct flow (grease, viscera, rags, plastic, tar, etc.).
- Medical waste, septic tank/cesspool overflow except as authorized.
- Unpolluted water (cooling water, storm water, single-pass cooling water) — prohibited as dilution substitute.
- Water softener regeneration brine — subject to volume/concentration restrictions (§ 3.8, 3.9); SBMWD may prohibit residential softener installation if POTW is out of NPDES compliance.

## Sampling Requirements (Rule and Regulation No. 26, § 2.5)

- **Grab samples** required for: pH, cyanide, total phenols, oil/grease, sulfide, volatile organics.
- **24-hour composite** (time or flow-proportioned) or grab at Director's discretion for all other pollutants.
- All analyses per **40 CFR 136** methods; laboratory must be accredited by California ELAP.

## Enforcement (Section 6)

- Discharge Violations → Notice of Violation (NOV), Administrative Order, Compliance Schedule
- Civil penalties up to amounts set in § 6.10; Criminal penalties in § 6.11
- Sewer Suspension Order (SUSP) or Sewer Termination Order (TERM) for serious violations
- Appeals process: Written appeal within timeframe specified in NOV; Judicial Review available (§ 6.16)

## Charges and Fees (Section 7)

Fees established by SBMWD Board resolution. Categories include:
- Permit Charges and Fees (§ 7.5)
- Monitoring and Inspection Charges (§ 7.6)
- Connection Application Fees (§ 7.3)

Specific fee amounts are set by Board resolution and not reproduced in Rule 26 text; contact SBMWD Environmental Control Section for current fee schedule.
