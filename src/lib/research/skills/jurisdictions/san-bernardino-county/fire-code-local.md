## Adopted Code Edition

**Adopted edition:** 2022 California Fire Code (CFC)  
**Adopting ordinance:** Ordinance No. FPD 23-01  
**Adopted by Fire Protection District Board:** February 28, 2023  
**Ratified by San Bernardino County Board of Supervisors:** May 23, 2023 (Consent Calendar)

## AHJ (Authority Having Jurisdiction)

**San Bernardino County Fire Protection District (SBCFPD)**  
Covers unincorporated county areas + most incorporated cities after absorbing multiple city and district fire departments. City of San Bernardino FD was annexed July 1, 2016 and is no longer a separate AHJ. Exception: City of Victorville maintains its own fire department and fire code administration.

**Scope:** Applies only to unincorporated areas within the SBCFPD boundary.

## Municipal Code Codification

Ordinance FPD 23-01 **repeals Chapter 1 of Division 3 of Title 2** of the San Bernardino County Code (the prior "Uniform Fire Code" chapter). The 2022 CFC, as locally amended by FPD 23-01, is the operative fire code for the district.

**Enforcement authority:** Fire Chief (or authorized representative) of the SBCFPD, per CFC §§ 102.9, 103, and 104.1 as incorporated.

## Local Amendments

Ordinance FPD 23-01 incorporates "certain changes, modifications, amendments, additions, deletions, and exceptions" to the 2022 CFC for local climatic, geological, and topographic conditions (authority: Health & Safety Code § 13869 et seq.). The District has also adopted a suite of numbered fire prevention **Standards** (A-1 through W-4) that constitute additional local amendments, including:

- **Standard S-1** — High-Piled Combustible Storage (design requirements for storage >12 ft or 500 sq ft in certain occupancies)
- **Standard F-1 / F-2 / F-3** — Fire sprinkler systems (one-family, two-family, non-residential)
- **Standard B-2** — Construction site fire safety
- Fire Hazard Abatement Ordinance
- Roofing Ordinance

Specific section-by-section amendments to the 2022 CFC text are contained in the FPD 23-01 ordinance document (see PDF link below). No individual CFC sections are republished on the public website.

## Permits Required (Community Safety Division)

The following construction and operational permits require SBCFPD approval (from FY 25-26 Fee Schedule, Section A and B):

**Construction permits (selected):**
- Commercial/Industrial NFPA 13 sprinkler — new system: Plan Review $761; Inspection 1-20 heads $401, 21-50 heads $481, 51-100 heads $603, 101-350 heads $703, >350 heads $802
- In-Rack Sprinklers: Plan Review $662; Inspection 1-10 heads $481 … >30 heads $1,123
- High-Piled Combustible Storage: Plan Review $542; Inspection 500-10,000 sq ft $443; 10,001-50,000 sq ft $603; 50,001-100,000 sq ft $764; each additional 100,000 sq ft $401
- Aboveground Storage Tank: Plan Review $434; Inspection 1st tank $481; each additional same type $241
- Fire Alarm — new system: Plan Review manual/auto $672; Inspection 1-10 devices $642 … >100 devices $160/100 additional

**Annual operational permits (selected):**
- Base fee by facility size: 1-5,000 sq ft $248; 5,001-10,000 sq ft $313; 10,001-50,000 sq ft $390; 50,001-100,000 sq ft $592; 100,001-500,000 sq ft $751; 500,001-1,000,000 sq ft $1,028; >1,000,000 sq ft $1,543
- High Hazard category (includes High-Piled Combustible Storage, Flammable/Combustible Liquids, Compressed Gases, Hazmat, CalARP-covered processes): $160/permit type in addition to base fee
- Medium Hazard category (e.g., Flammable/Combustible Liquids Mobile Fueling, Motor Vehicle Fuel Dispensing, Refrigeration Equipment, Dry Cleaning): $105/permit type
- Low Hazard category (e.g., Open Flames, Places of Assembly 50-299): $52/permit type

Total annual fee = Base Fee + applicable per-permit-type fee(s).

**Special events:** Minor Special Event Review $123; Major (501+ attendees) Inspection $314.
