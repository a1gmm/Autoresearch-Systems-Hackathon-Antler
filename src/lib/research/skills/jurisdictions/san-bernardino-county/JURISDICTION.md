## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

**Agency:** San Bernardino County Fire Protection District, Hazardous Materials Division  
**Designated CUPA for:** all unincorporated county + most incorporated cities — **except City of Victorville**, which runs its own CUPA through the Victorville Fire Department.

Six CUPA program elements administered:
- Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)
- Hazardous Materials Management Plan (HMMP)
- Hazardous Waste & On-Site Treatment
- Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)
- Underground Storage Tanks (UST)
- California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)

**Local code:** San Bernardino County Code, Title 23, Chapter 7 (CUPA Permit Elements for Hazardous Materials); key fee section is § 23.0722 (CUPA Permit and Fees Required).

**How to apply:** Submit hazardous material inventory through CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) at cers.calepa.ca.gov, then contact CUPA office.  
**Online payment:** govhub.com (linked from sbcfire.org/hazmatpermitsandfees/)  
**Permit inquiries / invoice copies:** CUPApermits@sbcfire.org  
**Office:** 598 S. Tippecanoe Ave., First Floor, San Bernardino, CA 92415-0153  
**Phone:** (909) 386-8401 | 24-hr spill hotline: (909) 386-8425 or (800) 338-6942 | CERS help: (909) 386-8432

### 2. Fire Code — AHJ & Adopted Edition

**AHJ:** San Bernardino County Fire Protection District (SBCFPD) — covers ~86% of the county after absorbing multiple city/district departments; note City of San Bernardino FD was annexed 2016-07-01 and no longer exists separately.

**Adopted fire code:** 2022 California Fire Code (CFC), adopted by **Ordinance No. FPD 23-01**, adopted February 28, 2023, ratified by Board of Supervisors May 23, 2023. Applies only to unincorporated county areas within the Fire Protection District. Includes local amendments, changes, and deletions for local climatic, geological, and topographic conditions (per Health & Safety Code § 13869 et seq.).

**Construction/fire permit types requiring a permit:**
- High-piled combustible storage (Standard S-1)
- Hazardous materials (via CUPA program above)
- Fire sprinkler systems — one-family, two-family, non-residential (Standards F-1, F-2, F-3)
- Special events
- Construction site fire safety (Standard B-2)

**Permit applications:** ezop@sbcfire.org or in person at 598 S. Tippecanoe Ave., San Bernardino, CA 92408; (909) 386-8400  
**Fee schedule (FY 25-26):** https://sbcfire.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/46/25-26-Fee-Schedule.pdf

### 3. Industrial Wastewater / Pretreatment

**Note:** Industrial wastewater is handled by the POTW serving the specific location, not a single county-wide agency. The largest POTW in the county seat area is:

**Agency:** San Bernardino Municipal Water Department (SBMWD), Environmental Control Section  
**Governing rule:** SBMWD Rule and Regulation No. 26 — Wastewater Facility and Non-Domestic Discharge Permits  
**Permit types:**
- Food Service User Permit
- Industrial User Permit
- Significant Industrial User Permit
- Inland Empire Brine Line (IEBL) Indirect Industrial User Permit

**Process:** New industrial users must submit a permit application to the Environmental Control Section before discharge commences. Facilities proposing tenant improvements must also submit for plan check review before construction. No separate online portal; applications submitted directly to the Section.

**Contact:** 1350 South E Street, San Bernardino, CA 92408 | (909) 384-5141 | M–Th 8 am–5 pm, F 8 am–4 pm

Source: https://www.sbmwd.org/170/Environmental-Control  
Source (permit application): https://www.sbmwd.org/DocumentCenter/View/311/Industrial-User-Permit-Application-PDF

> GOTCHA: For facilities in unincorporated county areas not served by SBMWD, the applicable POTW/sanitation district will differ (e.g., Victorville Water District, Big Bear Area Regional Wastewater Agency). Confirm the serving POTW by parcel address.

### 4. Stormwater — MS4 / Industrial Stormwater

**Principal permittee:** San Bernardino County Flood Control District  
**MS4 permit:** Order No. R8-2010-0036, NPDES Permit No. CAS618036, adopted January 29, 2010 (Santa Ana River Watershed). A Regional MS4 Permit covering San Bernardino, Orange, and Riverside Counties is under development (Tentative Order released March 1, 2024 by Santa Ana Water Board).  
**Mojave River watershed:** Separate MS4 Phase II permit for urbanized portions.

**Local stormwater ordinance:** San Bernardino County Code, Title 3, Chapter 1 — Pollutant Discharge Elimination System Regulations (updated through 2025 S-40).

**Industrial facility stormwater:** Co-permittees must implement business facility inspection programs; industrial sites with exposure of materials to stormwater typically require a General Industrial Stormwater Permit (issued by State Water Board, NOT county) plus local BMPs.

### 5. Zoning / Land Use — Industrial Districts

**Governing code:** San Bernardino County Development Code, **Title 8** (unincorporated areas only)  
**Code structure:** Chapter numbers follow pattern Division.Chapter.Section (e.g., 82.06.040 = Title 8, Division 2, Chapter 82.06, § 040)

**Industrial zoning districts (Title 8, Chapter 82.06):**
- **IC — Community Industrial:** light manufacturing, wholesale/warehouse, contract/construction services, transportation, agricultural support, incidental commercial. By-right for most light industrial uses.
- **IR — Regional Industrial:** heavy industrial uses with potential for significant impacts, salvage operations, industrial support, incidental commercial. Heavier uses typically require a Conditional Use Permit (CUP).

> NOTE: County no longer uses M-1/M-2 designations. Legacy parcels may show older designations; verify current zoning with LUS parcel lookup.

**Allowed uses by district:** Table 82-17 in the Development Code specifies by-right vs. CUP-required uses per district (§ 82.06.040).  
**CUP process:** Administered by Land Use Services (LUS); applications submitted through LUS portal.
