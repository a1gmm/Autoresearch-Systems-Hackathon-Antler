## CUPA Agency

**Agency:** San Bernardino County Fire Protection District (SBCFPD), Hazardous Materials Division  
**Status:** Designated by the California Secretary for Environmental Protection as the **Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA)** for San Bernardino County.  
**Coverage:** All unincorporated county areas + most incorporated cities **except City of Victorville**, which operates its own CUPA through the Victorville Fire Department.

## Local Ordinance / Municipal Code

**Primary code authority:**  
- San Bernardino County Code, **Title 2, Division 3, Chapter 7** — "CUPA Permit Elements for Hazardous Materials"  
- Key sections:  
  - **§ 23.0602** — Current CUPA Operational Permit Required (mandate for CUPA permit before operating)  
  - **§ 23.0712** — Requires facilities to submit hazardous waste/materials information via CERS  
  - **§ 23.0722** — CUPA Permit and Fees Required (UST program element); references corrective action oversight reimbursement at rates in Title 1, Division 6, Chapter 2

**Note:** Chapter numbering uses the county's section-number system (e.g., "§ 23.0602" is within Chapter 7 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the San Bernardino County Code). Codified at: https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/sanbernardino/latest/sanberncty_ca/0-0-0-155603

## Six CUPA Program Elements Administered

1. Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) — inventory, emergency response, site map
2. Hazardous Waste & On-Site Treatment — generator program, Permit-by-Rule, Conditional Authorization, Conditional Exemption
3. Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)
4. Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program
5. California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)
6. CA Fire Code — Hazardous Materials Management Plan (HMMP) & Hazardous Materials Inventory Statement (HMIS)

## Electronic Reporting

All HMBP, hazardous waste, and related inventory submitted through **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System) at cers.calepa.ca.gov. Facilities must complete CERS submission before the CUPA can process permits.

Source reduction plans required for generators producing ≥ 12,000 kg (26,400 lb) annually; reporting recurs every four years.

## How to Apply

1. Submit hazardous materials/waste inventory through CERS at cers.calepa.ca.gov
2. SBCFPD Hazmat Division assigns permit based on CERS data
3. Pay annual invoice via **GovHub** (linked from sbcfire.org/hazmatpermitsandfees/)
4. For permit copies/invoices: CUPApermits@sbcfire.org
