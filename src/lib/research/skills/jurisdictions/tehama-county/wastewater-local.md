> Wastewater reference for Tehama County. This is a predominantly rural county with
> no single county-wide sanitation district. Sewer service is provided by several separate
> entities depending on location. Industrial pretreatment programs (IWPs) are NOT in
> effect at any confirmed Tehama County POTW — verified for the largest system (City of
> Red Bluff) via its 2023 NPDES permit.

## Sewer Service Providers by Area

- **Facility address:** 700 Messer Drive, Red Bluff, CA 96080
- **NPDES permit:** Order R5-2023-0048 / NPDES No. CA0078891 (adopted 12 October 2023;
  expires 30 November 2028) — issued by Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control
  Board (Region 5), Redding office, (530) 224-4845.
- **Discharge point:** Sacramento River at 40.163°N 122.220°W.

  permit fact sheet. The permit states: "The collection system does not include any

### Discharge Rules Under Ordinance No. 2119 (Tehama County Sanitation District No. 1)

Ordinance No. 2119 §4.3 establishes the following **prohibited and limited discharges**
(applicable to facilities served by this district):

**Absolute prohibitions (§4.3a):**
- Gasoline, benzene, naphtha, fuel oil, or other flammable liquids (flashpoint <100°F)
- Toxic or poisonous solids, liquids, or gases in quantities endangering life, operations,
  or receiving waters
- Solids causing obstruction: ashes, sand, metal, glass, rags, tar, unground garbage,
  blood, hair, entrails, whole plastics, paper containers, etc.
- Any discharge that by interaction with sewer contents releases toxic gases, forms
  interfering suspended solids, or is deleterious to structures and treatment processes
- Any discharge that creates a public nuisance or hazard to life

**Conditional/limited discharges (§4.3b) — not to exceed levels that harm sewers or receiving waters:**
- **Temperature:** Not greater than 150°F (65°C)
- **Petroleum oil:** Not more than 25 mg/L non-biodegradable cutting oils or mineral oil
- **Garbage:** Must be properly shredded (no particle > 1/2 inch); grinders allowed in
  food prep facilities (homes, restaurants, caterers)
- **Metals (iron, chromium, copper, zinc, similar):** Not to exceed limits established by the
  District (specific numeric limits not set in the ordinance; set case-by-case by District)
- **Odor-producing substances:** Not to exceed District-established limits
- **Radioactive wastes:** Not to exceed limits established by the District in compliance
  with state/federal regulations
- **Slug discharges:** Prohibited — any discharge exceeding 5× average 24-hour
  concentration or flow for more than 15 minutes that adversely affects the system
- **Substances not amenable to treatment** at the WWTP's treatment processes

**Grease/oil/sand interceptors (§4.3d):**
Required when in the District's opinion they are necessary to handle floatable grease,
flammable wastes, sand, or other harmful ingredients. Not required for private
residences. Type and capacity approved by the District; owner responsible for proper
maintenance, pumping, and disposal records.

**Case-by-case industrial discharge review (§4.3c):**
If any discharge may have a deleterious effect on facilities, receiving waters, or creates
a hazard to life, the District may: (1) reject the waste; (2) require pretreatment to an
acceptable condition; (3) require flow control; or (4) require payment of added costs.
Where pretreatment is permitted, design and installation require District approval.

**Information requests (§4.3f):**
The District may require industrial users to provide: discharge peak rate and volume data,
chemical analyses, and sampling access.

## Industrial Pretreatment Summary

| POTW | Formal IWP Program | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| City of Red Bluff WWRP (CA0078891) | **No** | Confirmed "Not Applicable" in NPDES Order R5-2023-0048; no significant industrial users |
| Tehama County SD No. 1 / Mineral (CA0084069) | **No** | Very small rural district; no IWP program found; verify with TCPW |

Industrial dischargers in Tehama County discharging to either system are subject to the
general discharge prohibitions above but are NOT required to obtain a formal Industrial
Waste Permit. However, a discharge with unusual characteristics will trigger District or
City review under the case-by-case provisions.
