### Other Relevant Districts

- **Chapter 17.32 — G-R General Recreation District** (not industrial; noted for context)
- **Chapter 17.38 — Planned Development / Planned Land Dedication** (PDPLDEDI —
  applicable to planned industrial parks or development agreements)
- **Chapter 17.71 — Commercial/Farm (COFA)** — may include some agricultural-industrial
  uses; see node `TIT17ZO_CH17.71COFA`

> Note: Tehama County is predominantly rural/agricultural. Heavy industrial siting
> (e.g., hazardous materials storage, large-scale manufacturing) in the General Industrial
> district will also require CUPA/HMBP filings with Tehama County Environmental Health
> and potentially CalARP review for RMP-regulated substances. Coordinate zoning
> approval with CUPA compliance early.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) / Use Permit Process

- The CUP (locally called "use permit") process is governed by Title 17 provisions on
  administrative permits and use permits. Confirmed chapters include:
  - **Chapter 17.72 — Variances**
  - **Chapter 17.75 — Lot Line Adjustments**
  - **Chapter 17.81 — Administrative Permit / Agreement to Use** (node `17.81ADPEAGTOUS`)
  - **Chapter 17.87 — Administrative Permit / Limited Industrial or Other Operations**
    (node `17.87ADPELIPOOP`)
- The specific chapter governing use permits (CUPs) requiring Planning Commission
  hearing was not confirmed from search results. [VERIFY — contact Planning Department
  or review Title 17 table of contents on Municode for the use permit/conditional use
  permit chapter number.]
- **Planning Commission** is the approving body for use permits; Chapter 2.20 governs
  Planning Commission composition and authority.
