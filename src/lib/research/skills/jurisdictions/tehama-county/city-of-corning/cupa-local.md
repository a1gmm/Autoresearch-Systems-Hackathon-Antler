# CUPA (Certified Unified Program Agency) — City of Corning, CA

## Controlling Authority

The City of Corning **does not operate its own CUPA**. All Unified Program elements for facilities within the city are administered by:

**Tehama County Environmental Health Division**
Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all of Tehama County (incorporated and unincorporated)

## Programs Covered by the County CUPA

For facilities located within the City of Corning, Tehama County Environmental Health administers all six Unified Program elements:

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — facilities storing hazardous materials above threshold quantities
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator Program** — small- and large-quantity generators, including on-site hazardous waste management
3. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — permitting, inspection, and corrective action oversight
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — facilities storing petroleum above 1,320 gallons aggregate
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) Program** — facilities with regulated substances above threshold quantities (RMP/PSM equivalent)
6. **Tiered Permitting / Hazardous Waste Treatment (onsite)**

Tehama County also responds to hazardous-materials incidents, performs site analysis, and verifies compliance with HMBP requirements throughout the county, including within Corning city limits.

## Household Hazardous Waste

A Corning Household Hazardous Waste (HHW) Facility is located at 3281 Highway 99W, Corning, operated as part of Tehama County's solid waste and hazardous materials infrastructure.

## Notes for EHS Research
- Submit HMBP, hazardous waste, UST, and CalARP materials to Tehama County EH, not to the city.
- The county uses the statewide CERS (California Environmental Reporting System) portal for electronic submissions: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/
- For emergency spills, Tehama County OES and Cal OES Region 2 coordinate response.
