# Fire Code — City of Corning, CA

## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

**Corning Volunteer Fire Department** (city-operated; all-volunteer except paid Fire Chief and dispatchers)
- Address: 814 Fifth St., Corning, CA 96021
- Fire Administration: (530) 824-7044
- FAX: (530) 824-7042
- Hours: 7:30 AM–5:30 PM daily

The City of Corning runs its own fire department — it is **not** served by Tehama County Fire or CAL FIRE for structural fire/fire code enforcement within city limits.

**Building Official (fire-code plan check / building permits):** Benjamin Mierzwak
- Phone: (530) 824-7027
- Email: bmierzwak@corningca.gov
- The Building & Safety division enforces Title 24 and the California Fire Code per the city's website.

## Adopted Fire Code

**Chapter 15.16 — Fire Prevention Code** (Title 15, Building & Construction, Corning Municipal Code)

The City of Corning adopts the **California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9)** by reference through Chapter 15.16 of its municipal code. ### Current Statewide Edition
The **2025 California Fire Code** (based on IFC 2024) became effective **January 1, 2026** statewide (Cal. Code Regs., Title 24, Part 9). Cities that did not file local amendments with the California Building Standards Commission (CBSC) default to the statewide edition. The City of Corning **did not file any local building/fire code amendments** with CBSC for the 2022 triennial cycle, per the CBSC ordinance filing database.

## Local Permits / Programs
- Burn Permit: available through the fire department (corning.org/burn-permit-information/)
- Fireworks Permit: issued by the fire department
- Weed Abatement Program: administered by the fire department

## Notes for EHS Research
- For fire suppression system plans, fire alarm plans, and hazardous-materials occupancy permits (sprinklers, high-piled storage, etc.), contact the Fire Department at (530) 824-7044.
- The Corning Volunteer Fire Department is a single-station department protecting approximately 5 square miles and 7,500+ residents.
- CalARP / RMP Tier 2 / HMBP for facilities in Corning are administered by **Tehama County Environmental Health (the county CUPA)**, not the city fire department.
