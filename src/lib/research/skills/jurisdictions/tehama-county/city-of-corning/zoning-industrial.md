## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

CUPs for industrial uses (and other land use permits including variances, rezoning requests, subdivision maps, and annexations) are reviewed by the **Planning Commission**, which meets on the **third Tuesday of each month at 6:30 PM**.

Applications are submitted to the Community Development / Planning Department.
