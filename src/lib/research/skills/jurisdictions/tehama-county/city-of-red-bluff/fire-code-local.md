## Fire Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

**City of Red Bluff Fire Department** — independent municipal combination career/reserve department. NOT served by Tehama County Fire or CAL FIRE for city-limit fire prevention; RBFD holds the AHJ for fire code enforcement within city limits.

## Adopted Fire Code

The City has adopted the **California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9)** by reference in the Red Bluff City Code.

- The RBFD Fire Safety & Prevention Division website (as of 2026-06-04) states the **2019 California Fire Code** and its Standards are in effect.
- AMlegal search results indicate **Ordinance 1071** (adopted December 6, 2022) amended fire-related code provisions to address the **2022 California Fire Code** cycle. [VERIFY — confirm current adopted edition with RBFD; the RBFD website may lag ordinance adoption.]

**Municipal code location:** Red Bluff City Code (RBCC) Chapter 5, § 5.28 — California Fire Code; and Chapter 8, Article II — Fire Code. Also §5.20 — Local Responsibility Area Fire Hazard provisions.

## Local Amendments

- **Open burning prohibited:** RBCC § 8.17 / Chapter 8, Article II (§ 8.21 Very High Fire Severity Zone construction standards also adopted). Residential open burning including burn barrels is prohibited within city limits at all times.
- **Portable outdoor fireplaces** require 15-foot clearance and a fire extinguisher on hand.
- **Very High Fire Severity Zone** (VHFSZ) construction standards adopted per RBCC § 8.21; CAL FIRE LRA map applies to the city's local responsibility area within Tehama County.
- Local fire permit fee schedule (Exhibit H, adopted May 7, 2019, effective July 6, 2019) governs plan-check and inspection fees; fees include hourly rates for Fire Chief, Division Chief, Captain, Engineer, and Firefighter, and per-system fees for new fire alarm systems (e.g., 1–50 devices: $376; 51–100 devices: $470; 101–500 devices: $564).

## Inspection Program

- Commercial, industrial, and public buildings: annual inspections by on-duty fire crews.
- Business occupancies: alternating self-inspection / fire crew inspection cycle (every other year each).
- Automatic fire suppression systems: quarterly self-inspections; five-year professional testing by licensed contractor.
- New construction/change of occupancy: initial inspection by Fire Prevention Division, then annual cycle.

## Mutual Aid

RBFD holds automatic/mutual aid agreements with CAL FIRE Tehama-Glenn Unit and Tehama County Fire for emergency response; CAL FIRE and Tehama County Fire are NOT the fire code AHJ within city limits.
