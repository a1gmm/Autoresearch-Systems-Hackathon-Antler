## Sewer Use Ordinance

Sewer regulations are codified in **Red Bluff City Code (RBCC) Chapter 18** (Sewers/Wastewater):

- **§ 18.2** — Establishment of Wastewater Department and municipal control
- **§ 18.44** — Wastewater Discharge Permit Requirements (conditions, monitoring, self-reporting, pollution control plans, spill control plans, pretreatment requirements where applicable)
- **§ 18.47** — Wastewater Discharge Permit Revocation

Sewer service rates are established by **Ordinance 832** (effective July 15, 2012; rates as shown in city fee schedule Exhibit B). Rate classes: single-family ($34/mo), mobile home ($24/mo), commercial minimum ($34/mo), commercial over-minimum by volume (bakery: $3.63/100 cu ft; restaurant: $2.99/100 cu ft; all other commercial: $2.69/100 cu ft).

## Industrial Pretreatment Program (IPP/IWP)

**No Industrial Pretreatment Program (IWP).** The 2023 NPDES permit (Order R5-2023-0048, Section VI.C — Special Provisions for POTWs) states: **"Pretreatment Requirements — Not Applicable."** The NPDES Fact Sheet confirms: "The collection system does not include any significant industrial users."

Industrial or commercial dischargers must still comply with general RBCC Chapter 18 prohibitions on discharge of substances that could interfere with the POTW, and federal categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR Part 403/405–471) apply where triggered by the nature of the discharge, even absent a local IWP. Contact the City of Red Bluff Public Works / Utilities at (530) 527-2605 ext. 3065 prior to any industrial discharge.
