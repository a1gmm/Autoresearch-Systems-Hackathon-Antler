## CUPA Agency

The City of Red Bluff does **not** operate its own CUPA. All Unified Program elements for facilities within Red Bluff city limits are administered by:

## Programs Administered by Tehama County CUPA

The county CUPA administers all six Unified Program elements for Red Bluff facilities:

- **HMBP** — Hazardous Materials Business Plans (facilities storing hazardous materials above threshold quantities)
- **Hazardous Waste Generators** — inspection and oversight of hazardous waste generators
- **USTs** — Underground Storage Tanks (gas stations, vehicle repair, other commercial facilities)
- **ASTs / APSA** — Aboveground Storage Tanks subject to the Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act
- **CalARP** — California Accidental Release Prevention program (facilities with regulated substances above thresholds)
- **Tiered Permitting** — hazardous waste tiered permitting under the CUPA umbrella

## Reporting System

Facilities report through **CERS — California Environmental Reporting System**: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/

## Local Hazmat Ordinance

No standalone City of Red Bluff hazmat ordinance has been identified online. The county CUPA program operates under state statute (Health & Safety Code § 25404 et seq.) and Tehama County Environmental Health regulations.
