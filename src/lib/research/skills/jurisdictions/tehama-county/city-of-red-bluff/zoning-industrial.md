## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

CUPs and administrative use permits are processed by the **Community Development Department / Planning Division**:

- **Use Permit (CUP):** Planning Commission decision (RBCC Ch. 25 — Zoning); fee: $2,826 (base, as of August 2012 fee schedule; fees may have increased — verify current fee schedule)
- **Use Permit, Administrative:** Planning Director decision; fee: $1,356 (as of August 2012)
- **Variance, Zoning (RBCC Ch. 25):** Planning Commission; fee: $486 (as of August 2012)
- **Rezone, Map/Text:** Planning Commission/City Council; fee: $3,165 (as of August 2012)
- **CEQA — MND (prepared in-house):** $1,696 (plus 13% admin if consultant-prepared)
- CUP fees reference: City fee schedule Exhibit G, adopted June 5, 2012, effective August 19, 2012 [VERIFY current fees — schedule may be outdated]

Appeals from Planning Commission go to City Council (fee: $113 as of 2012).
