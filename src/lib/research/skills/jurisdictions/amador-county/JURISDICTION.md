## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air — Amador County Air Pollution Control District (APCD)

- The **Amador County Air Pollution Control District (APCD)** is the sole local air district for the entire county (all unincorporated and incorporated areas). It is **not** part of any regional AQMD; it is a stand-alone district.
- Administers New Source Review (NSR) permitting, Title V operating permits, and attainment/nonattainment SIP programs. Stationary source permits (Authority to Construct / Permit to Operate) required from APCD for any equipment emitting regulated air pollutants.
- Office: 810 Court Street, Jackson, CA 95642. Phone: (209) 257-0112.
- EPA-approved SIP rules on file; NSR program approved by EPA (Federal Register 2023-09-25, Docket 2023-20669).
- Source: https://www.amadorcounty.gov/services/amador-air-district
- Source: https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/amador-county-air-pollution-control-district
- Source: https://www.epa.gov/air-quality-implementation-plans/epa-approved-amador-county-air-district-regulations-california-sip

### CUPA — Amador County Environmental Health Department

- **Amador County Environmental Health Department** is the sole CUPA for the entire county, including all five incorporated cities (Amador City, Ione, Jackson, Plymouth, Sutter Creek). Certified by CalEPA December 1997.
- Programs administered: Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generators, Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA), and CalARP.
- CERS (California Electronic Reporting System) required for all regulated businesses.
- Local hazmat ordinance: **Amador County Code Chapter 7.25** ("Hazardous Materials") — adopts Health & Safety Code § 25501 definitions; establishes local CUPA; creates Hazardous Materials Advisory Committee (representatives from fire chiefs association, OES, health officer, businesses, agriculture, public).
- Fees: **Chapter 7.42** (Environmental Health Department Fees) — adopted February 13, 2018 (Board of Supervisors); hourly rate = **$120/hr** for EH services; specific CUPA program fees in §§ 7.42.050–7.42.160 [VERIFY current amounts — 2020 schedule remains in effect until altered]; state surcharge added on top of county fees.
- Contact: ACEH@co.amador.ca.us | 810 Court Street, Jackson, CA 95642.
- Source: https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-cupa
- Source: https://ecode360.com/AM4323 (Chapter 7.25 and Chapter 7.42)

### Wastewater — No County-Level POTW; Cities Operate Independent Systems

- **Amador County does not operate a county POTW.** Each city operates its own municipal wastewater treatment plant (POTW) with its own NPDES permit from the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 5):
  - **City of Jackson WWTP** — NPDES permit renewed October 2007; discharges to Jackson Creek (subject to ongoing review given Lake Amador potable-water concerns). [VERIFY current permit number and industrial pretreatment program status.]
  - **City of Sutter Creek WWTP** — also receives wastewater from Martell (piped from Amador Water Agency service area). [VERIFY NPDES permit number.]
  - **City of Ione WWTP** — independent POTW. [VERIFY NPDES permit number.]
  - **Amador Water Agency** — operates small community wastewater systems (Fairway Pines, Tiger Creek Estates, Gayla Manor, Wildwood Estates, Surrey Junction, Jackson Pines, Pine Grove, Martell, Viewpoint Estates, Eagles Nest, Lake Camanche Village Unit 6); these are small systems unlikely to have formal industrial pretreatment programs.
- County Code **Chapter 14.12** covers on-site sewage disposal (septic); **Chapter 7.23** covers stormwater (not industrial wastewater discharge to POTW).
- Industrial dischargers to city sewers must get pretreatment/IWP approval from the relevant city (Jackson, Ione, or Sutter Creek) — not from the county. POTWs below 5 MGD are not required to have formal pretreatment programs under federal regulations (40 CFR § 403), though categorical standards still apply.

### Stormwater — Phase II MS4 (County) + NPDES Industrial GP

- Amador County unincorporated area is covered by the **Phase II (small) MS4** program; administered by the **County Director of Public Works**.
- County Code **Chapter 7.23** ("Storm Water") — prohibits illicit connections and discharges to the storm drainage system; requires compliance with any applicable industrial or construction NPDES stormwater permit; administered by Director of Public Works.
- Industrial facilities in Amador County subject to industrial stormwater general permit must obtain coverage under the **California Industrial General Permit (IGP)** from the State Water Board.

### Zoning — County Code Title 19

- **Title 19** is the Amador County Zoning Code; administered by the **Amador County Planning Department** (810 Court Street, Jackson, CA 95642).
- Industrial/manufacturing district: **"M" (Manufacturing District)** — single manufacturing zone (no M-1/M-2 split found in county code; [VERIFY whether sub-districts exist]).
  - All manufacturing uses must be conducted within a building; outside storage permitted only if clearly subordinate/incidental to in-building activity and not unreasonably offensive to surrounding owners or public.
  - Conditional uses: light manufacturing uses compatible with the area and consistent with the General Plan, subject to Use Permit from Planning Commission or Board of Supervisors.
  - Parking: see § 19.36.010 for permitted-use parking requirements; use permit process sets parking for conditional uses.
  - Also: commercial junkyards allowed in **C-2 (Heavy Commercial)**, **M (Manufacturing)**, and **X (Special Use)** districts with an approved use permit (Ch. 19.56).

## CUPA (Unified Program)

- Amador County Environmental Health Department — single CUPA for the **entire county**, including all five incorporated cities (Amador City, Ione, Jackson, Plymouth, Sutter Creek). Runs HMBP, hazardous waste generator, UST, APSA (aboveground petroleum storage), and CalARP programs. Certified by CalEPA as the county CUPA in December 1997. CERS reporting required for regulated businesses.
- Local ordinance: County Code Chapter 7.25; fees: Chapter 7.42 ($120/hr hourly rate; specific schedule in §§ 7.42.050–160).
- https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-cupa

## Fire authority (AHJ)

- CAL FIRE Amador-El Dorado Unit (AEU) — state-responsibility/wildland and contract structural fire protection for **unincorporated Amador County** (HQ at Camino, El Dorado County). https://www.fire.ca.gov/contact-us/cal-fire-units (Amador-El Dorado Unit)
- Amador Fire Protection District — local fire protection district (formed July 1990) serving unincorporated communities. Adopted 2022 CFC as Ordinance AFPD-011; 2024 CFC adoption in progress (Nov 2025 hearing). https://www.amadorfire.org/
- Several cities run their own departments/districts (see city files): City of Jackson Fire Department, City of Ione Fire Department, Sutter Creek Fire Protection District (covers Sutter Creek and Amador City).
