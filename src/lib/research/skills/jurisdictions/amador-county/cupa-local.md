## CUPA Agency

**Amador County Environmental Health Department (ACEH)**
- Single CUPA for the entire county — serves all five incorporated cities (Amador City, Ione, Jackson, Plymouth, Sutter Creek) in addition to unincorporated Amador County.
- Certified by CalEPA: **December 1997**.
- Office: 810 Court Street, Jackson, CA 95642
- Email: ACEH@co.amador.ca.us

Source: https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-cupa

## Programs Administered

ACEH administers all six Unified Program elements as the sole CUPA:

| Program | Description |
|---|---|
| HMBP | Hazardous Materials Business Plans |
| HWG | Hazardous Waste Generators |
| UST | Underground Storage Tanks |
| APSA | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (tanks ≥ 1,320 gal or aggregate ≥ 660 gal) |
| CalARP | California Accidental Release Prevention Program |
| CERS | California Electronic Reporting System — required for all regulated businesses |

- Agricultural businesses handling hazardous materials are included under HMBP jurisdiction.
- HMBP thresholds: ≥ 55 gallons liquid, ≥ 500 pounds solid, or ≥ 200 cubic feet compressed gas.

Source: https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-cupa/hazardous-materials-business-plans

## Local Hazmat Ordinance

**Amador County Code Chapter 7.25 — Hazardous Materials**

- Formally titled the "Amador County Hazardous Materials Ordinance."
- Adopts the definitions from California Health & Safety Code §§ 25501–25501.4.
- Designates the Environmental Health Department as the CUPA for both incorporated and unincorporated areas.
- Establishes a **Hazardous Materials Advisory Committee** appointed by the Board of Supervisors, composed of: fire chiefs association representative, Office of Emergency Services, health officer, large-business representative, small-business representative, agriculture representative, and general public representative.
- Full text: https://ecode360.com/AM4323 (navigate to Chapter 7.25)

## CERS Reporting

- All regulated businesses must submit HMBP, hazardous waste, UST, and APSA information through **CERS** (California Electronic Reporting System).
- CERS portal: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/
- Source: https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-cupa/cers

## Hazardous Waste Generator Program

- ACEH oversees generator compliance for small-quantity generators (SQGs) and large-quantity generators (LQGs) within the county.
- Source: https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-cupa/hazardous-waste-generators

## APSA (Aboveground Petroleum Storage)

- Administered by ACEH; businesses with aboveground petroleum storage tanks meeting APSA thresholds must file a Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure (SPCC) plan and register with CERS.
- Source: https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-cupa/aboveground-petroleum-storage-tank-program

## Area Plan

- ACEH maintains a Hazardous Materials Area Plan for emergency response.
- Source: https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-cupa/area-plan

## Notes

- No city within Amador County operates its own CUPA — all five incorporated cities are served by the county CUPA.
- Agricultural hazardous materials handlers (pesticide users, fuel storage, etc.) are covered under HMBP.
- Source: https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-cupa/agricultural-hazardous-materials-handlers
