## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air

- **District:** Amador County Air Pollution Control District (APCD) — covers all of Amador County including all incorporated cities.
- Permit programs: Authority to Construct (ATC) + Permit to Operate (PTO) required for any article, machine, or equipment that may emit air contaminants (Regulation III, Rules 1–3).
- Exemptions include: vehicles, comfort A/C, equipment under 250 MMBtu/hr fired solely on natural gas/LPG, residential equipment (≤4 families), brazing/welding, lab bench-scale equipment.
- Contact: 810 Court Street, Jackson CA 95642 | (209) 257-0112 | http://www.amadorgov.org/services/amador-air-district
- Burn day info line: (209) 223-6246
- Source: https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/amador-county-air-pollution-control-district; https://www.epa.gov/air-quality-implementation-plans/epa-approved-amador-county-air-district-regulations-california-sip

### CUPA (Unified Hazardous Materials Program)

- **Not its own CUPA** — the City of Ione is served by the **Amador County Environmental Health Department** (county-wide CUPA covering all incorporated cities: Amador City, Ione, Jackson, Plymouth, Sutter Creek).
- Programs administered: Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generator Program, Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program, Aboveground Petroleum Storage Tank (APST) Program, California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP).
- Reporting thresholds: ≥55 gal liquid / ≥500 lbs solid / ≥200 ft³ compressed gas → electronic report via CERS (cers.calepa.ca.gov).
- Fee authority: Amador County Code Chapter 7.42 (Environmental Health Department Fees); hourly rate = $120/hr; state CUPA surcharge collected in addition.
- Local hazmat ordinance: Amador County Code **Chapter 7.25** (Hazardous Materials).
- CUPA contact: Amador County Environmental Health, 810 Court Street, Jackson CA 95642.
- Source: https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-cupa; https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-cupa/hazardous-materials-business-plans

### Fire

- **AHJ:** City of Ione Fire Department — municipal combination department (est. 1952), all-risk response. 22 W Jackson St., Ione CA.
- Governing code chapter: **Title 15, Chapter 15.22** of the Ione Municipal Code — "California Fire Code."
- Code codified through **Ordinance No. 466** (adopted December 18, 2018); confirm current CFC edition adopted (2022 CFC was in effect statewide through 2025; 2025 CFC effective 2026-01-01 — local adoption update may be needed [VERIFY]).
- Burn permit required for burning vegetation within city limits.
- Fire Marshal oversees all fire prevention activities including plan review for fire, life safety, and environmental requirements.
- Source: https://library.municode.com/ca/ione/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT15BUCO_CH15.22CAFICO; https://www.ione.ca.gov/fire-department

### Wastewater

- **POTW:** City of Ione Wastewater Treatment Facility (WWTF), 1600 West Marlette Street, Ione.
- Treatment: Lagoon/pond system (7 ponds, ~28 acres); Ponds 1–4 secondary treatment (aeration + settling); Ponds 5–7 percolation/evaporation disposal. No NPDES discharge to surface water; land application only.
- Regulatory authority: **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 5)**; current WDRs: **Order R5-2013-0022** (adopted 11 April 2013). Renewal pending [VERIFY].
- Design capacity: up to 1.2 MGD ADWF (per prior Order 95-125); interim limits under 2011 CDO set at 0.55 MGD ADWF influent.
- Industrial pretreatment: Facility primarily serves domestic wastewater (~1,525 EDUs as of 2013); no formal pretreatment program published online — contact city Public Works for industrial discharge inquiry.
- Sewer service ordinance: Ione Municipal Code **Title 13** (Public Services), Article II (Sewer Service Charges and Rates), Chapter 13.52+; Chapter 13.56 contains sewer construction and use / industrial wastewater discharge provisions [VERIFY chapter number].
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/centralvalley/board_decisions/adopted_orders/amador/r5-2013-0022.pdf; https://www.ione.ca.gov/wastewater

### Stormwater

- City is within the jurisdiction of the **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board** for stormwater.
- Construction projects ≥1 acre must obtain a Construction General Permit (CGP) via SMARTS.
- Industrial facilities subject to Industrial General Permit (IGP) — statewide permit; no separate local industrial stormwater permit found [VERIFY].

## Notes

- Ione runs its **own municipal fire department** — AHJ is the city, not the county.
- Air permitting flows through **Amador County APCD**, not BAAQMD or SMAQMD.
- CUPA/hazmat oversight is entirely at the **county level** (Amador County Environmental Health).
- The city's WWTF uses **land-application / percolation-pond** disposal — no surface water NPDES discharge permit as of 2013 Order; verify current status.
- US Mine Corporation (kaolin clay) operates a major industrial facility near Ione under separate WDR Order R5-2025-0042 (adopted June 2025), regulated by Central Valley Water Board.
