## CUPA Agency

The City of Ione is **not its own CUPA**. All Unified Program elements are administered by:

**Amador County Environmental Health Department**
810 Court Street, Jackson, CA 95642
Phone: (209) 223-6465 (Environmental Health main)
Web: https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-cupa

The county CUPA covers all five incorporated cities in Amador County: Amador City, Ione, Jackson, Plymouth, and Sutter Creek.

## Reporting Thresholds (HMBP)

Businesses must electronically report via **CERS** (cers.calepa.ca.gov) if they handle on-site at any one time:
- ≥ 55 gallons of a liquid hazardous material
- ≥ 500 pounds of a solid hazardous material
- ≥ 200 cubic feet of a compressed gaseous hazardous material

Agricultural operations with reportable quantities are also covered.

Source: https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-cupa/hazardous-materials-business-plans

## Fee Schedule

- **Hourly rate for Environmental Health Department services: $120.00/hr** (established by Board of Supervisors in Chapter 7.42).
- Fees for permits and regulatory functions associated with hazardous materials are assessed annually (Sections 7.42.050–7.42.160).
- Unusual circumstances requiring additional staff time above budgeted hours billed at $120/hr in half-hour increments.
- For all facilities under one or more CUPA oversight program elements, the applicable **state CUPA surcharge** is collected in addition to local fees.
- Specific per-program fee amounts (UST, HMBP, APST, etc.) are set in Sections 7.42.050–7.42.160 of the county code — current dollar amounts [VERIFY at https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/fee-schedule].

Source: https://ecode360.com/46588297 (Amador County Chapter 7.42); https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/fee-schedule

## UST Program

- Amador County Environmental Health is the **Local Implementing Agency (LIA)** for USTs in Amador County (including within Ione city limits).
- Compliance achieved through inspections, permits, monitoring, and oversight of UST installation/removal per California HSC and CCR.
- Source: https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-cupa/underground-storage-tank-program

## Aboveground Petroleum Storage

- Facilities with aboveground petroleum storage ≥ 1,320 gallons aggregate (or any single tank > 660 gal) must comply with APST Act and register via CERS.
- Source: https://www.amadorgov.org/departments/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-cupa/aboveground-petroleum-storage-tank-program
