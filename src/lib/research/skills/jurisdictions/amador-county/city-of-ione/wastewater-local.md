## POTW

**City of Ione Wastewater Treatment Facility (WWTF)**
1600 West Marlette Street, Ione, CA 95640 (corner of West Marlette Street and Dave Brubeck Road)
Contact: City of Ione Public Works, (209) 274-2412
Web: https://www.ione.ca.gov/wastewater

## Treatment System

- **Type:** Pond/lagoon system — 7 ponds covering approximately 28 acres.
- Ponds 1–4: Secondary treatment via aeration and settling (no engineered liners).
- Ponds 5–7: Disposal via percolation and evaporation (un-disinfected effluent).
- Adjacent to Sutter Creek; closest pond approximately 100 feet from creek.
- **No surface-water NPDES discharge** — effluent disposed by land application and percolation only.
- Additional land application areas (LAAs): WWTF Field (11 acres), Town Field (67 acres), Greenrock Ranch LAA (operated by Greenrock Ranch Lands, LLC).

## Industrial Pretreatment

- The WWTF primarily serves **domestic wastewater** (~1,525–1,900 EDUs); no significant industrial base reported in permit.
- The facility does accept: AWA filter backwash, COWRP tertiary filter backwash, and ARSA secondary effluent.
- **No formal pretreatment program published online.** For industrial discharge inquiries, contact City of Ione Public Works at (209) 274-2412.
- Industries generating non-domestic wastewater should contact the city before connecting — categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR Part 403 and applicable categorical standards) may apply even without a local pretreatment program if indirect discharge is significant.

## Related Facilities
