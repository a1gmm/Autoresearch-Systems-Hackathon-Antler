## Industrial District Names

Specific industrial zone designations (M-1, M-2, or equivalent) for the City of Ione are housed in Title 17, Article II. Based on search-visible metadata, industrial districts exist in Ione consistent with the city's General Plan land uses accommodating:

- Clay processing / kaolin mining (US Mine Corporation — Highway 24, outside city limits in unincorporated Amador County but near Ione)
- Light manufacturing
- Storage and warehousing
- General industrial uses
