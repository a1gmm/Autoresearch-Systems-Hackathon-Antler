## Industrial / Manufacturing District

Amador County uses a single **"M" (Manufacturing District)** designation for industrial land uses in the unincorporated county. No sub-districts (M-1 light / M-2 heavy) were found in the available code text — [VERIFY whether the adopted code has sub-districts by reviewing Chapter 19.12 (Designation of Districts) directly on eCode360].

### Permitted Uses in the M (Manufacturing) District

- All manufacturing uses — **must be conducted within a building**.
- Outside storage of material related to the manufacturing use is permitted only if:
  - Clearly subordinate and incidental to the in-building activity, AND
  - Conducted in a manner found by the county not to be unreasonably offensive to surrounding property owners or to the general public.

### Conditional Uses in the M (Manufacturing) District (Use Permit Required)

- Light manufacturing uses that, in the opinion of the County Planning Commission or Board of Supervisors, would be appropriate, compatible with the area, and consistent with the **Amador County General Plan**, subject to such conditions and environmental impact restrictions as the county deems necessary.
- **Commercial junkyards** — allowed in M district with an approved use permit (also allowed in C-2 Heavy Commercial and X Special Use districts with use permit). [Ch. 19.48 / 19.56]

### Parking Requirements

- Required parking for permitted uses: see **Section 19.36.010**.
- Parking requirements for conditional uses requiring a use permit: determined in the use permit process.

## Use Permit (CUP) Process

- **Chapter 19.56** governs use permits (conditional use permits) for uses not allowed by right.
- Decision-maker: County Planning Commission or Board of Supervisors (depending on use type and scope).
- Environmental review (CEQA) required for discretionary approvals.
