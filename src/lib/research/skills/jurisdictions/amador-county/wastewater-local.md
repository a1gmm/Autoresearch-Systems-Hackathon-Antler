## Key Structural Fact

**Amador County does not operate a county-level POTW.** There is no county sewer use ordinance covering industrial wastewater pretreatment for POTW discharge. Each incorporated city operates its own municipal wastewater treatment plant (POTW) with its own NPDES permit, its own sewer use ordinance, and its own (or absent) industrial pretreatment program.

Industrial dischargers who want to connect to a city sewer must work directly with that city — not with the county.

## Amador Water Agency — Small Community Wastewater Systems

**Amador Water Agency (AWA)** owns and operates several small community wastewater systems:

Fairway Pines, Tiger Creek Estates, Gayla Manor, Wildwood Estates, Surrey Junction, Jackson Pines, Pine Grove, Martell, Viewpoint Estates, Eagles Nest, Lake Camanche Village Unit 6.

- Martell wastewater is piped to the City of Sutter Creek WWTP for treatment.
- These are small community systems; industrial pretreatment programs are unlikely given small flows (federal pretreatment programs required only for POTWs ≥ 5 MGD receiving industrial pollutants that could interfere — 40 CFR § 403).
- Source: https://amadorwater.gov/about/

## County Code — On-Site Sewage Disposal (Septic)

**Amador County Code Chapter 14.12 — On-Site Sewage Disposal Systems**

- Governs individual and community septic/on-site systems for the unincorporated area.
- Community sewer systems may serve residential or non-residential structures but shall receive only domestic wastewater or high-strength wastewater from commercial food service buildings that does not exceed **900 mg/L BOD** and has a properly sized and functioning oil/grease interceptor.
- Administered by: Amador County Environmental Health Department.
- Source: https://ecode360.com/AM4323 (navigate to Chapter 14.12)

## County Code — Stormwater (not POTW discharge)

**Amador County Code Chapter 7.23 — Storm Water**

- Covers stormwater discharges to the county storm drainage system and waters of the US within the Phase II (small) MS4 permit area (unincorporated county).
- Administered by: County Director of Public Works.
- Prohibits illicit connections and unauthorized discharges.
- Requires compliance with any applicable NPDES industrial or construction stormwater permit.
- This chapter does **not** regulate discharge to a POTW.
- Full text: https://ecode360.com/AM4323 (navigate to Chapter 7.23)

Source: https://ecode360.com/AM4323

## Industrial Wastewater Discharge — Practical Guidance

For an industrial facility in unincorporated Amador County that wants to discharge process wastewater to a city sewer:
1. Identify which city's sewer system serves the parcel (or whether county on-site disposal is required).
2. Contact that city's Public Works/Utilities department for connection permit and any local pretreatment requirements.
3. Federal categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR Part 400 series) apply regardless of whether the receiving POTW has a formal pretreatment program.
4. For on-site disposal (no city sewer available), county Chapter 14.12 and Environmental Health Department govern.
