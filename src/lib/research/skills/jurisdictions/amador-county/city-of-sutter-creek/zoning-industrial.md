> The City of Sutter Creek's zoning ordinance is codified as Title 18 of the Municipal Code. Two industrial zone designations exist: I-1 (Light Industrial) and I-2 (Heavy Industrial). Ordinance No. 373 is the most recently confirmed major amendment to Title 18.
