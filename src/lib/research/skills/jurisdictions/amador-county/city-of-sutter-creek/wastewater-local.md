> The City of Sutter Creek owns and operates its own Publicly Owned Treatment Works (POTW). The facility discharges treated effluent to the Amador Regional Sanitation Authority (ARSA) outfall for land application — there is no direct surface water discharge. Industrial dischargers to the city sewer system are subject to the city's sewer use ordinance (Title 14 of the Municipal Code) and the Central Valley RWQCB permit governing the POTW.

## POTW Identity

- **Owner/Operator:** City of Sutter Creek Public Works Department
- **Facility:** Sutter Creek Wastewater Treatment Plant
- **Location:** Section 7, T6N, R11E, MDB&M, Sutter Creek, CA (adjacent to surface drainage to Sutter Creek)
- **Technology:** High-rate trickling filter plant; processes include automatic bar screen, primary sedimentation (rota-strainers), trickling filter, secondary clarification in two combination clarifier/anaerobic digesters, sodium hypochlorite disinfection
- **Design capacity:** 0.48 million gallons per day (MGD)
- **Actual dry-weather flow:** ~0.35 MGD (as of 1994 WDR; verify current)
- **Collection system:** ~114,750 feet of sewer piping (city-maintained)
- **Service area:** City of Sutter Creek; also receives wastewater from Amador City (up to 39,000 gpd under contract) and the Martell area (equalization basin operated by Amador County Water Resources)
- **WWTP upgrades:** New WWTP project in planning/construction; current trickling filter plant is pre-1949 vintage. Upgraded facility expected to require nutrient limits in revised WDR/NPDES permit. [VERIFY status]
- **Public Works contact:** City of Sutter Creek Public Works, 18 Main Street, Sutter Creek, CA 95685 | (209) 267-5647
- **Public Works page:** https://www.cityofsuttercreek.org/public-works

Source: RWQCB Central Valley Order No. 94-152 (PDF); Aquality Water Management project page (https://aqualitywm.com/project/sutter-creek/)

## POTW Effluent Limits (Order No. 94-152)

Effluent to the ARSA outfall shall not exceed (monthly average / daily maximum):

| Constituent | Units | Monthly Average | Daily Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|
| BOD (5-day, 20°C) | mg/L | 30 | 60 |
| Suspended Solids | mg/L | 30 | 60 |
| Settleable Matter | mL/L | 0.1 | 0.5 |
| Total Coliform | MPN/100 mL | 23 | 500 |

- Monthly average dry-weather flow limit: **0.48 MGD** (shall not exceed)
- pH: not explicitly stated in the above limits section (verify if pH limit appears in monitoring program)
- Discharge of wastes classified as "hazardous" or "designated" under Chapter 15 (Title 23, CCR) is prohibited
- No bypass or overflow of untreated/partially treated waste permitted

Source: RWQCB5 Order No. 94-152, Discharge Specifications Section B (https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/rwqcb5/board_decisions/adopted_orders/amador/94-152.pdf)

## Industrial Pretreatment Program

- **Pretreatment program status:** Not confirmed. The POTW design capacity (0.48 MGD) and predominantly domestic character suggest this is a **small POTW** that may not be required to operate a formal EPA pretreatment program (40 CFR Part 403 requires programs for POTWs with design flow ≥5 MGD, or smaller POTWs with SIUs that pass through or interfere).
- The 1994 WDR (Order No. 94-152) does not reference a formal pretreatment program requirement.
- The Amador City contract with Sutter Creek explicitly prohibits discharge of industrial waste to the Amador City equalization basin (i.e., industrial waste cannot be trucked in).
- **For any industrial discharge to the Sutter Creek sewer system:** Contact the City of Sutter Creek Public Works at (209) 267-5647 to determine if an industrial waste discharge permit or approval is required under Title 14.

Source: RWQCB5 Order No. R5-2002-0224 (Amador City WWTF; references Sutter Creek receiving plant and industrial waste prohibition); Order No. 94-152

## Discharge Prohibitions (from WDR Order No. 94-152)

- Discharge of wastes to surface waters or surface water drainage courses: **prohibited**
- Bypass or overflow of untreated or partially treated waste: **prohibited**
- Discharge of waste classified as "hazardous" or "designated" (22 CCR §2521(a), §2522(a)): **prohibited**
