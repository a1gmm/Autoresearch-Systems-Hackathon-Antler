> Amador County Environmental Health Department is the CUPA for all of Amador County, including the City of Sutter Creek. The City of Sutter Creek is NOT its own CUPA and does not act as a Participating Agency with independent authority — all six unified programs are administered directly by the county.

## CUPA Identity

- **CUPA:** Amador County Environmental Health Department
- **Address:** 810 Court Street, Jackson, CA 95642
- **Phone:** (209) 223-6439
- **CUPA program page:** https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-cupa
- **Fee schedule:** https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/fee-schedule
- **Coverage:** All of Amador County — unincorporated areas plus all five cities (Amador City, Ione, Jackson, Plymouth, Sutter Creek)

## Programs Administered

Amador County EH implements all six CalEPA unified programs:

| # | Program | Acronym | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan | HMBP | Filed electronically via CERS; county EH reviews and inspects |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator Program | HWG | Generator registration, manifest compliance, inspections |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tanks | UST | Installation, modification, closure, leak monitoring |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act | APSA | Spill prevention for above-threshold petroleum storage |
| 5 | California Accidental Release Prevention | CalARP | County EH is the Local Implementing Agency (LIA) |
| 6 | Agricultural Hazardous Materials | Ag HazMat | Agricultural businesses subject to HMBP requirements |

Source: https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-cupa

## HMBP Program Details

- **Submission system:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov
- **Threshold quantities for HMBP filing:** ≥55 gallons liquid, ≥500 pounds solid, ≥200 cubic feet compressed gas
- Agricultural hazardous materials handlers are subject to HMBP requirements in Amador County
- CERS page for Amador County HMBP: https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-cupa/hazardous-materials-business-plans
- Area Plan (local emergency response coordination): https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-cupa/area-plan

## Hazardous Waste Generator Program

- Generator compliance details: https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-cupa/hazardous-waste-generators
- Generators must file through CERS; Amador County EH conducts inspections

## UST Program

- UST permits (installation, modification, closure, monitoring) administered by Amador County EH
- [VERIFY] Current UST inspection fee schedule via https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/fee-schedule

## APSA / Aboveground Petroleum Storage

- Facilities storing petroleum at or above APSA thresholds (≥1,320 gallons aggregate aboveground, or any single tank >660 gallons) must maintain SPCC plans and file through CERS
- Amador County EH inspects for APSA compliance

## CalARP

- Amador County EH serves as the Local Implementing Agency (LIA) for CalARP within the county
- Facilities with regulated substances above threshold quantities must submit Risk Management Plans (RMPs) to USEPA and coordinate with county EH for local review
