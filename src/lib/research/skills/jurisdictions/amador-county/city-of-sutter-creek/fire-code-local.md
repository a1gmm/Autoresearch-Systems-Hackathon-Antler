> The Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) for fire code in the City of Sutter Creek is the **Sutter Creek Fire Protection District** — an independent special district, not a city fire department. Fire code adoption ordinances are issued by the District's Board of Commissioners, not the City Council. The City's municipal code Title 15 (Building Code) may also contain fire-related provisions.

## Authority Having Jurisdiction

- **AHJ:** Sutter Creek Fire Protection District
- **Type:** Special district (California Health & Safety Code, Fire Protection District Law of 1987)
- **Service area:** Cities of Sutter Creek and Amador City, and surrounding unincorporated Amador County areas

## Fire Operational Permits

Under the California Fire Code, operational permits are required for facilities storing or using hazardous materials above CFC Chapter 1 threshold quantities. Applicable permit categories for industrial facilities include:

- Hazardous materials storage (CFC Chapter 50)
- Compressed gas storage (CFC Chapter 53)
- Flammable/combustible liquids (CFC Chapter 57)
- High-piled combustible storage (CFC Chapter 32)
- Other operational permits per CFC Section 105

**How to apply:** Contact the Sutter Creek Fire Protection District at (209) 267-0285 to request operational permit applications and confirm required documentation.

**Note on CUPA overlap:** Amador County EH (not the Fire District) is the CUPA. The Fire District does not administer HMBP/CERS programs — those go to the county. However, the Fire District may coordinate with or receive copies of HMBP filings for emergency planning purposes.
