## Wastewater / POTW

- **City of Sutter Creek** operates its own POTW — a trickling filter plant (constructed 1949, upgraded; design capacity 0.48 MGD, actual dry-weather flow ~0.35 MGD).
- Effluent is conveyed to the **Amador Regional Sanitation Authority (ARSA)** outfall for land application (reclaimed water to Castle Oaks Golf Course, Preston School of Industry, and agriculture); no direct surface discharge.
- Regulated under Central Valley RWQCB; most recent governing order: **Order No. 94-152** (WDR; older order — a replacement or NPDES update may be current [VERIFY with RWQCB5]).
- Wastewater from Amador City (and Martell) is piped to Sutter Creek's plant for treatment.
- Sewer code: Title 14 Water and Sewage, Sutter Creek Municipal Code — https://library.municode.com/ca/sutter_creek/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT14WASE
- Public Works contact: City of Sutter Creek Public Works, (209) 267-5647
- Source: RWQCB5 Order No. 94-152 (PDF); https://www.cityofsuttercreek.org/public-works

## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air (Amador County APCD)

- **Controlling district:** Amador County Air Pollution Control District (APCD) — all stationary source Authority to Construct (ATC) and Permit to Operate (PTO) applications filed with Amador APCD.
- Rule 400 (effective Jan 17, 2023; EPA approved Feb 6, 2024): NSR requirements for new and modified major sources in nonattainment areas.
- Rule 402: Exemptions from Authority to Construct requirement.
- Rules 501–521: Permit to Operate system including annual renewal.
- Rule 500: Title V operating permits for major sources.
- Amador County APCD is generally an attainment area for most pollutants; confirm current nonattainment designations with APCD.
- Source: https://www.epa.gov/air-quality-implementation-plans/epa-approved-amador-county-air-district-regulations-california-sip

### CUPA (Amador County Environmental Health — HMBP, HWG, UST, APSA, CalARP)

- Amador County EH is the CUPA for the City of Sutter Creek — NOT a city-run program.

- CUPA programs cover: HMBP, hazardous waste generators, UST, APSA (aboveground petroleum storage), CalARP.
