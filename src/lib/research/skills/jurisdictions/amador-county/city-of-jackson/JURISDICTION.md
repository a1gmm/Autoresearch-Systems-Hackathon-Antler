## EHS Local Rules by Family

### Air

- **Amador County Air Pollution Control District (APCD)** is the AHJ for all stationary-source air permits in Jackson. The city has no independent air program.
- Permit-to-Operate (PTO) and Authority to Construct (ATC) applications go directly to Amador County APCD.
- Source: https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/air-pollution-control

### CUPA (Hazardous Materials Unified Program)

- **Amador County Environmental Health Department** is the county-wide CUPA — Jackson is NOT its own CUPA.
- Address: 810 Court Street, Jackson, CA 95642 | Phone: (209) 223-6439 | Hours: M–F 8:00 AM–5:00 PM
- Programs administered under the CUPA umbrella:
  - Hazardous Materials Business Plans (HMBP) — electronic submission via CERS required for ≥55 gal liquid / ≥500 lb solid / ≥200 cu ft compressed gas
  - Hazardous Waste Generator oversight (onsite storage, treatment)
  - Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) program
  - Underground Storage Tank (UST) program — LIA for permitting, inspection, installation/removal oversight
  - California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) [VERIFY — confirm whether Amador County administers CalARP directly or defers to CALEPA]
- Reporting portal: California Electronic Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov
- Local hazardous materials ordinance: Amador County Code Chapter 7.25 (Hazardous Materials)
- Source: https://www.amadorcounty.gov/departments/environmental-health/hazardous-materials-cupa

### Fire

- **City of Jackson Fire Department** is the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) within city limits — municipal combination department, career + volunteer.
  - Station #131: 175 Main Street, Jackson | (209) 223-2147
  - Station #132: 10600 Argonaut Drive, Jackson | (209) 223-9039
  - Fire Chief: Ryan Pidgeon — rpidgeon@ci.jackson.ca.us
  - Fire Inspections: fire-inspection@ci.jackson.ca.us
- Governing codes: **2022 California Fire Code** adopted by City of Jackson Municipal Code Chapter 14.04 (Uniform Codes), via **Ordinance No. 727** (2022).
- Local amendments to the 2022 CFC are codified in JACKSON MC § 14.04.030.
- Note: The **Amador Fire Protection District (AFPD)** — a separate agency — covers unincorporated Amador County areas; AFPD adopted the 2022 CFC via Ordinance No. AFPD-011. As of September 2025, AFPD was evaluating repeal of AFPD-011 and adoption of the 2024 CFC. This does NOT directly affect City of Jackson (its own AHJ).
- Inspection fee: $150/hour, 1-hour minimum.
- Burn permits: online application required; Burn Day Line (209) 223-6246.
- Source: https://www.ci.jackson.ca.us/fire_department.php
- Source (Ordinance 727): https://ecode360.com/44230696

### Wastewater (Municipal/Industrial)

- **POTW:** City of Jackson Wastewater Treatment Plant — 39 North Highway 49-88, Jackson, CA 95642
- Provides tertiary treatment; design capacity 0.71 MGD (initial operational limit 0.43 MGD under WDR).
- **NPDES Permit:** CA0079391 | WDR Order R5-2018-0036 (Central Valley Water Board, adopted 31 May 2018; expired 31 July 2023 — reissuance [VERIFY]).
- Receiving water: **Jackson Creek** (discharges to Amador Lake downstream).
- Disinfection: UV (replaced chlorine in January 2019).
- Sewer chapter: City of Jackson MC Title 13 (Public Utilities), Article II — Sewers (eCode360 ref: 44230350).
  - Prohibits discharge of hazardous waste (CCR Title 22 §66261.1), bypass to surface waters.
  - Pretreatment/equalization facilities require City Engineer review and approval.
  - Grease/oil/sand interceptors required where necessary for proper handling of liquid wastes.
  - Ordinance history includes Ord. Nos. 198, 210, 236, 260, 304, 334, 371, 374, 379, 388, 411, 421, 436, 462, 485, 490, 495.
- Industrial Wastewater Permit (IWP): contact Public Works / Water & Sewer — water@ci.jackson.ca.us | After-hours emergency: (209) 223-1646.
- Sewer System Management Plan (SSMP): maintained and updated by Public Works; public copy at City Clerk's office.
- Source: https://www.ci.jackson.ca.us/public_works_wastewater_department/wastewater.php

### Stormwater

- City of Jackson is a small municipality (~4,600 pop.) in Amador County. **Phase II Small MS4 permit status [VERIFY]** — contact Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (Bryan Smith, Bryan.Smith@waterboards.ca.gov, (530) 226-3425) to confirm current NPDES MS4 permit number and conditions.
- City is developing a city-wide **Stormwater Master Plan** to address current and future stormwater infrastructure impacts.

### Zoning (Industrial)

- Zoning is administered by the City of Jackson Planning Department — contact: planning@ci.jackson.ca.us | Planner: Erin Ventura (contract planner).
- Municipal code: available at https://ecode360.com/JA4944 (also at qcode.us/codes/jackson redirecting to eCode360).
- Industrial zoning chapter: **Title 17, Chapter 17.12** (Commercial, Office, and Industrial Zoning Districts).
- Single industrial district designation: **I (Industrial)** — the most intense land use tier in the city's commercial/industrial hierarchy (PO → LC → HC → C → I).
- Uses allowed in I zone include manufacturing; coffee roasting and similar light industrial uses are permitted by right in I. Residential uses in I zone require a **Conditional Use Permit (CUP)** per § 17.76.
- Performance standards (§ 17.12): all uses in commercial/industrial districts must avoid impacts on public health, safety, or welfare — covers air emissions, glare/heat, and ground vibration.
- Minimum lot size in I zone: 8,000 sq ft.
- 2023 Land Use Zoning Map: available from Planning Department.
- General Plan (Adopted 10/23/2023): governs long-range land use policy.
- Source: https://ecode360.com/44230999
- Source: https://www.ci.jackson.ca.us/planning_department.php
