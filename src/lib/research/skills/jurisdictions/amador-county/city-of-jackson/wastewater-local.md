## POTW

**City of Jackson Wastewater Treatment Plant**
- Address: 39 North Highway 49-88, Jackson, CA 95642
- Treatment level: **Tertiary**
- Design capacity: **0.71 MGD** (initial operational limit 0.43 MGD pending compliance with WDR Special Provision VI.C.6.b)
- Population served: ~4,600
- Disinfection: **UV light** (switched from chlorine January 15, 2019)
- Discharge point: **Jackson Creek** (tributary ultimately reaches Amador Lake)
- Contact: water@ci.jackson.ca.us | After-hours emergency: (209) 223-1646
- Online bill pay: https://online.puwc.org/

Discharge prohibitions (per Order R5-2018-0036):
- Hazardous waste (CCR Title 22 §66261.1): **prohibited**
- Bypass/overflow to surface waters: **prohibited** except as allowed by Federal Standard Provisions I.G and I.H
- Discharge creating a nuisance (Water Code §13050): **prohibited**
- Pollutant-free wastewater in significant amounts that diminish system capacity: **prohibited**
- Discharge exceeding 5% volume of wastewater in Amador Lake (20:1 dilution): **prohibited**
- Exceeding average dry weather flow of 0.43 MGD (until compliance with SP VI.C.6.b): **prohibited**

## Sewer Use Ordinance — Municipal Code

- **Title:** City of Jackson Municipal Code, Title 13 (Public Utilities), Article II — Sewers
- **eCode360 reference:** https://ecode360.com/44230350
- Key provisions (extracted from search results referencing ordinance text):
  - Discharge of industrial wastes to sanitary sewer without suitable pretreatment: **unlawful**
  - Pretreatment/equalization facilities: design and installation subject to City Engineer review and approval, and all applicable codes and ordinances
  - Grease, oil, and sand interceptors: required when necessary for proper handling of liquid wastes containing grease in excessive amounts, flammable wastes, or sand; type and capacity approved by the city; location must allow easy access for cleaning and inspection
  - City may require payment to cover added cost of handling and treating industrial wastes with deleterious effects on sewage works
- Ordinance history: Ord. Nos. 198, 210, 236, 260, 304, 334, 371, 374, 379, 388, 411, 421, 436, 462, 485, 490, 495 (successive amendments)
