## Industrial District

City of Jackson uses a **single industrial designation** (no M-1/M-2 split):

| Designation | Name | Description |
|-------------|------|-------------|
| **I** | Industrial | Most intense land use tier in the commercial/industrial hierarchy |

District hierarchy from least to most intense: **PO** (Professional Office) → **LC** (Limited Commercial) → **HC** (High-traffic Commercial) → **C** (Commercial) → **I** (Industrial)

## Development Standards (Chapter 17.12)

- **Minimum lot size (I zone):** 8,000 sq ft
- Performance standards apply to all uses in commercial/industrial districts — must not be injurious to public health, safety, or welfare; specific standards cover:
  - Air emissions
  - Glare and heat
  - Ground vibration

## Permitted and Conditional Uses

Uses in each zoning district are governed by **Table 2-4** in Chapter 17.12.

Examples confirmed from code/search:
- **Manufacturing (general):** Permitted in I zone
- **Coffee roasting (as main use):** Permitted by right in I zone only
- **Residential uses in I zone:** Require a **Conditional Use Permit (CUP)** per § 17.76
- Uses not listed in tables or not shown in a particular district: **not allowed** unless a CUP is obtained per § 17.76

[VERIFY] — Full Table 2-4 listing all permitted (P) and conditional (C/CUP) uses requires direct access to eCode360 at https://ecode360.com/44230999 or contact with Planning Department.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

- **Trigger:** Required for uses listed as conditional in Table 2-4, for residential uses in I zone, and for uses not enumerated in the applicable district table (§ 17.76)
- **Application:** "Application for Conditional Use Permit" form available from Planning Department (planning@ci.jackson.ca.us)
- [VERIFY] — CUP procedural timelines, noticing requirements, and fee schedule not found on public pages; contact Planning Department directly
