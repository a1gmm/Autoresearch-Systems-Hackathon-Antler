## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

**City of Jackson Fire Department** — municipal combination department (career + volunteer) serving city limits and portions of unincorporated Amador County.

- **Station #131 (Main):** 175 Main Street, Jackson, CA 95642 | (209) 223-2147
- **Station #132:** 10600 Argonaut Drive, Jackson, CA | (209) 223-9039
- **Fire Chief:** Ryan Pidgeon — rpidgeon@ci.jackson.ca.us
- **Fire Inspections:** fire-inspection@ci.jackson.ca.us

## Adopted Fire Code Edition

- **2022 California Fire Code** (Part 9, Title 24 of California Code of Regulations, incorporating and amending the 2021 International Fire Code)
- **Adopting Ordinance:** Ordinance No. 727 (2022)
- **Municipal Code Chapter:** Title 14, Chapter 14.04 (UNIFORM CODES), Section 14.04.010 (Adoption of Codes)

## Permit Fees

- Inspection fee: **$150 per hour**, 1-hour minimum (all inspection types)
- Source: https://www.ci.jackson.ca.us/fire_department.php

## Inspection Program

- Target: all businesses inspected **semi-annually**; target hazard occupancies inspected **annually**
- Scope: fire/life safety plan review; defensible space (AB-38) inspections; business, residential, and commercial inspections
- Schedule: contact fire-inspection@ci.jackson.ca.us

## Open Burning Permits

- Open burning permitted seasonally within Jackson city limits (e.g., effective 17 October 2025 for the 2025 season — dates vary annually)
- **Burn permit:** online application required through city website
- **Burn Day Line:** (209) 223-6246 — call day-of to confirm burn day status

## Note on Amador Fire Protection District (AFPD)

AFPD is a separate agency covering unincorporated Amador County — it is NOT the AHJ for City of Jackson. AFPD adopted 2022 CFC via Ordinance No. AFPD-011; as of September 2025 AFPD was considering adoption of the 2024 CFC (repealing AFPD-011). This does not affect City of Jackson fire code jurisdiction.
