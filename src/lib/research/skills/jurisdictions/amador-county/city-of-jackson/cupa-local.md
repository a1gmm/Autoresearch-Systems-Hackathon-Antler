## CUPA Agency

City of Jackson is served by the **Amador County Environmental Health Department**, which is the county-wide CUPA for all of Amador County (incorporated and unincorporated areas). Jackson does NOT operate its own CUPA.

## Local Hazardous Materials Ordinance

- **Amador County Code Chapter 7.25 — Hazardous Materials** codifies the county's local hazardous materials program.
- Source: https://www.codepublishing.com/CA/AmadorCounty/html/AmadorCounty07/AmadorCounty0725.html

## Programs Administered

| Program | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** | Required for businesses storing ≥55 gal liquid, ≥500 lb solid, or ≥200 cu ft compressed gas of hazardous materials/waste. Electronic submission via CERS. |
| **Hazardous Waste Generator** | Oversight of onsite generation, storage, treatment, and disposal of hazardous waste. |
| **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** | Regulates aboveground petroleum storage tanks above threshold quantities. |
| **Underground Storage Tank (UST)** | LIA for UST permitting, inspection, monitoring; oversight of installation, repair, and removal of USTs in Amador County. |
| **Agricultural Hazardous Materials Handlers** | Separate program for agricultural sector hazmat handlers. |

## Reporting Portal

- **CERS (California Electronic Reporting System):** https://cers.calepa.ca.gov
- All HMBP, hazardous waste, APSA, and UST reporting submitted electronically through CERS.
