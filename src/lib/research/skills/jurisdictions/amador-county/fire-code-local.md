## Fire AHJ for Unincorporated Amador County

Two overlapping fire authorities serve the unincorporated county:

1. **Amador Fire Protection District (AFPD)** — local fire protection district (formed July 1990). Primary AHJ for fire code enforcement in its service territory. Contact: (209) 223-6391 | https://www.amadorfire.org/
2. **CAL FIRE Amador-El Dorado Unit (AEU)** — state responsibility area (SRA) wildland and structural fire protection under contract. HQ at Camino, El Dorado County.

City-specific AHJs (see individual city files):
- City of Jackson Fire Department
- City of Ione Fire Department
- Sutter Creek Fire Protection District (covers Sutter Creek and Amador City)

## County Code — Fire and Life Safety Chapter

**Amador County Code Chapter 15.30 — Fire and Life Safety Chapter**

- Adopted by **Ordinance 1778** (2018).
- Scope: establishes **minimum wildfire protection standards** for construction and development in the State Responsibility Area (SRA).
- Requirements include:
  - Emergency access (roads and gates)
  - Signing and building numbering
  - Private water supply reserves for emergency fire use (may be provided in a fire agency mobile water tender, or naturally occurring or manmade containment structure)
  - Vegetation modification (defensible space)
- This chapter operates alongside the adopted CFC (AFPD-011 / pending AFPD adoption of 2024 CFC).
- Full text: https://ecode360.com/46590625

## Burn Permits

- Outdoor fires larger than 4 ft × 4 ft require a **large pile burn permit** from the **Amador County Air Pollution Control District (APCD)**.
- CAL FIRE residential burn permits required May 1, 2026 in Amador County (seasonal requirement).
- CAL FIRE burn permit portal: https://burnpermit.fire.ca.gov/
- Air District burn info: https://www.amadorcounty.gov/services/amador-air-district/burning-and-burn-permits
