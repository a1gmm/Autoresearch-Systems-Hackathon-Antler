> Research reference for the Alameda County Fire Department (ACFD) as the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) for fire code in its service area. ACFD serves unincorporated Alameda County plus the Cities of Dublin, Emeryville, Newark, San Leandro, and Union City by contract. Oakland, Berkeley, Fremont, Hayward, Livermore (city), Pleasanton (city), and the City of Alameda have their own fire AHJs.

## Known Local Amendments (Chapter 6.04)

The following local amendments are confirmed from public sources. Municode Chapter 6.04 is paywalled; the full amendment list requires direct access.

### Fire Protection Systems (§ 6.04.070 — CFC Chapter 9, Section 903)
- Automatic fire sprinkler systems: local amendments to CFC § 903 confirmed to exist (per eLaws index reference to § 6.04.070); cover special-hazard rooms (laboratories, vocational shops with hazardous materials not exceeding maximum allowable quantities).

### Access Roads and Water Supply (ACFD Informational Bulletin 83.230, Feb 2024 — verbatim)
These are locally enforced standards codified under the adopted 2022 CFC:

| Requirement | LRA (Local Responsibility Area) | SRA (State Responsibility Area) |
|---|---|---|
| Fire apparatus road width | ≥ 20 ft unobstructed (2022 CFC § 503.2.1) | ≥ 20 ft (Title 14 § 1273.01) |
| Vertical clearance | ≥ 13 ft 6 in | per Title 14 |
| Road load capacity | 75,000 lb all-weather surface | 75,000 lb all-weather surface |
| Grass paving systems | NOT permitted | NOT permitted |
| Dead-end road turnaround | Required if > 150 ft (2022 CFC § 503.2.5) | Per Title 14 § 1273.05 |
| Maximum grade | 14% per County Road Standards (Appendix D D103.2 Exception) | ≤ 16% (Title 14 § 1273.03) |
| Gates | ≥ 20 ft wide (2022 CFC § 503.2.1 or Appendix D 103.5) | 2 ft wider than required road; 30 ft setback from roadway (Title 14 § 1273.09) |

**Fire water supply (LRA):** Municipal water system; flows per Appendix B, 2022 CFC. Hydrant distribution per Table C102.1, 2022 CFC.

**Building construction in high fire hazard severity zones:** New construction must comply with 2022 CBC Chapter 7A or 2022 CRC R337.

**Addressing (unincorporated Alameda County):** Address numbers must be 4" height, 1/2" stroke, reflective, with contrasting background.

### High-Piled Combustible Storage (CFC 2022 Chapter 32)
- State CFC § 3204 baseline thresholds apply: permit/fire protection plan required for high-piled storage (top of storage > 12 ft, or solid shelving > 6 ft) exceeding 500 sq ft for Class I–IV commodities or Group A plastics; 2,500 sq ft for public access/retail.
- [VERIFY] No confirmed Alameda County local reduction of these thresholds was found. Contact ACFD Fire Prevention to confirm.

### Hazardous Materials Operational Permits
- CFC Chapter 50+ hazmat operational permit thresholds follow state CFC 2022 baseline.
- [VERIFY] The specific Alameda County local amendments to CFC Chapter 50 hazmat permit thresholds were not accessible via public web sources (Municode paywalled). Contact ACFD at (510) 632-3473.
