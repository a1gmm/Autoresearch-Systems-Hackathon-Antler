> Research reference for Alameda County Department of Environmental Health (ACDEH) as the CUPA for unincorporated Alameda County and the City of Oakland. Seven separate CUPAs operate within Alameda County — verify the correct one for each facility address before permitting.

## CUPA Identity

- **CUPA agency:** Alameda County Department of Environmental Health (ACDEH) — Hazardous Materials Division
- **CalEPA-certified:** Yes. ACDEH is a full CUPA (own-CUPA, not under-county). Oakland's CUPA was decertified by CalEPA on 2015-01-05 (first ever in CA); Oakland facilities transferred to ACDEH in March 2015.
- **Address:** 1000 San Leandro Blvd., Suite 300, San Leandro, CA 94577
- **Phone (general):** (510) 618-3452
- **Phone (CUPA / hazmat fees):** (510) 567-6702
- **CUPA web:** https://health.alamedacountyca.gov/department/environmental-health-department
- **Reporting portal:** CERS — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov

## ACDEH Service Area (CUPA Jurisdiction)

ACDEH CUPA covers: unincorporated Alameda County + City of Oakland + Cities of Alameda, Albany, Castro Valley, Dublin, Emeryville, Newark, Piedmont, San Lorenzo, Sunol; parts of Byron, Mountain House and Tracy; also unincorporated fringe areas of Fremont, Hayward, Livermore, Pleasanton and San Leandro.

**Other CUPAs within Alameda County** (facilities must identify the correct one):
1. ACDEH — unincorporated areas + Oakland + above cities
2. City of Berkeley — Toxics Management Division
3. City of Fremont — Fire Department
4. City of Hayward — Fire Department
5. Cities of Livermore + Pleasanton — shared CUPA
6. City of San Leandro — Fire Department
7. City of Union City — Fire Department

## Enabling Ordinance / Municipal Code Chapter

- **Fee authority:** Alameda County Code of Ordinances, **Title 6, Chapter 6.92 — "Health Fees"** (§ 6.92.050 — "Businesses and activities subject to the environmental health service fees")
- All CUPA program fees authorized under Title 6, § 6.92.050 (confirmed from ACDEH General Fees PDF, Exhibit A)
- **Full ordinance text:** https://library.municode.com/ca/alameda_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT6HESA_CH6.92HEFE

## Programs Administered by ACDEH CUPA

| # | Program | Acronym | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan | HMBP | Filed via CERS; ACDEH reviews and inspects |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator / Onsite Tiered Permitting | HWG/OTP | Generator ID, tiered permits, manifest compliance |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tanks | UST | Installation, operation, modification, closure, leak response |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act | APSA | SPCC plan for above-threshold petroleum storage |
| 5 | California Accidental Release Prevention Program | CalARP | Local implementing agency for RMP submissions |
| 6 | California Fire Code Hazardous Materials (Unified Program element) | CFC HazMat | Coordinated with ACFD as fire AHJ |
