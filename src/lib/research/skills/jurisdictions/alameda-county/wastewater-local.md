> Research reference for East Bay Municipal Utility District (EBMUD) as the primary POTW and pretreatment authority for the majority of Alameda County. Facilities in southern Alameda County (Fremont, Newark, Union City) may be served by Union Sanitary District — verify service area before permitting.

## POTW / Pretreatment Agency

- **Primary POTW:** East Bay Municipal Utility District (EBMUD) — Special District No. 1
- **Service area:** Oakland, Berkeley, Emeryville, Piedmont, San Leandro, parts of Castro Valley, Alameda, Albany, and surrounding unincorporated areas. Does NOT serve Fremont, Newark, Union City, or Milpitas fringe (those areas served by Union Sanitary District).
- **Pretreatment phone:** (510) 287-1651
- **Permits & wastewater page:** https://www.ebmud.com/wastewater/commercial-waste/permits-and-wastewater-discharges
- **Director of Wastewater:** Amit Mutsuddy — (510) 287-1149

## Local Discharge Limits Table (Title II, § 3(a) — verbatim from Ordinance, eff. Sept. 13, 2024)

No Person shall Discharge Wastewater from a Sewer Lateral into a Community Sewer if the concentration of any of the following constituents exceeds the following limitations:

| Pollutant | Limit | Units |
|---|---|---|
| Arsenic | 2 | mg/L |
| Cadmium | 1 | mg/L |
| Chromium (total) | 2 | mg/L |
| Copper | 5 | mg/L |
| Cyanide | 5 | mg/L |
| Iron | 100 | mg/L |
| Lead | 2 | mg/L |
| Mercury | 0.05 | mg/L |
| Nickel | 5 | mg/L |
| Oil and Grease (animal/vegetable) | 300 | mg/L |
| Oil and Grease (mineral) | 100 | mg/L |
| pH | not less than 5.5 | S.U. |
| Phenolic compounds | 100 | mg/L |
| Silver | 1 | mg/L |
| Temperature | 150 (1) | °F |
| Total Toxic Organics (TTOs) (2) | 2.1 | mg/L |
| Zinc | 5 | mg/L |

**(1)** 150°F (65.5°C), or any thermal discharge which as a result of temperature and/or volume causes the influent of the wastewater treatment plant to exceed 104°F (40°C).

**(2)** TTO is the summation of compounds defined as "TTO" in 40 CFR 413.02(i), excluding 2,3,7,8-Tetrachlorodibenzo-p-dioxin (TCDD) — "dioxin."

**Rule on stricter limits:** If a pollutant has both a Local Limit (above) and a Federal Categorical Pretreatment Standard (40 CFR Parts 405 et seq.), the more stringent limit applies (Title II, § 4).

**Additional permit limits (Title II, § 3(b)):** The Director may establish additional concentration limits in individual Wastewater Discharge Permits based on available treatment technology or site-specific conditions. Low-pH waste (pH < 5.5) may be accepted at designated receiving stations (Title II, § 3).

## Permit Types (Title IV)

| Permit Type | Who Needs It | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Industrial Facility Discharge Permit | Categorical Industrial Users (CIUs) + facilities with local-concern pollutants, high flow, or high-strength wastewater | Annual; requires application, monitoring, and reporting |
| Groundwater Discharge Permit | Long-term groundwater discharges | Required for remediation sites |
| Limited Term Discharge Permit | Short-term, limited-volume discharges | Temporary construction dewatering, one-time events |
| Estimation of Wastewater Volume Permit | Customers discharging < 20% of incoming water to sewer | Reduces wastewater disposal charges |
| Material Acceptance Permit (MAP) | Hauled waste at EBMUD receiving stations (Title VII) | Distinct from the four above; for trucked waste only |
| General Permits | Group of IUs with same operations/waste types (Title V, § 1) | Issued at Director's discretion |

**Middle Tier CIU designation:** A CIU may qualify if its categorical discharge does not exceed 0.01% of POTW design capacity or maximum allowable headworks loading, and has not had Significant Noncompliance in the past 2 years (Title I, § 3(z)).

## Permit Application Process

1. Download application forms (PDF, under 1 MB each) from https://www.ebmud.com/wastewater/commercial-waste/permits-and-wastewater-discharges
2. Submit completed Wastewater Discharge Permit Application to EBMUD Wastewater Department
3. EBMUD reviews application, may require sampling/monitoring data
4. Permit issued with facility-specific conditions; annual renewal required for IU permits

**Special requirements for:** cannabis facilities, dental facilities, and restaurant/food service establishments (contact EBMUD for sector-specific guidance).

## Pretreatment Program Details (Title V)

- **Source Control Requirements (Title V, § 2):** EBMUD may require BMPs, pollution prevention plans, self-monitoring, and reporting as permit conditions.
- **Sampling methodology:** Analytical and sampling per Title V, § 5; grab or composite samples per permit conditions.
- **Records retention:** 3 years minimum (Title V, § 4).
- **Inspection authority:** EBMUD may inspect premises during business hours (Title V, § 8).
- **Significant Noncompliance (SNC):** Publicly notified per Title V, § 6.

## Enforcement (Title VI)

- Director's orders: compliance schedule, cease-and-desist
- Administrative fines: up to $1,000/day per violation (Title VI, § 2)
- Criminal penalties: misdemeanor (Title VI, § 3)
- Civil enforcement: injunctive relief (Title VI, § 4)

## Note on Alternative POTW (Southern Alameda County)

Facilities in Fremont, Newark, Union City, and adjacent areas are NOT in EBMUD's service area. They discharge to the **Union Sanitary District (USD)** — verify USD pretreatment requirements separately. USD contact: https://www.unionsanitary.com
