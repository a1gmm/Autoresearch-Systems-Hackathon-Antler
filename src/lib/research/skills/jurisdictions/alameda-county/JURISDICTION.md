## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials / Unified Program

**Agency:** Alameda County Department of Environmental Health (ACDEH) — the CUPA for unincorporated Alameda County **and** the City of Oakland (Oakland's own CUPA was decertified by CalEPA on 2015-01-05, the first ever in CA; Oakland facilities transferred to ACDEH in March 2015).

**Seven CUPAs operate in the county** — businesses must identify the correct one:
- ACDEH — unincorporated areas + Oakland + several cities
- City of Berkeley — Toxics Management Division
- City of Fremont — Fire Department
- City of Hayward — Fire Department
- Cities of Livermore + Pleasanton — shared CUPA
- City of San Leandro — Fire Department
- City of Union City — Fire Department

**Programs administered by ACDEH CUPA** (CalEPA-certified): Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generator / Onsite Tiered Permitting, Underground Storage Tank (UST), California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA).

**Reporting portal:** CERS — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov

**ACDEH contact:**
- Address: 1000 San Leandro Blvd., Suite 300, San Leandro, CA 94577
- Phone (general): (510) 618-3452
- Phone (CUPA/hazmat fees): (510) 567-6702

- CUPA program page: https://deh.acgov.org/hazmat/cupa-programs.page (redirects to health.alamedacountyca.gov)

### 2. Fire Code — AHJ & Permits

**AHJ:** Alameda County Fire Department (ACFD) — formed 1993.

**Jurisdictions served by ACFD:** Unincorporated areas (Ashland, Cherryland, Castro Valley, Sunol, unincorporated Livermore, unincorporated Pleasanton) + Cities of Dublin, Emeryville, Newark, San Leandro, and Union City (by contract). LBNL/LLNL also served. Other cities (Oakland, Berkeley, Fremont, Hayward, Livermore city, Pleasanton city, Alameda city) have their own fire AHJs.

**Adopted fire code:** 2022 California Fire Code (CFC) — confirmed by ACFD "Development Guidelines CFC 2022" (doc 83.230, Feb 2024).

**Local amendments:** Codified at **Alameda County Code of Ordinances, Title 6, Chapter 6.04 — "Alameda County Fire Code"** (Municode).

### 3. Industrial Wastewater — POTW / Pretreatment

**POTW / Pretreatment agency:** East Bay Municipal Utility District (EBMUD) — the primary wastewater authority for most of Alameda County (Oakland, Berkeley, Emeryville, Piedmont, San Leandro, parts of Castro Valley, and surrounding areas).

**Local authority instrument:** EBMUD Wastewater Control Ordinance (most recent version: 2024 edition — PDF available from EBMUD).

**Permit types issued by EBMUD:**
1. Industrial Facility Discharge Permit (categorical industries + local-concern pollutants)
2. Groundwater Discharge Permit
3. Limited Term Discharge Permit
4. Estimated Wastewater Volume Permit

**Key local limits** (from Wastewater Control Ordinance, 2024):
- Arsenic: 2 mg/L | Cadmium: 1 mg/L | Copper: 5 mg/L | Lead: 2 mg/L | Mercury: 0.05 mg/L | Temperature: 150°F max | pH: ≥5.5 S.U.
- 17-parameter local limits table; stricter of local vs. federal categorical limit applies.

**Pretreatment contact:**
- Phone: (510) 287-1651 (wastewater permitting)
- Permit applications: https://www.ebmud.com/wastewater/commercial-waste/permits-and-wastewater-discharges (PDF application forms downloadable)
- Ordinance text: https://www.ebmud.com/wastewater/collection-treatment/wastewater-control-ordinance-discharge-limits

**Note:** Cities not in EBMUD service area (southern Alameda County — Fremont, Newark, Union City, Milpitas fringe) may be served by Union Sanitary District — verify service area before permitting.

Source: https://www.ebmud.com/wastewater/commercial-waste/permits-and-wastewater-discharges

### 4. Stormwater — MS4

**Permit:** Municipal Regional Stormwater NPDES Permit No. R2-2022-0018 ("MRP 3"), adopted by SF Bay Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) May 11, 2022; amended October 2023. Covers Alameda, Contra Costa, San Mateo, Santa Clara, and Solano counties.

**Local enforcement ordinance (unincorporated Alameda County):**
- **Alameda County Municipal Code Chapter 13.08** — Stormwater Management and Discharge Control Ordinance (originally Ordinance O-94-36, 1994; administered by ACDEH for unincorporated areas).

**Administering programs:**
- Unincorporated areas: Alameda County Public Works Agency (ACPWA) — Clean Water Program
- ACDEH inspects businesses per MRP requirements
- County-wide coordination: Alameda County Clean Water Program — https://cleanwaterprogram.org

**Key MRP 3 targets:** Trash discharge to storm drains 100% reduction by June 2025; mercury 50% reduction by 2028; PCBs 90% reduction by 2030.

**Contact:** info@cleanwaterprogram.org
**Stormwater discharge permit info:** https://cleanwaterprogram.org/about-us/stormwater-discharge-permit/

Source: https://cleanwaterprogram.org/about-us/stormwater-discharge-permit/
Source: https://deh.acgov.org/hazmat/clean-water.page

### 5. Zoning / Land Use — Unincorporated Areas

**Zoning authority:** Alameda County Community Development Agency (CDA) — Planning Department.
**Planning Director:** Albert Lopez | Contact: https://www.acgov.org/cda/planning/contactus.htm | Phone: (510) 670-5410

**Zoning code:** Alameda County Code of Ordinances, **Title 17 — Zoning** (Municode): https://www.municode.com/library/ca/alameda_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT17ZO

**Industrial district designations (unincorporated):**
- **M-1 — Light Industrial:** Light industrial, manufacturing and processing; all uses must meet performance standards for noise, vibration, odor, emissions, and flammable/explosive materials.
- **M-2 — General Industrial:** General industrial uses; same performance-standard requirements as M-1 with higher intensity uses permitted.
