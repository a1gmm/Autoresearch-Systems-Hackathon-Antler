Verify numeric limits directly from the published ordinance before quoting.

## Oakland Municipal Code — Sewer Connection

Oakland's own code covers connection to the local collection system:
- **OMC Title 13, Chapter 13.08** — "Building Sewers" (governs connection to Oakland's collection system)
- **OMC Title 13, Chapter 13.16** — "Commercial/Industrial Discharge Regulations" (Article II covers illicit discharge prohibition and local stormwater/discharge requirements for the Oakland collection system)

Industrial pretreatment compliance is governed by EBMUD's ordinance, not Oakland's OMC, once wastewater enters EBMUD interceptors.

## Local Discharge Limits (EBMUD WCO Title II, Section 3)

No person shall discharge wastewater with concentrations exceeding the following local limits (mg/L unless noted):

| Pollutant | Local Limit | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| Arsenic | 2 | mg/L |
| Cadmium | 1 | mg/L |
| Chromium (total) | 2 | mg/L |
| Copper | 5 | mg/L |
| Cyanide | 5 | mg/L |
| Iron | 100 | mg/L |
| Lead | 2 | mg/L |
| Mercury | 0.05 | mg/L |
| Nickel | 5 | mg/L |
| Silver | 1 | mg/L |
| Zinc | 5 | mg/L |
| Total Toxic Organics (TTOs) | 2.1 | mg/L |
| Phenolic compounds | 100 | mg/L |
| Oil and Grease (animal/vegetable) | 300 | mg/L |
| Oil and Grease (mineral) | 100 | mg/L |
| pH | ≥5.5 | S.U. |
| Temperature | 150 (65.5°C) | °F |

Note on temperature: 150°F or any thermal discharge which, due to temperature and/or volume, would raise the receiving collection system's temperature to a harmful level; the more restrictive applies.

Federal categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR Subchapter N, Parts 405–471) apply to categorical industrial users; when both a local limit and a federal limit exist, the more stringent applies.

## Who Needs a Permit (Title IV, Section 1)

EBMUD issues industrial wastewater discharge permits to:
1. **Federally regulated Categorical Industrial Users (CIUs)** — industries subject to categorical pretreatment standards under 40 CFR Subchapter N (e.g., metal finishers, electroplaters, printed circuit board manufacturers, chemical manufacturers, food processors).
2. **Industries discharging pollutants of local concern** — constituents in the local limits table above at or near threshold levels.
3. **High-flow dischargers** — significant flow relative to POTW capacity.
4. **High-strength dischargers** — significantly elevated BOD, TSS, or other parameters.

## Permit Types

| Permit Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Industrial Facility Permit | Standard permit for CIUs and significant industrial users; sets effluent limits, monitoring requirements, reporting schedule |
| Groundwater Discharge Permit (Long-Term) | For long-term discharge of contaminated groundwater to sewer |
| Limited Term Discharge Permit | Short-term, limited-volume discharge; e.g., dewatering, tank cleaning, construction |
| Estimation of Wastewater Volume Permit | For businesses discharging <20% of incoming water to sewer; may reduce disposal charges |
| General Permit | Issued to a group of IUs with similar operations, waste streams, and limits |

## Permit Terms and Conditions (Title IV, Section 3)

Permits include: effluent limits, monitoring frequency, sampling locations and methods, reporting schedule (quarterly or annual DMRs), self-monitoring program, BMP requirements, slug discharge notification, right-of-entry for inspections, and compliance schedule if needed. Permits are non-transferable (Title IV, Section 5).

## Enforcement (Title VI)

Enforcement tools: Director's Orders (cease-and-desist, compliance schedule), administrative civil penalties, criminal penalties (fines and imprisonment), and civil enforcement. Significant Non-Compliance (SNC) triggers public notification under Title V, Section 6.
