> Research reference for the CUPA programs applicable to Oakland. Oakland has NO city CUPA — the Oakland Fire Department's own CUPA was decertified by CalEPA on 2015-01-05 (first CUPA decertification in CA history). All ~3,000 regulated facilities in Oakland were transferred to Alameda County Department of Environmental Health (ACDEH) in March 2015. Verify fee schedule with ACDEH before quoting.

## CUPA Identity

- **CUPA:** Alameda County Department of Environmental Health (ACDEH) — Hazardous Materials Division
- **Status:** County CUPA (own-CUPA, NOT a participating agency). ACDEH administers all unified programs for the entirety of Alameda County including all incorporated cities.
- **Legal authority:** Alameda County General Ordinance Code, Title 6, §6.92.050 (fee authorization); Oakland Municipal Code **Chapter 8.42** ("Certified Unified Program Agency") — OMC Ch. 8.42 references the CUPA structure and enables local enforcement coordination.
- **Address:** 1000 San Leandro Blvd., Suite 300, San Leandro, CA 94577
- **Phone:** (510) 618-3452
- **Billing/Fees page:** https://health.alamedacountyca.gov/billing-fees-permits/
- **CUPA program page:** https://health.alamedacountyca.gov/department/environmental-health-department

## Programs Administered

ACDEH administers all six CalEPA unified programs for Oakland facilities:

| # | Program | Acronym |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan | HMBP |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator Program | HWG |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tanks | UST |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act | APSA |
| 5 | California Accidental Release Prevention Program | CalARP |
| 6 | Hazardous Materials On-Site Mitigation / Release Response | HMRRP |

Submission system for all programs: **California Environmental Reporting System (CERS)** — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/business

## HMBP — Hazardous Materials Business Plan

- **Threshold quantities** (state baseline per H&S Code §25507): 55 gallons liquid; 500 lbs solid; 200 cubic feet compressed gas.
- **Filing deadline:** Within 30 days of first bringing threshold materials onsite; annual review/resubmission required.
- **ACDEH HMBP form:** https://www.acgov.org/forms/aceh/Hazardous_Materials_Business_Plan.pdf
- **Fee basis:** Number of hazardous material types ("categories") reported and facility size bracket (see fee table below).

## Oakland Municipal Code Ch. 8.42 — CUPA Ordinance

- OMC Chapter 8.42 ("Certified Unified Program Agency") establishes Oakland's local framework coordinating with the county CUPA.
- The ordinance references the county-administered programs and provides local enforcement coordination authority.
- Full text: https://library.municode.com/ca/oakland/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT8HESA_CH8.42CEUNPRAGCU

## Relationship to Prior Oakland Fire Dept. CUPA

Oakland Fire Department was the city CUPA until **January 5, 2015**, when CalEPA issued the first-ever CUPA decertification in California history. ACDEH assumed responsibility for all ~3,000 regulated Oakland businesses in March 2015. There is no current city CUPA; ACDEH is the sole CUPA authority for Oakland.
