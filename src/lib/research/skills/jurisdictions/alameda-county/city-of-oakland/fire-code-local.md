## Adopted Code Edition

- **Edition adopted:** 2022 California Fire Code (CCR Title 24, Part 9), based on the 2021 International Fire Code (IFC 2021).
- **Adopting ordinance:** Ordinance No. 13720 C.M.S. (File #: 22-0900)
- **Adopted by:** Oakland City Council
- **Final action / effective date:** December 20, 2022
- **Municipal Code location:** Oakland Municipal Code (OMC) **Chapter 15.12** ("Oakland Fire Code")
- **Action:** Repealed prior Chapter 15.12 (which incorporated the 2013 CFC via Ord. No. 13362 C.M.S.), made local climatic/geological/topographical findings, and recodified with 2022 CFC + Oakland local amendments as the new Chapter 15.12.

Note: Earlier amendment Ordinance No. 13362 C.M.S. (passed April 19, 2016) amended §15.12.020/§114.3 (fee collection via lien process for reinspection/abatement costs) — this provision carries forward under 13720.

## Local Amendments — Summary

The Exhibit A to Ord. 13720 contains the full Oakland-specific amendments to the 2022 CFC. Key categories of local modification per the ordinance findings and prior practice:

1. **Local climatic/geological/topographical findings** — Required under H&S Code §13869.7 to justify deviations from the state model code. Oakland cites Oakland Hills fire history (1991 Tunnel Fire), steep terrain, and wind patterns.
2. **§114.3 — Fee collection** — Extended to allow lien process to recover unpaid administration/reinspection fees, fines, penalties, and collection costs (first amended by Ord. 13362 CMS; carried forward).
3. **Appendix D — Fire Apparatus Access Roads** — Pending additional amendments were scheduled for Public Safety Committee in September 2023 (aerial apparatus access, residential development standards).
4. **Hazardous materials operational permits (§105)** — All storage/use of hazardous materials and hazardous waste must be reviewed and approved by the Fire Department prior to commencement. Thresholds follow 2022 CFC Chapter 50 baseline; no confirmed Oakland-specific lower thresholds located in public sources (verify with Fire Prevention Bureau).

Full text of local amendments: Supplemental Exhibit A to File #22-0900 at https://oakland.legistar.com/View.ashx?M=F&ID=12296568 (see legistar attachments).

## Hazardous Materials Inspection Fees (FY 2025-26)

- Standard HazMat inspection: **$381/P/H**
- After-hours HazMat inspection: **$534/P/H (2.5 hr minimum)**
- Investigation/Inspection of unpermitted work: $381/P/H
- Work commenced without permit: Double all fees
- Work completed without permit: Quadruple all fees
