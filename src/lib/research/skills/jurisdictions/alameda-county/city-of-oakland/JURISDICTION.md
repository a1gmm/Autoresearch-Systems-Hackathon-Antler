## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials / Unified Program

- **CUPA authority:** Alameda County Department of Environmental Health (ACDEH) — Hazardous Materials Division. Oakland has **no city CUPA**; the Oakland Fire Department's own CUPA was decertified by CalEPA on 2015-01-05 (first CUPA decertification in CA history) and its ~3,000 regulated facilities were transferred to ACDEH in March 2015.
- **Programs administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generator, UST, CalARP, HMRRP, Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA).
- **Address:** 1000 San Leandro Blvd., Suite 300, San Leandro, CA 94577
- **Phone:** (510) 618-3452
- **HMBP form:** https://www.acgov.org/forms/aceh/Hazardous_Materials_Business_Plan.pdf
- **Billing/fees:** https://deh.acgov.org/billing-fees-permits.page (annual state surcharge raised to $79/regulated business effective 2025-08-22)
- **CUPA program page:** https://deh.acgov.org/hazmat/cupa-programs.page
- Source: https://health.alamedacountyca.gov/department/environmental-health-department

### 2. Fire Code — Oakland Fire Department (AHJ)

- **AHJ:** Oakland Fire Department, Fire Prevention Bureau
- **Adopted code:** 2022 California Fire Code (CCR Title 24, Part 9) — adopted by Oakland City Council 2022-12-20 via **Ordinance No. 22-0900**, codified at **Oakland Municipal Code (OMC) Chapter 15.12** ("Oakland Fire Code")
- **Local amendments:** Chapter 15.12 includes Oakland-specific amendments to the 2022 CFC; findings of local climatic/geological/topographical conditions support the modifications.
- **Permit types (Fire Prevention Bureau):** operational fire permits required for hazardous materials storage/use, high-piled combustible storage, spray booths, flammable/combustible liquid storage, compressed gas systems, and others listed in CFC §105.
- **Permit services page:** https://www.oaklandca.gov/Public-Safety-Streets/Fire/Fire-Prevention-Bureau-Permitting-Services
- **Plan review page:** https://www.oaklandca.gov/Public-Safety-Streets/Fire/Fire-Prevention-Bureau-Permitting-Services/Fire-Prevention-Plan-Review-Services
- **Fee schedule:** Master Fee Schedule referenced in OMC §15.12.020(§114.3); 2024-2025 update at https://www.oaklandca.gov/news/permit-fee-schedule-update
- **Fire dept home:** https://www.oaklandca.gov/departments/fire
- Source: https://oakland.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=5921543&GUID=727EDF0E-82CF-4242-8C1B-4084BDDB57BB

### 3. Industrial Wastewater / Pretreatment

- **POTW/administering agency:** East Bay Municipal Utility District (EBMUD) — the regional wastewater treatment authority for Oakland. Oakland's own municipal code (OMC Title 13, Chapter 13.08 — "Building Sewers") governs connection to the local collection system, but industrial pretreatment is governed by **EBMUD's Wastewater Control Ordinance** (2024 edition).
- **EBMUD local discharge limits (representative):** Cu/Cyanide/Ni/Zn ≤ 5 mg/L; Pb/Cd ≤ 2 mg/L; Hg ≤ 0.05 mg/L; mineral oil & grease ≤ 100 mg/L; temperature ≤ 150°F.
- **Who needs an IWP:** federally regulated categorical industries (40 CFR Subchapter N, Parts 400–471) and industries discharging pollutants of local concern or high-flow/high-strength waste.
- **Permit types:** Industrial Facility permit, Groundwater Discharge permit, Limited Term Discharge permit, Wastewater Volume Estimation.
- **Application/portal:** PDF applications at https://www.ebmud.com/wastewater/commercial-waste/permits-and-wastewater-discharges
- **EBMUD pretreatment contact:** (510) 287-1651 | 375 11th St, Oakland, CA 94607
- **Wastewater Control Ordinance & limits:** https://www.ebmud.com/wastewater/collection-treatment/wastewater-control-ordinance-discharge-limits
- **Oakland sewer building code:** OMC Title 13, Chapter 13.08 (Building Sewers); Chapter 13.16 (Commercial/Industrial Discharge Regulations)
- Source: https://www.ebmud.com/wastewater/commercial-waste/permits-and-wastewater-discharges

### 4. Stormwater — MS4

- **MS4 permittee:** City of Oakland Public Works — covered under the **Municipal Regional Stormwater NPDES Permit (MRP)** issued by the San Francisco Bay Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 2). MRP 3.0 took effect 2022-07-01 (Order No. R2-2022-XXXX, successor to R2-2015-0049).
- **Local ordinance:** OMC Title 13, Chapter 13.16, Article II — Discharge Regulations and Requirements (covers illicit discharge prohibition and stormwater management requirements).
- **Industrial stormwater:** industrial operators within Oakland must also hold a state Industrial General Permit (IGP) from the State Water Board; Oakland's MS4 permit requires the city to track and inspect industrial sites.
- **Oakland stormwater resources page:** https://www.oaklandca.gov/topics/storm-drainage-master-plan
- **Stormwater permit resources:** https://www.oaklandca.gov/Planning-Building/Permit-Process-Overview/Municipal-Regional-Stormwater-Permit-Resources
- **Regional Board (Region 2):** https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/rwqcb2/
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/rwqcb2/water_issues/programs/npdes_wastewater_permit.html

### 5. Zoning / Land Use — Industrial Districts

- **Code:** Oakland **Planning Code, Title 17** (separate from the Municipal Code). Administered by Oakland Planning & Building Department.
- **Industrial zone designations** (current; Oakland does NOT use legacy M-1/M-2 labels):
  - **CIX** (Commercial/Industrial mix) — sub-zones CIX-1A, CIX-1B, CIX-1C, CIX-1D, CIX-1, CIX-2: light industrial, maker/flex; CIX-2 allows heavier uses with CUP
  - **IG** (General Industrial): manufacturing, warehousing/distribution, recycling, construction-related, clean tech
  - **IO** (Industrial Office): R&D, product development, office-industrial hybrid
- **CUP triggers (examples):** uses >5,000 sf floor area in CIX-2, IG, IO zones; uses within 300 ft of a residential zone require CUP regardless of zone; hazardous materials operations above thresholds.
- **Governing chapter:** Planning Code Chapter 17.73 ("CIX, IG and IO Industrial Zones Regulations")
- **Planning code portal:** https://library.municode.com/ca/oakland/codes/planning_code
- **Oakland Planning & Zoning:** https://www.oaklandca.gov/Planning-Building/Planning-Zoning
- **Planning codes page:** https://www.oaklandca.gov/Planning-Building/Planning-Zoning/Planning-Codes
- Source: http://oakland-ca.elaws.us/code/pc_ch17.73_sec17.73.020

### 6. Air Quality

- **No city air program.** Oakland is within the **Bay Area Air Quality Management District (BAAQMD)** jurisdiction for stationary source permits, Title V, and CEQA air assessment. Oakland is also within the **West Oakland Community Air Monitoring** zone — BAAQMD has additional community health protections in this area.
- **BAAQMD permits:** https://www.baaqmd.gov/permits
- Source: https://www.baaqmd.gov
