> Research reference for Oakland's industrial zoning districts. Oakland uses CIX/IG/IO nomenclature — NOT legacy M-1/M-2 labels. Administered by the Planning & Building Department. Verify the live planning code before quoting.

## Governing Code

- **Code:** Oakland Planning Code, **Title 17** (separate publication from the Oakland Municipal Code)
- **Industrial chapter:** Chapter 17.73 — "CIX, IG AND IO INDUSTRIAL ZONES REGULATIONS"
- **Administered by:** Oakland Planning & Building Department
- **Planning Code portal:** https://library.municode.com/ca/oakland/codes/planning_code
- **Planning & Zoning page:** https://www.oaklandca.gov/Planning-Building/Planning-Zoning

## Industrial Zone Districts (Chapter 17.73)

Oakland does NOT use legacy M-1 / M-2 industrial labels. Current districts:

### CIX — Commercial/Industrial Mix
Seven sub-zones with progressively higher impact tolerance:

| Zone | Name | Character |
|---|---|---|
| CIX-1A | West Oakland Business Enhancement | Incubator space, artisan workshops, flexible small-business; West Oakland Specific Plan Area |
| CIX-1B | West Oakland Low Intensity | Custom/light manufacturing, light industrial, warehouse, R&D, service commercial; West Oakland Specific Plan Area |
| CIX-1C | West Oakland High Intensity | Higher-intensity commercial, retail, office, advanced manufacturing; strong location advantages; West Oakland Specific Plan Area |
| CIX-1D | West Oakland Retail Commercial | Large-scale retail and commercial in prominent street locations; West Oakland Specific Plan Area |
| CIX-1 | Commercial Industrial Mix-1 | Wide variety of businesses; flexible for new technologies; citywide (non-West Oakland) |
| CIX-2 | Commercial Industrial Mix-2 | Uses with greater off-site impacts when meeting performance standards; must be buffered from residential |

### IG — General Industrial
- **Purpose:** "intended to create, preserve and enhance areas...appropriate for a wide variety of businesses...that may have the potential to generate off-site impacts such as noise, light/glare, odor, and traffic."
- Accepts manufacturing, warehousing/distribution, recycling, construction-related, clean technology, and similar uses.
- Higher tolerance for off-site impacts than CIX zones, subject to performance standards.

### IO — Industrial Office
- **Purpose:** Campus-style development with office, R&D, light industrial, and distribution uses under more restrictive performance standards.
- More restrictive than IG on off-site impacts.

## Permitted vs. Conditionally Permitted Uses (Table 17.73.020)

The code uses: **P** = permitted; **C** = conditionally permitted (CUP required); **L** = permitted with limitations; **—** = prohibited.

Key industrial use designations:

| Use Category | CIX-1A–1D | CIX-1 | CIX-2 | IG | IO |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Light/Custom Manufacturing | P | P | P | P | P |
| General Manufacturing | P (w/limits) | P | P | P | — |
| Warehousing/Distribution | P (w/limits ≤25,000 sf) | P | P | P | P |
| R&D / Product Development | P | P | P | P | P |
| Recycling Centers (primary) | C | C | C | P | — |
| Construction-related uses | L | P | P | P | — |
| Hazardous materials transfer/storage (small-scale) | — (within 600 ft of residential) | C | C | C | — |
| Industrial hazardous materials operations | — (within 600 ft) | C | C | C | — |

**Note:** Zoneomics extracted these designations from the published planning code; always verify against the current OMC Chapter 17.73 Table 17.73.020 for the authoritative designation for a specific parcel and use.

## Key CUP Triggers (§17.73.020 footnotes / limitations)

1. **Floor area threshold:** Uses exceeding **5,000 sq ft** of floor area in CIX-2, IG, and IO zones require a CUP.
2. **Proximity to residential (general):** Uses within **300 feet** of a Residential Zone boundary require a CUP regardless of zone.
3. **Proximity to residential (hazardous materials):** Hazardous materials storage/transfer/operations are **prohibited within 600 feet** of a Residential Zone boundary; CUP required beyond 600 ft with performance standards compliance.
4. **Warehousing in CIX-1A/1B/1C:** Limited to **25,000 sq ft** maximum floor area; exceeding requires a CUP.
5. **Outdoor yard activities:** Any use involving outdoor yard activities requires a CUP.
6. **Work/Live units:** Establishment of any Work/Live unit in CIX, IG, or IO requires a CUP plus Regular Design Review (§17.73.040).
7. **Proximity to BART (½ mile):** Certain industrial activities within ½ mile of a BART station may require a CUP.
8. **Recycling (primary processing):** CUP required in CIX zones regardless of floor area.

## Performance Standards Referenced

- **Chapter 17.120** — Performance standards for noise, odor, vibration, smoke, and other objectionable impacts. Industrial uses with potential off-site impacts must comply; CUP conditions enforce these standards.
- **Chapter 17.110** — Buffering regulations: screening of parking, loading, glare, and storage from Residential Zones.
- **Chapter 17.124** — Landscaping and screening including street trees.
- **S-19 Health and Safety Protection Overlay Zone** (§17.100A) — Applies in portions of West Oakland; adds health and safety review for industrial uses.

## Property Development Standards (§17.73.030)

Key standards (from Chapter 17.73.030):
- **FAR:** Ranges from 1.0 to 4.0 depending on zone and residential proximity.
- **Height:** Varies by zone; IG and IO allow taller structures.
- **Setbacks:** Setback requirements from residential zone boundaries vary; buffering requirements in Chapter 17.110 apply.
- **Parking:** Per Chapter 17.116.

## CUP Process and Portal

- **Governing chapter:** Chapter 17.134 — Conditional Use Permit Procedure
- **Additional criteria for CIX/IG/IO CUPs:** Applicant must show compliance with performance standards (Ch. 17.120), buffering (Ch. 17.110), and design review (Ch. 17.136).
- **Application portal:** Oakland Permit Center / Planning & Building: https://www.oaklandca.gov/Planning-Building/Planning-Zoning
- **Planning codes page:** https://www.oaklandca.gov/Planning-Building/Planning-Zoning/Planning-Codes
- **Zoning map:** Available via Oakland's GIS/Zoning portal at the Planning & Zoning page above.

|---|---|
