## Agency

**City of Berkeley is its own CUPA** — separate from Alameda County Department of Environmental Health (DEH). Do not route HMBP, hazardous waste, UST, APSA, or CalARP inquiries to the county.

- **Agency**: Toxics Management Division (TMD), City of Berkeley Planning and Development Department.
- **Address**: 2180 Milvia Street, Berkeley, CA 94704
- **Phone**: (510) 981-7460
- **Email**: toxics@berkeleyca.gov
- **CERS portal**: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/ (all HMBP filings)

## Municipal Code — Local Hazardous Materials Ordinance

- **BMC Title 15** — "Hazardous Materials" (the Berkeley local hazardous materials code).
  - **BMC Chapter 15.08** — Definitions (includes definition of "Hazardous material or waste" at § 15.08.060, and "Hazardous materials manager" at § 15.08.070).
  - **BMC Chapter 15.12** — Hazardous Materials and Wastes Management. This chapter adopts by reference:
    - California Health & Safety Code Division 20, Chapter 6.5 (Hazardous Waste Control)
    - Cal. H&SC Division 20, Chapter 6.67
    - Cal. H&SC Division 20, Chapter 6.7 (Underground Storage of Hazardous Substances)
    - Cal. H&SC Division 20, Chapter 6.75
    - Cal. H&SC Division 20, Chapter 6.11
    - **Cal. H&SC Division 20, Chapter 6.95** (Hazardous Materials Release Response Plans and Inventory — HMBP)
    - California Water Code Division 7, Chapter 10, Article 4
    - Elements of Article 79 and 80 of the Uniform Fire Code
    — with specified local modifications.
  - History note: BMC Title 15 was created in 2004 when Chapters 11.18 and 11.52 were severed from Title 11, repealed, and reenacted as Title 15, Chapters 15.04, 15.08, and 15.12.

## Programs Administered by TMD (CUPA)

TMD administers **five state unified programs**:

| # | Program | State Authority |
|---|---------|----------------|
| 1 | Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) — Release Response Plan and Inventory | Cal. H&SC § 6.95 |
| 2 | Hazardous Waste Generator Program | Cal. H&SC § 6.5 |
| 3 | Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program | Cal. H&SC § 6.7 |
| 4 | Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA) | Cal. H&SC § 25270 et seq. |
| 5 | California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) / Risk Management Plan (RMP) | Cal. H&SC § 25531 et seq. |

### III. Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP) Annual Fees

Fee is determined by TWO factors:
- **Table 1**: Total number of distinct hazardous materials on site (A = 1–10, B = 11–25, C = 26–50, D = > 50)
- **Table 2**: Aggregate maximum quantity on site (tier 1–5: e.g., tier 1 = 55–275 gal / 200–999 cu ft / 500–999 lbs; tier 5 = > 10,000 gal / > 100,000 cu ft / > 100,000 lbs)

Selected fee matrix cells (format: [Table1-type][Table2-tier] = fee):

| | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 | Tier 4 | Tier 5 |
|--|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| **A (1–10 materials)** | $362 | $725 | $1,389 | $2,053 | $3,381 |
| **B (11–25 materials)** | $483 | $906 | $1,811 | $2,717 | $3,864 |
| **C (26–50 materials)** | $544 | $1,087 | $2,234 | $3,381 | $4,347 |
| **D (> 50 materials)** | $665 | $1,328 | $2,717 | $4,046 | $5,072 |

Special registrations:
- Etiological Agent Registration: **$242**
- Radiological Materials Registration: **$242**
- Limited HazMat registration (below BMC Title 15 threshold but required under CFC Chapter 50): **$242**
- Gases — simple asphyxiants/pressure hazards only, aggregate < 1,000 cu ft: **$242**
