## POTW / Sanitation Agency

Berkeley is **not** served by a city-owned sewer authority. All industrial wastewater discharges are regulated by:

- **Agency**: East Bay Municipal Utility District (EBMUD), Special District No. 1.
- **Address**: 375 11th Street, Oakland, CA 94607
- **Wastewater permitting line**: (510) 287-1651
- **Email**: Cleanbay@ebmud.com
- **Director of Wastewater**: Amit Mutsuddy | (510) 287-1149

Berkeley's own municipal code contains only incidental sewer-use provisions:
- **BMC § 17.16.020** — Prohibits depositing refuse in sanitary sewers.
- **BMC § 17.16.050** — Sewer construction requirements.

These BMC sections do not establish local industrial discharge limits; EBMUD's ordinance governs.

## Governing Ordinance

- **EBMUD Wastewater Control Ordinance** (consolidated).
- **Effective date of current version**: September 13, 2024 (Ord. No. 377-24, § 2, adopted 08-13-2024; prior version was Ord. No. 355-11, adopted 07-12-2011).
- **PDF**: https://www.ebmud.com/download_file/force/2279/655?Wastewater_Control_Ordinance_2024.pdf

### Ordinance Structure

| Title | Subject |
|-------|---------|
| Title I | General (definitions, connection requirements, stormwater prohibition) |
| Title II | Regulation of Wastewater Discharges (prohibited discharges, local limits, pretreatment) |
| Title III | Discharger Classification and Wastewater Disposal Charges |
| Title IV | Wastewater Discharge Permits (types, application, terms, transfer prohibition) |
| Title V | Administration (inspection, source control, record retention, sampling) |
| Title VI | Enforcement and Penalties |
| Title VII | Resource Recovery Program |

**Compliance rule**: where both a Local Limit and a federal Categorical Pretreatment Standard exist for the same pollutant, the **more stringent limit applies**.

## Categorical Industry Pretreatment

- **Categorical Industrial Users (CIUs)**: subject to 40 CFR Chapter I, Subchapter N, Parts 405–471 (EPA Effluent Guidelines).
- CIUs must meet applicable Categorical Pretreatment Standards in addition to local limits.
- EBMUD enforces these standards as the Control Authority for Berkeley.

## Permit Types

EBMUD issues four categories of wastewater discharge permits (Title IV):

| Permit Type | Who Needs It |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Industrial Facilities / Groundwater Discharge Permit** | Federally regulated categorical industries; industries discharging pollutants of local concern, high flow, or high-strength wastewater |
| **Limited Term Discharge Permit** | Short-duration, limited-volume discharges |
| **Groundwater Discharge Permit** | Long-term groundwater discharge |
| **Estimated Wastewater Volume Permit** | Facilities discharging < 20% of incoming water volume |

Specialty programs also exist for cannabis, dental, restaurant/food-service facilities.

## Application Process

1. Download appropriate application form from https://www.ebmud.com/wastewater/commercial-waste/permits-and-wastewater-discharges.
   - Wastewater Discharge Permit Application (industrial/groundwater)
   - Limited Term Discharge Permit Application (PDF direct: https://www.ebmud.com/download_file/force/7419/643)
   - Estimation of Wastewater Volume Permit Application
2. Application includes: SIC code, estimated wastewater volume, estimated concentrations of each constituent for which a local limit exists, certification of insurance.
3. Submit to EBMUD Wastewater Commercial Waste team: (510) 287-1651 | Cleanbay@ebmud.com.

## Prohibition on Permit Transfer

Wastewater Discharge Permits are **non-transferable** (Ordinance Title IV, Sec. 5). New facility owners/operators must obtain a new permit.

## Key Prohibited Discharges (Title II, Sec. 2)

- Discharges that cause fire or explosion hazard (flash point ≤ 140°F unless diluted to safe levels)
- Discharges causing interference with POTW treatment processes or NPDES permit violations
- Stormwater, drainage, or groundwater unless permitted (Title I, Sec. 5)
- Radioactive wastes except as authorized

## Resource Recovery Program (Title VII)

EBMUD may accept wastes exceeding local limits via the **Resource Recovery Program** (pre-approval required). This is the pathway for low-pH wastes (pH < 5.5) delivered to designated receiving stations.
