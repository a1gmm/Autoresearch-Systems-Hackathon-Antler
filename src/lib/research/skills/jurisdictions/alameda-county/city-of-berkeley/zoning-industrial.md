## Zoning Code Authority

- **Code**: Berkeley Municipal Code **Title 23** — "Zoning" (Baseline Zoning Ordinance, BZO).
- The current BZO replaced the prior Title 23 in 2021, renaming legacy M-1/M-2 zones.
- **Table 23.108-1** (Zoning Districts) establishes the official district list.
- Industrial/manufacturing provisions are concentrated in **BMC Chapter 23.206** ("Manufacturing Districts").

## Manufacturing District Names and Symbols

As codified in Table 23.108-1 (current, confirmed in Ordinance No. 7,978-N.S., July 2025):

| Symbol | District Name |
|--------|--------------|
| **M** | Manufacturing |
| **MM** | Mixed Manufacturing |
| **MU-LI** | Mixed Use–Light Industrial |
| **MU-R** | Mixed Use–Residential |

Legacy note: The pre-2021 BZO used M-1 (Light Industrial) and M-2 (Heavy Industrial) designations. These no longer exist in the current code.

## Chapter 23.206 — Manufacturing Districts

### BMC § 23.206.020 — Allowed Land Uses and Permit Requirements

Uses in manufacturing districts are governed by **Table 23.206-1** (Allowed Land Uses and Permit Requirements in the Manufacturing Districts).

Permit type key used in Table 23.206-1:
- **ZC** — Zoning Certificate (ministerial/over-the-counter approval)
- **AUP** — Administrative Use Permit (staff-level discretionary, no public hearing)
- **UP(PH)** — Use Permit with Public Hearing (Zoning Adjustments Board)
- **NP** — Not Permitted
- **\*** — Use-Specific Regulations apply (see cross-referenced BMC section)

Any use **not listed** in Table 23.206-1 may be permitted with an AUP if the Zoning Officer finds the use compatible with district purposes (BMC § 23.206.020).

### BMC § 23.206.050 — Protected Uses

Manufacturing districts include protected industrial uses that cannot be displaced by incompatible encroachment. In **MM** and **MU-LI** districts, **Table 23.206-5** enumerates protected uses including:
- Manufacture, assembly, processing, repair, testing, storage, display, and distribution of goods.

### BMC § 23.206.060 — M (Manufacturing) District

- The broadest industrial zone; accommodates heavier manufacturing and industrial operations.
- Industrial uses are the primary intended use.

### BMC § 23.206.080 — MU-LI (Mixed Use–Light Industrial) District

Purposes (verbatim from BMC § 23.206.080):
- Encourage development of a mixed use-light industrial area for a range of compatible uses.
- Encourage an area where **light manufacturers can operate free from constraints caused by incompatible uses**.
- Encourage the creation and continuation of **well-paid jobs which do not require advanced degrees**.

Key rules for MU-LI:
- Manufacturing uses **within 150 ft of a residential use** require an **Administrative Use Permit (AUP)** even when otherwise allowed by right.
- An existing use in the MU-LI district may be modified or intensified **without** a Use Permit if no UP is required by the table and the Zoning Officer determines the modification will not increase any impact regulated under environmental performance standards.

### BMC § 23.206.090 — MU-R (Mixed Use–Residential) District

- Transitional zone between residential and manufacturing areas.
- Allows light industrial and live-work uses alongside residential.
- Less industrial-protective than MU-LI; more residential-influenced.

### BMC § 23.206.100 — Permit Findings

Findings required to approve a Use Permit in manufacturing districts (ZAB must find):
- The use is consistent with the purposes of the district.
- The use will not be detrimental to adjacent uses or the general welfare.
- The use meets applicable environmental performance standards.

## Permit Types and Process

### Administrative Use Permit (AUP) — Staff Level
- Issued by Zoning Officer (staff decision, no public hearing).
- Used for: manufacturing uses within 150 ft of residential; uses not listed in Table 23.206-1 but found compatible with district.
- Application: Permit Service Center, 2180 Milvia St, Berkeley, CA 94704.
- Email submittal: Planning@berkeleyca.gov | Subject: "Electronic Zoning Submittal – [address]"

### Use Permit (UP) with Public Hearing — ZAB
- Issued by **Zoning Adjustments Board (ZAB)** after public hearing.
- Required for: higher-impact uses; specific uses flagged in Table 23.206-1 with UP(PH).
- **ZAB Secretary**: zab@cityofberkeley.info | 1947 Center Street, 2nd Floor, Berkeley, CA 94704
- ZAB meets: **2nd and 4th Thursday, 7:00 pm**.
- Processing time: 6–12 months (standard), 9–15 months (Tier 2/non-residential in residential district), 12–24 months (mixed-use projects).
- Notice posted ≥ 14 days before hearing; 14-day appeal window after Notice of Decision.
- Appeals go to City Council; City Council review typically within 2–3 months.

### Application Fee Reference
- Planning application fees are set in the City of Berkeley Planning and Development Fee Schedule (effective 7/1/2025).
- ZAB/Design Review Committee review fee: $4,000 base (see fee schedule).
- Full fee schedule PDF: https://berkeleyca.gov/sites/default/files/2026-04/City%20of%20Berkeley%20Planning%20and%20Development%20Fee%20Schedule%20July%202025.pdf
