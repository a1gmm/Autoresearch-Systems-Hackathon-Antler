## Adopted Code Edition

- **Code adopted**: 2025 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9 of the California Code of Regulations), which reflects the **2024 International Fire Code (IFC)** with State amendments.
- **Effective date**: January 1, 2026.
- **Prior code repealed**: 2022 CFC chapter of BMC 19.48 (Resolution No. 71,710-N.S. rescinded; Resolution No. 71,837-N.S. rescinded).

## Adopting Ordinance and Municipal Code Chapter

- **Ordinance No. 7,990-N.S.** — adopted on second reading December 2, 2025 by Berkeley City Council.
- **Municipal Code chapter**: **BMC Chapter 19.48** ("Berkeley Fire Code") — repealed and reenacted in full.
- **Findings of local conditions**: Resolution No. 72,023-N.S. (adopted November 10, 2025) — establishes that Berkeley's unique climatic, geological, and topographical conditions require more stringent standards.
- **Fee schedule**: Resolution No. 72,024-N.S. (adopted November 10, 2025) — sets operational and construction permit fees effective January 1, 2026.

## Key Local Amendments — Hazmat and Industrial

### BMC § 19.48.090 / CFC § 5001.7 — Hazardous Materials Transport Restrictions (LOCAL AMENDMENT)
Verbatim local rule: No vehicle containing hazardous materials — including tank trucks, trailers, semi-trailers, or tank wagons carrying **flammable or combustible liquids, hazardous chemicals, liquefied petroleum gases, poisonous gases, or cryogenic fluids** — may be operated on any City street **without a permit from the fire code official**. Requirements:
- Route map must accompany permit application and be carried in the vehicle at all times.
- Permit may impose hour restrictions and vehicle size limits.
- **Prohibited routes** (BART purge chamber openings): Addison St (MLK Jr Way–Oxford), Shattuck Ave (University–Dwight), Virginia St (Franklin–Sacramento), Sacramento St (Virginia–Hearst), Hearst Ave (McGee–Milvia), Adeline St (Ward–Stuart), Adeline St (Ashby–Woolsey).
- No route may pass through the **Northbrae Tunnel** or through any **Environmental Safety Residential (ES-R) district**.
- Extremely hazardous materials may require Fire or Police Department **convoy escort**.

### BMC § 19.48.110 / CFC Ch. 57 — Flammable and Combustible Liquids (LOCAL AMENDMENTS)
Local sections adopted:
- **§ 5701.4.1** — Transfer of Flammable and Combustible Liquids (additional local section).
- **§ 5704.2.11.1.1** — Restrictions on Underground Storage Tanks (additional local section).
- **§ 5704.2.13.1.4** — Tanks Abandoned in Place.
- **§ 5704.2.14** — Removal and Disposal of Tanks.

### BMC § 19.48.120 / CFC § 6104.1.1 — LP-Gas (LOCAL AMENDMENT)
- **§ 6104.1.1** — Restrictions on Storage of LP-Gas Containers (additional local section).

### Other Notable Local Amendments (Non-Hazmat)
- **§ 903.4.3** — Sprinkler alarms: exception for 1- and 2-family dwellings removed (visual + audible alarm required).
- **§ 907.1.6/1.7/9.1** — Fire alarm certification and monitoring requirements added.
- Administrative sections 103, 104.7, 104.7.2, 104.7.6, 109, 115 aligned with Berkeley Building Code and Wildland Urban Interface Code.
- **Appendix L** (FARS) — no longer adopted.

## Permit Fees (Resolution No. 72,024-N.S., effective January 1, 2026)

Full fee schedule PDF: https://berkeleyca.gov/sites/default/files/documents/Fire-Fees-Inspections-Billing-Rates.pdf

Selected fee benchmarks (from prior schedule; updated schedule at same URL):
- Hazardous materials storage/use operational permit: minimum **$250**
- Flammable/combustible liquid storage operational permit: minimum **$250**
- High-pile combustible storage operational permit: minimum **$250**
- Outdoor assembly events: minimum **$500**
- Additive manufacturing (new in 2025 CFC): minimum **$250**
- Indoor plant cultivation (new): established per § 105.5.26
- Mobile food preparation vehicles (new): established per new operational permit
- IEE (Independent Event Evaluation, ≥ 50 attendees): **$125**

## Note on High-Piled Storage

The 2025 CFC (and Berkeley adoption thereof) does not include a separate locally amended high-piled storage quantity threshold distinct from the State CFC amounts. High-piled storage is governed by CFC Chapter 32 (State) as adopted. No local amendment to Chapter 32 appears in Ord. 7,990-N.S.
