## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials / Hazardous Waste

- **Berkeley is its own CUPA** — separate from Alameda County DEH.
- Agency: **Toxics Management Division (TMD)**, City of Berkeley.
  - GOTCHA: Do not route HMBP, hazardous waste, UST, APSA, or CalARP inquiries to the county; they go to TMD.
- Programs administered: HMBP (Cal HSC § 6.95), Hazardous Waste Generator (§ 6.5), UST (§ 6.7), APSA, CalARP.
- Legal authority: California Health & Safety Code Division 20, Chapters 6.95, 6.5, 6.7; CCR Title 19 Div. 1 Ch. 11; CCR Title 23.
- **Permit portal**: CERS — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/
- Contact: (510) 981-7460 | toxics@berkeleyca.gov
- Source: https://berkeleyca.gov/doing-business/operating-berkeley/hazardous-materials-business-plan

### 2. Fire Code — AHJ & Permits

- **AHJ**: Berkeley Fire Department, Fire Prevention Division.
  - 1250 Ninth Street, Berkeley, CA 94710
  - Phone: (510) 981-5585 | Email: bfdfireprevention@berkeleyca.gov
  - Permit submittals: FireSubmittals@berkeleyca.gov
- **Adopted code**: 2025 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9) adopted by reference into **BMC Chapter 19.48**, via **Ordinance No. 7,990-N.S.** Effective **January 1, 2026**. (Repeals and re-enacts the prior 2022 CFC chapter.)
- **Operational permits required** under the adopted CFC include:
  - Hazardous materials storage/use (CFC § 5001+)
  - Flammable/combustible liquid storage (operational permit fee minimum: $250)
  - High-pile combustible storage
  - Hot work operations
  - Tank vehicles transporting hazardous materials (CFC § 5001.7)
  - Outdoor assembly events (minimum $500)
  - Additive manufacturing (new in 2025 CFC, minimum $250)
- **Construction permits** (fire alarm, sprinkler, suppression, standpipe, ESS, fire pump): submit to FireSubmittals@berkeleyca.gov; plan review fees determined post-acceptance.
- **IEE inspections** (50+ attendees): $125, submit ≥10 business days before event.
- Fee schedule PDF (may require direct download): https://berkeleyca.gov/sites/default/files/documents/Fire-Fees-Inspections-Billing-Rates.pdf
- Fire permits landing page: https://berkeleyca.gov/construction-development/permits-design-parameters/permit-types/fire-permits

### 3. Industrial Wastewater / Pretreatment

- **POTW**: East Bay Municipal Utility District (EBMUD). Berkeley is an EBMUD service area customer; all industrial wastewater discharges are regulated by EBMUD, not the City.
- **Governing ordinance**: EBMUD Wastewater Control Ordinance No. 311 (current consolidated version: 2024). Establishes local discharge limits and pretreatment standards.
- **Permit types** issued by EBMUD:
  - Industrial Facilities / Groundwater Discharge Permit (for categorical industries and high-concern pollutants)
  - Limited Term Discharge Permit (short-duration, limited volume)
  - Estimated Wastewater Volume Permit (< 20% of incoming water discharged)
  - Specialty permits for cannabis, dental, restaurant/food service
- **Local discharge limits** (selected): Arsenic 2 mg/L, Cadmium 1 mg/L, Copper 5 mg/L, Lead 2 mg/L, Mercury 0.05 mg/L, pH ≥ 5.5 SU.
- **Categorical industries** subject to additional 40 CFR 400–471 pretreatment standards; stricter of local vs. federal applies.
- **Berkeley municipal code reference**: BMC § 17.16.020 (no depositing refuse in sanitary sewers); § 17.16.050 (sewer construction).
- Apply: download application at https://www.ebmud.com/wastewater/commercial-waste/permits-and-wastewater-discharges
  - EBMUD Wastewater permitting line: (510) 287-1651
  - EBMUD general: 1-866-403-2683 | 375 11th Street, Oakland, CA 94607
- Source: https://www.ebmud.com/wastewater/commercial-waste/permits-and-wastewater-discharges
- Source (ordinance + limits): https://www.ebmud.com/wastewater/collection-treatment/wastewater-control-ordinance-discharge-limits

### 4. Stormwater — MS4 / Industrial Stormwater

- **MS4 co-permittee**: Berkeley is one of 17 co-permittees under the **Alameda Countywide Clean Water Program (ACCWP)**, regulated under the San Francisco Bay Regional Water Board's **Municipal Regional Stormwater NPDES Permit** (MRP).
- **Local ordinance**: **BMC Chapter 17.20** — "Discharge of Non-Stormwater into the City's Storm Drain System — Reduction of Stormwater Pollution." Prohibits non-stormwater discharges; requires BMPs; references City NPDES Permit No. CA0029831.
- ACCWP consortium contact and industrial/commercial program information: https://www.cityofberkeley.info/ContentDisplay.aspx?id=27036 (redirects to berkeleyca.gov)
- TMD (Toxics Management Division) also performs industrial site stormwater pollution prevention inspections in Berkeley.
- Industrial facilities with their own NPDES General Industrial Permit file directly with State Water Board via SMARTS portal; the City enforces local ordinance BMC 17.20 as co-permittee.
- Source: https://berkeley.municipal.codes/BMC/17.20.070
- Source (ACCWP): https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/rwqcb2/water_issues/programs/stormwater/

### 5. Zoning / Land Use — Industrial Districts

- **Zoning code**: **BMC Title 23** (Baseline Zoning Ordinance, BZO). The 2021 BZO replaced the prior Title 23 and renamed legacy M-1/M-2 districts.
- **Manufacturing district names** (current BZO):
  - **M** — Manufacturing
  - **MM** — Mixed Manufacturing
  - **MU-LI** — Mixed Use–Light Industrial (BMC § 23.206.080): prioritizes light manufacturing, protects larger industrial spaces from incompatible encroachment.
  - **MU-R** — Mixed Use–Residential (BMC § 23.206.090): transitional district between residential and manufacturing areas; allows light industrial/live-work.
  - **M-RD** — Manufacturing–Research & Development (established 2025).
- **Allowed uses and permit triggers**: BMC Table 23.206-1. Manufacturing uses within 150 ft of a residential use require an **Administrative Use Permit (AUP)** even when otherwise allowed by right.
- **Use Permit (CUP)**: Larger or higher-impact uses require a Use Permit issued by the **Zoning Adjustments Board (ZAB)** after public hearing.
  - Apply: Planning@berkeleyca.gov | subject: "Electronic Zoning Submittal - [address]"
  - ZAB Secretary: zab@cityofberkeley.info | 1947 Center Street, 2nd Floor, Berkeley CA 94704
  - ZAB meets 2nd and 4th Thursday, 7:00 pm.
- **Protected industrial uses** (MM + MU-LI): BMC Table 23.206-5 — manufacture, assembly, processing, repair, testing, storage, display, distribution of goods.
- **Research zoning / parcel lookup**: https://berkeleyca.gov/construction-development/land-use-development/research-zoning-permits-and-zone-designations
- Source: https://berkeley.municipal.codes/BMC/23
- Source (manufacturing districts): https://berkeley.municipal.codes/BMC/23.206.080
- Source (use permit process): https://berkeleyca.gov/construction-development/permits-design-parameters/permit-types/use-permit
