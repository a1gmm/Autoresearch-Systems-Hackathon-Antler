## Zoning Code Location

Industrial zoning for unincorporated Sierra County is governed by **Title 15 (Zoning)** of the Sierra County Code, specifically **Chapter 15.12 — Zoning Districts**, Section **15.12.150 — IN Industrial District**.

Full code: https://sierra.county.codes/SCC/15
Chapter 15.12 districts index: https://sierra.county.codes/SCC/15.12

## Industrial District: IN (Industrial)

### Purpose

The IN Industrial District is intended to accommodate areas designated industrial in the General Plan that are appropriate for:

- Production, repair, distribution, and warehousing of goods and equipment that may involve use or storage of **hazardous materials** or that may produce visible gases, particulates, steam heat, odor, dust, or excessive noise.
- A limited group of business, professional, research and technical, light manufacturing, light industrial, and commercial uses that are clean and quiet, in a planned setting with extensive landscaping or open space.

### Key Development Standards

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Open Space | Minimum **25%** of total project site area maintained as open space with approved landscaping plan |
| Site Plan | Required for all development proposals in IN district — must show structures, parking lots, loading areas, driveways, pathways, landscaped areas, lighting, signing, fencing/screening |

### Hazardous Materials Requirement (EHS-Relevant)

> "Operations requiring the use or storage of hazardous or dangerous materials shall prepare a **hazardous waste business plan** as part of the use permit application."

This means any industrial use permit (CUP) application involving hazmat must include an HMBP consistent with Sierra County CUPA requirements (administered by Sierra County Environmental Health). The use permit and HMBP processes run concurrently.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

Industrial uses in the IN district requiring a use permit are processed by the **Sierra County Planning Department**:

- Planning Department: 100 Courthouse Square #200 (or 101 Courthouse Square), Downieville, CA 95936
- Phone: (530) 289-3698

## Notes

- Sierra County is heavily federally managed (Tahoe NF, Plumas NF, Humboldt-Toiyabe NF). Industrial siting on or adjacent to federal land requires USFS coordination — county IN zoning does not apply to federal land parcels.
- The City of Loyalton has its own zoning ordinance separate from the county code. Industrial uses within Loyalton city limits are governed by city zoning, not SCC Title 15.
