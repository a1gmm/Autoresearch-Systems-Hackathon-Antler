## Governing ordinance

**Zoning Ordinance No. 424**, adopted October 19, 2021 by the Loyalton City Council.
Codified as **Loyalton Municipal Code Chapter 12.08** ("Zoning").

The ordinance establishes six principal zone districts; industrial land is split into two tiers:

| District code | Name | Chapter |
|--------------|------|---------|
| **M-1** | Light Industrial Zone District | Ch. 12.08.10 |
| **M-2** | Heavy Industrial Zone District | Ch. 12.08.11 |

## M-1 — Light Industrial Zone District (Ch. 12.08.10)

### Purpose (§ 10-1)
M-1 is intended for areas where **manufacturing, heavy commercial uses, and large
administrative facilities** are the desirable predominant uses.

### Principal permitted uses (§ 10-2) — by right, no CUP required

Representative uses include:
- Administrative, business, and professional offices
- Agricultural product processing; agricultural supplies/equipment sales
- Alcoholic beverage sales (on-site)
- Automotive repair; automobile service stations
- Bottling plants; clothing manufacturers
- Carpentry and cabinet-making shops; electric component assembly and manufacturing
- Contractor's yards; equipment rental yards
- Fabrication of wood, metal, and fiber products
- Fire stations; government vehicle repair/storage yards
- Handicraft manufacture
- Light industrial uses within an enclosed building with **no noticeable noise, odors, or vibrations at the property line**
- Manufacturing (within enclosed buildings) of electrical/electronic equipment, household appliances, and metalworking
- Motor repair; paint booths; plumbing shops; pump sales and repair
- Propane sales; public utility buildings
- Research and development facilities; recycling facilities
- Recreational vehicle storage; storage warehouses
- Restaurants and outdoor eating areas; produce stands
- Towing services; tractor sales/equipment yards; truck storage, repair, and distribution centers
- Similar uses determined by City Council written findings (§ 10-2(A)(34))

### Conditional use permit (CUP) required uses (§ 10-3)

Uses subject to first obtaining a CUP from the City Council include (representative list):
- Uses generating noticeable noise, odors, or vibrations at the property line
- Heavy commercial uses beyond the M-1 by-right list
- Any use the City Council determines requires additional review for compatibility

## M-2 — Heavy Industrial Zone District (Ch. 12.08.11)

### Purpose (§ 11-1)
M-2 is intended to provide areas for **heavy industrial and manufacturing uses** that can
locate away from the restrictive influences of non-industrial uses, while maintaining an
environment free from offensive or objectionable noise, dust, or other nuisances.

### Permitted uses by right (§ 11-2)

All M-2 by-right uses must be conducted within a completely enclosed building, with up to 100%
of gross floor area for outdoor storage within a completely screened area on the same lot, and
**not exceeding 6,000 sq ft gross floor area per use or 12,000 sq ft total gross floor area**:

- All uses permitted in M-1 and C-3 zones (incorporated by reference)
- Wholesale sales, storage, and distribution centers (with incidental on-site retail)
- Truck terminals and truck repair
- Contractors' equipment storage yards and equipment rental yards
- Boat manufacturing and repair
- Sale of pre-sized rock for ornamental/monument uses (no on-site excavation, crushing, or sorting)
- Industrial and residential accessory uses and structures
- One security guard/night watchman quarters (incidental to commercial or manufacturing use); or accessory dwelling per Part requirements
- One administrative office (incidental to commercial or manufacturing use)
- Other heavy commercial uses of similar character

## CUP / Use Permit process

Use Permits (Conditional Use Permits) are issued by the **City Council** following a public hearing.
Appeals of any City Council decision on a CUP may be filed in writing with the City Clerk within
**15 days** of notification of the decision (Loyalton Municipal Code § 1.24.010).

## Administration and enforcement

- Zone changes require notification to the County Assessor and owner of record (§ 3-1).
- Permits issued in conflict with ordinance provisions are prohibited (§ 3-2).
- Violation of zoning provisions constitutes a public nuisance (§ 3-3).
- Penalty for violation: § 3-5 (misdemeanor or infraction per Loyalton Municipal Code Part 1).
