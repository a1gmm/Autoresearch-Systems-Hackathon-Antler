## Adopted fire code

The Loyalton Municipal Code (Part 10, Chapter 10.04, § 10.04.090) lists the codes adopted
by reference. As of the July 2024 codification the listed codes are:

- Uniform Building Code, **1997 edition** (ICBO)
- **Uniform Fire Code, 1997 edition** (ICBO) — § 10.04.090(D)
- Uniform Mechanical Code, 1997 edition
- Uniform Housing Code, 1997 edition
- National Electric Code, 1997 edition
- Uniform Plumbing Code, 1997 edition
- Uniform Sign Code, 1997 edition

> **[VERIFY]** The municipal code's codified text still cites the 1997 UFC. California adopted
> the California Fire Code (CBC Title 24, Part 9) statewide; the 2025 CFC (based on IFC 2024)
> took effect **January 1, 2026** statewide. As a California city, Loyalton is required to comply
> with the current CBC/CFC edition regardless of whether the local municipal code has been
> updated. Confirm with Loyalton City Hall or the Fire Chief whether a local ordinance formally
> adopting the 2022 or 2025 CFC has been passed and recorded; none was found online as of
> this research date.

## AHJ (Authority Having Jurisdiction)

**City of Loyalton Fire Department** — the city's own all-volunteer municipal fire department
at 206 Front Street, Loyalton, CA 96118.  Phone: (530) 993-6751.

- The Fire Chief (or designated officer) is authorized to issue fire permits and notices
  (Loyalton Municipal Code § 6.08.020).
- **Do not confuse** with Sierra County Fire Protection District #1, which serves surrounding
  unincorporated areas and has a direct-service contract relationship but is **not** the AHJ
  inside city limits.
- CAL FIRE Nevada-Yuba-Placer Unit covers SRA wildland zones adjacent to the city.

## Local fire permit / controlled-burn rules (§ 6.08)

Chapter 6.08 ("Fires and Fireworks") of the Loyalton Municipal Code:

| Section | Provision |
|---------|-----------|
| 6.08.010 | Permit required for all controlled burning outside approved incinerators or fire pits |
| 6.08.020 | Fire Chief or designee issues permits; orders served in writing |
| 6.08.030 | Controlled burning in outdoor grills/pits allowed under continuous supervision; garbage, hair, animal waste, and malodorous materials prohibited |
| 6.08.040 | Permissive burning hours set by Fire Chief |
| 6.08.050 | Approved incinerators |
| 6.08.060 | Fireworks of any manner prohibited in city limits |

## Statewide CFC override

Per California Health & Safety Code § 18938 et seq. and the California Building Standards Law,
cities must enforce the current edition of the California Building Standards Code. The 2025 CFC
supersedes any locally-codified older UFC reference for structural fire safety, sprinklers, high-piled
storage, hazardous materials, etc. Local amendments must be filed with the California Building
Standards Commission (CBSC) within 180 days of adoption.
