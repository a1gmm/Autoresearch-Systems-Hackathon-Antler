## CUPA agency

The City of Loyalton is **not** its own CUPA. All Unified Program elements for businesses
within Loyalton city limits are administered by:

**Sierra County Environmental Health** — the countywide CUPA, certified by CalEPA.

## Programs administered

Sierra County Environmental Health administers all six Unified Program elements:

1. **HMBP** — Hazardous Material Business Plan (§ 25500 et seq. H&SC); county fee $178 + state surcharge [VERIFY current fee]
2. **UST** — Underground Storage Tanks; county fee $214/tank + state surcharge $20/tank [VERIFY]
3. **APSA** — Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (tanks ≥ 1,320 gal aggregate); county fee $99/facility + state surcharge $26/tank [VERIFY]
4. **CalARP** — California Accidental Release Prevention Program; county fee $397 + state surcharge $370/business [VERIFY]
5. **Hazardous Waste Generator** — Very Small, Small, and Large quantity generators; add-on fees $45.50–$147 [VERIFY]
6. **Tiered Permitting** (DTSC-delegated) — [VERIFY whether Sierra County has DTSC TP delegation]

## Permit process

- Businesses submit electronically via **CERS** (California Environmental Reporting System).
- Sierra County CUPA staff conduct facility walkthroughs to confirm program categorization.
- Annual Consolidated Permit issued after walkthrough and payment of annual single-system fee.
- Late renewal penalty: 10% of regular fees, accrued monthly.
- Non-compliance activities and plan reviews: $91.00/hr [VERIFY].

## Geographic scope

Sierra County Environmental Health serves **all of Sierra County** including the City of Loyalton
(the only incorporated municipality in the county). The CUPA office is physically located in Loyalton.
