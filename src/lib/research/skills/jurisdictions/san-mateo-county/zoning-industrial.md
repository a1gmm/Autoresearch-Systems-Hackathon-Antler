## Industrial Zoning Districts

### M-1 Light Industrial District (Countywide and M-1/NFO Variant)

The county's primary industrial district designation is **M-1** (Light Industrial). A documented variant, **M-1/NFO** (Mixed Use Industrial / North Fair Oaks District), applies within the North Fair Oaks unincorporated community and carries the same M-1 use regulations with mixed-use overlays.

**Regulatory source for M-1/NFO:** County Ordinance Code, **Chapter 17 (Section 6276 et seq.)** — "M-1/NFO District." This is legacy Ordinance Code text that was operative pre-Title 8 reorganization; confirm current Title 8 chapter mapping with Planning at (650) 363-1825.

Some parcels carry layered designations such as **M-1/Edison/NFO** which, despite multiple labels, function as a single zoning district.

### M-1/NFO District — Purpose (§ 6276.1)

1. Light manufacturing with no more than moderate impact on surrounding area.
2. Compatible mix of trades/services, transportation, commercial, public, residential, communication, and institutional uses.
3. Mixed-use development for a vibrant, livable environment.
4. Protection of industrial viability by restricting incompatible uses.
5. Implementation of the General Plan and North Fair Oaks Community Plan policies.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) / Use Permit Process

**Six-step process:**

1. **New Business Zoning Conformance Review** — Submit form + site plan + floor plan to Planning Counter; staff evaluates zoning compliance.
2. **Advisory Body Review** — Required in North Fair Oaks and Midcoast areas.
3. **CEQA Environmental Review** — Scoping and determination; adds 2–12 months if full EIR required.
4. **Public Notice** — Property owners within 300 feet notified.
5. **Public Hearing Decision** — Zoning Hearing Officer (minor permits) or **Planning Commission** (major permits) decides.
6. **10-business-day Appeal Period.**

**Fees:** $7,700–$14,000 (varies by complexity, not including CEQA costs).
**Processing time:** 4–6 months (add 2–12 months for CEQA review).

**Appeal chain:**
- Staff/Zoning Hearing Officer → Planning Commission.
- Planning Commission → Board of Supervisors (final, non-appealable).

## Hazardous Materials Use in Industrial Zones

CFC (via SMCFD ORD-2025-003) restricts certain uses to industrial zones:
- Class I/II liquid storage in aboveground tanks — commercial, industrial, or agricultural zones only.
- Flammable cryogenic storage outside buildings — industrial zones only.
- LPG aggregate storage > 2,000 gal water capacity — prohibited in central business districts and residential zones.

## City of San Mateo M-1 Districts (Incorporated City Reference)

For the City of San Mateo proper (not unincorporated county), manufacturing is governed by City Municipal Code **Chapter 27.56 — M1 Districts — Manufacturing**, with performance standards covering noise (§ 27.56.090), smoke/particulates (§ 27.56.100), odor (§ 27.56.110), vibration (§ 27.56.120), toxic substances (§ 27.56.130), glare/heat (§ 27.56.140), and fire hazards (§ 27.56.150).
