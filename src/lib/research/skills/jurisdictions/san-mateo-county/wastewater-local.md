## POTW Landscape — Fragmented by City/District

San Mateo County has no single countywide POTW or pretreatment program. Jurisdiction depends on which sewer service provider collects the wastewater:

## Silicon Valley Clean Water (SVCW) — Primary Mid-Peninsula POTW

**NPDES Permit:** CA0038369 (current order: **R2-2023-0003**, adopted 2023-03-08, effective 2023-05-01, expires **2028-04-30**). Also regulated under CA0038849 (mercury/PCBs) and CA0038873 (nutrients).

**Regulations document:** *Regulations of Silicon Valley Clean Water* (SVCW, 2005, as amended). Local limits appear in Article II, Sections 2.3 (Wastewater Strength Limitations / Specific Wastes Prohibited) and 2.4 (Specific User Limitations).

### Who Needs an Industrial Wastewater Permit (SVCW)

Mandatory permit required for:
- Facilities subject to federal **categorical pretreatment standards** (40 CFR Parts 405–471).
- Facilities meeting SVCW's definition of **Significant Industrial User (SIU)**.

Also available:
- **Non-Routine Discharge Authorization** (one-time or short-term discharge).
- **Low Volume Discharge Authorization** (continued low-volume discharge).
- **Septic Waste / Restaurant Grease** hauler permits.
- **Groundwater Discharge** permit (groundwater to sanitary sewer otherwise prohibited).

Apply: https://svcw.org/businesses/permit-applications/mandatory-wastewater-discharge-permit-application/

## City of San Mateo — Municipal Pretreatment Program

**Code:** City of San Mateo Municipal Code, **Chapter 7.38** — Industrial Pretreatment.

**Permit process:** Apply to City of San Mateo Public Works, Attn: Regulatory Compliance, 1949 Pacific Blvd, San Mateo CA 94403. Phone: (650) 522-7300.
Portal: https://www.cityofsanmateo.org/1079/Wastewater-Discharge-Permits

**Who must apply:** Any industrial facility required under federal law and those the City believes warrant periodic inspection/monitoring. Categories: product manufacturing, commercial food preparation, photographic image processing, groundwater remediation.

### City of San Mateo Specific Discharge Prohibitions (§ 7.38.120)

| Parameter | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Flammable waste flash point | Must be ≥ 140°F (60°C) closed cup |
| LEL meter reading | ≤ 5% lower explosive limit (two readings) or ≤ 10% single reading |
| pH | Must be 5.0–10.5 S.U. |
| Heat (at POTW) | Must not raise POTW influent > 40°C (104°F) |
| Floatable Oil & Grease | ≤ 20 mg/L |
| Total Oil & Grease | ≤ 200 mg/L |
| Dissolved sulfides | ≤ 0.1 mg/L |

### City of San Mateo Required Pretreatment Systems (§ 7.38.100)

The City may require a pretreatment system when necessary to restrict/prevent discharge of certain waste constituents. Mandatory by facility type:
- **Food service:** Grease removal system serving all grease-generating drains; preferably external to building.
- **Steam cleaning / radiator flushing:** Closed-loop water recycling required; no direct discharge to sewer.
- **Photo-processing:** Electrolytic silver recovery unit + ion exchange + pH neutralization.
- **Dental clinics:** Amalgam capture device before drain.
- **Commercial/manufacturing floor drains:** Gravity separation interceptor required.

All pretreatment systems require City approval; engineering-designed systems need plans signed by licensed engineer. Control manholes required for inspection, flow measurement, and sampling.
