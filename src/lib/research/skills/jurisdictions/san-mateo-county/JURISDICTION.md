## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **Agency:** San Mateo County Environmental Health Services (EHS) — designated CUPA by the CA Secretary for Environmental Protection in 1996. Single countywide CUPA covering all incorporated cities and unincorporated areas.
- **Local Ordinance:** County Ordinance Code **Chapter 4.92** (Storage of Hazardous Substances). Revised August 10, 2018 to consolidate all six Unified Program elements under one chapter, formalize CUPA permit requirement, establish civil/administrative enforcement, and authorize unannounced inspections during normal business hours (after-hours inspections billed at hourly rate).
- **Six Unified Program elements administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP / Community Right-to-Know), Hazardous Waste Generator, Tiered Permitting, Underground Storage Tank (UST), California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP), and Aboveground Petroleum Storage (APSA).
- **Electronic Reporting:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) at cers.calepa.ca.gov; online permit management at MyEHS Portal — https://smcehs.my.site.com/s/
- **2025 Fee Schedule** (Board-approved Dec 10, 2024; 15% increase effective Jan 9, 2025; additional 10% delayed increase; annual CPI adjustment effective Jan 1, 2026):
  - HMBP: $458 (low hazard) to $1,115 (>32,000 gal / 224,000 lb stored)
  - Hazardous Waste Generator: $343 (≤27 gal/100 kg/yr) to $4,433 (>119,525 gal/453,000 kg/yr); Tiered Permitting (CE/CA/PBR): $286–$1,430
  - UST: $1,144 first tank, $429 each additional; installation plan review $3,432; abandonment/removal $1,859
  - CalARP: Program 1 $901 / Program 2 $1,602 / Program 3 $1,702
  - Special/after-hours services: $286/hr
  - State surcharges (CUPA, CalARP, UST, APSA) collected and remitted to state per annual Secretary determination
- **Contact:** (650) 372-6200

### 2. Fire AHJ — Fire Code

- **No single countywide fire department.** AHJ depends on municipality or unincorporated area:
  - **San Mateo Consolidated Fire Department (SMCFD)** — JPA serving City of San Mateo, Belmont, and Foster City (consolidated 2019). Issues fire permits and plan reviews for its jurisdiction.
  - **North County Fire Authority (NCFA)** — JPA serving Brisbane, Daly City, Pacifica. https://northcountyfire.gov/
  - **Redwood City Fire Department** — municipal (county seat, ISO Class 1). https://www.redwoodcity.org/departments/fire-department
  - **South San Francisco Fire Department** — municipal. https://www.ssfca.gov/Departments/Fire-Department
  - **CAL FIRE / San Mateo County Fire (CZU Unit)** — serves unincorporated areas. https://www.smcgov.org/san-mateo-county-fire/fire-code
- **Adopted Code (SMCFD):** **2025 California Fire Code** (Cal. Code of Regs., Title 24, Part 9, based on the 2024 International Fire Code) plus the 2024 International Wildland Urban Interface Code with 2025 CA WUI Code amendments — adopted by **Ordinance No. ORD-2025-003**. Also Ordinances ORD-2025-001 and ORD-2025-002 adopted concurrently. Prior cycle: ORD-2022-001, ORD-2023-001.
- **Local amendments:** ORD-2025-003 Part III modifies CFC Sections 101.1, 102.3 (change of occupancy triggers sprinkler requirement for more-hazardous uses), 102.6 (historic buildings), 103.1 (agency creation). Chapters/appendices not explicitly listed are only enforceable if adopted by the State Fire Marshal.
- **Plan submissions (SMCFD):** Email to Plansubmist@smcfire.org; Plan Check Application required.
- **Master Fee Schedule** for SMCFD (updated Dec 16, 2025) — download from https://smcfire.ca.gov/documents-forms-policies

### 3. Sewer / Industrial Wastewater Pretreatment (POTW)

- **County POTW context:** San Mateo County Department of Public Works manages 10 Maintenance and Sanitation Districts providing wastewater collection for unincorporated areas; treatment is handled by regional POTWs (e.g., South Bayside System Authority — SBSA; Estero Municipal Improvement District). Incorporated cities operate their own sewer programs (e.g., City of San Mateo Chapter 7.38 — industrial pretreatment standards, categorical pretreatment, local limits for toxics, baseline monitoring reports for new regulated dischargers).
- **County Sewer Services (unincorporated):** Contact (650) 363-4100 / sewers@smcgov.org; 555 County Center, Redwood City, CA 94063.
- **Industrial Wastewater Permits (IWP):** For industrial dischargers in unincorporated SMC, contact the applicable sanitation district or County Public Works. City of San Mateo issues Wastewater Discharge Permits via https://www.cityofsanmateo.org/1079/Wastewater-Discharge-Permits (City Chapter 7.38 — pretreatment, specific discharge prohibitions including flammable waste <140°F closed cup flashpoint).
- **NPDES / Pretreatment oversight:** San Francisco Bay Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 2). Categorical pretreatment standards per 40 CFR Parts 405–471 apply.

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater

- **Program:** San Mateo Countywide Water Pollution Prevention Program (SMCWPPP) — partnership of C/CAG, all incorporated cities/towns, and the County. Regulated under the **Municipal Regional NPDES Stormwater Permit (MRP 3.0)**, Order No. **R2-2022-0018**, issued by the SF Bay RWQCB; amended October 2023.
- **Local Ordinance (unincorporated county):** **County Ordinance No. 4913** (adopted November 4, 2025), codified as **Chapter 4.100 — Stormwater Management and Discharge Control**. Prohibits non-stormwater discharges to storm drains; requires compliance with trash control and pollutant inspections on private property. Commercial/industrial enforcement governed by the County's Commercial and Industrial Business Inspection Enforcement Response Plan.
- **New Development Triggers:** Projects creating/replacing ≥5,000 sq ft of impervious surface must implement Low Impact Development (LID) stormwater treatment measures; Operation and Maintenance Agreement required before final building permit approval; County inspects LID facilities every 5 years.
- **Resources:** https://flowstobay.org (C.3 Regulated Projects Guide, technical specs)
- **Illicit discharge reporting:** https://www.smcsustainability.org/water-protection/keeping-our-waterways-clean/stormwater-ordinance/reporting-illicit-discharge/

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts and CUP Process

- **Code restructure (Oct 8, 2024):** Board of Supervisors ordinance repealed Divisions VI–VIII of the legacy Ordinance Code and replaced them with **Title 8 – Zoning & Development Regulations** (also called the Development Code), **Title 9 – Subdivision Regulations**, and **Title 10 – Building Regulations**.
- **Industrial districts:** Zoning district "M-1" (Light Industrial) is confirmed; full list of M-type districts is in the Title 8 Development Code tables. Some light industrial uses (warehouses, storage, industrial office) require a **Conditional Use Permit (CUP)** / Use Permit.
- **CUP Process:**
  - Submit New Business Zoning Conformance Review form with proposed site plan/floor plan to Planning Counter; staff evaluates zoning compliance.
  - Full Use Permit: 6-step process — Initial Review → Advisory Body → CEQA Environmental Review → Public Notice (property owners within 300 ft) → Public Hearing (Zoning Hearing Officer or Planning Commission) → 10-business-day appeal period.
  - Appeals: Zoning Hearing Officer decisions → Planning Commission; Planning Commission decisions → Board of Supervisors (final).
  - **Estimated fees:** $7,700–$14,000; processing time 4–6 months (add 2–12 months for environmental review).
- **Zoning lookup:** GIS map at https://www.smcgov.org/planning/get-zoning-information; Current Planning staff (650) 363-1825.
- **Full code:** https://library.municode.com/ca/san_mateo_county/codes/code_of_ordinances

## Notes

- GOTCHA (CUPA placement): San Mateo's CUPA sits in **Environmental Health Services** (County Health), not in a fire department or Public Works. It is a **single countywide CUPA** — incorporated cities (incl. those with their own fire departments) are still regulated by the county CUPA for HMBP/hazwaste/UST/APSA/CalARP.
- GOTCHA (fire fragmentation): There is **no countywide fire department**. The AHJ is the city's own department, a JPA (SMCFD, NCFA), or CAL FIRE/County Fire for unincorporated areas. SMCFD's ORD-2025-003 applies only within Belmont, Foster City, and the City of San Mateo.
- GOTCHA (HazMat response vs. CUPA): Emergency HazMat response is contracted to the **Belmont FPD HazMat Team** — distinct from the County EHS CUPA that handles permitting/inspection.
- GOTCHA (zoning code transition): The legacy Ordinance Code Division VI/VII/VIII was replaced by Titles 8–10 effective 2024. References to old division/chapter numbers in older permits or literature should be cross-checked against the new Title 8 Development Code.
