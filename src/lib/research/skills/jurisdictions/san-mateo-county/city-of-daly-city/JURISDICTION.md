## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials / Hazardous Waste

- **CUPA agency:** San Mateo County Environmental Health Services (EHS) — designated countywide CUPA in 1996. Daly City is **not** its own CUPA; all CUPA programs are administered by the county.
- **Programs covered:** HMBP, Hazardous Waste Generator (tiered permitting: CEL, PBR, CA, CE-SQ, CE-SW), UST, CalARP, Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA).
- **Electronic reporting:** Businesses submit HMBPs via CERS (California Environmental Reporting System). Permits, invoices, and permit management are handled through the county's **MyEHS Portal**.
- **HMBP threshold:** Submission required within 30 days of handling/storing a hazardous material at or above minimum reportable quantities. Annual certification required; full triennial re-submittal required every 3 years.
- **Fee schedule (effective 1/1/2026):** HMBP by quantity tier — Low-hazard/multi-program sites $525/yr; 55–219 gal/$721; 220–1,200 gal/$787; 1,201–3,500 gal/$853; 3,501–7,000 gal/$919; 7,001–16,000 gal/$1,017; 16,001–32,000 gal/$1,083; >32,000 gal/$1,279. Hazardous waste generator (up to 27 gal/100 kg/yr) $393/yr; 27–324 gal/$820/yr; 325–1,583 gal/$1,968/yr. UST (first tank) $1,312/yr; each additional tank $492/yr. CalARP Program 1 $1,033/yr; Program 2 $1,837/yr; Program 3 $1,952/yr. Fee schedule revised 1/8/2026.
- **Contact:** (650) 372-6200 | 2000 Alameda de las Pulgas, Suite 100, San Mateo, CA 94403

### 2. Fire AHJ — North County Fire Authority (NCFA)

- **AHJ:** North County Fire Authority (NCFA) — a Joint Powers Authority serving Daly City, Brisbane, and Pacifica (formed 2003). There is no standalone Daly City Fire Department.
- **Adopted fire code:** **2022 California Fire Code** (Title 19 CCR) with local amendments codified in **Municipal Code Chapter 15.32**. Adopted by ordinance repealing and replacing Ch. 15.32 as part of the 2022 California Building Standards Code cycle.
- **Local amendments:** Chapter 15.32 of the Daly City Municipal Code contains local amendments consistent with Health & Safety Code §§ 17958.7 and 18941.5; Findings of Fact adopted per state requirements. Local amendments are "more restrictive in nature than those adopted by the State."
- **Permit types (NCFA):** Fire sprinkler systems, fire alarm systems, fixed extinguishing systems, other fire protection systems. Permit application form FD-301. Permits issued per California Health & Safety Code Ch. 9, Part 3, Division 13.
- **NCFA contact:** (650) 800-6580 | 25 Park Place, Suite B, Brisbane, CA 94005 | [email protected]

### 3. Industrial Wastewater / Pretreatment (POTW)

- **POTW operator:** **North San Mateo County Sanitation District** — a subsidiary district of the City of Daly City. The Department of Water and Wastewater Resources manages daily operations.
- **Treatment plant:** 153 Lake Merced Boulevard, Daly City, CA 94015.
- **Sewer use ordinance:** North San Mateo County Sanitation District Code (codification of general/permanent ordinances through Ordinance 79, passed September 14, 1992). Key chapters:
  - **Ch. 1.24** — Use of Public Sewers: prohibited infiltration/inflow, prohibited discharges, discharges that may be prohibited by district engineer, pretreatment facility maintenance requirements, grease interceptors, control manholes.
  - **Ch. 1.40** — Administration: discharge reports, notification of changed discharge, monitoring programs, inspection facilities, pretreatment facilities.
  - **Ch. 1.44** — Enforcement: notice/remedy, disconnection, misdemeanor penalties, appeal.
- **IWP process:** Contact Department of Water and Wastewater Resources at (650) 991-8200. No online IWP portal identified; apply through department directly.
- **Utility billing portal:** utilitybilling.dalycity.org

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater

- **MS4 permit:** City of Daly City holds NPDES Permit No. **CAS612008** (San Francisco Bay Regional Water Quality Control Board, issued October 11, 2023). Permit is the Municipal Regional Stormwater NPDES Permit (MRP).
- **Local ordinance:** Municipal Code **Title 14** — Stormwater and Drainage Code. Prohibits illegal discharges to storm drains consistent with MRP requirements.
- **C.3/C.6 requirements:** New development and redevelopment projects must implement BMPs per MRP C.3 provisions to reduce stormwater pollution and manage runoff flow/volume/duration.
- **Program partner:** San Mateo Countywide Water Pollution Prevention Program (www.flowstobay.org) administers the regional MRP compliance program.
- **SMC EHS stormwater fee (2026):** Annual inspection (every 2 yrs) $263; every 5 yrs $79; biannual ASBS inspection $328.
- **City planning contact:** (650) 991-8033 | 333 90th Street, Daly City, CA 94015

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts & CUP Process

- **Zoning code:** Municipal Code **Title 17 — Zoning**. Full ordinance also at dalycity.org/zo.
- **Zoning districts chapter:** Chapter 17.06 — Zoning Districts.
- **Industrial district:** Single **M (Industrial District)** zone — codified in **Chapter 17.22**. No M-1/M-2 split identified; Daly City uses a single industrial "M" designation.
- **Use permit chapter:** **Chapter 17.44 — Use Permits** (conditional use permit process).
- **Key commercial/mixed-use zones relevant to light industrial:** C-1, C-2, BC (Business/Commercial); C-MU (Commercial Mixed Use, Mission Street and Geneva Avenue corridors). Also: C-O (Office Commercial), BOC (Business/Office/Commercial).
- **CUP process:** Administered by the Planning Division. Contact: (650) 991-8033 | 333 90th Street, Daly City, CA 94015.
- **Zoning map:** Electronic zoning map available at dalycity.org; physical copies available from Planning Division (document preparation charge applies).

## Notes / Gotchas

- GOTCHA (fire): Daly City's fire AHJ is the **North County Fire Authority** JPA, not a "Daly City Fire Department." The same JPA also serves Brisbane and Pacifica.
- GOTCHA (CUPA fees): The SMC EHS fee schedule was revised **1/8/2026** with rates effective **1/1/2026**. Always pull the live fee document before quoting a number.
- GOTCHA (sewer): The **North San Mateo County Sanitation District** is a subsidiary of the City of Daly City — it is NOT a regional independent district. The POTW operator is the city's own Department of Water and Wastewater Resources.
- GOTCHA (zoning industrial): Daly City has only a single "M" industrial zone, not the M-1/M-2 tiers common in other Bay Area cities. Verify current mapping before advising a client on site suitability.
