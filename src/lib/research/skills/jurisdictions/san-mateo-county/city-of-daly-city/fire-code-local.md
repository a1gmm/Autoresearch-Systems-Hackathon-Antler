> Research reference for the Daly City fire code AHJ, adopted code edition, and local amendments.
> The fire AHJ is the North County Fire Authority (NCFA) JPA — there is no standalone Daly City Fire Department.
> As of 2026-01-01 Daly City adopted the 2025 California Fire Code via Ordinance No. 1461; the prior cycle (2022 CFC) was adopted via the unnumbered 2022 building code ordinance (Exhibit L).

## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

- **AHJ:** North County Fire Authority (NCFA) — Joint Powers Authority serving Daly City, Brisbane, and Pacifica; formed 2003.
- **Address:** 25 Park Place, Suite B, Brisbane, CA 94005

## Adopted Fire Code

- **Current edition (effective 2026-01-01):** 2025 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9)
  - Adopted via **Ordinance No. 1461**, which adopted all 2025 California Building Standards Code titles simultaneously.
  - Municipal code chapter: **Chapter 15.32 — Fire Code** (repealed and replaced by the new ordinance).

- **Prior edition (2022 cycle):** 2022 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9) and 2021 International Fire Code adopted together.
  - Adoption ordinance: unnumbered 2022 ordinance (titled "AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF DALY CITY REPEALING AND REPLACING CHAPTERS 15.00, 15.08, 15.10, 15.14, 15.16, 15.20, 15.23, 15.24, 15.32, 15.60, & 15.65…").
  - Chapter 15.32 was repealed and replaced as **Exhibit L** of that ordinance.
  - Appendices adopted: B (Fire-Flow Requirements), C (Fire Hydrant Locations), D (Fire Apparatus Access Roads), F (Hazard Ranking), L (Firefighter Air Replenishment Systems).

## Local Amendments (from 2022 CFC Exhibit L, Ch. 15.32)

All amendments below were codified in the 2022 cycle Chapter 15.32; they carry forward into the 2025 adoption unless specifically superseded by Ordinance 1461.

### Operational Permits (§15.32.040 — amends CFC §105)

The fire code official may issue operational permits for all of §105.5.1–105.5.58, including these Daly City additions:
- **§105.5.55** — Christmas tree lots (seasonal sales).
- **§105.5.56** — Fire alarm systems in any building (exception: smoke detectors in 1- and 2-family dwellings).
- **§105.5.57** — Group I and Group E occupancies accommodating more than six persons.
- **§105.5.58** — Residential care / assisted-living facilities (Group R, Division 4).

### Sprinkler Requirements (§15.32.100 — replaces CFC §903.2)

Daly City's sprinkler requirement is **more restrictive than state baseline**:

| Occupancy / Trigger | Requirement |
|---|---|
| All new occupancies | Automatic sprinkler system (NFPA 13) throughout |
| Existing building altered/renovated/added to ≥50% of original gross area within any 5-year period | Entire building must be sprinklered |
| Detached garages <400 sq ft | Exempt |
| Detached carports and greenhouses | Exempt |
| Sheds and auxiliary structures <200 sq ft not used for human habitation | Exempt |
| Garbage compartments, chutes, linen rooms, dumbwaiter shafts, storage rooms (all occupancies except R-3) | Required |
| Condominium conversions | Required |

### Car Stacker Sprinklers (§15.32.110 — adds §903.2.22)

Parking garage areas with car stackers: wet-pipe system designed to Extra Hazard Group 2. Non-extended-coverage standard sidewall sprinklers (Ordinary Hazard) required under each parking level (≤80 sq ft/sprinkler).

### Sprinkler System Alarms (§15.32.120 — amends §903.4.2)

An approved **exterior strobe light** is required in addition to required audible alarm devices.

### Hydrant Distance (§15.32.080 — amends §507.5.1)

A portion of any building more than **150 feet** from a hydrant on a fire apparatus access road (measured around the exterior) requires on-site fire hydrants. Exception: R-3 and U occupancies — 250 ft.

### Fire Apparatus Access Roads (§15.32.210–250 — Appendix D additions)

- Access road must extend to within **50 feet** of at least one exterior door that can be opened from the outside (exception: 1- and 2-family dwellings or townhouses with 13D/13R sprinklers — may be increased to 150 ft).
- Any exterior wall of the first story shall be no more than **150 feet** from a fire department access road (measured around exterior).
- Access roads: minimum **13 ft 6 in** unobstructed vertical clearance.

### Address Marking and NFPA 704 Placards (§15.32.070 — amends §505.1)

Buildings must be placarded per **NFPA 704**; location and size determined by the fire marshal. Address numbers must be internally or externally illuminated in all new construction or when alterations occur.

### High-Rise Safety (§15.32.160 — adds §914.3.9)

All Group B and R occupancies with floors more than **75 feet above** the lowest fire department access level must be equipped with an approved **Firefighter Breathing Air Replenishment System** (per Appendix L).

### Fire Alarm Maintenance Contract (§15.32.150 — amends §907.8.4)

Operators of fire alarm systems must maintain an inspection/maintenance contract with a California DCA-licensed contractor. A current copy must be submitted to the fire department **annually** before the annual fire alarm permit is issued. Inspections per NFPA 72.

### Vegetation Management — Southern Hills Area (§15.32.260)

Fire chief has designated the Southern Hills area (northeast Daly City near San Bruno Mountain County Park — South Hill Blvd, Alta Vista Way, Oakridge St, surrounding areas) as a fire hazard zone. Property owners must: clear all flammable vegetation (gorse, grass, weeds, brush); maintain tree clearance 10 ft from chimney outlets; maintain chimney screens (openings ≤½ in); maintain roofs free of dead vegetation.

### Fireworks (§15.32.170–180 — amends §5608)

Sale and discharge of fireworks only as allowed by **Daly City Municipal Code Title 8, Chapter 8.24**. CFC Chapter 33 provisions subject to Ch. 8.24 where in conflict.

## Permit Application

- **Form:** FD-301 (Rev. Dec. '14) — NCFA Permit Application
- **Permit types on FD-301:** Fire Sprinklers, Fire Alarm, Fixed Extinguishing System, Other
- **Per:** California Health & Safety Code, Ch. 9, Part 3, Division 13
- **Note:** Permit becomes null and void if work not commenced within 180 days of issuance or suspended for more than 180 days.
