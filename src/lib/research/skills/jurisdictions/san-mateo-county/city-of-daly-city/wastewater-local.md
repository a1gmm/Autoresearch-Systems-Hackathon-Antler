## Governing Ordinance — District Code Structure

The **North San Mateo County Sanitation District Code** is the operative local sewer-use/pretreatment ordinance. It codifies general and permanent ordinances of the District (codified through Ordinance 79, passed September 14, 1992).

## Industrial Waste / Pretreatment Permit (IWP) Process

- **No online IWP portal** has been identified for NSMCSD.
- Contact the **Department of Water and Wastewater Resources** directly:
  - Address: 153 Lake Merced Boulevard, Daly City, CA 94015
  - Phone: (650) 991-8200
- Pretreatment program administration is governed by Ch. 1.40 of the District Code (discharge reports, monitoring programs, inspection facilities).
- District engineer has discretion under §1.24.030 to prohibit additional discharges beyond categorical prohibitions.

## Enforcement

Per Chapter 1.44 of the District Code:
- **Violation → Notice → Remedy** (§1.44.010)
- Violation constitutes a **public nuisance** (§1.44.020)
- District may **disconnect** the violating connection (§1.44.030)
- Violation is a **misdemeanor** (§1.44.060)
- **Appeal** process available (§1.44.080)

## GOTCHA

- The NSMCSD is a subsidiary district of the City of Daly City, not an independent regional agency. Billing and permit contact go through the city's Water and Wastewater Resources department, not a separate sanitation district office.
- The publicly available District Code PDF (Attachment C) is a table of contents only. Numerical discharge limits require contact with the district or review of the full internal codebook.
- The current NPDES permit (Order R2-2006-0068) was issued in 2006; a reissuance (NOE R2-2022-0036) was filed in December 2022 — the reissued order may have updated effluent limits. Verify current NPDES conditions before citing.
