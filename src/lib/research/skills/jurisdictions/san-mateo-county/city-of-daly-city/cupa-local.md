> Research reference for CUPA programs applicable to businesses in Daly City.
> Daly City is NOT its own CUPA. All CUPA programs are administered by San Mateo County Environmental Health Services (SMC EHS), designated as the countywide CUPA in 1996.

## CUPA Agency

- **Agency:** San Mateo County Environmental Health Services (SMC EHS)
- **Designated CUPA:** San Mateo County (countywide); Daly City is in-county with no independent CUPA status.
- **MyEHS Portal (permits, invoices, management):** https://smcehs.my.site.com/s/?language=en_US
- **CERS reporting:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — statewide platform for HMBP submissions.
- **Address:** 2000 Alameda de las Pulgas, Suite 100, San Mateo, CA 94403
- **Phone:** (650) 372-6200

## Local Hazardous Materials Ordinance

- **County ordinance:** San Mateo County Code **Chapter 4.92 — Storage of Hazardous Substances**
  - Chapter 4.92 was expanded (effective **August 10, 2018**) to consolidate all hazardous materials/waste programs previously governed by separate ordinances into one comprehensive code.
  - Formalizes the CUPA permit requirement and consolidates: HMBP, Hazardous Waste Generator, Tiered Permitting, UST, CalARP, and APSA programs.
  - Authorizes unannounced inspection during normal business hours; after-hours inspections (after 6 pm) incur fees.
  - Establishes civil and administrative enforcement structure.
  - Removes requirement that removed UST components always be disposed as hazardous waste.
- **Municode reference:** https://library.municode.com/ca/san_mateo_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT4SAHE_CH4.92HAMASTOR_4.92.190SE
- **Ordinance changes summary:** https://www.smchealth.org/post/hazardous-materials-ordinance-changes

> Note: Because Daly City is unincorporated into the countywide CUPA, there is no separate Daly City hazardous materials municipal code chapter. Chapter 4.92 of the San Mateo County Code is the operative local ordinance.

## Programs Administered by SMC EHS CUPA

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — Required for businesses handling/storing hazardous materials at or above minimum reportable quantities (MRQs):
   - Liquids: 55 gallons
   - Solids: 500 pounds
   - Compressed gases: 200 cubic feet (at STP)
   - Threshold per Cal. Health & Safety Code Division 20, Chapter 6.95.
   - Initial submission: within 30 days of handling/storing at or above MRQ.
   - Annual certification required; full triennial re-submittal every 3 years.
   - Significant operational changes: revise and resubmit within 30 days.

2. **Hazardous Waste Generator Program** — Tiered permitting: CEL (Conditionally Exempt Large Quantity Generator), PBR (Permit-by-Rule), CA (Conditional Authorization), CE-SQ (Conditionally Exempt Small Quantity), CE-SW (Conditionally Exempt Small Waste).

3. **Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program**

4. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — Programs 1, 2, and 3.

5. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)**

## Electronic Reporting

- **HMBP, HWG, UST, CalARP, APSA:** All submitted via CERS at https://cers.calepa.ca.gov/
- **Permit management, invoices, correspondence:** MyEHS Portal at https://smcehs.my.site.com/s/?language=en_US
- **Payment methods:** In-person, mail, phone, or online through the MyEHS Portal.
