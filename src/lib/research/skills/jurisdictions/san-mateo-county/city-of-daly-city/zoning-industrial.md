## M Industrial District — Uses

### Confirmed Facts About M District

- Daly City uses a **single M district** — no light/heavy manufacturing split (unlike many Bay Area cities with M-1/M-2).
- The M district is one of the city's least-common zones; industrial land is limited in Daly City.
- Adjacent commercial zones relevant to light-industrial and office/R&D uses: C-2 (Heavy Commercial, Ch. 17.20), BOC (BART Office Commercial, Ch. 17.17), C-O (Office Commercial, Ch. 17.16).
- Cannabis operations require the CC Combining District overlay (Ch. 17.56) in addition to the base zone.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process — Chapter 17.44

Chapter 17.44 governs all use permits (CUPs) in Daly City.

### Key Provisions (sourced from Ordinance No. [2026 draft] and JURISDICTION.md)

- **Administering body:** Planning Division — contact (650) 991-8033, 333 90th Street, Daly City, CA 94015.
- **Approval authority:** Planning Commission (standard CUPs); City Council for specified large-scale or sensitive uses.
- **Expiration:** A use permit **automatically terminates if not used within 5 years** from the date of approval (§17.44.100, as amended by the 2026 draft ordinance).
  - "Used" means the permittee has obtained a building permit and commenced construction, OR has actually commenced the permitted use on the premises.
  - Prior to the 2026 amendment, the expiration period was shorter — the 2026 draft extended it to 5 years.
- **Condominium exception:** Where a use permit is issued with a tentative map for a condominium project, the use permit terminates 1 year after the final map approval (§17.44.100 proviso).

### General Provisions Reference (§17.44.010)

The 2026 draft ordinance added a "building height" definition to the general provisions of Chapter 17.44 to cover all buildings not in the C-MU zone.

### Administrative Use Permits (Chapter 17.49)

Chapter 17.49 provides for administrative (staff-level) use permits, which may be available for lower-impact industrial uses. Contact the Planning Division to determine whether a project qualifies.

## Design Review — Chapter 17.45

Most development in Daly City also requires Design Review under Chapter 17.45:
- **Expiration:** A design review permit terminates if not used within **5 years** from date of approval (§17.45.150, amended 2026).
- Design review permits for condominiums expire 1 year after final map approval.

## GOTCHA

- Daly City has **only one industrial zone (M)**; there is no light-industrial vs. heavy-industrial split as in many Bay Area cities. A client needing M-1 (light industrial only) should verify that the specific use is permitted or conditionally permitted in Daly City's single M zone.
- Industrial land is scarce in Daly City (largely residential/commercial city). Verify parcel zoning against the current electronic zoning map before advising on site suitability.
- The Sullivan Corridor Specific Plan (Ch. 17.21) and BART-area combining districts (Ch. 17.15, 17.17, 17.29) may overlap with parcels near industrial areas — check all applicable layers.
