## CUPA Agency

**Redwood City is NOT its own CUPA.** San Mateo County Environmental Health Services (SMC EHS) was designated the countywide Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) for all of San Mateo County in **1996**. All facilities in Redwood City are regulated under the county CUPA — no separate city HMBP permit exists.

- **Agency**: San Mateo County Environmental Health Services
- **Address**: 2000 Alameda de las Pulgas, Suite 100, San Mateo, CA 94403

## Local Ordinance

**San Mateo County Code, Chapter 4.92 — Storage of Hazardous Substances**

The Board of Supervisors adopted amendments to Chapter 4.92, effective **August 10, 2018**, to:
- Expand scope to cover all hazardous materials/waste programs administered by EHS (eliminating need for separate program ordinances)
- Formalize the requirement to obtain and maintain a CUPA permit
- Formalize CUPA authority to **inspect unannounced** during normal business hours; authorizes fees for after-hours inspections (post-6 pm)
- Remove outdated requirements and align with current state regulations
- Establish civil and administrative enforcement mechanisms

PDF of current Chapter 4.92 text: https://www.smchealth.org/sites/main/files/file-attachments/current_ordinance_4.92.pdf

## Programs Administered (All Six CUPA Elements)

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — businesses storing/handling hazardous materials at or above threshold quantities
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator Program** — Very Small (VSQG), Small (SQG), and Large Quantity generators
3. **Tiered Permitting Program** — conditionally exempt, conditionally authorized, and fully permitted facilities
4. **Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program**
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention Program (CalARP)** — Programs 1, 2, and 3
6. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Tank (APSA) Program**

## HMBP Reporting Thresholds

Electronic submission required (CERS) for any business that handles or stores hazardous materials at or above:
- **55 gallons** (liquids)
- **500 pounds** (solids)
- **200 cubic feet** (compressed gases at standard temperature and pressure)

File within 30 days of first handling reportable quantities; recertify annually; full re-submission every three years; revise within 30 days of significant operational changes.

Note: The city fire code (§12.11 of Chapter 12) cross-references HMBP requirements — references in the California Fire Code to HMMP/HMIS apply to SMC EHS's HMBP program.
