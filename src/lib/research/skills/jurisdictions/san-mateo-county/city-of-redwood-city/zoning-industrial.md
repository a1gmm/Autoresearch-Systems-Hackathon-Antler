## Zoning Code Location

Redwood City's zoning regulations are published as a **separate Zoning Code** (not part of the City Code), available on Municode:  
https://library.municode.com/ca/redwood_city/codes/zoning_code

The zoning code uses the title prefix "Article ##" rather than chapter numbering. Industrial districts are codified in Articles 17–19. The most recent bulk update is Ord. No. 1130-890, §4(Exh. A), adopted 8-26-2024, which amended Articles 17, 17A, and 18.

## Industrial Zoning Districts (from Article 3 — Designation of Districts)

| District Code | District Name |

| **LII** | Light Industrial Incubator (Article 17A) |
| **IP** | Industrial Park (Article 18) |
| **GI** | General Industrial (Article 19) |

## Article 17 — IR (Industrial — Restricted) District

**Section 17.2 — Permitted Uses** (as amended by Ord. No. 1130-890, §4(Exh. A), 8-26-24):

All uses must be conducted within a completely enclosed building unless a Use Permit is issued.

**A.** Machine shops, laboratories, and other establishments used for research, manufacturing, assembly, or repair operations. All such uses shall be conducted within a completely enclosed building.

**B.** Warehouses, wholesale businesses, **food preparation**, and storage or distribution operations. A Use Permit may be issued to conduct these uses outside a building; otherwise, all such uses shall be conducted within a building.

**C.** Cabinet shops, electrical, plumbing, or heating shops, sheet metal shops, upholstery shops, bakeries, canneries, creameries, bottling plants, laundries, and cleaning or dyeing establishments — so long as any activities normally capable of creating noise, odor, glare, or dust are located wholly within a completely enclosed room having no exterior entrance within **50 feet** of any street or adjacent property unless completely shielded by part of the building. A Use Permit may be issued for outdoor operations.

**D.** Automobile, truck, trailer, boat, plane, or heavy equipment establishments, including major repair facilities, rental, and sales. A Use Permit may be issued for outdoor operations.

**E.** Public utility buildings, substations, and service yards. A Use Permit may be issued for outdoor operations.

**F.** Public or quasi-public uses. A Use Permit may be issued for outdoor operations.

**G.** Family child care homes within residential structures (per §39.3).

**H.** Laboratory-type research and development.

**I.** Any use not explicitly listed is prohibited, except that the Zoning Administrator may permit a similar and/or compatible use.

*Footnote*: Uses A–H must comply with applicable San Carlos Airport Land Use Compatibility Plan (ALUCP) Safety and Noise Compatibility policies and State law.

## Article 18 — IP (Industrial Park) District

**Section 18.2 — Permitted Uses** (as amended 8-26-24):

A. Administrative, business, and professional offices < 10,000 sq ft GFA  
B. Research and development (laboratory and office)  
C. Manufacture of precision instruments and devices (electric/electronic equipment and appliances)  
D. Printing, publishing, or lithograph shops (not bookstores)  
E. Processing of food products and pharmaceuticals (not fish/meat products, sauerkraut, vinegar, or refining fats/oils)  
F. Manufacture, assembly, processing, or packaging of products from previously prepared materials (cloth, plastic, paper, leather, precious/semi-precious metals and stones) — not saw/planing mills or primary production from raw materials  
G. Warehousing and wholesale distribution or storage facilities  
H. Television or radio studios and stations (not transmission/reception towers)  
I. Indoor building materials sales establishments  
J. Combined retail/wholesale office equipment stores and accessory office supply sales  
K. Floor covering stores and furniture stores  
L. Appliance stores, including appurtenant repair service  
M. Business or industrial supply, maintenance, or service establishments  
N. Food preparation  
O. Any unlisted use if determined similar/compatible by Zoning Administrator  

## Article 19 — GI (General Industrial) District

**Note**: Article 19 was not amended in the 8-26-24 update (Ord. No. 1130-890). The following is drawn from JURISDICTION.md and existing Municode references; verify the full text at https://library.municode.com/ca/redwood_city/codes/zoning_code?nodeId=ART19GIGEINDI.

**Key sections**:
- **§ 19.2** — Permitted uses (broader manufacturing and industrial permitted by right)
- **§ 19.4** — Conditional uses requiring a Use Permit (CUP)
- **§ 19.10** — Standards for Floor Area Ratio (max FAR 70%)

**Known as-of-right uses in GI** (from Municode section headings and JURISDICTION.md):
- Delivery centers (permitted by right)
- Indoor nurseries (permitted by right, subject to §59.5 cannabis provisions)
- Broader manufacturing and industrial uses than IP or IR

**Known conditional uses in GI (§19.4)** — require Use Permit from Planning Commission.

**Geographic limits on hazardous materials storage** (from fire code §§12.29–12.30): Flammable cryogenic fluids and LPG bulk storage are **permitted** in GI (not listed in restricted residential/commercial zones).

## CUP / Use Permit Process

- **Review authority**: Planning Commission for conditional uses; Zoning Administrator for minor determinations
- **Application**: Community Development Department — https://www.redwoodcity.org/departments/community-development-department/codes-development-standards
- **Planning services portal**: https://www.redwoodcity.org/departments/community-development-department/planning-housing/planning-services

## Mixed-Use Districts Allowing Some Industrial Uses

- **Article 55 — MUT (Mixed-Use Transitional)**: Permits handicraft/custom manufacturing and industry, limited (both conditional use); prohibits major vehicle repair, R&D laboratory, and industry, general
- **Article 53 — MUC (Mixed-Use Corridor)**: Some light industrial/live-work
- **Article 54 — MUN (Mixed-Use Neighborhood)**: Limited industrial accessory uses

## Cannabis in Industrial Districts

**Article 59** governs cannabis cultivation and commercial cannabis activity. **§ 59.4** specifies permitted locations (GI and certain other districts are the primary eligible zones).

## All-Electric Reach Code (City Code Chapter 9, Article XV)

New industrial/commercial buildings must be all-electric (mandatory for new construction). **Exception**: F and H occupancies with gas manufacturing or R&D processes (§9.249 Exception 4). This affects permitting for new industrial facilities in all zones including IR, IP, and GI.
