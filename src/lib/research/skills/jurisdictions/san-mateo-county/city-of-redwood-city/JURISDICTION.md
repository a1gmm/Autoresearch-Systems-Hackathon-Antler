### 2. Fire AHJ — Redwood City Fire Department (RCFD)

- **AHJ**: Redwood City Fire Department — own municipal department (not consolidated with county or regional JPA). ISO Class 1 rated (only ISO Class 1 department in San Mateo County).
- **Fire prevention / inspections / permits**: https://www.redwoodcity.org/departments/fire-department/fire-prevention/inspections-and-permits
- **Adopted fire code**: City Code **Chapter 12, Article II — Redwood City Fire Code**. The chapter adopts the California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9) with local amendments. Municode confirms amendments to the **2022 California Building and Fire Codes** are codified in City Code Chapter 9, Article V (see nodeId CH9BU_ARTVAM2022CABURECO). Section 12.7 governs adoption and conflict resolution.
- **Local fire code sections of note**: § 12.23 adds Section 907.2.13.2.1 (high-rise alarm systems). Additional local amendments cover vehicle/fire-hazard safety zones (§ 12.7.1).
- **Gotcha**: The 2025 California Building Standards Code took effect statewide 2026-01-01; verify whether the city has adopted it locally — the 2022 CFC was current as of the most recent Municode publication.

### 3. POTW / Industrial Wastewater Pretreatment

- **POTW operator**: **Silicon Valley Clean Water (SVCW)** — regional wastewater authority serving Redwood City, Belmont, San Carlos, and Woodside. Treatment plant at 1400 Radio Road, Redwood City, CA 94065.
- **Mandatory Wastewater Discharge Permit** required for: (a) facilities covered by federal categorical regulations (40 CFR); (b) facilities meeting definition of "significant industrial user" (SIU) per SVCW Regulations § 1.3.30.
- **Local limits**: SVCW "Regulations" document (Uniform Standards for Use of Sanitary Sewerage Facilities); local discharge limits at §§ 2.3, 2.4, 2.5. Groundwater discharge to sanitary sewer is prohibited except by permit.
- **Other permit types**: Non-Routine Discharge Authorization; Low Volume Discharge Authorization; Septic/Grease Hauler discharge permit.
- **Redwood City Code**: **Chapter 27 (Sanitary Sewerage Facilities)**, Article IV (Reports, Permits, and Administration); § 27.31.3 governs mandatory wastewater discharge permit applications.
- **Permit contact**: Ben Padua, BPaduaJr@svcw.org | (650) 591-7121 | fax (650) 591-7122
- **Application portal**: https://svcw.org/businesses/permit-applications/mandatory-wastewater-discharge-permit-application/
- **Permit info page**: https://svcw.org/businesses/permit-information/

### 4. Stormwater / MS4

- **Ordinance**: City Code **Chapter 27A** — Stormwater and Drainage Control Program. Article II = Stormwater Treatment Measures and Maintenance Program.
- **Green Infrastructure Plan**: City Council adopted ordinance (2019) amending Chapter 18, Article XIV and Chapter 27A, Article II to implement Green Infrastructure Plan; applies to all new buildings and substantial commercial remodels.
- **C.3 requirements**: New development and substantial redevelopment subject to Redwood City Stormwater Management and Discharge Control Program (Ch. 27A).
- **Pervious area requirements**: minimum 40% pervious per lot in residential districts; minimum 20% pervious per lot in General Commercial district. Industrial districts: [VERIFY — not found online for GI/IR specifically].
- **Regional MS4 permit**: Redwood City is a co-permittee under the San Francisco Bay Region Municipal Regional Stormwater NPDES Permit (Order R2-2022-0018).

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts & CUP Process

- **Zoning code location**: Separate from City Code; published as Redwood City Zoning Code on Municode — https://library.municode.com/ca/redwood_city/codes/zoning_code
- **Industrial districts**:
  - **Article 17 — IR (Industrial-Restricted) District**: restricted industrial uses; § 17.2 lists permitted uses.
  - **Article 19 — GI (General Industrial) District**: broader industrial/manufacturing uses permitted; § 19.4 = conditional uses (CUP); § 19.10 = floor area ratio standards. Delivery centers and indoor nurseries are permitted as-of-right in GI.
- **Mixed-use districts that permit some industrial/live-work**: Article 53 (Mixed-Use Corridor — MUC); Article 55 (MULW — Mixed-Use Live/Work); Article 54 (MUN — Mixed-Use Neighborhood).
- **Cannabis**: Article 59 governs cannabis cultivation and commercial cannabis activity; § 59.4 specifies permitted locations.
- **CUP process**: Conditional Use Permit for industrial uses in GI district governed by § 19.4; planning application review handled by Community Development Department — https://www.redwoodcity.org/departments/community-development-department/codes-development-standards
- **All-electric reach code**: City Code Chapter 9, Article XV (adopted with 2019 energy code amendments) mandates All-Electric Buildings for new construction; exceptions for F/H occupancies with gas manufacturing/R&D processes (§ 9.249 Exception 4).

### Key Gotchas

- CUPA is county-administered (San Mateo County EHS), not city-administered — no separate city HMBP permit.
- Fire AHJ is city's own RCFD; it is NOT part of any regional fire authority or JPA.
- POTW is SVCW (regional authority), not the city directly — industrial permit goes to SVCW, not Redwood City Public Works.
- All-electric ordinance applies to new industrial/commercial buildings with exceptions for F/H occupancy gas use.
