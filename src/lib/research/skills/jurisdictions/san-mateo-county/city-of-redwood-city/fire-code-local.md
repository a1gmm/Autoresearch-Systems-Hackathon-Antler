## Adopting Framework

**Municipal code location**: City Code **Chapter 12, Article II — Redwood City Fire Code**  
**AHJ**: Redwood City Fire Department (RCFD) — own municipal department, ISO Class 1 rated (only ISO Class 1 in San Mateo County)

## Adopted Fire Code Edition

**Ordinance file code**: ATTY/ORD.435/FIRE CODE ADOPTION (Rev. 11-21-16)

**Adopted codes** (per the ordinance text):
- 2015 International Fire Code (IFC)

## Local Amendments (from ORD.435)

### § 12.11 — Section 102.13 Added: HMBP Cross-Reference
Where the Fire Code refers to Hazardous Materials Management Plan (HMMP) or Hazardous Materials Inventory Statement (HMIS), those provisions apply to the **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** as administered by San Mateo County Department of Environmental Health Services (the county CUPA — not the city).

### § 12.12 — Section 102.14 Added: RCFD Design Standards
Fire Marshal maintains design and installation standards for: address posting, special suppression systems, turnarounds, turnouts, Knox installations, and fire sprinkler systems.

### § 12.16 — Section 507.1.1 Added: Fire Main / Hydrant Specifications
- All water mains for fire protection: minimum **8 inches** inside diameter (Fire Code Official may require different sizes, minimum floor 6 inches)
- Minimum fire flow for one- and two-family dwellings: **1,500 gpm** with 2-hour flow duration; residual pressure ≥ 20 psi

### § 12.17 / § 12.18 — Section 903.2.13 Added: Automatic Sprinkler System Requirements
Approved automatic sprinkler systems required in addition to base CFC in:

**(a) New buildings**: All new buildings with total floor area **≥ 3,000 sq ft** (exception: one- and two-family homes covered by NFPA 13D regardless of size, including ADUs)

**(b) Existing buildings** — additions:
- Group R-3 (single-family): adding **≥ 1,000 sq ft**
- All other buildings: when addition causes total floor area to reach or exceed **3,000 sq ft**

**(c) Change of occupancy**: From less hazardous to more hazardous occupancy group

**(d) Limited area systems**: Existing Group R-2 apartment buildings with open parking garages below units when a re-roof permit is issued

**(e) Retroactive — R-2**: Effective July 1, 2020, all multi-unit R-2 buildings (4+ units) lacking a city-permitted automatic sprinkler system. Fire Marshal may approve alternative methods for pre-1989 buildings.

### § 12.23 — Section 907.2.13.2.1 Added: High-Rise Alarm Systems
Adds requirements for multistoried/high-rise safety including firefighter communications systems, emergency planning and information signage, and public address systems in buildings of four or more stories.

### § 12.29 — Section 5806.2 Amended: Flammable Cryogenic Fluids Geographic Limits
Storage of flammable cryogenic fluids in stationary containers is **prohibited** in: RH, R-1, R-2, RG, R-3, R-4, R-5, PO, CN, CB, CG, TP, and MH zoning districts. **Permitted** in: IR, LII, IP, GI, AG, CP, CO, and mixed-use districts not listed.

### § 12.30 — Section 6104.2.1 Added: LPG Storage Geographic Limits
Liquefied petroleum gas storage is restricted to the same residential and commercial districts listed above. Aggregate capacity of any one installation shall not exceed **2,000 gallons** water capacity in those restricted zones.

## Permit & Inspection Information

- **Fire prevention / permits page**: https://www.redwoodcity.org/departments/fire-department/fire-prevention/inspections-and-permits
- **Appeals process** (§12.14): Written appeal to Fire Marshal within 15 days → Fire Chief appeal within 15 days → Fire Chief decision is final
- **Hazmat business plans**: Submitted to county CUPA (San Mateo County EHS), not RCFD — see `cupa-local.md`

## Conflict Resolution

§ 12.7(C): In the event of any conflict between the provisions of the California Fire Code and the provisions of Chapter 12, Article II, the **local provisions prevail**.
