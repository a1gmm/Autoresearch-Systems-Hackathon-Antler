## POTW Operator

**Silicon Valley Clean Water (SVCW)** — regional wastewater authority (Joint Exercise of Powers Authority established 11/13/1975).

- **Treatment plant**: 1400 Radio Road, Redwood City, CA 94065-1220
- **Member agencies**: Cities of Belmont, Redwood City, and San Carlos; West Bay Sanitary District
- **Phone**: (650) 591-7121 | **Fax**: (650) 591-7122 | **Email**: info@svcw.org
- **Industrial permit contact**: Ben Padua — BPaduaJr@svcw.org

Industrial wastewater discharge permits are issued by **SVCW** (not the City of Redwood City Public Works). Redwood City Code **Chapter 27** (Sanitary Sewerage Facilities), **Article IV** (Reports, Permits, and Administration) — specifically **§ 27.31.3** — governs mandatory wastewater discharge permit applications within city limits, but the permit authority and standards are SVCW's.

## Governing Pretreatment Document

**"Regulations of Silicon Valley Clean Water Establishing Uniform Standards, Conditions and Requirements for the Use of the Sanitary Sewerage Facilities"**  
(Conformed through 2005 Amendments; original 1990 with 1991, 2000, 2005 amendments)

PDF: https://svcw.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/Regulations-of-SVCW.pdf

Member agencies are required by SVCW's Joint Powers Agreement to adopt these regulations as uniform wastewater ordinances enforceable in their respective jurisdictions (§1.1).

## Significant Industrial User (SIU) Definition (§ 1.3.30)

A user is an SIU if it meets **any** of the following:

(a) Any user in an industry subject to **Categorical Pretreatment Standards** under 40 CFR Chapter I, Subchapter N; **or**

(b) Any user that discharges an average of **≥ 25,000 gallons per day** of process wastewater (excluding sanitary, non-contact cooling, and boiler blowdown); **or**

(c) Any user that contributes a process wastestream that makes up ≥ 5% of the POTW's average dry-weather hydraulic or organic capacity, or that has a reasonable potential to adversely affect the treatment plant or violate pretreatment standards.

## Permit Types

| Permit Type | Trigger |
|-------------|---------|
| **Mandatory Wastewater Discharge Permit** (§4.2) | Required for all SIUs; no SIU may connect or discharge without one |
| **Discretionary Wastewater Discharge Permit** (§4.3) | Available to any user requesting special charge schedule or subject to non-routine discharge |
| **Non-Routine Discharge Authorization** | One-time or short-term discharges |
| **Low Volume Discharge Authorization** | Continued low-volume discharges |
| **Septic/Grease Hauler Discharge Permit** | Haulers discharging at SVCW facility |

**Groundwater discharge** to sanitary sewer is **prohibited except by permit** (§2.2(a)).

## Section 2.4 — Specific Wastes Prohibited

No person shall discharge wastewater:
- Temperature **> 150°F** (65°C)
- **> 300 mg/L** oil or grease of animal or vegetable origin
- **> 100 mg/L** oil or grease of mineral or petroleum origin
- pH **< 6.0** or with corrosive properties capable of damaging sewerage facilities
- Solid or viscous substances capable of causing obstruction (sand, grit, rags, tar, plastic, etc.)

## Section 2.5 — Specific User Limitations

Permit may be issued (subject to Authority Manager concurrence) for discharges otherwise prohibited under §2.3.

## Additional Prohibitions (§2.2)

Permit required for any discharge of: stormwater/groundwater/rainwater/sub-surface drainage; unpolluted cooling water; garbage grinder waste (with conditions); holding tank waste; radioactive waste (strict conditions apply).

Hazardous waste may be discharged only pursuant to a permit from Authority's Manager confirming no detrimental discharge (§2.1(b)).

## Pretreatment Compliance Reporting (§4.1)

- Baseline Monitoring Reports, 90-Day Compliance Reports, and Periodic Compliance Reports required per federal 40 CFR §403.12
- Certifications required signed by a principal executive officer (VP or above), general partner/proprietor, or duly authorized representative
- SVCW Manager or Agency Director may require periodic discharge reports at any time
