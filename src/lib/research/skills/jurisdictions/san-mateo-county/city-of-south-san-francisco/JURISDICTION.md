## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials Unified Program

- **CUPA agency:** San Mateo County Environmental Health Services (EHS) — designated countywide CUPA since 1996; South San Francisco is **not** its own CUPA.
- **Programs administered:** HMBP (Hazardous Materials Business Plan), Hazardous Waste Generator, Underground Storage Tank (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Tank (APSA), California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP), Tiered Permitting.
- **Electronic reporting portal:** MyEHS — https://smcehs.my.site.com/s/?language=en_US (CERS-linked)
- **State surcharges (as of Oct 13, 2023):** $94/facility oversight surcharge; $370 CalARP surcharge.
- **Fee schedule:** County Board of Supervisors fee schedule, effective Jan 9, 2025 (specific amounts on MyEHS portal).
- **Contact:** (650) 372-6200
- GOTCHA: South San Francisco runs its own fire department but relies on the **county CUPA** for all hazmat/hazwaste programs — there is no city-level CUPA.
- Source: https://www.smchealth.org/hazardous-materials-cupa

### 2. Fire AHJ — South San Francisco Fire Department (SSFFD)

- **AHJ:** South San Francisco Fire Department, Fire Prevention Division — own municipal department (founded 1908), five stations.
- **Adopted code:** **2025 California Fire Code** (Title 24, Part 9, based on 2024 IFC) — South San Francisco adopted the full 2025 CA code suite effective 2026-01-01; local amendments codified in **Municipal Code Chapter 15.24** (SSF MC §15.24.020 lists all local additions, amendments, and deletions to the CFC).
- **Operational permits:** 62 permit types tracked by Fire Prevention Division (CFC §105.5.1–105.5.60 plus local additions); examples include Additive Manufacturing/3D Printing, Cryogenic Fluids, Cutting & Welding, Energy Storage Systems, Explosives, Flammable & Combustible Liquids, Hazardous Materials, Refrigeration Equipment.
- **Fire permit fees (FY 2024-25 Master Fee Schedule):**
  - Operational permits (include up to 2 inspections); multiple permits: total less 25%
  - Hazardous materials operational permit: $628.85 (base)
  - Annual Fire Inspection (Basic): see Master Fee Schedule §5.109
  - Emergency Response Hazmat Cost Recovery: billed at 2% administrative surcharge
- **Online permit portal:** https://permits.ssf.net
- **Contact:** Fire Prevention Division, 400 Grand Ave, South San Francisco, CA 94080; (650) 877-8500
- Source: https://www.ssfca.gov/Departments/Fire-Department/Fire-Prevention/Guidelines-Regulations
- Source: https://www.ssfca.gov/Departments/Fire-Department/Fire-Permit-Center

### 3. POTW / Industrial Pretreatment

- **POTW:** South San Francisco/San Bruno Water Quality Control Plant (WQCP), 195 Belle Air Rd, South San Francisco, CA 94080 — operated by City of South San Francisco; serves South San Francisco, San Bruno, and Colma; avg. dry-weather flow 9 MGD, peak wet-weather capacity >60 MGD.
- **Local sewer-use ordinance:** Municipal Code **Title 14 / Chapter 14.08** ("Water Quality Control") — sets uniform requirements for direct and indirect dischargers to the POTW; implements Clean Water Act (33 U.S.C. §1251 et seq.) and General Pretreatment Regulations (40 CFR Part 403).
- **SIU permit:** Significant Industrial Users must obtain an **Industrial Waste Discharge Permit** from the city before discharging; proposed new dischargers apply at least 90 days before discharge start.
- **Environmental Compliance program:** controls ~26 industrial facilities via EPA-required Wastewater Discharge Permits (per city website).
- **IWP permit types (FY 2024-25 fee schedule, Section 11 — Water Quality Control Division):**
  - SIU Wastewater Discharge Permit (3-year validity): fee per Master Fee Schedule §11.02
  - Food Facility Discharge Permit (3-year): §11.01
  - General / Groundwater Discharge Permit: §11.03
  - Waste/Septage Hauler Discharge Permit: §11.04
  - Water Quality Compliance Review: §11.05
- **Permit applications:** downloadable from city website (Industrial Waste Discharge Permit Application, Local Discharge Limits, Grease Trap Maintenance Log); no online portal listed — paper/email submission.
- **Contact:** Water Quality Control Division, (650) 829-3848; web-pw@ssf.net
- Source: https://www.ssfca.gov/departments/public-works/water-quality-control-plant/environmental-compliance
- Source: https://www.ssfca.gov/Departments/Public-Works/Divisions/Water-Quality-Control-Plant-Division

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater

- **Program:** South San Francisco is a co-permittee under the **San Francisco Bay Region Municipal Regional Stormwater NPDES Permit (MRP)**, Permit No. **CAS612008** (issued Oct 14, 2009; reissued as Order R2-2022-0018 in 2022 for the 5th permit term).
- **Local ordinance:** Municipal Code **Chapter 14.04** ("Stormwater Management and Discharge Control") — construed to assure consistency with CAS612008; §14.04.240 addresses acts potentially violating the Clean Water Act / Porter-Cologne Act.
- **Enforcing division:** Public Works Department (stormwater program); Fire Department (emergency hazmat spill response).
- Source: https://ecode360.com/43447251 (SSF MC Ch. 14.04)
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/sanfranciscobay/water_issues/programs/stormwater/MRP/mrp5-22/R2-2022-0018.pdf

### 5. Zoning — Industrial Districts & CUP Process

- **Zoning ordinance:** Municipal Code **Title 20** ("South San Francisco Zoning Ordinance"), adopted pursuant to Cal. Gov't Code §65850; conventional district regulations in Division II, form-based districts in Division III.
- **Key industrial / business-park zone designations:**
  - **BTP-M** (Business Technology Park – Medium) and **BTP-H** (Business Technology Park – High) — BTP-H buildings subject to FAA regulations and SF International Airport Land Use Compatibility Plan.
  - **MI** (Mixed Industrial) — industrial uses including manufacturing, warehouse, wholesale distribution, general industrial.
  - **BC** (Business Commercial) and **FC** (Freeway Commercial) — limited industrial uses.
- **CUP process:** Conditional Use Permits governed by **Chapter 20.490** ("Use Permits"); required for certain industrial and hazardous-occupancy uses in applicable districts. Planning Division processes applications.
- **Industrial screening:** Industrial uses adjacent to residential districts require a solid wall ≥6 ft and 10-ft landscaped buffer yard (≥1 tree per 20 lineal ft, 15-gal min).
- **Interactive zoning map:** https://zoning.ssf.net (ArcGIS — search by address or APN)
- **Planning Division contact:** planning@ssfca.gov; (650) 877-8535
- Source: https://ecode360.com/43450037 (Title 20 Zoning)
