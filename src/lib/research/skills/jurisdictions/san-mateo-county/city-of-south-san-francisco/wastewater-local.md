## POTW

**South San Francisco / San Bruno Water Quality Control Plant (WQCP)**
195 Belle Air Rd, South San Francisco, CA 94080
- Jointly owned by Cities of South San Francisco and San Bruno; **operated by City of South San
  Francisco** — IWP permitting authority is the City, not the county
- Also serves City of Colma
- Average dry-weather flow: **9 MGD**; peak wet-weather capacity > 60 MGD
- Secondary treatment; discharges to San Francisco Bay
- Controls approximately **26 industrial facilities** through EPA-required Wastewater Discharge Permits

## Governing Ordinance

**Municipal Code Title 14, Chapter 14.08** — "Water Quality Control"
- Sets uniform requirements for direct and indirect contributors to the POTW
- Implements:
  - Clean Water Act (33 U.S.C. §1251 et seq.)
  - General Pretreatment Regulations (40 CFR Part 403)
  - California Water Code §1300 et seq.
- **§14.08.100** — Wastewater Discharge Permits: unlawful to discharge without permit; SIU must
  apply for and maintain an Industrial Waste Discharge Permit from the city
- **§14.08.220** — Local Discharge Limits: city-established technically based local limits (TBLLs)
  that supplement federal categorical pretreatment standards (40 CFR Parts 405–471)
- Categorical pretreatment standards modified by the city following changes to federal standards
- Non-significant categorical industrial user (NSCIU) determination: discharges ≤ 100 gpd total
  categorical wastewater

## Industrial Waste Discharge Permit (IWP) Requirements

- **SIUs** (Significant Industrial Users) must obtain an IWP before discharging
- Proposed new dischargers must apply **at least 90 days** before planned discharge start date
- Permit validity: **3-year intervals** (renewal required at expiration)
- Permit may require installation of pretreatment technology, pollution control equipment, or
  containment devices
- Environmental Compliance inspectors perform periodic compliance inspections of industrial and
  commercial dischargers

## NPDES Sampling Contract

The WQCP contracts with third-party laboratories (Dysert Environmental, Inc. through FY 2025-26)
for NPDES and Industrial Pretreatment regulatory sampling of the 26 permitted industrial facilities.
Sample frequency ranges from monthly to semi-annually depending on permit type.
Cost recovery: sampling costs are recovered through annual sewer service billings.

## Application Process

1. Submit an **Industrial Waste Discharge Permit Application** (available as downloadable PDF
   from the Environmental Compliance page)
2. Applications accepted by paper or email — no online portal as of 2026-06-03
3. New dischargers must apply ≥ 90 days before discharge commencement
4. City evaluates against local limits and categorical pretreatment standards
5. Permit issued with conditions; 3-year validity; annual reporting typically required

Additional downloadable forms:
- Grease Trap Maintenance Log Sheet (food facilities)
- Waste Hauler Discharge Permit Application
- Groundwater Well Discharge Permit Application
- Slug Discharge Management Plan guidance: https://www.ssfca.gov/files/assets/public/v/1/public-works/documents/updated-slug-discharge-guidance-manual.pdf
