## CUPA Agency

**San Mateo County Environmental Health Services (SMC EHS)**
Designated countywide CUPA by the California Secretary for Environmental Protection in **1996**.
South San Francisco is **not** its own CUPA — all unified program permits for facilities within the
city are issued and inspected by the county.

## Programs Administered

SMC EHS administers all six unified programs on behalf of South San Francisco:
1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — Community Right-to-Know; annual certification
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator Program** — small, large, and very small quantity generators
3. **Tiered Permitting (TP)** — on-site treatment/recycling (CE, CA, PBR tiers)
4. **Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program** — leak prevention, testing, closure
5. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Tank (APSA) Program** — spill prevention, annual inspection
6. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) Program** — RMP equivalent, Programs 1/2/3

## Local Hazardous Materials Ordinance

**San Mateo County Ordinance Code Chapter 4.92** — "Storage of Hazardous Substances"
- Amended effective **August 10, 2018** to consolidate all CUPA-program obligations into one chapter
- Expands Chapter 4.92 to cover all hazmat/hazwaste programs overseen by Environmental Health Services
- Formalizes the permit requirement; authorizes unannounced inspections during business hours
- Establishes authority to charge fees for inspections conducted after 6 PM
- Removed blanket requirement that UST components always be classified as hazardous waste on removal
- Establishes civil and administrative enforcement mechanisms
- Source: https://www.smchealth.org/post/hazardous-materials-ordinance-changes
- Full text: https://library.municode.com/ca/san_mateo_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT4SAHE_CH4.92HAMASTOR

## HMBP Filing Thresholds

A business must submit an HMBP if it handles hazardous materials at or above:
- **Liquids:** ≥ 55 gallons
- **Solids:** ≥ 500 pounds
- **Compressed gases:** ≥ 200 cubic feet (at STP)

Initial filing required within 30 days of first handling reportable quantities. Annual certification
required; full resubmission every 3 years; revised submission within 30 days of significant changes.

## Electronic Reporting Portal

**MyEHS** — https://smcehs.my.site.com/s/?language=en_US
Linked to CERS (California Environmental Reporting System). Electronic submission has been required
since January 1, 2013.
