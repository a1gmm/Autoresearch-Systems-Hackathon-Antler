## Industrial and Employment Zone Districts

South San Francisco does not use traditional M-1/M-2 labels. The key employment/industrial
zones are:

### BTP-M — Business Technology Park Medium (§20.100)
- Supports campus-like environments for corporate headquarters, research and development, and offices
- Typical uses: incubator-research facilities, prototype manufacturing, testing, repairing, packaging
- Implements the General Plan's "Business Technology Park" land use designation
- Limited retail and service uses allowed as accessory

### BTP-H — Business Technology Park High (§20.100)
- Same character as BTP-M but higher development intensity
- Buildings subject to FAA regulations and SF International Airport Land Use Compatibility Plan
- Implements the General Plan's "Business Technology Park High" land use designation

### MIM — Mixed Industrial Medium (§20.100)
- Supports a wide range of manufacturing, processing, general service, warehousing, storage and
  distribution, and service commercial uses
- Implements the General Plan's "Mixed Industrial" land use designation
- Minimum lot area: 10,000 sf; 1 acre east of Highway 101
- Maximum building height: 65 ft

### MIH — Mixed Industrial High (§20.100)
- Same use profile as MIM — manufacturing, processing, general service, warehousing, storage, distribution
- Implements the General Plan's "Mixed Industrial High" land use designation
- Minimum lot area: 10,000 sf; 1 acre east of Highway 101
- Maximum building height: 65 ft

### PQP — Public (§20.110)
- Civic zoning district for public facilities

**Note on GMP (Genentech Master Plan):** Development standards for the Genentech campus area are
governed by the Genentech Master Plan rather than the standard Title 20 tables.

**Key industrial use definitions (from Title 20):**
- *Industry, General* — Manufacturing from extracted or raw materials or recycled materials
- *Industry, Limited* — Light manufacturing; cleaner processes than Industry, General
- *Handicraft/Custom Manufacturing* — Small-scale manufacture to serve local market
- *Chemical, Mineral, and Explosives Storage* — Specialized storage of chemicals, minerals, or explosives
- *Indoor Warehousing and Storage* — Enclosed warehousing excluding Chemical, Mineral, and Explosives Storage
- *Personal Storage* — Mini-warehouse; storage units accessible exclusively to specific tenants

## Industrial Screening Requirements

Industrial uses adjacent to residential districts require:
- Solid wall ≥ 6 ft
- 10-ft landscaped buffer yard
- Minimum 1 tree per 20 lineal feet (15-gallon minimum size)

## CUP / MUP Process

Governed by **Chapter 20.490** ("Use Permits").

### Minor Use Permit (MUP)
- Decision maker: **Chief Planner** (administrative decision)
- No public hearing required unless appealed
- Used for lower-impact conditional uses

### Conditional Use Permit (CUP)
- Advisory body: Chief Planner
- Decision maker: **Planning Commission**
- Appeal body: **City Council**
- Required findings focus on consistency with General Plan, zoning ordinance, and compatibility with
  surrounding uses

### Applications
- Portal: Planning Division, 400 Grand Ave, South San Francisco, CA 94080
- Email: planning@ssfca.gov
- Phone: (650) 877-8535
- CUP application materials available through city website or Planning Division

## Hazardous Occupancy / Industrial Use Notes

- **Research and Development** in MIM/MIH requires a CUP (not permitted by right); the high
  concentration of biotech/pharma in South San Francisco means many tenants will trigger this path
- **Chemical, Mineral, and Explosives Storage** requires a CUP in both MIM and MIH — coordinate
  with Fire Prevention Division (SSFFD) for operational permits concurrently
- Airport Land Use Compatibility: uses east of South Airport Blvd and the Bayshore Freeway are
  subject to additional siting review per Chapter 20.300 and the SFO ALUCP
