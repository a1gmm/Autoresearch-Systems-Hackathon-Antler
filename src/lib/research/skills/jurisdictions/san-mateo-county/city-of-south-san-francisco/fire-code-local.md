## Adopted Code & Edition

**2025 California Fire Code** (CFC 2025, based on the 2024 International Fire Code), as published by
the California Building Standards Commission. Adopted by reference via **Municipal Code Chapter 15.24**
("California Fire Code"). Effective **January 1, 2026** statewide; South San Francisco adopted the full
2025 California code suite on that date.

Prior edition in effect through 2025-12-31: **2022 California Fire Code** (based on 2021 IFC), also
codified in Chapter 15.24.

**Adopting ordinance number:** Not publicly resolved online as of 2026-06-03 (the ecode360 and
legistar search interfaces returned 403 or empty results). The ordinance disposition table is at
https://ecode360.com/43455017. Contact the City Clerk at (650) 877-8535 to confirm the exact
ordinance number for the 2025 CFC adoption.

## Municipal Code Location

- **Title 15** — Buildings and Construction
- **Chapter 15.24** — California Fire Code
- Sections are renumbered: CFC §101.1 becomes SSF MC §15.24.101.1; CFC §5001.1 becomes
  §15.24.5001.1, etc.
- Local amendments codified in **§15.24.020** ("Amendments, additions, and deletions")
- Source of amendments: https://library.qcode.us/lib/south_san_francisco_ca/pub/municipal_code/item/title_15-chapter_15_24-15_24_020

## Scope of Adopted Appendices

Chapters 1–80 with Appendices **4, B, BB, C, CC, D, F, I, N, O, and P** adopted by reference.
The South San Francisco Fire Department **Fire Prevention Policy Manual** is adopted by reference
as a supplemental instruction and interpretation document.

## Local Amendments — §15.24.020

The city's local amendments include:
- **Fire Prevention Division created** — The Fire Prevention Division of SSFFD is formally
  established; the division's official in charge is titled "Fire Code Official."
- Section renumbering: all amended/local sections are cited as §15.24.XXXX rather than the bare
  CFC section numbers.

## Operational Permits (Fire Prevention Division)

62 operational permit types track against CFC §105.5.1–§105.5.60 plus local additions.
Key permit categories and FY 2024-25 fees (from SSF Master Fee Schedule §5):

| Permit Category | FY 2024-25 Fee |
|---|---|
| Hazardous materials | $281 |
| High-piled storage | $336 |
| Energy storage systems | $244 |
| Explosives | $336 |
| Flammable & combustible liquids | $227 |
| Cryogenic fluids | $244 |
| Compressed gases | $244 |
| Cutting and welding | $227 |
| HPM facilities | $281 |
| LP-gas | $336 |
| Refrigeration equipment | $281 |
| Multiple permits (total less 25%) | applies |

All operational permits include up to 2 inspections.

Additional fee categories:
- Fire Construction Permits — Plan Check (includes 3 reviews): sliding scale by project value
  (range: $400 base for $1–$6,000 projects up through tiered rates for $1M+ projects)
- After-hours inspection: $235/hr
- Expedited plan check: percentage surcharge if approved
- Emergency Response Hazmat Cost Recovery: 2% administrative surcharge
