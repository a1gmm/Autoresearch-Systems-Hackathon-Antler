## Scope and AHJ Note

There is no single countywide fire AHJ. The San Mateo Consolidated Fire Department (SMCFD) is a JPA serving the **City of San Mateo, Belmont, and Foster City**. ORD-2025-003 applies only within those three cities. Other jurisdictions have separate AHJs (NCFA, Redwood City FD, SSF FD, CAL FIRE/County Fire for unincorporated). This file covers SMCFD as the primary documented AHJ in the county.

## Adopted Code Edition

**Ordinance No. ORD-2025-003** (introduced 2025-09-17; adopted 2025-11-19 by SMCFD Board of Directors; effective **2026-01-01**).

Adopts: **2025 California Fire Code** (Cal. Code of Regs., Title 24, Part 9 — based on the 2024 International Fire Code published by ICC) with California Amendments, plus the **2024 International Wildland Urban Interface Code** with 2025 California WUI Code amendments.

Prior cycle: ORD-2022-001 (adopted 2022 CFC); ORD-2023-001 (supplemental). ORD-2025-002 separately adopted the 2025 FHSZ Map.

## Municipal-Code Chapter

SMCFD is a special district with its own ordinance code (not in the County Ordinance Code). The "Fire Code" under ORD-2025-003 replaces the prior SMCFD Fire Code. Copies kept on file with the SMCFD Fire Marshal's office.

## Local Amendments to the CFC (Part III, Division 1 of ORD-2025-003)

**Chapter 1 — Administration**

- **§ 101.1** (Title): Renamed "2025 California Fire Code … with amendments adopted by the SAN MATEO CONSOLIDATED FIRE DEPARTMENT (SMCFD)."
- **§ 102.3** (Change of Use/Occupancy): Any change to a more hazardous fire/life safety condition (determined by Fire Code Official) **requires automatic fire sprinkler system installation**. Relative hazard determined per IEBC Table 1011.5.
- **§ 102.6** (Historic Buildings): Amended to follow California Historic Building Code for designated historic buildings that do not constitute a distinct hazard.
- **§ 103.1** (Creation of Agency): SMCFD created; official in charge = Fire Code Official.
- **§ 103.2** (Appointment): DELETED.
- **§ 104.7** (Official Records): Records retained per SMCFD Record Retention Schedule and Title 24, Part 1.
- **§§ 104.7.1–104.7.6**: DELETED.
- **§ 105.3.1** (Permit Expiration): Construction permits expire if work not commenced within **180 days** of issuance, or suspended/abandoned for 180 days; permits not transferable; new permit required on change of occupancy, operation, tenancy, or ownership.
- **§ 108.2** (Permit Fees): Fees per the **SMCFD Master Fee Schedule** (updated Dec 16, 2025; available at smcfire.ca.gov/documents-forms-policies).
- **§ 112** (Means of Appeals): Board of Appeals = SMCFD Board of Directors. § 112.3 DELETED.
- **§ 113.4** (Violation Penalties): Reinspection fee per Master Fee Schedule; violations unabated after 5th reinspection = Administrative Violation: **$130** (1st), **$700** (2nd), **$1,300** (3rd and subsequent) per occurrence; each continuing day = separate offense.

**Chapter 2 — Definitions (Added)**

- *All Weather Driving Surface*: minimum 68,000 lb load capacity; minimum one layer asphalt, concrete, or road pavers.
- *Alteration*: work that increases fire area.
- *Driveway*: public vehicular access road including fire/emergency vehicles.

**Chapter 3 — General Precautions (Selected Amendments)**

- **§ 307.1.1 / 307.4.1**: Open burning and bonfires (other than cooking) **prohibited**.
- **§ 307.4.2**: Recreational fires ≥ 25 ft from structure require fire code permit when approved.
- **§ 324.1** (Mobile Food Preparation Vehicles — Added): Vehicles shall display a **San Mateo County health department sticker**.

**Chapter 4 — Emergency Planning**

Sections 405 and 406 (emergency evacuation drills; employee training) adopted.

**Chapter 5 — Fire Service Features**

- **§ 501.1**: Fire service features shall comply with this chapter and **Appendix D** (Fire Apparatus Access Roads).
- **§ 503.1.1**: Approved fire apparatus access roads required for every facility; access shall extend within **150 feet** of all exterior walls of first story.
- **§ 503.3.1** (Fire Lane Designation — Added): Three acceptable designation methods: white sign (≥ 12"×18", red letters ≥ 1") spaced ≤ 50 ft apart; outlining/hash marking with "Fire Lane – No Parking"; or red curb.

**Hazardous Materials — Above-Ground Storage Tanks**

- **§ 5704.2.9.6.1 / § 5706.2.4.4**: Storage of Class I and II liquids in above-ground tanks **prohibited** in all zoning districts except commercial, industrial, or agricultural.
- **§ 5806.2** (Flammable Cryogenics): Storage of flammable cryogenic fluids in stationary containers outside buildings **prohibited** except in industrial zones. Exception: hydrogen fuel systems per §§ 5806.3–5806.4.

**LPG (Chapter 61)**

- **§ 6104.2**: LPG storage prohibited in central business districts and all non-commercial/industrial/rural/agricultural zoning. Aggregate capacity of any one installation ≤ **2,000 gallons water capacity**.

**Energy Storage (Chapter 80)**

Adopted in entirety as in ORD-2022-001; **NFPA 855-23** (Standard for the Installation of Stationary Energy Storage Systems) added at § 1207.1.

**Explosive Materials (Chapter 56)**

In districts where explosive materials storage is permitted, quantities and distances per CFC **§ 5601.8**.

## Appendices Adopted

- **Appendix B** (Fire Flow Requirements): Modified Table B105.2 — with NFPA 13 sprinkler system, required flow reduced 50% but not below 1,000 gpm; with NFPA 13R, not below 1,500 gpm.
- **Appendix C** (Fire Hydrant Distribution): Modified Table C102.1 — average spacing 250 ft for most flow ranges; for 7,500+ gpm, 8+ hydrants at 200 ft spacing; max distance from hydrant to street frontage 150 ft.
- **Appendix D** (Fire Apparatus Access Roads): Adopted in entirety.
- **Appendix O** (Temporary Haunted Houses): Adopted in entirety.

## Wildland-Urban Interface (Division 2)

2025 CWUI Code adopted with amendments:
- Hazardous trees (dead trees posing fire hazard) within 30 ft of structure: maintain ≥ 10 ft crown clearance from combustible structures and between adjacent tree crowns; hazardous trees within 100 ft of important structure shall be removed.
- Automatic sprinkler systems per CFC Chapter 9 as amended by SMCFD.

## Plan Submission

Email to Plansubmist@smcfire.org; Plan Check Application required. Fees per Master Fee Schedule downloadable from smcfire.ca.gov/documents-forms-policies.

## Inspections

Fire/life safety inspections at frequency set by Fire Chief; reinspection fees per Master Fee Schedule.
