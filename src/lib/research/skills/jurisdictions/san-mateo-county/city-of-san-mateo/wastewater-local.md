## Who Needs a Waste Discharge Permit (WDP)

A WDP is required for:
- Industrial facilities regulated under federal pretreatment rules (40 CFR Part 403)
- Any facility the City determines warrants periodic monitoring to prevent discharge of
  problem pollutants into the sewer system
- Any food store, eating place, or food preparer that bakes or cooks on the premises
  (food service WDP)

Examples of regulated facility types: product manufacturing, commercial food preparation,
photographic processing, groundwater remediation operations.

## Permit Application Process (§ 7.38.220)

- Submit application to Regulatory Compliance at least **30 days** before commencing
  discharge. If discharge has already begun, submit within **10 business days**.
- Application contents (Director-established form) must include:
  - Legal business name; "doing business as" name; physical address; owner/operator info
  - Nature of industrial processes, volume of wastewater to be discharged, physical and
    chemical characteristics of wastewater
  - Site plans, floor plans, mechanical/plumbing plans showing all sewers and drains
  - Employee count and industrial classification (SIC/NAICS)
  - Statement certifying accuracy and completeness, signed by owner, corporate officer,
    general partner, or authorized representative
- Permit approved when: (1) applicant complies with Chapter 7.38 and applicable regulations;
  (2) all requested information is provided; (3) Director confirms adequate facilities for
  sampling, metering, conveying, treating, and disposing of industrial wastes.
- New sources may not begin operations before permit approval and completion of required
  pretreatment system installation.

## Permit Terms (§ 7.38.240)

- Permit includes effective date and expiration date.
- Holder must apply for reissuance at least **30 days** before expiration.
- Continued discharge after expiration = violation unless complete renewal application was
  timely filed.
- Standard permit conditions may include: pretreatment requirements, peak flow restrictions,
  specified discharge locations, prohibited wastewater components, time-of-day limitations.
- Director may require specific investigations/studies to reduce toxic constituents.

## Annual WDP Fee

- Fiscal Year 2024–25 annual waste discharge permit fee: **$778.00**
  (food service WDP; industrial WDP amounts may differ — contact Regulatory Compliance)
- Fee authorized under § 7.38.060; actual amounts set by City resolution.

## Pretreatment Technology Requirements (§ 7.38.100)

Pretreatment systems required when necessary to restrict/prevent discharge of certain
waste constituents or equalize peak discharges. All pretreatment systems must be approved
by the City.

| Facility Type | Required Pretreatment System |
|---|---|
| Commercial / manufacturing floor drains | Gravity separation interceptor to remove settleable and floatable solids |
| Food service (all kitchen drains) | Outdoor grease removal device (grease interceptor) — must never accumulate >25% FOG of designed capacity |
| Steam cleaning / radiator flushing | **Closed-loop water recycling and reuse system** — no discharge to sewer permitted |
| Photographic processing | Electrolytic recovery unit + two ion-exchange cartridges in series + pH neutralization block (or approved equivalent) |
| Dental clinics | Amalgam capture device on all equipment that might carry amalgam waste to the sanitary sewer |

Engineering design required for pretreatment systems; must be designed by a licensed engineer
if required by the Director.

## Local Discharge Limits (§ 7.38.120)

### General Prohibitions

| Parameter | Limit / Prohibition |
|---|---|
| Flash point | Closed-cup flashpoint < 140°F prohibited (fire/explosion hazard) |
| Explosive limit | Readings exceeding 5% LEL (two readings) or 10% LEL (single reading) prohibited |
| pH | Must be within **5.0–10.5** |
| Temperature | Discharge causing treatment process temp > **104°F (40°C)** prohibited |
| Floatable oil/grease | Maximum **20 mg/L** |
| Total oil/grease | Maximum **200 mg/L** |
| Dissolved sulfides | Maximum **0.1 mg/L** |

### Prohibited Solids / Viscous Materials

No discharge of: sand, glass, metal chips, bone, plastics, sludge, mud, straw, wood, bags,
rags, or grease from commercial kitchen operations in solid form, or any materials that
cannot pass a **0.5-inch screen**.

### Toxic Pollutant Limits (§ 7.38.120 — expressed as mg/L)

| Pollutant | Maximum (mg/L) |
|---|---|
| Arsenic | 0.1 |
| Cadmium | 0.2 |
| Chromium (total) | 0.5 |
| Copper | 2.0 |
| Cyanide | 1.0 |
| Lead | 1.0 |
| Mercury | 0.01 |
| Nickel | 1.0 |
| Silver | 0.2 |
| Zinc | 3.0 |

### Additional Prohibitions

- Non-removable color wastes
- Radioactive materials (except per California regulations)
- Foaming agents
- Unpolluted stormwater without Director authorization
- Trucked/hauled wastes without permit
