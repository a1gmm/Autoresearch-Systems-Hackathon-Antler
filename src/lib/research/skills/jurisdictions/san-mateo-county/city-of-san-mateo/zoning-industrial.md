## Zoning Code Location

**Municipal Code Title 27 — Zoning**
Full code: https://law.cityofsanmateo.org/us/ca/cities/san-mateo/code/27

**Framework chapter:** Chapter 27.54 — Manufacturing Districts (USE AND BULK
REGULATIONS, § 27.54.010). This chapter is organizational — it directs to Chapter 27.56
for substantive M1 regulations. No M2 or heavy manufacturing district classification
exists in San Mateo's current code; the M1 district is the sole industrial zone type.

**Primary industrial district chapter:** **Chapter 27.56 — M1 Districts (Manufacturing)**

## M1 District — Purpose

Create and maintain areas for light manufacturing, warehousing, and distribution where
air emissions, noise, and hazardous materials handling are limited. Residential uses
(other than on-site security housing) are prohibited. Non-security residential uses,
secondary units are prohibited.

## Permitted Uses by Right (§ 27.56.010)

1. All permitted uses in commercial districts, **except residential uses**
2. Assembly, production, processing, cleaning, testing, or repair of products
3. Contractor or construction shops
4. Laboratories: medical, dental, research, experimental, and testing
5. Residential dwelling units for security personnel when located on the premises where
   they are employed (on-site security only)
6. Wholesale and warehousing — **excluding storage of flammable (inflammable) liquids**
7. Accessory uses to principal uses
8. Other compatible uses as determined by the Zoning Administrator

## Special Uses (Require Special Use Permit — § 27.56.020)

1. All special uses allowed in commercial districts, **except residential uses and secondary
   units** (explicitly prohibited in M1 regardless of commercial allowance)
2. Storage of impounded motor vehicles on open lots
3. Drive-in theaters
4. **Wholesale storage of flammable (inflammable) liquids** (prohibited by right;
   requires SUP)
5. Other compatible uses as determined by the Zoning Administrator, subject to SUP approval

## Development Standards (Chapter 27.56)

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Maximum Floor Area Ratio (FAR) | **1.0** (§ 27.56.050) |
| Building Height | Per Building Height Plan of the General Plan (§ 27.56.055 — no numeric limit in code itself) |
| Minimum Front Yard | **15 feet** — may be reduced if 40% of lots in same block have shallower yards (§ 27.56.060) |
| Minimum Side Yard (each side) | **10% of lot width**, not required to exceed **10 feet** (§ 27.56.070) |

## Performance Standards (Chapter 27.56, §§ 27.56.090–27.56.150)

M1 operations are subject to the following performance-based controls:
- **§ 27.56.090** — Noise
- **§ 27.56.100** — Smoke and Particulate Matter
- **§ 27.56.110** — Odorous Matter
- **§ 27.56.120** — Vibration
- **§ 27.56.130** — Toxic or Noxious Matter
- **§ 27.56.140** — Glare and Heat
- **§ 27.56.150** — Fire and Explosive Hazards: "fire and explosive hazards shall be
  subject to the fire prevention regulations in **Chapter 23.28**" (the City's local fire
  code). No independent numeric limits are set in the zoning chapter itself — defers
  entirely to fire code thresholds.

## Special Use Permit (SUP) Process

San Mateo uses **"Special Use Permit"** terminology rather than "Conditional Use Permit."

**Governing chapter:** Chapter 27.74 — Special Use Permits

**Purpose (§ 27.74.010):** Three categories of uses may be permitted by SUP:
1. Municipally operated / publicly regulated utility uses traditionally affected by public
   interest
2. Privately operated uses requiring special permits due to locational needs, services
   provided, and potential neighborhood impacts
3. Nonconforming uses that can be made more compatible through permit conditions

**Authority to grant (§ 27.74.020):** The **Planning Commission** (or City Council on
appeal). Approval body must evaluate whether the permit would:
- Adversely affect the general health, safety, or welfare of the community
- Cause injury or disturbance to adjacent property through excessive noise, smoke, odor,
  noxious gas, dust, glare, heat, fumes, or industrial waste

**Eligible uses:** Officially designated "special use" items in any district, plus
non-designated uses that the Planning Commission determines are "virtually identical"
in impact and land use requirements to allowed uses.

**Changes to approved SUPs:** Any physical alteration, expansion, or change to an
existing approved special use requires Planning Commission review.

**Appeal:** Appeal of Planning Commission decision goes to City Council.

**Temporary Use Permits:** Chapter 27.74 also covers Temporary Use Permits (§§ 27.74.030–
27.74.050), a separate streamlined track for short-duration uses.

## Buffers

**§ 27.56.075** — Buffer requirements apply at M1 district boundaries. Specific dimensions
not retrieved; consult the code section directly for numeric standards.

## Hazmat Overlay Note

The M1 district purpose statement explicitly limits hazardous materials handling. Operations
involving significant hazmat storage above CUPA thresholds may require SUP review under
Ch. 27.74 in addition to the county CUPA permit. Flammable liquid storage (wholesale) is
a special use — requires SUP in M1 even at quantities below fire code permit triggers.
