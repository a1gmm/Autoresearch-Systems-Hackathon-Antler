## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA / Hazardous Materials

- **Agency:** San Mateo County Environmental Health Services (EHS) — designated countywide CUPA in 1996; the City of San Mateo is not its own CUPA and is fully served by the county.
- **Programs administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Hazardous Waste Generator, Underground Storage Tank (UST), Aboveground Petroleum Storage Tank (APSA), California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP), Tiered Permitting.
- **HMBP thresholds:** 55 gal (liquids) / 500 lb (solids) / 200 cu ft (compressed gas); initial submittal within 30 days of reaching threshold; annual update required; full review every 3 years.
- **Electronic reporting portal (MyEHS):** https://smcehs.my.site.com — businesses apply for CUPA permits, pay invoices, and manage permits online. CERS (CalEPA statewide system) is also accepted.
- **Fee schedule:** New fee schedule approved 2024-12-10, effective 2025-01-09. State surcharge $94 (raised from $84 in Oct 2023). CalARP surcharge $370 (raised from $270).
- **Contact:** (650) 372-6200 | smcupa@smcgov.org

### 2. Fire AHJ / Fire Code

- **AHJ:** San Mateo Consolidated Fire Department (SMCFD) — consolidated agency (formed 2019) serving the City of San Mateo, Belmont, and Foster City. Do NOT treat as a single-city department. Fire Marshal is the fire code official.
- **Adopted code:** 2025 California Fire Code (Cal. Code of Regs., Title 24, Part 9 — based on the 2024 International Fire Code) with local amendments, adopted via **Ordinance No. ORD-2025-003**. Also adopted: 2024 International Wildland Urban Interface Code with 2025 CA WUI amendments (Ordinance No. ORD-2025-002).
- **Key local amendments in ORD-2025-003:** Change of occupancy creating a more hazardous fire/life-safety condition requires automatic fire sprinkler installation (§ 102.3). Construction permits expire if work not commenced within 180 days or suspended 180 days (§ 105.3.1). Violations — reinspection fees per SMCFD Master Fee Schedule; unabated after 5th reinspection: $130 (1st), $700 (2nd), $1,300 (3rd+) per Gov. Code § 36900.
- **Fee schedule:** 2026 SMCFD Fee Schedule effective 2026-02-14 (approved 2025-12-16); most fees increased ~25%, some more than doubled. All permit and inspection fees set by SMCFD Master Fee Schedule referenced in § 108.2.
- **Fee schedule PDF:** https://www.smcfire.org/files/95fdc568e/SMCFD_Final+Fee+Schedule_12.16.16+final.pdf
- **Portal / plan submittals:** https://smcfire.ca.gov/plan-submittals

### 3. Industrial Wastewater / Pretreatment (POTW)

- **POTW operator:** City of San Mateo Public Works — Regulatory Compliance division (1949 Pacific Blvd, San Mateo, CA 94403).
- **Governing ordinance:** Municipal Code **Chapter 7.38** (Sanitary Sewer Use). Key sections: § 7.38.100 (Pretreatment of Industrial Waste), § 7.38.120 (Specific Discharge Prohibitions), § 7.38.220 (Waste Discharge Permit Procedure).
- **Waste Discharge Permit (WDP) required for:** industrial facilities regulated under federal pretreatment rules; any facility the City determines warrants monitoring to prevent discharge of problem pollutants. Examples: product manufacturing, commercial food preparation, photographic processing, groundwater remediation.
- **Pretreatment requirements by facility type:**
  - Food service: outdoor grease interceptor on all drains.
  - Steam cleaning / radiator flush: closed-loop water recycling; no discharge to sewer.
  - Photo-processing: electrolytic recovery unit + two ion-exchange cartridges in series.
  - Dental clinics: amalgam capture device.
- **Permit application:** filed at least 30 days before commencing discharge; submit to Regulatory Compliance (no online portal — paper/mail). Director may require process descriptions, wastewater characterization, site plans, metering details.
- **Contact:** (650) 522-7300

### 4. MS4 / Stormwater

- **Governing ordinance:** Municipal Code **Chapter 7.39** (Stormwater Management and Discharge Control). Key sections: § 7.39.030 (Definitions), § 7.39.090 (Discharge of Pollutants — illicit discharge prohibition), § 7.39.120 (Reduction of Pollutants in Stormwater), § 7.39.235 (Stormwater Management Permit).
- **Stormwater Management (SWM) permit:** issued by Director of Public Works; required before approval of any property that must install, operate, or maintain stormwater treatment facilities, parking lot inlets, or other stormwater pollution prevention infrastructure.
- **NPDES context:** City is a co-permittee under the San Francisco Bay Regional Water Quality Control Board Municipal Regional Stormwater NPDES Permit; County updated its overlay ordinance (Chapter 4.100) following the 2022 NPDES permit reissuance.

### 5. Zoning / Land Use (Industrial Districts)

- **Zoning code:** Municipal Code **Title 27** (Zoning) — online at https://law.cityofsanmateo.org/us/ca/cities/san-mateo/code/27
- **Primary industrial district:** **Chapter 27.56 — M1 Districts (Manufacturing)**. Purpose: create and maintain light manufacturing, warehousing, and distribution where air emissions, noise, and hazardous materials handling are limited. Permitted uses include assembly, production, processing, cleaning, testing, repair, contractor shops, laboratories, wholesale/warehousing. Warehousing of flammable liquids is explicitly prohibited. Non-security residential uses prohibited.
- **Also:** Chapter 27.54 (Manufacturing Districts — broader framework). Conditional/special uses covered in § 27.56.020 [VERIFY — section not retrieved].
- **Special Use Permit (SUP) process:** Chapter 27.74 (Special Use Permits); Chapter 27.62 (Planned Developments — Special Use Permits). Note: San Mateo uses "Special Use Permit" rather than "Conditional Use Permit" terminology.
- **Administration:** Chapter 27.06; review procedure Chapter 27.10.

## Gotchas

- **Fire AHJ:** SMCFD is a multi-city consolidated district — not the City's own department. Permits, inspections, and appeals all go through SMCFD, not a city fire bureau.
- **CUPA:** Entirely county-administered; city has no separate hazardous materials permitting track.
- **WDP portal:** No online application portal for wastewater discharge permits — paper application to Public Works Regulatory Compliance required.
- **M1 zoning + hazmat:** The M1 purpose statement explicitly limits hazardous materials handling; operations with significant hazmat storage may need Special Use Permit review under Ch. 27.74.
