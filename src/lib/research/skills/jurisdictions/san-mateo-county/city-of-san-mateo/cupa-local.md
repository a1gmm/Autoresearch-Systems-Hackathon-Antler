## Agency & CUPA Status

**San Mateo County Environmental Health Services (SMC-EHS)**
- Designated countywide CUPA in 1996 by the California Secretary for Environmental
  Protection.
- The City of San Mateo is **not its own CUPA** — all CUPA programs are fully
  administered by the county on behalf of the city.
- No separate city-level hazardous materials permitting track exists.

## Governing Ordinance

**San Mateo County Ordinance Code Chapter 4.92** — "Storage of Hazardous Substances"
- Consolidated ordinance encompassing all hazardous materials/waste programs
  administered by EHS.
- Major revision effective **August 10, 2018** (Board of Supervisors approved March 27,
  2018): removed outdated requirements, aligned with state regulations, formalized CUPA
  permit requirement, established civil/administrative enforcement structure, authorized
  unannounced inspections and after-hours inspection fees (after 6 PM).
- State enabling authority: California Health & Safety Code Division 20, Chapter 6.95.

## Programs Administered

All six CUPA unified programs:
1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — H&S Code §§ 25500–25520
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator Program** — H&S Code §§ 25100–25250
3. **Tiered Permitting Program** (on-site hazardous waste treatment)
4. **Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program** — H&S Code §§ 25280–25299
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP) Program** — H&S Code §§ 25531–25543
6. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Tank (APSA) Program** — H&S Code §§ 25270–25270.13

## HMBP Reporting Thresholds

Minimum reportable quantities triggering HMBP submittal (H&S Code § 25507):
- Liquids: **55 gallons**
- Solids: **500 pounds**
- Compressed gases: **200 cubic feet** (at standard temperature and pressure)

Initial HMBP submittal required: within **30 days** of first reaching threshold quantities.
Significant changes: update within **30 days**.
Annual update: required for Business Activities, Owner/Operator, and Hazardous Materials
sections.
Triennial certification: full plan recertified every **3 years**.

## Electronic Reporting / Portal

- **MyEHS portal:** https://smcehs.my.site.com — businesses apply for CUPA permits,
  pay invoices, and manage permits online.
- **CERS (CalEPA statewide system):** also accepted for HMBP submissions.
