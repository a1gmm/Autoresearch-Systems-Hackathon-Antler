## Adopting Agency

**San Mateo Consolidated Fire Department (SMCFD)** — consolidated district (formed 2019)
serving the City of San Mateo, Belmont, and Foster City. The Fire Marshal is the fire code
official. SMCFD is the AHJ for all three cities; the City of San Mateo has no separate city
fire bureau.

## Adopted Code & Ordinance

- **Code edition adopted:** 2025 California Fire Code (Cal. Code of Regs., Title 24, Part 9),
  based on the 2024 International Fire Code (IFC) published by the International Code Council,
  with California Amendments.
- **Ordinance number:** ORD-2025-003
- **Introduction date:** September 17, 2025
- **Adoption date:** November 19, 2025 (regular Board of Directors meeting)
- **Effective date:** January 1, 2026
- **WUI code also adopted:** 2024 International Wildland Urban Interface Code with 2025
  California WUI amendments — via the same ordinance (Part III, Division 2).
- **Municipal code chapter:** City of San Mateo Municipal Code Chapter 23.28 (Fire
  Code); zoning defers to Chapter 23.28 for fire/explosive hazard performance standards
  (see SMMC 27.56.150).

## Local Amendments to the 2025 CFC

All modifications below are from ORD-2025-003, Part III, Division 1.

### § 102.3 Change of Occupancy
Any change in use or occupancy creating a **more hazardous fire/life-safety condition**
(as determined by the Fire Code Official) requires installation of an automatic fire sprinkler
system. Relative hazard categories are determined by Table 1011.5 of the International
Existing Building Code (incorporated by reference).

### § 103.1 Agency Creation
SMCFD is established as the fire code agency. The official in charge is the fire code official.

### § 105.3.1 Permit Expiration
Construction permits expire if work is not commenced within **180 days** of issuance, or if
work is suspended or abandoned for **180 days** after commencement. Reinstatement requires
a new permit and payment of the Permit Reinstatement Fee per the SMCFD Fee Schedule.
Permits are **not transferable** — any change in occupancy, operation, tenancy, or ownership
requires a new permit.

### § 108.2 Permit Fees
All permit and inspection fees are set by the **SMCFD Master Fee Schedule** (no dollar
amounts embedded in the code itself).

### § 112 Board of Appeals
Appeals of fire code official decisions go to the **SMCFD Board of Directors** (not a
separate appeals board). Decisions issued in writing to the appellant through the fire code
official.

### § 113.4 Violation Penalties
Violations result in reinspection fees per the SMCFD Master Fee Schedule. Violations
remaining unabated after the **5th reinspection** become Administrative Violations:
- 1st violation: **$130**
- 2nd violation: **$700**
- 3rd and each successive violation: **$1,300**
(Each day of continued violation after notice = separate offense; authority: Government
Code § 36900.)

### § 307 Open Burning
- Open burning (other than cooking) is **prohibited** (§ 307.1.1).
- Bonfires (other than for cooking) are **prohibited** (§ 307.4.1).
- Recreational fires, if approved: minimum **25-foot separation** from structures;
  require a fire code permit (§ 307.4.2).

### § 503.1.1 Fire Apparatus Access
Access roads must extend to within **150 feet** of all portions of a building's exterior walls
(first story), measured by approved route around the exterior. Fire apparatus must support
minimum **68,000-lb** load; minimum surface finish = one layer of asphalt, concrete, or
road pavers.

### § 505.1.1 Multi-Tenant Addressing
All occupancies within a building must have numbers or letters. Addresses must be visible
from the street and internally or externally illuminated in all new construction.

### § 506.1 Knox Box
Key boxes must be **Knox Company** brand. Existing non-Knox boxes must be replaced
upon: (a) mandatory inspections in multi-residential buildings (H&S Code § 13146.2), or
(b) any permit-requiring work in other occupancy types. Exception: R-3 occupancies.

### § 508.2 Fire Control Room (Added)
Required in all new buildings or change-of-use occupancies requiring automatic sprinkler
systems. Minimum size: **35 sq ft** (200 sq ft if fire pump is present; 200 sq ft in
high-rise buildings). Must be at grade with exterior door access. Must contain sprinkler
riser, fire alarm control panels, and ERCES control equipment.

### § 903.2 Automatic Sprinklers — New Buildings
Approved automatic sprinkler systems required throughout **all new buildings** unless no
building permit is required. Exception: Detached Group U occupancies separated per
Table 705.5 of the California Building Code.

### § 903.6 Automatic Sprinklers — Existing Buildings
Existing non-sprinklered buildings must install full sprinkler systems if, over any
three-year period, additions/alterations/fire repairs exceed **50% of original gross floor area**.
Trigger also applies to any single addition exceeding 50% of existing gross floor area.

### § 510 Emergency Responder Communication (ERCES)
In-building two-way emergency responder communication coverage required in all new
buildings. New ERCES systems must be **UL-Certified**. Installing contractor must hold a
State-licensed C-7, C-10, or C-16 license.

### § 324.1 Mobile Food Vehicles (Added)
Mobile food preparation vehicles must display a **San Mateo County Health Department**
sticker.

## Hazmat & High-Piled Storage Notes

ORD-2025-003 does not contain explicit local amendments to CFC Chapter 50 (Hazardous
Materials) or Chapter 32 (High-Piled Storage) in the extracted text. The 2025 CFC baseline
hazmat permit thresholds and high-piled storage requirements apply without local
modification per this ordinance. For operations triggering CUPA thresholds, see
cupa-local.md.
