## Agency

**San Mateo County Environmental Health Services (EHS)** — designated CUPA by the CA Secretary for Environmental Protection in **1996**. Operates as a **single countywide CUPA** covering all 20 incorporated cities and unincorporated county areas. CUPA functions sit within County Health (not fire department or Public Works).

**Contact:** (650) 372-6200
**Portal:** MyEHS — https://smcehs.my.site.com/s/
**Electronic reporting:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov

## Local Ordinance

**San Mateo County Ordinance Code, Chapter 4.92 — Storage of Hazardous Substances** (Title 4 — Safety and Health).

Revised **August 10, 2018** to:
- Consolidate all six Unified Program elements under Chapter 4.92 (eliminated separate ordinances for each program).
- Formalize requirement to obtain and maintain a CUPA permit.
- Authorize unannounced inspections during normal business hours; after-hours inspections (after 6 pm) billed at hourly rate ($286/hr per 2025 fee schedule).
- Establish civil and administrative enforcement structure for violations.
- Remove blanket requirement treating all UST components as hazardous waste upon removal (replaced with case-by-case determination).

## Own-CUPA vs. Under-County

San Mateo County EHS is the **own-CUPA** — not sub-delegated to cities. All regulated businesses in the county (including those in cities with their own fire departments) are regulated by the county CUPA for all six program elements.

## Six Unified Program Elements Administered

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — Community Right-to-Know / CalEPA CERS reporting.
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator Program** — tiered by annual generation quantity.
3. **Tiered Permitting Program (TP)** — Conditionally Exempt (CE), Conditional Authorization (CA), Permit by Rule (PBR).
4. **Underground Storage Tank (UST) Program**.
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — three risk management programs.
6. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — tanks ≥ 1,320 gal aboveground or aggregate ≥ 660 gal.

## HMBP Reporting Thresholds (Statewide Minimums, enforced by county)

Business must file HMBP when handling hazardous materials at or above:
- **Liquids:** 55 gallons
- **Solids:** 500 pounds
- **Compressed gases:** 200 cubic feet (at STP)

Key exemptions: retail consumer products used as intended; lubricating oil ≤ 55 gal per type / ≤ 275 gal total; compressed O₂/N₂/N₂O < 1,000 cu ft for medical providers; solid hazardous materials < 5,000 lb classified solely as irritant/sensitizer.

Updates due within 30 days of significant operational change; complete review every 3 years; annual certification of key sections.
