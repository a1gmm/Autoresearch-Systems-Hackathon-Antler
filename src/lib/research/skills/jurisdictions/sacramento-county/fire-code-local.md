## Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)

Sacramento County has three overlapping AHJs depending on geography:

| Territory | AHJ |
|---|---|
| Unincorporated county + Citrus Heights + Rancho Cordova | Sacramento Metropolitan Fire District ("Metro Fire") |
| City of Sacramento | City of Sacramento Fire Department |
| Unincorporated areas outside Metro Fire boundaries | Sacramento County Fire Warden (Sacramento County Code Title 17) |

This file covers **Metro Fire**, which is the dominant industrial-area AHJ.

## Local Amendments in Ordinance 2025-02

Ordinance 2025-02 amends the 2025 CFC in the following sections (verbatim from PDF amendment summary table):

**Chapter 1 — Administration:**
- § 105.5.5 Carnivals, fairs, festivals or exhibitions — operational permit required (amended)
- § 105.6.26 Architectural — construction permit required for all new buildings, alterations, additions, tenant improvements (added)
- § 105.6.27 Civil Engineering — construction permit required for civil improvements (added)
- § 105.6.28 Underground Fire Service — construction permit required prior to installation (added)
- § 108.7 Cost Recovery Fees — costs may be charged for responses triggered by negligence or law violation (H&S Code 13916) (added)
- § 110.3.1 Electronic Filing — inspection/test/maintenance records shall be submitted electronically when required by fire code official (added)
- § 112.1 Board of Appeals — appeals heard by the Board of Directors of the fire district (amended)
- § 113.4 Violation Penalties — fine $100–$1,000 and/or imprisonment up to 180 days; each day = separate offense (amended)
- § 113.4.2 Citations — Chief or designee may issue citations under H&S Code 13871 (added)
- § 113.4.3 California Bail Schedule — bail amounts for violations: misdemeanors $2,700 total; other sections as scheduled (added)
- § 114.4 Failure to Comply — infraction or misdemeanor, $100–$1,000 fine (amended)

**Chapter 2 — Definitions:**
- "Supervising Station" — must be UL-listed, Type A, Full Service Central Station; must relay to Sacramento Regional Fire/EMS Communications Center or Sacramento International Airport Communication Center (amended)
- "False Alarm" — willful or negligent initiation when no danger exists (amended)

**Chapter 5 — Fire Service Features:**
- § 503.6.1 Electrified Security Fences — approval by fire code official required prior to installation (added)
- § 507.1.1 Connection — buildings without public water supply shall connect once supply becomes available (added; exception: Group U)
- § 510.4.1.1 Minimum Signal Strength into Buildings — inbound minimum −95 dBm, DAQ ≥ 3.4 (amended)
- § 510.4.1.2 Minimum Signal Strength out of Buildings — uplink DAQ ≥ 3.4 (amended)

**Chapter 9 — Fire Protection & Life Safety:**
- § 901.4.7 Pump and Riser Room Size — approved rooms required in all new sprinklered buildings; doors/passageways large enough for equipment removal (amended; exception: Group R-3)
- § 901.4.7.4 Lighting — permanent and emergency illumination in riser rooms, fire control rooms, fire pump rooms (amended)
- § 903.2 Where Required — automatic sprinkler system thresholds amended for Groups A-1 through A-4, B, E, F-1, F-2, M, R-3 (manufactured housing), S-1, S-2, U: trigger threshold is **3,599 sq ft** (amended from standard 12,000 sq ft or occupant-load triggers) for most assembly, factory, mercantile, and storage occupancies
- § 903.2.4.4 Group F-2 — sprinklers required when fire area exceeds 3,599 sq ft (added)
- § 903.2.9.1 Repair Garages — sprinklers required (amended)
- § 903.2.10.1 Commercial Parking Garages — sprinklers required (amended)
- § 903.2.10.3 Group S-2 parking garages — added
- § 903.2.18.1 Group U private garages/carports accessory to R-3 — added
- § 903.3.8.4 Supervision — amended
- § 903.3.10 Floor Control Valves — amended
- § 903.6 / 903.6.1 Where Required / Monitoring — amended

**Chapter 56 — Explosives:**
- § 5601.1.6 Fireworks Display — NFPA 1123 governs outdoor public fireworks displays (added; exception: California Safe and Sane consumer fireworks)

**Chapter 50 — Hazardous Materials:**
- § 5003.9.1.2 Emergency Response Support Information — ready access to floor plans, SDSs, HMMPs, and HMISs required as determined by fire code official (added)
- Note: CFC Chapter 50 (Hazardous Materials general) and Chapter 53 (Compressed gases), including maximum allowable quantities, are adopted from state CFC without additional local overlay per Ordinance 2025-02. See CFC § 5001–5003 for MAQ tables.

**Chapter 80 & Appendices:**
- Chapter 80: NFPA 1123-26 added as referenced standard
- Appendix B, Table B105.2: Required fire flow for buildings (amended) — graduated from 1,000 gpm for ≤3,500 sq ft up to fire-flow requirements per table; hydrant maximum distance 120–150 ft from any point on road frontage
- Appendix C, Tables C102.1, C104.2, C106.1: Required hydrant number/spacing and hydrant type (amended)

## High-Piled Storage Permit (CFC Chapter 32)

An **operational permit** is required to use any building or portion as a high-piled storage area exceeding **500 square feet**.

**Definition (CFC § 202):** Storage of combustible materials in closely packed piles or on pallets/racks/shelves where the **top of storage is greater than 12 feet** (or greater than 6 feet for high-hazard commodities such as rubber tires, Class A plastics, flammable liquids, idle pallets).

Relevant CFC sections: 3201.4 (evacuation plan), 3203 (commodity classifications), 3205 (housekeeping), 3206.2 (Table of general fire protection requirements).

Source (checklist): https://metrofire.ca.gov/files/a106113ed/high+pile+storage+checklist.pdf
