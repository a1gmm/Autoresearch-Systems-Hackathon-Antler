## CUPA Agency

**Agency:** Sacramento County Environmental Management Department (EMD), Environmental Compliance Division  
**Designation:** CalEPA-certified CUPA for **all of Sacramento County**, including all incorporated cities (City of Sacramento, Citrus Heights, Elk Grove, Folsom, Galt, Rancho Cordova, Isleton). The County is the single CUPA; cities do not run their own separate CUPAs.

## Six Unified Programs Administered

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — inventory + emergency response plan filed via CERS
2. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — operating permits, inspections, closure
3. **Aboveground Storage Tanks (AST)** — operating permits for tanks ≥ 1,320 gallons
4. **Hazardous Waste Generator / Tiered Permitting (HW/TP)** — generator fees + tiered permitting (CE/CA/PBR)
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — Risk Management Plan (RMP) review, three program levels
6. **Recycled Used Oil** — collection program oversight

## HMBP Reporting Thresholds

HMBP required when a business handles or stores hazardous materials at or above:
- Liquids: **≥ 55 gallons**
- Solids: **≥ 500 pounds**
- Compressed gases: **≥ 200 cubic feet** (at STP)

**Exemptions** (partial list):
- ≤ 55 gal of certain lubricating oils (max 275 gal total)
- ≤ 500 gal propane used solely for heating/cooking
- Consumer products for direct public distribution

**Electronic submission only:** via California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) at https://cers.calepa.ca.gov — paper plans no longer accepted.  
**Update trigger:** Within 30 days of significant operational change.  
**Inspection frequency:** At least once every three years.

## Permit Application Process

1. Complete the Business Survey Questionnaire (online at saccounty.wufoo.com) to determine which CUPA programs apply.
2. EMD may conduct a facility walkthrough to assess permit needs.
3. Register and submit all required documentation electronically via **CERS** at https://cers.calepa.ca.gov.
4. Pay annual permit fee (fee code depends on program and tier).
5. EMD issues permit; routine inspections occur at least every 3 years; violations result in Notice to Comply (typically 30-day correction period).
