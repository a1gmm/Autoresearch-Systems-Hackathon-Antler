## Adopted Edition

The City of Sacramento adopted the **2022 California Fire Code** (CFC), which is Part 9 of the 2022 California Building Standards Code, Title 24, California Code of Regulations (based on IFC 2021).

- **Municipal Code Chapter:** Sacramento City Code Title 15, **Chapter 15.36** — "2022 California Fire Code"
- **Adopting Ordinance:** Ordinance **2023-0014 § 3**
- **Adoption Language (§ 15.36.010):** The 2022 CFC "is hereby adopted by reference and incorporated into this code except as expressly superseded, amended, or not adopted by the local amendments set forth in this chapter."

## Local Amendments — Chapter 15.36.050

Sacramento's local amendments (§ 15.36.050) include the following material changes to the base 2022 CFC:

### Automatic Sprinkler System (§ 903.2)
- For all occupancies except Group R-3 and detached U: an approved automatic sprinkler system and **electronic monitoring system** shall be installed in every new building where **total floor area exceeds 3,599 sq ft** (1,097 m²).
- "Electronic monitoring system" is defined as an approved method to electronically detect and transmit information about activated fire sprinkler systems to an approved alarm service provider.
- § 903.2.11.7: Sprinklers required for all **covered floats, marinas, piers, and other covered floating structures** that are commercially operated and exceed 3,599 sq ft.

### High-Piled Combustible Storage (§ 503.1.3)
- Local amendments address high-piled storage provisions; operational permit required for high-piled storage areas exceeding **500 sq ft** (consistent with CFC Chapter 32).

### Hazardous Materials / HPM
- An operational permit is required to **store, handle, or use hazardous production materials** (HPM) under CFC Chapter 27.
- "Qualified attendant" is defined as an individual trained in the proper methods of handling, storage, and dispensing of any material regulated by the code.
- Note: General hazmat operational permit thresholds (Maximum Allowable Quantities) follow CFC Tables 5003.1.1(1)/(2) — no local table deviation found in the published amendments.

### Fireworks (§ 5601)
- Permits required for the **sale and display of "Safe and Sane" approved fireworks** as permitted and regulated by Sacramento City Code Chapter 8.48.

> NOTE: The **Sacramento Metropolitan Fire District** (metrofire.ca.gov) is a separate AHJ serving unincorporated county areas and some surrounding communities — it adopted its own ordinance (SMFD Ordinance 2025-02) adopting the **2025 CFC**. For facilities within City of Sacramento limits, the City Fire Dept is AHJ and the 2022 CFC (Ord 2023-0014) applies.

## Operational Permit Types Relevant to Industrial Facilities

Per CFC Chapter 1 as locally adopted, operational permits are required for (among others):
- Hazardous materials storage/use (CFC § 105.6)
- High-piled combustible storage > 500 sq ft (CFC § 105.6.26)
- Hazardous production materials (CFC Chapter 27)
- Flammable/combustible liquids
- Compressed gases
