## Governing Ordinances

### SASD Sewer Ordinance
- **Name:** Sacramento Area Sewer District Sewer Ordinance
- **Number:** SDI–0079
- **Adoption Date:** April 27, 2022
- **Effective Date:** May 27, 2022
- **PDF:** https://sacsewer-bucket.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/sacsewer-ordinance_effective-4-27-23.pdf

The SASD Ordinance governs connection, collection-system use, sewer impact fees, and wastewater discharge permits within SASD's service area. It expressly incorporates and enforces the **Regional San Consolidated Ordinance** for industrial pretreatment standards.

> "The District collects wastewater and diverts it to the conveyance and treatment systems of the Sacramento Regional County Sanitation District (Regional San) and therefore must enforce all the provisions of the Regional San Consolidated Ordinance in addition to the provisions of the Sacramento Area Sewer District Sewer Ordinance." — SASD Ordinance § 1 (Scope)

### Regional San Consolidated Ordinance
- Governs pretreatment/industrial discharge standards applicable to all SASD users
- Sets local discharge limits, categorical pretreatment standards, and enforcement procedures
- Available at: https://www.regionalsan.com/ordinance (redirects to https://www.sacsewer.com/ordinance)

## Discharge Prohibitions (SASD Ordinance § 2)

The following are **prohibited** from the sewer collection system (in addition to Regional San prohibitions):
- Stormwater, surface water, pool water, groundwater, roof runoff, or subsurface drainage
- Fats, oils, and grease (FOG) causing build-up in the collection system
- Trucked and hauled wastes
- Liquids or vapors with temperature **> 150°F**
- Substances causing excessive foaming
- Cementitious materials
- Garbage > ¼-inch in any dimension
- Substances damaging sewer infrastructure

## Wastewater Discharge Permit — Thresholds

A **Wastewater Discharge Permit** is required when discharge volume, rate, and characteristics warrant it as determined by the District Engineer. Published thresholds include:
- Discharges of **≥ 25,000 gallons/day** of process wastewater, OR
- **> 37.5 lbs/day BOD**, OR
- **> 37.5 lbs/day TSS**, OR
- **> 8.3 lbs/day Total Kjeldahl Nitrogen (TKN)**, OR
- Classification as a **categorical industrial user** under 40 CFR Part 403 (EPA categorical standards)

Common categorical users: industrial bakeries, laundries, food/beverage processors, metal finishers, water treatment plants, liquid waste haulers, mobile pressure washers, groundwater remediation, landfill leachate.

## Permit Application Process

1. File a **Wastewater Discharge Permit Application** on a form provided by the District
2. Submit: identifying info, flow rates, time/duration of discharge, specific pollutant concentrations, wastewater characteristics, all discharge point locations, description of activities
3. District Engineer evaluates and issues permit with conditions (flow limits, loading rates, monitoring, spill plans, inspection access)
4. **Temporary Discharge Permit** available for short-duration/intermittent discharges; apply ≥ 30 days before commencement

Contact for permits: wscs@sacsewer.com | (916) 875-6470
Forms: https://www.sacsewer.com/forms-documents

## Local Discharge Limits

The SASD Ordinance itself governs collection-system prohibitions; numerical pollutant limits (metals, pH, etc.) for industrial dischargers are set in the **Regional San Consolidated Ordinance**, not in the SASD Ordinance. The Regional San local limits are set to protect EchoWater's treatment process and NPDES permit.

> NOTE: The actual discharge-limits table (mg/L values for metals, pH, etc.) is in the Regional San Consolidated Ordinance. The document is available at https://www.sacsewer.com/ordinance but could not be fully extracted due to site access limitations. Researchers should download directly or contact wscs@sacsewer.com for the current local limits table.

## Pretreatment Program Administration

SacSewer's **Wastewater Source Control Section** administers the Pretreatment Program:
- Regulatory oversight of industrial/commercial sewer discharges
- Protects EchoWater RRVF, public health, and the collection system
- Source reduction programs, outreach, incident response
- Permit administration for non-domestic waste streams

## Preliminary Treatment Requirements

Grease interceptors required for food-service establishments (restaurants, cafes, fast food, etc.) and other facilities discharging significant FOG. Sand/grit interceptors may be required by District Engineer. All must be designed per California Plumbing Code.
