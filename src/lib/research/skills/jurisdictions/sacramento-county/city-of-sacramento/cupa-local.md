## CUPA Agency

The City of Sacramento does **NOT** operate its own CUPA. The city is served by:

**Sacramento County Environmental Management Department (EMD)**
Environmental Compliance Division
10060 Goethe Road, Sacramento, CA 95827
Phone: (916) 875-8550
Email: cupapermit@saccounty.net
Web: https://emd.saccounty.gov/EC/CUPA/Pages/default.aspx

EMD was designated as the Sacramento region's CUPA by **CalEPA** and administers the program "throughout the County of Sacramento and its incorporated cities."

## Local Ordinance Authority

Sacramento County Code governs the CUPA programs:

| Chapter | Title | State Authority |
|---------|-------|-----------------|
| **6.96** | Hazardous Materials Business Plans and the California Accidental Release Prevention Program | H&SC § 6.95 + § 6.11 (CalARP) |
| **6.98** | Hazardous Waste Generators | H&SC §§ 6.5, 6.11 |
| **6.99** | Environmental Management Department Regulatory Fees | (fee schedule enabling chapter) |
| **6.34** | Underground Storage Tanks | H&SC § 25280 et seq. |

Key provision from Chapter 6.96: "No person shall own or operate a business within the County of Sacramento that stores or manages hazardous materials in quantities in excess of threshold levels stipulated in Section 25503.5 of the Health and Safety Code unless by authority of a valid, unexpired, unsuspended and not revoked permit."

## Programs Administered (CUPA Unified Program Elements)

1. **Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP)** — H&SC Chapter 6.95
2. **Hazardous Waste Generator / Tiered Permitting** — H&SC Chapter 6.5
3. **Underground Storage Tanks (UST)** — H&SC Chapter 6.7
4. **Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act (APSA)** — H&SC Chapter 6.67
5. **California Accidental Release Prevention (CalARP)** — H&SC Chapter 6.11
6. **Uniform Fire Code Hazardous Materials Management Plans / Inventory** (coordinated with Fire Dept)

## Permit Thresholds — HMBP

A Hazardous Materials Business Plan is required if a business stores or handles hazardous materials at or above:
- **Liquids:** 55 gallons
- **Solids:** 500 pounds
- **Compressed gases:** 200 cubic feet (at STP)

Submission via **California Environmental Reporting System (CERS)**: https://cers.calepa.ca.gov

Inspections by EMD: at least every **three years**. Annual review/update required; revision within **30 days** of significant changes.
