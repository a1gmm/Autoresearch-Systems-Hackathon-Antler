## M-1 Zone — Light Industrial (§§ 17.220.100 – 17.220.160)

**Purpose:** Permits light manufacturing, warehouse, and industrial uses with minimal off-site impacts. Often serves as a buffer between heavier industrial and commercial/residential areas.

**Permitted Uses (§ 17.220.110):**
- Aquaculture and community/market gardens of all sizes (by right, subject to standards)
- Light manufacturing, assembly, and fabrication uses with minimal off-site nuisance
- Warehouse and distribution uses
- Wholesale trade establishments
- All uses allowed in the M-1 zone that are listed as permitted — all others are prohibited unless listed as conditional

**Conditional Uses (§ 17.220.130):**
- Uses requiring a Conditional Use Permit (CUP) as specified in the section
- All uses not listed as permitted, accessory, or conditional are prohibited

**Key Prohibition:** All uses not listed as permitted, accessory, or conditional uses are prohibited.

Source: §§ 17.220.110, 17.220.130 — https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/sacramentoca/latest/sacramento_ca/0-0-0-35496

## M-2 Zone — Heavy Industrial (§§ 17.220.300 – 17.220.360)

**Purpose:** Accommodates heavy industrial uses with greater off-site impacts than M-1.

**Permitted Uses (§ 17.220.310):** Heavy manufacturing and industrial operations beyond M-1 scope.

**Heavy Industrial Overlay (§ 17.440.104):** An additional overlay section governs M-2 heavy industrial zone requirements.

## M-1(S) and M-2(S) — Screened Variants

The "(S)" suffix designates a **screened** version of the underlying zone, typically requiring additional landscaping or visual screening between industrial uses and adjacent lower-intensity areas. Permitted uses generally mirror the base zone with additional screening standards.

- M-1(S) Permitted Uses: § 17.220.210
- M-2(S) Permitted Uses: § 17.220.410

A recent amendment — **Ordinance 2026-0001** (adopted January 13, 2026) — amended § 17.220.210 (M-1(S) zone), indicating the code is actively updated.

## MIP — Manufacturing-Industrial Park Zone

Intended for higher-quality, campus-style industrial development. More restrictive on outdoor storage and uses than M-1. Article V, §§ 17.220.500–17.220.560.

## MRD — Manufacturing, Research and Development Zone

Designed for R&D, tech manufacturing, and light industrial uses compatible with adjacent commercial/office development. Permitted uses at § 17.220.610.

## M-T — Industrial and Transit-Area Zone

Intended to leverage transit access; allows mixed industrial and transit-oriented uses. Article VII, §§ 17.220.700–17.220.760.

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process

CUPs in industrial zones are governed by Sacramento City Code **Chapter 17.808** (Conditional Use Permit, Variance, Legislative Change Request, Administrative Permit, and Emergency Building Permit).
