> Local EHS authority reference for **City of Sacramento**. Orientation for the research subagent: who administers the local programs and where their adopted codes live. Verify the live page before quoting; confirm the adopted Title 24 edition (2022 CFC via Ordinance 2023-0014; 2025 CFC NOT yet adopted by the city — that is the Sacramento Metropolitan Fire District serving unincorporated county).

## CUPA (Unified Program)

- **No city CUPA** — served by Sacramento County Environmental Management Department (EMD)
- https://emd.saccounty.gov/EC/CUPA/Pages/default.aspx
- County Code authority: Ch. 6.96 (HMBP/CalARP), Ch. 6.98 (HW generators), Ch. 6.99 (fees), Ch. 6.34 (UST)
- See → **cupa-local.md**

## Fire authority (AHJ)

- Sacramento Fire Department — https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/fire
- Adopted CFC: 2022 California Fire Code, City Code Ch. 15.36, Ord. 2023-0014
- See → **fire-code-local.md**

## POTW / Sewer authority

- **Collection:** Sacramento Area Sewer District (SacSewer / SASD) — https://www.sacsewer.com
- **Treatment:** Sacramento Regional County Sanitation District (Regional San) — https://www.regionalsan.com (redirects to sacsewer.com)
- SASD Sewer Ordinance SDI-0079 (adopted April 27, 2022); industrial limits in Regional San Consolidated Ordinance
- See → **wastewater-local.md**

## Building / Zoning authority

- City of Sacramento Community Development Dept. — https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/community-development/planning
- Zoning code: City Code Title 17, Ch. 17.220 (Industrial & Manufacturing) — districts M-1, M-1(S), M-2, M-2(S), MIP, MRD, M-T
- CUP portal: https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/community-development/planning/e-planning
- See → **zoning-industrial.md**

## Notes

- GOTCHA: the City of Sacramento does **NOT** run its own CUPA — hazmat regulation is County EMD.
- GOTCHA: the Sacramento Metropolitan Fire District (metrofire.ca.gov) adopted the 2025 CFC (Ord. 2025-02) but it serves unincorporated county areas — the **City** AHJ is Sacramento Fire Dept and the applicable code is 2022 CFC.
- Wastewater discharge limits (metals, pH, etc.) are in the Regional San Consolidated Ordinance, not the SASD Sewer Ordinance. Contact wscs@sacsewer.com for the current local limits table.
