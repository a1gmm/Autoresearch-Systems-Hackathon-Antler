## EHS Local Rules by Family

### 1. CUPA — Hazardous Materials / Unified Program

- **CUPA agency:** Sacramento County Environmental Management Department (EMD), Environmental Compliance Division. CalEPA-designated CUPA for **all of Sacramento County including all incorporated cities** (not just unincorporated areas).
- GOTCHA: the City of Sacramento does NOT run its own CUPA — the whole county uses County EMD.
- **Six unified programs administered:** Hazardous Materials Business Plan (HMBP), Underground Storage Tanks (UST), Aboveground Storage Tanks (AST), Hazardous Waste Generator/Tiered Permitting, CalARP, and Recycled Used Oil.
- **HMBP thresholds:** liquids ≥ 55 gal, solids ≥ 500 lb, compressed gases ≥ 200 cu ft. Electronic submission only via CERS; paper plans no longer accepted.
- **Permit portal:** California Environmental Reporting System (CERS) — https://cers.calepa.ca.gov
- **Fee schedule (FY 2025-2026):** https://emd.saccounty.gov/content/dam/emd/docs/transition/EC%20Fee%20Schedule%20Master%202025-2026%20070125.pdf
- **Proposed FY 2026-2027 fee schedule:** https://emd.saccounty.gov/content/dam/emd/docs/transition/EP%20Proposed%20Fee%20Schedule%202026-2027.pdf
- **Contact:** (916) 875-8550 | cupapermit@saccounty.net
- **Address:** 11080 White Rock Rd., Suite 200, Rancho Cordova, CA 95670
- Source: http://emd.saccounty.gov/us/en/environmental-protection/CUPA.html

### 2. Fire Code — AHJ and Adopted Edition

- **AHJ for unincorporated areas + Citrus Heights + Rancho Cordova:** Sacramento Metropolitan Fire District ("Metro Fire")
  - Adopted CFC: **2025 California Fire Code, Title 24 — Ordinance 2025-02**
  - Fee schedule: Ordinance 2023-02 (amended 2023), with FY 2025-2026 update — PDF at https://metrofire.ca.gov/board-approved-ordinances
  - Contact: (916) 859-4300
  - Source: https://metrofire.ca.gov/board-approved-ordinances
- **AHJ for City of Sacramento:** City of Sacramento Fire Department
  - Adopted CFC: 2022 California Fire Code with local amendments — Sacramento City Code Chapter 15.36; local amendments at § 15.36.050
  - Source: https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/sacramentoca/latest/sacramento_ca/0-0-0-28378
- **Sacramento County (unincorporated, outside Metro Fire territory):** Sacramento County Code Title 17 (Fire Prevention) — adopts state CFC; County Fire Warden enforces in areas without an organized fire district.
  - Source: https://ecode360.com/44037931
- Hazmat: CFC Chapter 50 (§ 5003.9.1.2 amended — SDS/HMMP/HMIS ready access required). High-pile storage: CFC Chapter 32 — operational permit required for areas > 500 sq ft; top of storage > 12 ft (or > 6 ft for high-hazard commodities). No additional local MAQ overlays beyond state CFC in Ordinance 2025-02. See fire-code-local.md for full amendment list.

### 3. Industrial Wastewater — POTW and Pretreatment

- **POTW/agency:** Sacramento Area Sewer District (SacSewer), Wastewater Source Control Section
  - Operates the **EchoWater Resource Recovery Facility** as the primary POTW for unincorporated Sacramento County and most incorporated areas.
- **Sewer use ordinance:** SacSewer Ordinance SDI-0079, adopted April 27, 2022, effective April 27, 2023. Governs all industrial/commercial discharge to the public sewerage system.
- **Industrial Wastewater Discharge Permit (IWP) required for:**
  - Federally regulated categorical industrial users (CIUs), AND
  - Non-categorical users discharging ≥ 25,000 gallons/day of process wastewater (excluding domestic, non-contact cooling, and boiler blowdown flows).
- **Application/portal:** https://www.sacsewer.com/pretreatment-program/
- **Contact:** Linda Stevens, Environmental Program Manager II, Wastewater Source Control Section — (916) 876-5287; fax (916) 854-9286; 10060 Goethe Rd, Sacramento CA 95827. Local limits are set by Regional San Consolidated Ordinance (eff. June 9, 2023); SacSewer enforces. See wastewater-local.md for full detail.
- Source: https://www.sacsewer.com/pretreatment-program/
- Source (ordinance PDF): https://sacsewer-bucket.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/sacsewer-ordinance_effective-4-27-23.pdf

### 4. Stormwater — MS4 / Local Ordinance

- **MS4 permit:** Sacramento County is a co-permittee on Phase I MS4 Permit **Order No. R5-2015-0023 / NPDES Permit No. CAS082597** (Central Valley RWQCB, adopted April 17, 2015), jointly with Cities of Citrus Heights, Elk Grove, Folsom, Galt, and Sacramento.
- **Local stormwater ordinance:** Sacramento County Code Title 15 Water and Sewers, **Chapter 15.12** (Stormwater Management and Discharge Control) — establishes discharge prohibitions and BMPs for MS4 compliance.
- **Administering agency:** Sacramento County Department of Water Resources (stormwater program); also Sacramento Stormwater Quality Partnership for the co-permittee group.
- Source: https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/sacramentoca/latest/sacramento_ca/0-0-0-27141 (§ 13.16.130 BMP compliance reference)
- Source: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/centralvalley/water_issues/storm_water/municipal_permits/

### 5. Zoning / Land Use — Industrial Districts and CUP

- **Zoning code authority:** Sacramento County Zoning Code, online at https://landuse.saccounty.gov/szc/
- **Industrial district designations (Chapter 2, § 2.10):**
  - **MP** (Industrial-Office Park) — research, service, and light industrial; park-like setting; site plan approval required; 15,000 sq ft minimum lot
  - **M-1** (Light Industrial) — fabrication, manufacturing, assembly that does not create smoke, gas, odor, dust, or noise offsite; 6,000 sq ft minimum lot
  - **M-2** (Heavy Industrial) — fabrication, manufacturing, or processing that may produce smoke, gas, odor, dust, or noise; 20,000 sq ft minimum lot
- **Conditional Use Permit (CUP):** governed by Chapter 3 (Use Regulations) and Chapter 6 (Administration) of the Sacramento County Zoning Code. Contact Planning for submittal requirements.
- **Planning contact:** sacplan@saccounty.gov | https://planning.saccounty.gov
- Source: https://landuse.saccounty.gov/szc/ch2/
