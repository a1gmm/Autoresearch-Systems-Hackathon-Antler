## Industrial Zoning Districts (SZC Chapter 2, § 2.10)

Sacramento County has three industrial zone designations:

### MP — Industrial-Office Park

**Purpose:** "To provide for well-designed and controlled groupings of research, service, and light industrial uses within an area containing visual and operational amenities."

**Minimum lot size:** 15,000 sq ft  
**Key requirement:** Site Plan Approval by the Planning Director is required **before** any construction begins.  
**Character:** Park-like setting; research, service, and light industrial only; operations designed to minimize visual and operational impacts.

---

### M-1 — Light Industrial

**Purpose:** Permits fabrication, manufacturing, assembly, or processing operations that **do not create objectionable influences** such as smoke, gas, odor, dust, sound, or other nuisances outside the site.

**Minimum lot size:** 6,000 sq ft  
**Operational standard:** Most uses must occur "completely within an enclosed building or behind an enclosed solid wall or fenced area." (SZC § 2.10)

**Examples of uses allowed in M-1 (from SZC Chapter 3, Table 3.1 reference and code text):**
- Light fabrication, manufacturing, and assembly without offsite impacts
- Industrial hemp processing (Conditional Use Permit required — Zoning Administrator approval)
- Large wineries/breweries — events with no restrictions on size/frequency provided outdoor activities comply with noise ordinance
- Multifamily residential prohibited, except projects qualifying under state Housing Acts (permitted by right under those statutes)

### M-2 — Heavy Industrial

**Purpose:** Allows intensive industrial uses including fabrication, manufacturing, assembly, or processing of raw materials where operations may produce "smoke, gas, odor, dust, sound, or other objectionable influences."

**Minimum lot size:** 20,000 sq ft  
**Character:** Accepts the broadest range of industrial activity; operations generating offsite impacts are accommodated through siting within M-2 areas rather than individual permit conditions.

**Examples of uses in M-2:**
- All M-1 uses, plus intensive manufacturing/processing with potential offsite impacts
- Industrial hemp indoor cultivation and processing — **permitted by right** (vs. CUP required in M-1)
- Large wineries/breweries — same event provisions as M-1

## Use Table Structure (SZC Chapter 3)

Use permissions are codified in **Tables 3.1, 3.2, and 3.3** of SZC Chapter 3 (§ 3.2.5 "Allowed Uses in All Zoning Districts"). The tables use the following codes:

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| P | Permitted by right |
| CUP (ZA) | Conditional Use Permit — decided by Zoning Administrator |
| CUP (PC) | Conditional Use Permit — decided by Planning Commission |
| CUP (BOS) | Conditional Use Permit — decided by Board of Supervisors |
| — / blank | Prohibited |

The full use tables are maintained at: https://landuse.saccounty.gov/szc/ch3/  
A zoning code interim guide (updated January 1, 2026) is at: https://landuse.saccounty.gov/archive/SZC_InterimGuide.pdf

## Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Process (SZC Chapter 6)

### What Triggers a CUP

Any use listed as "CUP" in the applicable use table (Tables 3.1–3.3). Industrial examples that require CUP in M-1 (or similar):
- Industrial hemp processing (CUP — Zoning Administrator)
- Certain recycling facilities
- Uses with potential compatibility conflicts with adjacent zones

### Approval Authority

| Approval Level | When Used |
|---|---|
| **Zoning Administrator (ZA)** | Standard CUP decisions per User Guide procedures |
| **Planning Commission (PC)** | CUP filed concurrently with a rezone; other permits heard by Commission; tentative subdivision map |
| **Board of Supervisors (BOS)** | As indicated in Table 3.1; when filed with Board-level applications; Airport Land Use Compatibility Plan areas (requires 4/5 vote) |

### Approval Criteria (SZC § 6)

The approving authority must find that the proposed use will not be "detrimental to the health, safety, peace, morals, comfort, or general welfare" and must verify:
1. Consistency with General Plan and Zoning Code
2. Compatibility with adjacent uses (scale, design, operations)
3. Adequate mitigation of adverse impacts
4. Availability of facilities and services
5. Compliance with all applicable standards and conditions

### Submittal Requirements

Application filed with Planning and Environmental Review "on a form prescribed by the Planning Director." Supporting materials per the SZC User Guide (PDF at https://planning.saccounty.gov).

## Special Industrial Use Standards

### Industrial Hemp (SZC Chapter 3)

- **M-1:** Processing — Conditional Use Permit (Zoning Administrator); must meet:
  - Odor mitigation (odor undetectable outside buildings)
  - Non-volatile processing methods only
  - Valid Agricultural Commissioner license
- **M-2:** Indoor cultivation and processing — Permitted by right (subject to same odor/licensing standards)

### Wineries and Breweries (Large)

- **M-1 and M-2:** Events with no restrictions on size and frequency, provided outdoor activities comply with the noise ordinance.
