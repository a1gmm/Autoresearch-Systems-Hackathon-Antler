## Core Thresholds (HSC 25507)

| State of matter | Threshold (at or above) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Liquid | 55 gallons | Aggregate per hazardous material name |
| Solid | 500 pounds | Aggregate per hazardous material name |
| Compressed gas | 200 cubic feet (STP) | Aggregate per hazardous material name |
| Radioactive material | Any quantity requiring state/federal license | |
| Extremely hazardous substances (EHS) | TPQ or 500 lbs, whichever less | EPCRA §302 list |

## Aggregation Rule
Count ALL quantities of the same hazardous material at the facility — storage, use, and manufacturing combined. A chemical in 10 drums of 6 gallons each = 60 gallons → triggers HMBP.

## Exemptions from Reporting (HSC 25507.1)
- Consumer products used/stored in quantities a consumer would use
- Hazardous waste subject to HSC Chapter 6.5 (managed under RCRA manifest)
- Fuels in a vehicle's fuel tank used for propulsion
- Materials in a pipeline (not storage)
- Radioactive materials regulated by NRC if separate reporting applies

## CUPA Variations
Local CUPAs may set **lower thresholds** — always verify with the local CUPA. Example: some Bay Area CUPAs require reporting at 1 gallon of acutely hazardous materials. The state threshold is the floor, not the ceiling.

## Key Quantities for Common EHS Chemicals
- Chlorine (gas): TPQ = 10 lbs → any reportable storage triggers EHS tier
- Ammonia (anhydrous): TPQ = 500 lbs → 200 cf compressed gas threshold also applies
- Sulfuric acid: TPQ = 1,000 lbs → 500 lb solid or 55 gal liquid threshold
