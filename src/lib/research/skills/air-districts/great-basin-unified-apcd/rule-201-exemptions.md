## Purpose

Rule 201 lists the categories of equipment and sources that do NOT require an Authority to Construct or Permit to Operate from GBUAPCD. Any source on this list is exempt from the permitting requirement of Rule 200 (Permits Required), but must still comply with all other applicable District rules and regulations.

**Critical carve-out:** "The exemptions contained in this Rule shall not apply to any new stationary source or modification as defined in Rule 209-A, (F), 2 and 3, which would emit any pollutants in excess of the quantities stated in Rule 209-A, (B), (2)." (Major NSR thresholds override all exemptions.)

Last revised: 01/23/06

## Key Provisions / Thresholds (Verbatim)

### Vehicles and Residential Equipment
- **(A)** "Vehicles as defined by the Vehicle Code of the State of California but not including any article, machine, equipment, or other contrivance mounted on such vehicle that would otherwise require a permit under the provisions of these Rules and Regulations."
- **(B)** "Vehicles used to transport passengers or freight."
- **(C)** "Equipment utilized, exclusively in connection with any structure which is designed for and used exclusively as a dwelling for not more than four families."

### Exempt Equipment (Section D – selected key items)
- **(D)(1)** "Comfort air conditioning or comfort ventilating systems which are not designed to remove air contaminants generated by or released from specific units or equipment."
- **(D)(2)** "Refrigeration units except those used as, or in conjunction with, air pollution control equipment."
- **(D)(3)** "Piston type internal combustion engines except for diesel engines greater than 50 brake horsepower that are subject to emission control requirements pursuant to the Airborne Toxic Control Measure for Stationary Compression Ignition Engines (Title 17, California Code of Regulations, Section 93115)."
- **(D)(4)** "Water cooling towers and water cooling ponds not used for evaporative cooling of process water or not used for evaporative cooling of water from barometric jets or from barometric condensers."
- **(D)(5)** "Equipment used exclusively for steam cleaning."
- **(D)(8)** "Equipment used exclusively for space heating other than boilers."
- **(D)(10)** "All sheet-fed printing presses and all other printing presses without dryers."
- **(D)(15)** "Crucible type or pot type furnaces with a brimful capacity of less than 450 cubic inches of any molten metal."
- **(D)(24)** "Abrasive blast cabinet-dust filter integral combination units where the total internal volume of the blast section is 50 cubic feet or less."
- **(D)(25)** "Batch mixers of five cubic feet rated working capacity or less."
- **(D)(31)** "Smokehouses in which the maximum horizontal inside cross-sectional area does not exceed 20 square feet."

### Exempt Equipment (Section E – exhaust/collector served)
- **(E)(3)** "Kilns used for firing ceramic water, heated exclusively by natural gas, liquefied petroleum gas, electricity or any combination thereof."
- **(E)(4)** "Laboratory equipment used exclusively for chemical or physical analyses and bench scale laboratory equipment."
- **(E)(11)** "Brazing, soldering or welding equipment."
- **(E)(13)** "Equipment used for buffing (except automatic or semi-automatic tire buffers), or polishing, carving, cutting, drilling, machining, routing, sanding, sawing, surface grinding or turning of ceramic artwork, ceramic precision parts, leather, metals, plastics, rubber, fiberboard, masonry, carbon or graphite."
- **(E)(14)** "Equipment used for carving, cutting, drilling, surface grinding, liquefied, routing, sanding, sawing, shredding or turning of wood, or the pressing or storing of sawdust, wood chips or wood shavings."

### Boilers / Steam Generators
- **(F)** "Steam generators, steam superheaters, water boilers, water heaters, and closed heat transfer systems that have a maximum heat input rate of less than 15 million British Thermal Units (BTU) per hour (gross), and are fired exclusively with natural gas or liquefied petroleum gas or any combination thereof."

### Storage Tanks (Section H – selected)
- **(H)(3)** "Storage of liquefied gases."
- **(H)(4)** "Unheated storage of organic materials with an initial boiling point of 300 F or greater."
- **(H)(7)** "The storage of organic liquids, except gasoline, normally used as solvents, diluents or thinners, inks, colorants, paints, lacquers, enamels, varnishes, liquid resins or other surface coatings, and having a capacity of 6,000 gallons or less."
- **(H)(9)** "Asphalt Melting Kettles or molten asphalt holding tanks with less than 250 gallon capacity."
- **(H)(10)** "Unheated solvent dispensing containers, unheated non-conveyorized coating dip tanks of 250 gallon capacity or less."
- **(H)(11)** "Storage of gasoline in underground tanks having a capacity of 250 gallons or less or installed prior to December 31, 1970."

### Furnaces (Section J)
- **(J)** "Crucible furnaces, pot furnaces or induction furnaces, with a capacity of 1,000 pounds or less each, in which no sweating or distilling is conducted and from which only the following metals are held in a molten state: [Aluminum >50%, Magnesium >50%, Lead >50%, Tin >50%, Zinc >50%, Copper, Precious metals]."

### Administrative Exemptions
- **(M)** "Structural changes which cannot change the quality, nature or quantity of air contaminant emissions."
- **(N)** "Repairs or maintenance not involving structural changes to any equipment for which a permit has been granted."
- **(O)** "Identical replacements in whole or in part of any article, machine, equipment or other contrivance where a permit to operate has previously been granted for such equipment under Rule 200; however, this exception shall not be applicable to equipment or air pollution control equipment with respect to the loading of gasoline into stationary tanks (Rule 419)."
- **(P)** "Open Burn/Open Detonation Operations on Military Bases, provided the operation complies with the requirements of Rules 217 and 432."

## Determination Notes

- The boiler exemption threshold is **< 15 MMBtu/hr** heat input, natural gas or LPG only — boilers at or above this size require a permit.
- Diesel engines **> 50 bhp** are NOT exempt; they must comply with ATCM (17 CCR § 93115) and typically require a permit.
- Solvent storage tanks **≤ 6,000 gallons** (non-gasoline) are exempt; gasoline USTs ≤ 250 gallons or pre-1971 are exempt but above those thresholds require a permit.
- Batch mixers **≤ 5 cu ft** and abrasive blast cabinets **≤ 50 cu ft** are exempt by size.
- Welding, brazing, and soldering equipment (Section E.11) are fully exempt regardless of throughput.
- All exemptions are voided if the source triggers Major NSR thresholds in Rule 209-A(B)(2) (250 lbs/day TSP or NAAQS pollutants, 80 lbs/day PM10 for modifications).
