## One-Line Decision Guide

- **Does the activity appear in Rule 201?** If yes and no Major NSR thresholds apply → no permit required.
- **Is it a new source or modification not in Rule 201?** → Authority to Construct (Rule 209-A) required before construction; Permit to Operate (Rule 209-B) required before operation.
- **Does net emissions increase reach 250 lbs/day (TSP, NOx, SOx, ROG) or 80 lbs/day (PM10 for modifications to large sources)?** → Full NSR with BACT and offsets required under Rule 209-A Section D.
- **Does the operation use organic solvents?** → Check Rule 417 thresholds: 15/3 lbs (flame/heat), 40/8 lbs (reactive solvents), 3000/450 lbs (non-reactive); exceed any → 85% control required.

## District Context

GBUAPCD is a small rural district covering Mono and Inyo Counties (Eastern Sierra Nevada, Owens Valley, Death Valley region). It is an attainment area for most pollutants except particulate matter (PM10) from Owens Valley dust. The district adopts its own permit and exemption rules and applies BACT requirements but does not face the stringent offset ratios common in the South Coast or San Joaquin Valley. Many CARB statewide measures (ATCM for diesel engines, consumer products regulations, architectural coatings rules) apply within the district's jurisdiction in addition to local rules.
