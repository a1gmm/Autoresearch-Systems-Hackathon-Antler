## District Overview

Lassen County APCD is a small, rural district serving Lassen County in northeastern California. The district operates under a lean, pre-1972 ruleset with no major-source NSR, no local VOC/coating rules, and no throughput-based permit tiers. Equipment-type categorization drives all permit decisions.

## One-Line Decision Guide

1. **Is the equipment on the Rule 2:1 exemption list?** (14 categorical types — welding, space heating, comfort HVAC, refrigeration, injection molding, dry cleaning, etc.) — If YES, no ATC/PTO required (but all emission limits still apply).
2. **Does the equipment "may cause the issuance of air contaminants"?** — If YES (and not exempt), obtain ATC before construction, then PTO before operation.
3. **Can it meet Rule 4:3 (0.3 gr/scf PM), Rule 4:0 (Ringelmann No. 2 opacity), Rule 4:10 (≤0.5% sulfur fuel), and the Rule 4:11 process weight table?** — If YES, permit is grantable; if NO, ATC/PTO must be denied (Rule 2:8).
4. **No local VOC/coating/solvent rule** — CARB/state rules apply; no local permit triggered by coating or solvent use alone.

## Notes on Coverage Gaps

- **No local architectural coatings or autobody rule** — CARB consumer product and coating regulations apply statewide.
- **No local NSR/major source program** — projects triggering federal major source thresholds are handled under CARB/EPA programs.
- **No permit registration tier** — equipment is either categorically exempt (Rule 2:1) or requires a full ATC/PTO.
- **Agricultural burning** is governed by a separate Agricultural Burning Implementation Plan (Regulation V / Articles I–VII) and burning permit system coordinated with USFS, BLM, Cal Fire, and the district.
