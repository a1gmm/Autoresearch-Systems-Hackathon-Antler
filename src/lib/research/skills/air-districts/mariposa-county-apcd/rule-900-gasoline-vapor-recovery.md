## Purpose

Regulation IX, Rule 900 implements the CARB Benzene Airborne Toxic Control Measure for retail service stations (CCR Title 17 and Title 26, Sections 93100 & 93101). It requires ARB-certified Phase I (tank filling) and Phase II (vehicle fueling) vapor recovery systems at retail gasoline stations.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Phase I Vapor Recovery — Applicability (verbatim)

> "No owner or operator shall transfer, permit the transfer, or provide equipment for the transfer of gasoline, and no other person shall transfer gasoline from a gasoline delivery truck equipped with a vapor recovery system into a stationary storage tank at a retail service station unless an ARB certified phase I vapor recovery system is installed on the stationary storage tank and used during the transfer."

### Phase I Exemptions (verbatim)

> "The provisions of subdivision (B)(1) shall not apply to:
> 1. A transfer to a stationary storage tank with a capacity of less than 1.0 cubic meter (260 gallons).
> 2. A transfer to a stationary storage tank used the majority of the time for the fueling of implements of husbandry…
> 3. A transfer to a stationary storage tank used exclusively to fuel motor vehicles with a fuel capacity of five gallons or less.
> 4. An existing retail service station with an annual throughput … of 480,000 or fewer gallons during the calendar year prior to District adoption of this measure. If during any calendar year thereafter the gasoline throughput … exceeds 480,000 gallons, this exemption shall cease to apply commencing with the first day of the following calendar year.
> 5. A transfer to a stationary storage tank at an existing retail service station which receives gasoline exclusively from delivery tanks that are not required to be equipped with vapor recovery systems."

### Phase II Vapor Recovery — Applicability (verbatim)

> "No owner or operator shall transfer, permit the transfer or provide equipment for the transfer of gasoline from a stationary storage tank at a retail service station into a motor vehicle fuel tank unless an ARB certified PHASE II vapor recovery system is installed and used during the transfer."

### Phase II Exemptions (verbatim)

> "The provisions of subdivision (C)(1) shall not apply to:
> 1. A transfer of gasoline from a stationary storage tank which is exempt from Phase I requirements under subdivision (B)(2)(a), (B)(2)(b), or (B)(2)(c).
> 2. An existing retail service station which is exempt from Phase I requirements under subdivision (B)(2)(d)."

### Compliance Schedule (verbatim, key provisions)

> "A. The owner or operator of any new retail service station subject to this regulation shall comply with the provisions of this regulation at the time gasoline is first sold from the station."

> "E. The owner or operator of a new retail service station shall file an application for an Authority to Construct Permit prior to construction. (Health & Safety Code section 42300 & 42303)."

> "F. The owner or operator of any existing retail service station, who must install Phase I or Phase II vapor recovery systems under the provisions of this Regulation, shall obtain an Authority to Construct Permit from the Air Pollution Control District prior to construction."

### Key Definition: Gasoline (verbatim)

> "GASOLINE- Any organic liquid (including petroleum distillates and methanol) having a Reid vapor pressure of four pounds or greater and used as a motor vehicle fuel or any fuel which is commonly or commercially known as or sold as gasoline."

### Key Definition: Throughput threshold

480,000 gallons/year is the annual throughput threshold below which existing stations are exempt from Phase I (and by extension Phase II) requirements.

## Determination Notes

- **ATC required for new stations:** New retail service stations must obtain an ATC before construction and ensure both Phase I and Phase II systems are ARB-certified.
- **Throughput exemption for small stations:** Existing stations at ≤480,000 gal/yr are exempt from Phase I/II requirements until their throughput crosses the threshold.
- **Tank replacement triggers compliance:** Any existing station undergoing tank replacement must install ARB-certified Phase I and Phase II systems at that time, regardless of throughput.
- **Small tank exemption:** Tanks <260 gallons (1.0 m³) are exempt from Phase I.
- **Rule 901 (Hexavalent Chromium):** The same Regulation IX also covers chrome plating and chromic acid anodizing operations. Relevant only for industrial chrome facilities — not addressed here as it is not typically a permit question for most projects.
