# El Dorado County AQMD — Skill Index

## Overview

El Dorado County AQMD is a small district covering El Dorado County, CA (excluding the Lake Tahoe Air Basin portion, which has a separate sub-district compilation). The district has its own permit rules and several substantive air quality rules covering architectural coatings, graphic arts, organic solvent cleaning, stationary engines, and boilers. The full EPA-approved rulebook (October 2023 revision) was downloaded and extracted to build this skill.

Three compiled EPA-approved SIP rule sets exist:
- **General District**: https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2022-09/epa-approved-edcaqmd-sips-compilation.pdf (15.81 MB)
- **Lake Tahoe Portion**: https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2022-09/epa-approved-edcaqmd-lake-tahoe-sip-compilation.pdf (2.01 MB)
- **Mountain Portion**: https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2022-09/epa-approved-edcaqmd-mountain-sip-compilation.pdf (2.05 MB)

## One-Line Decision Guide

**Does this project need an ATC/PTO?** — Yes if it installs, modifies, or replaces any equipment that may emit air contaminants, UNLESS it falls into a named Rule 501 exemption (≤50 BHP IC engine, <1 MMBtu/hr natural gas/LPG combustion, ≤1 gal/day surface coating, residential ≤4 units, agricultural, laboratory analysis, food prep, or <2 lb/day any pollutant with APCO approval). When in doubt, call (530) 621-7501.

**Does this project trigger New Source Review (Rule 523)?** — Yes if a new/modified stationary source emits a nonattainment pollutant above the offset trigger thresholds calculated per Rule 523.4 M-O. Major sources: ≥25 tpy NOx or VOC, ≥100 tpy CO/PM10/SOx.

**Architectural coatings compliance** — Verify product VOC content against Rule 215 Table of Standards before purchase; flat coatings ≤50 g/L, nonflat ≤100 g/L are the common limits.

**Graphic arts** — Track monthly VOC usage; facilities under 660 lb/month are exempt from substantive standards but must maintain usage records.
