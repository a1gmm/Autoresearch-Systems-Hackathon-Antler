## One-Line Decision Guide

**Does this equipment need a permit?**
1. Check Rule 2:4 exemptions — piston ICE, space heaters, LPG/NG space heating (not boilers), comfort A/C, residential equipment, and APCO-designated minor sources are exempt.
2. If not exempt, an Authority to Construct is required before building and a Permit to Operate before running — file with TCAPCD (530-527-3717), $193 filing fee (as of July 2025).
3. If potential to emit ≥ BACT thresholds in Rule 2:3A §5.1 (e.g., ≥25 lb/day ROC or NOx), BACT analysis required; if ≥25 tpy of a non-attainment pollutant, emission offsets required.
4. For architectural coatings applied in the district, verify VOC content ≤ Rule 4:39 §6.1 limits (flat: 100 g/L; nonflat: 150 g/L; nonflat-high-gloss: 250 g/L).
5. For retail gasoline stations, Phase I and Phase II vapor recovery required per Rule 6:1 (exemptions for tanks <260 gal and low-throughput existing stations).
6. Major HAP sources (≥10 tpy single HAP or ≥25 tpy combined) require T-BACT per Rule 6:8.

## Attainment Status Notes

Tehama County is a small rural district in the northern Sacramento Valley. As of mid-2026, check CARB for current PM2.5 and ozone designations — the district typically tracks CARB's Northern Sacramento Valley designations. The district has local non-attainment SIP obligations that drive the 25 tpy offset trigger in Rule 2:3A.
