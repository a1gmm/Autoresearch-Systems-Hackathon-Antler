# Mojave Desert AQMD — Air District Skill

## Decision Guide

**Does the equipment need a permit?**
1. Check Rule 219 exemption list — if the equipment is in an enumerated exempt category AND emissions are <2 tons/yr per NAAQS pollutant and <0.5 tons/yr per HAP, **no permit required**.
2. Key exemption thresholds: engines <50 bhp; combustion sources <2 MMBtu/hr on nat gas/LPG; printing equipment <2 gal/day graphic arts material (no dryer); cooling towers <10,000 gpm; residential structures (≤4 families).
3. If equipment is NOT exempt → Authority to Construct (ATC) under Rule 201 required before construction; Permit to Operate (PTO) under Rule 203 required before operation.
4. **Coating/solvent operations:** Check Rule 1113 (architectural coatings) and Rule 442 (general solvents) for VOC content limits — these are emission standards, not separate permit triggers, but violations generate enforcement exposure.
5. **Major source thresholds:** Regulation XIII (NSR) and Regulation XVI (PSD) apply for major modifications/facilities — see Rule 1301 and Rule 1600 definitions.

## Rulebook Structure

- **Regulation I** — General Provisions (Rules 101–109)
- **Regulation II** — Permits (Rules 201–222); Rules 201, 202, 203 (ATC/PTO core) are county-specific SIP-approved rules administered by the district
- **Regulation IV** — Prohibitions (Rules 401–476); includes VOC rules 442, 444, 461, etc.
- **Regulation XI** — Source-Specific Standards (Rules 1101–1160); includes architectural coatings (1113), wood products (1114), metal parts (1115), auto refinishing (1116), IC engines (1160)
- **Regulation XII** — Federal Operating Permits (Title V)
- **Regulation XIII** — New Source Review
- **Regulation XVI** — Prevention of Significant Deterioration

## Notes on District Character

Mojave Desert AQMD covers the high desert of San Bernardino County (including Barstow, Victorville, Twentynine Palms, Needles) and the Palo Verde Valley in Riverside County. The district is designated **nonattainment for ozone (8-hour)** in portions of its jurisdiction, which affects NSR major modification thresholds (12.5 tons/yr NOx + VOC triggers NSR review for agricultural facilities in nonattainment areas). The district enforces its own rulebook but also incorporates CARB standards and federal NSPS/NESHAP requirements. Many source-specific rules (1113, 1115, 1116) mirror SCAQMD provisions.
