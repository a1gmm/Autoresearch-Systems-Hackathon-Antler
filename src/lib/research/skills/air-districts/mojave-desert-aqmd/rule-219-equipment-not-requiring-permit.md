## Purpose

Rule 219 enumerates equipment categories that do not require a permit under Rules 201 (Permit to Construct) and 203 (Permit to Operate). It also identifies which exempt equipment need not be listed on a Federal Operating Permit (FOP) issued under Regulation XII. The burden of proving applicability of any exemption rests on the owner/operator.

Adopted: 01/09/76; most recently amended: 01/25/21. Extracted from EPA-compiled MDAQMD Rules and Regulations PDF (August 2022 compile).

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Section (B) General Provisions — Permit Exemption Standard (verbatim)

> "(1) The Air Pollution Control Officer (APCO) shall not require an Owner/Operator to obtain a permit for particular Equipment pursuant to District Rules 201 and 203 if all of the following are true:
> (a) Such Equipment does not emit air contaminants in excess of any of the following:
>   (i) Two (2) tons per year of any Regulated Air Pollutant for which a National Ambient Air Quality Standard has been promulgated;
>   (ii) A de minimis level for a Hazardous Air Pollutant, promulgated pursuant to 42 U.S.C. §7412 (FCAA §112);
>   (iii) A significance level defined in 40 CFR 52.21(b)(23)(i);
>   (iv) 0.5 tons per year of a Hazardous Air Pollutant.
> (b) Such Equipment does not constitute any of the following:
>   (i) A Major Facility as defined in Rule 1301, or
>   (ii) A Major Modification as defined in Rule 1301, or
>   (iii) A Major PSD Facility as defined in Rule 1600(B), or
>   (iv) A Major PSD Modification as defined in Rule 1600(B)."

### Section (D)(1) — FOP Exclusion Threshold (verbatim)

> "To be eligible for exclusion from a FOP pursuant to section (B)(2), any Equipment listed under this Rule shall not emit Air Contaminants in an amount greater than any of the following:
> (i) Two (2) tons per year of any Regulated Air Pollutant for which a National Ambient Air Quality Standard has been promulgated; or
> (ii) A de minimis level for a Hazardous Air Pollutant, promulgated pursuant to 42 U.S.C. §7412 (FCAA §112); or
> (iii) Any significance level defined in 40 CFR 52.21(b)(23)(i); or
> (iv) 0.5 tons per year of such Hazardous Air Pollutant, whichever is less."

### Section (D)(2) — Agricultural Facility Thresholds (verbatim excerpt)

Confined Animal Facilities exempt if fewer than:
> "(i) 1,000 milk-producing dairy cows; (ii) 3,500 beef cattle; (iii) 7,500 calves, heifers or other cattle; (iv) 650,000 chickens other than laying hens; (v) 650,000 laying hens; (vi) 650,000 ducks; (vii) 100,000 turkeys; (viii) 3,000 swine; (ix) 2,500 horses; (x) 15,000 sheep, lambs, or goats; or (xi) 30,000 rabbits or other animals."

Agricultural Facilities (non-CAF) exempt if Actual Emissions are less than:
> "(i) Twelve and one half (12.5) tons per year of NOx and VOC if the Agricultural Facility is located within an area designated nonattainment for Ozone … (ii) Fifty (50) tons per year of any other Air Pollutant … (iii) Five (5) tons per year of any Hazardous Air Pollutant; or (iv) Twelve and one half (12.5) tons per year of any combination of Hazardous Air Pollutants …"

### Section (E) — Specific Equipment Not Requiring a Permit (verbatim key entries)

**E(1) Vehicles and Transportation Equipment**
> "(a) Motor Vehicles as defined by §415 and/or 670 of the Vehicle Code … but not including any article, machine, Equipment, or other contrivance mounted on such Vehicle, that would otherwise require a permit …
> (b) Equipment mounted upon Vehicles that are used exclusively to transport materials on streets or highways including, but not limited to, cement trucks, and Gasoline tanker trucks (does not include asphalt or coal tar pitch roofing kettles).
> (c) Locomotives, airplanes, and watercraft used to transport passengers or freight."

**E(2) Combustion and Heat Transfer Equipment**
> "(a) Internal Combustion Engines and Gas Turbines - Piston type internal combustion engines with a manufacture's maximum continuous rating of **less than 50 brake horsepower**, or gas turbine engines with a maximum Heat Input rate of less than **3,000,000 Btu (756,300 kilogram-calories) per hour** at ISO Standard Day Conditions. The ratings of all engines or turbines used in the same process will be aggregated …
> (b) General Combustion Source - Any combustion Equipment that has a maximum Heat Input rate of **less than 2,000,000 Btu (504,000 kilogram-calories) per hour** (gross) and is equipped to be fired exclusively with Public Utilities Commission regulated natural gas, liquefied petroleum gas or any combination thereof. The ratings of all combustion Equipment used in the same process will be aggregated …
> (c) Internal combustion engines used exclusively for training at educational institutions.
> (d) Internal combustion engines registered pursuant to the California Statewide Portable Engine Registration Program … unless such engines have been determined to be stationary …
> (e) Fuel cells which use phosphoric acid, molten carbonate, proton exchange membrane or solid oxide technologies.
> (f) Power pressure washers and hot water or steam washers … rated maximum heat input capacity of **550,000 Btu per hour or less** … maximum NOX emission output is less than **one (1) pound per day** and use no more than **50 gallons of fuel per day**."

**E(3) Structures and Equipment – General**
> "(a) Structural changes which cannot change the quality, nature or quantity of Air Contaminant emissions.
> (b) Repairs or maintenance not involving structural changes to any Equipment for which a permit has been granted.
> (c) Equipment utilized exclusively in connection with any structure … designed for and used exclusively as a dwelling for not more than four families.
> (d) Laboratory Equipment used exclusively for chemical and physical analysis and bench scale or laboratory test Equipment."

**E(4) General Utility Equipment**
> "(a) Comfort air conditioning or ventilating systems which are not designed or used to remove Air Contaminants generated by or released from specific units of Equipment.
> (b) Refrigeration units. [This exemption does not apply to refrigeration units used as or in conjunction with air pollution Control Equipment.]
> (c) Water cooling towers and water-cooling ponds that have a circulation rate of **less than 10,000 gallons/minute** (37,800 liters/minute) and which are not used for: evaporative cooling of process water … and into which no chromium compounds are added.
> (e) Equipment used exclusively for space heating other than Boilers."

**E(5) Glass, Ceramic, Metallurgical Processing & Fabrication Equipment** (key entries)
> "(a) Crucible-type or pot-type furnaces with a brimful capacity of less than **452 cubic inches** (7400 cubic centimeters) of any molten metal.
> (b) Crucible furnaces, pot furnaces, or induction furnaces with a capacity of **less than 992 pounds** (450 kilograms) each … [specific qualifying metals listed] …
> (e) Brazing, hand-held soldering, and hot air solder leveling … Welding Equipment, oxygen gaseous fuel-cutting Equipment, laser etching Equipment, engraving of metal Equipment and associated Control Equipment. [Certain plasma arc cutters and laser cutters excluded.]"

**E(7) Machining Equipment**
> "(a) Equipment used exclusively for buffing, polishing, carving, mechanical, cutting, drilling, machining, pressing, routing, sanding, surface grinding or turning of ceramic art work, ceramic precision parts, leather, metals, plastics, rubber, fiberboard, masonry, carbon or graphite provided that any lubricants, coolants, or cutting oils used have **50 grams of VOC per liter or less**, or a VOC composite partial pressure of 20 mm Hg or less at 20ºC … [does not apply to automatic tire buffers, semi-automatic tire buffers and asphalt pavement grinders.]
> (b) Equipment used exclusively for carving, cutting, drilling, planing, routing, sanding, sawing, shredding or turning of wood … [does not apply when source of wood includes painted/treated wood or is commingled with construction/demolition materials.]"

**E(8) Printing and Reproduction Equipment**
> "(a) Printing and related Coating or laminating Equipment used in Graphic Arts Operations, without dryers, using **less than two (2) gallons** of combined graphic arts material per day. Dryers include, but are not limited to, UV lights and infrared lamps."

**E(9) Food Processing and Preparation Equipment**
> "(a) Smokehouses for preparing food in which the maximum horizontal inside cross-sectional area does not exceed **21.5 square feet** (2 square meters).
> (e) Equipment used in eating establishments for the purpose of preparing food for human consumption.
> (i) Barbecue equipment which is used for commercial purposes within the district but for not more than a combined total of **fourteen (14) days** in any calendar year."

### Section (B)(8) — Burden of Proof (verbatim)

> "The burden of proof regarding the applicability of this Rule to particular Equipment shall be on the Owner/Operator of such Equipment. Failure to provide proof of the applicability of this rule to particular Equipment shall be considered a violation of District Rules 201 and/or 203 …"

## Determination Notes

- **Key exemption thresholds to remember:** Engine <50 bhp; combustion <2 MMBtu/hr on nat gas/LPG; graphic arts printing <2 gal/day (no dryer); cooling towers <10,000 gpm; residential structure (≤4 families).
- **Note on ICE threshold:** The 2021 amendment lowered the engine exemption from **100 bhp** (in the original 1976/2000 version at CARB) to **50 bhp**. Always use the current compiled version for analysis.
- **Agricultural exemption** is separate from the equipment-category exemption and has its own threshold table at §D(2).
- **Control equipment:** Rule 219 does NOT exempt air pollution control equipment venting otherwise-exempt equipment unless all venting equipment is also exempt and specifically listed in §E. Control equipment cannot be used to meet a permit exemption threshold.
- **Major source overlay:** Even if equipment falls in an exempt category, if it causes the facility to become a Major Facility or trigger a Major Modification, NSR/PSD review under Regulations XIII/XVI applies.
