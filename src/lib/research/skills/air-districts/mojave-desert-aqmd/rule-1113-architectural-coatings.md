## Purpose

Rule 1113 limits VOC content in architectural coatings sold, supplied, manufactured, or applied within the Mojave Desert AQMD. It applies to anyone who supplies, sells, manufactures, blends, repackages, applies, or solicits application of architectural coatings within the District. Adopted as part of Regulation XI – Source Specific Standards. Amendments effective January 1, 2013 consolidated specialty categories.

Extracted from EPA-compiled MDAQMD Rules and Regulations PDF (August 2022 compile).

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Section (A)(2)–(A)(3) Applicability (verbatim)

> "(2) Applicability: Except as provided in subsection (A)(3), this rule is applicable to any person who supplies, sells, offers for sale, manufactures, blends or repackages any Architectural Coating for use within the Mojave Desert Air Quality Management District as well as any person who applies or Solicits the application of any Architectural Coating within the District.
> (3) This rule does not apply to:
>   (a) Any Architectural Coating that is supplied, sold, offered for sale, or manufactured for use outside of the District or for shipment to other manufacturers for reformulation or repackaging.
>   (b) Any Aerosol Coating Product.
>   (c) With the exception of Section (E), any Architectural Coating that is sold in a container with a volume of one (1) liter (1.057 quart) or less."

### Section (C)(1) VOC Content Limits (verbatim)

> "(a) Except as provided in subsections (C)(2) and (C)(5), no person shall:
>   (i) Manufacture, blend, or repackage for use within the District;
>   (ii) Supply, sell, or offer for sale for use within the District; or
>   (iii) Solicit for application or apply within the District,
> any Architectural Coating with a VOC Content in excess of the corresponding limit specified in Table 1 or Table 2, after the specified effective date in Table 1 or Table 2."

### Section (C)(2) Most Restrictive VOC Limit (verbatim)

> "If anywhere on the container of any Architectural Coating … any representation is made that indicates that the Coating meets the definition of, or is recommended for use for more than one of the Coating categories listed in Table 1 or Table 2, then the most restrictive VOC Content limit shall apply."

### Section (C)(6) Painting Practices (verbatim)

> "(a) All Architectural Coating containers used to apply the contents therein to a surface directly from the container by pouring, siphoning, brushing, rolling, padding, ragging or other means, shall be closed when not in use.
> (b) Containers of any VOC-containing materials used for thinning and cleanup shall be closed when not in use."

### Section (C)(7) Thinning (verbatim)

> "(a) No person who applies or Solicits the application of any Architectural Coating shall apply a Coating that is thinned to exceed the applicable VOC limit specified in Table 1 or Table 2."

### Section (F)(1) Administrative — Rule 442 Fallback (verbatim)

> "(a) Any Coating, Coating operation, or facility which is exempt from all or a portion of the VOC limits of this rule shall comply with the provisions of District Rule 442."

## Determination Notes

- **Flat coatings (post-1/1/2013):** 50 g/L VOC limit — a stringent standard comparable to SCAQMD Reg. XI.
- **Non-flat coatings:** 100 g/L; high-gloss nonflat: 150 g/L.
- **Aerosol coating products** are completely exempt from this rule.
- **Containers ≤1 liter** are exempt from VOC limits (but must comply with labeling requirements in Section (E)).
- **Industrial Maintenance Coatings** (250 g/L) is the key category for metal-structure coatings on bridges, tanks, refineries, and other heavy industrial uses.
- **Rule 442 is the backstop:** Any coating or operation exempt from Rule 1113 still must comply with Rule 442's 540 kg/month facility-wide VOC cap.
- **Graphic arts (sign paints):** 500 g/L limit — relatively permissive. Small printing operations (<2 gal/day) may also qualify for Rule 219 exemption from permitting entirely.
- **Shop vs. field application:** Rule 1113 applies to coatings applied in the field (at the site of installation). Shop applications (factory/manufacturing) are not architectural coatings under this rule.
- **Permit nexus:** Rule 1113 does not itself trigger a permit requirement; permits are governed by Rules 201/203. However, Rule 219 exempts printing equipment using <2 gal/day, so larger coating operations that exceed Rule 1113's VOC limits and generate >2 tons/yr of VOC will need a permit.
