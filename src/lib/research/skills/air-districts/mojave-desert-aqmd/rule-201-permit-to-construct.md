## Purpose

Rule 201 is the foundational permit-to-construct (Authority to Construct, ATC) requirement for the Mojave Desert AQMD. Any person who wishes to build, erect, install, alter, or replace equipment that may emit or control air contaminants must first obtain written authorization from the Air Pollution Control Officer (APCO) before commencing construction. The companion operating permit is issued under Rule 203.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Full Text (verbatim from PDF — Adopted: January 9, 1976)

> "A person shall not build, erect, install, alter or replace any equipment, the use of which may cause the issuance of air contaminants or the use of which may eliminate, reduce or control the issuance of air contaminants without first obtaining written authorization for such construction from the Air Pollution Control Officer. A permit to construct shall remain in effect until the permit to operate the equipment for which the application was filed is granted or denied, or the application is canceled."

### Key Points

- **Trigger:** Any equipment with **potential to emit** air contaminants — including air pollution control equipment.
- **Permit type issued:** Authority to Construct (ATC). Distinct from the Permit to Operate (PTO) under Rule 203.
- **Duration:** The ATC remains valid until the PTO application under Rule 203 is granted, denied, or canceled.
- **Alteration/replacement:** Also required for alterations and replacements of permitted equipment, not just new installations.
- **Exemptions:** Equipment categories exempted from permit requirements are governed by Rule 219 (Equipment Not Requiring a Permit). See `rule-219-equipment-not-requiring-permit.md`.

### Relationship to Other Provisions

- Rule 206 (Posting of Permit to Operate) governs where the permit must be physically posted at the equipment.
- Rule 219 lists equipment categories that are exempt from Rules 201 and 203.
- Regulation XIII (New Source Review) applies for major modifications; Regulation XVI (Prevention of Significant Deterioration) applies for major PSD facilities.
- The ATC must be in place **prior to the commencement** of any construction activity.

## Determination Notes

- **Threshold for permit requirement:** The rule is broad — any equipment with potential to emit triggers the ATC requirement, unless specifically exempted by Rule 219.
- **Practical exemption thresholds (from Rule 219 §E):** Engines <50 bhp; combustion sources <2,000,000 Btu/hr on natural gas or LPG; printing equipment using <2 gal/day of graphic arts material. See Rule 219 for the full enumerated exemption list.
