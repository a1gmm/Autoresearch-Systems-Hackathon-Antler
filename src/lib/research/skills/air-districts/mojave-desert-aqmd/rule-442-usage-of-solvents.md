## Purpose

Rule 442 limits VOC emissions from solvent-using operations not covered by a more specific source-specific rule in Regulation XI. It is the catch-all solvent/VOC rule for operations such as surface coating, cleaning, degreasing, adhesive application, and graphic arts where no other Regulation IV or Regulation XI rule applies. Adopted: 05/07/76; amended: 02/27/06.

Extracted from EPA-compiled MDAQMD Rules and Regulations PDF (August 2022 compile).

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Section (A)(1) Purpose (verbatim)

> "(a) To reduce volatile organic compound (VOC) emission from VOC containing materials or equipment which is not subject to VOC limits of any rule found in District Regulation XI – Source Specific Standards.
> (b) To provide emissions limits for the use of organic solvents which are not VOCs."

### Section (A)(2) Applicability (verbatim)

> "(a) This rule applies to any person using VOC containing materials or Emissions Unit which is not subject to the VOC limits of any other rule found in District Regulation IV – Prohibitions or in any rule found in the District Regulation XI – Source Specific Standards.
>   (i) VOC containing materials include, but are not limited to; coatings, resins, adhesives, inks, solvents, thinners, diluents, mold seal and release compounds, lubricants, cutting oils and quenching oils.
> (b) This rule applies to any person using a Non-VOC organic solvent and which is not subject to the limits of any other rule found in District Regulation IV – Prohibitions or in any rule found in District Regulation XI – Source Specific Standards."

### Section (C)(1) Emission Limitations (verbatim)

> "(a) A person shall not discharge VOCs into the atmosphere from all VOC containing materials, Emissions Units, equipment or processes subject to this rule, in excess of **540 kilograms (1,190 pounds) per month per Facility**.
>   (i) Compliance with the provisions of subsection (C)(1)(a) above may be obtained through use of any of the following or any combination thereof:
>     a. Product reformulation or substitution;
>     b. Process changes;
>     c. Improvement of operational efficiency;
>     d. Development of innovative technology;
>     e. Installation of a control device operated in accordance with section (C)(2) below.
> (b) A person shall not discharge into the atmosphere a non-VOC organic solvent in excess of **272 kilograms (600 pounds) per day** as calculated on a thirty (30) day rolling average.
>   (i) For purposes of this subsection, discharge shall include a drying period of 12 hours following the application of such non-VOC solvents."

### Section (C)(2) Control Equipment Alternative (verbatim)

> "(a) A person may comply with the provisions of (C)(1) above by using a VOC emission collection and control system that reduces overall emissions by **eighty-five percent (85%)** as follows:
>   (i) The system shall capture at least **ninety percent (90%)**, by weight, of the emissions generated by the Emissions Unit, material or operation and
>     a. Have a destruction efficiency of at least **ninety-five percent (95%)**, by weight; or
>     b. Have an output of less than **fifty parts per million (50 PPM)** calculated as carbon with no dilution."

### Section (C)(3) Storage and Disposal (verbatim)

> "(a) All VOC containing materials subject to this rule, whether in its form for intended use or as a waste or used product, shall be stored in non-absorbent, non-leaking containers which shall be kept closed at all times, except when filling or emptying, and disposed of in a manner to prevent evaporation of VOCs into the atmosphere at the Facility."

### Section (D) Exemptions (verbatim)

> "The provisions of this rule shall not apply to:
> (a) The manufacture, transport or storage of organic solvents, or the transport or storage of materials containing organic solvents.
> (b) The emissions of VOCs from VOC-containing materials or equipment which are subject to other Regulation IV rules or which are exempt from air pollution control requirements by such rules.
> (c) The use of pesticides including insecticides, rodenticides or herbicides.
> (d) The use of 1,1,1 trichloroethane, methylene chloride and trichlorotrifluroethane.
> (e) Aerosol products."

### Section (E) Monitoring and Recordkeeping (verbatim)

> "(1) Usage records for all VOC-containing materials subject to this Rule shall be maintained on a daily basis and shall include but not be limited to:
>   (a) The amount, type and VOC content of each solvent used; and
>   (b) The method of application and substrate type; and
>   (c) The permit units involved in the operation (if any)
> (2) Such records shall be retained for two years and shall be made available upon request."

## Determination Notes

- **Primary threshold:** 540 kg/month (1,190 lb/month) of VOC per facility. This is the trigger for potential permit requirement or enforcement action.
- **Non-VOC organic solvents:** Separate 272 kg/day (600 lb/day) limit on 30-day rolling average.
- **Applicability is residual:** Rule 442 only applies if no specific Regulation XI rule (e.g., Rule 1113 Architectural Coatings, Rule 1114 Wood Products Coating, Rule 1115 Metal Parts Coating, Rule 1116 Automotive Refinishing, Rule 1104 Degreasing) governs the operation. Always check Regulation XI first.
- **Control device alternative:** 85% overall reduction (90% capture × 95% destruction, or <50 ppm output) is accepted in lieu of the monthly VOC mass limit.
- **Recordkeeping required:** Daily usage logs must be kept for 2 years.
- **Aerosol products are exempt** from Rule 442 (but may be subject to CARB consumer products regulations).
