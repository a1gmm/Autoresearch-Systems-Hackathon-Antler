## Purpose

Rule 413 controls VOC emissions from organic solvent degreasing operations (cold cleaners, open-top vapor degreasers, conveyorized vapor/liquid degreasers). It applies to any commercial or industrial degreasing operation using organic solvents, setting design requirements, freeboard ratios, and control device requirements.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

Verbatim from Rule 413 (Adopted 1/16/2001):

**Applicability:**
> "Requirements of this Rule shall apply to Organic Solvent Degreasing Operations."

**Exemptions (Section A.1):**
> "A.1.a Wipe Cleaning.
> A.1.b Except Subsection B.1.c.2, degreasing equipment using Low Volatility Solvent.
> A.1.c Unheated non-conveyorized cleaning equipment as specified in Section E.9.b of Rule 202.
> A.1.d Degreasing equipment using halogenated solvents. Such equipment shall comply with the National Emission Standards For Hazardous Air Pollutants (Subpart T of Part 63)."

**Cold Cleaner Design Requirements — Freeboard (Section B.1.b.1):**
> "Freeboard Height shall provide Freeboard Ratio greater than or equal to 0.75"

**Cold Cleaner Cover Requirement (Section B.1.b.3):**
> "Apparatus or cover shall be provided which prevents Organic Solvent from evaporating when not degreasing objects in Degreaser. Such cover shall be designed to be opened and closed easily with one hand"

**Cold Cleaner Control Requirements — High Volatility Solvent (Section B.1.c.1):**
> "If High Volatility Solvent is used, then one of the following control devices shall be used:
> B.1.c.1(a) Water cover if Organic Solvent is insoluble in and heavier than water; or
> B.1.c.1(b) Any other system of emission control demonstrated to have overall capture and Control Efficiency equivalent to at least 85%."

**General Operating Requirements (Section B.1.a):**
> "B.1.a.2 Organic Solvent leaks shall be corrected immediately, or Degreaser shut down and drained."
> "B.1.a.6 No Organic Solvent shall be stored or disposed including waste Organic Solvent and Organic Solvent residues, in such a manner to cause or allow its evaporation into the atmosphere"
> "B.1.a.9 Cleaned parts shall be drained for at least 15 seconds after cleaning or until dripping ceases"

## Determination Notes

- Halogenated solvent degreasers are out of scope for Rule 413 — they are governed by EPA NESHAP 40 CFR Part 63, Subpart T (and therefore cannot claim Rule 202 exemptions per Rule 202 §A.1).
- Wipe cleaning is fully exempt from Rule 413.
- Small unheated, non-conveyorized cleaners that meet the Rule 202 §E.9.b criteria (≤ 10.8 sq ft open surface, ≤ 92.5 gal volume, low-volatility solvent, < 25 gal/year loss) remain exempt from permitting but still must comply with Rule 413 operating practices.
- Low-volatility solvent cold cleaners are mostly exempt from Rule 413's control requirements (except the freeboard requirement in B.1.c.2).
- For facility permit determinations: a degreaser using high-volatility solvents above the Rule 202 §E.9.b thresholds requires a PTO and must comply with Rule 413 design and control requirements.
