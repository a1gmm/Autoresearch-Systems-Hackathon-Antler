# Imperial County APCD — Skill Index

## District Overview

Imperial County APCD (ICAPCD) is a small district in Imperial County, California (southeastern CA, bordering Arizona and Mexico). The district operates its own full rulebook — it is NOT a delegated CARB-only district. Rules are organized in Regulations I–XI and individually available as PDFs at `apcd.imperialcounty.org/rules-and-regulations/`.

The district adopts and enforces both state (CARB) and local rules. The full EPA-approved SIP compilation is also available: `https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2022-09/epa-approved-imperial-county-apcd-california-sips-compilation.pdf`

## One-Line Decision Guide

1. **Is a permit needed?** → Start with Rule 202: if equipment is in a categorical exemption (e.g., ICE ≤ 50 bhp, boiler ≤ 5 MMBtu/hr gas, surface coating ≤ 1 gal/day, architectural coatings always exempt) or emits ≤ 2 lbs/day of any Affected Pollutant, no ATC/PTO required. NSPS/NESHAP-covered equipment cannot claim exemptions.
2. **If permit needed:** File Rule 201 ATC application before any construction; PTO required before operation.
3. **BACT required?** → Any new/modified unit with potential to emit ≥ 25 lbs/day of any Nonattainment Pollutant (VOC, NOx, PM10, PM2.5) triggers Rule 207 BACT analysis.
4. **Offsets required?** → Net emission increase ≥ 40 tpy VOC/NOx/SOx, ≥ 15 tpy PM10, or ≥ 10 tpy PM2.5 triggers offset requirement under Rule 207.
5. **Title V?** → Source with potential to emit ≥ 100 tpy any regulated pollutant (or ≥ 70 tpy PM10, or ≥ 10/25 tpy HAPs) must obtain Rule 900 federal operating permit.
