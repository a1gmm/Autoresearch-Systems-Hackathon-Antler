## Purpose / Applicability

Per Section 101: "To limit the emission of volatile organic compounds from degreasers."

Per Section 102: "This rule applies to solvent degreasing operations." A degreaser is "a tank, tray, drum, or other container in which objects to be cleaned are exposed to a degreasing solvent or degreasing solvent vapor" (Section 205).

Last amended: September 25, 2008.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Exemptions (Section 110)

> "110.1 The provisions of this rule do not apply to wipe cleaning."

> "110.2 Prior to September 25, 2009, the provisions of this rule shall not apply to non-vapor degreasers which use solvents that contain **50 grams per liter or less** VOCs including water and exempt compounds."

> "110.3 Effective September 25, 2009, the provisions of this rule shall not apply to degreasers which use solvents that contain **25 grams per liter or less** VOCs including water and exempt compounds, as determined by Sections 502.5 and 502.6."

> "110.5 Prior to September 25, 2009, the provisions of this rule shall not apply to solvent degreasing operations that are subject to the NESHAP requirements of 40 CFR Part 63 Subpart T — Halogenated Solvents Emissions from Solvent Cleaning."

> "110.6 The provisions of this rule shall not apply to products subject to the Air Resources Board Consumer Products Regulations as set forth at Subchapter 8.5, Article 2, Section 94507-94517 of Title 17 of the California Code of Regulations."

> "110.7 The VOC content limits in Sections 302.2 and 302.3 do not apply to degreasing of tools, equipment and machinery, regulated under Rule 456, AEROSPACE ASSEMBLY AND COMPONENT COATING OPERATIONS, and aerospace products using solvents that comply with the surface preparation and cleanup VOC limits in Section 304.7 of Rule 456."

> "110.8 The provisions of this rule shall not apply to degreasing of high-voltage microwave vacuum tubes."

### Non-Vapor Degreaser — VOC Solvent Content Limits (Section 302)

> "302.2 Prior to September 25, 2009, a person owning or operating a non-vapor degreaser shall use solvents with a VOC content of **50 grams per liter or less** including water and exempt compounds, or use an airtight/airless cleaning system."

> "302.3 Effective September 25, 2009, a person owning or operating a non-vapor degreaser shall use a solvent with a VOC content of **25 grams per liter or less** including water and exempt compounds, or use an airtight/airless cleaning system. The airtight/airless cleaning system shall comply with the requirements in Section 303."

### Non-Vapor Degreaser — Equipment Requirements (Section 302.1)
> "302.1 A person shall only operate non-vapor degreasers using one of the following control devices:
> a. Non-vapor degreasers shall operate with a **freeboard ratio equal to or greater than 0.75** if using solvents which are:
>    1. Agitated, or
>    2. Heated above 122°F (50°C), or
>    3. Volatile.
> b. Non-vapor degreasers using only low volatility solvents which are not agitated shall operate with a **freeboard height of at least 6 inches**.
> c. A water cover may be used as an acceptable alternative to Sections a and b if the solvent is insoluble in water and has a specific gravity greater than 1."

### Airtight/Airless Cleaning System — Compliance Path (Section 303)
> "303.2 The airtight/airless cleaning system shall not have a vapor leak of more than **50 parts per million measured as methane** at the outlet of the airtight/airless cleaning system."

### Vapor Degreaser — Equipment Requirements (Section 304.1)
A vapor degreaser must be equipped with all of:
- Automated parts handling system
- Circumferential primary condensing coils
- Circumferential trough
- Water separator
- **Freeboard ratio of at least 1.0**
- Superheated vapor zone
- Refrigerated freeboard chiller (chilled air blanket temperature ≤ 30% of solvent boiling point in °F, or 41°F)

> "304.3 Effective September 25, 2009, a person owning or operating a vapor degreaser shall use a solvent with a VOC content of **25 grams per liter or less** including water and exempt compounds, or use an airtight/airless cleaning system."

### Low Volatility Solvent Definition (Section 212)
> "LOW VOLATILITY SOLVENT: Any solvent with an initial boiling point which is greater than **248°F (120°C)**."

## Determination Notes

1. **Wipe cleaning is categorically excluded** from this rule (§110.1) — evaluate under Rule 441 instead.
2. **Key threshold (current):** If the solvent VOC content is ≤25 g/l (including water and exempt compounds), the degreaser is exempt from rule requirements under §110.3.
3. **Non-vapor degreasers** using volatile solvents need freeboard ratio ≥0.75 AND must meet the 25 g/l VOC limit (or use an airtight/airless system).
4. **Vapor degreasers** face stricter equipment requirements (seven required components) plus the same 25 g/l VOC limit.
5. NESHAP-regulated halogenated solvent operations (40 CFR Part 63 Subpart T) were exempt pre-September 2009; check current NESHAP status — the Subpart T exemption language in §110.5 contains a "prior to September 25, 2009" qualifier, so confirm whether current operations remain covered by NESHAP.
6. **Permit linkage:** Solvent degreasing equipment that doesn't meet Rule 454 exemptions typically requires a Permit to Operate under Rule 201 as a VOC-emitting source.
