## Purpose / Applicability

Rule 201 provides the foundational permit framework for Sacramento Metropolitan AQMD. Per Section 101: "To provide an orderly procedure for the review of new sources of air pollution and of the modification and operation of existing sources through the issuance of permits."

Any person building, erecting, altering, or replacing any article, machine, equipment or other contrivance whose use may cause, eliminate, reduce, or control air contaminants must first obtain an Authority to Construct (ATC) and then a Permit to Operate (PTO) from the Air Pollution Control Officer.

Sources also subject to Rule 207 (Title V) must obtain a federal operating permit under that rule in addition to this rule.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Authority to Construct (Section 301)
> "Any person building, erecting, altering or replacing any article, machine, equipment or other contrivance, the use of which may cause, eliminate, reduce, or control the issuance of air contaminants, shall first obtain authorization for such construction from the Air Pollution Control Officer as specified in Section 403 of this rule. An authority to construct shall remain in effect until a permit to operate the equipment is granted or denied or the application is canceled. An authority to construct shall expire two years from the date of issuance."

### Permit to Operate (Section 302)
> "Any person operating an article, machine, equipment or other contrivance, the use of which may cause, eliminate, reduce, or control the issuance of air contaminants, shall first obtain a written permit from the Air Pollution Control Officer."

### Standards for Granting Applications (Section 303.1)
> "The Air Pollution Control Officer shall deny an authority to construct or permit to operate... if the applicant does not show that every article, machine, equipment or other contrivance... is so designed, controlled, equipped, and operated with such air pollution control equipment that it may be shown to operate without emitting or without causing to be emitted air contaminants in violation of these rules and regulations or of such state or federal statutes as may be enforceable by the Air Pollution Control Officer."

### Permit Renewal (Section 305)
> "Every permit to operate... shall be renewable annually on the permit's anniversary date, commencing one year after the date of issuance."

### Built-In Exemptions (Sections 111–122)

These exemptions do NOT apply if the equipment is subject to NSPS, NESHAP, emits toxic air contaminants at reviewable levels, or the APCO determines it may not operate in compliance (Section 110).

**Section 112.1 — Combustion/Heat Transfer:**
> "Internal combustion engines with a manufacturer's maximum continuous rating of 50 brake horsepower or less or gas turbine engines with a maximum heat input rate of 3,000,000 British Thermal Units (Btu) per hour or less at ISO standard dry conditions. The ratings of all engines or turbines used in the same process will be accumulated to determine whether this exemption applies."

**Section 112.2 — Small Combustion Equipment:**
> "Any combustion equipment that has a maximum heat input of less than 1,000,000 Btu per hour (gross) and is equipped to be fired exclusively with purchased quality natural gas, liquefied petroleum gas or any combination thereof. The ratings of all combustion equipment used in the same process will be accumulated to determine whether this exemption applies."

**Section 113 — Residential Structures:**
> "Equipment utilized exclusively in connection with any structure, which structure is designed for and used exclusively as a dwelling for not more than four families."

**Section 114 — Agricultural Operations:**
> "Equipment/processes used exclusively in the growing of agricultural crops, or in the commercial raising of fowl or other animals, and is not a new major stationary source or a major modification as defined in Rule 202."

**Section 115 — Cooling Systems and Vacuum Cleaning:**
> "Refrigeration, air conditioning, ventilating, water cooling towers or vacuum cleaning systems not designed to remove air contaminants generated by equipment which would require a permit under these rules and regulations."

**Section 116 — Plastics and Ceramics Processing:**
> "Ovens, kilns, or furnaces fired by electricity used exclusively for the heating, curing, softening, or annealing of plastics or ceramics, and not emitting more than 5 pounds of volatile organic compounds in any one day. This Section shall not apply to ovens used for heating or curing of fiberglass reinforced plastics."

**Section 117 — Storage and Transfer:**
> Tanks and containers used for:
> - "117.2 Unheated organic materials with an initial boiling point of 150°C (302°F) or greater... or with an organic vapor pressure of 5 mm Hg (0.1 psia) or less at 20°C"
> - "117.3 Organic liquids with a vapor pressure of 77.5 mm Hg (1.5 psia) or less at 20°C... having a capacity of 23,000 liters (6076 gallons or less)"
> - "117.4 Unheated solvent dispensing containers of 380 liters (100 gallons) capacity or less."

**Section 118 — Surface Coating and Preparation:**
> - "118.2 Surface coating operations using a combined total of one gallon per day or less of coating material and solvent."
> - "118.3 Unheated non-conveyorized solvent rinsing containers or unheated non-conveyorized coating dip tanks of 380 liters (100 gallons) capacity or less."

**Section 120 — Laboratory Equipment:**
> "Laboratory equipment used exclusively for chemical or physical analysis and bench scale tests, including associated vacuum-producing equipment."

**Section 122 — Other Equipment (De Minimis):**
> "Other equipment deemed by the Air Pollution Control Officer and which would emit any pollutants without the benefit of air pollution control devices less than 2 pounds in any 24 hour period."

### Action Timing (Section 404.1)
> "In the event said notification is not received by applicant within 30 days of the filing of the application... the applicant may, at his option, deem the application to construct or permit to operate denied."

## Determination Notes

1. **Threshold question:** Does the equipment "cause, eliminate, reduce, or control air contaminants"? If yes, ATC + PTO required unless an exemption applies.
2. **Check exemptions Sections 111–122** in order. Key practical exemptions: ICE ≤50 BHP (§112.1), combustion <1 MMBtu/hr on gas (§112.2), surface coating ≤1 gal/day total (§118.2), de minimis <2 lb/day emissions (§122).
3. **Override check (Section 110):** Even if equipment would otherwise be exempt, review is required if subject to NSPS/NESHAP, emits listed toxic air contaminants, or APCO determines compliance risk.
4. **Title V crosscheck:** If the source meets major source thresholds, also evaluate Rule 207 (Title V) and Rule 202 (New Source Review) requirements.
5. ATC has a **2-year expiration**; good faith extension requests must be submitted before expiration.
