## Purpose / Applicability

Rule 201 provides the list of source and equipment categories exempt from Rule 200's ATC/PTO requirements. From §1.1:

> "The purpose of this Rule is to provide a list of source and equipment categories which are exempt from the requirements of District Rule 200 (Permits Required) to obtain an Authority to Construct (ATC) or Permit to Operate (PTO)."

Per §1.2.1, exemptions apply only to the ATC/PTO requirement — exempt sources must still comply with all other applicable District Rules and Regulations (emission standards, etc.).

Per §1.2.2, exemptions apply to individual articles of equipment or process lines unless otherwise indicated.

**Effective date:** October 15, 2014 (most recent revision per PDF).

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Mandatory Permit — Exceptions to All Exemptions (§1.3)

The following sources ALWAYS require a permit regardless of any exemption category:

> "§1.3.1.1 Any source subject to a: [§1.3.1.1.1] New Source Performance Standard; or [§1.3.1.1.2] National Emission Standard for Hazardous Air Pollutants (NESHAP) except for the Demolition and Renovation requirements of the Asbestos NESHAP … ; or [§1.3.1.1.3] federal Maximum Achievable Control Technology standard (MACT); or [§1.3.1.1.4] state air toxic control measure (ATCM)."

> "§1.3.1.2 Any source providing offsets to which Section 6.11 of District Rule 207 (Review of New or Modified Sources) applies."

> "§1.3.1.3 Any Large Confined Animal Facility as defined in the California Code of Regulations in Title 17, Division 1, Chapter 1, Section 86500."

> "§1.3.2 A Federal Operating (Title V) Permit shall always be required for any source that is a Major Source as defined in District Rule 218 (Title V: Federal Operating Permits)."

### Exempt Categories (Part 4) — Key Thresholds

**4.1 Abrasive Blasting**
- Blast cabinet dust-filter combos with total internal volume ≤ 50 cubic feet
- Wet blast (abrasive-in-water suspension)
- Equipment with manufacturer's sand capacity ≤ 100 lbs (45.4 kg) or ≤ 1 cubic foot
- Equipment using ≤ 800 lbs (360 kg) total abrasive per calendar year

**4.2 Agricultural Operations**
- Equipment for growing crops or raising fowl/animals where actual emissions < 50% of Major Source threshold (Rule 218)

**4.3 Architectural Coatings**
> "Equipment used for the application of architectural coatings, as defined in Rule 426 (Architectural Coatings)." (No permit needed for the application equipment itself; coating VOC limits governed by Rule 426.)

**4.4 Contaminated Soil or Water Aeration**
- Projects containing diesel fuel only, or < 25 lbs Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons AND < 0.05 lbs benzene

**4.5 Crude Oil Production**
- Well heads and electric motor driven well pumps
- Crude oil/natural gas pipeline electric motor driven transfer pumps
- Crude oil tankage and truck loading with total production < 3,650 barrels/year

**4.8 External Combustion Equipment**
> "§4.8.1 Any steam generator, steam superheater, water boiler, water heater, or closed heat transfer system at a stationary source that has an aggregated heat input rate of less than:
> §4.8.1.1 2 million British Thermal Units (BTU) per hour (gross) when fired with natural gas or liquefied petroleum gas or any combination thereof.
> §4.8.1.2 250,000 British Thermal Units (BTU) per hour (gross) when fired on any other fuel."

> "§4.8.2 Process heaters or any combustion equipment at a stationary source that has an aggregated heat input rate of less than:
> §4.8.2.1 2 million British Thermal Units (BTU) per hour (gross) when fired with natural gas or liquefied petroleum gas or any combination thereof.
> §4.8.2.2 250,000 British Thermal Units (BTU) per hour (gross) when fired on any other fuel."

**4.10 Fermentation**
- Wineries in operation as of May 14, 1997 with annual production < 1.25 million gallons (4.7 megaliters)
- New or reconstructed wineries with annual production < 150,000 gallons (570 kiloliters)
- Breweries with annual production < 2.5 million gallons (9.5 megaliters)

**4.13 Heating and Cooling**
- Comfort air conditioning / ventilating systems not designed to remove equipment-generated contaminants
- Cooling towers/evaporative condensers without chromium additives with total water recirculation rate < 5,000 gallons (19 kiloliters) per minute
- Equipment used exclusively for space heating other than boilers

**4.14 Internal Combustion Engines**
> "§4.14.1 Any stationary piston-type internal combustion engine of less than 50 brake horsepower (bhp)."
> "§4.14.2 Any turbine at a stationary source that has a heat input rate of less than 3 million British Thermal Units (BTU) per hour (gross), and is fired exclusively with natural gas or liquefied petroleum gas or any combination thereof."

**4.15 Low Emitting Unit**
> "§4.15.1 Any process not identified in any other category within this Rule which has emissions of less than 2 pounds per day of any affected pollutant unless the affected pollutant is defined as a Toxic Contaminant in District Rule 1000."

**4.16.5 Wood Working**
- Wood-working equipment (sawing, sanding, routing, etc.) at a stationary source where the aggregated electrical horsepower of such equipment does not exceed 50 horsepower (dust collection equipment HP excluded)
- Wood chippers powered by engines < 50 brake horsepower (bhp)

**4.19 Printing and Reproduction Equipment**
> "§4.19.1 All sheet-fed printing presses and all other printing presses without dryers, which emit less than 10 pounds (4.5 kg) per day of any organic solvent or coating."
- Photographic process equipment reproducing images via radiant energy
- Lead-melting furnaces used exclusively in printing processes

**4.22 Residential Structures**
- Equipment used exclusively in connection with structures designed and used exclusively as dwellings for not more than four families

**4.23 Storage and Transfer Equipment**
- Tanks for storage of organic liquids (except gasoline) normally used as solvents, diluents, thinners, inks, coatings with capacity ≤ 250 gallons (950 liters)
- Unheated solvent dispensing containers ≤ 250 gallons (950 liters)
- Gasoline storage tanks with capacity < 250 gallons (950 liters)

**4.24 Surface Coating, Cleaning, and Adhesive Operations**
- Hand application (brush, dauber, roll-up) of nonarchitectural coatings, solvents, adhesives
- Aerosol cans (non-refillable handheld)
- Airbrush with cup size ≤ 2 ounces
> "§4.24.4 Equipment used for the application of any coatings or adhesive with a VOC content of less than 20 grams/liter."
> "§4.24.5 Powder Coating Operations where total usage is less than 10 pounds per day of powder coatings."
- Unheated non-conveyorized solvent rinsing containers or coating dip tanks ≤ 250 gallons (950 liters)
- Unheated non-conveyorized washers used solely for cleaning spray equipment

## Determination Notes

- Work through Rule 201 categories before concluding a permit is needed. Many common pieces of equipment are exempt.
- Critical override: even if a source falls in an exempt category, it still needs a permit if it is subject to NSPS, NESHAP, MACT, or ATCM (§1.3.1).
- The "Low Emitting Unit" catchall (§4.15.1, < 2 lb/day of any non-toxic pollutant) is a useful backstop for unusual equipment not fitting other categories — but does not apply to toxic air contaminants.
- Key thresholds for common determinations:
  - Boilers/heaters: ATC/PTO required at ≥ 2 MMBtu/hr (gas) or ≥ 250k BTU/hr (other fuel)
  - Stationary ICE: ATC/PTO required at ≥ 50 bhp
  - Printing presses (sheet-fed, no dryer): ATC/PTO required at ≥ 10 lb/day organic solvent
  - Surface coating equipment: ATC/PTO required if VOC ≥ 20 g/L and not another listed exemption
  - Powder coating: ATC/PTO required at ≥ 10 lb/day powder usage
