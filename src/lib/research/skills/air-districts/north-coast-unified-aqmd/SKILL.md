## District Overview

The North Coast Unified AQMD operates under Regulation I (General Provisions, Permits & Prohibition), which contains the permit rules (102), NSR/PSD (110), and other core requirements. The district is a small, rural AQMD covering the North Coast Air Basin — it adopts relatively few local source-specific rules (no coating, solvent, or graphic arts rules were identified on the rules page). State CARB ATCMs (e.g., the benzene/gasoline dispensing ATCM, Rule 301) fill those gaps.

Full rulebook is also compiled in the EPA-approved SIP document: https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2025-02/epa-approved-compiled-north-coast-unified-aqmd-district-wide.pdf

## One-Line Decision Guide

**Does the project need an ATC/PTO?** Yes — unless it falls squarely in one of the Rule 102(D) categorical exemptions (residential HVAC, food-prep equipment, <1 MMBtu/hr natural-gas boilers, steam cleaners, non-diesel space heaters, agricultural equipment, transit vehicles, etc.) AND its potential to emit does not exceed any Rule 110 Table 1.0 significance threshold. If it exceeds any threshold (e.g., NOx ≥ 50 lb/day or 40 tpy, PM10 ≥ 80 lb/day or 15 tpy, ROC ≥ 50 lb/day or 40 tpy), exemptions are void and BACT applies. At ≥ 25 tpy of any non-attainment pollutant, emission offsets are also required.

## Common Permit Exemptions (Rule 102(D))

- Residential HVAC / comfort ventilation (not removing process contaminants)
- Steam cleaning equipment
- Natural gas / LPG boilers/water heaters < 1 MMBtu/hr (30 HP)
- Non-diesel space heaters
- Food-prep equipment in eating establishments
- Agricultural equipment (crops, livestock, fowl) per H&SC
- Vehicles (per CA Vehicle Code) and passenger/freight transport vehicles
- Mobile construction equipment (not pavement burners)
- Residential structures ≤ 2 families (incl. incinerators)

## No Coating/Solvent/Graphic-Arts Local Rule Found

The NCUAQMD rules page lists no district-specific coating, solvent use, or graphic-arts rules. Such activities are governed by applicable CARB ATCMs and state regulations. Contact the district at (707) 443-3093 to confirm applicability of any state-level requirements.
