## Purpose

Rule 102 establishes the requirement to obtain an Authority to Construct (ATC) and Permit to Operate (PTO) before building, modifying, or operating any article, machine, equipment or contrivance that may cause or control the issuance of air contaminants. It also enumerates the equipment categories exempt from permit requirements.

Adopted: July 9, 2015 (Resolution 2015-9). Original adoption: 1982.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### A. General Requirements

> "No person shall cause or permit the construction or modification of any new source of air contaminants, including an indirect source, without first obtaining an Authority to Construct Permit from the APCO, which specifies the location and design of such new source and incorporates necessary permit conditions so as to ensure compliance with applicable Rules and Regulations and State and Federal Ambient Air Quality Standards."

### B. Authority to Construct

> "Before any person building, erecting, altering or replacing any article, machine, equipment or other contrivance or indirect source, the use of which may cause the issuance of air contaminants or the use of which may eliminate or reduce or control the issuance of air contaminants, the person shall first obtain written authorization in the form of an Authority to Construct Permit for such construction from the APCO."

ATC term: does not exceed two years from date of authorization, unless extended by the APCO for good cause.

### C. Permit to Operate

> "Before any article, machine, equipment or other contrivance described in Section B above may be operated or used, or leased or rented for operation or use including any indirect source, a written authorization must first be obtained from the APCO in the form of a Permit to Operate."

PTO term: not beyond the fiscal year of authorization; renewable annually for up to one fiscal year.

### D. Exemptions to Permit to Operate

> "The exemptions contained in this Rule shall not apply to any new stationary source or modification of an existing source which would result in the emission of any pollutants in excess of the Best Available Control Technology significance thresholds listed in Table 1 of Rule 110 (E). The exemptions set forth do not supersede the provisions of Regulation V..."

**An Authority to Construct and Permit to Operate shall not be required for:**

1. Vehicles as defined by the Vehicle Code of the State of California (not including equipment mounted on such vehicles that would otherwise require a permit).
2. Vehicles used to transport passengers or freight.
3. Equipment utilized exclusively in connection with any structure designed for and used exclusively as a dwelling for no more than two (2) families, including multi-chambered incinerators used exclusively in connection with such a structure.
4. Comfort air conditioning or comfort ventilating systems which are not designed to remove air contaminants generated by or released from specific units or equipment.
5. Reserved.
6. Equipment used exclusively for steam cleaning.
7. Water cooling towers and water cooling ponds not used for evaporative cooling of process water or from barometric jets or barometric condensers, except those which have the potential to emit or may emit chrome in any chemical forms (e.g. hexavalent chrome).
8. **Steam generators, water boilers or water heaters fired exclusively by natural gas, liquefied petroleum gas or a combination thereof, having a maximum fuel input heating value of less than one million (1,000,000) British Thermal Units (BTU) per hour or thirty (30) horsepower.**
9. Space heaters which do not operate on diesel fuel.
10. Equipment used in eating establishments for the purpose of preparing food for human consumption.
11. Self-propelled mobile construction equipment other than pavement burners.
12. Any equipment used in agricultural operations in the growing of crops or the raising of fowl or animals that are exempt from District permit requirements pursuant to the applicable provisions of the H&SC.
13. Any article, machine, equipment or other contrivance which the APCO finds emits air contaminants below the significance level and he determines should be exempted. **No exemption from the requirements listed herein under Rule 102(E) for an Authority to Construct or Permit to Operate may be allowed for any individual source which is subject to new source review.**

### G. Permit Term

- ATC: not to exceed two years from date of authorization (extendable by APCO for good cause).
- PTO: not beyond the fiscal year of authorization; annually renewable for up to one fiscal year.

## Determination Notes

- The Rule 102(D) exemptions are limited: they do not apply to any source that would exceed the BACT significance thresholds in Rule 110 Table 1.0, nor do they supersede Title V (Regulation V) requirements.
- The APCO retains sole authority over emissions calculations (Section F).
- Equipment mounted on otherwise exempt vehicles still requires a permit if it would independently require one.
- The boiler exemption (item 8) is key for small commercial/industrial combustion: under 1 MMBtu/hr on natural gas or LPG only.
- Space heaters are exempt unless diesel-fired.
- Food-preparation equipment in eating establishments is fully exempt.
- APCO may grant case-by-case exemptions (item 13) for sources below significance levels — but not if the source is subject to NSR.
