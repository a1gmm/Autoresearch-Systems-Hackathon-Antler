## Purpose / Applicability

Rule 11 lists equipment, operations, and processes that are exempt from Rule 10 permit requirements (Authority to Construct and Permit to Operate). It was most recently revised and effective August 14, 2025. The exemption applies only to the permit requirement — all other applicable district rules, state, and federal regulations (including NESHAP and NSPS) still apply. The rule contains 20 numbered exemption categories covering mobile sources, combustion equipment, laboratory equipment, printing, coatings, solvents, and registered equipment.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

**Global Overrides — Exemption Does Not Apply When (Section a):**

> "(4) This rule shall not apply to any equipment, operation, or process described in Subsections (d)(2) through (d)(19) that emits more than 100 pounds per day of any one of the following criteria air pollutants: particulate matter (PM10), oxides of nitrogen (NOx), volatile organic compound (VOC), oxides of sulfur (SOx), carbon monoxide (CO), or lead (Pb)."

> "(5) Except for equipment specified in Subsection (d)(20)(iii), Section (d) of this rule shall not apply to any equipment, operation, or process that (i) emits or may emit toxic air contaminants, as defined in Rule 1200 – Toxic Air Contaminants – New Source Review, and (ii) has emissions of toxic air contaminants that, in the absence of any emission control device or limitation on material usage or production, may be expected to exceed any standard specified in Rule 1200 (d)(1)(i), (d)(2), or (d)(3) as determined by the Air Pollution Control Officer."

> "(3) This rule shall not apply to any equipment, operation, or process that violates Rule 50 – Visible Emissions or Rule 51 – Nuisance as determined by the Air Pollution Control Officer."

**Section (d)(2) — Combustion and Heat Transfer Equipment:**
- Any reciprocating internal combustion engine with a **brake horsepower rating of less than 50**.
- Any gas turbine engine with **output power rating of less than 0.3 megawatt (MW)**, or maximum gross heat input rating of less than 1 million BTU per hour.
- Any boiler, process heater, steam generator, or water heater with manufacturer's maximum gross heat input rating of:
  - **less than 1 million BTU per hour fired with any fuel**, or
  - **2 million BTU per hour or less fired exclusively with natural gas and/or liquefied petroleum gas**.
- Any oven having an internal volume of **27 cubic feet (0.765 cubic meter) or less**.
- Curing or baking ovens in which **no volatile organic solvents** or materials containing volatile organic solvents are introduced.

**Section (d)(4) — Laboratory Equipment and Related Operations:**
- Laboratory testing equipment and quality control testing equipment including associated wipe cleaning, used exclusively for chemical and physical analysis or quality control.
- Research and development equipment, including associated wipe cleaning.
- Equipment used to manufacture biotechnology pharmaceutical products for exclusive use in FDA approved clinical trials, provided that **total uncontrolled VOC emissions from all such operations do not exceed 5 tons per calendar year**.

**Section (d)(5) — Replacement of Equipment:**
Identical replacement equipment and like-kind replacement equipment are exempt from ATC requirements, subject to limitations. Does not apply to replacement that would constitute reconstruction or modification under NSPS.

**Section (d)(11) — Printing and Reproduction Equipment:**
> "(i) Any graphic arts operation or group of graphic arts operations located at a stationary source, that emit less than an average of **15 pounds of VOCs per operating day for each calendar month** from all such operations."
- Inkjet and laser printing equipment: exempt.
- Digital printing operations where print capacity of any individual printer using **solvent based inks is less than 1,000 ft2/hr**, or using **water-based or UV inks is less than 10,000 ft2/hr**: exempt.
- Large commercial digital printing operations: exempt if records specified in Rule 67.16(f) are maintained.

**Section (d)(15) — Coating and Adhesive Application Equipment:**
> "(iii) Any coating or adhesive materials application operation (portable or stationary) where **20 gallons or less of liquid coatings or adhesive materials are applied per consecutive 12-month period**. Monthly purchase records and daily or monthly usage records of all coatings or adhesive materials applied must be maintained on-site for three years to claim applicability of this exemption."

> "(iv) Any coating or adhesive materials application operation (portable or stationary) where the **VOC emissions from the application of liquid coatings or adhesive materials are 150 pounds or less per consecutive 12-month period**."

- Powder coating operations where **less than 0.5 gallons per day** of any surface preparation or cleaning material containing VOCs are used: exempt.
- Application equipment used exclusively to apply coatings/adhesives to stationary structures at the site of installation, pavement, or curbs: exempt. (Does not apply to off-site shops or non-stationary structures such as airplanes, ships, railcars, and automobiles.)

**Section (d)(16) — Solvent Application Equipment and Operations:**
> "(i) Cold solvent cleaning or stripping operations and/or vapor degreasing operations that exclusively utilize materials with a **VOC content of 25 grams per liter (g/l) (0.21 lbs/gal) of material or less**, as used."

> "(ii) Cold solvent cleaning dip tanks, vapor degreasers, and paint stripping tanks: (A) with a liquid surface area of **1 square foot or less**, or (B) with a maximum capacity of **1 gallon or less**."

> "(iii) Cold solvent cleaning remote reservoirs with a sink cross-sectional area of **1 square foot (0.09 square meters) or less**."

> "(iv) Batch-type waste solvent recovery stills for on-site recovery of waste solvent with a **maximum solvent usage of 350 gallons per day**, provided the still is equipped with a device that shuts off the heating system if the solvent vapor condenser is not operating properly."

**Section (d)(20) — Registered Equipment:**
Certain equipment may register with the District rather than obtain individual permits (see Rule 12).

## Determination Notes

- Rule 11 is the "off-ramp" from Rule 10. Work through the applicable equipment category in Section (d) and check whether the stated numeric threshold is met.
- Always check the four global overrides first: >100 lb/day criteria pollutant, toxic air contaminant exceedance, visible emissions violation, or major stationary source status strips the exemption.
- Recordkeeping is a condition of the exemption for many categories (coating volumes, VOC emissions, purchase records) — the exemption is forfeited if records cannot be produced on request.
- If a unit does not qualify under Rule 11, it falls back to Rule 10 for full ATC/PTO requirements, and Rule 20.1–20.4 for New Source Review.
- The 100 lb/day threshold is per pollutant, not aggregate — an operation emitting 99 lb/day of VOC and 99 lb/day of NOx would still qualify on the emissions threshold (but both individual pollutants must stay below 100 lb/day).
