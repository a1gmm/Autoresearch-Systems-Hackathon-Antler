# Amador County APCD — Air District Skill

## Decision Guide (one-line)

Any non-exempt equipment that causes, reduces, or controls air contaminants needs an Authority
to Construct before building and a Permit to Operate before running; major sources (≥100 tpy
regulated, ≥10/25 tpy HAP) additionally need a Title V operating permit under Rule 500.

## District Character

Amador County APCD is a small, rural district serving Amador County in the Sierra Nevada
foothills. The district has a very small regulated universe — historically only one facility has
exceeded the 50 tpy NOx threshold. Most regulated activities are:
- Fuel stations / storage tanks
- Concrete batch plants
- Agricultural burning
- Internal combustion engines (irrigation, standby generators)
- Small industrial/commercial boilers
- Autobody shops

The district relies heavily on CARB statewide regulations for categories such as architectural
coatings, consumer products, motor vehicle fuel, and in-use equipment (engines, vehicles). Local
district rules are sparse beyond the core permit/prohibitions framework.

## Permit Process Overview

1. File ATC application with APCO before construction or modification.
2. APCO reviews and issues ATC (approval, conditional approval, or denial) within 30 days
   (or applicant may deem denied after 30 days).
3. ATC valid 2 years from filing; equipment must be built and PTO obtained within that window.
4. File PTO application once equipment is installed; APCO conducts compliance inspection.
5. If source is major (≥100 tpy or ≥10/25 tpy HAP), also apply for Title V permit under Rule 500
   within 6 months of commencing operation.
6. Sources emitting ≥25 tpy NOx or VOC must file annual Emissions Statements under Rule 428.

## Exemptions Quick-Reference

- Comfort HVAC (not process-related): EXEMPT
- Piston engines (ICE): EXEMPT under original Reg III Rule 5(d)(3) — but CARB ARB rules apply
- Welding / brazing / soldering: EXEMPT
- Boilers/water heaters <250 MMBtu/hr on natural gas or LPG: EXEMPT
- Food preparation equipment: EXEMPT
- Residential ≤4 families: EXEMPT
- Identical replacement of permitted equipment: EXEMPT
- Pavement burners: NOT EXEMPT (explicitly excluded from mobile equipment exemption)
- APCO may grant additional "minor significance" exemptions on case-by-case basis
