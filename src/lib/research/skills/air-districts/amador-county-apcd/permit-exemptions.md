## Purpose

Two sets of exemptions exist: (1) Regulation III, Rule 5 exempts equipment from the Authority to
Construct (ATC) requirement; (2) Regulation V, Rule 502 allows the APCO to exempt equipment from
the Permit to Operate (PTO) requirement, referencing the same list as Rule 401 Exemptions.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds (Verbatim)

**Regulation III, Rule 5 – Exemptions:**
> "An authority to construct or a permit to operate shall not be required for:
>
> a. Vehicles as defined by the Vehicle Code of the State of California but not including any
>    article, machine, equipment or other contrivance mounted on such vehicle that would otherwise
>    require a permit under the provisions of these rules and regulations.
>
> b. Vehicles used to transport passengers or freight.
>
> c. Equipment utilized exclusively in connection with any structure, which structure is designed
>    for and used exclusively as a dwelling for not more than four families.
>
> d. The following equipment:
>    1. Comfort air conditioning or comfort ventilating systems, which are not designed to remove
>       air contaminants generated by or released from specific units or equipment.
>    2. Refrigeration units except those used as, or in conjunction with, air pollution control
>       equipment.
>    3. Piston type internal combustion engines.
>    4. Water cooling towers and water cooling ponds not used for evaporative cooling of water
>       from barometric jets or from barometric condensers.
>    5. Equipment used exclusively for steam cleaning.
>    6. Presses used exclusively for extruding metals, minerals, plastics, or wood.
>    7. Equipment used exclusively for space heating, other than boilers.
>    8. Equipment used for hydraulic or hydrostatic testing.
>    9. Equipment used in eating establishments for the purpose of preparing food for human
>       consumption.
>    10. Equipment used exclusively to compress or hold dry natural gas.
>
> e. The following equipment or any exhaust system or collector serving exclusively such equipment:
>    1. Laboratory equipment used exclusively for chemical or physical analyses and bench scale
>       laboratory equipment.
>    2. Brazing, soldering, or welding equipment.
>
> f. Steam generators, steam superheaters, water boilers, water heaters, and closed heat transfer
>    systems of less than 250,000,000 British Thermal Units per hour (gross), and capacity are
>    fired exclusively with one of the following:
>    1. Natural gas.
>    2. Liquified petroleum gas.
>    3. A combination of natural gas and liquified petroleum gas.
>
> g. Natural draft hoods, natural draft stacks, or natural draft ventilators.
>
> h. Identical replacements in whole or in part of any equipment where a permit to operate has
>    previously been granted for such equipment.
>
> i. Repairs or maintenance not involving structural changes to any equipment for which a permit
>    has been granted.
>
> j. Vacuum cleaning systems used exclusively for industrial, commercial, or residential
>    housekeeping purposes.
>
> k. Agricultural implements used in agricultural operations.
>
> l. Self-propelled mobile construction equipment other than pavement burners.
>
> m. Other sources of minor significance which may be specified by the Air Pollution Control
>    Officer."

**Regulation V, Rule 502 – Exemptions to Rule 501:**
> "The Air Pollution Control Officer may exempt from the requirements of Rule 501 any items of
> equipment specified in Rule 502 Exemptions to Rule 401."
> (Rule 502 directs to the same exemption categories as listed under Regulation III Rule 5 / Rule
> 401 Exemptions for the Permit to Operate requirement.)

**Regulation III – Exemptions note on pavement burners:**
> "Self-propelled mobile construction equipment other than pavement burners."
> (Pavement burners are NOT exempt; they require ATC/PTO.)

> "The exemptions as set forth above do not supersede the provisions [of applicable state law]."

## Determination Notes

- Exemption (d)(3) — piston-type internal combustion engines — is notable: under the original
  Regulation III these are exempt from ATC/PTO. However, engines are subject to CARB ARB
  in-use regulations independently of the district permit system.
- Exemption (d)(1) — comfort HVAC — is limited to systems not removing contaminants from
  specific process equipment. Industrial ventilation tied to a process is NOT exempt.
- Exemption (f) — boilers/water heaters below 250 MMBtu/hr fired on natural gas or LPG are
  exempt. Units fired on other fuels (diesel, biomass, waste gas) are NOT exempt.
- Exemption (h) — identical replacements are exempt only if the original unit already holds a PTO.
  A new replacement of previously unpermitted equipment is not covered.
- Exemption (m) — APCO has discretion to grant additional exemptions for "sources of minor
  significance," making it worth calling the district for borderline equipment.
- Welding/brazing/soldering (e)(2) is categorically exempt — relevant for fab shops.
- Small residential dwellings (≤4 families) are fully exempt (c).
