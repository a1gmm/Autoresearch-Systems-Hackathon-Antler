## Purpose / Applicability

Rule 201 is the foundational permit trigger for the South Coast AQMD. It requires written authorization (Authority to Construct / Permit to Construct) from the Executive Officer before any construction activity begins. The rule applies to all equipment whose operation may cause the issuance of air contaminants OR may eliminate, reduce, or control such issuance.

Adopted January 9, 1976; last amended December 3, 2004.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

**Operative text (verbatim from PDF):**

> "A person shall not build, erect, install, alter or replace any equipment or agricultural permit unit, the use of which may cause the issuance of air contaminants or the use of which may eliminate, reduce or control the issuance of air contaminants without first obtaining written authorization for such construction from the Executive Officer."

> "A permit to construct shall remain in effect until the permit to operate the equipment or agricultural permit unit for which the application was filed is granted or denied, or the application is canceled."

**Key points:**
- Trigger is extremely broad: any equipment that *may* cause OR *may control* air contaminant emissions requires a written permit to construct.
- Applies to agricultural permit units as well as industrial equipment.
- No minimum threshold or size exemption stated in Rule 201 itself — equipment that qualifies for exemption must meet separate criteria under Rule 219.
- The Permit to Construct remains valid until a Permit to Operate is granted, denied, or the application is canceled.

## Determination Notes

- **Step 1 — Does the equipment emit or control air contaminants?** If yes (or even potentially yes), Rule 201 is triggered.
- **Step 2 — Check Rule 219.** Rule 219 lists specific equipment categories, sizes, and usage thresholds that are exempt from the written permit requirement. If the equipment meets a Rule 219 exemption, no Permit to Construct is needed (though Rule 222 filing may still be required for some exempt categories).
- **Step 3 — If not exempt, file for Permit to Construct** with the South Coast AQMD before any construction or installation begins.
- Altering or replacing existing permitted equipment also triggers Rule 201; it is not limited to new installations.
- Permit to Operate under Rule 203 is a separate subsequent requirement after construction is complete.
