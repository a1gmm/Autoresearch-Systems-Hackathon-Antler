## Purpose / Applicability

Rule 1171 is a Regulation XI source-specific rule governing the VOC content of solvent cleaning materials used in operations across South Coast AQMD. It sets quantitative limits rather than a permit threshold — compliance is required regardless of permit status.

**Verbatim purpose:**
> "The purpose of this rule is to reduce emissions of Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs), toxic air contaminants, and stratospheric ozone-depleting compounds, and global-warming compounds from the use, storage and disposal of Solvent Cleaning Materials in Solvent Cleaning Operations or Solvent Cleaning Activities."

Last amended: June 6, 2025.

**Verbatim applicability:**
> "This rule is applicable to any Person who uses Solvent Cleaning Materials in Solvent Cleaning Operations or Solvent Cleaning Activities during the production, repair, maintenance, or servicing of parts, products, tools, machinery, equipment, or general work areas as part of a business or public service within the South Coast AQMD. The rule shall also apply to all Persons who store and dispose of Solvent Cleaning Materials used in Solvent Cleaning Activities; and all Solvent Cleaner Suppliers who supply, sell, or offer for sale Solvent Cleaning Materials for use in Solvent Cleaning Operations or Solvent Cleaning Activities within the South Coast AQMD."

### (d)(2) Cleaning Devices and Methods Requirements

**Verbatim:** "A Person shall not perform Solvent Cleaning Activities listed in paragraph (d)(1) unless one of the following cleaning devices or methods is used:
(A) Wipe cleaning, which is a Solvent Cleaning Activity conducted by physically rubbing it with a material such as a rag, paper, sponge or a cotton swab moistened with a solvent;
(B) Closed containers or handheld spray bottles from which Solvent Cleaners are applied without a propellant-induced force;
(C) Cleaning equipment that has a Solvent Cleaner container that can be closed, and is closed during Solvent Cleaning Activities, except when depositing and removing objects to be cleaned, and is closed during non-operation...;
(D) Remote Reservoir Cleaner...;
(E) Non-atomized Solvent Flow method where the Solvent Cleaner is collected in a container or a collection system that is closed...;
(F) Solvent Flushing where the Solvent Cleaner is discharged into a container that is closed..."

### (j) Exemptions

**Verbatim — activities entirely exempt from this rule:**
> "(j)(2) The following Solvent Cleaning Activities are not subject to any provision of this rule:
> (A) Cleaning operations subject to Rule 1122 – Solvent Degreasers, or Rule 1425 – Film Cleaning and Printing Operations;
> (B) Cleaning operations subject to Rule 1102 – Petroleum Solvent Dry Cleaners, or Rule 1421 – Control of Perchloroethylene Emissions from Dry Cleaning Operations;
> (C) Cleaning operations subject to Rule 1164 – Semiconductor Manufacturing;
> (D) Cleaning operations subject to Rule 1124 – Aerospace Assembly and Component Manufacturing Operations, except coating application equipment cleaning and storage and disposal of VOC-containing materials used in Solvent Cleaning Activities;
> (E) Cleaning operations subject to Rule 1141.1 – Coatings and Ink Manufacturing;
> (F) Janitorial cleaning, including graffiti removal;
> (G) Stripping of cured coatings, cured ink, or cured adhesives;
> (H) Cleaning operations in Printing pre-press or Graphic Arts pre-press areas, including the cleaning of film processors, color scanners, plate processors, film cleaning, and plate cleaning;"

**Verbatim — VOC limits do NOT apply to:**
> "(j)(3)(A) Cleaning of solar cells, laser hardware, scientific instruments, and high-precision optics;
> (B) Cleaning conducted with performance laboratory tests on coatings, adhesives, or inks; research and development programs; and laboratory tests in quality assurance laboratories;
> (F) Medical device and pharmaceutical Facilities using up to **1.5 gallons per day** of solvents;"

**Recordkeeping exemption:**
> "(j)(1) The recordkeeping provisions in subdivision (g), shall not apply to Solvent Cleaning Activities using Solvent Cleaning Materials containing no more than **25 grams of VOC per liter** of material..."

## Determination Notes

- Rule 1171 is a **product-content rule**, not a permit threshold rule. It applies even to facilities whose equipment is exempt from a written permit under Rule 219.
- The key determination is: which Table 1 category does the solvent cleaning activity fall into? General manufacturing/surface prep has the tightest limit (25 g/L). Some printing and electronic categories are much higher.
- **Graphic arts / printing** operations should be mapped to (D)(i)–(vii) by printing technology type (flexographic, gravure, lithographic, screen, energy curable).
- If a facility's cleaning operations are governed by Rule 1122 (Solvent Degreasers) or another specific Regulation XI rule, Rule 1171 does not apply — check for cross-references first.
- Recordkeeping is required under Rule 109 for all activities subject to this rule (except facilities using only materials at ≤25 g/L VOC).
- Alternative compliance paths exist under (e)(2)–(e)(5) using PW-MIR (photochemical weighted maximum incremental reactivity) limits — relevant for facilities that want to use higher-VOC solvents in limited volumes.
