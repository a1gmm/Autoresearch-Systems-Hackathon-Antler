## One-Line Decision Guide

**Does this equipment need an APCD permit?**
- Uncontrolled emissions < 2 lb/day AND not NSPS/NESHAP/TAC → no permit (Rule 201.A.1)
- Engine < 50 bhp OR external combustion < 2 MMBtu/hr on natural gas/LPG → no permit (Rule 201.B)
- Architectural coating application (commercial or residential) → no permit for equipment (Rule 201.J.1); Rule 433 still governs VOC content of the coating product
- Metal parts coating ≤ 4 gallons/day → no permit and exempt from VOC limits, but records required (Rule 411.C.2.b)
- Anything else that may cause or control air contaminant emissions → ATC required before construction, PTO before operation (Rule 202.A.1–A.2)

## Permit Workflow

1. Pre-application: Contact Engineering Division at (805) 781-5912 or permits@slocleanair.org to screen for exemption
2. Submit ATC application with equipment specs and emissions calculations
3. District evaluates per Rule 204 requirements
4. ATC issued → installation → written notification to District
5. ATC converts to temporary PTO (Rule 202.A.5)
6. Startup inspection → final PTO issued

ATCs expire 1 year from issuance if unused. PTOs are non-transferable across location, equipment, or person.
