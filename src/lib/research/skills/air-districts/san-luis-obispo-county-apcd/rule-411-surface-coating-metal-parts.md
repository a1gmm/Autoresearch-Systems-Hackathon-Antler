## Purpose

Rule 411 controls VOC emissions from surface coating operations applied to metal parts and products. It establishes VOC content limits by coating category, application method requirements, and solvent disposal/cleanup rules.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Applicability (Section A)

> "This Rule is applicable to any person who applies or specifies the use of surface coatings to metal parts and products."

### Exemptions (Section C)

> "The provisions of this Rule shall not apply to:
> a. Aircraft or aerospace vehicle coating operations.
> b. Marine vessel exteriors (below water-line).
> c. Automobile refinishing.
> d. Architectural surface coating, as defined in District Rule 105." (C.1)

> "The provisions of Subsections D.1 and D.2 of this Rule shall not apply to:
> a. Any coating used in volumes of **less than 20 gallons in any calendar year**, provided that the source demonstrates that no complying coatings are available. Written approval must be obtained from the District.
> b. Stationary sources using **not more than four (4) gallons of paint, varnish, lacquer, thinner, and other solvent containing materials in any one day** based on a monthly operating day average, provided the recordkeeping requirements in Subsections E.1 and E.2 of this Rule are satisfied.
> c. Stationary sources electing to utilize control equipment demonstrated to the satisfaction of the APCO to result in the same or greater emission reduction as would compliance with this rule." (C.2)

> "The provisions of Subsection D.2 shall not apply to:
> a. Any coating operation where it can be demonstrated to the satisfaction of the APCO that a transfer efficiency of **65% cannot be achieved**. In such case, the most efficient transfer method shall be used.
> b. Metallic coatings which contain more than **30 grams of metal particles per liter** of coating as applied." (C.3)

> "Any emission unit subject to the requirements of this Rule shall be exempt from the requirements of Rule 407, Organic Material Emission Standards." (C.4)

### VOC Content Limits – Section D.1

> "A person shall not use, apply, or specify any coating for use on any metal part or product subject to the provisions of this Regulation which contains volatile organic compounds in excess of the following limits, as applied:"

VOC content formula (excluding water and exempt compounds):
> VOC (g/L) = (Ws − Ww − Wec) / (Vm − Vw − Vec)
> Where: Ws = weight of volatile compounds; Ww = weight of water; Wec = weight of exempt compounds; Vm = volume of coating; Vw = volume of water; Vec = volume of exempt compounds.

### Application Method Requirements (Section D.2)

> "Except as otherwise provided in Section C, a person shall not use or operate any coating application equipment subject to the provisions of this Rule unless the coating is applied by use of one of the following (properly operating) methods:
> a. electrostatic application, or
> b. flow coat, or
> c. dip coat, or
> d. high-volume low-pressure, or
> e. such other coating application methods as are demonstrated to be capable of achieving at least **65 percent transfer efficiency** and for which written approval of the APCO has been obtained."

### Solvent Disposal and Cleanup Requirements (Section D.3)

> "Surface Preparation, Clean-up or Coating Removal: Closed containers approved by the local fire agency shall be used for the storage or disposal of solvent-containing cloth or paper used for surface preparation, clean-up or coating removal. Containers shall be nonabsorbent." (D.3.a)

> "All VOC containing materials, including but not limited to fresh or spent solvent, coatings, and reducers, shall be kept in **closed containers when not in use**." (D.3.b)

> "No person shall dispose of a total of **more than one (1) quart per day** of any organic solvent by means which will permit the evaporation of such solvent into the atmosphere." (D.3.d)

### Recordkeeping (Section E)

Monthly records required for: amount and type of coating per piece of application equipment; VOC content and volume of each coating as applied; solvent and exempt compound amounts; VOC content of each solvent; application method; amount of solvent disposed of or recycled.

Records retained for minimum 2 years (5 years for Title V sources).

### Test Methods (Section F)

Coating solvent content: EPA Reference Method 24. Transfer efficiency: EPA document EPA/600/2-88-26b methodology with prior written APCO approval.

## Determination Notes

- **Primary exemption route:** Sources using ≤4 gallons/day (monthly average) of coating/thinner combined are exempt from the VOC limits and application method requirements but must keep records (C.2.b).
- **Small-volume exemption:** Sources using <20 gallons/year total are also exempt if no complying coating is available and District written approval is obtained (C.2.a).
- **Architectural coatings excluded:** Rule 411 does not apply to architectural surface coatings — those are governed separately (Rule 433).
- **Auto refinishing excluded:** Automobile refinishing operations are not covered — see Rule 423.
- **Aggregation:** All equipment in a single stationary source is aggregated to determine the daily usage threshold.
