## Purpose

Rule 201 defines the categories of equipment and processes that are exempt from the permit requirements of Regulation II (Rules 202–210). It is the primary exemption screen for Authority to Construct (ATC) and Permit to Operate (PTO) determinations in San Luis Obispo County APCD.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### General Permit Exemption Threshold (Section A.1)

> "A permit shall not be required for any process, article, machine, equipment, or other contrivance if it is listed in Sections B - N or if **uncontrolled emissions never exceed two pounds per day** unless:
>
> a. The process, article, machine, equipment, or other contrivance is subject to New Source Performance Standards (NSPS) or National Emission Standards for Hazardous Air Pollutants (NESHAPS), or District Rule 423 subsection D.4, or
>
> b. The process, article, machine, equipment, or other contrivance emits, in quantities determined to be appropriate for review by the APCO, substances identified as toxic air contaminants or which are under review as candidate toxic air contaminants by the California Air Resources Board pursuant to Health and Safety Code Section 39662, or
>
> c. The APCO makes a determination that a permit shall be required because the equipment may not operate in compliance with all District rules and regulations.
>
> d. A Federal Title V Permit shall always be required for any source that is subject to District Rule 216, Federal Part 70 Permits, including agricultural sources as allowed for in the California Health and Safety Code."

> "An otherwise exempt piece of equipment requires a permit if it is part of a process that requires a permit." (Section A.2)

> "The APCO may require recordkeeping to verify or maintain any exemption." (Section A.3)

### Section B – Combustion and Heat Transfer Equipment

> "Piston type internal combustion engines with a manufacturer's maximum continuous rating of **less than 50.0 brake horsepower (bhp)** or gas turbine engines with a maximum heat input rate of **3,000,000 British thermal units (Btu) per hour or less** at ISO Standard Day Conditions. The ratings of all engines or turbines used in the same process will be accumulated to determine whether this exemption applies." (B.1)

> "Any external combustion equipment that has a maximum heat input rate of **less than 2,000,000 Btu per hour (gross)** and is equipped to be fired exclusively with purchased quality natural gas, liquefied petroleum gas or any combination thereof. The ratings of all combustion equipment used in the same process will be accumulated to determine whether this exemption applies." (B.2)

> "Internal combustion engines, except diesel fueled engines, used solely as a source of standby power when normal power line service fails or are only used for the emergency pumping of water. ... No engine shall operate more than **100 hours per year** for maintenance and testing purposes under this exemption." (B.3)

### Section C – Vehicles

> "Motor vehicles as defined by the Vehicle Code of the State of California but not including any article, machine, equipment, or other contrivance mounted on such vehicle, that would otherwise require a permit under the provisions of these rules and regulations." (C.1)

> "Locomotives, airplanes, and watercraft used to transport passengers or freight. This exemption is not intended to apply to equipment used for the dredging of waterways or to equipment used in pile driving adjacent to or in waterways." (C.2)

### Section D – Residential Structures

> "Any structure designed for and used exclusively as a dwelling for **not more than four families**." (D.1)

### Section E – Cooling Towers

> "Water cooling towers that have a circulation rate of **less than 10,000 gallons/minute** and which are not used for cooling of process water, water from barometric jets or water from barometric condensers." (E.1)

### Section F – Printing and Reproduction Equipment

> "Printing and related coating or laminating equipment not using more than **two gallons of graphic arts material per day**. This exemption shall not include equipment associated with wood flat stock coating operations." (F.1)

> "Photographic process equipment by which an image is reproduced upon material sensitized to radiant energy and control equipment venting exclusively such equipment." (F.2)

### Section G – Food Processing

> "Equipment, excluding boilers, used in eating establishments or other retail establishments for the purpose of preparing food for human consumption." (G.1)

> "Ovens at bakeries whose total production is **less than 1,000 pounds of product per operating day**." (G.2)

### Section H – Plastic/Rubber Processing

> "The following uncontrolled equipment or process using material containing VOCs, when emissions of VOCs from the equipment or process **do not exceed five pounds in any one day**: a. Foam manufacturing or application; b. Fiberglass reinforced plastic fabrication; c. Plastics manufacturing" (H.1)

### Section I – Storage and Transfer Equipment

> "Unheated organic liquid containers with capacities of **not more than 250 gallons**." (I.1)

> "Equipment used exclusively for the unheated underground storage of **6,077 gallons or less** of organic liquids with a vapor pressure of **1.5 psi absolute or less** at actual storage conditions." (I.4)

> "Equipment used exclusively for the storage and transfer of gasoline having a storage capacity of **less than 1,500 gallons**." (I.9)

### Section J – Surface Coating and Cleaning Operations

> "Application equipment for architectural surface coatings used for commercial or residential applications." (J.1)

> "Surface coating equipment which is not subject to District Rule 423 subsection D.4 and which uses a **combined total of one gallon per day or less** of coating material and solvent." (J.3)

Unheated non-conveyorized cleaning equipment is also exempt when: open surface area is 10.8 sq. ft. or less with internal volume of 92.5 gallons or less; using only organic solvents with initial boiling point of 302°F or greater; and less than 25 gallons of solvent per year are lost to atmosphere. (J.2)

### Section K – Agricultural Operations

> "Any equipment used in agricultural operations in the growing of crops or the raising of fowl or animals where the **total emissions from stationary emission units, and portable engines not used to propel implements of husbandry, on contiguous property under common ownership or control is less than 50 tons per year** of any air contaminant, excluding fugitive dust." (K.1)

### Section L – Structure and Equipment

> "Repairs or maintenance not involving structural changes to any equipment for which a permit has been granted." (L.1)

> "Identical replacement in whole or in part of any article, machine, equipment or other contrivance, where a Permit to Operate had previously been granted for such equipment under Rule 202, Permits." (L.3)

### Section M – General Utility Equipment

> "Comfort air conditioning with **fifty (50) pounds or less of working fluid** or ventilating systems, which are not designed or used to remove air contaminants generated by or released from specific units of equipment." (M.1)

> "Refrigeration units with **fifty (50) pounds or less of working fluid**, except those used as or in conjunction with air pollution control equipment." (M.2)

### Section N – Miscellaneous

> "Brazing, soldering or welding equipment and control equipment venting exclusively such equipment." (N.2)

### Section O – Portable Emissions Units

> "A portable emissions unit for which a written permit is otherwise required, shall be exempt from the permitting requirements provided that all of the following conditions are met: 1. The emissions unit has a valid registration obtained in accordance with the provisions of Section 2450 (et seq.) of Title 13 of the California Code of Regulations; and 2. The portable emissions unit is not subject to Rule 216, Federal Part 70 Permits." (O)

## Determination Notes

- **Primary screen:** If uncontrolled emissions < 2 lb/day AND not NSPS/NESHAP/TAC-listed, no permit is required. Apply categorical exemptions (Sections B–N) as secondary check.
- **Aggregation:** Engine/boiler ratings accumulate across a single process — a 30 bhp + 30 bhp engine setup is 60 bhp aggregate and loses the <50 bhp exemption.
- **Coatings:** Architectural surface coatings are categorically exempt (J.1); industrial/commercial coatings are exempt only if ≤1 gallon/day combined (J.3).
- **Graphics/printing:** Exempt at ≤2 gallons graphic arts material per day (F.1).
- **Loss of exemption:** Within 90 days of written APCO notification, formerly exempt equipment that loses its exemption must apply for a Permit to Operate (per Rule 202.A.8).
- **Title V sources:** Always require a federal permit under Rule 216 regardless of local exemptions.

Rule adopted 8/2/76; last revised March 23, 2005.
