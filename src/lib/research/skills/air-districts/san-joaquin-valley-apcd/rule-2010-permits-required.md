## Purpose / Applicability

Rule 2010 (Adopted May 21, 1992, Amended December 17, 1992) requires any person constructing, altering, replacing or operating any source operation which emits, may emit, or may reduce emissions to obtain an Authority to Construct (ATC) or a Permit to Operate (PTO).

Applicability per Section 2.0: "The provisions of this rule shall apply to any person who plans to or does operate, construct, alter, or replace any source operation which may emit air contaminants or may reduce the emission of air contaminants."

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

**Authority to Construct (Section 3.0):**
> "Any person building, altering or replacing any operation, article, machine, equipment, or other contrivance, the use of which may cause the issuance of air contaminants or the use of which may eliminate or reduce or control the issuance of air contaminants, shall first obtain authorization for such construction from the APCO."

An ATC remains in effect until the PTO is granted or denied, or the application is canceled per Rule 2050.

**Permit to Operate (Section 4.0):**
> "Before any new or modified source operation described in Section 3.0, or any existing source operation so described may be operated, a written permit shall be obtained from the APCO."

**New Equipment (Section 4.1):**
> "A person shall notify the APCO before operating or using any source operation granted an Authority to Construct. Upon such notification, the Authority to Construct shall serve as a temporary Permit to Operate for the source operation until the Permit to Operate is granted or denied."

**Modified Equipment (Section 4.2):**
> "The Authority to Construct granted to modify any source operation having a valid Permit to Operate shall serve as a temporary Permit to Operate for the source operation until a new Permit to Operate is granted or denied."

**Existing Equipment (Section 4.3):**
> "When an application for a Permit to Operate is filed for any existing source operation, the application shall serve as a temporary Permit to Operate for the source operation."

**Posting Requirement (Section 5.0):**
The PTO must be affixed to the source operation in a clearly visible manner, or if impractical, mounted within 25 feet of the source operation.

**Prohibition (Section 6.0):**
> "A person shall not willfully deface, alter, forge, counterfeit, or falsify a Permit to Operate any source."

## Determination Notes

- **ATC required before construction/modification**: Any new equipment or modification to an existing permitted source requires an Authority to Construct from the APCO before work begins.
- **PTO required before operation**: A Permit to Operate must be in hand before any new or modified source goes online (ATC serves as interim PTO after notifying APCO).
- **Scope**: Covers all source operations that "may emit" — the threshold is potential to emit, not actual emissions. This is a broad catch-all; exemptions are governed by Rule 2020.
- **Alterations trigger new permitting**: Any alteration to an existing source requires a new ATC, which then serves as a temporary PTO until the updated PTO is issued.
- **See Rule 2020** for the list of source categories exempt from the ATC/PTO requirement.
- **See Rule 2070** for the standards the APCO applies when granting applications.
