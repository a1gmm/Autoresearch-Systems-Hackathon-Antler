## Purpose / Applicability

Rule 2020 (last amended December 18, 2014) specifies emissions units that are not required to obtain an Authority to Construct or Permit to Operate. It applies to any source that emits or may emit air contaminants. It also specifies recordkeeping requirements to verify the exemption and outlines the compliance schedule for emissions units that lose the exemption.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Conditions That Override Any Exemption (Section 5.0)

Even if a unit falls within a District Exempt Source Category (Sections 6.0 or 7.0), the exemption does NOT apply if:

> "5.1 The source is a NSPS source;
> 5.2 The source is a HAP source;
> 5.3 The APCO makes a determination that a permit shall be required because the source may not operate in compliance with all District rules and regulations; or
> 5.4 The owner specifically requests a Permit to Operate."

### Key Definitions

**Low Emitting Unit (Section 3.10):**
> "An emissions unit with an uncontrolled emissions rate of each air contaminant,
> 3.10.1 Less than or equal to two pounds per day, or
> 3.10.2 If greater than two pounds per day, is less than or equal to 75 pounds per year."

**HAP Source (Section 3.6):** An emissions unit subject to NESHAP/MACT standards, California ATCMs, or District Regulation VII (Toxic Air Pollutants) requirements — excluding piston ICEs ≤50 bhp.

**NSPS Source (Section 3.12):** An emissions unit subject to 40 CFR Part 60 requirements — excluding piston ICEs ≤50 bhp.

### District Exempt Source Categories (Section 6.0 — selected key categories)

**6.1 Combustion and Heat Transfer Systems:**
> "6.1.1 Steam generators, steam superheaters, water boilers, water heaters, steam cleaners, and closed indirect heat transfer systems that have a maximum input heat rating of 5,000,000 Btu per hour (gross) or less and is equipped to be fired exclusively with: [natural gas or LPG meeting specified sulfur/composition limits]"

> "6.1.2 Piston type internal combustion engines with a manufacturer's maximum continuous rating of 50 braking horsepower (bhp) or less."

> "6.1.3 Gas turbine engines with a maximum heat input rating of 3,000,000 Btu per hour or less at ISO Standard Day Conditions."

> "6.1.4 Equipment used exclusively for space heating, other than boilers."

**6.2 Cooling Towers:**
> "Water cooling towers that have a circulation rate of less than 10,000 gallons per minute, and that are not used for cooling of process water, water from barometric jets, or water from barometric condensers."

**6.3 Graphic Arts Equipment:**
> "Printing, coating, or laminating facility with a total graphic arts material usage of:
> 6.3.1 Less than or equal to two gallons per any day, or
> 6.3.2 If greater than two gallons per day, is less than or equal to 30 gallons per year.
> 6.3.3 Graphic arts materials are any ink, coating, adhesive, fountain solution, thinner, retarder, or cleaning solution."

**6.8 Surface Coating Operations:**
> "6.8.1 Application equipment for architectural surface coatings used for commercial or residential applications. [Excludes coating application equipment used in manufacturing of architectural components coated before installation.]"

> "6.8.2 Surface coating operations, except for powder coating operations, which use less than one quart of coating per day or less than eight gallons of coating per year."

> "6.8.3 Powder coating operations that use less than five pounds of coating material per day or less than fifty pounds of coating material per year."

**6.9 Solvent Cleaning Operations:**
> "Unheated, nonconveyorized cleaning equipment (not including the control enclosures):
> 6.9.1 With an open surface area of 10.0 square feet or less, and internal volume of 92.5 gallons or less;
> 6.9.2 Using only organic solvents with an initial boiling point of 248ºF or greater as determined by ASTM 1078; and
> 6.9.3 From which less than 25 gallons of solvent per year are lost to the atmosphere from all such equipment at the stationary source."

Note: Section 6.9.3 requires monthly records to substantiate exempt status.

**6.10:** Brazing, soldering, or welding equipment — exempt.

**6.19 Low Emitting Units:**
> "Low Emitting Units, except those which belong to a source category listed in Sections 6.1 through 6.18 shall not require an Authority to Construct or Permit to Operate."

Exception (Section 6.19.2): Low Emitting Units with uncontrolled HAP emissions that may cause a significant health risk still require ATC/PTO.

**6.20 Agricultural Sources:**
> "6.20.1 Agricultural sources at a stationary source that, in aggregate, produce actual emissions less than one-half of the major source thresholds."

Does not apply to sources required to obtain Title V permits (Rule 2520).

### District Exempt Activities (Section 7.0 — selected)

> "7.1 Routine replacement of a whole or partial emissions unit where the replacement part is the same as the original emissions unit in all respects except for the serial number and the action does not create a reconstructed Stationary Source."

> "7.3 Repairs or maintenance not involving structural changes to any emissions unit for which a permit has been granted (California Health and Safety Code, Section 42310(a)(5)(A))."

### Recordkeeping (Section 8.1)
> "Recordkeeping shall be required to verify or maintain any exemption for which the exemption is based on a throughput or emissions limitation. Such records shall be retained for at least two years and provided to the APCO upon request."

## Determination Notes

- **Stepwise logic**: First check Section 5.0 override conditions (NSPS source? HAP source?). If either applies, no exemption is available regardless of the category.
- **Low Emitting Unit as catch-all**: If equipment does not fit a named category in §6.1–6.18 but has uncontrolled emissions ≤2 lb/day or ≤75 lb/yr for every pollutant, it qualifies as a Low Emitting Unit (§6.19) and is exempt — unless it is a HAP source with significant health risk.
- **Graphic arts small-quantity exemption**: ≤2 gallons/day or ≤30 gallons/year of ALL graphic arts materials (inks, coatings, adhesives, solvents combined) is the threshold. Exceeding either daily or annual limit requires a permit.
- **Surface coating micro-exemption**: <1 quart/day or <8 gallons/year for non-powder coatings. <5 lb/day or <50 lb/year for powder coatings.
- **Solvent cleaning narrow exemption**: All three conditions (size, boiling point, annual loss <25 gallons) must be met simultaneously; monthly records required.
- **Portable equipment**: Exempt if registered under Rule 2280 or CARB's Statewide Portable Equipment Registration Program and not incorporated into a fixed stationary source.
