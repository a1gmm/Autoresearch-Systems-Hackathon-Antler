# Calaveras County APCD — Skill Index

## District Overview

Calaveras County APCD is a small mountain-foothill district (Sierra Nevada foothills, east of Stockton). It has a compact local rulebook. There is no district-specific online permit portal — applications are submitted directly to the APCO by paper or mail. The district relies on CARB statewide regulations for many source categories (coatings, consumer products, toxic air contaminants).

## One-Line Decision Guide

**Does this equipment need a permit?**
1. Is the equipment on the Rule 202 exempt list? → **No permit needed** (key exemptions: nat-gas/LPG boiler <250 MMBtu/hr, piston ICE engines, welding/brazing, residential ≤4 family, comfort A/C, ag implements, identical replacements, maintenance without structural change).
2. Not exempt → **ATC required before construction; PTO required before operation** (Rule 201).
3. New or modified major source (≥100 tpy for most pollutants) in ozone nonattainment area → **Rule 428 NSR also applies** (LAER + 1.1:1 offsets for NOx/VOC).

## Notes on Missing Rules

- **No local coating/solvent VOC rule.** The district has no architectural coatings, auto refinishing, or graphic arts rule in its local rulebook. Apply CARB Consumer Products Regulation and CARB Architectural Coatings Regulation statewide standards.
- **No BACT clearinghouse rule.** For permitted equipment below major-source thresholds, Rule 208 requires the applicant to show compliance with H&S Code §§24242–24243 and district rules — the APCO applies case-by-case best controls.
- **Open burning** is governed by Regulation III (Rules 301–323); agricultural burning requires a permit from a designated agency on permissive burn days.
- **Abrasive blasting** governed by reference to Title 17, Subchapter 6 of the California Administrative Code (Rule 215).
