## Purpose

Establishes the requirement to obtain written Authority to Construct (ATC) before building, erecting, altering, or replacing any emission-producing equipment, and a Permit to Operate (PTO) before operating such equipment. This is the primary permitting gate for industrial and commercial sources within Shasta County AQMD.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Authority to Construct (Section a)

Verbatim from PDF (p. compiled rules, Rule 2:1A adopted resolution):

> "Any person building, erecting, altering or replacing any article, machine, equipment or other contrivance, or multi-component system including same, portable or stationary, not exempt under Section 42310, the use of which may cause the issuance of air contaminants or the use of which may eliminate or reduce or control the issuance of air contaminants, shall first obtain written authority for such construction from the Control Officer."

### Permit to Operate (Section b)

> "(1) Before any article, machine, equipment or other contrivance or multi-component system including same, portable or stationary, not exempt under Section 42310, the use of which may cause the issuance of air contaminants, may be operated or used, a written permit shall be obtained from the Control Officer."

> "(2) Where an application for or issuance of a permit is pending or in the event of an emergency occurring as a result of an excusable malfunction of a device under permit, the Control Officer may authorize the operation of the article, machine, equipment, device, or other contrivance or multicomponent system for which a permit is sought for periods of time not to exceed sixty (60) days each for the purpose of testing, experimentation, or obtaining necessary data for a permit or correcting a malfunction. No fee or application will be required for such authorization."

> "(3) No permit to operate or use shall be granted by the Control Officer for such article, machine, equipment or contrivance if it was constructed or installed without an authority to construct until the information required is presented to the Control Officer and such article, machine, equipment or contrivance is altered, if necessary, and made to conform to the standards set forth in these Rules and Regulations."

### Permit to Operate Issuance — Rule 2:1, Part 500, Section 514

> "The Air Pollution Control Officer shall issue a permit to operate a stationary source subject to the requirements of this Rule if it is determined that any offsets required as a condition of an Authority to Construct or amendment to a Permit to Operate will commence not later than the initial operation of the new or modified source, and that the offsets shall be maintained throughout the operation of the new or modified source which is the beneficiary of the offsets. Further, the Air Pollution Control Officer shall determine that all conditions specified in the Authority to Construct have been or will be complied with by any dates specified."

> "Where a new or modified stationary source is, in whole or part, a replacement for an existing stationary source on the same property, the Air Pollution Control Officer may allow a maximum of 90 days as a start-up period for simultaneous operation of the existing stationary source and the new source or replacement."

### Transfer of Permits — Rule 2:18

> "An authority to construct, [or permit to operate] ... [is transferable subject to Control Officer approval]."

### Denial — Rule 2:12 / 2:13

> "Acting upon a Permit to Operate, if the Control Officer finds that [equipment was] constructed not in accordance with the Authority to Construct, he shall deny the Permit to Operate."

## Determination Notes

- Trigger: ANY equipment that "may cause the issuance of air contaminants" requires ATC before installation — threshold is possibility of emissions, not a quantity cutoff.
- Exemptions flow through Rule 2:5 (see separate file) and California Health & Safety Code Section 42310.
- The 60-day emergency/testing authorization (Section b.2) requires no fee or application — useful for equipment malfunctions.
- "Multi-component system" is treated as a unit — starting/operating/shutting down all components simultaneously constitutes a single source.
- A PTO cannot be issued retroactively for equipment built without ATC until equipment is brought into conformance.
- Portable equipment is explicitly covered — same permit requirements apply.
- Fee schedule governed by Rule 2:11.
