# Mendocino County AQMD — Air District Skill

## Decision Guide (one-line)
Permit required if source emits air contaminants AND does not fall under Rule 1-200(d) exemptions (vehicles, ≤4-unit dwellings, non-commercial BBQ, agricultural ops, non-emitting conveyors, gasoline tanks <250 gal, or APCO-approved below-significance sources); NSR/BACT only kicks in if emissions hit significance thresholds (e.g., 220 lbs/day NOx, 80 lbs/day PM10) — which few small sources reach.

## Key Permit Threshold Summary

### Sources Exempt from ATC/PTO (Rule 1-200(d))
- Vehicles (Vehicle Code definition)
- Equipment in ≤4-family dwellings
- Non-commercial barbecues
- Orchard/vineyard/citrus heaters
- Agricultural operations (crops, fowl, animals) per H&SC §42310(e)
- Non-emitting mixing/blending/conveying systems
- Gasoline/organic liquid storage tanks **< 250 gallons**
- Sources below significance level at APCO's discretion

### NSR/BACT Significance Thresholds (Rule 1-130(s2)) — triggers full NSR review
- CO: 550 lbs/day | NOx: 220 lbs/day | SOx: 220 lbs/day
- PM (total): 135 lbs/day | PM-10: 80 lbs/day
- VOC (ozone precursor): 220 lbs/day
- Any single HAP (CAA §112(g)): 10 tons/year
- Two or more HAPs: 25 tons/year combined

### No Local Coating/Solvent/Graphic Arts Rule
Mendocino AQMD has no district-specific VOC coating or solvent rule. Sources such as auto body shops, printing, and industrial coatings are regulated through:
- **Regulation 3.1** (benzene/vapor recovery at gasoline dispensing, >480,000 gal/yr throughput)
- **Regulation 3.7** (PCE dry cleaning)
- **Federal NSPS (Rule 1-490 / 40 CFR Part 60)** and **NESHAP (Rule 1-492 / 40 CFR Part 61/63)** adopted wholesale
- CARB Statewide ATCMs for specific toxic categories
