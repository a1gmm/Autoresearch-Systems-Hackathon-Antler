## Purpose

Rule 1-130 provides definitions for Regulation 1. Sub-definition (s2) defines "Significant" emissions rates — the thresholds above which New Source Review (NSR), BACT analysis, and public notice requirements are triggered under Rule 1-220(b). [Amended 5/6/03]

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Rule 1-130(s2) — Significant Emissions Rates

> "(s2) Significant: The potential of a new or modified source to emit air contaminants that would equal or exceed any of the following rates, calculated on the basis of 24-hour emissions profiles:"

| Air Contaminant | Significant Emissions Rate |
|---|---|
| Carbon monoxide | 550 lbs. per day |
| Nitrogen oxides | 220 lbs. per day |
| Sulfur dioxide | 220 lbs. per day |
| Particulate matter | 135 lbs. per day |
| PM-10 | 80 lbs. per day |
| Ozone (as VOCs*) | 220 lbs. per day |
| Lead | 3 lbs. per day |
| Asbestos | 0.038 lbs. per day |
| Beryllium | 0.002 lbs. per day |
| Mercury | 0.5 lbs. per day |
| Vinyl chloride | 5.4 lbs. per day |
| Fluorides | 16 lbs. per day |
| Sulfuric acid mist | 38 lbs. per day |
| Hydrogen sulfide (H₂S) | 54 lbs. per day |
| Total reduced sulfur (including H₂S) | 54 lbs. per day |
| Any single HAP listed under CAA §112(g) | 10 tons per year |
| Two or more HAPs listed under CAA §112(g) | 25 tons per year combined |

> "* Volatile Organic Compounds except for ethanol sources below the EPA yearly threshold (40 tons per year)."

> "Not withstanding the above significant emissions rates for various air contaminants, significant also means any net emissions increase from any new or modified stationary source that would be constructed within 10 kilometers of a Class I area and have an air quality impact on such area equal to or greater than 1 microgram per cubic meter (24 hour average). (40 CFR 52.21(b)(23)(iii))"

> "The above Significant Emissions Rates are not to be used for CEQA determination."

### Rule 1-130(i2) — Indirect Source Thresholds

> "(i2) Indirect Source: A facility, building, structure or installation, or combination thereof, that indirectly results in, or is projected to result in unmitigated emissions in excess of the following: ROG 180 lbs/day, NOx 42 lbs/day, CO 690 lbs/day, PM10 80 lbs/day. Projected unmitigated emissions are to be generated using the latest ARB approved version of URBEMIS with the Mountain and Rural Counties default settings, or other ARB approved indirect source model. In any model the latest available fleet, meteorology, and trip generation information will be used and the model run for each season. [Amended 5/6/03]"

## Determination Notes

- Significance thresholds are used ONLY to trigger NSR/BACT review under Rule 1-220(b). They are NOT CEQA thresholds.
- Mendocino is an attainment area for all criteria pollutants — no offset requirement for new sources even above significance thresholds.
- For a small commercial or industrial project (auto body, dry cleaner, wood mill, diesel generator), the daily lbs thresholds are very high relative to typical emissions; most small projects will not approach significance.
- HAP thresholds (10 tons/yr single, 25 tons/yr aggregate) align with federal major source thresholds and trigger MACT/NESHAP review under Rule 1-492.
- Class I area adjacency (within 10 km) is a separate trigger — Mendocino County includes wilderness areas; verify proximity for projects near the Mendocino wilderness.
