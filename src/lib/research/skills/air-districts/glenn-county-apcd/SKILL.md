## District Character

Glenn County APCD is a very small rural district in the northern Sacramento Valley. The district's rulebook is compact: it covers agricultural burning (Article II), permit/NSR (Article III), and emission prohibitions (Article IV). There are **no dedicated local coating, solvent, graphic-arts, or VOC rules** — operators subject to those source categories must comply with CARB/state regulations and applicable ATCM rules directly. The APCO also has broad authority to designate sources as "minor significance" exemptions on a case-by-case basis (Section 57.N).

## One-Line Decision Guide

**Does the project need a permit?** → Check Section 57 exemptions first; if not exempt, any equipment that may emit air contaminants requires an ATC before construction and a PTO before operation (Section 50/50.1). If potential to emit reaches 25 tpy NOx or ROC (or other NSR thresholds), BACT and offsets are required under Section 51.
