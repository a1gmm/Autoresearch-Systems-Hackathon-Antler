## Purpose

Establishes the two-tier permit requirement for all stationary sources in Siskiyou County that may emit air contaminants: (1) Authority to Construct (ATC) before building/altering equipment, and (2) Permit to Operate (PTO) before operating. Rule 2.2 (Exemptions) carves out specific categories.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### A. Authority to Construct

> "Except as provided in Rule 2.2, any person building, constructing, erecting, altering, replacing, operating or using any article, machine, equipment or other contrivance, or multi-component system, the use of which may emit air contaminants, or which may eliminate, reduce or control air contaminant emissions, shall first obtain written authorization for such construction from the Control Officer."

### B. Permit to Operate

> "Except as provided in Rule 2.1.F., before any article, machine, equipment or other contrivance, or multi-component system, described in Rule 2.1.A. may be operated or used, a written permit shall be obtained from the Control Officer."

> "No Permit to Operate or Use shall be granted either by the Control Officer or the Hearing Board for any article, machine, equipment or contrivance constructed or installed without authorization as required by Rule 2.1.A, until the information required is presented to the Control Officer and such article, machine, equipment or contrivance is altered, if necessary, and made to conform to the standards set forth in these regulations."

### C. Posting Requirement

> "A person who has been granted a Permit to Operate any article, machine, equipment, or other contrivance shall display such Permit to Operate, or an approved facsimile, in such a manner as to be clearly visible and accessible."

### E. Existing Sources

> "Any existent source of air contaminant emissions, operating on or before January 1, 1987, shall apply for a Permit to Operate within 30 days of written notification by the Control Officer."

### F. Temporary Permit to Operate

> "Before operating or using any new or modified stationary source for which an Authority to Construct has been issued pursuant to the provisions of these regulations, the applicant shall notify the Control Officer in writing. Upon such notification, the Authority to Construct or modify shall serve as a Temporary Permit for Operation of the equipment until the Permit to Operate is granted or denied."

### G. New Source Siting Trigger

> "All new stationary sources and modifications to existing stationary sources, including power plants and cogeneration and resource recovery projects, with a net emissions increase of air contaminants that would exceed the limits stated in Rule 6.1.B. shall be subject to the provisions of Regulation VI."

Rule 6.1.B triggers at: net increase ≥ 250 lb/day of any criteria pollutant (except CO), or ≥ 2,500 lb/day CO.

## Determination Notes

- Every new or modified source that may emit air contaminants requires an ATC + PTO unless squarely within Rule 2.2 exemptions.
- The "significance level" thresholds in Rule 1.2-S4 define the emission rates at which an ATC is mandatory (see rule-1-2-significance-levels.md).
- Title V sources (Rule 2.13) have additional federal operating permit requirements that augment and supersede conflicting provisions.
