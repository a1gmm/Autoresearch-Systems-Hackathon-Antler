## Purpose

Establishes emission prohibitions applicable to all sources, including visible emission limits, nuisance prohibition, non-agricultural burning restrictions, and particulate matter controls. Violations of these rules can be cited independently of permit requirements.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Rule 4.1 – Visible Emissions

> "Except as provided in these regulations, a person shall not discharge into the atmosphere from any single source of emission whatsoever, any air contaminant for a period or periods aggregating more than three minutes in any one hour which is:
> A. As dark or darker in shade as that designated as No.2 on the Ringelmann chart as published by the United States Bureau of Mines.
> B. Of such opacity as to obscure an observer's view to a degree equal to or greater than does smoke described in subsection A. of this rule."

**Exceptions to visible emission limit include (4.1.C):**
- Fires set pursuant to Rule 4.3
- Agricultural burning with a permit (Regulation VII)
- Aircraft distributing agricultural aids
- Orchard/citrus heaters compliant with Rule 4.11
- Agricultural operations for crops/fowl/animals
- Government agency training fires
- Teepee burners operating under PRC Section 4438 (startup/shutdown only, ≤ 30 min/24-hr period)

> "When the presence of uncombined water is the only reason for the failure of an emission to meet the visible emissions limitation (Rule 4.1), that rule shall not apply."

### Rule 4.2 – Nuisance

> "No person shall discharge from any source whatsoever, such quantities of air contaminants or other material which cause injury, detriment, nuisance or annoyance to any considerable number of persons or to the public or which endanger the comfort, repose, health or safety of any such persons or the public or which cause or have a natural tendency to cause injury or damage to business or property."

> "The provision of Rule 4.2.A. relating to odors shall not apply to odors emanating from agricultural operations in the growing of crops or raising of fowl or animals."

### Rule 4.3 – Non-Agricultural Burning

> "Except as otherwise provided in these rules, no person shall use open outdoor fires for the purpose of disposal or burning of petroleum wastes, demolition debris, tires, tar, trees, wood waste, or other combustible or flammable solid or liquid waste; or for metal salvage or burning of motor vehicle bodies. Burning under this sub-division shall be authorized by a burn permit issued by the Siskiyou County Air Pollution Control District."

**Permitted exceptions include:**
- Prevention of fire hazards with no other abatement means
- Public officer instruction of employees
- Backfires necessary to save life/property (PRC Section 4426)
- Disease/pest prevention with immediate need
- Open outdoor fires for cooking food or recreation (paper, cardboard, vegetation, clean wood, commercial charcoal only)
- Right-of-way clearing by public entity on permissive burn days
- Disposal of natural vegetation on permissive burn days with prescribed drying times:
  - Prunings and small branches: 3 weeks
  - Grass clippings: 3 weeks with turning once per week
  - Large branches (6 inches and larger) and trees: 8 weeks
  - Unwanted trees: 3 months; trees over 6 inches diameter must be felled and dried first

## Determination Notes

- Ringelmann No. 2 corresponds to approximately 40% opacity — this is a lenient standard compared to many districts (Bay Area: 20% opacity / Ringelmann No. 1).
- The nuisance rule (4.2) applies to all sources regardless of permit status; it is an independent compliance obligation.
- Non-agricultural burning requires a burn permit from the district; burning is only allowed on permissive burn days.
- Rule 4.3 burn permit is distinct from the Rule 2.1 ATC/PTO; both may apply to stationary combustion equipment.
- Other prohibitions in Regulation IV not extracted here: Rule 4.4 (Specific Air Contaminants), Rule 4.5 (Particulate Matter), Rule 4.6 (Circumvention), Rule 4.7 (Gasoline Storage), Rule 4.12 (NSPS incorporation), Rule 4.13 (NESHAP incorporation), Rule 4.14 (Sulfur in Fuels).
