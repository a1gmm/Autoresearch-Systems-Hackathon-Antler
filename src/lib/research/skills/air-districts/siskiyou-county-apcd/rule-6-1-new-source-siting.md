## Purpose

Establishes New Source Review (NSR) requirements — Best Available Control Technology (BACT) and emissions offsets (mitigation) — for new stationary sources and modifications that result in significant net emissions increases. This is the district's Prevention of Significant Deterioration (PSD)-equivalent rule for criteria pollutants.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### B. Applicability Thresholds

> "Sections B., C., D., E., F., G., H., and I. shall apply to new stationary sources and modifications which result in either:
> a. A net increase in emissions of 250 or more pounds during any day of any pollutant for which there is a national ambient air quality standard (excluding carbon monoxide), or any precursor of such a pollutant; or
> b. A net increase in emissions of 2500 or more pounds of carbon monoxide during any day."

### B.2 Offset Exemptions (BACT still required)

> "New sources and modifications shall be exempt from the requirement for offsets (Section E.), although BACT is still required providing the source:
> a. Will be used exclusively for providing essential public services, such as schools, hospitals, or police and fire fighting facilities, but specifically excluding sources of electrical power generation other than for emergency standby use at essential public service facilities.
> b. Is exclusively a modification to convert from use of a gaseous fuel to a liquid fuel because of a demonstrable shortage of gaseous fuels [with conditions]...
> c. Is portable sandblasting equipment used on a temporary basis within the district.
> d. Is a cogeneration project, a project using refuse derived or biomass-derived fuels for energy generation, or a resource recovery project using municipal wastes, provided the applicant established by modeling that the new source or modification will not cause a violation or exacerbate an existing violation of any national ambient air quality standard..."

### D. BACT Requirement

> "New stationary sources and modifications excluding cargo carriers, shall be constructed using best available control technology."

### E. Mitigation (Offsets)

> "For new stationary sources and modifications mitigation shall be required for net emissions increases (i.e. increases after the application of best available control technology):
> 1. Of each pollutant for which a national ambient air quality standard was exceeded three discontinuous times (for annual standards, one time) within the district within the three years immediately preceding the date when the application for the permit to construct was filed..."

> "Net emissions increases subject to this section shall be mitigated (offset) by reduced emissions from existing stationary or non-stationary sources. Emissions reductions shall be sufficient to offset any net emissions increase and shall take effect at the times, or before initial operation, of the new source, or within 90 days after initial operation of a modification and shall continue as long as the new or modified source is operating."

### E.4 Offset Ratio

> "A ratio of emissions offsets to emissions from the new source or modification (offset ratio) of 1.2:1 shall be required for emissions offsets located within a 15 mile radius of the proposed new source or modification and based upon emissions profiles."

> "For proposed emission offsets in which the offset ratio is from 1:1 to 1.2:1 the applicant shall conduct modeling to verify a net air quality benefit in the area affected by emissions from the new source or modification."

### E.8 Emission Banking

> "The Control Officer may allow emissions reductions which exceed those required by this rule for a new source or modification to be banked for use in the future by the applicant. Such reductions shall be used only to offset emissions increases from proposed new sources or modifications owned or operated by the applicant within 15 miles of the site where the reductions occurred."

### G. Public Notice Process

> "Within 10 calendar days following such decision, publish a notice by prominent advertisement in at least one newspaper of general circulation in the district stating the preliminary decision of the Control Officer and where the public may inspect the required information. The notice shall provide 30 days from the date of publication for the public to submit written comments on the preliminary decision."

## Determination Notes

- **NSR trigger:** 250 lb/day net increase of any criteria pollutant except CO (2,500 lb/day for CO). This is a daily threshold, distinct from the annual significance level thresholds in Rule 1.2-S4.
- **Converting to annual:** 250 lb/day ≈ 45.6 tpy if operating 365 days, but the threshold is a daily rate, not annual — a source can trigger NSR even if annual emissions are below the significance levels.
- **Attainment area:** Siskiyou County is generally in attainment for most criteria pollutants. Offsets are only required where NAAQS violations have been documented (per Rule 6.1.E.1). BACT is required regardless.
- **Offset ratio:** 1.2:1 (within 15 miles); modeling required if ratio is between 1:1 and 1.2:1.
- **No NSPS floor:** Permit conditions may not set emission rates above applicable NSPS standards (40 CFR Part 60).
