# Siskiyou County APCD — Skill Index

## District Overview

Siskiyou County APCD is a small rural district in the northeastern corner of California (Northeast Plateau Air Basin). The district is in attainment for most criteria pollutants. It administers its own permit system under state authority and has adopted a full local rulebook covering permits, prohibitions, agricultural burning, and ATCMs. The district does NOT rely solely on CARB/state rules — it has its own locally adopted Regulations I–VIII.

## One-Line Decision Guide

**Does this equipment need an ATC/PTO?**
1. Is it listed in Rule 2.2 exemptions? → No permit needed (but stay below significance levels and comply with all other rules).
2. Does it emit at or above the Rule 1.2-S4 significance levels (e.g., ROC ≥ 40 tpy, NOx ≥ 40 tpy, PM10 ≥ 15 tpy, CO ≥ 100 tpy)? → ATC required.
3. Does it emit below significance levels? → Control Officer discretionary exemption under Rule 2.2.I may apply; confirm with district.
4. Will it produce a net increase ≥ 250 lb/day of any criteria pollutant (or ≥ 2,500 lb/day CO)? → NSR (Rule 6.1) applies: BACT required, offsets required if pollutant is in violation in the district.
5. Is it a Major Source (≥ 100 tpy any pollutant)? → Title V (Rule 2.13) federal operating permit required in addition to district ATC/PTO.

## Boiler / Combustion Equipment Quick Rule

Boilers, water heaters, and space heaters < **5 MMBtu/hr** firing natural gas, LPG, or distillate oil → exempt under Rule 2.2.F. At or above 5 MMBtu/hr → ATC + PTO required.

## Storage Tank Quick Rule

Gasoline/fuel oil tanks ≤ **20,000 gallons** → exempt under Rule 2.2.G.4 (except bulk plants and retail service stations subject to Rule 8.1).
