## Purpose

Rule 401 (amended 08/01/89) lists the categories of equipment that do NOT require an Authority to Construct or Permit to Operate registration under Rule 400. This is the primary exemption list for Colusa County APCD.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Full Exemptions (Section 1.1)

> "An Authorization to Construct or Permit to Operate registration shall not be required for:"

**Section 1.1.1 – Motor vehicles:**
> "Vehicles as defined by the Vehicle Code of the State of California, but not including any article, machine, equipment or other contrivance mounted on such vehicle that would otherwise require a permit under the provisions of these regulations and rules."

**Section 1.1.2 – Transport vehicles:**
> "Vehicles used to transport passengers or freight."

**Section 1.1.3 – Residential dwellings:**
> "Equipment utilized exclusively in connection with any structure, which is designed for and used exclusively as a dwelling for not more than two (2) families."

**Section 1.1.4 – Specific equipment categories:**

> "1.1.4.1 Comfort air conditioning or comfort ventilating systems which are not designed to remove air contaminants generated by or released from specific units or equipment;"

> "1.1.4.2 Refrigeration units except those used as, or in conjunction with air pollution control equipment;"

> "1.1.4.3 Water-cooling towers and water-cooling ponds not used for evaporative cooling or process water, or not used for evaporative cooling of water from barometric jets or from barometric condensers;"

> "1.1.4.4 Piston-type internal combustion engines used exclusively for agriculture."

> "1.1.4.5 Equipment used exclusively for steam cleaning;"

> "1.1.4.6 Presses used exclusively for extruding metals, minerals, plastics or wood."

> "1.1.4.7 Residential incinerators when used for burning of paper or leaves."

> "1.1.4.8 Brazing, soldering or welding equipment."

**Section 1.1.5:**
> "Space heaters."

**Section 1.1.6 – Food service:**
> "Equipment used in eating establishments for the purpose of preparing food for human consumption."

**Section 1.1.7 – Mobile construction equipment:**
> "Self-propelled mobile construction equipment other than pavements burners."

**Section 1.1.8 – APCO discretion:**
> "Other sources of minor significance specified by the Air Pollution Control Officer."

**Section 1.1.9 – Agricultural implements:**
> "Agricultural implements used in agricultural operations."

**Section 1.1.10 – On-site farming facilities (production cap):**
> "On-site farming facilities using general production equipment, in lieu of compliance with other requirements of the Rules and Regulations pertaining to particulate matter, shall be deemed to be of minor significance if the emissions of particulate matter from the facility does not constitute a nuisance off the property and finished product does not exceed 1500 tons per year."

## Determination Notes

- **Agricultural IC engines** are explicitly exempt (§1.1.4.4) — piston-type ICEs used exclusively for agriculture do not require a permit.
- **Agricultural implements** broadly exempt (§1.1.9).
- **On-site farming** exempt if PM does not constitute an off-property nuisance AND finished product ≤ 1,500 tons/year (§1.1.10).
- **Comfort HVAC** (not process exhaust) is exempt; process ventilation capturing contaminants is NOT exempt.
- **Welding/brazing/soldering** equipment is explicitly exempt (§1.1.4.8).
- **Refrigeration** units are exempt except when used as/with pollution control equipment.
- **APCO discretion:** The APCO may designate additional "sources of minor significance" as exempt (§1.1.8) — worth confirming with the district for borderline equipment.
- Equipment mounted on vehicles that would otherwise require a permit is NOT exempt simply because it is vehicle-mounted.
