# Colusa County APCD — Skill Index

## District Overview

Colusa County APCD is a small California district serving rural Colusa County. The district has a self-contained rulebook (Regulations I–IV and beyond). Permit authority rests with the Air Pollution Control Officer (APCO). The district is not a SCAQMD-level district — it adopts its own rules but many facilities are agricultural. The EPA maintains a compiled SIP document at https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2018-01/documents/colusa_county_air_pollution_control_district_apcd_rules_compilation.pdf.

## One-Line Decision Guide

1. **Does the equipment appear in Rule 401's exemption list?** If yes → no ATC/PTO needed (confirm with APCO for ambiguous cases).
2. **If not exempt** → ATC required before construction (Rule 400 §2.1); PTO required before operation (Rule 400 §2.2).
3. **Does the new/modified source's potential to emit exceed BACT thresholds** (e.g., ROC/NOx > 25 lb/day, PM-10 > 80 lb/day)? → BACT analysis required (Rule 430 §5.1).
4. **Does potential to emit exceed 25 TPY** of any non-attainment pollutant or precursor? → Emission offsets required (Rule 430 §5.2); offset ratio depends on distance (1:1 on-site up to 2:1 over 50 miles).
5. **Applying architectural coatings?** → Check VOC g/L limits in Rule 230 §6.1 table; aerosols and ≤1-liter containers are exempt.
6. **Agricultural engine (piston ICE)?** → Exempt from permit under Rule 401 §1.1.4.4.
7. **Title V major source?** → Additional Title V permit required under Rule 1101, separate from standard PTO.
