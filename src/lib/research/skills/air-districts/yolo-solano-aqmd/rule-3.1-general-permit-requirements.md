## Purpose / Applicability

Rule 3.1 establishes the Yolo-Solano AQMD permit system for stationary sources. It applies to any person who builds, erects, alters, or replaces, or operates any facility, article, machine, equipment, or other contrivance "the use of which may cause the issuance of air contaminants, or the use of which may eliminate or reduce or control the issuance of air contaminants."

Exemptions from permit are governed entirely by Rule 3.2 (EXEMPTIONS). Per Section 110: "An Authority to Construct and Permit to Operate shall not be required for equipment listed in Sections 101 to 113 of Rule 3.2, EXEMPTIONS."

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

**Section 301 — Authority to Construct (ATC):**
> "No person shall build, erect, alter, or replace any facility, article, machine, equipment, or other contrivance, the use of which may cause the issuance of air contaminants, or the use of which may eliminate or reduce or control the issuance of air contaminants, without first obtaining an authorization to construct from the Air Pollution Control Officer as specified in Section 401 of this Rule."

**Section 302 — Permit to Operate (PTO):**
> "No person shall operate any facility, article, machine, equipment, or other contrivance, for which an authorization to construct is required by these Rules and Regulations without first obtaining a written permit from the Air Pollution Control Officer."

**Section 302.2:**
> "No person shall operate any facility, article, machine, equipment, or other contrivance, the use of which may cause the issuance of air contaminants or the use of which may eliminate or reduce or control the issuance of air contaminants, without obtaining a permit from the Air Pollution Control Officer or the Hearing Board."

**Section 303.1 — Standards for Granting Applications:**
> "The Air Pollution Control Officer shall deny authorization to construct or permit to operate... if the applicant does not show that every facility, article, machine, equipment, or other contrivance... is so designated, controlled, equipped, and operated with such air pollution control equipment, that it may be shown to operate without emitting or without causing to be emitted air contaminants in violation of Sections 41700 or 41701, of the Health and Safety Code, of these Rules and Regulations, or any federal statutes or regulations."

**Section 305 — Permit Renewal:**
> "Each permit to operate shall be renewable annually on the permit's anniversary date, commencing one year after the date of issuance."

**Section 401 — Applications:**
> "Requests for an authorization to construct or a permit to operate shall be initiated by filing an application with the Air Pollution Control Officer or his designee together with the filing fee required by these Rules and Regulations."

**Section 404 — Applications Deemed Denied:**
> "The applicant may, at his option, deem the authorization to construct or permit to operate denied if the Air Pollution Control Officer fails to act on the application within thirty (30) days after filing."

**Section 407 — Term of Authority to Construct:**
An ATC must be acted upon; failure to begin construction within a set period may void the authorization. (Rule refers to Section 407 for specific expiration terms.)

**Section 405.3 — Breakdown Reporting:**
> "The owner or operator of any facility... for which a permit to operate is in effect shall notify the District office whenever a breakdown, malfunction, or operational upset condition exists which would tend to increase emissions of air pollutants... no later than four hours after occurrence during regular workday hours."

## Determination Notes

- **Trigger:** Any new or modified equipment at a facility that may emit or control air contaminants requires an ATC before construction and a PTO before operation.
- **Check Rule 3.2 first:** If the equipment category appears in Rule 3.2 Sections 101–114, it may be exempt (subject to override conditions in Rule 3.2 Section 100).
- **NSR / Major source overlay:** If the project triggers New Source Review thresholds, also apply Rule 3.4; if Title V major source, also apply Rule 3.8.
- **Building permit interlock (Section 301.2):** No building permit may be issued for work involving air-contaminant equipment unless the APCO has first issued an ATC.
- **Annual renewal:** All PTOs renew annually; conditions are reviewed at renewal for compliance with current rules.
- **Permit transfer:** Permits are non-transferable between locations or equipment (Section 304); new owner must apply to District.
