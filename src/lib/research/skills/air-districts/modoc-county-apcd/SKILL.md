## One-Line Decision Guide

**ATC/PTO required** for any equipment that may emit air contaminants, except: residential HVAC/heating, welding/brazing/soldering, steam cleaning, sheet-fed printing presses without dryers, batch mixers ≤5 cu ft, space heaters (non-boiler), laundry/dry cleaning equipment, or equipment exclusively serving ≤4-family dwellings.

**PSD review (BACT + offsets analysis)** triggered at net increases ≥ 250 lb/day for criteria pollutants or ≥ 2,500 lb/day CO; significance thresholds in tons/year per Rule 1.2-S3 definition.

**Operational limits for all sources**: Ringelmann No. 2 visible emissions (Rule 4.1); liquid/solid fuel sulfur ≤ 0.5% by weight (Rule 3:10); particulate per process-weight-rate table (Rule 3:12); gasoline storage tanks ≥ 250 gal / ≥ 4 psi RVP require vapor control (Rule 3:16).

## District Context

Modoc County APCD is one of California's smallest air districts — a single-county rural district in the northeastern corner of the state. The district has **no local coating rule, no local solvent rule, and no graphic-arts rule**. Coating and VOC-emitting operations are subject to general visible emissions and particulate limits only, plus any applicable CARB statewide rules. The district is in attainment for most criteria pollutants, so offset requirements are rarely triggered in practice. For BACT determinations, consult the CARB Technology Clearinghouse (DRDB Rule MODAPCD 6-1 at https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/sites/default/files/classic/technology-clearinghouse/rules/RuleID1744.html).
