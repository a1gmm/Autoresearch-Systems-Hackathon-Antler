## Purpose

Rule 400 requires any person constructing, altering, or operating a source that emits or may emit air contaminants to obtain an Authority to Construct (ATC) or Permit to Operate (PTO) from the Air Pollution Control Officer (APCO). Verbatim:

> "The purpose of this Rule is to require any person constructing, altering, or operating a source that emits or may emit air contaminants to obtain an Authority to Construct or Permit to Operate from the Air Pollution Control Officer (APCO) and to provide an orderly procedure for application, review, and authorization of new sources and of the modification and operation of existing sources of air pollution."

Adopted January 4, 1972; last amended April 24, 2014.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Exemptions
> "An Authority to Construct, Permit to Operate, or other written authority shall not be required for emissions units listed in Rule 401-Exemptions of these Rules and Regulations, provided the required records, documentation, and other stipulations of Rule 401 are met." (Section 2)

### Authority to Construct Trigger
> "Any person building, erecting, placing on site, altering or replacing any article, machine, equipment or other contrivance (emissions unit), the use of which may cause, eliminate, reduce, or control the issuance of air contaminants, shall first obtain written authorization for such construction from the APCO as specified in Section 4.4 of this Rule. The emissions unit(s) shall not commence construction until the APCO takes final action to approve the Authority to Construct." (Section 4.1)

### ATC Validity
> "Except as noted in Section 4.1.2 below, an Authority to Construct shall expire one (1) year following the date of permit issuance." (Section 4.1.1)

Up to two (2) one-year extensions may be granted if: commencement of construction has occurred, project parameters remain the same, and construction has not lapsed for more than 18 consecutive months (Section 4.1.2).

### Permit to Operate
> "Any person operating an emissions unit subject to this Rule shall first obtain a permit from the APCO." (Section 4.2)

After issuing notice and commencing operation, the ATC may remain in effect as a Temporary Permit to Operate for up to 12 months while a PTO is pending (Section 4.1.4).

### Public Notice Threshold
> "For the sources applying for a permit that have a potential to emit over 25 tons per year for any AAQS pollutant (except Carbon Monoxide PTE shall be 50 TPY), the APCO shall: Publish within ten (10) calendar days following a preliminary decision to issue an Authority to Construct ... a notice ... inviting written public comment for at least a thirty (30)-day period following the date of publication." (Section 4.5)

### Standards for Granting Applications
> "The APCO shall not grant an Authority to Construct or Permit to Operate unless the APCO determines the source or emissions unit will comply with all applicable requirements and will not interfere with maintenance or attainment of an AAQS." (Section 4.4.1)

### Application Completeness Timeline
> "The APCO shall determine whether the application is complete not later than thirty (30) days after receipt of the application, or after such longer time mutually agreeable to the applicant and the APCO, and notify the applicant in writing." (Section 5.2.1)

### Performance Testing
Performance tests must occur within 60 days of achieving maximum production rate and not later than 180 days after initial startup. Test protocol must be submitted 30 days before the scheduled test date (Section 4.9).

### Permit Renewal
Every PTO shall be renewed annually. The APCO shall review conditions prior to expiration to ensure compliance and enforceability (Section 4.6).

## Determination Notes

- Rule 400 is the core permitting rule for ALL stationary sources in Butte County AQMD. Any equipment that emits or may emit air contaminants needs an ATC before construction and a PTO before operation, unless specifically exempt under Rule 401.
- The ATC-then-PTO two-step sequence is mandatory; no PTO may be granted for a source built without a prior ATC (Section 4.4.3).
- Sources with PTE > 25 tpy (non-CO) or > 50 tpy CO trigger public notice requirements.
- Rule 400 cross-references Rule 430 (State NSR) and Rule 432 (Federal NSR) for major sources.
- PDF source is the EPA-compiled Butte County AQMD SIP regulations (September 2022), which is the authoritative compilation of CARB-approved rules.
