## Purpose

Rule 401 specifies emissions units that are categorically or conditionally exempted from the requirement to obtain an Authority to Construct (ATC) or a Permit to Operate (PTO) from the APCO. It also specifies recordkeeping requirements to verify exemption criteria.

> "This Rule specifies emissions units that are categorically or conditionally exempted from the requirement to obtain an Authority to Construct or a Permit to Operate from the Air Pollution Control Officer (APCO). This Rule also specifies recordkeeping requirements to verify exemption criteria and outlines a compliance schedule for emissions units and sources that exceed the exemption status or otherwise become subject to Rule 400-Permit Requirements of these Rules and Regulations." (Section 1)

Adopted January 4, 1972; last amended April 24, 2014.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Overriding Emission Caps — Exemptions Do NOT Apply If:

> "This Rule shall not apply to any equipment, operation, or process in Section 4 with actual emissions greater than 80 pounds per day of PM10, PM2.5 or oxides of sulfur (SOx), 25 pounds per day of oxides of nitrogen (NOx) or volatile organic compounds (VOCs), 500 pounds per day of carbon monoxide (CO), or 3.2 pounds per day of lead (Pb)." (Section 2.4)

> "This Rule shall not apply to any equipment, operation, or process that is subject to the provisions of the National Emissions Standards for Hazardous Air Pollutants (NESHAPs), 40 CFR (Code of Federal Regulations) 61 or 63, or the New Source Performance Standards (NSPS), 40 CFR 60, unless the equipment is an internal combustion engine with a manufacturer's maximum power rating of less than fifty (50) horsepower." (Section 2.5)

### Categorical Exemptions (Section 4 — No Permit Required)

**4.1 Combustion and Heat Transfer:**
- Steam cleaning equipment (4.1.1)
- Water cooling towers not used for process water evaporative cooling (4.1.2)
- > "Steam generators, water boilers or water heaters fired exclusively by natural gas, liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) or a combination thereof, having a maximum fuel input heating value of less than one (1) million British thermal units (MMBtu) per hour or thirty (30) boiler horsepower." (4.1.3)
- Equipment used for space heating, other than boilers (4.1.4)
- Internal combustion engines:
  - > "Any reciprocating internal combustion engine with a brake horsepower rating of less than fifty (50)." (4.1.5.1)
  - > "Any natural gas, propane, or LPG fueled engine rated at 250 brake horsepower or less and operating less than 200 hours per calendar year for non-emergency purposes." (4.1.5.3)
  - > "Fuel cells or microturbines used in power and/or heat generating equipment that meet the following emission standards: NOx 0.07 lb/Megawatt-hour (MW-hr), CO 0.10 lb/MW-hr, and VOCs 0.02 lb/MW-hr." (4.1.5.4)
- Crucible/pot/induction furnaces with maximum rated capacity of less than 450 cubic inches (4.1.6.1)
- Kilns for firing ceramic ware, heated exclusively with natural gas, LPG, and/or electricity (4.1.6.2)

**4.2 Food and Beverage:**
- Eating establishment food prep equipment, except coffee roasters (4.2.1)
- Coffee roasters rated at ≤ 25 lb/hour throughput (4.2.2)
- > "Breweries with an annual production of less than one (1) million gallons, unless boilers or other heating sources are in excess of the capacities in Section 4.1.3 of this Rule." (4.2.5)

**4.3 Commercial Surface Coating:**
> "Non-automotive coating operations using less than one (1) quart per day and eight (8) gallons per year of spray coatings, including those operations that use non-refillable handheld aerosol spray cans." (4.3)

**4.4 Solvent Cleaning:**
> "Unheated, non-conveyorized cleaning equipment with an open surface area of ten (10) square feet or less and internal volume of 92.5 gallons or less using only organic solvents with an initial boiling point of 248°F as determined by the American Society for Testing of Materials (ASTM) 1078-78 and losing 25 gallons or less of solvent per year to the atmosphere (excluding solvent recycled or properly disposed of). A material usage log shall be maintained for each emission unit claiming this exemption." (4.4)

**4.5 Composting:**
> "Operations that receive and compost less than 125 tons (1000 cubic yards) of green waste annually." (4.5)

**4.7 Residential/Commercial:**
> "Equipment utilized exclusively in connection with any structure which is designed for and used exclusively as a dwelling for not more than four (4) families, including incinerators used exclusively in connection with such a structure." (4.7.1)
> "Any architectural or industrial coatings that are subject to the requirements of these Rules and Regulations when applied to structures and their appurtenances." (4.7.4)

**4.9 Abrasive Blasting:**
> "Abrasive blasting cabinets with a confined volume of less than 100 cubic feet that are vented through a control device." (4.9.2)

**4.10 Agricultural Sources:**
Exempt unless the source is a major stationary source or emits ≥ 50% of major source thresholds for regulated air pollutants, ≥ 5 tpy single HAP, ≥ 12.5 tpy any combination of HAPs, or ≥ 50% of any lesser HAP threshold set by EPA (4.10).

**4.12 Printing and Reproduction (Graphic Arts):**
> "Any graphic arts operation or group of graphic arts operations located at a stationary source, that emit less than an average of fifteen (15) pounds of VOCs per operating day for each calendar month and less than 300 pounds of VOCs annually from all such operations." (4.12)

**4.13 Wastewater Treatment:**
> "Facilities with a design throughput capacity of less than one (1) million gallons of wastewater per day." (4.13)

**4.14 Portable Equipment:**
Any portable equipment registered under Rule 440 or the CARB Statewide PERP is exempt (4.14).

**4.18 Low Emitting Sources (Categorical):**
> "As determined by the APCO, other low emitting sources with an uncontrolled emissions rate of each air contaminant of: CO at less than the 500 pounds per day; and Particulate matter (PM10 or PM2.5), NOx, VOCs, or SOx at: Less than or equal to two (2) pounds per day or, If greater than two (2) pounds per day, is less than or equal to 75 pounds per year." (4.18)

### Conditional Exemptions (Section 5 — Require APCO Written Confirmation Before Start)

**5.1.1 Identical Equipment Replacement:**
> "Identical replacement in whole or part of any article, machine, equipment or other contrivance for which a Permit to Operate has previously been granted for such equipment. Identical means the same manufacturer, model number, and type, and resulting in emissions less than or equal to those from the original equipment or device and not requiring a change in permit conditions." (5.1.1)

**5.1.2 Other Low Emitting Sources:**
> "As determined by the APCO, other low emitting sources with an uncontrolled emissions rate of each air contaminant of less than 500 pounds per year." (5.1.2)

### Compliance Schedule
> "For sources that are exempt from the requirements of Rule 400 and subsequently exceed the standards and/or thresholds of this Rule, the owner or operator of an emissions unit or operation shall apply for a Permit to Operate within thirty (30) days of the date of the loss of exemption." (Section 7.2)

## Determination Notes

- Rule 401 is the primary go-to for determining whether a piece of equipment needs a permit in Butte County. Work through Section 4 categorical exemptions first; if none applies, check Section 5 conditional exemptions.
- The hard caps in Section 2.4 (e.g., 25 lb/day NOx or VOC) override any categorical exemption — if actual emissions exceed those thresholds, the exemption does not apply regardless of equipment type.
- NSPS/NESHAP-subject equipment (40 CFR 60/61/63) is NOT exempt unless it is a <50 hp internal combustion engine.
- Architectural coatings applied to structures (4.7.4) are categorically exempt from permit — the VOC limits come from the state-level architectural coatings rule (Rule 2.12(f) / CARB consumer products rules), not from a Butte County permit.
- Graphic arts: exempt at <15 lb VOC/day average per month AND <300 lb VOC/year — both tests must be met.
- Surface coating spray: exempt at <1 quart/day AND <8 gallons/year — very low threshold; most commercial spray operations will exceed this.
