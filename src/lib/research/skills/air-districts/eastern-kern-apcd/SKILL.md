# Eastern Kern APCD — Air Permit Skill

## District Overview

Eastern Kern APCD (EKAPCD) covers the eastern portion of Kern County, California — including the Mojave Desert region, Ridgecrest, and Edwards AFB area. The district operates its own rulebook organized in five regulations (Series 100–500). It is a distinct agency from the San Joaquin Valley APCD, which covers the western/valley portion of Kern County.

## One-Line Decision Guide

1. **Does the equipment fall in Rule 202's exemption list?**
   - Key bright lines: NG/LPG boiler <5 MMBtu/hr, engine ≤50 bhp, gas turbine ≤3 MMBtu/hr, small solvent cleaner (≤10 ft² open surface, ≤92.5 gal, <25 gal/yr loss), printing <2 gal/day graphic arts materials, LEU ≤2 lb/day any pollutant (or NOx/VOC ≤10 lb/day and ≤180 lb/yr), architectural coatings (applied to structures), welding/brazing.
   - If YES and no NSPS/NESHAP override → no ATC/PTO required (recordkeeping may still apply).

2. **If not exempt → Rule 201 ATC before construction, PTO before operation.**

3. **Rule 210.1 NSR applies to all Rule 201 sources:**
   - BACT required for all new/modified sources.
   - Offsets required for nonattainment pollutants (NOx, VOC, PM10 are key in Eastern Kern).
   - Major source threshold: ≥50 tpy any affected pollutant.
   - Major modification triggers: >25 tpy NOx or VOC, >15 tpy PM10, >40 tpy SOx, >100 tpy CO increase.

4. **Graphic arts (printing/coating/laminating):**
   - <2 gal/day graphic arts materials → exempt from ATC/PTO (Rule 202 II.D).
   - <75 lb/day VOC → exempt from Rule 410.7 substantive controls (recordkeeping still required).
   - ≥75 lb/day VOC → Rule 410.7 applies: low-VOC inks (<300 g/L) or 75–95% control efficiency.
