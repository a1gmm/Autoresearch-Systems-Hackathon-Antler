# Ventura County APCD — Air Rules

Controlling air authority for all of Ventura County (incl. Oxnard, Ventura, Camarillo, etc.).
Permit portal: https://www.vcapcd.org/permitting

## Decision guide
A stationary source needs a VCAPCD permit (Rule 10: Authority to Construct → Permit to Operate)
UNLESS it qualifies for a Rule 23 exemption. Graphic-arts/printing VOC limits come from the 74-series.
