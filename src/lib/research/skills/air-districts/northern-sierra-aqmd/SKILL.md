## One-Line Decision Guide

1. **Does the project build, alter, or replace equipment that emits air contaminants?** → Rule 401 requires an Authority to Construct first; check Rule 402 exemptions (< 1,000 lb/yr criteria pollutants + toxic score < 1, or < 3 MMBtu/hr nat-gas/LPG combustion, or 4-family-or-less dwelling).
2. **Will the equipment be operated?** → Rule 501 requires a Permit to Operate; same Rule 402 exemptions apply via Rule 502.
3. **Does the project involve applying architectural coatings in Western Nevada County?** → Rule 230 sets VOC limits (Flat: 50 g/L, Nonflat: 100 g/L, specialty categories up to 730 g/L); aerosols and containers ≤ 1 liter are exempt.
4. **Is the source potentially a Title V major source?** → Rule 522 and Rule 526 (limiting potential to emit) apply in addition to the above.

## Notes on District Scope

- Northern Sierra AQMD is a small district covering Nevada, Sierra, and Plumas Counties in the northern Sierra Nevada foothills.
- Rule 230 (architectural coatings VOC limits) applies **only within Western Nevada County** (the ozone nonattainment subarea); the remaining district area relies on state/CARB baseline standards for coating VOCs.
- The district adopts its own local permit rules (Reg IV/V) and several CARB-aligned ATCMs (Reg IX). It does not have heavy industrial rules found in larger districts (e.g., no refinery or cement-specific rules).
- Open burning is extensively regulated under Regulation III (Rules 300–318) — relevant for agricultural and forestry operations in the district.
