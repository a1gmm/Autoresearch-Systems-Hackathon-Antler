## Purpose

Rule 201 (Permit to Construct) is AVAQMD's Authority to Construct (ATC) requirement. It prohibits construction, erection, installation, alteration, or replacement of any equipment — whether it emits air contaminants or controls/reduces such emissions — without first obtaining written authorization from the Air Pollution Control Officer (APCO).

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

Verbatim from Rule 201 (Adopted: 1/9/76; Amended: 1/5/90; Amended: 8/19/97):

> "A person shall not build, erect, install, alter or replace any equipment, the use of which may cause the issuance of air contaminants or the use of which may eliminate, reduce or control the issuance of air contaminants without first obtaining written authorization for such construction from the Air Pollution Control Officer. A permit to construct shall remain in effect until the permit to operate the equipment for which the application was filed is granted or denied, or the application is canceled."

Key operational notes:
- Applies to **any** equipment that may emit OR control/reduce air contaminants.
- The ATC remains valid until a Permit to Operate (Rule 203) is granted, denied, or the application is canceled.
- Equipment exempt from permitting is enumerated in Rule 219 (Equipment Not Requiring a Permit).

SIP status: Submitted as amended 8/19/97 on 3/10/98; Approved 11/9/78, 43 FR 52237, 40 CFR 52.220(c)(39)(iii)(B); 40 CFR 52.220(c)(31)(vi)(C).

## Determination Notes

- Rule 201 is the ATC trigger for AVAQMD. Any new installation, modification, or replacement of process equipment requires pre-construction authorization unless exempt under Rule 219.
- No numeric emission threshold is stated in Rule 201 itself — the requirement is categorical: any equipment with potential to emit or control emissions needs authorization.
- Exemptions flow through Rule 219 (equipment-category exemptions) and Rule 202 (conditional exemptions, not downloaded here).
- For permit application details see: https://www.avaqmd.ca.gov/permitting-information
