## One-Line Decision Guide

**Does this equipment or activity need an AVAQMD permit?**

1. **Check Rule 219** — is the equipment in a listed exempt category AND does it emit < 2 tons/year NAAQS pollutant AND < 0.5 tons/year HAP? If yes to all → no permit required (burden of proof on owner/operator).
2. **If not exempt → Rule 201** (ATC before construction/installation) then **Rule 203** (Permit to Operate before startup).
3. **For combustion equipment** → exempt at ≤ 50 bhp (ICE/turbine) or ≤ 2 MMBtu/hr (boilers/heaters on PUC gas).
4. **For coating/adhesive spray equipment** → exempt at ≤ 3 lb VOC/day or ≤ 1 gal/day solvent coatings.
5. **For graphic arts** → exempt at ≤ 3 lb VOC/day or ≤ 2 gal/day graphic arts materials (Rule 219 E(8)).
6. **Regardless of permit status**, VOC content of architectural coatings must comply with Rule 1113, adhesives/sealants with Rule 1168, and graphic arts operations with Rule 1130.
