## Purpose

Rule 1130 reduces VOC emissions from Graphic Arts Printing Operations, Digital Printing Operations, and Paper, Film, Foil or Fabric Coating Operations, including Solvent Cleaning materials and processes associated with such operations.

(Adopted: 10/03/80; most recently Amended: 11/19/13)

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Applicability (Section A(2))

> "(a) This rule is applicable to any Graphic Arts Printing Operations, Digital Printing Operations, Paper, Film, Foil or Fabric Coating Operations and to the Solvent Cleaning materials and processes associated with such operations in the District.
> (d) This rule does not apply to screen printing operations subject to the provisions of Rule 1130.1 – Screen Printing.
> (e) This Rule does not apply to the application of coatings to any plastic, rubber or glass products subject to the provisions of Rule 1145 – Plastic, Rubber and Glass Coatings."

### VOC Content Limits — Table 1 (Effective 12/01/2014)

Verbatim from Rule 1130, Table 1: "VOC Content Limits for Adhesives, Coatings and Inks — Grams of VOC per Liter (lb/gal), Less Water and Less Exempt Compounds, as applied, effective on and after 12/01/2014":

| Material | g/L VOC (lb/gal) |
|---|---|
| Flexographic Ink on Porous Substrates | 225 (1.88) |
| Printing Ink | 300 (2.5) |
| Coating | 300 (2.5) |
| Adhesive | 150 (1.25) |
| Web Splicing Adhesive | 150 (1.25) |

### Fountain Solution VOC Limits — Table 2 (Effective 12/01/2014)

> "Total Grams of VOC per Liter (lb/gal) including any VOC containing materials added to the original Fountain Solution, effective through 11/30/2014: 100 (0.83)"

After 12/01/2014, percent VOC by volume limits:

| Press Type | % VOC by volume |
|---|---|
| Heatset Web Offset Lithographic Printing | 1.6 |
| Non-Heatset Web Offset Lithographic Printing | 5.0 |
| Sheet-fed Offset Lithographic Printing (max sheet ≤ 11×17 in) | 5.0 |
| All other presses | 8.0 |

> "(a) On and after 12/01/2014, an Operator performing Non-Heatset Web Offset Lithographic Printing shall use Fountain Solution that is **five percent (5%) alcohol substitute or less**, by weight, and shall have **no alcohol** in the Fountain Solution."

### Flexographic Specialty Ink Limits — Table 3

> "(a) An Operator using a Flexographic Printing Operation shall not use a Specialty Ink in excess of the VOC limit in Table 3, and shall not use more than **two (2) gallons of Specialty Inks in a calendar day** and **120 gallons of Specialty Ink in a calendar year**."

| Specialty Ink Type | g/L VOC (lb/gal) |
|---|---|
| Metallic Ink | 460 (3.8) |
| Matte Finish Ink | 535 (4.5) |
| Metallic Ink and Matte Finish Ink on Flexible Package Printing | 383 (3.2) |

> "(b) On and after 12/01/2014, facilities with the Potential to Emit or with actual emissions of at least **ten (10) tons VOC in any calendar year** shall not use Specialty Inks with VOC content greater than **300 grams VOC per liter**, less water and less Exempt Compounds, as applied."

### Paper, Film, Foil or Fabric Coating Operations (Section C(3))

> "(a) An Operator shall not use any Coating in any Paper, Film, Foil or Fabric Coating Application Process, with or without Ovens, unless:
>   (i) The Coating contains less than **265 Grams of VOC Per Liter (2.2 lb/gal)**, Less Water and Less Exempt Compounds, as applied; or
>   (ii) VOC emissions are collected and reduced by an approved emission control system..."
>
> "(b) An Operator shall not use or apply any Plastisol to any paper, film, foil or fabric substrate unless the Coating contains less than **20 Grams of VOC Per Liter (0.16 lb/gal)**, Less Water and Less Exempt Compounds, as applied."

### Approved VOC Emission Control System — Table 4

Overall VOC Capture and Control Efficiency (% by weight, effective 12/01/2014):

| Process | Efficiency |
|---|---|
| Publication Gravure | 85% |
| Packaging Gravure | 75% |
| Lithography | 75% |
| Letterpress | 75% |
| Flexible Package Printing (all technologies) | 80% |
| Fountain Solution | 75% |

Paper/Film/Foil/Fabric controls must achieve:
> "(i) The emission collection system shall collect at least **90 percent**, by weight, of the emissions generated by the source of emissions.
> (ii) The control device shall reduce emissions from an emission collection system by at least **95 percent**, by weight, or the output of the control device is **50 ppm**, by volume, calculated as carbon, with no dilution."

## Determination Notes

- Rule 219 Section E(8) provides a permit exemption for graphic arts operations emitting ≤ 3 lb VOC/day or using ≤ 6 gal/day UV/EB/plastisol or ≤ 2 gal/day other graphic arts materials — so small printers may avoid ATC/PTO entirely.
- Operations exceeding Rule 219 thresholds must obtain permits AND comply with Rule 1130 VOC limits.
- Rule 1130 does not cover screen printing (Rule 1130.1) or plastic/rubber/glass coatings (Rule 1145).
- High-volume heatset web offset printers (Potential to Emit > 25 tons/year) must use add-on control devices (90–95% overall capture and control efficiency).
