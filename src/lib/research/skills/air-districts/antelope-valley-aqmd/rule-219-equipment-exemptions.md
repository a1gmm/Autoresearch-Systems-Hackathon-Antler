## Purpose

Rule 219 defines which equipment categories are exempt from Rules 201 and 203 permit requirements, and which equipment need not be listed on a Federal Operating Permit (FOP) under Regulation XXX (Title V). The rule also sets exemptions for Agricultural Facilities.

(Adopted: 01/09/76; most recently Amended: 06/15/21)

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### General Permit Exemption Conditions (Section B(1))

The APCO shall not require a permit if ALL of the following are true:

> "(a) Such equipment is contained in the list of particular Equipment in section (E) below; and
> (b) Such Equipment does not emit air contaminants in excess of any of the following:
>   (i) 2 tons per year of any Regulated Air Pollutant for which a National Ambient Air Quality Standard has been promulgated;
>   (ii) A de minimis level for a Hazardous Air Pollutant promulgated pursuant to 42 U.S.C. §7412 (FCAA §112).
>   (iii) A significance level defined in 40 CFR 52.21(b)(23)(i);
>   (iv) 0.5 tons per year of a Hazardous Air Pollutant.
> (c) Such Equipment does not constitute any of the following:
>   (i) A Major Facility as defined in Rule 1301, or
>   (ii) A Major Modification as defined in Rule 1301, or
>   (iii) A Major PSD Facility as defined in Rule 1700, or
>   (iv) A Major PSD Modification as defined in Rule 1700."

### FOP Exclusion Threshold (Section D(1))

> "To be eligible for exclusion from a FOP pursuant to subsection (B)(2) above, any equipment proposed to be excluded shall not emit Air Pollutants in an amount greater than any of the following:
>   (i) 2 tons per year of any Regulated Air Pollutant for which a National Ambient Air Quality Standard has been promulgated; or
>   (ii) A de minimis level for a Hazardous Air Pollutant promulgated pursuant to 42 U.S.C. §7412(g) (Federal Clean Air Act §112(g)); or
>   (iii) Any significance level defined in 40 CFR 52.21(b)(23)(i); or
>   (iv) 0.5 ton per year of such Hazardous Air Pollutant, whichever is less."

### Agricultural Facility Exemption Thresholds (Section D(2))

Confined Animal Facility eligibility — must have LESS THAN:
> "(i) 1,000 milk-producing dairy cows; (ii) 3,500 beef cattle; (iii) 7,500 calves, heifers or other cattle; (iv) 650,000 chickens other than laying hens; (v) 650,000 laying hens; (vi) 650,000 ducks; (vii) 100,000 turkeys; (viii) 3,000 swine; (ix) 2,500 horses; (x) 15,000 sheep, lambs, or goats; or (xi) 30,000 rabbits or other animals."

Aggregate Agricultural Facility emissions must be less than ALL of:
> "(i) 12.5 tons per year of NOx and VOC; or (ii) Fifty (50) tons per year of any other Air Pollutant for which a National Ambient Air Quality Standard has been promulgated; or (iii) 5 tons per year of any Hazardous Air Pollutant; or (iv) 12.5 tons per year of any combination of Hazardous Air Pollutants."

### Key Equipment-Category Exemptions (Section E — Selected Excerpts)

**Combustion and Heat Transfer Equipment (E(2)):**

> "(a) Internal Combustion Engines and Gas Turbines - Piston type internal combustion engines with a manufacturer's continuous rating of **50 brake horsepower (bhp) or less**, or gas turbine engines with a maximum heat input rate of **2,975,000 Btu (749,866 kg cal) per hour** at International Standardization Organization (ISO) Standard Day Conditions or less. The ratings of all engines or turbines used in the same process will be aggregated to determine whether this exemption applies.
> (b) General Combustion Equipment - Boilers, process heaters or any combustion equipment that has a maximum heat input rate of **2,000,000 Btu (504,000 kg cal) per hour** (gross) or less and is equipped to be fired exclusively with, Public Utilities Commission regulated natural gas, methanol, liquefied petroleum gas or any combination thereof..."

**Printing and Reproduction Equipment (E(8)):**

> "(a) Printing and related Coating and/or laminating Equipment used in Graphic Arts Operations, and associated dryers... not emitting more than **3 pounds of VOC emissions per day**, or not using more than **6 gallons per day** of ultraviolet, electron beam, or plastisols type, including cleanup solvent, or **2 gallons per day** of any other graphic arts materials."

**Coating/Adhesive Application Equipment (E(12)(p)):**

> "Coating or adhesive application or laminating Equipment such as air, airless, air-assisted airless, high volume low pressure (HVLP), and electrostatic spray equipment, and roller coaters, dip coaters, vacuum coaters and flow coaters and spray machines provided that:
>   (i) The VOC emissions from such Equipment (including clean-up) is **3 pounds per day or less**; or
>   (ii) The total quantity of UV or electron beam (non-solvent based and non-waterborne) coatings, adhesives and associated VOC containing solvents (including cleanup) used in such Equipment is **6 gallons per day or less**; or
>   (iii) The total quantity of solvent type coating and/or adhesive used is **1 gallon per day or less**, including cleanup solvent; or
>   (iv) The total quantity of water reducible or water based type coating and adhesives and associated VOC containing solvents (including clean-up) is **3 gallons per day or less**; or
>   (v) The total quantity of polyester resin or gel coat type material and associated VOC containing solvents (including clean-up) is **1 gallon per day or less**."

**Batch Mixers (E(11)(a)):**

> "Batch mixers which have a capacity of **55 gallons or 7.35 ft³ (0.21 m³) or less**."

**Powder Coating Ovens (E(12)(l)):**

> "Batch ovens used exclusively to cure **30 pounds per day or less** of powder coatings."

**Unheated Cleaning/Coating Equipment (E(12)(j)):**

> "Unheated, non-conveyorized, cleaning or coating equipment:
>   (i) With an open surface area of **10.8 square feet or less** and an internal volume of **92.5 gallons or less**, having an organic solvent loss of **3 gallons per day or less**; or
>   (ii) Using only organic solvents with an initial boiling point of **302°F (150°C) or greater** as determined by ASTM D1078-11...; or
>   (iii) Using materials with a VOC content of **2 percent (20 grams per liter) or less** by volume."

**Hand Application (E(12)(x)):**

> "Hand application of resins, adhesives, dyes, coatings and solvents using devices such as brushes, daubers, rollers trowels, rags, swabs and squeeze bottles."

**Structures/General Equipment (E(3)(d)):**

> "Equipment utilized exclusively in connection with any structure which is designed for and used exclusively as a dwelling for not more than **4 families**, and where such Equipment is used by the owner or occupant of such a dwelling."

### Key Caveat (Section B(5)–(9))

> "Nothing in this rule shall be interpreted to exempt the emissions from such equipment from being considered in any emissions calculations required pursuant to District Regulation XIII – New Source Review..."
> "Nothing in this Rule shall be interpreted to exempt air pollution Control Equipment venting otherwise permit exempt Equipment from obtaining permits."
> "The burden of proof regarding the applicability of this rule to particular equipment shall be upon the Owner/Operator..."

## Determination Notes

- Rule 219 exemptions are self-certified by the owner/operator — the burden of proof rests on the owner/operator.
- Even if equipment is categorically exempt, it must still stay below the 2 ton/year NAAQS pollutant and 0.5 ton/year HAP emission caps.
- Air pollution control equipment venting exempt equipment is NOT itself automatically exempt.
- Emissions from exempt equipment still count toward NSR/PSD major source thresholds under Regulation XIII and XVII.
- The APCO may override exemptions via written notification if a NSPS, NESHAP, MACT, ATCM, or source-specific rule applies, or if the equipment may not operate in compliance with all applicable rules.
- For combustion equipment: ratings of all engines/boilers in the same process are aggregated.
