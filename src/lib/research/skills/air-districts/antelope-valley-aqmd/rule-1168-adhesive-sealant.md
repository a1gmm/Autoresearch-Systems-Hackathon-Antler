## Purpose

Rule 1168 reduces VOC emissions and eliminates emissions of chloroform, ethylene dichloride, methylene chloride, perchloroethylene, and trichloroethylene from the application of Adhesives, Adhesive Primers, Sealants, Sealant Primers, or any other Primers. Applies to all commercial and industrial sales and applications unless specifically exempted.

(Adopted: 04/07/89; most recently Amended: 09/20/11)

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Applicability (Section A(2))

> "This rule shall apply to all commercial and industrial sales and applications of Adhesives, Adhesive Primers, Sealants, Sealant Primers, or any other Primers, unless otherwise specifically exempted by this rule."

### General VOC Limit (Section C(1))

> "After January 1, 1993, a Person shall not apply any Adhesives, Sealants, Adhesive Bonding Primers, Adhesive Primers, Sealant Primers, or any other Primer which have a VOC content in excess of **250 g/L** less water and less Exempt Compounds unless otherwise specified in subsection (C)(2)."

### Small Quantity Exemptions

> "...the application of Adhesives or Sealants that contain less than **20 g/L** of VOC per liter of Adhesives or Sealants, less water and less Exempt Compounds." (Section — exempt from most requirements)

> "...less than **55 gallons per Facility per rolling 12 month period**..." (small facility de minimis threshold)

> "...VOC content of **50 grams per liter**, less water and less Exempt Compounds." (another small-use threshold referenced in Section)

> "...the Facility uses a total of **three (3) gallons per day or less** of these Adhesives." (small daily-use exemption)

### Prohibited Compounds

Rule 1168 eliminates emissions of:
- Chloroform
- Ethylene dichloride
- Methylene chloride
- Perchloroethylene (PCE / PERC)
- Trichloroethylene (TCE)

from Adhesive and Sealant applications.

## Determination Notes

- Rule 1168 covers both the manufacturer/supplier side (sales) and the applicator side (use).
- The general 250 g/L backstop applies where Table 1 does not specify a lower limit.
- Very low-VOC adhesives (< 20 g/L) are de facto exempt from most compliance obligations.
- Small facilities using ≤ 55 gal/year total may qualify for simplified compliance.
- Applications using ≤ 3 gal/day of low-VOC adhesives may qualify for exemption.
- Note Rule 219 Section E(12)(x) exempts "Hand application of resins, adhesives, dyes, coatings and solvents using devices such as brushes, daubers, rollers trowels, rags, swabs and squeeze bottles" from permit requirements entirely — Rule 1168 VOC limits still apply to the materials used.
- Cross-check Rule 1107 (Metal Parts & Products) and Rule 1145 (Plastic, Rubber, Glass Coatings) for adhesives used in those specific industrial contexts.
