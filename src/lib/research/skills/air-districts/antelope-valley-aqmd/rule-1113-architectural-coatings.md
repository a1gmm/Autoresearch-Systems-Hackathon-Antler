## Purpose

Rule 1113 limits the quantity of Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs) in Architectural Coatings — paints, varnishes, sealers, stains, and related products applied to stationary structures or their appurtenances at the site of installation, pavements, or curbs.

(Adopted: 09/02/77; most recently Amended: 06/18/13)

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Applicability (Section A(2))

> "Except as provided in subsection (A)(3), this rule is applicable to any person who supplies, sells, offers for sale, manufactures, blends, or repackages any Architectural Coating for use within the Antelope Valley Air Quality Management District (District) as well as any person who applies or Solicits the application of any Architectural Coating within the District."

### Exemptions (Section A(3))

> "(a) Any Architectural Coating that is supplied, sold, offered for sale, or manufactured for use outside of the District or for shipment to other manufacturers for reformulation or repackaging.
> (b) Any Aerosol Coating Product.
> (c) With the exception of Section (E), any Architectural Coating that is sold in a container with a volume of one (1) liter (1.057 quart) or less."

## Determination Notes

- Rule 1113 applies to suppliers, manufacturers, repackagers, and applicators — not just industrial operators.
- Rule 1113 does NOT apply to coatings applied in shop applications, or to non-stationary structures (airplanes, ships, boats, railcars, automobiles), or Adhesives.
- Aerosol Coating Products are categorically exempt.
- Containers ≤ 1 liter are exempt from VOC limits (except labeling Section E).
- Flat Coatings (50 g/L) and Nonflat Coatings (100 g/L) are the common thresholds for general exterior/interior paint. These align with CARB's 2011 Architectural Coatings Suggested Control Measure.
- Portable coating equipment and pavement stripers used exclusively for architectural coatings per Rule 1113 are also exempt from permit requirements under Rule 219(E)(12)(r).
