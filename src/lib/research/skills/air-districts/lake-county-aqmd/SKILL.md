## District Character

Lake County AQMD is a **small, attainment-status district** covering a single-county basin (Clear Lake area). It is notable for:
- **Geothermal operations**: The Geysers steam field straddles the Lake/Sonoma county line; Sections 608–609 provide a prescriptive ATC pathway for geothermal power plants (H₂S ≤ 5.0 lb/hr per 1,000,000 lb/hr steam).
- **Few local VOC/coating rules**: The district does not have standalone ROG/VOC coating rules. Coating and graphic arts operations are regulated via NSPS adoption (§440) and the general ATC/PTO requirement.
- **Very short exemption list**: Section 640 lists only 5 categories (vehicles, small residences, non-commercial BBQs, orchard heaters, maintenance). No HP-based or throughput-based general equipment exemption list exists.
- **Agricultural burning**: Extensive agricultural burning regulations (Chapter VIII) including economic exemptions for no-burn days (application fee: $125; form at https://lcaqmd.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Economic-Exemption-Application-Form-Fillable-5_1_26.pdf).

## One-Line Decision Guide

**Does this equipment need a permit?** — If it can emit air contaminants and is not a vehicle, small residential dwelling (≤4 families), non-commercial BBQ, orchard heater, or routine maintenance on existing permitted equipment, it needs an ATC before construction and a PTO before operation. Insignificant sources (Category I: markets, small stores, essentially no detectable emissions) carry no fee and will not necessarily need a PTO — confirm with the district by phone (707-263-7000).

**NSR full review kicks in at:** >20 lb/hr or >150 lb/day NOx/ROG/criteria pollutants (except CO); >150 lb/hr or >1,500 lb/day CO; >27 lb/day lead.

**MACT required when:** ≥10 tpy single HAP or ≥25 tpy combined HAPs.
