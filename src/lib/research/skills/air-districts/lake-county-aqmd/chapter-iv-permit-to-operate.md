## Purpose

Governs the issuance, conditions, and denial of Permits to Operate (PTO) for stationary sources within Lake County AQMD. The PTO is the operating-phase companion to the Authority to Construct.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Section 610 — Trigger
> "A Permit to Operate may be required to operate any article, machine, equipment or other contrivance which causes or may cause the issuance of an air contaminant."

### Section 611 — Application Requirements
Applicants must submit:
- Name, address, owner, and nature of business
- Description of production process and flow chart
- Plot plan showing location and height of all air contaminant sources and nearest residential/commercial property
- Type and quantity of fuels used or wastes consumed
- Amount, nature, and duration of air contaminant emissions
- Estimated collection efficiency of air pollution control equipment

### Section 613 — ATC Prerequisite
> "The Air Pollution Control Officer shall deny a Permit to Operate to any stationary source subject to the requirements of Article I of this Chapter until the source has obtained an Authority to Construct granted pursuant to the provisions of Article I, except as provided in Section 611."

### Section 614 — Emission Consistency Requirement
> "The Air Pollution Control Officer shall not grant a Permit to Operate to any stationary source that he determines emits quantities of air contaminants greater than those assumed in the analysis required for the Authority to Construct for the source, unless the Air Pollution Control Officer performs the air quality impact analysis required by Section 605 and determines that the actual emissions from the source may not be expected to result in the violation or a measurable contribution to the continued violation of any local, state or national ambient air quality standard."

### Section 614.A — Permit System Baseline Requirements
> "A. Insure that the article, machine, equipment or contrivance for which the permit is to be issued shall not prevent or interfere with the attainment or maintenance of any applicable air quality standard.
> B. Prohibit the issuance of a permit on the basis of criteria adopted by the District Board unless the article, machine, equipment or contrivance for which a permit is to be issued will comply with all applicable orders, rules and regulations of the District and of the State Board and with all applicable provisions of these Rules and Regulations."

### Section 615 — Conditions
The APCO shall impose conditions necessary to ensure the source operates as assumed in the ATC analysis. Where appropriate, new sources may be prohibited from operating concurrently with an existing source for no more than 30 days.

### Section 616 — ARB Notification
Within 30 days of granting a PTO, the APCO forwards a copy to CARB including conditions and supporting calculations.

### Section 630 — Non-Transferability
> "An Authority to Construct or Permit to Operate shall not be transferable, whether by operation of law or otherwise, either from one location to another, from one piece of equipment to another, or from one person to another. If a new owner can prove compliance with the most recent permit conditions as well as all District Rules and Regulations applicable to the previous owner, the Air Pollution Control Officer shall issue a new Authority to Construct or Permit to Operate to the new owner."

### Permit Fee Categories (Section 660 / Schedule of Fees)
| Category | Description | ATC Fee | PTO Fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| I – Insignificant Sources | Essentially no detectable emissions (markets, small stores) | None | None |
| II – <25 tons/year potential | Hospitals w/ approved incinerators, bulk oil plants, small gravel | $154.62 | $77.32 |
| III – 25–100 tons/year | Asphalt plants, large gravel, concrete batch plants | $541.18 | $309.25 |
| IV – >100 tons/year or Geothermal Wells | High-emitting operations | $1,546.25 (app) / $1,159.69 (renewal) | $773.12 |

> Note: Major source minimum fee: "$29.26 per ton of actual regulated pollutant emissions adjusted annually starting on October 1, 1994, using the Consumer Price Index."

> Fee penalty: "If the fee is not paid within sixty (60) days of the invoice mailing date, the fee shall be increased by one-half the amount thereof [...] If the increased fee is not paid within ninety (90) days of the original invoice mailing date, the permit shall be deemed withdrawn and suspended."

## Determination Notes

- The PTO trigger is broad: any equipment that "causes or **may cause**" an air contaminant requires a PTO (Section 610).
- Category I (insignificant sources) carries no fee and will typically not require a formal PTO for small commercial/retail operations.
- A PTO cannot be issued without a prior ATC (Section 613) — sequence is always ATC first, then PTO.
- Actual emissions at startup exceeding the ATC assumptions trigger a new air quality impact analysis before PTO is granted (Section 614).
- Non-transferability means any ownership change requires new ATC/PTO applications (Section 630).
