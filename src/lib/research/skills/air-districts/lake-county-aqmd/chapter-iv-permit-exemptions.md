## Purpose

Lists the narrow categories of equipment and operations that are exempt from the requirement to obtain an Authority to Construct or Permit to Operate under Lake County AQMD Chapter IV. This is a deliberately short list — the district's general permit trigger (Sections 600, 610) is broad, so exemptions are limited.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Section 640 — Permit Exemption List (Verbatim)
> "Permits shall not be required for:
>
> A. Any vehicle as defined in the Vehicle Code.
>
> B. Any structure designed for and used exclusively as a dwelling for not more than four (4) families.
>
> C. Barbecue equipment which is not used for commercial purposes.
>
> D. Orchard or citrus grove heaters described in Section 438 of these Rules and Regulations.
>
> E. Repairs or maintenance not involving structural changes to any equipment for which a permit has been granted."

### Section 660 / Fee Schedule — Category I Insignificant Sources (De Facto No-Permit Tier)
Although not a formal exemption under Section 640, the fee schedule establishes an **insignificant sources** category that carries no fee and typically does not require a PTO:

> "Category I - Insignificant Sources: Operations estimated by calculation and/or analysis which conform to the insignificant sources as commercial or industrial operations producing essentially no detectable emissions, will not necessarily require a Permit to Operate. Examples: Markets, small subdivisions, stores, etc. Authority to Construct Fee: None. Permit to Operate Fee: None."

### Section 602.B — APCO Discretionary Exemptions from Full NSR Review
(Not a permit exemption, but relevant to permit pathway.) The APCO may waive full New Source Review for:
1. Modifications that eliminate, reduce, or control emissions with no net increase
2. Additions/replacements to existing sources with no net emission increase
3. Sources with demonstrable basin-wide air quality benefits (requires CARB/EPA concurrence)
4. Sources used exclusively for essential public services (hospitals, police, fire) employing best practicable controls

## Determination Notes

- The Section 640 exemption list is **extremely narrow**: only vehicles, small residential dwellings (≤4 families), non-commercial barbecues, orchard heaters, and routine maintenance on already-permitted equipment.
- Unlike many larger districts, Lake County AQMD does **not** publish a general equipment exemption list (e.g., no exemption thresholds by equipment type, HP rating, or throughput in Section 640).
- The Category I "insignificant sources" language is the practical equivalent of a de minimis exemption, but it is discretionary ("will not **necessarily** require") rather than a hard exemption.
- Coating operations, spray booths, printing equipment, and similar common small-business sources are **not** listed as exempt — they fall into Category I or II and must be evaluated case-by-case.
- Agricultural compression ignition engines >50 BHP are not exempt under Section 640; they are regulated under Section 470 (ATCM for agricultural CI engines) with their own registration/permit pathway and exemptions for engines ≤50 BHP, wind machines, and mobile agricultural equipment.
- The economic exemption form on the district website (application fee: $125.00) is specifically for **agricultural open burning on no-burn days** — it is not a general permit exemption.
