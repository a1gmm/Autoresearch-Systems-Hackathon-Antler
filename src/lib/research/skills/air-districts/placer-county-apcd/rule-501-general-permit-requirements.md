## Purpose / Applicability

Rule 501 (adopted 12-08-70, last amended 04-08-21) provides "an orderly procedure for the review of new sources of air pollution and modification and operation of existing sources through the issuance of permits." It applies district-wide to all stationary sources and emissions units unless an enumerated exemption applies.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Permit Trigger (Section 300)

Section 301 requires an **Authority to Construct** prior to construction of any new or modified emission unit. Section 302 requires a **Permit to Operate** prior to operating any emission unit.

### Exemptions — General Override Conditions (Section 110)

An exemption does NOT apply if the emissions unit is:

> "110.1 Subject to New Source Performance Standards, except engines less than 50 horsepower subject to NSPS JJJJ…; or
> 110.2 Subject to National Emission Standards for Hazardous Air Pollutants; or
> 110.3 Subject to the requirements of Rule 507, FEDERAL OPERATING PERMIT PROGRAM; or
> 110.4 Emits, in levels deemed appropriate for review by the Air Pollution Control Officer, substances identified as a toxic air contaminant…; or
> 110.5 The Air Pollution Control Officer makes a determination that the emission unit may not operate in compliance with the District Rules and Regulations; or
> 110.6 An emissions unit or stationary source for which emission reduction credits have been requested or granted in accordance with Rule 504…
> 110.7 An otherwise exempt piece of equipment that is part of a process that requires a permit."

### Exempt Equipment Categories (Sections 111–122)

**112 — Combustion and Heat Transfer Equipment:**
> "112.1 Internal combustion engines with a manufacturer's maximum continuous rating of **50 brake horsepower or less** or gas turbine engines with a maximum heat input rate of **3,000,000 British Thermal Units (Btu) per hour or less** at ISO standard day conditions… The ratings of all engines or turbines used in the same process will be accumulated to determine whether this exemption applies.
> 112.2 Any combustion equipment that has a maximum heat input of less than **1,000,000 Btu per hour (gross)** and is equipped to be fired exclusively with natural gas, liquefied petroleum gas or any combination thereof. The ratings of all combustion equipment used in the same process will be accumulated to determine whether this exemption applies."

**117 — Storage and Transfer:**
> "117.2 Unheated organic materials with an initial boiling point of **150 degrees Celsius (302 degrees Fahrenheit) or greater**… or with an organic vapor pressure of **5 mm Hg (0.1 psia) or less** at 20°C…
> 117.3 Organic liquids with a vapor pressure of **77.5 mm Hg (1.5 psia) or less** at 20°C… stored in containers having a capacity of **23,000 liters (6076 gallons or less)**…
> 117.4 Unheated solvent dispensing containers of **380 liters (100 gallons) capacity or less**."

**118 — Surface Coating and Preparation:**
> "118.2 Surface coating operations using a combined total of **one gallon per day or less** of coating material and solvent.
> 118.3 Unheated non-conveyorized solvent rinsing containers or unheated non-conveyorized coating dip tanks of **380 liters (100 gallons) capacity or less** with an open surface area of **one square meter (11 square feet) or less** providing **no more than 25 gallons of solvent are evaporated or lost** to the atmosphere from all such equipment per calendar year."

**122 — Other Equipment:**
> "Unless subject to the requirements of Rule 507…, other equipment authorized for exemption by the Air Pollution Control Officer and which would emit **less than 2 pounds in any 24 hour period** of any pollutants without the benefit of air pollution control devices."

### Key Definitions

- **Authority to Construct (Section 205):** "A preconstruction permit authorizing construction prior to the starting of construction and conforming to the requirements of Rule 502, NEW SOURCE REVIEW."
- **Emissions Unit (Section 208):** "An identifiable operation or piece of process equipment such as an article, machine, or other contrivance which controls, emits, may emit, or results in the emissions of any air pollutant directly or as fugitive emissions."
- **Stationary Source (Section 216):** Defined to include buildings, structures, facilities, or emissions units under common ownership/control on contiguous or adjacent properties in the same SIC grouping or common production process.

## Determination Notes

1. **Trigger question:** Is the equipment an "emissions unit" at a "stationary source" in Placer County APCD jurisdiction? If yes, a permit is presumptively required unless an exemption under Sections 111–122 applies.
2. **Combustion equipment:** Check BHP (≤50 hp exempt) and heat input (< 1 MMBtu/hr exempt for gas-fired). Aggregate all equipment in the same process.
3. **Storage tanks:** Check vapor pressure (≤77.5 mm Hg at 20°C + ≤6,076 gallons) or boiling point (≥150°C) for exemption.
4. **Surface coating:** Check daily usage (≤1 gal/day exempt) and annual solvent loss (≤25 gal/year for small dip tanks).
5. **Catch-all:** Equipment emitting < 2 lb/day of any pollutant may qualify for APCO-authorized exemption under Section 122, but requires APCO approval.
6. **Override:** Even if an exemption applies, check Section 110 override conditions — NSPS/NESHAP-subject equipment, TAC emitters, and ERC-granted sources are NOT exempt.
7. **Cross-reference:** Rule 502 (New Source Review) and Rule 511 (Potential to Emit) set additional thresholds that may trigger major source review independent of individual unit permitting.
