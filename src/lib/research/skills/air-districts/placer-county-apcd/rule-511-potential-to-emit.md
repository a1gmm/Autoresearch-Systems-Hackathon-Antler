## Purpose / Applicability

Rule 511 (adopted 06-08-95) applies to any stationary source that, absent the limitations in this rule, would have the **potential to emit** at or above the major source threshold for regulated air pollutants or HAPs. It is the mechanism by which a source can remain a "synthetic minor" rather than a Title V major source — by capping actual emissions below specific thresholds and maintaining required records and reports.

**Applicability trigger (Section 101):** The rule applies when:

> "101.1 In every 12-month period, the actual emissions of the stationary source are less than or equal to the emission limitations specified in Section 301 below; or
> 101.2 In every 12-month period, at least 90 percent of the emissions from the stationary source are associated with an operation limited by any one of the alternative operational limits specified in Section 601 below."

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Emission Limitations — Synthetic Minor Cap (Section 301)

> "No stationary source subject to this rule shall emit in every 12-month period more than the following quantities of emissions:
> 301.1 **50 percent of the major source thresholds for regulated air pollutants (excluding HAPs);**
> 301.2 **5 tons per year of a single HAP;**
> 301.3 **12.5 tons per year of any combination of HAPs; and**
> 301.4 50 percent of any lesser threshold for a single HAP as the U.S. EPA may establish by rule."

### Major Source Thresholds (Sections 206–207)

> "206.1 **100 tons per year (tpy)** of any regulated air pollutant;
> 206.2 **50 tpy** of volatile organic compounds or oxides of nitrogen for a federal ozone nonattainment area classified as serious, **25 tpy** for an area classified as severe, or **10 tpy** for an area classified as extreme; and
> 206.3 **70 tpy** of PM10 for a federal PM10 nonattainment area classified as serious."

> "207 MAJOR SOURCE OF HAZARDOUS AIR POLLUTANTS: A stationary source that emits or has the potential to emit **10 tons per year or more of a single HAP**… **25 tons per year or more of any combination of HAPs**…"

**Therefore the Rule 511 synthetic minor caps (50% of major source thresholds) work out to:**
- ≤ 50 tpy of any regulated air pollutant (non-HAP, in attainment areas)
- ≤ 5 tpy of a single HAP
- ≤ 12.5 tpy of any combination of HAPs

### De Minimis Exemption from Recordkeeping (Section 102)

Recordkeeping and reporting requirements do NOT apply to a source that emits:

> "102.1 In every 12-month period, the stationary source emits less than or equal to the following quantities of emissions:
> a. **5 tons per year** of a regulated air pollutant (excluding HAPs);
> b. **2 tons per year** of a single HAP;
> c. **5 tons per year** of any combination of HAPs; and
> d. **20 percent** of any lesser threshold for a single HAP that the U.S. EPA may establish by rule."

### Alternative Throughput-Based De Minimis (Section 102.2)

A source is also de minimis if at least 90% of its emissions come from operations below:
> "a. **1,400 gallons** of any combination of solvent-containing materials but no more than **550 gallons** of any one solvent-containing material (no specified halogenated solvents); or
> b. **750 gallons** of any combination of solvent-containing materials where the materials contain [methyl chloroform, methylene chloride, perchloroethylene, or trichloroethylene]…
> c. **4,400,000 gallons** of gasoline dispensed from equipment with Phase I and II vapor recovery systems;
> d. **470,000 gallons** of gasoline dispensed from equipment without Phase I and II vapor recovery systems;
> e. **1,400 gallons** of gasoline combusted;
> f. **16,600 gallons** of diesel fuel combusted;
> g. **500,000 gallons** of distillate oil combusted; or
> h. **71,400,000 cubic feet** of natural gas combusted."

### Potential to Emit Definition (Section 209)

> "POTENTIAL TO EMIT: The maximum capacity of a stationary source to emit a regulated air pollutant based on its physical and operational design. Any physical or operational limitation on the capacity of the stationary source to emit a pollutant, including air pollution control equipment and restrictions on hours of operation or on the type or amount of material combusted, stored, or processed, shall be treated as part of its design **only if the limitation is federally-enforceable**."

## Determination Notes

1. **Step 1 — Screen PTE:** Calculate uncontrolled (or APCO/federally-enforceable-control-limited) PTE for all regulated pollutants. If PTE < 100 tpy (non-HAP regulated) AND < 10 tpy single HAP AND < 25 tpy combined HAPs, the source is not major and Rule 511 likely does not apply.
2. **Step 2 — Rule 511 applicability:** If uncontrolled PTE meets or exceeds major source thresholds but actual emissions are below the Section 301 caps, the source qualifies to operate under Rule 511 (synthetic minor) with annual recordkeeping/reporting obligations.
3. **Step 3 — De minimis:** If actual emissions are ≤ 5 tpy regulated (non-HAP) and ≤ 2 tpy single HAP and ≤ 5 tpy combined HAPs, even the Rule 511 recordkeeping obligations are waived.
4. **Title V trigger:** If actual or PTE emissions exceed major source thresholds, the source must obtain a Title V permit under Rule 507 and Rule 511 does not apply (Section 104).
5. **Federally enforceable limits only:** Operational caps that reduce PTE count only if they are federally enforceable (e.g., permit conditions, not internal policies).
