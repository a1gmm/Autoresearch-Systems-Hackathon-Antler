# Feather River AQMD — Air District Skill

## District Overview

Feather River AQMD covers Yuba and Sutter Counties in California's Sacramento Valley. It is a small district that has adopted a focused set of local rules, primarily in the areas of open burning, stationary source emission standards, permit requirements, and new source review. The full approved rulebook is submitted to CARB and EPA as part of the California SIP.

**Key fact**: FRAQMD's rules are organized into district-wide rules (Regulations II, III, IV, VII, IX, X) plus legacy county-specific rules that remain in the SIP from the pre-consolidation era (Sutter County Regulation IV Rules 4.0–4.15; Yuba County Regulation IV Rules 4.0–4.5). The county-specific permit rules (especially Rule 4.3, Exemptions from Permit) carry essentially identical text.

## Decision Guide

**Does this project need an FRAQMD permit?**

1. Is it a **vehicle** (Vehicle Code definition) not carrying on-board emission-generating equipment? → **No permit needed** (Rule 4.3.a/b).
2. Is it equipment in a **1–2 family dwelling** exclusively? → **No permit needed** (Rule 4.3.c).
3. Is it one of the **named minor exemptions**: comfort HVAC, residential refrigeration, non-process water cooling towers, steam cleaners, metal/plastic/wood extruder presses, residential incinerators for paper/leaves, space heaters, food-prep kitchen equipment, mobile construction equipment (non-paver), or agricultural implements? → **No permit needed** (Rule 4.3.d–i).
4. Has the **APCO specifically designated this source category as minor significance** (Rule 4.3.h)? → Contact district to confirm.
5. If none of the above applies → **ATC required before construction; PTO required before operation** (Reg IV Rules 4.0–4.1).
6. Does the project trigger a **net emissions increase ≥ 23 lb/day** of any regulated non-CO pollutant (or ≥ 1,000 lb/day CO, or ≥ 50 tpy particulate for seasonal sources)? → **NSR review under Rule 10.1** also required, including BACT analysis.
7. Is the project a **major source** (typically ≥ 25 tpy VOC or NOx in nonattainment area)? → **Offsets required** under Rule 10.1.E.2.

**Key solvent/coating question**: Does the facility use solvents for surface prep/cleaning?
- If VOC content ≤ 25 g/L → exempt from Rule 3.14 requirements (but still needs permit if equipment otherwise requires one under Reg IV).
- If using aerosols < 160 fl oz/day → exempt from Rule 3.14.
- Otherwise → must comply with Rule 3.14 VOC limits.

## Notes on District Structure

- FRAQMD is a small district; it adopts relatively few local rules. Many emission standards (particularly for stationary sources) reference or are consistent with CARB statewide rules.
- The district has **no coating-specific permit exemption list** beyond the general Reg IV Rule 4.3 categories; coating booths and related equipment must be evaluated on a case-by-case basis under the standard permit criteria.
- Rule 3.15 (Architectural Coatings), Rule 3.19 (Vehicle Coating), Rule 3.20 (Wood Products Coating), Rule 3.21 (Boilers), Rule 3.22 (Stationary ICE), Rule 3.23 (Water Heaters) are all in the district-wide SIP compilation and may impose additional operational requirements independent of the permit need determination.
- Rule 4.8 (Further Information, amended 8/3/2020) requires annual NOx/ROG emission statements from any source emitting ≥ 25 tpy of either pollutant.
