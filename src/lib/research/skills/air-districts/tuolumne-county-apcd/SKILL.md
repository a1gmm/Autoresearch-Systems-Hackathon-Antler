## District Overview

**Tuolumne County Air Pollution Control District (APCD)**
Sonora, CA — Sierra Nevada foothills district
Ozone nonattainment (2015 8-hour NAAQS, designated 2018)
Primary nonattainment pollutants: VOC, NOx (ozone precursors)

## One-Line Decision Guide

1. **Does the equipment appear on the Rule 202 exemption list?** (piston engines, comfort HVAC, space heaters, welding, food prep, <250 MMBtu/hr natural-gas boilers, identical replacements, agricultural implements, etc.) → **No permit required.**
2. **Not exempt?** → ATC required before construction; PTO required before operation (Rules 201, 501).
3. **Potential to emit ≥ 100 tpy VOC or NOx?** → Federal NSR applies (Rule 429); BACT + ERC offsets required; public notice required before ATC.
4. **Using architectural coatings with photochemically reactive solvents in containers ≥1 quart?** → Prohibited (Rule 422); switch to compliant coating.
5. **Disposing of >1.25 gallons/day of photochemically reactive solvent by evaporation?** → Prohibited (Rule 423); engineer a closed or capture system.

## Key Thresholds at a Glance

| Threshold | Rule | Trigger |
|-----------|------|---------|
| Any emission-causing equipment | Rule 201 | ATC + PTO required |
| <250 MMBtu/hr (natural gas/LPG only) | Rule 202.f | Boiler/heater exempt from permit |
| 100 tpy potential to emit (VOC or NOx) | Rule 429 | Federal NSR / major source ATC |
| 40 tpy significant increase (VOC or NOx) | Rule 429 / 40 CFR 51.165 | Major modification NSR trigger |
| 1.25 gallons/day | Rule 423 | Solvent evaporation disposal limit |
| Containers ≥1 quart with reactive solvents | Rule 422 | Architectural coating prohibition |

## Notes for Agents

- Tuolumne County is a **small district** with a compact rulebook. It does not have district-specific rules for many categories (e.g., no standalone diesel engine rule, no graphic arts rule, no numerical VOC g/L limits beyond the photochemical reactivity prohibition).
- For sources not covered by district-specific rules, **CARB statewide regulations** apply (e.g., CARB engine standards, CARB SCM for coatings).
- The county's ATC/PTO structure (Regulation II, Rules 201–213) is older SIP-era language; the updated Regulation IV/V framework (Rules 401+, 500+) mirrors it with cleaner modern text — both sets are in the EPA compiled PDF.
- Rule 429 NSR adopted 2021 is the most recently adopted rule and is the operative rule for major source review.
- ATC expires 2 years from application date (Rule 205); permits are non-transferable (Rule 203).
