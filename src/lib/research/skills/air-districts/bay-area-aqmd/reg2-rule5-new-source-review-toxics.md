## Purpose / Applicability

Per Section 2-5-101: "The purpose of this rule is to provide for the review of new and modified sources of toxic air contaminant (TAC) emissions in order to evaluate potential public exposure and health risk, to mitigate potentially significant health risks resulting from these exposures, and to provide net health risk benefits by improving the level of control when existing sources are modified or replaced."

Applies to any new or modified source of TACs that is already required to have an A/C or P/O under Regulation 2, Rule 1. It is a second-layer review on top of the standard permitting requirement — a source that is exempt from Regulation 2, Rule 1 permitting is also exempt from Rule 5.

Adopted June 15, 2005; last amended December 15, 2021.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Section 2-5-110 — Exemption, Low Emission Levels

> "A project (and each new or modified source included in this project) shall not be subject to this rule if, for each toxic air contaminant, total project emissions are below the acute and chronic trigger levels listed in Table 2-5-1 Toxic Air Contaminant Trigger Levels."

Trigger Level is defined in Section 2-5-223 as:

> "The emission threshold level for each TAC, as identified in Table 2-5-1 Toxic Air Contaminant Trigger Levels, below which the resulting health risks are not expected to cause, or contribute significantly to, adverse health effects."

(Table 2-5-1 lists substance-specific trigger levels and is maintained and updated periodically by the APCO — see current version on the BAAQMD website.)

### Section 2-5-114 — Limited Exemption, Modified Source with No Increase in Toxicity Weighted Emissions

> "The provisions of Section 2-5-401 shall not apply to a modified source, if the post-modification toxicity weighted emissions are less than or equal to the pre-modification toxicity weighted emissions."

### Section 2-5-301 — TBACT Requirement

> "The applicant shall apply TBACT to any new or modified source of TACs where the source risk is a cancer risk greater than **1.0 in one million (10⁻⁶ or 1.0E-6)**, and/or a **chronic hazard index greater than 0.20**."

TBACT (Best Available Control Technology for Toxics) is defined in Section 2-5-205 as the most stringent emission control device or technique that has been successfully utilized or is technologically feasible for the source type, considering cost, non-air-quality impacts, and energy requirements.

### Section 2-5-302 — Project Risk Requirement (permit denial thresholds)

> "The APCO shall deny an Authority to Construct or Permit to Operate for any new or modified source of TACs if the project risk exceeds any of the following project risk limits:
> 302.1 A cancer risk of **10.0 in one million (10 × 10⁻⁶ or 10E-6)**; or for a project located within an Overburdened Community as defined in Regulation 2-1-243 (other than a project at an Essential Public Service), a cancer risk of **6.0 in one million (6.0 × 10⁻⁶ or 6.0E-6)**;
> 302.2 A **chronic hazard index of 1.0**;
> 302.3 An **acute hazard index of 1.0**."

### Section 2-5-303 — Net Project Risk Requirement

Same thresholds as 2-5-302 apply to net project risk at any receptor (accounts for contemporaneous health risk reduction projects).

### Section 2-5-401 — Health Risk Assessment (HRA) Requirements

> "An application for an Authority to Construct or Permit to Operate for any project subject to this rule shall contain an HRA conducted in accordance with Section 2-5-603 or the information necessary for the APCO to conduct an HRA."

## Determination Notes

1. **Trigger screen first**: Check whether TAC emissions for each substance are below Table 2-5-1 trigger levels. If yes for all TACs, Rule 5 does not apply — no HRA needed.

2. **TBACT threshold**: Source cancer risk > 1-in-a-million OR chronic HI > 0.20 triggers TBACT requirement (controls must be applied before the HRA is finalized).

3. **Permit denial thresholds**: Project cancer risk > 10-in-a-million (or 6-in-a-million in Overburdened Community), chronic HI > 1.0, or acute HI > 1.0 — the APCO must deny the permit. These are hard limits; there is no offset mechanism for TAC risk under this rule.

4. **Overburdened Communities**: Defined in Regulation 2-1-243 — stricter 6-in-a-million cancer risk threshold applies. Check district maps for facility location.

5. **Modified source shortcut**: If post-modification toxicity weighted emissions ≤ pre-modification, the HRA requirement of § 2-5-401 is waived (§ 2-5-114), though TBACT still applies if source risk thresholds are met.

6. **Stacking with Rule 2**: Sources with HAP emissions may also face MACT requirements under Regulation 2, Rule 2, Section 317 — independent analysis required.
