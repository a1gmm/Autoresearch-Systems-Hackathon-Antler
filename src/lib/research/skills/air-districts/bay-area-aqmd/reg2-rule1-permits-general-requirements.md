## Purpose / Applicability

Regulation 2, Rule 1 (General Requirements) establishes the core permitting framework for the Bay Area AQMD. Per Section 2-1-101: "The purpose of Regulation 2 is to provide an orderly procedure for the review of new sources of air pollution, and of the modification and operation of existing sources, and of associated air pollution control devices, through the issuance of authorities to construct and permits to operate."

The rule applies to any article, machine, equipment or other contrivance whose use may cause, reduce or control the emission of air contaminants — unless a specific exemption under Sections 2-1-103 through 2-1-128 applies.

Adopted January 1, 1980; last amended December 15, 2021.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Section 2-1-301 — Authority to Construct (A/C)

> "Any person who, after July, 1972, puts in place, builds, erects, installs, modifies, modernizes, alters or replaces any article, machine, equipment or other contrivance, the use of which may cause, reduce or control the emission of air contaminants, shall first secure written authorization from the APCO in the form of an authority to construct. Routine repairs, maintenance, or cyclic maintenance that includes replacement of components with identical components is not considered to be an alteration, modification or replacement for the purpose of this Section unless the APCO determines the changes to be non-routine."

### Section 2-1-302 — Permit to Operate (P/O)

> "Before any person, as described in Section 2-1-401, uses or operates any article, machine, equipment or other contrivance, the use of which may cause, reduce or control the emission of air contaminants, such person shall first secure written authorization from the APCO in the form of a permit to operate."

### Accelerated Permitting Program (Section 2-1-302.2) — Expedited temporary P/O available when:

> "The source will not have the potential to emit POC, NPOC, NOx, SO₂, PM₂.₅, PM₁₀, or CO in an amount of 10 pounds or more on any day, determined without taking into account the effect of any abatement device or equipment; or the source has been pre-certified under Section 2-1-415; and … The source will not have the potential to emit toxic air contaminants in an amount that exceeds any of the trigger levels set forth in Table 2-5-1 of Regulation 2, Rule 5"

### Section 2-1-103 — Exemption, Source not Subject to any District Rule

A source already not covered by specific exemptions (Sections 2-1-105 to 2-1-128) is nonetheless exempt from A/C and P/O if **all** of:

> "103.1 The source is not in a source category subject to any of the provisions of Regulation 6(1), Regulation 8(2) excluding Rules 1 through 4, or Regulations 9 through 12; and
> 103.2 The source is not subject to any of the provisions of Sections 2-1-316 through 319; and
> 103.3 Actual emissions of precursor organic compounds (POC), non-precursor organic compounds (NPOC), nitrogen oxides (NOx), sulfur dioxide (SO₂), PM₂.₅, PM₁₀ and carbon monoxide (CO) from the source are each (i) less than 10 pounds per highest day; or (ii) if greater than 10 pounds per highest day, total emissions are less than 150 pounds per year, per pollutant; and
> 103.4 The source is not an ozone generator (a piece of equipment designed to generate ozone) emitting 1 lb/day or more of ozone."

### Section 2-1-113 — Exemption, Sources and Operations (statutory / categorical)

Statutory exemptions per California Health and Safety Code (Section 2-1-113.1) include:
- Single/multiple family dwellings used solely for residential purposes
- Agricultural sources with actual emissions of each regulated air pollutant (excl. fugitive dust, GHGs) **less than 50 tons per year**
- Any vehicle (and attached equipment that is integral to the vehicle)

Additional district exemptions (Section 2-1-113.2) include:
- Road construction, widening and rerouting
- Restaurants, cafeterias and other retail establishments for preparing food for human consumption
- Structural changes which do not change the quality, nature or quantity of air contaminant emissions
- Architectural and industrial maintenance coating operations exclusively subject to Regulation 8, Rules 3 or 48 (applied to stationary structures)
- Teaching laboratories used exclusively for classroom experimentation/demonstration
- Laboratories in buildings where total lab floor space is **less than 25,000 sq ft** OR total fume hoods **less than 50**, using Responsible Laboratory Management Practices

### Section 2-1-114 — Exemption, Combustion Equipment

> Boilers, heaters, steam generators, duct burners: exempt if **less than 1 million BTU/hr** rated heat input; or **less than 10 million BTU/hr** if fired exclusively with natural gas or LPG.
> Internal combustion engines and gas turbines: exempt if **maximum output rating ≤ 50 bhp**; or portable engines at a location **less than 72 consecutive hours**.

### Section 2-1-118 — Exemption, Surface Preparation and Cleaning Equipment

Key thresholds:
> "118.4 Equipment, including solvent cold cleaners using an unheated solvent mixture for surface preparation, cleaning, wipe cleaning, fluxing or stripping by use of solutions with a VOC content **less than or equal to 50 grams per liter (0.42 lb/gal)**."
> "118.9 Wipe cleaning at a facility that meets one of the following: 9.1 net cleanup solvent usage **less than 20 gallons per year** from all wipe cleaning operations; or 9.2 emission to the atmosphere of **less than 150 pounds per year** of uncontrolled VOC from all wipe cleaning operations."

### Section 2-1-119 — Exemption, Surface Coating and Printing Equipment

> "119.2 Any coating, adhesive, dipping, laminating, screening, masking, electrodeposition, resist application, or similar source or operation at any facility that is not operated or conducted as part of a graphic arts operation, which: 2.1 Consumes a total of **less than 30 gallons** of coating, adhesive, laminate or resist per year on a facility wide basis, or emits **less than 150 pounds per year** of uncontrolled VOC on a facility wide basis … or 2.2 Uses exclusively materials that contain **less than one percent VOC (wt)**."
> "119.5 Any graphic arts operation that emits **less than 400 pounds of uncontrolled VOC emissions per month** on a facility-wide basis."

### Section 2-1-120 — Exemption, Dry Cleaning Equipment

> "Any dry cleaning facility which uses (gross consumption) **less than 200 gallons of petroleum solvent or any other non-halogenated solvent in any single year** is exempt … the equipment does not use solvent that contains perchloroethylene or more than 1% by weight of any other halogenated compound."

## Determination Notes

1. **Default rule**: Any new, modified, or replaced equipment that may emit air contaminants requires both an A/C (before construction) and a P/O (before operation).

2. **Exemption cascade**: Work through Section 2-1-113 general exemptions first (residential, restaurants, vehicles), then equipment-specific exemptions (§§ 2-1-114 through 2-1-128). If no specific exemption matches, apply the catch-all Section 2-1-103 thresholds (10 lb/day per pollutant OR 150 lb/year per pollutant, plus must not be in a Regulation 8 source category).

3. **Graphic arts carve-out**: Coating/printing exemptions under § 2-1-119.2 explicitly exclude graphic arts operations — those operations must instead qualify under § 2-1-119.5 (< 400 lb/month facility-wide uncontrolled VOC) or comply with Regulation 8, Rule 20.

4. **Loss of exemption**: An exempt source that subsequently exceeds the applicable threshold loses its exemption and must obtain permits. Accelerated Permitting is available for low-emission new/modified sources (potential < 10 lb/day each criteria pollutant).
