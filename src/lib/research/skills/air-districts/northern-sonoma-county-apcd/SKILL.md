# Northern Sonoma County APCD — Skill Index

## About the District

NoSoCo Air is a small district serving the northern portion of Sonoma County (south boundary runs along the Rancho Estero Americano / Green Valley Road / Highway 116 corridor). It shares the North Coast Air Basin with Del Norte, Humboldt, Trinity, and Mendocino counties. The district is an **attainment/unclassified** district for all criteria pollutants, making it one of the least-restrictive permitting environments in California.

The district has a **very limited local rulebook** — no coating, solvent, graphic-arts, or fugitive-dust control rules exist. VOC and particulate-matter operations are evaluated exclusively under the general NSR/permit framework. State and federal rules (CARB ATCMs, NSPS, NESHAPs) apply directly.

## One-Line Decision Guide

**Does this equipment or operation need an NSCAPCD permit?**

1. **Exempt categories (Rule 200(d)):** Residential equipment (≤4-family dwellings), non-commercial BBQs, orchard heaters, agricultural equipment (unless ≥50 tpy aggregate or ATCM/NSPS applies), gasoline/organic liquid tanks < 250 gal, purely mechanical systems with no air contaminant emissions → **No permit needed.**

2. **De minimis discretionary exemption (Rule 200(d)(8)):** Potential emissions < 50% of Rule 130(s2) thresholds (e.g., < 20 tpy NOx, < 20 tpy VOC, < 12.5 tpy PM, < 7.5 tpy PM10) AND no applicable NSPS/NESHAP → **APCO may exempt; contact district for confirmation.**

3. **Standard ATC + PTO path:** Any source above those thresholds or with NSPS/NESHAP applicability → **Authority to Construct required before construction; Permit to Operate required before operation.**

4. **Full NSR (BACT + air quality analysis):** Net emissions increase ≥ Rule 130(s2) thresholds (e.g., ≥ 100 tpy CO, ≥ 40 tpy NOx/SO₂/VOC, ≥ 25 tpy PM) → **Full NSR review with BACT, public notice, 30-day comment period.**

5. **Temporary source (< 90 days):** NSR waived under Rule 260(a), but BACT still required.
