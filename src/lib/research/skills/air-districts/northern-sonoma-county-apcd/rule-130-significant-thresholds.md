## Purpose

Rule 130 provides definitions governing the entire NSCAPCD permitting regime. The most operationally significant definition is Rule 130(s2) — "Significant Emission Rates" — which sets the NSR (New Source Review) and PSD (Prevention of Significant Deterioration) trigger thresholds used in Rule 220(b) review and the Rule 200(d)(8) de minimis exemption analysis.

## Key Provisions / Thresholds

### Rule 130(s2) — Significant Emission Rates

> "(s2) SIGNIFICANT: The potential of a new or modified stationary source to emit air contaminants that would equal or exceed any of the following rates: (Ref: 52.21(b)(23)(i))"

| Air Contaminant | Significant Emission Rate |
|---|---|
| Carbon monoxide | 100 tons per year (tpy) |
| Nitrogen oxide | 40 tpy |
| Sulfur dioxide | 40 tpy |
| Particulate matter | 25 tpy |
| PM10 | 15 tpy |
| PM2.5 (direct) | 10 tpy of direct PM2.5 emissions; 40 tpy of sulfur dioxide emissions; 40 tpy of nitrogen oxide emissions |
| Ozone | 40 tpy of volatile organic compounds or nitrogen oxides |
| Lead | 0.6 tpy |
| Fluorides | 3 tpy |
| Sulfuric acid mist | 7 tpy |
| Hydrogen sulfide (H₂S) | 10 tpy |
| Total reduced sulfur (including H₂S) | 10 tpy |
| Reduced sulfur compounds (including H₂S) | 10 tpy |
| Municipal waste combustors — organic emissions | 3.5 × 10⁻⁶ tons per year (measured as total tetra- through octa-chlorinated dibenzo-p-dioxins and dibenzofurans) |
| Municipal waste combustors — metals | 15 tons per year (measured as particulate matter) |
| Municipal waste combustors — acid gas | 40 tons per year (measured as sulfur dioxide and hydrogen chloride) |
| Municipal solid waste landfills — nonmethane organic compounds | 50 tons per year |

> "Notwithstanding the above significant emission rates for various air contaminants, significant also means any net emission increase from any new or modified stationary source which would be constructed within 10 kilometers of a Class I area and have an air quality impact on such area equal to or greater than 1 microgram per cubic meter (24 hour average). (Ref: 52.21(b)(23)(iii))"

**Note on Class I area:** The only Class I area within 300 km of NSCAPCD is all lands encompassed within the Point Reyes National Seashore.

### Major Stationary Source — Rule 130(m2)

> "(m2) MAJOR STATIONARY SOURCE: The definition of the term 'Major stationary source' as defined in 40 CFR 52.21(b)(1), which is otherwise incorporated by reference for the purpose of Rules 200 and 220, shall be revised to read as: Any stationary source which emits, or has the potential to emit, a regulated pollutant above which exceeds the significant emission rates specified in Rule 130(s2)."

### Actual Emissions — Rule 130(a1)

> "(a1)(ii) In general, actual emissions as of a particular date shall equal the average rate, in tons per year, at which the unit actually emitted the pollutant during a two-year period which precedes the particular date and which is representative of normal source operation."

## Determination Notes

- **De minimis exemption link:** Rule 200(d)(8) exempts sources emitting less than **50% of the Rule 130(s2) thresholds**, subject to APCO discretion. Example: a source emitting < 20 tpy NOx or < 20 tpy VOC, with no federal NSPS/NESHAP applicability, could qualify for exemption.
- **NSR trigger:** A new or modified source with a net emissions increase meeting or exceeding any Rule 130(s2) threshold triggers full NSR (BACT analysis, air quality analysis, public notice) under Rule 220(b).
- **No local VOC significance threshold:** The district uses the federal PSD ozone surrogate of 40 tpy VOC to trigger NSR — there is no lower local threshold.
- **Greenhouse Gases:** GHG significance thresholds reference Rules 221.4.1–221.4.3 and are incorporated for Rule 220 only, not into the SIP.
