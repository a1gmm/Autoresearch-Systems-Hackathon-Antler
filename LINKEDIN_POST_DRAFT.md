## Version A — story-led (recommended)

72 hours, one whiteboard, and a question that wouldn't leave me alone: *why does staying on top of research still feel like a second full-time job?*

This weekend at the **Antler hackathon**, our team built **Autoresearch** — an AI research agent that does the hardest part of research *for* you. You give it a direction or a field; it goes out, reads the frontier, and comes back with the most relevant, cutting-edge findings — plus concrete suggestions for where to take your work next.

Under the hood it's a "deep research" pipeline wired to a clean chat interface: pick a topic → the agent surfaces and synthesizes the latest work → you get a digest *and* next-step recommendations, not just a pile of links.

What I'm taking away from the weekend isn't just the demo. It's how far a small, focused team can go when everyone trusts each other to move fast. Grateful to my teammates and to Antler for the push. 🙏

We're continuing to build on this. If autonomous research agents are something you think about — as a researcher, a builder, or a skeptic — I'd love to hear from you. 👇
